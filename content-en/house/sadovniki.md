## The Order's Tradition (Father David's Account)

In late May 1775, [[otets-david|Father David]] told [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] a history the order had passed down only by word of mouth for centuries. Scripture, he said, lied in claiming that complete harmony had reigned among the Forebears.

In year 69 after the Descent, when Faunterra flourished, Orwell and Arden grew, Wilhelm the Great proclaimed himself King of Polaris, and the “cunning” Yanmei became his infallible adviser, four Forebears — two Forefathers and two Foremothers — observed the Star and saw “another path of development,” harder and more dangerous than the one Wilhelm and Yanmei were following, but offering an unprecedented reward. They called themselves Gardeners: the great goal was like sweet fruit at the top of a tree that had yet to be planted and grown.

Wilhelm and Yanmei rejected their path. Wilhelm's kairs seized and destroyed the Gardeners' books, and the law threatened those who spread the heresy with burning if they were commoners and exile to the western steppes if they were firstborn. The Gardeners fell silent, but the first wave of disciples had already received their knowledge. The disciples passed it on: through the Ages of Heresy, when the teaching might have been lost among hundreds of cults; through the age of the Lightbearers, when the Agathans burned away everything except Agatha's teaching; through the Sweet Centuries under the Third Dark-Eyed Dynasty, when the well-fed nobility lost interest in faith and poor commoners preserved the teaching; and through the Crimson Turmoil, into the new Yanmeian kingdom.

Three attempts were made to write the teaching down: under Wilhelm, the result was the burning of books; under the Lightbearers, twice, it was the burning of their authors. From then on, the Gardeners' followers did not trust paper. Persecution weakened over time, but faith faded too, and to protect themselves and overcome unbelief, the disciples withdrew into the shadows and became a secret order. Of the founders, only Forefather Maximian is known; the other three concealed their names to protect their children from persecution. David expressly said he could not name Agatha the Radiant among them, however tempting that might be.

## Organisation

Initiation proceeds by degrees. First, adepts observe a candidate for a long time and, once convinced that the candidate is ready, tell the history of the order: this is the first degree. For four years, the novice serves the order and learns that its principal concern is knowledge: it studies the sciences, distributes books, opens schools, helps scholars and inventors, and conducts its own experiments, the most important of which involve Sacred Objects. Once the novice understands that science will be the key to the great goal, the novice becomes an adept. At least another four years are needed to comprehend and accept the goal itself, which “the unprepared human mind” rejects as nonsense; only then does the adept become a master.

According to information Erwin obtained from Mireille Nen-Clair, there are four degrees of initiation: her mother had brought Mireille to the third, but the final, fourth degree was never revealed to her, meaning the order has another secret. There are three supreme masters; the eldest and wisest is the [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]], Queen of Darkwater. [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] holds the highest degree of initiation, though he joined only in 1775, after spending eight years in a monastery. Members of the order call one another “brothers.” The Second of the Five — Count [[kuindar|Quindar]], abbot of Maximian-at-the-Abyss and “keeper of the Bottomless Chasm” — belonged to the order and had once invited Francis-Ilian to join. Maximian monks participate in the order; the Lady in the Dark's trusted vassals in Nen-Clair, Father Loris, Admiral Fanno Grok, and Lord Emmon Darklin, apparently belonged to it too, speaking of the Tree as a shared undertaking. Neither Tellurian nor Adrian initially belonged to the order: according to David, Adrian valued progress but was “excessively despotic,” while the order dreamed of a world “subject to knowledge and law, not a tyrant's will.” Sovereign Ingrid was not initiated, but helped the order. Priarch [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] learned of it through informants; attempts were made to recruit him, but about a year earlier he had turned away.

## Knowledge and Methods

According to information Francis-Ilian gathered during his years of seclusion, the order had suggested the idea of the wave to imperial scholars while remaining in the shadows; learned to manufacture convincing imitations of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]]; acquired “highly secretive enemies”; and discovered remarkable properties of Shimmering Objects. The order also possessed genuine Objects: Francis-Ilian's palace housed the [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]], an original Object of Elena the Traveller.

Gardening imagery permeates the masters' speech. The Lady in the Dark told Erwin that the Empire, “like a tree,” grows towards the light, and that “one gardener will cultivate a great garden; seven gardeners will destroy it”; to Minerva, she spoke of “the gardener's skill” in knowing when to let a garden rest. In Nen-Clair, the phrase “The Tree will grow” serves as a formula by which the Lady in the Dark and her vassals affirm their adherence to the plan.

## Activities in the Series

### Before 1775

- For decades, the order hunted [[navi|Nathaniel]], tortured and killed witnesses, and later experimented with the blood he had left behind when he staged his suicide. For a long time, the masters believed Nathaniel and [[paul|Paul]] were the same person.
- The order's counterfeit [[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]] inadvertently disrupted the Puppeteer's plans (see [[lzhe-sfera]]).

### The Gates of Trust

When it emerged in February 1775 that Manson had been forced to kill Adrian with [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]] on the day the bridge collapsed, the Lady in the Dark's vassals panicked: the priarch had “strayed from the path” and “possessed the Secret”; “the plan is collapsing, the Tree is shaking”; “the Serpent is becoming too strong”; it was necessary to “use the last resort”; and the Seventeenth Gift had “changed everything.” The queen stopped the panic: the Second of the Five had “acted wilfully,” it would not happen again, and they must “follow the plan.” “The Serpent means nothing to the Tree”; the Seventeenth Gift had only brought the day of Salvation closer. On the way to the capital, Francis-Ilian was initiated and learned the two greatest secrets: what Tree the brothers were cultivating, and what rupture threatened Polaris in three hundred years if the Tree did not bind the world together with its roots.

### The Yanmei Hunt

David confirmed Erwin's suspicion: the Lady in the Dark's “conspiracy” against Minerva, proposed to Erwin on the train, and her “illness” had been tests of Erwin and [[minerva|Minerva]] — the queen had been choosing an ally — while Francis-Ilian's provocations at Manson's trial served the same purpose (“mice are needed to test the cat,” as Erwin himself put it). David befriended [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] and learned which Object the Puppeteer was seeking, enabling the Lady in the Dark to set a trap in the burial vault. According to him, the Lady in the Dark sent [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] the warning that triggered the [[perevorot-v-uejmare|rebellion in Waymar]]. General [[aleksis-smajl|Silver Fox]] admitted to Minerva that he served a “second mistress” with “bright and noble” aims — apparently the order. Minerva later acknowledged with irritation that the order had “used” her: the masters had tempted her with boundless progress, the triumph of science, and universal education.

### Humans and Gods

On 1 June 1775, the Lady in the Dark, Francis-Ilian, and David offered to help Erwin assemble a coalition of lords. In return, the masters wanted victory over the Puppeteer, the recovery of the Dynasty's Objects, and a “tribute of respect” to the order: Paul handed over alive, whom they believed had arrived “from other turns of the [[vselenskaya-spiral|universal spiral]].” Francis-Ilian reminded him that the order had existed for seventeen centuries.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree

Through Count Quindar, [[adrian|Adrian]] learned the order's goal. He gave the Lady in the Dark the Radiant Sphere and the [[poyas-kolduna|Sorcerer's Belt]], and supplied Shavans with firstblood; in the autumn of 1775, the masters recognised him as sovereign. For experiments with the [[chrevo|Womb]] and the Bird without Flesh, Adrian used an Object from the University of Faunterra, the Bird sent by Francis-Ilian, and Darkwater essence of moon-eye from the queen's servants. When the Bird finally activated, Adrian, the king, and the Second of the Five “laughed with happiness”: according to Adrian, they saw that the Forefather Gardeners' goal was still attainable and the Great Tree would “bear fruit as early as next year.” Together with Francis-Ilian and Quindar, he spent hundreds of hours over sacred texts, researching the [[absolyut|Absolute]]. When the Lady in the Dark swore allegiance to Adrian, David assured Erwin that the order “takes no one's side” and wants only to grow the Tree, after which wars will cease. Through David, the masters demanded that Erwin hand Nathaniel over.

## The Goal: The “Great Tree”

Different versions of the order's goal are presented in the series:

1. **David's version** (November 1775): the Gifts fall from the Star, their resting places are impact marks, and the Objects themselves are enclosed in a casing that burns up during the fall. The order wants to ascend to the Star, drop millions of Objects to the ground, and use Paul's or Nathaniel's [[pervokrov|firstblood]] to give every person a speaking Object. According to David, the Forefather Gardeners left clues leading to the Star. Erwin considered this a lie, “too simple” for the secret: eight years in a monastery were not needed to memorise the phrase “Objects fall from the Star.”
2. **Mireille Nen-Clair's version**, from the daughter of the Lady in the Dark, who had renounced the order (December 1775): the First Tree was the work of the Foremothers, who carried three “reactors” across the continent to the Bottomless Chasm, Smoky Distance, and beneath the valley of First Winter, creating the cloud that made the continent habitable. “The Second Tree will turn humans into gods.” She called David's account “almost” correct. Mireille warned that the order would seek to obtain the [[absolyut|Absolute]], a bearer of firstblood, and Nathaniel.
3. **Navi's version** (at the end of the series): the Gardeners were several crew members of the wrecked colony ship who, observing the false beacon on the Star, calculated that its fuel would last two thousand one hundred years. The order's goal is to build a flying machine within that time, ascend to the beacon, and change its signal so the “gods'” ships can find Polaris. The task seemed impossible: no flying craft had survived the crash, and orbit had become “as distant as a neighbouring galaxy.” Navi warned that such contact would turn the people of Polari into playthings or animals in a reserve: the descendants of the “gods” would not recognise them as their own species.

Adrian, who had seen the dead “goddess” of the Seventeenth Gift as a prince in 1756, called the order's goal his life's work, said that the essence of the Second Tree was “to reach the Star in twenty-one centuries,” and begged Minerva to “let him grow the Tree.” After hearing Navi, Francis-Ilian began to doubt: was it the Second Tree the Foremothers had warned him about in his dream of the world's destruction?

## Position at the End of the Series

In December 1775, [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] broke the Lady in the Dark's neck in revenge for his father's abduction and torture in 1765. According to Navi, however, the order had “suffered losses but had not been destroyed”: if Navi remained alive, there was about an 80 % chance that it would obtain his knowledge and, after receiving the Absolute from Adrian, change the beacon's signal.

## In Fantasies

In Fantasies (which the author describes as possible destinies for the characters), the Maximians and Evrians, led by the Second of the Five, declared Navi a god and Adrian's cause holy, although they were hindered by the contradiction that Navi was helping Minerva, not Adrian. Infanta [[demetra-nellis-lajon|Demetra]] complained that her father Francis-Ilian, “carried away with the Tree,” had forgotten his promise not to force her into marriage. When Mireille Nen-Clair came to Laem in the summer of 1776, she wore a pendant bearing Stagfort's white seagull, although, as Katrin Katrin observed, she had formerly worn one “with a mystical tree.”

## Interesting Facts

- The Foltians believe Polaris will split into pieces in three hundred years; the Lady in the Dark acknowledged that this belief “echoes” the danger the order foresees.
- Almost as soon as he became accustomed to Polaris, Navi deduced from “indirect signs” that a secret order must exist there.
