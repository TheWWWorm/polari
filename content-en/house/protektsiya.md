## Organisation

The Protection is the secret guard of the emperors of Polari. Secretary [[itan|Ethan]] told [[minerva|Mira]] that Sovereign Tellurian had created it a couple of months after the [[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]], which had been uncovered by Duke Almera rather than the secret guard. After the conspiracy, the formerly kind and gentle sovereign changed greatly; he was credited with saying that a conspiracy, like the plague, could be stopped only with a wall of deaths, and in the three years after twenty-five conspirators were executed, four times as many people were arrested. Mira calls the Protection the “Brown Guard.” The formula “the sovereign has three guards: Scarlet, [[lazurnaya-gvardiya|Azure]], and Invisible” is spoken in Only One Star by the mercenary [[berk|Burke]] and his companions, who were merely posing as Protection agents to rob the peasants of Almera. The Protection deals only with threats to the Dynasty; ordinary crimes are the responsibility of sheriffs and constables, as Mark explicitly reminded Deputy Sheriff Fairfax.

According to Ethan, about five hundred agents served in Faunterra alone — at court, in the [[palata|Chamber]], among grandees and their servants, priests, and merchants. Mark's chapters state that more than a thousand people of every estate serve under the Raven — servants, warriors, secretaries, merchants, townspeople, and nobles — many of whom do not even know him by sight. Most serve for money or because they have “dirtied their hands,” and only a few, like Ethan, out of conviction: in Mark's opinion, the noble and brave join the guard. The Protection had its own informants at stations, who kept arrival and departure logs. At the Palace of Pen and Sword, the head of the Protection was counted among the sixteen senior officials in imperial service. Mark ate with the servants in the south wing's kitchen, handed out silver to the “whisperers” every day, and kept spies among the guards' squires, grooms, clerks, adjutants, and maids; he even bought people in Farway's and Shayland's entourages. The head of the Protection met Adrian personally once a week, over tea on Thursdays, to report on affairs.

The Protection's interrogation rooms are staffed by questioners: young Sammy, “not yet a complete scoundrel,” and the butcher's son Hog, who can barely write his own name. An agent nicknamed [[ryzhij|Red]] is reputed to be the capital's foremost expert on corpses. Agents travelled in the Protection's black carriages, which Karen Lightheart compared to a coffin.

## Methods

Mark valued deduction above confessions under torture, though torture was also practised in the Protection's cellars. He recruited those he arrested. In the summer of 1774, besides Aiden Almera's known servants and guards, the Protection captured sixty-five of his secret agents; Mark released everyone for whom any justification could be found, keeping a secret list and building a network of people he controlled through the threat of trial. Over tea, he reported on the traitor's accomplices at court: forty-two awaited trial, fifty-eight had been released but remained on record, and thirty-nine had repented and were serving as Protection agents. Adrian asked how this web was any better than Aiden's, and Mark replied: “Mine protects the Dynasty.” In Arrow, Coin, Spark, Mark's agents disguised themselves in the livery of Aiden Almera's guards to frighten a former jailer into handing over a document incriminating the duke of his own accord.

In the spring of 1775, in preparation for sessions of the Chamber, the Protection searched everyone entering the capital, with no exceptions for nobles or priests, under the guard of kairs. Each district was patrolled by trios consisting of a kair, a constable, and a local thief or beggar; a report about suspicious people earned a gloria, and any overheard conversation mentioning Ice, Paul, the Puppeteer, or Rib earned an elena. On Erwin's orders, the Protection intercepted and copied all the Chamber lords' pigeon and courier mail in search of a message to the Puppeteer; Mark and Grandfather directed the interception, and only Kair Horton was allowed to open the letters. Erwin reproached the Raven for being slow: “You have five hundred subordinates!”

## History

### Under Tellurian and Adrian

For six years leading up to 1774, the Protection was headed by [[mark-voron|Mark]], who earned the nickname Raven of the Crown: three years under Tellurian and three under [[adrian|Adrian]]. In Arrow, Coin, Spark, he investigates Sir [[adamar|Adamar]]'s death and uncovers [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]'s conspiracy; his agent at court was Secretary Ethan, who had been assigned to Mira. In the summer of 1774, the secret guard hunted down the fugitive Aiden's spies and investigated the disappearance of a charter of enfeoffment from the archive. At the time, [[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]], captain of the Scarlet Guard, called the Protection “a useless pack of jackals” before the sovereign and advised him to disband it.

### Mark's Fall from Favour (1774)

The secret guard failed to detect preparations for [[myatezh-oridzhina|Origin's rebellion]]. For this, Adrian sent Mark to [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] and handed his network of agents to Beckfield. Under Beckfield, the Protection intercepted the northerners' letters, but according to Red it declined because everything was run along army lines. During the northerners' assault on the capital, Beckfield mobilised everyone, down to constables and bandits, secretly removed [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] to the [[ostrov-faradeya-rajli|Faraday-Riley clinic]], and then fled. After Beckfield's flight, as [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] observes in Useful People, the Protection's leadership collapsed and no replacement was appointed; deprived of their wages, the “whisperers” became eager for money. Buckley bought arrival logs from a station informant for a hundred and forty hilts. Reading the daily [[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]] that winter, Mark noted that no replacement for Beckfield had yet been found.

### Beckfield and Whitmore's Protection

In the winter of 1775, the fugitive Beckfield joined forces with [[uitmor|Whitmore]], captain of the Azure Guard, who was searching for the stolen [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects of the Dynasty]]; the detachment in blue cloaks that attacked Mark's train consisted of their men. Beckfield, still styling himself head of the Protection, later handed the witness [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]] over to Minerva in the hope of securing her patronage. The major claimed that Minerva had promised him the post of head of the Protection if he brought her Sybil Northwood.

### Under Lord Chancellor Origin (1775)

In the spring of 1775, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] reappointed Mark as head of the Protection and made the retired northern judge [[ded|Grandfather]] his deputy, responsible for liaison between the secret guard and the army; Kair [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] supervised the Protection. It intercepted pigeons, conducted surveillance at court, and carried out investigations; on the palace roofs, Kair Dagobert, the former forest bandit Merritt “the Hawk,” and Agent Pike shot down pigeons. Mark acted as prosecutor at [[sud-nad-mensonom|Manson's trial]], while Protection agents arranged mannequins and a screen on the stage for identification experiments. During the trial, Francis-Ilian deliberately elicited from Minerva that the Protection answered to the lord chancellor and that Erwin had appointed Mark both head of the Protection and prosecutor; meanwhile, in Waymar, Lady Nexia Flame remarked sarcastically to Iona that the Protection had still not found the stolen Objects. When the Chamber discussed an empire-wide investigative service (a sacred guard), the Protection offered to assume that role, but the lords objected: it answered to the Crown, and no Great House wanted “the sovereign's bloodhounds” prowling through its lands.

### Under Minerva

While serving [[minerva|Minerva]], Mark acted on the duke's orders to block roads, bridges, and river ports along [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade's route to Waymar. In Arden, Protection agents watched the Temple of Farewell, and in June 1775 the secret guard arrested Beckfield, Sybil Northwood, [[navi|Navi]], and [[karen|Karen Lightheart]] there. Later, four guardsmen and as many Protection agents kept Navi under constant watch in the palace; the agents also detained other suspicious people, including [[veselchak|Jolly]], who was looking for Kair Forty-Two. They intercepted letters too: one of the reports Lady Sophia sent from her sea expedition was intercepted in Faunterra and delivered to the empress. The Raven's men searched the court archives for 1763–1765 for information about Navi, then traced him to Patrick's Chapel through witnesses at stagecoach stations. On Minerva's secret orders, Mark disabled her train at Patrick's Chapel and arranged a leak to lure Johan Reis's Shavans into the path of Adrian's army.

### Adrian's Return (August 1775)

Before Adrian's return, Mark questioned [[kharmon|Harmon]] about the sovereign's plans; Harmon replied that Adrian had called Beckfield a faithful fighter on whom the capital had depended during the Northern Flare. After occupying the palace, Adrian's men reported that the Raven of the Crown had disappeared, some agents had fled with him, and others were ready to serve a new master; they proposed releasing Beckfield from the citadel and restoring him to office. Adrian agreed. Mark himself submitted his resignation to Minerva at Patrick's Chapel, and his agents left with Origin's kairs. When Adrian marched towards First Winter, he promoted Beckfield to colonel and left him to guard Faunterra with a spark battalion and five thousand toughs.

## In Fantasies

In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], which the author describes as possible destinies for the characters, the secret guard serves Adrian, now burgomaster of Faunterra, in the spring of 1776. Its current head was Guards Colonel Croydon Beckfield, assisted by the retired Sergeant Hand Dodge, leader of a “pack of former bandits,” utterly devoted to Adrian and reporting to him on the mood of the common people. In the story The Device, Adrian sent them to recover the stolen plans for Professor Nicholas Ollie's sound-recording device. The agents found signs that the lock had been forced, while Beckfield accused the professor himself of serving Minerva. At the university, students and the porter, Eugene, beat Dodge's toughs and threw them out of a window. Beckfield surrendered his rapier; it was broken, and the students hung the fragment on the wall as a trophy. Meanwhile, by all indications, the former Raven of the Crown had become the private lawman Macfrid Crow.

## Known Agents

- [[mark-voron|Mark Frieda Stanley]] — the Raven of the Crown.
- [[itan|Ethan Gladys Norma]] — the emperor's fifth secretary and Mark's agent by conviction.
- [[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]] — captain, later major, of the Scarlet Guard, nicknamed the “Red Sickle”; head of the Protection after Mark's fall from favour and again under the returned Adrian (a colonel from autumn 1775).
- [[ded|Grandfather]] — deputy head of the Protection in 1775.
- [[ryzhij|Red]] — an agent and pathologist, a witness at Manson's trial; he removed Inzhi Price's shackles when Price was released.
- Pike — an agent involved in intercepting pigeons.
- Sammy and Hog — interrogators.

## Interesting Facts

- His subordinates called the Raven “chief”; at the trial, Red called Mark “chief” out of habit, then corrected himself to “the head of the Protection.”
- On retiring, Mark suggested that Minerva summon him through an advertisement in the Voice of the Crown.
