The **Great Houses** are the noblest lineages of the Empire of Polari. According to the imperial observer Philip Lowfert, “Houses that possess a patrimony of more than twenty Objects are called Great. There are only thirteen such houses,” while families with fifty Objects number “only eight in the whole world.” The Great Houses rule the lands of the empire apart from the [[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]: “of the thirteen Great Houses governing the lands of the empire…” [[minerva|Mira]] reflected. Their heads — dukes, counts, and kings — are called “territorial rulers” or great lords. Each of their lands sends representatives to the [[palata|Chamber]].

## Lands and Houses
The novel does not give a complete list of the thirteen Houses. The following are explicitly called Great Houses or represented in the Chamber:
- [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]] — dukes of the North from the lineage of Agatha the Radiant, ruling the [[oridzhin-zemlya|lands of Origin]]. They own 87 Objects. Their army consists of [[kajry|kairs]].
- [[dom-almera|House Almera]] — the richest duchy of the centre; Agatha's lineage.
- [[dom-labelin|House Labelin]] — dukes of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] (about 60 Objects).
- [[dom-litlend|House Litland]] — a land of Yanmei's lineage, neighbours of the Crown.
- [[dom-nortvud|House Northwood]] — counts of the northern forests, the “bears.”
- [[dom-shejland|House Shayland]] — counts of the Forest Edge and Riverside, former bankers. Many dispute their right to the title of Great House.
- [[dom-farvej|House Farway]] — rulers of [[nadezhda|Hope]] after the destruction of the [[dom-lajthart|Lighthearts]].
- House Hollywell — counts of [[kholliwell|Hollywell]] in the West.
- [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] — a southern kingdom ruled by a king of House Nellis-Lyon (a “House” in the Guide, and the “Great House of Nellis-Lyon” in one letter), while laws are passed by a Council of the Five greatest feudal lords.
- The Lady in the Dark's [[darkvoter|Darkwater]]. According to the Guide to the Lands of Polari, under a fourteenth-century vassalage treaty, the Great House of Darkwater bears a royal title, although in power and patrimony (38 Objects) it ranks alongside the counties.
- The Chamber also includes representatives of the County of [[rejs|Reis]], [[melnitsy|Mills]], and the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]].

Lady [[karen|Karen]], in the [[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark]], disputes Lowfert's account: a Great House is defined by its rule over land, not its number of Objects. According to her, the thirteen houses correspond only nominally to the thirteen vassal lands. In [[rejs|Reis]] and [[kholliwell|Hollywell]], steppe customs prevail, and the Shavans follow the strongest leader, not the holder of a title. [[melnitsy|Mills]] is ruled by a council of Barons Fairis, Minnis, and Ofert. Few nobles recognise the Counts of Shayland as great. Thus, by Karen's count, there are nine truly great houses: Shimmery, Litland, Darkwater, Hope, Almera, the Southern Way, the Sunset Coast, Northwood, and Origin. She also gives an example: Duke [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] has only thirty-two major Objects, whereas his vassal Count [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]] has fifty-one.

The Guide describes thirteen lands of the empire besides the Crownlands: Origin, the Southern Way, Northwood, Almera, Hope, Litland, Shimmery, Darkwater, Reis, Hollywell, Mills, the Sunset Coast, and Shayland. Their number matches that of the Great Houses and the calculation of seats in the Chamber. However, the Guide names Baron Fairis as the nominal ruler of Mills, with only 18 Objects, below the threshold for “greatness.” Nevertheless, the Fairis family “is called a Great House” and has representatives in the Chamber, while Fairis is the only baron among the great lords. Yet in the Chamber of 1775, Mills was represented by “Counts Fairis and Minnis.” The Guide calls the ruling houses of Reis and Mills merely nominal suzerains, and deliberately says nothing about the Sunset Coast.

**Lightheart**, which ruled Hope, was a former Great House. According to contemporaries, it was older than the Farways, the Labelins, and even the Illustrious Dynasty itself. The house was destroyed after the [[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]], and the Farways have ruled Hope since 1755. The Shaylands' right to the title of Great House was also disputed: in 1775, during discussion of the county's fate, it was said that “the present Shaylands cannot be called a Great House,” but others objected that one could not abolish a House defended by the Origins and honoured by a visit from the emperor himself.

## Objects and Prestige
[[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]] are the chief measure of a House's greatness. Erwin explained to Jemis that Great Houses “are constantly taking” Objects from one another, giving them as ransom and capturing them as trophies, but no self-respecting lord would sell a sacred treasure from the family patrimony. Houses boast of their Objects “as they do of clothes, luxurious palaces, ceremonial weapons, and past victories.” Every Object from the Gifts is described and entered in books, and the Great Houses possess copies of all the lists. Secret ownership of an Object is therefore pointless for a lord, which led Erwin to suspect that the Crown was behind the thefts.

## The Crown and the Great Houses
The Great Houses depend on the Crown in one way or another. For generations, the Dynasty has held a trump card against each. Litland receives its supply of spark from the Crownlands. Almera grows rich through trade with the capital. Hope is ruled by the old emperor's appointees. The West fears the spark infantry. The Southern Way needs protection from Origin, while the Origins constantly borrow from the emperor. The Dynasty's power also rested on the Great Houses balancing one another: the North was at odds with the West, Almera with Hope. Traditionally, only the Crownlands pay the Dynasty a regular tax, while the Great Houses fulfil their vassal obligations through military service.

The Great Houses can also restrain sovereigns. Gravely ill, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] reminded his son that history recorded two occasions when the Great Houses had forced an emperor to abdicate in favour of his heir because he “committed lawlessness and sacrilege.” Desmond demanded that Erwin swear not to begin a war until he was certain of Adrian's guilt.

### 1774
In 1774, Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]]'s reforms — universal taxation and [[zheleznaya-doroga|railways]] — were supported by only two of the thirteen Great Houses: Shayland and Almera. Neither the Southern Way and Litland, the Crown's closest neighbours, nor Hope, nor Origin, “the emperors' faithful blade for two hundred years,” appeared among “the sovereign's allies.” Coalitions formed around the choice of an imperial bride: several Great Houses stood behind each of the three candidates. Almera supported its heiress [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], while [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] was building an alliance of Origin, Northwood, Hope, and Litland.

Earlier that winter, over tea with Adrian, Erwin had assured the emperor that an alliance with the Houses he named would give the Crown “successful reforms and complete sole authority.” Shimmery, the Southern Way, Almera, and Shayland, he said, were positioned so that any rebellious land would be attacked “from at least two sides, by land and sea,” while the presence of kairs in the Crownlands would keep the rest in check. Adrian replied with a smile that, as a descendant of Yanmei, he was obliged to look after her interests.

In the autumn of 1774, after the burning of Evergard, Adrian abolished the Chamber and demanded personal oaths of allegiance from the lords. Erwin said then: “The Great Houses are living out their final days.” Origin's rebellion against the Crown (the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]) was an attempt by the Great Houses to defend their power.

### 1775
In the spring of 1775, the Chamber assembled again, and most lands sent their rulers in person. Its composition was scandalous: two Northwood brothers while their father was still alive, the rival king and prince of Shimmery, a Foltian in place of Darkwater's sick queen, and the slave trader Ganta Storm from Reis (see [[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]).

## Customs
According to the unwritten rules for choosing an empress, as described by [[rebekka-litlend|Becca Litland]], “for a century and a half, no emperor has married the heiress of a Great House” (“three centuries” in early editions of Arrow, Coin, Spark). The Crown does not want a threat from an empress's powerful relatives. A bride from Origin is considered impossible because that House is “too strong,” while Darkwater and Shayland are “too young.” The Great Houses maintain agents and dovecotes in other capitals.

The Great Houses compete in peaceful pursuits too. Of the thirteen Great Houses, eleven sent their knights to the [[turnir-v-aleridane|tournament]] in [[aleridan|Aleridan]] in the summer of 1773; only Shimmery and the Origins were absent. Applicants to [[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena's boarding school]] must know the Great Houses' heraldry, and Lady Theodora commissioned portraits of all the students from Great Houses, accumulating more than two hundred.

## In Fantasies
In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], stories about possible destinies of the characters in 1776, the balance among the Great Houses changes after the war.
- **The North.** In March 1776, [[adrian|Adrian]] considered the North unready for an election: Northwood was in turmoil and famine, Shayland lacked a ruler because the sole heiress to the title was not assuming her rights “for personal reasons,” and the Origins were in mourning. Erwin reminded his sister [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] that she was Countess of Shayland and the empress's prime vassal.
- **Almera.** The steppe army left Almera, “leaving it to Henry Farway,” and the duke became known as Farway-Almera. His daughter Evelyn Farway is betrothed to the young [[albert-almera|Albert Almera]]. [[karen|Karen Lightheart]] saw this marriage as “a small chance of survival” for the Great House of Almera: according to her, Alanis and Alfred were dead, while Albert had obeyed orders and was alive. In another story, Alanis is apparently alive, teaching children at a school in Almera under the name Mrs Gale (see [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]).
- **The [[dom-lajthart|Lighthearts]].** Somerset Flame told Karen that [[henri-farvej|Farway]] had forbidden him to marry a firstborn woman while [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]] remained with Erwin; otherwise, the Lighthearts would become too powerful and might overthrow Farway.
- **The South and West.** At Shimmery's court, [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]] was called the queen to whom Darkwater's crown belonged “by right.” Kair [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]], in proposing to [[demetra-nellis-lajon|Demetra]], remarked that the Lilliday lineage was eight hundred years old, while the Nellis-Lyon lineage was only three hundred. Mills, led by Fairis, “Origin's traditional ally,” feared the horde.
- **The Crown.** [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] told his daughter [[magda-labelin|Magda]] that they “effectively rule the Crownlands” and considered preserving this his chief aim.
- Karen reminded Nexia: “We are ladies of Great Houses. We marry for politics and power.”
