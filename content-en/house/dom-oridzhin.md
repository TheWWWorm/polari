## Overview
The Origins are one of the Great Houses of the Empire of Polari, rulers of the northern Duchy of Origin, whose capital is [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. The full names of members of the house include the formula “of the lineage of Agatha”: the Origins regard themselves as descendants of Foremother Agatha the Radiant, the “grandchildren of Agatha”. In the North, it is believed that they inherited their pride, perceptiveness, and a measure of the gift of foresight from her. Local legend also links the house to Agatha herself: in the [[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]] above First Winter, the Foremother is said to have milked a goat and given its milk to the first duke. The Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant in First Winter, built, according to Vittor Shayland, by the legendary Lord Reidan nine hundred years ago, is described as “as old as House Origin itself”. The northerners and their armies are called Agathans after the Foremother, and their battle cry is “Glory to Agatha!”

The house's coat of arms depicts a black bat with an arrow in its claws. According to the “Guide to the Lands of Polari”, the dynasty's founder, Reidan Adler, chose a little bird capable of catching an arrow in flight as his emblem, symbolising deliverance from a hopeless situation. His descendants replaced it with a bat to make the coat of arms look more intimidating. In the southern lands, northerners are contemptuously called “bats”, “claws”, and “icicles”. By the mid-1770s, the house possessed eighty-seven Sacred Objects, one of the largest collections among the Great Houses. This collection was a constant source of disquiet for Duke [[moris-labelin|Labelin]], who had about sixty Objects. The Origins' holdings are so ancient that they do not appear in the newer volumes of the imperial register. Duke Desmond displayed two Objects, the All-Seeing One and [[vechnoe-techenie|Eternal Flow]], in the cathedral at Iona's wedding. The ancestral relic is the sword [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]]. By the time of the story, [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] was the only living person who knew the family's special fencing techniques, including “Agatha's Lightning”, and he taught them to Erwin.

## Origins of the House (According to the “Guide”)
The “Guide to the Lands of Polari” gives the following account of the house's beginnings. The North's first towns were the silver-mining settlements of [[lid|Leed]] and Main in the third century, and Steel in the fourth. First Winter began as a shepherds' settlement and nominally belonged to the Baron of Leed. In the mid-sixth century, Baron Arthur Agatha of the lineage of Agatha became Lord of Leed. He was a fanatical devotee of Foremother Agatha and considered her the greatest of the Forebears. In 572, he met Valensius, abbot of the largest Agathan monastery, in the [[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]], and the abbot founded a monastic order of knights, the Bearers of Light, or Lightbearers. The order proclaimed Agatha the greatest of the Foremothers and believed that she had foreseen the history of the world in an encoded plan. First Winter became the order's headquarters, and the Monastery of Light and Cathedral of Agatha were founded there.

Over the seventh and eighth centuries, the Lightbearers turned from scholars into warriors and brought the present-day lands of Origin and Northwood, half the Southern Way, and northern Almera under their rule. In the early ninth century, King Orsein the Miriamite twice lost to their general, Baron Heinrich Leed, with defeats in the Main Valley and at the [[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]. Only on his third attempt did the king win the war, aided by an Agathan adviser, the old knight and master of [[stratemy|stratems]] Sir Reidan Adler. Contrary to his mentor's final advice, he secretly led a regiment into the mountains, attacked Heinrich Leed's ambush from the rear, and drove his army into the valley of First Winter, where it surrendered three months later. These conflicts became known as the Wars of Despair. The order was banned, and belief in Agatha's supremacy was declared heretical. Leed's warriors nevertheless remained the best in Polaris and entered the service of a new lord.

As Adler himself had stipulated, his reward was the title of duke and First Winter. He founded the Origin dynasty and named the lands around First Winter the Duchy of Origin. In the northern tongue, the word means “source, origin, beginning”: the beginning of the Forebears' journey, the beginning of a new dynasty, and the source of Agathan wisdom. According to the guide, the last of these meanings reconciled the northerners to their new lord.

The novels give a similar account of events, but with different emphases. Vittor Shayland describes the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant, built by the “legendary Lord Reidan” nine hundred years earlier, as being as old as House Origin itself. In “The Shadow of the Great Tree”, Richard recalls how King Orsein's army perished at the Gates of Heaven during the first War of Despair. “Arrow, Coin, Spark” states that the Agathans have ruled Origin for six hundred years. Lady [[karen|Karen]], in the [[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|collector's edition of “Arrow, Coin, Spark”]], corrects this to one thousand one hundred. According to her, the North was united under Agathan rule as early as the seventh century, with its capital at [[lid|Leed]], before either the duchy or the surname Origin existed. After defeating the Lightbearers, King Orsein once again placed an Agathan who had remained loyal to the crown in charge of the North: his foremost commander, Sir Reidan, who received the title of duke and the surname Origin. Thus, the present ducal line is itself nine centuries old.

## Members of the House
- **[[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Gerda Lenore]]** — Duke of Origin, a stern and renowned commander. In 1774, he was gravely ill with Stone Sickness and abdicated in favour of his younger son. He was killed by his eldest son, Richard, on the night after the Battle for First Winter (2–3 December 1775).
- **[[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]]** — Desmond's wife, a duchess and lover of theatre and the arts.
- **[[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]]** — Desmond's eldest son and heir. Believed to have drowned at sea, he reappeared under the name “Ice”, fighting on [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]'s side. He led the Puppeteer's army against his own house and was killed by Erwin on the night leading into 3 December 1775.
- **[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Sophia Jessica]]** — the second son, duke since August 1774, Lord Chancellor of the Empire, and victor of the Northern War and the Battle for First Winter.
- **[[iona-oridzhin|Iona Sophia Jessica]]** — Desmond's daughter, the “Northern Princess”, wife of Count Vittor Shayland (imprisoned in the Womb at the end of the series), and a bearer of firstblood. In “Fantasies”, she is Countess Shayland and Minerva's prime vassal.
- **The “Steps of Heaven” branch**: [[valter-oridzhin|Walter Origin]] (missing since 1765; later revealed to have been tortured to death by monks of a secret order) and his sons—[[robert-oridzhin|Robert]], [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon the Handsome]] (killed in Faunterra in the winter of 1774–1775), and another son who had died earlier. The branch's ancestral domain is the [[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]] gorge. It was once jointly held by First Winter and Leed. After the Second Northwood War, the Origins acquired full control of it from the Lillidays in exchange for western pastures, and the duke gave the gorge to his younger brother. An avalanche once swept away this branch's family estate. Robert accepted it with his characteristic “It happens”, while Damon blamed even his own beauty for the disaster. According to Robert, in his great-grandfather's day each branch had a dozen young men; now only Erwin and Robert himself remain.

One ancestor mentioned is the ghostly Odar the Haughty, who, Erwin jokes, long refused to give up the family chair to the new duke.

## Vassals and Army
The house's mainstay is the [[kajry|kairs]], northern knights in red-and-black bicoloured cloaks, and the elite X-cloaks. The Origins' prime vassals included the Counts Lilliday of ancient Leed (the family of [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] and his brother [[sajmon-lillidej|Simon]]), the Counts of Main, and the Barons Statham, Horton, [[blekberri|Blackberry]], and Icewind. According to Erwin, the Lillidays served House Origin for eleven generations, and members of the family married Origins on four occasions. Leed is also home to the company of Leed Wolves commanded by [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]], which had served the Origins for sixteen generations. The least reliable vassals were considered to be the Counts Fleming of [[belomorye|Belomorye]]: they had rebelled against their liege lords three times, and then a fourth. According to tradition, the Lillidays acknowledged themselves as vassals of First Winter after a duel between their count and the Duke of Origin in the courtyard of the fort at the Gates of Heaven. Neither combatant dared strike a fatal blow, and the count conceded that the duke had proved his wisdom by being the first to realise that they must not kill each other. Other lands outside the duchy also served the house. According to the guide, the County of [[rejs|Reis]] was a vassal of the Origins until 1732, and its tribute, now symbolic, pays for the upkeep of two Origin garrisons on the coast.

## Lands and Economy
According to the “Guide”, the house's domains lie in the northeast of the continent, beside the Sea of Ice. They border Northwood to the west and the Southern Way to the south, and provide access to the Beyond through the Crystal Mountains. About forty percent of the land consists of mountains, and another forty percent of hilly foothills, leaving little fertile land. The largest cities are First Winter, Belomorye, Leed, and Main. Income comes from sheep's wool, weapons, armour and steel, iron and silver mining, and the spoils and tribute brought back from military campaigns. Since the Empire's centralisation, wars have become less frequent, income from warfare has fallen, and steel from the Crownlands has displaced northern steel, leaving the duchy's economy in decline. In the novels, this is reflected in First Winter's perpetually empty treasury: in August 1774, the treasurer [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] found fewer than three hundred hilts in it.

The guide describes Origin's society as highly conservative and feudal, with stern customs, many unwritten rules, and firm notions of honour. Northerners are very proud, a pride rooted in the antiquity of their land, its military glory, and the sanctity of First Winter. The common people are deeply religious and particularly revere two Foremothers, Agatha and Gloria, and the ducal line uses this faith to assert its own greatness.

## History in the Series
- **1774.** Erwin went on an [[ekspedicija|expedition beyond the River]] and discovered that Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]]'s men were recovering [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] there. On his return, he took power from his sick father and raised the North against the emperor (the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]). The Origins' army took Labelin, was defeated at Pixie on 2 December 1774, but held the palace in Faunterra. After Adrian's supposed death, Erwin placed [[minerva|Minerva]] on the throne and became Lord Chancellor.
- **1774: Abdication.** On 30 August, the ailing Desmond abdicated in Erwin's favour before witnesses. Erwin agreed to accept power only after his father had heard the account of the emperor's crimes.
- **1775.** The struggle against the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]] divided the house. Iona rose in rebellion in Waymar ([[perevorot-v-uejmare]]) and was captured by her husband. Richard joined Shayland's side. Erwin became bogged down in the war against Gallard and [[paul|Paul]]'s Shavan horde. Desmond lost the battle at [[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]. The Chosen One's army entered the duchy. On [[sajmon-lillidej|Simon Lilliday]]'s orders, the people of Leed feigned submission, while forty thousand Leed residents left with Desmond. Belomorye went over to Richard, Robert surrendered the Gates of Heaven without a fight, and First Winter came under siege. On 2 December 1775, the combined forces of the Origins and Minerva defeated the invading army in the [[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]. That same night, Richard killed his father, and Erwin killed Richard.
- **After the Battle.** Minerva moved the Empire's capital to First Winter, bringing House Origin to the centre of imperial politics. At the end of the series, Erwin remains head of the house; his mother, sister, and cousin Robert are alive.

## The Author's Article
The final part of [the author's article “History of the Duchy of Origin”](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%98%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%8F_%D0%93%D0%B5%D1%80%D1%86%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0_%D0%9E%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%B4%D0%B6%D0%B8%D0%BD) on Fandom (2021) is devoted to the dynasty's birth. Its text matches the “Guide”, but the account above leaves out two details.

- **The Bird on the Coat of Arms.** For his third campaign (816), King Orsein mustered only seventeen thousand warriors, half as many as the Lightbearers had. His mother cursed him for his recklessness: “An arrow is aimed at your chest, and you expect a little bird to catch it in flight!” This was the bird Reidan Adler later adopted for his coat of arms.
- **Adler's Three Conditions.** After spending a full day at the stratems table, Sir Reidan set out his conditions to the king: if they won, he would receive the title of duke and First Winter; he would serve only as an adviser, not as a commander; and the king would follow his advice without question—until the moment came when that advice must be disobeyed: “You will disobey—and win the war.” That is exactly what happened.

## In “Fantasies”
The author describes “Fantasies” as possible versions of the future. In them, House Origin stands at the centre of imperial politics after the victory over the Puppeteer.
- **The Peace of 1776.** In January 1776, at First Winter, Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia]] and the judge ([[ded|Grandfather]]) negotiated with [[magda-labelin|Magda]], while the exhausted Erwin merely attended. Under the treaty, Magda ceded [[uindli|Windley]] and Main to the Origins. [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] personally shook hands on the agreement with the “Duke of the North”, lifted the blockade of Windley, and allowed the Origins to “spit on the frozen white mug” of Vittor in the Womb.
- **The Capital in the North.** Minerva ruled from [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], where the imperial secretariat and ministries occupied the city centre. Adrian reckoned that “the Origins are overwhelmed by grief” and would not have time to prepare for the election of the sovereign. Maurice considered an Origin victory in the election the only outcome that would truly harm his interests.
- **The Family.** After returning from her penitential pilgrimage, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] treated the sick free of charge at the Mercy Clinic founded by her mother. [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] was serving an honorary term of “penal servitude” at the Monastery of Agatha the Radiant. Sophia ran the city's cultural life and dreamed of marrying the judge. Erwin prepared for the election and, contrary to [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]]'s expectations, became involved with Minerva. On behalf of the chapter, Mother Alicia asked him to guarantee peace during the election by keeping the kairs in the County of [[erroubek|Arrowback]].
- **Ghosts.** The castle was believed to be haunted by ancestral spirits, foremost among them Odar the Haughty, bound to the ducal chair in the great hall. Erwin saw him in that chair during the first month of his rule. The spirits of Desmond and Damon appeared to Sophia and Robert, but the kairs eventually declared that all the “ghosts” had been the shape-shifting [[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]].
- **Vassals.** At his father's wish, [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] travelled to Shimmeri to seek the hand of Princess Demetra Nellis-Lyon.

## The Family's Character
Northerners see the Origins as the embodiment of sternness and honour. Erwin himself, however, believed the family's greatest virtue was the ability to “grow up an Origin and keep your soul”, rather than cruelty. Richard, who had imitated their father in everything, became his example of the opposite.
