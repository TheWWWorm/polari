## Description
The Farways were counts, the foremost vassals of [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]. After the [[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]], the Lighthearts lost power, and Emperor Tellurian granted Hope to [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], choosing him over Count Flame. The Farways were therefore considered new dukes: they had ruled for only around twenty years, while their vassals still remembered the Lighthearts' rule, and the Flames sympathised with the fallen family. Almost all Farways belong to Foremother Elena's lineage.

### Land and Wealth
According to the Guide to the Lands of Polari, [[nadezhda|Hope]]'s largest cities are Heart of Light, Farway, and Leongard. Heart of Light, surrounded by three rings of fortifications, is reputed to be the Empire's best-defended fortress. A railway connects Farway to Heart of Light, from which trains also run to Labelin. Gold and copper mines underpin the economy; there are also two spark workshops, textile factories, and stud farms. The Guide notes that House Farway, having recently come to power, is developing industry but expends too much effort keeping its vassals, the [[dom-lajthart|Lighthearts]] and Flames, obedient, resulting in stagnation. Hope also competes fiercely with Almera: for years, the late Aiden Almera worked to have the emperor forbid Hope from building a dam.

### Coat of Arms and Reputation
The Dukes of Hope placed a bone on their arms, symbolising the desperate endurance shown during the siege of Heart of Light, and a shining goblet, symbolising good fortune. Yet endurance and pride were traits of the [[dom-lajthart|Lighthearts]]; according to the Guide, the currently ruling Farways are better known for caution and their ability to manoeuvre while avoiding direct conflict. On the banners of the Farway army withdrawing from Fliss in 1775, Alanis saw “bones and hearts.”

Lady [[karen|Karen]], a native of Heart of Light, gives a different account in the [[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark]]. According to her, the cup and bone are specifically the Lighthearts' arms, and only they and their subjects were entitled to the motto “Death gnawed our bones.” After Jonas's execution, she bitterly recalls, Farway's arms rose above the capital's towers: an armoured warrior bearing a cross.

## Members of the House
- [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], duke; by 1774, he had twelve grandchildren.
- Finley Farway, his second son, an intriguer and freethinker. He beat his wife, who fled, and Henry ordered that she be considered dead. Finley played a double game between Gallard and Origin; in anger, he called his daughter “as stupid as her late mother.”
- Finley's wife, an Agathan — mother of Laura and Jeremy; officially “on the Star,” but actually alive.
- [[laura-farvej|Laura]], Finley's daughter and Priarch Gallard's wife; murdered in 1775.
- Jeremy, Laura's brother, a freethinker and lover of Stratems. Gallard banished him to eight years as a novice. He later married Ganta Cort's daughter, sealing peace between Hope and the Steppe; the wedding was held in the Steppe, and Jeremy lived with his wife in Rei-Roy.
- Evelyn, Henry's daughter, a tall, forty-year-old widow with four children; she was widowed in the winter of 1775/76. In Fantasies, she is engaged to the adolescent Lord Albert Almera in 1776.
- “Farway the younger,” the duke's son, represented Hope in the Chamber in 1775.

## History in the Series
### Before the War
In 1773, Henry supported [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]], who promised Hope a second spark workshop, but switched to Erwin Origin's side in exchange for a promised marriage to Laura. Laura's Agathan blood made her a suitable bride for the northerner.

### Rebellion and War
During the rebellion, the Farways avoided war, sent only half a battalion to the capital, and, after [[adrian|Adrian]] visited Heart of Light, handed over their grandchildren as hostages; the emperor demanded six battalions from Hope. After Adrian's death, they played a double game between Origin and [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]. The Farways persuaded the prophet-king [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] to sell Shimmerian spark eyes outside the Crown's monopoly, purchased eyes themselves, and lent Erwin money for his own purchase. Laura's marriage made them Gallard's allies, and they agreed to extend the railway from Aleridan to Heart of Light. Erwin's mother thought neutrality had earned Farway the nobility's respect; Erwin, by contrast, included the duke among his suspects for the Puppeteer's identity.

### 1775
When [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade seized a train on Hope's railways, three companies of mounted archers attacked the halted train on Farway's orders; the surviving heretics fled into the desert. The Farways' ten-thousand-strong army then marched towards Almera, five regiments occupied [[aleridan|Aleridan]], and later the fleet and Flame's corps blockaded Gallard in Fliss by lake and land. As the horde approached, Farway withdrew his troops without a battle. Afterwards, they made peace with the Steppe, sealing it through Jeremy's marriage. Following Gallard's and Laura's deaths, the Farways seized Almera's ports and the remnants of the priarch's fleet, and their ships transported Horton's kairs to the rear of Adrian's army.

## In Fantasies
In Fantasies, which the author describes as imaginings of the characters' possible fates, Henry has become ruler of two lands by 1776 and holds court in Aleridan. Not all Almeran lords have submitted to him: Countess Daynight and Count Arrowback, in particular, resist. Evelyn's marriage to Albert is intended to legitimise Farway rule over Almera. Henry promises to restore Heart of Light, the ancestral domain of the [[dom-lajthart|Lighthearts]], to Lady Karen's family if Nexia Flame marries Origin, but postpones fulfilling the agreement. He forbids Somerset Lightheart-Flame from marrying Count Arrowback's daughter. Jeremy lives in Rei-Roy with his Shavan wife; outside the cathedral in Aleridan, the crowd boos Evelyn — “Old woman! Overripe!” — while a cowed Albert picks up her dropped fan at her command. In “An Essay on a Theme,” Almera is ruled by “Duke Farway-Almera,” who pays retired soldiers a pension of four glorias a month.

## Vassals
The house's vassals included the Flames, among them [[edgar-flejm|Count Edgar]] and [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]. Edgar is a son of the [[dom-lajthart|Lighthearts]]; Farway acknowledged him as his strongest vassal but doubted his loyalty and in 1775 sent his regiment north with Horton's kairs. Henry negotiated with Erwin through Edgar.
