## Description
The Almeras are one of the Empire's [[velikie-doma|Great Houses]], descended from Agatha the Radiant. They ruled the richest duchy in Polaris, the Red Land. In the number of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]] held by the house, Almera was second only to the Illustrious Dynasty: the Almeras had one hundred and twenty Objects, while the Dynasty had more than three hundred. On the Day of the Descent, the dukes sometimes displayed part of their ancestral collection in Aleridan's central square for the townspeople's enjoyment.

The house's coat of arms and cloaks bear a tower and a sun. Joaquin remarked that the sun setting behind Evergard's tower looked exactly like the ancestral coat of arms. At the summer games of 1774, Almera's knights wore white and gold. In a scene set in 1756, Duke Aiden is described riding a white horse, dressed in snowy white with a golden coat of arms, beside the “bicoloured” Desmond Origin.

According to Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the [[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark]], only Almeran nobles are admitted to the Almeran guard: they are considered more firmly attached to their land and better acquainted with the laws of honour.

According to the Guide to the Lands of Polari, Almera has fully preserved its aristocratic spirit: art here celebrates the greatness of dukes, the glory of knights, and the wisdom of the Foremothers, while great lords patronise culture. Almerans were reputed to be proud and haughty, like most Agathans.

## History
### Early Centuries
According to the Guide, Almera became the Empire's first duchy, receiving that status in the V century. At the time, the “Duchy of Almera” was a tiny patch of land no larger than the present-day Barony of Smolden. Its western boundary was vaguely described in the documents, and several generations of dukes methodically “shifted” it: they moved villages mentioned in the charters and felled and replanted forests. The seventh Duke of Almera surpassed them all by digging a new channel for the river named in the feudal charter and diverting it thirty miles farther west.

The darkest chapter in the house's history was the First [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse War]]: Darian the Vile's horde conquered the duchy and burned Aleridan and Blackmore. The Shavans raped the sisters of the reigning duke; deprived of weapons and even rope, both young women bit off their tongues and bled to death. Recovery took an entire century.

### Rivalry with Hope
Until relatively recently, Almera's development was held back by its rivalry with [[nadezhda|Hope]]: the Dukes of Lightheart pressed the Almerans both in politics and on the battlefield, while the Shavans attacked from the west. The Almerans frequently had to seek the Crown's help and became skilful courtiers: for the last two hundred years, no imperial cabinet had been without an Almeran. In the early XVIII century, the dukes sent mechanics to the sacred River [[kholliwell|Hollywell]] to assess whether a dam could be built. The Shavans attacked the duchy's western lands for half a year, captured Clermy, and besieged Blackmore; meanwhile, the mechanics concluded that a dam was impossible. The conflict was nicknamed the Stupid War.

Around 1750, during the [[zolotaya-vojna|Golden War]], [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]] successfully attacked Almera and seized some of its lands; the Almerans recovered only part of them, and with great difficulty. The [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]] came to their aid, and [[ajden-almera|Aiden]] remembered that the northerners had saved his father.

### Rise to Prominence
According to the Guide, the land owes its prosperity to its last two dukes, Astor and Aiden. Having obtained permission to establish spark workshops, they made them the driving force of industry, while banks under their control lent money to craftsmen and manufacturers. The old duke, who died shortly before the Jesters' Conspiracy, left his domains to his younger son Aiden and sent his elder son, [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], to a theological seminary.

The house achieved its greatest success after the [[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]: Aiden “saved” Emperor Tellurian, became his chief adviser and the Empire's most powerful feudal lord, while [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]] fell. Aiden secured trade privileges and permission for a second spark dam for Almera, obtained a priarch's mantle for his brother, and prepared his daughter [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] to become empress.

## Members of the House
- [[ajden-almera|Aiden Ellis Gwenda]], duke — died in 1774.
- Duchess Alicia, his wife.
- [[alanis-almera|Alanis Adelia Abigail]], heiress — presumed dead after being tortured in Waymar in 1775; in Fantasies, she is alive under the name Mrs Gale.
- Alfred, the middle child and elder son — died at Evergard in 1774, aged fourteen.
- [[albert-almera|Albert Adelia Abigail]], younger son — survived; betrothed to Laura Farway, and in Fantasies engaged to Evelyn Farway.
- [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], Aiden's elder brother, priarch — regent and then governor of the duchy; died in 1775.
- [[laura-farvej|Laura Almera]], née Farway, Gallard's widow — claimed the duchy; murdered by Martin Shayland in October 1775.

## History in the Series
### Fall
In 1774, Aiden's intrigues were exposed and the house fell from favour. On the night before 2 September, [[pozhar-evergarda|Evergard was destroyed]], and Gallard seized power, declaring himself Albert's regent. Alanis fled to the Origins; after the war, an agreement with Gallard recognised her as the lawful heiress, with the priarch ruling as her governor. Albert grew up at Evergard under the supervision of his uncle and his monks; his consolation was a pony sent by his sister from the capital.

### War and Extermination
In 1775, the Ecumenical Council removed Gallard from office, Erwin Origin went to war against Almera, and the forces of [[nadezhda|Hope]] occupied Aleridan. In summer, [[paul|Paul]]'s horde passed through the duchy; deprived of central authority, the Almeran lords shut themselves inside their castles. Gallard opened Fliss's gates to the horde. He died in Waymar, poisoned by Alanis; she herself was presumed dead after being tortured. Gallard's widow Laura declared “the entire Duchy of Almera” hers, and the commanders of the knightly and monastic battalions she had brought recognised her as its lawful duchess. In October, [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] strangled her; unwilling to quarrel with Farway, Vittor intended to blame the murder on infiltrators. In the summer of the same year, Henry Farway had used [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]] to propose that Erwin recognise Laura's rights and marry her to Albert, but Erwin insisted that the heiress was Alanis, followed by Albert.

## In Fantasies
In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], which presents possible futures, Henry Farway rules Almera in 1776 and holds court in Aleridan, although not all Almeran lords have willingly submitted to him. He betroths the last Almera, the boy [[albert-almera|Albert]], to his daughter Evelyn — a tall, forty-year-old widow with four children, whom her bridegroom fears. Townspeople in the cathedral boo the bride, and the betrothal is held on a Monday while the Almerans are in their workshops. Karen tells Farway to his face that he is serving as “the hammer driving nails into the lid of House Almera's coffin.” Before this, the southern part of the divided horde, led by Ganta Cort, spends a month plundering the Red Land and then makes peace with Farway: Cort recognises him as Almera's ruler, and Farway recognises Cort as the Steppe's foremost chieftain. A dynastic marriage seals the treaty; according to Farway, his grandson [[dzheremi-farvej|Jeremy]] marries a steppe ganta's daughter for the sake of peace with the Shavans. Karen Lightheart sees in this marriage a small chance for the Great House of Almera to survive: Alanis and Alfred are dead, but Albert lives and will outlast his no-longer-young wife. Meanwhile, all indications suggest that Alanis is alive: in “An Essay on a Theme,” she teaches children at an Almeran parish school under the name Mrs Gale (see [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] for details).

## Trivia
- Almera is one of four lands of the Empire with a tradition of permanent theatres; three of the five modern schools of painting originated here, according to the Guide to the Lands of Polari.
- Evergard served not only as the duke's residence but, under Gallard, also as that of the head of the Church of the Forebears.
