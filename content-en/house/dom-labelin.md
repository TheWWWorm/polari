## Overview
House Labelin is a Great House descended from Foremother Sophia. According to the Guide to the Lands of Polari, Sophia the Majestic is the patroness of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], Foremother of the family hearth, care, and maternal love. Her veneration is evident in southern daily life: at Marquess Doyle's feast, when Magda's army approached Doyle, “Sophia was praised three times as often as Yanmei.” The house rules the Southern Way, through which the main trade route runs from the Crownlands and Shimmery to the North. Its tolls and markups brought the Labelins fabulous wealth. According to the Guide, the duchy's merchant fleet numbers around 1200 vessels and is the largest in the Empire. Duke Maurice himself boasted to Erwin that his fleet was “many times stronger” than the northern one.

The Origins possessed eighty-seven [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]], while the Labelins, according to Vittor Shayland, had around sixty. Maurice sought to close the gap through purchases. In the summer of 1774, the duke himself said that his collection contained seventy Objects.

## Coat of Arms and Colours
The soldiers of the Southern Way wore caftans embroidered with Labelin's arms: blue dolphins and golden sheaves of wheat. The battle standard that Magda ordered raised against the Shimmerians depicts a single black dolphin on a blue field, “flying above the waves” and baring a fanged mouth like a shark's. It was nicknamed the “angry dolphins.” Raising it meant declaring war. This also gave rise to the Shimmerians' nickname for Magda, the “dolphin lady,” and the duke's mocking nickname, the Fat Dolphin.

## Members of the House
- **[[moris-labelin|Maurice Elvira Dorothy]]**, Duke of Labelin — the corpulent, cautious head of the house.
- The duchess, Maurice's wife. In the summer of 1774, she accompanied her husband at court. In autumn 1775, she and their sons visited Adrian's travelling court; her husband sent them back to Labelin the following day.
- The duke's sons; the eldest is heir to the duchy. In Fantasies, the sons remain behind to restore order in the Southern Way while their father lives in the capital.
- **[[magda-labelin|Magda Fiona Lilith]]** — daughter and infanta, wife of [[adrian|Adrian]] from 1775, and later governor of the Crownlands. In Fantasies, she is pregnant in the spring of 1776 and dreams of giving birth to a crown prince.
- Magda's great-grandfather — the Duke of Labelin, a “king among schemers,” who engineered an intrigue in Northwood half a century earlier.

## Vassals and Servants
- The Marquesses of Graysend — owners of the port city of Grays, where the Labelins held court after losing their capital. The Labelins promoted their daughter [[valeri-grejsend|Valerie]] as a prospective bride for Adrian; she later became Magda's chief lady-in-waiting and wife of the [[aleksis-smajl|Silver Fox]].
- Marquess Windley — owner of the port of Windley, one of the duchy's most profitable cities and the headquarters of the First Maritime Guild. Early editions of Arrow, Coin, Spark call him a baron. During the Northern War, he lost his lands and remained in Grays with a handful of men.
- Marquess Jeremiah Doyle — ruler of Doyle, the last major city on the road north.
- Baron Hugo Doris Martha [[derril|Derrill]] of Leonora's lineage — the house's “iron fist” and chief military commander.
- Lord-Captain Courtney Benefit — commander of Magda's squadron during the Shimmerian expedition.
- The agent [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] — the duke's “useful little man,” and later a traitor.
- In 1775, Colonel Chorey's mercenary Sun Regiment and the Brigade of the Holy Ostrich from Shimmery entered the house's service. The Labelins also bought off the [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrop]] leaders [[zub|Tooth]] and Dodge's Hand.

## History
### Trade Monopoly
According to the Guide to the Lands of Polari, the Southern Way joined the Empire in the IV century. Control over it repeatedly passed from one lineage to another, and the local peasants grew accustomed to saying, “The dogs fight; the cows graze.” Trade flourished particularly in the XIII–XIV centuries, after sea routes opened to Litland, Shimmery, and Darkwater. According to the Guide, hostility towards the North had entirely material causes: the Southern Way's lords coveted the silver mines of Main and [[lid|Leed]], while the northern lords dreamed of access to the sea.

In Humans and Gods, Magda recalls that for almost a century her grandfathers and great-grandfathers maintained a trade monopoly over the North and resorted to any trick to preserve it. They inflamed conflicts between the North and the Crown and repeatedly sabotaged the direct road from Faunterra to Lakecity: four ministers lost their posts, while the road remained a “ditch.” They drove Almerans out of lake trade, bought up the fleet on [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]], built ports, and bribed guilds.

Magda was especially proud of her great-grandfather's intrigue. Half a century earlier, Count Northwood allocated funds for a new port on the marshy shore of Smoky Distance. Duke Labelin arranged a marriage between the Northwood heir and a neighbouring baron's daughter and incited the newlyweds to attack the port's builder. He then offered the builder a loan to hire mercenaries, only to refuse the loan once they arrived. The construction funds went towards paying the troops, and the port was never completed.

With the money they earned, the Labelins built palaces, laid rails, and lit spark lamps. Men of Sophia's lineage became ministers and generals; women became ladies-in-waiting and, twice, empresses. Yet according to Magda, trade remained their only talent. They maintained thousands of knights but invariably lost every war in which the Crown did not intervene on their behalf. The Origins and Northwoods repeatedly fought the Southern Way's “bloodsuckers.”

### 1774
In 1774, the Labelins supported Valerie Graysend's bid to marry Adrian and allied with the Reis family for that purpose. Mira briefly suspected Maurice of involvement in the conspiracy, but immediately before the bride was chosen, the duke withdrew his support for Valerie. In the same year, Maurice bought a counterfeit [[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]] from Harmon (see [[lzhe-sfera]]).

During the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]], the house remained loyal to the Crown. The northerners took Windley and the coast, the Northwoods seized a strip along Smoky Distance, and in November Erwin defeated the Southern Way's army outside [[labelin|Labelin]] and occupied the city. The Labelins retained a third of their duchy, and Grays became the new capital.

### The Shimmerian Adventure and Marriage to Adrian
In the winter of 1775, the duke sent Magda to [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] with two million in gold to buy up spark eyes. According to Moger Buckley's testimony, the Labelins secretly supplied the Snowdrops with spark weapons; the duke admitted only to “a dozen eyes.” In the Chamber, Maurice unsuccessfully demanded the return of his lost lands. Magda seized an enormous stock of eyes and the mercenary Sun Regiment in Shimmery, then forced Adrian, whom she had rescued, to marry her. The Labelins supported Adrian's return to power and became his last remaining support. In exchange, the house recovered the Southern Way, received the County of Main with its silver mines, and acquired buildings in the capital as collateral for loans, including the Palace Bridge and the [[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]. King [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] gave Maurice the [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]].

## In Fantasies
After the [[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]], Magda ceded Windley and Main to the Origins. In return, Maurice was entrusted with custody of the frozen Puppeteer, and Minerva recognised the Dynasty's debts: the Imperial Citadel, the Aerial Bridge, the Admiralty, and other buildings in Faunterra passed to the Labelins. Magda became governor of the Crownlands. The Battle for First Winter proved “a triumph for the Labelins, who lost not a single soldier.” The duke and his daughter settled in the Imperial Citadel and, in Maurice's words, “effectively rule the Crownlands.” He saw preserving these gains as his principal goal, and Erwin Origin's election as the only outcome of the election of the sovereign that would threaten the house. The Labelins kept spies at Adrian's town hall, refused him further loans, and delayed the delivery of Nicholas Ollie's sound-recording device, which he hoped to use to appeal to the common people.

## Trivia
- According to the Guide, the inhabitants of the Southern Way are considered stupid and greedy by northerners, but this is “a product of northern propaganda.” In reality, they fall short of the North in valour but surpass it in enterprise.
- Popular Southern Way folklore contrasts kindly Mother Sophia with the “wicked bitch Agatha.”
- The merchant Harmon believed that the Radiant Sphere “did not want to be sold” to the Labelins because it disliked people with dark souls.
