## Description
Gates of Heaven is a gorge in the mountains of the north, along the bottom of which there is a road from [[lid|Leed]] to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. The two rock ridges converge here so closely that the gorge resembles a chopped wound in the ground, and the bridge, thrown across, looks like a seam. There is a fort at each end of the bridge.

The northern fort occupies the only level platform on the flat top of the rock. The whole rock around is cut by cracks, at the bottom of which lies thousands of years of ice. The southern fort is smaller. He clung to the stone ledge facing the gorge, and the rear to the main peak, which rises above it for another thousand feet. Once an avalanche came down on him: a mush of stones and ice blocks still rests on the back wall, but the wall has withstood. Next to the same ledge once stood another, less durable building, from which only the foundations remained. The southern fort is older than the northern fort, so it is called the Elder, and the northern fort is called the Younger. In the courtyard of the Elder there is a sculpture: the stone Duke of Origin and the Earl of Lilliday shake hands, tired of relying on swords.

The bridge between the forts is mapped on all northern maps. It served as a border post: to the east, the domain of the Dukes of Origin began, to the west, the possession of the Earls of Lilliday. The Northerners knew the gorge as a natural trap. The slopes are steep and smooth, like walls, and the army that entered the gorge is compressed and helpless. According to [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]], in the first war of despair, the army of King Orsein died here: one small detachment blocked the exit from the gorge, another climbed the rocks and brought down the avalanche.

## History
### The Avalanche in the Sky
The Polari Lands Guide describes this episode differently. In 810, King Orsayn of Myriamets went to war for the second time on the Order of the Light-bearers, whose headquarters was in the First Winter. The Agathan general, Baron Heinrich Leed, exhausted his army with ambushes and false reports. The king learned from reliable sources that the gorge is poorly guarded, and the main forces of the Light-bearers are again at Main. He sent reconnaissance three times and, convinced, moved the army through the Skvorki. The defense was indeed weak, but as soon as the knights broke through it and entered the gorge, an avalanche of unprecedented strength fell on them. Henry Leed foresaw her, so he did not defend the Skvorki. The rest of the royal army fled in terror. The guide calls this avalanche the "apotheosis" of Orssein's second campaign. Richard, on the other hand, says that the avalanche was arranged by a squad that climbed the rocks.

### The border of Leed and the First Winter
In those centuries when the ancient capital of the North, Leed, argued for primacy with the First Winter, there was only one fort, the Elder. He ruled over the most dangerous part of the road, and without him it was impossible to break into enemy land. It was useless to storm the fortress a hundred yards above the road, it was taken only by exhaustion. A week of riding the army took place in six months, and the siege of Skvorok Heaven devoured money, supplies and morale.

Eventually, the Origins and the Lillidays decided that it was more profitable for them to fight in the steppes, a thousand miles from home, than to storm each other. The dispute about the championship was decided by a duel in the yard of the Elder. The Duke of Origin offered to play the strata, the Earl of Lilliday demanded a sword fight, and the Kairs supported the Count. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) The Duke knew the deadly family trick, but could not apply it: if the Count died, his children would continue the war. Lilliday, for his part, also held the death blow at the last moment. The fight lasted a quarter of an hour. The wounded count lowered his sword and said that fighting half-heartedly was too tiring, and the Duke’s mind had already proved, the first to understand that it was impossible to cut to death. So Lilliday recognized himself as a vassal of the First Winter.

In order to consolidate peace, the parties agreed on marriage between children, and the gorge was made a common possession. He was guarded by two companies of mountain guards, the Leed and the Origin. For the sake of justice, a second fort was built: for a long time, the Younger owned the Lead, the Elder owned the First Winter, and the fee for the passage was divided in half.

### The property of the younger branch of the Origins
During the [[vtoraya-nortvudskaya-vojna|Second Northwood War]], the Origins acquired large pastures in the west of the Duchy, closer to Leed than to the First Winter. They were exchanged: the Lillidays received pastures, and the Origins received complete control over Gates of Heaven. The Duke gave the gorge to his younger brother, and since then it has belonged to the second branch of the family. In the 1770s, it was owned by [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], who was called the "Lord of the Squaw of Heaven". Robert himself in The Voice of Winter called the two branches of the house "Origins of the First Winter" and "Origins of the Steps of Heaven". He reminded Erwin that with his great-grandfather in each branch there were a dozen young men, and now "you are alone in the First Winter and I am alone in the Steps", and therefore did not dare to risk myself until he left offspring.

When [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] became duke, Robert as treasurer transferred to him the already promised expenses from the almost empty treasury. Part of the payments was ordered to be made by Erwin's mother, Sofia: fifty ephesas to the bishop and the Gloria-deputy hospital. ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]]) Another three hundred Hilt was due to the masters for strengthening the bridge over the Skvorki Sky.

### Autumn of 1775
When the army of the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]] took Leed, [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] (his words were confirmed by the well-known Count Fleming) called the Sky Scrolls the second of three possible positions for the defense of his father, between Leed and the First Winter itself. ([[fleming|Benedict Fleming]]) Richard was appointed commander of the entire army, and his vanguard of White Seamen and Almerans was to clear the road and take the gorge. Before Leed, he warned his comrades that Robert, the "worst stubborn man in the First Winter", was holding the fort for a week or two. This would have been enough for the snow to close the road and the offensive would have broken down.

However, Robert gave the forts without a fight: the gate was unlocked, there were no soldiers left, the cellars were full. [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]]'s retreating army only filled the gorge with stones, and this blockage was dismantled. Richard, standing on the wall of the Elder, said with sadness that his cousin had surrendered the family estate: neither in Waymar, nor in Leed, nor here, the family did not go out to fight, and he took this as a sign that the house was "bleeding out." Later, he noticed that after the Sky Scraper, the enemy no longer put any obstacles in the way, as if his father wanted the army to approach the First Winter on the Day of Descent. The entire army of the Chosen passed through the gorge, and food was waiting for it in the forts. Fleming said his battalion was in the Sky Scrolls. Vittor Shayland himself, on his way to the First Winter, also set up a meeting for his people at the Skyrocket.

Erwin, with his sister Iona and a detachment of leads, hurried to the First Winter before the battle, bypassed the enemy-occupied forts along the side trails. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) After the defeat in the Battle of the First Winter, when Vittor disappeared, the general of the Chosen One's army commanded a retreat to the Sky Shards, and the remnants of the Shaylands went there. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) [[magda-labelin|Magda]] dreamed of sending a detachment there, snatching the Dust from the "Shelland defects" and, together with her husband, getting a firstblood. Iona, on the other hand, offered to send Robert and [[stetkhem|Statham]] battalions with the Gauntlet of Power, "ideal for the siege of mountain forts", to storm the forts in order to capture Vittor's remaining Items.
