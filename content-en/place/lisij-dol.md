Fox Dole is a town and barony in [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]. Here was born Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]], and in the fall of 1775 at its walls decided the fate of the war in the North.

## Description
According to the Polari Lands Guide, Lysias Dol, along with the Bear Fang and Birbis, are Northwood's largest cities. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) Cities of this land are usually fortified settlements among the forest and small by the standards of the central lands.

In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], Lysias Dole “darkened with log walls between the river and the forest.” The Baron's castle stood on a cape and from a distance resembled a shoe dropped in a puddle. From Lisego Dol to Origin itself, according to [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]], there are dense forests crossed by ravines. The surrounding area is famous for strong hanty. In "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]", a flask of Fox Dole's hanty is found at the deceased, and the detective Macfried ([[mark-voron|Mark]]) remarks that this drink is "expensive for a simple coachman".

## Homeland Sybil
Lady [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] is the daughter of Baron Bald Dole. In [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], [[minerva|Mira]], who took the name Gloria Northwood, takes over her new ancestry: she is now the granddaughter of Dorina, the "Baroness of Bald Dole", who once gave out an eighteen-year-old daughter for a sixty-year-old count, "changing her youth for a title". In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], Sybil, who has lost her memory and lives under the name Dorothy, heard from [[karen|Karen]] that she was the Countess of Northwood, and confusedly remembered: “I am from Bald Dol!.. My father was...” "Baron Fox Dol, right," Karen replied. "He has given you up as Count Elias Northwood."

Earlier, in the “Yanmei Hunt”, the fugitives from the hospital were picked up by a Shimmer schooner, and [[navi|Navi]] introduced the captain of the companions: “Lady Dorothy from Fox Dol, Susan’s family, and Lady Karen from the Heart of Light, Elena’s family.” ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) According to him, the ladies sailed to Northwood on a visit to the family of Lady Dorothy. Karen then wondered how Navi knew she was from the Heart of Light, and Dorothy from Bald Dole: "It can't be calculated by formula."

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Army of the Chosen
In September 1775, the army of [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], who had proclaimed himself Elect, marched through Northwood, and the county cities opened the gates one by one without a fight. Lysias Dol also opened them. The count, as usual, first publicly dealt with the local lord, and then entered the city and opened his arms to ordinary people. While the townspeople looked from the walls, the Shavans robbed the baronial castle: swords, armor, ladies' dresses and hunting trophies were thrown from the walls, ale and hanty barrels were rolled out of the basements. Then the ring-bearers burned the castle from the inside, and the servants dumped charred bodies into the river, "it must be assumed that the former owners of the castle." Who were these owners and whether they were Sibyl's relatives, the text does not say.

At the city gate, the army was overtaken by Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]] with the learned bear of Countess Northwood Maverick. ([[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]]) He told me how he fooled [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]] under the Bear Fang for two weeks. Among other things, he proposed to draw a line along which the lands of Elias would begin “from Bald Dole to the north.” The next morning, the Chosen One solemnly entered the city and made a speech from the shield seat. The Northwoods enlisted in his army, and he chose his new adopted children and told Brother [[martin-shejland|Martin]] to find them another governess. So [[gvenda|Gwenda]] was in the Shayland retinue. At the military council, Richard, whose name was Ice, warned that Craig's regiments were coming from the west, and his father [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] was retreating from the east, and offered to hit the bears first. General Horis, [[chara|Chara]] and [[paul|Paul]] demanded to chase the kairs, and [[fleming|Fleming]] and Dorcaster supported Richard. ([[oris|General Oris]])

The feast in Lysiy Dol came out clenched: the army was in a hurry to battle with the Kairs.

### The Battle of Bald Dole
In early October 1775, Craig Northwood with twelve thousand soldiers finally came to the rescue of Desmond. Desmond's army stood in the woods, convenient for fighting against the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]. The Duke took the fight without serious preparation and without agreement with Craig, hoping for a numerical advantage and, possibly, that the forest will be able to repulse the wagon train with the captive daughter Iona. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]])

At night, the Chosen One's army camped in a damp lowland. The bears secretly led a whole battalion through the forest, which was considered impassable, and struck simultaneously from the road and from the thicket. The most dangerous area was at Iona's cell. Joaquin and Martin fought off with their fingers from the roof of the wagon, and Iona, who had heard the attack in advance, wounded Joaquin with a sharp piece of bone. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) Later, Vittor explained to his brother that the attack was Richard's trap: the army deliberately set itself up to lure the enemy. On the road from the west came ten thousand bears, from the east - six thousand "wolves" of Origin. Ice and General Horis stood to death, and Paul left the camp in advance and struck from the forest from the flank.

The battle ended with the defeat of the Allies. Craig Northwood was killed, thousands of bears surrendered to Richard, many defected to Shayland, and the county returned to Elias, essentially a puppet of the Puppeteer. The Kairs lost an entire battalion, and Agatha's army, according to the narrator, decreased by a quarter. Desmond reported that about a thousand soldiers were wounded and half of them did not recover. The Shavans were given about a thousand dead kairs for looting. Desmond himself admitted that he had "gone a premature fight and suffered unacceptable losses", and Richard later sadly remarked that Lisius Dol was the only battle his father had given, "and even there he was convinced by Northwood". The victory brought Richard the respect of the Almerans and the sunsets. Among the bears that passed to the Chosen, a regiment was formed, later storming the First Winter.

## Contradictions
- The chapter with the night battle is simply called "County of Northwood", but later all participants call this battle the Battle of Fox Dol.
- Sources differ on Craig’s forces: in one chapter he has twelve thousand soldiers, Vittor speaks of ten thousand bears and six thousand “wolves”, and Richard says that the enemies had “almost twenty thousand fighters”.
