"**Jack Buckler**" is an inn in the [[erroubek|Arrowback]] County Forest in [[almera-zemlya|Almera]]. It was owned by the Buckler brothers, who secretly served [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade. In December 1774, a massacre took place here: the northerner Ice killed almost all the guests, and the inn burned down. The story of the inn is told in "[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]". It is restored in parts: first according to rumors collected by [[mark-voron|Mark]], then according to the story of Inge Price. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]])

## Place
The tract stood three miles west of Road Pillar (locals were offended when he was called a village). To get there, you had to turn from the village to the path between the hills and round the old quarry. According to the owner of the cake Helga, in the summer there were hunters, and in the winter the inn was empty, and few people knew about it. Polyana with the inn was surrounded by a strong fence of new boards, with the only gate to the road. Above the gate hung a sign "Jack Buckler". The county tract passed behind the forest, three miles to the northwest.

From Jack Buckler you can ride a day to the port of [[fliss|Fliss]] in the north, to [[bek-most|Beck]] Castle in the east, to [[evergard|Evergard]] in the south and to Flathill in the west. From here there are three roads: back to Back, north to Smoky Distance, and south to [[aleridan|Aleridan]]. There was no road to the west.

## Hosts
The parcel was owned by the Buckler brothers, Chett and Logot. One was plump, the other was cucie. They had a cook and a maid, but on the night of the massacre, they both spent the night in Blystock and survived. At the end of the summer of 1774, three dozen “very serious people” in scarlet guard uniforms stopped at the inn. A week before the massacre, there was a note that they would be back. Captain [[uitmor|Whitmore]] later found witnesses that the brigade was standing here in August, before the attack on Evergard. ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) Mark concluded that the Bucklers' inn, like Jonas's forge and Forest Shelter, was a long-proven "norm" of the gang.

## The Massacre
In mid-December 1774, in the days of the siege of the palace and the abduction of the Objects, a cakemaker from the Road Stolb sent a van of refugees traveling from Fliss to Aleridan to Jack Buckler. It was driven by Inge Price, who called himself an "honest watchmaker", a deserter Kol with two friends, the newlyweds Matt and Reena, the moneylender Anna Greta with her husband and adopted daughter, Baby G. The Buckler brothers said the inn was closed. Then the deserters beat them, dug up the house and stole all the valuables. Buclair warned: “My brother and I work for the same people. They are very serious people.”

At night, two scouts of the brigade arrived - Rebro and Ice (later it turned out that this was [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]]). The deserters attacked them. The rib was tied, and the ice was thought to be dead. In exchange for his freedom, Rebro showed Inge the scheme of [[absolyut|the Absolute]] hidden under the scar on his hand. Then the ice came up and killed almost everyone. Inge and Baby G hid in the basement. They were rescued by a burned Matt with a crossbow shot. Inge set fire to the inn and ran south with the girl, and Matt and Reena left on their way.

## Ashes
In the morning, residents of the Road Stolb saw smoke behind the quarry. According to them, they immediately realized: "Here Dark Ido himself visited." The truck burned to the ground. Of the eight dead, seven were ordinary "stags", and from the eighth there remained only a handful of ash with a ring and a belt buckle. The residents decided that he was a victim of the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]. Monks from the monastery of Marek and Simeon served and buried the dead on the monastery's monastery site. On the ashes, the peasants put sticks with wooden spirals. The three plaques bore the names: Chett Buckler, Logot Buckler, and Couple. A local dog, Fred, took the dog. A short entry appeared in the registers of Earl Arrowback: the inn "destroyed by fire, the owner died."

## Mark's investigation
Mark and Grandpa followed in the footsteps of the gang that stole the Dynasty Items. ([[ded|Grandfather]]) A trail of dead bodies and burned hotels led them from Beck Castle to Jack Buckler. Mark counted in the history of the inn a continuous “devilishness”: why the killers missed three guests and a girl, where the survivors went and what happened to the “special deceased”. In the courtyard of the ashes on them came the "blue gang" with spark spears. Mark in the fog was looking for a second exit along the seven-foot fence and cursed the Buckler brothers: "We made a fort instead of an inn!" He fell off his horse and broke his ribs.

Later, Mark found Inji in Aleridan and, reminding him of "Jack Buckler", reached a deal. He concluded that the gang had returned to the ashes because they had left something valuable there. Inge's account of Rebre tearing off a piece of skin and then growing it led Mark to believe that [[absolyut|the Absolute]] is immortality. Major Backfield considered the foray into "Jack Buckler" a hoax, and the Items were hidden in Arrowback Castle. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]])
