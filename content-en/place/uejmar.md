## Description
Waymar is the main town of [[dom-shejland|Shayland]] County. It stands on a large hill near the northern shore of Lake Smokey Dahl, where the [[torrej|Torrey]] River flows out of the lake. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) The top of the hill is the county castle. The southern slope, descending to the lake, is called Warm City: here the terraces are the houses of nobles, shipowners and merchants, below live sailors. The northern slope, forever lying in the shade, is called the Cold City. There the artisans settle and keep the cellars of the taverns. [[minerva|Mira]] recalled Waymar as a city squeezed into a ring of fortress walls and densely built up, similar to bee honeycombs.

According to the Polari Lands Guide, Waymar is the largest city in Shayland and the financial center of the North: the northern banking system was born here.

### Ports
There are two ports in Waymar and a third outside the city.
- The lake port is the largest and most noisy. Ships from the South Road, Almera, Holliwell and Sunset Coast come here. The harbor is divided by piers into factories, and above each pier stands a tower with the flag of its land, so that the Almerans, travelers and sunsets are moored separately. The lake schooners are plump, "like pigs", and the sailors are cheerful.
- The river port at the source of [[torrej|Torrey]] receives high-bore ships from the Ice Sea, Northwood and Origin with shooting grounds and ballistics. Waymar people do not like it: the northerners bring with them harshness and cold.
- The abandoned port outside the city is an old lighthouse and the ruins of piers, abandoned under the grandfather of Count Vittor. There are only smugglers.

### Tracts
Waymar is famous for fogs and inns. According to barber Harry Hog, "Waymar is a town of taverns; there are simply no bad establishments here." ([[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]]) Each has its own burrow. Dear Kabaks are standing on a rich Maple Street. In the "Dead Baron", where the small lord was once killed, the walls are decorated with bones and rusty blades, and a skeleton in armor stands in the hall. In the inn for veterans are allowed only without weapons: all the blades are put in a barrel at the entrance. In the basement of the One-Eyed, they play cards and arrange rat races in a board maze. The Guide confirms that Sheilanders love pubs, maps, bones, and rat races.

### The Bank Tower
The headquarters of Shayland Bank is a massive hexagonal tower in the waters of Smoky Distance, two hundred yards from the shore. From the gallery on the roof you can see the lake, the city on the slope and the harbor. The bridge to the tower has a lifting area of fifty steps, which can not be overcome by siege equipment. There are shooting ranges and catapults on the roof. There are two upper floors and a gallery. The lower floors and the basement where the items and gold are stored are closed to outsiders. Count Vittor spent the night in the castle, and worked in the bank tower.

### Other Places
The city has Foremother Vivian Cathedral, built with the money of Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent]], and the best city hospital in Waymar, where Iona left her companion Jane. On the outskirts lie the ruins of an old monastery. There [[martin-shejland|Martin]] set up his secret cellar. To the bed of the Seventeenth Gift from the city half a day riding.

## Castle
The castle is made of gray stone and is stuffed with ivy, as is the coat of arms of the Shaylands. According to Harry Hog, in the spring of 1775, the count razed ivy from the walls and doubled the garrison to please Iona, who grew up in the First Winter. In the autumn of 1774, about four hundred people lived in the castle: a garrison of about two hundred soldiers (armored companies and infantry), forty northerners of Iona, two dozen count's knights, the huntsmen of Martin, guests, about fifty servants and a dozen financial agents. By May 1775, the garrison had grown to about three hundred.

### Donjong
Dongjong is a tall square clock tower. On the first floor of the refectory. The second floor was occupied by the rooms of the castellan and guest rooms: stone stairs, draperies on the walls, dark from time portraits of former counts. On the third floor were Iona's bedroom and study, and the library, where, according to Vittor, "many pearls." On the fourth are the bedrooms of the count and Martin, the count's office with a portrait of the "full-cheeked" old count and the guard room. In the count's bedroom there is a tap with water and a balcony overlooking the courtyard. In the earl's office under the floor stones was a hiding place where Wittor kept Wilhelm's Feather. Behind the fourth panel on the left in the same office began a secret passage.

### Temnitsa
The underground prison has two circles. The upper one, with stone chambers, is designed for noble prisoners, for whom you can get a ransom. The second guard of the upper circle is heated through the ceiling by the fireplace of the count refectory. The lower circle is for "impersonalities": they are walled in earthen burrows with a window for food. In 1774, there were prisoners who spent four, seven and nine years in burrows. One cell was destroyed from the inside: a prisoner who had been there for ten years “run away”. The lower circle was dug later, after the Second Horse War, for especially dangerous criminals. From the count's office through the dungeon leads a secret passage to the ruins of the monastery, and in the dungeon there is a dead end with an iron door. The caretaker of the dungeon and the undertaker was Master [[master-sajrus|Cyrus]]. In 1775, Iona locked Martin here, and after her coup in the lower circle was transferred to the dying Alanis Almera.

## History
### Before 1774
According to Wittor, Waymar was burned to the ground three times. Vittor's grandfather kept a two-thousand-man Northwood regiment in Waymar for years so that the vassals would perform the oath. In August 1756, when the Seventeenth Gift came to Shayland, Emperor Thelurian, Prince [[adrian|Adrian]] and the great lords came here. At the same time, young Vittor sheltered in the castle of the future [[paul|Paul]]. About this time they say “[[teper-ty-koldun|Now you are a sorcerer]]”, where a mysterious young man Nathaniel appears in the city, and the flashback “The Price of Fear”. Paul was later entombed in the lower circle of the dungeon for many years, and then "run". Count Vincent died of cardiac arrest in a locked office while Vittor was in church.

### 1774 year
On May 8, 1774, the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] arrived in Waymar. Here the count instructed him to sell the Light Sphere, and [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] was beaten in the yard by the count's knights. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) In autumn [[minerva|Minerva]] was kept in the castle. Here Martin Shayland put his experiments on people and kidnapped girls. On November 26, he captured Mira, and in December Iona's cayres found barrels of corpses in the dungeon.

### Coup
In winter, Waymar was covered with snow, and through the frozen Smokey Dahl lay a sled, and to the cargo piers instead of ships docked sled. On May 27, 1775, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] rebelled against her husband in the castle. ([[perevorot-v-uejmare|Coup in Waymar]]) Donjon was partially burned out. The castle saved Finger of Wilhelm in the hands of Harry Hog, and Iona's kire was interrupted at the gate by an electric grid.

### The Siege of 1775
In June 1775, the North landed in the city. Within two hours, they occupied the port area, dragged residents out of their homes, and killed one in four. The bodies were dumped on Merchant's Descent. The siege was commanded by Desmond Origin. During the night assault, Vittor used the Sword of the Gods: the blow cut off the slopes of Castle Hill and killed thousands of people. But most of the "stormers" were Waymar citizens, whom Desmond chased to rams and siege towers as a human shield, and the kairs, meanwhile, destroyed Fleming's Belomorye battalion in the river port. About three thousand soldiers of the count, Horis and Fleming were locked on the Castle and Bald hills surrounded by the abyss. ([[oris|General Oris]]) The siege began. Between the castle and the nearest houses remained about two hundred yards of empty space, where there were pillars with the bodies of Iona’s kairs.

The well in the castle was more than two hundred feet deep. The northerners from the first day of the siege dug a passage to it from the city blocks, deeper than the ravine left by the “Sword of the Gods”, and poisoned the water. The second well, on Bald Hill, was in a sunset camp. As Paul's horde approached, Desmond Origin retreated. The Northerners lost only about five hundred people in Waymar, and the city turned into ruins.

### Chosen
On 1 August 1775, Gallard's fleet brought Waymar Paul, Gallard, Laura Farway and Alanis Almera along with food supplies to Waymar. On August 2, the "test" of the Chosen One was held in the castle chapel: [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] stabbed Paul and poisoned Vittor and [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s parish. Gallard died, and Vittor “resurrected” by [[absolyut|the Absolute]]. Alanis was tortured in the basement of the arsenal on the orders of the count, and then the dying woman was transferred to the lower circle of the prison; in the main cycle she is considered to be dead (in "Fantasies", by all indications, she lives under the name Mrs. Gale). The townspeople shouted “Glory to the Chosen One!”, but uncertainly: every tenth inhabitant of the city died in the war. People rebuilt houses: some burned everything, others “Sword of the Gods” cut off part of the house along with the land. The Count held a parade of his regiments and a feast in the squares from the stocks brought by the parish. The banks were robbed by northerners. Soon, the Chosen One's army went north, to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]].

## Trivia
- The Sheilanders, according to The Traveler, like to “come out into the people,” and they have a saying: “Only the rich and fools rest at home.”
- In the Waymar harbor, each land has its own trade, so that the ships of different peoples do not mix.
- In the autumn of 1774, Mira found four soldiers on the roof of the barracks, led by Lieutenant Brock, drinking the scythe, "tincture" of such a fortress that "you will gnaw - on your feet." Threatening them with Iona's wrath, she learned from them about the missing Lindsay. ([[lindsi|Lindsey]])
