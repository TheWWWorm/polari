It is a city in the Polari Empire. Little is said about him, and all the information is scattered over several episodes of the first two novels.

## References
- **Luis Maria's home.** The exploits mechanic introduced himself to [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] as follows: "Louis Maria of Otmeli, Imperial mechanic of the second guild." According to his story, he was the fourth child of a master builder. When the roof of the church, built by his father, collapsed during the rains, the family went into debt to the moneylender, and the boy was given to him for service. After serving the three years, he was never released; then Louis tied up the old man, took more than twenty Hilt and fled “to the west, to the Duchy of Hope”, because it was impossible to go to his father. On the road between Farway and the Heart of the Light, he was robbed, and he turned out to be a laborer on the railway construction of the Duke of Farway. As an adult, he worked on a rail construction in [[marengo|Marengo]], where he met his "lady". Among the people that Louis later called Erwin was the abbot "from his hometown", that is, apparently from Otmel (see Luis Maria). ([[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]])
- **The sanctuary of the cathedral.** Wandering across the empire, [[kharmon|Harmon Paula]] saw Sacred Objects on the altars of the central cathedrals of Labelin, Farway and Mistletoe. The shrines of Otmeli and Labelin, unlike those of Farway, did not perform any miracles and “silently looked at the parishioners from their marble pedestals.” And yet the parishioners walked away from the altars with shining eyes and blessed smiles (see [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]]).
- **City of [[berk|Burke]].** The mist is named among the cities where Burke, [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]'s companion in the second novel, "would prefer not to appear even at night", leaving "too warm a memory". The same list includes Farway, Whiteshed, Labelin, Grey Cliff, Twinsea and Belomorye.

## Location
Where exactly the hole is located, the book does not say. From the story of Louis it follows only that the moneylender, with whom he served, lived east of the Duchy of [[nadezhda|Hope]]: from there the young man fled "to the west". Whether the moneylender lived in the Pit itself is not said.
