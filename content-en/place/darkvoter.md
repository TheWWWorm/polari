## Geography
Darkwater is a "shadow kingdom" of marshes in the southwest of the mainland, on the coast of the Ocean of the Abyss. According to the Polari Land Guide, it borders [[rejs|Reis]] in the north and [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] in the south, across the White Bones Mountains. The prophet Francis-Ilianus made his way there by sailing barges along Holliwell. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]; [[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) The land is lowland and marshy: it is a multitude of large and small islands of hard land among lakes and swamps. The climate is warm and very humid, there are no dry months, and even in weeks without rain, air humidity reaches 90–100%.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collectible edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark noticed that the flora and fauna of the Beyond — widow snakes, hares, flightless birds, net grass — are found in the swamps of Darkwater, thousands of miles from there. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]; [[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) The grid here is so common that it disguises a moat with a quagmire around the royal castle of Nan Claire.

A unique natural phenomenon - Tops of Dark Kings. A row of the Voltic Islands sixty miles from the shore separates a quiet well from the ocean; the rivers of Darkwater carry foliage, humus, and remains into it, the southern sun warms it all year round, and the plant is so overcrowded with algae that it has turned black and green out of blue. ([[folta|Folta]]) The ship does not sink in this water, but it can not move: having strayed from the fairway, it is entangled with a keel in the “sea jungle” and grows into the sea - then they say that it was “captured by the Dark Ones”. According to Admiral Fanno Grok, there are three reliable fairways, and all three know only eight pilots in the world.

## Cities
- Nan Clair is the capital and, according to the “Guide”, a rare full-fledged city in these parts. The houses are like mushrooms and arches, the streets are boarded with logs under which the water murmurs; one of the hotels is arranged like an anthill - each room has its own balcony connected with the rest of the stairs and bridges. In the city there is the Cathedral of the Forefather Kristen with a Sacred Object - a statue of Kristen healing the sick; "The Pits" are sewage pits where rats eat sewage; a royal castle with a moat of loose grass-net and a Bridge Tower. The harbour overlooks the Tops of the Dark Kings, and the promenade is teeming with ships from Shimmery, Mills, and [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] waiting for weeks for a free pilot. ([[melnitsy|The Mills]])
- According to the Guide, Olvia is the oldest and most colourful city in Darkwater, founded in the IX century: 94 thousand inhabitants on one hundred and five small islands. All the buildings in it are wooden, and a third of the streets are bridges on the branches of the brabuses, which is why it was called the Wood City. The only stone structure is the entrance gate, which ironically was destroyed by fire three times.
- Plantations are ordinary settlements of swamps: not cities and villages, but islands of land “grown into swamps” and covered with dwellings. The largest plant is inhabited by tens of thousands of people, the smallest - a dozen.

The cities of the marshes are squeezed into the islands of the firmament, narrow and overpopulated; any conversation is heard by neighbors, so here they are used to talking with hints, and the marsh appreciates those who easily catch a hint. Erwin called the language of swamps "the language of hints".

## Economy
According to the “Traveler”, Darkwater is a “supplier of the strange”: spices, medical drugs, unusual drinks, edible algae and shellfish, pearls and shells. From the usual goods, he exports only fish, so trade is small, and the land itself is isolated from others. A significant part of the islets is devoted to farming: poultry, cows, pigs and moisture-loving crops. A considerable income is provided by Topi: through them there is an important way from the western lands to Shimmery, and only the Darkwater pilots know it. In addition to the Darkwaters, only the pilots of the Volta know the fairway.

Swamps are famous for poisons and drugs: corzo seeds, tincture of lilanna, the essence of the lunoeye, giving sweet dreams and addictive. From the topei also comes the dour-berry, the wine from which was served for tastings in the palace.

## People and Customs
The Guide calls the Darkwater a small nation in every sense: in terms of territory and population, only Shayland is smaller. Swamps are short and thin; six feet tall and two hundred pounds of weight for them is a fairy tale hero. Their dwellings are small and cozy, with rounded forms, living plants and drapery; stone and brick are rare here, and swamps do not build cathedrals, castles and mansions. Their main values are home comfort, family happiness and prosperity of their native plant. The songs of the marshes are about protecting the native land and about unhappy love: if a guy and a girl are mentioned at the beginning of the song, then at the end the guy will certainly leave it or drown in the quagmire. Even after living here for ten years, a stranger is unlikely to become his own.

From his past experience, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] knew swampers as “quiet, sweet, pleasant people in communication.” A foreigner cannot get to the queen or a noble swamp until the “gate of trust” passes: three trusted vassals must, each in their own way, test the guest, vouch for him.

### Funerals and Brabuses
According to the "Guide", unworthy people in Darkwater are burned so that the ashes are dispelled with smoke, and the worthy ones are lowered into a bottomless swamp so that they fall on the feast of the Underworlds - if possible, where century-old brabuses grow. On the bark of the brabus, the name, the year of death and exactly eight farewell words are carved. Brabuses are the only trees growing right in the quagmire; their apple-sized nuts are edible, and the contiguous crowns of marshes are connected by bridges, turning them into paths between the plantations. Trees are considered sacred and never cut.

### Department
Darkwater is a mystical land where evil spirits, spoils and amulets are commonplace, and witches, sorcerers and healers are in high esteem. Love potions are not considered sinful and belong to the “light witchcraft”. The imperfect love spell lasts for two years, and then it is not shameful to part: the demand is not from the lover, but from the sorcerer. Swamps are able to summon the souls of the dead, believing that after death the soul becomes eight times wiser.

According to the "Guide", sorcerers are divided into two schools. The school of Asphena, named after a rare beautiful flower, practices light witchcraft: healing, spells, illusions, predictions, interpretation of dreams. The Kribola School, named after the most poisonous swamp frog, is dark: poisons, reincarnation, enslavement of souls, and murder. Cribola stings are an order of assassin sorcerers; they are said to kill with a handshake or spit and arise from the air. In Marengo, they came ashore in spotted camouflage raincoats, "at first sight unarmed", with a sneaking catwalk. The glory of the swamp sorcerers is such that the orphan Mick in Waymar took the mysterious Nathaniel as a Darkwater sorcerer and studied the black wind technique from books. ([[mik|Mik]])

### War
By the standards of the North, in the Darkwater do not fight at all: marshes do not wear armor, do not collect large armies and do not throw cavalry at the attack, and the outcome of conflicts is solved by sorties, point strikes, scouts and assassins. The Queen's soldiers in Marengo were "grey short marshes" with crooked swords, short bows and poisoned arrows - formidable among the forests and toppling, but useless in the stone streets. According to the "Guide", the marshes survived in countless wars with Shavans, widely using camouflage, secret trails, traps and poisons.

## Ruling House
The lords of Nan-Claire rule Darkwater. According to the Guide, the 14th-century vassal agreement gave the Great House of Darkwater a royal title, and the land the right to be called a kingdom, although in strength and property (38 Objects) Darkwater stands on a par with the counties. According to Becky Litland, Darkwater, like Shayland, is considered "too young" for his daughter to become an imperial bride. ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]])

In 1774–1775, the queen was a blind Lady in Darkness, Madeline Nan-Claire, a pure-blooded Yanmean and great-granddaughter of Julian the Great; she is called the cousin of the lord Telurian and the lord Ingrid, the aunt of Ingrid and Adrian, and [[minerva|Minerva]] Erwin called her grand-niece. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) From Nan Clair came the very mistress [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]], Adrian's mother: "though a Yankee, but a marsh," she was betrayed for Telurian by order of his father Mainir and despised by the court. In 1762, Tellurian met at Darkwater to visit his cousin. Above all, she put the Yangmei family, but even higher - her own principles, and she sent her relatives who violated them forever into disgrace. Her daughter Mireille quarreled with her mother around 1766 and was expelled from the kingdom; it was later revealed that the Queen had dedicated her daughter to the [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] until the third stage, and the expulsion followed Mireille's refusal to serve the order. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) The Queen's court consisted of her nephew Lord Emmon Darklin, a Yanmean, and the Foltian Fanno Groc, the first navigator and admiral of the fleet. Along with Francis-Ilian, the Queen was a Master of the Order of the [[sadovniki|Gardeners]]; General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] became her eyes and hands at Faunterra after Telurian's death.

## History
According to The Traveler, Darkwater's origins are shrouded in mystery: answers are kept in ancient books of Nan-Claire's secret libraries, inaccessible even to imperial scholars. It is known that for ten centuries the marshes have been fighting off the Westerners. In the fifteenth century, the Shavans of Reis recaptured the meadows along the Hollywell and drove the Darkwater people into the marshes; since then, once every ten to twenty years, a new generation of Shavans raids for women and pearls from the Marshes, usually retreating after losing a quarter or a third of its army. Mira in Arrow, Coin, Spark knew that the Barons of the Sunset Coast, considering the blind Queen an easy prey, had repeatedly attacked her lands and "bitten off pieces". Hunter-Adrian told how in the XV century, the king of Darkwater quarreled with the emperor and declared war on him; the Yankee General Albert Cray took the capital, despite the losses from poisoned arrows, and gave the townspeople a frightening burial of the dead, after which the marshes no longer attacked the Empire. Shavan chiefs boasted of raids deep into the swamps: [[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]] - by killing five archers with poisoned arrows and a witch in snake skin, Cosmatius - by smelling and stabbing an invisible warrior. Raids of Westerners on Darkwater happen almost every summer. At the court of 1775 it was believed that the safest in Darkwater, because “no horses will pass through the swamps.”
