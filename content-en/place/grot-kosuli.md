The Grotto of Kosuli is a cave in the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]] above the valley of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], one of the main shrines of the North. It is connected with the legend of Foremother [[pramater-agata|Agatha]], and at the end of the cycle it turned out that the grotto leads to the secret of the Forebears themselves.

## Tradition
In [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] recounts a legend. Tired of hunger, Agatha the Radiant settled down for a night in a cave and saw a thin snow-white roe. The warrior who was with the Prammother grabbed the spear, but Agatha held his hand:
> “Let her go. She is weak and alone, be merciful."

The deer ran deep into the grotto, and the Mother followed her out of curiosity. The cave penetrated the entire rock, and soon Agatha went to the western slope of the mountain and saw a fertile valley blooming from there. In the "Shadow of the Great Tree", the western entrance of the grotto is called the same through which Agatha saw the valley "exactly one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five years ago." ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

The story was popular in art. In the First Winter, the canvas “Agata in the grotto of the roe deer” hung: in 1774, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] offered to hang him in a shady prostenka, and his mother, Lady Sophia, agreed. ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]]) In "[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]", Erwin shared with Iona the idea of a new Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant in Faunterra: in the underground floor, he wanted to make an imitation of Cosuli's grotto with a fresco, where Agatha saves the goat. Agatha there had to hug the roe, scratch her breast and rejoice, with a rain-wet coat and hair: “not at all canonical, but very true.” Shavanka [[chara|Chara]] knew the shrine under the name "Goat Caves" and retold the legend in her own way: it was as if Agatha milked the goat and gave milk to the first Duke of the North.

## Description
The grotto lies on the slope of the summit of Agatha. On the mountain there is a man-made terrace with a church, a font and a dormitory for pilgrims, and on the rock at the grotto there is a female Agata monastery. A path leads down from the church, and yards after three hundred it leads to the west entrance. In summer, pilgrims flock to the grotto. In [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], on August 30, 1774, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] and [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]], returning home, saw the First Winter, standing “on the same slope” from which Agatha the Radiant saw the valley. Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of the novel explains that there is a second, hidden exit from the grotto of Kosuli. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) Laz, she said, is barely noticeable, but "the mysterious beauty of Cosuli's grotto will reward your efforts."

A wide path fenced with chains leads to the cave, and the entrance is framed by marble slabs with skillful carvings. There are spark lanterns inside, and a cave scheme on the wall. The main course goes straight through the mountain, and the other is deflected down to an underground chapel with a statue of Agatha with roe, mosaics and a granite altar, and then returns to the main path. On the way there are sculptures of the Forebears.

At the time when the Order of the Light-Bearers was created according to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", in 572 the Abbot Valencia, the head of the largest Agath monastery, and Baron Arthur Leed, a fanatic admirer of Agatha, met in the grotto of Kosuli. Returning from this meeting, the abbot proclaimed the creation of a monastic-knightly order of Light Bearers.

## The Secret of the Grotto
In The Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] discovered that the grotto is the entrance to a cavity under the valley of the First Winter. According to his story, the "roots" of the First Tree, that is, the surviving bioreactors of the starship, required three deep reservoirs in the vertices of the triangle. There are only two, Smokey Dale and [[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) The forebears crossed the hostile mainland on foot, carrying reactors on the shoulders of robots, and for the third found a cavity under the valley, "the entrance to which is now called the grotto of Kosuli." Long before that, Navi, building a celestial ship, asked [[minerva|Minerva]] if she had been in the grotto of Kosuli and if the lake of the First Winter had ever dried up to the bottom.

After the battle, Captain [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] descended under the grotto. According to him, there is a huge cave, most of which occupies an underground lake. The water in it is “sticky like syrup and glows in the depths” with a greenish shimmer.

## Battle of December 2, 1775
In the battle for the First Winter, on the Day of Descent, the balloon of Minerva, beaten by [[paul|Paul]], collapsed on the slope of the summit of Agatha. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) The boat with Minerva, Navi, Inji Price and the captain imperceptibly landed at the Agate Church. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) Navi took [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]] from Minerva, went to the grotto, and Inji went with him.

Paul and Chara followed to finish off Nathaniel. The Seeker showed that he was in a cave. In the underground corridors, Inji killed Chara, and Paul overtook Navi in the chapel. Navi hid behind the altar, hurled the benches with the Glove, and received the fire of Perst at Hilt. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]) It was a trap. Navi drove Paul into a niche, where the real Light Sphere, the “searcher”, was hidden in advance, and put a fake on the altar. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) Paul decided that the Sphere was the enemy, and turned on the deconstructor of the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]]. An invisible sphere rose up in the niche around the Object, and everything that fell into it disappeared: a piece of wall and floor, sculptures, benches and Paul himself. At the last moment, he still managed to shout to Navi: “Run from the altar! It's a bomb!" There was a round hole in the place. The floor slab under Navi failed, and the young man collapsed down.

Minerva stayed at the monastery and called Navi into the breach, throwing stones at him, but received no response. The nuns were horrified: "The Grotto of Kosuli is desecrated! A hole in the abyss!” The next morning Minerva sent Shatterhand with ropes and men to bring Navi down the boat. The soldiers found him on the shore of an underground lake without breathing and considered him dead, but when they began to raise him, he coughed. Minerva Glove carried the boat with the wounded to the castle to Iona. Even before the battle, the lady closed the grotto for a visit.

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, which the author calls possible futures, in the summer of 1776, Mother [[mat-alisiya|Alicia]] came to the First Winter to consecrate the restored relics. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) The Grotto of Kosuli and the fresco "Agatha's Choice" are the greatest shrines of the Agat Order, and both suffered in the war. Erwin admitted that the grotto has not yet been restored: "The deconstructor's blow left a big hole in the rock." Under the attic of the guest house of the monastery, [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] served an honorary penal servitude.

## Uncertainties
- In the first book, Erwin sees a "narrow dark cave", called the Cosuli Grotto, on the sixth day of the expedition's journey through the Crystal Mountains. In the Shadow of the Great Tree, the grotto lies on the slope of the summit of Agatha just above the valley of the First Winter. How these places are related, the text does not explain.
