The Citadel is the oldest castle of [[faunterra|Faunterra]], standing on top of a steep mountain in the upper city, on the right bank of the Hanai. It was built more than a thousand years ago, and for seven centuries it served as a home for rulers. In the era of the Brilliant Dynasty, the Citadel became a prison and an underground treasury of Sacred Objects. Along the ravine at the foot of the mountain to the river descends the Merchant (Citadel) descent.

## Face
The citadel does not look like a normal castle. It does not have an external wall, separate towers, courtyard, barracks and arsenals. [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], who first saw her at night, described the fortress as a "whole granite cube, an indestructible monolith" of dark stone. Only the “tiny fireflies of the loopholes” showed that there were holes in the hromada. In the gate - a lattice and an oak leaf with a gate.

Centuries ago, the Citadel was considered impregnable. But when the residence was moved to the island, the city came close to it. The exclusion area was overgrown with streets, the bazaar grew on the place of the filled ditch, warehouses and merchant houses stuck to the bastions. Therefore, in 1774, it was already easy to storm it: enemies made their way along the alleys to the roofs of houses, and from there they climbed the walls along the ropes and stairs.

An air bridge leads to the Citadel. It was built in the early 18th century, under the emperor Alexian. One end of the bridge rests on a hundred-foot tower on Palace Island, the other enters the monolithic wall of the Citadel. On this bridge, the emperor led the guests to look at the treasury.

## History
- **Dark-eyed dynasty.** Seven centuries Citadel was the residence of the kings of the family [[pramater-miriam|Miriam]]. During the Troubles, the rebels learned the secret of the underground passage, penetrated the fortress and killed Emperor Algon III with the whole family. Thus was cut off the Dark-Eyed Dynasty and the Miriam family. After that, all the underground passages were blocked.
- **Damned place.** The brilliant Dynasty retained the role of throne residence for the Citadel, although the castle was called cursed. Two centuries ago, during the First Horse War, in the throne room of the Citadel, Vladyka Evrian received news of defeat. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]) Upon learning of the fall of Hope, he ordered the guards to slaughter the two brothers-dukes who had brought this news. After defeating the Barbarians, Juliana moved the residence to the new Pera Palace and Sword on the island. ([[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]; [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]])
- **Two hundred years without change.** According to [[minerva|Mira]]'s observation, nothing has changed in the Citadel since the time of Juliana. Half a century ago, only spark lighting was carried out.

It is said that in the dungeons of the Citadel are still haunted by the ghosts of the kings of Miriam. Any Yankee, going down there, as if he feels cold moisture on his skin - "ghost tears".

## Storage of Items
The soil beneath the Pera and Sword Palace is too loose and damp for reliable storage. Therefore, the property of the Brilliant Dynasty - three hundred and twenty-four Sacred Objects - was stored in the dungeons of the Citadel, in the granite thickness of the cliff. By a special directive of the Crown, at any danger to the Citadel, the company of the Azure Guard was to immediately take the property to the Summer Palace in [[marengo|Marengo]]. The directive was known to the burgomaster, the commandant of the Citadel, generals Smile, Gore and Davey, and advisors to the emperor.

The way to the treasury led along the stone staircase, worn to the dents, into the underground hall with tapestry. It was locked by three gates: oak, cast iron and bronze. Behind them stretched the main gallery "in the Yangmei pathos": marble emperors, gilded standards, crystal chandeliers. There were grottoes on both sides of it. Above each purpur there were numbers - numbers of Divine Gifts, and in the walls there were niches for Objects on velvet pads. The gallery ended with a blank wall with the coat of arms of the Dynasty. Behind it was a secret tunnel with rails for a trolley, leading to the south-west, to the town hall.

## Capture by Northerners
On the night of the [[bitva-za-faunterru|capture of Faunterra]], in December 1774, the X's climbed the Citadel by rope. There were no guards in the gallery. They lit a fire on the roof and ambushed the guards, who ran out to put out the fire, was killed. Then, on the Air Bridge, the advanced detachment of the Origins, dressed, penetrated into the palace. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] commissioned the Cyre Derrek Citadel. He ordered to explore the dungeons and bring the Wilhelm's Fingers, and the fortress itself after that to throw: from all sides it was surrounded by city houses.

There were few defenders. Major Backfield attacked the Citadel from the rooftops, and Erwin sent [[brant-stil|Brant Steel]] over the Air Bridge to help. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) All night, Derrek's men were breaking three storehouse gates with rams and levers. When they opened it, Erwin and [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] saw the empty grottoes. While the kairs were breaking down the doors, the people of the burgomaster Baron Escher came through the tunnel in a trolley and took everything out. ([[baron-esher|Baron Esher]]) Later, the Citadel fell: the guards struck simultaneously on the wall and through the dungeon. Backfield executed all the prisoners taken at the Citadel and dumped their bodies on the island.

The items of the Asher Dynasty were loaded onto a train to Marengo, but they were kidnapped on the way. The kidnapper was the Puppeteer who collected [[absolyut|the Absolute]]. Hence the question that Erwin and Minerva later fought over: the Puppeteer knew from the registers what Items were stored in the Citadel, but there was not one necessary Item there. In June 1775, [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] returned Minerva to the Hand of the Legendary as an Item stolen from the Citadel. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]])

## Prison
There is no dungeon in the Palace of Pen and Sword, so the prisoners were kept in the Citadel. In 1774, Secretary [[itan|Ethan]] hinted to the World that the members of the Shutov conspiracy could still live "in a stone coffin under the Throne Citadel." ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]])

The cells for the noble prisoners were “better than other hotels.” They had a bed, a table and a secretary, a bath, a bookshelf and large windows on Hanai, albeit behind bars. In February 1775 Minerva went down a narrow stone staircase to the arrested ministers Albert Viahl and Morlin-Mei. ([[albert-vial|Albert Vial]]) They became her mentors in finance. She later kept the Snowdrop Chiefs in the Citadel. ([[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]])

## Citadel of Labelinov
At the end of September 1775 [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] demanded the Citadel as a pledge for a new loan to the Crown. [[magda-labelin|Magda]] objected, "The citadel is a symbol of history! She's over a thousand years old. The first residence of kings... The Duke replied, "That's why I want to." Later, Adrian, according to Valerie, "resigned his father-in-law" to the Citadel.

In Fantasia, Minerva officially recognized the debts of the Dynasty to Labelin. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) The Citadel, the Air Bridge, the Admiralty and other buildings passed to the Duke. Father and daughter settled in the Citadel, filling the upper floors with luxury, and the lower floors with knights of Baron Darryl. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]])

## In "Fantasies"
- In the Citadel, under the supervision of representatives of both branches of the Church, [[chrevo|the Womb]] with the frozen [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] was kept. When Minerva had the ghost of Vittor, all requests to the capital concerned the Citadel. The responses reassured: “There are no alarms in the Citadel.”
- During the election of the Emperor Magda invited Minerva to stay in the Citadel as in "the most fortified building of the capital". The Azure Guard feared the Labelins and offered to take control of the fortress. Mira refused.
- In the square in front of the Citadel, a crowd demanding "Adrian, show your son!" clashed with the scarlet guards and the Knights of the Labelins. Baron Darryl had Maurice's orders to defend the Citadel and Magda, but not to engage the Kairs.
