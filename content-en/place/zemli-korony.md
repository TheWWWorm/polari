## Geography
According to the Polari Land Guide, the Crownlands lie to the east of the mainland and face the East Sea. In the north, they border the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], in the south - with [[nadezhda|Hope]] and Mason's Bay. The relief is flat, with few hills and many rivers, the largest of which is Hanai. ([[khanaj|Khanai]]) The climate is moderate. Unlike dry Hope, the Crown is replete with forests: on the border of the two lands, the forests are cut off, “as if someone cut them off with a single stroke of a fiery sword.”

The Crownlands are connected to the rest of the empire by rail and wave lines. ([[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]]) From here come the branches of Faunterra — Aleridan and Faunterra — the Heart of Light; according to Harmon, to the Heart of Light six hundred miles. On Canaan, there are bridges and ferries, including the Hampton Bridge, which was burned in 1774 by the retreating troops of General Alexis. Litland, which does not have its own sparkle, is supplied with a spark from the Crown Lands.

## Cities and places
- **[[faunterra|Faunterra]]** is the capital of the Empire on the Hanai River.
- **[[marengo|Marengo]]** is a seaside city with a summer residence of the lords, “the richest port city”.
- Arden is an ancient city sixty miles from Faunterra: four hours by train or a day by ship. It was founded in the first century from the Descent and is considered the title possession of the emperors.
- Ruilde is "the second after Marengo harbor of the Crown Lands", where ships from the Shimmerian Orcad go.
- **[[niar|Niar]]** is one of the great cities of the Crown, devastated by the sea in 1774.
- The bend is a town in the bend of Hanoi with a single ferry crossing for a hundred miles. ([[izluchina|Riverbend]])
- Lowville is the first city of the Crown on its way from the South Road. His baron kept the Sacred Object in the dungeon, and the Crown decided to build the temple of Yangmei the Merciful here.
- **Hampton** is a city in Canaan.
- Orwell is a city on the rail route to Marengo, "half way" to it. Three roads diverge from it - to Marengo, Arden and Faunterra. According to the Guide, Marengo and Orwell were once called Almera.
- Pale Meadow is a former post office seven miles from Faunterra that has grown to a town. Forty years ago, there was only a stable, a tavern and a post courier service, until an enterprising merchant built a hotel for those who can not afford the capital housing. The local church bears the name of the Forefather Kristen, and the nobles do not like it, preferring the temples of the Foremothers.
- **[[piksi|Pixie]]**, Julianum in the south of the Crown, Yalestock, Lacey's Manor and Patrick's Chapel near Arden.

## Economy
The Guide calls the Crownlands a prosperous land, the first in the Empire in terms of GDP. Sparking machinery, rail roads, agriculture, arms and armor production, crafts and trade are developed here. Loyal taxation and the disintegration of feudal relations stimulate industry. The crown uses advanced methods of smelting steel, against which the northern steel of Origin does not withstand competition. Here are quartered spark regiments of the imperial army.

Taxes in the Crown are special. As Erwin explained at the beginning of the cycle, traditionally only the Lands of the Crown pay the Dynasty a permanent tax. The great Houses owe to the Crown military service and other services, but not the filing, and pay it only to those who are paid off from service. The blacksmith Jonas kept the forge three miles from the Almer village of White Stone, but already in the Crownlands, perhaps because "the taxes from the masters are lower".

According to Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, by the time of the Northern Land Flash, the Crown followed Almera's replacement of the poll tax with an income tax. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]; [[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]])

## People and Customs
According to the “Guide”, the power here is highly centralized, and the influence of the imperial court is huge. The feudal lords are weak, but the officialdom is very strong, the so-called “dogs”. Culture, art and science are highly developed. There are two universities, in Faunterra and Marengo, the Academy of Arts in Faunterra, libraries, theatres and conservatories. Residents are proud of the proximity to the emperor and the level of culture and technology, ahead of the marginal lands “for a couple of generations”. Crime is low, there is almost no feudal arbitrariness, but corruption is palpable, with which the Dynasty is struggling with variable success. The Guide admits that the culture of the capital lands cannot be described even briefly: Faunterra absorbed the entire history of Polaris.

The Crown is special. According to the explanation heard by the World, in Faunterra and the Lands of the Crown, the Lord is "God, every word is law", while in the rest of the lands the law comes into force only after the consent of the House. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) By the same explanation, the Lands of the Crown are not sent to the House of Representatives like other areas. However, the text later refers to two representatives from the Crown.

The novel also mentions a local custom: giving up a gift in the Lands of the Crown is considered an insult. The Lowvilles themselves, according to their countryman, are "a quiet people."

## Ruling House
It is ruled by the Brilliant Dynasty itself, aka the Ardennes Dynasty, aka the Yangmei Dynasty. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) The Guide notes that none of the three names are accurate. Arden is considered the original title possession of the emperors. Adrian's full title is "Adrian Ingrid Elizabeth of the Janmay family, Duke of Arden, Emperor of Polari". But the lords live in Faunterra and Marengo, and in Arden they find peace only after death, in the tomb of the temple Farewell. Therefore, the title "Duke of Arden" sounds gloomy and usually applies to the dead. The founder called the dynasty Brilliant, to at least in words equal to the luxury of the deposed Miriamians. Emperors are not always Yangmei by blood, and the name indicates rather the principles of the rule of Pramateri Yangmei.

## History
### Arden and the Age of Despair
According to legend, in the Age of Despair, when the Agathans conquered the world and Faunterra fell, the kingdom of the Myriamians shrank to three cities - Arden, Marengo and Ruilda. According to the Guide, King Orsayn Miriamets in the Wars of Despair gave way to the Light-bearers of Faunterra and moved the capital to Arden.

During the Sweet Ages, the Myriam kings moved their court to Arden so that the noise and dirt of the Faunterres would not prevent them from enjoying life. For two hundred years, the city became the “small capital of Polaris”. Black was not allowed, except for the servants of the court with bracelets of the Myriam coat of arms, and dogs, cats, ostriches and lemurs of dark-skinned kings who loved animals roamed the streets. After the Crimson Troubles, the Yankees moved the summer residence to the practical seaside Marengo, and Arden froze for almost five centuries, "as if covered with a cap." The townspeople lived the remnants of the past: they sewed clothes in the former royal workshops and fished in boats on which the Myriamians rode their young women. The city was reborn only at the end of the last century, when Arden built a spark dam on Canaan. The main shrine of Arden remains the temple of Farewell - the tomb of the Forefathers and Kings, where in the crypt bubbles with the blood of the Pramatery and the Forefathers are stored. There are more than 100 temples and two seminaries in Arden.

## In the Novels
### 1774 year
In the autumn of 1774, during the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]], the spark regiments of General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] remained in the Crownlands. After his defeat in Labelin, the northerners went to the capital. In the winter of 1775, the Minister of Taxes [[drejfus-born|Dreyfus Born]] pressed forward from the crown lands.

### Snowdrop Rebellion
[[vosstanie-podsnezhnikov|Snowdrops]] coming from the Southern Way entered the Crownlands via Lowville. There the peasants drove out the Bourne collectors, and the burghers approved of it. [[zub|Tooth]] doctor from Lowville became the leader of the townspeople, and seven hundred Lowvilles entered his column. Then Niar, a dozen towns, Izluchina and Hampton joined the uprising. Hampton met the rebels with “bread and honey”, exposed five thousand people and raised money for them. According to Joaquin, thousands of burghers and only hundreds of peasants joined the campaign on the lands of the Crown. By the time the Snowdrops left Hampton, there were forty thousand, and two-thirds of them were citizens.

At the Holy Field, [[minerva]], through the new Minister of Taxes, Frank Morlin-Mei, announced a tax reform for the Crown Lands. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) Each farm, workshop and workshop was assigned a fixed annual tax. In each city, a bank branch was opened, and it was necessary to pay only by depositing money to the account of the Imperial Treasury, without tax collectors. Unlike the Southern Way, where the Great Houses are taxed, the lady could decide for herself.

### 1775 year
In the spring of 1775, Minerva took refuge in Marengo. [[paul|Paul]]'s heretics strike brigade attacked Arden and lost half the fighters. They ravaged the tomb of Lady Ingrid in the Farewell Temple, after which the city was flooded with constables and protection agents. In the summer, the southern part of the Shavan horde under the leadership of Juhan Reis and Gant Cort broke into the Crownlands and devastated the city outside the city. ([[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]]; [[ganta-kort|Ganta Kort]]) Shavans hastily gutted the houses and rushed to the main prey: from Orwell, roads to Marengo were opened, crowded with the temples of Arden and Faunterru. The returning [[adrian]] moved to the capital from the north, and Minerva was "between the hammer and the anvil." Minerva's train, which had left Faunterra, was at Patrick's Chapel near Arden. Retreating from the capital, the horde went to Almera. After the Battle of the First Winter, Minerva stayed in the First Winter and decided to appoint a viceroy or viceroy to the Lands.
