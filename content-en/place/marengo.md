## Description
Marengo is the second largest city of the Crown Lands and the "richest port city" of the empire. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]) It stands on the shore of the High Seas. According to Erwin’s entourage, it is the summer capital of the lords and “the refuge of the emperors, it always was.” The Polari Land Guide names Marengo among the largest cities in the Crown (along with Faunterra, Arden, and Ruilde) and notes that the actual residences of the emperors are in Faunterra and Marengo. It is one of the two universities of the Crown.

The Imperial residence is the Palace of Silence. It is three times inferior in size to the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] and is divided into many courtyards, wings, turrets and gazebos. The main building is lost behind a maze of green hedges, only birds and fountains are heard in the courtyards, and the guest seems to have got into the garden of the monastic monastery. The palace has dungeons, a dovehouse, a wave station and a pink gazebo for the audience. The city is famous for shady alleys. For several centuries, every self-respecting Lord Marengo puts somewhere in the city a modest bronze miniature, and experts are looking for these sculptures in the shadows.

Marengo is connected to Faunterra and Labelin by rail. ([[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]]) Halfway to the capital lies Orwell. There is also a bypass to the capital through Marengo when the bridge over the Back is destroyed. ([[bek-most|Beck]]) The Imperial Navy is based in the port, and the second harbor of the Crown after Marengo is Ruilde. The Marengo Seafaring Academy has a marine printing house where maps are printed. The local archive duplicates the Empire-wide record of cases being kept in Faunterra. There is also a tax office, where Minister [[drejfus-born|Dreyfus Born]] sat in the spring of 1775.

The coast around the city is replete with fishing villages, in which there are almost no fishermen. Under Telurian, the prosperity of the capital allowed not only nobles, but also officials, merchants and even craftsmen to get out on vacation once every couple of years. Fishermen in turn set up inns, and the shore was overgrown with hotels for any wallet. It is easy for fugitives to hide here: “there are more strangers here than locals.”

## History
According to legend, in the Age of Despair, the kingdom of the Myriamians shrank to three cities - Arden, Marengo and Ruilda. In the Sweet Ages, the Myriam kings kept a small court in the Ardennes. After the Crimson Troubles, the practical Yankees moved the summer residence to Marengo, because "the seaside city provided more benefits than the river". According to the Foltic admiral, the Shimmerians used to consider Lord Lymon's crew from Marengo as the discoverer of the Laem Cape, although the Foltians had circled the Cape much earlier.

## In the Events of the Series
### 1774 year
In the summer of 1774, [[kharmon|Harmon]], who was fleeing the chase, took a ticket for the nearest train, which was going to Marengo. There he bought a handicraftsman's clothes and a new ticket to the south of the Crown. Later, [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]], [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]]'s agent, traced it to Marengo and at the seaport discovered that a merchant under the name "Glorious Horam" had chartered the ship to Laham. ([[lajem|Laem]]) Botsman Biven, talking about swimming around Polaris, mentioned that in Marengo they sold the goods.

In December 1774, under a directive in the event of an assault on the capital, Burgomaster Escher sent the Items of the Dynasty by train to Marengo under the protection of Captain Whitmore's company of azure guards. On the way, the water on the train was poisoned, and in Orwell, "half way to Marengo", the train was ambushed, and the items were stolen. At the same time, snowfalls cut off the spark line at Marengo, and the trains stopped running. During the Siege of Pera Palace and Sword, a half battalion of Marines arrived from Marengo to assist Backfield. In the story "[[khochu-poni|I Want a Pony]]", General [[stetkhem|Statham]] and the Farway family pass through Marengo. [[laura-farvej|Laura]] remembers the sweet cocoa at the station.

### The Flight of Minerva (Spring 1775)
In the spring of 1775, [[minerva|Minerva]], who was in the care of Origin, fled the theater at night. She boarded the Labelin-Marengo merchant train, which passes the capital at four in the morning, and left under the protection of Whitmore's azure mouths: one held the station, the other the palace, and the wave. Erwin wanted to send forty-two for her, but he was dissuaded, reminded that two southern kings were about to arrive in Marengo and Minerva could open the Chamber right there.

On May 6, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] arrived here without an army, only with keir [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] and the dog Sagittarius. The Azure Guards kept the guests for more than four hours in the gazebo, nestled with roses, without lunch, and by evening announced that the mistress refused to receive. On the way to the gate, Jemis fell into a trap, and on the dark alley of Erwin, the Shavans were waiting - the Gant Groza and the giant Sormach, whose father and brothers were killed by the northerners. ([[ganta-groza|Ganta Thunderstorm]]) Erwin cut off Sormachu's brush with the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]], and when Thunder took up throwing knives, Minerva came out of the shadow and stopped the fight: she promised the Shavans a chance to kill Origin only one of them. The owner admitted that she herself arranged all this to see how the Duke would behave in mortal danger. That night, she discovered to Erwin that General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] was serving as Lady in Darkness. A [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]] squadron entered the harbor: a flagship under black sails, the rest of the ships under dark gray. Francis-Ilianus arrived with the queen, and they handed the living [[menson|Manson]] to the Crown. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) Erwin welcomed them "on behalf of the Crown and the Great House Origin" and returned with Minerva to Faunterra by one train, which was then courted by the whole court.

In May 1775, in the Palace of Silence, Backfield and Whitmore reported to Minerva about the search for stolen items. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Here, the mistress ordered to throw in the cells of witnesses - [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]] and Baby G. Minerva went to the prison to Inji, her former abductor, asked to teach her to forgive and, after listening to his confession, offered him the choice of returning to the galleys or taking an injection, giving herself to the trial of Pramatery Ulyana. Minerva later admitted that after a visit to Marengo, where she had to “judge, evaluate, forgive or not forgive”, she decided to become “at least a little better” and limit herself in guilt. At the end of May, Navi, [[karen|Karen]] and Dorothy arrived by train from Ruilde to Marengo in the spring rain: in the local court, Navi learned that the imperial court record, which is being kept in Faunterra, was duplicated in the Marengo archive. From Marengo, Navi sent waves of the Priory to Evergard, to the Northwood Mansion in Faunterra, to Treasurer [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], and to the Lord's Secretary Dorian Amber. ([[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]]) There he became involved in a street quarrel with two noble offspring who ridiculed his pronunciation. On 29 May, she sailed from Marengo for the [[folta|Folta]] on Lady Sophia Origin's Sea Arrow. ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]])

### Summer of 1775
When a horde of Shavans approached from the west, Ferguson and Day's bank began to take reserves to Marengo, and the entire nobility stocked up on train tickets there. The Horde, taking Smallden and Keysworth, set out on the roads to Marengo, Arden and Faunterra. Marengo was for the Shavans the most tempting prey as the richest port city. [[paul|Paul]], refusing to beat off Farway's Aleridan for the archpriest Gallard, offered him in compensation any city of the Crown Lands to choose from, "Orwell, Arden, Marengo." According to [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], Erwin traveled to Marengo again in the summer to meet the empress. In the autumn, newspapers reported that a celestial ship was seen at Marengo, which drew a harness of sixteen eagles.

## In "Fantasies"
In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], stories of a possible post-war future, in 1776, the printing houses of Faunterra and Marengo were controlled by the people of [[adrian|Adrian]], the mayor of the capital. Therefore, Dorian Amber, who was looking for a printing shop for Minerva under a secret circulation, found it only in Ruilde. ([[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]]) In November 1776, during the election of the emperor, Adrian summoned two corps of the scarlet guard from Marengo and Arden, but their transfer to the capital was disrupted: the power lines were damaged, and the way to Marengo, according to the mocking remark of Minerva, was “invalid” - spark guilds broke equipment “only at the right moments”. Merchants of Marengo sold goods in the far south: the Shimmer Princess Demeter offered a perfume with a note of lavender "delivered from Marengo by the only lot."

In the "Death of an Assassin" (spring, by all indications 1777) in the "picturesque corner of Marengo County", among the flowering cherry and apple gardens, stands the estate of the former Minister of the Court of [[khoksvort-fellion|Hawksworth Fellion]]: a white stone house in the Julian style on a gentle hill, a well-groomed park with a pond and swans, five miles away. The retired minister arranged a copy of the imperial court with a turquoise guard here, and in the underground storage he keeps the family item - the Clock of Destiny. Here, the lawyer Mac solves the murder of the imaginary Inge Price, and in the finale, the estate is decided to turn into a paid attraction.
