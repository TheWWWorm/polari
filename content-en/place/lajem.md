## Description

Laem is the "pearl of the Shimmery Kingdom", a white-stone port city with tea houses, bridges and arches sheltered from the sun, and gardens under domes. According to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", Laem is among the largest cities of Shimmery along with the Orcada and Surlion, and the king of the House of Nellis-Lyon is considered its master. Laham is the patrimony of Prince Hector, the First of the Five; his titles include "the honorary burgomaster of Whitestone Laham". ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]])

### Face of the city
During the day, Laam gets tired of the heat, and in the evening he comes to life: the townspeople have dinner on the terraces and balconies, taverns put tables right on the street. The palaces of the rich are crowned with huge domes, their narrow figured windows are woven into an intricate elm. There are many bridges and arches in the city. Marble galleries connect neighboring palaces, covered markets - with merchant mansions, tea - with hotels, and bridges are thrown over the roofs of poor houses so that you can pass by the slums. One bridge is four blocks long.

### Harbours and Quarters
Mention is made of the Golden Harbor, which includes large squadrons, the Albatross Port with cabacs in the backyard and the Tea Port with the Abundant Descent neighborhood, where they buy althess. In the port there is a large teahouse Exila, where althess are traded. There is a university in Laem: Master [[gortenzij|Hortensius]] listened to open lectures there in his youth, and later, when Harmon was building a ship, Onoriko brought books about gases and a prominent master alchemist from the university, and he explained how to retain and produce hydrogen. There is also the Black Swan Hotel near the trading house, away from the port and stagecoach station, a refuge of poets, artists and philosophers overlooking the mountains, where they rent rooms "for creativity or for tenderness." Morning Laem of those: the nobility does not wake up before noon, and artists and musicians sleep.

### Palaces
In the royal palace live Prince Hector, his father and the court. The daughters of the king are assigned a separate Maiden Palace among the waterfalls, where later one Infanta Demeter remained. Many consider it the most beautiful building of Laham; its maintenance costs seventy ephes per month.

### Trade and roads
From the north gate of Laham to Pentago leads the White Path - according to the narrator, the best road in the South, and perhaps in the whole Empire; two miles west of it, in a secluded valley, lies the wine town of [[melison|Melison]]. Laem merchants have the privilege of trading in silk, so in Orcad, according to the scout Neymir, posing as a Shimmer merchant, silk is traded only by a fool. Laeme, he says, is much richer than Orcad. The Shimmerians are proud that the Laem Cape opened the crew of Lord Lymon of Marengo; the Foltian sailors claim to have circled the Cape long before.

## History in the Series

### August 1774 (The Skyship and the Girl)
At Laem Square, Master Hortensius for three months unsuccessfully offered the citizens to fly in a [[vozdushnyj-shar|balloon]] - a "heavenly ship": the Laemites were frightened by gossip, as if flying takes away male power. Onoriko-Meysor introduces the visiting merchant "Horam", that is, [[kharmon|Harmon]], with Shavanka [[niza|Niza]], brought to Laem for sale. ([[onoriko|Onoriko-Maysor]]) Harmon buys her and the ball and moves to Melison.

### Spring of 1775 (The Yanmei Hunt)
On the trail of Harmon, who kidnapped the Light Sphere, [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] comes to Laam on the corvette "The Dog on the Barrel". He questions the cabbies and water carriers, goes out to Onoriko and his wife Vanessa-Lilit and kidnaps them. Onoriko dies in Melison: on Buckley's orders, he is cut throat by the mercenary Rodrigo.

Meanwhile, [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]'s squadron enters Golden Harbor, and Laem celebrates without interruption: fireworks, elephants, musicians. For the sake of meeting her, the first nobles of the kingdom come to the city. Hector leaves the palace for the first time after his return from Litland, the Third of the Five Count Auguste-Riemar brings the wine wagon, and the Second sends his Marquis. Magda wants to buy the Shimmer spark "eyes", but the nobles five days to treat her flattery and gifts, and about the weapons are silent. After her angry speech, Hector, referring to the heart, leaves with his violas, the other members of the Five also refer to ailments, and then only the secretaries speak with the guests. Then the Prince’s fleet leaves the bay without a farewell, and the Prince bills the travelers for twenty days in the best residence of Laem, where a thousand of their soldiers lived. According to the prince’s secretary, the party of eyes has already found a buyer and is about to go north. Whitestone Laham's regiment leaves with Hector for Faunterra.

### Winter of 1775 (The Shadow of the Great Tree)
There were rumors at the [[minerva|Minerva]] court that the Empress would move the residence to Laham: the minister of the court discussed this with the Shimmerian ambassador, and the banker Conto called the white-stone Laham the only one of the five great capitals that the war would not reach, and sent the bank's gold reserves to the Southern Way, from where they were to be transported to Laham. ([[konto|Konto]]) Minerva, however, told him to buy buildings in Faunterra.

### August 1776 (Fantasy)
In the story "Amicable", a northern mission arrives in Laem Harbor: [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]], [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]], and Mireille Nan-Claire. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) They are met with fireworks, elephants and an orchestra; the procession is led by Sheriff Laema himself, and among the participants there is even an ostrich in the lieutenant's uniform. By that time, Lady in Darkness had died, and Mother Alicia took King Francis from Laham to Faunterra. Here the story of the night auction of althess at the ganta Farhan, the search for the kidnapped nephew of Hyder Barney, the love potion of Mireille and the matchmaking of Jemis to the infant Demeter takes place. The Permanent Ambassador of Darkwater to Laham, on behalf of Emmon Darklin, invites Hector to arrest Mireille. Ganta Farhan brought to the auction the prisoners taken in the battle for Perst from Rei-Roy, including the Almer girls; the market was “sudden”, because after the defeat of the Gnoi-Ganta, the shavana ceased to fight among themselves. In the auction pavilion, separate lodges are reserved for ladies and gentlemen. The investigation into Barney was led by Sheriff Laema Halintor.
