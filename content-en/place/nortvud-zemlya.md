## Geography
Northwood is a county in the North of the Polari Empire. According to the Polari Land Guide, it occupies the central part of the North between [[shejland-zemlya|Shayland]] in the west and [[oridzhin-zemlya|Origin]] in the east and overlooks the Ice Sea. ([[more-ldov|Sea of Ice]]) Northwood has access to the Smokey Dahl, but the heavily swamped coast makes it difficult. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) The area is slightly hilly, covered with dense, mostly coniferous forests, with many lakes and a network of small, almost non-navigable rivers. The climate is cold, temperate continental. The winds from the Ice Sea seem piercing, but in fact soften the winter: even in the coldest winters, the frost does not fall below thirty degrees. The county, according to the "Guide", includes the Granite Islands in the Ice Sea.

In the novels, Northwood is primarily a dense forest in which there are many places for ambushes. Traveling through the county of [[minerva|Mira]], he saw how the forest was replaced by tiny fields, “bored in the thicket”, and then again approached the path itself. Sincerely Northwood villages are like forts: they are surrounded by ditches and high palisades, and oncoming people are “invariably stocky and gloomy”. Around Lisye Dol, peasants burned hundreds of acres of forest along the river to fertilize the soil with ash. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) On such patches they planted turnips, onions and beans, and the pigs were grazed in the surrounding forests. In the far north, along the Ice Sea and on the Granny Islands, there are emerald bears. Bear down Northwood peasants get cunning: nailed to the trees wooden "crests", which the animals rub during moulting.

Communication with the South is difficult. The only overland trade route from the Crown Lands to the north goes through the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], which Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] called "a chokehold on the neck of the northerners." ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]) Northwood's army can go south and over the water, through the Smoky Dahl. The land road from county to south passes through the city of Doyle in the Southern Way. In Shayland, the Northwooders get through the Pre-Lessee or on Torrey: in 1774, Count Elias and his sons drove to the capital up the Torrey to Waymar, and then across the lake. ([[torrej|Torrey]])

## Cities
- The Bear Fang is the capital and ancestral castle of the counts, a port with a fleet. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) The castle is "huge and smells of pine": tall shady halls, fireplaces, skins everywhere and walls hung with hunting trophies. Among them are buckets, wolves, moose, armoured sidekicks and fang skulls, but never bears. The city walls are wooden. According to the "Guide", here and on the Granny Islands are the largest shipyards of the North.
- Lysias Dol is a wooden city between a river and a forest, with a baronial castle on a cape, similar from a distance to a "basket dropped in a puddle". ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) This is the birthplace of [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]]: her mother Dorina was the Baroness of Fox Dol. Fox Dol had a decisive battle in 1775.
- Birbis is by "Guide", one of the three largest cities in the county.
- Woods and Magrid are the cities through which the army of the Chosen passed in 1775.
- The granular islands are an archipelago in the Ice Sea with its lord. In the narrow straits between the islands with the pirates fought the Origen sword Voice of Winter, and the Belomorye Fleet successfully operated there in the sixties of the XVIII century.

According to the Guide, the cities of Northwood are usually fortified settlements among the forest. Fifty thousand inhabitants is a medium city for the central lands, but a large one for Northwood.

## Economy
The Guide calls Northwood a land of average prosperity. The farm is kept in the forest: hunting, furs, export of wood and wooden products. A special industry is shipbuilding. Fishing is developed, and a little gold sand is found in the streams. The Counts have a port and a navy. The old Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias]] was, in Mira's words, "indifferent to everything but the navy." The most famous Northwood commodity in the novels is a strong tincture of hanty, which is drunk throughout the Empire, from kairs to capital lords. ([[alkogol|Wine and Spirits]]) A good bowler should know sixteen marks of hanty. One of the best is considered to be the hanti of old Grimsdale; the hanti of Bald Dole is also famous. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]])

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark writes that according to Julianne, the vault of the Northwood forest is a public property. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]; [[yulianov-zakon|Juliana's Law]]) Peasants have the right to hunt, collect and cut down the forest if they pay a reasonable tax to the local authorities. In the rest of the empire, the forest belongs to the feudal lord, and he can completely prohibit fishing.

## People and Customs
The Northwoods are lumberjacks, sturgeons, sailors, and artisans, a "volunteer and obstinate people" who "never let down grudges to anyone." According to the Guide, these are harsh and straightforward people who respect strength and are hardened by forest life, but are generous in soul and hospitable. The obligatory elements of their hospitality are a feast and a bath. In the novel, Erwin kept a story about Northwood baths, common to men and women. No feast is complete without a ballad, and bragging about hunting trophies has never been considered a lie. According to the "Guide", Northwooders painfully perceive reproaches in savagery and easily get into a fight. But they are sincere and reliable friends: “to tell or to show mercy — the heart will tell”. In the Fang of the Bear, fighting on the streets is common. Cairo Murdoch, at the sight of another mordovia, contemptuously threw: "Bears".

The kitchen is spicy and spicy, a lot of meat and fish, berry sauces and pickles. The sacred beast of the county is a bear, a symbol of his power: no hunter of Northwood will dare to kill a bear. At the court of the Countess lived a scientist emerald bear Maverick. He knew how to dance, bow, "weep" to the chivalric ballad and distinguished people in the face.

Northwood's army bears little resemblance to Erwin's army. In November 1774, the Northwoods entered the Labelin in uneven crowds, failing to keep up. Many went in open leather jackets or in the same shirts: the white Southern weather seemed to them hot. Black-bearded men carried round shields behind their backs, and axes, long swords and hammers on their shoulders. They reminded Erwin of “a huge gang of forest robbers.” The main weapon of the Northwoods is battle axes, and the warriors themselves are called “bears”.

## Ruling House
The county is ruled by the Great House of Northwood. ([[dom-nortvud|House Northwood]]) As stated in Arrow, Coin, Spark, the county is ruled by "descendants of free shooters and northern pirates" who bent their knees before the Dynasty less than two centuries ago and have since been forced to look back at the Origins. During the years of the cycle, Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias]] was the formal chapter. In fact, his wife [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] ruled, and after her fall, the son of Elias [[krejg-nortvud|Craig]], who, according to Elias himself, is “loved” by two-thirds of Northwood warriors. The middle son, Horace, was a viceroy in the Bear Fang.

## History
According to the Guide, the forests of Northwood began to be settled in the VI-VII centuries. Here went the poor, outcasts and free men who did not want to obey the feudal lords, and Northwood was known as the land where "every man is his own lord". Hunters went to the Sea of Ice, engaged in fishing, and then piracy. In the VIII century, the land fell under the power of the Order of the Light-Bearers and a century later, after the Wars of Despair, returned the former liberties. Piracy of the 11th and 12th centuries caused a long feud with Origin, which, according to the Guidebook, ended only during the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]] in the 16th century. It was she who forced the Northwoods to rally around the strongest warriors and led to the establishment of county power. The Northwooders were the first to call kire "wolf", and it was thought to be a mockery: by the standards of Northwood, a wolf is a small animal next to a bear.

In the novels of the war with Origin are mentioned later. This is the [[vtoraya-nortvudskaya-vojna|Second Northwood War]], as well as the "recent war" with Northwood, which weakened the Origen fleet by 1756. In 1756, during the War of the Objects, Northwood's relationship with Shayland was so strained that "bears" on any day could join the sunsets. In the 18th century, Northwood, according to the Guide, is almost a good neighbor of Origin: they are united by a northern spirit and a general contempt for the Southern Way.

## In the Novels
### 1774 year
On 21 March 1774, Sir Clive Stagforth was killed in the Pre-Less when his detachment was on its way to the Bear Fang. ([[klajv-stagfort|Clive Stagfort]]) Mira rode to the Northwood capital, and the Countess Sybil took her into care. Then Sybil arranged a change of brides: her daughter [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]] went to look at the Faunterra under the name of Minerva Stagforth. In the autumn, Northwood's army (about 13 thousand people) crossed the Smokey Dahl and joined the rebellion of Origin. ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]]) The fanged Knight Craig and in the battles of the Northern War acted in his own way, ignoring the orders of the Virginia General [[stetkhem|Statham]].

### 1775 year
In the summer of 1775, the county, according to the story of [[navi|Navi]], did not rejoice in prosperity. Craig, who received the title of commander-in-chief from Minerva, left his native land, settled in the imperial capital and feasted on military trophies. His army absorbed a considerable part of the budget, the land became poorer, men and barons united in discontent. In March, a riot broke out, which Horace suppressed with difficulty.

In the summer, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] sent Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]]'s Bear with a detachment and the old Count Elias to the Fang. Dorcaster declared Elias a legitimate ruler and took control of the garrison with the help of Perst. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) Craig, who was going to help [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] to Waymar, left an ally and turned to the capital. It was his regiments that had to block the bays around Waymar in order to prevent [[paul|Paul]] from landing. Beneath the Fang Bear walls, Dorcaster threatened to sink the fleet if Craig set fire to the wooden city, and for two weeks led him by the nose with power-sharing negotiations. When the south of Northwood had already submitted to Vittore, the Baron surrendered the Bear Fang to Craig and went to Lysias Dol, taking Maverick. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]])

The Chosen One's army marched through South Northwood without encountering resistance: the knights left for Craig, and Desmond's cues retreated. Vittor captivated the inhabitants with miracles and feasts. In Woods he fed the whole town at tables along the main street, in Magrid he bought four fish barges and treated all the inhabitants, and at a feast in Birbis the local bishop died at the table from gluttony. Desmond, retreating, set traps, raided and poisoned wells. The bridges collapsed under the feet of the Shaylands, dry flashing. Lysias Dol, like other cities, opened the gates without a fight.

In early October 1775, Bald Dol Desmond took the fight along with twelve thousand Craig. The Northwood forests seemed to be a profitable place to fight against the Persians. In addition, Desmond hoped to beat off his captive daughter [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] from the wagon. The Northerners were defeated, Craig died, and Northwood came under the rule of Elias - in fact, puppets of the Puppeteer. Elias and Horace, whom Craig kept in prison, were brought to light. Supporters of the old earl called themselves "sedentary" and marked clothes with a white line, and Craig's followers, "the rams", were beaten in the streets. Bear regiments were now fighting on the side of the enemy: in the battle for the First Winter, the Northwooders in brown and red furs were advancing along with the sunsets, and the second Northwood regiment was in the earl's reserve. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])
