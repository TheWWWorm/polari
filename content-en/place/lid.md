**Leed** is one of the largest cities of the Duchy of Origin and the capital of the Counts of Lilliday. Northerners call it the "ancient capital of the North": the city is older than the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] and once argued with it for the championship.

## City
The ice stands on a plateau in the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]. The water in it is supplied by an aqueduct about half a mile long: it stretches from Mount Soratnitsa over the abyss a hundred yards deep and passes directly over the central square. The city is surrounded by a double ring of granite walls seventy feet high and twelve feet thick, with moats and dozens of towers. The whole of Leed is stone; the shutters have cut corners to get more light into the narrow streets.

Leed is famous for gunsmiths - "Leed swords are the best in Origin," said [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], - winemakers and warriors. The Northerners put the orchid above all else: even [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] admitted that only in the ability to cook an orchid the First Winter did not surpass the ancient Leed. ([[alkogol|Wine and Spirits]]) Wooden cups for an orge are called "lidkas". The city has a quarter of gunsmiths, the chapel of Ulyana Sad, the temple of Gloria the Intercessor and a monument to the lord on horseback.

In the castle of the Lillidays, according to ancient custom, twenty-eight skulls of enemies are walled in the walls. According to the same "Leed custom" in the wall of the First Winter, the ghost of the "Leed general" from the story "Let's catch him."

## History
According to the Guide, Leed was founded in the 3rd century on silver mines, and the First Winter was initially nominally owned by a Leed baron. Around 572, Baron Arthur Agatha (Arthur Leed) stood at the origins of the Order of the Light-Bearers, and Leed with the First Winter became "two of its capitals"; General of the Light-Bearers Baron Henry Leed defeated Orssein Miriamz. The dispute between the two cities was resolved by the fight between the Earl of Lilliday and the Duke of Origin in the courtyard of Fort Superior at the Skvorok Sky: the Count recognized himself as a vassal of the First Winter. The bridge between the forts still serves as a border: to the east lies the domain of the Origins, to the west - the lands of the Lillidays. Leed master Conrad forged the sword of the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]].

### The War of 1775
In July 1775, the Snow Count Lilliday, father of Jemis and Simon, died with the corps under the [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]]. In the autumn, the army of the Chosen moved to the First Winter, and Desmond Origin retreated to Leed. None of the residents left: “We are the leaders. Where else can we be?” Within 24 hours, forty thousand citizens enlisted in the militia under the leadership of [[sajmon-lillidej|Simon Lilliday]]. To burn the city, so that it did not get the enemy, Desmond refused. Simon told the others to portray submission, and "the ancient Leed surrendered to the Chosen One without a fight" to [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] and the army of [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]].

Vittor and his entourage settled in the castle. On the first night, [[gvenda|Gwenda]], a governess who had found her firstblood, mutilated [[martin-shejland|Martin]] and Joaquin in the basement of the castle and almost freed the captive [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], but [[paul|Paul]], who appeared, killed her with a kiss. In early November, Vittor left for Adrian, leaving the city to Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]] with Perst and the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]]. Martin asked to be made ruler of Leed, but his brother only made fun of him.

Shavan Paul demanded to give them the city for plunder; Dorcaster refused, threatening with the Voice of God. Then Martin stole the Baron's Item and in exchange for Iona sold to Leed Paul. The Horde knocked out the gate. Meanwhile, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] and Hyder Leed and [[obri|Aubrey]] made their way into the castle via an icy aqueduct. Iona Ulyanina Dust forced Martin to give her the Ring, burned Dorcaster and locked his garrison in the barn; the Shavan leader Knut took the horde by order. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Martin ran through the cesspool. The raid cost the city seven hundred killed and maimed, about five hundred driven women, and several burnt-out neighborhoods. Leaving the city, Erwin, Iona, and the seven leaders on the day of the Descent broke the wagon train on the Leed road.

After the Battle of the First Winter, [[minerva|Minerva]] mobilized the Leed refugees — even the "mouth of harlots" made nurses — and instructed Jemis to help her brother and prepare Leed's defenses. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])

## In "Fantasies"
In Lovely (August 1776), Jemis restores the "half burned" Leed and jokes that the castle has now been renamed Black, "according to the new color", and the aqueduct has become "mirror". Barney, Hyder's nephew, was taken away from the burning city. Queen [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille]] is going to Leed to Hyder's wife. In the "near-further" Neymir repents before Minerva, and she believes that he needs to repent before the inhabitants of Leed. ([[nejmir|Neimir]])
