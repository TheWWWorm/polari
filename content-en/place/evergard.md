## Description
Evergard, the citadel of the Dukes of Almer, lay on a hill by Aleridan, "like the crown lying on the emperor's head." He did not need moats: the steep slopes themselves protected the fortress from siege towers. Three rings of walls surrounded the hill - at the foot, in the middle and under the very top. The only road ran up between the walls, clamped by them on both sides, and the grief was to the enemy army, who risked to step on it. The walls divided the castle into three courtyards: lower, middle and upper, girded with granite.

Like the teeth of the crown, the top was dotted with towers. Twelve pointed watchtowers surrounded the thirteenth, the central, square in section, not less than two hundred feet in height and fifty in width. Half of its walls were occupied by a huge fresco of gold and mosaics: a knight kneeling before the Agatha the Radiant. The hair and cloak of Agatha, the helmet and sword of the knight were flaming with gold, the armor sparkled with silver, and the coats of arms with rubies. Mercenary [[berk|Burke]] was amazed at how much money was spent on this "piece", and [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] considered the fresco a mockery of enemies. On the spires of the towers were rinsed flags: the upper one was the duke, the other twelve were the coats of arms of the noblest knights of the garrison.

### Inside the Castle
In the donjon were the Duke's father's office and reception room. Within the walls of the donjon there were secret passages with auditory windows; young [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] loved to hide in them and eavesdrop on everyone. According to young Albert, there are thirty-two auditory vents in the castle, leading to all the main rooms, so that the uncle-arch hears everything they say. In the castle there is a chapel with ancient frescoes and icons, survived the fire, a large library and a music salon. On the southern slope of the castle hill, a vineyard sprawled, where lovers couples appointed meetings.

From the castle, a secret underground passage leads to a nearby wooded hill with a burned mill. The entrance to it was masked by stones that fell into the ground, opening a square loophole. A mile from the castle was the Duke's hunting ground.

## History
### Residence Almer
Evergard was the home of the Dukes of Almer. Alanis recalled that in the upper courtyard, she was “dad’s daughter”, and in the lower one, where strangers were allowed, “her lordship is the infant Almera”, and always chose how to dress there. Erwin Origin traveled here with his father to visit the Duke of Aiden on a high road coming from the Beck Dam.

### Death
In the summer of 1774, Aiden Almer, accused of treason, took refuge in Evergard with a doubled garrison, considering the castle impregnable. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) On the night of September 2, about fifty horsemen with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] in less than an hour blew up the gate and burned the citadel (see Death of Evergard). ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) The Duke and his son Alfred were killed. The wounded Alanis escaped through an underground passage; little [[albert-almera|Albert]], a handful of warriors and a couple of dozen servants also survived.

### Recovery
[[gallard-almera|Gallard]] immediately began to rebuild the castle and fortify it against the Persians. Within a few weeks, Joaquin saw scaffolding and supplies around: long-range ballistics platforms were erected on the towers, a moat was expanded, and the inner road was blocked with barriers. Locals praised the archbishop: the castle will be even better than it was before the fire.

By December, the towers had a new red tile, only a few buildings stood in the woods. Gallard made Evergard his residence, and the castle became the residence of not only the Duke, but also the head of the Church. His lower court was open to supplicants, pilgrims, seekers of protection and blessing; there crowded craftsmen, merchants, the poor, monks in blue sutans and warriors with cloaks in sacred spirals. In the lower court, the priest erected a pillar for the burning of heretics, and after each execution, a new one was immediately erected there, as an edification. In the main building lived the priory and its prime vassals.

### Hostages
In the winter of 1774–1775, [[laura-farvej|Laura]] and [[dzheremi-farvej|Jeremy Farway]], grandsons of the ruler of Hope, lived here; Brother Clement supervised them. Laura admired the architecture and mosaics of the castle, but was afraid of the pillar in the courtyard. Here grew up Lord [[albert-almera|Albert]], who lived in constant fear of uncle monks; the hearing vents of the castle were once shown to him by his sister. General [[stetkhem|Statham]] came here with an ultimatum from Empress Minerva. Through the auditory outlet, Laura and Jeremy overheard Gallard talking to a mysterious interlocutor associated with the owner of the Rings.

### The War of 1775
During the War of Origin with the Priory in 1775, Gallard's troops first gathered at Evergard, but then the collection point was moved to the fortress of Voronovo Pero. In June, the priory marched from Evergard to Fliss; the North expected him to bring a second Aleridan Regiment from there. Mother Cordelia announced that the Church of the Foremothers was heading to Evergard to present evidence of his collusion with the heretics to the archdiocese.
