## County

According to [[kharmon|Harmon]]'s explanation in Arrow, Coin, Spark, the Duchy of Almer was made up of three counties and a magnificent [[aleridan|Aleridan]] with a district. ([[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]]) [[erroubek|Arrowback]] County occupied the northeast. After driving it for a week to the west, you will fall into the possession of "another Almer Count - Blackmore." A week later, the road and turn to the south began County Daynite. Each of the three counties, Harmon said, was larger than Shayland. According to the Polari Lands Guide, the city of Blackmore was among the largest cities in Almera along with Aleridan and [[fliss|Fliss]].

The history of the county is associated with the main troubles of Almera. According to the Guide, in the First Horse War, the horde of Darian the Bad captured the entire duchy and burned Aleridan and Blackmore, and later, during the war with Holliwell due to the dam, Holliwell's troops kept Blackmore under siege. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]])

In the west of the county was the cave monastery of Ulyana Pechalnaya, where in 1774 the Peace was languished (see Attempt on the Peace). ([[minerva|Minerva]]; [[otravlenie-miry]]) Later, while rehearsing a conversation with [[adrian|Adrian]], Mira imagined that he would designate this monastery as a place of her confinement. In the county there was also the city of Laxette, to which in the "A Doll on the Throne" came a detachment of the Sorcerer. ([[mik|Mik]])

## Earl of Blackmore

### The Betrayal of Alanis

The Earl of Blackmore (his name is not named in the books) owned the city of Blackmore and was considered one of the closest vassals of Duke Ayden Almera. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) Sir Humphrey said that he had “twenty years of service to his Lordship.” After the death of Evergard in the summer of 1774, the wounded [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] was taken to him as a vassal, "which you can certainly trust." ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) His estate was about a hundred miles away.

The castle turned out to be a new building: white, light, with many windows and almost no walls. [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] shook his head skeptically. The count, black-haired, with a noble sowing on his temples, went out to meet travelers along with the fat whiteness of the countess. When Alanis opened his face in the carriage, he only sighed and promised the detachment protection. Then the count asked Sir Humphrey for an hour, who else knows about the duchess, set watchmen at her rooms, who did not let the maid out, and sent messengers to the parish of [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]. Joaquin, Squire [[berk|Burke]] and the knights tried to make their way to My Lady. Blackmore's warriors attacked them on the staircase of the guest house. The fugitives had one horse: Joaquin took Alanis, and Burke, Sir Humphrey and Sophie's servant had to be left in the castle. Then Blackmore's men chased the fugitives for a long time.

Since then, Alanis hated the count: “The villain Blackmore will answer in full!” She later called him along with Gallard and Adrian, and Erwin briefly said, "We are faced with betrayal by the Earl of Blackmore, and my poor knights are dead."

### In politics

In 1775, the Count, together with Alanis, represented Almera in the House. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) Erwin noted how scandalous this composition: Alanis hated "the Count of Blackmore who betrayed her" and sat next to him. In the description of the Chamber, the graph is called “sly” and “not willing to explicitly protect the interests of the Puppeteer”. Along with Lord Farway, Blackmore expressed extreme concern about the circumstances of Queen Darkwater's illness that began on the train.

The fate of the count became a bargaining chip. Under Gallard's contract with Origin, about which the prior told Laura, the traitor Alanis Count was to be put on trial, and his spark shop passed to Count Arrowback. Alanis also promised Arrowback the very county of Blackmore. At the trial, Erwin told what the price was: according to Alanis' plan, proposed in mid-December 1774 in the besieged palace, Joaquin was to give the Earl of Arrowback a deal - he would adjust the collapse of the bridge and the death of the lord on the right day, and in return he would receive from Alanis the County of Blackmore, when the Earl of Blackmore would be executed for treason. By June 1775, the earl was under arrest in Faunterra, under the authority of Origin.

## Colonel Daniel Blackmore

In the summer of 1775, in Erwin's war with the priory, the first Black Sea regiment was commanded by Colonel Daniel Blackmore, a nephew of the Earl, nicknamed Big, with a thick black beard. Prisoners reported that the regiment is in the fortress of Voronovo Pero, and the second Black Sea follows. [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] offered to release the prisoners with a message: Erwin would let his uncle go if Daniel went over to his side, or, conversely, would stab his uncle so that the county would go to his nephew. Erwin accepted the plan.

General [[veksler|Wexler]] wanted to wait for Fliss, and Blackmore demanded an immediate attack, citing the will of the Forefathers. Major Toystone, looking at it, wondered if he had not bought the Colonel's bat. In the night battle, the knights in white coats with Almeran pennants (in fact, the Northerners) killed Wexler, and then “mighty blow” killed Blackmore. The remains of the regiment, deprived of the commander, suffered heavy losses in a forest fire. Four knightly companies, Blackmore's vassals, disappeared without a trace after his death. Major Toystone feared that the rest would desert: most of the regiment's officers were direct vassals of the House of Blackmore, and the colonel was killed, and the count sat in captivity at Origin's. Later, an unbeaten Black Sea regiment suddenly appeared at the cliff under Fliss and confused Erwin's calculations (see Fliss). ([[fliss]])

## After the war

In July 1775, the horde of [[paul|Paul]] walked through Almera, and Blackmore and Arrowback, according to the steppeman, "hid to their castles." Henry Farway, picking up Almer's hands, mapped the counties of Blackmore and Daynite following the domain of the Almer dukes. Through these same counties, the horde went to the Steppe, finishing off the remnants of the Almeran troops.
