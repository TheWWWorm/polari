## Title and history
According to legend, it was in this valley that the [[pramateri|Foremothers]] came down from the Crystal Mountains, and spent their first winter here, which is why the valley got its name. They then went six hundred miles south and established a capital in the South Road. [[minerva|Minerva]] has repeatedly wondered why the Foremothers did not stay here. According to other sources, the First Winter remained a village of shepherds and monks for a long time, until the Agathans about a thousand years ago, in the Age of Despair, decided to conquer the whole world. The ancient capital of the North is considered to be [[lid|Leed]], founded long before the First Winter. The Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant, according to Vittor Shayland, erected the legendary Lord Raidan nine hundred years ago - he is "the age of the House Origin."

### By "Guide to the Lands of Polari"
The guide describes the early history in more detail. The first winter arose as a shepherd village, when after the silver miners in the valleys of the Crystal Mountains came shepherds. In the IV-V centuries it had no castle, no walls, no knights, and it nominally belonged to the Leed baron, who was paid a ridiculous tax. The pious people and the peaceful nature made it almost crime-free: there have been no murders here for years. Founded in 572, the Order of the Light Carriers chose the First Winter as its headquarters as the holy place where Agatha's legs first touched the lands of Polaris. Baron Arthur Leed allocated a place in the valley for the monastery of Light and the cathedral of Agatha. The city became a center of science and education, and in the 7th century it was home to more than one hundred thousand people, two academies, a dozen schools, four monasteries and eight libraries.

In the Wars of Despair, Baron Henry Leed wanted to lure the royal army into the valley of the First Winter, considering it the most reliable trap, but King Orsayn bypassed the ambush and drove the army of the Light-bearers into the valley. After three months of siege, she surrendered. The reward for the adviser to King Reidan Adler was the title of Duke and the First Winter, and he called its surroundings the Duchy of Origin. The city, according to the guidebook, retained the title of the strongest in the world, second only to Faunterra.

## Face
The valley is surrounded by two semicircular ridges of rocks, above it stands the peak of Agatha. To the observer from above, the valley resembled an eye: snow meadows - protein, blue lake - iris, black castle - pupil. From the west, the Leed Road enters the valley, from the north - the Belomorye. In the spring of 1774, while descending from the pass, Erwin saw the valley as an “emerald spot” and “a spring flower that made its way through the snow.” On the Belomorye road, a couple of miles from the city, there is a gorge with ambush-friendly ledges, and above it there is a herding path. Once Erwin, wandering along it, was warming up in a cave by the fire, bred by the shepherd [[brant-stil|Brant Steel]], and the smoke through the gap in the vault was visible very far away.

The castle stands on a peninsula overlooking the lake. It is a "dark monster with eight heads of towers in the crowns of teeth", with a donjon, North and Lake towers. From the west it is covered by a lake, from the north and south - two wide ditches and ramparts, and from the rear - the city itself. Three bridges lead to the castle: north and south through the moats and east from the city. In the castle there is a library, and in the ducal chambers there is a chair, which, according to Erwin's joke, was long guarded by the ghost of Odar Spesivogo. When asked by Minerva, the lake never dried up. At its bottom are sometimes found the bones of ancient kairs and rusty armor, and the locals believe that the ghost of a drowned lady lives in the lake, dragging girls under the water.

The city is surrounded by a fortress wall, the spires of the cathedrals and the clock tower of the town hall rise above it. The streets are built up with narrow houses in three windows, compressed into a dense line. Among them are the pointed towers of temples, gloomy squat barracks, trading rows under the red tiles, and arched aqueduct stretches from the mountains, at the water pipes of which queues are built. On the square of Glory there are granite horsemen, on the square of Mercy there is a plague monument. Erwin himself saw his hometown as "an ancient city full of soldiers and beggars", and his mother compared it to Aleridan: Aleridan rushed into the future, and the First Winter "forever froze into the past".

According to Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collectible edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, the slope from which Agatha the Radiant first saw the valley leads to a second, hidden exit from Cosuli's grotto. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]; [[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) Laz is hardly noticeable, and Karen advises travelers to look for him.

The main shrine is the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant with a high vault and chapels of the Forebears. Its community is crowned by a hundred spires and guarded by three hundred and thirty-six chimeras, including two "funny winged pigs" under the gutters of the southern tower. In the southern nave, in the chapel of the Descent, there is a huge canvas depicting the descent of the Forebears into the Under Kingdom. Underneath the cathedral is a tomb, which Desmond expanded in 1774 to ransom Iona by building an underground chapel and rebuilding the western side chapel. At Iona's wedding on the small altars of the cathedral, the Objects of the All-Seeing and the [[vechnoe-techenie|Eternal Flow]] were exhibited, and the townspeople stood in line for hundreds of yards to them. In one of the chapels there was a fresco “Agatha’s Choice”: a light-haired girl on the fortress wall breaks a feather. There is a crypt under the cathedral. On Mount Agatha there is a monastery, and under it is the grotto of Kosuli, a northern shrine: according to legend, here Agatha milked a goat and gave milk to the first duke. In the depths of the grotto there is an underground chapel and a huge underground lake with glowing greenish water.

## Mystery of the lake
At the end of the cycle, Mireille Nan-Claire revealed that the Foremothers had raised the "First Tree" that made the world livable. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) The tree had three roots, and each needed a huge fresh water body. One of these reservoirs, as [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] understood, was the lake of the First Winter. The roots fed on special items called "reactors" in the annals of the order.

## The first winter cycle
- **"Arrow, Coin, Spark".** From here [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] went on an expedition for the River and returned here on August 30, 1774, after which he assumed ducal power.
- **“Only one star.”** In the valley under the First Winter, Erwin mustered twelve battalions to fight Adrian. The Earl of [[fleming|Fleming]] plotted to suddenly take over an almost defenseless city.
- **"The Shadow of the Great Tree".** In 1775, the remnants of the Origin forces retreated here. By decree, given during the retreat from the Lands of the Crown, Minerva moved the capital of the Empire to the First Winter. Arriving here in the autumn of 1775 on a flying ship, she created a secretariat, ministries and a new courtyard here. This is where [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] brought the imperial treasury. Minerva built an ice maze in front of the castle. [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]], who had retreated, brought with him forty thousand soldiers, and Minerva mobilized them before the battle, taking a company of harlots to the nurses. The army of the Puppeteer approached the city through the Squaws of Heaven handed over without a fight. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]) December 2, 1775, on the Day of Descent, the Battle for the First Winter took place here. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) A third of the castle buildings, including the donjon, burned down. The city lost about a thousand soldiers and eight hundred citizens. Upon entering the cathedral, Vittor Shayland exclaimed, “Now he is ours!” The city rose up against the invaders, and the fresco "Agatha's Choice" on the orders of Shayland was shot by the Perst. On the night after the battle, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]], [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]] and [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] were killed in the castle. Soon in the First Winter there were negotiations between Minerva, Erwin and [[adrian|Adrian]], during which Robert Origin executed Lady in Darkness. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]])
- **After the battle.** In the battle, the second Lake Tower of the castle burned down.

## In "Fantasies"
"Fantasies" the author calls possible options for the future. In 1776, the First Winter was the capital of the Empire, ruled by Minerva.
- **Construction.** In the hot summer of 1776, the city was full of work: houses, shops, hospitals, stations, ministries, spark windmills, poles of transmission lines and supports of the future [[zheleznaya-doroga|railway]] viaduct, which Minerva erected every morning with the Glove of Power. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]) Thousands of artisans lived in temporary dwellings under the walls. The Imperial Secretariat and the ministries are located in the city center, there is also the city palace of Minerva.
- **City.** With the transfer of the capital, Merchant Street blossomed: showcases with porcelain, silk, carpets, tea and coffee were pushed into the alleys of gunsmiths and potters. On the outskirts lies the Little Deadlock, the artists' quarter. In front of the castle stood an ice “minervin labyrinth”, where Sofia staged a performance for the birthday of the mistress.
- **Ghosts.** Rumor inhabited the castle and the city spirits: Odar Spesivyi, "the king of ghosts", chained to the ducal chair in a large hall; Morray the Chronicler (Longbeard), who remained in the city library; Swollen Lady, drowned in the lake; He Who howls in the gate tower. There are three guesthouses around the castle. The X-cloaks ghost hunt describes "Let's catch him."
- **Monastery and clinic.** On Mount Agatha stands the convent of Svetloy Agatha with a guest house for pilgrims to the grotto of Kosuli; [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] served an honorary penal servitude in his attic. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) The Mercy Clinic, founded by Lady Sophia, became famous when she began to treat Iona: on the first floor - the queue and sorting, on the second floor - the treatment, on the third - the hopeless patients, a flying carriage sat on the roof. Shops, queue trading and a fast stage station have grown around.
- **The cathedral.** The Cathedral of the Agatha the Radiant was closed for restoration: frescoes were restored, including the “Agatha’s Choice”, and new frescoes began to be painted on four pylons instead of patterns. The first of them, where Iona treats the wounded Lord with the Hand of the Infirmary against the background of the burning First Winter, was consecrated by Mother Alicia. In the evening, the wind pipes of the cathedrals sang in unison.
- **Dungeons.** Under the castle, in addition to the dungeons, there is a glacier: in one half there is ice, in the other there are frozen dead, including Count Benedict [[fleming|Fleming]] and the Shavan chiefs. In the basement, under the arsenal, there is an inscribed vessel with [[chara|Chara]]'s ashes.
- **Surveillance.** From the summer of 1776, the First Winter, according to the story "Closer - Further", was watched by Adrian's "Bird" - by all indications, [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without flesh]], - which was ruled by a one-armed Shavan Zlataya Griva: she saw the streets, offices and bedrooms, but did not hear the words.
