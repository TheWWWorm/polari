The **Palace of Pen and Sword** is the residence of the Polari emperors in [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. The palace entirely occupies a large island in the middle of Hanoi and amazes with space: within its walls “it was possible to arrange races or place several regiments.” ([[khanaj|Khanai]]) Lady Sybil called it the royal residence of the Dynasty.

## Name
Above the door of the central entrance shines a huge gilded imperial coat of arms: "crossed pen and sword against the shield, crowned with a crown." The pen and sword are signs of the House of Yangmei, and they are repeated everywhere. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) Handrails of the Palace Bridge are made in the form of crossed swords and peacock feathers. Above the palace are usually raised "imperial pen and sword", under the flags of Per and the Sword are the troops of the Crown. The countess at the audience wishes the lord health with the words "The power of the Feather and the Sword is the support of the underworld." Sometimes the palace is called short: Erwin says goodbye to Alanis with the phrase "See you at Per and the Sword."

## Island and Bridges
The palace island lies a hundred yards from the waterfront. The rear side is facing the navigable fairway, and in the south it narrows and stretches a long slant along the stream. The palace is surrounded by a wall, but inside it is not a fortress yard: behind the wall there is a huge area with flower beds and gardens. A strip of sand stretches between the wall and the water. In summer, it serves as a beach, and Erwin Origin often bathed there with Suzanne or Nexia.

The Palace Bridge leads to the island from the quay, from the Quay Square. It is made of white stone, at least fifty steps wide, with spark lights on the pillars. The far end of the bridge rests on an iron gate with a gate tower. In the middle of the bridge is hidden "Yanmei engineering": a stone square with a side of ten yards on the turn of the helm in the tower rolls over to its side, and the road disappears. Merchant ships were forbidden to go to Canaan in front of the Palace Island (see below). ([[mejnir|Maynir]]) In 1775, burgomaster [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] allowed merchant ships to pass under the lift bridge, which the merchants had been seeking from the emperors for thirty years.

The air bridge takes off from the palace to the right bank. One end of it rests in a hundred-foot tower on Palace Island, the other enters the wall of the Table Citadel on the cliff. Inside the tower, a spark platform lifts guests along the mine to the bridge. The bridge was built in the early 18th century, under the emperor Alexian. The soil under the palace is too loose and damp for a reliable storage, so the objects of the Dynasty remained in the dungeons of the Citadel, and the bishop led the guests to look at the treasury on the Air Bridge.

## Building and halls
The palace is a long building, shining with whiteness. The facade is decorated with hundreds of spiral columns, with towers with spires rising from the roof into the sky. Each tower has directional lights. There is no dungeon in the palace: it was built “in the years of great triumph” and was supposed to embody joy, light and celebration, and the sandy soil of the island is bad for dungeons. Prisoners were held in the Holy See. There is a cellar in the palace.

Lady Sybil explained to Meera that the emperor receives visitors in three rooms.
- The Grand Throne Hall accommodates many people. All nobles are allowed there, starting with knights, clergy and mayors, and even commoners on Saturdays. Therefore, there are no important conversations here.
- Tea (imperial tea salon) - for the closest circle: advisers, ruling dukes, holders of special privileges. The only commoner allowed there is the former nurse Adrian Leonor. It is a small loggia with walls of malachite and amber mosaic, a stucco ceiling and oval windows to the cherry orchard. The invitation here is a sign of the Lord’s special trust.
- Small Reception near the southern portal is something in between: a conversation with almost no outsiders, but with a secretary. The parquet here forms three circles of ornament that diverge from the throne. Near, golden, marks a place for high-ranking guests, eight steps from the throne.

In addition, the text names a small throne room - "the heart of the palace", the hall of ambassadors with tapestries, where the lord bow messengers of all lands, a marble ceremonial staircase and a gallery with bronze statues of knights. The enfilade of the ballroom has banquet halls, a music parlor, a game room where stratums are played, and a room with a marble waterfall and a mechanical nightingale. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) Along the imperial chambers there are an office, a game room, a reading room, a tea room, a music salon and a painting hall. There are eight harpsichords in the palace. The sovereign [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]] favoured only one, made in Aleridan in the sixteenth century. In the portrait there are portraits of rulers, who, according to Vladyka Telurian, “have something to learn.” The galleries traditionally contain at least three portraits of each sovereign: on the day of the coronation, at the hour of greatest glory and before the burial. There is also a “post-mortem room”. The ladies' hall for dancing is called by its ceiling fresco with snakes - "Ladies' Feast". Ingrid ordered a ceiling painting for the palace with ruins, among which snakes swarm. The tables in the Hunting Hall are made of halves of pine trunks. At the kitchen of the south wing, a man eats, and there they feed with scraps from the table of the lord himself.

## Gardens and Island Buildings
Around the palace there is a park with alleys, ponds, fountains and gazebos. During the ball in the garden, fountains are buzzing and couples are walking. On the southern end of the island there is a Grove of Love, "a kind of imitation of the jungle." In the northern part of the island there is a long squat pavilion of the Shrine of Pera - the palace archive and library. All originals have the right to read manuscripts there. The palace dove is similar to the animal jaw: out of five sharp-top turrets, four are given to bird cages, and in the fifth, birdmen are on duty in blue uniforms. On the island stood the barracks of the palace guard.

In the south, on the spit, there is a cargo dock. In the summer there is water, and in the winter on the sleigh supplies for the palace. The pier inspired many artists: the picture of the “cargo pier in May noon” hung in [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]’s room in the boarding house of Elena-u-Lake. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) In winter, when the river rises, beggars are drawn to the scythe on the ice and wait for scraps to be taken out of the quay gate after yesterday's feasts.

## Courtyard and etiquette
According to [[mark-voron|Mark]], in the Palace of Pen and Sword live one thousand three hundred people, and at least five hundred more are there every day. At the top of this pyramid stands the emperor, below the bride, generals and advisers from the highest nobility, then eight ministers, sixteen senior officials and the imperial Chancellery.

The stairs of the palace are guarded by the spearmen of the scarlet guard - the "elite infantry of the emperor" with spark spears. She is recruited from the most worthy warriors of the Lands of the Crown, and once a year she passes a review before the Lord. (Early media reports say that the scarlet guard guards palace gardens, entrances, and stairs.) On the second floor is the watch of the [[lazurnaya-gvardiya|azure guard]]. Guests are not allowed to enter the palace: the imperial guards meet them at the carriage. Guests conduct ceremoniesmaster with staff. The third ceremony of the master, a southerner in a caged livery, the courtiers called "the caged rat".

Lady Sybil taught the world the subtleties of audience. Reaching exactly the appointed time or in five minutes is considered audacity. For half an hour comes the one who knows his worth, but polite, and for an hour - the one who is going to beg. The Lord speaks first. Guests are silent, hold the bow and look at his chest level, not in the eyes. The only answer to the compliment is “Your Majesty.” You can smile at the Emperor's joke, but not laugh. Particular attention should be paid to the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]]: the fingers of the lord on the handle mean the importance of words, and the blade put forward by an inch is an accusation or a hard order. Women of the House of Northwood have the right to hide their hands in the presence of the emperor.

After the failure of the Shutov plot, the palace lived almost on a siege. No one came in armed. The three guardsmen who accompanied Adrian were appointed the following day at night. Servants were taken only from families that served the Dynasty for many generations, and only those who had families in Faunterra could approach the emperor. The servants were told that their wives and children would be executed if the lord was killed. Adrian's food was tried by three: Baron Horsing by hereditary privilege and two randomly chosen servants.

Every year in early June, the palace was given a summer ball, after which the nobility remained in the capital until the August summer games. There was also a spring ball. The jester [[menson|Manson]] found that all the feasts in the palace were "painted in purple and gold, but sound like fanfare." Meeting guests fanfare canceled Adrian in the first month of his reign.

## History
### Construction and early centuries
For more than seven centuries, the rulers lived in the Table Citadel on the mountain above Canaan. After the Crimson Troubles, the Citadel became known as a cursed place. Her fame became even worse when, during the First Horse War, Vladyka Evrian ordered the slaughter of two brothers, the Dukes of Hope, who brought news of the fall of their land, right in the throne room. After the victory over the Shavans, Lady Juliana moved the residence to the "newly built marble Palace of Pen and Sword on the island in the middle of Hanoi". ([[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]) In 1774 Alanis said that the palace was not empty for two hundred years.

The world has read about three successful coups in eight centuries. The most dexterous was the plot of the Night Falcons: the bribed ceremonymaster let sixty rebellious knights in blue armor, similar to the azure guards, enter the palace. The rebels forced the emperor to accept the poison, and for a week no one was released from the palace while his cousin was confirmed on the throne. In the Shutov plot, twenty-five conspirators freely passed by the guard at the bridge and the bribed guard at the entrance. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) They struck the spark of the azure guards on the second floor, but in the rooms they were waited for by the knights of the emperor and Duke Aiden Almer with crossbows. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]])

Damon Origin, in the form of a palace guard, crossed the Air Bridge and fought off its far end. Erwin turned off the spark in the palace, and the Kairs interrupted the guards in the dark. In the morning, a bat of the Origins and a seagull of Stagforth rose above the palace. Alanis drove Erwin to the empty rooms: "Ten years this place was my home!" ### 1775 year
In [[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]], Minerva settled in a ruined palace. It lacked half of the windows, the roof was broken, in the corridors there was snow mixed with broken glass. About fifty former servants returned to the service. The coronation was held here, and Lord Chancellor Erwin undertook a reconstruction: the renovation of both the throne rooms, the reception and the imperial chambers was estimated at twenty-five thousand ephesas. ([[koronatsiya-minervy|Minerva's Coronation]]) The work was directed by Iona. She made floor-to-ceiling windows in the archers of the ballroom and offered to hang a blue silk drapery similar to a waterfall behind the throne of the small throne room. Minerva persuaded Erwin to remove two battalions of kairs and a Northwood regiment from the island and staged an April fair right on Palace Island. The townspeople were attracted by the opportunity to see the island and buy a “paper tree”. In the guest rooms lived the defeated Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]]. In the throne room, Minerva hosted the Shimmer Prince Hector. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]])

In the "Yanmei Hunt" to each officer on duty of the scarlet battalion guarding the palace, Erwin put a "shadow" - a northerner of a special squad. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) Lady in the Dark he proposed the palace as a place "more comfortable and safer than any estate". In the art gallery Erwin and father David saw on the life portrait Ingrid pendant - A Drop of Sun. ([[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]) Erwin liked the palace more: the palace, in his opinion, is “luxurious, sophisticated, but too Yankee”. According to the lady in “[[te-kogo-my-proshchaem|Those we forgive]]”, in April Znaharka, a servant of the Puppeteer, appeared here. The Kairs killed her and kept her body in the cellars of the palace.

In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], Minerva took Manson as a court jester: the palace is his home, and the jester's clothes are "second skin". At the end of June, Mark brought to the palace [[karen|Karen]], [[navi|Navi]], who lost the memory of Countess Sybil and Backfield. Karen admitted that she had never been here, "but dreamed all her life", and Manson took her to inspect the palace. Navi served in the palace library.

In Shadow of the Great Tree, Minerva gathered generals at a table for a stratagem and watched spark shelves enter the island from the balcony of the game room. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]) In August 1775, the ambassadors of Adrian brought the Bright Sphere to the palace. Navi shouted "It's a bomb!", grabbed Hilt and ran through the window. Hilt was later found on a bench in the palace park. Navi later explained that the Sphere had been set up by [[paul|Paul]]. Soon Minerva left the capital, taking the regalia, and the azure companies left with her. Adrian returned to the palace with his young wife [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]. The Minister of the court reported that all damage after the siege was removed and the glass was inserted, and new sculptures appeared on the front staircase. Magda first entered the tea salon, and even the hostess. While Adrian was on the campaign, she gave the Palace Bridge as a pledge to her father for a loan of one hundred thousand hilt. The Crown Lands taxes went to Minerva in the First Winter, and the Pera and Sword Palace "remained poor".
