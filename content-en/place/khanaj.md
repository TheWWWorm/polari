## Geography

According to the Polari Land Guide, Hanai is the largest of many Crown Lands rivers. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]) In the description of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], the Guide also calls the largest river of the duchy "Hanai with its tributaries". On the map that Erwin studied in "Only One Star", Hanai with its tributaries resembles a tree: "a mighty trunk that goes back roots to Mason Bay", and thin branches stretching to the north. One of the branches, the Mila River, curves downwards and, together with the Hanoi bed, forms a triangular "pocket" south of the [[labelin|Labelin]], surrounded by water on three sides. Closer to the sea, the river breaks up into a whole network of sleeves, factories and ducts. The last town before entering the sea is Morrowind. In the spring, the river "stretched to the Extreme Sea", carrying the remains of ice.

Hanai is wide, deep and dangerous. In Arrow, Coin, Spark, he is described as "deep to black, wayward, whirling whirlpools." The bend standing in his bend held the only ferry crossing for a hundred miles. ([[izluchina|Riverbend]]) At the capital, the width of the river reached "good half a mile". Shavan [[salem|Salem]] lived in Summerswith in a hut on the hill above the thresholds of Hanoi and called friends to sit "at the hut face to Hanai" and admire.

## Hanai and Faunterra

[[faunterra|Faunterra]], the oldest of the world's cities, originated as a tiny settlement in a ravine descending to Canaan. In the capital, the river is “brimmed”: bounded by seven bridges, dotted with sails, its banks are built up with palaces and towers. In the Beyond, Erwin liked to look at the wild River, because with majestic power she reminded him of Hanai: “the rivers are like features, like a brother and sister.” ([[reka|The River]]) Gondolas, bows and barges were scurrying across Canaan, river schooners were swinging at the piers. The palace island with the Pera Palace and the Sword connected the bridges to the city, and from the windows of the palace overlooking the river. ([[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]) When the spark connection between Aleridan and the capital was shown in 1774, one of the test questions from Almera was: "how many bridges are there across Hanai?" The house "with a view of Hanai and the palace" was considered the pinnacle of the capital's chic.

## The War of 1774

In "Only One Star" the river became an obstacle and a weapon. As they advanced south, Erwin's army marched between the branches of Hanai and erected six bridges across Mila. [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] in the retreat destroyed bridges across the Hanai and railroads, so that the main forces of the northerners were forced to walk on the left bank.

When taking the capital, [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon Origin]], with two Xs, disguised himself as captured palace soldiers and crossed Hanai over the bridge, then held its end until the Duke's Hundred approach. ([[bitva-za-faunterru|Capture of Faunterra]]) On the night of the storming of the palace, Hanai "quieted with barques, like rotten meat with worms." The lanterns on the towers were beamed on the water, and the December ice floes broke the structure of the boats. In the morning at the foot of the bridge lay a frozen barque with dead rowers. Later, when the river froze, Erwin mistakenly found the fresh ice to be too fragile, but five of Backfield's bravest men crawled over the ice under the bridge, climbed into the gate tower and tried to lower the bridge, but were knocked over, and the mistake cost Erwin four warriors. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) In the spring of 1775, from the windows of the prison chambers, where two Mira teachers were kept, Hanai was seen "dropping chains of ice from himself."

## Winter of 1774/75

Along the road along the bank of the Hanai, snow-covered hills stretched for many miles: there lay sparkle soldiers who lagged behind the army and froze or fell to the northerners. The brewer showed them to the Sorcerer’s detachment so that the peasants would collect uniforms, armor and weapons for sale from the dead. ([[mik|Mik]]) In the winter of 1775, on the bank of Hanoi, under the capital, there was an army of Northwoods - "ten thousand war bears", which, as Mira realized, would not leave until Sybil and Gloria and "a cart of gold in addition" were given to him. On Palace Island, meanwhile, there were two battalions of kairs, "defacing the view of Hanai".

## Latest events

In the spring of 1775, Hanai was still covered with ice and unnavigable, which prevented the army from moving to help Melorange. Later, a column of ships ready to sail and the arch of the Air Bridge were visible from the windows of the Citadel. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) Hampton Bridge across Hanai burned down and collapsed a week before the crash at Back. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) In the vicinity of Arden, an ancient city on Canaan, at the end of the last century built a dam spark shop, "mercilessly broke the century-old way of life" of the Ardennes. In May 1775, [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade, after robbing a tomb in Arden, descended on Canaan on a river-going boat - on the river "half of such rascals". Entering the delta, where the river splits into a network of sleeves and ducts, the ship sailed into the Mason Sea between Marengo and Morrowind; a rapid current carried the boat out into the open sea past the coastal cliffs. Erwin calculated that Paul's shortest route to Waymar, via Hanai, Lake Tract, and Leixity, was blocked in a dozen places by outposts. At the beginning of June, according to reports, Hanai was "infested with transport vessels": the Agatov army was preparing to march to the north.

In Shadow of the Great Tree, [[minerva|Minerva]] saw Hanai from a window in Patrick's Chapel, where ten ships of her faithful fleet stood at the small dock. The position of the town was considered profitable: the flanks are steep hills, in the rear of Hanai. Mira, however, asked [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] to load on the ships and leave, "until Hanai is blocked by enemy forces", and she was going to stay with one azure company.

## In "Fantasies"

In "Ruthing", one of the "Fantasies", which the author calls possible options for the future, the free city of Finboro in the Southern Way stands "on the Bay of Hanoi": speakers bring raw materials there on barges, and weavers make matter out of it. ([[finboro|Finborough]])
