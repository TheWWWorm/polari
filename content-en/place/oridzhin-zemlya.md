## Geography
The Duchy of Origin occupies the northeast of the mainland of Polaris. According to the "Guide to the lands of Polari", it goes to the [[more-ldov|Sea of Ice]] and comes into contact with the East Sea, but before the rebellion Erwin had no ports on it. In the west, the Origin is bordered by [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]], in the south by the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], and through the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]] has access to another continent - Beyond. The land is mountainous: the Crystal Mountains cover about forty percent of the area, and hilly foothills cover as much again. Therefore, with a large area of fertile land is small. The climate is cold, moderately continental, but the "Guide" stipulates that by Polarian standards, the "cold north" is not so cold: The first winter is comparable in climate with Moscow, and not with Norilsk.

The southern border with the Southern Way runs along two twin rivers. ([[bliznyashki|The Twins]]) Between them lies a narrow patch of land, where there is a border station. The bridge to the Southern Way is guarded by a powerful fort, and the bridge to the Origin is not guarded by anyone: "no lord of the Southern Way will risk disturbing the peace of the northern sheep." To the north, hilly emerald meadows with hamlets begin, and snowy peaks of the Crystal Mountains rise on the horizon. According to [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], there are "hundreds of mines and millions of sheep" in Origin.

To the east, beyond the Crystal Mountains lies the Outback with the only settlement of [[spot|Spot]] on the isthmus. Erwin's expedition for the River passed through these lands. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) The north coast is washed by the Sea of Ice; here stands the port of [[belomorye|Belomorye]]. The neighboring county of Northwood lies on the way from Shayland to Origin: Desmond retreated on this road in 1775 and the army of the Puppeteer went. The [[pervaya-zima|first winter]] is at the crossroads: one leads to Main, the other to [[lid|Leed]], the third in the Belomorye.

## Cities and places
- The **[[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]** is the capital and ancestral castle of the Origins in the mountain valley; according to legend, the place where Agatha first set foot on the land of Polaris.
- **Leed** is the ancient capital of the North, founded long before the First Winter, the patrimony of the Counts of Lilliday. The city was famous for gunsmiths, winemakers (the local drink is an orge) and warriors. Its seventy-foot-high granite walls are surrounded by a double ring of death traps at the gates. Before the war, there were seventy-eight thousand people in Leed. From Leed comes the company of the Leed Wolves. The old lady of Leed recalled how in the old days every spring two hundred kire remained to guard the city, and four hundred went to fight "either in Main, or in Steel, or in the First Winter."
- Main - "the third city of Origina, the capital of iron mines", the same age as Leed and "grandfather of the First Winter". A tight ring of walls squeezes its narrow streets and houses into three or four floors, and behind the walls of the mountains are scattered villages of miners near the mines. The County of Main owns six silver mines. His count Mein Hammer, a "descendant of a mining brigadier", was one of Erwin's largest vassals.
- According to the “Guide”, **Steel** is one of the earliest northern cities, founded in the fourth century at silver mines.
- **[[belomorye|Belomorye]]** is a port city on the Sea of Ice, owned by the Earls of Fleming. Here stands one of the twenty-four monasteries of Ulyana Sad in the Empire.
- **[[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]** is a narrow mountain gorge on the road from Leed to the First Winter with two forts, the Elder and the Younger. According to legend, the dispute over the gorge between the Origins and the Lillidays was resolved by a duel, after which Lilliday recognized himself as a vassal of the First Winter. Now it's [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]]'s birthright.
- **Windley** is a port in the foothills of the Crystal Mountains, according to Erwin "originally granted to the Northerners"; as Sybil Northwood observed, "that was three hundred years ago." The Origins have fought for him many times. In 1774, Erwin took the port, and he remained with the Northerners (see [[uindli|Windley]]).

## Economy
According to the "Guide", the main sources of income of Origin - sheep wool, weapons, armor and steel, iron and silver mining, as well as trophies and tributes of military campaigns. With the centralization of the Empire, wars became smaller and military revenues fell. Northern steel loses out to Crown Lands products, where more advanced smelting methods are applied, so the duchy's economy tends to decline.

This picture is confirmed by [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] in a dispute with his father at the beginning of the cycle:
> "We are poorer, father. We are almost poor.”
He recalled that two centuries ago, battalions of kires kept the whole North and West in fear, and in the First Winter “a golden river of taxes and gifts flowed.” A century ago, the Crown, fearing the strengthening of the Origins, took away a significant part of their land. The last half-century of peace and stability, he said, has been “our ruin”: the duchy barely contains twenty-five thousand kairs, squeezing the last juices from the peasants in straw houses. Exit to the sea and the port of Windley Erwin considered a means to break the trade monopoly of the South Road. The Origen wool spread throughout the North: the merchant Harmon bought it in Waymar almost twice cheaper than the wool that the merchants of the South Road carried over land.

## People and Customs
The North is the land of [[kajry|kairs]], stern warriors proud of their descent from Agatha the Radiant. The local nobles — the Lillidays, Maines, Stathams, Hortons, Blackberrys, Flemings, and others — serve the Origins as prime vassals. ([[blekberri|Blackberry]]) Southerners call the northerners "non-peaks" and "ice". The Duchess Sophia, a native of Aleridan, said that both there and here the nobles are proud of the blood of Agatha, but Aleridan "fell into the future", and the First Winter "forever froze into the past."

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark ironically retells the northern beliefs: fallen northerners "so long to become ghosts" - to take revenge, to share, to raise a son or to redeem shame. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) It is even believed that a ghost can enter a baby. According to legend, two caira enemies, a traitor and killed by him, for four hundred years were reborn one after another in foreign bodies and reconciled only under Erwin's grandfather. Westerners, she said, fly away with the [[orda-strannikov|horde of Wanderers]] and forget this world, and the Almerans fall on the Star. Calling Origin "bold" is almost audacity: intelligence and courage are considered innate traits of all the lords of the house, and praise sounds like a former doubt.

The Guide describes Origin as a very conservative feudal society. Strong ducal power rests on the military aristocracy, hence the harsh laws and manners, many unwritten rules and firm concepts of honor. The people are proud, with a strong sense of superiority, the origins of which are the antiquity of the earth, the military glory and holiness of the First Winter. Ordinary people are very pious and especially honor two Foremothers, Agatha and Gloria, and the ducal family uses this to assert their greatness. In the military sphere, there is a “social elevator”: a commoner can become a gray, and then a kire. The land is heavily militarized. The Code of Kair, according to the “Guide”, is an unwritten hierarchy of values. Loyalty to the will of the suzerain stands in it above the protection of his life, following the Northern Law above the protection of his own life and honor, and the general imperial laws stand in the last, ninth place, "one more than the sacred eight." The Northern Law states that the North is always united in the face of a common enemy, honors its history and traditions, and is ruled by “courageous and intelligent” warriors and lords.

## Ruling House
The duchy is ruled by the [[dom-oridzhin|Great House Origin]] with a coat of arms-bat. During the years of the cycle, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] was Duke and then his son Erwin. According to the "Guide", the founder of the dynasty was Sir Reidan Adler, a master executioner and adviser to King Orssein Miriamz in the Wars of Despair. As a reward, he received the title of Duke and the First Winter. Adler called the area of the First Winter the Duchy of Origin. In the northern dialect, the word meant "the source, the beginning": the beginning of the procession of the Forebears, the beginning of a new dynasty and the source of Agate wisdom. His first coat of arms was a bird capable of catching an arrow in flight; descendants replaced it with a more formidable one.

## History
### Antiquity and Light-bearers
According to the "Guide", the Crystal Mountains began to be explored in the III century thanks to the maps of the Mother of Elena, on which silver deposits were noted. The first northern cities grew up on the mines: Leed and Main (III century) and Steel (IV century). Following the miners came the shepherds, and the First Winter emerged as a pastoral village, nominally owned by the Leed baron. In the middle of the 6th century, the Leed Baron Arthur Agatha proclaimed Agatha the greatest of the Foremothers. In 572, his meeting with the abbot of Valencia in the Grotto of Cosuli marked the beginning of the Order of Lightbearers (Lightbearers) with headquarters in the First Winter. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) The order accumulated knowledge and wealth, and by the 7th century, more than one hundred thousand people lived in the First Winter. After the Duke of Doyle of the South Road took the silver mines from the order in 709, the Lightbearers engaged in war. By the ninth century, they controlled the present-day lands of Origin and Northwood, half the Southern Way, and northern Almera.

### Wars of Despair
King Orsayn Myriametz was twice defeated by the general of the Light-Bearing Baron Heinrich Leed: first in the Main Valley, then in the campaign of 810, which ended in an avalanche in the Squalls of Heaven. To gain time, the king ceded the Order of Faunterra and moved the capital to Arden. He led the third campaign with advisor-agateman Reidan Adler and won. Breaking his mentor's advice, Orsayn circled the First Winter with mountains and hit Leed in the rear. An army of light-bearers trapped in the valley surrendered three months later. The order was banned, but the glory of its soldiers passed to the new seigneur, and the First Winter retained the glory of the strongest city in the world after Faunterra.

### Before the cycle
Later, the Origins went to war on the South Road for the port of Windley, but each time the Crown opposed them. There were also wars with Northwood (see the [[vtoraya-nortvudskaya-vojna|Second Northwood War]]). In 1756, the Duke of Desmond helped the Shaylands in the fight against the sunsets for the Seventeenth Gift and marched to the West, which cost the western lands of the three burned cities.

## In the Novels
### Northern War
Until 1774, the duchy remained a stronghold of calm. Even under the rule of the sick Desmond, battalions came out of here, which brought Erwin victories in the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]. The Earl of [[fleming|Fleming]] from the Belomorye tried to take advantage of the lack of troops and capture the First Winter, but failed.

### War with the Puppeteer
In 1775, the duchy became a battlefield. Lord [[adrian|Adrian]] promised the Labelins the County of Main and signed a map on which Main, with its six silver mines, withdrew to the Southern Way. In the Main Valley, General William Davey's spark regiment stood for a month, and later Adrian himself and his army stood there. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) After Desmond's defeat at Lysias Dol, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]'s army (about nineteen thousand soldiers and dozens of [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) entered the Origin. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) Most of the castles, hamlets and villages were empty: Desmond retreated using scorched earth tactics, and the Chosen One's army had nothing to plunder. Desmond didn't have the strength to defend Leed. With the money from the Shayland banks, he hired about forty thousand leads and took them to the First Winter, and Count Simon Lilliday ordered the remaining townspeople to portray submission. Leed opened the gates to Richard without a fight. Later, [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]], in exchange for the right to kill Iona, gave the city to the horde of [[paul|Paul]], and the Shawnees took it by storm. The Belomorye also sided with the Chosen One, and under [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]]'s rule was half the Duchy. Both forts of the Rock of Heaven the army of the Chosen found empty - "the gates are open, the cellars are full": Minerva moved Robert Origin's battalion to the First Winter. The first winter was blocked from three sides: from Leed, from the Belomorye and from Main.

The war ended with the defeat of the army of the Puppeteer in the Battle of the First Winter on December 2, 1775. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) On the night of December 3, Richard killed his father and himself died at the hands of Erwin, and Erwin became Duke. The Duchess of Sofia sailed for five months to the Metoc Islands, landed in Windley in early November and returned to the First Winter only the day after the Descent, when Desmond was already dead. ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]]) The first winter was the capital of [[minerva|Minerva]]. Magda Labelin offered peace: to return the Main and leave Windley to the northerners. Even before the battle, Minerva wanted to lay rail branches from the First Winter in the Belomorye, Labelin and the Bear Fang in two years.

## The Author's Article
Roman Surzhikov has written two articles on the Duchy for Fandom: a review article by the author (2018) and The History of the Duchy of Origin (2021). ([Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%93%D0%B5%D1%80%D1%86%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%BE_%D0%9E%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%B4%D0%B6%D0%B8%D0%BD); [Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%98%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%8F_%D0%93%D0%B5%D1%80%D1%86%D0%BE%D0%B3%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0_%D0%9E%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%B4%D0%B6%D0%B8%D0%BD)) Almost all of their text coincides with the “Guide” recounted above; this is what is added to it.

- **Holy Valley.** The valley of the First Winter is called the first place of long parking of the Forebears on the Polaris, and the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]] is a place of pilgrimage, with which many parables are associated. The Romans agree: the Foremothers, having descended from the mountains, spent the first winter here.
- **Date of foundation.** The 2018 article attributes the foundation of the First Winter to the second century. This diverges from both The Guide (Leed and Main - 3rd century, First Winter grew out of the herding village later), and novels where Leed was founded "long before the First Winter", and Main is called its "grandfather".
- **Light-bearers.** Under the rule of the Order, the wars in the North ceased: Baron Mein himself joined the Light-bearers, and Baron Steel (genus Yumin) was forced to accept vassalage. In the 7th century, two academies, a dozen schools, four monasteries and eight libraries operated in the First Winter, and the Leed barons became richer than the dukes of the central lands. In 735, five years after the death of Duke Doyle, the Light-bearers captured the entire north of the South Road along with the port of [[uindli|Windley]] in a four-month campaign; only the capital, Doyle, survived. ([[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]; [[dojl|Doyle]]) The subdued lords retained power, but each had an "adviser" from the order.
- **Oh, thank you.** The North was already famous for its military power during the Third Myriam Dynasty (VI-VIII centuries), and reached the heights of power in the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]] of the XVI century. The empire then shrank to four lands - the Crown, South Road, Origin and [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]] (in the novels - "up to the size of three dukedoms"). The Origin fleet landed troops in the West, in the deep rear of the nomads: this stopped the expansion in the First Horse War and returned to the Crown part of the western lands in the Second. Since then, the Origins have been considered the saviors of the Empire and the military support of the Crown.
- **Internal disturbances.** Until the beginning of the 18th century, the duchy remained restless: vassal rebellions and feuds were regular and subsided only when the whole North was at war with the West.
