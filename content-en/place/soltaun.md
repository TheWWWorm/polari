## Description

Soltown is a port on the East Sea in the Duchy of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]. According to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", it is one of the largest cities in the duchy along with [[labelin|Labelin]], Doyle and [[uindli|Windley]]. Like neighboring Fishtown, Soltown was a free city: it was ruled by a board of merchant elders who paid a tax to the Duke of Labelin. In the bays of both cities was based the Second Marine Guild of the Southern Way, one of the three marine guilds of the largest merchant fleet of the Empire. The first guild sat in Windley, and the third in the bays of the Grey Sands at the Marquises of Greysend.

The picturesque Soltown Bay was protected from storms by two rocky peninsulas that far out into the sea. Azure water was swept by ships and boats. The wide promenade was shaded by a series of mighty plantains, and the entire city nobility built houses, villas and palaces along it with columns, flowering terraces and sculptures on the roofs. Richest of all was the floating palace of the Negocyanate Hobart-Synthal, rebuilt from a galleon and anchored so that the central street rested directly on board. ([[gobart-sintal|Gobart-Sintal]]) There was a rich port market in the city. The local merchant nobility flaunted scarlet suede boots and leather pants with a white pattern. The nobles of the South preferred to be addressed by "the lord" rather than "the lord" or "the lord". In the spring of 1774, there was no rail road in Soltown, but they built it quickly and hoped that spark trains would soon reach the city. In 1775, trains were already here.

## Trading for the Light Sphere

Soltown was the first city where [[kharmon|Harmon Paula Roger]] took the Light Sphere after [[uejmar|Waymar]]. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) Neither he nor his companions had been here before, and the road had to be questioned. The peasants waved their hand somewhere to the east and said that the city was "on the sea", although the seashore stretched along the entire edge of the duchy for two hundred miles. Melnick sent them past the wrecked church to the left, and drove north until a mail courier from Bear's Fang laughed, "You're going to Northwood, not Soltown." When the train finally got on the right track, there was a skirmish with Sir Ben Womack. ([[ben-vomak|Benjamin Womack]]) We had to flee, and we got to Soltown by a roundabout, about two weeks.

In late May or early June 1774, Harmon sold three trophy Squire horses in Soltown for eight gold (in early media editions, for fourteen). Then he bought himself an outfit of a rich travel merchant, and [[polli|Polly]] vanilla perfume from the south. Negotiant Hobart-Synthal guessed that he was offered the Sacred Object, and reached thirty-three thousand Hilt, but Harmon was never able to part with the Sphere. He left Soltown "in the drizzling mushroom rain" without ever selling it (for more details, see Hobart-Synthal and the Light Sphere Sale). ([[gobart-sintal]]; [[lzhe-sfera]])

As [[otets-david|Father David]] later explained, it seems that the merchant was already being followed from Soltown by “other” object hunters, those who later burned the Maximian monastery. Later it turned out that a detachment of a northerner nicknamed Ice was seen on [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]], then in Soltown and Labelin.

## Capture by Northerners

In the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]] [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]'s army after the victory at the Wise River turned not to the Labelin, but to the sea, capturing ports on the coast. [[adrian|Adrian]]'s generals warned that Soltown had no troops or fortifications and would fall quickly. In early October 1774, the city was taken. The merchants waited out the fight, and the militia garrison "had the imprudence to take the fight", and therefore Erwin could consider all the property in the city a war trophy. He placed a bet in the town hall and told [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] to study the tax archives.

The feast in the floating palace of Hobart-Synthal ended in a deal. Merchants of the Second Marine Guild were reduced to submit from a third to a fifth of the profits, the right to build ships in any shipyards, not only in the captured First shipyards of Soltown, and thirty-four ships taken from escaped merchants. Erwin demanded fifty thousand ephesas and ordered to flood a dozen ships at the entrance to the harbor, so that the fairway became so narrow that it would be possible to pass through it only one ship. Here Erwin first heard the fable that the Duke of Labelin bought the Light Sphere from an unknown merchant for forty thousand. He explained to his generals, who were waiting for a naval landing on Faunterra, that he had taken the coast, which his ancestors had dreamed of for two centuries. Leaving the garrison in Soltown, the North made it to Doyle within a week.

Erwin later recalled Soltown as the only place where the victory did not turn into new troubles:

> "The only time I was in Soltown. I took the city, I was well fed, drunk with wine and given a girl. I want to go back there!”

In January 1775, Baron Darryl estimated that there were only two companies of northerners at Soltown, as at Doyle, Windley, and Elfort. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]]) The Northerner Ice later said that in Doyle, Soltown, and Labelin, Erwin went to the unarmed townspeople every time.

## 1775 year

In the spring of 1775, before Maurice Labelin's speech in the House "on the results of the war", it was expected that he would ask back some of his lands, for example, Labelin or Soltown. In The Shadow of the Great Tree, when Adrian returned the Southern Way to the Labelins, King Shimmery [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], at the request of the Lord, landed at Soltown. Unwilling to fight the Kairs, he exchanged birds with [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]], and he withdrew the Soltown garrison in exchange for the king's oath not to fight on Adrian's side. Adrian arrived in Soltown by train in the cold autumn rain, hoping to get another Sacred Object here. On the platform he was met by red sparklers and snow-white southerners. The king greeted the jester [[menson|Manson]] and [[karen|Karen]] earlier than the ruler himself. Here, [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]] was angry at her husband for colluding with the Shimmerians and learned the lessons of [[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]].

Discussing the terms of peace with Erwin and [[minerva|Minerva]], [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] proposed to return the entire Southern Way to the Labelins, except for Windley. Erwin countered that "Labelin and Soltown are also profitable cities", but Iona replied that justice demands their return.

## Trivia

- The merchant Hobart-Synthal held a black schooner called Sea Arrow in Soltown with a Northwood team being mistaken for a pirate. Erwin spared her as the ships were sinking on the fairway, and she stayed at the House Origin.
- One of the sailors in "Only One Star" told how his team went around the whole Polaris, returned to Soltown and sold a schooner here.
