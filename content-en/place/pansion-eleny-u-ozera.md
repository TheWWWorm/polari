Pension Elena-u-Lake is a closed educational institution for girls from noble families in the foothills of the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]. In the "[[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]]" it is said that the boarding house enjoyed the fame of the "best school of brides" in the Polari Empire. Other boarding houses taught girls secular manners, the arts, conversation and humility. Elena-u-Lake gave the students the “mastery of the ruler”: it was believed that her graduates could be a support to their husbands in politics, diplomacy and land management.

## Location and appearance
The boarding house is located in the lands of [[oridzhin-zemlya|Origin]], near the border with the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]. In "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]", Mark calls him lying "in the foothills of the Crystals" and almost on his way to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. From the border station on Gemini to the boarding house a few hours drive. ([[bliznyashki|The Twins]]) The main road goes to Fairis and Lilliday, and to the boarding house they turn to the northeast, past the hamlets, along the increasingly steep hills.

The lake, which gave the boarding house a name, is tiny - "not a pond, not an overfed puddle." It lies on the slope of a rocky hill at the very top: the spring rose through the thickness of the earth for a good seven hundred feet and washed a bowl of sapphire water among the cliffs. There was a hull attached to this cup. Buildings made of white stone are narrow and tall, with pointed windows, attic, spires, turrets and arch bridges between the buildings. Everything was designed to be light and airy, but next to the small lake, the boarding house still seemed heavy.

The road leads to the stone platform, where the guest house and the guard wing stand. The boarding house is guarded by young swordsmen in coats of mail and white coats. In the guest house there is a reception room with a pond, where headfish swim, a small refectory and a hall of fame. Lady Theodora ordered portraits of all the students from the Great Houses, and by 1774 they had gathered more than two hundred. Behind the guest house, a wide staircase rises along the slope to the arch with calligraphic inscriptions and to the inner courtyard. On three sides, the courtyard is surrounded by buildings, and the fourth is occupied by a lake and a gazebo with a sculpture. A marble girl holds water in her palms, folded by a boat, and looks at her reflection. According to Lady Marta Valeria, the boarding house is built where, according to legend, Pramatery Elena visited. The legend itself is not retold in the text. The boarding house has its own stable.

When the guest house was not occupied by noble visitors, it was opened for poor travelers. Crowds of pilgrims passed through the Crystal Mountains, and the boarding house received and fed them.

## History
According to the story of Lady Martha Valeria, the boarding house was founded in the XIV century, under Lord Lexian II. Since its foundation, only the building of the Sister Corps has survived. It is home to freshmen to “feel the spirit of our ancient history.” Many believe that the boarding house was opened by Juliana the Great, but this is a mistake. When Juliana visited the boarding house, he was already two centuries old. ([[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]) The owner was delighted with the local views on women's education and donated thirty thousand ephesas. Since then, according to Martha, began a “true flowering”.

The boarding house is a feudal subject under the seigneurate of [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]]. Ruler by tradition on a short leg with the Duke. Mark was afraid that the pigeon would inform about his visit to the First Winter in an hour. Around 1771, Duke [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] visited the boarding house and conducted a class on military affairs: "war is first of all a work of the mind." According to Mark, in the boarding house, the Origin family are “painfully trembling.”

## Training and Orders
Lady Martha Valeria explained the principles of the boarding house: the Lord’s mission is to exercise power, the Lady’s mission is to be his companion. A true lady is not created by manners and arts, but by an understanding of the nature of power and the ability to rule people. Girls are taught politics, diplomacy, land and financial management, the basics of military strategy and feudal law. The table books of the students are the diaries of Pramateri Janmei, “Notes on the Nature of Power” by Wilhelm the Great, “Minutes” by [[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]] and “People and Lands” by Elena. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]])

- **Course.** Training lasts four years. The first year is devoted to secular skills and diplomacy, the second to history, geography and the arts, the third to economics and law, the fourth to feudal politics and the foundations of military strategy. The course of management of estates, cities and lands goes through all four years.
- **Order.** Every day, except Sunday, four hours of lectures, four hours of practical classes, three hours of independent work. At least an hour is given to sports games and exercises, half an hour to prayers. It takes six hours to sleep, and seven on Sunday night.
- **Courses apart.** Students of different years are dressed in different colors and settled in different buildings. Courses eat, pray and walk at different times and intersect only a few times a year. The fourth year wears blue dresses with white cuffs and collars.
- **Incoming.** The new girl is required to read and write fluently, count in her mind, general geography, and begin the etiquette and heraldry of the Great Houses.
- **Rules.** Possession of weapons is prohibited. Mentors don't sit at the table with male guests. The charter does not allow male teachers, although occasionally guests held single classes. The secrets of the graduates, according to the manager, are kept “more sacred than their own”.
- **What they taught.** Alanis remembered the advice to pray to Foremother Susan and devote an hour to exercises every day, then doctors will not be needed. Iona said that the disciples memorized the military power of each land. The mentor Francesca explained it as follows: the infantryman of the Southern Way is a ferret, the gray of Origin is a wolf, the sparkler of the emperor is a cobra. Demeter in the boarding house was taught that the northerners like to fight and do not like to count money.
- **Textbooks.** The boarding house has long used the finance challengers of the masters of Berkeley, Tommen and Lloyd. In "[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]", the palace librarian offered them to [[minerva|Minerva]] and noticed that the boarding house refused them: for most girls, they turned out to be too difficult.

## Mentors
- Lady Theodora Azalea Berta was the steward of the boarding house in 1774. This is a skinny, stern and yellow-faced lady of sixty years with a squeaky voice. She recognized Crow Crown and demanded that he leave, and then still allowed him to "work".
- Lady Martha Valeria Edna is a teacher of twenty-two years of the Lucia family, with emerald eyes and a birthmark around her neck. She showed the guests the boarding house and supervised the occupation of Mark. She later warned Mark and Ethan that they should run immediately, and gave them horses. In Yanmei Hunt, Iona calls her "the best mentor she's ever met in a boarding house." ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]])
- Lady Francesca is a teacher from the time of Iona and Alanis. In Alanis's memoirs, she is called the steward. Francesca taught that helping the needy is a noble duty of an aristocrat. Iona considered her to be "the boring creature in the sublunar world."
- Myriam’s mentor, Alanis, recalled, only did that she embarrassed the students and watched them twist: “Manson’s joke in a skirt.”

## Pupils
According to Lady Martha, since the time of Juliana, four princesses of blood, seven infants of the Brilliant Dynasty and three future empresses have studied in the boarding house, including the late mother of Adrian. (Early editions of Arrow, Coin, Spark named the late Empress Margaret, Duchess of Farway Hope, and Iona Origin among the graduates; there is no list in the 2026 edition.)

- **[[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]** and **[[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]** studied in the same course and became close. According to Iona, they lived in the same room for four years, sharing secrets and understanding each other in half a word. Alanis was particularly successful in managing cities, Iona in diplomacy and the arts. Alanis entered the boarding school at fourteen years old, and after her father’s training, it was “very easy” for her to learn. The wall of Iona’s room was decorated with a picture with a cargo pier of the Palace Island at noon in May. Iona hated the lessons of finance and hated to take notes. It was in the boarding house, where weapons were banned, that she used to hide weapons in the room.
- Demeter Nellis-Lyon, a Shimmer princess, studied with them. ([[demetra-nellis-lajon|Demetra Nellis-Lyon]]) According to Iona, a third of the course became fans of the southern woman, and Iona and Alanis had to team up to put her in her place. Alanis remembered her as a "beggar", behind which the younger girls ran around.
- Helissa is a fellow student whom Alanis recalled because of her love letters and eagle.
- **[[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria Northwood]]** came three years later Iona and faced her a couple of times. Her portrait in the Hall of Fame is captioned: "Gloria Sybil Dorina R. Susan, Inf. Northwood, 1772". She studied for three years out of four: in the winter of 1773/74, her mother took her home to the Bear Fang. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) (In the early edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, it is said twice "four years"; in the text of the collector's edition, this phrase is corrected for "three years", as in all subsequent books.) ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) Fellow students explained that [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] wanted to bring her daughter to the court in the year of the wedding of the lord. They themselves considered Gloria a "guzzling pacifier": she is good at dancing and racing, does not drink coffee, and in strata "would lose to the cat." ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) But they didn't think she was evil.
- Meredith was in her third year before the war. In Fantasies, she serves as a scribe at Dorian Amber's secretariat in the First Winter. ([[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]])

## Boarding in the cycle
- **"Arrow, Coin, Spark".** Mira argues that the boarding house "did its job" with capricious Gloria. Later, posing as Gloria, she refers to the boarding house in front of General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis]]. Looking at the real Gloria, whom the court takes for Minerva, Mira thinks that in the boarding house she was "excellently trained in the art of making an impression." [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] at Sybil's table is outraged that young girls are playing stratagems and studying politics. According to him, the boarding house is a “strange invention of the Foremother Church”, which “does not know how to get rid of students.” Becca, portraying how [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] would have married Sister Adrian, praises Iona with the words: "She was educated in the boarding house of St. Helena." Valerie Gracend throws Myra, whom she believes Gloria: "The best school of brides - still! ([[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]]) Girls are made into haughty ice-caps!”
- **“Only one star.”** [[katrin-katrin|Catherine Catherine]] told Mark strange: Gloria studied in the boarding house for three years, although the course lasts four. Going to the First Winter, Mark and [[itan|Ethan]] on September 1–3, 1774 drove to the boarding house under the guise of a brother and secretary choosing a school for young Jennifer. Ethan confused the name "sisters", and the deception was revealed. Theodore's steward recognized the Crown Raven, but Mark traded a "class" for nineteen fourth-year students. On it, he, breaking his promise, together with the girls, dismantled the mystery of Gloria. The description of the classmates did not match with Gloria, whom Mark knew at court. In the hall of fame, he saw the face of “Minerva Stagforth” under a sign with the name Gloria and realized that Sybil had replaced the brides. Marta Valeria told the guests to run away, but a few miles from the boarding house they were intercepted by kairs and taken to the First Winter. Ethan's report was later read under Adrian. The evidence of the substitution was stored in the boarding house and in the Bear's Fang, where the Crown could not reach. Alanis recalls how Francesca's mentor made her set the table for the pilgrims and wash the dishes for mocking them. Alanis has since disliked pilgrims, but it was with them that she left Fliss. Speaking with Joaquin, Alanis mentions boarding school and his four years of study. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) When she took Faunterra, she called herself a drunken student "Alice from the boarding house of Elena-u-Lake."
- **"A Doll on the Throne".** [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] reminds Mark that he “came to the boarding house to find out” about the fate of Minerva. Minerva is studying finance for tasks, which the boarding house refused.
- **"Yanmei Hunt".** Iona tells Joaquin about four years in the same room with Alanis and fondly remembers Martha Valeria. Thinking about how to become a good wife, Iona learns three lessons of his life: military education, the example of his mother and boarding school. Mentors told her that the lady should be smart, educated, knowledgeable in strategy and politics and able to rule the land.
- **“Humans and Gods.”** On board the Guardian, [[ded|Grandfather]] begins to tell the story of a boy who served as a guard at Elena's boarding house. He is interrupted by a cry about the gurken, and the story remains unfinished.
- **"The Shadow of the Great Tree".** In captivity with [[paul|Paul]] Alanis recalls how much she adored the boarding house. In the autumn of 1775, [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] headed the Treasury and the Ministry of Finance and staged an outing in the boarding house. He recruited all the mentors: many were well versed in finance, and the rest went to teach at the court school of Leyla Talmir. ([[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]]) Robert’s students who were left without teachers “mobilized too.” Some fled home, others went to work in ministries, and most settled in the secretariat of Dorian Ember.
