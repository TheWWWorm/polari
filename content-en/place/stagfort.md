## Description

Stagforth is a tiny estate, by [[adrian|Adrian]]'s definition, "small flax in the north of the Foreforest". The prelesier enters the Shayland land, which is ruled from [[uejmar|Waymar]], so Stagforth's owners are vassals of the Shayland Counts. ([[dom-shejland|House Shayland]]) According to the Polari Land Guide, the Pre-Less is the Upper Shayland lying closer to [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]. The further north, the greater the influence of the Northwoods. Residents of the Pre-Less "Guide" describes as lovers of hunting, hospitality and wide gestures, patriots of the North.

The castle is small. Hotel "Star of the Empire" in [[labelin|Labelin]], according to the observation of Mira, surpassed it in area along with walls and moats. There was almost no trade in Prelesye: the peasants exchanged goods in the old fashion, and copper stars walked out of the money, which served mainly for delivery. Stagforth's coat of arms is a seagull. She was even embroidered on wallets: a bag of silver with an embroidered seagull of the World identified the money stolen from her. There was no dungeon in the castle: Clive punished the guilty with rods, publicly and immediately.

## History

This is where Sir [[klajv-stagfort|Clive]], the former captain of the scarlet guard who fell out of favor after the Shutov plot, brought Gemma Alessandra's fiancée. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) The wedding was played in Stagforth, and a year later their only daughter Minerva (Mira) was born here. The Stagforthians had known her since she was a child, and she herself had hardly met anyone who would have to talk about herself.

On 21 March 1774, Sir Clive was killed in an ambush in the Pre-Less when a detachment was on its way to the Bear Fang. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) Peace is saved. To return to Stagforth, her companions were considered dangerous: it was possible to be ambushed again. After her father's funeral, she stayed at Stagforth for two more nights. The native walls, contrary to the saying, did not help, and each stone reminded of his father. She left with the Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]], taking only her clothes, three favorite books, a bag of silver and her father's box of papers. Sybil discouraged her from returning home: a "pity of people" remained in Stagforth, and one of them, according to the countess, did not serve Mira.

At an audience in May, Adrian ordered Count Shayland to secure Minerva's entry into possession of his father's flax. Becca Litland warned that the lone noble orphan in Stagforth could not survive: someone from the northern barons would seize the castle and marry her to himself. ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]) Moreover, the prey will not be tiny flax, but Yangmei’s blood and kinship with the lord. When in July 1774 Mira, exposing the plot Sibyl, asked to let her go to Stagforth, the countess refused: Stagforts would be surprised if one Minerva married the emperor, and the second at the same time came home, in Prelesia.

For Minerva's court, she remained Minerva of Stagforth, Minerva Stagforth and Lady Stagforth. During the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]], [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] ordered to order according to the heraldic guide at least a hundred flags with the coat of arms of Stagforth, the “seagull over the waves”: he wanted everyone to know the name of the hostess of this “unknown and not glorious” possession. In December 1774, a bat of the Origins and a Stagforth seagull flew over the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] captured by the rebels instead of imperial banners.

Becoming Empress, Mira sent a letter to Stagforth: she called her best and favorite servants to the capital. She didn’t expect an answer until spring. In the summer of 1775, [[mark-voron|Mark]] invited her to become his assistant when she "becomes Lady Stagforth again". In the same year, the Earl of Shayland, who became Puppet Master, ordered an unscheduled double tax to be collected from all baronies "from Waymar to Stagforth". Flying on a celestial ship over the Smokey Dahlia, Mira asked [[navi|Navi]] to turn to Stagforth: she had not been in her homeland for a long time and wanted to see her father's house. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) Navi refused because it was deadly to appear over Shayland.

In Fantasias, Mireille Nan-Claire wears a Minerva pendant with a white gull instead of the old wood pendant. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]; [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) Catherine Catherine explains that the white seagull above the wall is the coat of arms of Stagforth, and sees in this exchange of jewelry a sign of union, a message that will be read in Darkwater.
