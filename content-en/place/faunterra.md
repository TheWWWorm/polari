## Description
Faunterra is the oldest of the cities of the sublunar world. Eighteen centuries ago, it emerged as a tiny settlement in a ravine descending to the Hanai River. ([[khanaj|Khanai]]) Along the spring stream, wild apple trees grew on the hills and goats grazed, and all the one hundred and fifty first inhabitants were placed in the ravine itself. Since then, the city has grown along coastal cliffs, valleys, river islands and the eastern shore. The forests around were cut down, the meadows were built up, the sloping lowlands were strengthened by piles. The fortress walls were repeatedly demolished when the city outgrew them, and the stones of the last wall went to the dwellings of the rich. It is almost impossible to get around the capital in a day. Three hundred thousand citizens live in it, “not counting beggars, vagabonds, thieves, minstrels and visiting merchants.”

The Metropolitan nobility traditionally attaches to their houses towers with flags or tin coats of arms, so that the home of the nobles was visible from afar. Hence the nickname of Faunterra - City of Three Hundred Towers. The eyes of the traveler, approaching the capital on a winter night, opened “hundreds of towers on the backs of the hills, brilliant arcs of bridges over Hanai, an island in a thousand lights.” Most of the buildings in the city are stone. In summer, it is exhaustingly hot here.

## Areas and buildings
### Palace Island
The residence of the lords is the Palace of Pen and Sword, which occupies an entirely large island on Canaan. Within its walls “it was possible to arrange races or place several regiments.” The island is connected to the city by the Palace Bridge, the middle of which, thanks to “Yanmei engineering”, can overturn. The island has served as a refuge. As early as 36 AD, when the wall of Faunterra was not completed, the Forebears built a fortified bridge to the island, "now known as the Palace", so that the townspeople could take refuge on it from the nomads.

### Citadel and Air Bridge
To the right bank of the palace rises the Air Bridge - "the miracle of imperial architecture". From the right sleeve of Hanai rises a single column, "exquisite as a sword", two steel arches diverge from it, and on their crests lies a bronze ribbon of the bridge. One end of the bridge rests on a hundred-foot tower on Palace Island, the other enters the monolithic wall of the Holy See. It was erected in the early 18th century, under the emperor Alexian, to show the "triumph of engineering thought". The Citadel is an old palace in the upper city, where the objects of the Dynasty were stored and the prison was located. From the Citadel to the embankment and Palace Island descends the main street - Merchant descent.

### Temples, Science and Spectacles
- The Cathedral of Mercy is built in the form of a sacred spiral. ([[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]]) Eight curved covered colonnades lead from the round central nave to the eight portals, and the building “twisted like a stone whirlpool.” No nave or altar is visible from the threshold, the main hall opens gradually.
- The Church of Mercy kept the Gauntlet of Power. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]])
- Imperial University - with the only printing house in Polari, producing the "[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]", and a repository of twenty items. According to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", there are only two universities in the Lands of the Crown, in Faunterra and [[marengo|Marengo]], and in the capital there is also an academy of arts.
- The Pera Sanctuary is an archive and library.
- Great arena of summer games of white stone. It occupies several blocks, goes portals to three squares and twice as much as Stagforth, the hometown of Myra. From no point in the city it can not be looked at completely.
- **House of Representatives building.** By tradition, the secretary every morning displays a chalk on the blackboard at the entrance of the list of questions of the day. The custom has survived from the time when the doors of the Chamber opened directly to the street. It was said that in the Age of the Forefathers all the inhabitants of Faunterra were literate (see House of Representatives). ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]])
- The square of the Forefathers is the largest square of the capital, the place of executions. In 1774, a military parade was held here, which opened the summer games: battalions of thirteen lands were lined up on the far side, and the Crown army marched through the center of the square.

### Outskirts
Behind the stone center lie the suburbs, “grown with workshops, taverns and houses of the poor.” On the outskirts are huts and brothels, where the secret guard was looking for murderers. Behind the narrow bridge over the tributary of the Hanai begins the Yeger forest, where the nobility goes for horseback riding. In the suburbs there was a regiment of the scarlet guard.

## Management and citizens
According to the Guide, the Crownlands are characterized by a strong centralization of power and a huge influence of the court. The feudal lords are weak here, but the officialdom is very strong ("dogs"). Crime is low, feudal arbitrariness is almost absent, and corruption is palpable, and the Dynasty struggles with it with varying success. The pride of the townspeople is the proximity to the emperor and the culture, “ahead of the marginal lands for a couple of generations”. Faunterra, writes the Guide, “absorbed the whole of history”: it imprinted all the eras from the Foremothers to the sparkle of the Ages of Science.

The city was ruled by a burgomaster, originally a protégé of the emperor. He was opposed by the city council of elders of shops and guilds, who defended the interests of the philistines, so any serious decision in the magistrate was taken "with battles". There were 1,000 police constables in the city.

## History
### Foundation and antiquity
According to the Guide, in 1 year from the Descent, the Forebears passed through the lands of the future Southern Way to the south in search of a place for the city. Going to Canaan, they built boats and rafted down the river to the place where they founded Faunterra. Stirring from the capital heat, Mira wondered why the Foremothers, who spent the first winter in the valley of the First Winter, went “six hundred miles to the south” and founded the capital “in the midst of hell”. Later, the residents of the First Winter, who approved the transfer of the capital to the North, also reasoned.

In 36 AD, the state of the Foremothers consisted of only two cities, Faunterra and Orwell. The defense of the capital was commanded by Wilhelm the Great and Janmei the Merciful. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) The horde of Shavans tricked Orwell. Agatha learned of this, but withheld the news, and this decision went down in history as the “Agatha dilemma.” ([[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]]) On the stormy night of May 2, nomads broke into Faunterra and massacred. Then Wilhelm burned the leader of the horde with his horse with the Rings, and the Shavans recognized the power of the Forebears (see [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]).

### The Age of Despair and the Sweet Ages
According to the “Guide”, in the early ninth century King Orsein the Miriamite, hard-pressed by the Order of the Lightbearers, surrendered Faunterra to the enemy in exchange for a truce and moved the capital to Arden. In the text of the novels, the same time is called the Age of Despair: "Funterra fell", and the kingdom of the Myriamians shrank to three cities - Arden, Marengo and Ruilde. In the Sweet Ages, the Myriam kings moved their court to Arden for two hundred years "so that the noise and dirt of the Faunterres would not interfere with them enjoying life." After the Crimson Troubles, the practical Yankees made the seaside [[marengo|Marengo]] a summer residence, and Arden remained only the tomb of the lords.

## In the Events of the Series
### 1774 year
Each year in early June, the Pera Palace and the Sword gave a ball, after which the nobility of all lands remained in the capital until the summer games in August. In the summer of 1774, the city was also waiting for the engagement of the emperor: [[adrian]] chose the bride. Faunterra "shrouded, cleaned her gold feathers": houses were decorated with garlands, window sills with flowers, and the streets were filled with spark lights in the evenings. Here, under the guise of Gloria Northwood, Peace arrived. ([[minerva|Minerva]]) She reflected on the fact that one of the three hundred thousand citizens killed her father, and investigated the murders of the heirs to the throne.

At the beginning of December, when the spark army had gone south, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], with a small detachment of kairs, suddenly arrived by train and on December 6 captured the Palace of Pen and Sword (see [[bitva-za-faunterru|Capture of Faunterra]]). Burgomaster Escher managed to load the items of the dynasty from the Citadel on the train to [[marengo|Marengo]], but on the way they were kidnapped. ([[baron-esher|Baron Esher]]) Major Backfield laid siege to the island. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) The siege was accompanied by arson, shelling and famine, and in the upper city around the Holy Citadel there was a camp of remnants of the spark army. Thousands of residents fled to the west. After the alleged death of Adrian on [[bek-most|Beck]], [[minerva]] arrived in the capital. Passing through the Palace Bridge between barricades and corpses and crossing the icy log through the breach in the middle of the bridge, she alone approached the gate of the palace and made peace with Erwin.

### 1775 year
The coronation of Minerva took place in Faunterra. ([[koronatsiya-minervy|Minerva's Coronation]]) Chancellor Origin organized festivals - dog races, theatrical premieres. The mayor of the capital, Erwin, appointed Alanis Almer, in part to take over her business. ([[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]) Never before has a city manager been so prominent. The elders at first misled, but soon found it easier to negotiate with her than with her predecessor. Alanis cared little for the interests of the Crown and easily agreed to reasonable requests: she allowed merchant ships to pass under the lifting Palace Bridge, which the merchants had unsuccessfully sought from the emperors for thirty years, and ordered the creation of a second guild of bakers. When she insisted on her own, the council did not dare to vote against. By her will, the capital strengthened the fire service and began to build a city train from small spark wagons, as in Aleridan. Guilds of cab drivers, road builders and spark mechanics fought against the wagons, until Alanis sent the entire council of elders by express to Aleridan to ride in such a wagon. She also took up the police, considering the constables "sloppy" and poorly armed.

In the spring, a session of the Chamber opened in the capital and Manson was tried. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]; [[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]]) Minerva stopped the [[vosstanie-podsnezhnikov|snowdrops]] on the Holy Field thirty miles north of the city. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) When the Kairs and Northwood “bears” marched through the streets and went to war, the city, at first quiet from their strength, suddenly found that “it was easier to breathe.”

In the summer, a horde of Shavans with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] approached from the west. She freely took Smallden, Caseworth and several other cities. The main prey was waiting ahead: from Orwell, roads to Marengo, Arden and Faunterra were opened. From Caseworth, Orwell, and Arden, people fled to the capital. All over the lands of the Crown spread a rumor about the Gauntlet of Power and the construction of fortifications, in which the mistress herself participates. Minerva swore before the army that she would stay in Faunterra and protect her, and erected ramparts around the city that grew "literally before her eyes". I had tickets for the train to Marengo.

In August 1775, [[adrian]] returned. Before dawn, three spark regiments without resistance entered the city, and then poured carts of wine and treats: Faunterra woke up from the wedding songs, and in the portraits of the announced wedding was Adrian's bride - [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]. Later, for the second time in a month, Adrian, at the head of a victorious column, with captured Shavans and finger-bearers, marched through the city; at the Table Citadel, before the beginning of the Merchant's descent, the column was stopped by a group of southern knights, among whom was Magda. Minerva, bound by an oath not to shed the blood of soldiers in the fight against Adrian, took away the treasury, the crown, the Eternal Hilt and the Gauntlet of Power. By two decrees signed before Adrian's return, she temporarily moved the capital to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] "in connection with a military threat", and to the post of burgomaster that had been vacant after Alanis was appointed Adrian; this work was declared "corrective work" for the lord accused of heresy and tyranny. While Adrian was on the campaign, [[magda-labelin|Magda]] arbitrarily appointed herself treasurer and borrowed on behalf of the Crown one hundred thousand Hilt from her father, [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]], giving him the Palace Bridge as a pledge. Adrian subdued the Faunterra tax office, although other cities in the Crown Lands continued to pay Minerva taxes; Backfield was given back command of the protection. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Going north, by the First Winter, Adrian left Major Backfield "on the guard of Faunterra" with a spark battalion, five thousand thugs and one Perst carrier. After the Battle of the First Winter, Iona offered peace terms: Adrian gives up [[chrevo|the Womb]], and in return he is guaranteed security and power over the Faunterra until May, the House will decide further. Maurice Labelin, he said, had by then received a "quarter of Faunterra".

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, the author shows a possible future after the war. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) According to the world concluded in the First Winter, the capital remained in the North, and Faunterra - for Adrian: he recognized himself as a burgomaster, "to make peace and save thousands of soldiers' lives", and ordered to call himself "my lord". Adrian worked in the town hall, decorated with sixteen sculptures of city heroes and eight winged chimeras. His private secretary in a white mask - [[itan|Ethan]] (in the story of the election Adrian directly refers to him by name; the secretary stuttered, was injured, "defending the honor and freedom of a great man", and was in love with Minerva), introduced a protocol in the town hall, not inferior to the palace, and pulled out with the root of the "living efficiency" that reigned under the lady-burgomaster.

The lands of the Crown were ruled by [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]], the Viceroy of Minerva; the metropolitans nicknamed her the Unexpected Lady. Half of the taxes she sent to the First Winter, and the second contained palaces, the Admiralty, spark shelves, protection and the three remaining ministries in Faunterra - Science, Lands and Arts. In payment of the debts of the Dynasty, [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] received from Minerva the Throne Citadel, the Air Bridge, the Admiralty and a number of other buildings, so that he owned more than the Dynasty itself in Faunterra. Father and daughter lived in the Citadel, filling the upper floors with luxury, and the lower floors with knights of Baron Hugo. In the Palace of Pen and Sword, to her husband, Magda visited occasionally. In the Table Citadel was also kept a Womb with a frozen [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], under the supervision of representatives of both branches of the Church. ([[chrevo|The Womb]])

In the spring of 1776, at the university's physics department, at the entrance with statues of the Forefathers and a mosaic model of the universe, Professor [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]] and his assistant [[elis-kavendish|Alice Cavendish]] built a recording device for Adrian. The gatekeeper of the faculty was Eugén's bearded - by all indications Inji Price, who secretly worked for Minerva. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) Later, Adrian joined his voice to the recorded address of the prophet Francis-Ilianus and invited the people to the capital to support him in the election of the emperor, promising free housing and food. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) According to Alicia's mother, the visitors were recorded in "young men", from whom there were many fights and riots; thousands of people in Faunterra "glorify Adrian day and night", and the mayor, constrained in money, began to print paper money. ([[mat-alisiya|Mother Alicia]]) The printing houses of Faunterra and [[marengo|Marengo]] were controlled by Adrian's men. Shavan Zlataya Griva raised the bird every day over the city, Backfield's men recorded what they saw, and the lads smashed the found caches; by the summer, "the enemies in Faunterra were over." ([[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]]; [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]])

There were other familiar faces in the capital. The lawyer McFreed Crowe, by all accounts [[mark-voron|Mark]], said that "he lived in Faunterra for many years and knows every corner." Retired General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] was invited to teach a military history course at the University of Faunterra.

In November 1776, a House met at Faunterra to elect the emperor. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) Minerva and Erwin arrived by train from the First Winter and saw the city captured by Adrian's supporters: crowds at the station and the streets shouted "Ad-ri-an!", wore his portraits and flags with a pen and a sword, and the mansion of the Origins was extinguished, and a stone flew into Erwin's window with the inscription "Baby, die!" The new arch of the [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]] branch announced that Wilhelm's fighting brotherhood would ensure the security of the elections, and Adrian as mayor forbade the police to expel anyone from the city; the prices of his paper money grew rapidly. Erwin promised the House to dissolve the scarlet guard, and Adrian decided to storm the House with the forces of the scarlet guard of General Joseph Gore and the rooks of [[ruka-dodzh|Hand Dodge]]. In the midst of the battle, Adrian died in convulsions. By all accounts, he was killed by the spark of the mute [[sofi|Sophie]] from the detachment of the one-eyed Sir [[khamfri|Humphrey]], the former lieutenant of the guard Duke Aiden Almer, who avenged the house of Almer. Golden Grive's finger burned Sophie and himself was trampled by the crowd, and [[dzhonas|Jonas]]' coachman shouted that Adrian "punished Janmei". ([[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]]) Erwin was one of the few who knew who killed Adrian and why. The cry of "Adrian is dead!" stopped the guards, General Gore fled the capital, the thugs robbed the neighborhoods until the morning, and in the morning the battalions of kairs entered the city with the knights of the Earls of Arrowback and Flame. Adrian's body was never found. Minerva for three days again settled in the Palace of Pen and Sword, and then the prophet [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] invited her and Erwin to share power: Minerva retains the crown and Eternal Hilt, and Erwin becomes the “shogun” at the head of the army.
