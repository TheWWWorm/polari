**Spot** is the last village of the empire on the edge of the Beyond. Erwin's father, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]], called him "the only village on the isthmus". Further to the east lie only Zamshevy forest, the tops of the Soft Fields and [[reka|the River]].

## Geography
Spot stands at the sea, at the foot of the mountain Servants, under the white as an apron, the foothills of the Servants, from where the trail descends through the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]. Next to the Servant runs the river Lydyanka: its stones are stacked with huts, and in the summer, when the water is scarce, the villagers put a dam on it. Spot is a border town, the extreme settlement of the empire. (In the early media more: east of the mountains is the only settlement, about two hundred inhabitants, along the ice - in the spring along the belt, in the summer the stream - passes the border of the duchy and the empire, Spot lies on the west bank, and the forest behind the river is nothing. There is no such description in the 2026 edition.)

The pier in the village is tiny, fishing, and the bay is dangerously shallow: a schooner from the Belomorye rises to anchor two hundred yards from the shore. According to Colemon, from Spot to the Soft Fields, the experienced walker is ten days' journey, and the large detachment is two weeks with a day in reserve; the Fields themselves, according to the elder, stretch "a mile two hundred" wide and six days' journey deep.

## Residents and Homes
All residents of Spot are "either red-haired or yellow-haired and snub-nosed": the village grew out of several families. The huts are made up of ice flake stones on lime and clay - strong, small and oblique, without windows, only with slits tightened by deer bubbles. Fur is everywhere, and bread, cereal, milk, ale, wine, linen fabrics, and iron tools are a luxury. Once a month, a sailboat from the [[belomorye|Belomorye]] or the Bear Fang comes: it brings flour, wine and cheap axes, takes fur, and the Duke's people, according to Kid, also come for the runner's eggs. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]])

There are spotters fishing and hunting, walking in leather moccasins. On the Soft Fields, they collect the eggs of a runner, a flightless bird nesting at the edge of black water. In the spring of 1774, many had bony faces, jaundice, and fallen teeth. The villagers say, "Enthusiastic eye." They are ruled by Elder Howard Katrina William - a gray old man in a suede zhupan, a winding orator who talks about the Soft Fields "dryly and accurately like an experienced officer"; there are also "rural nobility" - the eldest of the hunters.

Little is known about the big world. Kid was sure that “Humanity” has no less than “fifty hundred” people (in early editions — “two hundred”) and is ruled by the Duke. But there are many legends about Zarechye: “Besovsky fire” flickers behind the River, walking trees wander, people with human heads and bites with red eyes, and “no one returned alive from that shore.” Erwin later reasoned that Spot's hunters "would never winter for the River".

## History

### Explorada (April 1774)
At the end of April 1774, Erwin's detachment of forty men descended from the Pillow of the Servant to Spot. A crowd of young men escorted the column, and the villagers took Baron [[filipp-loufert|Philip Lowfert]] in a scarlet camsol for the duke's son and bowed to him in the ground. Erwin threw the elder a bag of silver as a gift from his father, the tables were taken directly out into the street, and a feast began with dried fish, deer, berry sauces and cloudy grayberries. Through the Soft Fields, Erwin hired the hunters [[kid|Kid]] and Coleman — twenty agates each, more than they earn a year here. ([[kolemon|Colemon]])

At the feast, Hank Seaman, graying [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]], sat a local girl on his lap. Her fiance grabbed the gravy by the gate, and Jemis cut the guy's hand to his elbow. Erwin stopped the feast, told the doctor to help the wounded and forced Jemis to pay a fine and apologize to the cripple and the bride; the kair poured silver onto the ground, and the villagers picked up the coins only when he left.

The next evening, a schooner came under the flag with a non-stop of the Origins: supplies for four months and a dozen mules, and from the Duke of the village - only a few bags of flour. Erwin gave Spot half the load, "every other bag and barrel," and the elder wept with gratitude.

### The Return (August 1774)
In August 1774, near Spot Erwin and Jemis, two survivors, were met by hunters, among them Kid. Erwin did not go to the village: he sent the Kid for bread, fish and ordzhem, and he waited at the Icehouse, at the beginning of the path to the Basement of the Servant.

### Later
In the autumn of 1774, on the way to the Beyond, the fleet of Count [[fleming|Fleming]] with [[mark-voron|Mark]] was anchored at Spot, "a tiny village on the other side of the Crystal Mountains", and the team was walking on the shore with rural girls. Erwin later recalled "the case of Jemis in Spot", judging the soldiers for their grievances to the peasants, and in the spring of 1775 asked Jemis: "Remember the armless boy from Spot?"
