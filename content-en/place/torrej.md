**Torrey** is a major navigable river in northern Polaris. It flows out of the lake Smokey Dahl and carries water north, to the [[more-ldov|Sea of Ice]]. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) The county of [[shejland-zemlya|Shayland]] stretches along Torrey, and at the source of the river stands its capital, [[uejmar|Waymar]].

## Geography
According to the Polari Land Guide, Shayland is an elongated "crane-shaped" land expanding to the north. It lies on either side of Torrey, which takes its source in Smoky Distance and flows into the Sea of Ice. In [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], Torrey is called "the great river that bound the whole county together." After the wars with the neighbors from Shayland, only a narrow strip along the river remained: the same one hundred and fifty miles from south to north, from Smoky Distance to the Sea of Ice, but only sixteen miles wide, eight on each bank. A chain of locks was built along the coast. Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] is called the ruler of the Preforest and the Porec, a "narrow and scanty strip of land" along Torrey.

The banks of the river are described as idyllic meadows with oak trees in the neighborhood and dozens of villages. On the west bank, fifteen miles from the river, a bed of the [[semnadtsatyj-dar|Seventeenth Gift]] arose in 1756.

### The Bridge of Eight Prayers
According to the "Travel Guide", the banks of the Torrey River are connected by the Bridge of Eight Prayers, which is 1050 meters long, the second longest bridge of the Empire. For a long time it was the longest until it was surpassed by the Julian bridge over Holliwell. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) The bridge cannot be crossed without the blessing of the Foremothers. One day, [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]'s army crossed it, going to war on the Sunset Coast, without the bishop's blessing, and a few days later the bridge burned, cutting off the army's way back. Forty years later, the restored bridge collapsed under the cavalry of the Sunset Coast, which also did not receive a blessing. Since then, on both ends there are gazebo-chapels with statues of Foremothers, and the traveler reads eight appeals to the Foremothers, whom he believes most. The bridge is called the Bridge of Prayer.

## Value
According to the Guide, Torrey became Shayland's main defense and spawned a strong river fleet. Among the Sheilanders there was a special class - burlaks. Hence the Burlat songs and sayings like “useful as a one-armed rower” or “Prince on a gold ship”.

The river is the main road between the North and the central lands. The novel says that the Earls of Shayland in the first century and a half of his reign created a fleet that carried goods through the Smokey Dahl and up the Torrey, and the silver of Almera and the Southern Way flowed north. The merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] every spring bought in Waymar the Virginian sheep wool delivered by ships on the Sea of Ice and Torrey: it was almost twice cheaper than what the travelers carried by land.

In Waymar, according to [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]’s observations, there are three ports, and the second of them is a river port, “at the source of Torrey.” Ships from the Sea of Ice, [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]] and [[oridzhin-zemlya|Origin]] are moored there. They do not look like lakes: the sides are high and strong, like a shell, on the decks of shooting grounds, and even ballistics, and sailors are gloomy bearded in helmets. The Waymar people don't like the river port.

Sailing on Torrey is long. Captain [[dzheff-bamber|Bamber]] in "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]" calculated the path from the Beyond as follows: a month from the River to the [[belomorye|Belomorye]], another one and a half or two weeks to the mouth of Torrey, and from there up to Waymar will have to go against the flow on the oars or with burlaps for two or three weeks, depending on the size of the vessel. Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] and his sons made their way from the Bear Fang to Faunterra by Torrey in 1774 and across the Smokey Dahl.

## History
### Seventeenth Gift
In the summer of 1756, the [[semnadtsatyj-dar|Seventeenth Gift]] appeared on the west bank of Torrey. The Westerners considered it unfair that the Gift went to a narrow strip of Shayland, and the war began. When Dar arose, the west wind drove fire from the bed into the eastern woods, all the way to Torrey, almost burning dozens of villages along the shore. In horror, people fled, and some even rushed to swim across the river. For a week, the entire county army, peasants and townspeople fought the fire to cut it off before the fire reached the coastal villages.

In the story “[[tsena-strakha|The Price of Fear]]” in August 1756, the nobility gathered for the celebrations on the occasion of the Gift. The northerners of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] were a fortified camp: one side was covered by Torrey, the other three were pulled by ramparts with palisades. In "Now You're a Sorcerer", a Waymar librarian offers a book by Master Hodgson, The Hard Fate of the City on Torrey. ([[teper-ty-koldun|Now You Are a Sorcerer]])

### The “Yanmei Hunt”
In May 1775, Joaquin took goods from Northwood not to the castle, but to a large old estate on the bank of Torrey outside the city. Count Elias Northwood lived at the manor on Torrey, ten miles from Waymar in those days. The road from Waymar to Dara's lodge ran through the meadows along the river and then turned to the west.

### In "Humans and Gods"
Vittor reproached the vassal that he had brought only two hundred warriors, although he could gather all the troops from the shores of Torrey, "about a thousand copies." In the Battle of Waymar in June 1775, the northerners captured almost the entire city, "from Torrey in the east to an abandoned monastery in the west." The Earl of Shayland learned of [[fleming|Fleming]]'s battalion, as soon as he entered from the Sea of Ice into Torrey, but did not tell [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] about it.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree
Count Vittor granted Joaquin the estate on Torrey.

## Contradictions
- In "Only One Star", the Northwoods are first going to "go down Torrey to Waymar" and then say they "went up Torrey". As the river flows from Smoky Distance to the north, sailing from the north to Waymar goes up, against the stream.
- Minerva says that only one river flows from [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]], Holliwell (see Smokey Dahl), while the "Guide" and scenes in Waymar call Torrey's source this lake too.
