## Geography
According to the Polari Land Guide, Almera lies in the center of the mainland between Smokey Dahlia, Crown, [[nadezhda|Hope]], and Shepherd's Meadows. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) It is a plain, sometimes wooded, sometimes hilly, with a moderately continental, rather warm, dry and windy climate. Almera is fed by the sacred [[kholliwell|Hollywell]] River.

The Duke of Almer owned fifty cities, six navigable rivers, and twenty million acres in the heart of Polaris. The novel describes the duchy as three large counties, each larger than Shayland in area, and a magnificent Aleridan with adjoining lands. Arrowback County occupies the entire northeast of Almera. After driving it for a week from east to west, the traveler gets to Blackmore County, and a week later, turning south, to Daynite County. In Almer there are two spark shops with dams, about a thousand villages and at least fifty cities.

### Red Earth
Almera is called the Red Earth. Locals explain the nickname by the fact that “red” means “beautiful”: the local cities and towns, even the smallest, are elegant, washed and painted, and the petty-bourgeois houses are neat, “neither give nor take, marzipan”. Visitors associate the nickname with careers. The hilly center of the duchy is riddled with quarries of red clay, around them there are dozens of brick factories. The excavated land becomes barren, the ravines turn into brown lakes, and the roads are covered with terracotta dust.
> Locals say: our roads are covered with gold sand! The visitors say: dried blood.
The favorite material of the local architects is a brick made of red clay. Even the castles in Almera and the Crown are often brick. The Guidebook adds that the nickname was retained by the duchy due to the mass production of bricks and tiles.

## Cities and places
- **[[aleridan|Aleridan]]** is the capital, according to the "Traveller" 306 thousand inhabitants; next to the castle stood [[evergard|Evergard]].
- **Blackmore** is the center of the county of the same name. In the First Horse War, the horde of Darian the Bad burned him along with Aleridan.
- **Fliss** is a port on the bank of the Smoky Distance, where the summer palace of the Dukes stands. From here the ships went to Stonekip in the Southern Way.
- Smolden is a town in the east, on the Snake River, owned by the Smolden Baron. ([[smolden|Smolden]]) The baronies of Hogarth and Bonigan lie nearby, and the flax of Baron Fliss to the north.
- Arrowback is a dam castle on the [[bek-most|Beck]] River, the residence of the Earls of Arrowback. ([[erroubek|Arrowback]])
- The monastery of Marek and Simeon is a monastery halfway from Aleridan to Fliss. She owns the village of Road Pillar at the fork of the roads to Fliss, Blackmore and the capital. About two hundred years ago, the abbot opened a fair and a brewery there, and the “pillar beer” spread throughout the Red Land.
- Flathill, Toystone, and Clermi are Almeran cities. [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] fled through Flathill in 1774, and banks were robbed in Toystone and Clermie in the winter.
- Laxett is a town on a hill in Blackmore County, entirely girded with an imposing wall. Unlike free cities, it is owned by a feudal lord. Baron Laxette had a "precious passion" to hang people, especially visitors.
- The underground monastery of Ulyana Sad in the west of the duchy, where [[minerva|Minerva]] was kept and where the story of "Plax" begins. ([[plaksa|Crybaby]])
- Kosoy Yar, Break and Six Mile Forest are also mentioned. ([[shestimilnyj-les|Six-Mile Wood]])

## Economy
The Guide explains the prosperity of Almera by the sensible policies of the last two dukes, Astor and Aiden. Having received the right to build spark shops, they made them the engine of industry. Banks controlled by the dukes issued loans for new productions, artisans and manufacturers were exempt from taxes for the first years, and investments were paid off with interest, taxes and a spark payment. Almera came in second place in the Empire in terms of GDP and became the only land without a shortage of handicrafts. The main products are tools and mechanisms, glass, dishes, household utensils, wine and beer.

In the novel, [[ajden-almera|Aiden]] obtained from Telurian "unprecedented trade benefits" and the right to a second spark dam, and under him Almera became "the richest land of the empire." It was also considered the second after Origin in military strength. Almer wine appreciated even such a connoisseur as Duke [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]]: “somewhat peculiar to the taste, but quite worthy.” There are many free cities in Almera. The sorcerer and his gang robbed the banks in them, until they changed tactics.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark writes that under local law in Almera, the per capita tax is replaced by an income tax. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) This greatly revived the trade and crafts, and by the time of the Northern Flash, this order had taken over the Lands of Crown and [[nadezhda|Hope]]. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]])

## People and Customs
Despite the rapid growth of the bourgeoisie, Almera fully preserved the aristocratic feudal spirit. The Lords are powerful, surrounded by brilliance, and art celebrates the greatness of the Dukes, the glory of the knights and the wisdom of the Foremothers. Almera is the keeper of old-fashioned noble values: nobility, honor, willpower, sharpness of mind and subtleties of manners. But if in Origin they are based on military traditions, then in Almer they are based on culture and art. Science and technology flourish, engineering schools appear, and the University of Aleridana is not inferior to the capital. Three of the five modern schools of painting originated in Almera. It is one of the four lands of the Empire, along with the Crown, the Origin and the Shayland, where there is a tradition of stationary theater.

Duchess Sofia Origin, a native of Aleridan, said that Aleridan was “cluttering into the future”, and the First Winter was “forever frozen in the past”. According to the Guide, about Aleridan, Vladyka Adrian said: “I want the whole Empire to resemble this city.” The Aleridans themselves joke that Faunterra is their "far edge".

## Ruling House
The Duchy was ruled by the Grand House of Almer. ([[dom-almera|House Almera]]) The last legitimate dukes are Astor and his son [[ajden-almera|Aiden]], Telurian's first advisor. Aiden had three children: [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], “the first beauty of the whole state”, fourteen-year-old Alfred and baby [[albert-almera|Albert]]. At the death of Evergard, Aiden and Alfred died, and Albert survived. ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) Aiden's brother, the Prior [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], declared himself the guardian of his nephew and regent, although some barons considered this regency illegal: he was to be approved by the council of primaries. After Gallard's death in 1775, the duchy was effectively taken over by [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]].

## History
### Before the cycle
According to the Guide, Almera became the first duchy of the Empire, gaining this status in the 5th century. Then the "Duchy of Almer" was called a tiny piece of land the size of the present Smolensk barony, and Aleridan did not yet exist. Later, in the sixth century, the County of Almera also included the cities of Marengo and Orwell and their surrounding lands, now imperial territories. The western boundary in the letter was not clearly described, and the dukes “moved” it for generations: they moved landmark villages, cut down and planted forests. The seventh duke even dug a new riverbed mentioned in the charter and let it go thirty miles west.

Later, the Tenth Gift came to Almera - right into the lake Smoky Dahl, near the town of Comley. To extract it, the then Duke built a two-mile dam, dug canals and reservoirs, and drained several square miles of the lake. The pumps on the exposed bottom rotated twenty windmills. It is said that the blades of several mills still stick out over the water half a mile from the shore.

The darkest page in the history of Almera, the Guide, calls the First Horse War: the horde of Darian the Bad captured the entire duchy and burned Aleridan and Blackmore. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]) It took a century to rebuild. For a long time, Almera was squeezed by his neighbors, the Lightharts of Hope and the Shavans of the west. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) Around 1750, the Lightharts captured part of the Almeran lands in the [[zolotaya-vojna|Golden War]]. The dukes often had to seek the help of the Crown, and they became skillful royals: in the last two hundred years, no imperial cabinet was complete without an Almertz. The greatest success was achieved by Aiden, who revealed the Shutov plot, after which the Lightharts fell. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]])

## In the Novels
### 1774 year
In the summer of 1773, the Duke of Aiden staged a large tournament in Aleridan. ([[turnir-v-aleridane|Tournament at Aleridan]]) In 1774, [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] served as a mercenary in Almera. After Evergard's death, Alanis fled across the duchy. ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) The news of the Duke's death instantly circled the Red Earth. Alanis was driving through Flathill, thinking of seeking help at Toystone, but changed her mind. Her knights died due to the treachery of the Earl of Blackmore, and through the abode of Marek and Simeon she reached Doyle in the Southern Way, to Erwin. Power in Almer passed to Gallard's parish as regent of young Albert. He was busy taming the late Aiden's vassals and, according to Erwin, obtained an oath of allegiance from three-quarters of them. The regiments of the Red Earth never came to the capital to help the Crown against the rebel. In December 1774, an imperial train collapsed on Becca. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]])

### Winter of 1774–1775
In the winter, Almera was hit by a wave of robberies. Shavans were robbed by Shayland banks in the counties of Daynite and Blackmore. The Sorcerer's gang took banks in the free cities: there were almost no living employees from the offices in Toystone and Clermi. ([[mik|Mik]]) Then, confusing the tracks, she came to Laxett.

### 1775 year
In the summer of 1775, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] waged a holy war in Almera against Gallard: fighting was going on at the Beck Dam, at Fliss and at Breakdown. [[nadezhda|Hope]]'s troops entered the duchy and occupied Aleridan. Then the horde of [[paul|Paul]] entered the Julian bridge into Almera and passed over it, robbing and killing. Even during Erwin’s campaign, a forest fire approached the villages and the monastery of Marek and Simeon with its richest cathedral, and the monks called to the aid of the nearby battalion of the Wilhelm Combat Brotherhood from the troops of the parish. So the battalion of the Duke of Origin and the battalion of the Priory of Almer put out the same fire. The Horde took Smallden and moved on to the Crownlands. The Almeran knights in white coats then fought in the army of the Chosen of the First Winter.

On August 2, 1775, in Waymar, Gallard died, Alanis was poisoned, and Alanis herself, after being tortured in Waymar, was considered dead. The rights to the duchy were claimed by Gallard's widow Laura, who called herself Laura Almera: she was followed by two Almeran battalions, knightly and monastic. ([[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]]) She was killed by Martin Shayland in October. The southern part of the horde under Juhan Reis and Ganta Cort, retreating from Faunterra, robbed Almera for a month. Court then made an alliance with Henry Farway and sealed it with his daughter's marriage to the Duke's grandson. ([[ganta-kort|Ganta Kort]]) Court declared himself chief of Reis County and Farway the ruler of Almera. Five battalions of General Horton's kire were stationed at Back; Farway gave them a fleet to go to the aid of his lord, and so gained control of the Red Land. Even before that, Almer and Hope announced the doubling of the rail road between the Heart of Light and Aleridan.

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, which the author calls possible futures, by the summer of 1776 Henry Farway ruled Almera, but not all the Almerian lords submitted to him, and he moved with his army to Aleridan. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) The Earl of [[erroubek|Arrowback]] is the least willing to submit; Farway forbade Lady Karen Somerset's nephew to marry the daughter of the Earl of Rose. Albert's rightful heir, Almera Farway, betrothed his forty-year-old widowed daughter Evelyn. Lady Karen tells Farway that he serves as "a hammer hammer hammering nails into the lid of House Almer's coffin." At the time of the election, Bishop Erwin Origin is keeping his troops in Arrowback County, outside the Crown Lands.

Almera is among Minerva's wards of the lands where school reform is underway. The series is set in a poor Almeran town, where the school lives at the mercy of the Duke and the Church. The Duke of Farway-Almer pays retired soldiers a pension of four gloria per month. The teacher of this evening class, Mrs. Gale, is by all accounts [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], who in the main cycle is considered dead. She walks with a cane and wears black gloves, her upper lip is dissected, she has a slit in her mouth, and the children call her "gargoyles". She says to Bartleby's father, "I owe you a lot, father," and when asked if she would be fired, she says, "They don't fire convicts." In the desk of the schoolgirl [[salli|Sally]] there are continuations of her essays written by someone else's hand. In them, the lady “lacked two fingers”, as well as teeth, nails and pieces of skin. The knight was there while his friends tortured the lady, then left for the war and returned, losing, and he also lacked two fingers. This coincides with the fate of Alanis in Waymar and with [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]'s palpable hand. Sally adds the last line, "Lady killed a knight," and Mrs. Gale finds the ending successful. The Ministry of Education's auditors offer her a place in the ministry, but she refuses until a worthy replacement is found.
