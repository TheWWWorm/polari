## Description
Aleridan is the capital of Almera and its largest city. ([[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]]) According to the Guide to the Lands of Polari, it is a rapidly growing, very beautiful and developed city with around 306 thousand inhabitants. After visiting him, Bishop Adrian allegedly said: “I want the whole Empire to resemble this city.” The residents themselves say: “Here we have a cathedral, here is the town hall, here is the station, and there, on the far outskirts, is Faunterra.”

### Streets and Homes
According to the mercenary [[berk|Burke]], in the whole city there is not a single wooden house: all houses are brick or stone and not lower than three floors. About ten years before 1774, the duke ordered the demolition of the last beggarly street, to build brick houses in its place and give them to the former residents free of charge. The streets are paved with bricks, and there is a fountain or horse drinker at every corner. On the main streets there are small spark wagons of the city train: for a quarter of a gate you can get to the other end of the city, and cashless ride on the foot. When Alanis started such a train in Faunterra, she referred to Aleridan, where he had been for a long time.

In the winter, Mira saw snow caps on red tiled roofs, garlands of lanterns over squares and sellers of hot wine and considered Aleridan one of the most beautiful cities in the world.

### University and the “Wave”
In the city there is a university, which, according to the "Guide", is not inferior to the capital. In his building there was a car of the first line of the "wave" that connected the city with Faunterra. On June 12, 1774, at an open session in the capital, the car conveyed: “Aleridan welcomes Faunterra”, and the natives of Aleridan in the hall smiled amicably. Later, the “wave” came to Hope.

### Church
The city has a cathedral and Cathedral Square, and Bishop Aleridana belongs to the Proto-Patriarchal Church; in 1775 Bishop Gregor Amessin represented her in the House. In the vault of the Wilhelmina Cathedral of Aleridan until 1772 lay Ulyanina Dust, which was then transported to the palace of the archpriest. On the Day of Descent, the Dukes of Almer sometimes displayed part of the family's estate in the central square - Sacred Objects, and the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] once saw three Objects from twenty feet here.

### Transport
Aleridan is a [[zheleznaya-doroga|railway]] junction, from which branches run to Faunterra, Heart of Light, and Orwell; in 1775, Almera and Hope announced the doubling of the road between Heart of Light and Aleridan. The Aleridan tract passes by the town of Oblivion, where Gallard gave battle to Erwin in June 1775. On the outskirts, on a hill, stands the Duke's [[evergard|Evergard]] Castle.

## History
According to the Guide, Almera did not exist when it became a duchy. During the First Horse War, the horde of Darian the Bad burned the city, and the Shavans abused the sisters of the then Duke. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]])

### Before 1774
Harmon once lived in Aleridan and made a then low opinion of Agatha's special descendants. In the summer of 1773, a magnificent knightly tournament was held at the Damon the Great City Arena, hosted by the Duke of Aiden (see Tournament in Aleridan). ([[turnir-v-aleridane|Tournament at Aleridan]]) [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] helped her father manage the Duchy of Aleridan for four years and later called it the most beloved of her cities, ahead of the capital and Fliss. Captain Corvis, on the eve of the Shutov plot, went to a meeting with Aiden in Aleridan.

### Under Gallard's rule
In the summer of 1774, Aiden, accused of treason, took refuge in Evergard, and the city was flooded by mercenaries of his brother Gallard, who was collecting an army. Alanis, defying her uncle, openly went to the cathedral with a dozen knights, and the philistines greeted her with delight. After the death of Evergard, the city came under the power of the [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] Priory, and people were burned again at Cathedral Square. ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) In the autumn, Joaquin saw the execution at the stake here and heard the tramp in the inn shouting that Alanis owned the legitimate power.

### Adrian's visit
In December 1774, Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] arrived in Aleridan by train from the Heart of the World. Gallard met him on the platform with six bishops and a hundred knights. Here, in Aleridan, Adrian met with [[minerva|Minerva]], who had fled Waymar; Mira noticed the surveillance (she guessed whose spies they were, and did not exclude Gallard), and the security lieutenant forbade her to appear on the street. In the Aleridian palace of the Priory, where the joker Manson did not find a place at the table, Manson received a letter with dust, from which an order was formed to kill the emperor. ([[menson|Manson]]) From Aleridan, the emperor traveled by train to Faunterra and died in a crash at Back. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]])

### 1775 year
In 1775, while Gallard was at war with Origin under Fliss, [[nadezhda|Hope]]'s troops occupied Aleridan. After losing it, the priest lost the treasury. During the summer in Fliss, he asked Paul to expel Farway from "my father's inheritance, the city of Aleridan", but was refused: Farway generously paid Paul for peace. The horde did not reach the city and moved to Fliss.

## In "Fantasies"
In "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]", possible futures, the story "Annoying Little Things" ("Two Summer Days in Aleridan") takes place in Aleridan. In 1776, Henry Farway's court was held here. Not all the Almeran lords willingly submitted to him, and in order to hold power, he moved with his army to Aleridan and called for help from outside. In the city stood the regiment of Edgar Lighthart ([[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]]), who every month borrowed in a bank of three thousand ephes for military expenditures. Edgar's soldiers were guarding the hotel, where Karen and Manson settled, and then Nexia. In the cathedral, Farway introduced his fiancé daughter Evelyn, the last Almer, a boy named [[albert-almera|Albert]]. People shouted to the bride, “Old woman! Overripe!", and the engagement at the ducal palace was scheduled for Monday, when the Almerans work in workshops.

[[karen|Karen]] Lighthart described the city as "nothing is where it should be." The copper quarter is filled with shoe workshops, with Canal Street not seeing the canal. The monument to the Knight-Winner stands on the Square of the Holy Virgins, and the monument to the Holy Virgins stands at the House of Justice. Red Boulevard and Red Street are at opposite ends of the city. The Maritime Academy here “was not”, but there are several large printing houses, and bakeries — at every step. There is a bronze clock on the station platform. Somerset Flame added that in Aleridan "people are devoured by buns and do not know how to number at home", but the war did not reach there: the Shavans moved to Fliss.
