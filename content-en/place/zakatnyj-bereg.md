## Description
Sunset Coast is one of the free counties of the West, located in the northwest corner of [[polari-world|Polaris]]. Its inhabitants are called sunsets. ([[zakatniki|Sunsetters]]) The "Guide to the lands of Polari" almost nothing about him, explaining it with local mores: no one in Polaris - neither a swamp sorcerer, nor a faunal palace, nor an agate strategist - does not protect secrecy as much as the inhabitants of the Sunset Coast, so the author "respects the mores of this land and leaves it in the shade." The only city named there is the capital of Silens.

According to the "Guide", to the south of the Sunset Coast are Mills and Holliwell, and its neighbor is also named [[shejland-zemlya|Shayland]]. ([[melnitsy|The Mills]]; [[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) Dry way from [[uejmar|Waymar]] to Silens, according to mercenaries, about five days by cart; General [[oris|Oris]]' army stood "four days march". Sunset Coast ships call at Waymar Lake Port and at Nan Clair for pilots to pass through the Tops of the Dark Kings.

The county also includes the Barony of Davy, a triangular peninsula in the upper left corner of the map, "like a tooth sticking out into the ocean." It is owned by Baron Ismail Davey, a sundowner, with his wife and three daughters; his son is Imperial General William Davey. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) Next to the Baron's castle, "in the flight of arrows", stands the Origen fort on the shore of the bay - a "granite fool", which has long been commanded by the cyre captain Murdoch, an old friend of the Baron. The monasteries of Ulyana Sad, as [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]] found out, are not on the Sunset Coast.

## Silens
The county capital, Silens, is said to have been founded in the 12th century, "under the Iron Sun", by slaves who rebelled against the Swordsmen, threw off their chains and went north. After the defeat of the Iron Sun Empire, part of the liberated, fearing chaos in its ruins, made a three-month transition to the north and settled near a group of lakes, nicknamed Pacific, where she founded Silens, “from which the history of the Land of the Sunset Coast begins.” A Waymar mercenary recalled that "Waymar was not there then and in memory."

## People and Customs
Sunsets, according to Wittor Shayland, are "not Shavans but close to them in blood and culture", and steppe chiefs willingly enter into alliances with them; therefore, Wittor called the Sunset Coast "the key to the Great Steppe". ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark writes that sunsets are crafty and secretive, adore omissions and "darken literally in everything." ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) This line, as well as sad music and poems without rhyme, it derives from the past: the Coast was founded by fugitive slaves of the Iron Sun Empire. In the port of the barony, Davy, she says, stands an Origen fort, one set up after the Horse Wars, to keep the west at bay. Garrisons of such forts are now small, but the sunsets of the past have not forgotten, and they do not like kires there. Harmon disliked "the guy from the Sunset Coast", and his suspicious companion Booth, whom Harmon took on a flight on a celestial ship, turned out to be from there.

### The Fallen Cult
A powerful Fallen cult is prevalent in the northwest. His ministers, Lacht Mies and Hash Eilish, called themselves ambassadors of the Sunset Coast and "ministers of the cult that worships the Fallen One"; [[paul|Paul]] explained that with him in the world went the Mother, but died on the way, her sunsets and called Fallen. The fallen, according to their faith, loves strong people and collects a generous “harvest” in battles; the sunset soldiers went on the attack with the call “For the Fallen!”. Hash Eilish also allowed a ritual "attachment to the dead": a dead person, according to her, is "full of spirituality" and close to the Fallen. During the siege of Waymar, the sunsets of General Horis ate the dead, making it a "cult", and received from the allies the nickname "trumpets"; Count Fleming proposed to give water from the well on Bald Hill to the knights, and "not bad cultists", and send the sunsets into a hopeless attack; Richard objected that one should not beat in the back those who fight under their flags, and Vittor called it a test for sunsets.

## History
The sun has long been at war with the North. The great-grandfather of the Earl of Fleming fought with their pirates, who settled on the Seal Islands.

### War of Objects (1756)
In 1756, Count Rantigar's army, led by General Oris, looted Dar's lodge in Shayland and took away the Sacred Objects. According to Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] in conversation with Iona, it was not an ordinary war: the Westerners raid every summer, but here the sunset general came for the Objects. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) Oris had six thousand horsemen and a fortified camp at Dara's lodge with ramparts, moats and pit-traps, while the Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] could only move two battalions by sea; however, Oris retreated "in the stupidest way" - the day before the kairs approached - and was defeated. Part of the Army then stood in Silens awaiting payment of tribute, and the Northmen returned with trophies taken in Silens. Wittor Shayland later claimed that the mysterious prisoner of the Waymar dungeon was a soldier of the Sunset Coast who stole some of the Items, and that he was rescued by "friends from the Sunset Coast"; Iona doubted it.

### Before starting the cycle
Two years before the start of the cycle, [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]] sailed on behalf of his father for negotiations in the Sunset Coast, but his caravella disappeared on the way. In the summer of 1773, the Earl of Rantigar unleashed the War of the Mills to seize the possessions of the Melnik neighbors. ([[melnichnaya-vojna|Mill War]]) Rantigar's army of twelve thousand swords won at first, but the Melniks went on to exhausting defense, and when the First Winter sent them to help the Kairs, the count was defeated. In this war, young Joaquin Yves Hanna served as a mercenary, and Orys fought for Rantigar. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]])

## 1774–1775
### The Death of Rantigar and the Tripower
In the autumn of 1774, Rantigar came to the court of [[adrian|Adrian]] to assure the emperor of his devotion. Right in the palace, in the training hall, where the emperor took oaths, he was stabbed by the multi-eyed ambassador of Reis with the words that now the Sunset Coast will side with the West. The Earl's eldest son, the rightful heir Corwin, who was all called simply the Elder Son, led the army to help Moran the Steppe Fire, fought along with the Shavans against Adrian, was known as a glorious horseman and found allies among the prominent gentry. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) The youngest son, Dainis Rantigar, an opponent of the war with Adrian, became a hero after the defeat of the horde, called himself a count, occupied the three main ports of the Sunset Coast and sought allies in Darkwater, Shimmery and even on the Folta. Rantigar's chief general, General Oris, whom the Elder Son had left to hold Silens, captured the capital. So there was a tri-power in the county. There was no real siege of the capital - Oris threw away the count's sons - but scouts, saboteurs and looters roamed around Silens. The Shavans of the Moran hordes, as Minerva's advisors noted, came together from three counties — Reis, Holliwell, and the Sunset Coast.

### Alliance with Shayland
In 1775, Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] corresponded with Orys, sought an alliance with the Sunset Coast and opened a bank there, for the protection of which he hired swords. The ambassadors of Orissa, Lacht Mies and Hash Eilish, came to Waymar at the invitation of Vittor and told how skillfully the general keeps the capital and pacifies the Elder Son. General Oris, who called himself Horis, led his army to help Shayland, and two sunset regiments fought under the banners of the Chosen One. Horis mentioned that the Elder Son holding Silens offered him a reward for betraying Vittor: when Horis left, the Elder Son returned to the capital, hung his flags, and declared himself the sole ruler of the Sunset Coast. The Northwood Bears had to pass Cylens on their way to Waymar.

In the Battle of Bald Dol, the sunsetrs sent by Horis repelled the Northwood bears' attack and then rushed to the kairs calling "For the Fallen!"; Ambassador Lacht Mies, who wore an iron hand, was stabbed with a northern sword. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) On the campaign of the Chosen One to the north, Horis commanded the corps of the Sunsets and Northwoods. Under the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], this corps attacked the city, but fell into the trap of spark garlands in the streets and retreated; General William Davey hit the sunsets at a crucial hour, but by night they, unlike the Shavans, kept order and left the valley. Hash Eilish inspired [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] by the name of Fallen. At a small meeting of the House in the First Winter, Erwin called representatives of the Sunset Coast.

## In "Fantasies"
In "Fantasies", which the author calls possible future options, the Sunset Coast is mentioned as follows:
- "Let's Catch Him" (July 1776). Ambassadors from Fairis and the Sunset Coast appeared in the First Winter with congratulations right on the Empress's birthday, which was considered "rare ignorance". The ghost of Morray in the First Winter is anyone who will take from the shelf the "History of All the Northern Lands" of the monk Goblet from the Sunset Coast, "so as not to read nonsense"; the kair of Aubrey threatens the ghost of Dallia to dig up her skeleton and sell it to the sunsetrs for rituals so that the priests gnaw the bones.
- "Closer - Further" (August 1776). Erwin says that after the defeat at the First Winter, General Horis "had the courage" to meet with him and discuss the terms of surrender; The Origin and the Sunset Coast signed the peace, and the regiments of Horis left with honor, while with the Shavans there is no peace. Part of the sunset cult worships [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]] as fallen in battle, and the cult idolized Paul and Navi as “fallen gods”.
- "Ad-ri-an!" (Election 1776). Power in the Sunset Coast recently changed and did not have time to strengthen. A student gang in Faunterra threw a motorcade of sunset ambassadors with bones shouting "Get it, cadavers!". Adrian secretly promised to give the Sunset Coast the Belomorye, and General Horis the county title, and the Sunset Coast was listed among his votes; in fact, Horis and the sunsets were on the side of Origin from the very beginning: in the winter Erwin did not cut them out, but released them alive with provisions, and under the terms of surrender they pledged to vote for him, only portraying enemies. Francis-Ilianus assumed that after the reign of Minerva, the Sunset Coast could leave the empire.
