The Ice Sea is a sea off the northern coast of Polaris. On it are the lands of [[oridzhin-zemlya|Origin]], [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]] and [[shejland-zemlya|Shayland]], and in the east, beyond the Crystal Mountains, the shores of the Beyond. The name of the sea, according to one captain, is “not for nothing”: to the Descent, its water is covered with ice.

## Geography
According to the Polari Lands Guide, Origin has access to the Ice Sea, and Northwood "adjoins" it. In Shayland, the [[torrej|Torrey]] River flows into the Sea of Ice, which originates in Lake Smokey Dahl. Even in ancient times, Shayland was called the “healthy uninhabited square of the earth” one and a half hundred miles from Smoky Distance “to the very Sea of Ice”. Later, only a narrow strip along Torrey remained from the county, but it still stretches from the lake to the sea.

Among the islands of the sea are mentioned Granified Islands: a scattering of islands with winding rocks and two cliffs rising from the water on the sides of the Strait of Klykan. A river flows into the sea. ([[reka|The River]]) According to Captain Bamber, from the River to the Belomorye is a month's journey by sea. ([[dzheff-bamber|Jeff Bamber]]) Winds from the Ice Sea strongly affect Northwood's climate: The Guidebook says they create a sense of extreme cold, but actually soften the climate. In the far north, along the Ice Sea and on the Gran Islands, there are emerald bears, and there are many whales in the sea itself.

## Trade and fisheries
The sea served the northerners as a trade route. Every spring, [[kharmon|Harmon]] bought the [[uejmar|Waymar]] sheep wool, which was delivered by ships on the Sea of Ice and [[torrej|Torrey]]. It came out almost twice cheaper than the one that the merchants of the Southern Way carried by land. At the river port of Waymar, at the source of Torrey, ships from the Sea of Ice, Northwood and Origin were moored. These were high-board ships with shooting grounds and ballistics, with gloomy bearded sailors in helmets. The journey from the Belomorye to the mouth of Torrey took one and a half to two weeks, and the ascent along the river to Waymar against the flow of another two or three.

According to the "Guide", the Northwoods, having reached the Sea of Ice, engaged in fishing, and then piracy. Shayland could not resist the Northwoods, "tempered by harsh forest life and pirate fishing in the Sea of Ice." [[minerva|Mira]], who grew up in Northwood, remembered among the brightest pictures of childhood the Sea of Ice and fishing sailboats.

## Flemings
The north coast was owned by the Earls of Fleming, the Prime vassals of the Origins, who had four cities on the Sea of Ice. The father of Count [[fleming|Benedict Fleming]] said to his son: “We have ruled the entire coast of the Ice Sea for centuries.” The originals tried to subdue the Flemings, but they revolted three times.

## In the Events of the Series
### Fleming's Flotilla (Autumn 1774)
In the autumn of 1774, after the return of Erwin from Beyond, Fleming's flotilla left the Belomorye to the mouth of the River. On her board climbed the kire of [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] and [[mark-voron|Mark]]. The sailors were whispering about the order to “fill the holds with Objects.” Someone assured that in the autumn in the Sea of Ice there are no storms, but the flotilla was covered by a storm, and the ship Albatross was killed. For more, see [[reka|the River]] article.

On the way back, Mark made an investigation on the ship. Botsman Biven boasted that he had passed through the Beyond with water from the East Sea to the Ice Sea. Mark promised him a hundred gold from the conditional “Big A” if he showed such a path on the map, and Bivén admitted that he lied. His schooner bypassed the entire Polaris, but rushed to the Beyond and searched for a whole month in vain for a strait or canal. Then the sailors returned to Soltown, sold the schooner, reached the Smoky Distance and from there on [[torrej|Torrey]] went to the Sea of Ice. Captain Bamber confirmed that from the River to the Belomorye is a month's journey. Mark concluded that it was convenient to get to the fort beyond the River by the Sea of Ice: "We walked along the Sea of Ice, then along the River and arrived at the fort without much difficulty." This location of the fort, he admitted, "as if puts under suspicion the northerners", although it could be a trick of the villain.

### The Disappearance of Richard
The eldest son of the Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]], [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]], "missed in the Ice Sea." His caravel went to the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] past Northwood and Shayland. So Erwin suddenly became the heir. In 1775, Richard appeared alive, and [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] wondered if he had frozen "all parts of his body" while sailing on the Sea of Ice.

### Revolt of Fleming (1775)
Deciding to go over to the Emperor's side, Fleming plotted to suddenly capture the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] while Origin's troops were fighting in the south, but one ship fought off his squadron, walked away from the chase, and alerted the garrison. Then the count summoned all the remaining troops on the Ice Sea, attracted the relatives of the captured Baron White, removed some of the commands from the ships and, collecting one and a half thousand swords, marched to the First Winter, but then the news of the death of Adrian came. Since then, the rebel count (according to reports of spies, he had five hundred kairs) held the coast of the Ice Sea. The Count summoned all his troops to the coast, brought faithful squadrons to the harbor of the Belomorye and made sure that no news left the Ice Sea to the south. Wittor Shayland offered Fleming mediation before Erwin, and in payment asked for the rights of duty-free trade through the Belomorye and the right to open banking offices throughout the coast. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) It was said that in case of danger, the count would leave with the fleet to the Gran Islands, where he had a network of fortresses prepared. Later, Fleming's battalion entered [[torrej|Torrey]] from the Sea of Ice.

Vittor showed [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] a model of a ship with a spark mill. Such an engine would allow you to walk against the wind in narrow fairways and among ice floes and "move further north on the Sea of Ice".

### The Return of Erwin (Autumn 1775)
In October 1775, a chartered galleon with [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] and his men - Lieutenant [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]], [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]], [[shram|Scar]], [[otets-david|Father David]], as well as new companions, whom the text calls Commandant and Squire - waited in the Ice Sea, off the coast of the Gran Islands, of a contracted vessel. The captain hurried the officers: “We are in the sea of ice. That is not what it is called.” By the descent, the water was to be covered with ice, and he had to take the detachment and return in seven weeks. Finally, a ship appeared in the Strait of Clycan under the flags of Lord Velentyne, the ruler of the Granite Islands and the son-in-law of the Earl of Fleming. Galeon was walking under a white-sea pennant, which Squire sewn from memory, and to convince Erwin to give from the deck volleys from the Gun - chained to the chair of the Shavan with Perst. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) Valentine took the ship as the ship of his father-in-law, the “knight of divine armies”, and boarded with his wife Molly and his mother, Baroness Cecilia. There he was declared arrested in the name of the Duke of Origin, and his soldiers laid down their weapons in front of the X-cloaks.

## Trivia
- In a court game of questions, Erwin replied that he would have lowered the cake he had been given to the water and sailed to the Ice Sea, where he would have "drifted" for years, giving rise to legends about the Torta Ghost.
- In the summer of 1775, chasing a whale in the High Seas, Sofia Origin recalled that in the Ice Sea “there are many like him.”
