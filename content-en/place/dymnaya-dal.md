Smokey Dahl is a huge fresh lake in the center of Polaris. [[minerva|Minerva]] called it “the greatest lake in the world, the fresh sea.” They say, “The road and the wall.”

## Geography
The Smoky Dahl is a "fresh sea" stretching five hundred miles from northeast to southwest along the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] border. The lake got its name because of the foggy haze: even in the narrowest place, you can not see either the far shore or the line where the sky converges with the water. According to Minerva, twelve rivers of the North and East pour fresh water into the lake, and one river “beginning from the Smoky Distance and carries the waters south into the ocean.” This is Holy Halliwell, the “father of all rivers.” ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) In the spring, the lake "gains strength", and Halliwell spills for a mile.

To the north of the lake flows the [[torrej|Torrey]] River. The Polari Lands Guide explicitly refers to it as the source of the Smokey Dahl. In Waymar, the river port stands "at the source of Torrey", and ships go up the Torrey to Waymar against the current, from the Sea of Ice. For sources, see “Contradictions.”

The wind in Smoky Distance is especially strong. The lake "is like breathing: it inhales at night, exhales during the day", so that at night the wind blows to the lake, and during the day from it. The north wind drives foggy clumps from the lake, and clouds with Smoky Distance extinguish the sunsets of coastal cities. According to the Guide, the lake's evaporation makes Shayland foggy and damp, "a sort of Polarian Albion", and together with the sea brings rain to the Southern Way. In winter, a sleigh path is laid along the frozen lake. The Northwood coast is swampy and "slippery", extremely inconvenient for parking ships. There are also deserted coves on the coast, for example, ten miles from Fliss.

## Coasts and cities
- **[[uejmar|Waymar]]**, the capital of Shayland, stands on a large hill. Its southern slope descends from the county castle to the lake. Of the three ports of the city, the lake is the largest and noisy: the harbor is divided by piers into factories with towers under the flags of different lands so that the Almerans, travelers and sunsets do not interfere with each other. Shayland’s hexagonal banking tower stands right in the water, two hundred yards from shore.
- Leixiti is a port on the bank of the Southern Way, to which the Lake Tract leads from Faunterra, "the disgrace of the Lands of the Crown". Swimming from Leixity or Fliss to Waymar takes five days to a week.
- **Fliss** is the port of Almera, in the words of the Shavans, "the largest on the Smoky Distance." Nearby on the cliff above the lake is the handicraft town of Obryv. Along the banks there are also monasteries, for example, the Foremother Susan monastery.
- Corden in Holliwell. According to the "Traveler", near him under the water is the famous Cathedral of Water Deities. Through hundreds of mica windows from it you can see the bottom of the lake, strewn with stone idols and bones of sacrificial animals.

## Trade
During the First Horse War, the lake stopped the nomads and prevented them from ruining the Southern Way as they had ravaged Almera. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]) In peacetime, it became a trade route between the six surrounding lands and a source of prosperity for coastal cities. In spring and summer, “money flows into the city through the thawing Smokey Dahl,” the Waymar people admitted. According to the Guide, trading across the lake is one of Shayland's main revenues, and the Earls of Holliwell hold their power over the Shavans through trade routes through the Smokey Dahl.

The [[dom-labelin|Labelins]], who held a trade monopoly over the North, were all involved in preventing direct trade across the lake. They repeatedly sabotaged the road from Faunterra to Leixiti, fiercely competed with the Almerans, driving them out of the lake trade, and bought a fleet on the Smoky Distance. Half a century ago, Magda Labelin’s great-grandfather by cunning intrigue prevented the Northwood Count from building a new port on the swampy shore of the lake. Under the contract of Count Damir Reis with [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]], Reis received a trade route through the Smokey Dahl for the sale of livestock. The pirate Freemason of Smoky Distance, a detachment of the Black Flag, hired Shayland, but took money from others.

## People and Beliefs
In the old days, when the North was not yet inhabited, people believed that behind the lake lies the edge of the eternal fog, never seeing the sun, and called it Shayland - the land of shadows. There were also legends about monsters that turn into fog and pluck the flesh from the sailors. According to the Guide, the hillbilly believe that in Smoky Distance and Halliwell live myrids (underwater witches), mermaids, lake fairies, birches (people with pike heads) and marosines (spirits of currents and whirlpools). They are ruled by the Drop-God, whose body consists of all the water of Holliwell and Smoky Distance. Even after learning about the Ocean of the Abyss, the hillocks continue to be considered the home of the Drop-God lake. The inhabitants of Lower Shayland, who live around the lake, are flexible and adapted, like any border people. On the banks of the Smoky Distance lives and the people of the halls.

## Mystery of the lake
At the end of the cycle, it turned out that the lake is associated with the origin of the human world. According to the story of Miray Nan-Claire, the First Tree had three roots, and each required a huge fresh water body: one Pramateri was planted in the Bottomless Gap, the other - "in the western part of the Smoky Distance, off the coast of Holliwell County"; ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]; [[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] immediately realized that the third is in the First Winter. [[navi|Navi]] said: "Roots" were the surviving bioreactors of the ship, and they required three deep reservoirs at the tops of the triangle. There are only two, the Smokey Dale and the Bottomless Gap. The forebears crossed the hostile continent on foot, carrying reactors on the shoulders of robots, laid the second reactor in Smoky Distance, and for the third they found a cavity under the valley of the First Winter, the entrance to which is now called the Cosuli Grotto. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) Navi himself in the crash of his ship escaped thanks to the catapult and fell into the Smokey Dahl; his companion Lilanna died. According to him, the annihilation of the heaviest Object, the Womb, "could have vaporized the entire Smokey Dale."

## In the Events of the Series
- In August 1756, the Almers provided their fleet to transport across Smokey Dahl to Waymar's imperial court of Telurian.
- In 1774, the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] sailed to Shayland with the Light Sphere, and then [[minerva|Minerva]]. The Northwoods from the Bear Fang made their way to the capital via [[torrej|Torrey]] and the Smokey Dahl.
- In Now You're a Wizard, [[navi|Nathaniel]] tells Mick that the portal threw him right into the lake and he barely floated out on the outskirts of Waymar. ([[teper-ty-koldun|Now You Are a Sorcerer]]; [[mik|Mik]])
- In early June 1775, the heads of the House Origin rushed to the lake to transfer troops to Waymar, where the Puppeteer held Iona. According to [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]'s plan, one half of the army went through the Southern Way and Smokey Dahl under his father [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]], and the other he himself led through Almera. There were not enough ships in Leixity: half the battalions and all the Northwood forces sent land around the lake, and the four best battalions waited for transport for a long time. Eventually Desmond, with Colonel [[blekberri|Blackberry]] and four battalions, moved towards Waymar on the water. Erwin, with one horse company of the Leed Wolves, broke out ahead to Fliss and went out to a deserted bay ten miles from the city. The Battle of the cliff over the lake with the army of the archpriest Gallard was one of the battles of this campaign. Later in the summer, [[krejg-nortvud|Craig]]'s Northwood army marched along the north coast of Smoky Distance.
- In the summer of 1775, [[paul|Paul]] with [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], Archpriest Gallard and the Shavans crossed the Smokey Dahl on ships of the Fleece Fleet and arrived at Waymar. The swim filled Alanis with “disgusting melancholy”: the shawns sat on the deck for hours looking at the water, and the lake was too calm and too often covered with fog. Paul received from Gallard a fleet at Smoky Distance and the support of monastic orders; the route of his flotilla ran from Fliessa Bay across the lake into Shayland. Minerva was going to intercept Paul at the crossing: “With the help of the Bird, I will watch him from a distance, with the help of the Glove I will quickly approach, and with Hilt I will strike.”
- In September 1775, the sky schooner Minerva flew over the lake. From a height of five hundred yards, it seemed as smooth as a mirror. The schooner spent 24 hours sailing on the water, boarding a ship under the Shayland flag, and then left via Northwood for the First Winter.
- At the end of 1775, the Almeran ships rose from Smoky Distance on Gemini and landed Horton's troops in the rear of Adrian. ([[bliznyashki|The Twins]])

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, the 1775 campaign is retold through the eyes of the Shavans: in August, the Great Horde split in Fliesse, and the northern part, led by the Gnout-Ganta, crossed the Smokey Dahl, the Northwood forests and the Crystal Mountains to suffer defeat under the walls of the First Winter.
