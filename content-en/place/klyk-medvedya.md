## Description

The Bear Fang always appears before the traveler suddenly: the road turns into a slumber more often, and suddenly the forest diverges. The slope of the hill and the river bank are covered with a maze of brown log houses, boats are crowded at the piers, and a huge, heavy and angular castle stands on the hill. Like Waymar, the city is compressed with a ring of fortress walls; the walls here are wooden. From the capital of Northwood to the south, the Trade Route stretches through endless forests.

The castle smells of pine: it has high gloomy halls, huge fireplaces, crosses of swords and halberds on the walls, massive furniture, everywhere skins and hunting trophies - pails, wolves, moose and even fang skulls. There are no bear heads among the trophies: the bear is a symbol of Northwood's power, and no hunter of this land will dare to kill him unless the beast attacks first.

At the castle there is a menagerie, where the scientist Maverick lived, the favorite of Countess Sybil and [[minerva|Mira]]. ([[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]]) A huge beast weighing far beyond a thousand pounds could dance on his hind legs for a treat, bow, pretend to be dead and "weep" if he was sung a chivalrous ballad; under the countess, he got up all his height and raised his paw like a guard on a guard.

## City and Economy

According to the Polari Land Guide, Bear Fang is [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]'s largest city along with Birbis and Fox Doll. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) The cities of Northwood are usually fortified settlements among the forest and are small by the standards of the central lands. The Bear Fang and the Granny Islands are home to the largest shipyards in the North. Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias]] was famous for his passion for ships; according to Mira's observation, he hated to leave the castle and even went to the city once a month to inspect some ship.

In the southern part of the city there is a wooden church of the Forefather Maximian. In 1775, her preacher was a secret agent of the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]].

## Events

- **1756.** The Northwoods went from their capital to [[uejmar|Waymar]] to celebrate the Seventeenth Gift. In the same year, a young [[navi|Nathaniel]] told a Waymar boy, Mick, that he had passed through a magical portal aimed at the Bear Fang, but found himself in [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]. ([[mik|Mik]])
- **Winter 1773/74.** The countess took [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]]'s daughter from Elena's boarding house after her third year, and she returned to the Bear Fang. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) The mother sent a letter to the Bear Fang only at the end of May, but in mid-June Gloria appeared in the capital under the name of Minerva Stagforth - before the letter could reach her.
- **March 1774.** After an ambush in the Pre-Less, where her father died, Mira found shelter in the Bear Fang at Countess Sybil. Here the countess stated that the girl was fourth in line of succession, and took her to the capital; before Mira left, she said goodbye to Maverick, gently touching his paw. Mira later left a letter of “insurance” here for a deal with [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]].
- **Autumn 1774.** On the orders of Adrian, Count Elias and his sons left the Bear Fang for Faunterra, but were detained in Waymar.
- **1774–1775.** Elena-in-the-Lake boarding house and Bear's Fang were the places where the evidence of the bride's substitution was stored and where the Crown could not reach. While [[krejg-nortvud|Craig]] was fighting in the south and sitting in the capital, his brother Horace, who was not respected, was the viceroy here; in March 1775, he barely suppressed the rebellion.
- **Summer of 1775.** Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]], by order of [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], brought the old Earl Elias Bear to the Fang and declared him a legitimate ruler, interrupting Craig Perst's supporters. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) Craig laid siege to his own capital: he gave a volley of crossbows and sent rams to the gate, which Dorcaster burned with a feather, and threatened to set fire to the wooden city. Elias tried to throw himself off the tower. Dorcaster threatened to sail and sink the Northwood fleet, and fooled Craig for two weeks with negotiations, and then handed over the castle and left, taking Maverick's bear.
- **September 1775.** Returning the castle, Craig beat his father and walled it with Horace in prison.
- **October 1775.** After Craig's death at Fox Dole, the city was engulfed in anarchy. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) Craig was supported by soldiers and bandits, Elias - sailors, artisans and sturgeons. Supporters of the "Seventh Earl", brandishing themselves with white stripes, beat and robbed the "drums" - supporters of Craig, and then anyone who did not wear stripes. Elias and Horace were taken out of the prison and shown to the crowd at the gate of the citadel, and the count blessed those who wanted to go into the army of the “Chosen One”. In the same days, [[otets-david|Father David]] arrived here to receive news of Adrian's alliance with the order from a secret agent of the order, but [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] intercepted him in the church of Forefather Maximian.
- **Plans.** [[minerva|Minerva]] was going to spend two years from the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] rail branches in the Belomorye, Labelin and the Bear Fang.

## The fate of the bear Maverick

Taken away by Dorcaster, Maverick danced for the soldiers of the "Chosen One" under the baron's lute, and then lived with him in the captured castle of [[lid|Leed]]. After the death of Dorcaster in Leed, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] found a bear: by winter he was watering and became white and silver.
