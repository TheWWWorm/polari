## Geography
Shayland is the narrowest of the possessions of the Great Houses. According to Arrow, Coin, Spark, the county stretches four hundred miles from south to north, from [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]] to the [[more-ldov|Sea of Ice]], but is only forty miles wide: twenty miles on each bank of the [[torrej|Torrey]] River. (In early editions of Arrow, Coin, Spark, it was one hundred and fifty miles long and sixteen miles wide, eight on each bank.) In a printed book that Mira read on the train, her sister-in-law's lands are called the "narrow and meager strip" stretching along the navigable Torrey. There, the count is called the “ruler of the Predlesya and Porechya”. And in the thoughts of Erwin Vittor - "the ruler of the navigable Torrey River and a narrow strip of coastal land."

According to the Polari Land Guide, the land has a funnel shape and expands to the north. Torrey takes its source in Smoky Distance and flows into the Ice Sea. The county is conditionally divided into two parts:
- Lower Shayland is the inhabited county around the lake where the capital [[uejmar|Waymar]] stands;
- The upper Shayland, or Preforester, is the northern part, closer to [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]. Here, near the Northwood border, lies the tiny Len Stagforth, the birthplace of [[minerva|Minerva]]. ([[stagfort|Stagfort]])

Shayland's neighbors are Northwood to the east and [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] to the west. The Torreya swimming pool is fertile and is occupied mainly by gardens and gardens. The Guide calls the county's climate the Polarian Albion. The evaporation of the Smoky Distance goes to the north, so there are frequent fogs and rains, winters are not very cold, but dirty, and summers are warm and foggy. In the novel, this “raw foggy land” is constantly shrouded in smoke. The solar spring of 1775 seemed to the Waymar people "the strangest weather" in their memory (as Harry Hog says).

The river links the county together. Seagoing vessels enter Torrey from the Sea of Ice and rise against the flow to Waymar "on oars or with burlaps". At the source of Torrey, at Smoky Distance, stands the river port of Waymar, where high-board ships from Northwood and Origin moor. This way in 1774 they went to the capital of Northwood: from the Bear Fang up the Torrey to Waymar, and then across the lake. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) On the way from Origin to Waymar, the traveler boarded a ship at the northern port, went out to the Preforest and transferred to the river.

## Cities and places
- **[[uejmar|Waymar]]** is the capital and largest city, the port at the source of [[torrej|Torrey]] and the financial center of the North.
- The Lodge of the Seventeenth Dar is a giant flop on the west bank of the Torrey, fifteen miles from the river and four hours' ride from Waymar. By the time of the cycle, this is a place of pilgrimage with a chapel, prayer pillars and a monument to the knights. A newly planted forest is growing around.
- Stagforth is "small linen in the north of the Preforest". ([[stagfort|Stagfort]])
- Bridge of Eight Prayers - according to the "Guide", the second longest bridge of the Empire (1050 m), connecting the shores of Torrey. For a long time, it was the longest bridge, until Juliana's Bridge over the [[kholliwell|Hollywell]] surpassed it (1300 m). It is believed that the bridge can not be crossed without the blessing of the Mothers. One day, Northwood's army crossed it without the bishop's blessing, and a few days later the bridge burned, cutting the army's way back. After forty years, the restored bridge collapsed under the cavalry of the Sunset Coast. Since then, on both ends there are gazebo-chapels with statues of Foremothers, and the traveler reads eight appeals to the Foremothers, whom he believes most.

In total, Count Vittor owned eight cities, thirteen castles, a navigable river and a thousand square miles of fertile land. A chain of castles on the banks of Torrey held the defense of the county.

## Economy
In the first century and a half, the county got rich on flax. The crops were rich, the looms worked year-round, and the silver of the Almera and the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] flowed north through the lake in exchange for a brightly colored canvas. ([[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]])

In the 18th century, Shayland's main wealth was the bank. The Count's network of banks was "thrown across the north of the empire" and brought "unknown darkness of income". The merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] kept his savings in a bank in Shayland. The Guide calls Waymar the financial center of the North, where the northern banking system was born. The bankers of the earl assured that “Shayland’s banks are reliable and strong” and that in troubled times they are especially willing to carry money. Other important sources of income are trade through the Smokey Dahl and a strong guild of builders: most Sheilanders live in cities. Waymar was also served with northern goods. Every spring, Harmon purchased the Virginia wool brought by Sea of Ice and [[torrej|Torrey]], almost twice cheaper than that carried by the merchants of the Southern Way by land.

The pre-forest, on the other hand, lived with almost no money. According to the memoirs of Myra, there was almost no trade there: the peasants exchanged goods in the old days, and copper stars served mainly for change. Silver agates, and even more so gloria and deer, were a curiosity.

## People and Customs
The Guide emphasizes that residents of the two parts of the county differ in composition. The people of Lower Shayland, living near the Smoky Distance, are borderline cosmopolitans: flexible, easy to accept new, appreciating primarily the home comfort and well-being of the family. The inhabitants of Preblesia have been strongly influenced by the Northwoods: they love hunting, hospitality and broad gestures and consider themselves patriots of the North. Both halves dislike each other, but they are united by a common enemy - the Sunset Coast, whose raids taught the Shaylands to quickly unite and appreciate simple joys.

Raw and mist are associated with drinking. According to the “Guide”, a light “undershof” is inscribed in the weekdays of the Shayland, in cities and villages there are many pubs and taverns, and the best brewers of Polari live here. Shaylandians like to “go out into people.” There is a saying: “Only rich and stupid people rest at home.” A dozen friends with a barrel of beer are valued above one or two friends “in the fire and water”. Leisure Shayland — pub, guests, theater, Sunday service, kayaking and water games, as well as gambling: cards, dice, rat races. Shayland is one of the four lands of the Empire, where there is a tradition of stationary theater. Many professional cheaters come from here, and minor financial fraud here is more a sign of taking care of the wallet than a disgrace. A good tone in the cold season is to wear bright things.

In Shayland, according to the "Guide", the greatest equality of the sexes in the entire Empire. Frequent wars have forced women to take their husbands' places, and among them are well-known artisans, financiers, and shipwrights. The faith of the Shaylands is “philosophical”: they are not zealous, but willingly observe rituals and sacrifice to parish caskets. The most revered is the Mother Vivian, the patroness of the holiday. There are many churches. Rural superstition explains this by the many witches who have long lived in cities. The river gave rise to a special class - the Burlaks - and Burlat folklore with sayings like "useful as a one-armed rower".

In the novels, Shayland cuisine in peacetime is beans, oatmeal and buckwheat. It was this food that became a torment in the siege of Waymar. The soldiers of the garrison, according to the observation of the Virginia Greys, it was easy to talk: a mug of ale was enough. The soldiers of the count wore green.

## Ruling House
The county is ruled by the Great House of Shayland. ([[dom-shejland|House Shayland]]) Its coat of arms is a signal horn, whisked by ivy. The current branch of the house of merchant origin: [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]]'s father was a banker, his grandfather was a merchant. Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent]] told his sons the story of the family. His grandfather fled Halliwell with his whole family when the Count there decided to take money from the merchants for the maintenance of the army, and Vincent's father was born on a ship. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) With his mind, he became the richest man in Shayland, although the originals disdained to deal with him, and the counts tried to arrest his condition three times. He then bought up so much of the county's debt notes that the Shayland House essentially became his. The Counts had to marry Vincent their daughter and call him heir. The barons and knights brought the merchant a vassal oath, but they began to fulfill it only when half of them were paid, and the other half were intimidated: for this he kept a hired Northwood regiment of two thousand “bears” in Waymar for years. The Guide calls this family the Harper banking house, and the heiress' husband Vincent Harper; the name of the Shayland House has survived. For a long time, I did not take the “newly minted bankers” seriously. The situation was changed by the Seventeenth Gift, which many considered God’s blessing of the Shaylands. Count Vincent's favorite saying was, "Torture what you have."

In 1756, Count Vincent ruled. During the years of the cycle, his son Vittor rules, and since 1774 his wife [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] became the Countess.

## History
### Base and Decline
About three centuries ago, the entire desert land north of Smoky Distance, a rectangle of two hundred miles from east to west and four hundred from south to north (in early media, a square of one and a half hundred miles), was called Shayland. She was asked by a knight who had served before the emperor. The foggy land, rumored to be full of monsters, was not needed by anyone, and the emperor willingly gave it up along with the title of count. The horn on the coat of arms was an instrument of the scout of the imperial army, and the ivy was a symbol of a foggy land: it braided trees, fortress walls and even the sides of ships pulled to land.

For a century and a half, the counts ruled successfully: they built a dozen cities and castles, generously endowed the peasants with land, started a fleet on the lake and [[torrej|Torrey]]. Wealth attracted neighbors. From the east, the "cloudy Northwoods", hardened by forest life and piracy in the Sea of Ice, from the west - numerous nomads. The county melted until only a strip along Torrey was left. The chain of river castles was impregnable: the Shaylands learned to quickly transfer their small forces along the river. According to Wittor, Waymar was burned to the ground three times.

### Seventeenth Gift
In 1756, one and a half to two decades before the events of the cycle, the Seventeenth Gift of the Gods arrived on the west bank of the [[torrej|Torrey]]. It originated late at night in the forest wilderness. The earth shuddered for twenty miles around, the sky turned scarlet from thousands of acres of burning forest. The peasants fled in horror, some rushing across Torrey. For a week, the entire count's army, peasants and hundreds of townspeople fought the fire. They turned a forest road running from south to north into a clearing twenty yards wide and fought off the fire that the west wind drove on the coastal villages for two days. Saved all the pouring rain: never before in this damp foggy land so pleased with the rain.

The exhausted count's army was late for bed. Sunsets came from the west, where the fire was weaker, defeated the Shaylands and took up defenses around Dar. The Westerners considered it unfair that Dar came exactly on the Shayland strip: the gods, apparently, "missed for some couple of miles." Count Vincent spent a month gathering troops and looking for allies, but there was almost no help. Tellurian was in no hurry to extinguish the war of the Great Houses, Aiden Almera despised the banker, Labelin asked for a mad fee. Only the Duke of Orange helped. Relations with Northwood were then so tense that the count kept part of the troops on the eastern border. The War on Objects began. When the troops of Origin arrived a month later, the Westerners managed to extract only twelve Items and took them to the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] (in early media - to Reis); the Duke of Desmond undertook an expedition to return them, and it cost the West three burned cities. The merit of the fleet of Count Fleming, who moved the northern troops, called the victory over the Sunset Coast and the "rapid return of stolen items." Of the hundred and forty-two objects of the Gift, Count Vincent received thirty-nine. Thus began the Shaylands' friendship with the Origins, crowned by the marriage of Iona and Vittor. ([[brak-vittora-i-iony|Wedding of Iona and Vittor]]) At the celebration in honor of Dar in Waymar gathered the nobles of the Empire. Among the guests was [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]].

With the bed of the Seventeenth Gift, the main mystery of the cycle is also connected. There young Vittor first met the future [[paul|Paul]]. Later, the Puppeteer said that he and his brother "first met the Forefather Paul", and referred to the sign shown in the bed of Dar.

## In the Novels
### 1774 year
In March 1774, Sir Clive Stagforth was murdered in Prelesia, near the Northwood border. ([[klajv-stagfort|Clive Stagfort]]) His daughter Mira fled to the Bear Fang (see Stagforth). ([[stagfort|Stagfort]]) In the audience, Adrian reminded Countess Northwood that the Pre-Lessier "belongs to the Shayland land administered from Waymar." In the same year, Minerva was held in Waymar. During the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]], Vittor maneuvered between the Origins and the Crown. The Virginian strategists were even considering a way south through the Shayland: to pass the Pre-Less, to climb the oars on [[torrej|Torrey]] in two weeks, to cross the Smokey Dahl in five days and hit Almera.

### The War of 1775
After the Waymar coup of 27 May 1775, Erwin declared Wittor the Puppet Master. ([[perevorot-v-uejmare|Coup in Waymar]]) The Lord Chancellor established a water blockade of Waymar, and in the capital arrested the lords-representatives of Shayland and employees of his banks. The Count demanded that the vassals must assemble "all the troops from the shores of [[torrej|Torrey]]", about a thousand copies. Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]] brought only two hundred people and referred to the fact that six days had passed from the Preforest. Even earlier in Almera, a Shavan gang was operating, robbing “only banks of Shayland”.

In the summer, the Northerners landed in Waymar, defeated the city militia and besieged the Count on Castle Hill. Details of the siege, the strike by the Sword of the Gods, and Vittor's "resurrection" are set out in [[uejmar|Waymar]]'s article. Desmond's retreating kairs devastated Shayland's banks and captured about two hundred thousand ephes. Having become the Chosen One, Vittor led an army of sunsetrs, Shavans, Almerans and Shaylands to the north. For the needs of the army, he instructed the banker [[perkins|Perkins]], who manages the central branch of his bank, to conduct an unscheduled tax collection "in double the size" throughout the county, from the townspeople and from all baronies "from Waymar to Stagforth", promising that next year it will be possible not to pay at all. Only the possessions of Baron Dorcaster were freed from the tax: according to the count, only he came to the rescue "with full force". In the Battle of the First Winter, the Shaylands in green coats were part of Richard Origin's northern corps. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) After the defeat, the remnants of the Shaylands retreated to the Squalls of Heaven. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]])

During the battle, the Earl's younger brother, [[martin-shejland|Martin]], walked from [[lid|Leed]] to the Fiddle of Heaven and admitted that he had let Iona go. Vittor stabbed him with a dagger. After the defeat, the remnants of the Shaylands went to the Sky Creatures; there, with Vittor's people, the Dust Ulyanina remained. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]; [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) On December 3, Adrian stunned Vittor with a spark and imprisoned him in [[chrevo|the Womb]]. So the count ceased to rule Shayland. Iona formally remained Countess of Shayland (see House of Shayland). ([[dom-shejland|House Shayland]])

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, which the author calls possible futures, the county remains without a ruler in the spring of 1776. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) Adrian says that in Shayland "powerlessness": the only heiress of the title "for personal reasons will not enter into law." In the summer of the same year, Erwin in the First Winter calls his sister "the Countess of Shayland" and the prima vassal of Minerva. Vittor himself is frozen in the Womb, which by peace treaty is kept in the Venerable Citadel of Faunterra under the supervision of Maurice Labelin until the decision of the ecclesiastical court. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) Iona on Sophia's days is his ghost: she often sees the ghosts of "the creeps" who were killed or who wanted to kill her.
