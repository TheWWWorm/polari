## Geography
Shimmery is a southern "solar kingdom" in the far south of the mainland. According to the Polari Lands Guide, most of it is occupied by the Lion Mountains; there are also jungles and fertile valleys, and the climate is the "classical tropics". In the northeast, the kingdom comes into contact with Litland: the city of Surlion in the Holliwell Delta stands "with one foot in Litland, the other in the Shimmery Kingdom". ([[litlend|Litland]]; [[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) To the west, beyond the White Bones mountains, lies [[darkvoter|Darkwater]].

The Guide calls two wonders of Shimmery's nature. The first is the bottomless chasm, a crevice in the mountains deeper than half a mile, the bottom of which no one saw because of the eternal fog; ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) The church has declared the gift lying in it to be forbidden, and the descent there is forbidden. At the Propasta stands the Maximian monastery, where the hermit king lived for eight years. The second miracle is the Lion's Gate: two three-hundred-foot cliffs that connect over the gorge of Lviv, through which the main land road to the kingdom passes. The upper jumper is decorated with carvings of the Sun, Moon and Star, pylons are a string of souls of heroes ascending the Star, and the bases are decorated as if the Gates were standing on the backs of two stone lions lying with their faces to the north. Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] dreamed that the rails would link the entire Polari "from the First Winter to the Lion's Gate". Among the Shimmerian wonders, the shipbuilder Hobart-Synthal in Soltown also called the palace on the waterfall and the ten-mile Alley Magnolia (in early media - also Cloud Abyss).

## Cities
- Laem is the white-stone capital of the Shimmerian kings and the patrimony of Prince Hector, a port on the Laem Cape. ([[lajem|Laem]])
- Orcada is the “pearl of the South” and one of the largest ports of the kingdom, a city of marble houses in patterns and covered bridges over the streets. The most profitable commodity of its harbors is tea, and the silk trade, according to Neymir, is given to Laem merchants. According to Neymir, who pretended to be an Orkad merchant, according to the tradition of Orkada, the merchant gives his son to another merchant for training, and the best shipyards are here. The route from Orcada to Ruilde is one of the usual sea lines.
- The Surlion is a huge port in the Holliwell Delta, with houses on stilts; in the Shadow of the Great Tree it is called "a pit for the poor".
- Pentago is a city of the Second of the Five, "lying in a bowl between five rocks, surrounded by five white walls and crowned with five towers." Here stood the castle of Count Kindar.
- Iserin is a city in the County of Iserin, a possession of the Fourth of Five; Izerin County in the novel was called "deaf and forgotten gods" - according to the master Hortense, his uncle and aunt lived in one town.
- **Melison** is a quiet wine town in a secluded valley two miles from the White Tract. Its inhabitants speak with a funny mountain accent and are in no hurry, and Laemians consider them tight-minded. It was formerly owned by Baron Monat-Erlin, and more recently passed to the Earl of Auguste-Rimare, Third of the Five. Here [[kharmon|Harmon]] under the name of Horama built his heavenly ship.

Laam and Pentago connect the White Tract - according to the townspeople, the best road in the South, and maybe in the whole Empire: it is paved with hexagonal sandstone slabs and smoothly surrounds the mountains. By the end of the second day of the journey lies the Small Pass with a dozen hotels and taverns.

## Economy
According to the "Travel Guide", Shimmery is a rich land that lives on trade; sea routes provide for the export of tea, coffee, sweets, spices and precious stones. The main thing is about ninety percent of the deposits of [[iskra|spark]] eyes, which makes the economy and sovereignty of the kingdom “almost invulnerable”. Only five genera of the Council of Five can produce and delimit the eyes, and only one trusted relative knows the secret of cutting in each genus.

For thirty years, the Crown had a monopoly on the purchase of Shimmerian eyes, and the lion's share of them went to the imperial army. By 1774, the term of the contract was expiring, the storehouses of the nobles had accumulated surpluses, and the Council of Five was preparing to sell them to anyone; the merchant Ira Medea, cousin of Ira-Mercury of the Council of Five, was looking for buyers still in the Useful People. The mercenary army is also an item of income: in 1775, a regiment of Pasynks stood in Orcade, in Iserina - the brigade of St. Strauss, in Pentago - the Golden Swords, and the Solar Regiment was considered the best of the mercenary regiments of the South. The symbol of the Shimmer ships is the golden lion on the sail.

## People and Customs
“The Guide” writes that if in the North the glorious are called fearless warriors, then in Shimmery they are successful merchants. Sudden lack of money is the main fear and shame of the Shimmer, and the number of women with whom a man can simultaneously live is “directly proportional” to his wallet. But the main feature of the southerners is not greed, but hedonism: life should bring pleasure. Tables are bursting with wine, fruit, meat and sweets, dinners go into dinners with business negotiations; in honor of the harpist in tea houses, bright murals on the walls and poets who are hired to put the host's thought into rhymes.

Shimmery is a kind of cult of women: “In no land Polaris girls are not carried so carefully in their arms.” The status of a man is determined by the number of children - the treatment of the "father of six" sounds like a sign of respect - and a truly successful man should contain at least one althessa. ([[alter|Alter and Altessa]]) There is a market for althess, half veiled slavery (buying prisoners of the Shavan wars), half pimping; whites are most valued. Noble ladies, "white-blooded", carefully protect the skin from the sun: covered galleries and air streets are built for them in Laem. In the novels, this way of life is shown on the example of the pimp Onoriko-Meysor and his white-blooded wife Vanessa-Lilit, and Hector kept an altess of various "profiles".

According to the "Travel Guide", civilization came here late - in the era of the Expansion of the Borders, under the Yangmei Dynasty - and the colonists who founded Laem and Orkadu quickly established trade between the aborigines of the Lion Mountains and the Land of the Crown. Almost all the local tribes were assimilated; independence was preserved only by goat-horned zunds - mountaineers who do not understand imperial speech. The Sundae are also mentioned in the Yanmei Hunt: their chief Zandur helped the Hunter's squad by demanding the return of the ancestral lands "from the Cool Stream to the Erytero Gorge". According to Shimmerian legend, the coast of Laem cape was opened by the crew of Lord Lymon of Marengo; the Voltians, however, claim that they were the first to visit here.

According to Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collectible edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, Shimmery's local code legitimizes polygamy, and any althessa child can be the heir. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]; [[alter|Alter and Altessa]]) At the bottomless hole, according to her, is the headquarters of the Maximian Order.

## The Ruling House and the Council of Five
According to the "Travel Guide", Shimmery enters the Empire with conditionally independent status: the king observes the main laws of Polari, takes a vassal oath to the emperor and pays Faunterra a tax, and the conditions for the extraction and supply of eyes are strictly regulated. Otherwise, it is an independent state ruled by a king from the House of Nellis-Lyon, the master of Laem. The genus Nellis-Lyon, according to the sarcastic words of Count [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] in Fantasies, is only three hundred years old (and the genus Lilliday is eight hundred).

In the early seventeenth century, after seven kings had succeeded one another in ten years, the five most powerful families, long linked to the royal family by marriage, made an agreement: the first would occupy the throne, while the others would renounce their claims but gain a vote in the most important affairs; the king must yield to the majority, and anyone exposed as a conspirator must leave the council. Each family was particularly successful in its work: the first in architecture and mercenary troops, the second in the tea trade, the third in winemaking, the fourth in silk, the fifth in shipbuilding. Each of the Five owns a mine of eyes and chooses one close relative who personally masters the secret of cutting; even one of the sixteen daughters of Francis-Ilian knows how to border the eyes. The council was not always unanimous: Neymir, who served as a mercenary in Shimmery, recalled the War of Velmozh, when the Third and Fourth of the Five turned on the Second and put an army of horsemen on two-legged birds. Representatives of Shimmery, according to Minerva, two hundred years did not miss a single meeting of the House. By the number of Objects in the sanctuary, the kingdom ranks third in the Empire - ninety-one, after the Dynasty (over three hundred) and Almera (one hundred twenty).

The title of the First of the Five is the rightful ruler; in 1774–1775 it was King [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] - "the grandson of the sun, the stretcher of the blessed shadow, the keeper of the keys to the Lion's Gate, glorious over glorious." He was so rich that he once bought an island for one walk with his beloved, and had one son and sixteen daughters. The second of the Five was Count [[kuindar|Quindar]], the abbot of the monastery at Proval, sitting at Pentago; The third was Count Auguste-Rimard. The fifth, the owner of many shipyards, staged a naval parade in honor of Magda Labelin. While the king lived in the monastery, Hector himself was called the First of the Five ("Hector Elvira Adelaide of the genus Sophia the Great, First of the Five, Baron of the Marble Gorges, Count Astellit, honorary burgomaster of the White Stone Laam, crown prince"). According to the schedule that Henry Farway laid out in 1775, the Council was divided into parties: Hector supported the Fourth of the Five, the Second stood with all his strength for Francis-Ilianus, the Third held the Second, and the Fifth threw itself between them, depending on where it was more profitable; each pair had reserves of eyes for about a million ephes.

## History in the Series
### Francis Ilianus
Under Francis-Ilian, the kingdom, according to the townspeople, "drowned in abundance." Eight or nine years before the events of 1775, the king went as a hermit to the monastery at the bottomless Gap, and Prince Hector ruled by deeds. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) In the fall of 1774, the Shimmerian fleet (three hundred and twenty ships) and an army led by Hector ("eight thousand dancers", as he joked) came to the aid of Adrian in the war against [[moran|Steppe Fire]] in Litland. At the Battle of [[melorandzh|Melorange]] on 2 December 1774, the Shimmerian avant-garde blocked the dry bed of Orange and took on the blow of the hordes, and then, retreating, lured the Shavans under the spark spears of the imperial infantry. The battle was won, but two and a half thousand Shimmerians fell; Hector was angry that instead of the soldiers of the lord, his men were killed, although the "lion's share" of the dead were mercenaries. Adrian left the Shimmerian army in Melorange to finish off the savages, and in the winter of 1775 Hector's fleet was going to pick it up from the Litland port of Livniy Les. In Melorange, Hector caught the scouts of the horde of Neymir and Charu and ordered their execution, but they were saved by a sorcerer. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) Soon the prince suddenly led his army out of Melorange, and marched quickly east to the Rain Forest; the Shavans, standing in the jungle, struck him in the side and in the back, and the Shimmerians fled to the ships, throwing carts, shields and weapons - they survived only because the Lithuanians made a sortie out of the city. In the spring of 1775, Hector arrived at Faunterra for the House. In circumvention of its treaty with the Dynasty, Shimmery sold thousands of spark-eyes to the Labelins and Moger Buckley, while Hector offered Erwin fifteen thousand eyes. The Shimmerian defeat and death of Adrian saddened even the inhabitants of the quiet Melison: Litland for them is the younger brother, also the South.

### The War for the Eyes
In 1775, the [[moris-labelin|Labelins]], [[ervin-oridzhin|Origin]], and Farway fought for the eyes of the Shimmers. The Council of Five refused [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]] and sold the eyes of the Northerners and Hope, after which the Southern Way declared war on the kingdom. Magda's squadron trapped a fleet with a hired Solar Regiment; General Lorinal Gitan was killed, and the new commander, Colonel Horey, moved to her side along with a load of eyes. Then the travelers and the Solar Regiment landed in Shimmery, captured Iserin, where the St. Straus Brigade stood, and moved to the Bottomless Gap. Francis-Ilianus later admitted that the Labelins had defeated some Shimmerian regiments, overbought others, reached Pentago, captured many eyes, and the kingdom lost half its wealth; in part he blamed the idle son. The situation was saved by the fact that Hector attracted a battalion of kire to Shimmery's side - Erwin sent him to the South, "where there was no means of northern influence." Together with him, the king and the prince sailed home, on the way they reconciled, and upon arriving, they learned that the travel troops had already left. While the war with the Labelins was going on in Shimmery, the Minister of the Minerva Court discussed with the Shimmerian ambassador the establishment of a residence in Laem.

At the precipice appeared the dead Adrian - "Hunter" - with a detachment of Shavans and zounds: he captured the Maximian monastery, captured the Second of the Five, and then joined with Magda. Soon the Second of the Five of the captives became his guest and adviser; Harmon, on the other hand, told the Shavans and Colonel Horey (he wrote the story of Rei-Roy on the move), that the Count had revealed to the lord a secret related to the holiness of Faunterra and Rei-Roy, and that Adrian wanted to go down to the bottom of the Proval on a celestial ship behind the talking Objects; all this is known only from Harmon's words. The Lord's army descended from the Lion Mountains to the sea and sailed on the ships of the Labelins; the squadron passed by Surlion, although Adrian demanded to go there. In Laem was kept the Bird without Flesh, the personal Item of Elena the Traveler; in Soltown, Francis-Ilian presented it to Maurice Labelin "in token of renewed friendship", and Adrian took advantage of it.

### Adrian's Second War and After
Having put things in order at home and punished the guilty, the king and the prince returned to the north “bound by strong kinship”: Hector’s althessa [[katrin-katrin|Catherine Catherine]] was expecting a child, and Francis-Ilian told everyone to congratulate him on his future grandson. At Adrian's request, the Shimmerians landed at Soltown, but, not wanting enmity with the Kairs, exchanged birds with [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] and did not fight against the Northerners. By the condition of alliance with Adrian, the king put the punishment of the regiment of the Burning Sun, who defected to the side of Magda: he and his son considered it treason. In Adrian's second war, the Shimmerian regiments under Hector guarded the borders of the South Road, and Francis-Ilianus and the Second of the Five were at Adrian's side with Lady in Darkness. In 1775, the Chavans of Cort were considering robbing the weakened Laem and Orcada.

In Laem in 1774 begins the story of the balloon master Hortensia, bought by Harmon; there Harmon bought shavanka [[niza|Niza]]. ([[kharmon|Harmon Paula Roger]])

## In "Fantasies"
In "Fantasies", which the author calls possible options for the future, Shimmery is described as follows:
- "The Device" (Spring-Summer 1776). The marriage proposal of the king, received by Kair [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] at the end of the main cycle, gets the move: in the summer of 1776, Jemis goes to Shimmery to get acquainted with the "miracle bride", and the king's relatives arrange for him to watch.
- The Rutin Affair (June 1776). In Finborough Cathedral, the mechanical device reproduces the voice of Francis-Ilian: “First of the Five, I speak on behalf of both branches of the Holy Church” — with a call for the consent of the branches of the Church and for the power of the Objects not to turn to evil.
- "Amicably" (August 1776). Hector’s life “turned into pure honey”: his wife Catherine Catherine gave the baby to the nurse, and the Lady in Darkness, whom he feared, died. Hector's sister, Infanta Demeter Nellis-Lyon, the last inhabitant of the Maiden's Palace among the waterfalls, is the legislator of Laem's mod; she invented to send figurines to the grooms instead of portraits, and the three princesses successfully married. Ladies of the Nellis-Lyon family are famous for the beauty of their bodies, but “their faces lack grace”; Jemis says Lilliday is eight hundred years old and Nellis-Lyon is only three hundred. Hector recalls that he refused both the ambassadors of Stepi and Adrian, who asked for troops against Origin: “Origin is my friend!” After the defeat of the Ganta Gnome, Farhan brought to the auction in Laem the Almer girls who had been kidnapped to the Steppe, but the althess market was "dry". Shimmery makes huge profits from the eastern seas, and the Western way is blocked by the Tops of the Dark Kings: hiring a pilot eats up to a quarter of the profit of the flight. Therefore, Mireille Nan-Claire offers Hector a fairway through Tope in exchange for a regiment and a squadron; Darkwater's ambassador Emmon Darklin, on the contrary, asks to be chained. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) As a result, Mireille received a regiment of the Burning Sun and took the throne of Darkwater, promising to be an ally of Shimmery and Origin, and Jemis' engagement to Demeter was terminated.
- "Ad-ri-an!" (Election 1776). Francis-Ilianus claimed the Eternal Hilt, although he admitted before the House that for almost twenty years of his reign Shimmeri had “satisfied” with power. The cardinals of the Proto-Fatherly branch admitted that Bishop Amessin became a priest only through bribery, and in fact Francis-Ilian received more votes; Amessin lost his mantle. He offered a compromise: Minerva became empress, and Erwin became the "shogun", the head of the military defense of the empire; the king-prophet himself preferred the rank of the archpriest of the Protothenic branch, admitting that wearing Hilt was "too tedious a matter". He warned that after the accession of Minerva, which gave the lands the right to refuse its services, the Shimmery kingdom will almost certainly come out of the empire: the southern nobility has dreamed of independence for centuries and is sure that it will heal better without a “crowd of poor”.

## Trivia
- Neymir served as a mercenary in Orcad for three years and pretended to be "the glorious Ney Lukkum, the citizen of Orcad", so that even Prince Hector almost believed him.
- According to the Guide, the Aboriginal matriarchy and many half-breed children made love to enjoy the kingdom's popular trait, but women in Shimmery are denied the right to participate in politics. Local girls willingly gave themselves to the colonists, believing that especially healthy children were born from foreigners, and there were few colonists, but there were many girls.
- Royal wine, served for tasting in the "A Doll on the Throne", was made by the Marquis of Eileen-Fry: for his sake he acquired land on dizzying heights above the entrance to the Lion's Gate Valley.
- The schooner Sea Arrow came down from the slipway of the third Orcadian shipyard; the king commissioned her as a gift to his beloved althesse, and she then became a courier ship of the Shimmery Court under the command of the nobleman Cadiz-Rimar.
- According to the Shimmerian belief, twins have one soul for two. Francis-Ilianus once “abolished” the winter in Shimmery by decree.
- Count Lyon, the first to explore the southern lands of Shimmery, became a legend; among Mira's books were The Glorious Adventures of Sir Lyon in Shimmery.
