**The river** is a great river in the Beyond, which does not even have a name. The Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] explained to his son that this is "the only river in a certain part of the Beyond", and legends call it simply the River. It flows from south to north behind the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]], the Soft Fields and the Shadow Forest, about three hundred miles from the mountains, and flows into the Ice Sea. ([[more-ldov|Sea of Ice]]) Further east, "east of the River", lies an unexplored part of the world. Even the ancient nomads who roamed the Beyond have long since left for Polaris, and since then, as [[mark-voron|Mark]] thought, “there has been no foot in the river.”

## Description
[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] saw the River in late May 1774. It was a full-flowing black stream more than a quarter of a mile wide. The fallen branches sailed northward at the speed of a marching infantryman. The east bank of the River gnawed into a three-hundred-foot steeple dug by the burrows. Downstream the width reaches six hundred yards, the current is twisted by whirlpools. Fifty miles upstream, the River remains wide, followed by the shifting Soft Fields. From the east, a tributary flows into the River, on which the fort of the "red shirts" stood. In the middle of the channel is a large island, through which the exploit was transported on rafts. Later, at the mouth, the water seemed to the northerners “oily” and “disgusting.” The east bank is downstream hilly and cut by ravines and mills.

Father instructed Erwin to find a place on the River for a spark dam. Mechanic Luis Maria also considered his task not only a rail road, but also a place for the "spark shop on the River". ([[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]]) Erwin realized at first sight that the dam was impossible: it would need a bypass channel and granite from the Crystal Mountains, three hundred miles away.

## Traditions
Animal [[spot|Spot]] told terrible tales about the District. According to them, the sky shines behind the River at night and a knock is heard, trees walk, and animals change nature. There are supposedly ogres - buckets with human heads, trampling and devouring strangers, and biting - people with wolf heads and red eyes. From there, they said, no one comes back. According to the belief of the northern hunters, somewhere beyond the River lies the Bottomless Lake with the underwater "Bubble-city", from where they came and where they returned to. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]])

## Beyond the River
- **The Lodge of the Eighteenth Gift.** In June 1774, the exploit crossed the River and two days later found a huge failure - the bed of the unaccounted Gift of the Gods, already devastated by unknown. Erwin wanted to declare the "Blessed District" the land of the Origins. That night, the group was cut (see Explorada for the River). ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) Erwin later reasoned that the enemies were watching the River: whoever came from the west would come through it. The northerners became a camp on the west bank, felling trees for rafts and burning fires, and the enemy watchmen noticed them before crossing.
- **Fort.** On the tributary of the River stood a log fort with windmills and towers. His red-shirted garrison tested [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] on the captives, and [[paul|Paul]] commanded them.
- **The Cape of the Dead.** On the cape at the bend, below the crossing, lay two hundred or three hundred bodies of people killed by Objects: burned, melted, dried, among them women and children. [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] realized that the prisoners were killed in the fort, and the bodies were thrown into the river, which carried them to the River, and she nailed them to the cape. As the bodies of the murders went “all the last year”. Here Erwin swore that east of the River there would be "not a single living creature" of those who wear scarlet shirts, "not a single log" from the fort and "not a single Object".

## Erwin Crossing
Escaped after the massacre at the Lodge, Erwin and Jemis were separated: the kair took the chase, and the lord had to wait for him at the crossing place, on the west bank. Erwin went out to the River earlier than he expected and decided to swim it right away. As he walked around the edge of the water, he came across two men from the fort. He called himself the recruit Arthur Hood, whom Paul had sent, and on the move invented that the fort had been attacked, Paul had been shot with an arrow in the shoulder, and they were ordered to go up the River to the island and cut off the enemy's way. The deception was revealed: in the fight Erwin was wounded in the shoulder, but killed both, took their spark spears and supplies and sailed through the six-hundred-yard River with a stallion of Rain and a mare.

The journey “has been endless.” In the middle of the course of the horses twisted the whirlpool, the mare pulled out the reins and clogged, faced with the Rain, and Erwin himself was dragged to the depths. He sailed to the west coast:
> “We are saved. The worst is over the river!”

From there Erwin moved south along the shore, fought off wolves at night, found stumps from pine trees cut down by his men for rafts, and saw the island again. A day later, a dog appeared on the island, followed by Jemis. Together they reached the cape with the bodies, and then, through the Forest of Shadows and Soft Fields, returned to Spot.

## Fleming's campaign
Fulfilling an oath to destroy the fort beyond the River, Erwin sent [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] to the Beyond, although he objected that Adrian's men from the fort had already been transferred to Almera. Erwin believed that the hike would be beneficial even if the fort was empty: Jemis was tasked with figuring out how the enemy had found Dar's lodge, and if necessary, setting a trap on his searchers. In the autumn of 1774, Count [[fleming|Fleming]]'s flotilla entered the mouth of the River, losing the ship Albatross in the storm. Two dozen boats climbed the oars upstream. We went at night and camped on the west bank during the day. Past the cape, still covered with a "layer of rotting flesh", Jemis and [[mark-voron|Mark]] unwittingly scouted the fort. They killed a small guard and found in it hundreds of Objects and two insane prisoners - [[gvenda|Gwenda]] and Luis Maria. ([[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]]) The Count refused to leave until all the Items were taken out of the fort, and then the fort disappeared with a quiet cotton along with a hundred Fleming warriors, leaving a round cup-shaped crater into which water from the stream flowed. Gwenda, before begging to leave, called the fort a "trap" and said: "Behind the River - no one." In the evening on the east bank on the dead read a waste. Fleming accepted the disappearance as a sign of Gloria and defected to the emperor.

On the way back, Mark reasoned that it was convenient to get to the fort by the Sea of Ice and the River: "We walked on the Sea of Ice, then on the River and arrived at the fort without much difficulty." If the villain is Adrian, why would he build a fort off the north coast? Such an arrangement cast suspicion on the northerners, but could also be the cunning of the enemy.

## Origin of Fort
The mystery was only clarified in 1775, from a story by [[navi|Navi]] Minerva. [[paul|Paul]], a prisoner of Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], expanded the search for the Light Sphere beyond the edge of the continent and found in the Beyond "gift for the River" - a bunch of Objects that no one knew about. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) Vittor feared that the Dynasty and other houses would come to take the find away. Then Paul promised: “I will get the Rings beyond the River, and you will buy more from the Church.” So Paul conceived the idea of making “a company of shooters armed with Objects.”
