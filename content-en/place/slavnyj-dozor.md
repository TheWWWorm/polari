**Glorious Watch** is the fortress of the Crown, covering the entrance to the Julian Bridge from the Steppe side. This is the westernmost outpost of the empire. From here, in 1775, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] moved to Rei-Roy, and here the horde of [[paul|Paul]] destroyed the corps of the Snow Count Lilliday. The fortress appears in "[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]" and "Shadow of the Great Tree". ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

## Appointment
When Lady Juliana threw the Julian bridge over Holliwell, the path through the Steppe was trampled from it. ([[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]; [[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) The first led to Rei-Roy, the capital of [[rejs|Reis]]. Then the Shavans of Holliwell County paved the way to their Corden, and the barons of Mills to the ancient city of Fairis. ([[melnitsy|The Mills]]) The Halls did not like the Rays, and both could not stand the descendants of the Sword-bearers. At the entrance to the bridge, quarrels broke out, which often ended in blood. Therefore, Juliana ordered to build a glorious Watch. The fortress was built not to protect Almera from the Westerners, but so that the Western peoples did not quarrel with each other. The garrison made sure that the Shavans on the approach to the bridge were “peacefully and culturally built in line.”

In the observation area of the garrison lay close to the bridge eight miles of steppe. At the ninth mile, the roads diverged: south to Rei-Roy, north to Corden, and west to the very Mother Mills.

## Trident
At the fork, nine miles from the Watch, stands the town of Trident. On top, it looks like a chicken’s foot: three long claws look at Rei-Roy, Faerys and Corden, a short back one looks at Glorious Watch. The town is wooden and has burned four times since Juliana. It is inhabited equally by rei, halli and descendants of swordsmen. The sheriff here is chosen by male votes with the consent of the Commandant of the Glorious Watch. Residents live by trade, gambling, kabaks and brothels. The trident is accustomed to safety "in the shadow of the Glorious Watch".

## Gathering of the Origin troops
At the end of June 1775, Erwin, with four hundred Xes, passed through the Julian Bridge. The soldiers sang, "We're going west!" In the Glorious Watch, he was awaited by the Snow Count Lilliday Corps of three full battalions. Count in a few days threw it across Almera: half of the troops were traveling by train, half were riding, and at the hub stations they changed places. The X-cloaks celebrated the Snow Count in one voice. The fort buzzed, overflowing with kairs, officers and horses.

The commandant of the fortress handed Erwin a letter from [[minerva|Minerva]] with an order to give the Origins full cooperation. There was no word from [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]], who drove here four days ago. Shavani, according to the commandant, behaved "wonderfully calm." Erwin sent Minerva her last letter from here before going to Rei-Roy.

Here the Snow Count jokingly married his son, [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]], a certain noble bride. Here [[gvenda|Gwenda]] broke up with Jemis. She told Erwin that she would go to Waymar to save [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] for the title, and Jemisu that she was going to the archive of the First Winter to look for relatives.

Erwin sailed down the river with the Xes, and Lilliday's hull went to Rei-Roy by land. Erwin ordered a retreat to the Glorious Watch when faced with overwhelming force.

## Fall of the Glorious Watch
Intelligence officer Alisher noticed three battalions under the coat of arms of the Snow Counts twenty miles from the horde of Paul. They went to the glorious Watch. Ganta Kort advised to catch up with the northerners in the field until they took refuge in the fortress, but Paul said, "No." [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] advised to pass the Watch to the north. She was sure that Lilliday with three thousand soldiers would “throw down the skin” from the Gaunt Pus.

In the morning, smoke rose over the fortress. There was no gate: the crack cut the gate tower from the ground to the roof. The Shavans were thrown from the walls into the ditch of the body of the northerners. The Snow Count is dead. Hanid Perstom cut off his right hand, and the count stabbed himself with daga, so as not to be captured. Paul ordered him to be taken alive and for disobeying he broke both hands of the shavana by Perst.

Almost a hundred prisoners - kire, grey, spark guards - Paul let go to run to Holliwell. None of them moved. The prisoners shouted “Glory to Agatha! Glory to the first winter! Glory to the Origin!", until [[chara|Chara]]'s students opened fire on the score of twenty. Alanis screamed along with them. Three prisoners, in whose eyes Paul and Fly saw fear, were left for interrogation. Alanis later recalled her "rebellion in the Glorious Watch".

After the victory, Paul declared [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] an ally and divided the horde between campaigns to the North and to [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. In the Watch, he left an ambush of two fingernails in case Erwin came back. In the battle with Lilliday fell a half dozen Khanida Vir Kanna. Alisher received the Feather in the Glorious Watch after the death of one of the personesses.

## Aftermath
Later it was said that the turning point of the war was the Glorious Watch. Of the three battalions of the Snow Count, one was killed, two retreated to Beck and stuck there with Horton's corps. Erwin and a handful of X's were cut off in the Steppe. Survivors of the Glorious Watch counted more than three dozen of [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] among the Shavans. [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] in Faunterra drew a straight line on the map from the Glorious Watch to the capital and warned that the horde would go the way of Erwin in the opposite direction.

After the victory at Roydan, Erwin's army returned to the Glorious Watch in August. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] offered to send intelligence to the fort itself, but [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]] recalled that the area around is open. On [[shram|Scar]]'s advice, the three scouts were openly sent to Trident. There they were told about the death of the Snow Count. The news was especially difficult for the Leed Wolves, who came from his fiefdom. Jemis sat down on the ground and cried, hugging the dog. In Trident Erwin was waiting for [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]. She reasoned that if Erwin was alive, he would return to the Glorious Watch, but would first send intelligence to the town.

## Trivia

The stragglers robbed the town, stole a dozen women, and killed five men. The inhabitants began to blame each other: the halls were reevs, the rei were swordsmen.
