The bottomless chasm, or bottomless chasm, is a huge chasm in the Lion Mountains in the south, in the kingdom of [[shimmeri|Shimmery]]. As a "creation of the gods" it does not belong to anyone, but the surrounding lands, including the rock with the monastery, belong to the Counts of [[kuindar|Quindar]], one of the families of the Shimmerian Council of Five. According to the Polari Lands Guide, this is one of the two wonders of Shimmery's nature. The second is the Lion’s Gate.

## Description
The gap blackens with a hole in the face of the earth. Its slopes can be traced down a quarter, half a mile, and then they are lost in white fog. Below the mist, the chasm goes to such a depth that the bottom, "if it was at all", is hidden by dusk. On top of the black abyss, like ice floes in the northern sea, clouds float. The Guide adds that the depth of the abyss definitely exceeds half a mile, the bottom is not visible from above due to constant fog, and there is no safe path down.

The Marquess of Miral-Senelius described it as a valley so deep that no rock can be seen from any of the surrounding rocks. Abandoned stone does not produce the sound of a blow, and a rope of sufficient length is torn under its own weight. From three sides to the bowl are paths from the passes, but each breaks into a thick fog. Foltic admiral Fanno Grok said that in the XII century on the Polarian account, no one could go down into it “and not get mad.” Asked by Francis-Ilian if this was the first crack in the mainland's forehead, the admiral replied that the gods had done the Fail. When Polaris cracks, the Fail will be filled with water, and it will be seen that it is not bottomless: all the garbage thrown there by the Shimmerians will surface. Nearby, in a bowl between five rocks, lies the city of Pentago, surrounded by five white walls with five towers.

## Beliefs and Traditions
Scientists believe that mountains grow from the bottom up when the gods collide two parts of the earth’s firmament. The bottomless chasm, according to the Marquis, arose differently, from top to bottom: a gigantic force struck the ground with a hammer and broke the hole. Some theologians believe that it leads directly to the lower turn of the [[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]], to the kingdom of the gods. Others believe that the abyss is the bed of the First Gift, the father of all Gifts, whose treasures surpass all other Gifts combined. This belief is not propagated by theologians, fearing that treasure hunters will multiply the number of victims.

The "Guide" also mentions the opinion that the Gap is a bed of a particularly great Gift. In the XIV-XV centuries, many attempts were made to go down to the bottom for Objects, and none succeeded. The Church concluded that this Gift is forbidden and the gods do not want people to touch it, “at least not at this time.” Since then, the descents are prohibited, and tourists are only allowed to look at the Gap from above. According to Miral-Senelia, over the centuries, at least a thousand people have tried to come down on ropes, wedges and hooks. Many fell within hearing, and their death cry reached the monks of the monastery, others disappeared without a trace.

Pilgrims go to the abyss. Among the exquisite Shimmer wines, which were treated to Mira, was a drink made of berries, as if they had fallen off the slopes into a bottomless failure, but lingered on the edge, for “the gods themselves were eager to taste” them.

## The Abode-of-the-Abyss
On the cliff above the abyss stands the Tower-Zub. This is not a castle, but the central structure of the monastery of the Maximian Order, known as the Abode-u-Bezna or the Abode of Maximian-u-Bezna. Here is one of the best libraries of sacred texts in the South. Counts of Kindar have long patronized the monastery. Count Horey Quindar, the Second of the Five and a merchant of spark eyes, visited the monastery in the fourth year of his reign, studied in the seminary and took the rank, and after the death of the abbot was elected abbot. The monastery became a pillar of the secret order of Lady in Darkness, in which Kindar was composed. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) Adrian called her monks “subjects” of the Second of the Five, “and therefore a secret order.” Monks of the monastery knew how to wave truncheons and shoot bows on occasion, and a regiment of Golden Swords, subordinate to the count, stood nearby. According to the Marquis of Miral-Senelia, his lord pours one cup into the Fail before each tea party as a gift to the gods.

Eight years in the monastery lived the abdicated King Shimmery Francis-Ilianus. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) Nine years earlier, on the eve of the Days of Abundance, the Second of the Five invited him to visit, told him about the order and offered to join it. The king slept in the guest room, and the same night the goddess of dreams showed the king the first prophetic dream: the sublunar world bends through the funnel, and everything - people, cities, plants and birds - falls into the Boneless Failure. Only the smooth surface of the [[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]] remains, but it also goes in cracks. Shocked, the king stayed in the monastery and studied the works of the Foremothers and Forefathers for eight years. About once a year he had new dreams. In the spring of 1775 he left the Maximian Tower with a retinue of seven people. In Darkwater, upon hearing this story, one of the Order’s supporters shouted: “Bottomless Failure! The wise man is right: the world is falling into the abyss!” Later, the prophet asked Erwin Origin what he would do if the world were to collapse into a bottomless failure.

## Events at the Gap
### Flight of Harmon (May 1775)
Marquess Miral-Senelius on behalf of Kindar invited [[kharmon|Harmon]], the owner of the first celestial ship, to descend on the ball to the bottom of the abyss. The purchase of the ship from the very beginning was conceived for the sake of this descent. After two weeks of flight, the ball of Harmon and the inventor Hortense approached the abyss. Harmon decided to fly over it and not come down. Then the servant of the Marquis Booth snatched the dagger and demanded: “Come down.” Nisa stabbed him and slit his thigh, but Booth grabbed her and put a blade to her throat. Then Harmon took out the Light Sphere and put it overboard the basket: let Booth drop the knife and take the Item instead of descending. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]])

The sphere is alive. Her outer ring flashed with a bluish light, and in the inner with each flash appeared a deeper view. At the bottom was a black, “mirroredly smooth from the windless” lake. Above its water, multicolored dots appeared, white, yellow, red and blue, connected by thin light lines, as if the Subject drew a map. While the satellites were looking at the Sphere, Harmon looked away, snatched Nisa out of Booth's hands, and beat him with the Sphere. The ball flew across the chasm without ever coming down. Later, Harmon noticed that the Sphere gave a sign when it smelled the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]'s servant, and decided that it had sensed Adrian over the Fail.

### Capture of the monastery (28–29 May 1775)
Even earlier, a prisoner of the sorcerer called "Tower-Tooth, Boundless Failure, Second of the Five" a place where you should look for the Lord of the Rings. ([[mik|Mik]]) The mysterious "Hunter" intercepted Harmon and flew on his ball to the monastery, calling himself Booth. He tried to kidnap Kindar, but he escaped. That same night, the Hunter's Shavans, dressed as monks, entered the monastery and took it. [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]] and Baron Darryl recognized in the Hunter the emperor [[adrian|Adrian]], who was considered dead. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]])

### The Emperor of Failure
For several days Adrian interrogated the Second of the Five and the monks, "looked into the bottomless Fail" and read the sacred texts. He learned something about the Fail, the secret order, the talking Objects, the Star and the gardeners. He later said to the joker Manson, "Would you know, my friend, what I discovered at Bottomless Failure!" According to him, it was there that he “lighted up with the understanding of the new goal” — “the dreams that many generations carried in their hearts.” Then the army of Adrian and Magda marched from the precipice to Surlion.

Meanwhile, in Faunterra, [[minerva|Minerva]] herself figured out where to look for the missing emperor. The first of the Five was away, so it was possible to negotiate with the Second, and it is easiest to find him in the Abode-of-the-Abyss. In addition, in Pentago, near Proval, there are huge warehouses of eyes, and the small town is not teeming with agents of the Great Houses. She concluded that Adrian most likely visited the monastery and left with Magda's army.

At the court of Adrian there was a rumor that the emperor was going to build a heavenly ship, go down to the bottom and pick up a lot of talking Objects from there. This rumor deliberately dissolved Harmon, and Kindar called such plans “complete nonsense.” The hired Colonel Horey asked the emperor if the gods would anger the rise of the Objects from the Failure.

## The Mystery of the Bottom
In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], the mystery is revealed. Lady [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille]] said that the First Tree, by which the Forebears transformed the nature of Polaris, had three roots. For each root, a huge fresh water reservoir was required. One of them was planted in the Bottomless abyss, the other in the western part of the [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]] off the coast of Holliwell, and the third under the valley of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]].

[[navi|Navi]] added the main thing. Eighteen centuries ago, a colonial starship disappeared: two hundred crew, six thousand colonists, terraforming reactors and “embryos of the biosphere”, a whole “flying city”. “All that remains of him now lies at the bottom of the bottomless Fail.” The surviving crew became the [[pramateri|Foremothers]] and Forefathers, and the colonists became commoners. For the roots of the Tree, three deep reservoirs were needed in the tops of the triangle, but there were only two: the Smoky Distance and the Bottomless Abyss. The third reactor Forebears carried on foot on the shoulders of robots and laid in a cavity under the valley of the First Winter, the entrance to which is now called the grotto of Kosuli. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]])
