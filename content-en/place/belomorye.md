## Description
Belomorye is a port on the north coast of the Duchy of Origin, off the [[more-ldov|Sea of Ice]]. According to the Polari Lands Guide, it is one of the four largest cities in Origin along with the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], [[lid|Leed]] and Main. The northern seamen recognize it from afar by the coastal hills: on the one hand they are gentle and covered with pines, on the other - steep and absorbed by the wind. Here is one of the twenty-four monasteries of Ulyana Sad in the Empire.

### Sea Routes
By sea from here you can get to the mouth of [[reka|the River]] beyond the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]; the road takes about a month. Another one and a half to two weeks go from the Belomorye to the mouth of [[torrej|Torrey]], on which they rise to [[uejmar|Waymar]]. To the Bear Fang in Northwood - a few days of swimming. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) The Belomorye ships go much further: the schooner with the mercenaries, on which [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] met [[berk|Burke]], went from the Belomorye to the Flight, and the bossman Biven from the Seal boasted that he had left here to sail around the entire Polaris. ([[rejs|Reis (County)]]) [[navi|Nathaniel]] himself on board the "White Panther" managed to make a course from the Belomorye to Fais.

### Bishop
Bishop Belomorya was famous as one of the wisest people of the duchy and a wonderful player in strata: once he defeated the Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] three times in a row. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) According to Erwin, the Duke asked to train his son, and Erwin received lessons in this game. ([[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]) In the same line, Erwin called the bishop Uncle Kair Martin, and the teacher - "your father", that is, the father of this kair.

### The Road and the Navy
The Belomorye Road leads from the First Winter to the Belomorye; a couple of miles from the First Winter it passes through the gorge between the flat-topped cliffs. Minerva in 1775 planned to build a rail track here. The Earls of Fleming hold most of the northern fleet. Half of it consists of ships of free captains, such as [[dzheff-bamber|Jeff Bamber]], who pay the Count a tax on each flight; Jeff Bamber, according to Mark, dreamed of putting profits in his pocket, rather than giving half to the count.

## Flemings
The city is the ancestral domain of the Earls of Fleming, who once ruled the entire coast of the Ice Sea. According to family legend, they revolted three times against the Origins, defeated them twice in great battles and once took the First Winter by storm; Benedict's rebellion became the fourth. In the cycle, the count was [[fleming|Benedict Fleming]], a devout fanatic. His family is the wife of Lady Agnia, eldest daughter of Molly (married to Lord Velentyne of the Gran Islands), youngest daughter of Violet and son of Osmund.

## History in the Series
- **"Arrow, Coin, Spark".** For the expedition Erwin, waiting for the ship in the fishing village of [[spot|Spot]], came a round-sided schooner from the Belomorye under the flag of the Origins with a clawed bat.
- **"Only One Star" and "Speaking to God".** Returning from the Beyond, Fleming saw in the disappearance of the fort the sign of the gods, declared Adrian the viceroy of the gods and renounced Origin. His squadron left for the Belomorye. The Count planned to take the almost defenseless First Winter with a sudden blow, but the conspiracy failed: one ship fought off the squadron, and his team warned the Duke's soldiers. Sailors who had escaped with Kair [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] bypassed the Belomorye side, considering his master a traitor; Fleming's three ships still tracked them off the coast.
- **"A Doll on the Throne".** Among the convicts that Minerva dealt with was Boyd Mart Boyd of the Belomorye - a deserter, a pirate and a hired killer. Erwin asked to be pardoned with ostentatious astonishment as “the pride of the northern region.”
- **"Yanmei Hunt".** After the victory of Erwin Fleming with a thousand swords dug into the Belomorye and waited for the reckoning; from Faunterra to the Belomorye, the army had to go for two months. Wittor Shayland offered the Count mediation in exchange for the right to trade duty-free through the Belomorye port and open bank offices on the coast of the Sea of Ice. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) Iona, in negotiations with Abbot Hosch, demanded that taxes be transferred to the Origins from the free captains of the northern fleet. She said that when Erwin took the Belomorye, Fleming would be put on trial.
- **“Humans and Gods.”** Fleming's Belomorye battalion came from the Ice Sea to [[torrej|Torrey]], and the ships dragged up the river to Waymar. At the same time, it was said that Fleming could support the rebellious barons of neighboring Northwood.
- **"The Shadow of the Great Tree".** In the river port of Waymar, Fleming's landed warriors came across twice the number of kairs of the First Winter, and the Kairs of the Belomorye and the First Winter came together in the fiercest battle Joaquin had seen. In 1775, the Belomorye, like Leed, moved to the side of Richard Origin and the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]]; so two of the four largest cities of the duchy changed flags. The city was under siege: armed men were allowed only with a special password, and only about two hundred soldiers remained in the city. One battalion was taken away by the count himself, the second, Ice, by his son Osmund. Erwin captured Fleming's son-in-law, Lord Velentyne, and with Molly's help lured Lady Agnia with Violetta from the Belomorye. From the city to the Green Bay, they were accompanied by castellans and a dozen kairs, and there Erwin himself took them hostage. So he wanted to make Fleming not interfere in the battle. The Count nevertheless ambushed the Belomorye Road and was defeated and captured on the day of the Battle of the First Winter. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Some of his kair warriors Origin drove away towards the Belomorye. Kayras in two-color coats, which then came out of the Belomorye road to the castle, only pretended to be White Sears: they were Erwin's disguised X's that captured Richard. After the battle, the remnants of Fleming's keel left for the Belomorye.
- **"Fantasies".** In 1776, an Origen garrison with a captain stood in the Belomorye. The Shavan ambassadors, led by Neymir, circled Polaris by the sea, offered their condolences to the “recently widowed” Lady Fleming, reported to the captain of the garrison, and, under the supervision of the Kairs, made the rest of the way to the First Winter. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) The count himself was frozen alive in the glacier of the First Winter.
