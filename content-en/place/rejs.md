## Geography
The flight is a free county in the West inhabited by [[shavany|Shavans]]. According to the Polari Land Guide, it lies on the west coast of the mainland, south of the median line: in the south it is bordered by [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], in the north by Holliwell and Mills, and in the east by [[litlend|Litland]] via the Luga Shepherds. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]; [[melnitsy|The Mills]]) These are steppes with light hilliness, with a small number of rivers and forests and frequent strong winds; the coastline at the Ocean of the Abyss is very rugged with rocks, wells and bays. The climate is dry, windy, moderately continental.

The flight borders Litland on the Holliwell River. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) Between them lie the Shepherd's Meadows - common pastures that since the time of Julian the Great can use all the neighboring lands. The Reiss rivers are also mentioned in novels: the slow Roydana (Lazy River), on which the capital stands, and the fast Brokkah. Shavans are distinguished within the county of the region: The bottom in the "Shadow of the Great Tree" is said to originate, apparently, "from the Upper Flight, somewhere from the border."

Knight of the Litlands Sir Gage spoke contemptuously of Reis: the county he only called, but in fact it is a piece of land infested with raiders, horse thieves and nomads, who without the "iron hand" would have killed himself.

## Cities
According to the “Travel Guide”, urban culture in Reis is poorly developed: a significant part of people live in small villages or roam with herds, most cities stand on the coast, and in the depths of the mainland there are only four of them.

### Rei-Roy
The largest of the inner cities, Rei-Roy County's capital, according to the Guide, has only 45,000 inhabitants; the novel says that there are many more cows in it than people. The city stands on the slow Roydan River near fast Brockie. The stone castle is surrounded by a sea of tents: here are the Gantt camps with families, servants and slaves. According to legend, the castle was built during the First Horse War by the ganta Royhar, nicknamed the Lazy Wolf, who pastured cows on the banks of the sleepy Roydana; for the sake of construction, he paved two hundred miles of road and arranged a ferry crossing. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]) Above the gate, Royhar ordered a giant statue of a warrior with a bull's head and a battle axe, whose horns rise two hundred feet; his son completed it. Rei-Roy is called the new capital of Reis and the City of the Flaming Bull. It was to him that the first trade route, laid from the Julian bridge, led. According to Groza, Juhan Reis is a descendant of Royhar. In Rei-Roy worked and masters of carpets: a portrait of the mistress of Juliana, presented to her by the gant Mirlai, was "made by a famous master from Rei-Roy."

Around the capital graze herds of hundreds of thousands of heads or more. When the horde of Moran in 1775 was under Rei-Roy, its maintenance cost the young Count Johan a hundred cows daily.

### Paths through the Steppe
From Julian of the bridge to the west there are three trade routes - to Rei-Roy, to Corden in Halliwell and to Fairis in Mills; at their fork stands the wooden town of Trident, inhabited equally by reys, halls and descendants of the Swordsmen. In the summer of 1775, the corps of Lilliday and the horde of Paul passed by him, and the halls were accused of robbing reys, since the horde of "Ganty Pus" consisted mainly of shavanks of Reis.

## Economy
According to the "Guide", the economy of the Reis is 90% based on cattle breeding - cattle and horses. The main export is horses: smaller and weaker than the Litland or Hope, worse suited for battle, but hardy and cheap; every third rider of the Empire rides a horse born in Reis. They also export leather goods and livestock, which are sold to Almera and Hope in herds. The cattle are driven across the Julian bridge, and under the contract of Count Damir with the Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] - and on the [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]. Exotic goods are slaves: the slave trade is traded in all Western lands, but Reis is a leader in this.

## People and Customs
The entire culture of Reis, according to the "Guide", is associated with cattle breeding and nomadic life. People live in communities of no more than a few hundred people, closely related to their herds; each community is ruled by a ganta — the “pointing the way.” The main value is personal freedom: Flight values it above all because it knows how easy it is to lose it. In honor of the skill of the rider, the military edge and accuracy of the bow; the saying goes: "What you can not protect - not yours." Clashes between communities for cattle and slaves are frequent, but not bloody: murder is condemned, because the ability to move is a gift of the gods, and breaking a man's legs is considered almost as grave a sin as killing. Theft is strictly punished, but capture in battle is legal. The land belongs to man only if his house stands on it; pastures and rivers are common, and vassalage is expressed through cattle: "My vassals are those who pasture my flocks."

The most respected class is the Shavans: originally "shepherds", now skillful and fast warriors. Reis warriors do not wear jewelry on clothing; their rank shows patterns on belts and spurs, and, according to the Guide, patterns on the belt reflect combat successes and abundance. The arts are poorly developed, but high skill has reached the making of leather. The Pramatery cult is recognized, but revered primarily by the spirits of nature and the “primordial animals” — the firstborn Ramsun and the first-born Aristad. Evil is everything that takes away mobility, and its master is the Spirit of the Worm; the souls of worthy Shavans after death will join the [[orda-strannikov|horde of Wanderers]]. According to the Guide, the belief in the Pramatery was finally established here only when the descendants of Yangmei built a rail road: the train is the largest movable object in the world. The steppes usually burn the dead, believing that the ashes should fly freely with the wind.

The historical enemies of the Rays are the Holliwell Hillsmen: the Hillmen despise the Rays for their savagery and worship of the horned beast, and the Hillmen's Rays for digging in the ground "like worms."

## Ruling House
According to the “Guide”, the Counts of Reis are considered Reis’s nominal liege lords: they rule Rei-Roy and own the largest herds, numbering more than 7000 horses. In fact, their power depends on personal qualities: the Shavans do not obey the one who is not respected, and are united in one horde only during a great war. For the last two centuries, Reis has been heavily influenced by the Dukes of the Origin: until 1732, he was their vassal and paid tribute, and now pays only a small amount for the maintenance of the two Virginia garrisons on the coast. Lady Jessica, Iona's grandmother, once lived in Reis hostage. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) Once the Westerners took a dozen stolen items to Flight, and the Duke of Desmond's expedition, which returned them, cost the western lands of three burned cities and several thousand dead.

## History
According to the "Guide", the lands of the Reiss are inhabited for a very long time; it is said that in the VIII-IX centuries in his herds there were more heads than in the then Empire - people, and the desert of Hope was allegedly caused by the fact that the herds of shavanks destroyed its soil. Due to internal disturbances, Reis had little involvement in mainland affairs: the 12th-century Scarlet Troubles passed without him. In the twelfth century, half of Reis was absorbed by the Swordbearers’ Empire of the Iron Sun. In the fifteenth century, the Shavans of Reis recaptured the meadows along the Hollywell from Darkwater. Before the First Horse War of the sixteenth century, the Empire regarded the Rei as savages from whom horses and leather jackets could be bought cheaply; Count Darian the Foul first demonstrated Reis’s military might, and the Shavans of Reis and Hollywell overran Almera, Hope, and Litland and brought Faunterra to its knees (the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]).

## In the Novels
### The Siege of Lacey and the Great Horde
In 1774, Count Damir Reiss came to negotiate with the [[dom-litlend|Litlands]] and offered to build a guarded road to the west coast through the Shepherds' Meadows and Reiss; the negotiations ended with him being shot at their table (an ambush in Lacey). ([[zasada-v-lejsi|Ambush at Lacey]]) His closest associate, [[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]], took power. His ambassadors — knights of the chavana, not the count himself, although the emperor demanded to appear in person — accused [[adrian|Adrian]] of having given sacred crossings to the Litlands and "mixed with dirt the ancient freemen of the West", and slaughtered the Earl of Rantigar from the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] at his court, and the horde of Reiss crossed Holliwell and ravaged Litland. ([[graf-rantigar|Count Rantigar]]) Under the banner of Moran stood the Shavans of three counties - Reis, Holliwell and the Sunset Coast, as well as thousands of Mill warriors.

After the defeat of Adrian at Melorange in December 1774 and a new siege in the spring of 1775, Moran swore an oath to [[minerva|Minerva]] and returned to Rei-Roy, where the young Count [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]] ruled with him. In the House, Reis represented [[ganta-groza|Ganta Thunderstorm]], one of the largest suppliers of captives; ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) Erwin noted that the county is represented by a "robber and slave trader". Reis and Halliwell's representatives supported Maurice Labelin's displeasure with the bidding for the Shimmer eyes, and then condemned his daughter's diversion. While the horde was at Rei-Roy's, Ganta Cort offered to raise her on a new march, best of all on the weakened Shimmerians. Meanwhile, in the army of Adrian in Shimmery there were rumors that the lord would move the capital to Rei-Roy - a city, which, according to the Second of the Five, allegedly also founded the Foremothers. Upon learning of Paul, [[minerva|Minerva]] ordered Erwin to go to Rei-Roy as soon as possible and negotiate with Steppe Fire before sunset General Oris got there; Erwin moved his ships down the Smokey Daly and down the Halliwell.

### Paul in Rei-Roy
In the summer of 1775, [[paul|Paul]] came to Rei-Roy. In the square in front of the castle, he turned Moran into an immovable figure and led the horde; Chara took over Perst back in Rei-Roy, "literally in a day." Leaving the army, Paul left the city of Ondea with five hundred horsemen and three men. At the end of June, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] arrived at Rei-Roy with kire companies. At the gate, locked for the first time in years, he found the frozen bodies of Moran and [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]. A hundred horsemen with a finger-bearer broke out of the gate, the second finger-bearer beat fireballs from the wall; the sortie cost the northerners many lives, including the lives of the gauntlet of Thunderstorm, and Erwin's ships on Holliwell burned. Erwin's force withdrew from the city, and the Gant Ondei, who had burned the northern ships, moved after him. According to the old, but accurate maps of Reis, Erwin went to Roydan, occupied the village of Berch and set up a trap on the river: in the battle of Roydan, the northerners defeated Ondei's detachment and took away Perst, although they lost almost a whole company. Before the prisoners, Erwin repeated that he came to Rei-Roy in peace, and his message to the Shavans of Rei-Roy had an effect: after that, no Shavan dared to attack the detachment or even follow it. Having lost more than half of the fighters, the northerners returned to the [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]], learned about its fall and went west to [[melnitsy|the Mills]].

### Ord Juhan Reis
Paul made the young Johan Reis one of the two leaders of the horde and through the Subject spoke to him; the Gants were to carry out the orders of the young Reis. After the split of the horde at Fliss in August 1775, Johan led the southern part of the army together with the ganta Court - fifteen thousand horsemen with a dozen Rings; they plundered a dozen towns of Almera and Crown Lands and approached Faunterra from the south. Minerva deliberately leaked, as if carrying the treasury by train to Arden; Johan, with the avant-garde and the best riders, rushed there, the horde split, and at Patrick's Chapel he was defeated by Adrian (according to Paul, Adrian was helped by Minerva and Nathaniel). Juhan was imprisoned and beaten, but in chains he called Vladyka a "tyrant, usurper, jackal without honor"; on the orders of Adrian, he contacted Paul through the Voice of God. In his veins flowed primrose, and Adrian with the help of crybola stings and swamp drugs forced the intoxicated Johan to use the Objects - Bird without Flesh, Worm and Deconstructor. ([[pervokrov|Firstblood]]) On December 3, 1775, in Main, at the court of Adrian, watching through the Bird without Flesh for the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], Johan died suddenly when Adrian ordered him to destroy the Hand of the Znaharka: [[menson|Manson]] poisoned him with the "Black Mary pill", a poison of clockwork action. The command of the surviving Shavans passed even earlier to Court, who took them to Almera.

### After the war
The horde, retreating from the capital, robbed Almera for a month, after which the ganta Court made an alliance with Henry Farway, sealed by the marriage of the daughter of the ganta with Farway's grandson; The "two impostors" swore to support each other, and Court declared himself chief of Reis County and Farway the ruler of Almera.
