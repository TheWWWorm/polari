## Description

Fliss is a port city on the northern edge of Almera, on the shore of the lake-sea Smokey Dahl. ([[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]]; [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) According to the Polari Lands Guide, it is one of the three largest cities in the Duchy along with [[aleridan|Aleridan]] and [[blekmor|Blackmore]]. In the books, it is called "the main lake gate of Almera" and "the largest port on the Smoky Distance". Every day there was a fair, and merchant ships went from here to the north and to the Southern Way, for example to Stonekip. From Fliss through the Smokey Dahl, you could swim to [[uejmar|Waymar]] in a week.

According to the merchant from Arrow, Coin, Spark, the north of Almera belonged to the Earl of [[erroubek|Arrowback]], and "further north" lay the lean possession of Baron Fliss. When [[minerva|Mira]] passed through the city in December 1774, she was told that Fliss was "the capital of a prosperous barony" and merchants lived in it, like everywhere along the banks of the Smoky Distance. She saw the Bishop's Palace with a rod and spiral coat of arms and the Cathedral of Helena the Traveler with a mosaic lady with a lantern. [[otets-david|Father David]] obeyed Bishop Fliss and therefore refused to serve [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]. In Fliesse, on the other hand, was the headquarters of the Williamite Brotherhood, the most militant of the monastic orders. His battalions served at [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s.

Housing inside the walls was expensive, “as if in the capital”, and the shops jealously guarded their monopoly: a stranger, secretly baking a cake without the permission of the bakery guild, was immediately expelled from the city. However, the power of the magistrate ended at the city walls. Outside, the land belonged to a local baron, who allowed anyone to settle for a elen. Therefore, around Fliss grew towns of artisans, divided into workshops: Skobyanoe, Painters, Sewing. A separate place was the Break, where scribes, barbers and hatters lived. Burgomaster Fliessa in the Almer tradition wore a yellow-white scarf around his neck, a symbol of his power. [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] considered Fliss to be the third favorite city after Aleridan and the capital, "the pearl of northern Almera". As a child, she spent hours in craft towns.

## Before the war

In northern Almere, halfway from Aleridan to Fliss, stood the monastery of the Forefathers-hermits of Marek and Simeon. There was once a road to Fliss through his village Road Pillar. Later, Count Arrowback for six thousand hilt paved a new, Count's road from Fliss to Aleridan bypassing the monastery.

In "Only One Star", Alanis, after escaping from the Earl of Blackmore, reached Fliss, sold a horse at a fair, and at the port joined a group of poor pilgrims who were taken aboard to the South Road. In early December 1774, the ship brought Myra and her saviors to Fliss from Waymar. Mira fell ill with a fever and spent several days in a hotel on the third floor. Here, according to the text, for the first time she felt a “new faith”, faith in happiness, and then went in carriages to Aleridan.

## "A Doll on the Throne"

In the winter of 1774/75, refugees from the north fled to the south through Fliss. Inge Price said he saw hundreds of them there. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) His van was from the port of Fliss by the Count's Road into Almera. The investigation of the abduction of items of the dynasty showed that from the inn “[[dzhek-bakler|Jack Buckler]]” for the day you can get to the “port Fliss”, and from there for a week to swim the Smokey Dahl. [[mark-voron|Mark]], [[ded|Grandfather]] and [[vnuchok|Grandson]] called Fliss their goal to sail from there to the North.

## Execution of altessa burgomaster

In "I Want a Pony," Gallard explained to [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]] what the "witch on the pole" is punished for. She was the hostess of the music salon in Fliss and the altess of the burgomaster and persuaded him to go over to Alanis' side and close the arch access to the Smoky Distance. Gallard said that by executing her, he avoided an assault that would have killed thousands. He added that all these executions could have been avoided if he had killed Alanis in time.

## Fliss and Puppeteer

Erwin, reporting to Cordelia's mother about the involvement of the priory in the plot, reported that Ulyanina Dust was taken from the vault of the Wilhelmina Cathedral in Aleridan in 1772 and transferred to the palace of the priory. ([[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]]) From there, she was escorted to Fliss and loaded onto a ship. Through Fliss went the way of [[paul|Paul]] himself in the spring of 1775: before the attack on Evergard, he named his warrior the route "Ink Nightrock - Evergard - Fliss".

## The War of 1775

### Hiking to Fliss

In June 1775, Erwin, fighting in Almer against the prior, accepted [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]]'s offer to go to Fliss, capture part of the fleet there and sail to Waymar. In fact, Erwin hoped that the archpriest himself needed the port of Fliss to communicate with the Puppeteer, and used the hike as a bait. Almerian General [[veksler|Wexler]] was going to "lead the enemy to Fliss" and press him against the walls. After a night battle on the old Fliss road and a forest fire, about three hundred of Wexler's knights fled to Fliss. They gathered the elders and prepared the city for defense.

On June 17, a battalion of northerners approached the locked-up Fliss. He had no siege guns, the frontal assault was hopeless, the bay was covered with bastions with ballistics, and the entire fleet of elders was driven to a protected harbor. [[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]] climbed the “sad tower” built of forests and demanded to open the gate, promising the townspeople a year without a temple tax. The gray burgomaster replied that the townspeople trembled before the will of the gods, but were afraid of the prince's revenge: "More than once and not twice we had bonfires lit by them." Cordelia prayed on the tower for a day, the nuns carried the message to the temples, but the white knights did not allow the gate to be opened.

### Battle of the cliff

Gordon Sue was fortified in the cliff, a town under the walls, protected from the north and east by the creek above the lake. Gallard brought in two incomplete regiments, knightly and monastic, three times superior to the Northerners. When the attack of the knights choked in the streets of the cliff, the parish gave a signal with a staff, and the garrison of Fliss began to shell the town with fire shells from ballistics, dragged from the harbor. The break is on fire. The Northerners did not expect this: Fliss was not attacked from land for many years, and all his ballistas stood on lake bastions. [[shram|Scar]] and [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]], who were here, assured that the guns would not reach the cliff. Then Erwin came up with five hundred kairs, and Gallard was defeated, but managed to lock himself in Fliss. The battle destroyed a good quarter of the Breakout houses. At first, the inhabitants hated that day, but then became proud of them and assured that the Duke of Origin personally chose their town for the battle. Local legends spoke of five or even eight thousand kire at the cliff, although in fact there was one battalion. [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]], Gallard's wife, later called the "Battle of Fliss" one of the most eerie spectacles of her life.

Erwin could not take the city: there was not enough force for the assault, for the siege of time, and all the pigeons of Fliss remained in the hands of the archpriest. Then [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]] on behalf of [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] offered to take over the siege of Fliss, and Erwin was able to leave. Flame promised to take this port for Erwin. The city was besieged: the Farway fleet blockaded it from the lake, and Edgar Flame's corps from the land. Fliss's doves remained in the hands of the priory, and Erwin could not even get news from his father from Waymar.

### Paul's Parish

In July 1775, the horde of [[paul|Paul]] invaded Almera. Back in the Steppe, Paul announced to the Shavans that he was waiting for a "debted" prince in Fliss with two thousand soldiers and a huge fleet: on ships he would sail north, and the rest of the horde would go from Fliss by a direct road to Arrowback and Faunterra. Shavan Hailakh said that "all hope is on Fliss", the largest port of Smoky Distance, where silver "floods like blood from a sheep". Farway's army went south without a fight. Only Mother Cordelia and her assistant remained at the gate, forbidding the horde from entering the city. Gallard went to the gate tower, called Paul a "true friend" and declared Cordelia unworthy of the dignity. After that, Paul Perstami broke both women. Alanis saw it with her own eyes.

Gallard opened the gates to Paul. Wilhelmine abbot on behalf of the townspeople asked to spare Fliss. Paul swore that "the maidens of Fliss can walk naked at night", and temples and guilds collected four carts of silver for him as a reward. Fleece Bay was full of ships: Gallard had brought the entire fleet of the Duchy here and seemed to have hired more ships at Holliwell. On these ships, Paul was about to sail north to Waymar. Navi predicted that Paul would take the fleet of the archpriest and a third of the Shavans, and another leader would lead the horde to the capital. A message of Paul's progress, sent from Wilhelmina Abbey, "waved" from Fliss to Aleridan, Arrowback and Faunterra.

After the defeat of the Shavans, Erwin proposed to return the Almeran fleet stationed at Waymar to Fliss and put it at the disposal of Hope's troops.

## In "Fantasies"

In the Fantasias, which the author calls possible futures, it is said that on a sultry August day in 1775, the great horde divided in Fliesse in two. The northern part, led by Gnu-Ganta (Paul), crossed the Smokey Dahl and went to the First Winter, where it was defeated. The southern part, led by Johan Reis and the Cort Gant, looted a dozen cities in Almera and the Crownlands. ([[ganta-kort|Ganta Kort]]) The war never reached Aleridan: the Shavans moved to Fliss.
