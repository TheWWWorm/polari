The fourth novel of the cycle. The name is taken from the words of Erwin’s father: he calls the hunt for the Puppeteer “Yanmean”, but Erwin himself considers it his. Five storylines converge around one subject - the Light Sphere, which turns out to be the missing part of [[absolyut|the Absolute]]. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) The novel ends with one of the main turns of the cycle: the emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] survived the train crash and all this time acted under the guise of an unnamed "Hunter".

## Structure
Each chapter is titled with the code name of the line and the number, and the date and place are indicated under the title. The “Coin” storyline opens the novel in the autumn of 1774, while the other storylines centre on May 1775; the chapters are not in strict chronological order: “Coin — 5” and “Coin — 6”, for example, return to March and April, while “Witness — 4…6” (18–19 May) follows Erwin’s chapters dated 25–28 May. The chapters of the "Sword" line are written in present tense. Between the chapters are inserted the issues of the official newspaper “[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]” from May 10, 12, 15, 26 and 28, which tells how events are presented to the public. Two lines, the Sword and the North Bird, merge in the general chapter of Sword, North Bird, which describes the night of May 27 in Waymar.

## Plot
### Coin
**Melison (chapters 1–2).** In the autumn of the Northern Flash, an abandoned estate in the wine town of Melison, sheltered in a valley west of the White Tract, bought for four hundred ephemera a certain “famous Horam Pauline Robert”, that is, [[kharmon|Harmon]]. With him came the shavanka [[niza|Niza]], bought in Laem for five ephes, the inventor-hypotherapist [[gortenzij|Hortensius]] and the ruined Laem pimp Onorico-Maisor (Rico), and then - silk, ropes, barrels of acid and other strange loads that deprived the sleep of the burgomaster Cornelia. ([[onoriko|Onoriko-Maysor]]) Harmon bought his celestial ship (a balloon) from Hortense. Travelers found out that at different heights the wind blows in different directions, and with the help of a master alchemist from Laem University decided to fill the ball with hydrogen. In the basement of the estate, Harmon hid the real Light Sphere, wrapped in the cloak of Silent Jack with the coat of arms of the Origins.

In the winter, news came to Melison of the death of Emperor Adrian and of the power of the Northerners in the capital, and then of the defeat of Prince Hector by the Shavans in [[litlend|Litland]] and the siege of [[melorandzh|Melorange]]. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) The ball cost Harmon almost a fifth of his fortune. On March 8, the day of the Spring, the first test was held: the wind tore the tethered ball with the Bottom in the basket, Harmon himself hit the winch with a shovel, and the girl, finding a gas valve, landed safely at the end of the valley. On the eve of the Spring Dawn, the Marquis of Miral-Senelius, a vassal of the Second of the Five, appeared to Harmon. ([[kuindar|Quindar]]) He offered a thousand ephesas for the ship, without a dispute agreed to two thousand, but demanded a monopoly: all the drawings and the oath never to build balls for anyone else.

**The Merchant Hunter (Chapters 3–4, 6)** Meanwhile, [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] arrived in Laam. He arrived on the corvette "A Dog on a Barrel", lent by Baron Greyhound, three days before the squadron of [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]], which all the voyage flattered, and brought with him four thugs recruited aboard the galleon "Great": Seven, Eight, Nine and Dave: Buckley told them to forget their former names, but Dave refused and remained Dave. On the trail of the sold shavanka, he went to Exil's tea house, then to Rico and his wife, the white-blooded Vanessa Lilith. Deputy Halintor warned Buckley that Magda's patronage in Shimmery was not worth much. Magda's plans also fell apart: she hoped to buy up Shimmerian spark eyes, but four of the Five had already sold them to the Farway and [[ervin-oridzhin|Origin]]. ([[dom-farvej|House Farway]]) Humiliated, she told Buckley to hire a killer for Erwin. Buckley found Rodrigo, a broad-brimmed man, and he calculated that Rico would send a gift to his wife on Spring Dawn. The thugs broke into Vanessa's house, killed the servants, and waited for Rico.

Harmon, meanwhile, asked Rico for a meeting with the Second of the Five: he wanted not to sell the ball, but to become the chief shipbuilder in the service of the count. Burgomaster told him the story of Khorey Kindar: the third child in the family, he learned the cutting of spark eyes, inherited the title, and somewhere near Shutov plot entered the seminary and received the rank, without departing from power and eye trading. ([[kuindar|Quindar]]) Later, the monks of the Abode-u-Abyss chose him as their abbot. Harmon hid the ball, and Hortense and his assistant sent to his family in the County of Iserin.

**The Betrayal of the Bottom and the Death of Rico (Chapter 5)** Left alone with Harmon, Nisa told the steppe legend about the brothers Getta and Hanosh and the magical calf Ollay. ([[legenda-o-gette|The Legend of Gett]]) The harsh Gett took all the power from the calf and died as a conqueror, and the good Hanosh returned Ollay to his mother. Harmona Nisa compared it to Ollay: he is rich in gifts, but does not know how to use them. Touched, he showed her the Sphere and admitted to killing for her — two, then four, “maybe five.” Niza replied that he was "more Gett than Ollay", and left the estate. Before dawn, a white carriage arrived: Buckley and Rodrigo brought the related Rico, Vanessa and their children. While testing a mercenary, Buckley bargained for the price of each life, and Rodrigo cut Rico's throat for fifteen ephemera. Harmon was told to pay one and a half thousand Rodrigo, and give the rest to Buckley.

**Basement and Galleon (Chapter 6).** In early April, Buckley kept Harmon, Vanessa, and the children in a basement under Laem. Harmon transferred all the money from the banks to him. Buckley himself was afraid and not looking. His new plan was two million ephes of gold, hidden in barrels of water on the galleon “Pride of Grace”. Rodrigo recruited seventy pirates on the old ship "Liana". When the children in the basement were already dying without water, Harmon suffered a fracture: he admitted to being a greedy coward and vowed to live differently. At the same moment, the hatch opened Nisa, who followed him, “like Hanosh behind Allay.” Night boarding with acid ballistics failed: the knights in patches came out of the stern cabin, and Rodrigo, who had told Magde everything in advance, jumped overboard. Harmon paid him a thousand and a half for three services: his life and the life of a girl, freedom and punishment of the Buckley gang. Magda sent Buckley, chained to the Dog on a Barrel mast, to his father in [[faunterra|Faunterra]].

**Treaty with the Second of the Five (Chapters 7–8).** With no money, Harmon and Niza walk back to Melison. He gave her his real name, confessed to his father Eliza and decided to give the sphere to the righteous [[otets-david|father David]] in Izluchina. ([[izluchina|Riverbend]]) The mayor was waiting for him Marquis Miral-Senelius with a new proposal: protection from the Labelins, the salary of an adviser on celestial shipbuilding, life in the Lion Mountains - but with the main condition. Harmon had to go down on a ball to the bottom of the [[bezdonnaya-propast|bottomless abyss]]: the valley, the bottom of which no one saw, and theologians considered it a hole in the lower spiral, the bed of Pervodar. In May, Hortensius built a new double ball: an upper hydrogen tank and a lower one heated by an oil burner. It was called the "Heavenly Sphere" (later - "Double Sphere"). Harmon was just going to draw the descent. He had the eye of Marquess Booth, a blond archer with pale eyes and a habit of spinning a coin, the same man who once brought Harmon a choice of black and white rounders. Over the chasm, Booth drove them down with a knife. The bottom struck him with a dagger, Harmon took out the Sphere, and the Object came to life: the inner ring showed through the fog the bottom - a black lake of thick greenish water and a network of colored luminous points connected by lines above it. Harmon stunned Booth with the Sphere and decided to fly to the capital to give the Object to the Lady.

**The Hunter's Captive (Chapters 9–10).** The archer [[chara|Chara]]'s arrow set a burner on fire, and the ball collapsed into the gorge. The captives were taken by a detachment of the Hunter, a nobleman in a horned helmet, with whom were the [[ganta-biraj|ganta Birai]] with Shavans, the "Satellite" Neymir and Char, and the "goat people" of the old Zandur, who demanded the return of the lands taken by the Second of the Five. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) Zandur took the Sphere. This time, Harmon told the truth about everything: about [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]]'s deal with [[moris-labelin|Labelin]], about [[polli|Polly]]'s death, about the fake, about Rico and the Abyss. The hunter realized that someone had made a huge hunt: the Sphere is a tool, Harmon is a bait, and he himself is a fly that they wanted to poke. His goal was the Second of the Five, the master of the Sorcerer. ([[mik|Mik]])

On the fixed ball, the Hunter under the guise of Booth, Chara, Harmon and Hortensius descended into the courtyard of the Abode-u-Abyss, into the Tower-Zub, as if with the news that they had seen the kingdom of the gods at the bottom. Quindar believed in the blue abbess, but the deputy sheriff Halintor, who arrived with the order of Prince Hector to seize the Sphere, almost broke the deception. The hunter dragged Kindar into the basket and cut the rope, but the count jumped to the wall, and the pierced ball pulled into the chasm. The travelers cut the basket, hung on the ropes and moved to the ledge, and the empty ball was blown away by the wind. Soon, the Hunter’s men, dressed as monks, still seized the monastery. The hunter cut Quindara's ear with a knife, questioned him about the "organization", forced him to write bills (a thousand to each Shavan, two thousand Chara, Harmon and Hortensia) and return Zandur land with a mine of spark eyes. Harmon only took his share. When Colonel Cleon of the regiment of the Golden Swords besieged the monastery, and Quindar set the zunds on the Shavans, Baron Darryl and Magda Labelin arrived with the Solar Regiment and defeated the Golden Swords. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]]) Magda and Darryl recognized the Lord of Adrian in the Hunter, and Magda swore to him, gaining the rights to the captured eyes. The charm, disappointed, left.

**Magda (scenes in chapters 7–8).** At the same time, the line shows how Magda, having exhausted negotiations, poisoned General Lorinal Gitan with the hands of a sent wide-brimmed assassin and lured his successor, Colonel Horey, along with the Solar Regiment and holds full of spark weapons. ### Witness
**The Hospital (Chapters 1–2)** On an island in the Mason Sea is the Faraday and Riley Hospital. In the mid-17th century, the Marquis George Faraday founded it for his wife Miranda, who heard voices, and after his death, the hospital turned into an unofficial prison for uncomfortable relatives. Patient Dorothy Sly of Marengo lost her memory and resigned herself to the regime of "the abode of love and care" with her punishments - "thinking" and "hitting the disease". It turned out that she was literate, and she was taken as a copyist to the hall of the master Gustav. [[navi|Navi]] became a neighbor on the table - a young man who endlessly demanded: "Say the number." For slapping Navi, Gustav hung Dorothy by the hand in the basement. Neighbor [[karen|Karen]] (Kaitlin) explained that Navi squeezes all the “right” numbers out of each copyist, and each can only be given once. Dorothy, to her astonishment, knew the prices of herring in the Belomorye market and the depth of the northern fairways, although she believed that she had never left the Lands of the Crown. She named seven. Navi did not remember who he was; according to Karen, he spent more than ten years on the island and did not age.

**Daughter (chapters 3–4).** Navi calculated that Karen's chance of ever running on a boat was 0,15%, and asked Dorothy to arrange a meeting with Erwin Origin or Minerva: he had "six questions". For some reason, the name of Minerva alarmed Dorothy. Karen recalled that Dorothy screamed in her dream the name of her daughter, Gloria, and Dorothy remembered an eighteen-year-old red-haired daughter who loved to dance. On 18 May, a "cousin" arrived for her - a loud major of the scarlet guard Backfield, and Master Mullin agreed to discharge her after a conversation with him. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Karen warned: “You will be killed on the mainland”, because Yanmei’s grandchildren never forgive offenses. On the question recorded by the cipher, Navi answered the probability of escape: 37,42%.

**Escape (chapters 5–6).** On the night that the nurses were selling patients to seamen from the schooner, Navi led Dorothy past security, calling Coddy's older nurse several numbers from which he turned white, and extracted the key. Karen at first refused to go, but Dorothy shouted that "he still loves you", and she followed. The fugitives stole the boat and, according to Navi's calculations, went into a drift along the western stream, and did not row north, where the chase rushed. In the morning, they were picked up by the Shimmerian tea schooner "White Panther" Captain Lu-Aramon. Navi proved that he knew the currents of a Thousand Shards better than a navigator, and persuaded the captain to hide them. When Karen asked who he was, he replied, "God..."

### The Northern Bird

## Significance in the Series
The novel reveals the structure of [[absolyut|the Absolute]] (seventeen Objects, one from each Gift, except the cursed eighteenth) and the role of the Light Sphere, brings to the stage the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] and closes the main riddle of previous books: Adrian is alive, and Manson acted under the rule of Ulyanina Dust. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]; [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Adrian's downfall and Erwin's nomination as emperor, the bloody clash in the Ardennes and the [[perevorot-v-uejmare|coup in Waymar]], where Iona finds himself in the power of her husband, create the tie of the fifth novel, [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]. The Witness line introduces the reader for the first time to [[navi|Navi]], one of the most enigmatic figures of the cycle, and the Magda line brings out the first spark army in the service of the Great House.
