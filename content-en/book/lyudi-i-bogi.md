Humans and Gods is the fifth novel in the series and the first major book since the revelation of Victor Shayland. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) The puppeteer is known, [[adrian|Adrian]] was alive, and [[minerva|Minerva]] after the meeting of the House became only a temporary ruler. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) The action diverges on many fronts. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] is waging a "holy war" against Archbishop Gallard Almer, in [[uejmar|Waymar]] the court of the Puppeteer is gaining strength, and [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade leaves through the deserts of [[nadezhda|Hope]] to Steppe. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) Next to this is a cell line of three escapees from the clinic: Dorothy, Karen and a young man named Navi, who calls himself a god. In the finale, retrospection transports the reader to 1756 and reveals where Paul came from.

## Structure
As in previous novels, the chapters are named by the codenames of the lines; the date and place are indicated under the title. The novel opens on May 20, 1775, a week before the finale of [[yanmejskaya-okhota|the Yanmei Hunt]], and first draws each line through the night of May 28, an attack on Iona's Farewell and Rebellion. The two central chapters on the war are titled double (Arrow-5/Sword-6, Arrow-6/Sword-7): events in Fliss and in Waymar develop in them in parallel. Arrow 7 is marked “June 1, 1775 (after the news of the death of Lady Iona)”: it goes back and reveals Erwin’s plan, hidden from the reader in previous chapters. The new line "Mask" is given to Erwin's mother, and the final two-part retrospection "The Price of Fear" is given to young Vittor Shayland.

## Significance in the Series
The novel reveals the central mysteries of the later part of the cycle. Paul is the "prisoner of Waymar", who came out of the box of the Seventeenth Gift, and the power of the Puppeteer grew out of the oath of silence given by the rulers over the body of the dead goddess. The question of Nathaniel in this book remains confusing: the Order and Father David consider Nathaniel of 1756 (see "Now you're a sorcerer") Paul himself, while Navi calls this name his old one, and his first assistant in Polari is a Waymar librarian. ([[teper-ty-koldun|Now You Are a Sorcerer]]) This is finally allowed in [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]: Nathaniel is Navi. The book closes the story of [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] and [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]], returns Richard to the plot, reveals the purpose of the secret order (flight to the Star) and the plan of the "deconstructor". The novel's finale, Paul at the head of the steppe horde, Erwin ambushed at Rei-Roy's, sets the starting situation for The Shadow of the Great Tree.
