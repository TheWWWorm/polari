The Shadow of the Great Tree is the sixth and final novel in Polari's main plot. In the Steppe, [[paul|Paul]] became the god-leader of the horde of "Ganta Pus". [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] is cut off from his lands. Victor Shayland has found the immortality of [[absolyut|the Absolute]]. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) [[adrian|Adrian]] returns to power with a secret order. All lines converge in the valley of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] on the Day of Descent of 1775. It also reveals the main mystery of the world: what is a Star and where do the [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]] come from. The cycle ends not with victory or defeat, but with the choice that the empress must make.

## Structure
The novel retains the codename system, but shakes it up. The Arrow line in the middle of the book is conducted through the eyes of strangers: the commandant of Fort Sunset, Lieutenant Claude Berengar, Baron Ismail Davey, David's father and Lady Agnia Fleming. The reader learns about Erwin's secret return to the North only by indirect signs. The Witness line passes from the personified Faunterra to Magda Labelin and Karen Lighthart, and the new Kolpak line is given to Manson. The climax is broken into three multi-voiced chapters — "Early Morning", "Before the Day Song" and "After the Day Song" — describing December 2 by the hour. The book ends with five chapters without numbers: “Arrow”, “Kolpak”, “Iskra”, “Mask” and “Northern Bird”. In the chapter "Early Morning" in a quiet county appears Mac - Macfried Crowe, who hangs the sign "Investigations and Legal Services" on the house; by all indications, this is the retired Mark (see the "Spark" line).

## Significance in the Series
The novel completes the main plot of Polari. He answers the main questions of the world order: what is the Star, Gifts, Objects and [[pervokrov|Firstblood]], who are the [[pramateri|Foremothers]] and what the secret order of the Great Tree achieves. Here are finally divorced two “alien”, which the heroes of the previous books confused: Nathaniel is Navi, “light”, and Paul is “dark”, a former marine of the same crew. Navi left him, thinking he was dead. At the same time, the fate of the world remains unresolved: the last words of the book, “The choice is yours”, convey the decision to Minerva and the reader. The author in the preface to "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]" directly calls this finale the completion of the main plot. Subsequent stories he considers only fantasies about the fate of individuals.
