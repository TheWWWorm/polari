A short interlude showing the events after "[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]" from the point of view of [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], who is not the narrator in the novel itself. The Lord Chancellor talks to an imaginary althess named [[dalliya-rejven|Anxiety]], visible only to him, training with an ancient sword of his kind and making plans to capture the Puppeteer. The name is given by the name of the sword - the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]].

## Plot
### Sword
The interlude opens with the story of the Sword of the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]] - a narrow, no wider than three fingers, blade with a gray stripe on steel. It was forged two hundred and twenty years ago by Master Conrad of [[lid|Leed]] for Duke Odar Origin during the First Horse War. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]) With this sword, the great gauntlet of Hadinai was killed at the Battle of Doyle, which stopped the Steppe's movement to the north. They also had a son who gave a fatally wounded Odar a blow of mercy and received a sword as an inheritance. The sword was heard in the Second and Third Horse Wars, smashed the shavanks, gant, and barons of Fairis, who were beating in the back of the imperial troops, and went through a Golden campaign against the rebellious counts in the deserts of [[nadezhda|Hope]]. He was prayed to before the battle by Arthur Origin's army, surrounded at Fort Lance, forty-four men against the battalion. In the straits of the Gran Islands, he sank into the abyss with a burning schooner and was raised from the bottom. When the Earls of Flemings deceived the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], Duke Liorei Origin struck them with twelve kairs and folded the sword only when the traitor threatened to kill the inhabitants of the castle. At the Duke's death wish, the sword followed him into the underground dungeon, where Liorei was buried alive. For four years the sword lay on his bones, until the heir raised it and cut off the head of the traitor Fleming. According to the description of the master, for two hundred and twenty years the blade has lost three and a half ounces of weight. He got his name because he was quick as sound, and now he is the fastest sword in the world.

His father, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]], gave the sword to Erwin the day before with the words "you deserve it" - in honor of the fact that his son defeated the tyrant's army, took the capital and won the war. The imaginary althessa, which Erwin now calls Trevagoi "with a capital letter", eerily inserts for his father: "I will not be useful anymore." Erwin confesses to her that the sword is on his hand simply because it is light. His former nameless blade was too heavy for him: his father hoped that his son’s hand would grow stronger, but his hopes were not fulfilled. Erwin killed half a dozen already running penny miners at [[uindli|Windley]] and a dozen soldiers of Major Backfield, held out against [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] for a minute and threw the knight from the horse in a bloodless assault of [[labelin|Labelin]]. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) This sword was with him on the day he signed the peace treaty with Minerva Stagforth.

### Five family receptions
Cousin [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] reverently tries the sword, but refuses to take it. With a great-grandfather in each branch of the family there were a dozen young men, and now Erwin is alone in the First Winter and Robert is alone in the Steps of Heaven, and Robert does not dare to risk until he has a wife and son. Erwin asks to be taught family techniques, which are now owned by Robert alone. There are five:
- The fist of the Light-bearer is a fit and a direct blow to the face. In his youth Erwin's sword flew out of his hands on a fint;
- Mountain Thunder - a chopping blow to the neck and an injection of daga into the armpit of the patch. Here Erwin was not given the slip that Brother [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] sought from him;
- Alysena and Fresh Blood - a series of provoking attacks with a blow to the hip or elbow;
- Agatha lightning is a double cross-cross stroke, the second blow of which is ahead of the enemy's thought. It needs a very strong and very light blade, like the Voice of Winter.

Robert shows Agatha's Lightning, and the candle on the table breaks into three equal pieces.

Erwin trains every day, purposely before dinner, so that the court ladies can see him with a sword. At lunch, he casually drops: “Why is there such an appetite...” Lady [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] beams fiery glances at whispering ladies, and [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] giggles. In the fencing hall, he does not get anything: the Voice of Winter “did not forgive doubts, did not tolerate humanity.” Anxiety makes a diagnosis: Erwin is too kind, there is no malice in him, and the Puppeteer in his thoughts is "some abstract villain without a soul." The enemy must be slaughtered like the lianas in the jungle or the snow that shuts the door. "But people are not snow!" Erwin counters.

### Beck's Report
In March, Captain [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] of the Leed Wolves and protection agent [[itan|Ethan Gladys Norma]] bring a forty-page report of the Beck disaster (see Crash at Beck). ([[protektsiya|The Protection]]; [[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) With a hundred grays and a hundred agents, they combed the banks thirty miles down from the county castle and picked up ninety-two bodies, and among the wreckage of the train, they identified two hundred and sixteen. Twelve of the three hundred and twenty people aboard the train have not been found. [[adrian|Adrian]]’s body, identified by teeth, marks on bones and the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]], had been brought to the capital two weeks earlier. Erwin even made [[minerva|Minerva]] sniff the remains.

Leed is proud that the Leed Wolves of sixteen generations serve the family without a single mutiny, and his company distinguished itself in the night Labelin and in the pursuit of the Silver Fox and put three hundred trophy spark copies to the Duke's feet. ([[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]]) Erwin praises him as the "best interrogator" in his army. Leed interrogated the fishermen-witnesses for three visits, scared, but, on the orders of Erwin, did not torture. Witnesses, according to Leed, “all tightly knit and sealed.” They cried, swore to "Saint Gloria the Intercessor" and expounded the same as the official version printed in "The [[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]". On it, the bridge gave a crack, in the cold, the frozen water strengthened the fault, at dawn the train collapsed, the lord came out of the burning car, and the jester in a cap with tambourines hit him with a dagger in the back. Leed suspects Ethan is hiding something.

Erwin himself knows who destroyed the bridge: he put together two facts, although he never spoke about it with Alanis. The Witnesses were well-trained by Count [[erroubek|Arrowback]]. But out of their smooth lies comes a ridiculous detail. [[menson|Manson]] allegedly killed his beloved nephew - the only one who needed - and the jester who always got up late, an hour in the tenth or eleventh, at dawn was fully dressed and in a cap. Erwin concludes that no one would have imagined such an absurd part: “So the joke with the knife really was.”

### Ethan's interrogation
Erwin puts Winter's Voice on the table and interrogates Ethan. The alarm reminds that he is in love with Minerva and loyal to the Crow Crown ([[mark-voron|Mark]]), and Erwin is afraid that he met Ethan when he came to “poison” him with the Crow. Ethan is chosen because he is loyal to both Minerva and Adrian: if there is any chance that the emperor survived, Ethan will find him. The secretary responds smoothly that Manson had gone insane from the shock and fled. Erwin asks point blank if Adrian is alive and whose body was sent to the capital, and rises with a sword in his hand. Ethan's pale, but he's on his own. The alarm whispers, "Damn it, dear." Erwin tells Leed to put Ethan in a stone bag for bread and water, without walks and without "methods". Alarm puts a candle on the table:
> You are too human, my love. The puppeteer will eat you.”

The Voice of Winter knocks the candle down on the floor.

### Good luck in April
Robert advises his cousin to stop being “too duke” and think of the sword as a carpenter thinks of a hammer: the enemy who gets in the way is only a barrier. Everything else is going well. My father’s illness eased, but it didn’t go away. The trophy talking subject, already the second, though he did not speak, but came to life in the hands of Erwin. Prince [[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]] offers fifteen thousand spark eyes, and a loan from the Duke of Farway allows them to buy. ([[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]]) Erwin dreams of a personal guard, "like the X-rays of [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon]]", but armed with a spark. Prior [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] announces his engagement to [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]]. Alanis is furious, and Erwin is glad: Alanis will not risk war against the two duchies, so the massacre in the center of the Empire is excluded, and the young wife makes the “granite prince” vulnerable. Minerva goes to negotiate with [[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]], and this “suicide”, but the beautiful act for the first time causes Erwin sincere admiration.

The main thing is that the insurgents of [[salem|Salem]] found a spark weapon. ([[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]]) Erwin is sure that the Puppeteer put up an unexpected force to trap him. The plan is to move the Northwood peasants, and when the main detachment of the Puppeteer, [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade, hits them in the flank, slam the trap and take the "spark" prisoner. After such a defeat, the Puppeteer will have to surrender or flee to the Voltaic Islands. ([[folta|Folta]]) In the evening, drunk from excitement, he tells his sister everything, flirts with Minerva and kisses Iona, as Anxiety notes, "to Elena on the third lap." Altessa makes him count how many people will die when "the bears run into a spark." She recalls the twenty-five executed prisoners, after which he mourned as friends. Erwin tries to prove that he has “learned to be evil”, chops a plug on a candle and gets into the countertop. Anxiety says that her business is not to let him forget: the jester wanders unknown where, Ethan is silent, the healer whispered something Alanis, and the Puppeteer can avoid the trap.

### Holy Field
The plan doesn't collapse because of the Puppeteer. Minerva with a small escort rushed to the Holy Field, drove through the order of the troops, agreed with the Snowdrops and forgave them. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) It became impossible to throw an army at the forgiven peasants, the rear headquarters did not remain a bait, and Paul left the trap. Erwin’s mounted patrols found no trace of the brigade where an army twenty thousand strong had passed. Vassals are outraged. According to the laws of the North, the peasant is punished even for the audacity of the kairu, and these men rebelled, fought with the knights and shot the ambassadors in negotiations, now two out of a hundred thousand rebels will stand before the court. The nearest companions offer to throw the doll: “Your doll is insolent!” Only a couple of close aides knew that Minerva had thwarted the hunt that Erwin had put six months in. Erwin imagines in anger how he will cover Minerva with spies, close her accounts with the banker Conto, or completely replace her, though Manson, even "a gloomy old woman from [[darkvoter|Darkwater]]." ([[konto|Konto]])

Two days of march to the capital Anxiety argues with him until his anger is exhausted. She recalls that in three months Minerva introduced paper money, pacified the steppes and settled the rebellion, with only crumbs of power. The Empress did not know his plan and saved him from accusations of softness. She wasn't his vassal, and she didn't take an oath. His father said, “To find a worthy ally is more valuable than to kill an enemy.” And he proposes to marry Mimi - not the forgotten lady of [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]], not Alanis, "who whispered with an enemy agent", and not her sister. He swore not to do so by force, and then his son would be born emperor. Erwin replies that he dreams of more - a victory over the Lord of the Rings. When the althessa disappears, he adds inaudibly, "Mimi ruined my hunt."

### Snowdrop
In the palace, Erwin feels a change: servants and officials look at him with the “eyes of dogs. Small, unmistakable tentacles...but no more rabbits." He asks [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] to shout "Glory to Agatha!", and the yard responds a little quieter than "Glory to Yangmei!". The office is waiting for the gift of the Empress - snowdrop, although snowdrops bloomed two weeks ago. On the ribbon in girl's handwriting says:
> Accept your place in the world with dignity. You are not the Lord, my lord. Come to terms."

Erwin quietly lists: [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]], Captain [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]], Banker of Conto, Minister of Ways. Forty-Two adds mentors, [[albert-vial|Albert Vial]] and Frank Morlin-May. Erwin orders to gather all the "Minerva's henchmen" and tell Leeda to shake everything out of Ethan in any way. The alarm tries to stop him, but he pushes her away, and she becomes translucent from the offense. A flower is seen through her silhouette. The voice of winter flies to the ceiling and falls down. The cut flower a moment hangs over the stem and falls on the table - for the first time the blow was successful.

## Significance in the Series
Here, Erwin first gives the name to Anxiety, the althesse that will accompany him in the "Janmei Hunt" and beyond. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) Here he receives the famous sword The [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]]. The interlude explains the Lord Chancellor's motives after "[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]", reveals that he is hiding the truth about the bridge over the Back, and leads to an open confrontation between Erwin and Minerva, from which the "Yanmei Hunt" begins.

Consequences:
- Erwin fulfilled the order to “gather everyone”: in the “Yanmean Hunt”, Minerva demands the return of her people, the dissolution of a special company and the withdrawal of Ethan. Erwin responds that her assistants are being held in Labelin, and Ethan will return after completing "one assignment".
- Ethan was tortured by the Leed Wolves. Leed reports that he “remained almost in full health,” but in fact Ethan remained crippled: “hand, teeth, skin.” At the trial of Manson, Erwin revealed that, having put some pressure on Ethan, he learned the main thing: Hilt on the corpse was inserted into the sheath incorrectly, which means that the body found did not belong to Adrian.
- Robert's advice "do not be angry, but just do your job" Erwin recalls in a duel with Shavan Sormah in the "Yanmei Hunt". The voice of Winter cuts off the giant's hand, and Erwin spares him.
- In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], Agate's Lightning first succeeds Erwin "in all its glory". In the finale of the cycle, when the protective belt stops Lightning, Erwin defeats Richard's brother (Ice) by making Winter's Voice "become sparkly", and finishing him off with this sword.

## In "Fantasies"
In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], which the author calls possible futures, the characters of the interlude appear again.
- The alarm turns out to be the ghost of Dalia Raven's girlfriend. ([[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]]) The Cairos find her and give her to Minerva, and Erwin explains to the empress: "The Cairos gave you my althessa" - and asks the Alarm not to disappear for long. In "Closer - Further" she "after two years of dating" is a painter at the request of Erwin, and he promises her five more years not to look for a bowl.
- Erwin, Duke of Orange, wears the Voice of Winter at official receptions and becomes Minerva's secret favorite.
- Robert is serving Minerva's "honorary" penal servitude at a monastery on Mount Agatha, and Erwin and Iona visit him.
- Hyder Leed commands a squad of X's in Shimmery, where he is returned to Barney's Shavan-kidnapped nephew, and he falls in love with Mireille Nan-Claire ("Amicable").
- Forty-Two (Henry Horton) becomes Assistant Minister of Aeronautics [[kharmon|Harmon Paula]].
- Adrian's nameless secretary in a white mask, stuttering and mutilated "defending the honor and freedom of a great man", is by all indications Ethan, mutilated by order from this interlude ("The Device").
