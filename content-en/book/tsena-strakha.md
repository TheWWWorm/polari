The Price of Fear is a story carried nineteen years ago, to [[uejmar|Waymar]] August 1756. It is told by young [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], the future Puppeteer. In the text of the series, she stands at the end of the novel “[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]” as the chapters “The Price of Fear-1” and “The Price of Fear-2”, and on FantLab and in stores is a separate posthuman. The story answers two main questions of the later part of the cycle: what was found in the box of the Seventeenth Gift and where [[paul|Paul]] came from.

## Time and place
The action takes place several days in August 1756. The [[semnadtsatyj-dar|seventeenth gift]] of the gods fell into the land of Shayland. The Sunsets captured Dar's lodge, but [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] smashed them and kept the lodge guarded by kairs for several months. Now in Waymar, winners and the yard come to the celebrations. The Northerners are camped on the bank of the [[torrej|Torrey]], the emperor arrives with the fleet through the Smokey Dahl, and the bed itself lies in the afternoon of the way from the city.

The same month and the same city is dedicated to the interlude "Now you are a magician." ([[teper-ty-koldun|Now You Are a Sorcerer]]) Mick sees in the cathedral the same two Objects from the new Gift and meets the young Nathaniel.

## Plot
### The price of fear-1
**Father's lesson.** Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent Shayland]], the grandson of a fugitive merchant, straightening his ashes with forceps in the fireplace, explains to his sons that their kind only stands on the threshold of high society. The motto of the family is “Use what you know, sell what you have”. The younger, Martin, killed the cat in front of Baroness [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]], and his father punishes him with fireplace tongs. The eldest, Vittor, he examines: which of the guests will come first and why.

**Northerners' camp.** Vittor, with his brother and bodyguards, the strongman Sam and Donald-Donnell, who can read lips, overtakes his father's delegation and is the first to greet Desmond. Vittor deliberately plays a good-looking fool: flatters, bows to the ground and immediately expands. So he wins the sympathy of [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] (in the form of twelve years), who serves as a warm. Captain [[artur-khajrok|Arthur Highrock]]'s urgent arrival suggests to Vittor that something special has been found in Dar's lodge.

**Deal about Items.** For several days, Count Vincent and Desmond walk around. Both want the aid fee to be Items, not gold, but neither can say it directly. Eventually, on the advice of the bishops, Shayland "gifts" some of the Objects to the Northerners. Meanwhile, Vittor takes Richard to an old lighthouse and finds out that his father has forbidden him to get into the secret of the lodge. Richard makes a promise to Wittor: if he finds out the secret, he will tell him.

**Emperor.** At night, the Crown fleet arrives in the harbor. Lord Telurian, Prince [[adrian|Adrian]], [[ajden-almera|Aiden]] and [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] and Archimandrite [[ellina|Ellina]] solemnly enter the city. On the altar of the cathedral guests are shown two New Gift Items. Ellina calls them the Light Sphere and the Rose of Happiness. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) Donnel reads the conversation at the altar: Desmond asks the lord to postpone the holiday by the bed and examine it together, but at Aiden he does not reveal a secret. The Northerners are showered with honors, the Shaylands are honored only by protocol. On the days of the visit, a little more than a week after the cat story, Martin molestes an novice from Ellina's entourage.

### The price of fear-2
**A celebration without the Shaylands.** The Emperor leaves for the lodge, and Vittor receives the late [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] in Waymar with [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]]'s young wife, Bishop Gallard, and the Archimedes and arranges a feast for them. He covers up another trick of his brother: the novice promises to remain silent if she is shown the bed of the Gift. Vittor learns that Aiden Almera once advised Telurian to abolish the Shayland House.

**Richard's promise.** Aiden hints to the lord that it is time to hand over the guard to the lodge, and Telurian agrees. Insulted Richard demands that Vittor immediately keep his promise: tomorrow the Lord’s people will take everything out of the box.

**The Lodge of Dara.** Jaeger was found in the forest of a naked savage without memory and without name, and he knows the way in bed. At night, Vittor and Martin, with their bodyguards and a novice, go down there for him. In the depths of the cave, they see Telurian, Adrian, Desmond, Aiden and Hyroc standing at a transparent sarcophagus. In it lies a silver-haired girl pierced by a piece of rock. Her body is untouched by decay. The masters understand that it is the Mother who died at the Descent, and decide that her existence will undermine the faith in the infallibility of the Forebears, and with it the power of the Houses. Adrian considers her a goddess. Desmond demands to call the Church, but under the threat of weapons swears to remain silent. Tellurian orders to burn the body in lava, and Adrian removes something from the deceased's neck "for memory" and whispers: "Such as I ..."

**The price of fear.** Martin gives out his brothers. Sam and Donnell are killed, the novice is thrown into lava, the savage is struck with a spark knife, and he falls into a fire pit. Aiden and Desmond are told to kill the boys. Victor realizes that he can only sell his fear. He urinates in horror, kisses the floor of Desmond's cloak and swears that fear is stronger than honor. Desmond refuses to kill the frightened child, and the emperor makes him a guarantor: if the Shaylands speak, Origin will destroy all their kind. When the lords leave, a charred savage crawls out of the lava: "Paul. We need to eat. And lie down. You owe me.” Vittor, stuttering, replies, "Welcome to Waymar Castle, Lord Paul."

## Significance in the Series
The story reveals the origin of the power of the Puppeteer. An oath over the body of the dead Pramati bound Thelurian, Adrian, Desmond and Aiden, and the Shaylands bought their lives with fear and have lived under the threat of the Origins ever since. Out of the box comes Paul, the future “prisoner of Waymar” and the right hand of Vittor, to whom he “owes”. In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], Erwin reconstructs this story according to the jailer Lard, and [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]] explains who Paul and the dead goddess were.

## Publication
The story was written in 2022. In the text of the series, it was published the last chapters of the novel “Humans and Gods”, and on FantLab and in stores is a separate post-human, published in 2025. The action takes place long before the main plot, but it is worth reading after “Humans and Gods”: it reveals the secrets on which this novel is based.
