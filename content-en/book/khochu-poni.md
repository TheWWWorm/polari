An interlude told entirely through the eyes of [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]], the granddaughter of Duke [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], Erwin's ex-fiancée. ([[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]) Her life motto, repeated with a refrain, inspired by her parents and grandfather: be a good girl, and everyone will love you, and with love you will get everything you want. The title is the last line of the heroine, when the priest asks what reward she wants.

## Plot
### Invitation of His Eminence
In December 1774, Laura and her older brother Jeremy, taken hostage by the emperor, have breakfast at [[aleridan|Aleridan]]. An officer runs in and tells him to leave town immediately. In the carriage, he reports a rumor that [[adrian|Adrian]] has died: the imperial train derailed from the bridge over the Back. Jeremy says, "Now the northerner will win," and Laura realizes that he is right: her fiancé took the capital. The carriage is stopped by a dozen people, five with raised crossbows, led by a monk in a blue hood. The monk "invites" the young lords to visit his Eminence. Four guardsmen with naked swords are slow, not knowing whether to fight for the dead emperor. Laura, not wanting bloodshed, goes out and politely accepts the invitation: she always chooses to be “good.”

### The Bridegroom from the North
In the spring of 1774, his grandfather, Duke [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], announced that he had found Laure's fiancé, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], the future Duke of the First Winter, "smart as the Agatha the Radiant". He promised her a house in Faunterra overlooking Hanai and the palace, such outfits that "Alanis Almera will grow old and die of envy in a year", and showed the album "Polaris Castles" with the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] over the azure lake. In return, Laura had to be good: to teach science, painting, poetry and, at the request of Origin, stratum. Jeremy mocked: the Northerners are poor, the Origins are in debt, and Erwin, according to capital gossip, never held a sword. Laura replied, “He’s Agatha’s grandson. Only Agatha can understand Agatha.”

In the autumn the north rose. Laura taught sonnets and took lessons with execution, and Jeremy predicted that Adrian would hang Erwin, and at the same time Laura and her grandfather. When the emperor came to the [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]] and patted Laura on the neck, she was not afraid. Grandfather broke off the engagement, sent troops against Origin and gave his grandchildren as hostages. On the train, Laura poured Adrian tea and asked about the victory over the Shavans at [[melorandzh|Melorange]]. The emperor promised to show the maneuver on the stratum field in the next train, but in the composition from Aleridan to Faunterra, the same one that collapsed from the bridge, there was no place for the hostages.

### Evergard
[[evergard|Evergard]], recently rebuilt after a fire, meets the children with a mosaic of Agatha the Radiant on the donjon and a new red tile. In the lower courtyard, a woman is burned on a stone platform. Monk Clement explains that this is a heretic and a witch, who was judged by his eminence himself. Children are placed in adjacent rooms in custody. Over dinner, they meet the pale, frightened Albert Adelia Abigail, Almera's rightful heir, [[alanis-almera|Alanis]]' younger brother. Albert says that the gods do not send him dreams. Later, Laura sees that a new pillar was erected at the place of execution: after each execution, it is erected anew as a reminder. Out of the main building runs the oblique lame monk in a black cassock and roars at her: "Y-yyy!" Clement explains that it's mute.

### Proposal of the Priory
Prior [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]], regent of Almera, takes the children in a locked room. He reports that the throne "with the consent of Lord Origin" was ascended by [[minerva|Minerva]] Stagforth, "a flabbergasted maiden from the far north." Jeremy offers to speak with the army of Hope, and Gallard lands him with the "first commandment of the Forefathers": "Take your place in the world with dignity." The war is over, the Farway has hesitated too long. Priarch explains that the situation at Farway's house is dangerous. The Farway were Erwin's allies, and then sent four companies to storm the palace. They only became dukes twenty years ago, the vassals remember the Lightharts, and the Flames sympathize with them. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) Gallard proposes a marriage-bound alliance: Laura will marry Albert, who is still too young to be confirmed, but may bring marriage vows. Jeremy should write to his father.

Laura is afraid that a brother-freethinker who does not believe in the holiness of the Foremothers will be burned as a heretic. Five times a day, she recites prayers aloud to him, so that he repeats. She takes him to the chapel to unravel the subjects of the frescoes: Gloria with the motto "We are different, but we are one", Marek and Simeon, a rare icon "The bearers of light play in the strata" about the forbidden Order of the Light-bearers. He gives her theological books. Jeremy only taunts: Gallard is "honoured for piety", hostages are held for the sake of pressure on his grandfather.

### General Statham
Ten days after the letter, the children are called to the tea room, where their father, Finley Farway, is waiting in furs, and the northern Colonel General [[stetkhem|Statham]] is "Garret Amanda Gene of the Agatha family." Statham arrived in a simple carriage with paper from the Empress. To get inside, he counted the seconds and called the head of the guard to the fight. Finley puts pressure on the general's Agathan pride, but he coldly replies that the marriage contract is over, as well as the alliance: the Farway companies stormed the palace. Statham himself puts his first-born company on the most dangerous site, not on the safe. To Laura's request to keep the friendship of the houses, he answers only with a weak nod.

Gallard breaks in with the knights. Statham hands him a letter with a pen and sword coat of arms. The owner orders an end to the terror against the vassal lords, release Albert "from captivity under the guise of regency" and transfer power to the legitimate heiress. Laura echoes, "Lady Alanis is alive?!!" Statham replies that she is alive, although the parish with Count Blackmore worked hard to kill her. Gallard calls the letter a forgery. He hints that he knows: in the last days of the siege, two came from Faunterra to Count [[erroubek|Arrowback]]'s Castle Back, and one of them looked like a travel mercenary helping Alanis, and the next day Adrian's train collapsed a mile from there. Statham observes that in such a case, Origin has a reliable vassal in the east of Almera with control of the rails, and in the month of the war the archer will lose everything east of Aleridan. He offers Gallard life, title, and authority over the Church, but not Almer. Gallard refuses to give the duchy to Alanis, but lets the children go.

### Double game
In a train compartment on [[marengo|Marengo]] Finley shows Statham a large "raw eye" given to his grandfather by [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], and reveals the plot. The prophet, who left the cell, is looking for about a million Hilt, probably to regain the throne of Shimmeri. The Council of Five, which after thirty years of the Crown's monopoly has accumulated a surplus of eyes, wants to sell them. The first of the Five is Prince Hector, with the Fourth. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) The second is the Maximian abbot, with him the Third, both on Francis-Ilian's side. The fifth change. The Farway will buy the stocks of the Second and Third, the Origins the First and Fourth, and together they will hold about eight-tenths of all eyes. Hope will even lend half a million to the north. Statham, who prefers steel, is clearly enthusiastic and promises to talk to Origin. In farewell, he condoles to Finley when he says that his wife is "on the Star".

But in Marengo Finley buys tickets back to Evergard. Laura wants to say something, and he interrupts: “Keep your desires to yourself,” she is “stupid like her late mother.” Jeremy explains to his sister a double game: pressure through Origin on Gallard, and through Gallard on Origin.

### Union with the Church
Gallard is now receiving the Farway immediately. Finley complains that Origin broke the promises of both Gallard and Henry and assumed the fictional title of "Lord Chancellor" contrary to the commandment of humility. Union terms: Laura marries Albert; Hope helps the troops in the attack of the northerners; the houses are completed by the frozen project of the late Aiden Almer - the expansion of the rail path from Aleridan to the Heart of the World with two new tracks. The Church gives Hope a loan of two hundred thousand “for the development of the army”, Finley is silent about the purchase of eyes. Laura's firstborn will be blessed by the Church as the rightful heir to Almera. When Laura asks her father why she needs this marriage, he slaps her: “Glad shit! “Apple from an apple tree!”

### The Bride of Albert
Children are no longer hostages: Laura is Albert's bride, Jeremy is his father's voice at the court. But Laura has nowhere to go. Albert follows her on the heels, asks questions on the list, trembles in front of his uncle. He says that in the castle there are thirty-two auditory vents leading to all the main rooms: “Uncle hears everything in this castle.” His sister, Ellie, showed them to him. Alanis sends his brother from the capital a pony named Sire Adler, and Laura rides him. A mute monk in black, though Gallard's monks wear blue, watches over Statham's northern swordsmen and enters the arch, "as if his own".

Gallard leaves Laura more often after dinner. He reproaches her that she does not pray for her late mother, and asks, "Do you want anything, my lady?" She asks for cocoa, as in Marengo, and it is brought immediately. Later, the priest tells about the conditions of peace with Origin. He is considered to be the governor of Alanis and rules Almera on her behalf, after his death the duchy passes to her, and if she is not, to Albert. The Earl of Blackmore will go on trial, and the Earl of Arrowback will get his spark workshop. Laura carefully answers that the law is for Alanis, and the highest justice is for him, and receives a marzipan from the best Aleridan workshop as a reward. In February, she prayed with him. A favorite prayer of the archpriest is the prayer of Leopold: “Gods of wisdom, give me the ability to distinguish the age from the minute...” She also asks for albums with sketches of Alanis dresses. One day, the priest knocks on the wall panel in the refectory: “Here is the auditory window.” He knows that Laura told Statham that she disapproved of burning the heretic.

### Confession at the Fireplace
On a cold February evening, Gallard lays out his credo to Laura. The gods are at war with the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]], chaos, and expanding the [[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]]. The forefathers brought commandments, but more important than their content is the unity of men, and he who is strong enough must unite them, if necessary, by force. Wilhelm, using the Rings against the Shavans, violated the sacred prohibition, but united the Empire. The priory then admits: the burned "witch" was the hostess of the music salon in [[fliss|Fliss]] and the altess of the burgomaster. She convinced the burgomaster to side with Alanis and cut off Gallard's way to [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]. She did not violate the commandments of the Foremothers, but served Ido, sowing confusion, and if her plan was successful, Fliss would have to be taken by storm. And yet he adds that he killed her in vain: if he had killed his niece Alanis in time, he would not have had to kill this woman, nor officers, nor barons. Laura asks to teach such decisions to Albert.

### Overheard conversation
Jeremy shows his sister an encrypted note from his grandfather: Henry almost arranged with King Shimmery to buy eyes and even offered to marry Jeremy to one of the sixteen royal daughters. Jeremy speculates that the Feathers stole Adrian's "dog" that burned Evergard, and Origin would hunt him, to the benefit of the Farway. He then blackmails Albert into showing the outlet. In the attic near the spark boiler, in the closet behind the panel, Laura and Jeremy (Albert soon escapes) hear Gallard's muffled conversation with the unknown. The voice, subtle and dangerous, sounds as if from a distance, as if through two outlets, but it seems to emanate from the mouth of a mute monk. The stranger says, "You have received . . . a whole duchy, and I have received four Subjects. The exchange was uneven.” He demands to find according to the church archives, censuses and letters, who has the Object "... of the Sun", and to transfer it "in the old way". When Gallard threatens to demand more, the interlocutor asks: “Life ... and Alanis?” But the priest demands another: “to speak with the Objects”, because if the speaking Objects are given by the gods, they must be owned by the head of the Church. The stranger refuses, referring to the blood in the veins of the priest. Jeremy is excited to use the secret against Gallard.

### I Want a Pony
Then Laura, having made a tearful face, goes to the arch. She complains that her brother offends her and as if he ridiculed the mute monk at the door of the waiting room: “Why didn’t the gods return the speech to you if you pray so much?” She confesses that her father and brother are freethinkers, and their words about God’s laws are hypocrisy. And he discovers that her mother did not die: her father, mocking her faith, once brutally beat her, and she left. When asked if Jeremy could hear something outside the door, Laura rounds her eyes: "What could he hear if you had a mute?"

Jeremy, who is going to run by train to the Heart of Light, is brought from the stables. Gallard tells Agatha to recite a prayer of thanks, and he gets confused and shuts up: he does not remember his sister's lessons. The sentence is eight years of obedience in a faraway monastery. As long as Gallard lives, he will not allow heretics to rule the Great House. Priarch praises Laura for her courage, asks if she is strongly attached to Albert, and offers her a "more mature groom" without giving a name. This groom was Gallard himself: soon he announced his engagement to Laura. He then asks again what kind of reward she wants.
> “I want a pony!”

## Characters
- [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]] is the granddaughter of Henry Farway, Erwin's former fiancée, in the finale Albert's fiancée and the favorite of the archpriest. She has golden curls, loves trains and sweets, and grew up in arid Hope.
- Jeremy Farway is her brother, two years older, a freethinker and lover of execution. He calls his sister a “chicken.” He was sent to a monastery for eight years.
- Finley Farway is their father, a sharp-nosed intriguer, Henry's son. The wife who left him was ordered by Henry to consider the deceased.
- [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] - Prior of the Church of the Forefathers, regent, then viceroy of Almera, a "granite" fighter against chaos.
- Albert Adelia Abigail is Almera's young legal heir, brother [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] ("Ellie"), owner of the Sir Adler pony.
- Garret Amanda Gina of the Agatha family, Colonel General Statham, is the envoy of Origin and Minerva.
- Clement is a monk in blue, attached to children.
- The mute monk in the black robe, oblique and lame, — watch the priest. Through him, apparently, the voice of the master of the Rings sounds.
- [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], Hector, [[adrian|Adrian]], [[minerva|Minerva]], Earl of [[erroubek|Arrowback]] are mentioned. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]])

## Significance in the Series
The interlude explains [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s engagement to [[laura-farvej|Laura]], mentioned in The [[glas-zimy|Voice of Winter]] and [[yanmejskaya-okhota|the Yanmei Hunt]], and Almera's alliance with [[nadezhda|Hope]]. The main thing is overheard conversation: he shows that Gallard made a deal with the owner of the Rings (Almera in exchange for four Objects), and introduces a mysterious Object, which later turns out to be a Drop of the Sun. This information makes the priest the main suspect in the eyes of Erwin in the fourth novel.

The fates of the heroes of the interlude are such. In The [[glas-zimy|Voice of Winter]], Gallard announces his engagement to Laura, and she becomes his wife. In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], Gallard is killed in [[uejmar|Waymar]], Alanis is poisoned, and [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] is strangled by a widowed Laura in October 1775. In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], Henry Farway, mourning his granddaughter, tells that Jeremy married the daughter of a steppe gauntlet for the sake of peace and lives with her in Rei-Roy, and Farway himself rules Almera from Aleridan. Albert Almer was engaged to his widowed daughter Evelyn, a mother of four.

## Trivia
- The refrain of interlude is an instruction that Laura was inspired by her parents and grandfather: “Be a good girl, and everyone will love you. With love you will get everything you want.” Every request of Laura to the priory (cocoa, marzipan, prayer, Alanis albums, ponies) is an answer to his "Do you want anything, milady?"
- Marzipan, which Laura receives, is a half-foot golden sun with a red heart and bone, the emblem of her hometown.
- Statham is the same general who utterly defeated the Crown's spark army, acting according to the plan of Origin. Gallard calls him "the executioner of sleeping soldiers."
- Jeremy fails the test on the very prayer of gratitude to Agatha, which his sister read aloud to him five times a day.
