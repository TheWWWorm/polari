The third novel of the cycle begins immediately after the victory of the North in the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]] and the death of Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] in the crash at Bake. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) [[minerva|Minerva]] Stagforth is enthroned, but her "senior ally" [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], who became Lord Chancellor, is in charge of everything, and in the eyes of the court, the young ruler is a doll on the throne. The novel leads five storylines: the empress, a student of power; A raven investigating the theft of items of the dynasty; ([[mark-voron|Mark]]) [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] among the rebellious peasants; [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] at his brother's court; and a pair of Shavan scouts in the Western war.

## Structure
The chapters are numbered within each line: “Iskra-1”, “Satellite-1” and so on. The novel opens with the chapter “Satellite 1” and ends with the chapter “Iskra 12”.
- Iskra - [[minerva|Minerva]] (12 chapters).
- "Pero" - [[mark-voron|Mark]] (9 chapters; the chapter "Pero - 7" is told on behalf of Inge Price and returns to December 1774). ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]])
- "Sword" - [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] and in the first chapter the peasant [[salem|Salem]] (6 chapters).
- "North Bird" - [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] (6 chapters).
- "Satellite" - Shavan Neymir and [[chara|Chara]] (7 chapters). ([[nejmir|Neimir]])

The chapters of "Pera" often open with a parable, which [[ded|Grandfather]] tells. The chapters of Iskra in the second half of the novel are built as "steps" of financial science, which Minerva is taught by her mentors: to determine added value, to prioritize, to find initial capital, to start a business.

## Story: Spark - Minerva
### Coronation and "Your Majesty Must" (chapters 1–3)
Minerva is strangled by the court machine: every appeal of servants and secretaries begins with the words “Your Majesty should...”. The [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] is half destroyed by a siege: windows are knocked out, snow lies in the glass. At the coronation, the elderly Archimandrite [[ellina|Ellina]] forgets to bless her. ([[koronatsiya-minervy|Minerva's Coronation]]) Mira herself brings the hand of the Archimati to her forehead and utters both parts of the rite. The next morning, she signs a decree making Erwin Lord Chancellor. During the day, she depicts a idle lover of novels, and secretly makes a plan of fifteen points, from “drinking coffee” to “take power from the Chancellor” and “find the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]].”

General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] (Silver Fox) calls her to take the oath of the spark army. Soldiers who remember how much she risked for peace swear to her viciously. Erwin, meanwhile, appoints his cousin [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] as treasurer, gives the Ministry of Taxes to Dreyfus Bourne (according to Schelier, he bought the post for a hundred and twenty thousand), and [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] is the mayor of the capital. ([[drejfus-born|Dreyfus Born]]) She herself put away old friends: by her first decrees of Peace, she appointed [[itan|Ethan]] as head of the visiting department of the secretariat, and [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] as captain of the azure company, and she is now ashamed to look them in the eye. When Erwin is half an hour late for a meeting, Mira demands real power. The chancellor says she signed it herself. Then he brings her three insoluble questions at once: about the treasury, about the tax service and about the verdict of the Supreme Court.

Following Yangmei's instruction about the "eight enemies of the ruler", the first of which is idleness, Mira begins to use the court vanity for business. Through the chamber-lady Mollia, who served as Empress Ingrid, she under the pretext of aesthetic whims changes servants. Of Adrian’s ministers, only three remained in the capital: science, roads, and the court. Science Minister Master Isaac Flevin tells her about the University. The chancellor arranges northern entertainment, such as dog sledding races, where Shatterhand acts for the Crown, and for the Virgin kair, nicknamed [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]]. The world meets the bankers.

Iona replies that her wife and sister's debt tells her to keep an alliance with the Shaylands, and that she owes Minerva nothing. In the next intermission, the drunken Minister of Railways Liam Shelier arranges a scandal: six bridges are destroyed, nine stations are ruined, a hundred miles of wires are stolen and sold. ([[liam-shele|Liam Shellier]]) The chancellor's cayres then hang him naked, and Erwin himself comes in with the coffee and frees him. Shelier asks for his resignation, but Mira "sentences" him to remain Minister of Ways.

The second lesson is to prioritize: the Chancellor and the Minister of the Court force her to choose seventeen of the forty-four convicts. It is immediately revealed that in December the Supreme Court, at Backfield's request, sentenced Erwin to be burned and Minerva to be beheaded for rebellion against the Crown. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) Erwin, who spent the whole night with Hector for "the study of the Shimmer culture", refuses to intervene in the morning. He shows the World a caricature: Alexis in the form of a powerful tractor that can not get off the rails. Then Mira announces that she herself will go to negotiate with Moran. Erwin is appreciably shocked: “Don’t do this...you’re making a mistake.”

At the opening of navigation, she climbs the schooner "Straight". There are eight barrels of “wine” in the hold, and in six of them sit the related relatives of Minister Bourne: wife, three sons, brother and sister. Citing [[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]'s justice, Mira forces Bourne to sign a confession of embezzlement, acquit Vial and Morlin-Mei, and return the stolen to the Crown's account in Conto's bank rather than Robert Origin's coffers. ([[konto|Konto]]) Alexis beats Bourne. Then Mira herself is shocked by her own cruelty. Mira's personal train travels through [[nadezhda|Hope]] to the south. With her, the kair of [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]], who called to accompany her out of curiosity, and the purified Eternal Hilt. At Fort Big Luck, she is surrounded by about four hundred Moran horsemen. They scatter human bones, call her a "mussel" and demand all lands from Meloranj to Holliwell. Mira offers the leader in return a bouquet of lilies and a vassal oath. When Moran's companion whips on the flowers, Jemis cuts the whip, and Shatterhand accurately throws a spark knife kills under the horse's shroud. Mira reminds of the spring spill: from the crossings through Hollivel in the spring, only the Cool Threshold, which has the people of Moran, and the Julian Bridge, remain. The leader gives way: lifts the siege, spares Litland, releases the prisoners and goes to the Steppe. The Luga Shepherds remain common under Julian law, and the Litlands will no longer claim them. Instead of an oath, Moran serves Minerva a bouquet raised from a pile of bones with the words "Your Majesty". Then Mira admits to Jemis that the regiment really is: he stands at Julian Bridge. Back, Mira finds herself on Palace Island fair: on the first day of paper appropriations sold for forty thousand gold. She immediately learns that the peasant uprising was hidden from her. ### Holy Field (Chapters 11–12)
On the eve of the battle to Minerva is [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] with a letter of recommendation from [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]. He repeats the word “twofold.” According to him, about eighty thousand peasants are at stake. ([[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]]) He admits that he himself gave them a "sacred dozen" sparked self-shooters. Labelins are ready to hold up the party of spark copies and self-shooters promised to the peasants if the bishop pays ten ephes for each saved life. Minerva says to him, "I don't trust you personally" - and in the eyewitnesses-officers writes only a bill-deposit of one tenth of the amount, eighty thousand ephes; the rest she promises to pay if everything happens as Buckley described. Mira later calls the deposit forty thousand. Leila checks Minerva and finds out that she does not want the death of twenty thousand peasants even in order to destroy Origin. After this conversation, Mira decides to stop the carnage herself.

At dawn on the Holy Field, thirty miles north of the capital, she passes through a huge army of Origin. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) The bet is in the bottom, at the most disadvantageous place. Mira passes the racers [[stetkhem|Statham]] and Lilliday, passes through the front line of the Northwood "bears" and enters between the armies. With her, Shatterhand, Morlin-May, and the Marquis of Gracend. Six leaders come out to meet, among them Salem of Summerswith and the Tooth Tooth of Lowville. ([[salem|Salem of Summersweet]]; [[zub|Tooth]]) Salem says he's not the chief. After the war, the Southern lords and Adrian's foragers took everything, and the people had nothing to eat. Minerva announces tax reform: Bourne confessed and was put on trial, a firm annual tax is introduced in the Crownlands from the yard, workshop and guild, a bank branch is opened in each city, and you can pay only by contributing to the imperial treasury. The owners of the spark self-shooters are arrested by the azure guard, and the rest of the peasants are told to disarm and go home. Coming Lilliday and Craig Northwood, who received the certificate of the commander-in-chief the day before, she orders to withdraw the troops: “the Crown coped with its own forces.” Erwin's trap breaks.

In the morning, Mira convenes his closest advisors: Leila, Shatterhand, Alexis, banker Conto, Schelier, Ember, Vial, and Morlin-Mei. She lists her successes and says that everything was too easy. Someone, a certain "player", moves it like a spark on a stratum board. He faked Adrian's death and wanted both Erwin's victory and her coronation. One by one, she removes suspicions from Layla, Shatterhand, Vial, and Morlin-Mey. Shelier, who hid the siege of Melorang from her, is too weak to be someone's tool. Amber turns out to be a secret admirer and agent of Erwin, and Mira forgives him with a warning. Finally, she blames Silver Fox. In December, he did not storm the palace, but waited for it and took an oath in his camp, that is, he prepared it for the role of a puppet. Robert's kairas surround the general. Alexis swears to Sophia that he knows nothing about Adrian's death and did not surrender the Labelin on purpose, and puts a spark dagger to his heart. Minerva trusts him and recalls the kire. Alexis asks to listen to him alone, but Layla and Shatterhand refuse to leave, and Mira gives him the floor in front of them. This romance ends.

## Story: Pero - Mark
### The Chancellor's Task (Chapters 1–3)
Former Crown Raven [[mark-voron|Mark]] lives in a dilapidated Origin villa on Milky Avenue. He is guarded by a mysterious old man, Grandpa, who plays a shepherd's dudke-chimp and tells parables, and his young companion Granddaughter. ([[ded|Grandfather]]; [[vnuchok|Grandson]]) On the way from the First Winter, Mark met Iona in the Labelin, who was leaving Waymar without her husband. The [[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]] has evolved into a cheap daily newspaper that glorifies Minerva as the "Carrying World". Erwin, now Lord Chancellor, instructs Mark to find the items of the Dynasty stolen from the train in December, and the missing Lady [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]]. She waited for the verdict in the casemate and disappeared on the last day of the siege. “But the main thing is to find objects!”

Deputy Sheriff Fairfax presents the official version: guilty former mayor Baron Asher. ([[baron-esher|Baron Esher]]) He ordered the items taken out when he learned that the northerners had taken the palace, and the train was captured on the way. Mark sees three oddities in the case. Three drugstores where rat poison was sold burned. Captain [[uitmor|Whitmore]] of the Azure Guard was poisoned too weakly. The two railroads were not clear. Lieutenant August Mays talks about the removal of the Items, and Mark's former subordinate, Agent Red, the best connoisseur of corpses, secretly helps him. At Snake Station, Mark finds the ambush site. In the forest lies a forester nailed with a spear to a tree, then there is the burned inn "Forest Shelter". Mark concludes that the gang was moving fast and sophisticated, headed for the capital and may have fled into it herself. Baron Escher cannot explain this.

### On the trail of the brigade (chapters 4–7)
Mark comes out on the trail of the gang, which prepared the route in advance: the inns, Jonas' blacksmith at the White Stone. She then burned it all behind her. In [[erroubek|Arrowback]] County, Grandfather humiliates the captain of the county guard. In a cake in the Road Table, the hostess tells the legend of the burned inn "[[dzhek-bakler|Jack Buckler]]". In his yard behind the new seven-foot fence, Mark barely escapes from the squad in blue raincoats with spark spears. Leaving the chase in the fog, he falls off his horse and breaks his ribs, and Grandfather and Granddaughter find him by the sound of a jimbuk. At dawn, he is arrested by the people of the parish of [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] and in the prison of Aleridan, his brother Abel is interrogated. It turns out that Grandfather, bound by an eight-year vow not to give his name, is a former imperial judge of the northern lands. He hunts fugitive convict Inge Price, nicknamed Couple, convicted of murder by hire. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]])

Mark finds Inge. He calls himself an honest watchmaker. Raven reveals to him who he is, and offers a deal: Inji asks not to give him to the Empress, the Chancellor, or the Chancellor's sister and to arrange his pupil G in a good boarding house. A separate chapter tells, on behalf of Inji, what happened in December. In "Jack Buckler" arrived two scouts brigade in cherry coats. The first, Rebro, Inji captured: on his forearm under the horseshoe scar was hidden a scheme on the skin called “Absolute”, stolen from the commander. ([[absolyut|The Absolute]]) The second, northerner Ice, tall, with wolf eyes, interrupted the guests and owners. Rib died in a duel with Ice, to which he was forced by Inzhi.

### The Blues and the Absolute (Chapters 8–9)
By train through [[marengo|Marengo]], Mark takes Inge to the capital because the bridge over Back is still destroyed. Grandfather tells the parable of the demon Hen, who secretly brought the boy good luck. Mark forces Inge to confess: in Waymar, he was Minerva's jailer, teaching her almost like a daughter, and when the North was losing the war, he sold her to the killers. Iona, who came with the cairns, saved the girl. The train is stopped by logs on the tracks, and it is captured by "blue". They are the azure guards of Captain Whitmore and Major Backfield, conducting their own investigation. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Backfield is sure that the thief is Erwin, and Count Arrowback holds Objects for him. Whitmore objected: if Erwin Perste had, he would have used them in the siege of the palace. He also considers Asher innocent. He explains that under a special directive of the Crown, the items of the Dynasty are taken with a select azure company to the summer palace in Marengo when the Citadel is threatened.

At Whitmore's cousin's estate, Mark draws a circle around "Jack Buckler" on the map and records questions on points. Grandfather's parable about the wheels in the mechanism tells him the answer. Mark suggests that the entire Northern mutiny could have been a grand distraction for the purpose of stealing Items. The Absolute, he surmises, is immortality, composed of several Objects: “An Object needs another Object, the key.” This is evidenced by Paul's torn crossbow bolt, and overgrown rib skin. The owner of the Rings, according to his calculations, should be close to Erwin. In the list of suspects Raven, to his own horror, brings Jemis Lilliday, Alanis and even Iona. Grandfather answers the parable of the master of Toystone, to whom Agatha in a dream allowed one unpunished murder, and never gives a direct answer.

## Story: The North Bird by Iona
### Elixir and Brother (Chapters 1–2)
Iona brings to the capital a glass bubble the size of a plum with the “elixir of immortality”, which [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] extracted from human life. She reflects on the “signs”: the crow pen in Mira’s diary, the raven-Mark, the divination that ended in a joke. On her way through [[piksi|Pixie]], she sees mass graves. In the capital, she finds Erwin graying on his temples. Her brother chastises her for what she experienced of Vittor: she had to keep the union with the Shaylands, loving her husband. Iona resents Erwin's waste: instead of court, help for the hungry and roads, he arranges celebrations such as the "Festival of the Horge" and "Night of Fireworks". On the wasteland behind the block of medniks, he shows her the place of the future Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant. She is entrusted with the reconstruction of the palace for twenty-five thousand ephes. Robert and Bourne report on the peasant uprising, but Erwin refuses to send an army. Iona consults his mother, Alanis, Robert, Jemis, and [[otets-david|father David]]. She then secretly feeds Martin's elixir to her father, who is dying of [[kamennaya-khvor|stone sickness]].

### The Fairy Tale (Chapters 3–5)
To the cargo pier of the Palace Island comes a false hunchback. With two fingers in the thimbles, she breaks the guard's sword and announces that the Puppeteer has sent Znacharka to heal the old lord. ([[znakharka|The Healer]]) Desmond gives her word that after healing, she will see Sophia and receive a "talking Object". The signer puts her hands down and promises to come back twice more. Later, Desmond gets better: he moves his head and hands and speaks. Why, from the elixir of Martin or at the hands of the Instructor, the text does not directly say. Erwin thinks it's all bait, and he's telling me to grab Znacharka at the gate. Iona, along with [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], secretly takes her to the summer theater and checks her gift on his cut hand. Alanis asks for the scar to be removed, and Znaharka replies that she can, but the price is high. At this moment, the screams of the people of Jemis are heard. The noblewoman runs, tears off the leg of one of the guards and dies in the camp of the Northwoods by the sword of the guard [[verner|Werner]]. Werner himself is immediately killed by a Northwood hatchet.

The body and glowing object requires [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]], threatening a duel. Erwin gives him the post of commander-in-chief of all spark forces instead of General Gore, and the Subject remains with the Origins. The originals are trying to talk to the "Hand of the Witch." ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) The object adjusts to the hand and glows at [[gvenda|Gwenda]], Erwin and [[luis-mariya|Louis]], but does not heal anyone. All three went to the Beyond and all three "white crows". Erwin jokes that there is another chosen one of the Items, "The Master of Wearing Strange Gloves." Iona confesses to Father David in his "first madness": in Waymar, she threw Minerva into prison.

### The Mask of the Chancellor (Chapter 6)
Iona realizes that she misses her husband. Erwin hands her a seven-page letter from Vittor. At the military council, General Gore reports that he has retreated in front of the Snowdrops, and Craig hits him in the jaw. Erwin appoints Craig as interim commander in chief. ## Significance in the Series
The novel moves the conflict from the war of the North with the Crown in search of a true enemy, the Puppet Master, who owns the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]. Here, for the first time, the concepts of [[absolyut|the Absolute]] and the brigade with the Rings are heard. Minerva from the “doll” turns into an independent ruler, and the alliance of Erwin and Minerva is replaced by rivalry. Each of the two main rivals comes to the conclusion that he is ruled by a third force: Erwin writes down the signs of the Puppeteer in a black book, Minerva is looking for a "player" among his own advisers. Open finals - the recognition of Alexis, the fate of prisoner Joaquin, the trace to the Second of the Five - are picked up by the interludes "The Gate of Trust", "The [[glas-zimy|Voice of Winter]]", "[[khochu-poni|I Want a Pony]]" and the novel "Janmei Hunt".
