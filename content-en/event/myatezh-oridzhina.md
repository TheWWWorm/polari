## Name

In the cycle, this war is called the "Northern Flash" - so it, according to Erwin himself, was nicknamed "with the light hand of the singers" ("what is called the Northern Flash - about my rebellion"). The characters also speak of the "Origin mutiny", the "Northern mutiny", and 1774 is called the "Year of the Northern Outbreak". The name "Northern War" is not found and is used here only as a descriptive.

## Causes

In the summer of 1774, an expedition for the River, which [[adrian|Adrian]] instructed to lead [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], came across a secret fort and fighters with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] in [[reka|the Beyond]]; ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) Erwin found Adrian guilty. Returning to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], he became duke after the abdication of the broken [[kamennaya-khvor|stone disease]] [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]]. At the end of August, Erwin read a letter of ultimatum to the emperor before three hundred vassals: to stop sacrilege, to execute the guilty, to release the prisoners, to pay ten thousand ephesas to the families of the victims and to apologize to the House Origin. Twenty-three kairas who doubted him left with his permission.

On the night of September 2, a detachment of horsemen with the Rings destroyed Evergard, the castle of the Dukes of Almera, in an hour; the Duke of [[ajden-almera|Aiden]] died, [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] was badly burned (see Death of Evergard). ([[evergard|Evergard]]; [[dom-almera|House Almera]]; [[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) Adrian issued an edict essentially abolishing the House: all the lords had to appear in the capital and swear in person. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) Mark-Crown, sent to Erwin, was seized: poison was found in his presence, and Erwin considered it an attempt (Mark himself claimed that he took poison for himself). ([[mark-voron|Mark]])

## The course of war

**Invasion of the Southern Way (September-November)** In September, Erwin performed from the First Winter. The border forts on the Lower Twin were abandoned, and he ordered them burned. Entering the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], Erwin divided the army into three parts, and the army began to resemble a trident. Seven battalions of Colonel-General Statham went southwest, to Doyle. Another seven, led by Erwin himself and his cousins, moved directly south along the Trade Route. Six battalions of the Snow Count Lilliday went southeast, to the trading port of [[uindli|Windley]]. The Lords of the Way did not wait for side blows, and the lands that went to Statham and Lilliday remained untouched and rich in provisions. But on the way of Erwin, the barons made a desert: they burned grain and barns, killed peasants who hid supplies, threw corpses into wells. In response, Erwin hanged the captured nobles.

On the Natural Route under Windley, Erwin, drinking the juice of the snake-grass, himself led a cavalry attack against about five hundred knights and ten thousand infantry and archers of the Marquis of Windley. He did not wait for his own infantry, which was two days behind, and the enemy's phalanxes were broken. In mid-October, the assault on Upper Doyle, undertaken by Statham without Erwin's orders, did not take the fortress, and the Northers lost about two hundred men. Under Doyle, Erwin ordered the execution of twenty-five unarmed prisoners, later publicly recognizing this act as shameful, and the city soon surrendered. In Doyle, [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], who had escaped, joined him; her presence was kept secret. Erwin also hosted a knights tournament.

In early November, [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]]'s army waited on the Trade Route in front of the Labelin, hiding behind moats and palisades. ([[labelin|Labelin]]) The Greys moved the carts forward and threw logs across the moats, and the crossbowmen under the rifle shields took aim at the sights. Erwin drove along the enemy system. Those who laid down their weapons, he promised to let them go home, and those who wanted to fight called for a fair fight. Eighteen Labelin knights left against six X's and were knocked out of their saddles; [[brant-stil|Brant Steel]] was among the northern duels. Erwin won the battle, losing, according to him, two people. Labelin's cavalry fled, the infantry surrendered, and the fifteen thousand travelers to whom he had promised food and pay moved to his side, so that the army nearly doubled. Duke Maurice fled by train with family items, and the next day the city opened the gates without a fight.

**Pixie (December 2).** On November 20, a Northwood force under [[krejg-nortvud|Craig]] arrived at Erwin. ([[dom-nortvud|House Northwood]]) Under pressure from the Allies, Erwin agreed to the offensive. Adrian, meanwhile, pretended to lead ten regiments out of the capital, but in Mason's Bay he put seven of them on ships and transferred them to the Southern Way to connect with Alexis Smile, and he himself went to fight with [[moran|Steppe Fire]] in Litland. On the Day of Descent near the town of [[piksi|Pixie]], beyond the Mila River, an army of northerners was caught by surprise by twelve spark regiments of the Silver Fox. ([[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]]) Erwin handed over the command to Statham and in front of the troops ran away with a detachment of the best fighters. The Northerners were defeated: Craig Northwood was captured, the Earl of Main was killed, but Robert held the bridges across the Mila, and about twenty thousand people retreated. For more information, see [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]].

**Capture of the capital and night Labelin (December 6–21).** The escape was a fraud. Erwin, with a detachment of about six hundred kire and archers, boarded the trains at Fairwell Station and raced not north, but past the enemy army into the depopulated [[faunterra|Faunterra]], burning bridges behind him. The Almera barrier on the border of the Crown Lands missed it by letter with the seal of the Gallard Priory. On the night of December 7, the northerners took the Holy Citadel and the Palace of Pen and Sword, turning off the spark in the palace. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) Over him were raised a bat of the Origins and a seagull of Stagforth. For three weeks, Erwin, Damon, Alanis, and a handful of kairs fought off Major Backfield's troops. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Derrek, Brant Steel and [[dzhoj-khemmond|Hammond]] were killed, and [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon]] died from an arrow in the eye. For more information, see Taking Faunterra. ([[bitva-za-faunterru|Capture of Faunterra]])

Meanwhile, Silver Fox entered the Labelin after the victory and fell into a trap, the plan of which Erwin left Statham in a sealed package. Five northern battalions, hiding in the catacombs, went out at night and cut out the army that stood on the stand. Robert Origin captured eight and a half thousand soldiers, and the Northerners captured at Pixie were given freedom (see Night Labelin). ([[reznya-v-labeline|Night in Labelin]]) In retreat, the fox destroyed bridges and rails, and the northern battalions walked to the capital on foot through the snow.

**Dissolution (December 22–28).** Adrian hurried to the capital by train from Aleridan, but on December 22 the bridge over the Back collapsed and the emperor was declared killed by the jester [[menson|Manson]] (see Crash on the Back). ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) The Empress was proclaimed [[minerva|Minerva]] Stagforth. Appearing at the palace with the Glove Yangmei (which in fact could not use - it was a bluff), she convinced Erwin to make peace: she became empress, restored the Chamber and the rights of the Great Houses, and Erwin kept what he had won, remained at court with his troops and received the right to approve her marriage and unions. Thus ended the war; Erwin became Lord Chancellor and de facto ruler of the Empire. By this time on the left bank of the Hanai already stood twelve thousand kire and eight thousand "bears" of Northwood.

## Aftermath

In the spring of 1775, Erwin himself brought his rebellion to the court of the House, calling it directly: "about my rebellion." Mother Cordelia from the Church of the Foremothers and the Black Sea bishop from the Church of the Forefathers blessed his act, Minerva evaded evaluation, citing inexperience, and Prince Hector, the younger Farway, and the envoy of Shayland delivered speeches in favor of the rebellion. Opponents remained silent, and two dozen flags called for a quick vote. When Maurice Labelin demanded the return of the lands lost in the war, the Snow Count Lilliday, a representative of the Origin, spoke in defense of the suzerain: the lands were captured in a just war, in compliance with the laws of honor. Craig Northwood Erwin approved the lands of the South Road, which passed his army, gave half of the trophies of the night Labelin and released him with his brother from captivity. However, the Fangish Knight still considered himself to be deprived of fame: “Why does everyone think that the war was won by just one kair?” However, neither the war nor the alleged death of Adrian solved the main mystery: who owns the Rings. Erwin concluded that the events had a third force, the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]], and Adrian was at best his accomplice. It soon became clear that Adrian had survived.
