## Name
There is no special name for the event. Characters talk about the "taking of Faunterra", "storm of the palace" and "storm of the capital", but most often about the "set of the palace": "in the days of the siege of the palace", "for valor in the defense of the palace".

## Throwing at the capital
On the morning of December 2, 1774, at the beginning of the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]], Erwin, in front of the army, handed over the command to [[stetkhem|Statham]] and rode away with a detachment of the best warriors. On the eve of the battle, a detachment of X-cloaks took Fairwell Station on the banks of the Mila, twelve miles from Pixie, and detained three of the troops there. In a report later received by [[mark-voron|Mark]], the commander of this unit is named Damon, although on the morning of December 2, Damon was at Erwin's camp. ([[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon Origin]]) The Northmen loaded the horses into the wagons and went not to the north, but to the south, past the fighting army. In the refectory car, Erwin declared the truth to the stunned kairs. Escape was the only way to get around the enemy army. Behind them, they will burn a rail bridge across the Mila, and their train will be the last to go to the Land of the Crown. In two days they will be in the empty capital:
> “We will do what no one has done. The day after tomorrow, we'll take Faunterra!"
He didn't hide the risk. If Statham does not fulfill the orders from the package, he will die, or if the bishop left at least one spark regiment in the capital, “we will all die.” The first to shout "Glory to Origina!" was the guide Joaquin Yves Hanna, Alanis' bodyguard. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]])

At night, the bridge over the Mila burned and collapsed into the river. At sunset on 3 December, a company of Almer soldiers met the train at the Crown Lands border station. Kair [[dzhoj-khemmond|Hammond]] handed them to the commander, Sir Godard, with a letter stamped and signed by their suzerain prince, [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], and the Northerners missed without a fight.

## Capture (night from 6 to 7 December)
Faunterra, the "city of two hundred towers", was seen by the North on the night of December 6 to 7. The streets were decorated for the New Year. Two dozen guards at the station were killed, not allowing anyone to leave. Horse Xes split into twenty and disappeared into dark streets so that no one could count them or intercept them. Foot archers and some of the kires were a single unit led by kire Hammond. All the defense they encountered along the way was reduced to three drunken students-theologians. Alanis, called "Alice from the boarding house of Elena-u-Lake", found out from them that there were no troops nearby, and did not allow them to be shot. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]])

The Throne Citadel, the old residence of the emperors in the form of a granite cube, the Xes took first. They climbed up the ropes, set fire to the roof and interrupted the guards who ran out to extinguish them. The palace sent three soldiers to find out what the fire was. Damon and the two Xs changed into their uniforms, crossed Hanai on the suspended Air Bridge and held its far end until the Duke's Hundred crossed there. Erwin then turned off the spark in the palace. The location of the guards and the way to de-energize the palace were tested in August from the prisoner's chief of protection. While the sleepy scarlet guards were looking for candles in the dark, the kairs were cutting them. The gates opened, and Erwin greeted his men with the words "Welcome to the Palace of Feather and Sword!" Alanis exclaimed, “We are the heart of the Empire! We are only one star!

In the morning over the palace instead of a pen and a sword rose a bat of the Origins and a seagull of Stagforth. Erwin immediately warned that by noon thirty thousand people would gather at the bridge: a regiment of scarlet guards from the suburbs, a thousand constables and philistines loyal to the emperor.

Erwin commissioned cyre Derrek the Throne Citadel. It was necessary to explore her dungeons, where more than three hundred Sacred Objects of the Dynasty were stored, in the hope of finding the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]. Kair Hammond was cleaning up the palace and interrogating the servants. Henry Horton, nicknamed [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], received a rear wall facing the navigable fairway. [[brant-stil|Brant Steel]], along with Erwin, held the front over the bridge. Damon had to replace his cousin: Erwin wanted his grandson Agatha to command every fight. The best shooter of the Northerners John Sokolik was at Erwin, and Alanis got a dove. ([[dzhon-sokolik|John Falcon]]) On the same day, fifty-three birds carried the news of the capture of the capital to all the cities of the Empire and the call for [[minerva|Minerva]] Stagforth to arrive for the coronation.

## Siege
### The first days
Commanded by the besiegers, Major Croydon Backfield, head of the Lord's Secret Guard and commander of the Red Guard Battalion, nicknamed the Red Sickle. He offered Erwin to surrender, and in response received the arrow of Socolik. The first attack directly on the bridge, with a ram and stairs, the northerners were allowed to the gate and shot. Erwin then set in motion "Yanmean engineering": with a wheel in a copper box, he started the mechanism, and the central span of the bridge stood up, throwing the guards into the water. Damon calculated the losses: “Losses are a hundred to zero.”

The townspeople throughout the embankment made stones and began to shell the island. At night, Backfield threw a lot of boats with guardsmen and men in the same towel at the island. The Northerners recaptured them, and a simultaneous breakthrough through the breach in the bridge. In the hand-to-hand tower, Erwin's shoulder was dislocated. The Citadel vault was empty after a night of hacking. While the cairns broke three doors, the Items were taken out on a trolley on a secret rail. Alanis guessed it was Burgmaster Escher. ([[baron-esher|Baron Esher]]) Derrek's foray into the town hall did nothing: the cairn returned without the Rings and lost its men. Soon, Backfield hung on the bank of the shields with the inscription: “Silver Fox won. Help will not come.”

### Steel's outing and fire
Brant Steel, a former Grey of Derrek, volunteered to take revenge and with a horse detachment walked along the waterfront, burning stones and dispersing the burghers. On the way back, a bolt entered his throat over his breastplate, and Steel died. Thirty horses returned without saddles. After his outing and the fall of the Cathedral Citadel in the infirmary, arranged in the barracks of the palace guard, there were more than fifty wounded. At night, the barracks were set on fire by "reliable" palace servants, and most of the wounded were burned. Erwin demanded to find the culprits and kill their relatives, but Hammond took him to the wall: at this point, an unprecedentedly strong assault began. All the prisoners taken at the Citadel were executed by Backfield, and their bodies were thrown to the besieged.

Backfield was approached by reinforcements. First, a half-battalion from the Duchy of Hope arrived by train, then two hundred marines from Marengo. A few days after Steel's foray, tapes arrived with news of the night's Labelin: Statham and Robert defeated Alexis' army and went to the rescue, but on foot, through the snow, in about twenty days. ([[reznya-v-labeline|Night in Labelin]])

### Exhaustion
Backfield harassed the garrison with attacks at any time of the day and shelled it with rotting corpses of people and animals until the frosts festered the cemeteries. His men also fired arrows, scrawled in impurity, to make the wounds rot. Every couple of hours, someone from the North died: of the six hundred brought by Erwin, barely three hundred remained combatable, and by the arrival of Minerva, according to the report of Backfield, only two hundred and twenty Northers remained in the palace. Erwin, by his own admission, made mistakes from fatigue. He released the palace servants, executing only ten, whom he considered guilty of fire, and they told the enemy about the weakness of the garrison. He did not expect an attack on the fresh ice of Hanoi. Once he believed in a false attack on the Air Bridge, while the main forces of the enemy on the sleigh landed from the northern end of the island. A direct hit from the catapult mortally wounded Hammond's cyre, and Erwin himself finished him off with a blow of mercy. Some of the archers of the Falcon escaped on the ice, grabbing the palace trinkets. Erwin and Alanis quarreled: secretly from him she sent Joaquin with a letter to the Earl of Arrowback from Almera (see Crash on the Back). ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) Erwin considered this plan “dirty and disgusting” and expressly banned it.

The forces of the besiegers melted away in the same way. Less than a company remained of the battalion of the scarlet guard, the battalion of Hope was defeated in two assaults, the police lost a third. Backfield had to throw in the fight mercenaries, bandits, beggars, free companies of deserters. After storming the half-battalion from Marengo, it turned out that Damon had an arrow in his eye. In the infirmary, he awoke for a moment and said, “I’m scared. I don't want to die." Erwin, unable to tell the truth, swore that his cousin would survive, and promised him a scar, "as you dreamed." Damon's dead. The defense was taken by Forty-Two, wearing Damon's armor. Erwin fell in the snow in exhaustion, and he dreamed of Agatha the Radiant, terrible and skinny like a mummy. She read him a tape from Robert: "Cousin, we're going... One week left. Hold on... Post script: It gets worse."

## Interchange
On December 22, Adrian, who was rushing to the capital by train from Aleridan, was declared dead in a crash at Back. The Empress was proclaimed Minerva. In Faunterra, she arrived with Alexis and immediately condemned Beckfield's methods: according to her, he did not frighten the kire, but angered them. Minerva ordered to dress all the soldiers on the shore in red uniforms and create trebuchets a band of fire on the ice behind the island. Backfield was ordered to take a hundred men "unburdened with piety" and force the keepers of the Church of Mercy to open a mine with the Yangmei Gauntlet of Power, the only Item left in the capital. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]])

With the Glove on Minerva's hand, one entered the palace. She saw a "skinny and sick old man" with gray strands, not a legendary general. Erwin, who by then had twelve thousand kairs and eight thousand Northwood “bears” stationed on the left bank of the Khanai, was awaiting capitulation. Minerva showed Yangmei the Glove and gave three arguments. Erwin figured out that she could not own the Glove, and she realized that he did not own the Rings, because he believed her trick. The treaty was sealed by the word of Agatha's grandson and the word of the lady of the Yangmei family. Minerva did not marry Erwin, kept her troops and continued Adrian's reforms. Erwin obtained the right to approve her marriage and treaties with the Great Houses and remained at court with his troops. Fire barrels falling on the ice outside the window, he called the fireworks in honor of her coronation. See also Northern Outbreak. ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]])

## Aftermath
- Of the commanders with whom Erwin entered the capital, Damon, Derrek, Hammond and Brant Steel died. After Steel's death, [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]] was appointed company commander of the X-cloaks. Forty-two survived, Damon's former assistant and favorite.
- On December 7, upon learning of the seizure of the palace, Burgomaster Escher ordered the removal of the Sacred Objects from the capital. The train with them went to Marengo; on the way, one hundred and fifty selected guards were killed, and the Objects were stolen. In the spring of 1775, this case was investigated by Mark. He found out that one of the three trains that the northerners came on was a "custom" train with a reinforced wagon for valuables. He was served in Fairwell by order of an unknown rich man under a false name, and later it was on him that the guards took out the Items.
- [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] later reproached Erwin with a mountain of corpses on his conscience: "The Battle of Pixie is seven thousand dead on both sides. The night labelin is nine thousand. The siege of the palace is another thousand or two.”
- In the days of the siege, according to the text, “the ring friendship of the X-cloaks was forged.” Therefore, the X-cloaks later became jealous of [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]: he did not stand with them on the palace wall and did not weep for Damon and Brant Steel, but became the Duke's best friend.

## In "Fantasies"
In the story "Let's catch him" (in the summer of 1776, according to the author - one of the possible fates of the heroes), Robert Origin appeared the ghost of Damon with a fragment of an arrow in the eye socket. He rebuked his brother: “I died because of you. You could have come to Faunterra faster. I waited, but I did not wait.” Robert pleaded not guilty: he pointed to the extreme difficulty of the offensive in the winter, to the fatigue of the soldiers after the “double battle in the Labelin” and to the hotness of Damon himself, which made him reckless in battle. Most of all, Robert was upset that his brother did not feast on the Star in the palaces of Agatha, but wandered unrepentant, chained to his bones.
