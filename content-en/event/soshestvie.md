The descent of the Mothers is a legendary event that begins the story of Polari: the saints of the Mothers and Forefathers came to the world of people. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) From it is kept the count of years ("March 21, 1774 from the Descent of the Mothers"), and it is dedicated to the main winter holiday of the empire - Descent Day (see [[kalendar|Calendar]]). Holy Scripture includes the book "The History of the Descent", which is quoted in chapters and verses.

## Before Descent

Little is known about the time before the Descent. Even before the arrival of the Foremothers, as Erwin thinks, nomadic tribes roamed the Beyond, but even they gathered with spirit, crossed the Crystal Mountains and settled in Polaris - so bad was the land behind the mountains.

## Canon and Traditions

The way in which the forebears came into the world, the Scripture does not directly explain. According to the canonical version, the gods opened a tunnel for them directly from the Under Kingdom. Some philosophers teach that the Forebears descended from the Star, for the Star communicates with the Underworld "on the principle of dualism." The northern zookeepers say that far out beyond [[reka|the River]], there is a bottomless lake with an underwater "Bubble-city", from which they left and where the Foremothers returned. But all versions agree on one thing: the first days in the sublunar world, the Forebears spent in the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]. Therefore, the mountains are full of holy places and legends and from time immemorial attract pilgrims - blessed, sick, poor and desperate. It was even said that the Foremothers “went into the world from these very rocks and brought love with them.”

Descending from the mountains, the Forebears spent the first winter in the valley, which in honor of this was called the First Winter. The valley is revered as the place of Descent; according to the Polari Land Guide, it is "the holy place where Agatha's feet first touched the lands of Polaris." One of the shrines of the Origins is the grotto of Kosuli. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) The very name of the land of Origin, given to it later, in the northern dialect means "the source, the beginning" and, among other things, recalls the beginning of the procession of the Forebears on the earth.

Then the mothers went south. In year 1, according to the “Guide”, they passed through the lands of the future Southern Way in search of a site for a city; it was spring, the rivers had overflowed, and the Forebears did not settle in the marshy region. When they reached Canaan, they built boats and went down the river to where Faunterra was founded, six hundred miles south of the First Winter. Minerva, suffering from the southern heat, considered this decision “pure madness”.

## The first centuries

Scripture tells us that the Forebears with the help of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]] healed the sick, called for rain and sun, and created the first state. In 36 it consisted of only two cities, Faunterra and Orwell, and fought off the nomads of the Steppe. Father Wilhelm overcame them with the Rings. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) By 69, according to David's father, Faunterra blossomed, Orwell and Arden grew up, and Wilhelm proclaimed himself King of Polaris; in the same year, four Forebears founded the [[sadovniki|Gardeners]]' teachings.

## Day of Descent

The anniversary is celebrated on December 2. The streets are decorated with garlands, gifts are given to the Day of Descent, noble ladies arrange concerts; the companion of the holiday is hot wine with "a pleasant smell of zest and cinnamon". Descent is considered a “festival of miracles”: according to Adrian, on this day “the most incredible dreams are realized and strange miracles are performed.” On this day, "the door opens with greater ease" - it is easier to get to the rulers with a petition. There's a Descent Day chapel in Faunterra. It sounds and congratulations: "The forebears in the world came down, we won."

The day is considered holy, and in winter it is not accepted to fight. However, on the Day of Descent there were decisive battles. On December 2, 1774, during the Northern Flash, the Crown's spark army defeated Origin's army at the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]], and [[adrian|Adrian]]'s army at Litland defeated the horde of Steppe Fire at the Orange River. ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]]) On December 2, 1775 there was a battle for the First Winter, which the troops of the Kuklovod intended to complete by taking the city on holiday. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Origin]] was going to return to the First Winter on the Day of Descent, “like the Foremothers.”

## The Author's Article
In 2018, the author published an article on the Polaripedia — the cycle wiki on Fandom — entitled "The Religion of the Forebears". ([Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%A0%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%B3%D0%B8%D1%8F_%D0%9F%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%B9)) It is written before the last books of the cycle and retells the Descent from church sources. Here's what she adds.

- **Way.** How the Forebears got from the kingdom of the gods to the under-moon world is unknown. The first document, the Yangmei Diaries, begins when they have already been to the Beyond. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) From August to December, the Forebears walked about two thousand kilometers through the forests and swamps of the Beyond, then through the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]], and on December 2 they stood for wintering in the valley of the First Winter.
- **Testing.** For such a campaign, the ancestors, who grew up in the kingdom of the gods, were not ready: they had no pack animals, no supplies, and no warm clothes. At the Descent, one Mother, Ulyana, and twelve Forefathers were killed. ([[pramater-ulyana|Ulyana the Sorrowful]]) Records along the way were kept by one Yangmei, the rest of the memoirs were written later from memory, and almost all the drawings of those days were destroyed - probably the Forebears looked sorry for them. The Church sees in the descent a test of the spirit by which the gods have tested the readiness of the messengers for the mission.
- **First year.** In the spring of 1 year, the Forebears moved south through the lands of present-day Origin and the Southern Way, in June they went to the upper reaches of Hanoi and rafted to the place of the future Faunterra. By December, they set up hunting and fishing and overwintered in huts, and from the age of 2 they took up their mission in full force.
- **Let's count.** The years were initially counted from arrival in the First Winter. Then it turned out that the shortest day of the year falls on the twenty-sixth day after the Day of Descent. The beginning of the year was moved to the first lengthening day, and the year was divided into months by the lunar cycle. So the last day of the year was December 28, and the holiday of Descent remained December 2.

## What Really Happened

In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], the mystery of the Descent is revealed. According to Lady [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille]], who transmits knowledge of the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]], in the first years after the Descent, Polaris was poisonous and inhabited by monsters, and people hid in burrows. The ancestors raised the "First Tree" with three roots - in the [[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless abyss]], in the [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]] and under the valley of the First Winter - which changed the nature of the continent. The reactors proved to be too heavy even for the Yangmei Glove, and they had to be carried across the continent in the usual ways.

[[navi|Navi]] completed the story. The descent was the crash of a colonial starship — a flying city with two hundred crew, six thousand colonists, terraforming reactors, and biosphere embryos — knocked off course by a false beacon, the Star. Its wreckage lies at the bottom of the Bottomless Gap. The Foremothers and Forefathers are the surviving crew, the commoners are descendants of the colonists. The colonists wanted to expend the energy of the three surviving bioreactors on a distress signal, but Captain Wilhelm and Chief Engineer Janmei, having heeded the arguments of navigator Elena and astrophysicist Agatha, suppressed their mutinies by force and began bioforming. The forebears crossed the hostile continent on foot, carrying reactors on the shoulders of robots, laid them in the "roots" of the Tree - they needed three deep reservoirs approximately at the tops of the triangle, but there was only a Smoky Dahl and the Bottomless Gap, and the third reactor was placed in a cavity under the valley of the First Winter - and managed to change the nature of one continent, protecting it from the rest of the planet. When it became clear that the descendants had to live here for centuries, they swore to keep the truth: “who is willing to know that you are a victim of a shipwreck.”

Thus, the “first days in the Crystal Mountains” and the “first wintering” of the Forebears receive a new explanation: the valley of the First Winter was the place where they found the cavity for the third reactor, the entrance to which is now called the Kosuli Grotto. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]])

## In "Fantasies"

In "Fantasies" (the possible fates of the heroes), the victory of Origin at the First Winter on the Day of Descent of 1775, the higher mothers considered the capitol supporting Erwin to be a sign of rightness, and "enjoyed their rightness" for exactly two months - from the Descent to the death of Archimandrite Ellin on February 2, 1776. ([[ellina|Ellina]]) [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]’s address to the Church set the Day of Descent (1776) as the deadline for repentance for all those who used the Ring of Wilhelm in battle: those who do not repent to this day “will be found, tried and justly executed.”
