The 17th Gift is the last Gift of the Gods, known to the Empire before the start of the cycle. He came in 1756 in the forests of [[shejland-zemlya|Shayland]], midway from [[uejmar|Waymar]]. For the small county of bankers, this event became the main in history: according to the “Traveller of the lands of Polari”, Dar raised the authority of the House of Shayland, undermined by marriage with the bankers, “the gods themselves blessed Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent]] and his children.” The mystery found in his bed twenty years later became the basis of the conspiracy of the Puppeteer.

## Fall and fire
The gift came late at night in the forest wilderness on the west bank of [[torrej|Torrey]]. The earth shuddered for twenty miles around, thousands of acres of forest caught fire. For a week, the count’s army, peasants and townspeople fought the fire: they turned the forest road into a seepage twenty yards wide and fought off the fire for two days until the rain broke. The bed cooled very slowly. Its walls were covered with slippery volcanic glass, and people were dying from falls, poisonous fumes, burns, collapses, and "Flying Objects." For more on the bed, see [[dary-bogov|Gifts of the Gods]].

## War for Objects
The sunsets came from the west, where the fire was weaker, defeated the tired Shaylands and occupied the bed. [[graf-rantigar|Count Rantigar]]'s army, led by General [[oris|Oris]], fortified the camp around him. According to [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], this was not an ordinary summer raid: the Westerners came for the Objects. Count Vincent called for allies. The fleet of the Earl of Fleming transferred two battalions of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] to the south, Oris retreated the day before the approach of the Kairs and was defeated. ([[fleming|Benedict Fleming]]) Westerners managed to take out twelve items. Desmond marched west to get them back, and this hike cost the Sunset Coast three burned cities. Part of the northern army then stood in Silens, waiting for tribute, and the kairs guarded the bed for several months and did not allow anyone there.

Details: [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]], the land of the Origin. ([[oridzhin-zemlya|Lands of Origin]])

## Celebrations in Waymar
In August 1756, the victors and court came to Waymar: Desmond with the twelve-year-old [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]], Emperor Thelurian with Prince [[adrian|Adrian]], [[ajden-almera|Aiden]] and [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]], the Archpriest of Ellin, [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] with a young wife [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]]. ([[ellina|Ellina]]) Shayland paid off the Origin Items instead of gold. Of the hundred and forty-two objects of the Gift, Count Vincent received thirty-nine, and a third received the Crown. This began the Shaylands' friendship with the Origins, later crowned by the marriage of Iona and Vittor. ([[brak-vittora-i-iony|Wedding of Iona and Vittor]])

On the altar of Waymar Cathedral, two New Gift Items were exhibited. The Archpriest called them the Light Sphere and the Rose of Happiness. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) The same Sphere in 1774, Vittor will be awarded to merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] in "[[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]]". In the same days, orphan Mick meets a young Nathaniel in Waymar, who awakens the Sphere in the cathedral ("[[teper-ty-koldun|Now you are a sorcerer]]"). ([[mik|Mik]]) According to [[navi|Navi]], [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria Northwood]] was conceived at a feast for young Vittor.

## The Secret Lodge (The Price of Fear)
On the eve of the celebrations, two grays found in the depths of the bed a transparent sarcophagus with a silver-haired girl pierced by a fragment of the breed. Her body didn't decompose. Captain Arthur Hyroc brought the news to Desmond, and he tried to secretly show the find to the emperor; the feast of Dar was postponed while the lord examined the bed. ([[artur-khajrok|Arthur Highrock]]) In the cave, Thelurian, Adrian, Desmond, Aiden, and Hyroc decided that the dead Mother, who died at the Descent, would undermine faith in the infallibility of the Forebears, and the New Descent would strengthen the Church. Desmond demanded to call the archimper, but under threat of weapons swore to remain silent. The body was ordered to be burned in lava, and the two Greeks who found it were ordered to be executed. Adrian removed something from the deceased's neck "for memory" and whispered: "Such as I ..."

Young [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]] and [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] have been watching the oath. Their bodyguards and novice from the retinue of the archimper were killed, and Vittor bought the brothers life with fear. Desmond became the guarantor of their silence. Out of the lava crawled a burned savage with no memory, whom the huntsmen found at the lodge, and called himself Paul. “[[tsena-strakha|The Price of Fear]].”

## Aftermath
- **Waymar's prisoner.** For about a year Paul lived in the tower of Waymar Castle, then Count Vincent kept him in prison for several years. Around 1769 the prisoner fled, a week later the count died, and Vittor became ruler of Shayland. Paul's primrose gave Vittor power over the Objects (see primrose). ([[pervokrov|Firstblood]])
- **Oath of the Lord.** Silence over the body connected Telurian, Adrian, Desmond and Aiden. In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], Gallard thanks Paul for "giving fire" to Aiden, one of those who silenced Dar.
- **Objects of Gift.** [[chrevo|The womb]] lay on the fifth, most dangerous level of the bed. A [[kaplya-solntsa|drop of the Sun]] is buried with Empress [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]]: in the "Yanmei Hunt", Erwin guessed from her portraits that this is one of the missing Gift Items.
- **Pilgrimage.** By the time of the cycle, the bed had become a place of pilgrimage with a chapel and a monument to the knights, and the levels deeper than the second are closed. In May 1775, Hog and [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] took the envoys of the Sunset Coast ([[khash-ejlish|Hash Eilish]]) there. ([[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]])

## The solution in the “Shadow of the Great Tree”
According to the story of Navi, the Seventeenth Gift is the crash site of a starship on which three "gods" flew: Navi, Paul, and Lilanne. ([[navi|Navi]]) Lillianna died on impact, and her body was apparently found in the sarcophagus. The Fallen Cult honors her as the very Mother of the Lodge of Gift.

## The Gift in the Teachings of the Gardeners (The Gates of Trust)
In the Gate of Trust, vassals of the Lady in Darkness of the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] say that the Tree is swaying, and the Serpent gained strength after the Seventeenth Gift. ([[vrata-doveriya|The Gates of Trust]]) The Queen reassures them: the Serpent means nothing to the Tree, and the Gift only brought the Day of Salvation closer.
