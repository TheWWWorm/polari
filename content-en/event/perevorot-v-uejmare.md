## Background
In May 1775, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] returned to her husband in [[uejmar|Waymar]] with a detachment of forty northerners. Her relationship with [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]] was broken: the count freed [[martin-shejland|Martin]], and after the massacre, which Seymour Steel threw in the One-Eyed Inn, publicly humiliated his wife. In private, he called her "ice" and "northern trash." Iona investigated the fate of the prisoner who had escaped from the castle prison, and the death of the old Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent]].

Then she heard from old [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] about the "two arrows": the first is needed for shooting, the second kills. So Iona realized that the Puppeteer was sending letters with Ulyanina Dust: the joker Manson read one letter and raped the lackey, and after reading the second, killed Adrian. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Lady [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] went naked for breakfast after reading the book. Iona concluded that the Puppet Master was her husband. The last straw was the words of the sunset [[khash-ejlish|Hash Eilish]] in the fugitive’s cell: “Your husband is a happy man.” Kira [[irving|Irving]] Iona sent with her ring to the nearest city outside Shayland to send birds to Erwin and herself to Faunterra. She gave the command and detailed instructions to Kyra Brandon. One of Seymour's grays she left in the ruins of the monastery, where she led a secret passage from the count's office.

## Deployment of forces
Seymour estimated that Iona had thirty-seven men in the castle. They were confronted by a company of penny-makers in the yard, fifty riflemen in the galleries and probably the same number in the towers, as well as a hundred reserves in the barracks. In total, the count had about three hundred soldiers, eight times the superiority, as he himself shouted during the battle. In addition, the Count kept Wilhelm's Feather in the cache. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) Iona knew the rest of the Rings were far from Waymar. She suspected that one husband left with him, but hoped that he would not kill her: he needed her alive.

## Developments
### Evening
On the evening of 27 May, Vittor announced that the northerners would leave the castle that night: the ship was waiting in the river port. Iona humbly obeyed and gave Brandon detailed orders: ten men in storerooms for provisions, the rest to saddle horses and build in the yard in two columns. Departure is scheduled for 10:30. Joaquin was alarmed that the orders sounded too detailed, "like a sergeant." The garrison poured into the yard, allegedly for a farewell parade, but in fact stood on alert. The Count took guests from the Sunset Coast, Hash Eilish and Lacht Misa, along with Harry Hog and Joaquin to dinner in his office on the fourth floor of the donjon. ([[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]]; [[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]])

Iona dressed up in a riding dress. She attached the dagger to the belt, and the spark dagger hid in the sleeve. The maid was ordered to bring two jugs of oil, citing the fact that the lamps fade. At 10:06, Seymour asked if she was scared. Iona told how at the age of four she first saw death, and about the lullaby, which she has been singing to herself since then, when she is afraid: “Star rise, look in the eye...”.

### Assassination of commanders
At 10:15 Iona called [[garold-greta|Sir Harold]], commander of the garrison, out of the window of the castellan, allegedly because of issues with the provisions. She gave the short-sighted castellan a book page instead of a list of supplies and, while he was bringing it to his eyes, stabbed him in the throat right through the paper. Seymour turned his neck to his squire at the same moment. At 10:20, Iona went barefoot on the stairs, threw dresses and shirts on the steps, poured oil on them and draped them on the walls. From the second floor, he and Seymour threw the lamp up the stairs. Donjon broke out, and the count was cut off by fire on the fourth floor. The library, the guest and castellan rooms, Iona's bedroom were burning.

Running out into the courtyard shouting "Save the count!", Iona approached Captain Leus, the right hand of the castellan, who was in charge of extinguishing. Hanging in his arms, she killed him with a spark dagger, and Seymour stabbed his aide. At 10:30 a.m., the clock tower broke through.

### Fight in the yard
Immediately four heavy horsemen flew out of the stable in full armor. They rushed through the soldiers with buckets and spears, turned at the gate and walked back along the walls. Eight of Stevens's kire, allegedly sent for provisions, seized the tar tower and flooded the yard with fire from barrels of resin. The fire divided the garrison into small groups without commanders. The rest of the kire two lines moved from the center to the walls, hiding from the windows of the donjon barracks, horses and the miners themselves. A few keels burst onto the wall. The reserve was commanded by Lieutenant Blask, and Kair Brandon killed him with a sharp dagger throw.

In the office, Joaquin urged the count to go to the balcony and take command. At first, Vittor waved, “I have it! Damn it! Three hundred! The man!” Then he threw a bottle into his father’s portrait, pulled it out of a secret niche in the floor of Wilhelm’s Feather, and threw it to the barber. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) Harry Hog put on a Perst and from the count's balcony killed three keels on the wall. The Count forbade him to kill his wife and told him only to frighten her.

### Iona and the Phalange
Iona decided that Vittor was shooting himself, and realized that her husband was afraid of her. She came out of the shadow and went straight to the triple range of the reserve. Fireballs burst at her feet, the pavement was swollen with bubbles, the dress smoldering, the feet were covered with burns, but she walked and sang lullabies in a whisper. Stopping three steps from the enemy, she said:
> “My husband is a criminal and a heretic. He has committed grave crimes, and I will bring him to justice. But I wish you no harm. Put your weapons down and leave. The gate is open.”

Some soldier threw a spear at her and missed. Iona refused to let Cyre Brandon execute him and let him go. Then, line after line, she ordered the soldiers to step forward and disperse, and the reserve left the gate. Separate groups of the garrison began to surrender. Brandon reported that Perst was carrying Harry Hog, who was shot by Kair Stevens, that the Earl had fled and that the loss of the Northerners was still nine men. Iona said, "When the fire is over, get up and take the puppeteer."

### The Count's Escape
Meanwhile, Vittor refused to give the Ring to Joaquin, saying that he needed special blood. He didn’t want to go into the yard either. Martin opened the wall panel, and the count with his brother, the knights Bert and Reynolds and Joaquin went down in secret. But the passage to the monastery was blocked: apparently, it was made by a gray, which Seymour left in ruins. The count did not dare to break through the dungeon and yard, and they hid in the dungeon, "neither give nor take rats."

### Fracture
Joaquin felt ashamed, and he returned to the balcony. The sunsets briefly brought the mortally wounded Harry to his senses. Joe picked him up on the balustrade and guided his hand himself. The first shot was fired through Seymour's kair, then the grey that once kicked Joaquin lost its leg, and Brandon's kair was left without its head; several more northerners were burned by fire, and one gray was burned on the run to the donjon's door. Recognised county crossbowmen began to beat where Joe was shining fireballs. The second arrow hit Harry's chest, and he soon died, "and with him the Feather." The North didn’t know that.

Joaquin shouted to Iona that her dozen fighters had no chance against fifty crossbowmen and Perst, and offered to surrender. Vittor from the darkness of the bedroom swore, "Iona, I will forgive you and let yours go!" Iona went out to the burning barrel and gave up. Thirteen of the surviving kairs clung around her and didn't want to leave until she ordered, "He won't kill me. Erwin will save me. Go and help him.”

### Slaughter
When the Kairs moved to the gate, Vittor for the first time in the whole battle went to the balcony and ordered the archers to fire a volley. Of the thirteen northerners in patches of arrows fell only three. The rest retreated with a box, covering the lady. At the gate, Martin shouted at the gate tower: “Throw a net!” A wire network fell to the northerners, which was hit by an electric discharge, and the front ones died on the spot. Iona, wounded by an arrow in her leg, stabbed herself under her left chest. The last three kairas were scored by archers.

Martin exulted, and Vittor slipped impotently down the wall with the words: "This is the end... We are dead..."

## Aftermath
### In the castle
The count, whose heart “caught”, sent his brother to restore order. Martin announced to the soldiers that the "north bitch" wanted to kill her husband and take the county, but the count was "kissed by the gods" and he has talking Objects and the Wilhelm's Fingers. Chedder asked if it was a catapult. Then Martin ordered to nail to the gates of the cyre with the chest burned through, “so that every blind person could see.” Martin made Joaquin his assistant instead of Harry's dead.

The knife in Iona's chest closed the wound, and she survived. Martin wanted to pull out the blade, but the knight [[ajvi|Ivy]] grabbed his hand. Vittor healed his wife with the Object to keep the hostage and show strength to the sunsets. Martin, mocking, stood with his foot on the throat of the unconscious prisoner. The soldiers who fled the castle told in the city inn that Iona showed nobility: she let all those who wanted to leave alive, even throwing a spear at her.

### War
Kyr Irving, with Iona's letter and the only remaining Kyr, reported the incident to Erwin. The news caught him in the House of Representatives on May 29: “My Lord, trouble with your sister.” Erwin considered his sister dead, publicly called Wittor Shayland the Puppeteer and started a war against him: he blocked Waymar, arrested Shayland representatives and bankers, moved the northern battalions. He admitted that he was mistaken, considering the head of the heretics of the parish of Gallard, but declared him a suspected accomplice.

Iona remained a prisoner of her husband for more than six months. She was locked up and then locked in a cage. Richard "Ice", who arrived in Waymar with the Sun Drop, along with Vittor and Martin forced her to rewrite the fake confession in connection with Erwin. ([[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]]; [[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]) The Count wrote down the order to Dust, and was later delivered to Erwin by the enchanted Nexia Flame.

In June, the northerners landed in Waymar, occupied the port and executed one in four citizens. During the night assault, Vittor used the Sword of the Gods, but Desmond Origin replaced most of the kairs with townspeople in advance and destroyed the Belomorye battalion of the Earl of Fleming. The remnants of Shayland's army were besieged on the Castle and Bald Hills. Prisoner Iona was taken out of the walls to show her kairam alive in exchange for provisions.

## Ratings
- Joaquin considered the order of the count to shoot at the surrendered “worst of all possible”: the count, “the master of intrigue and the genius of finance”, showed himself to be a complete fool in tactics.
- Joaquin himself did not despise the Count’s cowardice: for a year he understood “the value of life, the wisdom of retreat, the folly of heroism.” However, the fact that the count abandoned his people seemed to him too much.
- Iona herself during the battle considered it her only mistake that she came under fire barefoot.
- Erwin, after receiving both letters, considered the sister of the deceased: the report of the surviving cyre ended in her suicide.

## Trivia
- Starting with the murder of the castellan, Iona for a quarter of an hour deprived the garrison of all senior commanders: the castellan, Captain Leus and Lieutenant Blask.
- The heavy cavalry in the courtyard of the castle seemed to Vittor "nonsense": there, according to him, there was nowhere to turn.
