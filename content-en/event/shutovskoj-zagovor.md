The Shutov plot is a failed palace coup, conceived by [[menson|Manson]] Louise Violetta of the Yangmei family, the younger brother of Emperor Telurian and the uncle of the future Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]]. The name of the conspiracy was received by the shameful fate of its head, who was made a court jester. Duke Aiden Almer's book "The Fool's Plot: Persons and Roles" tells more about him. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) She read Peace in the palace archive in the spring of 1774. ([[minerva|Minerva]])

## Background
Two decades earlier, Manson was one of the most powerful men in the empire. He directed the Crown’s naval forces, commanded a corps of ten thousand marines and three hundred ships, and was charismatic, brave, and beloved. At the age of twenty-eight, he married Lady [[karen|Karen]], daughter of Duke Jonas of the Great House of Lighthart, who ruled the then richest land of the empire - [[nadezhda|Hope]]. Manson lavishly distributed court and navy posts to his friends; Lighthart coats of arms, according to Aiden, flashed at the court more often than imperial ones. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) The Telurian, who pitted the Great Houses for the sake of balance ("The weakness of the Lords leads to the might of the Crown"), could not calmly look at the growth of Hope. He publicly chastised his brother under Lord Ingrid and Prince Adrian, and Manson replied, "The wrong head wears a crown!" In the morning, Manson's squadron went on a year-long voyage with an inspection of the ports and fortresses of the far West. Back, Manson conceived a coup: in the event of Telurian's death, he would become regent under the underage Prince Adrian.

In the early media, the background was different: Manson managed the seaports of the Crown Lands and the fifteen thousandth corps, married at twenty-seven years old, and fell into disgrace, trying to give Lightharts a loan of half a million hilt to the spark shop; the emperor canceled the loan and expelled his brother from the capital for a year.

### Lighthart Version
In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], the story of the conspiracy is told differently - from the Lighthart House side. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) In the mid-eighteenth century, Duke Jonas Caroline May of the lineage of Elena ruled all of [[nadezhda|Hope]]. He received a quarter of all gold mined there, took taxes from two trade routes, held an army of ten thousand swords and was inferior in aggregate influence only to Vladyka Telurian. Having achieved everything, he found himself the last goal - to bequeath the crown to his grandchildren. Emperors never married a lady of the family Elena, so the Duke chose a workaround and introduced the daughter of the brother of Emperor [[menson|Manson]], not constrained by tradition. At the wedding, the guests said that “such a glorious couple has not yet worn the earth.” The duke then gave his son-in-law a few hints, and Manson "answered them roughly as the oil answers the fire": Tellurian, "this frowning bore", must go to the Star, and the throne will go to Manson and the Duke's daughter. The plot involved Jonas’ two eldest sons, both serving at court and being “very helpful.” The youngest son, too devoted to knightly ideals, and the daughter, who desperately loved her husband, were not secretly dedicated.

Thus, the book Aiden calls the head of the conspiracy of Manson, and this story is the Duke of Lighthart, who only directed the ambition of his son-in-law.

## Conspiracy and its disclosure
More than two dozen influential noblemen joined Manson, including several captains of the guard; commanders loyal to Tellurian were expelled from the Palace Island under various pretexts, and three battalions of marines stood in the suburbs. One of the conspirators, according to Aiden, was the guard captain John Corvis, a native of [[aleridan|Aleridan]]. Upon learning that his sister-in-law, Aiden, would arrive at the palace on the day of the coup, Corvis awkwardly tried to dissuade the Duke from traveling. Aiden suspected a lie, threw the captain into the dungeon of [[evergard|Evergard]] Castle, and he died under torture, having managed to tell the details of the plan. Aiden bought a train, rushed to Faunterra with forty swords and warned the emperor. Tellurian did not give up his awareness and even played a game of strata with his brother in the evening. ([[stratemy|Stratems]])

On the appointed night, twenty-five conspirators entered the Palace of Pen and Sword, struck the spark of the faithful azure guards and broke into the chambers, where they were waited by the knights of the emperor and duke with crossbows and spark spears. ([[lazurnaya-gvardiya|Azure Guard]]) Two committed suicide.

## Slaughter
The trial went all over the empire. For a whole week in the square of the Forefathers, three executions were carried out a day: some were burned, others were quartered, three were buried alive, four were refreshed. According to the book of Aiden, Duke Jonas Lighthart and his three sons died on the plow; Lighthart House lost its capital, [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]], and power over the duchy, retaining only small holdings on the shores of the High Seas, and Hope passed to the Farway, Jonas' former vassals. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) Seventeen Lighthart Items, including Ulyana Dust and the Clock of Destiny, shared the Crown, the Church, and the House of Farway. (Early media reports said only that the high nobility of Hope and several close relatives of the emperor had been destroyed.) Five Red Guards captains were executed as traitors. The sixth captain, Sir Clive, Mira's father, was lying with a pulmonary disease in a monastery hospital for two months in those days. The court found no evidence of his guilt, but the suspicion was a stain on his name, and he retired to his Stagforth estate in northern Shayland. ([[stagfort|Stagfort]]) His fiancee Gemma Alessandra, the second cousin of the master on the maternal side, refused to end the engagement and left with him. A year later, Minerva was born.

The convicts waited their turn in the Arsenal Tower in the Square of the Forefathers, a hundred feet from the scaffold, and heard everything that happened at the executions.

The Duke Jonas Lighthart was not arrested at once: he was in the Heart of the Light, where the hand of the Lord did not reach. But his two eldest sons and daughter were in custody. Tellurian promised them freedom if the duke came to Faunterra alone, without troops. Jonas knew that he was going to die, but he came and surrendered - and the lord deceived him. The Duke died on the platform with his sons, and before his death he learned that his daughter was sent for execution “to another place where her body will never be found.” During the interrogations, he swore that his daughter knew nothing, but the lord chose not to believe.

The court stripped Manson of his admiralty and sentenced him to death; Amber's secretary later carried the sentence to 1755. Tellurian replaced the execution with a jester cap. To make Manson's jokes "especially funny", he was forced to drink an echootte four times a day. Aiden wrote:
> "Echiota dissolved Manson's willpower just as acid melts metal."

For four years, Manson resisted until he broke down and turned into a humiliated, potion-dependent creature. It's a sight and gave the conspiracy a name.

## Consequences and Secrets
- The Great Lighthart House was destroyed. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) The Crown even thought of abolishing the Duchy of Hope and distributing it piecemeal to faithful vassals. The heart of the Light would not recognize the stranger, and the choice stood between Lighthart's first vassals, the Counts Flame and Farway. The Flame was the father-in-law of Jonas' third son. Through the third secretary, he was hinted that the title of duke could be obtained at the cost of "substantial proof of devotion", but he did not fulfill the hint. The Earl of Farway brought the emperor a purse of gold and gave him one hilt, the share of taxes that the Lightharts paid to the Crown. Then he gave up his entire purse, scooping up a handful of coins: “Honestly, Your Majesty. I will not lie about being poor.” A month later, [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] received the Heart of Light and the title of Duke.
- Jonas' youngest son, [[edgar-flejm|Edgar]], did not know about the plot. He had only Leonhard, and out of ten father battalions he was able to maintain only two. His father-in-law confessed to him that he had been offered to kill his son-in-law for the Duke's title, and gave him the upper half of the Flame River. Since then, Edgar and his children bear the surname Flame. Manson’s wife, Lady [[karen|Karen]] Lighthart, did not die: she spent about twenty years in a hospital on Faraday Riley Island and returned to the court only in 1775. ([[ostrov-faradeya-rajli|Faraday-Riley Island]])
- The object of Ulyanina Dust, which previously belonged to the House of Lighthart, was handed over by decree to the Church. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]])
- Tellurian, shocked by the conspiracy, terminated most of the contracts with the feudal lords, including the marriage contract of the heir. Therefore, Adrian in 1774 was free to choose a bride.
- Mira questioned Aiden's version. She found a letter from Corvis to her father dated 19 October 1755, where the captain asked to take care of his bride Leila Talmir because the Duke was summoning him to Aleridan. [[lejla-talmir|Leila]] said that a month before his death, Corvis had seen Aidan and learned a secret of the Duke ("The Duke hides for his daughter"; in early editions - "takes care of his daughter"). Mira concluded that Corvis was killed not for treason, but for learning the Duke's secret about his daughter [[alanis-almera|Alanis]]. From her hints, Alanis was illegitimate. Later, this secret, recorded in the letter of the Aleridian jailer, brought down the situation of Aiden and his daughter.
- In 1775, before the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], Erwin told the joke the words of his father: “The plot was a battle of charisma against calculation. Charisma has been defeated.”
- In Myra’s conversation with Ethan about protection, it was said that twenty years ago, the secret guard had “slipped” a conspiracy (in early editions, eighteen): it was discovered by the Duke of Almera, not a protector.
- Stripping the names from the dynasty's pedigree, Sybil Northwood noted that a dozen nobles "got on the plate or lost their titles" after the Shutov plot.
- At Erwin's court in 1775, [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]] was mocked, Erwin's vassals saw her as an insult to the rejected Iona, and Alanis was furious that she was the bride of John Corvis, the "rebellious vassal" of her father.
- Erwin first saw Manson as a six-year-old, in his third year of "exquisite torture," and was appalled by his struggles.

## In "Fantasies"

In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], [[karen|Karen]] herself recalls the plot. She calls it a conspiracy “set up by my father and husband.” According to her, she married Manson out of mutual love, and therefore they were hiding from each other: she did not tell her husband about her suspicions, and he told her about the conspiracy. The failure was a “complete surprise” for her. Tellurian executed her brothers and imprisoned her herself for twenty years. She was then pregnant and lost the baby, and then could not conceive. She never revealed it to her husband. Her younger brother Edgar Lighthart, she said, would have been executed "like brothers" or exiled "like a sister" had it not been for the Flame family's surety. Manson himself, in a toast to Edgar, said that half the blame for Lighthart's loss of the [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]] lay with him. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]])
