## Background
In July 1774, [[minerva|Mira]] and [[mark-voron|Mark]] exposed Aiden Almer's secret about the Shutov plot, and the Duke fled the capital with his daughter [[alanis-almera|Alanis]]. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]; [[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) He locked himself in Evergard, doubled his garrison, and never left the castle. The emperor summoned him to court and threatened to deprive him of the title; the news of this came to Aleridan on a newfangled “wave”. The Duke's brother, Archbishop [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], left Faunterra for Aleridan and gathered an army of poor knights and mercenaries. The city was flooded with his people.

In the same days, under the walls of the castle was [[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]], who dreamed of entering the service of Lady Alanis. Together with the mercenary [[berk|Burke]], he spent five days camping on a wooded hill with a burned mill, from which three rings of walls, thirteen towers and a gold fresco of Evergard were visible. Joaquin considered the castle impregnable and even looked for an underground passage on the hill, over which Burke laughed.

## Developments
On the night of September 1 to 2, 1774, about fifty lightly armed horsemen appeared on a chestnut alley illuminated by a lantern with almost no armor. Something was shouted from the tower, but the riders did not slow down. The gate tower cracked and collapsed, and the two front ones threw their hands, as if in a knight's salute: the "talking" Objects, [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]], threw shimmering clumps of force, and the gates burst like a bubble. Lantern turrets on the walls exploded one after another, and on the defenders who fled on the walls, the riders beat fire from their hands. They rushed so fast that the band of fire lagged behind them; the second gate and the grille scattered into chips and dust. The impregnable castle fell in less than an hour.

Joaquin rushed to the rescue, but Burke threw him to the ground and held him: “In five minutes, everyone will be cut out who is left!” Soon an underground passage opened on the same hill. From it came Lieutenant Sir Humphrey and Knight Rolden, followed by a servant Sophie, two guys with closed stretchers and a closing knight. Having mistaken Joaquin and Burke for enemies, the knights first attacked them. Upon learning Joaquin, Sir Humphrey said that the Duke and Lord Alfred had died, and he did not know about Lord Albert. On the stretcher lay an injured lady Alanis, and Humphrey decided to take her to the Duke's hunting ground a mile from where the doctor was.

At dawn, Archbishop Gallard's warriors found an ash-filled corpse. On the head of the dead duke killers put a hood. The middle son, Lord Alfred, was also killed. [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] was considered burned alive, but in fact she was wounded in the face: the cheek burned through. A handful of warriors, a couple dozen servants and little Lord [[albert-almera|Albert]] survived. The archbishop declared himself the guardian of his nephew and regent-ruler of Almera.

### Losses of attackers
Information about the losses of the brigade is divided. According to Mark, a dozen of her fighters died in Evergard, and the losses had to be replenished with new sets. Alanis told herself in the summer of 1775 that three hundred defenders of Evergard had "laid almost half of the brigade."

## Aftermath
### Gallard Regency
Gallard declared himself regent at Albert. His mercenaries hunted Alanis, and the Earl of Blackmore tried to extradite her to the parish. The priory immediately undertook to restore and strengthen the castle against the Persians.

### Adrian's Edict and Rebellion
Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] openly took responsibility. In his message to the Great Houses, he wrote that the intrigues of the feudal lords overflowed the cup of patience, that his word "sounded in Evergard" and would be repeated for anyone who would follow the path of Aiden Almer, and demanded from all the great lords a personal oath in the capital. A letter about the death of Evergard received in the First Winter, and this edict became the reason for the war: [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] declared it, naming Evergard among the reasons (see [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]). There were rumors that Erwin hated the emperor for allegedly burning Alanis alive.

## The real culprits
The real culprits have long remained unknown. [[mark-voron|Mark]] noticed that pigeons do not fly to the Beyond: Aiden’s plot revealed the World in early July, and the order could not reach the brigade behind the River in time. So he doubted Adrian's guilt and decided that the emperor had only taken on another's sin to intimidate the lords. [[minerva|Minerva]] later asked Adrian himself about the reasons for Evergard, and from the falsity in his voice realized that he had lied: Evergard was not burned by him, and he did not own the Rings.

The attack was carried out by [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade serving Puppeteer [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. Captain [[uitmor|Whitmore]] and Major Backfield later found witnesses of a gang with the Rings staying at the [[dzhek-bakler|Jack Buckler]] Inn not only in December 1774, when they kidnapped the Dynasties, but also in August — before the attack on Evergard. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) A soldier of the brigade named Bury confessed to Alanis that he was in Evergard, "like everyone else." Erwin suggested that the Puppeteer agreed with Adrian: it is spectacular to kill his enemy Aiden in exchange for the Emperor's silence about the atrocities of the brigade. In 1775, in Fliss Paul was taken to the bed of Alfred killed by him.

### The Role of Gallard
Many pointed to Gallard, to whom the death of his brother brought the greatest benefit. From a conversation overheard in Evergard by Laura and [[dzheremi-farvej|Jeremy]] Farway, it turned out that the priory had long been associated with the master of the Rings: his mysterious interlocutor recalled that Gallard received “a whole duchy”, and he himself only four Objects. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] publicly accused the archpriest of burning Evergard and ordering the murder of his brother. He explained his case to Cordelia's mother. The puppeteer wouldn't give Gallard such a gift for nothing. As early as 1772, Ulyanina Dust was taken from the Wilhelmina Cathedral in Aleridan to the palace of the archpriest and sent through Fliss by ship. Wilhelm's Fingers were previously considered small objects and were kept in two dozen Almeran temples until they were seized by the messenger archers. Cordelia called it a “monstrous deal.” In the summer of 1775, Gallard told Paul that Aidan was among those who had once silenced the Seventeenth Gift, and thanked Paul for having set Aidan on fire.

## Rana Alanis
Alanis' wound was unusual. A doctor in Flathill noted her "character", and Joaquin decided that she had been touched by Perst and she was the first of the mortals to survive his stroke. The wound, which began to rot, was later cured by his brother [[brat-marius|Marius]] at the monastery of Marek and Simeon, but the scar from the corner of the mouth to the ear remained with her forever.

## Trivia

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, a possible future, the secretary of the burgomaster Adrian recalled that the Northern Flash began with the burning of Evergard, which the lord formally took over.
