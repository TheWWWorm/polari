## What we know

The Second Northwood War is mentioned only in the "Shadow of the Great Tree", in the chapter "Sword - 11", where the history of the Skvorki Sky Gorge is told. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]; [[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]) Once upon a time, the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]] and Lilliday, rulers of the ancient northern capital of [[lid|Leed]], argued for primacy in the North. The dispute was resolved by a duel between the Duke and the Earl in the courtyard of Fort Elder: Lilliday recognized himself as a vassal of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], and the gorge became the common possession of two houses.

Then, during the Second Northwood War, the Origins acquired large pastures in the west of the Duchy. These lands lay closer to Leed than to the First Winter, so the houses were arranged for an exchange: the Lillidays received pastures, and the Origins received complete control over Gates of Heaven. The duke gave the gorge to his younger brother, and since then it has been fixed to the second branch of the family. In 1775, the forts were owned by [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]].

The date of the war, its causes and the course of hostilities are not named; nothing is reported about the First Northwood War.

## Historical context

According to the “Guide to the Lands of Polari”, by the eleventh and twelfth centuries Northwood piracy so troubled the Dukes of Origin that a long confrontation began between the two lands. It ended only in the XVI century, during the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]], and it was the enmity with a dangerous neighbor that forced the free Northwoods to rally around the strongest warriors, which led to the establishment of county power. Whether the Second Northwood War is connected with this confrontation is not specified in the sources.

## Other references to wars with Northwood

In the Janmay Hunt, Harry Hogue, speaking of the War of the Objects of 1756, mentions that the Duke of Origin's fleet was then weakened "by the recent war with Northwood" and was able to transport only two battalions to Shayland's aid. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]; [[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]]) This is the only mention of an armed conflict between the Origins and Northwood so close to 1756: the Guide dates the end of their long confrontation to the XVI century. Whether this "recent war" was the Second Northwood War is unknown. In the chapter on [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]'s visit to [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] (October 1773) it is said that Northwood and the First Winter are connected by "complex relations", and by 1775, according to the observation in the "Shadow of the Great Tree", Northwood and Origin "in recent years" lived relatively harmoniously.
