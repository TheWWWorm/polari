## Background
In December 1774, after Adrian's death on the Back, Minerva Stagforth as the rightful heiress arrived at [[faunterra|Faunterra]], where [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] held the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) In the negotiations in the besieged palace, they made peace. Erwin insisted that Mira put on the crown, convene the House, and reverse Adrian's decisions. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) Mira achieved that she did not have to marry him and dissolve her army, but promised to marry only with his approval. Erwin called the fire barrels poured onto the ice "fireworks in honor of your coronation."

The exact day of the ceremony is not named. The "[[poleznye-lyudi|Useful People]]" interlude, where the capital is preparing for coronation, is dated January 1775. In the "A Doll on the Throne" it is said that "it was December, it was January", when the Duke of Origin put the crown on the head of the young mistress. In the story “[[khochu-poni|I Want a Pony]]”, the parish priest [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] informs the guests that “Yesterday, in agreement with Lord Origin,” an “ignorant, fluffy maiden” ascended to the throne.

## Training
The coming weeks of the World remembered as the work of a “mechanical hammer”: each phrase of the court began with the words “Your Majesty should...”. The half-destroyed palace was hastily put in order: it lacked half of the windows, the roof was broken, snow was mixed with broken glass in the corridors, and spark machines and furnaces did not work. About fifty servants returned to the palace, and the ministers fled from the capital. The world was not allowed to even settle in a hotel: both sides would take it for a conflict with Agatha.

The young lady sewed sixty-four outfits: sixteen everyday, eight official, tea, sports, evening, dance and others. She had to examine one hundred and ninety sketches (three versions of each outfit) and seventy-two sketches of shoes. According to tradition, the coronation dress is worn once and then burned: the mistress of Eloise in the XIV century preserved her and was overthrown after a year and a half. The dress was supposed to be sewn in the colors of the Dynasty - azure, scarlet and brown. For Mira, they chose a blue atlas with rubies and a manto with sable trim. She asked for a simple green dress, but the Northwoods' green color was forbidden because it would remind of Countess Sybil's intrigue.

The requirements went one by one:
- By tradition, the empress goes out to the people with long curls and after the ceremony cuts them off. Myra's hair, short cut in monastic style, reminded of the hives and death, so a wig was made for her.
- Paleness and shadows under the eyes needed to be hidden with powder and blush. Mira countered that last time she was painted, “and ended up with poison in coffee.”
- After the prayer, the empress should have cried, and for this purpose, powder was prepared for the eyelashes from sour berries.
- Mira had to learn a twenty-minute speech written by the ceremonymasters on mandatory topics and pronounce it with a capital reprimand, softening consonants and stretching vowels.
- She had to master for ten lessons the ceremonial prayer of the Dynasty “Your Hand on My Shoulder” from thirty-two stanzas. The assistant to the archimper, [[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]], rehearsed with her to receive a blessing in case the ninety-year-old archimper Ellina would get knocked down.
- Finally, it was necessary to choose a perfume, because its smell will feel only the closest and guess in it the future policy of the court. Violet meant softness and submission to a strong man, rose - military policy, fruit - trade and finance, woody flavors - justice and law-making.

A particular difficulty was caused by the loss of [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]], who disappeared with the body of Adrian: the ritual provided for the transfer of Hilt to the new ruler. The ceremony masters found a way out. Minerva had to swear to protect the country from new blood and on the first day of his reign not to take the blade in his hands, asking only the Star Crown with a blue diamond instead of Hilt. Myra's request to at least let her mourn Adrian was dismissed as untimely.

There were guests in the capital. The tracts called out, "To the guests of the coronation, clean rooms!" and the arms dealers offered gifts "on the occasion of the coronation of Her Majesty." In Useful People, Labelin agent [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] looked out for the right people among the visitors, and his companion Sixth grumbled that he hated coronations.

## Ceremony
The very day of the coronation of the World almost did not remember, although she knew that everything was done as it should be. There are two episodes in memory. Erwin, dressed in white with silver and younger in appearance for ten years, praised her perfume:
> "It's a wonderful smell, my lady. The smell of a woman, not a crown.”

He caused Mira the deepest disgust, but spoke as a man, not a mechanism.

The second incident occurred after the prayer. Archpriest Elina forgot to bless the empress and stared blankly as Mother Cordelia hissed, “Your Holiness!” Minerva was confused and forgot to cry. Then she took the palm of the archimatrix, touched her forehead with it, and herself uttered both parts of the rite: “Long years of strength and power. Yangmei's hand is on your shoulder" - "With all my heart I serve the Pramas and the people." Lady Iona, due to bad weather in the Southern Way, did not have time for the coronation. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] did not appear at the ceremony either.

## Aftermath
At night, Minerva got drunk - by her account, for the third time in her life: for the first time it happened at a summer ball, the second time - in Waymar with the soldiers of Count Vittor. Now she was drinking deliberately to drown out the “robbery of the mechanical hammer.” She was crying and listening to the sound of her own name. The next morning, she demanded an Orge, as Inge Price had once advised. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) The First Secretary informed her that by a decree which she had signed the day before, the Duke of Origin had been made Lord Chancellor, and had signed the appointment of his men.

The daily “[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]” always dedicated the first pages to the new ruler. One of the articles nicknamed her "Minerva the Peace Bearer". The real power is in the hands of the Chancellor. Mira came to terms with the routine of fittings, novels, blank reports and signatures and decided to get drunk every evening, and in the afternoon to pretend that she was reading reports. In the third week after the coronation, the Silver Fox came to ask her to take the oath of the spark regiments: according to custom, the army swears to the new ruler after the coronation. This oath was Mira's first step in the struggle for real power.

## Trivia
- Ceremonymasters reminded Mira that on the eve of her coronation, the servants barely got up: a strong sleep was considered a sign of confidence, and insomnia was a sign of anxiety. Shadows under the eyes of Mira herself, they proposed to hide with powder.
