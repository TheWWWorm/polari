## Background
In November 1774, after the capture of [[labelin|Labelin]], [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] hesitated for a long time to go to [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. On 20 November, he was approached by a Northwood force led by [[krejg-nortvud|Craig]], and under Allied pressure, the army marched south. ([[dom-nortvud|House Northwood]]) [[adrian|Adrian]], meanwhile, deceived the rebel. In front of the whole Empire, he led ten regiments out of the capital and led them to Litland, against the Steppe Fire. But in a small pirate bay on the shore of Mason Bay, he put seven regiments on ships and sent north. The regiments landed in the Southern Way, in the Greysend possessions, and joined forces with the troops of [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]], nicknamed the Silver Fox. The Lord himself went further with only three regiments.

Three days before the end of autumn, the frost hit. The northerners reached the river Mile, where there was only one narrow stone bridge. Lilliday, Statham, and Erwin decided to build a few more, and within a couple of days, the warriors built five additional bridges. On December 1, on the eve of the Day of Descent, the army crossed and camped for the night a few miles from the town of [[piksi|Pixie]]. The area here was a "pocket": from the west it was limited by the channel of Hanoi, from the north and east - the bends of Mila, and only to the south was the exit open. ([[khanaj|Khanai]])

The northern army numbered about fifty thousand men: seventeen thousand kairs and greys, twelve thousand soldiers of Northwood, six thousand hired gunners and thirteen thousand foot soldiers of the Southern Way, who passed to Erwin. Alexis was standing less than a dozen miles away. He hid the army in the forest, and put only small detachments under the flags of the Southern Way on the edge, and the northerners' intelligence took them for the militia of the local lords. At midnight, the fox learned of the mutiny, under the cover of darkness moved the regiments and at dawn approached the distance of visibility.

## Erwin's escape
In the early morning of the Day of Descent, [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon]] woke up his cousin shouting, “They’re here!” In the spyglass Erwin saw the entire imperial army deployed with a two-mile front. In the staff tent Craig Northwood roared about the decisive battle, and the Earl of Main Molotov noticed that you can not retreat: behind the back of the river. Statham and Earl Lilliday suggested that Erwin hand over tactical command to one of them. Erwin agreed unexpectedly. He handed Statham a sealed envelope with his "last request" and announced to the entire tent that he was handing over command and recanting. With Damon and three hundred select warriors, he galloped north, leaving what appeared to be an army trapped.

For the army, it looked cowardly: in the imperial retelling of the battle, it was said that the "lord-finch" could not stand the test, trembled at a critical moment and the army lost its head. In fact, the envelope was ordered to withdraw from the field as many kairs as possible and indicated the way to do it. Erwin himself rushed to Fairwell station, which his men had taken up in advance, and on the rails he moved past Alexis' army to the south, to the defenseless capital (see Taking Faunterra). ([[bitva-za-faunterru]])

## Course of battle
The details of the battle are known from Agent [[itan|Ethan]]'s account given by [[minerva|Minerva]], i.e. from the imperial side. Statham was not obeyed by the entire army. The fanged Knight with his regiments ignored his orders, and the Earl of Main hammered them selectively, so that three separate pieces of troops entered the field. The fox placed spark shelves in the center. On one flank he sent Darryl's traveling cavalry, reinforced by the scarlet guard, on the other - the imperial cavalry and marines. The cavalry attacks were twice repulsed: on the right flank of Northwood, on the left flank, Count Lilliday. Inspired by the success, Craig rushed into a counterattack without an order.

In the center of the spark immediately put a few thousand people. However, most of the stunned were not northerners, but put in the front by travelers: so the kairs saved their strength. When the first ranks had cleared the spears and moved away to rebuild, Statham moved the center forward. This was the most dangerous moment for the Crown, but the attack destroyed the inconsistency. Statham, chasing forward Northwood, fell behind, and a gap opened between the flank and the center. The fox threw all the heavy cavalry there and cut the army in two. Count Main the Hammer spontaneously rushed to Craig's aid. The center weakened, and Statham had to remove some of Lilliday's keels from his left flank.

Craig broke through to the rear of the enemy and saw the pennant of the Silver Fox, but he covered himself with two reserve spark regiments. The Northwoods were tied up, surrounded by the cavalry of the Crown and the South Road, and they could not build a circular defense. Under Craig, he killed a horse, fought on foot, was struck by a mace and captured. His brother Donald, trying to save him, suffered a severe wound: the sword of Baron Darryl pierced his jaw and broke it. Statham and Robert Origin held the center for two hours. The kairas repelled attacks, the mercenary archers beat with a canopy, the ghres fired at close range from crossbows. But when Count Hammer and his vassals were killed, Statham commanded a general retreat.

Fox's light riders with still-charged spears chased the runners, and thousands of Northerners fell between Pixie and Mila. The river could have been an anvil for them, but the bridges held Robert Origin's cairns. Nearly twenty thousand people left for Mila.

## Losses and valuations
According to the imperial report, the field infantry was completely scattered, and more than two thousand kire and five thousand grays were killed or captured, deafened by the spark. Northwood's army was almost halved and lost its suzerainty. The Crown considered it "the end of the mutiny", and Alexis declared a thousand hilt for the head of the fugitive Erwin. Robert Origin later wrote to his cousin that the North had lost five battalions at Pixie. Statham estimated losses in two battles, at Pixie and Labelin, at a third of the troops. Later, the sparkstone dealer Ira Medea, praising [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]]'s eyes of the Chrysanthemum class, claimed that at Pixie they had killed six thousand rebels.

Sources differ in the composition of the imperial forces. Erwin counted twelve spark regiments, four regiments of Marines, two regiments of scarlet guard and cavalry of the Southern Way: twelve thousand spark copies, eleven thousand horsemen and six thousand long bows. Ethan called twelve spark regiments, four thousand heavy cavalry, eight thousand marines, one regiment of the scarlet guard and all the traveling cavalry.

## Aftermath
- The victory turned into a trap for Alexis. He entered the Labelin to resupply and charge his spears, and there his army was destroyed by the cayres hiding in the catacombs (see Night Labelin). ([[reznya-v-labeline|Night in Labelin]]) The Northerners, captured at Pixie, were released.
- The news of the victory reached Adrian at a holiday in Melorange. His tape-report, he showed the joke Manson as a real "triumph": "By the will of the gods, we won! The Northerners are defeated and retreating. The mutineer fled the battlefield. The Northwood brothers are in captivity." In captivity, thus, was wounded Donald Northwood. Sick [[minerva|Minerva]], hearing this news from Ethan, shone and asked to tell about the battle in detail.
- According to Erwin, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], after the defeat at Pixie, tried to cross over to Adrian and came to his senses only when the capital was in the hands of the northerners.
- Exactly one year later, on December 2, 1775, on Descent Day, the Origins won the Battle of the First Winter. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])
- The name "Pixie" became a household name. About the street brawl, which was taken apart by the guards, it was said that “there would be a pure battle at Pixie.” And [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]] reasoned that her father's plan, to buy up a supply of Shimmer spark eyes, guarantees: no one will give the Northers a "new defeat at Pixie."
