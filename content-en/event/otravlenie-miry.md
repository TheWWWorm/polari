The assassination attempt on Mira was a slow poisoning of [[minerva|Minerva]] Gemma Alessandra of the Janmay family, which was arranged by her patron, Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]], in the summer of 1774. This is the culmination of the Iskra line in [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]]. The ending is played out in Chapter 46.

## Background
After the death of her father, Sir Clive Stagforth, Mira became the fourth (then third) heir to the throne. ([[klajv-stagfort|Clive Stagfort]]) On her way to the capital, Sybil dressed her as her daughter Gloria. In Labelin, the barber repainted her hair in a pale red color and cut her hair a foot, henna was placed with freckles, and drops from the juice of the marina and the Shimmery locket once every two days made the gray eyes green. The countess explained this as protection from murderers, who, according to her, were looking for Minerva at court, and promised to reveal the substitution as soon as the offender was caught.

In [[faunterra|Faunterra]], "Lady Gloria" became a prominent figure of the court. The emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] gave her the first dance at the summer ball, and then lost to her the part in the strata. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) Mira conducted her own investigation and revealed the secret of Ayden Almera: she decided that the murders of the heirs, Baron Rosbet and Sir [[adamar|Adamar]], were the Duke’s business. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) Meanwhile, in mid-June, the real Gloria arrived in the capital under the name of Minerva Stagforth. The emperor placed her in the palace under personal protection.

## The imaginary disease
On June 9, Mira visited with Secretary [[itan|Ethan]] at St. Teresa's Hospital and saw a creepy yellow "waxy" old woman there. On the same day, the priest Gallard Almer married her, and Mira scared him away. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) She later recalled that the pain began the next day. On June 15, during a conversation with the countess, her right side pierced pain for the first time, "like a hot needle." On the night of June 20, the attack was repeated.

On the morning of June 20, an old doctor in a pensne found her "liver disease" from miasms. He prescribed bloodletting every other day, red wine in the morning and evening and herbal decoctions of wormwood, St. John's wort and chamomile. The doctor warned that at a late stage the patient will become foul-smelling breathing and she will have to wear a mask impregnated with onion essence. Mira thought she was infected in the hospital and wrote Ethan to see a doctor. On the same day, dressed as a citizen, she went with the knight [[vanden|Vanden]] to Pale Meadow to Leyla Talmir, and on the way back she suffered an attack longer than an hour. ([[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]])

In the last decade of June, Mira lived on bloodletting and red wine and watched from the tower as the city prepares for Sophia’s days. At the same time, the horse Clarence died in a duel, and Vanden grumbled that the doctor did not understand anything and that everything was “from coffee”: he himself once tried this drink and suffered from heartburn all day. Mira's health was enough for the military parade on June 24, which opened the summer games, and for a deal with Aiden Almera in his carriage. On the eve of the denouement, on July 2, [[mark-voron|Mark]] promised to send his doctors to her.

## Solve
On the morning of July 3, Mira realized that she was dying, and went down to breakfast. The countess kindly urged her to drink sweet coffee. Mira, remembering the words of her father, “Love those who are dear to you...”, wanted to hug Sybil and drink coffee to comfort her. But to the question “Do you want me to drink?” Sibyl flashed a fright on his face. While the countess agreed on one phrase, Mira put the picture together:
- Symptoms did not coincide with the disease of the old woman: the hands did not turn yellow, there was no smell. Ethan, who was with her in the hospital, remained healthy.
- The pain began the day after Gallard's matchmaking.
- Knight Vanden once became ill from coffee, and coffee in this house constantly drank only Mira.
- The murder of Clarence's horse, the Countess' alter, coincided with his hints of Sybil's connection to the dockyard.
- In the absence of [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], Sybil contacted members of his coalition, and the monastery where the jailer's letter about the secret of Aiden was kept belonged to the Proto-Father Church of Gallard.
- The assassins in Prelesia did not touch the two girls of the detachment, Mira and her servant, although they were easy targets.
- Sybil's letter to her daughter went to the North only in late May, and Gloria appeared in Faunterra in mid-June. So, she couldn't read it, and the substitution was intended in advance.

Sybil's goal became apparent. After the scandals that drove the three former brides of the emperor out of the way, the "northern Yanmei" Minerva Stagforth became the inevitable chosen one. The whole North would have voted for her, and if Adrian had postponed the engagement and died, she would have been the first heiress. Under this name, the daughter of the Countess already lived at the court. The real Minerva could only be quietly killed by “disease”.

## Escape to the Tower
Mira threw the cup and ran up the tower stairs. Two swordsmen chased her, and the first of them was Vanden. The world thought he was running slower than he could. On the way, she lost one shoe and threw another. Locked in her room, she pulled out a crossbow from under the bed, which she had been storing since the night she was waiting for the killers, and raised it. Then she put on a baby apron with pockets, brought back from Stagforth, put two bubbles from the stash in her pockets, climbed up the window sill above the street at a height of twenty yards and aimed a crossbow at her chest.

She showed Sybil and Gallard bubbles: smaller - with eye drops, more - with hair dye. If her body with a bolt in her chest falls on the pavement, the Crown Raven will reveal the switch on this evidence. Mira demanded that the countess confess to the deception and answer for him and for the murder of her father, and the poison in Mira's coffee was ready to forgive. Sybil refused: according to her, she has been walking on the blade of the blade for six months. Gallard assured that the poison in the girl’s blood is enough, because she drank it every day for two weeks. Mira countered that the countess, frightened by the doctors promised by Mark, poured a “horse dose” today, which means that the former were not fatal.

Mira asked to let her go to Stagforth, but Sybil refused: “No, it’s impossible,” Mira and herself ironically noticed that two Minerva, one under the crown, and the other at home in Pre-Lessia, will raise questions. Then Mira reminded the countess of her favorite story about her grandfather, who died in a duel, but before his death, "he took the enemy with him." She called Sybil to take a step and “go together.” When the girl's finger trembled on the descent, Gallard offered a way out: the cave monastery of St. Ulyana in the west of Almera, where the sisters dig deep cells and keep a vow of silence. Sybil agreed, saying that she would not take this sin on her soul. The world demanded an oath from the dearest. Sybil swore by “our daughter’s life,” and Gallard swore by her. So Mira realized that Gloria is not the daughter of the old Earl of Northwood, but a priest.

## Note by Becke
At this time, [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]] came to the house. Mira begged the countess to at least give her a farewell note to her friend. Gallard was opposed, but Sybil agreed with the shade of respect: "Yanmei breed..." A note signed by Gloria's name, Sybil read over her shoulder. It said that the disease had receded and the author went to the monastery, where he would spend years in "silent prayers":
> “The sun of my worldly life has set, but I will always remember you.”

Separately, Mira mentioned a “ball and a walk in the forest”, that is, those cases when the help of a friend saved her. Sybil found the note helpful: "The Horsewoman" will carry the news herself that Gloria has gone to the nuns. The door of the room was pulled up with a stick, after which Mira was taken away.

In The [[dusha-zapada|Soul of the West]], Becca, in letters from Litland, parses a note and looks for a second bottom in it. At first, she decided that the "sunset" indicates the Sunset Coast, but there were no monasteries of Ulyana, and Becca began to look for another meaning. According to Ethan, in the end, Rebecca understood the cipher: the northern woman is in the monastery of Ulyana Sad in the west of Almera. Her father locked her up when she asked for an escort.

## Aftermath
- She spent about three months in a cave monastery in western Blackmore County. Thirty-eight novices lived there, who were allowed to utter no more than five words a day. This is told by “Only One Star”; the life of the Ulyana monastery is also shown in the “Plaque”. ([[plaksa|Crybaby]])
- Ethan told Becky Mark the answer. He, already a prisoner of Erwin in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], called the monastery Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], Stagforth's sister-in-law, in exchange for the promise of freedom. The Count freed Mira by the hands of his man Inge Price and took her to [[uejmar|Waymar]]. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]])
- Gloria under the name Minerva Stagforth became Adrian's fiancée. On October 14, 1774, his mother and daughter were arrested in the palace. The countess was charged with the murder of Sir Clive and four of his servants, both of whom were deceiving the emperor. According to an archival record later found by [[karen|Karen]], the court on November 2 failed to prove the murders and sentenced them to exile rather than death. Soldiers in “Only one star” reported that the court is scheduled for December 15 and are waiting for the death sentence.
- During the siege of Faunterra, Major Backfield smuggled Sybil out of the casemate and hid him in a hospital under the name Dorothy Sly. Gloria was executed by order of Adrian: her body was walled up in the base of the gazebo at the temple of Farewell.
- The Northerners made the real Minerva their banner. In December 1774, after the crash at Beck, she became empress. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]])
- In March 1775, Peace, at the request of Erwin, signed the pardon of Sybil and Gloria. She did not forgive the poisoning and later reminded [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] that Sybil poured poison into her coffee. Having met the broken Countess in the summer of 1775, Mira realized that revenge was no longer possible. Later she wrote to her and immediately snapped an unsent letter: “You were like a mother to me! They stroked with one hand and poisoned with the other.”
- The poisoning also happened later. Pharmacist [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] promised that Mira would be the "best" for the experiments: Yanmei's granddaughter, a northerner, Ulyana's novice, and even "visited the Star and returned."
