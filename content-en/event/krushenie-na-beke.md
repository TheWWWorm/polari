## Background

In December 1774, in the midst of the Northern Outbreak, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] suddenly seized the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] at [[faunterra|Faunterra]] and kept it under siege. ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]]) [[adrian|Adrian]], returning from the campaign against the [[moran|Steppe Fire]], decided to go to the capital by rail from Aleridan with three hundred guards with spark weapons: the officers found the plan reasonable, confused only by the fact that Erwin himself had already used the same maneuver and could predict it. Meanwhile, [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] proposed to Erwin to destroy Adrian, destroying the bridge over the Back by the hands of Earl Arrowback, - as a reward, the Earl would receive the County of Blackmore. Erwin rejected the plan as dishonorable, but Alanis secretly sent a letter to Count Joaquin Yves Hanna. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]])

In Aleridan, the jester [[menson|Manson]] received a letter impregnated with Ulyanina Dust, with the order: "You will kill the emperor." ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) On the train, he tried to resist: he threw tea in Adrian’s face instead of a knife, at night biting the skin on his forearm, and at dawn, speaking in a powerful voice of the emperor’s uncle (Dust, apparently, raised her old personality from his memory), demanded that he be allowed to the lord, warning of the danger ahead. Dust herself, not finding any other way to get close to the emperor, turned to the intuition of the jester, and in it Manson saw a hungry monster ahead on the rails, "the plexus of tentacles, claws, teeth", ready to devour the entire composition.

## Collapse

On a frosty morning, the train passed Arrowback Castle; by this time, according to the sentries, a third of the way had been covered. The Emperor's wagon was the last. The jester went out with the guard on the balcony of the car and begged Adrian to look out and look at the river. Captain Grace, the commander of the Guards Company, objected that the engineer and the guards had seen nothing. The Master replied, “Do you understand that I will not see what you see?” and, angry at the cold, went inside. At that moment, Grace exclaimed, “Look, he’s right! There, on the river, some detachment!” — and the canvas of the bridge broke under the tractor. The whole team fell into shallow water. The assailants, with crossbows, axes, and torches, walked straight through the wreckage, checked "all dead," and set fire to a pile of wagons with barrels of oil for fidelity. The upper car, where Adrian and Manson were, was shaky, and they did not dare to climb there - "we'd better wait until it burns." Manson with a broken rib freed Adrian from the corpse of the guard, helped him out and took a spark dagger from the dead. When the arrows on the shoal noticed the fugitives and gave a volley, Adrian pulled the jester to the edge to jump into the river; in front of the crossbowmen, obeying Dust, he struck the emperor in the back with a sparked dagger, and both collapsed into the river. 320 people were on the train; later, investigators found 92 bodies on the banks and identified 216 dead among the wreckage. Rescued, in particular, the guardsman Sir [[sanders|Sanders]], Lord Sutherzweit, and aide-de-camp Manson Forlemey: they were picked up by a fisherman, and then sheltered by a village priest.

## Aftermath

Mira, who was in Aleridan, was awakened by the news of the death of Adrian and legally proclaimed empress; she was informed that the "mad" Manson stabbed the lord. A few days later, she came to the Pera and Sword Palace with the Yangmei Glove and made peace with Erwin, ending the war.

The body, recognized as Adrian's body, was found two miles from the crash site, frozen in the ice twenty yards from shore; Earl Arrowback and Agent [[itan|Ethan]] had previously identified him by the Eternal Hilt and the medallion, and the teeth and blade mark on the thigh were then shown by the coroner. Erwin assigned the investigation to [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] and Ethan and suspected that the testimony of the fishermen witnesses was rehearsed. The official version, printed in the "Voice of the Crown", read: the bridge gave a crack, water leaked into it, the ice froze and strengthened the fault. Fishermen said that the people of the count shouted to Vladyka: “Jump into the water, Your Majesty! We'll save you!" and then the jester hit him with a dagger from behind. Erwin knew who had destroyed the bridge, so he was sure that Arrowback’s men had “embezzled” the witnesses. But out of this smooth lie fell Manson's absurd act: why would he kill the only person who needed him, and why did he, who was always up by ten o'clock, get dressed at dawn, even in a cap? Erwin concluded that there was no need to invent a meaningless detail for liars, which means "there really was a joke with a knife." According to the fishermen, in the crash, they sent Larsen to the Count's castle, Smith - to Kosoy Yar, and Beard and Jacob climbed into the wagons to help. In the spring of 1775, at the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], the Minister of Paths, Liam Schelier, defended the version of the water frozen in the joints, and [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] argued that the bridge could not collapse itself, Erwin confessed to Alanis' plan, and [[mark-voron|Mark]] suggested that the Earl of Arrowback's men played a murder scene before the fishermen; the Count's vassal Baron Bonegan was arrested. ([[liam-shele|Liam Shellier]])

## Truth

In the House, Manson laughed and declared, “Adrian is alive!” ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) According to him, after falling into the river Dust “unchained” from him, deciding that the emperor is dead. The spark only reduced Adrian's leg, and he, unable to swim hundreds of yards, took the help of a jester "from despair"; holding on to the wooden wreckage, they sailed downstream, realizing that "we will not live at the wagons." Downstream, they found Captain Grace's body, Adrian swapped clothes with him and hooked Hilt with a pendant on him. They were picked up on a boat, apparently by the same fisherman who saved Forlemaeus. Adrian did not go with him to the village to avoid being burned, but asked for food, dry clothes and a boat down the river. The fisherman dropped them off five miles from the bridge by the track. Paying a sparkly eye, they sat down in a stagecoach to the south and by evening were in the county of Daynite; the wound of the emperor "without further questions" hit the healer. They did not have time to return: the capital was already in the hands of the North. Adrian sent Manson to Darkwater to [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]], and he himself fled to later return - in the Shadow of the Great Tree he again claims the throne.
