## Background
In the fall of 1775, the House Origin's war with [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], the "Selected" and the Puppeteer, approached the denouement. Shayland possessed [[absolyut|the Absolute]], which made him immortal and able to move instantaneously, and his army was armed with dozens of [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]. Lord [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] skillfully retreated from Waymar, but at Lisium Dole, succumbing to Craig Northwood, took the fight in the forest and lost a significant part of the army. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) The Chosen One's army of about nineteen thousand soldiers entered the Duchy of Origin. She was commanded by Desmond's eldest son, Richard "Ice". ([[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]]) [[lid|Leed]], on the orders of [[sajmon-lillidej|Simon Lilliday]], portrayed submission and surrendered to Richard without a fight, but later [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] gave the city to plunder [[paul|Paul]]'s Shavans in exchange for the right to deal with Iona, Belomorye went over to the enemy, and [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] gave up the forts of Svorok Sky without resistance.

Desmond withdrew into the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] with five battalions exhausted by retreat and losses at Fox Vale. It was impossible to defend Leed, and it was dangerous to leave seventy thousand townspeople with supplies to the enemy. On the advice of [[stetkhem|Statham]], Desmond, with money taken by the kairs in Shayland banks (about two hundred thousand ephesas), hired the residents of Leed — stables, cooks, carpenters, laundries — by hilt per month. Within a day, forty thousand leaders stood under the banner with the uncircumcised and left with the army. Desmond twice rejected the proposal to burn Leed with the remaining thirty thousand inhabitants in one word: “No”. From the end of the summer [[minerva|Minerva]] was there with the Yangmei Gauntlet (Glove of Power) and the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]]; she moved here the capital of the Empire. [[navi|Nathaniel]] was with her, promising to kill Paul. Minerva led General William Davey's spark regiment and Robert Origin's battalion into the First Winter. This brought the force of defenders to eight battalions, two of them sparks, according to Desmond, half as much as the enemy. The gates of the castle were covered with earthen ramparts from the fire of the Rings. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], cut off from the house in the steppe, still there, thought out a defense plan with [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]: to divide the enemy army and reduce the battle to three separate feasible tasks. In the letters, he gave the plan to his father. General Horton's corps on Duke Farway's ships and Count Edgar Flame's Hope Regiment landed on the Upper Twin, in [[adrian|Adrian]]'s rear, and prevented him from intervening.

Before the battle, Erwin took the wife and daughters of the Earl of [[fleming|Fleming]] hostage, trying to get the White Seamen out of the game, and then, along with [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] and Kair Aubrey, freed [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] from the cage in Leed. The sister captured Perst and the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]] by [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]. Iona, Erwin, Hyder, Aubrey and the seven leaders (cousins, apprentices, a master crossbowman, and Sher's widow, who traded in the Orge) — only eleven men — marched to the First Winter with a margin of jests. On the eve of the battle, the sick, feverish Erwin appeared to Agatha the Radiant and said, “Tomorrow we will meet.” He understood this as a prediction of his own death.

Adrian's court in Main watched the battle from afar: the young Shavan [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]], with the help of the Object Bird without the Plot, showed the guests the battlefield, and the courtiers made bets. Commented on the course of the battle of the Silver Fox. ([[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]]) [[karen|Karen]], Manson's wife, first warned her husband that Adrian's plan would not work, and then put "three Elenas, one Gloria and four agates" on Origin.

## Course of battle
### Morning: The Ice Labyrinth
The army of the Chosen entered the valley along the Leed road and dug into an ice maze, which Minerva built from hundreds of huge blocks of ice. Three paths through the labyrinth led to the lake and the castle, to the southern gate of the city and bypassing the lake to the northern wall of the castle. Eighteen thousand soldiers could not turn around and were stuck in a marching column. Shayland divided the army into three corps: General Horis with sunsets and Northwood "bears" went to the city, Richard with the Almerians and Sheilandians - along the northern path, Paul with the horde - through the center. ([[oris|General Oris]])

Nathaniel with the Yangmei Glove climbed a mountain above the valley and began throwing ice blocks pre-cooked by Minerva into Shayland's columns. One lump mowed out a whole company. To distract the enemy, the Aerial Guild ([[kharmon|Harmon]], [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], [[salem|Salem]], Veselchak) on the orders of Minerva launched a [[vozdushnyj-shar|balloon]] with straw stuffed in the basket. ([[veselchak|Jolly]]) Paul realized that death threatens the entire army, and demanded from Vittor the deconstructor - the Voice of God. With him, with archer [[chara|Chara]], Hanid Muha, and Joaquin, he broke through the maze. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) The fly, which fell from a horse and broke its leg among the keels, disappeared with the ground: in its place there remained an ideal semicircular hole in the snow. The ball was shot down, but instead of the crew it was stuffed. The defenses of the labyrinth collapsed, and Shayland's troops moved towards the castle and the city.

### Death of Paul
Nathaniel deliberately allowed Paul to think that he had overtaken him, and lured him into the grotto of Kosuli under the monastery on Mount Agatha. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) There, Inji Price (“The Couple”) killed Chara with a bolt from a hidden self-shooter and took her Perst. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) In the underground chapel, Paul attacked Nathaniel, but he consumed the fire with Eternal Hilt. Then Paul turned on the Voice of God (deconstructor), but the calculation of Nathaniel, who slipped a fake on the altar, turned out to be more accurate: an invisible sphere of destruction soared around the Object hidden in the niche where Paul stood, and destroyed Paul himself. Then the half of the chapel collapsed, and Nathaniel fell into a bottomless failure. He was later found alive, but with a broken spine and skull. General Horis felt Paul's death "during the day's song": the voice of the Fallen, at whose call the sunsets went, fell silent.

### The ambush on the Belomorye road
Count Fleming, contrary to Erwin's agreement, ambushed [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]]'s X-ray column in a gorge on the Belomorye Road, just a couple of miles from the First Winter. Erwin foresaw exactly this place. The commandant and Murdoch's kairas, along with Jemis, who had dragged the Gun to an inaccessible site, climbed into the mountains and struck the White Seamen from above. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) The signal that Jemis was in position was smoke from a crevice in the mountain. Whites are brave. Fleming and about a hundred of his men were taken prisoner, the rest were pushed to the Belomorye. Lady [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] on the eve of the battle, dressed as a shepherd, slipped through the positions of the Belomorye and informed Desmond of Erwin's plan. Due to this, the blows of Colonel [[blekberri|Blackberry]]'s kire, Baron Statham's cavalry and General Davey's regiment were able to agree on time.

### Strike from the rear
Erwin's small force attacked the convoy in the tail of Shayland's army. The widow Cher and Hyder Leed made jokes, Iona burned the carts with a Perst, and Erwin shouted, "Fourth company, get in from behind!" In a panic, the men rushed forward into the valley, and Erwin's detachment was lost among them. At the castle, where the horde shot the walls, Iona, lying among the corpses, imperceptibly killed the Hanids - including one-armed Sormah. When Vittor appeared, he ordered to kill the beckoners, and the Persians turned the crowd into ashes. At this moment, Erwin grabbed the signal horn from the body of the deceased and jumped on the burning ice of the lake to the castle, blowing an attack. The Origins' heavy cavalry flew out of the gate, and Erwin led her straight to the horde. He was sure that he would not die, because Agatha promised: “Tomorrow we will meet,” and he waited for a meeting in the cathedral, at her fresco. Rolling in the front row with the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]], he received a blow with the lash of Perst: he was turned over by armor plates and broke his ribs.

### Afternoon: storming the castle and the uprising of the city
Richard, protected by the [[poyas-kolduna|Sorcerer's Belt]] from bolts, led his men to the north shaft of the castle. The Cairos burned him with bottles of butter and fire arrows, and he rolled off the shaft. He then rallied the wavering Shavans and led the horde into an attack on the steppe tactics of "shoot and run." The waves of horsemen fired at the castle, set fire to the donjon, the South and Lake Towers. However, on the northern flank, the Belomorye Kairs met Richard with a crossbow volley, knocked down and tied up. They were Gordon Sue's disguised Xs. The Sorcerer's Belt protected Richard from bolts, but the horse was killed. Richard, who jumped out of the saddle, was wrapped in an arcana: the belt held up iron, but not bare hands with ropes. At the same time, Colonel Blackberry's cuirres struck the Shaylands and Almerans from the front, the Xs from the flank. The Duke's gunman, who had settled in the rocks, beat Shayland's pincers with Wilhelm's lash. When they did not see him, they began to water the rocks with fire and only gave themselves away; he killed three, and the fourth fled. The Wilhelmin monks, for whom defeat meant betraying Gallard's memory, bypassed the battle on the northern flank and, in a desperate toss, beat Richard off. They covered him with a monastic cloak so that the shooter could not distinguish him. The Shayland soldiers fled, the Almeran knights surrendered, and Gordon, not wanting to ruin the company and the monks, allowed them to go to the mountains.

Meanwhile, Horis and Joaquin entered a city that seemed submissive. Vittor entered the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant and ordered Joaquin to destroy the Perstom fresco "Agatha's Choice" as a "monument of the Virginian spesy". Soon, the wire holiday garlands, stretched over the streets according to Nathaniel's plan, simultaneously fell and discharged with a spark, killing hundreds of sunsets. Immediately all the windows and doors were opened: stones, axes, boiling water and burning oil fell on the soldiers, and the soldiers of the spark regiment turned out to be in the dressed-up citizens. According to Horis, about twenty thousand people rose against his corps. Sunsets have fled the city. Joaquin, in a blind shooting with the lash of Perst, mutilated (apparently fatally) Salem of Summerswith, who was defending the city, and, having recognized him, fled in despair; Joaquin's former squire, the cheerleader, was nearby.

### The End of the Chosen Army
At the headquarters of Horis outside the city appeared half-frozen Martin Shayland, four days running on foot from Leed. He reported that Iona had taken away his finger and the voice of God. In anger, Vittor stabbed his brother with a dagger. Joaquin immediately shot Vittor in the head and killed him again and again while Absolut resurrected the host. After the tenth death, Horis stopped the knight: “Ulyana does not want to take him.” Vittor's gone. Horis gave the order to withdraw to the Sky Creatures, and Joaquin took off the Perst and said he would never shoot again.

## Outcomes
According to Desmond, the Puppeteer's army "fell into the flaps of which it consisted." After Paul’s death, the troops did not want to fight, the Shavans left in a hurry, until the roads ran out. The remains of Fleming's keels went to the Belomorye, the Shaylands to the Sky Scrolls. Northwood Reserve Regiment stood at the entrance to the valley: no one bothered to give him the order. The enemy lost about three regiments, the lion's share of the Hanids died; of the Persunnosians, only five survived. The first winter lost about a thousand soldiers and eight hundred citizens killed, the wounded were several times more. A third of the castle buildings, including the donjon, burned down, and fires raged in the city. Desmond called the fresco "Agatha's Choice" the most serious loss.

In Main, the Origins' victory destroyed Adrian's designs: he hoped that Vittor would take the First Winter, and the Origins would be forced to seek protection from him. Adrian ordered the arrest of [[karen|Karen]], [[menson|Manson]]'s wife, for what she knew about Horton's landing in his rear.

## The Night After the Battle
Severely wounded Erwin healed Iona with the help of the Hand of the Znaharka; at the same time, she cured his long-standing pulmonary disease. Gordon Sue at the Duke's bedside in his faces talked about the ambush on the Belomorye road. Erwin and Jemis made the main, backup and backup plans, and the backup worked. Fleming thought that there were only one and a half hundred Xes in the gorge, and in the mountains there were kairs of Commandant and Murdoch. The White Seamen were cut in two: one half was driven back to the Belomorye, the other was overcrowded. That's why the X's were so late to the main battle. At night, Iona, Nexia, Jemis, Gordon Sue and Desmond gathered at his bedside. Jemis confessed to fleeing at Rei-Roy, Desmond spoke warmly to the children for the first time. He praised Erwin for enlisting the support of the Flames and getting Adrian into the rear of Horton's corps: without these battalions, the First Winter had to be hard, but the threat from Adrian was removed. After learning about the destruction of the fresco, Erwin realized that a meeting with Agatha “in the cathedral” would not take place, and felt the alarm.

Richard, hiding among the dead monks, broke into the castle. Burned and lame, he entered the bedroom with the Perst and the [[poyas-kolduna|Sorcerer's Belt]] and offered a "game": each in turn calls the victim, and the last survivor will leave. Gordon Sue pointed to Richard himself and was killed. Desmond called himself, saying that he was proud of Erwin and Iona and was ready to answer for not being able to raise his first child, and Richard broke his skull. Erwin challenged his brother to a sword fight. The Sorcerer's Belt stopped the irresistible "Lightning of Agatha", but Erwin switched the Voice of Winter to spark mode and defeated Richard with a discharge. Iona removed the Belt and the Feather from his brother, and Erwin thrust the blade into his jugular vein: "Brother, you killed a very good man."

### “A brilliant calculation”
The battle takes place in "The Shining Calculus", where General William Davey, after the war, tells the Minister of Education how things really were. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) In the school textbook "Modern History" the battle is devoted to the paragraph "The brilliant calculation of Agatha and Yangmei."
- The original defense plan was made by Davey, Desmond Origin, Statham, Blackberry, and Hyrock. Three battalions of kire had to hold an ice maze for a couple of days at the entrance to the valley while the Shavans froze. Then the enemy, according to the calculation, will be divided: Richard's monks will go directly through the lake, sunsets - to the northern gate, where Blackberry stands, and the horde - to the city, to the prey and warm houses. Nathaniel the Eternal Hilt melts the ice under the monks, the cayres are crushed by the number of sunsets, and the shavanks are passed through the Rust Gate to the Main Market Square, surrounded by hidden pits and stakes. There, Minerva Glove drops up to twenty blocks of a thousand poods on them, and the survivors are met by Davy's spark regiment disguised in the Weaving District.
- **Militia.** The city was defended by about ten thousand burghers under the command of retired kairs Hasler and Frederick. While the general was ill, they were preparing for a long siege: barracks by the wall, warehouses of beans and onions, stones, resin and roof over the gallery, visible ice-cold barricades from afar. Davey ordered everything to be removed: the Rings will burn tar and roof, the barracks of the shavana will burn first, and the barricades will scare the vanguard.
- **Change of plan.** Minerva and Nathaniel, fearing that the Puppeteer, bypassing the roof, will notice the ambush (according to Nathaniel's calculation - with a probability of 93%, and the pits - 62%), transferred the bombing to the entrance to the labyrinth to lure [[paul|Paul]] into the chase. Hilt was also needed against Paul, and the ice on the lake remained strong. Davey predicted that the castle would be attacked from three sides.
- **News from Erwin.** Nexia Flame, dressed as a shepherdess, reported that Erwin's summer plan from Reis was to bring the X-rays and battalions of the Snow Count from the north, and from the south Horton's battalions fell off: the Gnoi-ganta defeated the Snow Count, Horton's duke cut Adrian off from Main, and by the First Winter only about 280 X-rays go to Gordon.
- **The General's decision.** Davey told officers the truth and allowed all those who do not want to fight for the “temporary ruler whose rights are ambiguous” and for yesterday’s rebels to leave. The regiment relocated to Agatha Cathedral, turned the cloaks lining outside and hung a tinsel over the streets under the spark. How many guards left that day, the general never said.
- The conclusion of the general: no one, neither the duke, nor the mistress, did not calculate the battle - "there were two wills in the field, and in that case there were all five"; the northerners won, because they were better guided on the move, and the Origin's vision was invented retroactively. Nevertheless, he advises not to edit the textbook: children have nothing to learn that "Minerva is a fool, Duke of Origin is a fanfaron, cairns are stubborn rams, and plans are useless shit."
