## Background
In the mid-eighteenth century, Duke Jonas Caroline May of the lineage of Elena, head of [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]], ruled all of [[nadezhda|Hope]]. From each ounce of gold mined there, he received a quarter, and gold in Hope was mined exactly as much as in all other lands combined. The Duke held an experienced army of ten thousand swords, and the amount of military, financial and political influence was ahead of him only one person - Emperor Telurian. According to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", the Dukes of Lighthart have long pressed the Almerans both in politics and on the battlefield, and half a century ago Almera was attacked from the south, from Hope.

The name of the war in the text is not directly explained.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark refers to another reason for the war - a spark. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) According to her, Jonas Lighthart repeatedly asked for permission to build a large spark shop on the Beck River, on the border of the Shepherd meadows, and Telurian refused under various pretexts. The Duke of Almera then asked for a dam on the same river upstream, in the Almeran Naitrock, close to the Crownlands, and obtained consent. Jonas, who had petitioned earlier, went to war and took Knightrock. The Emperor refused to help Almera: he remembered that the niece of the Almeran Duke was married to the Duke of Origin. The Almerans called the northerners, and together the Agathans returned the city. Karen sees this as an example of the Crown's old policy of pitting the great lords: the dam is built where the lord wanted, the armies of the three duchies are weakened, and he has kept his troops. This version does not contradict the story of the Breimel Bridge, but the novels do not explain it that way.

## The course of war
The story of Jonas Lighthart says that when the Duke wanted a piece of Almera land, he took it; apparently, it is about the Golden War, although it is not directly named there. The war broke out right in the center of the Empire, “under the nose of the lord.” The powerful House of Lighthart then successfully attacked Almera and probably would have taken away a considerable part of her land. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) Emperor Thelurian did not intervene: his brother, who was friendly with the Lightharts, dissuaded him. The Emperor's brother [[menson|Manson]] was married to the Duke Jonas' daughter and later became a party to his plot.

To the aid of Almera came the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]], and with common forces the Agathans won. The decisive battle of the war was the Battle of Braimel Bridge. It was included in the atlas “The Biggest Battles of 1724–1774”: the battle scheme is drawn there with accuracy to the company, all maneuvers are equipped with time marks, and a stratum grid is imposed on top. This atlas was later shown by Navi, who memorized patterns of past battles.

## Outcomes
The Almerans "in the great war works" returned only a part of the captured Lightharts. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) By intervening, the North entered directly into the Crown's policy and defeated its then allies. [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] was known as "the destroyer of the sunset hordes, the triumphalist of the Golden War."

The Almerans are indebted to the Northerners. In August 1756, at the Lodge of the Seventeenth Gift, Duke Aiden Almer told Desmond, "You saved my father in the Golden War." ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) These words were not only flattery: they reminded the Lord that the northerners are strong and wayward and that they should firmly point out the place. Tellurian understood the hint and sided with Aiden in the dispute over the protection of the lodge.

A few years later, the Lighthart House fell after the Shutter Plot: Jonas Lighthart and his two eldest sons were executed, and Hope was given to the Earl of Farway, and Aiden, who uncovered the plot, became the first adviser to the emperor. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]])

## Legacy
In 1775, during Erwin Origin's war with the [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] Priory, Erwin, while circling the Almeran prisoners, thought that some of the older officers might have fought in the Golden War alongside Desmond Origin. Previously, Erwin thought that this long-standing intimacy would help draw the knights of Almera to his side.
