## In "Only One Star"
### Evergard
Sir Humphrey Elsa Carina was a lieutenant in Duke Aiden Almer's guard and commanded [[evergard|Evergard]]'s gate guard. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) In the summer of 1774, he refused Joaquin Yves Hanna's service: "You're not a bad guy by all accounts. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) And it would serve right," but the castle is full and warriors are not needed.

On the night of September 2, 1774, Evergard burned the Wilhelm's Fingers (see Death of Evergard). ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) Humphrey led out of the underground walk stretcher with a seriously wounded [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], with him was the servant [[sofi|Sophie]] and the knights Rolden and Lance. Joaquin and [[berk|Burke]] came across them. Humphrey recognized Joaquin, accepted his help, and said that the Duke and Lord Alfred were dead, but he did not know about Lord Albert. In the Duke's hunting lodge, the squad was ambushed by riflemen. Humphrey, hiding behind a human shield, was the first to rush at the archers. One prisoner he cut off three fingers, and he admitted that he serves the parish of [[gallard-almera|Gallard]].

### Escape
Humphrey led the unit in roundabout ways, confusing tracks, through Flathill. Alanis rode under a veil and portrayed his pregnant wife. He brought her to the Earl of [[blekmor|Blackmore]], "twenty years" serving her father, and informed the Earl of the secret in private. However, Blackmore put watchmen to the milady's rooms and sent messengers to the parish. When the soldiers of the count attacked the guest house, Humphrey went upstairs for milady and helped her down the window. Then he held the gate against the crossbowmen while the fugitives got out of the castle. The horse was alone, and Joaquin took Alanis away, leaving Burke, Humphrey, and Sophie behind.

## In "A Doll on the Throne" and "Humans and Gods"
In 1775, at a wine tasting in Faunterra, Alanis, already a lady-burgomaster, made a toast to her achievements: she “managed to survive the past year”, and was the first among the “decent people” who helped her called “Lieutenant Humphrey from the garrison of Evergard”. In Humans and Gods, held captive by Paul's brigade, she recalled "running away from the burning Evergard with the Sire Humphrey".

## In "Fantasies"
### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the fall of 1776, Humphrey came to Faunterra by "Adrian train" along with old coachman [[dzhonas|Jonas]], fortune teller Murriel and mute [[sofi|Sophie]]. He had no right eye, and the broken cheekbone left a black dent in his skull. To Major Hand Doge, he called himself "Humphrey One Eye," a blacksmith, and about the eye he said, "The girl kissed unsuccessfully." ([[ruka-dodzh|Hand Dodge]]) He carried a short sword with him, “loved by forest robbers.” The cry of "Ad-ri-an" he shouted hoarsely, "as if a crow crow crowed." In the four, Jonas was the "voice", Murriel the "intelligence", Sophie the "soul", and Humphrey the leader. Around the four gathered the "one-eyed gang", or "Sophie's gang", first at twenty people, then up to a hundred. She caught the disgruntled Adrian, smashed the Ministry of Taxes and threw the bones of the ambassadors of the Sunset Coast. Upon raiding the Origins' mansion, the vat overturned the sculptures and smashed windows, and Humphrey "aptly threw the stone right into the Duke's office." There was a note on the stone saying, "Die, baby!" and, groping her in his pocket, Erwin said, "Adrian's definitely not going to be chosen. I know a reliable way.” The monk of Wilhelm's brotherhood, looking at Humphrey, asked: "Almer's guard? Officer?", and he "frustratedly grumbled." Student [[elis-kavendish|Alice Cavendish]], he deprived of the leaflet Minerva: “We are the people.” When Sophie reached out to him at night, Humphrey softly refused, "I'll be with you later. Maybe a star.”

The gang stopped Almera's carriage, where Albert and Evelyn Almera were sitting. On the son of his deceased seigneur, [[albert-almera|Albert]], Humphrey “looked strangely”, putting his hand on the sword, but at the request of Sophie let the boy go. Evelyn Farway he ordered to be stabbed in a carriage, poured with oil and set on fire. Then the fire broke out, and the people turned away from him. [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] himself, who captured Almera, Humphrey called "a crook and an impostor." On the day of the opening of the Chamber, Adrian brought his newborn son to the crowd. Sophie with tears took the baby, and Humphrey “breathed” held him in his hands and bowed: “Glory to the heir. Glory to the Dynasty."

On the third day of the meeting, Adrian threw troops to storm the Chamber, and next to him were only "a pale girl, a woman with sloping breasts, a one-eyed bandit, a vocal coachman." Adrian suddenly fell in convulsions. By all indications, he was killed by Sophie's "spark". The Golden Grive burned her with a Perst, and then "Sir Humphrey, lieutenant of the guard of the Grand Duke Aiden Almer", cut the shavana of the tendon of the elbow and crushed the jaw. ([[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]]) The crowd trampled the Golden Gryve to death.

After that, Humphrey, Jonas and Murriel disappeared, carrying Sophie's mutilated body. The people of Origin had long sought them to reward them for their faithful service, but they served, according to the narrator, not the Duke of the North, but "the late lords, Lord Aiden Almer and Lady Alanis Adelia Abigail." In the summer, a one-eyed knight came to [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]’s clinic and asked for help, not healing: he wanted revenge on the one who destroyed the Great House of Almer. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] officially refused, because the laws of honor and Bird without the Plot did not allow him to send agents behind Adrian's head. But through his sister, he said that the chance would arise "either on the first or fourth day of the meeting of the Chamber", and gave a "credulous letter" with his hand: "Nezhenka, die." When searched, such words would not be evidence, but if necessary, they would open the doors of the Origin mansion. Adrian's body was never found.

## Personality
Humphrey is a sharp, decisive and experienced warrior, stingy in words, but able to tell everyone his task. In Fantasia, his "dark hardness" served as a support for the entire squad. To Sophie, he treated carefully, “gentlely, but without passion.” Having spared Albert, he was merciless to the Farway.
