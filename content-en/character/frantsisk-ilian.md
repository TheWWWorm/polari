## Who He Is

Francis-Ilian of the family of Sophia the Great - the sovereign king [[shimmeri|Shimmery]], who bore the titles "grandson of the sun", "spacer of the blessed shadow", "keeper of the keys to the Lion's Gate", "glorious over the glorious" and the First of the Five. The title of the First of the Five belongs to the rightful ruler Shimmery and means the primacy in the Council of Five - the five most prominent families of the kingdom, who held in their hands all the prey of spark eyes. During his reign, the king was so rich that he once bought an island for a single walk with his beloved, and Vladyka Telurian, according to the narrator, considered it right to get up when he appeared. The king had one son, Prince Hector, and sixteen daughters, as well as countless altess. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) According to the Polari Land Guide, Shimmery is ruled by a king from the House of Nellis-Lyon; in a royal marriage letter about Demeter, this genus is called the "Great House of Nellis-Lyon". While the king lived in the monastery, the First of the Five was called Hector.

Shimmerians remembered his reign as a time of unprecedented prosperity. Mayor of [[melison|Melison]] master Cornelius recalled how the guild of winemakers twice in a row raised prices by a third, and sales did not fall: so much money came to Laam that the townspeople almost forgot to bargain. For his daughters, the king built a separate palace amid artificial waterfalls and multi-storey gardens, saying it was "a worthy place for a happy childhood."

## Recluse

About eight or nine years before the events of the “Gate of Trust”, the king, on the eve of the Days of Abundance, visited the Maximian-u-Bezdna monastery on the edge of the Boneless Gap, drank well with the abbot and the same night in the guest room he had a dream. ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) The goddess of dreams showed him how the sublunar world is bent by a funnel, under the neck of which there is a bottomless Failure; people, buildings, animals and plants slide into the abyss, and in the end even the smooth, glass-like [[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]] cracks and collapses. It was later revealed that on the eve of the abbot - the Second of the Five, Count [[kuindar|Quindar]] - told the king about the secret order and offered to join it. Sleep allowed two interpretations: either the order will save the world from disaster, and then the king must give him all his strength, or the order is the source of danger, and then the king's duty is to raise troops and wipe him off the face of the earth. This and the rest of his dreams and how they came true, is told in the article “[[sny-frantsiska-iliana|Dreams of Francis-Ilian]].”

The king was in no hurry. He handed over the affairs to his son, locked himself in the monastery library and studied the works of the Foremothers and Forefathers for eight years, prayed and meditated, and most of his vassals sent out to collect information about the order. So he learned that the order secretly suggested the idea of waves to imperial scientists, learned how to make believable fakes of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]], acquired hidden enemies and discovered the amazing properties of Glowing Objects. The forebears, as the king found out, believed in the eternity of the world, and he did not find any interpretation of the first dream; he did not dare to tell it to anyone, for the dream would sound the worst of heresies.

In the first year of hermitage, at their request, he released from the vassal oath all those wishing to court, and the majority went to the court of Hector. About once a year he had a new dream - not as dazzling as the first, but prophetic. Through the Maximian brothers, novices, parishioners and merchants, he learned about dreams all over the south "from Laem to the Heart of the World", and the king was known as a prophet and a dreamer. Meanwhile, one of his daughters, Demeter, began to publish a monthly journal, The Diary of a Dreamer, where secular gossip was interspersed with "fresh prophecies"; she wrote most of these "dreams" herself, and her philosophical friend wrote interpretations.

The king himself later admitted that he was confident only in his first dream.

## Journey to the North

Eight years after the first dream and about three months before the events of the Gate of Trust, the prophet left the Maximian Tower with a retinue of seven people. There was a Northern Outbreak and a [[moran|Steppe Fire]] mutiny, and he deliberately chose the path bypassing both "boiling boilers" - [[melorandzh|Melorange]] and [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]]) Three weeks later, on his way to the Shimmer Mountains, he came to Surlion in the Holliwell Delta. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) The merchants of Surlion, hearing his dream of ships going without oars and wind, wanted to give him the best ship of their shipyards; the prophet asked to give the ship to the Maximian order, and he himself made only one voyage on it - to Leongard, the sea gate of [[nadezhda|Hope]].

In Leonhard he was greeted with the news of Adrian's victory over the Steppe Fire, which he had predicted. In the underground sanctuary of the temple of Elena-Na-Wave, he took in his hand a small flickering object, to which three centuries no one dared to approach, and remained unharmed; the priests begged him to take the object as a gift. He told the Earl of Lighthart what he was sure of: "The worst days of the House of Lighthart are behind us." ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) In the [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]], he consoled the Duke of [[henri-farvej|Farway]], whose grandchildren were held hostage by the lord, with a dream about a girl rescued from the clutches of the monster, and the next day the news of the death of Adrian came. In gratitude, Farway gave him the book Divine Mechanics. The prophet, in turn, gave Farway a large raw spark eye and hinted at a deal for a million ephes: he offered Hope to buy stocks of the eyes of the Second and Third of the Five. Surrounded by the Farway, they decided that the king was looking for money to buy over his son's vassals and regain the throne.

Publicly, the prophet told about his dreams only what concerned listeners: Yangmei and Agatha, sharing a bed as mistresses (he was interpreted as the reconciliation of the North and the Crown through dynastic marriage), a girl saved from the monster, ships without oars. No one asked about more, and he “did not impose the truth on those who did not need it.” By the time he barged down Halliwell to Darkwater, there was a procession of hundreds of pilgrims.

## In the Gate of Trust

At the end of February 1775, the prophet arrived in Nan Clair, the capital of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], to open his first dream to at least one person - Queen Lady in Darkness, "the wisest woman of the South." ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) At the city gate, he stood up for a dirty beggar, who was not allowed into the city, and ordered the guard to consider him part of his entourage. The beggar was [[menson|Manson]], a former jester of the emperor and alleged king-killer.

To get to the Queen, it was necessary to pass the swamp custom of the “gate of trust”: the guest must be recognized by three trusted vassals. The first gate was Father Loris of the Cathedral of the Forefather Kristen. There the prophet solved the mystery of the shrine - a statue of Kristen with ulcers on his body and a extinguished candle in his hand: he noticed that the candle was rudely made, ordered to cover the statue with a cloak and saw three rings of radiance in the darkness. So he realized that the statue is related to the flickering Objects, — knowledge he learned from the “Divine mechanics”. The second gate was the Foltic admiral Fanno Groc, who showed him the Tops of the Dark Kings, and the third was the Castelians Lord Emmon Darklin, the Queen's nephew. Emmon gave the guests a test of confidence in the owner: of the two bowls, the prophet, who took the cup with both hands as a sign of sincere hospitality, drank the real poison of Lilanna, but remained unharmed, since Emmon’s handshake left an antidote on his palm.

Meanwhile, the aide-de-camp of King Gerion, who had served him for ten years and had been absent from the monastery for eight years, planned to "save" the seigneur: he put two brown stripes on the prophet's back, hid the deadly corzo seeds under the Manson's camsol and hoped to shield the king at the right moment so that he would return to the throne, and Gerion himself became the first minister. ([[gerion|Gerion]]) The sting of the crybola, assigned by the queen to follow the prophet, pierced the poisonous bubbles of the seeds in advance: when they bit the prophet, he felt only an itch and, trying to scratch, pinched his lower back.

In the “mothball room” the king laid out the truth to the queen. He proved that Manson did not kill Adrian of his own accord: the jester was tormented by ephesses, Objects, letters and trains that reminded him of the disaster on the Back. ([[bek-most|Beck]]) Then he talked about the invitation of the Second of the Five, about his dream and about eight years of research. Finally, he announced that he had deliberately kept Gerion, "the most distant and greedy" of the vassals, as a leaking channel: his men made sure that Lord Emmon and the king's own son bought the secrets. He presented Gerion to the Queen "as a gift" as a future supplier of false information; the narrator left the fate of the adjutant to the discretion of the prophet.

When it was revealed that Manson was acting under the rule of Ulyanina Dust, the king referred to Foremother Yumin's work "Justice and Action": a crime committed under the influence of Dust is not the fault. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) The Queen decided to go to the capital to meet with the "northerner" and the one "who calls himself the Peace Bearer". On the way, Francis-Ilian entered the secret order, took an oath and learned two of his greatest secrets: what kind of Tree the brothers are cultivating and what kind of schism threatens Polaris in three hundred years, if the Tree does not hold the world to its roots. Even him, with all his wisdom, it shook to the core.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

### Marengo and the Train

In May 1775, the prophet arrived in [[marengo|Marengo]] - not as an ambassador with the fleet, but as a guest on the ship Lady in Darkness, in a simple monastic balahon. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] and [[minerva|Minerva]], who expected hints from the southerners, offered a game of questions without subtext at lunch; the prophet had a hard time - in the question of a cake the size of a galleon given on the Day of Descent, the subtext of "huge power" still crept in.

In the imperial train on the way to Faunterra, the king and queen invited Erwin to their home and “straight, without embellishment” offered him a deal: Minerva “will peacefully go to the Star due to a disease that affects the lungs”, Manson will be declared insane and exiled, the throne will take Lady in Darkness, and after her death, Erwin is married to her daughter. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) For himself, Francis-Ilian asked for a "tiny favor" - to convince Hector to return him to legal authority over Shimmery. Erwin refused furiously, threatening to cut both if Minerva, Hector, or any of his friends "sick with the lungs". At the same moment, the queen became ill: at the sight of the living seed in the cup that Erwin brought, the prophet pulled out her saucer and covered the cup, and the queen erupted with black liquid and fell. Masters later admitted that the conversation on the train was an "experiment" to form an opinion on Erwin; Erwin himself suspected that the Queen's illness was part of the game.

### Trial of Manson

At the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], the king suddenly volunteered as an adviser (defendant) of the accused. When asked about the knowledge of the laws, he replied that he himself published them, copied them and canceled them, and once gave his son a cuff with the Julian Codex. His defense consisted of a series of provocations. He referred to Manson's madness and to the potion of the echiot, which the jester had been drinking for nineteen years - but when the accuser [[mark-voron|Mark]] drank from the bubble, there was pure water there. Then the prophet offered to immediately recognize the defendant guilty and advised him to ask for mercy; Minerva was outraged, reminding that the court must seek the truth.

He then recalled Minerva and Erwin's verdict in absentia, in violation of all norms, and asked the lady to re-elect the Supreme Court. He gave the fishermen witnesses a portrait identification, and none recognized Adrian. He noticed a scar on Mark's arm and asked Minerva if she knew his origin. He argued that the body of Adrian was not found where the others were, and that the lord could go ashore alive and die from a blow to the head, and demanded the release of the defendant "for lack of a corpus delicti". According to him, he did not seek an excuse:

> "The accuser's business is to prove, ours is to sow doubt."

When Mark proved that a nobleman in a military campaign is equated with a commander’s vassal, the prophet was confused: it turned out that he did not have his own list of witnesses. He later testified that the bridge over the Back could hardly collapse himself, questioned witnesses from the train and summoned Erwin himself to the witness seat. With questions about his relationship with Alanis, he forced Erwin to publicly extradite her: Alanis offered to destroy the bridge. ([[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]) Erwin recalled that two weeks earlier the king had asked him for military assistance against his own son. According to Erwin, all the antics of the prophet at the trial were a test - "the mice are needed to check the cat": on the same day, [[otets-david|Father David]] was allowed to reveal to Erwin the secret of the order. David confirmed that Francis-Ilianus, although he had recently joined the order, had the highest degree of initiation.

### Chamber and elections

Minerva later admitted that the king volunteered as a defender only to get a rostrum, and spoke in such a way that everyone heard his own. In the House, he and Lady-in-the-Dark supported the idea of electing a new emperor and proposed that Minerva be made interim ruler until the final election, which was expected after the victory over the Puppeteer; in the vote, both Shimmerians supported Erwin's candidacy. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) Erwin realized that the masters had long known from Manson that Adrian was alive, and went to the capital with the intention of changing the unpredictable ruler.

On the same day that the Duke Maurice Labelin declared war on Shimmery in the House, Prince Hector threw a glove in his face and demanded a duel and then the Duke's arrest. Minerva forbade the fight and refused to be arrested, as war was declared by law, and both Shimmery's representatives - father and son - left the House indignantly.

## In "Humans and Gods"

At the end of May 1775, shortly after Minerva became a temporary ruler, the king, along with Lady-in-the-Darkness, appeared to her in Faunterra - right in the bedroom, past the sedated sentries, accompanied by two kribola stingers. When the Queen's metaphors about the gardener's "mastery" failed, the prophet, while serving water to Minerva, said bluntly: Erwin is a feudal to the bone and will become a comfortable ruler for the lords, and the return of Adrian, who has three powerful enemies, will lead to civil war and probably to the reign of the Puppeteer. He asked her to "rise above personal motives" and not harm the Empire. Leaving, the sting of the kribola put a spark of self-shooting at the door, which Minerva hid in the nightstand. Minerva, turning to him mentally, admitted: the temporary rule is his “brilliant maneuver”, because now there is not a single full-fledged ruler, and the Chamber “looks into his mouth”.

On June 1, 1775, after the news of [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]'s death, the king, along with Lady in Darkness and Father David, came to Erwin. He apologized for "the previous provocation on the train" and offered to help assemble a coalition of lords in exchange for victory over the Puppeteer, the return of the stolen items of the Dynasty and respect for the order: the magisters needed a living "special warrior" - [[paul|Paul]], whom they considered arrived "from other turns of the [[vselenskaya-spiral|universal spiral]]." The king said that Magda Labelin, who invaded Shimmery with stolen eyes, unwittingly rallied him with his son and there is no longer any disagreement between them, and asked for at least one battalion familiar with spark weapons. Here he also referred to "Divine Mechanics", speaking of Objects capable of changing the course of time.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

### Soltown

In the autumn of 1775, the king and Hector, with the "Kire Battalion" attracted by the prince, returned to Shimmery, put things in order and, according to the king, settled all differences along the way. They then landed at [[soltaun|Soltown]] at [[adrian|Adrian]]'s request. Unwilling of enmity with the Kairs, the king exchanged birds with [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]]: he withdrew the Soltown garrison in exchange for the king's oath not to fight on Adrian's side.

Meeting Adrian's train, the king enthusiastically recognized [[karen|Karen]] from the Lighthart House and slapped Manson on the shoulder ("Happy to see you at large, defendant"), and the jester clenched him in his arms. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) At lunch, he made everyone congratulate him on his future grandson from Lady Catherine Catherine and told his ninth dream: a crucified man with a finger burning like a candle (the king considered it a sign that Paula would be caught), a sea monster embracing a naked woman, a girl with a pharmacy weights and a watchmaker with a severed hand. The dinner ended in a deal: the Shimmerians refused to fight against the Kairs, but promised to cover the rear of Adrian against the Shavans and Farway. Maurice Labelin agreed after the war to return the captured eyes or their value, and the king demanded the execution of Colonel Horey, the commander of the transferred to the enemy of the Solar regiment. In the end, he clapped his hands three times, and the servants brought the [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]], a personal Item of Elena the Traveler, which the king gave to Maurice Labelin with a request to give Adrian full access to it.

### Absolute and First Winter

For Adrian's experiments with Worm and Bird, the king delivered one of the two necessary Objects; together with Adrian and the Second of the Five, he watched "with great trepidation" as Bird first came into action and showed that the goal of the Gardeners' Forefathers was "still attainable." ([[chrevo|The Womb]])

Together with Adrian and Count Quindar, the king spent hundreds of hours on sacred texts, seeking an answer whether [[absolyut|the Absolute]] would protect against sparks. He himself took on the third question: according to the allegory of the Forefather Evrian, he concluded that a person frozen in [[chrevo|the Womb]] remains alive and well, which means that freezing the bearer of the Absolute will not cause it to work.

On December 2, 1775, the day of the Descent, the king, together with the court of Adrian, followed through the Bird for the Battle of the First Winter. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) He commented on the battle (of Richard Origin remarked that he "swept between the parts like a lieutenant, not a general"), willingly promised Manson a horse from his stable, "the best in the Shimmery kingdom", and along with Lady-in-the-Darkness and the Second of the Five was present when [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] was frozen in the Womb.

On December 3, in Main, where Adrian’s court stood, the king was present when he spoke to Erwin through the Voice of God. When Adrian demanded surrender and ordered Johan Reis to destroy the Hand of the Znaharki, the prophet tried to reason with him ("Lord, I fear you are superfluous..."). When Shavan fell, poisoned by Manson, the king coldly said to Adrian, "He's dead, my lord... This happens to people.” Magda, who immediately attributed the poisoning to herself and forced her husband to ask for peace, he bowed with a thin smile, and this support gave her strength.

A few days later, Francis-Ilian entered Adrian's delegation — along with Magda, Lady in Darkness, Silver Fox, and Manson — who had come to negotiate the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. Erwin hoped to rely in negotiations on the masters of the order: Adrian is too vindictive, and the prophet and the swamp think soberly. When the sentinel reported the arrival of Nathaniel, the prophet, along with Adrian and Magda, insisted that he be allowed in. At the same moment, it was revealed that [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] had broken Lady in the Dark's neck. The king was the only guest who did not raise his weapon. Iona, who held everyone in the crosshairs, doubted only about him, and Erwin whispered to her that no one needed to be touched. The Prophet calmly called the incident “an unfortunate and bitter incident”, explained it with old accounts between Queen Madeline and Robert and supported Minerva: the personal enmity of the two should not be borne by everyone. Then he and the others looked through the Bird without Flesh to the Star and listened to [[navi|Navi]]'s account of the false lighthouse and the world of the gods. When Navi demanded from Minerva a choice between his death and the triumph of the order, the prophet doubted his goal: perhaps the Foremothers in a dream warned him about nothing but the Second Tree.

## In "Fantasies"

"Fantasies" the author calls the possible fates of the characters, not the continuation of the plot.

In the First Winter, shortly after the battle, the king came to Iona "not as an ambassador to Shimmery, but as a friend"; her bodyguard kair Aubrey remained at the door. The Prophet told her that the prophetic dream "nine years ago" was the answer to the question "What have we done?" - they prevented the death of the world - that all his dreams came true, up to the "wooden structure" to which her Gun was attached, and thereby proved the existence of genuine, "higher" gods. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) He's talked to Erwin about the same thing. He advised Iona to confess to a respected and impartial person - the capital of the supreme mothers in Faunterra - and take a strict penance, but first visit the mistress and sleep. The narrator calls him the hero who “saved the princess from the tower” for this: Iona’s eight-page penitential letter suggested to the Church how to regain its authority.

At the beginning of 1776, both branches of the [[tserkov|Church]] were left without heads, and the king, according to the narrator, resolved most of their problems in a month: he reconciled the Forefather's branch with the Pramaternal one and submitted the idea of repentance for the Persones. In the spring, he brought the priests — mother Orlando, mother [[mat-alisiya|Alicia]], Bishop [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]] and the Second of Five — to the show of the talking device of [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]] and joked their arguments: “You know, I have joy: the granddaughter was born. His eyes are narrow, he's half Shavan. Wilhelm's warrior is growing." Then he returned to the scientist and explained that the Church is poor: the Forefathers spent on a military campaign, the Foremothers gave Cordelia a huge loan to the Empress, and the central treasury of both branches without heads is not available. So he bought the device with his own money. At the same time, he announced that he was claiming the mantle of the priory, although he was “just an ordinary monk” (“not yet Eminence, but only Majesty”), and the next day he also claimed the imperial throne, since “three other candidates are young.”

His recorded address "on behalf of both branches of the Holy Church", certified by the High Mother Alicia of the Order of Agatha and Bishop Amessin of the Order of Wilhelm, was lost in the cathedrals: everyone who used the Rings must repent, and anyone who does not repent before the Day of Descent will be found, brought to trial and justly executed. After him, the parishioners went to the temples in droves, the donations increased several times, and the three centenarians accepted penance. Adrian later persuaded him to add his personal word to the appeal. In the summer, High Mother Alicia took the King from Laham to Faunterra for church affairs, and Shimmery remained in Hector's hands; Ambassador Darkwater, on behalf of Emmon Darklin, offered Hector the votes of the marshes for the prophet in the Lord's election — in exchange for the arrest of Mireille Nan-Claire. The king's name was on the list of candidates for the mantle of the priory, which Amessin gave to sign Minerva in August 1776.

Demeter's daughter reproached him for promising not to force her into marriage, but "forgot his words, being carried away by the Tree", and promised her to Jemis Lilliday.

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the autumn of 1776, the capital of the Pramatery was seen by Francis-Ilianus. The election of the priory, however, took place “before the time”: in the cathedral of Wilhelm Amessin received forty-one cards, and the prophet received thirty-nine. Together with his [[mat-alisiya|mother Alicia]], the Archbishop of Laem and another priest, the king listened to the repentance of Minerva, who signed the permission for this election and hid the deal with Amessin. When she put on the table a piece of paper “with two words”, the prophet realized that she was offering to check “not at all a priori”. At the election of the Lord in the House, he came “with his son” (Prince Hector also spent the night in a besieged building) and spoke third. The night before his speech, he put out a barrel of Laem wine to the lords, and in the morning he treated a hangover with a witch's drug. He began with an ironic “repentance” in the passion to save and interrupted it to listen to the cardinals. They admitted that Amessin bribed them and defeated them by deception. Mother Alicia stripped Amessin of her robe, and [[minerva|Minerva]] transferred it to the Prophet’s shoulders: “Congratulations on your election, Your Majesty.” The legate of the Wilhelmins was ordered by the king to withdraw his troops, maintain neutrality and deploy infirmaries and kitchens at the cathedral of Wilhelm. The legate did this, stating that the Church of the Forefathers had not yet recognized him and, apparently, a repeat election would be needed. To the cardinals, the prophet promised to intercede for them only to be deprived of their dignity, without torture and execution, and to Amessin, who would “honor the memory” of Gallard with a “bright fire”. He then withdrew his candidacy for the title of Emperor: "I will not take away your bread, Bringer of Peace."

After the death of Adrian, the king invited Erwin and Minerva to the Cathedral of Wilhelm and revealed his plan to them. Ollie's device he purposely allowed to use Adrian, so that the conflict of the common people with the nobility concentrated in one city and became manageable. The second bottom of the plan was to check the “future crowned couple”: in a prophetic dream, he saw “Agata in the same bed with Yangmei.” After showing the book of the Forefather Haruko “Empire of the Rising”, where the emperor and the military ruler-“shogun” rule, he proposed to share the power. Minerva becomes empress with secular and financial power, Erwin becomes military ruler, "shogun" or "Lord Chancellor". The troops of the Crown are reduced to one or two regiments, and the House and Church serve as the third and fourth poles of control. His arguments he called the disappearance of the body of Adrian, who can “resurrect” or leave the applicant son, and the threat of a “machine” in the sky. The marriage of the shogun with the empress, he, on the advice of the lords, considered undesirable. He warned that the empire was likely to leave Northwood, the Sunset Coast and Litland, and Shimmery for sure, because the Southern nobility has been dreaming of independence for centuries. The king admitted: "I did not want to be the lord", and the rank of the archpriest seduced him always. On the canonization of Iona, he said that the Church does not object. At Erwin's request, he willingly agreed to marry [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] and [[roza-erroubek|Rose Arrowback]]: "Never done that."

## Relationships

### Hector

The king loved his son, but in 1775 he partly blamed him for the defeats from the Labelins, considering him prone to "idleness and frivolity". Hector, in turn, was afraid that his father, relying on the people, the clergy and the swamps, would take the throne from him, and asked Erwin for an army. The war with the South made them happy.

### Lady in the Dark

The Prophet sought her as the only person able to understand his dream, and became her associate in the order. In the later scenes, they conducted a conversation, passing the thread of conversation to each other without a stop, "just a sense of timeliness."

### Manson

The king twice stood up for the jester - at the gates of Nan-Claire and in court - and Manson trusted him alone. At a meeting in Soltown, the joker called him an “old whore” who “owed” him: at the height of the trial, the prophet promised him “half a hundred gold coins.”

### Erwin and Minerva

Erwin considered the king a dangerous intriguer, but over the years he acquired respect for him; Minerva had a long dislike for him, but, according to her, almost forgot her, so often praised the prophet Erwin and Iona.

## Personality and Appearance

In the Gates of Trust, he is described as a white-bearded old man in gray, without a single ornament; in Marengo, as a mature, broad-shouldered southerner with a round belly, kept free and calm, "the most powerful man of the delegation." The habit of smoothing his beard and raising his hands with a light bow does not leave him either at the court or at the table. Patient, witty, polite, does not impose the truth on anyone, affectionate with the poor. He is inclined to fads: once by decree he “abolished” the winter in Shimmery, ordering him to consider December, January and February “young spring”. At the same time, he has a keen analytical mind, he can speak with hints, like swamps, and, if necessary, with dry, indefatigable language of legalists.

## Trivia

- Erwin noticed that it would not take eight years in the monastery to memorize the phrase "Objects fall from the Star"; this helped him to understand that Father David was lying about the purpose of the order.
- According to one bishop in 1756, the king was a relative of the then archpriest Ramir-Aslan and a few years before got out of a military adventure in the Sinic Islands only thanks to the Origins, becoming a debtor to the Duke of Desmond.
- In the Gates of Trust, Divine Mechanics is called the work of Forefather Edward, written in place of Brother Kristen, who renounced heresy; in Humans and Gods, the king himself calls it the book of Forefather Kristen.
- The number of years of hermitage is called differently: “nine years ago” and “eight years” in the “Gates of Trust”, “nine years ago” in “Fantasies”.
