## Origins
Mick is an orphan from the Waymar Old Port. The first five years of his life, while his parents were alive, he almost did not remember. He spent the next five years climbing the streets of [[uejmar|Waymar]]. There he was beaten by both the older boys and the guard, and the only friend, the future guard Doug, then defended him, then took all the alms himself. One day, Mick pulled a rich gentleman's coat over the floor on the steps of Shayland Bank, and the guard almost beat him to death. A half-dead boy crawled to the steps of the city library. The keeper of the library, master Lynen Margaret Harvey, went out and took him as an apprentice, although Mick knew only one letter - "A".

Mick had a cleft lip and an ugly nickname for Guba, which he preferred to keep quiet: few people saw his face as a pleasant sight. Nathaniel was the first to look him in the face and called the “distortion of appearance” is not worth the attention — this he bribed Mick. He learned grammar slowly and even by the age of fifteen read badly. But he quickly learned the science to please visitors and had a wonderful memory: he remembered where each book stood, who took it and what he said about it. He didn't shy away from any black work.

### The Dream of Magic
The dream of magic Mick began because of Clarissa, the daughter of the elder of the shipping guild. She studied three times a week in the reading room and never looked him in the face. Mick overcame two books of imperial scientists about the magic of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]] in three months, hoping to find a recipe for a love potion. The recipe was not there, and then he began to dream of becoming a witch at all. He secretly worked on the book "black wind" - the technique stings kribola, and in his dreams he killed a security guard from a bank. A sorcerer, Mick dreamed, was easy to get money: to come to Shayland's bank, "the one where the guard almost killed you", turn around, fly into the room with gold and fill your pockets with ephesas.

## “Now you are a magician.”

### Nathaniel
In August 1756, the Gift of the Gods arrived in Shayland. The Origins took the Items from the sunsets, and two of them were given by the Northern Duke to the Earl of Shayland for display in the Cathedral of Vivian. At this time, a severely beaten boy crept to the library door. He shook and begged to be given a book, otherwise a "nervous collapse" would occur. Master Linen gave him Master Hodgson's "Difficult Fate of the City on [[torrej|Torrey]]", and the guest read and memorized Waymar's story in about ten minutes until the founding date and initial population. He called himself “Suppose Nathaniel” and said he was “bright” and not “shadowy.” Mick decided that the strange guest is a sorcerer from Darkwater, and he willingly confirmed this.

Miku Nathaniel was a miracle. He did not feel hungry, woke up at the appointed moment and instantly multiplied huge numbers. His bruises were gone in three days, and without a new book every couple of hours he panicked. Doug advised a friend to get rid of the "lorden" who will take his place, or even give it to his parents for a reward. But Mick was attached to the guest. He taught Nathaniel to live in the city, renamed him "Nathan" and composed him a legend of a student from [[faunterra|Faunterra]], by tricking the wrong ship. The emperor and the prince came to the city, and friends went to the market to eavesdrop on the capital’s speech of the retinue. Nathan did not know horses (“we have a ride on salamanders”), lost his mind to carts and almost got caught by two knight guards, Sir William and Sir Claude, but posed as a student of the Faculty of Physics and Sparkling Technique.

Nathan admitted to Miku that he came “magic way” through the portal, wanted to get into the Bear Fang, and ended up in Smoky Distance, and expects other sorcerers to open a new portal for him. Mick led him to the Cathedral of Vivian to look at the Objects: to break through without a queue, he pitted an elderly lady, "protruding a cleft lip." These were the Light Sphere and the transparent rod in silver drops, which Nathan called the Cleaner, and the priest called the Rose of Happiness. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) When Nathan reached out to the Palm Sphere, her outer ring lighted weakly.

### Seed of Power
At night, Mick found Nathan in tears over the list of Items. The chance that he would be followed by other sorcerers, he estimated in “one whole two-tenths of a percent.” Mick consoled him: they would steal money — “I know one bank” — and put Nathan on a boat to Darkwater, only let him first teach witchcraft. Nathan took an oath from him one day to fulfill one order, put a soaking weed on his tongue, salted with tears, and Mika went through the cold to the very bones.
> “You are the seed of magic. It will grow soon.”

Nothing happened for a few days. Nathan read books about the Objects, Gifts and treasures of the Great Houses, and Mick, imagining himself a sorcerer, for the first time boldly answered Clarissa, drank the “ugly lip” and winked at her.

### Doug's Betrayal
Doug alerted Mick at night, beat his legs and ribs with a truncheon and demanded a share from the "lord". Mick told him a secret: Nathan is a Darkwater sorcerer who came through the portal and lost his strength, and he has something planned for tomorrow evening. Mick was silent about the tears. Doug remembered that the new bishop was a Maximian monk who was “tightly engaged in sorcery,” and he himself became friends with the deacon, and decided to scare Nathan. Mick offered to force the wizard to open a portal to the bank, and Doug demanded a third.

### Night in the Cathedral
Nathan flattered the master of Linen with the idea of a new arrangement of books and left with Mick early in the cathedral, allegedly thanking his mother Emilia. Before that, he told Mick to grind a few shallows into dust. In front of the altar, he asked a friend if there was nobility and mercy, and freed him from the oath, but Mick refused. After Nathan's prayer, the Light Sphere itself fell from the altar. Friends hid among the marble statues, showered with chalk: the chance that they will be noticed, Nathan estimated “below two percent.” At night, Nathan admitted that he wants more knowledge than strength from the Sphere.

Doug came out from behind the column, who warned the deacon in advance. He demanded the Sphere, threatening with a horn. Then Nathan promised him magical power and for proof told Mick to take the Sphere and say one word - "idyan". The outer ring of the Sphere shone with blue fire, the inner one wrapped up, and circles of light ran around the temple. Doug vowed to give Nathan his uniform, helmet, baton and horn and drank blood from the sorcerer's cut palm. He then took Rosa Happiness and said the first word. After gaining strength, he stabbed Nathan in the stomach and demanded a second word. Nathan whispered to him, and Doug, in Mick's eyes, withered and dwindled into the ancient mummy. Nathan healed the wound and said that he really did not want to kill anyone.

Then, according to Mick, the Sphere unfolded a glowing Polaris map under the dome. Nathan had long viewed clusters of white dots on it, first in Waymar, then in the east, center, and south. Mick decided he was choosing where to go. “I’m just looking for something,” the sorcerer replied. When the door of the cathedral was knocked on, he put on Doug's uniform. He explained to Mick that the portal would only miss one thing, and that things would have been different if Mick hadn't been given a secret. Then he listed Mick his exits: true, a lie about the "dark sorcerer" Doug or an attempt to hide. After that, he sounded the alarm and, along with the frightened guards, ran out of the cathedral. Mick never saw the portal.

### “The Magician of Great Power”
Mika was pulled out of the niche, and a hefty guard brought a baton over him. Then Mick realized that he was no longer an Orphan Mick or a Fuzzy Mick. He pointed his fingers at the guard and hissed a deadly word. He did not know how to kill without the Object, but the guard did not know this and turned back.
> “I’ll tell it to the bishop! Call him here, and say, I am a magician of great power!

Mick demanded that the bishop swear that no one would touch him. The interlude ends here.

## Mick and the Order
In Humans and Gods, Father [[otets-david|David]] tells [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] that the order learned about the alien in the year of the Seventeenth Gift: "with his firstblood, he initiated a boy named Mick", who fell into the hands of the church guard and "got" the order. According to Mick's account, confirmed by an old librarian, the then Nathaniel was a kind and sympathetic eccentric. He endured the beatings of the philistines, although he could destroy them, and gave the [[pervokrov|firstblood]] only because Mick himself asked. David believed that Nathaniel and Paul were one person and that Paul had been hardened for ten years in Waymar prison. ([[paul|Paul]])

However, in The Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] tells a different story. He, the "light", fell into the Smokey Dahl at the crash, and Paul, the "dark", fell "straight into hell", into a melt, and survived separately from him; torture in the Waymar prison was for Paul "not torture, but sweetness". So, the young man from the story of Mick - the "light" who fell into the Smokey Dahl - was Nathaniel-Navi himself, and Paul got into the Shayland prison; David, by all indications, merged the two aliens into one.

The bearer of the firstblood, Mick became the order ([[sadovniki|Gardeners]]) “performer” — those who can talk with Objects. Exactly when and how he left the order is not told; by 1775 he was already called a sorcerer, wore a protective belt stolen from the order and was apparently associated with the "Second of the Five" - Count [[kuindar|Quindar]].

## Magician

### Rescue of Satellites
The sorcerer appeared in "A Doll on the Throne", in the chapter "Satellite - 3": at the end of January 1775, in the basement of a city house in Melorange, the capital of Little Earth, where Prince Hector's people were going to hang Neymir and [[chara|Chara]], captured by the Shimmerians. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]; [[nejmir|Neimir]]) No one heard the creaking of the hinges — just on the threshold suddenly there was a man in a wide-brimmed hat, hiding his face, and a gray cloak; his palms were lying on his lap belt, and there were no weapons with him. “He who does not care about your iron,” he replied to the guard, threw a pack of red sparks in his face and put a light slap on the shoulder. He placed his hand on the bald top of the head: blue threads stretched from the fingers, the head flashed with an invisible flame without fire, heat and smoke, and a bare skull remained on the shoulders of the corpse.

> "I'm a magician. Call me that.”

As he lifted his hat fields, Neymir saw a broken, curled-up nose, a golden tooth "under a shimmering cleft lip" and such oblique faded eyes that it was impossible to know where he was looking. In return for salvation, the Sorcerer demanded that the Companions jump to Moran's Steppe Fire, warn him of the ambush the Prince was preparing, and then bring the Sorcerer with the chief and vouch for him. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) Moran and Neymir considered him to be a swamp man from [[darkvoter|Darkwater]]. The companions told Moran about it, but then they tried to kill the leader who betrayed them. Running from under Melorange, the Companions wandered for a week in search of gold. ([[nadezhda|Hope]]) In February 1775, in the inn of a remote town in the east of Hope, they came across the gauntlet of Birai with its Shavans, who called the sorcerer "master", and immediately the sorcerer himself appeared. He reflected Chara's arrow with an invisible shield and burned Neymir's hand with sparks, and then concluded that "Steppe Fire is lost to him", and changed the plan.

### The Bank Robber
For the new plan, the Sorcerer hired seven Shavans of the Ganty of Biray, and added Satellites to them. In February and March 1775, they were to rob the banks of the Earl of Shayland in the free cities of Almera: Crystal Fall, Toystone, Clermi and others. The Free City Police could not find the robbers behind its walls. The sorcerer has set two conditions: to work at least ten banks and not to touch each other while his plan is going. So that no one remembers the faces, he advised everywhere to shout that there are nine of them. Neymir and Chara, playing a rich southerner and his slave, distracted the clerks and conducted reconnaissance, and the people of Biray then cut the servants, cashiers and casual customers: the sorcerer ordered not to leave the living. He took a third of the booty himself, threatened to kill everyone if someone killed his own, and after the business he left somewhere, and returned without money. Of the two banks robbed after Crystal Falls, the gang took two thousand and a half thousand ephes.

The Magician’s power was in fear. After the massacre, he passed among the corpses, spread the dead eyelids and, licking his lip with pleasure, “absorbed” their glass eyes; one day he took out of the bag the severed head of the bank manager and pressed his thumbs to her eyelids. The Shavans whispered that he drinks the souls of the dead and prays to the Spirit of the Worm, and the money for him is only "to divert his eyes." When Shavan Haggott received a severe wound, the sorcerer only winked at Birai - why, they say, to tinker; the gauntlet still took the wounded to the doctor. ([[khaggot|Haggot]]) When the guard with the crossbow burst into the hall, the sorcerer, without getting up, held his hand along the belt - there was a green shine on his fingers - and the bolt broke against the invisible shield; he opened the locked door, holding his hand along the belt and attaching his palm to the castle, so that sparks ran through the iron.

### Newbie and Laxett
The mysterious Newcomer entered the gang - an educated, courteous and deadly warrior. When the Wizard decided to take Laxett on the day of the public execution, Novichok called the plan risky; The sorcerer spread his fingers with the “claws of a crow” with a blue spark between them and threatened him, and the novice, lifting his hands, retreated. At the end of March 1775, during a raid on the Bank of Laxett ([[blekmor|Blackmore]] County), a signal gong sounded, the gates of the city slammed shut, the guard bolt knocked his hat off. The novice deliberately led the detachment into a trap: the wounded sorcerer took off his pants for dressing, and with them the belt. The novice hit him with a boot in the jaw, pulled out his belt and declared the Shavanam that the sorcerer was "not a magician and not a demon", but a "lucky scumbag", who was lucky enough to steal the talking Item.

> “He does not drink souls and does not possess magic! All this is a game. It is a fear that you will listen and obey.”

All the magic of the Sorcerer was in the belt, and the value of the Sorcerer himself was that he knew from whom this Item was taken.

### Interrogation at the cemetery
At the cemetery near Laxett, the Newcomer, who had been called the Hunter by the Shawnees, led the bound captive to the burial well, snapping his fingers across the stripped belt. The sorcerer sizzled the curse and called upon the Spirit of the Worm, but the Hunter offered him to "drink the soul" from his own eyes - and the prisoner recoiled. At first, the sorcerer claimed to have stolen the belt from an unnamed lord met at a hotel in Farway. The hunter threw him into the grave and covered him with earth, leaving only a bamboo tube for breathing; among the riders was a surviving Haggott. Finally, weeping, the sorcerer repeated, "Tower-Tooth, Boundless Failure, Second of the Five." The hunter considered it true and a place to look for the Master of the Rings. He left the fate of the captive to the Companions: “The world will become cleaner if you leave it to die. Your debt will be repaid if you save it.” The fate of the wizard is unknown.

## Mick and the Magician are one person.
The sorcerer never mentions his name, but it all fits with Mika:
- **Hare's lip.** Mick in the interlude "carried out a hare's lip" to complain of passers-by, and was called Guba for ugliness; the Sorcerer had a golden tooth glittering "under the gilded hare's lip", and the foam from the ale he licked "from the hare's lip".
- **Shayland's banks.** Shayland's bank guard nearly beat little Mick to death, and it was Mick the Wizard's dream to clean up that bank with magic. A sorcerer with a magic belt robbed only the banks of the Earl of Shayland, taking from the gang the word to work at least ten, and ordered not to leave the living.
- **The firstblood.** Only the bearer of the firstblood can speak with the Object; Mick got it from Nathaniel, and the Wizard freely ordered the belt.
- **Order.** Mick "got" the order; The sorcerer, according to Adrian, was the "performer" of the order and the "provost who betrayed" the Lady in Darkness, and his master was named the Second of the Five.
- **Self-titled.** The last words of Micah in the interlude are "I am a sorcerer of great power"; the first words of the sorcerer upon acquaintance are "I am a sorcerer. Call me that.” As in 1756, Mick frightened the guard with a deadly word, not knowing how to kill without the Subject, so the sorcerer kept the gang a horror story about a soul-eater.
- **Age.** Mick was about fifteen in 1756, so he was about thirty-four in 1775.

## Relationships

### Nathaniel
For Mick, Nathan was first a curiosity and a chance to become famous, then a friend, and finally a teacher. Nathan paid him with trust: he promised to repay for the help, gave the “seed of strength” and at the last hour released from the oath. But Mick betrayed his secret to Doug, and, according to Nathan, it was this that deprived Mick of the opportunity to leave with him.

### Doug
Doug was two years older than Mick and half a foot taller. He screamed loudly ("Gook"), called Mick "small" and used to decide for him what to do. Their friendship rested on Doug's strength, and eventually greed destroyed him.

### Master Linen
A short-sighted old librarian saw in the miracles of Nathaniel only the blessing of Foremother Emilia and fed the guest with books. He kept Mika strictly, but was the only benefactor for the orphan.

### Satellites
Neimira and Charu were rescued to mediate in front of Moran, and then taken to the gang. Chara never forgave him for his pleasure and added him to her list of traitors.

## Other "sorcerers"
The sorcerer should not be confused with the Znaharka, a woman with a "fingerprint" (Hand of the Znaharka), whom the Puppeteer sent to treat [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] and whom the Kairs killed in the spring of 1775. It is not associated with the "swamp wizard", as if sent instead of Erwin to Waymar (the invention of [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]]), and Nathaniel, whom Mick in 1756 took as a sorcerer.

## Belt Properties
According to the scenes with Sorcerer and Richard, it can be seen that the belt, strewn with multicolored plaques, gave the carrier:

- The “sparks” that the sorcerer took off with his fingers: red, blinding and falling from his feet, blue threads of invisible fire that burned flesh, and sparks that broke the locks.
