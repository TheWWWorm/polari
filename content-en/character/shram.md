## Who He Is

Scar is a kair from among the Xs, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]'s select guard. ([[kajry|Kairs]]) Another name in the text is not named: even turning to the ghost, he says about himself "My name is Scar". It's a bearded giant, nearly seven feet tall. His ancestors were pirates, and Scar willingly reminds of this: offers sea landings, tells pirate stories and jokes about the “board”. He plays well at the stake, sings under the mandolin and has been to [[fliss|Fliss]] and the Mother Mills before. In Fantasia, he has an iron tooth.

## In "Humans and Gods"

**Battle at the road-dam.** In June 1775, in a campaign against the parish of [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]], Lieutenants Manfried and Schram commanded the second and third companies of the X-cloaks. These companies have the most heavy riders. Erwin saw in both commanders the "severe bearded" ones his father loved: stubborn as bulls, and not too flexible in mind. General [[veksler|Wexler]]'s Almeran knights were the first to strike the third company. Scar managed to rebuild the fighters: forward, he put the Latvians in heavy armor, and let the lighters follow in two waves. The Latvians lost more than a dozen men, but withheld the blow, and Manfried's company arrived to help finish off the enemy. When it turned out that the captured general was a dummy, Scar took the blame: it was necessary to bypass Wexler from the rear, and he allowed the kairs to “run straight”. He even offered to resign.

**Council and siege.** At the war council, Scar and Manfried approved [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]]'s plan to take Fliss. After the night trap in which Wexler died, Manfried's and Scar's companies came to the aid of the retreating Leed Wolves. Upon learning of the victory, Scar was the first to shout "Glory to Agatha!". Under [[dojl|Doyle]], he remembered his ancestors and offered to seize the ship and enter the bay under the guise of merchants. He later assembled a "pirate team" from the kire and tried to break into Fliss harbor by boat at night, but retreated under the fire of a ballist. He and [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] claimed that the guns of Fliss were on the lake bastions and would not reach the outskirts of the cliff. When the Blast caught fire, Scar grimly remarked, "Soon we'll be roasting."

**Go to Steppe.** After the Battle for the Breakdown, Scar suffered moderate losses. It was Scar, the "descendant of pirates", who again proposed a sea landing: to select four hundred best warriors and go on eight ships along the Smoky Distance and Holliwell almost to Rei-Roy. Baron Icewind supported him. ([[kurt-ajsvind|Kurt Icewind]]) Under Rei-Roy, Erwin left a third company in reserve behind a hill with loaded crossbows to cover the retreat. [[otets-david|Father David]] was also with her.

## Personality

The scar is brave, straightforward and loyal to the Duke, but not devoid of cunning and humor. Under the jokes, he covers his comrades: when Erwin asked who was betting on his mistake, Scar replied: “no one doubted your orders.” He was ready to use prisoners as slaves, and to lead the traitor “on the board”. At the same time, he is sentimental: he sings ballads and admires Nexia.
