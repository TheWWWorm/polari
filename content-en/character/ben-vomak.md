## Who He Is
Sir Benjamin Womack is the small lord of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]. He owned land east of the church's ruins, along the road that led [[kharmon|Harmon Paula]]'s train to [[soltaun|Soltown]]. His home is a small castle, more precisely a fortified mansion on a hill beyond the grove, with a banner above the roof. With him was a detachment of six squires in dark green camsoles (one of them served as a squire at the shield), armed with bows, spears and long daggers; the sword was only with the lord himself.

Having learned the name of the local lord from the oncoming northerner, Joaquin Yves Hannah boasted that he knew "the sire of Ben Womack" by the army of Count Rantigar and remembered his coat of arms: three acorns and a bowl. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) He even offered to come to the knight for a light. Praise looks doubtful: shortly before Joaquin admitted to [[polli|Polly]] that he had only been in the War of the Mills for two months and had not been in any battle. ([[melnichnaya-vojna|Mill War]])

## Meeting with the train
In May 1774, Womack and his men returned from an unsuccessful hunt: only two pairs of hares hung at the saddles. There was mail under the collar. The unit blocked the road. Joaquin, who began to greet, the knight broke off and demanded the chief. To Harmon, he threw, "Drop your grace deep, I'm not a count!" Womack's armor-bearer had a good idea of the native lands of several travelers. Then the Lord announced that from the Northern Crown "crowded rod" sick with the gray sea, and ordered everyone to undress for inspection. ([[sizyj-mor|Blue-Grey Plague]])

While the squires drove people off the carts and broke the locks of the chests, Womack himself was amused: he ordered tossing bags of Shimmerian tea and cut them with a sword in the air. For Harmon, the order to undress was deadly: under the caftan he hid the Light Sphere (see Sale of the Light Sphere). ([[lzhe-sfera]]) Of all the travelers, only Louise seems to have kept her cool. ([[luiza|Louisa]]) The knight's squire, meanwhile, transplanted Polly into his saddle and took up the truncheons of her corsage.

## Fight with Joaquin
Joaquin, still dressed and armed, retreated from the copies and called himself "Sir Joaquin Eve Hannah of Sad Hill." He stated that he had fought with Womack at the Mill Ford and at Three Eve when he was "still a man of honor", and called him into the fight. Womack would prefer to raise the impudent spears, but Joaquin threatened that then people will learn how the knight in the mail was afraid of the hopeless young man. A reminder of the lost War of the Mills finally angered Womack, and he took the shield. ([[melnichnaya-vojna|Mill War]])

An experienced fighter, Womack pressed the enemy with a blade and a chained shield, knocked him down with a rim blow to the cheekbone and wounded him in the shoulder. He played to the audience and mocked: “Did the Three Eves treat you like that?” Squires met every blunder of the young man with a gully. But Joaquin was faster. Sitting under a wide blow, he drove a dagger through the chain mail into the knight's thigh, slashed his wrist and put a blade to his throat:
> “Thank you for the meal, good sire. I like it. What about you?"

The knight quickly bled and snorted at his men to obey. The Squires put their weapons in the van and gave the horses away, while Snip, Doxet and Whirlpool were armed with axes and axes. ([[snajp|Snipe]]; [[doksett|Doxett]]; [[vikhor|Whirlwind]]) The squire tied up the owner and, together with one squire, took the unconscious Womack to the castle. The others walked into the field. In farewell, Joaquin hit the squire in the jaw with a sword handle for "helping Polly with her clothes."

## Aftermath
No one in the wagon expected Womack to forgive the offense. His wounds were bloody, but not fatal when quickly leaving, so Harmon waited for the chase. The train turned back and in the rain went north, on the road that did not lead to Soltown. The chase, if it was, went off track.

The trophy horses were useful to Harmon. One of the horses, a toy horse, he gave Polly until now, the rest were going to sell in Soltown. Squire Womack's raven stallion later went to Joaquin. Later, on the way to Labelin, Harmon feared that one of the monks would remember a remarkable horse (he had a bald spot on his back foot); one of the brothers actually said that the horse was familiar to him. Victory over Womack became a source of pride for Joaquin.

The fate of the knight in the cycle is not described. In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]'s father, [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]], advising his daughter to create a personal guard, says he will select people, and calls among them "Sir Womack" and "Sir Peter". Whether it is a knight, the text does not specify.
