## Origins
Nisa herself told Chara that she was born in Holliwell. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) However, Shavan Haggott, Adrian's guard, took her to the Upper Reis border by word of mouth and misspoke, which he cannot say for sure. Nisa never told her story, but Harmon guessed a lot. Her Shavan parents were killed by other Shavans, although Stepi's children do not usually fight each other to death. The girl was taken prisoner and tried to teach obedience. She resisted, and she was severely beaten: two fingers on her left hand kept traces of fractures, as if the palm was trampled with a heel. She was then kept “like a wild animal” on a ship. For Shavan, according to the narrator, there is no worse torture than imprisonment.

## "The Skyship and the Girl"
In August 1774, Ganta [[ganta-groza|Thunderstorm]] auctioned Nisa at the Tea Exil in [[lajem|Laem]], [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] Kingdom. Of his forty captives, there were six by the last evening. Happiness architect Onoriko-Meysor accidentally saw her on the schooner of the Thunderstorm, and, according to the narrator, she "settled right in the chest" of the matchmaker. ([[onoriko|Onoriko-Maysor]]) He wanted to buy it for himself one day, but he had no money. On the platform, Nisa was able to squeeze only: “I am Niza.” She later served drinks to the guests. When the merchant Timeret grabbed her lower back, she tipped the jug over his head. Timeret hit her, Groza by ear brought her back, called her a "spoiled girl" and promised a dozen lashes.

Onoriko explained to his guest, the merchant “Khoram”, that is, [[kharmon|Harmon]], that the unsold proud gauntlet will throw overboard a mile from the shore: the West does not forgive this, “certainly not a commoner.” Harmon came back and bought it "not for bed". In the morning, when she first saw the celestial ship Onoriko, the girl could not hide her delight. When the bankrupt Onoriko told about his flying ball, the eyes of the Bottom caught fire, and Harmon seriously decided to try himself in aeronautics. Later, Harmon offered her to go anywhere, but she had nowhere to go: she alone would die or be captured again.

## "The Yanmei Hunt"
### In [[melison|Melison]]
In the autumn of 1774, Harmon, under the name of "Glorious Horam", bought an old estate in the winery Melison in Shimmery and settled there with Niza, the inventor [[gortenzij|Hortensius]] and the ruined Onorico. At the estate, Niza hardly spoke and answered everything: “Thank you, dear.” She ate not for the sake of taste, but in reserve, in case of hunger. The narrator writes that she had wings, but "embarrassedly hidden under clothes". One day at dawn, she left to walk alone, just to feel free. Harmon was horrified to think she had been kidnapped. In the evening she was brought by the priest Father Elisius. Nisa asked him for a "sacrificial altar to speak to the Spirit of the Steppe." He led her to the icon of Sophia the Great, and she prayed aloud in the steppe dialect, not embarrassed by the presence of others. Elysius reasoned that she was a “good man,” and Nisa wept with joy.

Harmon in anger reminded her how much he had done for her. The narrator explains that poke shavana nose into his duty is like laughing at the coat of arms of a northern knight. Nisa said, “You did what you wanted. Today I did what I wanted,” and squeezed the cup like a weapon. Since then, she's had dinner at Eliza's. The townspeople stared at "one of these" shavanks, and once Harmon heard in the bazaar that they were going to kill her.

### Sky
Her phrase about clouds suggested to Harmon that at different heights the wind blows in different directions. The sky became for her “the only ray of light”, and she called the ball “the heavenly horse”. On March 8, 1775, on a tethered ascent, as Nisa was sitting alone in the basket, a gust of wind tore the winch, and the ball swept away. Harmon stopped the winch with a shovel, and the tension rope broke. Hortensius suggested that Nisa herself found a valve to vent hydrogen, and in the evening the ball was found whole. Harmon then realized that he did not love her, and she decided that he deliberately tore the rope. The Marquis of Miral-Senelius offered for the ship on behalf of the Second of the Five Thousand Hilt, but made it clear that his seigneur did not want Harmon to build a new one for himself. She sighed and clasped Harmon’s hand, and he asked for time to think.

### Legend and Sphere
In late March 1775, Nisa asked Harmon why he didn't take her by force if he wanted to, and he didn't perform feats if he wanted fame. She then told him the steppe legend of the Gett brothers and Hanosha and the miracle calf Ollay. ([[legenda-o-gette|The Legend of Gett]]) According to her, the legend shows two habits of the Shavans: foreigners know only the Getts, who grab everything they reach, and she hopes to become Hanosh. Harmon she called Ollay - wonderful, but helpless without the care of others. Embarrassed, Harmon took her to the cellar and showed her the Light Sphere. Niza asked how many he had killed for her, and he confessed, "Maybe five." Stopping Sphere's ring with her palm, she said he was "more Gett than Ollay", and left.

### Salvation and captivity
Tyke Nisa still followed Harmon, "like Hanosh after Allay." When [[moger-bakli|Buckley]] took him away and locked him in the cellar with Vanessa Lilith, Onorico's wife, and her children, Niza got a horse, found their trail, and released the captives. “In the Steppe, every prisoner is left with water,” she sighed, seeing that Harmon was thirsty. It was at the beginning of April 1775 in Laem.

They went back to Melison for four days: Harmon gave Niza a saddle and walked until she, tired of the delays, stole the second horse. In Melison, meanwhile, frightened servants spread the rumor that Onoriko, Horama, and Hortense had been stabbed to death by Niza; when they realized that both the merchant and the girl had been kidnapped, the townspeople began to pray for the health of both. At a meeting with the Marquis of Miral-Senelius, Nisa sat next to Harmon, secretly finding a dagger in her boot, and directly asked the Marquis what all his words lead to. Harmon asked the marquis if he could take her on flights, and Nisa was angry that he had "sold" to the Second of the Five.

In May 1775, Nisa flew with Harmon on the "Heavenly Sphere" from Melison to the Bottomless Fail. She shone with quiet happiness, did not complain about any adversity, and Harmon then thought that he would like to see her as his daughter. Over the Fail, she looked down with childish delight and was ready to go down. When the Marquess Booth threatened them, she pulled a dagger out of her boot and wounded him in the thigh. Booth put a knife to her throat, and Harmon saved her by running the Sphere. Between 20 and 28 May, Hunter's archer set fire to the torch with an arrow, and the ball descended into the gorge. Niza grabbed the dagger: "The ship is ours!", but the old chief of the goat people, Zandur, struck her with the axe's ox. So she and everyone was captured by the Hunter, that is, [[adrian|Adrian]], and his Shavan. Char's archer freed her under Steppe's law: "Shavan does not feud with Shavan in a foreign land." ([[chara|Chara]]) Niza said that Horam bought her back and returned her freedom, and she stayed with him because he was a “good man”. When she was not taken on a dangerous flight, she seriously warned Chara: if something happens to Horam, she will answer to her. Later, Harmon recalled that the Shavans not only let Nisa go, but also paid her a lot of money. While the Hunter captured the Abode-u-Bezna, Niza remained on the other side of the Gap and, along with Shavan Gurlah and the Zundas, guarded the captive monks sent by the Second of the Five in pursuit.

## "Humans and Gods"
On May 29, 1775, the Ganta of Birai led Niza to beat Harmon at the Abode-of-the-Abyss. ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]) She herself cured his bruises with parks: “Glory, if you got as much as me...” Adrian told her to help Harmon serve on the table. Niza replied that it was not a great honor, but better than washing the floors or “dancing naked”. When the emperor explained to her the rules of etiquette, she clarified whether it was the same as saying "tyre tone of shooting" over the dead. She then innocently revealed that Harmon was hoping to become a minister of air navigation, and Adrian frowned. The bottom of the dish soap woke Harmon up at dawn and shared leftovers from the imperial table with him.

In June 1775, on a march across the Lion Mountains to the sea, Nisa did not return from the field kitchen one evening. [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]] kidnapped her to force Harmon to find out the secret of Adrian in four days, and threatened to send him her hand. In fact, Niza was kept in comfort and fed with knightly rations. She was bored and did not even realize that she had become a hostage. When she met with Harmon, she happily told him that the jailers did not believe in his success, and she replied to them: “Holy calf Allay, everything is possible!” The meeting took place in the tent of Magda at White Stone Bay, from where Adrian's army left for the sea. Having learned the necessary secret from Harmon, Magda promised to let Nisa go when they get off the ship and she checks his words. Nisa, not realizing that she was a hostage, offered her help to Harmon and left the tent herself, uninhibited by anyone.

## "The Shadow of the Great Tree"
### Freedom and engagement
In July 1775, sailing across the East Sea to Grace, Nisa and Harmon were on different ships. Harmon followed her into the spyglass and soon realized that Magda considered his assignment fulfilled and freed Nisa: she walked wherever she wanted, chatted with soldiers and sailors, and the knight, who had previously guarded her, treated her with tea and ale. The bottom remained with Magda's entourage. Harmon wrote her a letter offering her a hand and handed it over with a vest in an anchorage in Ruilde. The illiterate Niese was read by Sir Peter, the knight who had previously guarded her, and he missed the offer. In Grace, where Magda and her entourage came to the wedding with Adrian, Nisa came to Harmon with Peter at night and returned to him five ephesas paid for her: she wanted to be “completely free”. She announced that she and Peter fell in love and decided to get married, and Harmon promised to forever remain his friend.

### At Magda
In the autumn of 1775, in Faunterra Magda, who became Adrian's wife and sought a second maid of honor after Valerie Greysend, took a "straight" shavanka into the cups. ([[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]]) Niza objected that she was a simple girl with no titles and also a shavanka, but Magda replied that she liked obstinacy. The cup was supposed to be the first to taste her wine. She also asked Nisa to speak if her husband would "milk" her, and to get closer to Adrian's Shavans. Adrian Niza called “a mean jackal”. As soon as the imperial train left Faunterra in October, heading across the Southern Way to the north, Nisa came to Magda and reported that Adrian was carrying several Items, including a creepy Worm, in which she believed the Spirit of the Worm lives. ([[chrevo|The Womb]]) She heard the Lord call it a gift "to him who will go to the lighthouse." When Magda could not solve the mystery of Adrian about the cunning man and the lighthouse, Nisa remembered the legend of the Iron Sun, and she gave Magda an answer.

Magda instructed Nisa and Sir Peter to take the letter and purse to Colonel Horey in a thunderstorm: to warn that Adrian had promised his head to the Shimmerians. At the same time, Nisa brought Magda a note from her father hidden in a cleavage. On December 2, 1775, when Adrian's court in Main was watching the valley of the First Winter through the Bird without the Flesh and Magda recognized Harmon among the Minerva people by the celestial ball, Nisa sighed and went to the window. ([[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) During the battle for the First Winter, Niz, already a lady of Magda, saw the Rings knock down the ball, and begged the Wanderers to save the "glorious"; ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Magda reassured her that Harmon was not in the ball. The same evening, combing Magda’s hair, she reminded her of the legend of the Ghetto: the monster reached the capital of the Swordsmen and only there died, and “if Ghett had stopped earlier, he would have been alive.” ([[legenda-o-gette|The Legend of Gett]]) Then Nisa alone delivered to [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] and Minerva a letter signed by Magda and Adrian: on behalf of the House of Labelin and the "Lord of Adrian of Arden", Magda apologized and agreed to peace. She was "fragile and touching", but did not flinch in front of the nobles and directly said that she could not read. When the Origins jokingly called themselves "brutal", the shavanka petrified, and Erwin apologized for "a silly sense of humor". She's alive at the end of the cycle.

## Personality and Appearance
The bottom is thin and dark, with black hair, dark narrow eyes and a clinging nomad squint. She wore a leather sleeveless sleeve that opened her shoulders and stomach, and a dagger in a boot, and the cup was squeezed like a weapon. Short, proud and illiterate, she spoke directly and did not understand the hints. The words “fictitious” and “minister” had to be explained to her. The narrator notes that she never talked too much and knew how to listen. She loved freedom and the sky and measured people with steppe legends. She often repeated sayings such as "Any dust carries the wind", scolded "Fold tails!", and always called her patron "glorious".

## Relationships
### Harmon
[[kharmon|Harmon]] saved her life and regained her freedom, and she saved him twice: from Buckley's cellar and from Booth's dagger. Harmon hoped to marry her, but for Nisa, he remained "glorious" and a friend. After paying him five ephesas, she closed the debt. Harmon had long regarded her as a "traitor", although an inner voice reminded him that she herself had saved him from the cellar; eventually he decided, "Nisa has her way, I have mine."

### Magda Labelin
The kidnapper and then the lady. [[magda-labelin|Magda]] valued her directness ("I like it, she herself") and trusted her with the most dangerous assignments.
