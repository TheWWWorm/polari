## Origins

Minerva Gemma Alessandra of the lineage of Yanmei, known simply as Mira since childhood, is the only daughter of Sir [[klajv-stagfort|Clive Stagfort]], lord of the small fief of [[stagfort|Stagfort]] in the Forewoods. This northern domain is a vassal of Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. According to Mira's own account, her father, then a captain in the Scarlet Guard, proposed to Gemma Alessandra Hortensia, the sovereign's second cousin on his mother's side; Mira is therefore [[adrian|Adrian]]'s second cousin once removed. The engagement took place a month before the [[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]. Clive survived only because he was in hospital during the conspiracy, but suspicion stained his name, and after the conspirators' trial the young couple had to leave for the North. They married in Stagfort, and Minerva was born a year later. Her mother died of consumption when the girl was three.

Her father, who called her “Little Mia”, taught her reading and writing, mathematics, history, dancing, riding, [[stratemy|stratems]], and a little military science himself. He knew little about politics, so Mira initially knew little of the levers of state power. Together, they fed squirrels and went sailing. She turned seventeen in the winter of 1774.

## In “Arrow, Coin, Spark”

### Her Father's Death and the Disguise
On 21 March 1774, Sir Clive's party was attacked in the Forewoods. There were at least a dozen attackers, armed with crossbows and battle-axes and wearing no coats of arms. Clive died before his daughter's eyes when a bolt struck his temple. Mira and her companions were travelling to [[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]] at the invitation of Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]]. Her father's warrior Huron and the maid Lisha helped her escape, and a patrol belonging to the count met them at the Northwood border. Sybil sheltered her in the castle. She showed Mira the [[dom-yanmej|dynasty]]'s family tree: with the dead crossed out, Mira was fourth in line to the throne, or third if the disgraced jester [[menson|Manson]] was excluded. The countess reasoned that the girl had been the assassins' real target and took her to [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. Sybil herself did not attend Iona Origin's wedding, sending her husband, Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias]], and their children to First Winter. Mira had already seen Sybil's daughter [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]], a pupil at the boarding school attached to the monastery of Elena-by-the-Lake, at Bear's Fang.

After burying her father in Stagfort, Mira spent two more nights there. The countess's caravan then left Bear's Fang for the south along the Trade Road, through four hundred miles of northern forests. Carriages sank into the mud, they passed a burned village, and Mira's purse was stolen at one of the inns; they spent their nights at inns and with Northwood vassals. At the little rivers called the [[bliznyashki|Twins]], the travellers entered the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]. A month after her father's death, they reached [[labelin|Labelin]] and stayed at the Star of the Empire hotel on Cathedral Square. Sybil sent almost her entire retinue back, keeping only two knights, one of them Sir [[vanden|Vanden]]. There, she also disguised Mira as her own daughter Gloria, explaining that the criminal was apparently at court and must not recognise the victim who had survived. Mira's hair was dyed red and shortened by a foot, freckles were applied to her face with henna, and drops turned her grey eyes green. She was told to smile less often because the dimples in her cheeks betrayed Yanmei blood. After a week in Labelin, Mira, Sybil, and the two knights boarded a spark train and arrived in Faunterra on 7 May. Mira moved into the Northwoods' mansion in the capital, with its hundred-foot, three-sided green tower; she was introduced to the servants as Sybil's “daughter Gloria”. That evening, she met Lord [[lord-klarens|Clarence]], master of the imperial stables and the countess's alter.

### The Investigation
At an audience in the Small Reception Room on 9 May, Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] treated the countess coldly. He called Sir Clive's murder “an ordinary feudal quarrel” and Minerva herself a “little sickle”, the peasant piece in stratems. The insult roused Mira from the numbness in which she had lived since her father's death:
> “I am alive. I am clever. I am a spark.”

Despite the sovereign's anger, Sybil decided to remain in the capital and await the conspirators' next move. Under Gloria's name, Mira began hunting an unknown conspirator whom she nicknamed “Lord S”. Through the countess's alter, Lord [[lord-klarens|Clarence]], she gained access to the court archives, the Sanctuary of the Pen. There, she studied [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]'s book on the Jesters' Conspiracy and investigated the heirs to the throne. The death certificate of Baron Rosbet, who had supposedly died overnight of the [[sizyj-mor|Blue-Grey Plague]], led her to suspect poisoning and made her third in line. She befriended the secretary [[itan|Ethan]], and at the flower festival in the Lumini Gardens she tested the heir Sir [[adamar|Adamar]], telling him absurd Northwood hunting tales and an invented story about the death of her “friend Minerva of Stagfort”.

There, she also met [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]. In the Huntsman's Forest, Becca planned a prank on Adamar, while Mira nearly died at the hands of four mercenaries who, as it turned out, were preparing an ambush for Adamar himself. Becca saved her with her bow, and the girls swore to keep silent. The next day, Adamar died during a hunt, falling into that same ravine. Mira realised she could have saved him by revealing the ambush. At the summer ball on 2 June, Adrian jokingly drew the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]] a third of the way out of its sheath. The entire hall froze, but Mira laughed aloud. The emperor invited her, as “Gloria”, for the first dance. Mira fell in love with him, although she thought her love hopeless. On 19 June, she worked out Adrian's game against Duke Aiden six moves ahead, then beat the emperor himself. The jester Manson kissed her ankle, crying, “Clever girl!” At the same ball, Mira met her liege, Vittor Shayland, with Lady [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]. The count greeted her as “Lady Gloria” without exposing the substitution. On 9 June, Sybil tried to marry her to Priarch Gallard, but Mira put him off with deliberately impertinent remarks. That same day, she and Ethan searched for Leila Talmir, but the Linden Square address proved false. A letter from Becca told Mira that Erwin Origin had secretly sought Litland's support for reforms. After the game on 19 June, Adrian told her that the murders of the heirs were a warning, but that he would not abandon his reforms.

Her investigation led to Captain Corvis's letter in her father's casket and to [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]], who had kept an inn for twenty years in Pale Meadow, a post town seven miles from the capital. On 20 June, weakened by bloodletting, Mira travelled there with Sir Vanden in a Northwood carriage; on the way back, Mark transferred her to his own. The trail led from there to Aiden Almera's secret: twenty years earlier, the duke had learned of the conspiracy in advance and killed Corvis. Speaking with Mira, the Raven of the Crown, [[mark-voron|Mark]], called her vanity “a peculiar kind”: she cared nothing for success at court but loved being cleverer than everyone else. Mira wrongly suspected Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] of murdering her father and the heirs, and even used blackmail to turn Aiden against him. On 24 June, Sophia's Day, at the military parade opening the summer games, she handed the duke a page from his book inscribed “I know this is a lie”, then made a bargain with him in his carriage. Mira later realised her mistake and, through Mark, put the Protection on Aiden's trail. Meanwhile, on 15 June, the real Gloria had arrived in the capital under Mira's name, and the emperor lodged “Minerva” in the palace. On 2 July, Mark visited the already ill Mira at the Northwoods' residence and told her that the conspiracy had been uncovered. His men, disguised as Almeran guards, had intimidated a former jailer and obtained a letter showing that Corvis himself had informed Aiden of the plot, and that the duke had concealed it from the emperor for a month. The Crown also held Aiden responsible for the recent murders of the heirs, believing he had hired rogues from the slums to carry them out. Forewarned, Aiden and [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] fled the capital. Mira also learned that Ethan was an agent of the Protection and refused to see him. As her reward, she asked Mark only to put in a word for Becca with the sovereign and arrange a game with Adrian for her.

### The Poisoning
Mira grew weaker throughout the summer. A physician diagnosed “liver sickness”, and she was treated with bloodletting. On the morning of 3 July 1774, as Sybil insistently urged her to drink her coffee, Mira realised that the countess was poisoning her (the [[otravlenie-miry|attempt on Mira's life]]). She remembered that Vanden had once fallen ill after drinking the coffee that only she drank in the household, that her symptoms did not match liver disease, and that Gloria had not attended Iona's wedding. She rushed upstairs to her room in the tower and locked herself in. The countess and Priarch [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] were planning a substitution: Gloria, their daughter, was to become “Minerva” and the emperor's bride, while the real Minerva faced a supposed illness and death. Mira also accused Sybil of murdering her father. When the guards broke down the door, she stood on the windowsill twenty yards above the street, holding a crossbow to her chest. She threatened to shoot herself so that her body, with evidence in its pockets, would fall into the street. The conspirators yielded and swore “on our daughter's life” not to kill her. Gallard proposed the Monastery of Saint Ulyana in western Almera, claiming that there was already enough poison in her blood; Mira did not believe him. Sybil read and approved Mira's note to Becca, which contained a hidden meaning and was intended to explain “Gloria's” decision to become a nun. Mira spent the day locked up and was sent to the cave monastery that evening. She foresaw that “Minerva Gemma Alessandra”—Gloria—would be announced as the emperor's bride.

## In “Crybaby” and “Soul of the West”

In the underground monastery of Ulyana the Sorrowful, the novice Mia was allowed to speak only five words a day. She was set to digging tunnels alongside [[nora-plaksa|Nora, nicknamed Crybaby]]. Nora planned to kill herself by bringing a rock down on herself. Mia saved her and then devised an escape in a clay wagon, but could only get Nora out. She herself remained at the monastery. In “[[dusha-zapada|Soul of the West]]”, in August 1774, Becca writes letters to her friend from the capital and the country estate of Lacy. She judged that the farewell note signed “Gloria Sybil Dorina” was not in her friend's own words, and recognised “sorrow” as a hint pointing to a monastery of Ulyana the Sorrowful. She compiled a list of twenty-four such monasteries, but her father refused to give her men for the search.

## In “Only One Star”

### A Prisoner in Waymar
In early October 1774, a party of twenty Shayland soldiers led by the killer [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]], nicknamed “the Pair”, and [[frensis-myurrej|Sir Francis Murray]], nicknamed Eff, took Mira from the monastery. For three days, she travelled by carriage through Almera, where she managed to send letters to Adrian, Mark, and Ethan. She then sailed for six days aboard a lake schooner across [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]], arriving in [[uejmar|Waymar]]. From Inzhi's constant stories about himself, she recognised him as a former convict and hired killer. The prisoner was treated with honour but kept locked up. On the very first evening, she tried to escape: using a knife taken from a tray, she forced the count's young agent, Sir Francis Murray, known as Eff, to surrender his weapon, struck him with his own spark dagger, and locked him in a storeroom. Inzhi caught her at the gate. In the castle, she saw [[elias-nortvud|Elias]] and [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]], who were also being held there, and became convinced that the old count knew nothing of his wife's plot. The Northwoods had been travelling to the capital on the emperor's orders and were being detained in Waymar until Vittor arrived. A few days later, the count arrived with Iona and his brother Martin. Speaking for her brother, Iona invited the Northwoods to join the rebellion, and Craig swore to bring his army to Erwin. That same day, Mira first met Martin Shayland. Vittor showed her Adrian's letter: Sybil and Gloria had been arrested. Craig left with Northwood's army to join Erwin, while Elias remained under guard. Mira secretly warned Adrian of the Northwoods' collusion with the rebel, encoding her message in a book order for the shopkeeper Pierre. Eff intercepted the letter, but according to him, Vittor himself forwarded the warning to the emperor.

Mira taught her maid [[lindsi|Lindsey]] to write her name, and when the girl disappeared, she began investigating. She questioned the undertaker [[master-sajrus|Cyrus]], drew from the soldiers the information that Lindsey's secret lover used to take her underground, and found a hidden passage to the count's study. Among the papers there were invoices for the purchase of the schooner Rigmarole and funds received from the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]]. Mira discovered that Lindsey's lover was Eff and that the disappearances of the maids were the work of the count's brother, [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]. She read cards with Iona and her maid Jane. Soon Jane disappeared too. Following a second secret passage with Eff to the ruins of an old monastery, Mira encountered Inzhi, who served Martin. Inzhi stabbed Eff, and Martin beat Mira.

### In Martin's Hands
On 26 November, Martin and his “Apothecary” tortured her in the dungeon. The Apothecary bled her and burned her belly with coals, hoping to extract the “substance of life” from the blood of the dying. Lindsey, taken instead of Mira, had lasted only seventy-six hours, and Martin showed Mira her body in a barrel of brine. Mira was rescued by Ethan and Lieutenant [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] of the [[lazurnaya-gvardiya|Azure Guard]], sent by Adrian; they had entered the castle disguised as a travelling troupe. Mira took the Apothecary's ledger with her. On the road, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] and her kairs intercepted them. Mira surrendered to avoid bloodshed, but Iona took Martin into her own custody, acknowledged her debt to Mira, and offered her a choice: remain as an honoured guest or leave. Mira left.
> “You are the best enemy the gods could have created.”

### Empress
Sailing across [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]], Mira studied the ledger. It listed thirty-six people, excluding Jane and Mira herself, divided into “sources” and “samples”. For three days, she never left the deck of the oared vessel. The day before they reached [[fliss|Fliss]], she caught a chill and had to be carried ashore in a fever. She spent several days in an inn; in her delirium, she dreamed that Erwin was behind Martin's experiments. There, Ethan and Shatterhand told her of the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]], Craig's capture, and Erwin's escape. Once she recovered, Mira and her escort reached Aleridan in two days in three carriages that kept getting stuck in the snow. In [[aleridan|Aleridan]], she was seized by euphoria: she wandered the city, ate goulash in a stranger's courtyard, and sang a sailors' song, mentally forgiving everyone except Martin and Erwin. She toured the city alone, without an escort, and was reprimanded by the lieutenant, who reminded her that Gallard ruled Aleridan. The next morning, she went to the station to travel to the capital and learned that the northerners had captured the centre of Faunterra.

In December, Mira waited in Aleridan for news of the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]. Travelling to Adrian in Litland with a dozen guardsmen was dangerous, so she took refuge in an inn. When monks began watching them, Shatterhand forbade her to go out, and Mira suspected a secret connection between the priarch, Sybil, and Erwin. At last, Captain Hugh Grace of the emperor's vanguard arrived: Adrian was on his way to restore order in the capital and summoned her. Adrian met her and set her a stratems problem that hinted at his plan. After his victory over the Shavans, she was to take a train to the capital and assume command of the surviving regiments. Mira told him about Martin, shared her suspicion that Erwin and Vittor were involved in the experiments, and asked him to spare Iona. Adrian invited her to the capital, but not on the first train: she was to follow later with the main army. He himself left on the first train. She felt that Adrian had lied to her about [[pozhar-evergarda|Evergard]].

On 22 December, the emperor's train fell from the bridge over the [[bek-most|Beck]] (the [[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]). Mira woke to the words “Your Majesty” and learned that Adrian had died and Manson had been disinherited for his involvement in the emperor's death. By law, as the emperor's second cousin once removed through her mother, she became sovereign. She was secretly taken out of Aleridan before Gallard learned of Adrian's death, accompanied by Ethan, Shatterhand's detachment, and officers of the Crown. With the bridge over the Beck destroyed, the train took two days to reach the Crownlands by a detour through Orwell, where it overtook two of General Hor's regiments marching from Hope. Crowds of refugees greeted Mira in the capital. General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis]], with three understrength regiments, reported that a northern army twenty thousand strong was approaching along the left bank of the [[khanaj|Khanai]]. Meanwhile, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] had occupied the capital. Mira rebuked Major Beckfield for his cruelty and incompetent siege, and used his men to force the custodians of the Church of Mercy to hand over the Glove of Might. She ordered tents and flags set up on the bank and everyone dressed in red uniforms so that Origin would see nothing but scarlet. Beckfield's stone-throwers were to bombard the ice behind Palace Island without pause, creating a strip of fire to cut the besieged off from their army. She did not yet know how to use the Glove. Bluffing, Mira approached Erwin in the besieged palace alone under a white flag, leaving Shatterhand at the gate. She said she could kill him in a second, that other Yanmei would succeed her, and that Alexis would launch an assault in twenty minutes, while the melting ice left the northerners no escape. Above all, she convinced him that Adrian was not the real master of [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]], and that their true master was lying in wait while Yanmei and Agatha destroyed one another. Erwin demanded that the spark regiments leave the capital, and that after her coronation Mira convene the Chamber, repeal Adrian's “tyrannical decisions”, and enter a formal marriage with him. They reached a different agreement: Mira would not marry Erwin, would not disband her troops, and would continue Adrian's reforms, but she would restore the [[palata|Chamber]], recognise the former authority of the [[velikie-doma|Great Houses]], and accept the duke's control over her marriage and diplomacy. Erwin would receive any title he chose, retain his conquests, and remain at court with his army. He called Beckfield's burning barrels falling onto the ice “fireworks in honour of your coronation”. Soon, the arriving [[stetkhem|Statham]] and Robert Origin broke the siege ring, and Mira signed a peace treaty in the Empire's name.

## In “A Doll on the Throne”

### The Doll
After her [[koronatsiya-minervy|coronation]] in January 1775, Mira became a “doll” under Lord Chancellor Erwin. She drank in the evenings and played the part of an idle reader of novels. In the third week, General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] came to ask her to receive the spark regiments' oath of allegiance. Mira took it company by company, continuing by torchlight until night, so that she could see every soldier's face. From the general, she learned that only his five battalions remained to her. During a conversation with Erwin, she realised that he was deliberately drowning her in trifles. In secret, she drew up a fifteen-point plan, ranging from “drink coffee” and “find loyal people” to “take power away from the chancellor” and “resume Adrian's reforms”, memorised it, and burned it. In the same conversation, Erwin told her that, at Beckfield's insistence, the supreme court had sentenced both of them to death in absentia; Minerva later signed an appeal for the decision to be reviewed.

She recruited new servants, spoke to the remaining ministers, and beat [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] in a dog-sled race. At the theatre, she accused Iona of allowing Martin, the murderer of more than thirty people, to live comfortably under guard. During the interval, the drunken Minister of Transport, [[liam-shele|Liam Shellier]], exposed before the entire court that Minerva knew neither of Robert's appointment as treasurer nor of the deal with Born, and shouted that Adrian's body had been found. Mira identified the body and forbade its burial until the investigation was complete. There, Erwin gave her a letter apologising for the theatre and proposing that they convene the Chamber together. Immediately after identifying the body, she travelled with two guardsmen to the House with Ivy inn in Pale Meadow, offered [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]] the position of first lady-in-waiting, and took her back with her. On Leila's advice, she wrote Erwin a submissive reply, and on the return journey stopped at General Alexis's barracks to collect soldiers. When Shellier asked to resign, she “sentenced” him to continue serving as Minister of Transport. The former ministers [[albert-vial|Vial]] and Morlin-May were arrested—the first taken off a ship at Grace, the second seized at a villa in Orwell—and became her tutors in finance in their cells in the [[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]. They made her assess the “value added” by each of the Dynasty's fifty-three emperors. Mira judged her own to be negative.

Erwin insistently asked her to sign pardons for Sybil and Gloria Northwood. Mira signed, although she considered Sybil her father's murderer. Her decree, drafted with Erwin's involvement and delivered to Evergard by General Statham, required [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] to stop terrorising the vassals, release young [[albert-almera|Albert]], and hand Almera over to Alanis. Gallard called the decree a forgery (“Origin guided Minerva's hand”) and refused to surrender Almera, but released the Farway hostages. To raise money, she moved the April fair to Palace Island and introduced trade there using paper banknotes bearing her name. During confession to [[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]], she revealed Yanmei's Glove, which had settled onto her hand of its own accord. By threatening to show it to the people, Mira forced the Church to declare a miracle and invest its surplus of one hundred and forty thousand hilts in the Crown's banks. Then, aboard a schooner on the opening day of the navigation season, she held the embezzler [[drejfus-born|Dreyfus Born]]'s family hostage and forced him to confess and return the taxes he had concealed.

### Bringer of Peace
The court learned of Litland's fall only when Prince [[gektor|Hector]] arrived. Under General Alexis's plan, one spark regiment was moved by train to Juliana's Bridge, from where it could reach the Steep Rapid crossing on the [[kholliwell|Hollywell]]. Against the advice of her counsellors and Erwin, Mira decided to meet [[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]] in person and asked for only one kair; Jemis volunteered. On the opening day of navigation, immediately after dealing with Born, she departed in her private seven-carriage train, equipped with a “wave” terminal, accompanied by [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]], a dozen Azure Guards, and Jemis. They travelled south past Orwell, along the southern branch line through [[nadezhda|Hope]]. Moran waited in the brown desert between Mason's Bay and the [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]], beside the abandoned Great Fortune fort, with four hundred horsemen, and had a rampart of bones poured out around the envoys. The Shavans surrounded her as she held a bouquet of lilies of the valley. Mira bluffed about a regiment of spark cavalry ready to seize the Steep Rapid crossing and offered Moran vassalage and shared grazing rights in the Shepherds' Meadows. In this way, she secured the lifting of the siege of [[melorandzh|Melorange]] without a battle. Moran demanded Rebecca Litland's hand, and Mira answered that Rebecca herself would decide. Her escort, the kair [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]], observed that she had “something in common” with his lord. While they were still at the train, Jemis told her about the peasant rebellion that had been concealed from her. The journey lasted five days. Back in the capital, she found the fair on Palace Island trading in banknotes, and appointed Morlin-May Minister of Taxation in place of the confessed embezzler Born. At Erwin's request, conveyed through Robert, she confirmed [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]] as commander-in-chief of the spark troops against the rebels. She signed out of sympathy for Robert, who now owed her a favour. That night, Erwin visited her with ordzh, confessed many things, and jokingly advised her to begin every morning with the words “I hate you, Lord Chancellor”.

On the morning after the army's departure, [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]], an envoy of [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]], came to her. He admitted that he had given the Snowdrops a dozen spark crossbows and promised another thousand so that the peasants could slaughter the kairs. He demanded more than eight hundred thousand to cancel the delivery. Mira issued him a down payment and promised the rest if everything passed without bloodshed. Leila advised warning Erwin by letter, but Mira decided to go herself. That night, she departed in a carriage with an enlarged escort, taking Shatterhand, Morlin-May, and the Marquis of Graysend.

The people nicknamed her “Bringer of Peace”. At the [[svyatoe-pole|Holy Field]], she passed through Erwin's army to the rebel [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]]. She heard out [[salem|Salem of Summersweet]] and promised tax reform, the abolition of tax collectors, triple compensation for confiscated grain, and a pardon. The peasants laid down their weapons, crying, “Glory to Minerva!” Morlin-May proclaimed the Crownlands' tax reform: a fixed levy would be paid through banks, without collectors. On her orders, those who had fired the crossbows were arrested, and she instructed Craig to withdraw the army. Thus she thwarted both Erwin's trap for [[paul|Paul]] and his planned slaughter, telling the Northwoods that “the Crown managed with its own resources”. When Erwin returned to the palace, she sent him a snowdrop tied with a ribbon: “Accept your place in the world with dignity. You are not the sovereign, my lord. Resign yourself to it.” She slept all the way back to the capital. Robert approved of her tax reform and, in repayment of his debt, gave her a dozen kairs and arranged a meeting with Colonel-General Statham. Mira then discussed with her inner circle who might be acting for the unknown “player” moving her like a game piece. She exposed the secretary [[dorian-ember|Ember]] as Erwin's servant and forgave him for the last time. After Statham's account of the oath-taking day confirmed her suspicions, she accused General Smile of serving a secret “master”; Robert's kairs surrounded him. The general asked to speak privately. “[[te-kogo-my-proshchaem|Those We Forgive]]” reveals that Mira heard him out, spared his life and rank, and promised to “make an attempt to forgive”: her power lasted only while greater forces balanced one another, and the Fox was useful. Her first lady-in-waiting, Leila Talmir, asked for five thousand hilts and a fief, then brought a syringe of fluid from the Healer's body for an experiment based on Erwin's theory of blood that could speak to Objects. Mira realised that Leila had overheard her conversation with the duke. She could not forgive her (“I am too tired to forgive”), but gave her the money and a deed to the fief.

Next, in response to a secret summons from [[uitmor|Captain Whitmore]], conveyed through Shatterhand, Mira visited [[marengo|Marengo]]. Whitmore and [[krojdon-bekfild|Beckfield]] told her about the [[absolyut|Absolute]] and the thieves stealing Objects, and handed over witnesses: Little G and her former kidnapper [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]. She ordered both thrown into prison. Beckfield asked her to reconcile him with Erwin, and she promised to speak on his behalf. She visited Inzhi to “learn to forgive”, told him to call her Mira as before, and offered a choice: the galleys or an injection of a mixture of blood, corpse poison, and a potion as “the judgement of Ulyana the Sorrowful”. After returning to Faunterra, she drank no wine for six days.

## In “The Yanmei Hunt”

Mira has no chapters of her own in this novel; she is seen through Erwin's eyes and those of other characters. On the night leading into 5 May 1775, after a theatrical performance for which she had invited Maurice Labelin to her box, Mira fled from Erwin. Her escort knocked out a soldier of the special company, and she took a “merchant” train to [[marengo|Marengo]], placing herself under the protection of Captain Whitmore's two Azure companies. Erwin arrived without an army on 6 May, but she kept him waiting in the garden until dusk before receiving him. There, in the garden of the Palace of Silence, she arranged a test for him: [[ganta-groza|Ganta Thunderstorm]] and Sormakh challenged him to fight, and Erwin cut off Sormakh's hand with the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]]. Mira admitted arranging it, demanded the return of her people, and revealed that the [[aleksis-smajl|Silver Fox]] served the [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]. The Queen of Darkwater arrived with her fleet and presented her with [[menson|Manson]], alive. On 7 May, Mira and her guests departed for Faunterra aboard three imperial trains. On the journey, she tried to discover from Manson how Adrian had died, but the jester demanded a trial. The sovereign reached the capital on the evening of 8 May. On the night leading into 9 May, she told Erwin that she knew about the [[absolyut|Absolute]] from Whitmore, and they agreed to hunt the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]] together. She demanded that her assistants be returned, Ethan released, and the special company disbanded. Erwin swore to her that he had not ordered Adrian's murder.

On 11 May, she opened the [[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]. Afraid of public speaking, she let Erwin speak first, and her own speech was confused. She appointed [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]] as the Crown's second representative and announced a census of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects]] and Manson's trial. From her table, Erwin deduced that the Absolute consisted of seventeen Objects. Erwin had suggested the census, while she herself proposed holding Manson's trial in the Chamber hall. After the session, she began drinking alone, lamenting that everyone she had loved was dead. The next morning, Erwin ordered her sobered up and taken to the ailing Lady in the Dark, while the “[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]” explained her absence as a visit to the queen. On the night leading into 16 May, after they had identified the Absolute's seventeen Objects, Mira herself kissed Erwin. He responded, but she called the kisses a mistake and began avoiding private meetings with him: she already knew about the Drop of the Sun from the Lady in the Dark and was hiding it. Between 21 and 25 May, she accompanied the queen to Arden, to the graves of Tellurian and Ingrid. On 25 May, Erwin forcibly kissed her again in the carriage and revealed that the Puppeteer needed the Radiant Sphere. Together with the Lady in the Dark, Mira secretly laid a trap in the Farewell mausoleum at Arden. On her orders, the chamber was filled with hydrogen to burn whoever took the [[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]. When [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade burst in with [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]], the explosion achieved almost nothing. Erwin's kairs were killed or maimed, including [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], and Ice carried off the Drop of the Sun.

At the [[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]], she prevented a hasty verdict. On 17 May, she rejected Francis-Ilian's motion to re-elect the panel of judges. On 21 May, she threatened to dissolve the panel if it rushed and gave the defence a day to find witnesses. On 25 May, she ordered Baron Bonegan arrested, opened an investigation into the bridge's destruction, and reproached Erwin for concealing the truth about Adrian's death. On 26 May, in the Chamber, she urged that Alanis not be condemned before an investigation and forbade Prince Hector's duel with Labelin. On 29 May, at Erwin's advice, she sent Shatterhand to stop the trains and warn Arrowback about the brigade. When the kairs locked the hall's doors, she tried to prevent an immediate vote, calling the lords “sheep” and Erwin their “shepherd”. When Manson announced that Adrian was alive and Erwin demanded his deposition and Minerva's recognition as sole sovereign, Mira raised the red flag, not the blue:
> “You will not have my vote. The sovereign of the Empire is Adrian, not I.”

She later said that she had “abdicated in Adrian's favour”. The Chamber deposed Adrian nonetheless. Mira argued that the crown now passed to Manson and again refused her own candidacy. The election of a new sovereign, in which Erwin's name was already being mentioned, was interrupted by news of Iona's plight. Later, Francis-Ilian and the Queen of Darkwater offered Minerva the role of “temporary ruler” until the final election, expected after the Puppeteer's defeat, and she accepted.

## In “Humans and Gods”

### Temporary Ruler
In late May 1775, Mira drank to her enemies. The following morning, the Lady in the Dark, King [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], and two assassins from Darkwater entered her bedroom past the sleeping sentries. They advised her to “take a rest” from ruling: in their view, her leniency towards Adrian had made her unfit. Mira demoted Shatterhand to lieutenant, summoned Whitmore's company from Marengo, and secretly began gathering forces. She tasked the banker [[konto|Konto]] with hiring mercenaries and sought an alliance with Maurice Labelin and the Shavans. At a secret meeting, Mira seriously considered killing Origin while his army fought the Puppeteer. Becca initially played along, then mockingly suggested simply telling Shayland where the duke would be. Mira was outraged, and Becca reminded her that she herself had abdicated, while Origin was going to war against two men who had thrown her into prison. Mira ordered her friend out. Labelin also refused an alliance. Ganta Thunderstorm refused too, reminding her that she had betrayed Moran by abdicating in favour of Adrian, who had waged war on the Steppe. Mira pardoned the Snowdrop leaders imprisoned in the Imperial Citadel on condition that, within a month, they bring several thousand of their former fighters into the Crown's service. In early June, Origin's army left the capital, and Mira felt lost without the opponent she had grown accustomed to working against. Shayland's saboteurs damaged the spark lines, halting trains to Marengo, Labelin, and the Beck, while the townspeople began ransacking the Puppeteer's banks. Mira ordered the sheriff to stop the looting and placed the Shayland banks' assets under Crown administration.

Mark took her to the mutilated [[itan|Ethan]]. He had been tortured by [[kajry|kairs]] from the Leed Wolves for trying to conceal that the body found was not the sovereign's. Mira tormented herself with guilt towards him. [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] returned the [[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]] to her, but attempts to awaken the Object with Inzhi Price's firstblood failed. Robert also revealed his cousin's secret order: the battalion left in the capital was to withdraw rather than fight if the enemy outnumbered it six to one. She took the acquitted Manson into her service when he asked to be her “jester”, despite Becca's objections. He mocked every decision she made and kept insisting that she would have to choose when Adrian returned. At Becca's suggestion, Mira changed her eye colour, put on a wig, painted on freckles, and walked incognito through the capital with her friend. At the fair, she bought an ugly little dog named Brunhilda for twenty hilts. At first, she kept Becca, whom Erwin had asked to marry Moran, beside her. Studying a map, Mira deduced that Adrian had most likely visited the Abode by the Abyss and left with Magda Labelin's army, either as its commander or as a prisoner. Then, realising that she herself was avoiding a choice, and that the heretics had allied with Moran, she sent her friend to Rei-Roy to negotiate with Steppe Fire. She wrote to the commandant of [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]] near Juliana's Bridge, repeating Becca's news and ordering him to give the Origins every assistance. At the same time, she signed a pardon for [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]], nicknamed the Pair.

### Navi
In late June, Mark returned from a long journey and reported the capture of four prisoners: Major Beckfield, the broken Sybil Northwood, Lady [[karen|Karen]], and a young man named [[navi|Navi]]. Learning that Adrian had ordered Gloria walled into the foundations of a summerhouse and had thereby already destroyed Sybil, Mira realised that there was no one left to take revenge on and kept the countess at court. Navi touched her hand and discovered that Mira had been “initiated”: [[pervokrov|firstblood]] flowed in her veins. She later concluded that she had received it in Martin Shayland's cellar. Navi taught her to control the Glove of Might: it made objects weightless and moved them with rays extending from its fingers. The first thing Mira did was lift a chair and move it onto the windowsill.

## In “The Shadow of the Great Tree”

### The Defence of Faunterra
In July, Mira brought Navi to Ethan. Navi was willing to heal him, but Ethan's parents refused, and Ethan himself asked her not to visit again. Navi questioned Mira about herself, making answers the stakes in their stratems games. She lost game after game and won only one, after remembering her declaration, “I am a spark!” News arrived that Paul had killed Moran and taken command of the horde. It later emerged that Becca had died too, and the Litlands blamed Mira.

The court began talking about evacuation, and the nobles bought up tickets. Mira then assembled the spark troops for a review outside the palace. Using the Glove, she raised a train ticket to Marengo above the ranks, ordered a soldier to tear it up, and then made the generals tear up their own tickets:
> “I am staying in Faunterra. This is my city. As long as I live, the horde will not reach it!”

Under Smile's plan, the capital was encircled with earthen ramparts, and Mira felt useful for the first time. She learned to split firewood with the Glove so that she could later use it to set stone merlons on the walls. Navi demanded a ship, the Bird without Flesh, and the Eternal Hilt so that he could kill Paul. Mira considered him a fraud, although his warning frightened her: “Beware of the generals; beware of Labelin!” Meanwhile, Mark reported Fliss's surrender and Mother Cordelia's death.

One day, Mark brought her the legless [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], the kair Henry Horton, who had been maimed at Arden. With the consent of his father, General Horton, Lord Robert Origin had allowed Navi, then calling himself Nathaniel, to heal the kair with the [[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]. Forty-Two came to thank the sovereign, but Mira, knowing that the ambush in the tomb had been her doing, refused credit for someone else's achievement. Realising that Navi could speak to different Objects, she wondered whether she could too.

On learning of Adrian's return and his marriage to [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]], Mira announced in the throne room that she would keep the throne because the Chamber had deposed Adrian, but would not spill the blood of the spark regiments. Mark proposed killing Adrian, and she refused. The Silver Fox went over to Adrian. Mira released Sybil from prison, having signed her pardon back in March, and freed Inzhi Price. She thanked Inzhi because she had acquired firstblood through him. By her decree, Adrian was appointed burgomaster of Faunterra. As a man accused of heresy and tyranny, he was to regard the post as “corrective labour”.

### Patrick's Chapel
Mira found the runaway Navi working as a waiter in the small town of Patrick's Chapel. Through Mark, she deliberately allowed the horde to learn that she, the treasury, the Hilt, and the Glove were all there. When Adrian's regiments approached, she refused battle, keeping her oath. Mark resigned and took her diamond hairpin as a keepsake. In negotiations, Adrian offered love and forgiveness in exchange for the treasury and the “newcomer” Navi, and Mira sent him to defeat the horde. She sent the treasury away with Robert Origin. They were attacked that night. Navi took the Glove, lifted her schooner into the air, and they flew away from the former emperor's troops. The horde's vanguard under [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]], following the bait, ran into Adrian's army and was defeated. Afterwards, the court whispered that the Bringer of Peace had laid the foundations of the victory.

### First Winter
During the flight, Navi told her about Paul, the Absolute, and [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]. Mira asked him to turn towards Stagfort so that she could see her childhood home, but Navi refused. He carried the schooner in “steps”: flying for as long as his strength lasted, then landing on water and sleeping. After the battle, he remained airborne for only half an hour before dropping the vessel into a little lake among fields, where Mira guarded him with the Glove for a full day. The second “step” brought the schooner to the River Beck above the dam, where General Horton's Agathan battalions were stationed beside the count's castle. Horton told her that the battle at Patrick's Chapel had saved the Crown: Johan Reis had been captured, many ganas had broken away from the horde, and Ganta Kort now led it. Horton proposed raising five thousand kairs and marching on the capital with the Glove. Citing her oath not to shed the spark soldiers' blood, Mira refused and ordered him to follow Lord Desmond's commands. She told him that his son [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] had been saved, though he had lost his legs, and the northerner shed a tear. On Smoky Distance, the crew boarded a vessel flying Shayland's flag. From the prisoners, Mira learned that the Origins were retreating through Northwood and asked to fly to Desmond's aid or to Stagfort, but Navi refused, fearing Paul would shoot them down. General William Davy's spark regiment was stationed in the Main Valley. The general angrily reproached her for a pointless redeployment and the victory she had stolen from him at Melorange. Mira admitted signing the redeployment order while drunk; Davy nevertheless acknowledged that serving the Empire meant serving her and led the regiment towards First Winter. At Mira's request, their final landing was on a glacial lake in a mountain saddle. In September, at sunset, Mira brought the schooner down onto the lake at the foot of [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] Castle. She announced to the townspeople that the capital had moved there, that the Glove, the Azure Guard, and Davy's regiment were with her, and that Robert would bring the court and treasury. She obtained permission from Castellan Highrock and the burgomaster to erect “one structure” in the valley, swearing to dismantle it if Erwin disliked it. There, she rebuilt the government: Ember's secretariat, the ministries of Leila Talmir, Morlin-May, [[liam-shele|Shellier]], and [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], and a new department for [[kharmon|Harmon]] and Henry Horton. The merchant considered it a ministry of aeronautics, while Forty-Two regarded it as an intelligence department. Forty-Two himself brought Harmon, the “pioneer of aeronautics”, to her, and the plans for his balloon showed Navi how to lure Paul out. Mira chose Inzhi Price as Navi's assistant. To defend the valley, she built an ice labyrinth: in the mornings, she went up into the mountains with Navi, Shatterhand, and Inzhi and positioned blocks of ice with the Glove; in the afternoons, she descended in a flying boat into the fountain on the square and worked at the town hall. Through the banks, money from her tax reform reached her from right under Adrian's nose.

Bad news arrived in November: the Southern Way, Shimmeri, and Darkwater had recognised Adrian, while Ganta Kort had allied with Henry Farway. Following the wounded from [[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]], [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] entered the valley with the remnants of his army and forty thousand people from Leed. Ashamed, Mira bowed to him first. Desmond approved of the labyrinth, and the defence was prepared according to a plan sent by Erwin. She assigned Navi to treat the wounded with the Healer's Hand and sent a female student to the infirmary to take notes on his methods. Iona later learned to use the Object from those notes.

Together with Navi and Harmon, she prepared a balloon for the [[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]] on 2 December 1775. On the day of the battle, she ordered dummies placed in the balloon instead of people. She herself flew with Navi, Shatterhand, and Inzhi in the boat behind the mountain, where twenty blocks of ice she had prepared lay waiting. Unable to kill with her own hands, she ordered Navi to strike the Puppeteer's army. The boat then circled Mount Agatha and landed by the pilgrims' house above the [[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]. Mira gave Navi the Eternal Hilt; he gave her a scalpel and his notes as a Descent Day gift and kissed her cheek. She spent the rest of the battle in the Agathan monastery, remained beside the chasm that night, and at dawn dropped food and blankets into it. Paul died in the Roe Deer Grotto. Navi fell with the Glove into the cave beneath the grotto and did not use it. Shatterhand's men found him on the shore of an underground lake with a broken spine and a smashed head. They thought him dead, but he coughed as they raised him. Meanwhile, Mira reached the town on foot, learned of Desmond's death and Erwin's injury, and took charge. The burgomaster was mourning his dead daughter, while she allocated accommodation to refugees, set up a camp for prisoners outside the walls, and sent Shatterhand with ropes to the grotto. Jemis and Nexia told her of Adrian's ultimatum, and she replied that the deconstructor threatened only Objects Paul had touched. She assigned Jemis to plan the defence of [[lid|Leed]] and Nexia to maintain communication between town and castle. Mira took the Glove off Navi and herself carried the boat with him aboard through the air to the castle, to Iona and the Healer's Hand. Realising he would live, Navi groaned, “Why?..” Afterwards, in the castle courtyard, Mira reconciled with Erwin. She called herself an “ungrateful fool”; he called her the Empire's “light and hope”. Each promised to become the other's loyal vassal if the Chamber elected that person sovereign. A letter arrived from Magda agreeing to peace.

### The Ending
The series' final scene took place during peace negotiations with Adrian. Through her people at his court, Mira learned that he had imprisoned the Puppeteer in the Womb and that Magda had poisoned Johan Reis. During the negotiations, she cultivated Magda; after the Lady in the Dark's murder, she placed Robert under house arrest, apologised to Adrian, and agreed to show him the Star. She herself controlled the Bird without Flesh. Through the Bird, those present saw the Star, and Navi revealed the truth about the world. He explained that if he remained alive, there was an approximately eighty percent chance that the secret order would reprogram the beacon on the Star and the world of the “gods” would discover Polaris. Only Yanmei's Glove on the empress's hand could kill him. Adrian begged her to spare Navi, Iona and Jemis asked her to kill him, and Erwin argued against it. Navi sat on the windowsill and twice said, “The choice is yours.” Learning the truth, Mira wept as she embraced Navi and tried to remove the Glove, but understood that giving it to anyone else would also be a choice. Minerva's decision remained unknown.

## In “Fantasies”

The author describes “[[fantazii|Fantasies]]” as stories about the characters' possible fates, rather than a continuation of the series.

### After the Peace
Under the peace concluded at First Winter in January 1776, Minerva remained the “temporary” sovereign and ruled from [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. Adrian acknowledged his position as burgomaster of Faunterra but retained the right to stand in the election for sovereign. Represented by Minerva, the Dynasty acknowledged its debts to [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] and transferred the Imperial Citadel, the Aerial Bridge, the Admiralty, and other buildings in the capital to him. [[magda-labelin|Magda]] became her governor in the Crownlands and sent half the taxes to Minerva in First Winter. Magda herself said of her, “In that northern swamp, Mimi is the most important toad.” Ordinary people called Minerva the “Bringer of Peace”, and her tax reform, under which taxes were paid through banks, freed them from the collectors' arbitrary rule.

After the events of the winter, Minerva, Erwin, and Iona were bound by a shared secret that Magda also knew. Erwin was initially angry with the sovereign, while Mira and Iona tormented themselves. By all indications, the secret was [[navi|Navi]]'s death, his life having been left for Minerva to decide in the series' ending. [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] observed that Minerva had apparently previously been in love with “an amusing young man named Navi”. Now, she wore mourning, punished herself with privation and hard work, and lived on work and coffee. After Nexia once made a point of kissing Erwin in front of her, Minerva, with impeccable courtesy, appointed her envoy to the central lands the next morning and sent her away to Hope and Almera. She tried to rouse Iona from her apathy after her return from a penitential pilgrimage by teaching her to use the [[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove]], but Iona's stones flew skywards and pots shattered, so the sovereign politely took the Object back. Minerva sentenced [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] to “honorary penal servitude”, bound by oath, in the Monastery of Agatha the Radiant. His tables showing how much each inhabitant paid daily towards the court, army, and ministries later formed the basis of her election plan. Every morning, she used the Glove to position granite blocks for the railway viaduct across the pass. A thousand-pood block that took a crew a day to place took her two minutes, and the builders called these hours “the Sovereign's Shift”. She also planned a dam on the Twins. Her court occupied the town hall, but she often slept in a tiny room in the castle. Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Origin]], having lost her husband and son, lavished motherly affection on her: “Mia, my treasure.”

### A Secret Agent in Faunterra
In “The Device”, set in the spring of 1776, the porter at the faculty of physics, who calls himself Eugene, is by all indications [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]: he has a “very amusing” nickname that is “not for scholarly circles”, and an adopted daughter at boarding school. He secretly took Professor Olli's notebook to learn about Adrian's device and reported everything to his “mistress”. She sent word that she did not mind the machine falling into Adrian's hands and sent the professor money through Konto's bank, with which Minerva had already done business in 1775. In “Let's Catch Him”, Leila recalls that “the old assassin who could influence the sovereign” had left for Faunterra back in the winter, and Minerva sends an unofficial enquiry there to a “reliable person”. By all indications, the mistress is Minerva herself.

### “Let's Catch Him” (July–August 1776)
During a fortune-telling session at the July full moon, Mira said that she had spent her previous birthday aboard a ship above a battlefield, and the one before “in the grave of Ulyana's caves, with a shovel in my hand”. During that session, a ghost appeared with a voice “neither quite a woman's nor quite a youth's”. It repeated [[navi|Navi]]'s favourite request—“Name a number. Oh, please”—promised “then I'll forgive you”, and accused Minerva: “Or will you leave me to suffer again?” Thus the dead Navi appeared to the sovereign, reproaching her for his death; that month, the ghosts took the form of whatever each person feared. Mira shouted, “I forbid it!”, flicked her lighter, and the ghost disappeared. Afterwards, she fell into melancholy, put her mourning ribbons back on, and cancelled a meeting with the duke. Lady Sophia organised a celebration to cheer her up. Lady Sophia held the celebration in the castle for her twentieth birthday. Minerva's gifts included a flying carriage for use with the Glove from the Guild of Aeronautics, a white mare from [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]], a book of terrifying tales from [[dorian-ember|Ember]], a hand-embroidered handkerchief from [[lejla-talmir|Leila]], and a painting from [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]. The kairs, meanwhile, “gave” her the ghost of [[dalliya-rejven|Dallia]], Erwin's altessa. Ember prompted the thought that “there is more than one Star”, which inspired a new plan before the election. On the night of the celebration, dishevelled and barefoot, carrying a shoe, she ran into the refectory after Erwin and found the X-cloaks with the ghost. On learning that she had been “given” the duke's altessa, she agreed to give him a “chance” to explain, and they left with their arms around one another. This marked the beginning of their affair.

### “Closer—Farther” (August 1776)
By August, Mira had secretly been Erwin's lover for about a month. For the first time, she had taken a favourite, “exercising Miriam's right”. Ember, Whitmore, the castellan, and a few kairs and guardsmen knew about the relationship; Iona was the last to learn. Ember, who had planned her days since the dog-sled races, advised her in this matter too.

Mira planned to reform the Mercy Clinic and make medicine an exact science: establish a ministry of health at the medical faculty and train a hundred physicians each year. She invited Iona, with her Healer's Hand, to become minister: “The Object's energy will run out, but the knowledge will remain.” Iona refused. They quarrelled at the clinic, and Mira flew away in anger in her flying carriage. They reconciled that evening in the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant. Mira apologised for her commanding tone and explained the question troubling her—what benefit does an emperor bring the world?—and her answer: the “goods” only the Dynasty could provide, spark technology and scientific medicine. Iona agreed to become Minister of Health, and there Mira and Erwin also confessed their relationship to her. When Bishop Amessin demanded that she repent for using the Glove in battle, both Erwin and Mira swore that no one had died by her hand: Nathaniel had done the fighting. Without Erwin's knowledge, Mira signed the list of candidates for priarch from the Forefathers' Church without crossing out any names: “Minerva G. A., lineage of Yanmei”. In return, she secured the Church of the Forefathers' votes in the election and the Wilhelmian order's printing press for a secret print run that the burgomaster of Faunterra must not know about. The Foremothers' chapter was unhappy about the loan she had obtained from Mother Cordelia: “Some call it a bribe.” She refused Leila's request for money for a spy network: “The Origins don't lie to me!” When Ganta Kort begged forgiveness, Mira demanded that he send her a khanid vir kanna capable of “using his brains at least a little” to build dams and bridges. The ganta promised to send three for her to choose from. The Steppe's envoys prostrated themselves and kissed her hem. Flustered, Mira began pleading with them to stand, and granted forgiveness only after Erwin prompted her. She later admitted to Iona that she had felt ashamed, “as if I were the one on my knees”. [[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]] watched the scene through the Bird without Flesh; Adrian and [[magda-labelin|Magda]] used that same Bird to watch Mira's affair with Erwin.

### “Ad-ri-an!” (October–November 1776)
After Nathaniel's revelation, Mira spent half a year “in a pit”, finding refuge in the work of rebuilding First Winter. Summer brought “sweet frivolity” and her secret affair with Erwin. The turning point came after the consecration of a fresco in the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant: Mira felt she was sinking into a “swamp of honey”. From then on, not a day passed without her thinking of the future. Nathaniel had left the decision to the sovereign, and Erwin had sworn to be her “faithful vassal”. In October, she left First Winter with Erwin and five hundred warriors, travelling through Main, [[dojl|Doyle]], the [[bliznyashki|Twins]], and the Wise River to [[labelin|Labelin]], then by train to [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. On the journey south, she explained her “Third Tree” plan to Erwin: before the Star's energy ran out, Polaris must study how it worked and build a dozen combat starships. Erwin objected that such a leap would require “ferocious tyranny”. In Labelin, news arrived that Amessin had been elected priarch. They quarrelled on the eve of their arrival in Faunterra. Mira admitted secretly consenting to the priarch's election in exchange for the votes of the Church of the Forefathers. She flung at Erwin, “Who would fall in love with a barrel of booze?” She said she had no intention of marrying him: he needed “an obedient little wife, like Nexia”, and his marriage to Lady Flame was part of her plans. Her election leaflet promised “PEACE, HEALTH, and LOW TAXES”.

In the capital, [[magda-labelin|Magda]] and Amessin met her. Magda warned that the common people supported Adrian and offered Mira the post of Minister of Finance if she withdrew from the contest. Mira took up residence in the Imperial Citadel, guarded by two Azure companies. In one day, she acquired “three new enemies” and lashed out at [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]], who was in love with her; she apologised the next morning. When [[lejla-talmir|Leila]] asked whether she was pregnant, Mira replied, “No. It seems firstblood prevents me from getting pregnant until I want to.”

In the Cathedral of Wilhelm, she confessed before [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] and [[mat-alisiya|Mother Alicia]]: she had signed permission for the priarch's election despite being only a temporary sovereign, and had concealed her deal with Amessin. By then, the Crown had repaid [[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]]'s loan. Mira surrendered her vial of [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]] to the chapter and swore to go to the stake if she used it without permission. She had learned the key to the Dust from a drunken Iona. Then she laid a slip of paper “with two words” on the table, and the prophet understood that the person she proposed testing was “not the priarch at all”. When the one-eyed [[khamfri|Humphrey]]'s band stopped her carriage, Mira ordered the Azure Guards to put away their swords and answered the crowd's questions about peace, prices, and taxes. She gave the old woman Muriel the only copy of her tax-reform plan.

In the Chamber, Adrian announced that the election was cancelled, and Amessin recognised him as emperor. Mira then used the Glove to lift the priarch into the air: if Adrian was sovereign, her consent to the priarch's election was invalid. Amessin had to remain. Earlier, she had persuaded the spark guilds to disable the lines to Marengo, thwarting the transfer of the Scarlet Guard. On the third day, the cardinals confessed to fraud. With a single movement of her finger, Mira moved the priarch's mantle from Amessin to Francis-Ilian's shoulders: “Congratulations on your election, Your Majesty.” She later told Erwin that she had “exposed Amessin and neutralised the monks”, and told the prophet that she had used the Dust “for the good of the Church”. When Adrian died and the lords began acclaiming Erwin, Mira faltered: “But what about me? I haven't spoken yet…” For the two days remaining before her speech, she, still the temporary sovereign, occupied the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]], vacated by Adrian's men. She crossed Palace Bridge with the same Shatterhand behind her as two years before, while Ember printed pamphlets setting out her reform plan.

In the Palace of Pen and Sword, Erwin explained why he was winning. Under the terms of their surrender, the people of the Sunset Coast and the Northwoods secretly supported him, and he was engaged to [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]. Asked about love, he answered, “Of course I love you. But power matters more than love to each of us.” Left alone, Mira wept: she had herself driven everyone away—Erwin, Iona, Shatterhand, Ethan, and Magda. Then she summoned Ember to rehearse her speech. In the Chamber, Mira removed her diadem and the Hilt and proposed abolishing taxes. Under her plan, the empress would charge for services: education, spark dams, healthcare with Iona's participation, and guaranteed military protection. The price was one agatka a week per adult, and a lord would be entitled to charge the same. The treasurer, [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], had made the calculations. Each land could decline these services. Francis-Ilian was the first to applaud, and Erwin exclaimed, “Bravo, Minerva!”

In the Cathedral of Wilhelm, the prophet proposed dividing power according to the model in Forefather Haruko's book “Empire of the Rising Sun”. Minerva would become empress, with civil and financial authority. Erwin would become the military ruler, a “shogun” or “lord chancellor”, while the Crown's army would shrink to one or two regiments. Mira agreed, provided the crown and Hilt remained hers. The lords considered a marriage between shogun and empress undesirable. If they nevertheless had a child together, that child would not inherit both titles. Mira assured them that she and Erwin had “already parted. For ever!” The prophet warned that after her coronation, Northwood, the Sunset Coast, Litland, and certainly Shimmeri would probably leave the Empire, although the Shavans were fervent supporters of her plan: Ganta Kort dreamed of the first spark workshop in Reis. The prophet admitted that he had also been testing her and Erwin as a “future crowned pair”. They left the cathedral in separate carriages. As they parted, Erwin flung at her, “A doll on the throne,” and she replied, “Lord Softie.”

### Other Stories
In “Amicably”, [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]] wears Minerva's pendant bearing a white gull, the coat of arms of [[stagfort|Stagfort]], in place of her former tree pendant. Katrin Katrin sees this exchange of jewellery as a sign of friendship and alliance. In the same story, Jemis recalls that Minerva once stopped a train to gather a bouquet of flowers. In “An Essay on the Subject”, a unified school “standard” has been introduced in the lands under Minerva's care, including Almera, with three levels of schooling, subsidies, and inspectors. The teacher Mrs Gale, by all indications the surviving [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], remarks acidly that she realised how serious the situation was “when I noticed Minerva above the blackboard”.

### Empress: “Death of an Assassin” and “A Brilliant Calculation”
In the later stories, Minerva already rules as empress. In “Death of an Assassin”, she cuts taxes, disbands some regiments, reduces the Protection and the household staff, and abolishes the Ministry of the Court entirely: its duties are divided between the secretariat and the first lady-in-waiting. She pays the former Minister of the Court, [[khoksvort-fellion|Fellion]], five hundred hilts and offers him a position teaching ethics. The “Voice of the Crown” writes joyfully about the reduction of the state apparatus, and at the end Mac admits that, as “the empress keeps saying”, budget cuts really do stimulate business activity. She has two envoys for special assignments: [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] for good news and [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]] for threats. Her “May reform”, discussed by Fellion and his guest at dinner, is also mentioned.

In “A Brilliant Calculation”, General [[uiljam-dejvi|Davy]] tells the Minister of Education how he saw Minerva on the eve of the Battle for First Winter: wrapped in furs, with rosy cheeks and shrewd eyes. She mixed up the dead relatives of two retired kairs, and they forgave her; in the mountains, she had already tested throwing blocks of ice with the Glove. Without the general's knowledge, she and [[navi|Nathaniel]] moved the bombardment from Main Market Square to the entrance of the ice labyrinth to lure out [[paul|Paul]], and took the Eternal Hilt for the fight against him. For the city's defence, she proposed hanging up tinsel. Davy privately called her a “goat”, but the garlands she devised, electrified with spark, became part of the ambush. Later, a school textbook presented the battle as “the brilliant calculation of Agatha and Yanmei”.

## Appearance

Mira is short: the text explicitly states that, unlike Juliana the Great, both Foremother Yanmei and Minerva Stagfort were short. She displays the features of the Yanmei lineage: a broad face, a stubborn chin, slightly slanting eyes, and dimples in her cheeks when she smiles. Her eyes are smoky grey, and her hair is naturally a little wavy; after the monastery, it is dark chestnut and cut short like a boy's. In Waymar, Mira herself considered her shoulders awkward and too broad for a young woman, her hips inclined to plumpness, and her waist far from “wasp-like”. Seeing Gloria, who was using her name, from the outside made her realise she was no beauty. Yet her features showed the proud breeding of a descendant of dozens of generations of aristocrats. She speaks with a northern accent, which people tried to train out of her before her coronation.

## Character

Mira is bookish and accustomed to keeping to herself. She has a sharp, cold, often caustic mind. She passionately loves coffee and sweets, and in difficult times drowns her loneliness in wine and ordzh. Her greatest strength is rapid deduction and the ability to play people like pieces on a stratems board. Her favourite tactic is bluff: it secured what she wanted from Sybil in the tower, Erwin in the besieged palace, and Moran in the desert. Mark called her “a born intriguer”, Manson “a clever girl”. Those around her considered softness her weakness: she finds it hard to punish and forgives too readily. In moments of failure, she mocks herself cruelly.

## Relationships

### Clive Stagfort
Her father was the most important person in Mira's life, her teacher, and the only person with whom she corresponded before arriving in the capital. She recalls his words in her most difficult moments, and avenging his murder was her investigation's first objective.

### Adrian
[[adrian|Adrian]] is her first love and her model of a ruler. He insulted her at an audience, then gave her the first dance and lost to her at stratems. On learning that he was alive, Mira “abdicated in his favour”, then realised that he had sent her no word for months, returned with Magda Labelin, and demanded the throne. She became disillusioned with him and turned into his rival, but never allowed herself to kill him.

### Erwin Origin
[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] is first an enemy, then an ally and Lord Chancellor. During her childhood, he beat her father at stratems three times and did not deign to speak to her; in 1774, he raised a rebellion under her banner. She both fears him and is drawn to him, and reconciled with him in First Winter. In “Fantasies”, during the summer of 1776, they are secret lovers, and Erwin defends her before the Church. In autumn, they separate, compete in the election for sovereign, and ultimately divide power: she becomes empress and he military ruler.

### Sybil Northwood
[[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] was a “second mother” to her and became the poisoner Mira hated. In March 1775, at Erwin's request, Mira signed Sybil's pardon. In summer, she realised that Adrian had already broken the countess and let her go without saying goodbye.

### Rebecca Litland
[[rebekka-litlend|Becca]] is Mira's first female friend. She saved her in the Huntsman's Forest and deciphered her farewell note. Mira sent her to the Steppe to negotiate with Moran, and Becca's death became a source of profound guilt.

### Ethan and Shatterhand
The secretary [[itan|Ethan]] and the Azure Guardsman [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] together rescued her from Martin's dungeon. Ethan was her first friend at court. Mira felt personally responsible for his mutilation. Shatterhand became captain of her guard and one of her most faithful men.

### Leila Talmir
[[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]], the dead Corvis's fiancée, became her first lady-in-waiting and adviser. She restrained Mira's excessive drinking and repeatedly challenged her decisions.

### Mark
[[mark-voron|Mark]], the Raven of the Crown, initially suspected her, then became an ally and adviser. He urged her to forgive less readily and eliminate Adrian. Before he resigned, Mira gave him her diamond hairpin as a keepsake.

### Iona Origin
[[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] released her from Waymar and became Mira's “best enemy”. Their paths later crossed in the dispute over Martin and during Navi's treatment. In “Fantasies”, they reconcile in Sophia Origin's presence, and Mira invites Iona to become Minister of Health.

### Navi
[[navi|Navi]] is her friend and teacher in the use of Objects. He saved her ship at Patrick's Chapel and First Winter in battle. She had to decide his fate in the ending. In “Fantasies”, all indications suggest that she chose to kill him: she has worn mourning for him since the “winter events”, and during the fortune-telling in the summer of 1776, his ghost asks her to “name a number” and promises to forgive her.

### Forty-Two, Harmon, and Inzhi Price
The kair [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] (Henry Horton) lost his legs in the trap she arranged at Arden, yet thanked her for the healer Nathaniel, while Mira felt guilty towards him. He also brought her the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] with the balloon design that helped defeat Paul, and the two men headed a new department in First Winter. The killer [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]] took her from the monastery into captivity; Mira later pardoned him, thanked him for her firstblood, and took him into her service.

## Interesting Facts
- By Mira's own reckoning, she had been drunk only twice before her coronation: at the summer ball and in Waymar with Count Vittor's soldiers.
- Erwin called her “Mimi” and “Lady Mimi”; his notebook caricatures depicted her, for instance, riding a dog.
- Before the battle, while experimenting with the Glove, Mira tried to communicate with an axe, speaking to it as though it were alive.
- The first novel says that Mira turned seventeen in the winter of 1774, while “Fantasies” celebrates her twentieth birthday in August 1776. Lady [[karen|Karen]], in the [[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|collector's edition of “Arrow, Coin, Spark”]], acidly clarifies that Minerva was born on 24 August, and that “winter” in the first novel can be justified only as a metaphor for sadness.
- Mira initially judged her own “value added” as a ruler to be negative. Her teachers listed her advantages as her beautiful name, reminiscent of Miriam's, her youth, the springtime beginning of her reign, and her connection to the North.

## The Author's Word
- According to the author, the accusation that Minerva is a “Mary Sue” for whom everything comes easily is unfair. Initially, she is “nobody”, her power is illusory, and others constantly control her. She could turn that apparent power into real power only through her own efforts, and the entire third book is devoted to this (spring 2026 stream).
- According to the author, readers' admiration for [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]'s tricks and condemnation of the same tricks when Minerva uses them reveals “internalised sexism” (spring 2026 stream).
- According to the author, Minerva, alongside Erwin, is the character into whom he put the most of his own traits (stream of 18 April 2021).
- According to the author, the bloodless suppression of the [[vosstanie-podsnezhnikov|Snowdrop Rebellion]] is an unequivocal good (stream of 18 April 2021).
- According to the author, he enjoyed writing the scene in which Mira is tortured in the cellar of Shayland Castle: until then, things had come more easily to her than they should have, and he wanted to test her against harsh reality (stream of 18 April 2021).
- According to the author, Minerva did indeed acquire [[pervokrov|firstblood]] in [[martin-shejland|Martin]]'s cellar: either from torture instruments bearing [[paul|Paul]]'s dried blood, or from a drink in a cup. She does not know exactly which did it (author's letter, 2026).
