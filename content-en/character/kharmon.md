## Who He Is

Harmon Paula Roger is a merchant who has been driving around the east of the Empire for almost twenty years. He carried silk, velvet and dried fruit to Almera, glass and bronze to the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], silver and smoked sausages to the [[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]. The main income was brought to him by reclusive noblemen, whom he visited with goods chosen for their taste. His father taught him letters and a winding syllable, and the merchant kept in touch with the buyers by letters. On his favorite way he had four hotels where he was waiting for orders. One of them was standing in the cave. ([[izluchina|Riverbend]]) Many of his customers were regular: for example, the family of the Izluchin burgomaster took tea from him for seven years. He was in the merchant guild of the Southern Way, but her silver bracelet was rarely worn: travel merchants in other lands were not loved for their stinginess. According to Snipe, the trade brought Harmon about thirty ephes per year, and Louise then called forty.

In the spring of 1774, he had nine horses, two vans, an open cart, and six men: an old guard Doxet, a deserter Snape, a farmer Whirlwind with his wife Louise, who had been driving with the master for six years, and their children, Whirlwind and Sarah. ([[doksett|Doxett]]; [[snajp|Snipe]]; [[vikhor|Whirlwind]]; [[luiza|Louisa]]) He deliberately selected people "in his own way dexterous, but in something corrupt": such were cheaper and served unquestioningly. He had no wife. There were three women, one in Almera, the Southern Way and the Crown. The best of them he considered Mary, a governess from the baronial castle near Smallden. Once in the moon, he went to the temple and, if the month turned out to be profitable, donated agatka, “just in case.” His chapters in the novels are called "Coin".

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

In the autumn of 1774, Harmon, under the name of "Glorious Horam", moved from Laem to [[melison|Melison]], a wine-growing town in the mountain valley behind the Little Pass, and bought the old estate of Baron Monat Erlin there. He moved in several steps: Rico was carrying from Laem a huge wicker basket, books, clogged boxes, a learned old man and even an acid cart. Burgomaster Cornelius lost sleep from these oddities and ordered the constable to search the train, but after making inquiries, the townspeople decided that Horam was a good man from the north who had lost his wife, and reconciled with him at lunch. Harmon bought a [[vozdushnyj-shar|balloon]] from Hortense, and he began to build a ship on hydrogen. ([[gortenzij|Hortensius]]) The sphere Harmon hid in the niche of the wine cellar, wrapped in Jack's camsol, and at night he unwound it by candlelight. ## Personality and Appearance

Harmon was short, "no taller than the shoulder" of the desired guard, with a rounded abdomen and beard. At the age of 45, he had no home, no wife, and no children. He was proud that he always “acts according to the meaning”, and despised those who act “at the behest of the heart”: such, according to him, “between the ears semolina porridge instead of brains”. He loved the crowd, read people easily, spoke to everyone in his language and did not argue with anyone: “Disputes are empty expenses, and friendship is a direct benefit.” In a difficult moment, he consoled himself with the saying: “Better to laugh with grief than cry with grief.” At the same time, he recognized greed and cowardice: under torture he gave everything, and in battle he preferred to stay away. The sphere became an obsession for him, for which he killed and betrayed. After Polly's death and Rico's death, he was tormented by guilt and seriously tried to live honestly. The bottom in the steppe parable called him the miracle calf Ollay, who does not know how to use his gifts, the greedy Gett. ## Relationships

### Polly
Harmon fell in love with Polly, was jealous of Joaquin and was going to marry her, but he never dared to explain himself. He killed her with a random shot and could not forgive himself until the end of the cycle. In a dream, he was chased by a bolt in the girl's chest, the trains reminded him of her, and the hilt spent on her perfume seemed to him more valuable than all the later wealth.

### Joaquin
Security guard Harmon both despised and respected. He laughed at his dreams of a castle and a lady from a newspaper portrait, but acknowledged his skill. He once told Joaquin to his face that he was blinded by pride, and Polly later called it a friend's act. After Polly's death, Joaquin vowed to kill Harmon, and the merchant, counting the dead, added "possibly a fifth" to them - Joaquin.
