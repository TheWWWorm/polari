## Who He Is

Philip Doris Laura, Baron Lowfert, accompanied [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] on an expedition for the River in the spring of 1774 as an imperial observer. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) According to the law of Constantine, published in the XIII century, in each campaign beyond the known borders of the Empire was supposed to include a person directly serving the Crown. Philip was “well past forty”. He wore a goat's beard, flaunted clothes with gilded veneers "everywhere you can put them" and a scarlet "cocktail" camsol and considered himself a genius of eloquence. He spoke with a continuous stream of banalities with “such a sweet smile.” Erwin never met him at the capital's balls, and the baron did not look like a person from high society.

Most of all, Erwin was annoyed that the baron persistently called the heir to the duchy, Origin, “a young man.” Erwin himself defined it as "a lustful word with a goat's beard".

## In the campaign

He drove to the building behind Erwin and tirelessly ranted about women ("a woman is a pearl") and shrines, calling Erwin rocks and caves. At the fire, he treated everyone with tincture, “according to him, the Litland”, told tales of gentle girls and love in the snow and advised the mechanic Luis to grab the girls “stronger”. ([[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]]) He praised Adrian as ruler, which gods are sent once a century. As Erwin was riding in the tail of the unit, the Baron remarked to one of the keirs: “The tender youth is now gone. Women raise them, and dandelions grow.” On the night of the descent of the avalanche on the Bend of the Servant, the baron stuttered with horror and muttered female names, and in the morning announced that it was the gods who “took aside the weapon aimed at by death.” Kyr [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] countered that his warrior Hank Seaman had won the extra hundred steps of the squad.

In the border village of [[spot|Spot]], villagers took the Baron in his scarlet camzole for the Duke's son and fell to his knees in front of him. Philip accepted the honors with great importance until Elder Howard wished health to his “beautiful sister.” The Baron replied that he had no sister, and frightened the old man. Erwin, who was having fun with this scene, finally named himself. At a feast in Spot, one of the local girls was already sitting at the baron's knee. Later, Philip lagged behind the camp and, according to Erwin, remained in Spot "with some maidens."

In the Soft Fields, where he had to walk, he, like Erwin, all the old way in the saddle, barely kept up with the heat and grunted under his nose. When a poisonous snake was found in Erwin's tent and it was discovered that it had been caught by [[kid|Kid]]'s guide, the Baron confirmed: "I saw him catch it. Put the net on, and it’s done.”

### U Reka

Upon reaching [[reka|the River]], the expedition was convinced that it was unsuitable for the dam. Philip reasoned, "We had a task to reach the River, and here it is, before us... we can return." Erwin later recalled this moment. The decisive drop was his dislike of the baron: to agree with Philip was too pretentious, and Erwin ordered to build rafts and cross.

A dead body was found behind the river. Philip threw up, and then he attacked Erwin: is he going to look for what killed this man? Erwin touched the hilt and warned: if the baron once again calls him a young man, then "he can neither walk nor sit, nor lie without pain." Philip threatened to inform the Crown, but looking around among the thirty warriors loyal to the Origins, he calmed down and disappeared “from the eye.”

### Gift's resting place

When the expedition reached Dara's lodge, Philip was delighted. By law, a third of the Items of any Gift belonged to the Crown, and his presence for the first time made sense: he dreamed of returning to Faunterra with an unprecedented gift for the lord and become famous for centuries. The mechanic, who doubted the value of the Objects, was instructed by the Baron to explain that “the sacred property is an indicator of the greatness of the genus” and that the Great are called only thirteen houses that own more than twenty Objects. The lodge was devastated. When Erwin made a speech about the fact that the savages themselves extracted the Gift and it remains only to take it away, Philip sat down to drink it and again ran down on the "molo... my lord". Then he dreamed of luxury, maidens and honors waiting for him in the capital.

## Death

The expedition camp was cut at night (the attack apparently came from the forest). Waking up on 9 June 1774 in Dara's lodge and returning to the camp, Erwin, bypassing the chopped tents, found "imperial envoy Philip Lowfert" in the fourth of them. He could only be recognized by a goat's beard: the upper half of his face was blown off by a sword.
