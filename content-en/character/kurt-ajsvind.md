**Kurt Icewind** is a northern baron descended from Agatha the Radiant and an officer in [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]'s army during the campaigns of 1775.

## Who He Is
Icewind is the nephew of Count Main the Hammer, who died at [[bitva-pri-piksi|Pixie]]. He himself was a “hero of the Battle of Pixie”, an excellent warrior, and an Agathan. Unlike the other company commanders, he had not taken part in the defence of the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] and received his X-marked cloak later, in the spring of 1775. At first, therefore, he had to hold himself back. His fourth company wore light armour and rode fast horses, making it suitable for raids, flanking manoeuvres, and reconnaissance in force. According to the narrator, the baron had enough “ice” in his veins to tell commanders unwelcome things in the right tone.

## In “Humans and Gods”
**The Twin.** During the battle against [[veksler|Wexler]]'s corps at the dam, the kair Arthur the Twin, a member of Icewind's company, broke ranks to help the duke. The baron took the blame and demanded that the Twin be sent back to the North in disgrace. Erwin forbade any punishment and later made the Twin his bodyguard.

**The Council.** At the council of war, the baron asked whether the battalions of the Brotherhood of Wilhelm might join the northerners now that [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] had been stripped of his ecclesiastical rank. When Erwin decided to head for [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]], [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] advised him to take “Icewind's company—the fastest”. The duke rode ahead with it. Later, the baron's men worked alongside Arrowback's to stop the forest fire from crossing a stream.

**Fliss.** Near [[fliss|Fliss]], Icewind rebuked [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]] for departing from the duke's plan: “He can afford to depart from the plan. You cannot.” Twenty-eight kairs, he reminded him, had given their lives for that plan. The baron proposed repeating “the duke's stunt at Doyle”: rounding up captives from the outskirts and killing them until the city opened its gates. [[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]] strongly objected. During the fight for the small town of Cliff, he broke his father's sword and wandered around the square picking up blades from the dead. Erwin later acknowledged that Gordon had followed his orders exactly: “The baron simply did not know that.” Icewind's company suffered almost no losses. It was his scout who brought the news: “The Farways are coming.”

## In “The Shadow of the Great Tree”
**Rei-Roy.** In the Steppe, during the battle near Rei-Roy, the companies of Icewind and [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] attacked the Shavans and the Finger-bearer, rescuing the duke from enemy fire. After the battle, Erwin wanted to give Jemis command of a company. Icewind then asked, “Where was the kair Jemis while you were under fire?” Gordon Sue had lost an arm and Quentin his head, while Jemis “went off into the darkness and simply brought back a horse”. Jemis reached for his sword hilt, the baron challenged him to a duel, and [[shram|Scar]] remarked that the baron had spoken “the plain truth”. Erwin forbade duels on enemy soil. Later, when Jemis spoke of marching to his father's aid, the baron again put his hand on his hilt, and Erwin threatened to strip anyone who mentioned a duel of his cloak.

**The Oath Concerning the Weapon.** When Erwin sought someone to fire the [[orudie|Weapon]]—a Finger of Wilhelm in the hands of a captive Shavan—Icewind refused on behalf of all the officers. Firing a Finger at people was “a grievous sin”, making the holy army no better than heretics. He persuaded the duke to make his oath more specific: the Weapon would be used only against units armed with Fingers, and after the war the Shavan would be freed and the Finger given to the Church. “Baron, you are entirely right,” Erwin admitted.

**Death.** On the River Roydan, Ganta Ondey's horde broke into the northerners' fortified camp. Erwin ordered, “Baron Icewind, hold them back at any cost!” The baron's X-cloaks held the rampart under arrows and fire from a Finger while the Leed Wolves carried the wounded across the river. Only a handful of the company remained, “bristling with arrows like hedgehogs”. The baron's body was identified only by a scrap of cloak bearing a captain's rank stripes and an armguard with House Icewind's coat of arms. Erwin addressed Agatha: “Your grandson, Baron Kurt Icewind, gave his life to hold back the enemy. His heroic deed saved our entire army.”

**Remembrance.** The army remembered that the baron had twice wanted to challenge Jemis to a duel, and many grumbled that Lilliday had “taken someone else's place”. Assuring Erwin that he had no one left to quarrel with, Jemis said, “Icewind is already on the Star.” As he rode past the Rusty Giants, Erwin reflected that all that remained of Alanis, Kurt Icewind, and Ganta [[ganta-groza|Thunderstorm]] were “memorials remembered by no one alive”. On regaining consciousness in First Winter after the ambush, the half-asleep Erwin thought he had reached Agatha's palace, where Iona, Nexia, and Gordon Sue had gathered, with Damon, Icewind, and the Steel brothers somewhere nearby. Later, Jemis confessed to Erwin that he had deliberately been insolent when the baron accused him of cowardice, and that the duke's intervention on his behalf had only made his fault worse.

## In “Fantasies”
In the fantasy “Ad-ri-an!”, as Erwin recalls his victories, he also remembers Baron Icewind alongside Arthur the Twin, Ganta Thunderstorm, and the widow Cher.

## Interesting Facts
