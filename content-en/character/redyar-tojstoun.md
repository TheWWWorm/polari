**Redyar Toystone** is a former monk of the Order of Forefather Wilhelm, a major in Almera's army in the 1775 war. ([[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]]) Several chapters of "[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]" are shown through his eyes.

## Who He Is
Toystone is an aristocrat from the family of Pramatery Yumin. To serve, however, he did not go to the Foremothers, but to the Forefather Wilhelm: he "loved answers more than questions." The belief that the will of Wilhelm is holy, and the destiny of mortals is service, he absorbed yet a novice. During a dozen years of monasticism, he learned that his fate was in the hands of the Forefather, and therefore almost never worried. Instead of a rosary, he twists a throwing knife in his fingers. He has a powerful bass, “that would serve mass.”

In each feudal battalion, the [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] Priory had its own people. In the first Black Sea regiment there were two: the former monk Toystone, the commander of the intelligence, and the captain of the mercenary company Oliver Gold. They called each other "Brother Redyar" and "Brother Oliver".

## In "Humans and Gods"
**The first mention.** In June 1775, a captured Almeran scout named [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] his company commander: "Lieutenant Redyar Toystone". At a military council, defector Sir Michael warned that the Black Sea regiment had a recon force under the command of "Ander Toystone", a monk who would "kill anyone" when the commander was betrayed.

**The pursuit of Origin.** On 11 June 1775, General [[veksler|Wexler]]'s Almer Corps followed the Beck-Fliss road behind Erwin's army. The regimental commander, Colonel Daniel Blackmore, nicknamed Big, shouted at the major, demanding to find the missing company of the Leed Wolves. Toystone suspected that Origin had bribed Blackmore, but decided it was "early" to kill him. He even thought of holding down the intelligence report so the general wouldn't fight until Fliss. Gold called this the “Agatha dilemma.”

**The Beaver Forest.** On June 12, the ten-man Abe Tours brought the Duke's captive bodyguard and reported that Erwin had gone ahead with one company. Toystone reported this to the commanders, and Wexler decided to attack the northerners in the Beaver Forest at night. The night attack was a disaster. The Cairos in white coats, posing as Wexler's knights, killed the general and then slaughtered Blackmore and the staff officers. Toystone managed to cross the sword with one of them and wounded him, but was stunned by the impact of the shield. Gold told him to take command, and the forest was on fire.

**Brother Abel's orders.** The fire cut half off the regiment. With the remains, Toystone camped behind the lake. The archpriest's envoys, Cornet Vickers and Brother Abel of the Order of Forefather Ferdinand, listened to his report and ordered him to return to the fortress of Voronovo Pero. The major countered that deciding who was capable of fighting was not a monk. But he did, and commanded the horses. Then he built officers and gave them a sweep: the regiment lost half of the fighters, “without killing a dozen northerners,” and did not deserve to rest.

**Break.** After two days of march in the June heat, Toystone jumped to Gallard's headquarters near the town of Oblivion under Fliss. He left the regiment on Gold. The priest asked why he was here. The major replied that Abel's brother was not his commander, and the last order of the archpriest was to overtake the Duke of Origin. Gallard said that Origin was not here, that he had sailed to Waymar and that the regiment was to block the road to Aleridan at Crow's Cross. "I'm sorry, Your Eminence," said the major. He saw the attack of the knights and monks on the cliff and was the first to count the returnees: "Less than two hundred." Gallard did not allow him to return to the regiment: "This is not your regiment." At the end of the battle, Toystone saw Abe Tours galloping to the headquarters, shouting, "Origin is coming!"

**Bottom line.** Erwin later admitted that his plan was thwarted by the appearance of an "unfinished Black Sea regiment", although according to calculations he was supposed to go to Voronovo Pero. The regiment detained the northerners, and Gallard managed to hide behind the walls of Fliss.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
Oliver Gold, who had already served Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], told [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] about his friend: Major Toystone "obsessed with Gallard's parish" and went not where he was sent. As a result, he saved the parish, but was deposed and "honored before the system." Gold added that during the summer campaign, he and Toystone chased after the Northerners and, "to the great misfortune", caught up.
