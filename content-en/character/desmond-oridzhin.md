## Biography

Desmond Gerda Lenor of the Agatha family is the great lord of the North, head of the [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]] and host of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. His wife, [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]], married him at seventeen. They had a son, [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]], a son, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], and a daughter, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]. Erwin considered his father a man “made of iron.” Desmond did not like intrigue and demanded unconditional submission from the children.

Desmond was considered one of the best generals of his time and wrote the treatise Questions of a Commander. In 1774, Erwin referred his generals to his chapter on fortresses and castles. The Snow Count Lilliday replied that everyone remembers Lord Desmond's "outstanding work", but on the battlefield sometimes it is necessary to deviate from the general rule. It was said that before Desmond gained experience, the Snow Count himself carried the title of the best commander of the North.

### The Secret of the Seventeenth Gift (1756)

In August 1756, Desmond and twelve-year-old Richard were at [[uejmar|Waymar]], where, after the war with the sunsets, his cayres were guarded by the bed of the Seventeenth Gift. Together with Emperor Thelurian, Prince [[adrian|Adrian]] and [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]] Desmond saw in the bed the body of a dead girl, who was considered a goddess. They all swore to hide it. When Shayland's boys, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]] and [[martin-shejland|Martin]], looked into the mystery, Desmond and Aiden were told to kill them. Wittor, soaked in horror, begged for mercy and assured that he would never betray a secret, because fear is stronger than honor. Aiden loathed tossed "Badness" and carried a sword over Martin. Desmond stopped him, saying that he should consult with Agatha the Radiant, crossed his hands and rolled his eyes, and after three breaths retreated: "This is a frightened child. I won't." Aiden offered to do the job for him with a grin. Then the emperor made him a surety: if the Shaylands spoke, Desmond would have to destroy their clan. That same night, [[paul|Paul]], who had been burned, was taken out of his bed by Vittor in Waymar. Later, Vittor Shayland became the Puppet Master, the main enemy of the Origins.

### In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

In the spring of 1774, Erwin, returning from the capital, came to his father. From childhood, he remembered the "rules of communication with his father": the sixth forbade smiling even behind his door, the fourth - to justify himself, because the excuses are "for dogs and black people." The Duke stood at the loophole in a simple gray shirt without a gate, but in it he seemed to be chained in armor. A heavy gray head sat on a short neck, and the whole figure resembled a bull god worshiped by Western nomads. The meeting began with an unusual courtesy and the inevitable cup of the orge.

In 1774 Desmond allied himself with Ayden Almera and supported [[alanis-almera|Alanis]]' marriage to Emperor Adrian. The ransom for Iona paid by Vittor Shayland, the Duke spent on the tomb under the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant. He considered Erwin’s secret coalition to be a bargain of loyalty and almost treason. He made Erwin repeat the vassalage oath and reminded him of Julian's law of rebellion. ([[yulianov-zakon|Juliana's Law]]) As a punishment, Desmond sent his son to exploit the Crystal Mountains to look for a place for a spark dam. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) Later, Erwin realized that his father was testing him.

Soon after Desmond’s son left, he was struck by a [[kamennaya-khvor|stone disease]]: his skin petrified, he could barely whisper and read with a stand and a mirror.

### Abandonment (30 August 1774)
Erwin returned to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] on August 30 and found his father a "granite statue". Desmond's face was the color of a cobblestone pavement, the skin to the touch did not differ from brick. Near the bed there was a device like a well crane holding an open book, and a mirror was tilted above the bed so that the duke could look out the window. When Erwin called him “my lord,” his father corrected him: “Father.” He said his son had changed, and he admitted, “We were...waiting for you. Och... chen".

The Duchess explained that the house is in danger: the Earl [[fleming|Fleming]] and Baron White are gathering strength, and the cairns do not obey the woman. The disease did not damage Desmond's mind, and he realized that you can not delay. He ordered the castellan Arthur Hyrock to build a garrison for the oath to the new duke, called the Earls of Shayland and Lilliday and Baron [[stetkhem|Statham]] to witness, and in a whisper abdicated the title in favor of Erwin. However, when asked if he was ready to take power, Erwin replied, "No, my lord." The father should have known to whom he was handing over power. Erwin talked about the Lodge of the Gift beyond the River, the fort with sparkers and the fact that the exploit fighters served the emperor [[adrian|Adrian]].

After listening, Desmond asked to let him think, and the Duchess closed his eyes with a blindfold. A few minutes later, he said yes. Erwin knelt down and declared that Adrian had no place on the throne. Desmond recalled that history knows two cases when the Great Houses forced the lord to abdicate in favor of the heir, and took an oath from his son not to have the slightest doubt about the guilt of the emperor before taking action. Then, with a huge effort, tearing his head off the pillow, he repeated the question, received the answer "Yes, my lord" and called Erwin Duke and head of the Great House Origin.

### In "Only One Star" and Interludes

The paralyzed Desmond remained in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] as his son's advisor, and his creaking whisper was translated by Sophia. Before his illness, he managed to pay a deposit to the workshops for an underground tomb under the Agatha Cathedral, and Erwin learned with horror that his father “buried half the cost of the war in the ground.” On September 6, 1774, when news came of Evergard's death and Adrian's decree, according to Desmond's done with the House, Erwin came to his father for advice. ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) When asked how to overthrow the emperor within a month, he replied: "No way" - and one by one rejected all the paths proposed by his son: a march on Faunterra, a sea bypass through Windley, a hike through Northwood and Shayland, a search for allies and waiting in the First Winter. As Erwin left, his father whispered, “Don’t give up, Erwin.” On September 10, on the eve of the performance of the troops, Desmond and Sofia blessed their son: “We are proud of you ... We believe in you.”

At the same time, Desmond gave a secret order to the prisoner [[mark-voron|Mark]], the Crown Raven: he was to go with [[fleming|Fleming]]'s flotilla for the River and find out the truth about the fort and the Persians. In a letter written by Sophia's hand, Desmond asked [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] to take the Raven on board, and so that the kair would not doubt, Mark passed on to him the words Desmond had said to Jemis alone after the exploit. Changing Fleming was going to take the First Winter and throw at Adrian's feet the heads of Desmond and Sofia; Mark, convinced that the Rings did not belong to the emperor, hoped that only Desmond and Iona would be able to stop Erwin's rebellion.

In January 1775, Mark, with Jemis and the sailors, made it to the First Winter. His story was heard by Sophia, and Desmond, hidden by a curtained door, listened from the adjoining room. Sophia admitted: “My husband and I suspected such a thing” — Adrian is clean before the gods. Then she promised the mysterious owner of the Rings to return the found bracelet if he heals her husband, and at his direction took Desmond to [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. Fearing an ambush, the Origins let half a dozen sleds with coats of arms go ahead, and they themselves walked a day behind with a hundred keels and horse-drawn rides.

### "A Doll on the Throne"

In the capital Desmond was still considered incurable. When [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] came to him, it seemed to her that the gray fog of death filled the bedroom. The skin on his father's cheeks cracked and bled. Iona asked for advice: She doubted Erwin's policies. Desmond replied that since she considered herself a vassal of her brother, her duty was to "serve and believe, not evaluate and criticize", and her doubts were pride. As she left, Iona secretly poured [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]'s potion into his water.

Then came to the Duke sent by the Puppeteer Znahark with a healing Object. ([[znakharka|The Healer]]) Desmond stated that the real goal of the Znaharka is himself and Erwin, and the meeting with Sofia, which she sought, is a trick. He ordered her to be seized at the gate and questioned about the Puppeteer, explaining to her daughter that there was “no talk” about his healing. The noblewoman died, but the Duke's condition improved markedly. Desmond gave Erwin the family sword The [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]]. Erwin dedicated his father (as well as Robert) to plotting a trap for the Puppet Master on the Holy Field. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]])

### In "Yanmei Hunt" and "Humans and Gods"

In the spring of 1775 Desmond lived in Faunterra and moved in a wheelchair. He was always cold, curled up in blankets and told not to spare firewood. Previously, the disease caused him despair, now anger, and Erwin considered this a sign of recovery. Desmond refused to take a seat in the House: the high lords should not see "such a scarecrow instead of Desmond Origin." He offered his son his plan: to withdraw the battalions from Faunterra, to keep the lands in the Southern Way, to suppress the rebellion of Fleming, to marry [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], and to leave the capture of the Puppet Master to the Crown, because the hunt for state criminals is a “Yanmean affair”. Erwin bitterly replied that historians would then call the year of his blood "Yanmean hunting", and rejected the advice. To say good-bye, his father said dryly, "Beware, Erwin."

In mid-May, Desmond and Sophia suddenly moved from the palace to the Origin mansion. There, his father accused Erwin that the trial of [[menson|Manson]] was a distraction, and the invisibility of the Puppeteer was primarily beneficial to the Lord Chancellor himself, and showed a letter from the Prince of [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] sent to Desmond bypassing the suzerain: he proposed to create a sacred guard from monastic orders under the worldly supervision of Henry Farway. Desmond promised to show his son his answer before sending, but did not hide that he supports this idea. Parents did not believe that the Puppeteer was Gallard: Desmond called the Priory a zealous servant of the Church, unable to turn the Rings against the innocent. When Erwin told his mother to take the Volta to see Mireille Nan-Claire, Desmond said, "I forbid you!", but Erwin reminded him that he was now his father's suzerain. ([[folta|Folta]])

In the summer of 1775, ironically thanks to [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]'s potion, Desmond was so strong that he stood on his feet and put on armor. Erwin placed him at the head of the northern army against [[uejmar|Waymar]], hoping that the Kairs would not follow Richard against the former Duke; to do this, they assembled a slurred battalion of veterans who remembered Desmond as a triumphalist, under Colonel [[blekberri|Blackberry]], Richard's former mentor. In June, the "father's landing" on the water reached Waymar.

### The Siege of Waymar
Iona was a prisoner of her husband in the castle. Desmond offered the Shaylands a deal: a food cart for the daughter to be shown from the wall alive and unharmed every morning. So he protected her from torture and death, and the Shaylands considered him a fool, because he himself fed the besieged. Later, a scarlet flag rose over the positions of the Origins - a sign of refusal to negotiate: Desmond accepted only an unconditional surrender, and only with Iona. His men dug into the well of Waymar Castle and poisoned the water, and the besieged began to suffer from thirst; Richard, for all his hatred of his father, admired him: “The great House Origin cannot be blackmailed.”

During the night assault, Vittor hit his main Object, which [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] called the Sword of the Gods. The blow cut off the slopes of Castle Hill and killed thousands of stormtroopers, and Fleming's guineas landed in the river port to finish off the survivors. But Desmond saw it coming. The castle was stormed mainly by Waymar residents. Some, including women and children, were tied to rams and siege towers, others, dressed in gray raincoats, followed. Among them were only two hundred kairs, and the real army stood aside. Human shields were not used to intimidate, but to disguise. At the port, the White Seamen were met with twice their first winter’s cayres: the real purpose of the battle was to destroy the battalion of the traitor Fleming. About three thousand soldiers of Shayland, Fleming and the sunset General Horis were locked on two hills. ([[oris|General Oris]]) It was later estimated that only five hundred Northerners died in Waymar, and the city turned into ruins.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree

When Paul's corps approached Shayland on the lake, Desmond lifted the siege without a fight and skillfully retreated, and the Shavan ambush failed. Minerva he sent a bird: “The body of Paul arrived by the lake: about 8 thousand swords, the Wilhelm's Fingers. Under threat of encirclement, I'm retreating from Waymar. I'm going to Northwood to connect with Statham and Craig." According to King Shimmery [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], they exchanged bird-letters with Desmond, and the Duke withdrew the garrison from Soltown in exchange for the king's oath not to fight on Adrian's side. Retreating across Northwood County, Desmond set traps, raided and poisoned wells.

[[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]] was rescued by [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]] with twelve thousand soldiers. Desmond's army stood in a forest convenient for fighting the Persians, and he took up the fight, perhaps hoping to recapture the convoy with captive Iona. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) The battle was lost, Craig died. Desmond was able to withdraw five full battalions from it, but many Northwooders passed to the Chosen One, and Agatha's army was reduced by a quarter. No longer hoping for victory, the Duke took time: he took civilians to the mountains, took out supplies and hoped that winter would stop the enemy.

### Lead and retreat to the first winter ([[lid|Leed]])
In ancient Leed, Desmond was horrified to discover that all seventy thousand inhabitants remained in place: [[sajmon-lillidej|Simon Lilliday]] refused to flee Shayland. On the advice of General Statham, the Duke hired forty thousand citizens into the army with money captured in Shayland banks. When the generals silently offered to burn down the city so that the enemy would not get its supplies, Desmond twice said no. This meeting went down in the history of the North: it said one word, repeated twice. Then he retreated to the First Winter, only to fill the gorge of [[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]], and [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] surrendered the enemy the forts there without a fight.

In November 1775, Desmond brought the remains of troops and forty thousand unarmed leaders into the First Winter. He was met by [[minerva|Minerva]] who was there. The old duke, leaning on the hand of General Statham, bowed before her and reported that Erwin with the Xes was unknown where, and Iona was in captivity. He approved of the Empress's preparations, which deprived the steppe cavalry of mobility, and recognized that Erwin's plan - to divide the enemy's army and reduce the battle to three tasks as possible - was good, but after his own mistake at Lisius Dol, when he gave a premature battle, the northerners were twice inferior to Shayland and needed a miracle. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) He respectfully shook [[navi|Nathaniel]]’s hand, and when he ran away, he looked at him for a long time: “Poor young man.” [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] later passed on Erwin's designs to Desmond, and this allowed for concerted action: Blackberry's cairns knew when the Xes would exit the Belomorye Road. During the Battle of the First Winter on 2 December 1775, Desmond commanded the defenders of the castle and defeated Shayland's army. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) After the victory, he came to the wounded Erwin and unusually warmly congratulated him. He pointed out to his son a strategic error that stretched his strength, but admitted that the campaign to the west brought two Feathers and surprise, and admired the accuracy of Erwin's calculations, which foresaw both the day and the plan of the battle. He told his son not to participate in the sorties in person, said that he was happy with the return of Erwin and Iona, and stayed by the bed. According to him, the First Winter lost about a thousand soldiers and eight hundred townspeople, burned a third of the castle buildings, including the donjon, and the fresco “Agatha’s Choice” on the orders of Shayland was shot by the Ring.

Soon after his victory at night, Richard and Finger of Wilhelm entered Erwin’s room and began a cruel “game”: everyone had to point out the victim. Richard first killed Captain [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]], who pointed to Richard himself. Richard refused to call Desmond the father (“you denied your son!”), and he tried to convince him that he was bewitched by Ulyanina Dust. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Desmond volunteered next. Having stood up, he said that he was proud of his life and that his main victory was his son and daughter, “genuine grandchildren of Agatha”, and the first child became his shame:

> “I have not been able to instill in him either wisdom or dignity, and I am ready to answer for this mistake. I choose myself.”

Erwin was sure that Richard, who imitated his father in everything, would not shoot. But he replied, "Lord Desmond Gerda Lenore, your will is done" - and with the blow of the invisible "weave" Perst broke his skull. Erwin soon killed Richard. Sofia returned from swimming the next day and learned about the death of her husband from the sentry.

## In "Fantasies"
"Fantasies" the author calls possible options for the future. In Let's Catch Him (The First Winter, Summer 1776), Desmond remains a measure of northern severity. He once said, “The death of a queer is the most common thing. Seventeen days of mourning are enough with the head", and the kairs repeat his saying: "a war is fought with a blade for a third, and with a shovel for two thirds." When [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia]] lifted the mourning for the one hundred and seventieth day, the "spirit" of her husband appeared to her and, to her horror, with a sarcasm that Desmond did not know during his life, reproached her that she had forgotten him too quickly and was happy to go "for healthy"; Sophia again put on mourning and postponed dreams of marriage with the judge. Iona and Erwin told Robert about it, and he decided that Lord Desmond "at least look after" the unrepentant spirit of Damon, and was going to order a prayer service for the repose of both. At the end of the story, the Kairs announced that all the ghosts that were Iona, Shatterhand, Sophia and Minerva were changing the appearance of [[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]]; therefore, the "spirit of Desmond", by all indications, was her trick. The Cairos, comparing the ghost dukes, observed that the spesi in Odar Spesiv was "as in the three Lords Desmonds".

## Personality and Appearance

Desmond is an example of a harsh northern lord: a few-worded, proud, merciless. According to Erwin's memoirs, he burned cities, buried corpses with lime, tied women's bodies to siege towers, and executed graylings for the slightest mistakes. He was not a traitor, but he could not kill a child. Richard imitated his father in everything, even got a cat, because Desmond had a cat. But to Erwin he said that poison was a low weapon, and punished him for his cunning. Desmond made a declaration of love for his youngest children just before he died. Erwin, looking at his body, thought Desmond might have been a good father, but he himself "didn't know how to sympathize with him."

Desmond's skill was recognized by his enemies. Joaquin, after Waymar, was struck by his "idle calculability". In Shadow of the Great Tree, the narrator notes that, unlike his son, his father was a most experienced strategist and kept his army, “like the apple of his eye.” Vittor Shayland, on the contrary, called him an "unfortunate old man", weakened by illness, beaten at Lisya Dol and lost both sons. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]])
