## Overview
Alanis Adelia Abigail of the Agatha family is the eldest daughter and heiress of Aiden Almer, Duke of the richest of the [[velikie-doma|Great Houses]]. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) Platinum hair, dark eyebrows, brown eyes and sharpened "Agatov" cheekbones made her a recognized beauty of the yard. At court there was an expression "There is only one star" - so they talked about the emperor, but Alanis with a challenge attributed it to herself. Hence her nickname, and the name of the POV-chapters "Star", and the title of the second novel of the cycle.

> “There is only one star.”

From the age of four, she was raised by the future empress. The father arranged for her a semblance of the imperial court: at lunch he planted imaginary ministers and generals and demanded that the daughter tell everyone the right words and choose the right place for herself, and at dinner he checked whether she recognized the guests' coats of arms. At the age of seven, Alanis became the youngest lady of [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]] and lived for three years in the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]. Then, for four years, she helped her father manage Almera of [[aleridan|Aleridan]]: at the age of twelve, she could recite the budget of the duchy by heart. From the age of fourteen she studied in the boarding house of Elena-u-Lake in the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) There, she became close to fellow student [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]; according to a boarding school teacher, Alanis was particularly successful in managing cities, and Iona in diplomacy and the arts. For four years my friends lived in the same room. When the southern Princess Demeter gathered a third of the course around her, Iona and Alanis "had to unite to put her in her place."

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
### The Emperor's Bride
In the summer of 1774 Alanis was considered the main contender for the hand of Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]]. Her marriage was the price Aiden had assigned to the lord for supporting his reforms; this alliance was opposed by [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]'s coalition, and Erwin personally dissuaded the emperor from marrying. Later, Erwin explained: Alanis and her father would be able, if not to imprison the emperor, then to bar him, and their power would be equal to the power of Adrian.

At the ball on June 2, 1774, Alanis entered the hall with her father and master as if the Almers were members of the imperial family. She wore a defiantly simple short black dress without patterns, shoes made of silver threads and the only decoration is an openwork ruby bracelet. Becca Yuzhanka, her rival, grumbled that "The star ascended into the sky." ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]) Vladyka gave the first dance to Myra, posing as Gloria Northwood, and Alanis invited only the third, and she waited for a pause before giving him a hand. ([[minerva|Minerva]]) Later, surrounded by a retinue of young lords, Alanis made fun of Mira and Becca, but Mira disarmed her with a simple question of where she got the coffee.

### Exposing the Father
Mira suspected that the secret of Aiden Almer could destroy his daughter. On June 24, at the military parade that opened the summer games, she and Becca deliberately greeted Alanis as the future empress; Alanis took it at face value, and Mira, according to the reaction of others, checked who of the courtiers already knows the Duke's secret. Then Mira and [[mark-voron|Mark]] revealed that Aiden knew about the Shutov plot in advance, and killed the witness, Captain Corvis. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) Alanis's fiancée never did. Warned by his spies, the Duke fled the capital with his daughter, and they took refuge in the ancestral castle of [[evergard|Evergard]].

## In "Only One Star"
### Fall of Evergard
[[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s mercenaries were in charge in Aleridan, and the adult Alanis was a hindrance to his uncle on his way to power. Nevertheless, she openly rode with a dozen knights to the city cathedral and donated gold to Agatha the Radiant, as if declaring: “I am innocent, my city is right.” The locals greeted her with enthusiastic screams.

On the night of September 2, 1774, Evergard was destroyed by a detachment with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] (see Death of Evergard). ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) Alfred's father and brother were killed, Alanis received a terrible wound: her cheek burned through. She was officially declared burned alive. Guards Lieutenant Sir Humphrey carried her on a stretcher underground, which led to a hill with a burned mill. There, [[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]], long in love with the portrait of Alanis from The [[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]], joined the fugitives. The doctor at Flathill noted the unusual nature of the wound, and Joaquin decided that it was hurt by Perst, which means that she was the first of the mortals to survive his blow.

### Escape from Almera
The fugitives moved in roundabout ways under the guise of a couple of spouses: Alanis, always under a veil, portrayed the pregnant wife of Sir Humphrey. On the third day they reached the new castle of the Earl of Blackmore, who had served her father for twenty years. The Count recognized the Duchess and secretly sent messengers to the parish; his soldiers attacked the guests, and managed to escape only in the turmoil of the fire. Sir Humphrey, [[berk|Burke]], and Sophie's maid were left behind, and Alanis was left alone with Joaquin.

Wounded, in a fever, she hid her face and gave orders. Joaquin offered to go to Clermi, but she refused: their pursuers went there. She called Toystone's goal, whose mayor she considered honest, but later changed her mind, remembering that the city was underpaid handicraft, and decided to go to the barony of Bonegan. On the road, Alanis and Joaquin took two horses from the travelers, and for them she threw her ruby ring to the owner, the only jewel taken from Evergard. In an abandoned shed, in delirium, she prayed to Prammother Susan. Exhausted Joaquin confessed his love for her and showed her the page with her portrait; then Alanis burned her and opened her face, mutilated by the scar.

### Marek and Simeon Monastery
In mid-September, Joaquin took her unconscious for a day and a half to the monastery of Marek and Simeon in the north of Almera. The brother doctor [[brat-marius|Marius]] found a rotting burn and rot in the blood. Before he treated her, he asked if she had a reason to live, love or hate, and Alanis answered in the affirmative. The wound was cleared with larvae, and a few days later Marius announced that the blood had cleared and the fever had gone.

After recovering, Alanis coldly offered Joaquin three hundred ephemera and a letter to Count [[erroubek|Arrowback]], who had to pay. Joaquin demanded gratitude and respect. In response, Alanis gave a cruel speech: he saw her helpless, rotting and humiliated, which is worse than any touch, and she would rather agree to serve him on her knees. Joaquin left the convent that night.

### The Road to Origin
Alanis spent another week in the hospital arguing with Marius, who instead of arguments showed her a mirror. Upon learning that the abbot had summoned the priory, Marius advised her to disappear: he recognized her by the "Agate skulas". He got her an Emilian nun's robe. Under the name "Sister Alice", Alanis joined the pilgrims who went to the font in the Crystal Mountains. Among them was the priest [[otets-david|Father David]], the only one she had bothered to remember. The schooner went through the Smokey Dahl and then on foot along the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] for about three hundred miles without money and weapons and in October she came to the Origina camp near Doyle. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]])

### A prisoner and an ally
Erwin later said that he had seen four different Alanis in the days under Doyle. The first came on the day of the execution of the prisoners, soaked, with blue lips and a gap in the cheek through which the teeth could be seen, but asked in a secular tone about his military successes. Erwin entrusted her with the cares of [[dzhoj-khemmond|Kair Hammond]] and his wife Margaret and ordered her to be protected as a guest. The second Alanis, sleepy and dressed in black, demanded an alliance: Erwin had to raise her vassals and break up Gallard. Erwin knew that the prince had already been sworn in by three-quarters of the Almerian lords, and refused. The third Alanis treated him like a panther, and after being refused, accused him of cowardice. Then Erwin announced his plan to her: to keep her secret and blackmail Gallard with the threat of her “resurrection” so that he would not help the emperor.

Alanis boycotted him for several days, and then began to send notes with one number - "Four", "Five", "Six" - in fine handwriting, in contempt. The fourth Alanis came after the tournament in a simple cape and admitted her mistake. Erwin promised to return Almera to her after defeating Adrian, and she began a letter to her uncle saying "Despicable creature. Damn bloodthirsty jackal," which Erwin had to replace with a polite greeting.

After taking Labelin Alanis became his adviser and lover. She told Erwin how Adrian once asked his mother to give Alanis a different duty than combing Ingrid's hair: the girl liked it, and the future empress should do what she should. Alanis guessed that Erwin had given his team-mates spears a foot longer than usual, and offered herself as a strategist.

> "What am I not Agatha?!!"

Later, Joaquin, who fell into the army of the northerners, again became her personal guard. Alanis admitted that she insulted him twice, gave fifty ephemera on horse and equipment, and put on his hand a coat of arms with an Origin bat, regretting that she could not give him the tower and the sun of Almera.

### Siege of the Palace
In early December, Alanis participated in Erwin's daring throw on trains to [[faunterra|Faunterra]] and, with him, captured the Palace of Pen and Sword, where she occupied the chambers of the late Lady Ingrid. She led Erwin through the empty imperial chambers, showing room after room, and in the tea room reminded him that it was there that a year ago he advised Adrian not to take her as his wife. During the siege, she was in charge of the dovehouse and the infirmary and wrote letters on her behalf to the Great Houses. Adrian, he said, learned about her presence in the palace not only from his people, but also from these letters: her handwriting and the words "There is only one Star."

Secretly from Erwin, who called the plan dishonest and banned it, Alanis sent Joaquin a letter to Count Arrowback. To say goodbye, she gave him her bracelet, a ribbon of blue silk with a pearl. Upon learning of this, Erwin became furious, and from that day on, they rarely saw each other. Soon, the bridge over Beck collapsed under Adrian's train (see Crash on Beck). ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]])

## "A Doll on the Throne"
After the victory of the Northerners and the coronation of Minerva Alanis remained at Erwin - his mistress and ally. ([[koronatsiya-minervy|Minerva's Coronation]]) At the dog races, she lost to Minerva a few seconds and suddenly apologized to her for mocking at the summer ball. Iona, meeting her in the capital, noticed that Alanis had grown older and learned not to argue; when asked what the war cost her, Alanis answered in one word: “Illusion”. At a wine tasting of the guilds, she made a toast to her only achievement — to survive last year — and named the saviors: Sir Humphrey, Marius' brother, David's father, and Duke Erwin.

Together with Iona Alanis secretly met with Znaharka, an agent of the Puppeteer. ([[znakharka|The Healer]]) The healer felt her scar and said that a piece of her flesh died and was eaten by worms, but still promised to return beauty for a big price. The price she began to whisper, but she was frightened, and she ran away. Erwin later suspected that Alanis had heard the price. To capture Znaharka was invented by Alanis herself, and the failure of the plan deprived her of hope: Znaharka's hand without her mistress was useless. When the Origins took turns trying to make the Item work, Alanis suggested how she held the Znahark's fingers, and tried everything she could herself, - the Item responded to Erwin, but not to her. At the same time, Erwin for the first time admitted that Alanis could be an accomplice of the Puppeteer, but rejected this idea: “Alanis is a panther, not a snake.”
