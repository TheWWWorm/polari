## Overview
Jemis Lilliday is a cairn of the ancient northern Lilliday family, who ruled [[lid|Leed]], the son of Count Lilliday, nicknamed the Snow Count. Jemis is a famous duelist, one of the best swordsmen of the North, a rude and straight man who does not know how to keep his mouth shut. His constant companion is the Grey Shepherd Sagittarius. The mother of Jemis, whom he loved very much, was a devout admirer of Gloria; out of respect for her, he learned by heart the "Word of Mercy".

## Family and origin
According to Erwin, the Lilliday family has served the House Origin for eleven generations, nearly two centuries, and has been related to the Origines four times. Jemis's father, the Snow Count, was considered the best general in the North, "until the Duke of Desmond gained experience." The count himself said that he had five sons and Jemis was a favorite. (In early media reports, in Erwin's conversation with Henry Farway, the Earl of Lilliday is fully named "kair Robert Susan Elizabeth, Earl of Lilliday, ruler of the Snow Coast"; in a 2026 edition, Farway instead names the Earl of Fleming's son and heir.)

Jemis was not the heir to Leed. He himself explained this family tradition: the men of the Lilliday family always have several sons, the first son heads the city and the garrison, the second - the expeditionary army, and both are called counts. Leed and the surrounding lands are owned by his older brother, [[sajmon-lillidej|Simon Lilliday]], "the eldest son of the local Count" and "the heir of the Snow Count". The army, while he was alive, ruled by his father. After his death, Lead's army should have gone to Jemis, but, according to Jemis, the Duke "has objections" because he believes that the vassal "loves to argue too much." Therefore, in 1776, Jemis is called Count Lilliday, although under his leadership, as he joked, there was only one warrior - the Sagittarius dog. In the first novel, Erwin, declaring the sentence, called him "the hereditary Count Lilliday", and Jemis himself in anger called himself "the son of Count Lilliday" (in early editions - "the Baron"). Rod Lilliday, according to Jemis, is eight hundred years old.

([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) Erwin remembers him because Jemis took a dog camping. The inexperienced lord-the "gentle" he despised from the very beginning. He mocked the mechanic [[luis-mariya|Louis]]: he put a dead badger in him, poured thorns under the saddle of a donkey, and Gray Hank Seaman urinated on his records. In [[spot|Spot]] Jemis, he cut off the hand of a village boy who grabbed Hank by the gate. Erwin forced him to pay a cripple and ask for forgiveness from the "black man". When a poisonous snake was found in Erwin's tent, Jemis was among the suspects and took it as a deadly insult.

In the swamps of the Soft Fields, Jemis led a kair plot that wanted to remove Erwin from command by force and send him to the First Winter as a "prisoner" (see Jemis' Rebellion). ([[myatezh-dzhemisa|Jemis's Rebellion]]) Erwin went out to the conspirators, listed each of his merits and gender and stood before Jemis with his chest open. Jemis did not dare to kill the suzerain, threw the bandage and kneeled, asking only to take care of Sagittarius. He was stripped of his Kairov cape and expelled from the squad.

### Erwin's Rescue
The disgraced Jemis secretly followed the expedition, a day behind to get revenge: no one in his family had ever lost a cape before. The unit died at the Lodge of Dar. Jemis buried the fallen and went down to the Lodge, hoping to find Erwin's body there (he found only his sword at the edge). The Sagittarius then led him to the wounded Lord, and Jemis did not kill him. He treated Erwin's wound with snake-grass juice from the "father's doctor", stitched it with a medical needle and fed the patient. When Erwin recovered, Jemis honestly admitted that he was going to take revenge, but revenge is no longer going to:
> “I was unfair to you from the beginning. You are not what you seem.”
To understand Erwin, Jemis dissected his forearm and poured a few drops of snake-grass juice into the wound. He himself was the winner of all fights and was never seriously injured. Erwin responded by apologizing for his suspicion with the snake and thanking him for his "idiot sense of humor": due to the tricks of Jemis, Louis, the real killer, was followed all the way by the entire squad. At the fire they finally found a common language. Jemis talked about the girls he fell in love with and lectured Erwin on why the Imperial infantry wears scarlet shirts.

### The Pursuit and Return
On July 6, 1774, the two of them tracked down a wooden fort and saw Commander [[paul|Paul]] and soldiers in red shirts burning prisoners with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]. Jemis took the chase after himself, alerted the three enemies at the Lodge, wounded one, killed the other, and grabbed the Sagittarius in the third. But the wounded shot, and the sword of Jemis “broken like a reed”, and the hand flew out of the joint. On the way back, Jemis killed a boar with his sword and every morning taught Erwin to fencing on training swords from ash "to the fifth bruise." On 30 August 1774, on a slope above the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], Erwin re-incarnated him in a cairn and put a red and black cloak on his shoulders. Jemis had no sword of his own, and he saluted with a dagger. Over and above the traditional vows, Erwin took two promises from him: to exercise with him until he learns how to defeat the Southern Knights, and to always tell him the truth, "no matter how nasty it may be." Before Erwin's father and the vassals, Jemis confirmed the account of what he saw behind the River and added that in fact everything was "much worse": they entered "right into the lair of the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]".

## Personality and Appearance
Jemis is bearded, scarred (he had his ear cut with a stiletto on deck), rough, sarcastic and hot-tempered. He does not tolerate lies or flattery, and Erwin appreciates him precisely for being straightforward. His past as a rebel has long weighed on others: the Xes disliked him, and after the death of Baron Icewind, many grumbled that Lilliday had taken his place. However, from 1774, Jemis remained one of Erwin's most devoted men. Their friendship survived mutual grievances, Gwenda's death, and matchmaking to Nexia.

## Relationships
### Erwin Origin
From an opponent who called Erwin a "child", Jemis became his first assistant and friend. He kept the promise to always tell the truth literally and argued with the suzerain over anything. During the siege of the palace, Erwin, exhausted, thought: if Jemis were around, "he would be as gloomy as a coffin," and this would be reassuring.
### Sagittarius
In 1774, the dog lived with Jemis for six years. Kair loved him "no less than his horse." Sagittarius sensitively captured the mood of the owner and rocked when he was angry. It was the thought of the Sagittarius staying with Erwin that brought Jemis back from fleeing Rei-Roy.
### Father
The relationship with the Snow Count was humorous and grumpy. The father demanded for order to marry and continue the family, but did not force, and the son joked. The death of his father in the Glorious Watch was a “short hour of enlightenment” for Jemis: then he asked Erwin for forgiveness, but never dared to admit to fleeing.
