Gurlah is a Shavan from the detachment of the Ganta Biray, a lover of boasting and betting about the share of prey. ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]) After serving the Sorcerer, he, along with Birai, passes to [[adrian|Adrian]] and becomes one of his guardsmen.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
Gurlach first appeared in February 1775, in the chapter Satellites 4. In the evening, Nei sits down to the bonfire of the ganta, where Birai, Kosmatiy and Gurls sit, and they bargain about the share of the prey. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) Gourlah says, “You are one and a half, and we are seven and a half. Of the two of you, one woman, she needs less.” Later, having already agreed to concessions, he agrees to take a share “for two and a half”. He and Cosmat enthusiastically discuss how the battle would go in the inn: She shows a reception, and Gourlach shows a jump, which would reach the archer. Immediately, Gurlah tells two stories about himself: how he defeated the legendary Voronoi Gant (he hooked his horse's leg in the jump, then "Ganta flew out of the saddle and immediately weakened") and how in the battle of Melorange he burst into the tent and knocked down the knight with a spark spear, climbing into the camp of Vladyka Adrian.

In the chapter "Satellite - 5" Beginner (the future Hunter) repulses the attack of Cosmatogo, kicking him in the knee, and then hits the trailing Gurlach with a blade on the ear. The ear swells so that the helmet has to be carried in the hand. However, as Chara notes, Gurlach and Cosmatius were angry at Novichok for bruises, but respected him for his skill and politeness.

In Sputnik 6 (March 1775), Gourlach speaks about the newcomer ("He still speaks a lot of words. As if a boy or a woman") and, having learned about the danger in Laxette, offers: "We will wait for Charu in the morning and ride away. We’ll take another target.” In a raid on a bank in Laxette, he walks into a detachment of gaunt, Ney, Chary, Newcomer and Sorcerer: the latter enters the building, stabs the doorkeeper with a knife and drags the body inside. When retreating, the bolt kills his horse, Gurlah miraculously rolls over and is in front of the guards; She takes a sword at the blow intended for her. He then unlocks the gate with the gauntlet, and then by the sign of the Sorcerer knocks out the tower door, runs inside and runs out, shaking the blood from the blade. Return to the meeting place he has on the same horse with the ganta. In the final dispute about the Newcomer, he nods after Chara, Neymir and Haggott.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
In the chapter "Coin - 9" (May 20–28, 1775), [[kharmon|Harmon]], a prisoner of the Hunter, notes that Gurlach and Haggott are "literally ready for anything for the sake of the Hunter." In Moneta 10, Gourlach stays on the other side of the Boundless Disruption along with [[niza|Niza]] and the Zunds and watches over the captive monks.

## In "Humans and Gods"
After Adrian's resurrection, Birai becomes the commander of his new guard, and the first three guards call Haggott, Cosmatogo, and Gurlah ("Coin-1"). In Monet-3 (June 1775), Gourlach, after listening to Harmon, asks "more for order than for doubt": "Are you sure you're not lying?" - and Harmon responds by creating a spiral. Later, Gurlach and Cosmatius carry a watch at Adrian's tent and let Harmon in with the grog: Gurlach throws the canopy. When the Lord comes, Gurlach, along with Birai, jumps out of the tent. Cosmatius grabs Harmon by the neck, and Gurlah whispers: "Let's take aside, let's not disturb the master." Harmon is punched in the stomach, but without malice. He convinces the Shavans that the fiction of moving the capital to the Steppe is beneficial to Adrian himself, and leaves, and Gurlakh shouts after him: “Don’t lie to us, son of a jackal!”

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
In Moneta 1 (July 1775), Gurlach grins when he hears that Adrian is marrying [[magda-labelin|Magda]], and jokes about future princes. Vladyka sends him and Cosmatogo to Faunterra to guard [[minerva|Minerva]]'s gift, and in Monet 2, they guard the casket in the inn room near Labelin, where Harmon, finding them asleep, removes the key to the castle from Gurlach's neck.

In Witness 1 (August 1775), Adrian asks Gurlach and Cosmatogo to leave the meeting and go to Birai for a reward. Later, at the same reception, they, by order of the lord, grab Amber's secretary and fire him to dip in the pond. In Early Morning (2 December 1775), Gourlach places five coins on Shayland when Birai makes a bet. "After the Day Song" Gourlach with Cosmatim at Adrian's signal grabs [[karen|Karen]] of Arden and drags away into the prison of Main Castle. In the chapter "Kolpak" (December 3), on behalf of [[menson|Manson]], it is recalled how Gurlah spread his mouth and poured echiota, "as if into a dog's mouth."
