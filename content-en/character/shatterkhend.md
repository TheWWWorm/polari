## Biography

### Service to Minerva
Little is known about Shatterhand's past. During his life, he saw three emperors: Telurian, he considered the most formidable, Adrian - the most decisive. He was once Captain Whitmore's aide-de-camp. At the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], he himself said that he once wounded [[adrian|Adrian]] in the leg in a training match. The Lord did not punish him, but praised and rewarded him for his skill and honesty in battle.

### Saving Minerva in Waymar
On 26 November 1774, Lieutenant Chatterhand, together with Secretary [[itan|Ethan]], arrived at [[uejmar|Waymar]] by special order of the Emperor for [[minerva|Minerva]]. The guards broke into the cellar under the burned ruins, where Miru was dragged by [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]. They killed his gamekeepers, freed Mira and the tormented girl Jane, and captured Martin.
> "Lieutenant Shatterhand, Crown Guard azure. They came for you by a special order of His Majesty."

The squad — fifteen foot fighters — made their way to the dock when it was intercepted by two dozen horsemen of [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] led by Cyre [[sejmur-stil|Seymour Steel]]. To the demand to lay down his weapons, the lieutenant responded with boldness and seized the hilt. The bloodshed stopped Mira herself: she stepped forward and invited Iona to take her and Jane, letting the others go. Iona took Jane and prisoner Martin, and Mira, repaying the debt, gave her the right to choose. Mira chose to leave, and the Kairs missed the guards.

### The way to the capital
In early December 1774, the detachment sailed from Waymar: the ship went on oars down the Smoky Distance, under the first wet snow. On board, the lieutenant took care of the World in a military way. He told her to throw overboard a book of Martin's records (with reports of experiments) and threatened to carry it in a warm cubicle on his hands. He confidently predicted the death sentence of Countess Northwood. Before arriving in Fliss, Mira caught a cold and lay down. There, Shatterhand and Ethan solemnly announced to her that General Alexis had defeated the rebel (this was the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]]). On the way to [[aleridan|Aleridan]], the lieutenant entertained Mira with court fables, and one, about two drunken nuns in a tavern at Dostoy, Ethan did not let him tell. When Mira disappeared for the whole day to walk along Aleridan, the lieutenant severely reprimanded her.

In December 1774, after news of the capture of [[faunterra|Faunterra]] by the Northerners, the detachment hid in Aleridan. The guards kept Mira as a companion, not a prisoner, and allowed her to their meetings. The lieutenant told her not to go outside. He acknowledged her right about a beggar spy and with her changed inn, avoiding surveillance. Her impulse to urgently warn Adrian about Gallard he soberly cooled:
> “If you were a soldier, you would know that in war you rarely have to fight, but very often you have to wait.”

Soon they came to the people of the parish with the captain of the azure guard Hugh Grace. Shatterhand recognized an old comrade in him, and Grace turned out to be the vanguard of Adrian's army.

### News of the death of Adrian
On a date between the twentieth and twenty-ninth of December, it was Shatterhand who woke Mira with the words “Your Majesty”. He said that Adrian died, and [[menson|Manson]] can not inherit. He was the first to kneel in front of her. He accompanied her by train across Orwell to the panicked capital: the bridge over Back remained destroyed, and in two days the convoy reached the Crown Lands in a circular way. On December 24—28, they were already in Faunterra. There he questioned the refugees and watched with astonishment as the armed bandits of Backfield plundered the townspeople under the guise of "fortification". ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Before negotiating with the besieged at the palace, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] Mira ordered the lieutenant to leave his weapon and take the white flag. He led her to the gate over the Palace Bridge, where Mira let him go and went inside alone.

### "A Doll on the Throne"
In the first weeks of Mir's reign, ashamed of the role of the "talking doll", she almost did not want to see a handful of old friends - Ethan and Shatterhand with his azure knights. With her first decree on the appointments of Mir, she made him captains and gave him the only azure company left at the palace. Thus, he actually became the chief commander of the azure guard and was going to apply for the recruitment of the second company. He complained that the cairns filled the palace, but was proud that the azure received the highest honor - to protect the mistress.

Captain Harvey represented the Crown on the dog races, but, confident of victory, he missed the turn and came only third. He accompanied the mistress to the theater and pushed the drunken minister Liam Shelier away from her. ([[liam-shele|Liam Shellier]]) When Mira spent the night on financial tasks, [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]] gave her his greetings: according to the captain, the mistress is in vain, because her case is balls, and in extreme cases the war, in both he is ready to help. He helped interrogators interrogate surviving guardsmen poisoned during the transportation of Items: they agreed to talk only with him. At the exit to the cathedral, he calmed the World, which had sprung up in front of a roaring crowd:
> “Your Majesty, in battle it is more terrible.”

When the Shawnees attacked Litland, he advised to entrust the war to the Lord Chancellor, because the Origins from time immemorial - "the whip with which the Dynasty tames the West." In negotiations with [[moran|Moran]] under [[melorandzh|Melorange]], he threw a precision spark knife tossed the horse of the daring Shavan. Captain Whitmore called him his former aide-de-camp and said that to say Harvey's word was like saying it to the lady herself.

In Faunterra, Shatterhand, together with the duty scarlet officer of Alexis's staff, reported to Mira about a petitioner named Moger Buckley (the envoy of the Labelins), prevented him from approaching the mistress and examined the scarlet eyes he had shed. On the Holy Field, he rode with her in the same carriage between the buildings of the northerners, hurried the driver with a conditional knock, and at the sight of the peasant system he said in a low voice: "The only chance." ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) He was the first to get out of the carriage at the positions of the [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]] and gave the hand to the lady. Later, at a secret meeting in the execution room, Minerva thanked him and [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]] for their support and announced that she had no doubt about it. When Minerva told the others to go out to talk to General Smile in private, Shatterhand, along with Leila, stayed by her side, and she replied that their place was rightfully next to her.

### In The Voice of Winter
In the spring of 1775, after receiving a snowdrop from the Lady with the tape "You are not the Lord, my lord. Accept," Erwin Origin named Captain Shatterhand as "Minerwa's henchman" — along with Leila Talmir, Conto's banker and Minister of Paths. ([[konto|Konto]])

### The “Yanmei Hunt”
When [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], as a "scoundrel", expelled Minerva's advisers from the capital, he suddenly released Leyla Talmir and Shatterhand on the pretext that both were present at the master's memorable conversation with General Smile, and apologized with mockery to the "dangerous" azure officers. The captain immediately threatened that the azure guard would not forgive attacks on the officials of the mistress. Minerva later judged that both were dedicated to the secret of the Silver Fox and Erwin did not intentionally question them so that she herself would reveal the secret to him. One day Erwin was called to the master's bedroom, where the captain was holding the hair of a painfully drunk Minerva over his pelvis. As a means of sobering up, he offered black bread and tea with oak leaves. Erwin instructed him to wash off the lady with water and take her to Lady in Darkness.

At Manson's trial, he was summoned as a witness along with the lackey Wimas. The captain confirmed that he found a drunk prankster for an indecent act with a footman. The gossip at the court considered this proof: “the lapislator will not lie at the trial for anything.” At a subsequent meeting, when the defender Francis-Ilianus asked if anyone knew the warrior who had wounded Adrian in a training match, the captain rose up and admitted that it was him, and this became a precedent in favor of the defense. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) At the same meeting, he stood guard next to Minerva behind the screen, when the fishermen-witnesses identified the dagger and the Eternal Hilt, and picked up one of them, almost overturned the screen. On 25 May, Erwin jumped into the Lady's carriage on the way to the third meeting, asked the captain to transfer to the cab driver, and Minerva reluctantly let him go.

### “The ones we forgive.”
In the story, part of the chapters are written from the perspective of Shatterhand. The action takes place in Faunterra in May 1775. The captain was present at the recognition of [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] and was hardly restrained so as not to stab the "two-faced scoundrel". His belief in the mercy of Minerva, whom he admired more than Telurian and Adrian, kept him: he saw her in horror, in tears, in a hangover and in a hospital bed. When Leila Talmir brought a syringe with the blood of the dead Znaharka, the captain caught the syringe falling out and angrily shoved it back to the lady. Then he still dared to tell Minerva what she did not want to hear: the lady should be forgiven. Minerva disagreed.

Out of longing he was led by Lieutenant August Mays, "the most famous loser of the azure guard", with an oral request from Whitmore: the mistress should secretly come to [[marengo|Marengo]] for extremely important information. Hearing that Major Backfield would be in Marengo, the captain was heartbroken and conveyed Minerva's request. Three days later he accompanied Minerva to Marengo. In the Palace of Silence, he alerted Major Backfield at the dovehouse and demanded that the intercepted letters be returned. Having been refused, he deliberately insulted Backfield, forced him to fight in a narrow corridor and defeated him with a trick: the major's blade was stuck in the plaster. The captain nailed him with his sword and took the letters. These letters he handed to Minerva, saying that they relate to their conversation about forgiveness. Minerva called him her support. Backfield survived.

### In "Humans and Gods"
Events in Faunterra unfolded from late May to late June 1775. After the night visit of Lady-in-the-Dark and her companions to Minerva's bedroom, the lady fired the sedated sentries, and Shatterhand was promoted to lieutenants. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) She thought he should be glad he wasn't a private. He was the only fully loyal officer. When she told him to call Whitmore's capital with a company and find Backfield, the lieutenant tried to object to Backfield, but, turned away, only squeaked his teeth. He was ordered to secretly organize a series of meetings for Minerva (with Leila and Rebecca, with the banker Conto, with Labelin, with the Crow of the Crown), at her request he put surveillance on Conto (although his men are "guards, not sleuths") and entered into a narrow council with Leila Talmir and [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]. When Rebecca listed ways not to part with the crown, and Mira asked if he agreed with her, he chopped off: “No way!” He advised to wait until the army of Origin left the capital, and expand the azure guard. Later, hearing about the heretics in Hope, he reassured the lady: it is the concern of Origin and the Church, not hers. He accompanied Minerva to the Lithuanian bazaar and defended her right to care for Becca in front of Manson.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree
In July 1775, as the Chavans and the Rings moved towards the capital, it was Lieutenant Shatterhand who, at the military council, proposed the "evacuation" of the lady from Faunterra. He swore that he would die on the spot if a hair fell from her head, and assured that this was "not an escape, but an evacuation." The word spread throughout the capital in a couple of days, but Mira lifted the Glove over the Marengo ticket and announced that she was staying in Faunterra. When in the throne room she declared the claims of the landed in the Southern Way Adrian unfounded, Shatterhand, along with Whitmore, Amber and Crow applauded her.

In August, when Adrian drove troops on the rails, and the Silver Fox moved to his side, Minerva still left the capital: the azure guards “with all their brotherhood” surrounded her and jumped with her. Her train got up at Patrick's Chapel in Crownlands, where the missing [[navi|Navi]] was found. There, the captain with the guards carried the Queen's guard, pushing the onlookers away. The Minister of the court, reporting to Adrian about the escape of Minerva, along with azure companies, noticed that the captain was “very uneven breathing” to her. The court archivist mentioned a collection of poems about Minerva's "anonymous captain of the guard". Mira immediately assumed that it was Shatterhand, but the author was never named.

At the end of August, Adrian's regiments approached Patrick's Chapel. Minerva refused to shed blood and sent [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] with the treasury and cairns, leaving only a handful of guards with her; Shatterhand and Whitmore listened with stone faces. When Adrian jumped to his regiments after the negotiations, Shatterhand, seeing that the lady had only one company left, exclaimed that he “should have agreed.” He slept through the enemy’s night-time assault, and Mira called him a “sergeant” in anger. He then led the guards to the celestial ship and kept Minerva on a safety rope during the shelling. When the ship tipped, she made a remark to him by the hand on her thigh. He asked, "So I'm promoted again?" and got the answer, "As soon as you get your hand off." He's been the captain ever since.

In September 1775, a schooner, lifted into the air by Navi, flew over Smokey Dahlia to the north, to the Duchy of Origin. On the flight to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], he checked the insurance of the mistress three times in an hour, and when the schooner pumped up, he rushed to catch the empress, but was driven away. At sunset, the schooner descended on the lake at the foot of the castle of the First Winter. Since October, he remained at Minerva in the First Winter, where she re-established the work of the ministries. In the First Winter, he accompanied her to the mountains with Inji and Navi to work with the Glove: the captain guarded the empress and delighted her with enthusiastic views, and Inji made sure that he kept within the etiquette and at the feast advised her not to rush into marriage, following the example of Adrian with his favorites. Inge Price showed him his fist for it. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) When the second azure company returned to the First Winter, he and Whitmore exchanged "primitive greetings and jealous looks."

During the Battle of the First Winter, he and Mira, Navi and Inji participated in a boat raid to the prepared ice blocks over the Shayland headquarters and supported the lady on a slippery slope. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) In the midst of the battle of Peace, having given the azure guard to General Davey, she took refuge in the Agatov monastery at the grotto of Kosuli with only two fighters - Shatterhand and Inzhi. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]; [[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) When [[navi|Navi]] fell into the cave under the grotto, she demanded that they go for help, but both refused: the captain stated that he had no moral right to leave her alone. After the battle, Minerva sent him with ropes and a dozen men to rescue Navi; the captain tried to stay with her, and Whitmore and Inge jointly pushed him out on a mission. He returned only in the morning and reported that Navi fell not in the water, but on the shore of an underground lake, broke his spine and broke his head: he was considered dead, but when he got up, he suddenly coughed and breathed. While the soldiers were tinkering with the body, the captain swam on a boat across the lake and enthusiastically described its water — sticky as syrup and glowing green from the inside. Mira responded in anger that he was supposed to save Nathaniel, not consider the lakes.

### In "Fantasies"
In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, Captain Harvey Shatterhand still serves at Minerva in the First Winter. Northern officers thought to call him in cards to "undress the metropolitan to the thread." The ghost that frightened the inhabitants of the castle appeared to him when he carried a vacation report to the mistress: in her chair was a dead Minerva with her throat cut. The captain raised the alarm, fought with the sentries and felt the living Minerva for longer than the charter and decency allowed. Kastelian Khyrok threatened to expel him from the castle if he once again “drunk” to cheer up the garrison, and Dorian Amber praised: “Dare to the man.” ([[artur-khajrok|Arthur Highrock]]; [[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]]) According to one version, the ghost took the form of what the victim fears the most, and Shatterhand "shiver at the thought that Minerva will die." For the birthday of the mistress, he appeared empty-handed, apologized that the gift was late, and then famously drove into the refectory on a white mare - his gift - just before the birthday cake exploded; the frightened mare rushed him away, drenched in cream. Secret night dates with Erwin Mira arranged so that only old Whitmore knew about them: most of all she did not want Shatterhand to know. Whitmore, on the other hand, relayed to the owner complaints from the Shatterhand Company that their barracks were too close to the clinic. At the construction site, he was present when Minerva spoke with Bishop Amessin and shook his head in vain when she signed the list of candidates for the arch. Later, contrary to Erwin's sign, he intervened in Minerva's argument with Iona about the Hand of the Insignia and was reprimanded by the Lady.

#### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the fall of 1776, Shatterhand, along with Whitmore, commanded two azure companies guarding Minerva in Faunterra. Both doubted the loyalty of the Labelins, with whom the mistress settled in the Holy Citadel. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) On the first evening, he reported that the azure guard was in full combat readiness and would take control of the Citadel by one order. At the same time, he looked at Mira "with the look not of a soldier, but of a man" and wanted to ask if she was really with "bat". Mira, who had just quarreled with Erwin, answered him with deliberately frank details, and the captain left, "with each step as if becoming lower in stature." The next morning, she apologized: three people in a row reproached her with a connection to Erwin, and she “just became bitter.” Shatterhand replied, "You are the ideal of woman and empress" and accompanied her to the cathedral of Wilhelm. After Adrian's death, he again stood behind her as she walked into the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]. Left alone, Minerva thought she had pushed everyone away, and among them she named Shatterhand.

#### "The Death of the Assassin" and "Brilliant Calculation"
According to Mac, Minerva entrusts the captain with good news, honors and awards: he looks so brilliant that the addressee accepts the news as the highest good, while for threats she sends Inge Price. In General Davey's account of the days before the Battle of the First Winter, Shatterhand opens the carriage door and "brings out" the empress in Main Market Square.

## Nature and relationship
Outwardly, he is a tall, strong guard (“healthy in a blue cape”, according to Backfield) with brushed buttons, gold veins and a white-toothed smile. Shatterhand is brave, ironic in his speeches, militarily straightforward, and recklessly loyal to Minerva. He himself believed that the emperor should say only what he wants to hear, and offer the truth only by order. However, for the sake of Leila Talmir, he violated this rule. Minerva's subtle logic usually made him shy, but in the fray about the frailty, he felt a flaw in it. In his opinion, forgiveness is the only way to accept one’s own and others’ imperfections.

### Minerva
Minerva called him the most loyal of his defenders. For her, he was a man who saw her "both in horror and in tears", and behind this intimacy was a personal attachment. People around him noticed his enthusiastic views. In "Fantasies" Mira admits to himself that he is "faithful, reliable, brave, soulful", but still an ass, and that once she even thought of losing her virginity with him.

### Leila Talmir
With the maid of honor, he entered into the inner circle of the mistress. Both were initiated into the mystery of the Silver Fox, and with "Those We Forgive" they are commonly referred to together. It was he who stood up for Leyla when Minerva refused to forgive her.

### Whitmore and Backfield
Captain Whitmore was his former commander. When the second azure company returned to Minerva in the First Winter, the two captains exchanged "primitive greetings and jealous looks". Major Backfield Shatterhand despised Whitmore and Crow for cheating, and he responded with the same hostility.
