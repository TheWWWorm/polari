**Jeff Bamber** is a northern sailor, captain of the seal schooner on the Earl of [[fleming|Fleming]]'s expedition for the River and then the Sea Arrow schooner. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]])

## In "Only One Star"
**Captain of the Seal.** Sailors told [[mark-voron|Mark]] that Bamber was not born a "lord or a merchant", but a sailor's son and himself struck out as captains. He saved money to buy off the Earl of Fleming and become a free trader, and is said to have amassed a "whole barrel of silver", but the Earl was scarce. Mark realized that the captain was "not at all delighted" with the count: Bamber wanted to put the profit in his pocket, and not give half to the nobles. According to rumors among sailors, it was Bamber who told the navigator about the secret purpose of the campaign for the River, and then the rumor went on command. The captain himself called Crow to play dice on the stories, and over the evening, Mark furnished him with just one short story.

**Mutiny.** Fleming gathered for secret meetings of the captains, but Bamber, Jemis and Cyrene Moi did not call. He put his swords on the Seal. When the count defected to Adrian's side, a massacre began on the deck of the Seal between his men and the cyre warriors of [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]. Four of the Count's swords guarded the sailors, but one was distracted, and Bamber "did something stupid": heated him with a bagrom on the helmet. The captain with the boatman and sailors took the pursuers into the hold. There, in the dark and cramped, the sailors killed the Latvians, but lost the navigator and other comrades.

**The Crow's Questions.** At the secret council in the cabin, Mark asked about the question of Bivnu, [[luis-mariya|Louis]], [[gvenda|Gwenda]], the [[potomok|Descendant]] and the captain. He asked Bamber how much water to go from the River to Almera. The captain counted about two months, "maybe half." This time was enough to deliver the Rings from the fort beyond the River to Almera, but it was not enough to deliver an order to the Beyond before. So Mark argued that the burning of Evergard was not Aiden's business.

## "Speaking to God"
A [[potomok|descendant]] in the letter describes how the crew abandoned the ship and left Fleming's men on foot through the snow in northern [[oridzhin-zemlya|Origin]]. The main was Bamber - "a good man", who was very stuck, "when the ship was lost." At the bridge over the gorge, Jemis grabbed the captain by the breasts: sailors must pass over the bridge, but the chase is not. “Are we going to set the bridge on fire?” said Bamber, as if the spark was shining. He yelled at the team, as before on the "Tulene", and drove everyone south. The descendant writes that the captain for the team is the same as the head for the chicken. At the end of the campaign, Bamber was promised a schooner in the future Origin fleet on the East Sea.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
[[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], listening to the abbot, who asked for forgiveness for the rebellious Earl of Fleming, says that a good half of the northern fleet consists of ships of free captains "like Jeff Bamber." They pay Fleming a tax on every flight.

## In "Humans and Gods"
**"Sea Arrow".** In the spring of 1775, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] wanted to give Iona a black schooner, Sea Arrow, captured in Soltown. Iona recalled that the Origins owed the ship to the sailors from the Seal: of the entire fleet of Fleming, only this crew remained faithful to them. Erwin gave the schooner to Bamber's team. The ship and crew, according to the narrator, approached each other, “like a bridegroom.”

**Sailing to the Volta.** In the summer of 1775, the Kair, on behalf of the Duke, hired the Sea Arrow to take passengers to the Volta and bring them back. ([[folta|Folta]]) Bamber "had reason to trust the Origins" and agreed, but demanded money for a pilot: he knew the Foltia waters poorly. On board were Duchess [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia]], Kair Glenn with kairs, an unnamed old judge ([[ded|Grandfather]]) and his student. Bamber decided that the gray man was a "famous musician", and the sailors nicknamed him Skipper, because only Bamber could be the captain on the ship.

On Folt, the Voltians invited the northerners to hunt for the sea monster. Bamber laughed at their craft and warned the Duchess: the heavy Foltian ships of the Sea Arrow would not catch up, but the two could take her to the mites. When Sophia decided to go, he replied: “Your lordship’s business is to command, ours is to obey.” He advised to stay three hundred yards from the Allies. During the campaign, Bamber sat on the tank next to the Foltic captain Kolistad and, on Sofia's orders, led the schooner in pursuit of the "gurken" whale. ([[likejn-i-kolistad|Likein and Colistad]]) When the schooner became worse to listen to the steering wheel, the captain with a cue Glenn staged a team exercise in case of a fight. Kolistad ordered to pull the schooner to the shore of the island: its bottom was covered with a "kilecap", devouring a foltic tree. Colistad blamed himself for this, but Bamber replied to him, "You saved us."

## Trivia
- According to the narrator, the Sea Arrow changed six owners to Bamber’s team, and its new crew managed to visit the Beyond, survive Fleming’s rebellion and almost freeze in the snow.
