## Who He Is

Fitzgerald is a cairn from [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]'s select X Company, Deputy Captain [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]]. He's young and he's gambling. Lady [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]], painting the people of the campaign, believed that his young soul “need to love”, and for lack of a better subject, he turns this feeling into a care for weapons: only with a sword in his hand he looks real. The court knew him in person: in the “Shadow of the Great Tree” [[adrian|Adrian]] recognized him from a bird.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

**A mouthwash.** In July 1775, after an ambush at Rei-Roy's, one-armed Gordon Sue went down with "rotten blood". Erwin assembled officers to appoint a new company commander. FitzGerald almost begged if the captain had a chance. Scar punished him: "Fitzgerald, look, don't let Gordon Sue down!" Erwin was offered the company to Jemis, but Baron Icewind warned that the first company would not obey Lilliday, and Jemis declined the order. ([[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]; [[kurt-ajsvind|Kurt Icewind]]) When Erwin proposed to the companies of the captured pendant-"The Gun", Fitzgerald replied that he was too young for such an honor: Gordon Sue deserves it, and while he is ill, he will not dare. ([[orudie|The Weapon]])

**Battle of Roydan.** In the battle with the Shavans of the gant Ondei in Reis, Fitzgerald's cavalry was the first to hit the eastern detachment across the river. Erwin then ordered him to lift the ducal pennant and flee with the first company to carry the enemy behind him. Aubrey had to make sure the lieutenant left by "stabbing his ass hard" if he delayed. Fitzgerald sent the company under the pennant, and he returned with a broken nose to the Duke: “My place is near you.” After the victory, he reported: “I hope my lord is satisfied with the quality of our escape.” Erwin warned that for a new violation of the order, he would remove “both his cape and his head.” Fitzgerald roared cheerfully: “Happy to serve, my lord!”

**Hike north.** In the summer, Erwin was accompanied everywhere by Fitzgerald and [[obri|Aubrey]], fanatically devoted to him and confident of victory over any army. Erwin was furious: he had no one to share his worries with. In the evenings, the Duke played a peg with Scar and Fitzgerald. The lieutenant, according to the narrator, was given luck by Pramatery Vivian. He once beat both Erwin and Aubrey, but the next night Erwin took away both three months' salary. Fitzgerald cautiously asked the Duke if he would be angry if someone showed courtesy to Lady Nexia. Later, he and Scar and Aubrey watched her get closer to Jemis and told Erwin how Nexia saw the Gun.

In the County of Mills, the army was under foreign colors: Erwin hid the pennant and dressed as a private, and Fitzgerald “conditionally” accepted him into his company. ([[melnitsy|The Mills]]) At Jenna's giant mill, the lieutenant noticed that she was guarded, and assumed that an arsenal and a barracks were built into her foundation. In September, Lieutenant Fitzgerald, with two cuirres, came to Fort Sunset Star. In front of the Commandant, “subordination was grossly violated”: instead of the commander, a gloomy private spoke.

**Sea of Ice.** In October 1775, Lord Velentyne's ship was waiting at the Gran Islands. Fitzgerald, the commander of the first winding company, together with the captain of the ship, believed that it was impossible to wait any longer, and threatened to talk to my lord himself. Scar took his telescope and immediately saw the ship. Erwin asked who had bet on his mistake, and Fitzgerald admitted, "I offered to leave. My fault, my lord." With Scar, he disliked Nexia's fiancé, Jemis. Later, when Erwin began to break down on his subordinates, Fitzgerald said something off and was sent to another ship.

**The Battle for the First Winter.** In November, Erwin dedicated Gordon Sue, Scar and Fitzgerald to the main and two reserve plans, and he and the Leed Wolves went to rescue his sister in Leed. ([[lid|Leed]]) On December 2, 1775, the day of the Battle of the First Winter, Gordon Sue led a column of X's, and Scar and Fitzgerald helped him. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])
