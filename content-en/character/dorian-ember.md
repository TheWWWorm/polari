## Biography
Dorian Amber is a young original Yanmean, seven years older than [[minerva|Minerva]]. He served at the court under [[adrian|Adrian]], and at Minerva as the first secretary of the palace Chancellery made her schedule: decided when she eats, sleeps, with whom to meet and where to go. Everything that was recorded in the palace protocols passed through the secretariat: incoming and outgoing letters, visits, petitions, appointments, penalties. Therefore, he knew “everything that was included in the protocol”, and according to him, everything was included. In the House voting scene, the narrator refers to Lord Chancellor Erwin Origin as Amber's "patron and student friend".

### "A Doll on the Throne"
At the end of January 1775, the morning after Erwin Origin became Lord Chancellor, Minerva, without reading, signed the decrees of appointment submitted to him - among them was the decree on the first secretary. Mira soon met him on the dog races arranged by the Lord Chancellor. Amber jokingly described her morning in minutes (“Ten or thirty: the beginning of doubt ...”), did not let her free three and then five hours a day and admitted that flirting. He fingered her questions about Alanis Almer and Origin: Are they lovers? ([[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]) "Sometimes". Refusing to race against Alanis himself ("I'm afraid of snow, dogs and Lady Alanis"), he received a clutch from Mira. The empress won the race, and Amber quietly disappeared and left a three-point note on her table: “double dot” in the plans means unimportant points, if in the evening to ask for the third volume of “Diary” Janmay, it will be an envelope with interesting news of the day, and for flirting he apologizes.

Since then, Amber secretly supplied the lady with information. He wrote that the banking houses would offer loans, that the Virgins had arrived at Faunterra, that Origin, Alanis, and [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] had quarreled over the Duchy of Almera, and that Prince Hector would bring bad news. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) Before the performance, where the scandal Liam Shelier humiliated Minerva, he did not warn her: in the intermission, dressed in blue camzol, he reported only the return of the Minister of Railways Shelier, huddled before the second news, and then he was pushed by flatterers. ([[liam-shele|Liam Shellier]]) Minerva then alienated him and stopped taking Yangmei's Diaries. Amber passed through Leyla that he was not involved in the "theatrical casus" and asked for an opportunity to explain himself, and then through Leyla Talmir he asked for an explanation and wrote that he did not know what Origin was cooking, and he considered her informed about the appointments of the treasurer and the minister of taxes that had happened before their acquaintance. ([[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]]) When Minerva thought about what to do with the Steppe Fire, he advised giving "this dirty business" to the Origins or just waiting for the Duke to decide for himself. Of the [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]] he reported that they had shot the Parliamentarians of General Gore with sparked self-fires and that the Lord Chancellor, with twenty thousand troops, had moved to defeat them. Amber told her not to interfere. At Hector's reception, when it was discovered that the Crown was unaware of the siege of Melorange, the first secretary discreetly assured that no letters had been received by the secretariat, and offered to show the protocols.

At a secret gathering of his associates, Mira exposed him: "You are an agent of Origin." Amber slammed the cup. Minerva explained that she and the Duke were "a pair of two boots": both astute cheeks with a sense of humor, confident in their charm. She was alarmed at how frivolously he wrote about Origin in letters. Then she noticed that Amber always writes the truth, but exactly the part that is useful to the Duke, and advises the same thing that would advise the Origin: not to go to Litland, not to pay attention to the Snowdrops. She finally became convinced when Erwin began flirting with her in the same manner that Amber was on the dog run. There was no bribery, the baronet simply admired Erwin. Amber asked if he had been ordered to resign. Minerva left him in the service: he is smart, knows his business, did not go down to lies, twice really helped her, and the replacement could be a hundred times worse. She warned me that the margin of forgiveness was exhausted. The baronet delayed the bow, weighing whether the demand was feasible, and gave his word of honor.

### The “Yanmei Hunt”
As First Secretary, Amber presided over the meetings of the House in the spring of 1775. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) At the first meeting, he hissed to remind Minerva to name the representatives, gave the first word to Erwin, smiled subtly after the Gaunt Groze. ([[ganta-groza|Ganta Thunderstorm]]) He rang a silver bar across the glass sphere, calling for attention, gave the word to the lords, and before Minerva's speech he rose and bowed to her, as was supposed before the emperor's speech. When Erwin wanted to urgently call him, Leila Talmir objected that the baronet was a gossip:
> "Then he will call only his mistresses, and those - the whole yard."

At the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], he was the court secretary, announced the hearings and gave his testimony. At the first meeting, he asked the prankster if he refused the legal assistance of a defense lawyer, and at the second at the request of the defense, he examined the backs of mannequins and confirmed that the testimony of witnesses coincided "to the accuracy of three inches." At first, he was among the witnesses who remembered the misdeeds of the jester. Defender [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] called him his first witness. Amber declined for a long time, referring to the books, but under pressure announced the dates of the oaths of [[menson|Manson]]: May 5, 1744, he brought a vassal oath to Tellurian, April 8, 1746 - a military oath of an officer of the Imperial Navy. Amber admitted that the first lost power with the death of Telurian, and the second in 1755, when Manson was deprived of the admiralty. Lord Douglas Litland reminded him that Adrian did not take the vassal oath from Manson. The secretary objected that Manson had raised his hand against the emperor and that the training match was fought with blunt blades, but the prophet turned both arguments in favor of protection. Amber later read to the House the documents Erwin had submitted to him: a page of Pramatery Yumin's memoirs on Ulyana Dust and Telurian's decree to seize Dust from the Lighthart House. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]; [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]])

When voting to depose Adrian, Lady in Darkness asked him to reformulate the question: not to declare Minerva the mistress, but to deprive Adrian of his rights and elect a new emperor. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) Amber leaned on Erwin, got a barely noticeable nod, and put the question to a vote. He then announced that "the wisdom of the House of Representatives" had deprived Adrian of all power, and put to a vote Cordelia's mother's proposal to consider Erwin's candidacy in the first place.

### In "Humans and Gods"
When officials at Minerva's court bombarded her with complaints about Manson, Amber, according to the narrator, was one of two who did not complain. The other was Treasurer Robert Origin.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree
In early July 1775, Amber himself brought Minerva a letter with the news that [[paul|Paul]] had "killed" Moran and led the horde. When the capital was panicked in front of the horde, Minerva initially suspected that the rumor had let its secretariat, and ordered the Crow to investigate, but then she realized that the source was the headquarters of the spark army. He was pale, and the envelope trembled in his hand. He helped to prepare her speeches and led a series of witty stories about the life of the court, which the library master advised her to read as "not completely devoid of wit." Then, with a barely restrained grin, he reported that [[adrian]] landed in Grace and "immediately married". When Minerva announced in front of the court that she did not recognize Adrian's claims, but would not start a civil war, Amber applauded and "smiled with one eye", and at the time, when the court began to turn away from the temporary mistress, he directly told her: "I like you more, mistress. Adrian was smothered."

When Adrian returned to [[faunterra|Faunterra]], the baronet, fragrant with wine and feminine spirits, boldly appeared to him for a reception. He introduced himself to the commander of the shavanese guard of the [[ganta-biraj|ganta Birai]], “Dorian, doo, Amber”, so that he would not be confused with Count Darian of the Horse Wars. He wished Adrian long years of love, addressing him “my lord”: there are no legal grounds to consider him an emperor. Instead of decrees, he first pulled a handkerchief with a wenzel out of his pocket. He then read out two Minerva decrees certified the day before yesterday: on the temporary transfer of the capital to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] due to a military threat and on the appointment of Lord Adrian by the burgomaster of Faunterra. This work is "considered as corrective work" for the accused of heresy and tyranny. [[menson|Manson]] slid under the table with a laugh. Adrian ordered the drunk secretary to be dipped in the pond, until he first called him "His Majesty".

In August 1775, when Minerva had already left for the north, Amber fled by train to Labelin, where he was arrested as Minerva's spy. He paid off and went as a stagecoach to Windley, where the Kairs captured him as Adrian's spy. He was rescued from the basement by the surety of [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]]. In October 1775, he appeared in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], repenting that he did not immediately follow the mistress, because he did not like the northern cold. Minerva at the sight of him exclaimed with joy: she needed a person capable of creating a whole secretariat. In the First Winter, he became one of the five experienced officials of its apparatus, created and headed the secretariat, set up a department for hiring employees and entrusted it to Leila Talmir. Most of the student boarding house Elena-u-Lake, mobilized by Robert, settled in the office of Amber, and the narrator admits that his personal charm played a role here. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) At the feast of bachelors, he complained that all his althesies remained in Faunterra, and the first imperial secretary could not be alone, because this stained the reputation of the court. In November, when Minerva left to meet Erwin returning, in her procession was the first secretary with a detachment of clerks in expensive dresses.

The morning after the Battle of the First Winter, Amber came to the exhausted Minerva fresh, hairy and elegant, accompanied by two girls from the secretariat. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Mira attacked him, but immediately entrusted him with the control of the guilds, accounting for the homeless, orphans and wounded, and a message to the Great Houses of victory. His message was ready.

### In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, a possible future, Amber remains Minerva's first secretary and right-hand man. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) After the story with the "man in the coffin", he together with Leila Talmir represented the Minerva side in negotiations with the Origins and Magda Labelin. When rumours of ghosts spread in the First Winter, he sent out requests to his capital friends and friends. He himself became a hero of this story. Renting a floor in the apartment building at the Small impasse, away from the eyes of the courtiers, Amber assured that he was living as a recluse. However, one morning, Meredith squealed out of his house. This noblewoman of the Inesa family before the war studied in the third year of Elena-u-Lake, and now served as a scribe in the news analysis department of the Imperial Secretariat. According to her story, under the sheet lay a human skeleton in the rotting remains of the flesh. Amber denied it.

Before Minerva's 20th birthday, Leila called him to look for a gift and shared her anxiety that the Origins were manipulating the Empress. Baronet, a "fool's crust" to hide how deeply committed to Minerva's designs, said that he owns wineries near Ruilde and does not like taxes. He admitted that "the strange behavior of Her Majesty" is partly his fault: when asked by Minerva about the election, he twisted the saying "There is only one Star" into "There is more than one Star", and the mistress, it seems, then came up with her plan. He chose as a gift Megara Estelle Mila's book "Secret Horrors of the First Winter" with a silver skull on the cover. At the festival, the "artistic" baronet was refused to dance and was comforted by dancing with Lady Iona. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]])

Minerva later asked Amber to schedule a date with Erwin. The baronet inquired about what time it was, and advised a date for two or three hours, red semi-sweet wine instead of an orge and fewer jokes. A week later, she herself came to him for advice, because the novel should have been hidden from strict Leyla. Amber told her to forget the word "pink", to dress in gold and black, to wear only diamonds and to throw off the shawl when he came to the Duke. He also advised her to apologize to Iona first: in the competition in the hardness of the Origins can not be defeated, and softness can be disarmed. Amber was one of the first to learn about her secret affair with Erwin. On her orders, he was looking for a printing house for a secret order. The printing houses of Faunterra and Marengo were kept by Adrian's men, but through friends dissatisfied with the burgomaster, he found a small printing shop in Ruilde, and Minerva placed part of the order there. He also warned her that Bishop Amessin and mother Alicia thirst for the Duke's blood, because there is no promised peace with the Shavans. At the conclusion of the peace with Steppe, he did not give anyone the honor of being a secretary, he himself stood at the left shoulder of the Empress and submitted to the parties a primary agreement for peace.

#### "Ad-ri-an!"
From the end of August 1776, Amber was the lover of [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]. He tried to ask Erwin's permission "at Mia's birthday," but only "muttered a misunderstanding about men's friendship with women." On the train to Faunterra, Erwin caught them together. He called the baronet a "poisonous snake" because he knew some of Minerva's "sinister plans" and was silent, but through Iona asked him, as president of the House, to put the speeches in the right order: Erwin, Adrian, the prophet, Minerva. In the capital, Amber had a house three blocks from the Origin mansion, and Aubrey was ordered to keep Iona out.

In the election, Amber held meetings. For Ollie's recorder, he repeated the essence of each solution, and so the "sound protocol" was obtained. When Adrian, under the scope of Perst, announced an ultimatum, Amber calmly dictated it to the protocol, and to the demand to be silenced replied: “The secretary rejected his request.” In the besieged House, he proudly hosted “the first-ever overnight sitting of the House.” He placed the obvious supporters of Adrian on the balconies, where he led the creaking staircase, and at night he himself went somewhere. He settled the case with the cardinals who passed into the House and asked them if it was true that in fact the majority of votes in the election of the archpriest was received by Francis-Ilianus. After Adrian’s death, Amber joked that he was “no longer the first secretary.” Minerva threw him: “By exchanging me for an ice bed Iona Origin, you lost the right to ask about feelings!”, but called him to rehearse the speech. Together with Frank Morlin-Mei, he helped her to choose the price of the Empress's services - one agate per week.

### "Death of Assassin"
Following the abolition of the Ministry of the Court, Minerva handed over its representational and administrative affairs to the Ember Secretariat. The dismissed minister [[khoksvort-fellion|Fellion]] called him "lovelace and vertichoust".

## Personality and Appearance
Judging by the bending of the mouth, "self-confident cock", judging by the eyes, "not a fool." He is cold-hearted, ironic, charming, and is known as a gossip and a lovela, a "tiger of love jungles", with Yangmei dimples on his cheeks. Unlike Shelier, he is not shy in front of the strong. Mira noticed his idle hands of an aristocrat, polished nails and a "thin, like a girl's wrist", and the narrator in Fantasies calls him "skinny". In the theater, he wore a blue camsol and explained to Minerva:
> “It’s a sign that I don’t like tradition.”

According to him, Vladyka Adrian respected traditions, but raised those who did not follow them very much.
