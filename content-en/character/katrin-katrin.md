## Who She Is

Catherine Catherine is a dark, thin Western with amygdala eyes and a "slender body", similar to a lynx or wild cat, with a sharp look and thin lips. Behind the smoothness of her movements, a "rapacious force" was said to be "the best mistress in the world." She is “not a very lady”: she is not descended from any of the Pramaterie, was born in Holliwell, was sold to the South and served as an altess to a Shimmerian merchant, then a mayor, then Prince Hector. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) She is smart, "as hell", and knows the intricacies of the capital's love affairs, "like a spider - her own web." Since childhood, I have been used to counting money.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

By 1774, Catherine became an adviser to the emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] on secular intrigues and was loyal to him; the court called her one of the largest and "biters" of the imperial "dogs", although they remembered that she was the althessa of the Shimmerian prince. She and [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] had mutual animosity. At Adrian’s tea party in February 1774, to which only General William Davey and Erwin were invited, she taunted Origin (“I am waiting for you, Lord Erwin, when will you offer your lovely sister to the lord?”), and in the end, with a barely noticeable nod, she confirmed to the lord that she already knew the secret marriage contract of Origin with Farham from somewhere. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) Acquaintance with her Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] alter Countess Clarence considered dangerous: so she “sniffed” with people to whom the Bishop listens.

## In "Only One Star"

At the test of the miracle machine, Catherine "furnished the eyes" of the emperor's bride. Crown Raven [[mark-voron|Mark]] came to her for advice, and after bargaining ("I love candy"), they exchanged suspicions. Catherine said that the ruler should not marry Minerva: the Yanmeans under the manners have a "second layer, and below - the third", and under the manners of the bride "empty", and this marriage - "unclear deal". She was afraid to talk to Adrian (“The day I upset the lord will be the last”) and persuaded Mark to do it. In farewell, she told him another strange thing: Gloria Northwood stayed in the boarding house of Elena-u-Lake for three years out of four, and then her mother took her to Faunterra. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]])

In the fall of 1774, before marching on Moran's Steppe Fire, Adrian sent Catherine to the embassy at [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] on his fastest corvette to lead a fleet of Southerners. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) She returned with Hector and three hundred and twenty ships and the whole campaign in Litland was next to the prince. At the edge of the jungle, she showed the commanders the bodies nailed to the trees and explained that they were deserters executed by the Steppe Fire: the horde did not retreat because the West came for blood revenge, and the Shavans were probably already at the walls or in the walls of Melorange.

After defeating the horde, Adrian awarded her "for faithful service" the right to choose one of the Crown's Sacred Objects. Her self-control did not leave her, although her eyes flashed. She chose the Item from the top page - the Cup of Joy, which appeared with the Sixth Gift during the Early Myriam dynasty: he who drank from it, according to legend, learned his meaning of life and found true joy.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collectible edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark adds that, along with the Cup, Adrian offered Catherine an enviable diplomatic service. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) Before her, she took a month off to her alter and became pregnant. The prince was initially angry, but Catherine "showed such a point of view" that he made her his favorite. Throughout 1775, Karen said, she was busy with the child and did not participate in the great events. By the time Karen writes her notes, Catherine has two children. Karen became close to her, and it was Catherine who provided her with information about Adrian's court, but after reading the notes, demanded to write about herself as little as possible.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

In the spring of 1775, Catherine was the only one to sit next to Hector when the prince asked Erwin for an army against his own father. She taunted Erwin’s naivete, “smiling with one eye, with a subtle poison,” and dropped “Well, of course,” when Erwin offered only one or two battalions.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

In the autumn of 1775 in [[soltaun|Soltown]], Catherine, "the fairy althessa of Hector", was expecting a child. [[karen|Karen]] saw in her only “a life-worn shavanka by no means the first youth”, but Francis-Ilian made it clear that he deeply respected her mind and character and hoped to see at least part of her traits in his grandson; at his request, everyone drank for Catherine Catherine. When the king spoke about the war with the Origins, and Hector burst into flames (“Erwin and I drank the cup of brotherhood. I will not betray him!”), Catherine took the prince by the hand, and he died.

## In "Fantasies"

"Fantasies" the author calls the possible fates of the heroes. His father-in-law said, “My grandson was born. The eyes are narrow, he is half Shavan.”

In Amicable (August 1776, Laam), Catherine is the wife of the prince. She gave the baby to the nurse and “with all her might” returned to her husband’s bed. She met the northern guests in a dress that “no other shavanka in the world” and immediately took away Mirai Nan-Claire who came with them. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) Her ostentatious jealousy was only a disguise for the "fine calculation of the politician." While Mireille was taking a bath, Catherine's men, along with Alvaro, a connoisseur of poisons, searched her belongings and found antidotes and a bottle of landy, a sign of a love potion. Catherine explained to her husband what she wanted: a Shimmer regiment and a squadron to take the throne of Darkwater, in return for a fairway through the Tops of the Dark Kings. Darkwater's neighbors are Shimmery and Reis, and she, Catherine, is "still the niece of Ganty Cort", so friendship with their family is necessary to the queen of the swamps. She never lied to her husband, only “gives facts a light color shade.” At the night auction of althess she sat with Mireille in the ladies' box, and under her gaze Hector did not participate in the auction.

Catherine hated the slave trade, although she herself came from a Shavan family. When the ambassador of Darkwater on behalf of Emmon Darklin offered the votes of the marshes for Francis-Ilian in exchange for the arrest of Mireille, Catherine secretly "exalted", but for the sake of the species discouraged her husband and urged Hyder Leed not to interfere with the arrest, promising him the search for his nephew. When Hyder Leed and Mireille were allegedly bathed in a love potion, the court considered Catherine a poisoner. To maintain her husband's trust, she bought the only turntable potion in Laem for fifty ephesas, paying with a bill from her own account, rather than a royal one. After the whole story, Hector and Catherine’s love became stronger: they cheated on each other “no more than once a year”, which by the standards of the South is an exceptional fidelity.
