Barons Devo are two brothers who own the sparking technique of the free city of [[finboro|Finborough]]. The city does not belong to them, but they sell the spark to the townspeople, and the price is kept tolerable. Both are heroes of fantasy "Ruth Case".

## Brothers
Bernard Devo is an older brother, master and heir, a man sharp and rude. The man, who had slipped under his arm, he punched in the face. When the weaver's guild got too busy, he just "knocked out the spark" and the whole guild got up with the machines. Bernard was single and addicted to the girl Kitty from a brothel by the canal, nicknamed "the mouseman". In politics, he was against [[adrian|Adrian]]. In the Northern Flash, he served with the knight Duchess [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]].

Reginald Devo is a younger brother, quiet and reserved. He holds a posture, wears lacquer shoes and an elegant sword, which, according to the townspeople, no one has seen naked. He didn't go to the brothel. Fisherman Finch thought he was "good." Gossip Chuck said that Reginald agreed with the interlocutors in words, "not to argue", but did his own. He was for Adrian. The lawyer Mac saw him as the youngest son, who lives with a rough older brother "like a mouse."

## In "Fantasies"
**A fight.** In the spring of 1776, the knight Lady Magda brought the news of the election of the lord to Finborough. The townspeople argued over who to stand for. The knight went around with Bernard Kabaki and dropped that the Duchess "will not be offended at all" if on the day when Adrian's recorded speech is brought, a spark disappears in the city. The brothers quarreled. The abbot came to the city asking for a spark for the cathedral. Then it was announced that the brothers had reconciled, and Bernard himself left for Faunterra on June 10. Reginald gave a speech in the town hall about the fact that it is not worth fighting because of politics, and was known as a peacemaker.

**It's a dog thing.** Soon after, Reginald was bitten by the red dog of Kusaka fisherman Finch in the Third Docking Deadlock. The Baron refused the fine: the fine would ruin the poor, and his starvation would lay “on my conscience”. He sued the dog himself and obtained a death sentence under the Yumin Code. The request of the executioner [[uolter-dzhejn-dzhon|Walter]] and Finch to withdraw the charge he politely, but firmly rejected. He said, “Any evildoer will be punished, even a dog.”

**Exposing.** Lawman McFreed Crowe noticed the oddities. The elegant baron, fermented by a brothel, appeared at dawn in a stinking dead end, in high boots and without a sword. The good-natured dog, who knew Bernard and fondled him, attacked his brother. Kitty said she had Bernard on June 9, the day before Reginald was bitten. Mac realized that the abbot had asked for a spark for the cathedral, and Bernard, as Lady Magda wanted, refused to give it for Adrian's speech. Reginald killed his brother at a mouse collar and pushed the body into a clogged waste pipe. Kusaka smelled the corpse, the baron tried to drive him away, and the dog bit him. The execution of the dog Reginald demanded that he no longer approach the pipe. Bernard's body was found in the pipe, and Kusaku was acquitted.
