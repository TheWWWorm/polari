**Vincent Shayland** is the Earl of Shayland during the Seventeenth Gift, the father of Wittor and [[martin-shejland|Martin]]. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) He himself told his sons that his family had risen from the merchants: the firstborn disdained his father, and in order for the vassals to take the new dynasty seriously, they had to pay and intimidate. He is shown alive only in The Price of Fear, in other books others talk about him.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
The count himself does not speak in the book, but Vittor repeats his words. In Chapter 12 of The Coin, he tells [[kharmon|Harmon]], "Lords don't like to count" - and if the son doesn't learn to count, the father "will come down from the Star and pour it in properly"; he ends with the words "That's what the father told me." In Chapter 15 of the Coin, Vittor shows Harmon the Light Sphere: "The Gift contained one hundred and forty-two Objects. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) My father got thirty-nine. The one in front of you is one of them.”

## The “Janmei Hunt”
Here Iona, in the footsteps of the prisoner, restores the circumstances of the death of the old count.

**Death (chapter "Northern Bird - 3").** Prisoner Lard says that "first there was an escape, and a week later died Count Vincent." Because of the escape, the officers were furious, the watch was doubled, the gate was locked, and the count himself was “unwell, sat in his rooms and hardly showed up to the yard.” The count died in the office, locked from the inside with a key, and the door had to be broken: "Count Vincent Ulyan took to the Star - it's locked doors are not a hindrance." In a conversation with Irving, it is concluded that with the death of the count, "everything is clean", and Iona thinks that Vittor respected his father and spoke of him as "the wisest mentor".

**Fear of the Count ("Northern Bird - 5").** Irving finds out that Vincent "was terribly afraid of this prisoner": after escaping, he raised the entire garrison, called in Waymar additional troops, two thousand soldiers combed the city and the surrounding area. From his office led a secret passage into the prison and into the ruins of the monastery, where the count placed a company of cavalry. Vittor offered to stall, but his father refused. Iona, in conversation with her husband, says that the count's heart "stopped on its own, unable to withstand fear."

**Northwood version (North Bird - 6).** Old [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] believes that Vincent held a prisoner for intrigue against the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]], and then "split on him." Iona notices that the count's body was staring at the wall where the secret passage opens, and asks if someone who had "scared the count to death" came out of there. According to Northwood, at the time of Vincent's death, Vittor prayed in Vivian's church "for the health of his father."

**The War of the Gift (Sword 6)** From Harry Hog's story: after the victory of the sunsets at the Lodge of Dar, the count "feverily gathered troops and sought allies." ([[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]]) With [[dom-nortvud|Northwood]], the relationship was complicated, [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]] "despised the Shayland banker", [[moris-labelin|Labelin]] asked for an insane fee, and Origin agreed to help, but could only carry two battalions. Northwood adds that Vincent has fielded two thousand more warriors.

## "Humans and Gods"
In Arrow 1, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] gives Cordelia's mother his version: Waymar has a man talking to the Objects; ([[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]]) Vincent demanded to be taught, and after being refused, he threw him into prison, tortured and finally walled up. More than ten years later, Vittor proposed to the prisoner, and when he demanded revenge, “he allowed the Count Vincent to be killed.” Erwin adds that "poor Vincent had a weak heart, and the weather was hot", so that the prisoner did not have to strike: "his very appearance frightened the count and pushed on the Star."

## The “Price of Fear”
**The Father's Lesson (Chapter 1 of The Price of Fear, August 1756)** Vincent equalizes the ashes in the fireplace with forceps and explains to his sons that they have not yet entered the high society: "You just stand on the threshold, and to be allowed further, your shoes must be clean": his father was born on a ship, his grandfather fled from Halliwell from the count, who decided to take money from merchants. The father made a huge fortune, bought county bills, and the counts had to marry Vincent his daughter and call him the heir; vassals had to pay and keep a Northwood regiment of two thousand bears.

Martin, who killed the cat in front of Baroness Selina [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]], the Count punishes with fireplace tongs: "Lord must be able to keep himself in hand." Then he examines his sons: which of the guests will come first and why, how to talk to them, how many chapters of the “Diaries” they have read. He is satisfied with Wittor's answers ("thrown with barely noticeable approval"). About Martin, the Count had a single illusion: he believed that he lacked discipline and could be grafted on, while Vittor saw that neither discipline nor education would help.

**Deal about Items.** Before the war, the Count owned thirteen Objects, "and none brought him any benefit." He wanted to give the money instead of money to Origin a few Items, but so that the Duke considered it an expensive gift; the Duke [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] wanted the same and was also silent. After a few days of trickery, Vincent began on the last day before the Lord’s arrival: “My Lord Origin, I would not have dared to say this, lest I anger the gods...” and “toughly blushed.” The duke objected, addressed to the bishops, who "reported that in this case the Foremothers would not mind", and "the feudal lords joyfully struck hands".

**Desmond's analysis.** Later, the count utters the favorite saying "Use what you know, sell what you have" and deals with the mistake of Desmond, who, according to him, "tried to sell anything but swords" and was refused by Telurian.

## "The Shadow of the Great Tree"
In the chapter "Iskra - 6" [[navi|Navi]] explains to Minerva that [[paul|Paul]] is dark: he "expected Count Vincent to be afraid of his powers, to obey the will of the dark prisoner. But the count did something different: he buried Paul alive. In releasing the prisoner, Vittor was to give him three men, including his "own father": "He killed the jailers with his hand, and Count Vincent died of a heartbreak, depriving the prisoner of several sweet sips."

## In "Fantasies"
In "Fantasy of the Fifth: Amicably", he is referred to as "the late Count Vincent Shayland": seeing Princess Demeter, he "would be delighted", because she "embodied his favorite saying"; in the text it is transmitted as "Torture what you have." ([[demetra-nellis-lajon|Demetra Nellis-Lyon]])

## Origin and condition
The "Guide to the lands of Polari" tells that the banking house of the Harpers bought the debt bills of the former counts, and the count was forced to "give his daughter and daughter to Vincent Harper" (so in the text). It also says that the Seventeenth Gift "raised the authority of the House of Shayland, undermined by marriage to the bankers." It is estimated in the Author's Remarks that Vincent was customarily to receive forty-seven Items, and received thirty-nine: the author assumes that the remaining eight he gave to Origin in gratitude for helping in the war "and another important service" - "or our author just calculated."

## Personality
Thus the graph appears in the "Price of Fear": "inclined to illusions of any kind", calculating, trading everything that he has. He educates his sons with tongs, exams, and books. The Vivian Cathedral in [[uejmar|Waymar]] is built with his money.
