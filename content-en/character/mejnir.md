## About the Lord

**Maynir** is the Emperor Polari of the Glorious Dynasty, the father of Lord Telurian and [[menson|Manson]], and the grandfather of [[adrian|Adrian]]. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) In the main cycle, it is only mentioned. The old [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]], recalling the old days, thought of "Lord Mainir, who had fifty violass, who killed a hundred boars and did not issue a single law." So he thought, listening to [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]'s story about Martin Shayland, a discord with her husband and exhausted by the war of the Southern Way: in his lifetime, the dying lord saw and not so, so he did not find in her anxieties a cause for great excitement.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark writes that the favorite of the nobility was Mainir, not Tellurian. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) He adored women, hunting and wine, bored with politics and gave most of his affairs to advisers and ministers. He was famous for his easy temper, generosity and courage. Father Karen loved books, and Maynir laughed at him. But one day his grandfather found in the book a solution to the legal task of the emperor and as a reward he received the right to sit and read in the presence of the lord. He enjoyed reading right at the balls, in a chair in the middle of the dancers.

Karen also talks about “drowns.” Under Lady Alicia, the ladies took the fashion to jump into the water to be rescued by the cavalry. Alicia forbade boats and locked the gate, but in Sofia days one girl drowned seriously. Meinir ordered the body to be displayed in a gazebo and ordered the ladies of the court to wish the drowned woman good night every evening. Fashion is gone. And the ponds of the palace garden since then are only three feet deep - in case fashion returns.

## Burial

The Yangmei lords rest in the Temple of Farewell in Arden. His tomb goes deep into the earth tier by tier: the recently deceased lords lie below, long gone to the Star - above. In the Janmei Hunt, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] descended to the seventh tier, an octagonal hall set aside for the era of "Lord Mainir, his son Telurian, and Lord Ingrid." ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) Apparently, Mainir is buried there as well.

## Marriage of sons

Telurian's marriage was arranged by his father. When the nobility, led by Manson, poisoned [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]], even Tellurian himself, according to the description of her portrait in the palace gallery, lamented the "choice made by his father." Manson, the younger brother of the emperor, was “not constrained by tradition” and married at the behest of his heart.

## In "Fantasies"

In "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]", in the story "Annoying Little Things", Lady [[karen|Karen]] tells about this in more detail. She persuaded her niece Nexia to submit to the parental will in marriage. According to her, Meinir himself married whom he wanted and his beloved son Manson was also allowed to choose a bride, and Teluriana considered a bore and therefore ordered him to marry a swamp despised by the court. Karen saw in this a lesson about dynastic marriages: Tellurian, who performed his father’s will, became a great, albeit cruel emperor, and Manson, who was married for love, “a loser, a drunkard and a joke.”
