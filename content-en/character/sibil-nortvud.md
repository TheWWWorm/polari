## Origins

Sybil was born to Baron Fox Dole in [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) Her mother is a blond stately Lady Dorina; the Sorrow brothers taught her to climb trees, and her father drank old Grimsdale's hanty. The Countess loved to tell the story of her grandfather, who died in a duel, but before his death took with him the enemy (“Come with me”). Her mother, Baroness Doreen, married her eighteen-year-old to Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]], who was forty years older than the bride: in the year of the wedding, the count turned sixty. So Sybil became the stepmother of his three sons — [[krejg-nortvud|Craig]], Horace and Donald.

In August 1756, shortly after the wedding, the young Countess came with her husband to [[uejmar|Waymar]] for the celebrations on the occasion of the Seventeenth Gift. Elias introduced her to young [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] as the "first beauty of the North". Vittor found her too rough — too broad hips, too much glitter in her eyes, and gold in her hair — but recognized the prominent young lady, "just what an old count needs to shine with male power." At the feast, the Archpriest [[ellina|Ellina]] gave a brilliant wedding speech for the newlyweds, and Bishop [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] led the Countess in a dance, "mercilessly stepping on her feet." According to [[navi|Navi]], it was in that month and year that her daughter [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]] was conceived, whose real father is Gallard, and not the count. This secret Sybil hid to the last.

Sybil ruled the county on behalf of a decrepit husband who was not interested in anything but ships. She knew that after his death the power will pass to the stepson, and she herself will remain the fate of a poor relative. She was called Bear; about her and her scientist bear Maverick, the soldiers sang the song “Lady and the Bear”.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

In October 1773, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] came to Sybil for help, hoping to play on her pride. The countess accepted him in a home dress - Erwin, in her opinion, was not an important person to dress up for him. They made an alliance: Northwood would support the general tax and any bride of the emperor, and the Countess herself would personally receive part of the port of [[uindli|Windley]].

In March 1774, after the murder of Sir Clive Stagforth, Sybil was sheltered by his daughter [[minerva|Mira]] in the Bear Fang. ([[klajv-stagfort|Clive Stagfort]]; [[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) At breakfast, she revealed to the girl the genealogy of "The Origins, Current and Branches of the Brilliant Yangmei Family", struck out the names of the dead and disenfranchised in pencil and announced that Mira was fourth in line for the throne, and, apart from the joke of Manson, third, and that the attack meant a conspiracy against the throne. Count Elias went to the wedding in the First Winter, warning his wife that the Dynasty did not like to feel obliged. In [[labelin|Labelin]], the countess repainted Mira in red and brought her to [[faunterra|Faunterra]] as her daughter "Gloria". In the meantime, she introduced the real Gloria to the imperial palace under the name of [[minerva|Minerva]] Stagforth.

[[adrian|Adrian]]'s first audience turned to humiliation for the countess: the emperor announced that she had "showed cunning and greed", and only mercifully saved her from punishment. Coming out of the throne room, Sybil Bushevala (“Who am I — a dog begging at the table of the owners?!”), and then laughed: her “grim grumbling Elias” was right again. Soon, however, she joined the circle of trusted advisers to the Lord. Her alter, the stable Lord [[lord-klarens|Clarence]], in a private conversation reproached the countess for her too close friendship with the Gallard Priory and in relations with Erwin's men; he soon died in a duel: the Northwood baron considered that the connection with the countess stained the name of Count Elias.

In the summer, Mira slowly died from “liver disease” and on July 3 she realized that she was being poisoned in her morning coffee. Sybil and Gallard, caught by her, did not kill the girl: on the advice of the priory, she was sent to the cave monastery of Ulyana Sad in the west of Almera. Swearing on "our daughter's life", Sybil revealed that Gloria's father was Gallard. Mira considered the Countess and the murderer of her father; the court never proved this.

## In "Only One Star"

In the fall of 1774, Sybil was one of the emperor's advisors, and her daughter was his bride. [[mark-voron|Mark]] suspected the countess, but Adrian did not believe him. On September 15, 1774, when Adrian asked her opinions after a training match in the training hall, Medveditsa first assured that the ruler would "put down any kair", and then admitted the truth: "Erwin is not a kair, Your Majesty." At the military council, she assured Adrian of her loyalty to Northwood: Count Elias and his sons allegedly already went down [[torrej|Torrey]] to Waymar to take an oath in person in the capital in three weeks. She predicted that the Duke of Labelin would remain faithful under the sword of Origin, and Henry Farway, who spoke to the sick, only drags on the time, and honestly warned that the South would not come to help. From the Southern Way came the report sent by Mark [[itan|Ethan]] about the substitution revealed in the boarding house of Elena-u-Lake. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) On October 14, he was read aloud in the ladies' party hall: Sybil laughed, but Gloria gave herself up, and both were arrested right in the palace. Mira, who was visiting Waymar in those days, was convinced that Count Elias did not know about the plot of his wife; after the arrest, Sybil Craig led Northwood's army to Erwin, and the old Count was taken into custody. The countess was charged with the murder of five people - Sir Clive and four of his servants - and with deceiving the emperor. The investigation took two weeks, and on November 2, the Supreme Court found the mother and daughter guilty of deception (the murder could not be proved). Gloria was sentenced to life imprisonment. Sybil was kept alive to extract information from her about Origin, with whom she was allied.

In early November, Northwood joined the mutiny, and Adrian became enraged, calling the bears "wicked jackals". ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]]) He threw the Countess, "You'll never see your daughter. You won’t even see the sun!” She was tortured - skillfully, almost without damaging the skin, but painfully. Sybil laid out everything she knew about Origin's plans, but she knew little: the Duke only promised her one of Windley's harbors in exchange for military aid, and his march on the emperor became a thunderbolt in the clear sky for her. The executioners did not believe and tormented more and more terrible. Until the last, she kept one secret, hoping that the Lord would be more merciful to her legitimate daughter than to the bastard, but at the end of November she surrendered: Gloria is the daughter of the archpriest. Prisoner Claude threw her crackers and talked about the types of executions.

When the troops of Origin approached the capital, Claude disappeared, and the prisoner stopped feeding; the countess rejoiced, realizing that the Dynasty is kept from the last forces. One night, Major Backfield took her out of the casemate and drove her in a closed carriage. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Sybil decided that she was being taken to the gallows, and begged only for news of Gloria, but the major replied, "Your name is Dorothy Sly, you're my cousin." Four days later, she was on Faraday Riley Island. ([[ostrov-faradeya-rajli|Faraday-Riley Island]])

## The “Janmei Hunt”

The Witness chapters tell the story of Dorothy Sly's life in the hospital. Under the influence of "procedures" - blows to pain, sparks, darkwater potions and sedatives - and also at her own will to forget the Countess lost her memory. By order of the elder nurse, she was beaten and cut off by her wonderful golden hair. She was placed in a room with a silent Caitlin-Karen and a chatterbox by Annette; she was prescribed a "path of harmony", and the only "forgiven symptom" of Dorothy was that she did not remember her past. After Spring Dawn, the nightmares receded, and Dorothy, being literate, became a copyist in the scriptorium of the master Gustav, where she rewrote the novel “Rose and Death”.

Her neighbor on the table was the mathematician [[navi|Navi]], who gave thirty-seven flawless pages a day and constantly demanded: “Say the number!” At first, Dorothy hated him, once hit him on the cheek and for this midnight stood in the basement on tiptoes, fastened with her hand to the ceiling. But, searching in her memory for “numbers with meaning”, she found that she knew the Northern prices and measures, and Navi and [[karen|Karen]] told her “her” number is seven, the number of Foremother Susan. Gradually, Dorothy remembered her kind, Lisiy Dol and, finally, her daughter - a red, cheerful, who loved to dance "chains". ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) She had nightmares in which her hand in a mercury glove broke off the head of a doll - a red girl in a green dress.

On May 18, Backfield appeared on the island under the guise of her cousin, "Major Sly", announcing that he had found a "buyer"; Dorothy thought it was Minerva, and she was horrified. Karen warned that she would be killed on the mainland. After encrypting the question with numbers, Dorothy asked Navi if he knew how to escape from the island; he previously estimated the odds at 0,15%, and now wrote "37,42%". On the night of May 19, when the sailors of the schooner were buying patients from the doctors, Navi took Dorothy away, stopping Coddy's nurse with numbers alone. Karen initially refused to run, and Dorothy at random shouted to her husband, "He still loves you!" The three of them went to sea on a boat, and Dorothy rowed for a long time until she was exhausted; in the morning they were picked up by Captain Lu-Aramon's White Panther ship. Later, Dorothy asked Navi who he was and heard in a whisper, "God..."

## "Humans and Gods"

The fugitives were more than three hours ahead of the chase, and in Ruilde they confused their tracks by buying tickets to Arden and hiding in the port hotel "Fresh Breeze". Dorothy with grief went around five taverns, won cock fights, someone stuck a crack and sang “Lady and the Bear” three times. Navi told her that he was "the god of navigation", and Dorothy offered to bring alms to his temple; Navi begged to speak to him simply as a friend, otherwise he would “run back to the clinic.” The women formed an alliance: Karen will help find Dorothy's daughter, Dorothy's husband Karen.

Around May 23, the fugitives moved to [[marengo|Marengo]], where Karen found a court case in the archive and revealed to Dorothy that she was Countess Sybil Northwood, "the ruler of half the North, the failed mother-in-law of Lord Adrian." Dorothy at first became enraged, deciding that she was being bullied, but her memory returned to her by a "forest fire". Sybil could not accept that the intriguer was herself, and chose to remain Dorothy: the happiest, she said, she was "in the middle of the sea, in a miserable boat with two madmen, without a single coin in her pocket." She drank hanty without getting drunk, did not talk to Navi, who called her (as Karen passed on) a "stupid dark ignorant," and muttered scraps: "Poor my innocent Elias... “When Erwin started the rebellion, it was not too late to back down. We'd get everything except the crown."

After the attack of heretics on the temple, in early June, the trio sat in a seaside hotel near Marengo. Navi, obsessed with the history of wars, did not give rest, and Dorothy did not talk to him and drowned grief in the hanti. After learning from Fred's archival clerk that the Ardennes temple was mentioned in Gloria's case, Dorothy went to Arden in mid-June. There she went around the hospitals, where she met a wounded Cyre Reynolds, and, to Karen’s horror, she herself appeared to the abbess of the Farewell temple. Backfield ambushed the temple, but he himself fell into the hands of the fugitives and Reynolds and confessed where Gloria was. At night, Sybil opened the base of the gazebo at the temple of Farewell and found the body of her daughter, sealed by the order of Adrian; her desperate cry spread across the alley. She was immediately arrested by Mark's men. In late June, Mark took her along with Backfield, Navi, and Karen to [[faunterra|Faunterra]], to the Pera Palace and Sword. ([[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]) Presenting herself before Minerva, the countess did not say a word: Mira saw instead of the hated enemy a faded “dead body without a soul” and could not take revenge on her.

## Personality and Appearance

Sybil is tall and stately, and has wheaten hair and broad shoulders. She loves emerald outfits, hanty and spicy northern cuisine. The Countess is imperious, quick-tempered and ambitious, falling for loud names and always competing with the Origins, although, according to Erwin, unlike them, she does not believe in her own greatness. Count Elias valued her intelligence and charisma and called Sybil Northwood's hope: she had enough intelligence to rule, and charisma to cope with Craig. For the sake of her daughter, she went to murder and poisoning, but her love for Gloria remained in her until the end. In the hospital, becoming Dorothy, she turned out to be a kind, pious, quick-tempered and fearless friend: she sang in the taverns of “Lady and Bear”, fought and found joy in simple things.
