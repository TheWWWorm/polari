## Jesters' Conspiracy

Manson Louise Violetta is the younger brother of Lord Telurian. In his youth, he was one of the Empire’s most powerful men: he directed the Crown’s naval forces and commanded a corps of ten thousand marines and three hundred ships. He was called charismatic and active: he easily found friends and allies, generously distributed ranks and awards, fought in duels and led ships in a storm - "He was loved." (In the early media, he ran the seaports of Crown Lands, the Marine Corps was fifteen thousand, and Manson himself was "quite fooled by himself.") ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]) Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark recalls him differently: he commanded the fleet not as the brother of the lord, but as a fearless sailor and a brilliant admiral. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) His flagship was called The Wind of Elena. Once in a Thousand Shards Manson in a severe storm led the ship to the shore and landed without losing a single person. On another occasion, it rose from the Hanai Delta to the capital itself against the wind and current. On 5 May 1744, he took the vassal oath to Tellurian, and on 8 April 1746, he was sworn in as a military officer in the Imperial Navy. At the age of twenty-eight, he married Lady Karen, daughter of Duke Jonas Lighthart, ruler of the then richest [[nadezhda|Hope]], and gave his friends positions in the court and in the navy. Tellurian, who jealously held the balance between the Great Houses (“The weakness of the lords leads to the might of the Crown”), publicly chastised his brother at Ingrid and Adrian; Manson audaciously replied, "The wrong head wears the crown!" — and the next day left with the squadron on a year-long voyage with inspection of western ports and fortresses. When he returned, he staged a coup to become regent under young Adrian. (In the early media, he married at the age of twenty-seven, and fell into disgrace for trying to give the Lightharts out of the treasury a loan of half a million ephesas to a spark shop, for which the emperor expelled him from the capital for a year.) ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) The plot was revealed because of Captain Corvis: he tried to protect his suzerain Aiden Almer from death, and the duke reported everything to the emperor. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) For more on this, see the Jokes conspiracy. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) Twenty-five conspirators were ambushed in the palace. Duke Jonas Lighthart and his sons were executed, and Manson was deposed by the admirals in 1755 and sentenced to death. Tellurian replaced the execution with a cap of jester and an echiote tincture, which Manson was forced to drink four times a day.

According to the book by Ayden Almera, Manson was a man of strong character and struggled with stupidity for a long time. The courtiers saw him with his face broken into blood, then with his wrists gnashed, and the guards invariably answered: “He was separating himself.” Four years later he was broken. Then the lord allowed him no more to drink the echiota, and Manson began to be humiliated for every sip. It was a sight that gave the conspiracy its name. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], a six-year-old boy, saw him at court in his third year as an "echiot torture": then Manson "was still fighting" and shocked Erwin more than all the members of the imperial family. Adrian explained to Mira that Manson “did not want him dead”: all the conspirators confirmed at the trial that he wanted to become regent, but not to kill his nephew.

## At the court of Adrian

In 1774, Manson, a tortoise with a long, sharp beard, in a checkered suit and a three-horned cap with tambourines, sat on the floor at [[adrian|Adrian]]'s throne and shouted insults at guests. He was the first heir to the throne. He especially hated Ayden Almera. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) Adrian suggested that the fault was in the book, where the duke "described in too much detail the fall of Manson", or that Aiden had risen on the very day that Manson collapsed. [[minerva|Mira]] initially suspected a prankster of conspiracy, but then found him harmless and broken. Manson adored his nephew, and Adrian, in response, patted him on the back of the head and told Mira that the lord needed to learn to forgive. His favorite joke was “The cap and the crown are friendly!”

At the summer ball on June 2, 1774, the joker, sitting on the floor at Adrian's chair, pointed his finger at the guests who came up with the greeting. Aiden Almera, he shouted: “Stealing! Stealing-aah!.. Lord, Lord, do not let the serpent to the throne!", and then listed the dishes, shouting the Duke of Labelin. When a woman named Gloria Northwood sat down in front of the throne, Manson aimed her finger at her: “You are...you are!...” and the girl cooled, waiting for exposure, but the joker plugged his mouth with the tip of his beard. On June 19, in the garden gazebo, where Mira was the only woman among the courtiers of Adrian, the jester destroyed the awkward pause by shouting: “Smart girl! I love smart girls!” — and Mira then repeated his words to her friend.

Manson’s world perceived in a special way: words and feelings were his colors, sounds and images, lies were smoke or buzzing. For fifteen years he had difficulty giving long phrases, and he could not keep more than two objects in his mind at once. In the chapters of "Kolpak" it is clear that the echota ceased to act on it seven years ago. He understood this when someone jokingly replaced tincture with wine and there was no difference. Since then, he has been carrying regular water in a vial and drinking from it to appear invisible. With the adjutant Forlemaeus, assigned to him by the old emperor, he did not speak for years because of a long-standing vow of silence.

In September 1774, while Adrian was fencing with the guards and listening to reports of rebellion, Manson painted on the floor of the horse. He warned the Lord: “O mighty enemy, Lord! Do not belittle!” — and at the word “west” he shouted: “Ugrroza. Compressed spring. Auntie. It's boiling resin!" Soon, the messenger of Reis cut the throat of [[graf-rantigar|Count Rantigar]] right in the throne room. After the exposure of Sybil Northwood, Adrian was left alone with the joke and asked why he was betrayed by everyone he believed. Manson saw a peasant harnessed in a cart instead of a horse, and shouted: "Heavy! .. It's very hard..." That same evening, his accidental "There and wrong!" led Adrian to plan to move troops by sea and attract the Shimmer allies in the war against [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]].

Trriumph!" After the victory on 2 December 1774, the Day of the Descent, a festive Melorange welcomed the army on 3 December; the procession “resembled a triumph.” The little girl handed the joke to the doll, and he laughed, wiping away his tears. Twice in his life he has received such letters. The dust didn't let Adrian know. On the train, Manson walked around the cabin with a knife, cut paper and, obeying not thoughts, but intuition, which Dust could not track, splashed his nephew with tea instead of a blow. At night, as a punishment, Dust made him gnaw his own forearm. Then she turned to his intuition and saw a monster ahead on the rails. At dawn on December 22, Dust lifted the old personality from his memory, and Manson, in the powerful voice of the emperor's uncle, got him to be led to Adrian. He talked about the danger on the river, but Adrian did not look. Captain Grace noticed an armed detachment on the river and the bridge over the Back collapsed. ([[bek-most|Beck]])

After the crash, Manson with a broken rib freed Adrian from under the body of the guard, helped him get out of the burning car and pulled a spark dagger from the dead guard. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) When the crossbowmen at the shallows opened fire, Adrian pulled him to the river, and then Manson whispered “I’m sorry, Lord...” and in front of the shooters hit him with a dagger in the back. Hugging the bloated body, he howled until the wagon collapsed into the icy water. The Empire considered Manson a murderer.

In April 1775, Erwin, questioning Earl Erroback about the "witnesses" of the crash, noticed that the jester was never found alive or dead, although he, apparently, had to sob over the body of his nephew; this led Erwin to the idea that Adrian could be alive. ([[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]])

Lord Douglas Litland recalled that after the coronation of Adrian Aiden Almer, he said: “This scum should not swear an oath!” - and Adrian did not take a vassal oath from the jester. Manson laughed and joked about the murder charge, but only got angry when the court made him look like a man: Vimas showed that the joker once kissed him. Erwin later explained this "disgusting case" by the fact that the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]] experienced Dust on Manson at the ball. Then Adrian, having pondered the night, told him to go to Darkwater and tell Lady in Darkness everything, and he went "about life to reflect" and find someone; therefore, Lady in Darkness and Francis-Ilian had long known that the Lord was alive. When, after this confession, one of the lords cried out that with the deposition of Adrian, the power passed by inheritance to Lord Manson, the jester only answered "Gy-gy" and called out with tambourines. The court found the joker innocent.

## Joke Minerva

Justified Manson hid in the palace curtains and asked Minerva, "Take me for a joke, huh? I'll cheer you." In mid-June 1775 she agreed. Becca objected that the brother of the lord was already humiliated enough, but Mira gave three arguments: he himself asked, the court jester is the same attribute of the emperor as the crown and Hilt, and there is nowhere else for him to go. At first, Manson enthusiastically avenged the court for years of humiliation. Then he began to ridicule every decision of the mistress herself - from the purchase of the ugly dog Brunhilda to the duties of Litland. When Mira told him to stop, he clarified: “Stop today or always?” She did not dare to say “always” and doomed herself to daily lessons. The jester taught her paradoxes and demanded that she learn to choose:
> “Any choice will be wrong until you learn to choose.”

Soon his wife [[karen|Karen]] was brought to the court, which Telurian kept for almost twenty years in a hospital on the island of Faraday Riley. ([[ostrov-faradeya-rajli|Faraday-Riley Island]]) Manson greeted her with a joke, as if they were meeting for the first time, and led her to "show the palace." When Adrian moved to the capital, the jester advised Minerva to give up: “Kolpak, crown, smart girl!” Karen, on the other hand, advised me to run.

## At the court of the returning Adrian

In the summer of 1775, when Minerva learned that Adrian was alive and going to the capital, Manson shouted out loud every day: “The Lord is coming back! The Emperor is coming! The cap and crown are friendly!" Before Minerva left for the First Winter, she paid him the jester's salary, but Manson, along with Karen, remained in Faunterra. In August, the regiments of Adrian entered the city without a fight, and the jester, pushing the courtiers, broke through to the lord; when the shavang stuck a knife in his stomach, Manson spat on the blade. Adrian hugged him as an old friend and introduced him to his fiancée [[magda-labelin|Magda]]: "Lord Manson of the Yangmei family, my uncle, friend and associate." When First Secretary Amber read out Minerva's decree appointing Adrian as mayor of Faunterra "in the order of correctional labor", the jester laughed to tears. Adrian immediately recalled that his uncle appeared in an old naval uniform. He told Manson to strip naked, go back to his quarters, and leave only in the costume of a jester. Karen did not forgive this humiliation and called her husband to go to Leonhard, but Manson dismissed: “I made fun of him — he answered. In the order of things.” And when Adrian promised to tell the "friend" about the miracle that opened to him at the bottomless Failure, the joke caught fire and no longer wanted to hear about leaving: "Adrian is a reformer!.. And then the gods themselves approved of the reforms!”

At the end of September, Adrian at Manson interrogated Karen where [[navi|Navi]] had gone and ordered [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]], bound, to be brought to the gazebo. The jester tried to intercede for his wife (“Lord, why are you like this!”), and when Adrian let her go, he took Karen away “to drink from her nerves.” In October, on a train south, Karen finally told her husband that Navi was a god, and Manson explained to her for the first time why he had once concealed a plot against Telurian: "Whose problem is it if the brother is a goat? Only my own.” In [[soltaun|Soltown]], Francis-Ilianus met them on the platform, and Manson clasped in the arms of his former defender: “You owe me!”; the couple rode in the same carriage with the king. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]])

In the autumn, on the way to the north, Adrian sat down with him to play dice on the balcony of the town hall in Doyle. He reproached his uncle that he had taught Minerva "audacity and willfulness." Manson replied that he only gave her firmness: “Why give a choice to the Yankees? .. Lord, I do not understand the question.” Adrian demanded to swear to Karen's head that she was not plotting anything against him. Then Manson asked if Adrian knew Karen was alive. He replied, “I didn’t know,” and the jester saw bumblebees flying out of the mouth of the lord. From the day of the crash, Manson did not distort the words: Ulyana Dust saved him from this disease, but now she has returned, and the habit of seeing words with pictures. By bumblebees, he thought Adrian lied to him about Karen.

In Main, Adrian discovered to Manson and Magde the plan of the Great Tree. Asked what he wanted, the joker replied: “Living with Karen is very long. “Bringing and raising children.” He wanted to be an admiral again. Adrian handed him two teaspoons — future items that will stop aging and allow Karen to give birth — and a “shipyard” kettle for the flagship. When [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] was transferred to the balcony, Manson sneezed in his face, ridiculed his non-removable armor, and called him a snail: "Outside is the shell, inside is the slime!" Then the jester, together with Magda, fell on the lord for the alliance with the Puppeteer, who sent Dust to him, "that I might stab you in the back."

On 2 December 1775, the day of the Battle of the First Winter, the court observed a battle across the Bird without the Plot at Main Castle. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]; [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) Manson bet on the Origins a hundred ephes from the money of Francis-Ilianus. In the evening, Adrian arrested Karen for treason, and before her arrest, she gave her husband the pill "Black Mary". Shavan Gurlach forcefully infused Manson into the mouth of a real echiot. Half the night, Manson tried to understand who Karen had ordered to kill: herself, her, or the lord. He decided to throw out the poison, but then realized what he really needed.

On December 3, Adrian, through the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]], demanded the Origins surrender and threatened to destroy their Objects. The jester hung on his sleeve, begging for echiotes, and, having received the third dose in a day, fought in a chair and drooled, dragging time. When Adrian ordered [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]], the bearer of the firstblood, to destroy the Hand of the Znaharka, Johan fell dead: it was a pill of Black Mary, the poison of "hourly action". ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) [[magda-labelin|Magda]] realized that this was a joke, which even risked itself by drinking extra echo. She took the blame, forced Adrian to seek peace, and demanded the release of Manson's wife. Manson went with Adrian to negotiate in the First Winter. In the final scene, he shouted to Minerva that she would be "the last fool" if she killed [[navi|Navi]] and deprived humans of the ships of the gods.

## Relationships

### Adrian
Manson loved his nephew from his infancy and was loyal to him even after twenty years of jesting. Adrian, according to the jester, “never lied” to him, and Manson used to rely on his words, “as if on the stones of the pavement.” The harder it was for him to hit Beck and lie about Karen. Manson poisoned with his hand the man through whom Adrian wanted to conquer the First Winter.

### Karen
According to Lady Karen's notes in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, before the wedding, Manson explained to her in his own way: on the eve of Sophia's days, he sent a black feather with the ship's monkey Polundra, a sign of jealousy or separation, and when she rushed to his ship in anger, he laughed: "I knew you would buy it." ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) The two chapters were written by Manson. In them, among other things, he remembers his old man: he conceived a “romantic abduction” of his beloved, but the young lady poked the “kidnapper” with a spark dagger, the spark went through him into the horse, and everything ended with a stunned horse, a burned guard and the girl’s teeth knocked out. In old age, according to Karen, Manson plays stratagems with Captain [[uitmor|Whitmore]].
For twenty years, Manson thought his wife was dead. In Doyle, he confessed to Adrian that he could not name his desire at Melorange, and now knows him: "I want to be with her for the rest of my days." According to Karen, she “loves” him in a sea uniform. When Manson sent her on a train to safe Leonhard, she got off at the next station and returned to him.

### Forlemay
A nobleman and guard, Forlemaeus was assigned to Manson at Tellurian to serve him food. He read the letters of the jester, dressed and fed him, each time saying "Your porridge, Kolpak", and over the years he became an aide-de-camp. He survived the crash and testified in court. In The Shadow of the Great Tree, he still serves under Manson and Karen.

## Personality

Manson is rude, impertinent, capricious, and sarcastic. Under the mask of a jester hides the mind of a former naval commander and deep devotion to Adrian and his wife. He blamed himself for not being able to overpower Dust: in court, he pleaded guilty to precisely this. Erwin saw in him a charisma that lost its calculus. Minerva is the teacher who made her choose herself.
