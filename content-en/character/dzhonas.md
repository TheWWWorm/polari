## In "Only One Star"
When the Duke of [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]] fled from Faunterra, the protector questioned his servants. Among them was "Jonas Holly Martin, Duke of Aiden's coachman". The gray coachman was silent under Sammy's truncheon, spitting at him and cursing him. [[mark-voron|Mark]], the Crown Raven, talked to him about the children. He guessed that Jonas had children "before", and then translated the conversation to Lady [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], the only daughter of his master. Defending her, the coachman shouted that her lordship "knew nothing, participated in nothing." He said that he always drove her to meetings and heard her crying in a carriage once when she was “offended by one”. The real conspirator Jonas called the "red bitch", the daughter of the Bear: she allegedly blackmailed the Duke to get rid of Labelin with his hands. He also talked about where Ayden went after the games: to his main “whisper” Ushastoy, and then to the captain of the guard Dermot. Mark, realizing that it was about Gloria, told the interrogator to forget what he had heard forever.

## In "Fantasies"
### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the autumn of 1776, an old man Jonas came to Faunterra with a nearly white beard, “full of stubborn force.” To Major Hand Doju he said, "I'm a coachman." ([[ruka-dodzh|Hand Dodge]]) His companions were the one-eyed [[khamfri|Humphrey]], the mute [[sofi|Sophie]] and the fortune teller Murriel. By all accounts, this is the same Jonas Holly Martin: the name, craft and age coincide, and the narrator directly says that Humphrey, Jonas and Murriel served as "deceased seigneurs - Lord Aiden Almera and Lady Alanis."

Jonas "liked to say things that couldn't be argued with" and became the "voice" of the squad. The cry of "Ad-ri-an" he spoke calmly, "as a fact." In the evening, at the latrines, he trained and once uttered the cry "wrong, shaky, with the question: 'Ad-ri-an... died?' When Minerva answered the crowd’s questions about peace, prices and taxes, it was Jonas, “the gang’s best orator,” who raised him with a Glove a yard above the pavement. When Humphrey held Adrian’s son in his arms, Jonas loudly repeated to people: “Healthy baby! It will live a long time!” On the first day of the meeting, the loud “grandfather like a coachman” recounted Adrian’s speech to those who did not hear it.

During the assault on the Chamber, Jonas stood next to Adrian. When he fell in convulsions, the coachman shouted: “Adrian ordered to shoot with a Feather! Mother Yangmei punished him!” The Golden Grive burned Sophie's Feather, and Humphrey's cheese struck the shavana. Then Jonas “powerfully” shouted: “Adrian is dead! Sophie's dead! The finger is dead!” From this scream, the assault stopped. Together with Humphrey and Murriel, he took Sophie's body and disappeared. The Origin people searched unsuccessfully for them to reward.
