## Slave Trader
For the first time, Shavan Groza appears in the interlude "[[nebesnyj-korabl-i-devushka|The Skyship and the Girl]]". In the summer of 1774, his schooner stood at the port of Laema, and he sold captives at auction in the tea room. ([[lajem|Laem]]) Shimmerian matchmakers knew him as one of the best suppliers of girls, known for “military luck and excellent sense of smell on the althess”: he allegedly took prisoners only those who in the South are “cut off with their hands”. The sleazy matchmaker Onoriko-Meysor initially referred to him as a "disgusting savage" with a "rotten" product. ([[onoriko|Onoriko-Maysor]])

The storm was defiantly gradual and did not adapt to the manners of the south. He was wearing a leather sleeveless jacket with sheets, rough leather pants and tall sharp-nosed boots with spurs. A wide belt glittered with silver patterns in honor of his victories, a curved sword and a long dagger hung on his belt. He said: “I’m not used to talking. Look with your own eyes. Goods are good. I don’t sell shit.”

Thunderstorm brought from the West forty captives, by the last evening of trading there were six. He asked for two hundred and fifty ephes for a litland Melana and two hundred for a thirty-year-old Eliya and did not yield in price. Eliya, she said, was captured when Shavan killed her husband. Thunder, wanting to show the goods, asked if he beat her with whips; Elia said no, because she is a woman and remembers it well. When the unruly Nisa tipped the jug over Timeret's buyer's head, Thunder carried her back by ear and promised a dozen lashes. ([[niza|Niza]]) According to Onoriko, one thing was waiting for the unsold proud woman: a mile from the shore, she would be thrown overboard. The merchant "Khoram", that is, [[kharmon|Harmon]], still bought Niza. Groza warned that she would scratch the buyer's eyes, and threw: "Pay for an empty place." Later, Nisa said that it was Ganta Thunderstorm that took her prisoner and sold her into slavery.

## Under Moran
In [[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]], Ganta Thunderstorm is Moran's right hand of Steppe Fire under [[melorandzh|Melorange]]. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) Moran instructed him to catch Litland scouts, to put around the camp a ring of dozers for five or ten miles, to find a "birdman" and a master at siege machines, and to build stones from wood brought from the jungle. When Neymir and [[chara|Chara]], deciding that the leader had betrayed them, mutilated Moran, Groza was the first to shout: “Treasoners! ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) Kill the scoundrels!” — and with a dozen riders rushed in pursuit. The mutilated Moran still held the horde in submission: after the siege was lifted from Melorange, he took it to the West, and by the end of March 1775, messengers of the counties Reis and Holliwell arrived in the capital with the news of a successful retreat from Litland. In [[yanmejskaya-okhota|the Yanmei Hunt]], the Shimmerian who was looking for Nisa called Groza one of the largest suppliers of captives and said that he should look for him under the walls of Melorange.

## Ambassador Reisa
### The Marengo test
In the spring of 1775, the Thunderstorm with three dozen riders arrived at [[minerva|Minerva]] as a representative of Reis County. When the Empress left Faunterra in early May and went to [[marengo|Marengo]], the shavana were among her guests. On 5 May, the day after her arrival, she received the ambassadors of the three steppe counties there, and they all wished to harm Origin: the giant Sormah pounded his fist on the table and demanded that the "wolves" be driven out of Faunterra, and the rest of the Shavans supported him throughout the throat. ([[sormakh|Sormakh]]) On the evening of May 6, when Erwin arrived in Marengo, the empress gave Erwin an ordeal: she promised the Shavans that one of them would get a chance to kill the Duke, and allowed them to attack him at night in the garden. ([[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]) Groza introduced himself as "Moran's whip" and boasted that he had killed seventeen warriors, of whom three were kairs. The first to go into battle was his giant Sormach, whose father and brothers were killed by the Northerners. Erwin cut off his hand, but spared it, noticing that he had not yet killed a single shavana. The storm was no longer laughing. He said he would kill a northerner "only for glory", but upon seeing the spinning Voice of Winter, he turned back and took up throwing daggers. Then the Empress came out of the shadow and reminded that the chance promised one, not two. Ganta reluctantly hid the knives and bent over the wounded, and Minerva sent Shavans of guards and a doctor. She explained to Erwin that she had staged the attack to allow him to win, and that Ambassador Reiss was still alive. On the evening of 7 May, Groza and his horsemen returned with the imperial court by train to Faunterra.

### In the Chamber
In the House of Thunder represented Reis. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) When the lords were about to approve of the rebellion of Origin against the tyrant, he demanded the same glory for the Steppe: the horsemen also fought against Adrian and even crossed swords with him himself. To the indignation of Lord Litland, he replied that the Litlands had killed the ambassadors and appropriated the crossings of Holliwell:
> “Is there a law to cut ambassadors? Or take what belongs to everyone?”

He was supported by the Northwoods and Halliwell. The chamber secretary put the issue of Shavans to a separate vote four days later, and the Westerners did not notice the catch: so the chamber retained the opportunity to condemn their rebellion.

### Refusal by Minerva
On June 1, 1775, Minerva invited Groza to tea and proposed an alliance against Origin, recalling the two-century raids of the northerners on the Steppe. Groza gave her Moran's order: to teach the capital's lords to reckon with the Shavans. Then he refused. The empress had already offered him such an alliance in Marengo, and then “did not depart from Origin” for a month. In addition, the day before she abdicated in favor of Adrian, who fought with Steppe. He said, “You’re slick and slick, whatever you offer, then you take it.”

## With Erwin Origin
### Contract
On the same day, June 1, 1775, Erwin met with Groza, the day before he learned about the death of his sister. Ganta began the conversation by saying, “I heard your sister was killed.” Erwin himself poured him wine and proposed an alliance against [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. According to him, the crippled and ceded Minerve Moran loses the respect of the hordes, and a new glorious war with rich prey will return him to power. The horde will get [[uejmar|Waymar]] and the Shayland bank vaults for plunder, and Erwin will outrun [[paul|Paul]], the messenger of the Puppeteer.

Groza made the condition: first to see Erwin fighting, and if he overcame the archpriest [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] in a month - "faster than the ganta Darian" - to bring a horde to him. In response to objections, Groza clicked with two fingers on his chin: it was an "extreme condition". Erwin thought of arresting the stubborn man and finding an ambassador more accommodating, but realized that the massacre of the Lord Representative of Reis and Moran's right hand would mean war with Steppe. Groza explained that the death of the horde under the Rings would be on his conscience, so he could not trust the northerner blindly. Ganta shook Erwin's hand and wished his sister farewell to the Wanderers' Horde.

### The Almeran Campaign
The storm accompanied the northerners on their march through Almera. He caught up with them when the Kairs took over the first beachhead in [[erroubek|Arrowback]] County, and complained that he had not gone camping to drink beer with seeds, but had immediately sent a shavana for beer. Together with his mother Cordelia and father David, he came to the army during one of the battles, chased the fleeing knights, laid down two and, without a shadow of embarrassment, took a trophy spark self-fire. When Erwin was about to negotiate at Arrowback Castle, Groza mockingly clarified whether he was riding under the catapults. The narrator admits that of all the escorts, only his horse was fast enough to escape. Under Fliss, Kurt [[kurt-ajsvind|Icewind]] suggested killing prisoners from the suburbs until the city opened the gates. Groza supported him, but offered to kill only a hundred, and sell the rest in Shimmery; Mother Cordelia sharply objected. When Captain Gordon Sue decided to take up defenses in the town of Breakdown, the Thunderbolt clicked his finger on his forehead, which means: “I hear the speech of a donkey, who lost his brains.”

On June 19, when Gallard raised a dazzlingly shining staff over the army, Groza grumbled, saying: “Agata helps us badly: she did not give us items, she did not sleep enough.” When the enemy cavalry completed the round, he strained: “The bald tails. I regret that we have nowhere to run!”

In the battle for the town of Oblivion, which was fired with incendiary shells from the walls of Fliss, Groza committed, according to the narrator, a feat. Fearing that the horses would go mad with the fire, he jumped on the cart that blocked the exit, giggled and led the herd. Several hundred horses flew out into the field with an avalanche and swept away the knights of the arch, and after them they were struck by the northerners. However, this invention cost the army six hundred horses, mostly draughts, and Erwin was left without a wagon.

At the council after the battle, Groza admitted that the deal was not fulfilled - Almera was not subdued. But Erwin, he said, "fought well", and the gaunt considered him a worthy ally. He demanded all the money from the Shayland banks and Waymar to plunder, and then all the property of Shayland. Erwin confirmed the promise to give the gold banks, but for the integrity of Waymar did not vouche: the landing of his father will leave much from the city. In return, he offered several items by Vittor Shayland, and their exact number was left for discussion at the end of the war. Groza insisted that the alliance in the Steppe was made personally, "the chief with the chief", and therefore would go to Rei-Roy only with Origin.

### The Road to Rei-Roy
In mid-June, [[minerva|Minerva]] learned that Origin had moved west, taking Ganta Thunderstorm with him: the Duke had found an ambassador capable of persuading Moran, and this ambassador had to outrun the people of the Puppeteer. After the victory at Fliss, at the end of June 1775, Groza sailed with Erwin up Holliwell and then led the kairs through the Steppe. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) Among the Kairs, there were rumors that negotiations were just a cunning trick: to enter Rei-Roy, to cut Moran and put Groz in his place, "which is almost ours." The Northern song "We are going west" for some reason did not make him happy, and he blatantly repeated her words. At the river Roydana, he ordered the duke to water her horse: this is what anyone who goes to Rei-Roy in peace does. He told the legend of the gauntlet of Royhar the Lazy Will, which Rei-Roy built for the First Horse War, "The Place of the Lazy". Interrogating prisoners of old men, he himself translated their stories about Gnoi-gant and considered them a "donkey's roar". In his opinion, it was someone from the gant, probably Onday, who decided to push Moran. Juhan Reis, son of Damir and descendant of Royhar, he called a glorious and reserved young man, and the gaunt Cort - a cunning, "beautiful fox" like Henry Farway. When Jemis wanted to make his way to his father, the Thunder warned that around the horde for many miles of travel and he will die. Seeing the statue of the Flaming Bull, the Thunder rushed and bowed to her to the ground. He was the first to be alarmed when he saw the locked gate: it had not been closed for a century and a half.

### Death
When a hundred horsemen flew out of the gates of Rei-Roy, led by a finger-bearer, and the second gunman beat fire from the wall, a retreat would mean the death of the detachment. Thunderstorm first rushed to meet the enemy:
> "Follow me, you magicians! Follow me, you sons of bitches!”

The fire of the Perst burned it with the horse: the charred skeleton of the rider mixed with the bones of the horse. However, the commanders of the kairs followed his example, crushed the chiselling number and saved the Duke. In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], it is said that in a moment of despair, the bravery of one fighter — “the Shavan, the slave trader, and the former enemy” — saved the entire army. Later, Erwin told the captive pincer, a servant of the Gaunt Gaunt, that he had killed the Thunderstorm, and the Gaunt had managed to become a friend of the Duke and had to convince the Steppe to conclude an alliance. Passing by the Rusty Giants, Erwin thought that from the Thunder, as from Alanis, Kurt Icewind and the other dead, there were only "monuments that are not memorable to anyone alive." The captive riders of Ondea's gauntlet Erwin recalled that he had forgiven Groze's attempt on his life and had come under Rei-Roy to offer peace.

In Fantasia, a possible future, Erwin, recalling his victories, recalled Ganta Thunderstorm along with Arthur the Twin, Baron Icewind and widow Cher. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]])

## Personality and Appearance
Thunderstorm has a round face, slanted eyes and a shaved back of his head. At the slightest smile, he begins to look like a cunning trickster. He says, stretching whistling sounds ("sun-garden", "s-said"). According to the steppe habit, he rubs and twists a long mustache, and wanting to speak at the council, itches his neck. He is rude, greedy for prey, but brave and in his own way honest steppe man, proud of his brilliance. Erwin was surprised when the gauntlet "expressed much more intelligence" than he suspected. Thunderstorm was both a warrior and a trader and understood the value of business conversations.
