**Brother Marius** is a doctor of the monastery of Marek and Simeon, who impoverished the monastery in the north of Almera, near the village of Road Pillar. ([[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]]) [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] remembered him as “a very knowledgeable man with a huge arsenal of drugs and potions”: last winter, he served two weeks in a monastery for food and cared for the sick in a hospital. Marius is sharp and speaks without consolation, but he saved the life of [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]].

## In "Only One Star"
### The Past
Marius may have seemed old: thin wrinkled face, head "white as cabbage." Joaquin knew he was “almost forty.” For the last six years he lived in the monastery, and before that he was a regimental healer in [[darkvoter|Darkwater]] under the banners of Lady in Darkness. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) It was said that he took everything and only one in four died under his knife. One of these four was the noble son of Lady in Darkness. His armor-bearer wanted to kill the doctor, but he lowered his sword with the words: “Your conscience will gnaw more painfully than steel.” The narrator adds: “He seems to be right.”

### Treatment of Alanis (September 1774)
In mid-September, Joaquin brought Alanis to the monastery, which did not recover for a day and a half. Her facial wound, sustained in the Evergard fire, festered. ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) Marius examined the wound and lashed out at Joaquin: “Why so long? “You wanted to kill her?” He explained that the lady herself refused to go to the doctor. Marius replied, "The open burn... causes ferocious pain and fever. A person in this state is unable to think soberly. You should have thought about it instead.”

He brought the patient to a sense of tincture and checked her mind: he asked for a name, and when she refused to call, he ordered to list Pramatery. The drops Theophilus gave her a doctor in Flathill, he called a good drug, but said that they were already late: rot poisoned the blood. When asked if he would die, he said, “I don’t know.” Before treatment, he asked: “Tell me... do you have a reason to live?” Alanis refused to sleep during the procedure. Then Marius showed her a pot of maggots, which was supposed to clean the wound of dead flesh. She agreed to sleep and told me to put Joaquin out.

A few days on all questions Joaquin Marius answered with one word: “Fights”. Finally he called the guy and said, "Milady is alive... Not yet... She's alive. The blood was cleared, the fever was gone.” Joaquin sat down, and the brother doctor poured him wine.

### Escape Alanis
After Joaquin left, Alanis remained in the hospital for another week. Every morning she demanded an escort, clothes and a horse, and Marius silently offered her a mirror. As she grew stronger, he warned that the abbot had been summoned by the priory of Gallard Almer and by his return she would "better disappear." ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) Alanis was surprised that he recognized her. “I didn’t set such a goal for myself...,” the doctor brother shrugged his shoulders. - But your agate cheekbones are hard to hide. And I see that you used to be a very beautiful woman.” He couldn't take it further than the Road Pillar: "I took a vow of obedience." At her request, he got her the outfit of an Emilian nun. Saying goodbye, Alanis promised: “Do not be afraid, you will not be left without a reward. I will count on you.”

### After
In December 1774, in the Palace of Pen and Sword, Joaquin looks at Alanis' mutilated cheek and thinks, "Good, doctor Marius somehow slammed a hole, otherwise the duke would have seen the food inside the Duchess's mouth."
