## Who She Is

**Alice Cavendish** is a physics student at the University of [[faunterra|Faunterra]] and an assistant to Professor [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]]. Adrian's secretary describes her as a "boyish blonde" in a gray dress, "sort of strict, but with a note of coquetry." Her favorite subject is the philosophy of the universe. Ollie took her as an assistant during the Northern Flash instead of the banished chatty Felix because Alice was indifferent to the military news. She was an excellent assistant: she quickly understood what was needed, and went to work with her head. Only occasionally did she tell the professor about the palace: “Like any girl of Faunterra, she was not indifferent to the Dynasty.”

Alice was collecting the professor's instruments. Her "soft fingers" soldered circuits and manually wound the reels of the recording apparatus. She held his shows in front of the nobles. When the money for the job ran out, Alice made her "possible contribution" to it. Soon she was left completely without money: the last agate she spent two days before meeting with the Labelins, the day before she had dinner with a piece of cheese, “stolen from a neighbor on campus”, and that day she took a cake from an unrequited student in love with her.

Alice appears only in [[fantazii|Fantasies]]: in the story "The Device" and in the story of Somerset Flame in the story "Annoying Little Things".

## In "Fantasies"

### "The Device"
The professor once said that “the conflict of economics with physics has escalated to the limit.” Alice understood the hint and without his knowledge gave an article in the "Journal of Science" about the recording apparatus. The article attracted [[adrian|Adrian]]. In the town hall, Alice held a show, rescued the mentor, "translating" his academic language, and interrupted him when he almost spoke about his hopes for Minerva. Adrian's suspicious secretary asked for surveillance.

At a meeting with [[moris-labelin|Maurice]] and Magda, Labelin Alice, hearing of ten thousand Hilt, began to stutter from excitement and tried to bargain twelve. ([[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]) She took Maurice's "donation", which the professor did not touch, and on the way everything counted the banknotes: "the counting of paper money is the most soothing occupation in the world." Ollie reproached her that now two powerful people set them opposite tasks. Alice countered that the theft of drawings gives them freedom of choice: any of the buyers can be refused, citing theft. She jokingly called the professor to the chocolate parlor, where Minerva, Erwin Origin and [[karen|Karen]] Lighthart once visited. Ollie did not understand that behind the humorous tone she hides the hope of one day calling him to a cafe, and maybe to the theater.

During the pogrom committed by Adrian's agents, Alice did not participate in the fight, but the students still showered her with compliments, and someone even confessed their love for her. At a show for the highest ranks of the Church, she dropped her cylinder with embarrassment. King Francis-Ilianus presented it and found in it the refinement of the Foremother Emilia. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) For the transaction, Alice calculated the price of the invention: the cost, multiplied by four, “the coefficient of our talent.” It was at this price that the king bought the device.

When Ollie decided to flee to Minerva in the First Winter, Alice was gripped by the worst of her fears. She was sure that in the North the professor would find another assistant who would have the courage to invite him to a cafe and a theater. Alice begged him to stay and said that "only then did she do it all." Ollie understood this as a confession to the theft of a notebook. Alice explained that she only meant the article: she wanted to find patrons in Faunterra so that the professor would not have to turn to Minerva. But he said she was “immensely disappointing” and promised to create a new scheme “without your help.” Alice ran away to the lecture in tears. In the secretary, she left the professor a gift, which she managed to say, apparently, a couple of tickets to the mystery of “Carrog and Liola”, at the premiere of which he once did not get. ([[liola-i-karrog|Liola and Karrog]]) When a note from Eugene’s doorman revealed that he had taken the notebook, the professor rushed to look for Alice in all audiences.

### "The annoying little things"
In a story that Somerset Flame told [[karen|Karen]] and [[menson|Manson]] in Aleridan, Alice is named mysteriously, "E." When Ollie missed the lectures, they were led by her. For the first time, she comforted disappointed listeners and turned Somerset's cry "It's a hoax!" into an experience about sound waves of different frequencies. Somerset decided that he had met his true love. He placed bouquets under her door, wrote anonymous confessions and guarded her in bakeries, but E. "continued to serve the only lady: sacred physics." It was popular among students. She was usually taken home in a sleigh by Professor Ollie, and the bearded gatekeeper warned Somerset, "It's not about you." Somerset once took her to campus. She laughed a lot and said she was on the verge of discovery.

Then came “Lords, Bishops, Officers of the Protectorate.” After the pogrom, Somerset knelt down and confessed his love for her, but everyone took it as a joke. The day the professor accused her of theft, E. came to the lecture, sat down next to Somerset and cried silently. He wrote her a page of comfort, and she replied with a note: “Sir, do not build illusions. I love the other and am devoted to him.” Then Ollie walked into the audience and asked, "Is E.K. here?" Alice immediately jumped up and ran to him.

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the spring of 1776, Alice cheered for Adrian, the "fan of science." Then the guys searched the laboratory, and on the streets began to beat up supporters of Minerva and Origin, and her sympathies changed. The last drop was the day when she and her student friends handed out leaflets and the one-eyed leader of the guys ([[khamfri|Humphrey]]) snatched them from her hands: “We are the people!” Since then, Alice has been for Minerva. She only got cold for political reasons, not romantic ones. In November, along with him, she was serving a two-axis device on the Chamber stage When Adrian announced an ultimatum, Amber noticed that the device had "stopped spinning", and Alice quickly changed the part. On the night of the siege, Amber and Countess Shayland invited her to spend the night in the ladies' hall. Ollie she urged to leave first: "I'm with all my heart on the side of Minerva. And you support Adrian.” Ollie ended up at the device himself.
