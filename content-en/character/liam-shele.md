## Biography
Viscount Liam Shelier [[adrian|Adrian]] appointed him Minister of Ways, that is, entrusted him with the introduction of scientific inventions: rail roads, spark shops and “wave” lines. ([[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]]) Shelier was the emperor's first aide in the cause of progress. He was in charge of the railways and bridges of the Lands of the Crown, as well as the branches of Faunterra - Aleridan and Faunterra - Heart of Light. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]])

### "A Doll on the Throne"
After the coronation, [[minerva|Minerva]] tried for several weeks to meet with the Minister of Railways. ([[koronatsiya-minervy|Minerva's Coronation]]) In the winter of 1775, he returned to the capital after a month's journey on the ruined roads, and Amber's secretary reported his return. ([[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]]) Shelier came to the theater in a gray road suit, with a glass in each hand, and in the intermission, drunk, made a scandal in front of Minerva and the yard. In gestures, he showed what had become of the roads: six bridges destroyed, nine stations burned, a hundred miles of wires stolen and sold. It took a hundred thousand to repair, and the treasurer allocated five. So Minerva found out that [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] had been appointed treasurer without her knowledge. Shelier then pulled Dreyfus Bourne out of the crowd and announced that he had bought the post of Minister of Taxes from the Lord Chancellor for a hundred and twenty thousand. ([[drejfus-born|Dreyfus Born]]) Next, he pointed to [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] and reminded that the lady-burgomaster of the capital was not appointed by Minerva. Alanis admitted that she herself told the guard to let in a drunken viscount, whom she had known for a long time. Shelier made a toast to the rulers: the Lord Chancellor would trample on any progress for the sake of former glory, and the empress sees nothing, “only the world.” Northern Colonel General [[stetkhem|Statham]] hit him in the stomach and handed him over to the Guards. Already being led away, Schelier shouted that in Almera, in the river, the body of the lord was found.

After the theatre, the cairas, led by [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], held Schelier hanging naked on chains all night, discussing what to do with him, but without orders did nothing. In the morning [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] appeared with a cup of coffee and ordered to release the "poor man": he did not cause harm, and to punish him, if he wanted, let the empress be. Shelier realized that this was intended: the unpunished offender in front of everyone proved the impotence of the mistress. Through [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]], he tried to beg for forgiveness, (in Leila's words, "like a beaten puppy in habits"). He finally came to Minerva to ask for his resignation. She forced him to give the full name and reminded him that the name of the family does not belong to him alone, and then issued a “sentence”:
> "I hereby order you to serve as Minister of the Ways."

She threatened that if he decided to run away, he would remember that in torture "Agata is a baby against Yangmei". Shellier straightened his shoulders slightly. He reported that six rail bridges had been destroyed (three in the South Road, two in the Crown, one in Almera), that the copper wires previously guarded by the spark itself had been pulled by the poor for tens of miles, and that the damage was about eighty thousand ephesas. It turned out that on the allocated in February five thousand, he did nothing, only drank and waited for the massacre. It was in this conversation that Minerva understood why a merchant benefits from expensive transportation of goods by train: the time of circulation of money.

In a letter to the Lord Chancellor, Minerva called Shelier a "badly-mannered drunkard and profane," but admitted that one thing he was right about was that the roads needed repair. Erwin later assured her that he did not incite the Viscount, who "acted on his own initiative." Later, Schelier reported that the funds allocated for repairs had been exhausted, and no line had ever entered service. When Prince Hector at court was surprised that the Crown was unaware of the siege of [[melorandzh|Melorange]], Shelier became the first of the ministers to justify that the letters were not lost in his office. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) Asked about Litland, he grabbed his head: a new war will finish the treasury, but if the Lord Chancellor goes camping, the capital will sigh freer. For General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis]]'s plan against the horde, the minister, as promised, provided the trains. About the peasant unrest in the Southern Way, he learned from the repair crews, but remained silent, “was afraid to disturb” the mistress once again.

At a secret gathering of associates, Minerva thanked Shelier for the "unexpected" help. With his helplessness, he, a Yankee like her, showed her the bottom on which to fall, and she gained strength to climb up. Then she exposed him. The theatrical antics shook her out of her slumber and forced her to take care of finances, he also opened her eyes to Bourne and, in addition, concealed the siege of Melorange. His subordinates worked for several weeks in the capital department of the "waves" under the pretext of setting up a new receiving machine and intercepted reports. Crying, Schelier admitted that he was afraid, as if the empress did not leave with the army in Litland. Minerva felt that he was too soft and emotional for the secret “player” to choose him as his tool.

### The “Yanmei Hunt”
In the spring of 1775, Erwin included the Minister of Paths in the list of Minerva's "troublemakers" whom he ordered to collect. At the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], Shelier was first among the prosecution witnesses who recalled the misdeeds of the jester. [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] then questioned him about the crash at Back. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) The minister repeated monotonously that the bridge collapsed due to rain water frozen in poorly fitted joints. He himself did not inspect the bridge and did not send engineers, but retold the conclusion of the engineers of [[erroubek|Arrowback]] County. The Prophet pointed out that there was no ice on Bake that day, that the bridge had stood for forty years, and that the Hampton Bridge had been the last to collapse, set on fire by Alexis' retreating troops. Shelier bit his nail, then his finger, and finally admitted that he had no evidence against the version of sabotage.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree
In August 1775, when [[adrian|Adrian]] returned to the capital, and Minerva with azure companies ran from it by train, Schelier remained in [[faunterra|Faunterra]] and, together with the Minister of the court, came to the lord. When asked where Minerva went, the Minister of the court sent Adrian to the Minister of ways. He reported that Minerva's train was going to Arden, but was stuck in Patrick's Chapel due to a break in the spark line. The breakdown, he said, was a happy accident, but he told him not to rush it, and Adrian praised him for it. In October 1775, Schelier, together with the Minister of Taxes Frank Morlin-Mei, who was exhausted on the road without information about the fees, reached the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], where [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]]'s battalion with the treasury and the second azure company returned on the same days. He told Minerva how Adrian accepted this breakdown at face value. Mira laughed cheerfully, and then set him the task: in two years to hold three rail branches from the First Winter, in the [[belomorye|Belomorye]], [[labelin|Labelin]] and the Bear Fang, in a year to stretch the line of “waves” to Faunterra and build a spark dam on Gemini. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]; [[bliznyashki|The Twins]]) Shelier lost the power of speech.

In the new capital, he became one of the five experienced officials on whom the Minerva apparatus rested, along with Amber, Morlin-May, Robert Origin and the local burgomaster. It was almost constant, so whining seemed to be its main function. Nevertheless, he found six engineers in the northern wilderness, launched design work and presented to the lady estimates for the construction of “wave” lines, huge, but acceptable. Minerva joked:
> “In my new capital, whining is allowed only to one person: Minister Liam Schellier.”

### In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, a possible future, Schelier still serves as minister at Minerva's court in the First Winter in 1776. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) At the celebration in honor of her twentieth birthday, Shelier, number seven, made a toast to the word “barrel”: let the treasury of the Empire under her leadership be the same dense, dense and pleasing look.

## Personality and Appearance
Skinny, angular and vain. Filling him with a cup at a meeting of associates, Minerva noted painful joint nodes on the bony trembling fingers. Soft, emotional, cowardly, prone to drunkenness, self-humiliation and complaints, but committed to the cause of progress begun by Adrian. Minerva for a long time did not understand how Adrian could entrust the “puppy” with almost the most expensive, and wondered whether Shelier was like this before or broke only now.
