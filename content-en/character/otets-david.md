## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

**A meeting in the Izlyuchin.** Father David, an old friend of the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]], first appears on April 12, 1774 at St. Leonora's Cathedral in Izluchina. ([[izluchina|Riverbend]]) He sat down with Harmon in a blue conductor's cape. Harmon admitted that he met a great many people, but he spoke to David differently than to everyone else, and he always felt like a “stupid young man” next to him. David stopped for the night at the Izluchin abbot and in the morning was going to go to [[niar|Niar]], covered with the sizzling sea, to bring hope to the dying. ([[sizyj-mor|Blue-Grey Plague]]) Harmon urged him not to destroy himself, and David replied that “people need to believe and hope.” When asked about the divine remedy for the sea, he said that the Sacred Objects are silent: "I do not know of a person who is able to talk to them." That night, he noticed that those who want and dream are more alive. Harmon also remembered David’s earlier words that the Object, like a mirror, strengthens what is in the soul of the possessor.

**A confession.** In early July 1774, David again found Harmon in the same hotel in Izluchin. He said that the Maximian monastery was burned, and suddenly asked to give the Light Sphere. [[brat-lyudvig|Brother Ludwig]] showed him a fake letter from the Duke, and David recognized the handwriting of a friend. He confessed that he was an unnamed brotherhood, a "we" who had dedicated his life to learning the language of the Objects. To do this, you need to collect as many Items as possible: you can not learn to read one letter, and you will not understand blacksmithing by one nail. In the brotherhood, according to him, most of the soldiers and priests. There are several monasteries on its side, and they most often have to produce items in the same way as with Harmon. David fought against the violence, but most of the brothers did not support him. He warned that the monastery had been burned by other object hunters, "far less discerning in the means" who had been following Harmon from Soltown. He predicted a war that would "shake the Dynasty" and quoted Agatha:

> “The burden that everyone bears alone is the burden of choice. That is the right of the mortal, that is his curse.”

In farewell, David showed Harmon a vial of deadly poison and said, "I'm not the one you should be afraid of." Harmon, just in case, caused his vomiting and in the evening ran south.

## In "Only One Star"

In October 1774, shortly after Erwin executed twenty-five prisoners at Doyle's walls, a priest and an unknown woman came to see him. David, in his downed shoes, a dusty cloak and a hat that had lost shape, called himself a pilgrim from Almera. He said that with a group of cripples he went to the Crystal Mountains to the font of the Piece of Heaven, but met a girl who really wanted to see the Duke. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]) That girl was the disfigured [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]. On the way, David refused to sail without his poor companions, although the captains begged him. He stopped Alanis when she almost beat the pilgrim, and then helped her cry: “The bitterest thing in the world is the tears of a strong man.” Erwin checked the guest, asking for a prayer for the funeral, and David read three stanzas of "The Sad Ulyana's Guide" without hesitation. He refused to confess the duke: a priest below the rank of bishop does not have the right to do so. He said, "Forgive yourself, Your Grace." Erwin invited him to stay in the army, although David said that he was subordinate to Bishop Fliss. With the approval of Alanis, he became her confessor and remained with the northerners.

On the eve of the Battle of Labelin, David led Erwin to the ruins of the monastery of the Forefather Maximian twenty miles from the city. He said that the monastery was burned this summer because of the small Object, and the brethren, according to rumors, bought the Objects themselves: the monks try to help people, and the Objects are a “good tool”. When asked about the Light Sphere, he replied "No, My Lord" after a strange pause, and Erwin suspected a lie. David promised to pray for Erwin because the victorious Adrian would have drowned the North and the West in blood.

In the winter campaign to the capital, David said that he served as a regimental priest, and then found a patron in the person of the bishop of “one city in the central lands.” He gave him a job: to travel the world and help those in need. Erwin wondered if he was the brother of a secret order, and David joked that the charter, if it were, would have prohibited him from speaking about it. He said he was praying for Faunterra, "so that the two of you who win don't consider yourself the only Star in the sky." It seemed to Erwin that the priest was checking whether he was worthy of power.

## In "A Doll on the Throne" and "Yanmei Hunt"

In Faunterra, Erwin introduced David to his sister as “the brother of a secret monastic order,” and [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] liked to talk with him: about the non-existent, as she thought, the order he invented “juicy details.” Alanis fully trusted him and in a solemn speech before the Lady called the “Holy Father David” among the people who helped her survive the past year. Iona confessed to him that she had once thrown Minerva into prison. David asked her about Waymar's prison arrangement and about the legendary prisoner who supposedly left with the help of [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]. He then disappeared from the palace, and no one was watching him. Later, with pigeon letters without an address ring, he asked Iona to find out about Waymar’s “prisoner” and warned not to pick up the letters personally, but to give them to read to vassals. Agents of protection detained him at the estate of the swamps. Then David revealed to Erwin the history of the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]]. Four Forebears, who called themselves Gardeners, separated from Wilhelm and Janmei in 69 AD, and their teachings were secretly passed on from generation to generation. He talked about the stages of dedication. Lady in Darkness, he said, is the oldest of the three masters, and Francis-Ilianus, although having the highest degree of initiation, joined the order only recently. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) The "conspiracy" against Minerva and Lady-in-the-Dark's disease, he said, were Erwin and Minerva's tests, and the fake Light Sphere was a matter of order. He said that Lady Ingrid was not dedicated, but she was friends with Lady in Darkness and helped the order. Prior [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] himself learned about the order, tracking through the informers the participation of the Maximian brothers in it. The order tried to win him over, but about a year ago Gallard turned his back on him, and he was not told about the fake items. David apologized for lying about the Light Sphere, called the person who stole it Harmon his friend and asked to do mercifully with him. In addition, he asked for help in finding the escaped prisoner Waymar, who “seriously damaged the order.”

Meanwhile, Harmon, having decided to live honestly in the spring of 1775, dreamed of flying to Izlyuchina and giving the Sphere to David, “the best of people”, and maybe joining his “order of people doing good”. He later admitted that the idea with David was “an excuse for conscience.”

## In "Humans and Gods"

In the summer of 1775, as Erwin's army moved along the rails to Almera, David arrived at him by train in the gray stubble of a traveling preacher with his [[mat-kordeliya|mother Cordelia]] and the robber Groza. He told the Duke: “People are just dying, my lord. Don’t take on too much.” His companionship was arranged in advance: on June 1, 1775, in Faunterra, David came to Erwin, along with the masters of the order, Lady in Darkness and Francis-Ilianus. Erwin was pleased with him alone. When the duke asked about the purpose of the order, the magisters turned to David, and he replied that the order could not open it, bound by an oath. At this meeting, Erwin himself asked David to accompany him on the campaign, and the masters replied that they wanted to offer the same. He accompanied the Duke on a campaign through Almera and into the steppe, to Rei-Roy. In the campaign, he told the northerners about his first craft. The son of an actor, as a boy he played on stage in Toystone. Once he portrayed the elder of the acting guild so accurately that he himself applauded him. From then on, he said, David understood people by “playing their body.” At the same time, he bet with Jemis that the defector-Almeran Sir Michael would return after the watch, and won an hilt from the kair. The battle of Fliss, not wanting to look at her, he spent in a medical van, helping the wounded. He argued with the Duke about the nature of evil and preached that each person should receive his [[pervokrov|firstblood]] and his Subject: the coachman of the flying horse, the healer a remedy for all diseases. Erwin doubted his mind for the first time. After the defeat of [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], David told Cordelia how to debunk the priest: he called himself a soldier of Wilhelm and lost, and therefore was a “false knight”. David said that the order learned about Paul in the year of the Seventeenth Gift. Then he allegedly called himself Nathaniel, was a good eccentric and initiated the boy Mick, whom the church guard handed over to the order. ([[mik|Mik]]) Ten years in Waymar’s prison, according to David, turned this “lamb” into a beast.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

David remained a chaplain to Erwin and after the defeat at the gates of Rei-Roy in July 1775, when the detachment, deprived of ships, went to the steppe of Reis County. The Kairs were waiting for sermons only from the Duke, and the physician Merrit forbade the priest to "bubble" prayers over the wounded, so that David served in the infirmary as an orderly. His spiritual services were needed only by Erwin: David served him a mug of herbal tea instead of tea and asked how the day had gone. Together with the detachment, he passed through Trident, and in September, under the guise of the entourage of the Messenger of Hope, through the County of Mills, past the Mother Mill of Jenna and the Cemetery of Rust Giants, then to Faerys and finally by sea to the north. ([[melnitsy|The Mills]]) On the ship, he stood up for the Duke before the quarreling officers (“Bad is the confessor who does not want happiness for parishioners”). He talked about the Rust Giants Cemetery, where among the thousands of rusty statues there are two of the “immortal metal”, and about the Star, which hangs in the void as a separate object, and not as part of the celestial coil. He also spread the rumor about the nobility and dowry of [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]. The Cairos considered his dreams of a world where everyone would learn how to manage Objects, a "clever parable". [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] demanded that he be removed as a spy. He recalled that David lied about the Sphere, about the order and about his past, and his advice to Iona not to read letters hinted at Ulyana Dust Puppet. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Erwin defended the priest, but on the advice of Jemis offered to accompany the wounded to the monastery. David refused to leave the Duke "in the most difficult moment".

**Head of David.** In Arrow 8, David appears as a storyteller. Together with the deliberate spy Kair Murdoch, he goes through the Fang of the Bear, which is engulfed in pogroms. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) There, after the death of [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]], supporters of the "Seventh Earl" [[elias-nortvud|Elias]] beat the "drums". David believed that all mankind is a single organism, where there are no own and strangers, and the world is rich enough to feed everyone. That is why, he believed, an “invaluable agent” came out of him. From his reflections, it can be seen that he considered himself a brilliant agent. The Order sent him to the First Winter under the guise of a pilgrim to learn of Desmond Origin's illness, and on the way he "conducted an extremely successful improvisation" with Alanis. He became her friend and confessor and learned from her what the Puppeteer was looking for. This helped Lady in the Dark to set a trap in the tomb. It was he who inspired Erwin's sympathy for the order through a simple bundle of "secret order - romance - sister". He compared the information of Harmon and Iona and was the first to suspect Shayland's Puppet Master, after which the Lady in Darkness sent Iona a warning, prompting a mutiny in Waymar. ([[perevorot-v-uejmare|Coup in Waymar]]) And he was the first to suggest that Nathaniel and Paul are two different aliens. He did not allow himself to be proud, but considered himself probably "the most selfless agent in the world."

In the wooden church of the Forefather Maximian, the preacher inconspicuously conveyed to him the message of the masters: “Adrian understood us and brought valuable gifts: Objects, people, knowledge. The second visitor is in the first winter. Let’s go there together.” David locked the door behind him to confess to the preacher, but Captain [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] came out from behind the column and took him to the Duke.

On the shore of the sea, Erwin learned from David that the order recognized [[adrian|Adrian]] as Lord, receiving from him Objects and prisoners of Shavans with firstblood. David offered to help in exchange for the oath to extradite Nathaniel. He recalled that the Order has other Objects, such as the [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]] at the court of Francis-Ilian. He then revealed to Erwin the "secret of the Tree". Gifts fall from the Star, and caves are places of impact; Mother Agatha, “being a physicist,” would immediately understand this. The Order wants to ascend to the Star, drop millions of items on the ground and distribute them to all people. Mentally, David had already decided that the order would not release Erwin. Erwin drew a "golden proportion" of the story on the sand and noticed that the climax had not yet begun, which means that the secret was revealed too early. The dream king Francis-Ilian spent eight years in the monastery, and the mystery can not be so simple. He called David a liar who lies "so regularly that it has literally grown into a habit", and ordered Leed to interrogate him, starting with a code of communication with the order. David fell to his knees. When David was asked how to regain his trust, the Duke replied, “Try to die.” Erwin suggested that agents of the order have poison in their teeth, like Rebecca Litland, and if David wishes to remain himself, he will die "just as he lived - a liar." How the interrogation ended is unknown. Erwin later recounted what he had heard from David, and Mireille Nan-Claire confirmed that the main idea was "almost" true. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]])

## Personality

David’s deep velvet voice, calmness, and empathy helped him in every endeavor. To each ward he picked up the key, and most often this key turned out to be kindness. Erwin called him an actor from a family of actors. He knew how to “play courage”: for this, you need to take one step towards risk, “but for everyone to see.” And fear, if it is not hidden, it is better to pass out as sadness. David himself was not brave and never tried to break the street fight. At the same time, he almost did not hide from Harmon that the brotherhood does not disdain theft, and admitted: “If an act, not an intention, is important, then my soul is black as ashes.”

## David's words are true.

Much of what David said was inaccurate or false. Paul and Nathaniel are different faces, and David himself suspected it. The "Mystery of the Tree" about falling from the Star Objects, as it turned out from the words of [[navi|Navi]], is only partially true: Gifts are places of shipwreck, and the Star is a false beacon.
