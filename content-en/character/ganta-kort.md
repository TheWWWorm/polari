## Who He Is
Kort is the leader of the ghana in which Neymir the Werewolf and [[chara|Chara]] Without Fear traveled. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) "Three Springs Ago" he saved both lives, and the Companions considered themselves his debtors. Neymir considered him a smart leader who does not ask questions for no reason. [[ganta-groza|Ganta Thunderstorm]] described it to [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] as follows: "The court is a cunning, crafty fox, like your Henry Farway." Court likes to talk with hints and steppe sayings and knows how to give in time to the leader.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
### The Conspiracy Against Moran
In the winter of 1774–1775, after the defeat of the horde at [[melorandzh|Melorange]], the Court, on the orders of Moran, sent the Companions in a raid with the gauntlet of Birai. ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]) He knew in advance that Biray was deserting, and hoped that the Companions would not run away with him. He met his best horsemen in a tent of horse skins and treated them with a dried horse. At first, he cautiously asked for their opinion of [[moran|Moran]], and then spoke himself. He resented the fact that “shavan shavana breaks his legs, shavana nailed to the pole.” According to him, Moran will lead the horde into the jungle, where she will be cut like pigs. The whole war, he said, is "because of the woman": the leader wanted to take Melorange and the Litland girl at the same time. When Moran ordered every major gang to send a pair of warriors for reconnaissance, Court ordered the Companions to pay their debt and kill the chief in his tent. He said, “You’re going to be killed, you’re going to be killed, you’re going to have to give everyone three years of their life.”

The companions disobeyed, and after the horde's victory over Prince Hector, Court decided that Moran was right, and he himself was a "donkey". ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) He was feasting in a tent with southern wine and in a turban with a huge ruby, when he was visited by those who returned from the captivity of Sputnik. They thought it was Court who had given them to Hector by letter. Chara, kneeling as if for a kiss of her hand, nailed his palm with a dagger to the table, and Neymir put a blade to his throat. Court didn't plead. He recalled that he himself would go into the same trap with the horde and would not be left alone with the “survived dead” if he betrayed them. Neymir believed him, and Chara bandaged the wound. Court called the Sputniks "hard-necked rams" who would never become gants. According to him, Moran wanted if not half, then a third of the horde, so they will not find a traitor. In fact, Moran himself wrote the letter to Hector.

## In "Humans and Gods"
Birai, who passed to Adrian, promised the lord to persuade Cort to the side of the Crown: "Moran is now lame and weak - a great time to change the leader!"

In the summer of 1775, in Rei-Roy, Court played the same song about the "bad times" in Moran's tent every night. A month earlier, he had offered to raise the horde in a campaign against the weakened Shimmery Labelins, plunder Laam and Orcada, and then go to Origin. Moran replied that he would fight when he said it himself, and with whomever he chose. At the council, Court discussed the four forces of peace: the Crown, the North, the Center, and the Steppe. According to him, if Origin becomes the ruler, the wolves will come to carve the shavanks. About the rumors that the Companions broke the leader's will along with his legs, Court declined to speak. Moran called it "slippery as dog shit", and when Court rebuked the chief with "sitting on his ass", he splashed wine in his face: "Next time it'll be a knife".

When [[paul|Paul]] came to Rei-Roy, he asked the Shavans about Stepi's main enemy. Court dropped out, "Shimmery?" and heard, "Just? You're small, Ganta!" Turning Moran into a frozen figure, Paul called Chara, a young Johan Reis, and a “ganta with cunning eyes,” his assistants. The captive old men soon told Erwin Origin that the horde was led by Ganta Court and Johan Reis.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### At the Gnoi-gant
Cort and [[yukhan-rejs|Johan]] were among the first to pick up Wilhelm's Feathers from Paul's abandoned bag, and no one dared to rob them. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) Before leaving Rei-Roy, Paul chose which of the three - Court, Ganta Ondei or Johan - would remain to guard the city. Court said there were seven hundred men in his gan and they would be useful on the march. Paul left Onday. When the scout reported three battalions of kire, Court advised to catch them in the steppe, until they took refuge in the [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]]. Paul replied in one word, “No.”

On the council of the horde, Court, along with Johan, stood for a hike on [[faunterra|Faunterra]], while Chara and one-armed Sormach were called to burn the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. Paul decided to go for both goals, and Court and Reis boasted: “Moran did not beat the Yankees, but we will succeed!” At a feast at [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s Priory in [[fliss|Fliss]], Court nodded when Alanis reproached the Shavans for the robberies, and spread out in a smile, learning that each khanid would be allocated a thousand ephes. The commander of the southern army Paul did not appoint him: Kort was older and more experienced, but Johan idolized the Gnoi-ganta.

### Chief of the Southern Horde
At Patrick's Chapel, Johan's vanguard ran into [[adrian|Adrian]]'s regiments, and Johan was captured. Many Ghanaians separated and became ordinary gangs, but most riders took the beginning of Court. Fearing the Yangmei Gauntlet and the flying fleet, the prudent leader took the horde back to Almera to save trophies and plunder the cities of Red Earth. A month later, he formed an alliance with [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] and married him: the daughter of the Ganta was married to Farway's grandson. The "two impostors" vowed to support each other: Court declared himself chief of Reis County and Farway the ruler of Almera. Their alliance became the most formidable force south of the Smoky Distance.

## In "Fantasies"
The author calls the stories “Fantasies” possible destinies of heroes. In them, Court becomes the ruler of the Steppe.

In the "Device" (spring 1776), the Shavans, after the death of the Gnoi-Ganthas, renounced it and crumbled into flocks. The largest joined Court, who declared himself a warrior of the Foremother Elena: she seemed to live with the Spirits of the Steppe for a long time, and all the great Shavans come from her - Paul, [[moran|Moran]] and the Court itself. Lady Karen relates that Farway's grandson [[dzheremi-farvej|Jeremy]] married a shavanka and lives in Rei-Roy, where everyone laughs at the fact that he can't ride a wild horse. In Amicably, Prince Hector's wife, Catherine Catherine, calls herself "still the niece of Ganta Cort."

In "Closer - Further" retells his story after the split of the horde. The southern part under the leadership of Johan Reis and Cort plundered a dozen cities. After the capture of Juhan, Court took the Shavans to Almera, plundered the Red Land for another month, and made peace with Farway. Court recognized Farway as the ruler of Almera, and Farway Court as the first chief of the Steppes, and the treaty was sealed by a dynastic marriage. Court then stole several thousand head of cattle from Holliwell and returned to Rei-Roy by Descent Day. Upon learning of the death of the Gnoi-Ganta, Juhan and other leaders during the First Winter, he ordered to lay down the greatest memorial bonfire that the Steppe saw: his smoke was seen even from the Mother Mills. Court sang mournful songs along with thousands of Shavans, and thought to himself: "Race, Ondei and the Gaunt Dung fell into the dust. I am the ruler of the Steppe!”

Fearing an attack by Erwin Origin, Court held the army under banners, and the Chapter Pramatery replied that he had already sent ambassadors to the Duke. In May 1776, Neymir returned to Rei-Roy, who had long been considered dead. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) Court hugged him and feasted with him, but asked why he would forgive a horseman who had disappeared for the duration of the war. Neymir recalled that the Count of Kortu was not without the approval of the Lord, and volunteered to reconcile him with the wolf. Court promised to forgive and reward him if he agreed, and he also told me to look at Minerva. He could not send male spies with Neymir: no one dared to go to the wolves, and two archers volunteered to accompany them.

On behalf of Cort, Neymir offered Erwin a tribute: a thousand horses, three thousand cows and two Sacred Objects. Erwin only agreed to not fight Reis for three years. His condition for friendship was the frozen body of the Shavan, which almost killed the Duke: the body will be taken to Rei-Roy, and at every visit of the northern ambassadors, the chief Stepi must kiss his icy face. Before [[minerva|Minerva]], the ambassadors on their knees repeated the words of Cort: he was “stupid and daring”, “came together in battle with the one who flies through the sky” and swears on blood not to repeat this mistake. Minerva forgave him on the condition that Court would hand her one Vir Kanna, able to "work a little with the brain", to build dams and bridges; the ambassadors promised to send three to choose from. The Ten Major Gants of the Steppes gave Neymir the right to sign a preliminary peace agreement on their behalf.

## Personality
Court is cunning, cautious and greedy for prey. He prefers to remove the unwanted with other hands and speaks with hints until he is forced to speak directly. He is not a coward: with his hand nailed to the table, he did not pray for mercy. He is able to quickly recognize someone else's rightness when the wind changes, and in time leaves with the prey, and not looking for a glorious death.
