Colonel Blackberry is one of the senior warlords of the [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]]. According to Erwin, Lilliday, Blackberry, Statham, and Horton all trace their ancestry "from the depths of the ages."

## Personality
Blackberry commanded the mountain guard for ten years. It was created by Erwin's great-grandfathers when the First Winter was fought off by rebellious vassals. Since then, the guard was left idle, and the boring service in the mountains turned the colonel into “a blushing, dissatisfied with everything in the world old man.” Had Nexia been his portrait painter, she would have cost only crooked lines. He had known the Earl of Lilliday since childhood and almost never agreed with him, except when the two were united against Erwin. The Count was arrogant and stubborn, and Blackberry, "not a scorpion patient," could argue with him in gruffness. Victor Shayland found him glorious, experienced, self-satisfied, and "completely devoid of imagination" outside the war. The Colonel learned to speak quietly as a lieutenant and loved to talk about his exploits.

## “The Price of Fear”
In [[tsena-strakha|The Price of Fear]], in August 1756, after the War of the Objects, the Northmen stood at [[uejmar|Waymar]]. ([[semnadtsatyj-dar|Seventeenth Gift]]) Young [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] volunteered to show Colonel Blackberry the city. In fact, he was interested in the gray of the colonel - [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]], who looked like he was twelve years old. Vittor understood why the duke had entrusted his son to this particular man: the colonel would never betray the Origins, "just couldn't figure out why." Vittor brought Blackberry to the veterans' inn, and he gathered around him listeners. Richard admitted that he fell asleep from the bays of the colonel.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
In the fall of 1774, Blackberry was in the bedroom of the wounded Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]], along with Earl Lilliday, Baron [[stetkhem|Statham]], Colonel Horton, and castellan Hyrock. ([[artur-khajrok|Arthur Highrock]]) They were “the first warlords of the land of Origin.” Horton and Blackberry commanded the expeditionary corps. When Erwin said that the people of the House were killed on the orders of Lord Adrian, Blackberry blurted out: “I don’t believe. That can't be!"

## In "Only One Star"
At the feast, where Erwin declared war, Hyrock called it a bad idea, and Blackberry added, "The worst one can come up with." In the campaign, Erwin made the Colonel "not a forager, but a breadwinner of the army": while Blackberry is healthy, eighteen thousand soldiers can not complain about the provisions.

On the Wise River, the Colonel suggested secretly cutting rafts in a grove upstream and crossing elsewhere, and Erwin acted "on the advice of Lord Feeder Blackberry". Together with other generals, he hurried Erwin to go from Doyle directly to [[labelin|Labelin]] and grumbled that with a quick march, the enemy would not have had time to strengthen.

Erwin explained to [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] that he could not remove the officers appointed by his father: the personal loyalty of many kairs was addressed to "Horton or Blackberry or [[dzhoj-khemmond|Hammond]]". In November 1774, when [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]] was calling for the capital, Blackberry frankly told Erwin, "You are young." Adrian, in his opinion, is less afraid of Erwin than the Steppe Fire, and Lisa put up only to detain the northerners. However, Blackberry later advised an offensive.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
Erwin admitted to his mother that the plan of the battle on the Wise River was a fiction: he and Robert and Blackberry simply "bounced at a meeting" and moved to three places. Before the Holy Field, the colonel suggested to Erwin that the Snowdrops would have "moths" instead of weapons. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) At a debate about the best wine in the North, he stated: "the Blackberry family will never agree" with Count Lilliday.

## In "Humans and Gods"
**Waymar.** In June 1775, Erwin entrusted Blackberry with a "drip battalion" of kairs who remembered Desmond as a triumphalist and Richard as a "green snot". Erwin considered the colonel, Richard’s former mentor, to be the ideal commander: he knew how much “cheap spes” there was in the student. The bankers told Wittor that Blackberry and Statham were standing with the "small" on Smoky Distance. Richard-Led said, "Blackberry, old friend..."

When his father landed in Waymar, Ice came out to the ranks of the Kairs. He disarmed Captain Morgan with the family reception of Blackberry himself and asked the colonel to be his second in a duel with Erwin: "All the war for the Objects I went next to you." Blackberry was silent for a long time, but then he minted: “I do not recognize you, sir.” He ordered the gunmen to aim at Richard, took Iona's letter from him and handed it to the "Duke". He was Erwin’s double. The colonel told Richard: “You are under arrest.” Joaquin later noticed that the senior officer was Blackberry, but commanded everyone in the back rows with signals.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
In the summer of 1775 Desmond, with Blackberry and four battalions, walked on the water ahead of the Allied army. In [[lid|Leed]], on Desmond's advice, the colonel countered to Statham: the Snow Count had already taken the soldiers away, a dozen kairs and a hundred guards remained in the city.

In the Battle of the First Winter, his battalion stood before the moat. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) To walk on a narrow bridge under the fire of the Rings meant to die, and Blackberry threw up his fist: “Let’s hold on!” He lost a quarter of the fighters, and then, along with the Xes, attacked the Shaylands and the Almerans. Nexia passed on Erwin's designs to Desmond, and Blackberry's cues knew when the Xes would get off the Belomorye road. After the victory, the Items were stored in a fortified camp under the protection of its keels. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] at the same time thought that "the son of the colonel she returned with her own hands from the Star."

Erwin, remembering the Earl of Hammer who died at [[bitva-pri-piksi|Pixie]], bitterly noticed that Statham, Horton, Lilliday and Blackberry "saved themselves".
