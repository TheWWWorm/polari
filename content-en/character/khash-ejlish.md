## Appearance and gift
Hash Eilish is a tall, painfully thin woman, bony, flat-breasted and gray-haired. She pulled her hair off the back of her head. A narrow scar stretches from her left eye up through her forehead, and the cut eyebrow gives the face a surprised and arrogant expression. On her wrist, she wears a bracelet with a dead mouse glued to it. When Joaquin asked if the mouse was rotten, Eilish replied that it was "adorned with the best stuffed animal", and offered to replace it with a cat. Martin Shayland called her a “mummy.” After a few full months of hiking to the north, Eilish noticeably rounded, and the bracelet was about to break.

Touching a man's hand, she learned something that no one told her. At the first meeting, Eilish told Joaquin about two cases: on that day, he spared one person, and in less than an hour he wanted to kill another, but missed. She felt where and whose death happened nearby, and sometimes foreshadowed it. She explained it this way:
> “Sometimes the victim whispers in our ears.”

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
### Embassy
On 27 May 1775, Eilish and Lacht Mies, a man with an iron hand, arrived in Shayland as ambassadors of General Horis, the ruler of Silens. ([[oris|General Oris]]) Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] sent Harry Hog and [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] to meet the guests. ([[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]]) Both of the ambassadors wore brown coats in the heat. The first thing they wanted to do was to go down to the bed. Eilish took Joaquin's hand and warmly said to him:
> “You met her, but you didn’t call her. That's right."

When asked to whom they serve, she replied that Horis is "our light and guide", and then began to ask Joaquin if a warrior could be a priest. Lakht Mees on the way told Harry about the turmoil in the Sunset Coast.

### Prisoner's Chamber
At the ruins of the monastery, the ambassadors met [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] with cairns. Eilish explained that the count offered them a secret move so as not to disturb the townspeople with the view of sunsets. Iona took them to the castle. On the way, Eilish assured that she knew about the course because “a person becomes aware of exactly what he needs to know.” The prisoner, she said, was neither a sunsetr nor a soldier of Horis. In the dungeon, she rushed forward without a flashlight, feeling the air with her hands, found a breach in the former cell of a mysterious prisoner and shouted: “Gods!”. When she came out, she touched Iona’s cheek and whispered, “Your husband is a happy man.” Iona, repeating these words, for a moment seemed to understand something.

### Revolt of Iona
At Eilish's request, the Count invited Joaquin and Harry to dinner with the ambassadors. That same night, Iona rebelled against her husband. ([[perevorot-v-uejmare|Coup in Waymar]]) The soldiers watched the fight with keen interest. When the count gave Harry Finger of Wilhelm, Eilish exhaled, “She’s here!” Iona went to the soldiers of the garrison through the fire of Perst. Joaquin realized she was about to subdue them, and looked at Eilish. She nodded, "in a moment reading all his thoughts." Later, both sunsets bothered over the wounded Harry and briefly brought him to his senses.

## In "Humans and Gods"
### Trading with the Count
Vittor locked himself in the office with the ambassadors. It was there that Iona died. Eilish noted that a living Iona would have noticeably corrected the count’s situation, and the healing of such a terrible wound would have shown his “God’s blessing” and would have enticed the allies. The Count said that God was on his side and turning away from him meant betraying their faith. Lacht Mees replied, "Not to him, but to her, my lord." The ambassadors demanded to see the full force of the count and talk to him directly. When Vittor healed Iona's wound and summoned through the Object bracelet "the man from the Subject", i.e. [[paul|Paul]], Lacht Mies offered him the help of a cult. Paul replied that he did not care about them, and the sunsets “shined with happiness.”

### Joaquin's proposal
While the count was thinking, Eilish took Joaquin to the arsenal. She said that Origin would take the castle and hang the garrison, and offered to take him out as her soldier in exchange for one night. Joaquin refused to abandon the count, who "was the first to be really kind to him." Then Eilish laughed, kissed him, and explained what she needed:
> “There is such a figure in the divination cards — a fool. He has an empty head, but a good open heart."

According to her, in difficult cases, where the sages give a fly, the fool gets to the goal. The next morning, the ambassadors went to Silens.

### Waymar's Defense
In June 1775, Eilish returned to [[uejmar|Waymar]] with Horis' army. On the way to the castle, she asked Joaquin how many people he was ready to save at the cost of his life, and one touch realized that he had become bolder and more self-satisfied. After the failure of the negotiations, at which Joaquin opened fire from Perth, the kairs began to be covered by a human shield from the townspeople. Eilish took him to the wall to look at it and said, "You have opened the gate to her again," calling him the "gatekeeper" of Fallen.

She requested to go with Martin and Joaquin to the Earl of Fleming, stating that she also knew what had happened in the box of the Seventeenth Gift. On the way, Eilish teased both: she admitted that she wants to sleep with Joaquin and dreams of seeing them shoot each other from the Rings. Under Fleming, she vouched for Martin's every word about Dar's secret. On the ship going to Waymar, she explained with Joaquin:
> “I have been serving the Fallen for seventeen years. My faith is so strong that neither fear nor passion can destroy it.

Her co-religionists were outcasts for many years, and now, according to her, their time has come. Joaquina called her “the guide in the world of those who do not believe”, “the soul and voice of all honest guys”. While checking on him, she begged him not to go astray. Then she kissed him. Right during the night battle on the river, while Joaquin was sinking the ships of Origin with a Feather, they gave in to caresses.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Joaquin's mistress
Eilish and Joaquin became lovers and lived in the same room of the castle, although after his closeness everything in it was “infuriated”. One day he saw that she was waiting in the yard not for him, but for Horis, who was called the “master”, and became jealous, although the conversation was innocent. The Count instructed Joaquin to consult with Horis, and Eilish brought him to dinner at the General's tent. There, she explained why unreliable "backs" were placed at the bottom of the defense, and Horis outlined the doctrine of respectful eating of the dead, and Joaquin vomited. Lacht Mees remarked, "He doesn't accept her," and Eilish objected, "He doesn't accept himself. And the Fallen has long owned it." Later, Vittor publicly asked Joaquin if he was fed by the “lady meat”, and he realized that he was eating a dead body.

### Water testing
During the siege, the count decided to send the sunsets into a hopeless attack to take their well. Eilish had to call Horis to the castle, and if he refused, Joaquin was told to kill her. Eilish read through his eyes. She was not afraid at all and asked for an hour to pray: “Meeting with her is a great joy.” She added that she would be flattered if Joaquin himself became her “gatekeeper”.

Joaquin swore no one would kill her. The attack was postponed, and the next day the siege was lifted. Eilish threw herself around his neck: “I’m sorry I didn’t believe you!” When Vittor "resurrected" in the castle temple, Eilish, following Horis, fell to her knees in front of him and one of the first to proclaim him the Chosen One. About the prisoner [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], she said that she “decided to serve Falla is a thorny path.”

### Hiking to the north
In the campaign of the Chosen One, Eilish, at Joaquin's request, "felt" the kingdom of the dead through his hand and assured him that parents and brothers in the Southern Way were alive. She said, “Somebody is going to die. Close to you.” In return, she asked Joaquin what Vittor was saying about the Star. Eilish noticed that the count never describes the details. Horis believed that the count was met by the Fallen herself in the form of pure light. Eilish, on the other hand, suspected that Vittor was deliberately speaking vaguely, so that the Almerans would see him as the Forefather, the sunsets as the Fallen's favorite, and the Shavans as the Wanderer Spirit.

When Joaquin was lying in the infirmary with a swollen face, Eilish came to him and fondled, assuring him that the face would heal, "the main thing is that the male pride survived." In those days he wanted to see Lady Laura more. She once told him that Iona was praying to the goddess of death.

Before the Skyworks, she gave Joaquin the suspicions of a mentor about Count Fleming, who too willingly took over the flanking dozers. She knew from his face that Lord [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] had dealt with Iona. Iona actually survived, but Joaquin didn't know it then. He then spoke of the sacking of [[lid|Leed]] by Paul's Shavans. Eilish asked if he had been there himself, and if he hadn’t stopped: “The fallen woman would have embraced you. She loves strong people.” To Lord Richard, who was grieving that his family would surrender the North without a fight, she said, "You are the future of the House Origin. Only you, the others are in the past.” When Richard chased Joaquin away, Eilish remarked, "The Fallen takes the Northerners even without the help of a count." Returning from Main Vittor, she first rushed to her chest and asked what he would do with Minerva.

### Battle for First Winter
On the day of the Battle of the First Winter, Eilish remained in the rear with the children, who were under Vittor's care. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) While Joaquin was giving them a lesson in military affairs, her eyes glittered: The Fallen was gathering a "generous harvest". When a huge shell appeared in the sky, Vittor told her to help Sally take the children to a shelter, a previously watched grotto. Before the assault, Horis let Joaquin rest in the rear, where Eilish would be pleased with him, but he chose to go into battle with the sunset regiment. After the defeat, Horis took the sunsets to the Skates of Heaven. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]) Joaquin removed Perst and resigned. About the further fate of Eilish in the main cycle is not told.

## Relationships
### Joaquin Eve Hannah
Eilish was drawn to the simple-minded Joaquin and saw in him the measure of the correctness of her path. She constantly provoked him with questions about life and death and rejoiced when he made a choice “in our favor.” Joaquin then wished for it, then despised the corpse-eater, called himself a "thorough eater", repeatedly promised himself to break up with her and returned again.

### Horis and Lacht Mies
General Horis was her spiritual mentor. He kissed her on the top of the head, called her “clever” and “my girl”, shared a cake with her and consulted with her on military advice. Lacht Mies was her constant companion. He died at [[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]].

## Personality
Eilish is calm, insightful and mysteriously ironic. She is mocking, sometimes deliberately shameless and likes to test people. Her fanatical belief in Fall is combined with a sober mind: she noticed Vittor's political tricks before others. She honors death and strength, admires combat and someone else's death, but treats Joaquin with genuine tenderness.
