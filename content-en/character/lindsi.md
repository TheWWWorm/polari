Lindsay was a maid at Waymar Castle who served as a prisoner of [[minerva|Minerva]] in the fall of 1774. Her disappearance is the main mystery of the chapters of "Spark" in the novel "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]".

## Appearance and Personality
"Lindsey was one of those girls that men call pretty": black-haired, with a long braid, round brown eyes and a slightly swollen nose. I watched myself more than the maid should be - clean nails, powder, blush, and once even perfume. In the service she was hasty and silent, with Mira fighting, cheerful and a little vain; the soldiers remembered her as "boyish such, cheerful". Her age is not named.

Lindsay could not read: “They tried to teach me, but I did not even master my name...” Mira taught her to write her name in printed letters, as in The [[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]. Time on the hands of the clock Lindsay could read, and this was used by her secret gentleman: he assigned her dates with drawings of the dial. Her friends called her Lee.

## History
In early October 1774, Sir Francis promised the prisoner a “wise servant”, and Lindsay was put to Mir. She brought coffee and lunches, ran errands to Pierre's bookshop, and asked permission to cut her hair "like you" so that the groom, a carpenter Dave's apprentice, "couldn't miss it." According to her, Lord [[martin-shejland|Martin]] ordered him to collect food for hunting. On the fifth day of Mirin's captivity, Lindsay broke the cup in tears - then Mira realized that she had quarreled with a gentleman.

Through Lindsay, Meera sent the shopkeeper a book with an encrypted warning for [[adrian|Adrian]]. On the way, Eph took the book from her and solved the cipher overnight. ([[frensis-myurrej|Eff]]) In the evening of the same day, Lindsay did not come to class: she wanted to learn how to write “waiting at 8 o’clock.” The next morning, she was replaced by Frida's new maid, and the mayor announced that Lindsay was sick and went to her relatives in the village and that "Sir Francis Murray had already taken over all the expenses."

### Searches
I didn't believe the world. The maid of the castellan Bernadette, Lindsay's friend, said that Dave did not find her at her mother or in three hospitals, and on the last day Lindsay ran away with a note from the gentleman. The undertaker confirmed that there were no new deaths in the castle. Mira was going over the suspects - [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]], Martin, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]] himself - until the guards said that the lover took Lindsay to prison. There was a piece of paper in the guardhouse with a clock showing eight. In early November, Eff admitted: “Yes, really, I was the one who took her to the guard. Knight and maid," but swore he had not killed her, and gave Mira the knight's word to help in the search.

When Jane, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]'s companion, disappeared, Eph secretly said that only Inge Price knew about the code with the clock, whom he told drunk on a schooner. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) Mira realized that the girls were kidnapped by Martin, who left “to hunt” both times. Soon Inji killed Efa, and Mira was taken to Martin's basement.

### Death
In the basement, the Pharmacist explained that Lindsay was taken instead of her mistress: "The maid always bears the imprint of her mistress." She lived seventy-six hours and “disappointed” him. Martin tore the lid off the barrel with the brine, "Here's Lindsay for you, look." In the account book of the Pharmacist, which Mira then read on the road, Lindsay was listed as a “source”: “76 h. Pulse is not heard. There is no breath. The source is devastated.” On this account, she died about three days after her disappearance, in mid-October; the text does not give an exact date. Whether she was buried is not said.

## Legacy
Mira blamed herself for not burying Lindsay, and hoped the guires would. In a delirium on the road through the Smoky Dahl, she dreamed of Lindsay and Jane in barrels, Eph "with a hole in the eye", dancing with Lindsay, and "dead Lindsay". ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) In Aleridan, she reread a page of a book about Lindsay, and in December told her story to Adrian. In [[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]], the wine on the feast reminded Mira of the blood she was given in the basement, "possibly Lindsay's blood." In "[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]", a mutilated Ethan reminded her of a barrel with a corpse: "unlike Lindsay, Ethan was alive." ([[itan|Ethan Gladys Norma]])
