## Origins

The true story of Paul was told only at the very end of the cycle by [[navi|Nathaniel]] (Navi). According to him, Paul and a woman named Lilanna were “gods” from the rest of the galaxy, descendants of common with the Polarians ancestors who changed themselves over eighteen centuries. The three were married — “two husbands and a wife” — and went on an expedition to the “little boy”, like everything else in the world of the gods. Navi, the "light" god-navigator, paved the way for the outdated lighthouses - and the ship crashed against Polaris, to which the false lighthouse, that is, [[polari-world|the Star]], led. Lylanna died on impact: [[chrevo|the Womb]] preserved her body, but a sharp exit from the anabiosis destroyed her. Navi escaped with a catapult to Smoky Dahl, and Paul's capsule fell "directly into hell" and opened up, letting in lava. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]])

Paul belonged to the "dark" - those who changed the body, not the mind: the dark unusually tenacious, strong and hardy, but dependent on strong feelings - passion, fear, pain, including someone else's. In the world of the gods, Paul “played” the role of the legendary space Marine and instilled combat skills that later allowed him to collect [[absolyut|the Absolute]]. With him he had five kilograms of "eternity" - a substance that thousands of times slows down any process around the carrier; with each application, it is consumed. Thanks to her, Paul survived for a month in the melting zone of the reactor, losing, according to Navi, about half of his body weight. “A man would have died long ago in his place. But Paul was a dark god.”

In the end, Vittor came down himself. In the cave, the savage suddenly asked, “Why are they sacred?” — about the Objects, and in his voice Vittor felt threatened.

There they witnessed Emperor Thelurian, Prince [[adrian|Adrian]], Ayden Almera and [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] find a dead girl of unearthly beauty in a transparent box and vow to hide the find (apparently it was the body of Lylanna, which Navi later told about). ## The Prisoner of Waymar

The guest soon became a prisoner. Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent Shayland]] tried to extort power from him over the [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects]], and when torture did not help (according to Navi, for the dark, they were rather a pleasure), he buried Paul alive. Ten years of darkness and hunger, according to Navi, turned him into a monster. Freedom was given to him by Vittor in exchange for service; Paul demanded three heads: he killed two executioners himself, and Count Vincent died of a heartbreak. Vittor wanted from Paul power over the Objects, invulnerability and domination of the world. Later, the escape of “Prisoner Waymar” was investigated by [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], and Erwin was the first to associate this prisoner with the source of the firstblood: “where else could the brigade take the firstblood, but from Paul?” ([[pervokrov|Firstblood]])

## In the service of the Puppeteer

Vittor took him to a poor Shayland vault. Among the Objects there, Paul learned three of the seventeen parts of [[absolyut|the Absolute]], and then the Light Sphere (seeker). ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) During the day, he read the entire imperial register and showed the count where the remaining parts are stored. Vittor refused to go to war on the Dynasty and the Church, and then Paul, expanding the search for the Sphere, discovered an unknown Gift beyond [[reka|the River]]. There, in the Beyond, he extracted the Gift with the hands of slaves, found the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]], arranged "shooting" at prisoners and put deadly experiments with the Rose of Happiness, until he set it up on the "deconstructor" - a means of destroying Objects - and introduced the reconfigured Rose into his own blood. The fear of slaves, according to Navi, was a "drinking seasoning": "For the first time in many years, Waymar's prisoner was happy." Experiments with other Objects showed him that in Polaris they are weakening, so Paul realized: Nathaniel is alive, although the Sphere does not see him. He persuaded Wittor to let the Sphere go through the world through an inconspicuous merchant ([[kharmon|Harmon]]) so that she would sooner or later get to Nathaniel and destroy him.

## Personality and Appearance

Tall, silent, with an impenetrable, devoid of any sense of face and “oddly faded, almost colorless” eyes, “like spots of fog”; his face, according to the description, “looks like a gray granite column”, and his voice “is deaf as steps into the dungeon”. According to kairs, he extracted the crossbow bolt from his own bone. Cruelty not by calculation, but by nature: murders and other people's fear quenched his "thirst", and after the massacre he became noticeably calmer. At the same time, he was disciplined, never lied to Chara and, in her feeling, highly valued his own. Alanis noted the strangeness: Paul never tried to take it himself and kissed only once, in the neck, but every khanid kissed right on the lips. Before Navi, he showed not only hatred, but also the resentment of an abandoned friend: “I was only twenty years late.”

## Misconceptions about Paul

Churchmen and the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] long considered Paul and Nathaniel one person: [[otets-david|Father David]] assured Erwin that in 1756, "Paul" called himself Nathaniel and was a good eccentric until he was broken by the dungeon. The order's magisters asked Erwin to take Paul alive because they did not believe he could be killed. David himself later first suspected that there were two aliens, and Navi's confessions confirmed this; Adrian, in a letter to Lady-in-the-Dark, hinted that there were three "visitors". Navi’s stories about the “[[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]” that Paul supposedly serves and the fact that Paul and the Absolute “will punch a hole between the worlds” turned out to be a simplified version intended for the Polarians.
