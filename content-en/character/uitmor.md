## Who He Is

Captain Whitmore is an officer of the [[lazurnaya-gvardiya|azure guard]] of the Yangmei family. In the case of the abduction of objects, he is described as a “warrior of the old temper”. In "Janmei Hunt", he is gray-haired, and in "Fantasies" Mira notes that he is much older than her. His voice is normal. Colleagues and allies consider him an honest servant: even evil to the language of [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] admits that Whitmore was a “good servant”, who was simply unlucky.

## "A Doll on the Throne"

**Disappearing Items.** In the case [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] read to [[mark-voron|Mark]], the company commander who accompanied the Dynasty Items was named Sir Derek Whitmore. ([[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]]) The reserve azure company was raised on alert the night the rebel broke into the Throne Citadel (see [[bitva-za-faunterru]]). ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) The company was late: the palace was already occupied by the northerners. Lieutenant Augustus Mace stormed, but Whitmore "strictly besieged him" and ordered only to watch the island. The captain himself went to see Burgomaster Escher. ([[baron-esher|Baron Esher]]) The Burgomaster requested that the Items be taken out of town, and Whitmore found the request reasonable. Through the underground passage, the guards took the Items out of the storage and loaded them into a train, to which a fortified wagon for valuables was attached.

On the third hour of the journey, poisoning began. Whitmore first sinned on spoiled food and forbade eating, but the poison was in the water. He was one of the first to be poisoned because he had lunch with the second watch, and soon could barely speak. Mace took command. He stopped the train at Orwell to find the doctors, where the company was attacked, and the wagon with the Objects was stolen. According to eyewitnesses, the captain shouted through vomiting spasms: “Objects, Objects!” They found the body of a grey watch on the train. Whitmore then said that the warrior deserves posthumous honor and should be buried in [[marengo|Marengo]].

Mark was sure that Whitmore was dead, and was surprised to learn that he was alive and out of the infirmary at Orwell. He suspected that the captain had deliberately drunk an incomplete cup, but then, along with [[ryzhij|Red]], came to the conclusion that Whitmore was innocent.

**Blue in Almera.** In the spring of 1775, Whitmore, with three dozen surviving guardsmen, chased across Almera in search of the kidnapped. Officially, they were “on leave to recuperate.” Major Backfield joined them. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Almer men took the squad in blue coats for the gang. In late March, Whitmore's men stopped a train carrying Mark, [[ded|Grandfather]], and Inge Price and captured them, mistaking them for the Lord of the Rings' men. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) After recognizing Mark, Whitmore called him an ally, told him to give him a chair and food. Backfield was sure that the Items had been stolen by Origin, Whitmore disagreed with him: the Duke of the Persians, he would have used them in the siege of the palace. The captain found witnesses that a brigade with the Rings was standing in the [[dzhek-bakler|Jack Buckler]] Inn back in August, before the attack on Evergard. ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]])

Mark noticed that Whitmore always had the last word, although Backfield was older in rank: the detachment consisted of Whitmore's azure guardsmen, and he himself was Yanmean. Inji Whitmore promised to interrogate him without causing him "irreparable damage". When asked to whom he would report about the Master of the Rings, the captain minted: "The Azure Guard is the shield and sword of the Crown!" Mark spent the night at his cousin's estate, Sir Henry Whitmore, where he was received as an honorary guest.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

In the spring of 1775, when [[minerva|Minerva]] fled to Marengo, Whitmore responded to the wave from the capital: there are two azure companies in Marengo, one holding a station, the other a palace. ([[marengo|Marengo]]) Erwin wanted to send [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] with the Xs to get the damn doll, but he turned it down. The "Voice of the Crown" on May 10 printed Captain Whitmore's statement of Lord [[menson|Manson]]'s detention. At the first [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], Whitmore was among the respected prosecution witnesses.

On the night of May 28, in the tomb of the Bishop [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]] Whitmore came out to Erwin from a secret passage: "Lord Chancellor, do not light a fire!" At Minerva's orders, the Guards, along with Lady in Darkness's men, filled the hall with gas to burn whoever took the Sun Drop. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]; [[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]) Erwin didn't take it, and Whitmore told me to open the doors. When [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade broke in with the Rings, the captain, along with [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]], dragged Erwin into a secret passage. At the request of Jemis, he set fire to the gas, but too late: “Everything was weathered.”

## “The ones we forgive.”

In May 1775, Whitmore sent Lieutenant Mace to Minerva with an oral message: the lady should secretly come to Marengo. There, in the Palace of Silence, Whitmore and Backfield reported to her about [[absolyut|the Absolute]] and the Abductors of the Objects. Whitmore declined the award, saying, "My mouth has covered itself with shame by losing the Items." Backfield gave out witnesses, Inge Price and Baby G's girl. Whitmore recalled that he and Crow had vowed not to give Inge away, but Minerva had ordered that both be thrown into the cells. The captain wanted to take the girl to him, but the mistress did not let her go. Whitmore wanted to bet, but he didn't.

## In "Humans and Gods"

At the end of May 1775, Minerva summoned Whitmore with a company from Marengo to the capital and ordered him to bring a "secret weapon". The trains were not going, and the company came with a march-throw. Whitmore met Minerva when the freed [[menson|Manson]] hid behind a porter in her waiting room, and offered to drive him away. He later led to Lord Inge Price. They brought him with delay: the potion so undermined the prisoner’s health that the doctors predicted his death for three weeks.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

In the summer of 1775, Whitmore sat on the Minerva War Council alongside Shatterhand, [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], and spark generals. At the end of August, Minerva decided to send one of the two azure mouths to the Chapel. The officers "almost fought" for the right to stay, she left Shatterhand, and Whitmore sent away. Later, the second company returned to the empress in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], and Minerva felt "twice as powerful". After the Battle of the First Winter, she commissioned Whitmore to guard the town hall. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])

## In "Fantasies"

In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, Whitmore is the captain at Minerva in the First Winter. In Let's Catch Him, he defends the lady from the strict maid of honor by sipping Leyla Talmir at a celebration. ([[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]]) In "Closer - Further" he knows about Mira's connection with Erwin: the charter requires the officer to accompany the empress, and he escorts her to the castle at night. Mira is glad that he is “much older” than her. Almost all of the first company was dismissed, and the ruler was guarded only by his people. On the way, he solves official affairs: the leave of the guards, the relocation of the Shatterhand company away from the clinic. In "Ad-ri-an!" Whitmore, along with Shatterhand, accompanies Mira to Faunterra and inspects the palace before her arrival. Mira hopes that he will be the officer on duty, not Shatterhand.
