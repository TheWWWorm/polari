## Who He Is
Statham is one of the strongest vassals of the [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]], along with the Earls of Lilliday, Main, and [[fleming|Fleming]]. He is the Baron of the Valleys of the Sheep, and more than forty thousand peasants live in his lands. In addition, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] entrusted one of his battalions of mountain guards to him, and for twelve years Statham commanded them along with his own detachments. He was so proud of his military successes that he ceased to be called a baron and spoke of himself only as "Colonel General Statham". His full name is called in "I Want a Pony": "Garret Amanda Gene of the Agatha family."

He's twice the age of [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]. Erwin's face resembled a knight's helmet: a angular contour, a wide jaw, a heavy forehead and eyebrows, under which no eye can be seen. In the novels, he is called the "northern wolf", "mother dog" and "old bull". Marquess Jeremiah Doyle called him "an ass": "A vicious, chain dog, and can't talk to people."

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
In the last chapter of the novel, Statham, along with Count Lilliday and Colonels Horton and [[blekberri|Blackberry]], stands at the bedside of an injured Desmond in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. Desmond calls upon the Earl of Shayland, Lilliday, and Statham, and they repeat the words of their oath. ## In "Only One Star"
### Rebellion and Doyle
At a feast in the First Winter, where Erwin announces the campaign, Statham sits on a platform among the prime vassals. During the mutiny, Erwin divides the army into three parts. ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]]) Seven battalions of Colonel General Statham go southwest to take the city of [[dojl|Doyle]]. Statham threatened the townspeople to burn the city if they did not give up, and thereby only frightened them. He lost two assaults. Then the northerners cut down the grove, built a hundred stonemasons and pounded the walls for a month. Eventually, the Kairs took the lower city. However, Erwin realized that it would be much more difficult to take Upper Doyle on a 200-foot hill, and the oath to burn the city put the general in a stupid position.

Before the arrival of the Duke, Statham went to storm the fortress with "turtles" and lost ninety-six kairs and one hundred and two grays. To Erwin's rebuke, he replied, "You're willing to give the order, my lord," and considered the loss ratio "very beneficial to us." Erwin broke into a scream and then himself was ashamed of this outbreak. The Marquis of Jeremiah called the general "an ass" and "a dog Statham", but Doyle eventually fell. After the fall of Doyle, the generals did not dare to argue with the Duke, but their anger was accumulating: Statham seemed to Erwin to be a samovar on fire with a tightly closed lid. Before heading north, when Erwin decided not to wait for the Northwoods, Statham told him bluntly, "You're making a mistake of youth." Erwin laid siege to him: he would be able to reprimand the Duke of Statham when he became Duke and when his bets began to win.

### Pixie
Erwin later told [[minerva|Minerva]] that Statham was in "the fiercest opposition" to all his plans. The more surprising is that before going to the south, he alone spoke out against the offensive. According to him, Erwin’s strategy worked only at the expense of surprise, and the Duke will lose it. He said, “Not what the enemy wants, my lord.” When the dispute was resolved, the general still advised to advance: to cancel the order twice in a row means to lose authority.

On the eve of the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]], Erwin took Statham and Lilliday aside, handed Statham a sealed envelope, and handed him command. The Duke himself went with the Xs to [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. According to [[itan|Ethan]]'s account, Statham was not obeyed by the entire army: [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]] ignored his orders, and the Earl of Main hammered them selectively. Northwood rushed into a counterattack without orders, Statham had to catch up with him, and a hole formed between the flank and the center. It was used by [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]]. For two hours, Statham and [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] held the center. After losing both flanks, he commanded a general retreat.

### Night in Labelin
The package had orders to withdraw the troops and to transfer as many kire to Erwin's aid as possible. The Severians took refuge in the catacombs of Labelin, and the Silver Fox, entering the city, was ambushed (the night Labelin). ([[labelin|Labelin]]; [[reznya-v-labeline|Night in Labelin]]) Statham's report reached the besieged Faunterra: "Your plan has succeeded, my lord." The enemy army is defeated, the fox retreats to the south. You can't send help by train, because the fox destroyed the rails, and the general promised to come on foot in about twenty days. During the hopeless days of the siege, Erwin recalled that Baron Statham would have crept in, "like an old bull," and his horn could have been grasped.

At [[lid|Leed]], Statham offered to hire the money of all the inhabitants of the city captured in Shayland to leave with the army.
