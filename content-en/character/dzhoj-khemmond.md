**Joy Hammond** is a queer of the [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]], a veteran appointed to the army by the Duke of Desmond. Erwin called him "the most worthy warrior" and one of the best fighters of the Damon-Beauty squad.

## In "Only One Star"
**Guardian Alanis.** In the autumn of 1774, under the walls of [[dojl|Doyle]], [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] was captured by Erwin. She had to be guarded and cared for, and needed a woman to be trusted. Hammond, a mountain guard officer and now commander of the Duke's personal guard, took a wife, "noble Margaret", on the march. Erwin assigned a guest to the Hammonds. Margaret became her companion, and the kair received twenty fighters and five hundred ephemera. "If even one hair falls from her head..." began Erwin. "I swear by my heart, my lord," Hammond replied. Through him, Alanis passed on requests for a meeting to the Duke.

Erwin explained to Alanis that he could not remove the officers appointed by his father: the personal loyalty of many kairs was addressed to their commanders - "Horton or [[blekberri|Blackberry]], or Hammond".

**Joaquin.** When [[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]] came to the Duke with the news, behind the prisoner stood "the most worthy warrior, Captain Joy Hammond." Hammond was instructed to keep Joaquin "in a safe place", and later he took him around the camp. Then the cairn did not let Joaquin into Alanis' tent with a box and flowers: "the mad have nothing to do in the duchess' tent." [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] told me to skip it. If Joaquin came to Alanis, Hammond replied, "She's at my lord's."

**Bail and palace.** On December 3, 1774, the Northmen traveled to Faunterra by train under the guise of the people of the parish. At the Almer outpost, Hammond went to the platform and handed a letter to the commander, Sir Godard, with the seal and signature of Gallard's vicar: "from your suzerain saint." The Northerners missed. In the capital, Hammond led a detachment from Alanis to the Holy See, carried a pennant on a spear and commanded the arrows. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) He explained that the Duke turned off the spark in the palace: in the summer, the prisoner’s defense chief gave out how to do it under torture.

**Defense of the palace.** After taking the Palace of Pen and Sword, Erwin instructed Hammond to restore order: search the backstreets, collect weapons and provisions, interrogate the captive servants. The citadel was defended under the tutelage of Derrek, and the palace under Hammond. He warned that the first attacks only "feel" the defense, and advised Erwin to sleep in clothes. Both times he was right. At the feasts, he made sure that local women tried every dish. Together with Alanis and Damon, he demanded a sortie against the stonemasons and philistines who helped the guards. ([[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon Origin]])

**Death.** In mid-December 1774, during the siege of Major Backfield, a stone from a catapult demolished the tooth of the wall behind which Hammond stood. His death was “quietly accepted.” Erwin later thought that he should have taken someone over 40 to the capital: only Hammond and Derrek were, "and now both are dead."
