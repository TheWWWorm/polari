## Origins
Barney (full name Bernard) is the eldest son of Captain Hyder's younger sister Leed. ([[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]) His father, a cyre from the company of the Leed Wolves, died in the Steppe, and Hyder assumed custody of the widowed sister and her three sons. When the Shavans crushed [[lid|Leed]], the captain risked himself, and even the duke, to save his relatives, but the steppemen managed to break into the sister's house. They beat her up to find out where the money was hidden, and Barney ran out to his mother for help. He was forced into slavery and his younger brothers were not found. Shavans retreated in the winter, in the snow, hungry and threw prisoners wherever they had to, so Hyder considered his nephew dead.

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, Barney appears in the story "Amicable" (Laam, August 1776).

### Trades
The widow of the matchmaker Vanessa-Lilit found out that Leed's nephew would be put up for sale at a large auction of Ganta Farhan's althess, and reported this to Princess Demeter, who did not want to marry [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]. ([[vanessa-lilit|Vanessa-Lilith]]; [[demetra-nellis-lajon|Demetra Nellis-Lyon]]) Vanessa, on the other hand, invited Prince Hector to the auction with the northern guests. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) There Barney, already bought by a thin southerner in a white hooded cloak, overturned a tray of wine, and the Shavan kicked him. Hyder Leed recognized his nephew from thirty yards ("Cold Darkness! It's Barney!"), but the southern man took the boy through the side door. Leed interrupted and mutilated the shavanks who blocked his way, and extorted from the gant that the boy was bought by “timeret’s man”. The prince was outraged by the fight.

At night, Jemis and Leed broke into Timeret's pimp, but he did not send anyone to bid and referred to Barney as a "cowardly idiot" who was shaking all the time. Sheriff [[khalintor|Halintor]] picked up the queers for questioning, and Hector put them under house arrest, promising to look for the boy without haste.

### Searches
Demeter volunteered to “walk” with the groom, and under this cover, Jemis took up the search. At the Black Swan Hotel, the minister said that a white cloak, similar to a student, spent the night there with a boy. At first, Barney squirmed and tried to escape, but after a short conversation, he calmed down and even went for tea for his buyer. Jemis realized that the boy was called the name of a noble buyer - a woman who definitely will not dishonor him - and went to Vanessa Lilith, and through her to the design of Demeter.

### Return
When Jemis publicly broke off the engagement, Demeter explained that she had arranged everything to test the groom, and bought the boy only then to return to her uncle. According to her, Vanessa-Lilit brought Barney to the palace. Seeing his uncle, the boy squealed at him, but in a step from him stretched out in soldierly fashion:
> "Captain Leed, let me report! I am healthy, I have no losses. I will be glad to return home!”

“Freely, eat the darkness,” replied Leed, wiping his eyes and clenching him in his arms.

### Further Destiny
Barney returned safely to his mother. He dreamed of becoming a warm man, putting on a two-color cloak and joining the Leed Wolves, but the famous uncle convinced him that there is a job more worthy than “cut people’s throats”. Barney entered the Technical Academy of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] and became a spark engineer.
