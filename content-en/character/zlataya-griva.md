## Who He Is
The Golden Griva is a Shavan named Yellow Griva in the Steppe. At the court of [[adrian|Adrian]], he began to wear so much gold that he changed his nickname. He is one-handed: the second hand ends in a cult with a hook, and a cup decorated with stones is placed in this hook.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
In the main cycle, his name is not named, but by all indications he appears in the "Shadow of the Great Tree". Adrian told Magda that two "rebel horses" were taken alive and given to Lady in Darkness. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) She kept them in her sleep on the marshy "essentia of the luno-eye", preparing for future use. One of them was Juhan Reis, about the second Adrian said: "One-armed shavana will give Perst, let him shoot with the left." ([[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]]) Coincident one-armedness, Adrian's captivity and time: in the "Fantasies" it is said that the Golden Grive was captured "a year ago", even before the death of the Gnoi-ganta.

## In "Fantasies"
The author calls the stories “Fantasies” possible destinies of heroes. In "Closer - Further" (August 1776) The Golden Grive sits in the execution room of the Pera and Sword Palace, in a chair once beloved by [[minerva|Minerva]]. ([[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]])

### From Prisoner to First Blade
A year ago, he was captured, lost his hand and freedom and waited for the most shameful of deaths. Then he would give his soul to swap places with any horseman of the Gnome-Ganta. But less than six months later, as the [[paul|pus-ganta]] lay in the dust with the horde, and the Golden Gryva dressed in silk. Colonel Backfield, whom Shavan calls a "gaunt", announced to him: "You are Adrian's first blade. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) You will know nothing about rejection.” Soon, the Golden Grove did not know what to want, except for a good battle. Backfield replied that he was already in battle, “only weapons are not Wilhelm’s Ring.”

### Bird
Every day, the Golden Gryve lifts a Bird into the air and circles over the [[faunterra|Faunterra]], penetrating through walls and forests. No army would approach the capital unnoticed. Backfield told him to look for enemies in the city itself. The bird does not hear words, but sees everything: secret meetings, hidden weapons, seals on envelopes and even text on pigeon mail tapes. Golden Grive can't read, so there are two people on duty near Backfield and record everything that Bird shows. According to this information, the guys with batons smash the caches of Adrian's opponents. When Shavan asked to "punish" the enemies himself, Backfield replied: "You are a shining blade. You don't kill pigs."

By the summer, there were no enemies in Faunterra, and the Bird began to fly to distant lands. One day in the First Winter, the Golden Gryva saw Neymir the Werewolf and the two archers of Ganta Cort fall to their knees in front of Minerva and kiss her legs. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]; [[ganta-kort|Ganta Kort]]) He told them to write that they were wriggling “like worms in a pile of dung” and that “these abscesses are not my brothers”: he would be happy to kill them for the lord.

Then Adrian and his wife [[magda-labelin|Magda]] sat next to him. The bird showed them Minerva and [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] alone. Adrian made it clear to his wife that he knew about her rapprochement with Minerva, and Golden Griva added: “The Lord sees through everyone.”

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the autumn of 1776, the Golden Grove served Adrian "for more than a year". He said, "I want to be a sword," and the Lord replied, "You are my blade for a special occasion." Before the election, he was entombed in a cramped crypt in the House of Representatives wall, with a gap for light, a supply of water and food, and a bucket. From there he launched the Bird without a Plot over Faunterra and neighboring lands. He saw Erwin and Iona's performance, their meeting with Darkwater's sorcerers, and the loading of kairs on trains in Arrowback County, but had no connection with Adrian. When Erwin stepped out onto the podium, Adrian clenched his fist. The Golden Grieve punched through the wall and took aim first Erwin, then Iona, also armed with the Ring. Then he made his way outside, to the positions of the scarlet guard, and, leaving the latter, covered the departure of the lord.

At the military council Adrian Shavan asked to allow him to kill the "wolf". Adrian refused: Iona should be the first to open fire on the common people, and only when a dozen people lie down, she should be shot. Before the assault, Golden Grive felt a strange compassion for her: “She is a glorious warrior.” He recalled how he had once fought against the scarlet guard, shot twenty or thirty guardsmen, and cut off his hand when he was thirty-one. Three times asking for permission to shoot, he finally heard, "Okay. Kill it." At the same moment Adrian fell in convulsions. Shavan “understood everything”: the mute [[sofi|Sophie]], sneaking up, killed the lord “sincerely”. He shot and burned it with a feather. Then [[khamfri|Sir Humphrey]] cut off his elbow tendons and crushed his jaw, and the crowd trampled him to death. His last thought was, “That bitch Sophie killed Adrian! I took revenge on her!” On his body were found the Ring of Wilhelm and the Bird without the Flesh. The bird was returned to its owners, the Dukes of the South Road, and the Perst was seized by the Foremother Church.

## Personality
Golden Gryva is a simple and vain warrior, revelling in luxury and gold. He longs for the battle and despises relatives who bowed to Minerva, although he himself serves his former captor. About his fate, he says the steppe proverb: “The river flows, the horse jumps.”
