## Who She Is

The supreme mother of the Chapter Pramatery, the "bearer of the diadem" and the head of the Agat Order. In "Humans and Gods" and "The Shadow of the Great Tree" she is called the mother of Alice, in "Fantasies" - the mother of Alicia; we are talking about the same chapter of the Order of Agatha. In Fantasia, she is gray and old, comes out of the carriage, relying on the hand of an assistant, and easily gets tired of the road. The greatest shrines of her order are the grotto of Kosuli and the fresco "Agatha's Choice" in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]])

## In the main cycle

On June 1, 1775, [[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]], going to war with Erwin, left the organization of the Ecumenical Council against [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s parish to Octavia's mother of the Order of Emilia and Alice's mother: Cordelia told Erwin about her that "the agate is dreaming of seeing you on the throne." ([[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]])

In the summer of 1775, when crowds of poor preachers were gathering in Faunterra, Mother Alice, on the advice of [[minerva|Minerva]], demanded that they be dispersed: they did not have the permission of the Foremother Church, and the permissions were signed by Bishop [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]], "with the right hand of Gallard Almer, who is accused of heresy." Minerva refused, and Alice did not argue, apparently not feeling the full force of the capitulation: the arch-mother of Ellin "is completely plunged into the twilight", and Cordelia went to Almera. ([[ellina|Ellina]])

In The Shadow of the Great Tree, Gallard argued that the Ecumenical Council was made "at the instigation of higher mothers, especially Alice, Octavia, and the now deceased Cordelia."

## In "Fantasies"

"Fantasies" the author calls the possible fates of the heroes.

### “Device” and “Ruthwork”

In the spring of 1776, when the chapter was left without a head after the death of Ellina, Mother Alicia "in white with silver" came with her mother Orlando, Bishop Amessin, the Second of the Five and [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] to the show of [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]]'s speaking device. Even before the show, Abbess Isabella came to the scientist on her behalf. On the test of the record, Alicia "made an attack" against Amessin, quoting Wilhelm's taboo, and the king joked that she "squealed more than others". She outlined the intention of the Church: to give the parishioners a "single truth" - the use of the Rings is a terrible sin. Amessin gave her the right to buy the device, but she politely returned it: the idea of repentance belonged to the monk of the Church of the Forefathers.

The recorded address of the prophet "on behalf of both branches of the Holy Church" in the cathedrals ended with her voice: "I, the highest mother of Alicia of the Order of Agatha, assure the words of Francis-Ilianus." In the summer, she took the king from Laem to Faunterra for church affairs.

### “Closer and Further”

In August 1776, Alicia arrived in the First Winter as a Chapter Ambassador with Amessin: eight carriages and thirty-two horsemen, "only holy numbers". The Chapter entrusted her to examine and sanctify the restored grotto of Kosuli and the fresco "Agatha's Choice", and she was very upset to learn that neither was ready. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) From the coffee with the hanty, which served Erwin, she fell asleep in the middle of the conversation, and waking up, asked him to make peace with Steppe: Ganta Cort has already sent ambassadors.

In the clinic, she was outraged that Minerva forbade [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] to treat with the Hand of the Znaharka and ordered to limit herself to diagnostics. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) But when Amessin demanded from the mistress of repentance for the battle with the Glove, Alicia admitted that the commandments of the Forefather speak only of personal responsibility, and, since Minerva did not kill anyone with her hand, you can not recover from her.

In the Cathedral of the Agatha the Radiant, Erwin asked her if the Mother could come to him. Alicia replied that the Church recognized only four such cases in history, and did not recognize Agatha on the sketch, which he deliberately chose the least similar. Erwin repented in pride, and she imposed a mild penance: a week without meat and alcohol and seventeen prayers before bedtime. Public repentance did not require, because the rumors about the visions “strengthen the faith” of the subjects, but asked for a signed sketch — visible evidence for the future. She then outlined the situation in Faunterra. The appeal of the prophet was a success, but Adrian added his word to him and summoned ordinary people to the capital, his “young men” arrange fights, and the paper money he prints threatens inflation. The financiers of the Chapter believe that at current expenses it is impossible to reduce taxes. Alicia gave Erwin the letters of Count Erroback and told him to promise to become a guarantor of peace in the elections and after them: to keep battalions near the capital, without killing innocents and without influencing the course of the elections. Peace with Steppe, by her terms, Erwin was to conclude before she left. Delighting a new fresco on the pylon, she hinted that "an ancient and deep sympathy" for Amessin would have made her strike him out of the candidates for the archdiocese.

In negotiations with the ambassadors of Ganta Cort, she demanded that the khanid issued by Minerva repent and accept strict penance. After signing the peace, Erwin praised the firmness ("the last word was for you") and promised to report to the Chapter, but immediately reproached him for the fact that the "Agatha's Choice" was never started, and gave two days. Erwin thought Cordelia "couldn't say a kind word without a couple of reproaches."

On the eve of departure, Alicia in confusion told Erwin about the miracle: at night the master painter appeared Agatha the Radiant, sat in a chair and read “Illusions”, and the painter drew it with a birch branch. She called the drawing “divine” and promised to consecrate the fresco that will be written with it, and suggested to explore the symbolism of birch (Erwin advised to write this work to her). The phenomenon was actually staged by Erwin: he asked the ghost of Dalia Raven to take the appearance of Agatha and appear to the painter. ([[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]]) Alicia also consecrated a new fresco on the pylon, where Iona heals the wounded Lord with the Hand of the Infirmary against the background of the blazing First Winter, and this plot, according to Erwin, became canonical.

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the fall of 1776, Erwin summoned Alicia's mother at Faunterra, and it became clear by the swiftness of her arrival that "the captain was terrified". She admitted that the chapter without a head is "weaker than the Forefather's branch: they have a head, we do not." Higher mothers would prefer to see Francis-Ilian as the archpriest, but the election was held ahead of schedule and Amessin won. The Chapter forbade the introduction of kairs into the capital, so as not to start a war of branches, and insisted on the early election of the lord, because in winter the Chamber is not going to. Since then, the Chapter of Pramatery has become an ally of Erwin. In the cathedral of Wilhelm, Alicia, together with Francis-Ilian, listened to Minerva's repentance. She recalled that she was “convicted in financial games with the highest clergy,” and threatened with fire if she uses Ulyanina Dust. In the besieged House, she banned the use of Perst. When the cardinals confessed to the forgery, Alicia announced: “Bishop Amessin is deprived of the mantle of the priest,” and demanded to take him under arrest.
