Ef was a young knight of Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], a jailer and an unwilling ally of [[minerva|Minerva]] in the fall of 1774. His whole story is told in the chapters of the "Spark" of the novel "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]".

## Appearance and Personality
Eph was "young, if not young, arrogant, proud and hot-tempered", short, choleric and eternally dissatisfied with something. The soldiers of the detachment respected him on a par with Mother Inzhi - this respect is "easy to take for fear." He wore either a mother-of-pearl camsol on a bare shirt, or a black velvet with gold, and on his belt he wore a spark sword and a long spark dagger with a twisted handle. He spoke harshly and rudely, Northwood called "squints", was versed in military affairs and liked to insert a word about other people's campaigns. Mira believed that he "loves no one but himself and the count." When he was pressed against the wall, the inflated importance flew away, and he shrugged his nose like a boy.

Vittor appreciated his instinct: “Life experience is that the cat sneezes, but the smell is like that of a terrier.” Inji said: “He is proud that he is a golden candelabrum, but he knows the case, and the owner loves him.”

## History

### Captive
In early October 1774, Eph and Inji Price ("The Couple") with twenty soldiers took Mira from the monastery of Ulyana Sad and through Almera and Smokey Dahl brought to [[uejmar|Waymar]]. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]; [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) Vittor instructed him to take care of the guest, and Martin put his man, Inji, to him; together they reported to the count what Mira was doing, with whom he spoke and to whom he wrote.

On the first night, Peace tried to flee. After hiding the fruit knife from the tray, she asked to the bathroom, and Eph volunteered to hold it. In the semi-basement corridor of the World, she depicted the horror at the door of the storeroom, put a knife between his legs, told him to put his hands against the wall, took a spark dagger out of his knife and struck the buttocks with a discharge. Eph collapsed without feeling; Mira locked him in the pantry, but Inge caught her at the gate. On the morning of Inji said that Ef is alive, but "jumps like a crooked deer" and is angry: "bad piggy, did not know where to prick." At court it was joked that Mira had struck him "into the very heart" that Eph had in his buttocks. In revenge, he sent the prisoner vulgar romance novels.

### Worlds cipher
Mira secretly warned Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] of the Northwoods' collusion with the rebel, encrypting the message in a book order for the shopkeeper Pierre. Eph took Lindsay’s servant’s book, “deceived”, spent the night over it, found the system of marks and read the message, and then “shocked” Pierre and took the envelope. ([[lindsi|Lindsey]]) Vittor, however, ordered that the books be sent and sent Adrian a letter of the same content. For the interception, the count generously rewarded Efa and Inji.

### Lindsey
Lindsay's maid soon disappeared; the mayor announced that she was ill and went to the village, and "Sir Francis Murray had already taken over all the expenses." Mira, investigating the disappearance, found out that Lindsay had a secret gentleman who called her on dates with drawings of watches (she could not read). In early November, Eph locked Mira in the hunting hall and demanded an answer that she sniffed, but Mira laid out her conclusions, and he admitted: “Yes, really, I took her to the guard... A knight and a servant.” He hid the connection, ashamed of the count, and tore it when Vittor returned. He denied the murder: “I really didn’t kill her! The word of a knight!” Mira took from him the word of a knight to help her in her search.

### Secret passage and death
When Iona's maid Jane went missing, Mira was put in a lower-circle cell. Eph came to her reproaching her: “I was hoping for you in vain. You are not so smart," but after listening to her, he led her out of the cell with a weapon and a lantern. Behind the iron door of the dungeon of the world found a secret stone, and Eph first climbed into a narrow passage. On the way, he said that only Inji knew about the code with the clock, to whom he had drunk on the schooner, and that Inji was a fugitive convict and a servant not of the count, but of [[martin-shejland|Martin]]. So Mira realized that the girls were being kidnapped by Martin. The move led to the cellar under the burnt-out abode on the outskirts of Waymar; the place Eph recognized by the stamps on the barrels.

Inge caught them there. Eph drew his sword — “You and Martin stole Lindsay and Jane!” — and told him to drop his belt. Inji threw a belt with a dagger in his face, stepped close, too close for the sword, and when Eph snatched the spark dagger, he threw his left hand to his face: there was a narrow stiletto in his palm. “The dagger froze an inch from the target,” and Eph sat on the floor with a blood-soaked face. “I always have a couple of them in stock,” Inji explained his nickname. Martin and his Pharmacist took Mira to the basement.

## After death
Inzhi covered the move, and the move to the count's chambers "slightly opened" so that everyone would think that Eph helped Mira escape through Vittor's room. Later, Mire dreamed of Eph "with a hole instead of an eye" dancing with Lindsay and then pushing her away. In preparation for a conversation with Adrian, she described him as a "self-absorbed boy" who was assigned to guard her.
