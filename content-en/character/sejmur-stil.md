## Who He Is

Seymour Steel is the cairn of the [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]]. He and his brother [[brant-stil|Brant]] were first-generation kairs, born shepherds and the swords of the duke. Seymour saw his first death when he was twelve years old. On his wrist is engraved a falcon under the full moon. After Iona's marriage to Earl [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], he served as captain of her personal guard at [[uejmar|Waymar]]. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) According to him, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] instructed him to protect his sister even in case of disloyalty of the count and allocated swords for this purpose according to the then number of the castle garrison.

Iona believed that there was a special trust between them, which happens only between the lady and her vassal: she could not imagine what Seymour would be afraid to tell her. For the rest of us, Seymour was dangerous. He understood the Northern law literally and did not see the guilt in the murder of a commoner who contradicted him. [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] next to Iona compared him to a "wolf by the panther."

## In "Only One Star"

In the winter of 1774–1775, [[minerva|Minerva]], a guest in Waymar, often had breakfast at the same table with Iona, her companion Jane, and Cyre Seymour. In a letter to a friend, she describes such a breakfast. Iona proudly revealed that Seymour's brother, Brant Steel, was among the six wrestlers who brought the Northerners victory. Seymour shone: the brother had already received the rank of captain. When Mira stated that Adrian would strike "the day Duke Erwin doesn't wait", he was the first to ask what the emperor would do.

After Mira told about the secret passage in the count's office, Iona clapped her hands three times, and Seymour flew into the room. Iona told him to search the prison, and the guest was transferred to an underground cell of the lower circle. Later that night, Seymour, led by two dozen horsemen, along with Iona, intercepted a detachment of [[itan|Ethan]] and [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] on the road, taking Mira, Jane, and bound [[martin-shejland|Martin]]. He learned from the soldiers to build a "box" and demanded to lay down their weapons. The bloodshed stopped the Peace itself.

## "A Doll on the Throne"

### In Waymar

In Martin's dungeon, Seymour pulled an elixir bubble from a hiding place in the wall. Upon learning that Martin was making a drug out of dead people, he stabbed him in the face with the sword so that he flattened his nose, and put a blade to his neck. Iona told him not to kill, but to lock up the prisoner until the Count's decision. When news of Erwin's victories came, Seymour informed Iona that two birds had flown from the castle to Labelin and Faunterra. He bluntly said that Count Vittor had switched to Adrian, "having learned of the defeat at Pixie", and doubted that the blame for the murders lay with Martin alone. Seymour offered to test the count by giving him an elixir to drink, but Iona refused.

### On the road to Leixiti

At the end of March 1775 Seymour commanded forty riders of the escort Iona on the road from Faunterra. [[luiza|Louisa]]'s rail guards, the brothers Bud and Het, did not give way, and Het called him a "shit." Seymour brought the brothers and their sister Sally to their knees and told Joaquin to kill one of the brothers, otherwise he would kill both: "My law is the Northern law." Joaquin recognized the coat of arms on his wrist and said he had seen the death of "Lieutenant Steel" while defending the palace. Seymour turned pale, gave him his hand, and named himself. He forgot about his victims in a second: “Let them get the vans off the road.”

In Iona's carriage, Seymour initially praised Joaquin: "I like this guy." But then he examined his palm and doubted that he was a hired shooter. When Iona suspected that Joaquin was an agent of the Puppeteer, Seymour held his hand on the dagger, and then pushed the prisoner out of the carriage.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

### Captive

On the way to Waymar, Joaquin once rushed to Seymour to take the dagger away. Kair put it under the solar plexus with one stroke: "You are not in control of yourself, sir. It’s a sign of a bad warrior.” He drowned in the fire when the prisoner tried to set fire to the ship. When Joaquin dared Iona about love, Seymour cut him short: "You're not the one asking questions."

### Martin and the Doctor

On May 5, 1775, Iona instructed Seymour to release Joaquin, monitor Martin, and establish oversight of the secret move. Seymour immediately offered to "remove" Martin. He reported that there were almost no pigeons left to communicate with the First Winter, and the count brought the garrison to three full mouths, and asked for reinforcements. He openly said that he did not trust the count, but Iona forbade it.

Then Seymour brought to the castle, with a bag on his head, a doctor Galuers, who examined Martin. After hearing that Martin was treated only with drugs for fear, Seymour demanded: “I’ll kill him. Today. Allow me." Iona refused. Then Seymour coldly stamped "So sure" and walked out.

### The One-Eyed Massacre

Around May 20, Iona received Erwin’s letter and ordered Joaquin to be brought to her “extremely carefully.” Seymour and the Grey found him in the basement of the One-Eyed Inn. Gray punched Joaquin, and then barber Harry Hog stood up for a friend with Boundary Bram and a dozen drunk cardholders. ([[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]]) Seymour, without mail, wounded in the side, shoulder and thigh, interrupted the attackers with a dagger. Five were killed and eight were wounded. Joaquin, who ran out into the alley with a spark dagger, he took with his bare hands, removing the sword in the sheath: "So it will be more honest." At the gate of the castle, the count's garrison demanded the surrender of the prisoner and shot a horse under Seymour.

He waited for praise: thirteen people participated in the battle, and “it was not difficult.” With the prisoner, he said, he was extremely careful, as ordered. Iona cut off his own dagger strip from the bloody cloak and deprived him of his position: the command passed to the cyre [[irving|Irving]]. For any new harm to peaceful people, she threatened to take away his cape. Count Vittor kicked Seymour out of the office with one word: "Out." Harry Hog then told Joaquin that Iona had sent Seymour to "kill five", and did not consider himself a murderer: "Am I like Seymour?" Iona decided that Seymour knew Hog's face and therefore did not touch him.

Passing command, Seymour asked Irving to give him service away from Iona, believing that her appearance was unpleasant to her. Irving placed him with his greys to guard the underground passage in the ruins of the monastery. Irving stood up for him: Seymour "accurately complied with the northern law." Iona replied that they were not in the North. She later admitted that her mistake was in the wrong directions, and blamed the victims for the whole week.

### Coup

On May 27, 1775, Iona came to the monastery behind Seymour and led the sunsets with Joaquin and Harry Hog through an underground passage. In the evening, already in the castle, she revealed her plan to him. Seymour soberly listed the forces: a company of penny-miners, fifty shooters in the galleries, the same number in the towers, a hundred reserves, and they have thirty-seven people. “Do you believe it is possible?” Iona answered, “I believe,” and he thanked her with a smile that “looked like a splinter.” Before the case, he asked if she was afraid, and said that he first saw death at the age of twelve.

At 10:15, Iona stabbed [[garold-greta|Sir Harold]]'s castellan with a knife through the page, and Seymour twisted his squire's neck. The two of them poured oil on the donjon's ladder and threw a lamp at it, cutting off the count with fire. In the yard, when Iona killed Captain Leus, Seymour stabbed his aide-de-camp. He warned that the Count would use the Ring. When Iona went to the reserve phalanx under the fire of Perst, Seymour rushed to cover her, and she sent him back, into the shade. Gray, whom Seymour left in the monastery, blocked the underground passage, and the count could not run through it.

Deadly wounded Harry Hog fired from Finger of Wilhelm, and Joaquin directed his hand. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) The first shot of Seymour's kair "burned through". He was followed by Kair Brandon and Gray. Iona screamed and soon surrendered (see Waymar coup). ([[perevorot-v-uejmare|Coup in Waymar]])

## In "Humans and Gods"

Lady Cordelia received a letter about Iona's death from "the only surviving queer from Seymour Steel's detachment." Joaquin, having learned to shoot from Perst, admitted that he was a glorious swordsman, but not the best: Ice, "the late Seymour Steel" and the Duke's Xes would have defeated him quickly. In a fight with Baron Dorcaster, he seemed to him like a turtle after a "lightning fight with Seymour."

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] lists Seymour and Brant Steel among those who, after death, turned into "stone idols" - monuments that no living person remembers. Joaquin boasted that he was fighting "Kair Seymour, captain of Iona's personal guard", but was told that he was "beaten like a puppy". Iona herself, enumerating her losses, recalls: “Seymur Steel died defending me.”

## In "Fantasies"

In the fantasy "Let's catch him" Iona in a spiritualistic session says that he often sees ghosts of scoundrels, but would like to someday meet the spirit of a good man: "Seymur Steel or Alanis Almera."
