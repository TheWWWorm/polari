## Origins
Juhan Reis is the son of Count Damir Reis, the ruler of Reis County, and a descendant of the legendary Gant Royhar of Lazy Wola, who built Rei-Roy Castle. ([[rejs|Reis (County)]]) Above the gate of the castle stands a huge statue of a warrior with the head of a bull, which is why the Shavans call the city the City of the Flame Bull. After his father's death in an ambush in Lacey, Johan inherited Rei-Roy and the huge herds around him - according to [[chara|Chara]], a hundred thousand heads, or even more; he was richer than anyone else in those parts. ([[zasada-v-lejsi|Ambush at Lacey]]) Shavans born on the Brokke River called these lands the possessions of the Ganta Johan Reis. According to the Polari Land Guide, the Reis are only nominally considered to be Reis' suzerains, and the measure of their power depends on personal qualities: the Shavans are not inclined to obey someone who is not respected. Johan was young and inexperienced in the war, so the leader of the horde of shavana was recognized not by him, but by Moran of the Steppe Fire, and Johan himself considered Moran the best leader. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) [[ganta-groza|Ganta Thunderstorm]] described him as a "glorious young man" - not hot and not daring, but reserved, respectful of elders. ([[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]])

Around 1773, in preparation for the fair of brides, Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] made an alliance with Damir Reis: Flights received a trade route through the Smokey Dahl for the sale of livestock, Labelins - the support of the West for Valerie Gracend. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]; [[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]]) To strengthen the union, the Duke proposed the marriage of Juhan to [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]. Johan "bricked and twisted his nose", but was courteous enough; once after the feast, they even kissed, but the flights were delayed with the marriage contract, and it did not come to an engagement.

## In "Humans and Gods"
### Under Moran
In the summer of 1775, Moran's horde stood in Rei-Roy and ate Juhan: every day cost him a hundred cows. At first, the Horde paid generously for food, women and wine, but as the Lithuanian trophies melted, they stopped paying. Johan regretted his father's wealth and was ashamed of this feeling, and to Cort's reproaches he replied: "I will give anything for the glory of the Steppe!"

At the council in Moran's tent, he argued with the cunning gant Cort: Origina, they say, can not choose the master, and the "wolf sister" rebelled against the puppet master's husband, so that through her he will get nothing. When Kort evaded the rumours, Moran ordered Juhan to be told, and he blushed, bluntly telling the slander: that the Companions had broken the chief's will along with his legs, and Rebecca's refusal deprived him of his male power. Moran praised him: “Here is the answer of the real Shavan!”

### The Coming of the Gantts
Here, one of the first horsemen of Juhan reported on the detained detachment with two "slaves" - [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] and [[chara|Chara]]. Juhan ordered to speak aloud because he had no secrets from the chief; by order of Moran, he ordered the disarming of the aliens by a hundred archers and helped the lame chief to rise. It was Johan who first noticed the gray dust seeping through [[paul|Paul]]'s shirt, and, seeing a swarm of flies, trembled with understanding:
> “You are the devil, O Lord of hosts! You are made of flies and worms, like death itself!”
He was the first to see that the frozen arrows continue to crawl, and when Paul asked about the main enemy, Stepi shouted: “Wolves!” When Paul turned Moran into a frozen figure, he called Juhan to help along with the "rayon" and "gaunt with cunning eyes" - Cort. ([[ganta-kort|Ganta Kort]])

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Son of Gnome
When Paul threw a sack of [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] to the Shavans, Johan was among the first daredevils to pick up the Object; as a respected leader, no one dared rob him in the ensuing massacre. Before leaving Rei-Roy, Paul chose which of the three - Cort, Ondei or Johan - to leave the city. Ondei offered to leave Juhan ("Rei-Roy is his city"), and Juhan himself only bowed his head: "Ganta pus, you know better." Paul left Onday.

Johan became part of Paul's inner circle and, according to Alanis, idolized him. In July 1775, the horde marched from Rei-Roy to the east and crossed Holliwell. At the feast after taking the [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]], when Paul announced that he was going to help the besieged Waymar Shayland, Johan did not believe that Desmond Origin himself was there: "The old bat is there?.. They say he was sick.” At the council of the horde, he, together with the gant Cort, stood for a march on [[faunterra|Faunterra]] - she is closer and richer than the cold North - while Chara and Sormah called for the First Winter; Paul decided to go both ways, and Reis and Cort boasted that they would be able to do what Moran could not: defeat the Yankees. Paul then, along with Johan, the Hanids, and a thousand horsemen, moved forward on Almera, leaving Alanis with the main horde. At a feast at Gallard's Priory in Fliesse, Juhan timidly asked what the "good news" was, and lamented why the Gnout Ganta had not come to them before, and when Gallard had allocated a thousand ephesions to each khanid, calmly said that he would distribute his thousand to the riders: gold did not bother him, for him it was a matter of justice. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]; [[fliss|Fliss]]) Paul rubbed him on the shoulder and said, “You’re like a son to me.”

### Right hand
When Alanis advised Paul to divide the horde into two corps and give the subject of communication to the commander of the capital, he, of course, called Johan: Kort was older and more experienced, but the army, led by a loyal Reis, would listen to Paul, "like his own hand." In Fliesse, Paul appointed Juhan with his "right hand": he gave a dozen Rings and twelve thousand horsemen (later Paul spoke of Alanis about fifteen thousand) and ordered to take Faunterra, Arden and any other cities of the Lands of the Crown. Paul had shown the Gants in advance how he would speak to Johan through the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]], and the orders of the young Reis were to be carried out by the shavana as the orders of the Gnoi-ganta himself. In private, Paul warned him of Nathaniel, "a cunning kribola": if he met him, he would kill him immediately. Johan promised to take the skin off the traitor. Paul himself admitted that Johan's army partly serves as a decoy diverting [[minerva|Minerva]]'s forces to Back.

### Hike and defeat
At the end of July, while Paul was sailing with six thousand of the best horsemen through the Smokey Dahl to Waymar, the southern horde of Juhan moved to the capital. Shavans outwitted everyone: instead of storming Arrowback, they crossed the Back downstream, ahead of both the Silver Fox regiments and the kairs. The southern horde plundered Smallden, Caseworth and other cities and from Orwell could go to Marengo, Arden or the capital; in August, Faunterra found herself in mites between Adrian coming from the north and Johan coming from the south. Paul bragged to Wittor that "the younger Reis are obedient as a puppy" and would take Faunterra at one word; Vittor doubted it. Paul told Johan to look for Nathaniel, who had escaped from Faunterra.

According to Minerva, [[mark-voron|Mark]] planted false information: as if the empress with the treasury, the Eternal Hilt and the Glove were traveling by train to Arden, and then - as if her train had broken down in a small town, where she had only one battalion and Nathaniel. Johan with the avant-garde and the best riders rushed for prey. The horde split: many horses were worse, and others were tired of howling and continued on to the Arden. At the end of August, near the town of Patrick's Chapel in the Crownlands, the vanguard of Johan came across the shelves of [[adrian|Adrian]]. An inexperienced young leader led the exhausted horsemen in a rapid attack, the Rings set fire to the train that blocked the road and turned archers into flight, but Minerva and Nathaniel intervened from the sky on a flying ship: Navi threw Shavans into the air and dropped barrels on them. Juhan was captured, the vanguard was defeated, many ghanas turned into ordinary gangs, and the remnants of the southern horde under the rule of the ganta Cort retreated to Almera.

### The Prisoner of Adrian
In early September, when Adrian's victorious regiments entered [[faunterra|Faunterra]], a beaten, chained Johan was brought to Adrian and Lady in Darkness: in his veins flowed primrose, making him valuable for managing Objects. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]; [[pervokrov|Firstblood]]) The young man spat and scolded Adrian as a tyrant and jackal, and Birai's gauntlet as a "slug" serving "creepers". ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]) Adrian threatened him with a painful punishment and severed legs, after which the soul will not fall into the horde of Wanderers; Johan turned pale. It was not the threat that broke him, but Magda: recalling that their fathers never lied to each other, she promised him freedom and swore to Sophia that Gnoi-ganta would not be harmed. Johan then linked Adrian with Paul through the Voice of God — and spoiled the negotiations by saying that Nathaniel had escaped on a flying ship. From this conversation, Paul learned about the defeat and announced to his generals that the southern army was no more.

Adrian promised to let Juhan go, but kept him in his sleep on the swamp "essentia of the mooneye"; Magda, wrinkling with swamp magic, reminded her husband that Johan was promised freedom, in which the cherished dreams come true. Two Shavans were taken alive: Juhan and a one-armed prisoner, whom Adrian was going to give Perst to shoot with his left hand. By all indications, this prisoner is the Golden Gryve of Fantasia. ([[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]]) In October, as Adrian’s army was marching north through the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], a shadow-weary Johan woke up and shouted to Adrian: “You’re dead, jackal! I killed you!” — and in a whisper “cribola sting”, convincing him that all this was a nightmare, shot a dog (“Origin wolf”) from Perth and broke the poplar (“Tower of the First Winter”), but the Worm could not run. “I’m scared, I want to wake up,” he babbled in a childish way.

In late October and early November, Adrian spent several hours a day with Lady-in-the-Dark, the Prophet and Johan, preparing the Items for battle, and then the court moved to Main. After much training, Johan, barely awake, steered the Bird without the Flesh, through which Adrian's court in Main watched the Battle of the First Winter; one day he did not understand the order and led the Bird to a basket of balloons instead of taking her away. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) He also at the suggestion of the assassin set in motion the Womb, in which Adrian imprisoned [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]].

## Death
On December 3, 1775, the day after the Battle of the First Winter, Johan, still asleep and exhausted by Bird, brought four Shavans to Adrian's chamber in Main and put them in one of the three chairs at the Subject. Through the Voice of God in his hand, Adrian negotiated with the Origins and demanded that the deconstructor remain "in the hands of Johan Reis" under the protection of his people. Finally, Adrian ordered Johan to destroy the Hand of the Znaharka, the healing Item worn by [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]. Magda rushed to the Shavan to steal the Object from it, but at the same moment Johan snorted, stuck his nails in his neck and collapsed dead on his face on the table at the foot of the Bird. Lady-in-the-Dark defined the poison as "the pill of Black Mary", the deadly poison of the hour-long action. Magda took the blame, but realized that she had poisoned Johan with a joke [[menson|Manson]], to whom his wife Karen gave poison. With Johan's death, Adrian lost his "sole trump card" - he had no bearers of Objects - and Magda forced him to seek peace with the Origins. Minerva got the official version: Flight poisoned Magda.

## In "Fantasies"
The author calls the stories “Fantasies” possible destinies of heroes. In "Closer - Further" briefly recounts the fate of the southern horde. In August 1775, the horde split in Fliesse, and the southern part under the leadership of Johan Reis and Gant Cort sacked a dozen cities in Almera and the Crownlands. ([[ganta-kort|Ganta Kort]]) Not far from Faunterra, the vanguard faced the imperial scarlet guard and was defeated, and Johan was captured. The rest of the horde was taken to Almera. Later, the birds brought to the Steppe news that during the First Winter the Ganta Pus, Johan Reis and many prominent leaders died (in fact, Johan died the next day in Main). Court ordered that the greatest memorial bonfire seen by the Steppe should be laid out in honor of the fallen, and he himself thought: “Reiss, Ondei, and the Gaunt Pnoe fell into the dust. I am the ruler of the Steppe!”
