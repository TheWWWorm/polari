## Biography
### Before the start of the cycle
Richard is the firstborn of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] and [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]], the former heir to the First Winter. It was called the beauty and pride of the northern knighthood. Erwin recalled that his brother loved swords and fights, was distinguished by bear health, was a desperate warrior and not for years a skilled commander. He knew no fear and did not think about palace intrigues. Richard agreed with his father about everything, but not out of fear, but because they thought the same way. Richard imitated Desmond in severity, cruelty, fighting techniques and riding manner and even got a cat, although he loved dogs, because his father had a cat. The mother said that the first child was nursed by one nurse and four kire, so he learned the word “sword” before “mother”. He was a passionate hunter and went to the woods with a vaga of the same avid vassals, while his father, Erwin and Iona did not like hunting.

On the younger brother, book [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], Richard looked down and called him “small”. He taught him how to kick knives and said in training: “Slip to the left! Cross over!" According to Lady Sophia, Erwin learned to speak between the lines at the age of five so that Richard did not notice the mockery. Together with his father, Richard liked to scare little Erwin with stories of the terrible massacre of the Origins over traitors: his father did it for the love of harsh justice, and Richard for the sake of impression. In a hot delirium in the Beyond, Erwin dreamed how his brother in training repeatedly throws his wooden blade, knocks a blow to the chest and hits his face, saying: “It’s amazing how you can not do anything at all!” In the Cathedral of the First Winter, on church holidays, Richard stood next to his father as an heir, and Erwin and Iona were in front, in place of the children; together with his father, he took the ten-year-old Erwin to the family tomb. Erwin recalled arguing hundreds of times with his brother “to the point of hoarseness, and even blood” and eventually admitted his rightness.

In August 1756, the young Richard, Grey Colonel [[blekberri|Blackberry]], accompanied his father to [[uejmar|Waymar]], where the Origins guarded the Lodge of the Seventeenth Gift; seven-year-old Erwin was left to "preserve the First Winter". Young [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] deliberately placed it to himself. Richard admitted that he considers himself the chosen one of Agatha the Radiant, and asked Vittor to find out the secret of the lodge, which his father hid from him. When, at the suggestion of Duke Aiden Almer, the emperor handed over the guard of the lodge to his guards, Richard considered it a humiliation for his House. Taking Vittor by the gate, he demanded to fulfill the promise the same night. Richard later recalled that he had been with Blackberry "all through the War on Objects".

Around 1772, Richard, on behalf of his father, sailed on a caravel from the [[belomorye|Belomorye]] to the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] for simple negotiations with the Westerners, taking only one escort ship (in early media - from the port of Lilliday). Both caravels disappeared without a trace, and the family considered him dead. Since that year, the Duchess of Sofia wore only black with platinum.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark corrects the author: according to her references, Richard sailed on a schooner, not on a caravel. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) The second schooner accompanied him only in dangerous waters near the Granny Islands and then turned back. Only one vessel was missing, she said, the one the Lord was on.

### Under the name Ice
Under his real name, Richard does not appear in these novels. In "Only One Star" [[minerva|Minerva]] thought that Richard disappeared in the Ice Sea, and the route of his caravel ran very close to the lands of the Shaylands. She suggested that the prisoner of Waymar prison could get there just after the death of his caravella and that the Shaylands could keep Erwin's brother alive "like a trump card."

In [[paul|Paul]]'s heretical brigade there was a northerner called Ice. In the summer of 1774, he led a detachment of a dozen raiders from Shayland across the lake to the Duchy of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] - he was seen at Smoky Distance, in Soltown and in Labelin - on the trail of a merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]], to whom Vittor gave the Light Sphere for sale. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) Harmon took refuge in the monastery, where the monks made fakes; the Ice Squad burned the monastery to the ground, and the fake Ice Sphere found in the ruins, according to Erwin, took the Puppeteer to Almera. Erwin himself learned about this in May 1775 from Maurice Labelin: witnesses called the leader of the raiders a northerner named Ice. In A Doll on the Throne, fugitive convict Inge Price recalled how in December 1774, Ice and a partner named Rebro came to the [[dzhek-bakler|Jack Buckler]] Inn in the woods by the road to Fliss. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) Inge described him as tall, young, no older than thirty, beautiful, except for too sharp cheekbones and cold wolf eyes, with a thin scar on the upper lip. The ice almost imperceptibly quickly evaded the crossbow bolts. He explained what is called Ice, because he comes from the North, and that he loves dogs: he did not kill the guard dog, but fed it with meat. He interrupted the guests and owners, cut off the feet of the moneylender and killed Rebro, who stole the Absolut scheme from the commander. Wounded by a bolt to the side, he survived. Erwin later admitted that he suspected his brother in Ice, barely hearing the description of this massacre. Among the victims of Ice were Aunt and Uncle Baby girl, who was then sheltered by Inji. When questioned by [[mark-voron|Mark]] Inji, he repeated that the scheme had Rebro, and the Ice then dealt with him, and warned: "Ice is a special beast."

In the Yanmei Hunt, Richard is mentioned only in Erwin’s mind under his own name: he recalled how his brother taught him to beat off thrown knives, and on the train he told Lady in Darkness and Francis-Ilian that, having been born the second son with his brother, “the pride of the North, the kaire of kairs,” he did not hope to rule the First Winter. In the spring of 1775, special awards were awarded in the capital for information about Ice, along with Paul, the Puppeteer and the Rib.

On May 28, 1775, Ice appeared in person in Arden's tomb. Following Paul into the hall, where Erwin's cairns ambushed the sarcophagus of Lady Ingrid, a second masked fighter entered, with gray-steel northern eyes, and with "predatory fun" asked the commander to let him forward: "Slow creatures." Erwin, who watched the fight through the viewing gap, immediately realized: “This is who the Ice is.” After the explosion of the gas, Ice shouted "Zalp!" burning keirs with fire from Perst and beat with a lash, repulsed the knife of Forty-Two, and when Paul ordered him to shut up, he reported: "The Hall is clear. "Fifteen people lost." Having found among the dead a living younger Horton, the Colonel's son, Ice left him alive as a "gift to the Duke" and with hatred ordered to convey "best wishes". He had a drop of the sun, and he gave it to Paul.

### In "Humans and Gods"
After the massacre in Erwin’s tomb, there was no doubt who the Ice was. The prisoner [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] saw on the vessel of the brigade of a high warrior in a half-mask. His gray, steely eyes seemed familiar to her. He gave Paul a shiny piece of jewelry, called Alanis “Agatha’s granddaughter” and promised to kiss if she survived. Paul then sent him with a soldier named Beaver in a fishing boat to "dance" in Waymar.

On June 9, 1775, the Ice, soaked and deathly tired, appeared at Waymar's gate and brought the Count a Drop of Sun. ([[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]) According to him, the brigade would not have passed on the straight roads: he got by himself, first with Bobr, then without him. Vittor greeted him with tears of joy. Upon learning that the count had "infused" the [[pervokrov|firstblood]] into Iona, Ice wished to see her, and in the cage the captive Iona recognized his brother in him. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]])

Iona swore that he knew nothing about the death of his ship, but Richard considered it a bad game. Upon learning that [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] had beaten her, he knocked him down and, stepping on his throat, made him ask, "My Lord, let me beat your sister." He then allowed it and said his sister was "always trash." Later, he came to Iona only for ridicule. Richard demanded repentance from his sister, and then forced her to rewrite the fake confession in connection with Erwin and the conspiracy against his brother. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]])

Later, Richard, together with Hash Eilish and Martin, assured Joaquin, who was tormented by conscience, that Alanis torture was only a "quick way of inquiry", and himself accompanied the stretcher with Alanis dying into prison; when Paul bowed over her, Ice, calling him commander, respectfully said, "Enough."

At night, in the courtyard of the ruined monastery, he taught Joaquin and [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] the treatment of the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]. He showed a "ball" and a "flash", explained the command words spoken backwards, and beat Martin for aiming at people. Joaquin shot better than the teacher. Vittor, however, did not fully trust Richard and secretly instructed Joaquin to kill him if he turned against the Count.

On the night of June 19 in the port of Waymar, Richard went to the landing of the northerners. He openly called himself Richard Sophia Jessica, the true heir to Desmond, and accused Erwin and Iona of hiring Assassins to sink his ship. He said that he had been swimming in ice water for two hours, he had been picked up by fishermen, and the port doctor wanted to take away both his legs and his arm. Vittor Shayland, who happened in that port, healed him in six months, and Richard read the report on the interrogation of the allegedly caught murderers and believed him. He called his brother for a fight.

Captain Morgan Richard disarmed, catching his blade with a dagger guard and a bare left palm. He adopted this technique of the Blackberry family when he served with the colonel as a grouse. He then overcame the four Latvians by naming out loud four of the five family techniques of the Origins: "Fresh Blood", "Mountain Thunder", "Alicienna" and "Fist of the Lightbearer". He had his cheek cut in a fight. However, the Kairs refused to admit to him, "We don't know you." Fifty crossbows aimed at his chest, and Richard threw a sword and gave the Colonel a letter from Iona. Erwin read it and ridiculed it as a fake, and then Richard realized that he had a double in front of him. Crushed, he fell to his knees with the words "Have mercy on me, Light One... Agatha!". It was a signal, and Joaquin opened fire from Perst. Richard retreated to the castle with the wounded Joaquin. At the advice of Wittor, he accused General Horis of cowardice, openly admitted for the first time before sunset that he was Erwin's brother and heir to the First Winter, and bitterly said that the "puppy" did not dare to come even for the sake of his sister and sent a "dressed". ([[oris|General Oris]]) Vittor gave him the key to Iona's bedroom and instructed him to bring it to the wall as a shield against the kairs. Erwin himself in those days said that his brother “disappeared for two years to serve heretics”, did not win wars and was “stupid”.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Commander of the Chosen
During the siege of Waymar, Richard was one of Vittor's advisors. When the northerners hung a scarlet flag and a banner saying "Only with Iona", he explained that his father refused to negotiate. According to him, the Great House Origin can not be blackmailed: two dozen of his ancestors died in captivity or under torture. Joaquin noticed that despite all the hatred for his father, Ice admires him. When the Northerners dug a well in the castle, Richard, along with Fleming, supported Vittor's plan: to send General Horis's sunsetrs into a night attack so that they would "wash away the shame with blood". When the men of the count killed a double Erwin in the castle, who penetrated there under the guise of a sentry, Richard kicked the corpse: “He portrayed my brother! I finally got what I deserved.”

On 2 August 1775, Alanis struck Paul and Vittor in Waymar's chapel. Richard, along with Fleming and Joaquin, scattered the Shavans who rushed to defend Paul. Over the wounded Paul, he threatened to cut off his arms and legs and bury him alive, so that he gave the key to the salvation of Vittor. Paul said, "Transport..." Even before that, Paul mockingly asked Ice why Origin was still alive, as if Ice had killed him in a duel. After the healing of Vittor, the Count asked Richard about his dreams; he replied, "About revenge" - and demanded the return of the legitimate possessions: "I am the Duke of the First Winter!" Vittor shook his hand and promised the duchy to the Descent.

In September, the Chosen One's army marched northward from Waymar to [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]] County. In Lisieux Dole, Richard offered to turn west and defeat Craig Northwood before he joined Desmond, and declared himself the Earl's closest associate and best warrior. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) Vittor gave him a whole corps as proof of his trust, but, “asking the gods”, decided to pursue Desmond, and Richard had to offer a different plan. According to Wittor's account, Ice's plan was to place the camp under attack and lure the enemy to battle: Ice and Horis held out, "like rocks", and Paul, on Wittor's orders, struck the breakaway in the back. In the battle itself, Richard commanded a rearguard of sunsets and White Seamen and held the western flank with two regiments. Desmond's bears and cairns fled, [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]] died, and thousands of bears, according to Wittor, surrendered to Ice.

### Origin
In October, the army entered the Duchy of Origin. The ice with Shavans went ahead and demanded that the inhabitants swear an oath to themselves as the true duke; the hamlet and castles that did not obey Richard were burned. Before Vittor, he listed three of his father's strongholds: [[lid|Leed]], the Skate of Heaven Gorge, and the First Winter. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]) He assured that the vassals would bow down “when my father is on the Star,” and considered my brother weak and unimportant.

In November, ancient Leed, a city with seventy-foot granite walls, surrendered to Richard without a fight and was the first city in the North to recognize him. The townspeople shouted "Glory to Richard", but did so on the orders of [[sajmon-lillidej|Simon Lilliday]], who ordered a pretend submission. Richard did not know how to speak: he confused, insulted his father, repeated that his brother and sister sent murderers to him and forced his father to recant. Together with the Belomorye sided with Shayland, this made Richard the nominal ruler of half of the duchy. He was then appointed commander-in-chief of the Chosen One's army; in silver and general patches, at the aide-de-camp, he appeared to the wounded Martin. While Vittor was away, Martin let Paul's Shavans into Leed, and they sacked the city that recognized Richard. Vittor explained to his children that the quarrel of “Uncle Richard”, who wants to rule the North, with the Shavans who want to rob him, he is profitable: if one rebels, he will turn on the other.

On the wall of the fort in the Sky Rocks, Richard sadly said that his cousin Robert had surrendered the family estate without a fight. The father gave only one battle, and House Origin "bleeds." Hash Eilish assured him that he was the future of the House. His scouts with the Persians opened the way for the army through the gorge, and the Western Ice reported that the road to the First Winter was free. Before the storming of the First Winter, Richard suspected a trap: the enemy seemed to deliberately force them to divide into three corps. Paul demanded command, called Richard a "lieutenant", whose ceiling is a battalion, and said that for everyone except Fleming and the Almerans, he is a "simple wolf". Richard replied that it was better to be a wolf than a vulture who destroyed a peaceful city. Vittor took overall command, and Richard was left with a corps of Almerans and Shaylands.

### Battle for First Winter
On December 2, 1775, during the Battle of the First Winter, Richard stormed the northern rampart with the Wilhelmine and Almeran monks. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) The [[poyas-kolduna|Sorcerer's Belt]] made him invulnerable to bolts and swords, he mowed kairs and crossbowmen out of Perst, but the kairs threw him bottles of butter and orge and set fire to fire arrows. Then he jumped to the Shavans, who did not dare to go to the bridge. One-armed Shavan challenged him. Richard, hiding Perst behind his back, broke his sword against his knee and led the horde into attack. Fleming's Belomorye kairs never entered the battle as much as he would chase the soldiers with threats. King Francis-Ilianus, who watched the battle, remarked that Richard "swords between the parts like a lieutenant, not a general." Towards the end of the day, a crossbow volley killed a horse under him, and the kairs threw an arcana at him and bound him: The belt held up iron, but not bare hands. However, the Wilhelmin monks recaptured their commander. Captain [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]], whose Xes surrounded them, did not start the massacre and let the monks and Richard go to the mountains. Erwin approved of this and said he was glad his brother survived: Richard, he said, was "not too smart", easily suggestible and would perhaps understand his mistakes when Vittor and Paul were gone.

Richard did not go to the mountains, as the northerners thought, but hid among two hundred dead monks. At night, burned and lame, with spark fire in his eyes, he entered the room where Erwin and Iona lay, and locked the door. He told his father that he had disowned his son. Desmond said that his son was misled and bewitched by Ulyanina Dust, but Richard pointed to Iona's hand in Erwin's hand as proof of their connection. Captain Gordon Sue and [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] were also in the room. He started a "game in the spirit of the North": everyone had to indicate the sacrifice for Perst, and the last survivor was given freedom. Gordon Sue pointed to Richard himself, urging him to wash away the dishonor with death, and was killed. Desmond said he raised a son and a daughter "who even the Forefathers would envy," and the first child became his shame, and chose himself. Richard broke his father's skull with a lash.

Erwin demanded a sword fight. Richard taunted him as a "slinger" and "faced" and promised to cut off his hands first. Agatha’s irresistible lightning failed because the Belt stopped the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]] a foot from the neck. Richard explained that since his brother in Waymar deceived him with a double, there will be no honest fight. Then Erwin moved the brace on the hilt, and the spark blade discharge knocked Richard down. Iona took off his Belt and Feather and asked his brother to kill him. Erwin said, "Brother, you killed a very good man," and thrust his sword into his jugular vein. Lady Sofia, who returned from the voyage the next day, learned from the sentry: “He died. Richard is dead.”

## Personality
Richard is the embodiment of rough northern valor: a magnificent fighter capable of disarming an armed kire, straightforward, arrogant and cruel. Erwin and Iona, he considered the "second grade", and his father - the ideal. As early as 1756, Vittor saw him as a boy who enjoyed the service of graying and already hears in his mind the delights of his descendants. Joaquin, who first bowed to him, later decided that Ice was good as a swordsman and junior officer, but "green" as a general. Paul ridiculed him as a "lieutenant". Alanis was amazed that Desmond's "beloved son, the hope of the North" is feasting with "slugs" and advises how to break up his own father.

Before his death, Desmond said that Richard would be deceived and bewitched by Ulyana Dust and that Erwin and Iona had never plotted against him. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) The text does not directly explain what really happened to Richard's ship. Apparently, he was deceived by "savior" Vittor Shayland, but the version about Dust remains Desmond's words, not an established fact.

## Relationships
### Desmond
Richard idolized his father and imitated him in everything. When he returned, he believed that his father had betrayed him by disowning his son, and yet he continued to admire him. His father called him his shame and chose to die by his own hand.

### Erwin
The younger brother, "small" and "gentleman", Richard despised since childhood. He was sure that Erwin had sent murderers to him out of envy. His brother's double humiliated him in Waymar, and Erwin himself killed him the night after the battle.

### Iona
Richard's sister was considered an accomplice to the assassination and Erwin's mistress, he called "cunt" and "wolf", although he first asked who beat her. Iona called him back to the last, and then asked him to kill him.

### Vittor Shayland
A childhood friend, savior and patron. Vittor called him his "closest associate", promised him the duchy, but secretly kept Joaquin as "insurance" against him.

### Paul
Commander of the brigade in which the Ice served. After Leed's ruin, Richard avoided Paul and communicated with him only through adjutants.
