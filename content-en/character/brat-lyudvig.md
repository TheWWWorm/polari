**Brother Ludwig** is a monk from the Maximian monastery near [[labelin|Labelin]], one of the people of the secret order that hunted for Sacred Objects. Later, he appeared under a false name among the rebellious peasants.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
**Prisoner.** In June 1774, the monks kidnapped the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] and threw him into the dungeon of the ruined fortress, to the dead man, who was nicknamed Silent Jack. Six days later, Harmon was picked up, fed, and questioned by a fat, fat man with sagging cheeks. He was “like a brewer or the owner of a successful hotel.” The interrogator asked if Iona knew about the sale of the Item and whether the Origins were selling it, and then drank his fingers into Harmon's shoulders. At the end, he gave the prisoner the crackers and ordered him to return to Jack. Doxet later recalled seeing a bald, bubbling horseman, "neither give nor take a brewer", take a sum out of the barracks. ([[doksett|Doxett]]) It had a real light sphere. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]])

**Name.** Upon his release, Harmon looked for this debtor by the marks. The trader at the monastery recognized him: “brother Ludwig” from the Maximian monastery, “hairy, with a belly”, who sometimes walks without a fuss, dressed as a philistine. Through [[polli|Polly]], it was revealed that all the brothers were engaged in counterfeiting and robbery. Harmon wrote a letter to the abbot on behalf of the Duke of Labelin: he allegedly identified Ludwig’s brother as the villain and demanded the return of the Sphere. The abbot replied that the Object was hidden by Ludwig and would be delivered.

**Issuance of Sphere.** On July 4, 1774, Ludwig and six armed monks came to meet the "Sir Desmond" - disguised Joaquin. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) When he recognized Harmon, he said, "You should have been cut. I should have been sorry.” The abbot, apparently, told him to look closely and not to give up the Object at the slightest doubt. Ludwig hesitated, but laid out the Sphere on the table.

**The chase.** On the way back, one of the monks remembered a horse with a bite, which he saw at the hotel, where they grabbed the merchant. The deception was revealed, and the monks rushed in pursuit. Ludwig cut the spear of Doxet, and the second monk killed the old man with a mace. Ludwig then cut off [[vikhor|Whirlwind]]'s fingers and got together with Joaquin. His stomach did not stop him from sitting in the saddle. Joaquin evaded the blow and killed the horse under him, and the monk injured his leg.

**Survivor.** Soon the monastery was burned. According to David's father, only three maids, a doctor, brother Andrew and brother Ludwig, were saved. ([[otets-david|Father David]]) Ludwig "broke a bone in one unpleasant story" and lay in the hospital of Labelin. He showed David a letter of “his lordship” and confessed that he believed him. David justified the brothers by saying that the monks “try not to kill”: Ludwig did not kill Harmon’s servant, whom he stunned with a blow to the helmet.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
**A tramp.** In the winter of 1775, [[salem|Salem]]'s Snowdrops were joined by a man who called himself a former brewer, from whom "one lord" took the case. “Call me a tramp,” he said. The tramp showed the peasants along Hanoi frozen sparks, from which you can remove armor and weapons. In a skirmish with the knights, he fired from the crossbow without a miss. Salem held three advisors: Sergeant [[ruka-dodzh|Dodge]] knew the military, Joaquin knew the nobles, and the Tramp knew the philistine. The tramp commanded the intelligence, persuaded the crowd to go to the capital and negotiated. At Grace, he wasn't alone in front of the pickers to file. He demanded a decree from them, pulled out a spear from the rider and shouted "R-robbers!" in an officer's voice. Together with Joaquin, he took an ambush of archers, using a mace. In Salem's tent, he snatched two spark self-shooters from Moger Buckley's casket.

General Gore. In negotiations with the imperial general Gore the Tramp aimed "near his shoulder, in the neck of the general." After Zub and Dodge killed the guards and Salem was arrested, he kept his cool. ([[zub|Tooth]]) “It wasn’t us who killed him, it was Zub and Dodge,” he told Joaquin and advised him to speak “from a position of strength.” Joaquin, who was called Trihpal, asked: “How do I know you?”

**Exposing.** Upon leaving the rebellion, Joaquin met [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] and suddenly realized: “The tramp is brother Ludwig!” He recognized the abdomen, a calm low voice, military skill and limp from the wounded leg. Joaquin pleaded with Iona to stop the regiments: Salem is innocent, and only Ludwig needs to be caught, who will "lead to his master, the main villain." Iona didn't believe him.

**Missing.** After Saint Paul [[minerva|Minerva]] asked about the leaders of the uprising. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) She was told that there was also a "Wanderer, a brewer", but he "was lost somewhere." The owner announced that the tramp will be caught and hanged, and the rest can return home.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
Iona, in a letter to Erwin, who then printed The Voice of the Crown on May 15, 1775, wrote: "Monks with Brother Ludwig tried to kidnap her." She added that Joaquin met Ludwig among the leaders of the Snowdrops. Harmon recalled "Ludwig's brother with his torture machines" and placed him among the assassins he knew, alongside Moger Buckley and Snape.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
Harmon told [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] about Silent Jack and named one of his executioners, "brother Ludwig", from a burned monastery north of Labelin. ([[valter-oridzhin|Walter Origin]]) Robert recognized Jack's camsol and kept it. Later, while accompanying Queen Madeline, Lady in Darkness, Robert mentioned that he had been to that monastery before the Windley campaign and remembered "good brother Ludwig". ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) The old woman replied with a laugh that Ludwig did not remember him: “Ludwig is a glorious gardener”, and if a “bat” flew on his bed, he would tell her. Iona realized that Ludwig was serving the order. Robert soon broke the Queen’s neck.
