## Biography

### Origin and youth
Louis Maria introduced himself to Erwin in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] as "Louis Maria of Otmeli, Imperial mechanic of the second guild." They were introduced by the Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] on March 22, 1774, immediately after a tumultuous conversation with his son. Later, Erwin repeatedly wondered whether the mechanic had heard a lot through the door. Luis was identified as a mechanic in an exploit for [[reka|the River]], which was looking for a way for a rail road and a place for a spark dam. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]])

On the way, Louis told Erwin his story. He was the fourth child of a master builder and learned to read and write by the age of nine. His family taught him the way of the priest. During the rains, the roof of the church built by his father collapsed. The family got into debt, and the father persuaded the blinding moneylender to take his son as a scribe in debt. Instead of writing and counting, the boy scraped the floor, raked out the pit and received a dozen roses every Saturday "for the purpose of education." After serving three years, Louis was not released. Then one night he tied up a drunken old man, took more than twenty ephesas and fled west to Hope.

On the road between Farway and the Heart of Light, he was robbed and beaten. In the city of Farway, he hired to drive piles on the railway construction of the Duke of Farway. ([[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]]) He was paid with porridge and crumbs, and scolded for the slightest weakness. The plot was not finished by the cold, and the duke refused to pay. Workers for half the price was transferred to the winter construction site in Litland, where Louis almost died of inflammation and by spring began to gawk blood. In the summer, one of the mechanics noticed him and took him as an apprentice. Since then, Louis has decided that he will never be caught again and will be “his own master.” Erwin replied that there are no free people in the world: everyone lives in their own cage.

### Appearance
Erwin the mechanic seemed a “funny man” no older than twenty-five years. He had wavy straw-colored hair, naive green eyes, and freckles. Out of embarrassment, he became comically scattered: he put a knife in his mouth instead of a fork and endlessly asked for forgiveness. The soldiers called him "the canopy blonde." Later, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] saw him thin and white-faced, "like a maiden," with vortices of blond hair, an unhealthy blush, and the deer's huge wet eyes.

### "My Lady"
Louis was in love and could not help but talk about his beloved, whom he called “my lady”. In [[marengo|Marengo]], where he first worked as a mechanic on the station's expansion, he read books in the park every night. There, a noble girl ran past him, and one day her lace handkerchief fell at his feet. He brought it back, and that's how the meeting began. According to him, the lady was fragile, “like the thinnest white lace, like a swan fluff.” He went on a campaign to collect a worthy ransom for the bride and on his return to ask for her hand. Erwin considered her a girl from the small civil nobility, the daughter of an employee. Louis invited the Lord to visit Marengo and wrote poetry for his lady.

## The Expedition
### Route
The expedition came out of the First Winter in the spring of 1774. In April, the group was marching through the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]. Louis rode in the vanguard and stopped the horse every now and then to sketch the detail of the relief, so the whole squad had to wait. Once Erwin invited him to a "secular dinner" with the imperial observer Baron [[filipp-loufert|Philip Lowfert]]. Under the Shimmerian wine, the mechanic talked, talked about "his lady" and argued with Philip. Soldiers, who wondered what to do with the ledges and depressions in the path of the rails, he explained that across the gorges will have to build bridges. At the end of April, the detachment went to the mountain village of [[spot|Spot]], where Erwin hired guides through the Soft Fields - Kolemon and the young [[kid|Kid]]. ([[kolemon|Colemon]]) In the evening, after Kair Jemis cut off the hand of a local resident, Louis admitted to Erwin that he was not himself among the soldiers, and heard from the lord an explanation of how gray differs from the squire.

Then the exploit passed Zamshevy Forest and in May deepened into the tops of the Soft Fields. Louis rubbed his legs and wanted to take off, but the tracker Colemon showed him a “widow” snake ten steps from the shallows. In late May and early June, the detachment reached [[reka|the River]]. There, Louis proudly interpreted Kidu as a dam and a spark shop turn the flow of water into a spark. On June 6—7, the exploit crossed the River and released the conductors with letters. Louis promised to tell "his lady" about the campaign in detail and named Erwin his guarantors: an abbot from his hometown, serving as a baron from Marengo and [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]].

### Ridiculous Squad
Almost from the beginning, Louis became the laughing stock of the squad. He tied the horse badly, and it escaped and was eaten by wolves. Then Erwin put the mechanic on the donkey, and his reputation was over. The Cairos put thorns under the saddle of his donkey, and one day Louis found a rotten dead badger in the blanket. Jamisa Hank's dog urinated on his records. ([[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]) Theobarth considered these antics to be "pardonable pranks". ([[teobart|Theobart]]) Louis did not complain, but his eyes were "in a wet place." Erwin regretted the mechanic and brought him closer to him. He once suggested that Louis go home with a tracker, but he refused because the case is not over. Louis diligently corrected the age-old map of the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]] and for each line was in a hurry to make measurements. During the night avalanche, he clung to Erwin's shoulder and prayed aloud.

### Attempts
In fact, Louis was sent to kill Erwin from the very beginning. As Erwin himself later reasoned, the mechanic tried to pass off the murder as an accident. In the Soft Fields swamp, he walked ahead of the lord and cut a net of swamp grass, and then stole a poisonous snake from the young guide Kid and planted it in Erwin's tent. Then the suspicion fell on Jemis, and later Erwin asked him for forgiveness. Erwin also thanked Jemis for his "idiot sense of humor." The soldiers with Hank were so closely watching the mechanic for new jokes that Louis had almost no opportunity to set traps.

### The Dara Lodge
On June 8, 1774, the detachment came to a huge failure - the bed of the Eighteenth Gift. Louis could only speak in a whisper. At the looted lodge, Louis asked Erwin about the Sacred Objects and their values. At night, shortly before the attack, he called the lord to the edge of failure to look at the glimmering in the depths of the Object and strangely asked if Erwin had taken such Objects in his hands. Hearing the watchman's cry, Louis snatched the dagger and with the words "Excuse me, my lord" struck Erwin with a knife under his left collarbone. Erwin jumped into failure, escaping the second blow. Lying in the grotto, he heard the mechanic report to a certain “cheese” with a weak unfamiliar accent, as if he had hit “right in the heart” and threw the body down. "Sir" reproached him that he should have done away with the lord "before the River", and caused him pain. In fact, the blow passed a few inches past the heart and did not even touch the lung. On the same night, the camp was carved out by soldiers in red uniforms posing as imperial spark infantry, the [[paul|Paul]] Brigade. They did not kill Luis, but left him a prisoner and kept him in his wooden fort behind the River until late autumn.

Returning home, Erwin announced to his father and vassals that the emperor had sent Louis to kill him because Erwin was interfering with the lord. According to him, the mechanic was not needed to lay the route.

## Prison and Madness
In November 1774, the fleet of Count [[fleming|Benedict Fleming]] came to the mouth of the River. Kair Jemis Lilliday and [[mark-voron|Mark]] captured the brigade fort where the pile of Rings was stored, and Louis was found in the barn. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) He was kept with [[gvenda|Gwenda]], and the prisoners seemed to be unaware of each other. Louis burrowed into the front of the hay, chewed it and shouted: “I’m a goat! Meeee!" Jemis recognized the Duke's "failed killer" and decided to deliver him to Erwin alive. In the boat, he tore out Louis's nail, and Count Benedict told him to stop. At night, Louis tried to escape from the camp on all fours.

Mark got nothing from the prisoner at first. Then he stood up on all fours and declared himself a "bucket". This awakened a glimmer of reason in Louis: he demanded that Mark stop pretending, and in exchange for a promise to leave, he told about himself. According to him, in the capital “the man of the lord, the captain in red” with two assistants grabbed and beat him, and then showed him his lady, who was also beaten and burned with a candle. Louis was told to kill Erwin "before the River", otherwise the lady would die, and if successful, she would be spared and given money.
> "I didn't mean...the Duke is kind...and miserable. But then he did. He didn’t protect me when they laughed.”

Captain Louis described as a scary black-haired man with a broken nose and a scar on his lip. At the end of November, on the ship, when Mark was telling Jemis and the captain his version of events, he called Louis "the defendant": he rushed to the door, but when he heard Mark grunting, confirmed that the person who hired him was in red uniform, that assistants called him captain and that it happened in February. He refused to talk about the fort. Mark concluded that Louis was not killed just because the executioners left the "goat" for fun. From the choice of such a ridiculous killer, Mark concluded that Louis was not hired by the emperor, but by someone who wanted to denigrate Adrian.

### The Road Through the Snow
The return voyage ended in disaster: the Earl of Fleming betrayed the Origins, and the survivors had to flee from his men. In December 1774, Captain [[dzheff-bamber|Jeff Bamber]]'s ship Seal with Jemis, Mark, Moy's kire, his warm John-John, and both prisoners approached the shores of the Origin near the [[belomorye|Belomorye]]. The Count's ships pursued them, and the travelers abandoned the ship, landed in boats and on foot, on snow, moved south to the outposts of the mountain guard. Louis bleated on the way and at any opportunity stood on all fours. The sailors walked slowly, and after the sailor Hess froze to death, Moy and John-John decided to go ahead for help. They took Luis with them: “Goat” was supposed to “delight the Duke.” Jemis and Mark stayed with the sailors.

### The Origins
From Origin, the prisoner was sent south with a column of kairs, which in early 1775 was carried to the capital by the Duke of Desmond and Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia]]. In February, the column met with Iona in [[labelin|Labelin]], and Kair Jemis presented her with a captured mechanic. Iona was at first enraged that his brother’s torturer had not lost a single part of his body in a month’s captivity. Then, seeing his animal alarm and hearing the bleating, she felt pity. Erwin, looking at the prisoner for a long time, said:
> “Oh, sir, if you only knew what tortures I invented for you. But my imagination was duller than reality.”

Later, already in [[faunterra|Faunterra]], Iona was afraid that his brother would give Louis to the executioners. Brother and sister decided not to kill and not to let him go, but to give the doctor. Louis' madness was not sent by the gods, but by human atrocity, and Iona hoped to cure him. Erwin wanted to track down Louis' girlfriend in Marengo if the Puppeteer's men hadn't killed her, and tell her that Louis was alive and could recover. The doctor gave the patient soothing drops, and sometimes he came enlightenment. In March 1775, on the Palace Island, the Virgins tested the Hand of Znaharka, an object taken from a beggar-healer. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) In one of the hours of enlightenment, Iona, speaking to Louis with a motherly voice and recalling his lady, persuaded him to put on the Item. The object flashed with a thick glow, as before on Gwenda, but did not give healing. Discussing the experience, Iona noticed that Erwin, Gwenda and Louis were in the Beyond, and then suggested that the Subject "likes white crows" - people with strangeness.

### Theory of "choices" of Objects
This experience gave Erwin an important clue. The subject responded to only three people: Erwin himself, Gwenda and Luis, but not Ione, not Lady Sophia and not [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]]. Erwin told [[minerva|Minerva]] that Louis had been taken prisoner by [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade and found salvation in imagining himself to be a goat. According to him, the Objects recognize those in whose body the “liquids” of the fighters of the brigade got. When [[mark-voron|Mark]] was captured by the brigade, he tried to remember the people who had left her alive, remembered only Louis and Gwenda and fixed their names so that it became "a hair's breadth easier". In Shadow of the Great Tree, [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] recalls this theory ("Gwenda, Luis, Minerva, body fluids") and regrets that she did not believe it in time.

After that, Louis remained in the care of a doctor at the Origins and did not appear in the cycle anymore.

## Personality
Before his captivity, Louis was a timid, enthusiastic and talkative man, proud of his knowledge of mechanics and ability to read. He easily blushed and cried with resentment. His life was a chain of humiliation, and this made him vulnerable: by his own admission, the lack of protection from ridicule turned a forced attempt into a desired revenge. The madness in which he became a "goat", Erwin and Mark considered a kind of refuge that saved his life.
