## Origins

Nexia is the daughter of Count [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]], the ruler of Leongard, and, like her father, the granddaughter of Pramatery Elena. According to the story in Humans and Gods, Edgar was born the youngest son of Duke Jonas Lighthart. After the defeat of his father's house in the Shutov conspiracy, he took the name of his father-in-law, Count Flame, and gave it to the children so that the lord would forget about it. Somerset and Nexia studied in the capital boarding house of Lady Ingrid and were accepted in the light and even at court, although their relationship with the Lightharts was sometimes whispered. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]; [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]])

Nexia's mother's name is not named. [[karen|Karen]], Edgar’s sister, called her niece “a wonderful fool”: according to her, Edgar married only on the orders of his father. But it was "this chicken - and also her father" who saved him: without the guarantee of the Flame family, Edgar Lighthart would have been executed as brothers, or exiled as a sister. In Humans and Gods, it is said that when Nexia came into contact with Erwin, Edgar's foolish wife this time said something sensible and suggested using this connection to get Lighthart's ancestral nest back through the Origins. The Count’s hand rat answered “no” to this idea. Parents, according to Nexia, saw her beauty as a means of “making the right impact” on the future Duke and rejoiced when she came into contact with him.

## Nexia and Erwin in the capital

Nexia's affair with Erwin dates back to the time before the cycle: he got involved in [[faunterra|Faunterra]], and ended when Erwin left for the exploit. ([[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]; [[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) Edgar later dryly remarked that the relationship between the earl and the duke was strained, "as usual, when one sleeps with the daughter of another without the slightest plans for a wedding." Nexia was openly in the capital’s mansion of the Origins, in the summer he and Erwin bathed at the palace beach, and the courtiers gossiped about her for a long time. Somerset's brother once ran into a mansion with a sword to defend her honor. In A Doll on the Throne, Mira learned from Lady Mollia's cameras that before Alanis, Nexia Flame, "according to rumors, did not have a soul" in him, and that after the war Nexia did not appear at the court.

In the memoirs of Erwin Nexia - a blue-eyed girl from Faunterra, the best dancer in the capital. She asked him a lot about himself and really wanted to know the answers. Erwin remembered her fragile wrists and how she wrote: her feelings could be read more clearly from the movements of her hands than from the text of the letter. He remembered a tiny mole on his neck hidden under a heavy chestnut pile of hair. Nexia was able to draw a person in a few strokes with a pencil or ink so that his character was visible. Erwin once called her “foggy and rainbowy, restless, graceful, like an orchid.” According to him, they competed in mystery, he almost always won and then regretted: Nexia could become the second person after Iona, who understands him to the end, if he allowed her.

In the first book, Nexia only remembers Erwin. In the Beyond, he dreams of "blue-eyed" Nexia, who whispers: "Run next!" Wounded, in a forest hut (June 1774), he relives their date in a carriage: she has corny eyes, which “does not happen to Northern girls”, she asks to say what he loves her, complains that she can not consider him “as if in a fog”, and kisses him, “trying not to hold, not to have time”.

In the second book, on November 20, 1774, Erwin was visited by her brother [[somerset-flejm|Somerset]]. He was constipated, covered in a blanket and coughing, and brought a letter. Nexia wrote that she had sent Erwin six letters and had them all intercepted by the protector. He said that Erwin was playing with his sister's feelings, and admitted that he wanted to challenge him to a duel in the capital, but Nexia begged him not to.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

### Memories of Erwin
In May 1775, rising to the podium of the House, Erwin recalled how at the previous open debate from excitement he could barely speak and found a voice only by looking at Nexia and getting her smile; now Nexia was not in the hall. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) He told his father that, having become the best general of the Empire, he would be able to marry any girl - "perhaps Minerva, or maybe Nexia Flame." Minerva in a quarrel sarcastically reminded him that for everything except catching the Puppeteer, he has Alanis and Nexia.

### The Messenger in Waymar

In the spring of 1775, Nexia and Somerset came to [[uejmar|Waymar]]. According to [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]’s letter to her brother, she was invited by [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] himself: he discussed with [[nadezhda|Hope]] the project of the “holy guard” and told Farway that negotiations with Nexia would be successful. The acquaintance turned out to be accidental: at the gate of the castle, guests argued with a stubborn cab driver, Iona offered them help, and Somerset only then found out who he was talking to.

On the first night, Nexia opened to Iona in a long monologue. Her parents saw in her beauty a means of influencing the future duke. She loved Erwin, but knew she would lose him through his death or through his crown and his marriage to [[minerva|Minerva]], and so she cut off the connection herself. Iona replied that the Origins owed her an indemnity, and offered to be a "string" between Nexia and her brother. In response, Iona asked her to teach her to love. Nexia answered: you just need to know that in the world there is no one better than your chosen one.

Nexia comforted the depressed Iona, drove her around the city and chatted incessantly. Iona thought she was the right wife for her brother. At the morning meeting, Vittor presented Nexia with a rare toy mare. Later, Erwin, comparing the facts (Iona painted a horse in the winter, which did not give her rest), concluded that it was his own Leocant: [[paul|Paul]]'s people captured her in the Beyond and gave it to the count.

### Morning in the dining room

One morning Nexia entered the refectory of the castle completely naked and, not noticing the nakedness, wished everyone a pleasant appetite. When she woke up, she ran into the bedroom in a fever and shame. All she could remember was taking off her nightgown and then failing. Iona was looking for poison, but he found nothing. There was only a bookmark with the inscription of Erwin's hand, embedded in the love story "Rose and Death", and Nexia referred to the nerves shaken by fantasies.

Only later, when Nexia had already sailed from Waymar, Iona compared the facts. Manson, after receiving the first note, raped the footman, and after the second killed Adrian. Nexia was reading a book left overnight in the bedroom. Iona realized that this was the "shooting" of the Puppeteer, and immediately sent [[irving|Irving]]'s cairn with a ring-print to warn Erwin. Erwin later publicly called Nexia a victim of Ulyanina Dust and cited this case as an argument against Wittor. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]])

## In "Humans and Gods"

### Roses

From Waymar, Nexia and his brother sailed to [[fliss|Fliss]], and from there they reached [[aleridan|Aleridan]]. According to her, Vittor persuaded her to take Erwin's letter to Faunterra. After learning that Erwin was leading an army to Aleridan, she rushed to him, quarreled with Somerset, who accused her of female impermanence, and finally ran away from her brother through the window. Her stagecoach came across her father's army.

In June 1775, during Erwin's negotiations with Edgar Flame, Nexia entered the Duke's tent with a bouquet of roses and a letter from Wittor. Her will was ruled by Dust. Nexia demanded that Erwin read the letter immediately, and rushed to kiss him. Her voice then became mechanical, then again sounded still, dreamy and ironic - when Dust let her go. Erwin, along with his althess Trevagoi, realized that the poison was on the thorns of roses, and on the letter - Dust. He stroked the stems, said, "Oh, he pricked" and played death cramps. Nexia tried to shout “No...” but couldn’t stretch her teeth.

Based on Manson's story, Erwin reasoned that Dust's action takes place when the victim thinks she's obeyed an order. The girl was taken to her father with a note: leave her in error, at least until the evening. Edgar kept his daughter in the dark for two days. Nexia later admitted that she almost killed herself, thinking that she killed Erwin. After this meeting, Erwin told Anxiety that Nexia loved him, "this has not been changed by time, nor by war, nor by Alanis, nor by Dust." According to him, such devotion had previously met him only with Iona.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

### Map

In August 1775, Nexia found Erwin in the steppe near Trident. She hired a schooner under Fliss, climbed up to the source of Halliwell, and continued riding, reasoning that if Erwin was alive, he would first send intelligence to the Trident. It brought him bad news: the blow of the Absolute in Waymar, the invasion of Paul's horde in [[almera-zemlya|Almera]], the death of Cordelia's mother at the gates of Fliss. ([[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]]) There were six pigeons with her, from the First Winter, from the capital, and from Waymar.

Her main gift was a strategic card from Farway's bet, which [[edgar-flejm|Edgar]] later told her he gave himself. The map showed that Paul would get to Waymar before Erwin. When Erwin brushed aside her words, Nexia in anger poked her finger into the bay on the map so that she broke her nail. On this map, Erwin and [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] drew up a plan that led to the battle for the First Winter. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])

She refused Erwin's courtship. She’s not an agate and won’t be a “disreputable altess,” Nexia said, and her love for him was a heavy and dangerous burden, and she got rid of him. She went, according to her, on behalf of her father to Fairis, and Erwin, who was also heading there, took her with a detachment.

### First Lady of the Squad
From the steppe of Nexia, together with Erwin's detachment, she passed through the County of Mills to Fais (September 1775), from there to the Barony of Davy on the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] (late September), and then by sea, past the Granified Islands, to the north, in the vicinity of the [[belomorye|Belomorye]] (early November). ([[melnitsy|The Mills]]) At the foot of Jenna, the oldest of the Mother Mills, she wished to rise to the top, and Jemis led her by hand; in the swordsman's graveyard, she and Jemis rushed to look for two statues of incorruptible metal, of which Father David spoke. In the Barony of Davy, she went ashore for provisions accompanied by the dog Sagittarius. Erwin, having decided that Nexia was Jemis's bride, grew gloomy and assured himself that he was only angry at the Puppeteer.


Along the way, Nexia became the "first lady" of the army. She painted portraits of kairs, chefs of three companies competed for the right to feed her, and at the request of Erwin, she consoled Jemis, who lost his father. On the lands of [[melnitsy|the Mills]], she, on her own idea, spoke to the sentries on behalf of a noble lady from Hope, and the detachment concealed its belonging. One day, Nexia learned that a captured bearer of the Perst, called the Kairs, was being carried in a kite. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) She said to the Cairams, “Your enemies are monsters, but be human, gentlemen.” Then she asked Erwin to stop torturing this man, saying that she was afraid that his soul would not be sad after his palms.

### Before the battle
In November 1775 in the Duchy of Origin, having decided to personally go to Leed for Iona, Erwin ordered Captain Leed to provide an escort to escort Nexia and hostage Violet Fleming to a safe place. ([[oridzhin-zemlya|Lands of Origin]]; [[lid|Leed]]) Nexia begged him not to risk himself: “We are afraid for you.” Erwin was besotted with the words "You and your fiancé..." and sent Jemis with the Gun to the First Winter. Later, when he came to his sister in Leed, he was jokingly outraged that Jemis “got married to Nexia Flame”: although they broke up a year ago, Nexia, according to him, was supposed to “leap tears in inconsolable loneliness.” By the time of the battle, 2 December, Nexia had reached the castle of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]; Adrian later noted that she also played a role in [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]]'s giving the kairs ships and sending her father's regiment north.

### After the battle

The night after the battle, Nexia and Jemis obeyed the wounded Erwin: there was no engagement. Jemis had a marriage letter from King Shimmery offering him Princess Demeter, and they played a crush so that Nexia would have a reason to follow Erwin. Nexia admitted that she had obtained ships in Fais, agreed with Baron Davy and persuaded her father to enter the war. Gordon Sue added that the whole plan she gave to Lord [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] and this allowed to coordinate the blows of the Northerners in time. Nexia said that she still loves Erwin and the role of altessa does not suit her - in fact, she offered him a marriage. Confused and exhausted, Erwin postponed the conversation until after sleep and only jokingly threatened her with “the most cruel punishment.”

That same night, [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] broke into the bedroom and offered a "game" with a choice of victims. When the turn came to Nexia, Erwin ordered her to give a sword and called her brother to fight in her place. Nexia put the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]] in his hand. When the defeated Richard lay at the feet of Erwin and he hesitated, Nexia after Iona asked: “Kill him, my lord. This is a terrible man.” Over the body of Captain Gordon Sue, also killed by Richard, leaned it. When Adrian began to blackmail the castle with a deconstructor, Erwin sent Nexia and Jemis with a Sagittarius underground passage to the city to [[minerva|Minerva]]: according to Nexia, they are for him "the most expensive people (not counting sisters)". Minerva noticed that Nexia calls Iona a "lady" and the Duke just Erwin, and suspected that the former althessa of the lord chancellor wants to regain his position. She left Nexia to herself and entrusted her with the coordination between the city and the castle. At a banquet with Adrian's delegation, Erwin seated Nexia and Jemis next to him as a sign of forgiveness. Nexia "warmed about Erwin", and Manson ridiculed her.

## Personality and Appearance

Blue-eyed, chestnut-haired, with milky white skin and a mole on the neck. An artist, a chatterbox and a wit, she became interested in politics because Erwin liked to talk about her. Behind the frivolous manner lurked tenacity and cold calculation: in the steppe, she cold-bloodedly decided where to look for Erwin, and then one prepared the ships and allies for his plan. Erwin called her the “queen of deceit,” and Anxiety called her “a treacherous little sleuth.” She sincerely regretted even her enemies and was not afraid to argue with Erwin about mercy. Her main feature Erwin considered a rare devotion. Somerset called her falling in love an "Erwinian madness." Karen believed that her love for Erwin deprived her of a sober view of life.
