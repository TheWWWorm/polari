## Who She Is

Lady in Darkness is the queen of the swamp kingdom of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], whose real name is Madeline Nan-Claire. A blind old woman with white eyes without pupils, she survived three husbands and three children, personally knew four emperors, endured several hopeless wars, and possessed both witchcraft swamps. Francis-Ilian called her the wisest woman in the South.

### Kinship
According to Minerva, the Queen is a pure-blooded Yanmei, the great-granddaughter of Juliana the Great and cousin of Lady Ingrid. Minerva is her grand-niece, although she has not received a single letter from her in her entire life. In the words of the Queen, which Minerva recalls at the feast, she calls her “granddaughter”. Joke Manson rudely recommended her as "brother-in-law cousin, stubborn squalid old woman", familiar to him for thirty years and calling his father a fool. After Minerva, she was the next heir to the throne. The Queen placed the greatness of the Yangmei family and her principles above all else and forever excommunicated the relatives who violated them. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) This is what happened to Minerva’s mother and to the Queen’s own daughter, Princess [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille]], who was expelled from the country.

### Face
Age dried up her body, sharpened her features and made her joints knotty like branches; wrinkles furrowed her waxy face, and her eyes were staring at the "dimple moons". Her voice is senilely creaky, but firm, and with strangers - quiet and almost melodic. Despite her blindness, her movements are strikingly accurate: she was fingering the edge of the petal without washing it, and without a miss finding the dishes on the table.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

The Queen is mentioned as one of the few living heirs to the throne; in line she stands behind Mira. Mira, looking for the secret conspirator "Lord C", remembers that the Queen is the aunt of her late mother: outraged by the unequal marriage of her niece, Lady in the Dark betrayed her and her whole family, so that Mira did not receive a message from her cousin. Mira puts her on the list of suspects, but throws it away: the conspirator should have been interested in rivals, not to forget about them; besides, the old woman is blind, and her land has been tormented by [[rejs|Reis]]' Shavans for the past ten years. (In the early media, the queen is called a relative of the emperor on her father's side, and her enemies are the barons of the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]].) The Queen herself in the last year did not come to the capital and remained in Darkwater, and the conspirators, according to Mira, sooner or later had to bite on her as one of the heirs.

The queen is mentioned in the past by her brother-doctor [[brat-marius|Marius]] from the monastery of Marek and Simeon, where [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] left the wounded [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] in the autumn of 1774. ## In the Gate of Trust

### Moths Hall
In February 1775, the prophet [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] arrived in Nan Clair: the Queen was for him the "last hope" for the solution of his prophetic dream. Along with him were the southern [[gerion|Gerion]], a secret spy, and a beggar turned out to be a joke [[menson|Manson]]. The guests were tested by three vassals of the Queen, her “gate of trust”: the priest Father Loris, the Foltic admiral Fanno Grok and the nephew, Lord Emmon Darklin. Then they were taken in a glass greenhouse - a "mothball hall" with a pond and pitchers. The Queen sat in a wicker chair, covering her knees with a plaid, and told everyone to choose a flower and explain the choice.

When the unmasked Gerion began to scream, she besieged him:
> "I will not tolerate the screams in my greenhouse. Flowers can't make a noise."
The sting of the crybola removed from the prophet's back the leeches-seeds of the corzo planted by Gerion; their poison was rendered harmless beforehand. For eight years, which the prophet carefully tested interlocutors, the Queen considered the “gates of trust” passed by herself.

### Manson
Manson, not trusting the queen, played a crazy scene, questioning everyone if they would kill the emperor, and thus showed how Ulyanina Dust affected him. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) The Queen, a member of the secret order, realized that the jester did not act of his own will, and promised to testify to his innocence. The vassals panicked, deciding that "The Tree is swaying" and that the priory owns "The Secret," but she ordered the plan to be followed:
> “The tree will grow.”
Three days later, the queen and the prophet with their entourage went to the capital: she wanted to see a northerner and “that one who calls himself the Peace Bearer.” Along the way, Francis-Ilianus joined the order, and the Queen revealed to him two of his greatest secrets: what kind of Tree the brothers are raising and what kind of split Polaris faces in three hundred years.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

### Silver Fox
According to Minerva, after Telurian's death, General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]] (Silver Fox) conspired with the Queen and became her "eyes, ears and hand at Faunterra". With his help, she looked after [[adrian|Adrian]], and then on her orders, Lis helped Minerva to establish himself on the throne. The order of the general was simple: at any cost to prevent the throne of Agatha. Minerva knew that if she got engaged to Erwin, gave way to him, or just got tired of fighting, the Queen would replace her with someone like herself.

### Arrival in Marengo
In May 1775, her squadron arrived in [[marengo|Marengo]]. The flagman was under black sails, the rest of the ships were under dark gray, and flags and cloaks were the same color of the shadow; only white coats of arms were allocated. On the shore descended low-growing marshes with curved swords and poisoned arrows, and behind them stings kribola - Assassins in spotted raincoats, the master of dark witchcraft. With the queen arrived Francis-Ilian, nephew of Emmon Darklin, Admiral Fanno Groc and Minerva's "gift" - Manson with a bag on his head. At the feast Erwin and Minerva started the game “If you...” to avoid the hints that are famous for the swamps.

### Deal on the train
On the imperial train, the queen and the prophet invited Erwin to their home. The Queen offered him a new, not yet named flower of her garden with three petals - black, red and blue, in the colors of the northern nobility and the Brilliant Dynasty - and wished him to decorate his house. Then she and the prophet offered a deal. Minerva will “die from the disease” of the lungs, as her mother, Manson will be eliminated, and the throne will be occupied by Lady in Darkness. After her death, the throne will pass to Erwin in marriage to Mireille. Erwin threatened to cut them “along with the swamp swath” if Minerva or Hector suddenly fell ill. Then the queen showed him a cup in which something alive was turning, coughing, spewing "black trash" and fell. The whole court thought she had been poisoned. Erwin himself in the early days suspected of her puppeteer, but refused this idea: the main enemy is unlikely to poison himself. The Queen did not allow herself to be examined, the glass broke in confusion, and through her people she repeated that she was suffering from ordinary senile disease, did not blame anyone and did not want to investigate. The defense found out that her food is tried by a court witch and a sting of kribola, and on her finger she wears a ring with an poison-seeking fungus; [[mark-voron|Mark]] reminded Erwin and the daughter of the Queen, who, settling on Folta, six times sent to the mother of the murderers. Her estate in the capital was cordoned off by the stings of kribola, only her cousin, priest and doctors were allowed in her room, and from the whole yard - one Minerva. At the request of the doctors, visitors had to sit behind the screen and in masks; this came in handy when Minerva had to bring her after a heavy hangover.

Minerva twice visited the patient and reported to the House that the Queen’s health is improving and there is no danger to life. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) During [[menson|Manson]]'s trial, Minerva accompanied the Queen to visit the tombs of Telurian and Ingrid in Arden; this trip explained to Erwin why she avoided meeting him. The “Voice of the Crown” reported by the end of May that the Queen had been seen coming out into the courtyard of the estate, and then that she had finally overcome the illness and was ready to visit the House.

### Test
Later, Father [[otets-david|David]] informed Erwin that Lady in Darkness was the eldest and wisest of the three highest masters of the Order of Gardeners, and that Lady Ingrid, without initiation, was close to her and often helped the Order. About the conspiracy against Minerva, he said only that the Queen “lied”, allowing Erwin to guess for himself. Erwin decided that both the plot and the poisoning were a test for him and Minerva: the Queen chose an ally. The poisoning, in his opinion, was simulated: she took a safe potion to lock herself in the estate and freely meet with Minerva. He also suggested that Minerva conspired with the Queen when she found out about the Sun Drop in the tomb of Lady Ingrid. From Minerva, the Queen, according to Erwin, expected greater honesty: “whoever deceived one ally will deceive the other.” Later it turned out that the ambush in the tomb was arranged by the Queen herself: the agent of the order, Father David, who became a confessor Alanis, learned from her what the Puppeteer was looking for. Jemis reminded Erwin that the marsh was friends with Minerva while they had a general ambush plan, and when he fell off, she switched to Erwin. Minerva later recalled how the Lady in Darkness warmed up her vanity: “You will capture the brigade, you will get talking Objects, you will become the greatest mistress...”

### Chamber
On the decisive day of the House meeting, the Queen took her place for the first time. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) At the podium Erwin saw her flower: of the three petals there were two, scarlet and black, the colors of the kairs. The Lady in Darkness sided with Erwin and herself said what he had planned to say: she asked the House to depose Adrian and elect a new emperor by vote. Erwin realized that the Queen and the prophet had long known from Manson that Adrian was alive, and were on their way to the capital with the ready intention of legitimizing his overthrow. Earlier, Erwin, telling the House about the ambush in the tomb, publicly stated that the Queen knew about the Drop of the Sun and led her own hunt for the Puppeteer, and her poisoning was faked; the Lords began to look reproachfully at Lady in Darkness. However, in voting, she raised the flag for deposing and, when Erwin paused, she herself gave his full name: "I propose Lord Erwin to Sophia Jessica of the Agatha family." The Queen named Erwin as a candidate for the emperor.

## In "Humans and Gods"

On June 1, 1775, the Queen, along with Francis Ilianus and David, came to Erwin. She rebuked him for his weak policy: he defeated the lords, but did not make alliances, and the vote was postponed, and after the victory against him, a coalition would form. The magisters offered to build a coalition for him in exchange for a victory over the Puppeteer, the return of the Objects and [[paul|Paul]] - necessarily alive. The Queen then revealed to Erwin for the first time the purpose of the order:
> “We have to get to the star.”
To help him, she offered a double — the sting of the kribola, the master of reincarnation, similar to Erwin’s voice and behavior.

Her nocturnal appearance in Minerva's bedroom, along with the prophet and two kribola stings, was for the young empress a lesson in defenselessness: the guard was asleep, the bell cord was cut. Guests in garden metaphors advised Minerva to “rest” from power and left her a bottle of essence from a hangover. The Queen reproached the missing Adrian that he had “abandoned his garden for six months”. Minerva herself, preparing to fight for the crown, believed that the lords would not elect an old and “almost childless” queen, whose only daughter became a citizen of Volta, and General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis]], a faithful Lady in Darkness, was going to replace. Mireille Nan-Claire told Lady Sophia that her mother was only a “turning-key maid” and knew more heretics about the dangers of primrose; ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]]; [[pervokrov|Firstblood]]) Mireille wanted Erwin to prevent the Queen from allying with [[paul|Paul]].

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

### Alliance with Adrian
In the summer of 1775, Adrian, who had learned of the Order's aims from the Earl of [[kuindar|Quindar]], wrote an enthusiastic letter to the Queen: he called her "virtue" returning the lost infant nursing mother, asked for help to return the crown and detain Minerva "until the day when a sweet fruit ripens on the branches of the Tree." As a gift, he sent the Light Sphere with a copy, the [[poyas-kolduna|Sorcerer's Belt]] and the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] himself, in whose hands the Sphere once spoke. However, Harmon and his comrades escaped from Adrian's delegation, opened the box and handed the letter to Minerva, and took the Sphere with him. The Queen never received the Sphere or its bearer. In August 1775, when Adrian was already standing at Faunterra, the queen appeared to him in the tea room, leaning on the hand of the Second of the Five; ([[kuindar|Quindar]]) [[magda-labelin|Magda]] saw how pleased her master was. At that meeting, she informed him that the visitor [[navi|Nathaniel]] had escaped upon learning of their plans at Minerva's reception, and denounced the intemperance: The Great Tree has been growing for seventeen centuries and can bear fruit only with his children, grandchildren or great-grandchildren.

According to Father David, her hazy letter to [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], compiled according to his information, prompted a mutiny at Waymar Castle. In early September, after the solemn entry of Adrian into the capital, she came to him again, rejected tea and immediately went to work: in her opinion, the visitor was not hiding in Patrick's Chapel, but appointed a meeting there Minerva. The queen kept two captive chavans-peppers under the influence of the intoxicating essence of the luno-eye. At her second meeting with Adrian, she felt the objects of the captive Johan Reiss - Wilhelm's Feather and the Voice of God - and, hearing that through Johan, she could contact the one she had been looking for for almost two decades, she dropped: "Paul!" ([[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]]) Through the Voice of God, [[paul|Paul]] promised any reward to anyone who would bring him Nathaniel "in any form." The Queen took Johan to her, promising that in her "devils" he would become completely happy.

### Hiking to the North
In the autumn, the queen moved north with Adrian's army. In October 1775, in the duchy of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], she dined with Adrian, Magda, Maurice Labelin, Francis-Ilian, Hector and his wife Karen; when the prophet told his ninth dream, the queen interpreted the image of a girl with pharmacy scales as a sign of the weight itself - a foreshadowing of the fact that the ship of Minerva lost mass and went up into the sky, and the guess of the clock was called Karen. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]; [[karen|Karen]]) In Adrian's wagon, the crybola sting at her command brought the moon-eyed [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]], and he, seeing everything as in a nightmare, shot a dog and a poplar from Perst; but an attempt to force him to actuate [[chrevo|the Womb]] failed, and the Queen promised to prepare a "different approach". Adrian asked her to notify the agent at the Origin headquarters; she replied that she had no direct connection with him, but would leave the information where the agent found them.

Her agent at the Origin headquarters was Father [[otets-david|David]]. Erwin caught him trying to give her a report and pretended to agree to the order's terms; according to the order's code, Adrian was told that Erwin was ready to surrender and bring Nathaniel in. On the day of the Battle of the First Winter, Adrian rebuked the Queen, "Your agent lied." She just shrugged her shoulders: even the best agents are sometimes revealed. During the battle, while [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]] hovered over the city, the Second of the Five whispered to the blind queen what she saw; when Johan Reis fell dead, she determined that it was Black Mary's pill - the deadly poison of the hour-long action. When Adrian and his allies imprisoned Vittor Shayland with the Absolute in the Womb, her servants rolled up a crystal coffin, and the Queen congratulated the Lord: "The Great Tree grows faster than the field flower."

### Death
By this time, her daughter Mireille had defected to the Origins and told them that her mother had trained her to be a gardener until the third stage of initiation, and had expelled her to the Volta for opposing the order; On the eve of the negotiations, Iona studied the album of Mireille with all the "arrows" of Darkwater - poisons and tools for their delivery. To avoid a collision, Mireille and Nathaniel did not go to the table.

In early December 1775, the Queen joined Adrian's delegation in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. At the table she kept politely, but Iona, who knew how to feel people, did not feel any feelings from her - "full armor, the deaf took away." When Erwin talked about the gardens, the Queen cut him off:
> Do not force the flower to the top. It won’t grow faster, it will just break the stem.”

At the entrance to the tower, the old woman asked Erwin to give her a hand, but cousin [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] offered his help instead. Iona held the Perst in her back and felt that the “aura of murder” came from the Queen. On the stairs, Robert asked her about the order; the Queen confirmed that she was in charge of it, and noticed that “there are no officers among the gardeners.” Robert mentioned the monastery where [[brat-lyudvig|Brother Ludwig]] served, and the old woman, amusingly, said that Ludwig was a “glorious gardener”; Iona remembered this name: the monk forged the Light Sphere and incited the peasants against the Empress. In the officer's salon, sitting the old woman in a chair, Robert broke her neck; Manson noticed this. Robert declared it a legal execution: in the fall of 1765, his father [[valter-oridzhin|Walter Origin]] disappeared in the campaign at Windley, and it turned out that he had been kidnapped and tortured by monks subordinate to the Queen of Darkwater. The Silver Fox shouted for treason; Robert countered that the guarantee of security was given to Adrian and his people, and "there was no talk of a swamp witch." Minerva placed Robert under house arrest and promised an honest trial, the prophet urged not to tolerate personal enmity between the two at all, and Adrian agreed that Robert settled personal accounts and should answer personally, but put the Origins at fault for the violated security guarantee.

## In "Fantasies"
In “Fantasies”, which the author calls fantasies about the possible fate of the characters, the Queen is already dead. In "Amicable" it is said that the "Swamp Queen" "successfully died", to the relief of Prince Hector, who was afraid of the formidable old woman. Her place was to be taken by Mireille; the nephew of the late, Emmon Darklin, through the ambassador asked Hector to bind the "witch" in shackles, but Mireille with the Shimmerian regiment of the Burning Sun took the throne. In Deathly Hallows, detective Mack says that the order "ruled all the Lady in Darkness, but died", and now the order is going through difficult times, and the new Queen Mireille, according to rumors, did not approve of the affairs of her mother. According to his version, Mireille “started hunting” for the order, and Count Quindar warned one of the agents about this. In the fantasy of “Ad-ri-an!”, Adrian himself calls the death of the queen at the hands of Robert spontaneous, but very successful for him: “for some women, the corpse of the mother is the best gift,” and Mirei then accepted his support.

## Personality

Dry, strict, ironic, never panicking. Speaks in hints and garden metaphors, as is customary in Darkwater, and appreciates the sincerity of the allies: all her trials, according to Erwin's observation, were tests of honesty - "one huge gate of trust." For the great purpose of the order, it is ready for any means, including murder and torture, and considers people and monarchs as “seeds” and “seeds” of the Tree.
