## Who He Is

Arthur Elsa May of the Gloria family was a dedicated cyre, Baron Hyrock, and castle castellan of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. ([[kajry|Kairs]]) [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] called him the "left hand" of the Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]]. In red and black, Hyroc appeared to Erwin as a "scum" with a stone mask on his face, and Erwin ironically observed that the thickness of such a mask was as sure a sign of fertility as the number of names.

Erwin referred to Heirok as one of the Prime vassals of the Origins. When Erwin returned from Beyond, Earl Lilliday, Baron [[stetkhem|Statham]], Colonels Horton and [[blekberri|Blackberry]], "the first warlords of the land of Origin" gathered in Desmond's bedroom with Hyrock. The garrison of the First Winter and two hundred of its own vassals were subordinated to Heirok. Erwin listed him, along with [[fleming|Fleming]], Lilliday, and White, among those with "enough troops to raze" the enemy fortress.

## Captain at the Lodge of Dar (1756)

In "Humans and Gods", in the retrospective "The Price of Fear", which takes place in August 1756 in Waymar, Arthur Hyroc was still "captain of the kair". Commanding the guard of the Lodge of the Seventeenth Gift, he rushed to Desmond with an urgent report right during the Duke's conversation with young [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]] and [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]. Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent Shayland]] later made Wittor understand who had brought the news and what follows from it: the captain was neither a general, nor a bishop, nor a lord of the Great House, that is, he did not see the Sacred Objects often, and therefore the "strange" in the bed could be just a curiosity.

The discovery was the body of Pramati. He was present in the lodge with Lord Thelurian, Prince [[adrian|Adrian]], Dukes Desmond and [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]. When he smelled a threat, he took a stand beside his sister-in-law. The emperor ordered the body to be destroyed and expressed the hope that "Captain Hyrok would help them." The young [[martin-shejland|Martin]] then reasoned that the captain had not rushed from distant Silens: he was not too tired, and the cloak on it was not too dusty.

## At Desmond

On March 22, 1774, when Erwin returned home, it was Hyroc who came for him in a small refectory: "My Lord, my father wishes to see you." On the way, the castellans hummed the heir that he should have welcomed his father before his mother and sister. Later, when Erwin returned from Beyond, Hyrok again led him to his father. For the first time in Erwin’s memory, the voice of the queer sounded inert: “No, my lord, his lordship is alive...”

At the feast, where Erwin, becoming Duke, read to the vassals a letter-challenge to Adrian to warn the enemy of the war under ancient laws of honor, Hyroc objected from his seat at the top table: "My Lord, this is a bad idea." Colonel Blackberry added, "The worst you can think of."

## The War of 1775

When Minerva moved the capital to the First Winter in 1775, the Castelians took it to the refectory castle. He was embarrassed by the delay of dinner and the fact that the Empress was not allowed to take the main chair. Hyroc explained the tradition: since the time of Lioreus Origin, no one has sat in this chair except the Duke, and the daring "were removed from the chair and put on the coals", which happened six times in four centuries. An empty chair is also better not to look, because the ghost of Odar Spesivogo likes to sit there. For being late, he promised to punish the perpetrators, and Mira begged not to execute anyone. He offered her three varieties of Orja, including "The Joy of Agatha" twenty-four years of exposure. Mira stated that she would punish those who did not serve her this drink in Faunterra, and Khayrok "without a shadow of a joke" approved: "The right decision, Mistress." Assuring her of the accuracy of the old tapestry with a map of the valley, he did not specify how many weavers were cut off for mistakes.

Together with General Davey and Captain Shatterhand, the Castelians were full of pessimism as the power of the Puppeteer grew. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) Nevertheless, he diligently carried out Erwin's orders for the defense of the city: he mobilized able-bodied burghers, built up to forty trebuchets in the castle and city, shot them at key points, erected earthen ramparts against Wilhelm's Persians in front of the gate, and prepared ambushes and escape routes (see Battle for the First Winter). ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu]]) The wounded Erwin later told Iona that he had outlined his defense plan in only three pigeon letters: to his father, Robert, and Hyrock.

## In "Fantasies"

In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, Hyroc is still the Castelians of the First Winter. In the summer of 1776 ("Let's catch him"), he excommunicated [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]], who raised an alarm at night because of Minerva's "ghost": "Once again, make the garrison drunk - I will throw you out of the castle." Kair Shram then gloatingly said that “the crow kicked the castellan on his shoulder” — they say, it was a ghost. ([[shram|Scar]]) Preparing for the holiday on the birthday of the mistress, the Castelians, together with the Duke, smiled, but did not contradict the orders of Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia]]. At the ball of Peace, who chose partners "by the width of the shoulders", danced with him; after the venture with bathing, Erwin instructed the castellan to prepare a change of dress for the guests. In "Closer - Further" (August 1776), the Castelians are among the few who know about the secret novel of Erwin and Mira.
