## Who She Is
Rose is the daughter of Count Erroback, the ruler of the northeast of Almera, "the pure-blood granddaughter of Pramatera Agatha". ([[erroubek|Arrowback]]) She has no brothers. When the count dies, "the colossal profits from the spark dam and the navigable river" will go to Rose and her two sisters. It appears only in "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]", which the author calls the possible fates of the heroes.

She should not be confused with Rose from the Confidential interlude. ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]])

## In "Fantasies"
### "The annoying little things"
Rose's story was told to Aunt [[karen|Karen]] [[somerset-flejm|Somerset]], brother of [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]. In the summer of 1775, when the Shavans came to Almera, Rose boarded a train with refugees at Beck Station and spoke to Somerset all the way to Faunterra. In the capital, which lived a "war fever", she volunteered from the idea of self-sacrifice, and Somerset - "there is nothing to do." They spent days at train stations, construction sites, and infirmaries. At the end of the summer, Rose confessed her love to him, and he proposed to her. Rose wrote to her father, “in the best words signed a loved one”, and the count suddenly agreed. However, Somerset's Sen. [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] responded with a "decisive no." Somerset decided Farway was afraid of the Lightharts' over-strengthening. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) His father, [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]], asked him to be patient.

Later, when Somerset fell in love with another, Rose met him at the entrance to the physics department. She reproached him not for his submission to the suzerain, but for his cruelty: he could honestly say, "Don't wait for me, Rose." The war in Almera was long over, her father called her home, her sisters laughed, and she waited in Faunterra "for stupid, blind hope." Seeing E. passing by, Somerset threw her: “I don’t love you and I never loved you. Good-bye." In the summer of 1776, a ruined Edgar told his son that marriage to Countess Arrowback "could save" the family, but Somerset had by then already broken up with her.

### “Closer and Further”
In August 1776, Mother Alicia brought Erwin a letter from the Earl of Arrowback. The Earl lamented that his beloved daughter Rose had been "the victim of a treacherous tempter" and that the poor girl's heart was broken, and asked the Duke to write her a few words of sympathy. By all indications, the letter was accompanied by an offer of Rose's hand. Erwin handed the drawing to his cousin [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]], and he promised to think.

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the fall of 1776, Rose and her father came to Erwin's council at Faunterra. She politely intervened in the conversation of the lords: as a volunteer in hospitals, she often communicated with ordinary people and knew that “not everyone is on the side of Adrian.” Many support Minerva, there are supporters of the prophet, “just people are intimidated.” When Erwin offered to "pray", only Rose seriously promised: "I will pray to Pramati, too," and her father looked at her "like a mouse." After the election, Erwin told Francis-Ilian that Earl Arrowback had married Robert "his lovely daughter", and Robert and Rose "had liked each other since the first meeting and would be happy to get engaged soon". The Prophet willingly agreed to the ceremony.
