**Colin Felicia** is a young metropolitan official with whom [[minerva|Minerva]] spent one evening in Faunterra. Through him, she first figured out how the Chamber was arranged. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]])

## In early editions of Arrow, Coin, Spark
**Invitation.** At the beginning of June 1774, Mira was looking for a man versed in politics: she needed to understand who of the Great Houses would benefit from Adrian's death before the autumn meeting of the House. Among the morning letters was a message from "Colin Felicia Angela of Soltown", the assistant to the Lord Representative from the South Road. ([[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]) He called her to show off the University's new miracle machine. The world immediately agreed.

**University.** On the stage, the master showed a “voice-on-distance”: a phrase written by a volunteer was transmitted by a spark from one car to another. Mira was delighted, and Colin kept down: if you live in the capital for a long time, then “you are no longer surprised by all sorts of scientific inventions.” When the Master offered to name an honorary guest, Colin loudly announced "Lady Gloria Northwood" and forced Mira to rise.

**Politics.** On the way to the Garden Bridge of Peace, she flattered him with everything he knew. The House consists of thirty-nine representatives, three from each land, except the Crown Lands. Without her consent, the Lord's laws do not apply outside the Crown. At the April meeting, Adrian announced rail roads, new spark shops and a general tax. According to Colin, the emperor was supported only by [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] and Ayden Almer, but Almera will lose the most on the tax. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) Aiden, Colin thought, was counting on his daughter to become the Empress's fiancée by the vote. **The garden bridge.** Colin led Mira to an abandoned dark bridge over Hanai, put on a parapet above the breach, and began to spread his arms. Mira threatened to slap me. Colin began to claim that he had fallen in love with her at the ball when they were dancing. Mira recalled that she did not dance with him, but remembers him in Alanis’s retinue: he giggled when she tried to insult her.
