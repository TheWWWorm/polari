## Portrait

Adrian Ingrid Elizabeth is the son of Lord Telurian and Lord Ingrid, the reigning emperor of the Brilliant Dynasty at the start of the cycle. ([[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]]; [[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) He has sharp cheekbones, a neat dark beard, thin mocking lips, thick black eyebrows, and Yankee dimples on his cheeks. He is a fine swordsman and dancer, loves stratagems, always wears the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]] on his belt. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) On his neck he wears a small Sacred Object: a glass rod, in which you can see the image of a long-dead woman. She's too perfect for mortal, and it's not Adrian's mother or sisters. Father David believed that Adrian imitated a mother who wore a Drop of Sun around her neck. ([[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]) He is ironic and intelligent, his charm goes hand in hand with toughness. Adrian dreamed of a rail road across the empire, of a universal tax instead of a feudal service, of strengthening the Crown and weakening the [[velikie-doma|Great Houses]] (see [[reformy-adriana|Adrian's Reforms]]). ([[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]])

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

In the summer of 1774, Adrian had to choose a bride among three candidates. They were supported by various coalitions: [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]] and Valerie Gracend. ([[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]]) The Chamber's vote on reforms depended on the marriage. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] offered him an alliance that supported reforms regardless of the bride's choice. Adrian listened to him in the tea room, but left the choice to himself.

At first, he mockingly brushed off [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]]'s complaint of killing Clive's sire and called the young Minerva a "sorcery". However, at the summer ball, Adrian invited to the first dance "Gloria Northwood", under whose name [[minerva|Mira]] hid, and later recognized her superiority in strata. He explained to Meare that he was keeping the rebel Manson with him because the lord needed to learn to forgive. He considered the murder of the heirs a warning that should force him to retreat from reforms, and refused: the conspirators "will have to complete the case." After exposing the secret of [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]], he deprived that mercy. At the end of the Solar Games, Adrian called his fiancée "Minerva Gemma Alessandra". It was actually a fake [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]].

Mira was forbidden to bury the body until the end of the investigation.

Meanwhile, the living Adrian, having shaved his beard and cut his hair, hid under someone else's guise. In March 1775, Almera was ruled by a "robust gang" of sorcerers: hired by the Shavans of the gauntlets of Biray and expelled from the West, Sputnik, Neymir and [[chara|Chara]], robbed the branches of the Bank of Shayland in free cities. ([[mik|Mik]]; [[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]; [[nejmir|Neimir]]) Adrian made a list of these wards, mapped the path of the robbers and, learning from the constable that one of them was injured, found a gang in the doctor's house outside the city wall. In the squad he was called a novice. Neymir decided that he was an outcast nobleman who violated the code of honor. Under Laxett in Blackmore County, the Wizard sent the Newcomer with Chara to scout. The hunter wore the coat of the animal, spoke the language of a scientist and led the detachment of Shavans of the Ganty of Biray. The hunter caught sorcerers and lectured the captives about power. ([[mik|Mik]]) The ruler, who causes both love and fear, becomes for the subjects the likeness of a parent. At the end of March 1775, at the old cemetery near Laxett, the Hunter threw the bound Sorcerer into the funerary well and fell asleep with the ground, leaving him a tube of bamboo stem to breathe. ## In "Humans and Gods" and "The Shadow of the Great Tree"

When the news of the attacks on the Dynasty came, Adrian decided to immediately go to Faunterra. "I didn't plan to fall off the bridge in a train car. To risk the Empire with a detachment of Shavans, to sleep on damp ground, to mess with a sorcerer,” he told supporters. In early June, his small army of Shavans, monks and southern mercenaries moved across the Lion Mountains to the port of Surlion. After listening to the Lord’s conversation with Kindar, Harmon learned that Adrian was looking for a deconstructor, a means to destroy the Objects. Magda, through mercenaries Horey and Selina, submitted a report to Vladyka about the Miransky Pass, which had been washed away by a storm, in order to turn his way to the bay, where her father's squadron was standing. Magda recalled that only thirty Shavans and ten monks obey him, and she called her price for the army, money and the road to the capital: she, the infant of the Great House, is still unmarried at twenty-six years old. Harmon watered the ambassadors in the inn near Labelin and opened the casket: it contained a Light Sphere with a copy, a [[poyas-kolduna|sorcerer's belt]] and a letter from Adrian to Queen Madeline asking for Minerva to be kept in custody. In August, the regiments of Adrian under the guise of a wedding train without a fight entered the capital: a wedding train with barrels of wine, treats and musicians “invaded” Faunterra. However, Minerva, leaving Faunterra, appointed him mayor of the city "in order of correctional labor" and moved the capital to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]].

Immediately, he told Uncle Manson to strip naked for wearing a naval uniform, and continue to wear only a clown costume. Manson's wife, who returned from the hospital where she was imprisoned by Telurian, he met suspiciously and later interrogated about Navi.
