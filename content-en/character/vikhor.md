**Whirlwind** is a peasant who served as a stableman and loader in the cart of the merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]], Louise's husband. ([[luiza|Louisa]]) He died, in fact, because of Harmon and the Light Sphere, and his death then did not give the merchant peace. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]])

## Description
A whirlwind is a "dark-haired peasant." With his wife Louise and children, ten-year-old Sarah and son Wihrenko, he rode with a train of Harmon. Harmon put him low as a fighter: "Whirlwind with Wihrenko - peasants, what to take from them. The bulls are slow, with weapons they possess only globules.” On another occasion, he called it a "dumb-headed whirlwind" in his heart. The whirlwind was reverently afraid of large cities and never visited the capital. He always grumbled, dissatisfied with everything around him, but was, according to Harmon, “though good-natured, but strong.”

Harmon was amused by his quarrels with his wife. The whirlwind grumbled at Louise under her nose, but so that she heard:
> “Give me the ice... I’m tired again, my teeth are broken.”

Louise rarely answered, "but loud and tasty", moved to another cart for half a day and could run an apple or mug into her husband. The whirlwind tried to reconcile the parents and fell under the hot hand, and Sarah gave one or the other. Louise once called her husband an old donkey, “who had four rusty nails in his head.” Harmon distinguished their quarrels, "loud and unmistakable," from his girlfriend's quiet rebukes. When Whirlwind spoke too much to [[polli|Polly]]'s blondie, "sensible Louise" instantly weighed down his nape. Harmon himself later recalled that Whirlpool "got smarter when he got a rattle."

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
### On the way
In the spring of 1774, Whirlwind escorted the convoy from [[smolden|Smolden]] via Almera and the Southern Way. When in the Southern Way, [[ben-vomak|Sir Womack]], under the pretext of checking for the gray sea, ordered the retinue to undress, Wihor stepped forward and raised his hands, as if he were going to protect his wife. ([[sizyj-mor|Blue-Grey Plague]]) Harmon was afraid that a squire would pierce him through, but Whirlpool was smarter: he ripped off his shirt and took Louise's dress. Later, when [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] defeated Womack, Whirlpool, along with Doxet, armed himself with an axe. ([[doksett|Doxett]]) In Labelin, the couple admitted that they dream of buying a piece of land with a hut in some village, and immediately argued where to settle, in Almera or in the Southern Way.

### Monasteries
When the monks of the Maximian monastery kidnapped the Sphere, Harmon came up with the idea to return it by trickery. At first, Whirlpool accompanied with Snipe Joaquin, who appeared to the monks as a messenger. The peasant was told not to give a voice: one phrase of his rude rural saying, and the disguise ended. Then, on the day of the exchange, he was dressed in the camel of the Labelin Guard and girded with a sword. The whirlwind was so perplexed by his transformation into a fighter that confusion did not come from his face. Together with Joaquin and Snape, also dressed as guards, he waited for the monks in the ruins of the fortress and walked in circles, pestering Joaquin with the question of whether he would do without a fight, until Snape threatened to hit him. ([[snajp|Snipe]])

### Injury
On July 4, 1774, the Sphere returned to Harmon, but the monks rushed in pursuit. The arrow entered the vortex in the thigh. When [[brat-lyudvig|Brother Ludwig]] overtook the unarmed Harmon, Whirlpool, without even drawing a sword, rammed the monk with his horse's chest and saved the master. Then Ludwig fell on him. The whirlwind shook off and closed its hand, and in the place of his fingers remained a bloody cult. His horse, wounded by the same blow, had to be finished off, and Whirlpool was riding with Snipe. He was taken to the train without feeling. Louise wept over her husband, but immediately ordered to serve water, rags and wine.

## Death
In "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]" it is said that the Vortex "brought rotten blood to the grave."
> "A bad death is a long, painful one. Not every warrior will endure without moans. But the Vortex was not a warrior, only a peasant and a stable man.”

He voted for two days in a row until he died. Sarah's daughter, and even Wihrenok, were crying. Joaquin these days lay without feelings, and when he woke up, Louise realized over the gray face: “Whirlwind is over.” Harmon had by then fled, taking the Sphere and the money.
