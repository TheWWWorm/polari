## Overview
Martin is the youngest son of Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent Shayland]] and brother of Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]]. A hunter, lover of dogs and loud music, he was a sadist and rapist, afraid of death and all his life was looking for a remedy for it. He loved his brother sincerely and always recognized the first, but eventually died at his hands.

## Appearance
[[minerva|Mira]], who saw him for the first time in the autumn of 1774, described Martin as a short, round-faced man, not remarkable for either the width of his shoulders or the expressiveness of his features. Apparently, his own invisibility upset him, and he dressed more loudly: in red kamzol and purple bridges, later in bright yellow, lemon. His eyes were pale, skillful and “predatoryly mobile”: for a second they clung to a person and immediately jumped to the next victim. The world came across his "buffy-eyed look". In 1775 Iona broke his jaw with a rod. The doctor inserted new teeth into him, and Joaquin squeamishly noticed his “dead incisors”.

## Youth
As a boy, in 1756, Martin was cruel. He killed a cat that made him laugh at the young Baroness Selina Dorcaster, daughter of the Earl's greatest vassal. His father punished him by squeezing him with fireplace tongs, and reminded him that the lord must keep himself in his hands. Count Vincent hoped that discipline would wean his son from antics, and Vittor believed that there was something incorrigible in Martin's soul. His mother, according to Vittor’s memoirs, called Martin a “rat bastard”, and then completely abandoned his family. Vittor reminded himself that one should not be angry with his brother: “The gods did not add intelligence to him.”

At the same time, his brother saw the good in him: Martin was cheerful, simple with soldiers and huntsmen, recognized himself as the second after Vittor and loved his brother and even his father. At the celebration in honor of the Seventeenth Gift, on the first evening after the departure of his father, Martin tried to rape the young novice from the retinue of the archimandrite. Vittor explained to the crying brother that because of this, the Shaylands could lose the county, and ordered to beg the girl’s silence. Along with his brother, their bodyguards, and their novice, Martin secretly descended into Dara's lodge. They got caught. Bodyguards and a novice were killed. Aiden Almera carried the sword over Martin's neck, and the brothers survived only thanks to the intercession of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]]. White as chalk Martin almost fell into the lava, and then saw crawling out of the pit of a burned savage, the future [[paul|Paul]]. The physician Galuers concluded in 1775 that Martin was sick with fear: once, probably in his childhood or youth, he experienced "extreme horror". The doctor assumed that Martin saw the legendary Waymar prisoner.

## In "Only One Star"
### Hunter
Martin lived on his own estate north of [[uejmar|Waymar]]. Upon learning that Vittor was returning from the First Winter, he went to the port to meet his brother. With him there were a dozen horsemen of the "wild kind". His “jagers” were more like robbers, and the dogs, according to the story of master [[master-sajrus|Cyrus]], bit the boy in front of people. Martin was often lost in the woods at the head of his rangers, once for five days. At the table he looked lustfully at Mira and Iona, but Mira noticed that it was more fear than lust: Martin was afraid of Iona. When Iona laid siege to him in public, his eyes only began to “steal” guests.

### Dungeons
Along with his servant, fugitive convict Inge Price, Martin kidnapped girls through a secret passage from the prison, including Minerva Lindsay's servant and Iona Jane's companion. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]; [[lindsi|Lindsey]]) In the dungeon of the old monastery, he and his "Apothecary" tortured and bloodied people, obtaining the "substance of life" for the elixir of immortality. In the ledger, the victims were divided into "sources" who were bleeding to death (so Lindsay died), and "trials" that were given numbered elixirs. The victims, except for Jane and Mira, were thirty-six.

On November 26, 1774, Martin captured [[minerva|Mira]]. Yangmei’s blood and the poisoning she experienced made her the best “material” in his eyes. Tied to a chair, Mira heard him yelling about "lady, bitch to my brother's wife." Mira and Jane were saved by the Emperor's men: the lieutenant of the azure guard [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] and the secretary [[itan|Ethan]]. They captured Martin and his ledger, but on the way to the dock they were met by [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] with cairns, and Mira gave Martin to her.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
In December, Iona’s cayres found barrels in the dungeon with about thirty rotting corpses of men and old women. Martin pleaded to let him go and assured him that the “imperfect elixir” would prolong his youth, and Yangmei would be impeccable. Iona first locked him in the lower circle of the dungeon until her husband's decision. Vittor burst into flames ("This is my castle, my land, my brother!"), but swore to his mother Vivian that he did not order his brother to kill. Martin was later held in the Ivy Tower. One bubble of his "imperfect elixir" Iona kept and in February 1775 took with her to [[faunterra|Faunterra]], where she secretly poured a potion to the sick [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]], and her father became better. In the capital, [[minerva|Minerva]] reproached Iona that the offender was alive and well, and suspected that Lord Chancellor Erwin himself was interested in the fruits of his "research"; Iona replied that it was her wife's duty to obey the decision of her husband, who had spared Martin. In the spring of 1775, while Iona was in the capital, Vittor declared his brother "cured" and released him. Returning to Waymar on May 5, Iona found Martin at a common table: to hide him longer, the count said, would be to breed rumors and disgrace the house.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
Iona had set kire on Martin, and [[sejmur-stil|Seymour Steel]] wanted to kill him. The physician Galuers, whom the count called to examine his brother, treated him only with drugs against anxiety and fear and advised him to repeat the course every six months. The Count didn't tell him about Martin's crimes. According to the doctor, Martin is afraid of his own death, strong people and the very mention of [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]], and the dead do not frighten him. At the same time, Martin admitted to the doctor that he was afraid of Iona and another lady.

On the night of May 28, 1775, during a [[perevorot-v-uejmare|coup in Waymar]], Martin opened a secret passage for his brother to escape. When Iona surrendered, he shouted at the gate tower, “Throw a net!”, and the electrical network fell to the north. Then he exclaimed, “Victory! We made them!”, not realizing why his brother was in despair.

## In "Humans and Gods"
### After the coup
Vittor dismissed him as "cleansing up", and Martin showed an unexpected talent. He announced to the soldiers that the "northern bitch" had betrayed her husband, and the count had been "kissed by the gods" and he had talking Objects and Rings. He made the doubter nail a cyre with a burnt chest to the gate. He divided the inhabitants of the castle into detachments and assigned a knight to each. Martin did not attend the war, but explained that he had many gamekeepers - "also, consider the army." He wanted to finish off Iona’s wound and stood with his foot on her throat. Then he spent many months mocking the prisoner. He took [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] as his assistant instead of Harry Hog.

### Messenger to Fleming
When Waymar was besieged by the North in June 1775, Vittor, at a military council, instructed his brother to go to meet the Count [[fleming|Fleming]], who was on his way to help him and convince him to join the battle. To do this, Martin was allowed to reveal a secret that the brothers had kept for almost twenty years: what happened in the box of the Seventeenth Gift. "Whit, is it possible? Martin was scared, but agreed: “The truth, let them know!” He was accompanied by Joaquin with Perst and the sunset Hasch Eilish, who claimed to know the truth about the lodge as well. ([[khash-ejlish|Hash Eilish]]) On the way, Hash Eilish provoked Joaquin to kill Martin, but he did not succumb. At Fleming's bet, Martin spent half an hour telling the count and the abbot about the dead goddess and how her secret was hidden, killing witnesses. Robustness was replaced by anger, and in the end he spoke with heat. Fleming, shocked, asked why he had kept quiet until now, and lifted the cup in his honor: “Icons will soon be painted in your honor.” Martin, who had never heard such a thing, blushed and mixed. Fleming led his men to Waymar, and the assault of the Northerners fell: at Joaquin's signal, the slopes of Castle Mountain along with the stormtroopers disappeared.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### The Siege of Waymar and the Absolute
In July 1775, when [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] was besieging the castle, Martin and Joaquin took the captive Iona out every morning in a cart to be shown to the northerners in exchange for a grain cart; according to Joaquin, he was too timid in front of the "wolf" in those days. On August 1–2, he was present in the chapel at the test of [[absolyut|the Absolute]]. When the poisoned Alanis Vittor began to decompose alive, Martin fell to his knees (“Help him!”), and when he heard the whisper of the chained Paul, rushed to his brother: “Brother, the key is transport!” The key word resurrected Vittor, and he declared himself the Chosen One. Later, when the Count was handing out awards to the Allies, Martin asked one: "Whit, I'm a lord... I want to be a Lord. To be respected!”

### March of the Chosen
In September, Vittor's army moved from Waymar through [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]; the Count's adoptive children called Martin "Uncle Martin". In early October, during a nighttime attack by Northwooders and kairs on the camp, he was shot by Perst from the roof of a cart cage, where he hid from bolts, and Iona from the cage with a pointed bone wounding him in the leg and Joaquin in the arm. Shavani then ridiculed Martin, and Vittor chastised his brother for abandoning the position allotted to him.

### Laura Farway
In the summer and autumn of 1775, Martin hunted with dogs on the "rear", lagging and deserters. Prisoner Iona was taken in a dog cage and every morning brought out on display to the northerners; according to Martin’s plan, she was fed raw bones, “like a wolf,” and he barked at the cage and treated it like a dog. Vittor promised his brother to leave him Shayland: “You will become a count instead of me!”

Following Joaquin, Martin began courting [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]], the widow of Gallard's parish, and, to impress, was smoking from the Perst into the air. One day Iona, speaking for the first time in a month, said to him from the cage: “Master, your dog understood: there is no elixir. Immortality is given by the Absolute.” Martin attacked his brother in anger: “You deliberately hid the secret of immortality!” Vittor scolded him and advised him to marry a gentle woman.

In October, Martin sat down with Laura at the table, talked to her, and then, daring from hops, stayed with her alone and began to pursue her. Laura encouraged him to kill Iona, and when he fell on her, she began to resist. “And then the darkness fell on Martin.” When he woke up, he saw her with a strangled pillow. He swore to Joaquin that it was Iona's magic. Vittor only regretted the lost "key to Almera" and ordered Martina and Joaquin to catch two northerners, kill them and throw them into the room of the murdered as "Desmond scouts".

### [[lid|Leed]]
In November 1775, Leed opened the gates to Richard and the army of the Chosen. In the basement of the Leed castle, Iona broke out of the cage and crushed Martin’s jaw with a piece of iron rod and crushed his palm. The doctor told him to cut off his hand, but Char's shavanka, under Paul's supervision, cured her with a healing object, and then took up her jaw. ([[chara|Chara]]) Martin was terrified when he saw Paul. The doctor put new teeth in him, and Joaquin wondered where he got them. Count Fleming, judging by the thoughts of his wife, stuck his daughter Violetta in the bride of Martin.

In Leed, Martin made a long recovery at Lillydeev Castle, dined with Baron Dorcaster, and tried to convince him that Iona was a witch who bewitched Vittor "still at the wedding." After learning that Dorcaster was only to execute Iona in the event of a riot, Martin stole the Baron's [[golos-boga|Voice of God]] and went to Paul. He complained that Vittor had wronged both of them: Paul had not appointed a general, and he had not given him possession. Martin offered the Gnoi-ganta Leed in exchange for an opportunity to kill Iona. Shavans stormed the city, defeated the quarter of gunsmiths and trampled the townspeople.

### Ulyana's Dust
In celebration, Martin went down to the basement to Iona's cage, where [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] was also dressed. He put Erwin on his knees, and Iona told him to lick his hand. Then Iona showed him her forearm with a short inscription of Ulyana Dust received from Gwenda: "Take off the Ring, give it to me." ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Martin burst into tears, "like a baby taken from his mother," curled up on the floor and gave Perst away. Then, on the orders of Erwin, he ordered Knut's Shavans to leave the city. Iona learned Perst's commands in advance: "Watched, listened, read lips." Together with Perst, she took away Martin and the Voice of God.

### Death
Martin fled from Leed and walked for four days on foot in the freezing cold to the Sky Scrolls to warn his brother. In the midst of the battle for the First Winter, after the death of Paul, he was brought to the pond of General Horis frostbitten, with blue lips, and barely spoke out: "Brother ... ([[oris|General Oris]]) Brother, I've come." Instead of helping him, Vittor threw himself at him: “You... you cost me the victory, you lunatic freak!” Then Martin said coldly and without stuttering:
> "Whit, apologize to me. I've always been right. I said, kill her. He said, “It will destroy us.”

Vittor, moving his brother behind his back, grabbed a dagger from the officer and twice hit him in the stomach, saying: "Forgive me, brother... I'm sorry, I'm sorry." Martin died, and his face was frozen with a deadly resentment. The Count wiped the dagger about his clothes and calmly said, "One problem solved." The Northerners saw this murder from the city wall. Joaquin, shocked by this massacre, killed the Earl of Perth ten times in a row.

## Personality
Martin is impulsive, hot-tempered, childishly touchy and loves dogs. He thought of captive Iona as the "best bitch in the pack" who was sorry to shoot. He is a sadist and a rapist, but at the same time he gives orders well, invents propaganda and knows how to get supplies. He was afraid of many: Iona, Paul, the “prisoner”, and above all death. He loved his brother sincerely, to the last, and offended him like a child. Minerva considered him not just a madman, but an instrument of someone smarter.
