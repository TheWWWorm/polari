## Origin and marriage

Ingrid is the wife of Emperor Telurian and the mother of Lord Adrian (his full name, Adrian Ingrid Elizabeth, begins with her name). ([[adrian|Adrian]]) She was a native of Nan Clair, the capital of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], and the cousin of Queen Lady in Darkness. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) It belonged to Yangmei's blood. As it is said about the marriages of the dynasty, the emperors often married Yanmei women, and Telurian took as his wife “the swamp of Ingrid — slippery, like a snake, and as poisonous, but born of the blood of Yangmei.” In her youth, she studied at the boarding house of Elena-u-Lake: his ruler proudly called among the graduates “the late mother of Vladyka Adrian.” ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]])

The marriage was arranged by Telurian's father. The court met the new empress hostilely: the nobility, led by [[menson|Manson]], the emperor's brother, openly vilified the marsh as unworthy of Telurian, and even Telurian himself lamented the choice made by his father.

## Face

At least three portraits of Ingrid were kept in the gallery of the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]], as well as for each lord: coronation, "in the hour of greatest glory" and posthumous.
- **The Coronation Portrait.** At the coronation, Ingrid was young and pretty herself, "unlike other natives of Nan-Claire." The court painter, however, erased from the canvas the slightest signs of character: he did not dare to depict the worthy nature of the empress, who was poisoned by the whole yard, but did not denigrate her. From the portrait looked "the crowning wasteland".
- **Portrait of 1765.** It was written when [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] served as a younger lady. Maturity mutilated Ingrid: premature wrinkles, yellowed skin, fallen cheeks. Good tongues said that the death of her two daughters took twenty years of her life, evil ones - that the marsh was always terrible, and it seemed beautiful thanks to the magic potion. But [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] and Father [[otets-david|David]] saw in her eyes a “game-flowing” mind that could go around any obstacle, and the ability to laugh at everything, including yourself. One of her eyebrows was dark and straight, the other gray and curved "bird wing". These eyebrows said that both order and chaos live in it, and neither has power over it.
- **A posthumous portrait.** Death ennobled her: the ugliness was gone, only sorrow remained.

Ingrid had long, thick hair, "straight lion's mane." Only the ladies could comb them, since the servants were not supposed to touch the head of Her Majesty.

## Personality

Ingrid was remembered as sarcastic, sarcastic and arrogant, "like all Yangmei." However, the portrait showed that something in the world deeply wounded her and destroyed her from the inside. At the age of six, Erwin saw "the cunning Empress Ingrid" at court and admitted to Minerva that he almost did not remember her:
> She was monstrously older than me, sarcastic and unseemly clever. I was afraid of her and avoided her as much as I could."

[[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], a seven-year-old girl, entered her as a younger lady and served in the palace for three years. She liked to comb the lady's mane, although other girls were afraid: Ingrid was hot. Alanis played for her on a sixteenth-century harpsichord made by Aleridan's craftsmen. Of the eight harpsichords of the palace, Ingrid loved only this one, calling it the best instrument of the capital. The owner always asked for the same melody from “Theresa”, and then stroked the girl’s hair and said:
> “That’s right, child: if you take something, do it perfectly. Otherwise, why take it?”

Alanis remained her admirer for life. Leaving Faunterra in 1775, she stood for a long time in front of the portraits of her "deceased mentor". Lady-in-the-Dark also mentioned Ingrid with great respect. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) The queen is usually called Ingrid's cousin, but Erwin once called her "the aunt of Lady Ingrid".

Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent Shayland]], [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]]'s father, believed that Ingrid, "though a Yankee, but a marsh," was not as special as Tellurian, and it would have been easier to talk to her. Father David called her an amazing woman. According to him, she did not have initiations to the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]], but she was close to Lady in Darkness and often helped the order.

## Owner and Fashion

Ingrid, in alliance with several Countesses, changed the very views of noble fashion. In his youth, Karen Lighthart was considered fashionable deliberately impractical: crinolines, hard corsets, wigs, white. All this was supposed to show that the aristocrat was created only for the palace. Under Ingrid, the idea that a noblewoman lived a full life, engaged in finance, politics and even war, and therefore her movements should be free, took over. Fashioned soft shoes, comfortable dresses with a cut for riding, short haircuts and naturalness instead of powder and blush. It was believed that the powder hides the features of Pramateri, and wrinkles are a sign not only of age, but also of mind.

At her request, painted the ceiling of the hall "Ladies' Feast" in the Palace of Pen and Sword. The fresco depicts the ruins of the palace, where snakes of all stripes are infested. Only the attentive eye can see the allegory of the court: the "married" serpents conduct social conversations, the marshes kiss greedily, the hechidins in the colors of the House of Almer are looking for a victim, and the death-bearer rules with dimples on his cheeks, surrounded by snakes in gold chains and diamond necklaces.

## Family

Ingrid and Telurian had a son Adrian and two daughters who died in early childhood. The year that Telurian and Prince Adrian visited Shayland (Erwin Origin was seven years old at the time), Ingrid remained in Faunterra: she was still mourning the death of her daughter.

Father David said that Adrian imitated his mother when he began to wear the [[kulon-adriana|Sacred Object]] around his neck. Adrian later recalled that Ingrid had migraines, “and was not up to the jokes.”

## Drop of the Sun

For the last ten years of her life, Ingrid wore a pendant that she “fell in love with”: a ring of white metal with amber, inside of which a spiral of hundreds of tiny lights glowed, “the whole universe created by the gods.” It was a [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Object]] from the seventeenth Gift, accounted in the domain of the Dynasty as a [[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]. In her portraits, the chain broke in a half-inch from the pendant, because the Item hung in the air itself. After his death, Ingrid Telurian broke the tradition and did not return the Item to the vault, but put it in the tomb of his wife. Only members of the imperial family and perhaps the Lady in the Dark knew about it.

She was buried in the Temple of Farewell in Arden; the seventh tier of the tomb is described as referring to the times of Meinir, Telurian and Ingrid. In May 1775, Erwin guessed from her portraits that the Drop of the Sun was one of the missing Objects of the Seventeenth Gift. He rushed to Farewell, opened the sarcophagus and saw a dried mummy with a pendant on his chest. Then he became convinced that it was the Drop of the Sun - the missing detail of [[absolyut|the Absolute]], for which the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]] is hunting. Erwin asked the bishop for forgiveness and decided not to take the shrine: "This is the subject of the bishop Ingrid - with her and will remain." Instead, he ambushed her in the tomb. Heretics with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] still broke through in Farewell. According to the Voice of the Crown, thirty-nine people were killed, the crypt of Telurian was vandalized, the remains of Ingrid were desecrated and looted, and a Drop of the Sun was stolen.
