## Overview
Maurice Elvira Dorothy, Duke of Labelin, is the ruler of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] and head of the Great House of Labelin. ([[dom-labelin|House Labelin]]) His vassals were the Marquess of Greysend, the Marquess of Windley, the Marquess of Jeremiah Doyle and the Baron Hugo Darryl, the Duke's "chain dog" and "iron fist". ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]]) The House of Labelin held near-monopoly control over the North's trade. The Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] traded unsuccessfully with Maurice every year on trade margins and called him a "boar" who "doesn't want to hear anything". The Originals owned eighty-seven [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]], and the Labelins about sixty, and this did not give Maurice peace. According to Wittor Shayland, Maurice had bought the Item from a certain Western lord the previous year for thirty-six thousand hilt, entrusting the deal to sham people.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
### Summer Games
In the summer of 1774, Maurice arrived at the court with his wife and three Greysends to secure Valerie Greysend's marriage to [[adrian|Adrian]]. ([[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]]) He bought the support for this in advance: Count Reis received a trade route through the Smokey Dahl, and Maurice received the support of the West in return. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) At the reception, the joker [[menson|Manson]] met the Duke at the whole court with shouts: "Behemoth! That's a behemoth man!" — and for a long time listed the food. Maurice retweeted "red as cancer."

[[minerva|Mira]], trying to calculate "Lord C", convinced herself that Maurice Labelin had killed her father, Baron Rosbet and Ser [[adamar|Adamar]]. The error was soon discovered. Maurice staged a violent scene for Valerie’s mother, called the girl “nonsense” and “an empty place” that “would not even seduce a joke,” and refused to support her — even on the eve of Sofiin’s day. Mira realized he wasn't a conspirator.

### Buying Spheres
At the end of June 1774, [[kharmon|Harmon]] sold the Light Sphere to the Duke in Labelin. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) The negotiations were conducted by Darryl, and the Duke himself, "unimaginably fat", silently looked at the goods. Harmon asked for 41 thousand hilts, but Maurice, offended by having been kept waiting and by the Sphere having first been offered to another buyer, bargained the price down to 36 thousand. Later it turned out that Maurice got a fake (see Sale of the Bright Sphere). ([[lzhe-sfera]]) Soon after Harmon's plan, the abbot of the monastery of St. Maximian was delivered a fake letter with the caption "Maurice Elvira Dorothy of the Sofia family, Duke of Labelin." The monks were accused of sacrilege.

## In "Only One Star"
During the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]], Maurice remained loyal to the Crown. After Evergard's death, he was one of the few great lords to personally swear an oath of allegiance to Adrian. His army of fifty thousand, including thirty thousand peasant militia, spent weeks getting soaked in the mud on the Trade Road. The duke himself never appeared to the soldiers: there was a rumor that he was too fat to sit in the saddle. In November 1774, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] defeated a Southern Route force at [[labelin|Labelin]]. Maurice fled the city by train along with the palace guards, nearby vassals, family items, jewels and spark weapons, and the northerners entered the city without a fight. He threw the pigeons in a hurry, and the master mailman himself came to Erwin to sell the pigeon mail tapes. Adrian didn't come to his aid.

## In “The Useful People”
In the winter of 1775, the Duke had only a third of the Southern Way, a handful of landless vassals and three million gold ephes. Grace became the new capital. At the council, Maurice announced that he would no longer fight with Origin, but was ready to buy anyone, “even the whole damn North.” After listening to [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]], he himself outlined the plan: to buy [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] all the spark eyes and sell cheap to the enemies of the North, so that the knighthood is outdated. He was willing to lose more than a million. Two million in gold he entrusted only to his daughters, and he went to the capital to “represent a beaten puppy.” Buckley was tasked with finding Harmon and bringing the Sphere back. About the mercenary, who “wanted to leave with everything he knows”, the Duke only laughed and ordered to take the “Seventh” instead of the “Sixth”.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
In the spring of 1775 Maurice visited the Minerva Palace and dined with it daily. According to Buckley, it was on the orders of the Duke that he supplied the [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]] with spark self-shooters: the peasants had to kill some of the kairs. The leader of the rebels [[salem|Salem]] went to the capital, demanding to meet with "his Duke Maurice Labelin" or with the Empress. When the peasants approached the capital, Minerva did not take Maurice to negotiate with them, but the Marquis of Gracend. She reasoned that with the loss of the capital, the Duke lost half of his influence, and most of the Snowdrops were subjects of the Marquis.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
### In the Chamber
At the first meeting of the House, the Fat Dolphin climbed up the podium with difficulty and demanded the return of “every acre” of land lost in the Northern War, because he “just performed a vassal oath.” ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) The Snow Count Lilliday replied that there was no law against feudal wars. He presented letters of thanks to the citizens of Labelin to Origin and accused Maurice of allowing a peasant revolt. The Northwoods shouted "Shame on the ticket!", and even the Marquis of Graysend did not support the suzerain. Maurice later complained in the House that Shimmery had sold his eyes to Hope and Origin, preventing other lands from bidding. Upon learning of the upcoming Census of Objects, he wrote to Grace: "For the gods' sake, hide this crap out of sight."

### Conversation in Small Tea Room
Erwin lured Maurice into a conversation and showed him a crippled Buckley. Under the torture of the kairs, he confirmed that the Duke ordered the eyes to be transferred to the Snowdrops. Maurice admitted that he had given the rebels “only a dozen eyes,” and betrayed the Sphere’s secret. He paid for it, but received a fake: small objects assembled in one, with an axis ring that rotates for a long time, but not forever. He kept it in Grace. The monastery of St. Maximian, according to him, was burned five days after the purchase by raiders led by a northerner nicknamed Ice. The Duke blamed Harmon and the monks for the forgery. After receiving permission, Maurice himself took the knife off the table and stabbed Buckley.

At the end of the book, Maurice announced in the House that the South Road Expeditionary Corps under Darryl had started a war with Shimmery to capture his sparkly eyes; the war was to last until all eyes passed to the Knights of the South Road or Shimmery capitulated. Prince Hector threw the glove in his face and demanded an immediate fight and then the Duke's arrest. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) Minerva banned the fight in the House and refused to arrest: the war was declared by all laws. Both Schimmery's representatives, Hector and Francis-Ilian, left the meeting indignantly.

## In "Humans and Gods"
Minerva was looking for an alliance with Maurice against Origin. The Duke politely thanked her for her participation, lamented the luck of the northerners and the weakness of the travel infantry, but replied:
> "The great House of Labelin consists of the loyal vassals of the Crown. It has been and will continue to be so.”
The meaning of this courtesy was understood only later, when the Labelins stood up for Adrian.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Bet on Adrian
In the summer of 1775, even before Adrian's return to the capital, Maurice overbought the leaders of the [[zub|Tooth]] Sunflowers and the Hand of Dodge, whom Minerva had sent to pacify the Southern Way, and placed their army under Adrian's banner. Through Valerie Gracend, the Labelins were enticed into treason by her husband, the Silver Fox. ([[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]])

Upon learning of her daughter's engagement, Maurice arranged for her to be carried out in Grace. Adrian, he says, is a “leper” and “perfect scapegoat,” and sleeping with him is “just like having a jackal.” Magda called her father a coward who did not dare neither to support Origin, nor to fight to the end. Maurice became numb, but gave in and forgave her with a warning: "This is the only time." Sphere and her fake, he ordered to leave Adrian as a wedding gift, so that the son-in-law's debt grew, and pardoned Harmon.

### Loans and collateral
After the wedding, Maurice taught his daughter to stay alert. She must have a lady-in-waiting who shares food with her, and four knights nearby. The main thing is to get pregnant, because “after the victory” Adrian can change dangerously. He called his son-in-law’s plans “dog shit”: the treasury, the crown and Hilt remained with Minerva, Shayland had three dozen Rings, and five Horton battalions were on [[bek-most|Beck]]. Previously, Magda, acting on behalf of the Crown, borrowed from her father a hundred thousand ephesas secured by the Palace Bridge. Adrian gave the South Route the County of Main with six silver mines as a "reward for faithful service". In the new loan agreement, Maurice demanded the Citadel of the Throne, a "symbol of history" that is more than a thousand years old. Adrian wrote her off to her father-in-law.

### Hiking to the north
In the campaign, when Magda portrayed pregnancy, Maurice gained such weight at the court that the courtiers “roasted around him like wasps.” Every day he sent his daughter notes about how proud she was, and advised her to immediately get a letter from her husband for the First Winter. The Duke's wife and sons visited the court, "exhausted" by envy, and the next day he sent them back to Labelin. In October 1775, when the Shimmerians liberated [[soltaun|Soltown]] from the kairs, Maurice, as ruler of these lands, met the court there and rode in the same carriage with Adrian. At lunch he made peace with Francis-Ilianus. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) The terms he and Adrian discussed in advance, and the Duke was almost happy to give up the trophies. The Shimmerian eyes remained in service with the travelers until the end of the war, and then returned to Shimmery or paid for, with the Labelins receiving the right of priority purchase. In addition, Maurice agreed to give the king the head of Colonel Horey, who had fled to Magda, and shook the king's hand. He silenced his outraged daughter (“Don’t you dare!”) and with a furious look he led out of the table. The king then presented him with the Fleshless Bird, a personal object of Elena the Traveler, able to see at any distance, and asked to give Adrian full access to it; ([[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) Maurice was initiated into this surprise in advance and himself gave a sign to the servants. Adrian promised his father-in-law the First Winter.

On December 2, 1775, the Day of Descent, Maurice, together with Baron Darryl and the entire court of Adrian in Main, followed through the Bird for the Battle of the First Winter. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) When the Silver Fox predicted that the Puppeteer would execute all the inhabitants, the Duke frowned: “I strongly hope not,” because the city was promised to him. Seeing the turning point in favor of the northerners, stuttering, he asked: “K-k-how did this happen?” Manson imitated him and reminded him that the Kairs ambushed "just like you have in Labelin." After the victory of the Origins, Maurice broke into his daughter from the military council. He demanded peace: the city is “poor” and burned, and there is no need to risk everything good “for the sake of this hole”. His father admitted that the last time Magda was right: thanks to her risk, he returned the Southern Way, received a “quarter of Faunterra” and Main.

## In "Fantasies"
The author calls the stories “Fantasies” possible fates of the heroes after the end of the cycle.

The Duke wanted to pick up all the lands of the Crown, and at the same time hoped for “his own wealth, the fear of the lords in the North and the man in the coffin” and “an intelligent daughter”. When Magda came to him from the First Winter, he only reminded her who drank the Orge, "You're pregnant." After learning that she had given it to the Virgins Windley and Main, Maurice did not scream: he understood that his daughter would not have gone for it without a good reason. He personally vouched for her and struck a hand with the Duke of the North, becoming the actual guarantor of the deal. Maurice took Adrian's crystal coffin with a frozen [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]] and allowed the Origins to "split on a frozen white face". He lifted the blockade on Windley and handed over to the Northerners one of the most profitable cities of the Duchy. In exchange, Minerva officially recognized the debts of the Dynasty to him, and in Faunterra, the Throne Citadel, the Air Bridge, the Admiralty and a number of other buildings passed to Maurice. So he began to own in the capital more than the Dynasty itself.

The battle for the First Winter turned out to be “a triumph of the Labelins, who did not lose a single soldier.” Maurice left his sons to restore order in the Southern Way and settled with Magda in the Holy See. The upper floors filled with luxury, the lower floors filled with the knights of Baron Hugo Darryl. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]]) He now called his daughter a “golden cat” from a wish-fulfilling fairy tale, but kept her on a financial leash: without his consent, Magda did not spend and did not speak. Father and daughter kept spies at the town hall and Adrian's palace. New loans to his son-in-law the Duke was in no hurry to give.

In the spring of 1776, Maurice received Professor [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]] in the Citadel and proudly noted that the genius of science was also "of Sophia's kind": "The Great Prammother has lost Agatha's nose again." He gave the inventors about five hundred ephemera of "donation", promised to buy the device for ten thousand when he was brought to mind, and asked not to give it to anyone before October. His daughter explained his purpose: “to preserve what we have.” According to him, Minerva for the Labelins is not worse than Adrian, while sitting in the First Winter, and only the election of Erwin will do them harm. If Adrian “puts the cattle off the chain,” the House will turn away from him and choose Origin.

In the summer of 1776, Adrian, according to the Origins, was constrained in the means: the Duke of Labelin was in no hurry to give him new loans.

When Minerva outlined her program, offering the lords full political freedom at the cost of moderate levies on the subjects, Labelin, although he, as a successful ruler, would have to moderate his appetites, applauded one of the first - following Francis-Ilian, Iona and the gant Cort.

## Personality and Appearance
Maurice is unimaginably fat: fat folds of the neck over the gate, he has three chins, sagging red cheeks, "like a mastiff", nose like a plum and small "pig" eyes. It is rumored that he could not sit in the saddle, and it is not easy to get up from the table. He is rude to his relatives and swears no worse than his daughter. He is a miser, "even more mean than his daughter," cautious and cowardly. He prefers to act with money, bribery and other hands and values peace above fame. He reduces his wisdom in life to the difference between desires and resources: you can only hope for what you have. Magda calls him a coward, but he knows how to give in time and eventually finds himself in a big win. When necessary, he is ruthless: he stabbed Buckley himself and laughed at the mercenary who “wanted to leave”.

## Relationships
### Magda
He scolds his daughter “cow”, “pig” and “stupid”, but always was proud of her mind and entrusted her with two million. He considered the Shimmerian adventure crazy and forgave only after a quarrel, and after peace in the First Winter, “the soul does not tea” in his daughter.
### Adrian
Maurice's son-in-law despises and fears his revenge, but uses it as a bet. He gave him money on bail of the capital buildings, and eventually took away from him the Puppeteer and half of the influence in Faunterra.
### Origins
The Origins are longtime enemies of the House of Labelin. Erwin robbed Maurice of half the duchy and humiliated him in the House, but in the end Maurice became the guarantor of peace with the North.
