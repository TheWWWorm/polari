**Irving** is a kair from the northern guard of [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] in [[uejmar|Waymar]]. He is observant and prudent, and Iona values him above the straightforward [[sejmur-stil|Seymour Steel]]. At the end of May 1775, it was Irving who brought her letters and a printing ring to [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]].

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
**Adjutant.** When Iona wrote to Erwin about selling the Sphere, he sealed the letter, took it to the dovehouse, and traced the shipment to Kair Irving: on that day, he "performed the role of Iona's adjutant while Seymour was out of the castle."

**Prisoner.** When Iona was interested in the prisoner of Waymar prison, she summoned Irving, her second mate, and gave him several errands. He commissioned a survey of the Shayland veterans with the most talkative and good-natured ghres, judging that the soldiers would be afraid to frank with the kair officer. He also noticed the strangeness in the stories about the escape: what did the two guards in the guardhouse at the exit, if the camera was in the farthest corridor. Together with Iona, he reread the diary of [[minerva|Minerva]] Stagforth and offered to conduct his own exploration in the dungeon. There he pulled a pointed hook out of the wall ("The mystery with the weapon is solved, my lady. No naked hands"), and noted that the stones of the destroyed wall have no blood, and the prisoner would have enough four stones. Locking the iron door, he threw a stone at her, and then opened the door and began to wait: soon in the corridor, footsteps were heard and a soldier appeared with a torch, followed by Master [[master-sajrus|Cyrus]].

Six kairs with Iona found Lard's carpenter, a former jailer. Irving questioned him: Iona did not understand why Lard was asleep, and Irving understood "excellently, but did not give an answer." He yelled at the jailer, “Select the snot,” pointed to the scarlet sunset (“See the sun?”), to find out why he decided that the prisoner was “of ours,” and asked what had happened before — the death of the count or escape. When he heard the answer, he said, “It’s clear. You’ll be fine,” Larda said. On the way back, Iona noted that Irving proved to be "a good assistant in the investigation - observant, thoughtful", in contrast to Seymour's harsh straightforwardness. When the crew blocked the way on a dark street, the kairs surrounded Iona, and she, according to Irving, stood between her horse and his horse, and he stretched a shield over her.

**Captain of the Guard.** After the massacre in the inn, Iona stripped Seymour of his position and ordered the transfer of command to the "kairu Irving". In the following days, Irving, already "the new captain of her guards", asked Iona to meet in private: he heard rumors that the Kairs would be sent off to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], and offered to notify the Duke. He interceded for Seymour: he "exactly complied with the northern law." Iona replied, “We’re not in the North.” Irving replied, “We are the North!” and immediately saw a shadow on her face. Iona later refused to hear the “whispering” of these words within herself.

He also reported on the prisoner's investigation, which continued even though Iona had forgotten about him. He did not find out the origin of the prisoner: there is no name in the judicial archives or in the records of the prison keeper. There was only a thread: the old servants remembered that then a Northwood spy was acting in the castle, hanged two years later, which means that Count Elias, who lived ten miles from Waymar, could know about the prisoner. ([[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]]) Irving suggested that the fugitive was waiting for the ship in the old harbor and that the prisoner was a prominent sunsetr. He was embarrassed by two deaths: the jailer who allegedly drowned in October of the same year, and [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent]] himself. When Nexia went naked for breakfast, Irving, along with Somerset, rushed to remove the camsol to cover it.

**Chaser.** On 27 May, Iona asked Irving if [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] could be intercepted; he replied that the vessel had already set sail. She then told him her questions and conclusions about the prisoner. She then told him to jump into the first town outside Shayland, send two birds from there with letters sealed with her ring, and rush to Faunterra himself. Irving countered that he would take her away by force: the Duke ordered her to be protected at all costs. Iona replied, "I am the Origin. I am the North.” Then he wished for Agatha's help, and left with her the grayling, and rode eastward. Iona sent after him on a stronger horse to give the master a horse when Irving drove his.
