Znaharka is the nickname of the messenger of the Puppeteer, who owned the healing Object, which was called the Hand of the Znaharka by her. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) Her real name is not mentioned in the books. She only appears in two chapters of [[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]], but her visit has influenced the fates of Desmond, Alanis, Craig Northwood, and Minerva herself, and her Subject and blood then surface for the rest of the cycle.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
### Deal with the Puppeteer
The puppeteer offered Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Origin]] to cure her husband [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] of a [[kamennaya-khvor|stone disease]], and in return asked for a talking Object, which [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] mined in the Beyond ([[golos-boga|Voice of God]]). Sophia agreed. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] saw the deal as a trap and ordered the messenger to be intercepted at the gate.

### First visit
At the quay gate of the palace in Faunterra, she came hunched over like a beggar and demanded to be led to Lady Sophia. When the kair Tenn reached for the sword, the hump spread: the hump was a pretense. With two fingers in the thimbles, she tore the blade from the cross, and Ragolfa put her hand in the groin with a red spider web: “Do not twitch, boys, or I will tear it off, but I will feed you.” Then she told me to say, "The puppeteer sent the Old Lord's Infirmary to heal."

Jemis with three sentries - Werner, Tenn and Ragolf - took her to Desmond. Werner she seemed either a bazaar fortune teller, or the mistress of the brothel. She went to a crack, the kire called "boys", over Desmond said: "Oh, poor thing." When Jemis refused to call Lady Sophia, she wanted to leave and closed herself off from Werner's sword with a flickering pink shield. Desmond himself stopped her: he gave Lord Origin's word that she would see his wife and receive the Item after healing. "All right, old man, I'm good."

The red ones became salads. The noblewoman placed her hand on the Duke's forehead and quietly sang with larynx torn sounds, and the radiance slipped over his face. She announced that she would come twice more: "For the third time, my lord will recover, but not before." The second time she needed to see Lady Sophia and hear her noble oath that the messenger would not be touched and would give the Item.

### Meeting in the summer theater
In the morning, Desmond became noticeably better: he sat down, moved his hands, bent his legs. He and Erwin considered it bait and decided to intercept the sign at the gate. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] didn't know what helped her father: [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]'s Item or Potion. Together with [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], she decided to check whether the Subject is able to treat. Alanis reasoned that the killer would have had enough of Finger of Wilhelm, and the cure is given to the one who is sent to be treated.

Iona told the guards to bring Znaharka not to the cellar, where he ordered Jemis, but to the summer theater. The signmaker came to the palace under a sack on a grocer's sleigh. On stage, she took Iona for Lady Sophia, and when she cut her hand, she first advised her dog to lick. She still healed the wound for the diamond: the edges of the cut were tightened, as if they were stitched with a thread.

Alanis demanded the scar removed. The signmaker felt her face and cut off: “If I could return the beauty, do you think it would be?” But after leaving the Duchess left: “I can ... But the price is big" - and lured her to whisper the price in her ear. There were footsteps and someone yelling, and Znaharka jumped off the stage and ran.

### Death
The Cairos chased her down a dark alley to the tents of the Northwoods. The clerk called for help, ran into a tall warrior and fell. Werner's sword hangs over her. She put out her palm - "Prayer" - and he struck, only then realizing that the hand was naked: "Should be, the witch wanted to surrender." At the same moment, a Northwood soldier stabbed Werner with an axe, protecting, as he thought, an unarmed woman.

[[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]] came out to the noise and, finding the Object on the corpse, declared it his trophy. He lost the body and Hand to Erwin only in exchange for General Gore's corps and the post of commander-in-chief. Erwin summed up: The witch doctor died, one kair was killed, the other lost his leg, he had a conflict with Northwood, and the Chamber will open with a scandal. Alanis objected that now they at least know that the Puppeteer has not only a weapon, but also a means of healing.

### After
Erwin considered Znaharka an assassin. He explained to Iona that the Puppeteer had sent her to kill them, but did not make a major bet on her. The attempts of the Origins to awaken the Object, repeating its movements, did not lead to anything (see the Hand of the Znaharka). ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]])
