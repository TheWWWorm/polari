## Who He Is

Wexler is the general of the Dukes of Almer. His regiment, the first Aleridian, was considered the best in the Almeran army. It was served by heavy knights in white coats, "the color of the Almeran nobility". [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] knew the general personally. According to him, Wexler was to Duke Almer what [[stetkhem|Statham]] was to his father, and Baron Doyle to Labelin: "a mother dog as devoted as he is ferocious." Erwin regarded him as [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s best general and a "old-school" general, unlike himself, an "adventurer".

## In "Humans and Gods"

### The Battle of Beck Castle

In early June 1775, Erwin, with four hundred Xes, entered [[bek-most|Beck]] Castle on the Earl of [[erroubek|Arrowback]]'s lands. The Count had previously assured Gallard of his loyalty, and the prior sent Wexler's regiment to his aid. Arrowback suggested a stratagem: bring the battalion under yellow flags to meet the general and take him south. Erwin agreed, but secretly led the kairs into the forest by the road.

Wexler knew from a spy among the count's watchmen, on whose side the count was. He made a night throw of forty miles. Squires and light cavalry the general was in a hurry, and gave their horses to the knights as a winding. Without negotiation, his knights struck the count's column, which half got out of the passage between the hills and could neither build nor retreat. Mother [[mat-kordeliya|Cordelia]] noted that it was important for the church court who first drew the sword: Wexler's men were the first to begin.

When the general himself put the reserve into battle, Erwin hit him in the back. The Xs swept the right flank and rushed to headquarters. In a gilded helmet and a white cloak of the general there was Lieutenant Ronald Caseworth, and Wexler himself was already "in a good mile." Manfried's company chased him, but was ambushed hastily, and the general left. Wexler lost more than two hundred knights killed and about three hundred prisoners, almost all of the heavy cavalry.

[[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] later called the battle "a real shame": Wexler escaped because Erwin did not give out clear orders and staged "foolish races between the kairs." Captive knight Sir Michael, who passed to Erwin, said Gallard "bought Wexler with giblets" for a barrel of silver. General Malcolm's squire, who's blinking about it, was whipped for slander.

### Persecution and Death

Wexler took the remains of the regiment to the fortress of Voronovo Pero. There, along with the first Black Sea regiment, he had three thousand swords. As Erwin moved toward [[fliss|Fliss]], Gallard ordered, "Catch and intercept." Wexler led a corps of two regiments. Colonel Daniel Blackmore, nicknamed Big, was below him in rank, but higher in feudal hierarchy. Blackmore demanded an immediate attack, and Wexler wanted to press the Northerners against the walls of Fliss and raised his eyebrows at his advice.

Erwin pretended to throw an army and rode with one company to Fliss. Almera's scouts captured his bodyguard Arthur the Twin, and Wexler believed. He decided to encircle the camp of the northerners in the Beaver Forest at night and strike from three sides. The knights of the right flank, the general led around himself. On the way, they met riders in white coats with almer pennants. In fact, it was [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]'s disguised company, and she fired crossbows at Wexler. Then the white knights shouted “Wexler killed!” and broke into the headquarters and killed Blackmore. The forest fire destroyed it.

About three hundred of Wexler's surviving knights went north, to Fliss, and prepared the city for defense. Erwin's battalion, commanded by [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]], put out a forest fire and was a day late for the city. Lieutenant Caseworth and the General's standard-bearer, who saved Wexler at Beck, were captured for the second time. After the first fight, Erwin promised to hang them if he met them again, and Gordon Sue ordered them hanged.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

When Jemis used Perst against the enemy, there was talk among the X's. It was recalled that in Almera, the Leed Wolves killed General Wexler "without any Rings", and there were much fewer casualties then.

## Trivia

- Erwin calculated in advance that the general would not come to Beck blindly, and assumed that Wexler had a spy among the county watchmen. That is why he waited in the forest, and did not go with the count's battalion.
- In the battle of Beck, Erwin slaughtered Wexler's knights with the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]], and the sword "sung with delight".
- Erwin built the night plan as a “chain of conclusions”: the general, “trained in bitter experience”, will not rush into battle head-on, but will wait for Erwin to be at the head of the army.
