## Biography
Damon Emilia Gerda of the Agatha family is the younger brother of [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] and [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]'s cousin. He was hot, loved duels and at the same time devoted to his cousin.

### Appearance and the “curse of beauty”
Agathan features were embodied in Damon in all their splendor: expressive lips, sharply outlined volitional cheekbones, deep dark eyes and, worst of all, sunny light golden hair. Damon himself hated his appearance: the warrior of the North is not to the face of "girl curls". Because of them, he was called Cute, Handsome, Cute and even Golden Hilt. He fought as hard as he could: for months he did not wash his head so that his hair darkened, wore a cheap uniform with a necessary pair of spots and rushed to fights in the hope of getting a scar on his face. Nothing helped.

In all his troubles, Damon blamed beauty. Because of her, he believed, he was never given a battalion, and with all his abilities he commanded only a company in the garrison of the First Winter. Because of her it was impossible to choose from many in love girls. Even when the avalanche swept his family estate, he considered it a click on the nose from the Agatha the Radiant herself, so as not to get caught. Brother Robert tried to explain that it was a matter of quick temper, not appearance: “The mountain eagle will look at you obliquely — you will chase after him with a sword.” Damon listened to him out of respect, but stayed with his opinion.

According to Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collectible edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, on the day of Erwin's return from Beyond, Damon commanded a company of mountain guards in the Skvorki Neba gorge and therefore was not on advice at Desmond's bedside. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]; [[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]])

### Erwin's childhood friend
If painful and withdrawn [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] had a friend as a child, it's Damon, hot-tempered, healthy and pugnacious. They were related by the fact that both considered themselves white crows: Erwin was too kind-hearted for a northerner, Damon is too beautiful. At the same time, the narrator notes that he was a very successful warrior: he quickly considered, skillfully fought, knew how to inspire people and knew good tactics. Damon called Erwin a "brother" than he reminded him of the deceased, as it was then believed, [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]]. Robert’s older brother was jokingly called Bertie.

### In "Only One Star"
In August 1774, when Erwin became duke, Damon sat among the vassal primaries on a platform during a feast in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. It was he who broke into the Duke's office with two letters, from Aleridan by the ambassador and from the emperor himself, which showed that Adrian had struck the first blow. Damon was at his cousin's, receiving guests, and reporting on envoys — including [[mark-voron|Mark]] and [[itan|Ethan]] — who had arrived from [[adrian|Adrian]]. At night he sat in Erwin's office talking to the ambassadors, exchanged ambiguous courtesies with the Raven ("I will certainly be at the court in the near future - perhaps even before you"), and, leaving, left in the queer office of the night guard so that the duke would not be alone with the ambassador; he returned to the middle of Erwin's story of the Beyond. When Erwin, after listening to Mark, ordered to search the ambassador, find capsules with poison and send him to the dungeon, it was Damon who was instructed to wake the castellan and appoint interrogation masters.

Preparing a campaign against the emperor (the Northern Flash), Erwin distributed high-profile positions to trusted people. ([[myatezh-oridzhina]]) Damon, who had long dreamed of a battalion, explained that it was impossible to remove any of the commanders from his post before the war, but put him above them - "master of fights." Damon squeezed his cousin so that he had his shoulders crunched. Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica]], in a farewell conversation with her son, was sore that she could not even understand what the position of Damon was - "the rank of the future illustrious hero." Under his leadership there was a special squad of the best fighters - X, to which the winners of the tournaments were adjacent; the badge of distinction was the cherry cross on the chest, invented by Erwin. Damon Erwin himself was banned from the tournament: “Cousin, you command my best fighters. No one should see you get out of the saddle.” Damon cheered loudly for his assistant and friend [[brant-stil|Brant Steel]], who took second place.

On the river Wise Damon offered to cross at night under heavy shields. When Erwin deceived the travelers with a false camp, Damon’s horsemen, along with the battalion of the Earl of Main, were the first to come to the road shore, and in the evening Damon laughed: “Nervous, gentle, gentle ... and thought that everyone was lying!” Instead of marching on the [[labelin|Labelin]], which was demanded by the generals, Erwin led Robert and Damon with cavalry to the east, to the sea. A week later, the path to Windley was blocked by the army of the Marquis of Windley, four times superior to the cavalry of the northerners. ([[uindli|Windley]]) Damon asked if it was possible to get around, but Erwin, taking the snake-grass, led the riders in a frontal attack and himself rode shoulder to shoulder with his cousin. When the road in front of the hill was torn down and the archers fired a volley, Damon drove the kairs forward ("Walk! The Duke is with us!”), and after the defeat of the travel phalanges, he twice repeated Erwin’s order to stop the persecution.

In early October 1774, in [[soltaun|Soltown]], Damon, along with Erwin, feasted at the merchant Gobart-Synthal, where the dancer fed him strawberries. ([[gobart-sintal|Gobart-Sintal]]) In the morning he, along with Robert, Earl of Lilliday and Colonels Horton and [[blekberri|Blackberry]], came to the Duke with the question of why he had left the ships to the three richest merchants: the generals decided that the ports were taken for the sake of the naval campaign on Faunterra, and Erwin was surprised to learn about it. Damon insisted on hitting Labelin ("One week march - and the capital of the South Road is in our hands!"), and when Erwin declared [[dojl|Doyle]] a target, he offered to entrust the castle to the Northwoods. The news that Adrian had thrown the regiments to the south, against the Steppe Fire, only strengthened him in his desire to "wipe them into powder."

Under Doyle on October 15–16, after an unsuccessful assault, Erwin sent Damon with a white flag to arrange a meeting with the Marquis. In the cathedral of Gloria, Damon guarded the Duke with three Xes; when the young Marquis Jeremiah laughed at Erwin in the face, Damon slapped him, raised him from the gate from the bench, put a knife to his throat ("No one laughs at the Duke of the First Winter"). At the council, he spoke in favor of a new assault (“Yes, brother, one choice”), and this “brother” reminded Erwin of Richard. Damon aloud threatened the captured citizens with reprisal, himself not believing that the “soft” Erwin would decide on her. In the evening after the execution of the prisoners, he came to his cousin, who was drinking alone, and said to him, "You did the right thing," and reported two strange petitioners: the priest [[otets-david|David]] of Almera and the woman who refused to give his name. It was [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]. At the end of October, on the X-ray, when Erwin told the champions to train on spears fourteen feet long ("The champion's spear should be longer"), Damon added to the general laughter: "And thicker."

At the military councils, Damon always stood for a decisive frontal attack. At the beginning of November, on the Trade Route, he supported the Earl of Hammer ("Let's cut through and go on!") and was outraged that the army, so long waiting for the Northwoods, would no longer wait for them. At Labelin in late November, he urged his cousin: “Stop playing tricks. The Force is now behind us! Let him who is weak scheming." On the morning of December 2, 1774, on the Day of Descent, it was he who awakened Erwin at [[piksi|Pixie]] with the news that the entire imperial army was moving towards the camp, and on orders he raised the X-cloaks. When Erwin at the council handed over command to [[stetkhem|Statham]] and announced that he was leaving, he grabbed Damon's hand and led him out of the tent; under the protection of three hundred X's, they rushed north, leaving the army trapped (the [[bitva-pri-piksi]]). On the eve of the battle, a detachment of X-cloaks, commanded by Damon, was sent to the nearest station, Fairwell on the River Mile, twelve miles from Pixie. The Xes detained two passing trains there in addition to standing on the tracks of the empty custom train; the tractor crew was only three of six people, and Damon, asking the driver if he would prove that the three of them, did not waste time searching for the others. This train with a fortified wagon for valuables later played a role in the abduction of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]]. On three trains, the Duke's detachment rushed to the Land of the Crown. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]) At the capital station, Damon reported that none of the guards had left, and he was one of the first to jump at the head of the twenty to the palace. At [[faunterra|Faunterra]], he was one of the first to break into the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]], and when Erwin opened the gates to the army, he took Alanis, who rushed to meet him: “So unfair! I killed more than he did!” and I got a cuff from my cousin.

In the distribution of roles before the siege (Taking of Faunterra), Erwin appointed him as a "second himself": "Agatha the Radiant will smile more often if one of her grandchildren becomes in command of each battle." ([[bitva-za-faunterru]]) Damon cheerfully replied that maybe they will kill enemies in a day, and at night they will celebrate, and Alanis winked. To Major Backfield's boastful speech, he replied, "What do you want, a chatty dog?" and spat in the slaughterhouse. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) When the guards on the boats moved to the island, the Origins divided the flanks: Damon led the X-cloaks to the coastal sand and cut the landings, losing fifteen people. In the following days, he joked at dinners in the palace, and one day raised his head from the parquet, thrown a stone in the window, and informed his cousin that Derreck's kire's foray into the town hall behind the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] had failed. Later, he and Alanis insisted on a raid against the stonemasons, in which Brant Steel died, and assured an alarmed Erwin that "our swords give them hell." ### Death
During the siege of the palace by [[krojdon-bekfild|Beckfield]]'s forces, Damon was Erwin's right hand man and one of the few surviving officers. After half a month of siege, the exhausted Erwin admitted to him that he saw no chance. Damon was indignant ("Are you nuts?! We win!”), took from his cousin the snake-grass bubble on which he was holding, and sent him to sleep: the command must be Origin, so he will command. He was not going to die, except to earn a small scar on his face.

While Erwin was sleeping, a half-battalion of Marines from Marengo was thrown on the walls. The next morning, the doctor called the Duke to the infirmary. The arrow entered Damon's right eye, the tip sticking out at the temple. He remained conscious and repeated, “I’m scared, brother. I don't want to die." The doctor told Erwin to calm the wounded, and he, realizing that there was no hope, took his cousin by the hand and swore to Agatha that he would live. Erwin added that Damon will no longer be handsome, will get the scar as he dreamed, and finally everyone will respect him. Damon smiled and lost consciousness. Erwin asked the doctor not to call him if the wounded man woke up again, and only to report whether he was alive or dead. Damon died of a wound. Erwin later said that it was in the "darkest days between Damon's death and our victory" that he realized he was fighting the wrong villain. The senior officer was Damon's assistant and favorite, Henry Horton, nicknamed [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], whom Erwin ordered to put on Damon's armor and command the defense.

The death of Damon broke Robert: he asked Erwin to resign to leave the offspring: “My father had three sons, I am the last of them who are still alive.” Later, at a chamber dinner of the Origins in the capital after the victory, the family remembered the Handsome and the other dead, and Robert and Erwin flashed tears in their eyes.

## In "Fantasies"
"Fantasies" the author calls possible options for the future. In Let's Catch Him (The First Winter, Summer 1776), Robert served an honorary hard labor in the attic of the guest house of the monastery of Svetloy Agatha. One day at sunset, Damon’s ghost appeared in his cell with a fragment of an arrow in his eye socket: “I died because of you. You could have come to Faunterra faster. I waited, but I did not wait.” Robert did not take the blame: he recalled the difficulties of the winter offensive, the fatigue of the soldiers after the double battle in Labelin and the warmth of Damon himself, which made him reckless in battle. "Do you dare to blame me?" cried the ghost. “No, but don’t blame me either,” replied Robert, and Damon, still a little angry, disappeared. Robert thought that the brother feasts on the Star in the palaces of the Agatha the Radiant, and he wanders unrepentant, chained to his bones, like an old Odar to a chair. Erwin and Iona consoled him: The star may not be the best place on the Spiral, and to Sophia was the spirit of Desmond. Robert decided that Lord Desmond would "at least look after Damon", and was going to ask the abbess for a large prayer service for the repose of their souls.

## Personality
Fearless, noisy, self-assured, Damon loved fame and beautiful gestures. He lacked caution, but loyalty to Erwin was unconditional. His death is one of the most difficult scenes of "Only One Star" and one of the wounds that Erwin carried through the entire cycle. The Xs remembered him with Brant Steel. Kair Reynolds, beating the captured Red Sickle, condemned their names, and in the "Shadow of the Great Tree" Erwin remembers Damon among the fallen, who became "monuments that are not memorable to anyone alive."
