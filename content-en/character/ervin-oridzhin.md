## Origins and youth

Erwin Sophia Jessica of the Lineage of Agatha is the second son of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]], the reigning duke of the North, and Duchess [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]]. His elder brother, [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]], was considered an exemplary heir: a warrior and commander who agreed with his father in everything. His younger sister, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], was the person closest to Erwin. His cousins [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] and [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon]] belonged to the junior branch of the family, which owned the [[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]].

A sickly, bookish boy, he read his first book at four, knew the history of the Descent by heart at six, understood the principle of [[iskra|Spark power]] at seven, and had read a hundred volumes from the First Winter library by sixteen. At seventeen, he entered the University of [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. At twenty, he wrote an essay on the policies of Juliana the Great; at twenty-one, he spoke in the [[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]; at twenty-two, he became a guest at Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]]'s tea salon. Erwin was an excellent player of [[stratemy|stratems]], but disliked weapons, had never been knighted, and did not take part in tournaments.

With his father, Erwin followed “rules of conduct” learned by heart in childhood. The sixth was never to smile, even when standing outside his father's door; the fourth, never to make excuses about anything, because excuses were for dogs and the rabble. He described Richard without illusions: his brother loved swords and fighting, had a bear's robust health, knew no fear, and thought of nothing but war. He agreed with their father not out of fear, but because they thought alike. General William Davy, Erwin's stratems partner, called him a “cunning devil” and said that such cunning was shameful in a northerner, like a dog meowing.

Around 1772, Richard sailed by caravel from [[belomorye|Belomorye]] for negotiations with the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] and disappeared along with his escort ship. From then on, Erwin became the heir and, in his own thoughts, his father's “only son”; his father saw him as nothing more than an arrow on a bowstring. This gives his chapters their identifying name.

## In “Arrow, Coin, Spark”

### Returning home
On 22 March 1774, the twenty-four-year-old heir arrived by carriage from the capital in [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] for Iona's wedding to Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. From the mountain pass, he took pleasure in observing the signs of poverty in his home city. They confirmed that his father would need money, which meant that his plan would be accepted.
> “Nothing beautiful should smell of poverty.”

He explained his plan to his father. Despite Desmond's alliance with [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]], Erwin had secretly assembled a coalition that promised to support [[reformy-adriana|Adrian's land reform]] in the Chamber in exchange for benefits for the North. This included agreements with [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] and [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]]: the latter had been given hints of a marriage between Erwin and [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]]. The Crown would pay for a third of the Origins' army, saving the North a hundred and fifty thousand hilts a year, and forgive half their debts. In addition, the Origins would receive the “grand prize” their dukes had dreamed of for generations. Desmond saw this as selling one's loyalty: a vassal demanding payment from his suzerain. He made his son repeat the vassal's oath, reminded him of Juliana's Law against rebels, and, as punishment, sent him to lead an [[ekspedicija|explorada]] into the Beyond to find a site for a Spark dam on the [[reka|River]]. Erwin was forbidden to return to the capital. The party was ordered to depart as soon as the wedding celebrations ended. One of his future companions, the imperial mechanic Louis Maria, was already waiting outside his father's study; Erwin would later often wonder how much the man had heard through the door.

### Erwin's coalition
The details of the scheme emerge in Erwin's recollections during the expedition. On 2 September 1773, at a banquet in the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]], he spoke to Aiden Almera, the emperor's chief adviser. He learned that Desmond had promised to support Adrian's marriage to [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] in exchange for exemption from tax during her reign. Erwin considered the agreement extremely unfavourable, and his father the empire's best general and worst politician. He demanded more from Aiden, but Aiden refused to pay more, so Erwin decided to win the prize himself.

On 10 October 1773, he persuaded Countess Sybil Northwood to join him in offering the emperor support for his reforms, asking for nothing but inaction in return. Sybil, who was soon to surrender power to the young count, was attracted by the opportunity to control a “river of gold” in revenue. On 12 December 1773, in Farway, Erwin won Henry Farway over with the promise of a Spark workshop and hints of marriage to his granddaughter, Infanta Laura, the only member of the family descended from Agatha. He also met secretly with Priarch [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], who hated Adrian's reforms, and lied that Northwood and Hope stood behind him.

In winter, Erwin was invited to Adrian's tea pavilion as a “second vassal” of the emperor. There, in the presence of General William Davy and the adviser Katrin Katrin of Shimmery, he set out the whole plan:
- House Farway–Hope would receive a fief and an imperial loan for a Spark workshop;
- Litland would receive the four lower fords across the Hollywell River;
- Northwood would receive one of the bays of the port of [[uindli|Windley]] as a subfief;
- Gallard would retain his priarch's mantle;
- The Origins would receive the port of Windley itself, with the ships of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]'s First Maritime Guild, and the right to station five kair battalions in the Crownlands in return for payment.

The Crown only needed to “do nothing” while the allies occupied the crossings and Windley. The Great House Origin would also be “happy” to see Iona become empress, though the choice remained the sovereign's. In exchange, the coalition would vote for the reforms in the September Chamber, without demanding an immediate betrothal. The conversation went well until Erwin let slip a single word: “Hope.” He meant that Hope was loyal to him rather than to Aiden, but it sounded like a threat. Katrin Katrin already knew about Erwin's secret marriage agreement with Farway. The emperor wanted confirmation from Desmond, forcing Erwin to admit that his father still knew nothing. Later, speaking to Jemis after the explorada, Erwin called that word his foolish mistake: he believed it was what had made him dangerous to the emperor.

### The explorada
The party numbered forty: eleven kairs with their greys under Captain [[teobart|Theobart]], the imperial observer Baron Philip Lowfert, the mechanic [[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]], a physician, and a servant. In April 1774, they set out along a pack trail through the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]; [[filipp-loufert|Lowfert]] spent the journey discussing how the Forefathers had passed that way. Two weeks later, the explorada entered a poorly explored part of the mountains where the old map showed the river's course incorrectly, and the party came up against a sheer cliff. Only at the end of April, after three weeks of travel, did they reach the glacial plateau called the Maid's Hem. Descending from it, they arrived in [[spot|Spot]], a frontier settlement beside the Icy Brook and the empire's outermost inhabited place. The elder, Howard, warned them that before reaching the River they would have to cross the Soft Fields, an enormous swamp covered in net-grass. Erwin hired the trappers [[kolemon|Colemon]] and [[kid|Kid]] as guides, paying each twenty agathkas. The soldiers nicknamed the pampered lord Softie. Erwin defended the maimed grey Buck, gave the people of Spot half his father's supplies, and spared Kid after the guide caused a venomous snake to end up in his bed. The kairs considered all of this weakness. In early May, the guides led the party through the Suede Forest into the bogs of the Soft Fields. There, Captain Theobart lured an attacking tuskbeast into the mire; Erwin himself fell through the net-grass at night and nearly drowned; and in mid-May, their campfire ignited swamp gas, starting a blaze they struggled to extinguish. In the Soft Fields, Erwin suppressed a [[myatezh-dzhemisa|plot]] by the kair [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]], who wanted to remove him from command. The lord approached the conspirators with his sword held behind his back and his chest exposed to a blow. When Jemis backed down, Erwin stripped him of his cloak and banished him. By the end of May, the explorada had reached the River, about 380 miles from the empire's border. The River proved unsuitable for a dam. Lowfert proposed turning back now that their task was complete, but Erwin, by his own admission, decided to cross to the east bank simply to spite him. Before crossing, he paid off the guides and gave them two letters to send by ship from Spot: a report to his father explaining that the River was unsuitable and that he intended to travel another fifty miles or so eastward, and a four-page letter to Iona. On 6 June, the party crossed by raft, via a large island, to the east bank, where they found a desiccated corpse and abandoned dugouts. On 8 June, they reached the resting place of the gods' Eighteenth Gift, already emptied. That same night, Louis Maria stabbed Erwin beneath his left collarbone. Wounded, Erwin jumped down into the Gift's cavern and survived because of it. Lying in the pit, he heard the killer report on him to a man with a foreign accent, while the party was massacred by men in scarlet shirts armed with Spark spears. The dying Captain Theobart advised him to write everything down and shoot himself, but Erwin refused:
> “I will survive.”

The wound festered, and Erwin treated it with the poisonous sap of snake-grass. It was then that Agatha the Radiant first appeared to him. He was nursed back to health by Jemis, who had returned after initially following him with the intention of killing him, and now asked permission to serve him. Together they tracked their enemies. They found a fort where talking [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects]]—[[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]—were being tested on prisoners, and a promontory covered in hundreds of mutilated bodies. On 6 July, Erwin and Jemis watched as the Spark troops' commander, [[paul|Paul]], took his soldiers out for firing practice and burned bound prisoners with the Fingers. Erwin wounded Paul with a crossbow bolt, and the fugitives escaped in the confusion. Later, beside the River, Erwin passed himself off to two soldiers as a recruit sent by Paul and killed both in a fight; before dying, one said that they served the sovereign Adrian. Erwin swore to destroy everything beyond the River and whoever was giving the orders. For details, see “[[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]].”

### The new duke
In July, Erwin and Jemis, accompanied by Jemis's dog Archer, set out back through the Forest of Shadows. Along the way, Erwin made practice swords from ash wood and demanded daily fencing lessons from Jemis: every morning, they fought “to the fifth bruise.” In early August, the travellers reached the Soft Fields. Burned-out sections of the grass network blocked their former route, forcing them to skirt a vast opening to the south. Jemis fell through and nearly drowned; a hare-eating snake bit Erwin, leaving him paralysed for a day; and they spent their last days in the swamps starving. Near Spot, they met Kid. Erwin did not visit the settlement to tell its people that the party had been killed. Instead, he sent Kid for food and waited beside the Icy Brook, at the start of the trail to the Maid's Hem. During the journey, Jemis acknowledged that his lord was a “brave softie.”

On 30 August 1774, as they approached First Winter, Erwin made Jemis a kair. In the cathedral of Agatha the Radiant, he sat before the fresco “Agatha the Radiant's Decision.” He felt that the saint was more like an elder sister than a great-grandmother eighty generations removed. The answer he sensed was this: the chief enemy stood so high that only a very unusual northerner—an outcast, an odd one out—could raise a sword against him, and Agatha had chosen just such a man. The city greeted him with closed gates. Kair Glenn, commander of the gate guard, did not recognise the emaciated lord and admitted him only as Jemis's companion. At the castle, Erwin learned that Iona and Vittor Shayland had arrived about two weeks earlier at his mother's summons. Without the duke and heir, First Winter was vulnerable, and the duchess feared a rebellion by Count [[fleming|Fleming]] and Baron White. His father, who had fallen ill shortly after his son's departure, was afflicted with [[kamennaya-khvor|stone sickness]]. Desmond abdicated in his son's favour before witnesses—Vittor Shayland, Count Lilliday, and [[stetkhem|Baron Statham]]—in the presence of the castellan, [[artur-khajrok|Arthur Highrock]], and Colonels Horton and [[blekberri|Blackberry]]. Erwin initially refused the title and told the council everything about the explorada. He suggested that Louis had been sent specifically to kill him: the heir who had demanded payment for supporting the reforms. Count Vittor was the first to support the theory of the emperor's guilt, saying that the sovereign had “decided he was equal to the gods.” Erwin then gave his father a choice: condemn him as a rebel, or support Adrian's overthrow. After making his son swear never to doubt the emperor's guilt, Desmond handed over power, and Erwin became the reigning Duke of Origin. His mother and Iona took his side.

## In “Only One Star”

### Preparing for war
The new duke assembled twelve battalions in the valley near First Winter and, with the help of his cousin Robert, First Winter's treasurer, sought money for the war. The treasury was almost empty: the money received from Shayland for Iona had been spent on the wedding and the deposit for Desmond's underground tomb. In late August, Erwin sent Adrian a public ultimatum. Before three hundred vassals, he read out his demands: end the sacrilege, try and execute those responsible, release the prisoners, pay ten thousand hilts to the families of the dead, and apologise to the Great House Origin. Only then would the duke agree to remain a loyal vassal. He then allowed those with doubts to leave without punishment, and twenty-three warriors left the hall. Iona guessed that the ultimatum's purpose was to provoke the emperor and draw out the Finger-bearers; Erwin confirmed it.
> “I came back from the Beyond for you.”

On 6 September came news of Evergard's destruction and Adrian's decree effectively abolishing the Chamber. That same night, Erwin exposed an attempt by the imperial envoy [[mark-voron|Mark]] to poison him and forced the envoy to drink his own wine. There was no poison in it. Erwin showed Mark the letters about Evergard and told him about the Beyond, after which the envoy was thrown into a dungeon and questioned for three days about the “brigade” and the Fingers. On 10 September, the last day before the campaign, Erwin ordered Jemis to go beyond the River with Count [[fleming|Fleming]] and three hundred warriors to destroy the fort, assigned posts to his relatives and vassals, and said goodbye to his parents. The duke was troubled by how Adrian's men had found the Gift's resting place: had they searched systematically, or possessed an Object capable of sensing other Objects? That night, Erwin admitted to Iona that he was afraid, and she slapped him. He sent her with her husband to [[uejmar|Waymar]]. Thus began the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]].

### The campaign in the Southern Way
In September, the kairs invaded the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]. Erwin divided the army into three columns: Colonel General Statham took seven battalions towards Doyle, Count Lilliday headed for the port of Windley, and the duke himself went south towards Labelin with his cousins. The lords of the Southern Way burned their own villages and killed peasants who hid supplies; Erwin ordered that the survivors be fed. He hanged Baron Case, captured with a supply train, along with his son, and chose six of his knights by lot for execution. He released two others with a warning that he would hang a Southern Way nobleman for every village burned.

At the only ford across the Wise River, Erwin launched empty rafts carrying straw dummies, and the Southern Way troops fired twenty thousand arrows at them. Meanwhile, the northerners crossed elsewhere and attacked the enemy at night. Two hundred Southern Way nobles and three thousand mercenary archers were taken prisoner. Erwin set the ransom at three hundred hilts per knight and a thousand per baron, and offered the archers service in his army. Near Windley, after drinking snake-grass, he routed the marquis's army with a single attack. In early October, in [[soltaun|Soltown]], he demanded an immediate fifty thousand gold hilts from the merchants of the Second Maritime Guild, led by [[gobart-sintal|Gobart-Sintal]], reducing their tax and handing over captured ships in exchange. Learning that Adrian had taken his regiments south against [[moran|Steppe Fire]], Erwin turned towards [[dojl|Doyle]].

On 15–16 October, after an unsuccessful assault, the Marquis of Doyle rejected negotiations, and Erwin executed twenty-five prisoners in sight of the city, threatening to execute the rest; he later called them “unarmed and innocent.” That same night, [[otets-david|Father David]] and Alanis Almera, who had escaped from burning Evergard, came to him. Doyle surrendered without another assault. Erwin held a tournament, staged a propaganda play about [[minerva|Minerva]] having been replaced by an impostor, and trained crossbowmen and spearmen for single combat. He secretly kept Alanis close so that he could blackmail Gallard with the threat of her “resurrection,” and promised to help her regain Almera. In early November, without waiting for the Northwood army led by [[krejg-nortvud|Craig]], he advanced on [[labelin|Labelin]]. The northerners bridged the moats, the duke himself and six X-cloaks defeated Southern Way knights in duels of honour, and the militia laid down its spears. The city was taken with almost no losses, and [[moris-labelin|Duke Maurice]] fled. Rumours spread through the army that Erwin was a demon visited by Agatha. After Labelin's capture, Alanis became his adviser and lover. In Labelin, Erwin hired [[olaf-dvuborodyj|Olaf Two-Beards]] and his men, who knew the city's catacombs, and, at Alanis's word, detained [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] when he came to him.

### Pixie and the dash for the capital
Under pressure from Craig Northwood and his commanders, Erwin marched against the [[aleksis-smajl|Silver Fox]], although [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] warned of a trap. On 20 November, the Northwood army arrived in Labelin. That same day, Viscount [[somerset-flejm|Somerset Flame]] brought a letter from his sister: the Fox had gone north with only six regiments, while Adrian was preparing a surprise attack, and Nexia begged Erwin not to march straight towards the capital. Erwin wanted to wait for the Fox in Labelin, but Craig threatened to march out alone, the commanders supported him, and Statham and Lilliday advised against cancelling the advance a second time for fear of undermining his authority. When frost hardened the road, the army spent a couple of days bridging the Mila, crossed on 1 December, and camped a few miles from Pixie. On 2 December 1774, the entire imperial army appeared near [[piksi|Pixie]]. Erwin gave Statham a sealed packet of orders, handed over command, and rode north under the guard of three hundred picked warriors, seemingly abandoning his army ([[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]]). The flight was a ruse. His force crossed the Mila and turned towards Farewell station, the last before Labelin, which mercenary archers and fifty X-cloaks had occupied in advance; three trains were waiting there. Six hundred northerners and Alanis loaded their horses and travelled south past Pixie, where the battle was still raging. They burned the railway bridge across the Mila behind them and, at sunset on 3 December, passed the Almeran checkpoint on the Crownlands border with a letter signed by Gallard. On the night of 6–7 December, the trains reached the capital. The northerners killed the station guards, charged into the city in groups of twenty horsemen, and seized the [[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]] and the Palace of Pen and Sword in Faunterra ([[bitva-za-faunterru|Capture of Faunterra]]). Meanwhile, five northern battalions hidden in Labelin's catacombs defeated the Fox's army as it celebrated its victory during the night, and eight and a half thousand imperial soldiers fell into northern hands.

### The siege of the palace
The palace siege lasted three weeks and pushed Erwin to breaking point. He used the lifting span of the bridge to drop attackers into the river, but his force dwindled under [[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]'s bombardment. Kairs Derrek and [[dzhoj-khemmond|Hammond]], the X-cloak commanders, were killed, and his cousin Damon died from an arrow in the eye. Erwin himself dislocated his shoulder, went grey, and kept going on snake-grass. In the very first days, he learned that the store of Objects in the Imperial Citadel was empty: [[baron-esher|Baron Esher]] had removed them. Kair Derrek commanded the defence of the Citadel, and Kair Hammond that of the palace. Around 10 December, message strips arrived from Robert and Statham: the army concealed in Labelin's catacombs had defeated the Fox, but he had destroyed the railway tracks, so relief was marching through the snow and was expected in about twenty days. After Derrek, Hammond, and Damon were killed, Kair Henry Horton, [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], took charge of the defence. Around 21 December, the exhausted Erwin fell into the snow and began begging Agatha for help. He thought he saw the saint, gaunt and with a scar on her cheek, but it was Alanis carrying a message strip from Robert: the army had entered the Crownlands and had another week to march. Alanis ran the dovecote and infirmary. Without Erwin's knowledge—he had called the plan dishonourable and forbidden it—she sent Joaquin with a letter to Count [[erroubek|Arrowback]]. Erwin was furious when he learned of it, and they rarely saw each other afterwards. Soon the bridge over the Beck collapsed beneath Adrian's train ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]). By late December, Erwin had about two hundred half-dead defenders left, and, grey-haired and emaciated, looked like an old man himself. On the left bank of the [[khanaj|Khanai]], six miles from the city, twelve thousand kairs and eight thousand Northwood troops under Statham and Robert were already encamped. At first, Erwin dictated terms of surrender to Minerva when she came to him: the Spark regiments would withdraw, Beckfield would go to the block, and Minerva would be crowned, convene the Chamber, revoke Adrian's decisions, and enter into a formal marriage with the duke. Minerva replied that she had come for her crown and threatened that General Alexis would begin an assault in twenty minutes, while the northerners would be unable to flee across ice melted by fire. The new empress, [[minerva|Minerva]], had come to the palace with the [[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]. She convinced Erwin that Adrian had not been the Fingers' master, and he allied with her: Minerva would receive the crown, and Erwin the right to approve her marriage and agreements with the Great Houses. He sealed the pact with “the word of a grandson of Agatha the Radiant.” During the siege, Hope had sent four companies to storm the palace, and Erwin had killed some of those soldiers himself; this ended his alliance and marriage agreement with House Farway. Soon afterwards, Statham and Robert arrived and broke the siege, and Minerva signed a peace treaty on behalf of the Empire.

## In “A Doll on the Throne” and the interludes

In early 1775, after her coronation, Minerva discovered that she had signed a decree making Erwin Lord Chancellor, and he effectively governed the empire. “The Voice of the Crown” called him Erwin the Victor. At court, he was regarded as a vain spendthrift and the lover of Alanis, whom he made burgomaster of Faunterra. Erwin buried the empress in business, appointed his cousin Robert imperial treasurer, and made the former administrator [[drejfus-born|Dreyfus Born]] minister of taxation after Born paid him more than a hundred thousand hilts for the post. He forced Minerva to choose seventeen convicted prisoners for the April pardon and to sign pardons for Sybil and [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria Northwood]]. Behind the empress's back, he called her “Mimi.” He tasked Mark, now his agent, with finding the Dynasty's Objects stolen from the train and the missing Sybil. Erwin personally visited Mark at the abandoned villa where he was being held, promised to restore him as head of the intelligence service in exchange for the Objects, gave him only Grandfather and [[vnuchok|Grandson]] to help, and forbade him to contact the empress. When Iona arrived in the capital, he warned her that speaking of the “master of the Fingers” was forbidden: Adrian had been declared the villain. In private, he reproached his sister for driving Vittor away with her arrogance. Adrian's financial adviser was someone Erwin needed in peacetime; he could have kept the Northwoods, who demanded spoils and appointments, in check through Sybil or Count Elias, but Sybil had vanished and Elias remained Vittor's prisoner. Brother and sister reconciled. He showed her the site of the future cathedral of Agatha the Radiant, spent money on celebrations, refused to suppress a peasant revolt, and secretly intercepted letters to Minerva, which Iona soon discovered. At the Grand Crown Theatre, the drunken minister of transport, [[liam-shele|Liam Shellier]], revealed before the entire court that Robert had been made treasurer without the empress's knowledge and that Born had bought his office. Erwin later sent Minerva a letter apologising for “the deliberate nature of the incident” and proposing that they convene the Chamber together. When the Shavans besieged [[melorandzh|Melorange]], Erwin refused to go to Litland and announced that he, his vassals, and his kairs would remain in the capital. He tried to dissuade Minerva from visiting Moran, but Jemis volunteered to accompany her, and the duke reluctantly gave way.

In early 1775, Erwin sent General Statham to [[evergard|Evergard]] with a decree from the empress that he had helped draft: [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] was ordered to hand Almera over to [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] and release his hostages, [[laura-farvej|Laura]] and [[dzheremi-farvej|Jeremy]] Farway. The priarch refused to surrender the duchy and threatened to reveal that Alanis's man from the Southern Way had visited Arrowback on the eve of the Beck disaster, but he released the children. Under the eventual peace agreement, Gallard governed Almera as Alanis's deputy, and upon his death the duchy would pass to her or [[albert-almera|Albert]].

Meanwhile, Count [[fleming|Fleming]] had turned traitor in the North: his ships and horsemen pursued Jemis and Mark's party as it returned from beyond the River, and by January 1775 the count held the Sea of Ice coast with five hundred kairs. The party fought its way to First Winter with a captured Finger bracelet, which came to life in [[gvenda|Gwenda]]'s hands. Through this bracelet, [[sofiya-oridzhin|Lady Sophia]] offered the “god” the return of the Object in exchange for healing her husband, and received the command: “Go to Faunterra. Bring the bracelet and your husband.” A column of a hundred kairs brought Desmond and Sophia to the capital, along with Jemis and Mark.

Erwin was outraged by the bargain his mother had struck with the Puppeteer to heal his father. The [[znakharka|Healer]] sent by the Puppeteer died during an attempt to seize her, and Craig Northwood obtained her healing [[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]] in exchange for the post of commander-in-chief. Craig threatened to settle the dispute by duel, and Erwin gave him General Gore's corps on condition that the Silver Fox's regiments would come under Northwood's command only with Minerva's consent. On the night after the empress returned from Moran, Erwin visited her with ordzh. He explained that he needed Born to maintain the pretence of an empty treasury, and offered a theory: perhaps the Objects responded to people who carried the brigade's blood—he himself had killed a brigade soldier in the Beyond. He therefore asked whether Minerva had been Adrian's lover, but received no answer. The Origins tested the Object: it glowed only for [[gvenda|Gwenda]], Erwin, and Louis, but healed no one. Meanwhile, Iona secretly gave her paralysed father a potion from Martin Shayland, and by April Desmond had improved. His father gave Erwin the ancestral sword [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]], and the duke ostentatiously took up fencing.

In the novella “[[glas-zimy|Voice of Winter]],” told from his viewpoint, Erwin asked Robert to teach him the five ancestral Origin techniques that Robert alone in the family knew. The last and most elegant was “Agatha's Lightning,” a double, criss-crossing slash. It required a very strong, very light blade, precisely what Voice of Winter was. Erwin remained a mediocre swordsman, and his [[alter|altessa]], Anxiety, reproached him for his lack of anger. At the same time, he examined [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]'s report on the Beck disaster, suspected that [[itan|Ethan Gladys Norma]] was hiding something, and ordered him confined to a stone cell on bread and water. By April, his father's condition had improved. Prince [[gektor|Hector]] offered Erwin fifteen thousand Spark eyes, and with the loan Farway had promised, the duke dreamed of creating a Spark guard of kairs. He considered Gallard's betrothal to Laura Farway advantageous: it infuriated Alanis, but ruled out a massacre in the centre of the empire. Minerva's journey to Steppe Fire inspired his sincere admiration for the first time.

His idleness was an act: Erwin was preparing a trap for the Puppeteer. Against the [[vosstanie-podsnezhnikov|Snowdrop Rebellion]], he first sent General Gore, then appointed Craig Northwood commander and chose the [[svyatoe-pole|Holy Field]] as the battlefield. There he hoped to draw out and capture the Puppeteer's entire brigade, promising the peasants pardons and bread after his victory. Only his father and Robert knew the plan. General Gore returned from near Hampton without fighting—the rebels had shot his envoys with Spark crossbows—and at the war council, Erwin mocked him and temporarily made Craig commander-in-chief. The bears formed the vanguard, with the kairs behind them, while the duke declared that he would not personally march against peasants. He expected the brigade to strike from the rear while the kairs became entangled among the Snowdrops, so he placed his headquarters in a hollow, put pardoned convicts in northern officers' armour inside the command tent, and concealed his best battalion in the hills. Suspicious of Minerva as well, he gave her only information of little value and deliberately irritated her, watching her reactions. Alone with Iona in a secret gallery above the throne room, he revealed the plan: for three months, he had encouraged the Puppeteer to think him an idiot, and his sister's doubts had served as a test. Brother and sister then kissed, but immediately pulled away, understanding that it would not make them happier. Erwin persuaded Iona to return to her husband in Waymar. In spring 1775, however, Minerva went to the rebels herself. Her carriage raced through the northern battle lines, the kairs did not dare stop her, and by the time Lilliday and Craig arrived, the peasants had laid down their arms. The hunt had failed. His vassals demanded that the “doll” be restrained or replaced, and Erwin furiously considered how to strip her of power and money. During the two-day march back, however, Anxiety convinced him that Minerva was “a true Yanmei” and advised him to make her his ally and marry her. At the palace, he found the empress's gift: a snowdrop with a ribbon reading, “Accept your place in the world with dignity. You are not the sovereign, my lord. Resign yourself to it.” Erwin ordered her intimates assembled, told Hyder Leed to wring the truth out of Ethan “by any means,” and, in his anger, finally executed the ancestral technique, slicing through the flower.

## In “The Yanmei Hunt”

After the Holy Field, Erwin deliberately played the villain. He dismissed Minerva's ministers and created a special company within the Spark guard, made up of guardsmen who admired him personally and empowered to review any officer's orders. On 4 May 1775, at the “Rusty Rail” den, Mark told him that the Puppeteer was trying to assemble immortality from the Objects: the [[absolyut|Absolute]]. On the night leading into 5 May, Minerva invited Maurice Labelin to her theatre box and fled. Her escort knocked a member of the special company unconscious, and she left on a night “merchant” train for [[marengo|Marengo]], under the protection of two azure companies commanded by [[uitmor|Captain Whitmore]]. Erwin realised that without her he could not open the Chamber, while she could assemble the lords around herself. On 6 May, he travelled to Marengo by train without an army, taking only Jemis and the dog Archer—a move he called “the strength of weakness.” At the Palace of Silence, he was kept waiting in a pavilion until dusk and refused an audience. In the palace garden, the Shavans [[ganta-groza|Thunderstorm]] and Sormakh challenged him to a duel, and Voice of Winter helped him cut off Sormakh's hand. Minerva admitted that she had arranged this test herself. She demanded that he restore her people, disband the special company, recall Ethan from Almera, and let her appoint the Crown's representatives to the Chamber herself. Erwin promised to do so when he began to trust her. She revealed that the Silver Fox served the [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]] and that the Queen of Darkwater had given her the living jester [[menson|Manson]]. On 7 May, Erwin and Minerva met the Lady in the Dark and Francis-Ilian at the port; that same evening, they all departed for Faunterra on three imperial trains. The next morning, in the sovereign's compartment, Erwin joined her in trying to persuade Manson to tell the truth in exchange for acquittal, but the jester demanded a trial. On the evening of 8 May, the trains reached the capital, where an enormous crowd welcomed them.

On the train, someone planted a note for Erwin: “There must be no trial.” The Lady in the Dark and [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] proposed removing Minerva; after he refused, the queen “poisoned” herself with a korzo seed. Erwin reinstated Mark as Raven of the Crown and quarrelled with Alanis. On the night leading into 9 May, Minerva told him that she knew about the Absolute, and they agreed to hunt the Puppeteer together. Erwin proposed an extraordinary census of the Objects to find the one missing from the Absolute, promised to restore her assistants, release Ethan soon, and disband the special company after the hunt. Minerva demanded that he swear he had not ordered Adrian's murder, and Erwin swore. His father advised him to withdraw his troops, marry Alanis, and leave the hunt to Minerva, but he refused. The Chamber approved his “Northern Uprising” and rejected Labelin's demands. Prince [[gektor|Hector]] asked Erwin for half his army to fight his father. On 12 May, Leila Talmir summoned Erwin to Minerva, who had been drinking ordzh since the previous evening after the Chamber session and complaining that everyone she had loved was dead. Erwin ordered that she be sobered up and secretly taken to the Lady in the Dark, and explained her absence to the lords as a visit to the ailing queen. On the night leading into 16 May, after the two of them had worked out the seventeen Objects of the Absolute, Minerva kissed him, and he responded. She called the kisses a mistake best forgotten and began avoiding private meetings with him.

Among the intercepted pigeon letters was the question, “What should we do about the Radiant Sphere?” In the drawing of the [[svetlaya-sfera|Sphere]] in Shayland's register, Erwin recognised a symbol from his vision. Iona confirmed that Vittor had sold the Sphere. In Erwin's presence, Maurice Labelin admitted that he had bought a counterfeit Sphere and that Ice's men had burned the monastery, then stabbed the mutilated Moger Buckley to death. From Minerva's chart, Erwin deduced that the Absolute consisted of seventeen Objects and that one was still missing. At the same time, Erwin sent his mother to [[folta|Folta]] to stay with [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]].

On the morning of 25 May, Erwin stopped the sovereign's carriage and demanded an explanation for her coldness. Minerva cited business and her journey with the Lady in the Dark to Arden, to the graves of Tellurian and Ingrid, and accused him of having broken, in his thoughts, his oath not to seek her hand. Erwin forcibly kissed her, said that he wanted to be her friend, and revealed that the Puppeteer needed the Radiant Sphere. At the [[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]], Erwin was called as a witness. He publicly admitted that Alanis had proposed the scheme involving the bridge and Arrowback, and said that if she had carried it out, she had done so without his knowledge; he also admitted executing the prisoners at Doyle. Alanis left the capital, and Minerva publicly reproached Erwin: he should have told her the truth about Adrian's death. Father David revealed the order of the [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] to him, and in portraits of the sovereign Ingrid they found the [[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]. Erwin realised that only Alanis and the Puppeteer knew about it and became fully convinced of her connection to the enemy. On the night leading into 28 May, Erwin rushed to Arden with four dozen kairs aboard an express train consisting of a locomotive and two carriages to get there before the Puppeteer; three dozen subsequently went to the tomb's main entrance. Two more trains carrying a battalion were to arrive by dawn. In the Farewell tomb, he refused to steal the Drop of the Sun and laid an ambush for [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade. Captain Whitmore warned him that Minerva had ordered the chamber filled with hydrogen. The brigade and its Fingers crushed the kairs: according to “The Voice of the Crown,” thirty-nine people died. [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] was maimed by a falling marble slab, and Jemis barely managed to drag the duke into a secret passage. The heretics' commander, known as Ice, took the Drop of the Sun.

At the Chamber session on 29 May, the Lady in the Dark sided with Erwin. From the rostrum, he portrayed the ambush in Farewell as the work of the marshfolk and declared the queen's “poisoning” a pretence—a pretext for secretly meeting those needed for the hunt. He presented an intercepted letter from Bishop [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]], “What should we do about the apple?”, as evidence against the priarch. At his advice, Minerva sent Shatterhand to stop the trains and tell Arrowback to intercept the brigade riding towards Almera. He explained the Absolute and proved that Manson had acted under the control of [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]], whereupon the jester revealed that Adrian was alive. Erwin confirmed it: Ethan had established back in winter that the recovered body was not the sovereign's. At his signal, sixteen kairs barred the hall's doors, and he demanded an immediate vote, despite Minerva's insistence on four days for discussion. Erwin proposed deposing Adrian and declaring Minerva the sole sovereign, but she refused, remaining loyal to Adrian. The Chamber then stripped Adrian of all rights to the throne, and the Lady in the Dark proposed Erwin as emperor. Hector, the Northwoods, Shayland's envoys, and Mother Cordelia, representing the Church of the Foremothers, supported him. Jemis interrupted the vote with the news: “Your sister is in trouble.”

## In “Humans and Gods”

### The war against Gallard
Erwin received letters from Waymar indicating that the Puppeteer was Count Vittor Shayland and that Iona had died after a failed rebellion in the castle; in fact, she had survived—see the [[perevorot-v-uejmare|Coup in Waymar]]. On 1 June, he presented the case against Shayland and Gallard to [[ellina|Archmother Ellina]] and [[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]] and formed an alliance with the Church. According to his account, the Puppeteer had appropriated the Eighteenth Gift, the priarch had confiscated Fingers from Almeran temples where they had been mistaken for minor Objects, and Adrian had turned a blind eye to the slaughter in exchange for the secret of the talking Objects. That same day, he persuaded Ganta Thunderstorm to bring him Moran's horde if he defeated Gallard within a month. The Ecumenical Council removed the priarch from office and declared a holy war against him.

He divided the army in two: one half, led by his father, who was back on his feet, would march through the Southern Way and Smoky Distance towards Waymar; Erwin himself would lead the other through Almera and then along the Hollywell into the Steppe. In early June, the northern battalions left the capital. To avoid alerting Arrowback's and Gallard's spies, the troops travelled on ordinary scheduled trains: five miles outside the city, the passengers were disembarked and kairs put aboard. [[otets-david|Father David]], Mother Cordelia, and Ganta Thunderstorm accompanied Erwin; [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]] laughed and walked away when he proposed that she marry Moran. On 4 June, a force of X-cloaks took the Fisher Hut railway halt in the Barony of Bonegan, fifteen miles from Arrowback Castle, without a fight; the bridge beyond it was under repair. After waiting for a second train carrying the horses, Erwin led four hundred riders towards the Beck at night, following a stream bed. He still believed Iona was dead.

Count Arrowback rode out to meet them with fifty knights. Mother Cordelia announced that the Church was going to arrest the priarch, while Erwin apologised to the count for exposing Alanis at the trial and hinted that he would honour her debts: Alanis had promised Arrowback the County of Blackmore. The count admitted the northerners to his castle on the dam, gave them the keep, dovecote, and wave, promised a battalion, and wrote to the priarch pretending he would fight to the death. That night, Anxiety convinced Erwin that the letter reporting his sister's death might have been forged. Through [[gvenda|Gwenda]], who could speak to the Objects, he contacted Vittor using the bracelet and heard Iona's voice; she managed to cry out, “Kill the monsters!”

On 6 June, saboteurs burned the Spark substation on Almera's border, bringing the trains to a halt. Almost the entire army was stranded near Faunterra, and only two companies of [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]'s Leed Wolves arrived on horseback to help. The count proposed sending his own battalion under yellow flags to meet [[veksler|Wexler]] and draw the Almerans south. Erwin agreed, but secretly took the X-cloaks into the woods beside the causeway before dawn. When Wexler attacked the count's battalion, he struck from the rear and defeated Almera's best cavalry. A mace shattered Erwin's pauldron during the battle, and Kair Arthur the Twin saved his life. The man wearing the general's cloak proved to be a lieutenant acting as a decoy. Erwin released prisoners who refused to join him, threatening to hang anyone who took up arms against him again. Sir Michael, a former guardsman of Aiden's who defected to him, reported that Gallard was concentrating his troops at the fortress of Raven's Feather and that the Farways were coming to his aid.

On 8 June, Erwin had only eleven hundred warriors, including six hundred northerners, against almost eight thousand enemies. Jemis reproached him for careless command and for being in Waymar in spirit. After arguing with Jemis and Mother Cordelia, Erwin decided to go northwest towards [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]] to save Iona, rather than towards Evergard. He went ahead with [[kurt-ajsvind|Icewind]]'s single company, deliberately taking Michael in the expectation that he would prove to be a spy. On 12–13 June, a feigned retreat lured the Almerans into a night ambush: the white-cloaked Leed Wolves killed Wexler and Colonel Blackmore, and a forest fire scattered the infantry. Erwin himself, with a few companions, reached a deserted bay of Smoky Distance about ten miles from [[fliss|Fliss]], where eight of his father's ships arrived after being summoned by pigeon. [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]]'s battalion fortified Cliff, a suburb of Fliss, and on 19 June Erwin and five hundred of his father's kairs, who had arrived by sea, struck Gallard's army from behind as it stormed Cliff. The Blackmore regiment delayed him, allowing Gallard to reach safety behind Fliss's walls. The former priarch had lost Aleridan, his infantry, and his supply trains, but remained free, and Mother Cordelia blamed Erwin. The Leed Wolves lost almost three-quarters of their men. Ganta Thunderstorm, who had sent a herd of horses into battle, lost six hundred horses, but reaffirmed the alliance in exchange for all the gold in the Shayland banks.

### The double and the trap involving Nexia
Erwin realised that Ice, the brigade's commander, was his brother Richard, and reasoned that the Puppeteer would send Richard to kill him. On 1 June, the Lady in the Dark and Francis-Ilian had revealed their order's goal—to fly to the Star—and given him a double capable of assuming any appearance. Erwin therefore placed his recovered father in command of the army near Waymar and sent the double to answer Richard's challenge. On 20–21 June, he received Count [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]] with Henry Farway's offer: recognise [[laura-farvej|Laura]] as the priarch's heir in exchange for an alliance and Nexia's hand with an enormous dowry. Erwin agreed to recognise Laura's claim. In return, Flame's regiment took over the siege of Fliss, and the Farways, who had occupied Aleridan, let Count Lilliday's cavalry pass westward, freeing Erwin for a campaign in the Steppe. Nexia arrived carrying a packet from Shayland and proved to be under the influence of Ulyana's Dust. The letter, also coated in Dust, demanded that he abdicate in Richard's favour; if he refused, Erwin was to die. With the help of Anxiety and his bodyguards, Erwin worked out the trap: he pretended to die so that the Dust would consider the order fulfilled, and sent Nexia back to her father with a note. That same day, a letter arrived from Rebecca Litland: the brigade and its Fingers had appeared in Hope, and Rebecca herself was travelling by train to Juliana's Bridge to reach Moran before the heretics.

### The campaign in the Steppe
With four hundred X-cloaks aboard eight ships, Erwin sailed along Smoky Distance and then up the [[kholliwell|Hollywell]] into the Steppe, counting on his alliance with Thunderstorm. At Juliana's Bridge, in the fortress of [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]], he met Count Lilliday's corps of three battalions. There, Erwin read a letter from Minerva repeating Rebecca's news; Rebecca had passed that way four days earlier. Lilliday's corps headed overland towards Rei-Roy, while Erwin and the X-cloaks sailed on and landed in Horseshoe Bay. On the way, Father David told him that Paul had once been called Nathaniel. The Steppe was empty; captured shepherds said that a certain “Pus-Ganta” had led the horde east. Near the fortress of Rei-Roy in the County of [[rejs|Reis]], Erwin found [[rebekka-litlend|Becca Litland]] and [[moran|Moran]] frozen in eternity, and cavalry armed with Fingers burst out of the gates. His horse Rain was cleaved open, Kair Quentin was killed, and Jemis vanished from sight. At the end of the book, Erwin lay in darkness beneath Rebecca's frozen horse; the third company and Father David remained in reserve behind a hill, and Lilliday's corps was three days away.

## In “The Shadow of the Great Tree”

### The Steppe
On the night after the defeat at Rei-Roy, [[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]] spoke to Erwin for the first time: “Defeat them.” Having lost its ships and Thunderstorm, the force retreated into the Steppe ahead of Ganta Ondey. Erwin had three hundred and forty people left, seventy of them wounded. Captain [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]] had lost an arm saving the duke, and Erwin gave him his flask of snake-grass juice. Baron Icewind accused Jemis of being far away while the duke was under fire, but Erwin defended the kair. A captured Finger-bearer survived thanks to [[pervokrov|firstblood]], and Erwin conceived the “[[orudie|Weapon]]”: in the Shavan village of Berkha on the Roydan, the smith Bershan built a chair carrying the prisoner, which Jemis operated. Erwin released the village's girls and children and offered Ondey peace, but Ondey attacked. In the battle, the Weapon destroyed the Finger-bearers and killed the ganta, but Baron Icewind's company was wiped out. Erwin executed four prisoners and released the others.

In August, at a camp near the town of Trident, Nexia Flame found Erwin: she arrived with his scouts, who had been sent into town. She asked forgiveness, brought a map from Farway's headquarters, and persuaded him not to go to Almera. Erwin was cut off: horde patrols awaited him in Almera, the priarch's ships on Smoky Distance, and Paul himself in Waymar. With Jemis, he used the map to devise a risky plan that Lilliday rated at “two or three chances in ten.” Using the pigeons Nexia had brought, he sent out letters, including a brief plan for First Winter's defence to his father and Castellan Highrock: divide the enemy army and reduce the battle to three manageable tasks, assembling at least eight battalions in the castle. He revealed the plan to his officers piecemeal, once they were on the move. After learning that his father had died at Glorious Watch, Jemis reconciled with Erwin. The wounded were sent to the monastery of Foremother Susan under the guard of a dozen kairs chosen by lot, while the force of three companies headed west. It emerged that back in June, at Glorious Watch, [[gvenda|Gwenda]] had secretly admitted to Erwin that she could control Objects and volunteered to get into Waymar to save Iona; he had given her a ship and promised her a baronial title. When Jemis learned the truth, he accused the duke of sending a woman to her death, and they quarrelled again. Disguised as the retinue of Hope's envoy, the force passed through [[melnitsy|the Mills]]. On Nexia's advice, Erwin removed his ducal insignia and rode as an ordinary soldier in [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]]'s company, with Jemis listed as commander. On the way, he learned of Vittor's “resurrection.” At the market, Erwin heard of “the Pus-Ganta's woman,” who had suffered a fate worse than death, and realised that it was Alanis. He concluded that her nobility had prevailed after all.

### The fleet and the North
In September, the party reached Feyris. There, Nexia asked him to bless her marriage to Jemis, and Erwin, as he understood it, blessed their betrothal. By then, Adrian had already occupied Faunterra, and the Spark regiments had rallied to his banners. Dressed as a kair, Erwin appeared before Claude Berengar, commander of Sunset Star Fort: “You've sat idle long enough. We're going to war.” Calling herself a bride with a retinue of three hundred guests, Nexia bought supplies and corresponded with her father, providing cover for the assembly of a fleet. The fleet sailed north along the western coast. In the Barony of Davy on the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]], Erwin learned that the Southern Way was opening its gates to Adrian and that Vittor was advancing through Northwood. Off Northwood's coast, Hyder Leed's scouts brought news of Desmond and Craig Northwood's defeat at [[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]: Craig had died, many of the bears had joined the enemy, and the Puppeteer had entered the Duchy of Origin with nineteen thousand soldiers. Ill with a cold, Erwin grew gloomy and lashed out at his subordinates. In October, Erwin's fleet, flying false pennants, lured the ship of Lord Valentine, Fleming's son-in-law, into the Sea of Ice. In imitation of pirate tales, Valentine's crew was marooned on an uninhabited island, while the lord himself was suspended upside down from a yardarm for two days. Erwin then pardoned him and sent his wife Molly with an invitation to First Winter. Believing the forged letter, Lady Agniya Fleming left [[belomorye|Belomorye]] with her daughters Molly and Violetta and fell into Erwin's hands. At [[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]], Hyder Leed seized Father David, who revealed the “easy” part of the Tree's secret to Erwin: the Gifts fell from the Star. Erwin called him a liar. David reported that the order had allied with Adrian and demanded Nathaniel's surrender in exchange for help. Erwin concluded that the Lady in the Dark had switched to the stronger side; believing First Winter's fall likely, he reproached Agatha for her long silence.

In November 1775, at the “Nest on the Pass” inn, Erwin demanded that [[fleming|Fleming]] stay with his battalion in the [[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]] and keep out of the battle; withdraw his son Osmund's battalion from the northern road, leaving only a token checkpoint; provide a dozen wagons of provisions; and hand over passwords and a list of the Puppeteer's units and Finger-bearers. He promised to release the hostages immediately after the battle. Jemis and Nexia tried to dissuade the ailing duke from going to Leed himself, but he replied, “If you want something done properly, do it yourself.” Learning that Iona was in a cage in Leed, he went there personally, ill and feverish, with Hyder Leed and Kair Aubrey, while Jemis took the Weapon to First Winter. From the slope of Companion Mountain, they saw the horde storming Leed, descended along an ice-covered aqueduct into the Lillidays' castle, and found Iona alive. They were caught, however, by [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]], carrying a Finger. Iona subdued Martin with Gwenda's Dust, took his Finger and Voice of God, and burned [[dorkaster|Baron Dorcaster]]. Erwin stopped her: “Little sister, enough.” As a party of eleven, carrying firecrackers, they headed towards First Winter and learned that Fleming remained loyal to Vittor and that the northern road was a trap for Jemis.

### The Battle for First Winter
Nexia disobeyed his order to take shelter in a mountain fort. On the eve of the Descent, disguised as a shepherdess, she passed through the Belomorye troops' positions to tell Desmond about his son's plans. On 2 December 1775, the Day of the Descent, Agatha appeared to the feverish Erwin with the words, “Forgive me… Tomorrow we shall meet.” In the [[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]], he first attacked Shayland's supply train with firecrackers, then disguised himself as a wagon driver and mingled with the baggage servants alongside Iona, Hyder Leed, and Aubrey. Iona lay among the dead with a Finger, while Erwin obtained a horn, sounded the signal, and galloped across the frozen lake towards the castle. Iona fired at Shavans armed with Fingers who could not see her, while Erwin personally led First Winter's shock cavalry against the horde. A Finger's lash shattered his mare's skull and struck the duke across the ribs. Meanwhile, on the Belomorye road, Jemis and the Weapon defeated Fleming's men; Paul died near the [[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]], and Vittor stabbed his brother Martin to death and disappeared.

That night, Erwin awoke in his mother's bedroom. Having learned from the notes how to use the Healer's Hand, Iona had healed his wound and his lung disease as well. Jemis confessed that he had been unable to kill Richard and that he had fled at the gates of Rei-Roy. He and Nexia admitted that their betrothal had been a trick: Jemis was being matched with a Shimmery princess, while Nexia wanted to remain near Erwin and still loved him. Erwin forgave them both and, in response to her confession, told Anxiety, “I'll think about it.” His father congratulated him on his victory, pointed out his mistake—overstretched forces—and praised his alliance with the Flames and the deployment of General Horton's corps behind Adrian. Desmond also reported a loss: one of Vittor's soldiers had fired a Finger at the fresco in the cathedral.

That same night, Richard entered the bedroom disguised as a monk, carrying a Finger, and proposed a “game”: everyone would choose a victim. Gordon Sue pointed at Richard himself and was killed. Their father chose himself, calling his son and daughter his great pride and his firstborn his shame; Richard smashed his skull. Erwin challenged his brother to a sword duel. The protective Belt stopped “Agatha's Lightning,” but Erwin moved the catch on the hilt, switched Voice of Winter into Spark mode, and stunned Richard with a discharge. Iona removed the Belt and Finger from her brother. Iona and Nexia urged Erwin to kill him. Erwin said, “Brother, you killed a very good man,” and drove his sword into Richard's jugular vein.

### After the battle
Speaking through the Voice of God and threatening to destroy the castle with a deconstructor, Adrian demanded the Eternal Hilt, the crown, Nathaniel, and recognition of his authority. Erwin raised a white flag to gain time, sent Jemis and Nexia into the city through a secret passage, and himself spent a day and a half in the infirmary beside Iona as she healed the wounded. Then, at Magda's insistence, Adrian agreed to peace. Minerva, who had moved the capital to First Winter, reconciled with Erwin. Mireille Nen-Clair revealed the secret of the First Tree to Erwin, Iona, and Minerva. At the feast before the negotiations, Erwin seated Jemis and Nexia beside him as a sign of forgiveness. Iona held her Finger ready, but he stopped her from firing: “Sister, please. For our sake.” During the negotiations, Robert broke the Lady in the Dark's neck in vengeance for his father. Afterwards, Erwin accepted Adrian's offer to look at the Star through the [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]] and personally removed the Finger from his sister's hand: “When the mystery is gone, so will the curse be.” The protagonists saw the Star—a metal wheel—and [[navi|Navi]] revealed the truth about the beacon and the Gardeners. Erwin called a future ruled by “gods” a “freak show,” but begged Minerva not to kill the young man: “Mia, don't kill him. Together, you and I have the strength to tear this Tree out by its roots.” At the end of the main cycle, Erwin is alive and remains Duke of Origin.

## In “Fantasies”

The author describes the stories in “Fantasies” as possible futures for the protagonists, rather than a continuation of the cycle. In their version, peace with Adrian was concluded after the battle while Duke Origin was “barely alive from exhaustion”: Lady Sophia and the judge conducted the negotiations for the Origins. Erwin personally shook hands on the agreement with Maurice Labelin, who became its guarantor. Adrian became burgomaster of Faunterra, and [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]] became governor of the Crownlands. Maurice Labelin grumbled that, once he became sovereign, the “fop” Origin would sit on the throne with his feet up, and when he went away, would appoint “his little sister, his mistress, or even Mimi herself” as governor. Archmother Ellina, who died on 2 February 1776, had been fond of Erwin, stroked his head, and called him “Murchik.” On the morning after the battle, [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] convinced Erwin that the prophetic dreams and the precise time for the attack suggested by Agatha proved the existence of true, “higher” gods. Erwin recovered from the shock sooner than the others: his faith in Agatha and his work restoring order in the duchy sustained him. He refused to reveal the secret to Nexia. At first, he was angry with Minerva, but when Iona left on a penitential journey, he forgave the sovereign and joined his mother in comforting her.

**“Let's Catch Him”** (summer 1776). Erwin was preparing for the election of the sovereign, maintaining an enormous correspondence, and dining with Minerva every other day. When Iona saw an apparition of Vittor Shayland in July, Erwin himself calmed his sister and sent ribbons to his agents in the capital to find out whether the prisoner had escaped from the Womb. Together with Iona, he visited his cousin [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]], who was serving the “honourable hard labour” Minerva had imposed on him at the monastery of Agatha the Radiant: he was compiling tables showing how much each inhabitant of each land paid daily towards the court, the army, and the ministries, and Erwin laughed and called them “proof that the Yanmei dynasty is a useless parasite.” He admitted to Robert that, since that night, he had doubted whether the Star was “the best place on the Spiral.” He admitted to the kairs that he saw Agatha the Radiant (“the day before yesterday… she was in a good mood”), and that during the first month of his reign, the ghost of [[odar-spesivyj|Odar the Haughty]] had occupied the ducal chair, unwilling to give it up. For Minerva's twentieth birthday, he arranged a cake six feet across with twenty fireworks; someone turned the fireworks upside down, and the cake exploded in a shower of cream. When the kairs caught a ghost and brought it to Minerva, Erwin recognised his altessa: “The kairs have given you my altessa.” Anxiety turned out to be the ghost of Dallia Raven. Erwin promised the dishevelled, barefoot Minerva that he would explain everything “over a cup or two of ordzh,” and they walked off arm in arm; this marked the beginning of their secret affair.

**“Amicably”** (August 1776). Erwin sent an expedition south, consisting of two companies of X-cloaks, Jemis, and Mireille with her retinue, under Hyder Leed's command, and ordered them to conduct their visit to Laem “without any corpses.” He did not go himself because of the preparations for the election, and in his letters to Hector, he hinted at a forthcoming wedding without naming the bride. He gave Jemis his word as a lord that he would still be unmarried when Jemis returned. Jemis received fifteen hilts a month from his suzerain. Erwin taught Archer to howl.

**“Annoying Little Things”** (summer 1776). Henry Farway swore to restore the [[dom-lajthart|Lighthearts]]' ancestral city to them on condition that Nexia married Erwin. Karen Lightheart advised her niece to make this marriage: it would give Erwin the votes of Hope and Almera. One day, Nexia entered Erwin's study while he was having coffee with the empress and made a conspicuous show of caressing him; the following morning, Minerva appointed her envoy to the central lands and sent her away with letters to Hope and Almera. Nexia admitted that she had not slept with Erwin: “I was waiting for love.”

**“Closer—Further Away”** (August 1776). For about a month, Erwin was Minerva's secret lover and favourite: she had “exercised Miriam's right” for the first time. Ember, Captain Whitmore, the castellan, and a few X-cloaks and guardsmen knew; Iona was the last to find out. According to Iona, her brother had been with no one since Alanis's supposed death, and he and Nexia “do not share a bed before marriage.” When Iona learned of the affair, she said that she no longer had a brother and reproached him for treating Nexia badly. Before High Mother Alicia, Erwin repented of the pride he had shown in recounting his visions of Agatha. His penance was a week without meat or alcohol and seventeen prayers each evening, while Alicia took a sketch he had signed. He agreed with the chapter of the Foremothers to guarantee peace during and after the election: the northern troops took up positions in the County of Arrowback, outside the Crownlands. Erwin promised not to kill the innocent, not to act against Minerva's will if she was elected, and not to influence the election. Alicia hinted that, once he became sovereign, he would strike Bishop Amessin off the list of candidates for priarch, and Erwin agreed. He also passed on to Robert a letter from Count Arrowback in which the count hinted at his daughter Rose.

To Amessin, who demanded that Minerva repent for using the Glove in battle, Erwin swore that no one had died by her hand and that Nathaniel had done the fighting. He showed Ganta Cort's envoy, [[nejmir|Neimir]], the ice cellar beneath the castle with its frozen bodies, including Count Benedict Fleming, and set a condition: whenever northern envoys visited Rei-Roy, the leader of the Steppe must kiss the frozen face of the Shavan who had almost killed Erwin. He gave Chara's ashes to Neimir: “Her place is in the Steppe.” When the envoys prostrated themselves before Minerva, Erwin snapped at them, “You threw yourselves down. Lie there as long as you like,” and confirmed the peace only after the sovereign had forgiven them. Erwin and Minerva's affair did not remain a secret: through the Bird without Flesh, [[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]] showed them to Adrian and Magda. For a long time, Erwin could not finish the promised fresco, “Agatha's Choice,” rejecting every sketch. Then he asked Anxiety to appear to the painter in the guise of a learned woman who looked twenty-three but had spent seventy years on a desert island, and promised her that he would refrain from seeking Odar's skull-cup for another five years. Alicia declared the face a miracle. A new fresco also appeared on the cathedral's first left-hand pier: Iona healing a wounded lord with the Healer's Hand while First Winter burned behind her. Erwin wore the Voice of Winter at official meetings, although he told Minerva that he was “no great swordsman.”

**“Ad-ri-an!”** (October–November 1776). In October 1776, Erwin and the empress left First Winter with a retinue of five hundred soldiers, travelling through Main, [[dojl|Doyle]], [[bliznyashki|the Twins]], and the Wise River to [[labelin|Labelin]], where they boarded a train. At [[faunterra|Faunterra]] station, Erwin felt the power of the crowd chanting “Ad-ri-an!”: no carriage was provided for him, and he had to walk to the Origins' filth-smeared mansion. On the way to Faunterra, Erwin had analysed Minerva's plan to build starships: one could reach the Star only with an Absolute, there was no air there, and such a leap could be achieved only through “savage tyranny.” He saw his own “Third Tree” in humanity: “Let everyone in the galaxy become servants of the Id, but we shall remain human.” His programme included a ministry of education, schools for the poor, cheap books, tax-free printing houses, and wise people at the head of the Church. On learning of Mira's deal with Amessin, Erwin announced that he himself was standing for the crown: his oath as a “loyal vassal” meant only that he would not rebel if she was elected. That same night, he found his sister with [[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]]. Through Iona, he asked Ember, the Speaker of the Chamber, to arrange the speeches in the order “Erwin, Adrian, the prophet, Mia.” “Adrian's shouters” desecrated the Origins' mansion, and a stone was thrown through the window with a note saying, “Die, Softie!” To Count Arrowback, who advised him to marry Minerva, Erwin replied that he had sworn not to marry her. Alone with Anxiety, he said, “Adrian certainly won't be elected. I know a sure way to prevent it.”

Before the sitting, Erwin and Iona staged a performance for the Bird without Flesh to anger Adrian. He sat in a “demonic chair,” wearing a diadem and holding a stiletto in place of the Hilt; they “divided up” the Crownlands on a map and threw knives at portraits of Adrian. They also staged a forged letter signed “ESJ” for the Bird, while Erwin sent his real orders in code to Arrowback Castle, where his troops were stationed. He refused the witch of Kribola's offer to bring Lord Darklin under his control: “I shall become emperor honestly, or not at all.” He spoke in the Chamber on the very first day, called himself “Agatha's grandson,” and accused [[adrian|Adrian]] of looting Almera's banks and abducting people in spring 1775. Adrian admitted it. When [[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]] aimed a Finger at Iona, Erwin restrained his sister: “Little sister. Don't.” On the second day, in the besieged Chamber, he set out “three pieces of advice from the Foremothers.” He would cease to recognise the full rights of the lords who had fled. Three kair battalions had left Arrowback by train five days earlier and were stationed outside the capital. Taxes would be cut by fifteen per cent, the scarlet Spark army disbanded, and the defence of the Crownlands entrusted to the kairs. The narrator remarks that “at that moment, Duke Origin won the election.” After Adrian's death, the lords hailed Erwin as the future emperor, and in the morning, the kairs and the knights of Arrowback and Flame restored order in the city without a fight.

The narrator reveals the secret background. That summer, [[khamfri|Sir Humphrey]] had visited Iona's clinic, seeking revenge on the man who had destroyed House Almera. Erwin officially refused, but conveyed through his sister that an opportunity would arise on the first or fourth day of the sittings. He gave Humphrey “credentials” in his own handwriting: “Die, Softie.” Thus Erwin became “one of the handful of people who knew who had killed Adrian, and why.” He ordered Humphrey found and showered with honours, but the man vanished. Erwin explained the rest to Minerva. Under the terms of their winter surrender, the people of the Sunset Coast and the Northwoods had undertaken to vote for him and had merely been pretending to be his enemies. Mireille Nen-Clair had been won over with the help of half a year travelling with Lady Sophia, the murder of the Lady in the Dark, which Robert had committed spontaneously and without Erwin's knowledge, and the X-cloaks sent to Mireille with orders only to defend. He himself was betrothed to [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]], giving him an alliance with Farway and “the votes of the central lands.” In answer to Mira's question, he said, “Of course I love you. But for each of us, power matters more than love,” and after her speech about payment for the empress's services, he cried, “Bravo, Minerva!” In the same conversation, he explained her mistake with the Steppe: the Shavans respected only strength, and a ruler must not plead with prostrate envoys to rise. In Wilhelm's cathedral, Francis-Ilian proposed dividing power according to the model of the “Empire of the Sunrise.” Erwin agreed to become the military ruler (“shogun” or “Lord Chancellor”): “to shine at the head of an army, wear a handsome title, and never think about money.” The lords considered marriage to the empress undesirable, and Erwin agreed. Iona's or Robert's heir would succeed him as shogun. He asked the prophet to marry his cousin Robert to [[roza-erroubek|Rose Arrowback]]. The prophet admitted that he had also been testing “the future crowned couple”: in a dream, he had seen Agatha in the same bed as Yanmei. On the possibility of Shimmery seceding, Erwin remarked, “Or I'll simply conquer them if I start suffering from boredom.” By then, the kairs held the triangle between the Chamber, the Origins' mansion, and the Palace of Pen and Sword. Erwin and Minerva left the cathedral in separate carriages. His final words to Mira were “A doll on the throne,” to which she replied, “Lord Softie.”

### “A Brilliant Calculation”
Erwin does not appear himself, but General [[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]], whose portrait hangs above the Minister of Education's desk, has much to say about him. The general recalls how Erwin once won a game of [[stratemy|stratems]] thanks to someone else's mistake, then declared, “I had it all worked out from the very start.” At Erwin's wedding, where there were “too many northerners,” Davy's father tried to arrange a marriage for his son until the groom laid before the old man the full estimate of the celebration's expenses “for my spice, for my radiant little star.” On the eve of the Battle for First Winter, Nexia had brought the general Erwin's summer “brilliant plan,” which had already collapsed by then. According to Davy, the Origins' foresight was invented after the event, while a school textbook presented the battle as “Agatha and Yanmei's brilliant calculation.”

## Appearance

Erwin is tall and thin, pale, with grey eyes, sharp cheekbones, and a crooked, ironic smile. When he stood beside the fresco of Agatha after the explorada, Daniel, a cathedral attendant, recognised him as a lord by his family features: sharply defined cheekbones, ironically curved lips, and penetrating steel-grey eyes. The servant Tommy explained why the soldiers had nicknamed the lord Softie: he walked with a stoop, “like a monk,” his fingers were thin, “like a girl's,” and he washed his hair too often. Erwin returned from the war with grey at his temples.

## Personality

Erwin considers himself weak and cowardly—he admits to himself that he fears almost everything except death—yet repeatedly takes extreme risks. A brilliant intriguer and master of deception, he loves provocation, feints, and masquerades. Erwin is vain in public and self-mocking in private; he can be merciful to ordinary people and ruthless to his enemies. He imagines a companion, the altessa Anxiety, a mocking inner voice; in “Fantasies,” she turns out to be a ghost. He deeply reveres Agatha the Radiant, prays to her, and sees her. After returning from the Beyond, he remained dependent on snake-grass for a long time. He learned fencing from Jemis and Robert and, after receiving the Voice of Winter, mastered the family's signature stroke, “Agatha's Lightning.”

Many of his decisions are driven by stubbornness and wounded pride. He led the party beyond the River chiefly to spite the observer Lowfert. Yet he is also capable of compassion: on the Southern Way, he fed peasants ruined by their own lords, and he later described the prisoners executed near Doyle as unarmed and innocent. He regarded his family's greatest virtue as the ability to “grow up an Origin and keep one's soul,” rather than cruelty.

## Relationships

### Iona
[[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] is the person closest to him. Erwin could admit his fear to her. He sent her a four-page letter from the bank of the River, and in his fever, he recalled their childhood. Erwin said that he had returned from the Beyond for her sake. Before Holy Field, the brother and sister once kissed, but immediately drew apart. Richard accused them of an incestuous relationship. In Leed, Iona saved her brother by taking control of Martin Shayland; in the Battle for First Winter, she covered him with the Finger and then healed him with the Healer's Hand.

### Desmond
[[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] was a stern father with whom Erwin long struggled to find common ground. He exiled his son to the Beyond and sent him not a single line in Spot, which caused Erwin great distress. Nevertheless, it was Desmond who, after hearing the account of the explorada, supported the rebellion and handed power to his son. Later, he gave Erwin the family sword, brought ships to Fliss, and stood with an army outside Waymar. Before his death, Desmond called his son and daughter his greatest source of pride.

### His mother
[[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica]] would have been proud of his performance, according to Erwin: he had inherited his acting talent from her. He was outraged by her deal with the Puppeteer to heal his father, and in May 1775, he sent her to Folta, to Mireille Nen-Clair.

### Richard
[[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] was his elder brother, who despised “Softie.” In his delirium beyond the River, Erwin recalled his words:
> “You know, Erwin, nobody likes people like you.”

Richard became the commander of the Puppeteer's heretics under the nickname Ice, killed their father, and died by Erwin's hand.

### Jemis Lilliday
[[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] first challenged Erwin's authority and was banished without his cloak. He then followed Erwin intending to kill him, but nursed him back to health and became his most loyal kair and friend. He taught Erwin fencing, sailed beyond the River with Fleming's flotilla, saved the duke in the Farewell tomb, and operated the Weapon in the Steppe and outside First Winter.

### Minerva
[[minerva|Minerva]] is an adversary, ally, and rival. Erwin made an agreement with her in the palace, then tried to control her as “Mimi”; after Holy Field, he furiously wanted to curb her, and in May 1775, he joined her in hunting the Puppeteer. Their relationship shifted between hostility and reconciliation, with the cycle ending in the latter. In “Fantasies,” Erwin becomes her secret favourite. In autumn 1776, they separate and compete in the election, then divide power: she is empress, and he is military ruler.

### Women
- **[[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]**—Aiden's daughter and the emperor's leading prospective bride, whose candidacy Erwin's coalition opposed in 1773. In autumn 1774, she came to him near Doyle, and after the capture of Labelin, became his adviser and lover. Her unauthorised plan involving the bridge over the Beck, her lie about the Healer, and her connection to the Puppeteer destroyed their alliance. In the main cycle, Erwin and Iona believe that she died in captivity in Waymar; in “Fantasies,” all indications suggest that she is alive under the identity of a schoolteacher, Mrs Gale (see her article).
- **[[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]**—a girl with cornflower-blue eyes, of whose kiss Erwin dreamed in his fever beyond the River. She warned him of the trap at Pixie, tried to poison him near Dust, and in 1775, procured ships for him and persuaded her father to intervene in the war. At the end, she confesses her love to him, and Erwin promises to think about it. In “Fantasies” (November 1776), he is betrothed to her.
- **[[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]]**—his fiancée under a secret agreement with Henry Farway. In spring 1774, her grandfather announced that he had found her a bridegroom “as clever as Agatha the Radiant,” but in autumn, he broke off the engagement and joined Adrian's side. She later became Gallard's wife, and in autumn 1775, Martin Shayland strangled her.

### Companions
- **[[robert-oridzhin|Robert]]**—his cousin and treasurer, who raised money for the war and later became imperial treasurer; he taught Erwin the family's fighting techniques.
- **[[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon]]**—his cousin, the duke's right-hand man and commander of the X-cloaks, who died during the siege of the palace.
- **[[teobart|Theobart]]**—captain of the explorada and Desmond's squire, assigned to Erwin as his “nursemaid.” He died beyond the River.
- **[[mark-voron|Mark]]**—Adrian's envoy, who was supposed to poison Erwin, but then became his agent and the Raven of the Crown.
- **[[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]**—captain of the Leed Wolves, investigator of the Beck train disaster, and Erwin's companion in the Steppe and in Leed.
- **[[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue Rose]]**—captain of the X-cloaks, who lost an arm saving the duke near Rei-Roy and was killed by Richard on the night after the Battle for First Winter.
- **[[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]]**—the kair whom Erwin ordered to watch Alanis; he was maimed in the Farewell tomb.
- **[[otets-david|Father David]]**—a priest, Erwin's companion from autumn 1774, who later revealed to him the secrets of the order and the Tree.

## The author's comments
- According to the author, Erwin's name refers to Erwin Rommel and Erwin Schrödinger, but he did not copy Rommel's biography. If one seeks a model, Erwin's military and political career more closely resembles that of Gaius Julius Caesar (spring 2026 stream).
- According to the author, he gave Erwin and [[minerva|Minerva]] more of his own traits than any other characters, although no character is based entirely on him. Erwin's sense of humour is his own (18 April 2021 stream).
- According to the author, Erwin's march on foot through the swamps beyond the River was [[desmond-oridzhin|Duke Desmond]]'s idea. The expedition was scouting a route for a [[zheleznaya-doroga|railway]], but above all, it was a test intended to make his son grow up (18 April 2021 stream).
- According to the author, Erwin did not possess Firstblood. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]'s blood acted as a medicine for him but gave him no power over the Objects, and Erwin had never met any of the few people capable of performing initiation (7 January 2026 stream).
- According to the author, burning the prisoners in the Beyond and shooting the garrison of [[evergard|Evergard]] were evil without qualification (18 April 2021 stream). However, this does not make Erwin a villain: he knew why he was acting as he did (spring 2026 stream).

For more, see [[slovo-avtora|The Author's Comments]].

## Trivia
- The code name “Arrow” reflects his feeling that he is an arrow his father releases from a bowstring. This image comes to him in Spot, when he finds that his father has not sent even a letter.
- In one chapter, Erwin himself recalls that he spoke to Aiden “in early December,” although the meeting is dated 2 September 1773.
- By his own admission, Erwin first decided to cross the River to spite Baron Lowfert.
- Erwin remembered the guide Kid's kindly advice—“everything has a bright side”—long afterwards, even during the war.
- Erwin's former sword, too heavy for “Lord Softie,” had no name: he considered it foolish to name a tool that he wielded with no more than an apprentice's skill. With that sword, he struck down half a dozen spearmen at Windley and ten of Major Beckfield's soldiers.
- On the night Richard came to kill him, Erwin first thought that his vision of Agatha (“Tomorrow we shall meet”) foretold his death, but then decided that the saint had meant the fresco.
