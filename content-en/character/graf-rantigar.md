**Count Rantigar** is the ruler of the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]]. His name is not in the text, only the title and surname.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
**War for the Mills.** ([[melnichnaya-vojna|Mill War]]) For the first time about the count says [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] in the first chapter: before he served “in the army of Count Rantigar”, who fought for the Milllands. Harmon knew this war from stories. Rantigar gathered an army of twelve thousand swords from the western knights, militiamen and mercenaries, crossed the Gulkii ford and invaded the disputed lands of [[melnitsy|the Mills]]. He took four castles and won the battle in the fields, but the Melniks defended themselves stubbornly, and his army "attacked but melted". According to Harmon, everything that the count did would be called "carnage and robbery."

Joaquin loved to embellish his service. He claimed that the earl personally entrusted him with the night crossing with a hundred horsemen, and later recalled that he fought under the banners of Rantigar "on the Mill ford and at the Three Eves" along with Sir Ben Womack. ([[ben-vomak|Benjamin Womack]])

**At the ball.** In the summer of 1774 the count was at the court of [[adrian|Adrian]]. Becca showed him to [[minerva|Mira]] at the reception: "the same Count Rantigar", who started a big war in the West and "lost with a deafening crash". ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]) He lost more than a thousand soldiers and a lot of money, and the final battle was not: seeing the enemy’s formation, the count surrendered. But he was known as the “main villain of the West” and was in the center of attention of ladies. During the prayer, Rantigar and Alexis Silver Fox waited for her to end to continue the conversation. ([[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]])

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark" (collection edition)
Lady [[karen|Karen]] corrects the author in three points. The count's army consisted not of Western knights, but mainly of [[shavany|Shavans]]. No commanding talent was needed: the shavans love looting, and the Millers are their longtime enemies. Rantigar's first general was General Horis-desh. ([[oris|General Oris]])

## In "Only One Star"
**Death in the palace.** In the autumn of 1774, Adrian ordered the landowners to appear in person. Count Rantigar came first: in a black doublet with gold, with a scarlet belt and a crooked cavalry sword, which the guards immediately seized. He was followed by a huge squire in black leather, similar to his shadow. Manson's joker saw his face "from sharp corners": pointed nose and chin, narrow western eyes, whiskers, honed, "like a Manson pencil." ([[menson|Manson]])

Rantigar admired the Crown's victory over the "traitor" [[ajden-almera|Aiden]] and wished that the new rebel, the Origin, would go "straight to the Star". Manson realized that the Earl was seeking revenge for his disgraceful defeat by the Origins. One of [[rejs|Reis]]' ambassadors called his words "the words of a coward", and the count himself - a jackal who "licks the heels" of the emperor. Rantigar ridiculed Moran of the Steppe Fire as a "breedless warrior" and ordered the Shavans to pray for mercy. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) Then the distinguished ambassador broke past the squire and cut the count's throat with a spur. "Coward has no place at the head of the free land," said the Shavan and spat on the body.

Joaquin told [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] that he served with Rantigar in the [[melnichnaya-vojna|Mill War]] and then with Baron Broyfield. Alanis, who sought allies, called the count an "unprincipled scoundrel", who, however, has an army and a willingness to fight. Then Joaquin reluctantly admitted that he was not familiar with the count, but only served as a hired sword under his banners.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] in conversation with [[mark-voron|Mark]] put Count Rantigar on a par with Aiden Almera, Prince Hector and Lady [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]]: they are all liars and intriguers, each in their own way charming and endowed with charisma. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]])

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
**Three-power.** After the death of the Count, the Sunset Coast was in turmoil. The mercenaries Pat and Kroc told Joaquin that three lords were fighting for power. The Earl's eldest son, Corwin's rightful heir, who was simply called the Elder Son, led an army to help Moran and fought with the Shavans against Adrian. The youngest son, Dainis Rantigar, was opposed to the war, and after the defeat of the horde he called himself a count, occupied three main ports and sought allies in [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] and even on [[folta|Folta]]. The third lord is General Oris, the chief commander of the earl: he took the capital of Silens.

**War of Objects.** Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] told Iona that in the War of the Objects, both Count Rantigar and General Oris had urged him to side with them, but he held back. [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]] said that then the Kairs "broke Rantigar's army" (see [[semnadtsatyj-dar|Seventeenth Gift]]).
