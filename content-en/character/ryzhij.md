## Biography

Red is an agent of the secret guard and one of [[mark-voron|Mark]]'s closest assistants. His real name in the books does not sound: both the boss and his colleagues call him only by nickname, and he himself refers to the Crow "cheef". Of all the former subordinates of Mark, Red was the best connoisseur of dead bodies: he had a trained eye and enough medical knowledge to understand why a person died. The dead body is always a mystery to him; the Raven himself prefers conversations with the living and, as far as the corpses are concerned, trusts Red's observation more than his own.

Mark appreciates his work, but not his conclusions: the examination, according to Crow, may be good, but "you didn't understand anything from what you saw." Red willingly puts forward versions, which the boss immediately breaks, and in private conversations Mark calls him a “friend”, then a fool. Together with Secretary [[itan|Ethan]], he was one of those with whom Crow thought well out loud.

## In "Only One Star"

### Strangled at the mound

At the end of July 1774, Deputy Sheriff Fairfax pulled Mark and Red to the body at the rail embankment. Mark waved away - the protection deals only with the threats of the Dynasty - but Red asked to give him "only five minutes", and the Crow, noticing the second shoe of the killed, agreed. Red described the strangled burgher of thirty-five years, traces of ink on his palms, broken nails and empty pockets, and objected to Fairfax that he had not been killed for money: the rod was not the robber's weapon. On the way to the capital, Mark praised him - not for his inspection, but for his instinct - and promised Elena if the matter turned out to be important. Red was instructed to get a draft of the last “[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]” and brought the Raven a daily report from the “whispers”.

### Printing and archive

It was Red, according to Captain Croydon Backfield, who spat on the guard, as if the Crow had named the dog Lucia - the Mother of the Backfield family; ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Mark abdicated ("he is not a nobleman, he is forgiven") and cleverly sent the angry captain to guard the printing house. When, during the interrogation of the printers, Red flew into the hall with a sketch of the July Voice, he, to Mark's chagrin, "took the whole misanthrope." Then the Raven sent him to search for the missing archivist Lenard — “you know better than me where bodies usually go” — and listened to his guesses about the stolen sloth of the Holliwells; the version of the thief’s error Mark called stupid. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) Ethan found out that Jake's typewriter, whose body he and Red were examining by the roadside, had been taken away from the printing house by the same dark carriage.

## A Doll on the Throne.

### Meeting with the Risen Raven

In early 1775, after the opal of Mark, Red, like all agents, considered the Crow to have perished in the northern casemates. When Mark on behalf of [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] took up the case of the abduction of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]], he summoned Red, and he did not believe his eyes for half an hour and tried to slap him on the shoulder. Red complained that without Mark in the protection began a discord: the main appointed a major, and he began to bring the army, and in secret custody, “people are flexible”, they need a flexible approach. [[ded|Grandfather]] and Granddaughter Mark introduced him as a wandering philosopher with a student who made up his life story; Red quickly realized who had sent them, and spoke of Origin with great respect, "his lordship."

### Investigation

On the train, Red said that he examined in Orwell three bandits killed in the attack on the train with Objects: members of a strong gang from the central lands, two fugitive convicts. One, shot when he climbed into the wagon with the Objects, died with reverence on his face - so the bandits knew that they were being stolen, and someone inspired them. Red saw and dead gray-northern, but did not write it into the case, and the guilt of the mayor considered undoubted: Objects, in his opinion, already in Darkwater, in the chests of Baron Escher. ([[baron-esher|Baron Esher]]) When Mark asked why the saboteurs at the bridge across the Snake were riding instead of returning to the train, he found no answer for the first time.

Along with Mark, Grandfather and Granddaughter, Red followed the trail of the gang: he showed the place where the wires were torn off, determined that the forester was stabbed by the rider, driving him with his back to the tree, and that the detachment was well armed - no one took a good axe. ([[vnuchok|Grandson]]) At the burned inn "Forest Shelter" he first decided that two unknown people died in the fire, but Mark corrected: they were killed immediately. On the ashes of the blacksmith, Red, sighing heavily, unearthed the bodies of the blacksmith Jonas and his assistant and established that both were stabbed with a dagger, with the blacksmith still alive when the blacksmith was set on fire. He himself suggested that the blacksmith had known the gang for a long time and was waiting for it, not without reason sent out apprentices.

In [[smolden|Smolden]], Red asked to return to Faunterra: in the capital, the empress caught the ministers-kaznokradov, and his absence could be noticed. Mark let him go, instructing him to find out how a detachment of X-cloaks died at Pixie - "ask for kairs, greys, war horses, Duchess Alanis, Duke of Origin." In Aleridan, Crow was waiting for a package from Red: he talked to the surviving queer [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] and described the "composition A" standing at Fairwell Station before the Battle of Pixie. This discovery helped Mark to understand that the Master had foreseen the course of the war in advance.

## The “Janmei Hunt”

At the second session of the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], Mark summoned Red as a witness - after [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]'s kair and the Lord's healer. According to the drawing of the skeleton, Red told about the examination of [[adrian|Adrian]]'s body and, rejoicing at the attention of the hall, did not spare terrible details until Erwin asked to go to the main for the sake of [[minerva|Minerva]]'s nerves. He could not establish the cause of death, but pointed to a breach in the occipital bone from a strong blunt blow and explained how the body could rise and freeze into the ice of the bay. Francis-Ilianus asked why the body of the lord was carried twice as far as the rest; ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) Red rejected the suggestion that he was carried by a living person — no one would benefit from postponing the find — for which Judge [[albert-kantor|Cantor]] turned him down: "you are not a lord." Erwin supported the witness. When the prophet put forward the version of the second murderer, Red, with Erwin's permission, admitted that the state of the body did not contradict it, but noticed that the lord was found in the same wet uniform in which he fell into the water.

## "The Shadow of the Great Tree"

Red remained with Mark after his return to the post of Crown Raven. When Mark questioned Harmon about the Light Sphere, the conversation with the merchant was led by Red; he did not see a direct connection between Harmon and the fact that the Sphere was found, and on the orders of the Crow went to the garden to see who would pick up the discarded Item. ([[kharmon|Harmon Paula Roger]]; [[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) On the imperial train, he brought prisoner [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]] to Minerva and, on her orders, removed the shackles from him when the mistress granted Parochka a pardon.
