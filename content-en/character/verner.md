**Werner** is the cairn of the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]] from the garrison of the Pera Palace and the Sword in [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. ([[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]) His eyes show both visits of Znaharka, a servant of the Puppeteer, to the palace (see Hand of Znaharka). ([[znakharka|The Healer]]; [[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) The full name of Werner is not named in the text.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
### Watch at the beggar's gate
In the winter of 1775, Werner, Ragolph, and Tenn stood watch at the dock gate on the south spit of the Palace Island. Through this gate, the leftovers were carried out by the poor. The guards considered this service a punishment. Werner and Tenn "swallowed the spark discharge at Pixie" (the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]]) and lay in captivity bound while their comrades won the night's Labelin. ([[reznya-v-labeline|Night in Labelin]]) To pass the time, all three played "finger", and Werner and Tenn easily beat the simple-minded mountain Ragolf. Werner was angry at his fate: “I am a nobleman, eat the darkness! The only one at this gate!”

### First visit of the Signer (Chapter 17)
The hunchback came to the gate and demanded that she be escorted to Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia]]. Tenn drew his sword, and she tore the blade from the handle with two fingers. Together with [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]], the sentries took her to [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]]. On the way, Werner was angry at the insolent woman and dreamed of killing her, and at the same time getting an officer rank or a hundred gold. When Znaharka went to the door and Jemis ordered her to stop, it was Werner who was on the way. She created a shimmering shield in the air, and the cairn "fossilized, almost like Lord Desmond." The confrontation was stopped by the word of Desmond. During the treatment, Jemis placed Werner on the right of the healer to cut off her hand on orders. Returning to the gate, Werner was wet with sweat and dreamed of cutting off her brush with thimbles when she came for the third time. Later, Jemis suggested that Erwin replace the sentries: “Tenn is slow, Ragolph is a fool, Werner is a coward.” Erwin refused: when he saw his acquaintances, the Znacharka would relax.

### Death (Chapter 23)
On the morning of the second visit, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] told the guards to bring the Znaharka not to the cellar where Jemis had ordered, but to the summer theater. Werner was the only one of the three who realized that the sister was going against the will of the Duke, and began to look for a way out. He wanted to run after Iona. On the way to the palace, he was stopped by Jemis, and Werner lied that he was looking for the Princess to report on the roses brought. In the summer theater, he tried to dissuade Iona (“This Znaharka is very dangerous...”), but she did not listen to him.

When Znaharka rushed to flee, Iona, as if knowing that Werner "will never run first in the world," sent in the pursuit of Ragolph. Werner ran third, deliberately not catching up. Well, she ripped Tenna's leg off below her knee. At the tents of the Northwoods, she came upon a tall warrior and fell, and Werner struck her with a sword, although she no longer had the Subject on her hand: "A witch must have wanted to surrender..." At the same moment, a Northwood soldier put an axe in his stomach. Iona found next to the dead Znacharka "the corpse of a northerner." The Northwood captain explained: “One of yours, my lady, was chasing a woman with a sword. I cut him short, but he managed to stab the lady." [[sorok-dva|Forty-two]] demanded to get even, but Iona forbade a fight.

## Personality
Werner was proud of the noble family: his great-grandfather by his mother “got into the ballad”. He envied the fact that the Znaharka has a Sacred Object, and his family “not a single subject in the inheritance.” He despised southerners and beggars and dreamed of distinguishing himself, but he was lost in danger. In his thoughts, the desire for revenge is constantly mixed with fear.
