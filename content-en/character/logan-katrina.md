*Harmon's visit to Ser Logan is only in the early editions of [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]]: in the 2026 edition, which is considered a canon, the episode is cut out, and after Smallden the train immediately rolls into the Six Mile Forest. Information about the castle, the family, and the knight's past is below taken from the early editions.*

## Who He Is

Sir Logan Katrina owned a small castle on the northeast end of Almera, near the edge of the Six Mile Forest, and two neighboring villages. ([[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]]; [[shestimilnyj-les|Six-Mile Wood]]) He was not young and for many years remained one of the “always buyers” of merchant [[kharmon|Harmon Paula Roger]].

The knighthood, castle and villages of Sir Logan received not so long ago, earning them a long military service in the guard of Duke [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]. Prior to that, he was a hired sword, like [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], even before the penny-rebel, and began to apprentice a potter in the town of Dorothy Place, "long gone." He was not ashamed of his simple blood.

> "Blessings? What is it?.. Who was your mother and who was your grandmother? What matters to me is who you are and what you can do.”

He looked at his sons with pride: they were “whoo-goo” and would remain so when he himself died. Harmon thought he was an easy-going, but handsome man.

## Castle

Logan's Sire Castle was modest: two square towers connected by a gallery. However, the towers looked strong, flags were horribly fluttering on them, and the yard behind the palisade was full of work, as is the case "with fast-paced and sensible owners." Joaquin, leaving, contemptuously told Harmon: "This is not a castle. A piece of fortress wall with two stickers - that's what it is. Where's the bridge, where's the moat, where's the courtyard?"

## Harmon's Visit (early editions)

In the spring of 1774, a week's journey from [[smolden|Smolden]], Harmon's convoy, encircling the Six Mile Forest, approached the castle. The owner met the merchant with a hearty dinner under the canopy. The knight’s wife sat at the table, four “duches as for the selection” of the eldest sons and five younger children. Sir Logan hugged Harmon, sat on his right hand, generously poured ale, and laughed loudly. They talked about the future harvest, about the Duke of Aiden and his daughter, who went to the capital to the Sunny Games in the hope of Alanis' marriage to the lord. The knight with the meaning said, "A big man's place in the capital." But at the word "capital" he barely noticeably crooked, because the court fuss was disgusting to him.

There were rumors that the lord would tax the feudal lords. Sir Logan was not afraid of it: “There is land, there is forest, there are hands. We’ll have to — we’ll make money.” When asked by Harmon what the Crown would spend the money on, he shrugged his shoulders: "Irons will pull. The lord loves iron... what is a good horse worse? Traded simply. Harmon immediately called the price three times higher than the purchase price, and the knight paid without an auction, and examined the goods later, “out of love of order.” He bought five jugs of pepper honey from Litland, three pieces of cotton cloth, a copper mortar for salt, and a few combs for his daughters.

Soon after leaving, Harmon deliberately drove the train across the bridge, where a year ago forest robbers were trading. He expected to check on Joaquin and hoped that "Sir Logan or anyone else" had not bothered to catch them.

## Sons and Joaquin

In the fall of 1774, while fleeing from Aleridan, a hungry Joaquin made his way through Almera to his native [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] and plundered: he stole food, took warm clothes from travelers. Passing by the castle of Ser Logan, which, according to the text of the book, in the spring said "I myself much better build" (this remark remained only in the early media), he met on the road the sons of the knight. They asked him if he had seen the robber, and what was scaring the travelers in the area. Joaquin gave himself up and ran into the woods, and Logan's "boys", who had something to lose, did not dare to drive horses through the thicket just as recklessly. At the boundary pillar, where "the power of the archer ends", Joaquin moved to the land of the Southern Way.
