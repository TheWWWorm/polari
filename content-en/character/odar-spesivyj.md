Odar Spesivyi is the Duke of the [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]], who lived several centuries before the events of the cycle. In the main cycle, he is only remembered as a ghost sitting in the ducal chair. His story is told in detail in Fantasia. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]])

## Duke Odar in Traditions
The story "The Voice of Winter" tells the story of the sword "The Voice of Winter". ([[glas-zimy|Voice of Winter]]; [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter (Sword)]]) Two hundred and twenty years ago it was forged by the master Conrad of [[lid|Leed]] for the Duke of Odar Origin, in the darkest days of the empire - during the First Horse War. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]) With this sword in his hand, Duke Odar vowed to defend the empire to the last drop of blood. The voice of Winter took the life of the great gauntlet Hadinai at the Battle of [[dojl|Doyle]], which interrupted the movement of the Steppe to the north. With the same sword, the son of the lord struck his father with a blow of mercy and received the blade as an inheritance. Right in the text, this duke Odar Spesiv is not named, but he and Spesiv fell in the war with the steppes.

[[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] in the "Janmey Hunt" mentioned a different story: Count Godfrey Fleming took the First Winter and killed Duke Odar Origin, for which the Origins executed both the count and all his close relatives. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) Whether this is Odar is not specified.

## Ghost in the Chair
In Shadow of the Great Tree, when [[minerva|Minerva]] moved the capital to the First Winter, Castelians Arthur Hyrok explained to her why she would not be allowed to sit at the head of the table. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]; [[artur-khajrok|Arthur Highrock]]) Since the time of Lioreus Origin, no one has sat in this chair except the duke; the daring have been removed from the chair and put on coals, and this has happened six times in four centuries. In addition, Duke Odar Spesivyi had a heavy temper, after his death he remained in the castle to monitor the order, and his ghost likes to sit in a chair when it is not occupied by a living ruler.

## In "Fantasies"
In the story "Let's catch him" (summer 1776), Erwin told the Cairams that in the first month of his reign, Odar did not want to give up the ducal chair. I had to sit right on his femur. Decent ghosts, according to Erwin, do not go anywhere: Odar can not move away from the chair for more than three steps. The rumor called him the "ghost king" of the First Winter. Even the brave X's were timid: according to Lieutenant [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]], the speed in it was "as in the three Lords of Desmonds".

To catch the ghost that frightened the inhabitants of the castle, [[shram|Scar]], Fitzgerald and Aubrey decided to turn to Odar. You can conquer the spirit only by extracting its bone or naming it at the place of death, and Odar died near the Labelin, where it was not time before the holiday. Therefore, the kairs broke into the sarcophagus in the family tomb and brought the skull to the large hall. The ghost was silent until Aubrey began to read aloud the book by Megara Estelle “The Secret Horrors of the First Winter”. The hall was covered with frost, bottles of wine burst, and the words sounded in the cod of ice: “You shouldn’t have known.” The Kairs swore to keep the secret, and then the spirit told them to read on.

### The Mystery of Odar
The book reveals what happened. The body of the duke, who fell in battle, went to the Shavans: he was cut down and given to pigs and dogs, made a bowl out of the skull, and the surviving bones were decorated with shields and handles of swords. Out of fear of punishment, he brought home a corpse. He set it up for farewell for three days, sitting in a chair at the head of the table, the first to kiss a rotting hand and forced everyone, including the widow, to bow to the deceased. Admired by his courage, his relatives did not dare to challenge the inheritance. On the fourth day, he buried the stranger in the family tomb and sat in the chair himself. History remembered his son as a great lord, only slightly inferior to his father. Odar's soul could not fly to the Star. She was chained forever to a defiled chair so that no stranger would ever sit in it again. To ask the descendants to find a skull cup and return it to the crypt Odar could not: for this it would be necessary to confess what a coward and a liar the beloved son was. The skull brought by the kairs also belonged to a stranger.

### Dallia Raven
When in the castle died of a fever fell under the ice girl Dahlia Raven, the spirit of Odar caught her will and offered a deal: she will find in the Steps a bowl from his skull and a man who will crush the steppes and return his head to the family crypt, and he will help her, and together they will go to the Star. ([[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]]) For twelve years, Dahlia served the dead lord, and his character was imprinted in her: ferocity became her irony, stubbornness became her flexibility, cold military mind became the mind of a mistress and a spy. She then became attached to the new Duke of Erwin and became his althess Trevoga. She lied that the "little wolf" had not yet found the bowl, although ordinary ghosts are not able to lie.

After listening to the book, Odar forgave the kairs: a warm summer wind burst into the hall. In the story "Closer - Further" Erwin, who already knew about the bowl, promised Dalia not to look for it for another five years.
