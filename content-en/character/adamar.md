## Who He Is

Sir Adamar is the cousin of Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] of the Yangmei family. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) In line for the throne, he stood second, just behind the uncle of the Lord, the jester [[menson|Manson]]. This is a tall, bald knight with a beautiful strong-willed face and resinous hair. At court, he was considered simple-minded, unpretentious and not too rich by the standards of the Dynasty. His passions were hunting and knightly tournaments.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

### Tournament at Aleridan

The merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] told [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] about the tournament in Aleridan, which in the summer of 1773 was arranged by the Duke of [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]. ([[turnir-v-aleridane|Tournament at Aleridan]]) On the first day, Sir Adamar came out victorious. He defeated five opponents, among them the captain of the azure guard, a two-time winner of previous tournaments. The second winner of the day was the Fang Knight of [[dom-nortvud|Northwood]], but their match never took place. On the last day, Adamar overthrew Karl Krasavchik with the first blow of a spear, and in the final he dedicated the fight to the Duke's daughter [[alanis-almera|Alanis]].

The finale he fought with Sir Lauren of [[nadezhda|Hope]]. ([[loren|Sir Loren]]) Three horse-drawn faults did not knock Lauren out of the saddle, and the knights converged on the swords. Lauren knocked off Adamar's helmet, but lowered the sword and let him lift the helmet. Adamar later overthrew Lauren and returned the debt: "Get up, sire Lauren." In heavy patches, he was tired first, lost his balance in the fall and lost. Lauren became the champion.

### Check in the Lumini Garden

In the spring of 1774, [[minerva|Minerva]], who lived in the capital under the name of Gloria Northwood, was looking for a conspirator who killed the heirs to the throne. In the Shrine of Pera, the archivist gave her two books about Sera Adamar, Memoirs of a Guard and Outstanding Knights of the Crown and their accomplishments. They described him only as a valiant warrior and hunter. Mira reasoned that the master's cousin was "the most likely suspect."

Secretary [[itan|Ethan]] took her to a northern azalea celebration in the Lumini Garden, where Adamar was out for a walk with [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]. Hearing the word "Northwood", the knight immediately spoke about hunting bears. Mira began to tell him ridiculous northern fables. Northwooders do not hunt bears, but stop them with a “bear conspiracy” and cut off their downs. The fangs seem to be walking with a bronze tip, greased with fat. Adamar believed every word and promised after the summer games to go to the North for the “monstrous northern beast”.

Then Mira told how her “friend” Minerva from Stagforth died in a thunderstorm. Adamar only indifferently noticed that girls are rarely good in the saddle. He showed no surprise. Ethan explained to Mira that the knight was "simply sane, trusting, rude, in love with himself" and that he was a bad conspirator. Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]], upon hearing of the "bearish conspiracy", called him "a credulous fool".

### Becky's joke and ambush

Becca called Peace into the Jager Forest. She knew that the next day Adamar would go there for a fox hunt and jump over the ravine in the narrowest place. The girls decided to cover the snare with bear skin and nail a note with a “bear conspiracy” to the tree. Becca said that Adamar is not her lover, but “just a friend.” In the forest of Peace, she came across four suspicious people and decided that they had been sent by Adamar. Becca chased them away with arrows, and the girls vowed to keep quiet.

Adamard died the next morning. According to [[mark-voron|Mark]] from the patronage, he fell with a horse into a ravine and twisted his neck. ([[protektsiya|The Protection]]) Mira realized that those four were preparing an ambush on Adamar himself, and the girls came across by chance. She reproached herself: tell her about them, the knight could be saved. Sybil told Mark that she had known Adamara for a long time, sympathized with him and gave him a second dance at the summer ball last year. He had no enemies, she said.

Mark later told Mira that three of the assailants were found stabbed in a sewer drain. They were killed on the day of Adam's death. The fourth, whom Mira remembered by the northern reprimand, hired them in the slums, hiding his face under the hood; the scum recognized a stranger in it: clothes are inconspicuous, but the boots are too good, and smart words slipped in jargon. Mark found him a handyman of a certain lord and drew Mira's attention to the strangeness: the employer entrusted the murder of an experienced knight to unverified scum - then he had a backup plan or "something more interesting." In the early media, Mark instead reasoned that the fourth was a Northerner, a Northwooder, or a Sheelander, but not an Origen, and that he was probably sent by the northern lord, after which he removed the underlings and fled the capital. At the summer ball, Becca wore a black ribbon on her hand in memory of the knight. Mira believed that Adamar died because of her.

### Who killed him

At first, Mira considered Adamar and Becca as conspirators and later called it a “stupid double mistake”. She then attributed the murder to [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] and Graysend: Mark's "last argument" was Labelin's suspicious friendship with General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis]]. According to Mira, the conspirators instructed the murder of heirs to low-born bandits in order to later get rid of them and cut off the trail. She eventually decided that the heirs were killed on the orders of Ayden Almera. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) He, like twenty years ago in the Shutov plot, created the “illusion of a threat” to the lord: he hired crooks from the slums, because the goal was not so much to kill, but to scare the yard and the Chamber. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) Sybil exclaimed, “You want to say that Sir Adamar was killed only for appearances?”

## In a Collector's Edition

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition corrects Harmon: three golden drops on the helmet is the coat of arms of Sir Adamar himself, not the symbol of Yangmei. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) The symbol of the Mother is the hand in the Glove of Power. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]) She also writes that there is no direct evidence against Aidan, and the murder of Ser Adamar is now considered by many to be the work of the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]].
