Baron Dorcaster is a vassal of the House of Shayland, a merry man and a joker at the county table, who turned out to be one of the most dexterous and loyal servants of the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]]. ([[dom-shejland|House Shayland]])

## Appearance and Personality
Mira in 1774 saw a "round-faced man with a round plywood on his forehead", and later - a head "deprived of all vegetation and extremely egg-shaped", for which she nicknamed him Baron Vmytka. He is short, wears screaming red camsoles, first laughs his own anecdotes ("Ga-ga-ga!") and hears tales like "As one-legged Jack said...". She said: “Baron Dorcaster is a fool. Round." His age is not named.

Appearances are deceiving. [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] believed that on the battlefield on the baron can not rely, but in the "behind the scenes... Dorcaster is little suspected; he was a "saboteur" in the distribution of Vittor's roles. The baron himself soberly assessed the suzerain: “My suzerain is a genius of intrigue and banking, but not a commander at all.” He was a weak swordsman - a "ridiculous turtle", according to Joaquin. He joked in the most serious moments: about the invulnerable in the Absolute, Vittor said: "The main thing is not to put your face back."

## Family
The baron had a wife left in the estate, an adult son and two young daughters, Mary and Bassey: spoiled crooks who imitated at the table the guests of the count - Sir [[frensis-myurrej|Francis Murray]] ("fu-fu-fu!"), castellan, Iona. In 1756, Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent Shayland]] called the Dorcasters his greatest vassal, and young [[martin-shejland|Martin]] killed the cat because of the ridicule of the "younger Dorcaster", the young Baroness Selina. Whether it was the baron or his father, and who he belonged to Selina, the text does not specify.

## History

### Guest at Waymar (1774)
In the autumn of 1774 Dorcaster and his daughters lived in Waymar with the Count: he came "on a visit to the suzerain and remained, it seems, forever." At the table with the prisoner [[minerva|Minerva]], he discussed the war ("Three to one is not mathematics yet"), and Mira even listed him among the suspects in the disappearance of Lindsay's servant - as a person "funny and stupid." ([[lindsi|Lindsey]])

### Joaquin Test (June 1775)
After the [[perevorot-v-uejmare|coup in Waymar]], the baron came to Vittor with two hundred warriors. In the office of the count, a scene was played: the count reproached the baron, Dorcaster's bodyguards rushed into a fight, the baron rushed to Vittor with a sword, and Joaquin disarmed him. Then the baron gave him a fake order from Origin, promising a "whole barony" over Vittor's head: "I already have a barony, I will live without a second. Take it." Everything turned out to be a test of Joaquin's loyalty; his forge was made from an invitation to a wedding in the First Winter, and the second copy remained with the Baron's son - to catch infidel vassals. During the siege of Waymar, Dorcaster's warriors locked up the city, caught fugitives, and guarded the castle; at the council, the baron was the first to propose to go north, into his possession.

### The Bear's Fang (Summer 1775)
On the first day of the siege, Vittor sent a baron with Wilhelm's Perth and a hundred warriors to [[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]], the capital of the Northwoods. He carried with him the old Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]], whom he declared to be the rightful ruler and kept a puppet at the doctor's with a supply of sedative drops; when the old man rushed from the tower, the baron caught him and tied him up. [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]]'s men in the castle he interrupted with a Perst, and with pigeons he sent word across the county, and Craig abandoned Desmond's ally at Waymar. At the walls of his own capital, Craig fired a volley of crossbows and sent a ram - the baron burned it with a Perst. For about a month, he led Craig by the nose, bargaining for a partition, and when the south of Northwood submitted to Vittor, he surrendered the Fang on Elias' behalf and left, taking the learned bear of Countess Northwood Maverick ("Actually, I saved him!"). For his loyalty, Vittor freed his estates from double submission: "Of all the vassal lords, one Baron Dorcaster came to the aid with full force."

### Lysias Dol and Lyde (Autumn 1775) ([[lid|Leed]])
In September, the baron overtook the army of the Chosen of Bald Dole, told his story and brawled on the lute under which Maverick danced. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) Along with [[fleming|Fleming]], he supported [[rikhard-oridzhin|Ice]]'s plan to turn against Craig, but Vittor chose to chase Desmond. In October, the count handed over the keys to the cell of captive [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] to the people, and in November, leaving for Adrian, he left the baron in Leed: “will provide supplies and cover our rear.” Dorcaster held the castle of the Lillydeans with the Perst and the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]], sterilised Iona, and had orders to kill her if the Leads broke into the castle. Martin dined with him every night and persuaded him to maim the prisoner, but the baron refused: the count ordered her to be kept intact.

When [[paul|Paul]]’s messengers [[chara|Chara]], Sormach, and Knuth came to him and demanded that the city be plundered, the baron showed him (‘We have two dozen of them!’) and then God’s Voice: ‘I can make any of your Rings disappear. with its owner.” The messengers left, but in the morning Martin stole the Voice of God from him and sold it to Leed Paul.

### Death
On the day of the rebellion, the baron stood on the doorstep of the donjon and asked Martin to come to his senses for the last time, recalling that the count had ordered Iona to be killed in the event of a riot; when the shootout began, he shouted with his own words: "Resign, do not shoot!" He had about fifty men left, and they besieged the tower where [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]'s men were lodged. But Iona, having subjugated Martin to the Dust of [[gvenda|Gwenda]], had already taken the Feather from him. Erwin ordered the killing of "only one of him." The first fireball tore the Baron's hand off with the Perst, and then "four flashes nailed Dorcaster to the ground", leaving only a pile of black bones. His soldiers were locked in a barn. Vittor, having learned of the theft of the Voice of God, only blithely chided "Uncle Marty"; he did not remember the baron himself.
