## Origins (from his own story)

The whole truth about himself Navi discovered only in the last chapter of the cycle, and before he talked about himself in different ways: Mick - that he is a sorcerer from [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], who passed through a faulty portal; Dorothy - that comes from the "world of the gods" and got to Polaris through the "magic portal"; Minerva - who came from the "underworld". The final version is like that.

Navi is one of the “gods” who became the inhabitants of the rest of the galaxy, eighteen centuries changed their nature. He belongs to the "bright", who relied on reason: the bright do not forget anything, count in advance and can not live without calculations. Together with [[paul|Paul]] ("dark") and Lilanne, he was married and went on an expedition to the neutron star; according to him, everything in the world of the gods is made "a penny", as in the game. Paul, for example, “played a space Marine”, although the real paratroopers disappeared ten centuries ago; Navi was the navigator of the same team. In search of an "interesting task", Navi paved the way for archaic neutrino beacons, and the ship crashed into Polaris, to which the false beacon, [[polari-world|the Star]], was leading. Lylanna died on impact, Navi was rescued by a catapult that fell into the Smoky Dahl, and Paul collapsed into hell. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) Navi thought Paul was dead, and so, as he himself admitted, he left him.

## The truth about the Star and the last choice

A few days later, during negotiations with Adrian in the First Winter, Minerva with the help of the Bird without the Plot showed the Star to the audience. Polaris lies in a dark gap between the arms of the galaxy; a lighthouse, shifted by saboteurs during an ancient war, has guided ships straight into the planet for centuries. The gifts of the gods are places of ruin, and the [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]] are the surviving wreckage of starships. Eighteen centuries ago a colonial ship crashed here; the surviving crew members became the [[pramateri|Foremothers]] and Forefathers, the colonists the commoners. Elena and Agatha insisted on not spending reactors on a distress signal, and Wilhelm and Janmei suppressed the colonists' revolts and raised the First Tree, making the continent habitable. ## Personality and Appearance

A tall, thin young man who looks about sixteen to eighteen and does not age for decades; when embarrassed, he looked even younger. He is helpless in everyday life, afraid of dogs and horses, cries, does not know how to fight or lie convincingly; he prefers to flee from danger. At the same time, he hates worship, is arrogant (“a stupid, dark, short-sighted woman,” he abandoned Dorothy) and translates everything into interest. Without calculations, books and "numbers" fall into "collapse" or "attack". Kind and self-rejected: even Karen, who had long considered him mad and “blessed,” eventually believed he was a god. For the sake of his goal, however, he is capable of a cold calculation: he knew in advance that Dorothy would find the body of his daughter, and led her to this, because this was how he reached a meeting with Minerva. He called himself the world’s greatest villain.
