## Who He Is

Gerion is a Southerner from a rich family. Ten years ago, his father gave him a “fairy-tale place” of an aide to King [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]. The service was easy: Gerion performed simple tasks, yelled at servants and looked after secretaries. In return, he feasted at the royal table, wore expensive outfits and received a generous salary. The adjutant was "young and good" and sometimes loved with royal violas. He lived for two years in a fairy tale and only dreamed of the king making him the first minister.

## In the Gate of Trust

### Service to the Prophet

Then the king visited the monastery of Maximian-u-Bezdna at the Boneless Failure, saw a prophetic dream and handed over the affairs to his son. ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) Most of the courtiers went to the court of Prince Hector. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) Gerion remained: he considered the king's decision another whim like the one when he "abolished the winter." Years passed, the prophet did not return to the throne, and Gerion buried his youth in a monastic cell. About five years ago, he asked me to take off his vassal oath. The Prophet replied that through Gerion the gods were apparently going to say something, and he would let him go when he heard their words. Since then, Gerion has repeatedly tried to say something that would pass for a revelation, and every time failed.

Meanwhile, he made money selling the secrets of the master to everyone. The first dream of the prophet, about the death of the world, Gerion interpreted in his own way: man for himself is the whole world, which means that the dream portends the death of the prophet himself.

About three months before his arrival at Nan Clair, the prophet left the monastery with seven companions, and Gerion was among them. He was the best dressed in the suite.

### In Nan Claire

At the gates of Nan Clair, the capital of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], a dirty beggar threw a lump of swamp fluid into the elegant Gerion. Gerion grabbed the dagger, but the prophet stopped him. Later in the alley, Gerion put a blade to the beggar's nostril and told him not to be seen again. In the hotel, he “tortured” the owners and servants so that the prophet would be served the best dinner, wine and feathers. At dinner he comforted the master with flowery parables, but the prophet knew that the gods did not speak through the mouth of Gerion. In the Cathedral of the Forefather Kristen, the prophet asked for his cloak to cover the statue, and under the cloak, rays appeared around the candle. Father Loris, the first "gate", missed Gerion along with the prophet.

### Corzo seeds

At night, Gerion met with a swamp poisoner nicknamed "the sting of the crybola." He immobilized him with a sticky hot glove and took him to a room hung with herbs. The poisoner sold Gerion three corzo seeds with the condition of not killing anyone at Darkwater. The seeds jump onto a target marked with two brown stripes and kill with poison in about a minute. Finally, the poisoner blinded Gerion with pollen and showed him the Pits - drains where rats devour anything that can rot. The next day, the same man, under the guise of a merchant, sold Gerion a pillow with two scarlet features, and Gerion recognized him only when he took it in his hands.

Before the visit to the castellan, Gerion himself washed and clothed the beggar: he got white stockings, lacquer shoes, blue bridges and black patterned camsol. He sewed the seeds under the lining of the galley. In the morning he placed two brown stripes on the prophet’s grey balahon. The idea was: at the right moment to rush across, to “save” the king, to prove that the dream foreshadowed his death, to return the prophet to the throne and become the first minister.

### Third Gate

At the trial of Lord Emmon, Darklin, Gerion refused to drink from both a bowl of water and a bowl of poison. The smoke of candles caused a throat spasm in everyone, and half-live Gerion at the last moment drank a tincture of lilan. So he walked through the third gate with the others. In the carriage that took the guests to the castle, Gerion noticed the beggar’s scars on his left forearm, and they shocked him.

### Disclosure

In the throne greenhouse Lady in Darkness, Gerion chose a lush rose - the "greatness and luxury" of the courtyard. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) Three “gates” described him without flattering words. Loris' father saw a "low-gold man", Admiral Groc - a spy. Emmon recognized him as a corrupt southerner, from whom he bought information about the prophet through intermediaries for eight years. When the beggar turned out to be a jester [[menson|Manson]], Gerion declared him a spy of the northerner and turned the prophet's back to the seeds.

The prophet then spoke of a secret order. Gerion was surprised that in eight years he had not heard anything about him, and rejoiced: since the prophet discovered such secrets, then he began to trust him. But the prophet explained that he purposely kept with him the “most near and greedy” of vassals and dictated to him only insignificant letters. His men were watching to see who Gerion was selling secrets to. One thread led to Emmon, the other to Prince Hector. The prophet gave Gerion to the queen to continue feeding the enemy lies. To Gerion's cry of freedom, the prophet replied that the vassal oath was not a contract.

Mentally cursing the “cruel hypocrite”, Gerion saw the seeds jump on the prophet’s back, and this time he did not throw himself across. The sting of Kribola took the seeds off: their poisonous pouches had long since been emptied by someone. By order of the Queen, the poisoner immobilized Gerion and took him out of the greenhouse. His fate, the court or the role of a fake informant, decides [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]. The narrator says goodbye to him, hoping that none of the readers "will miss him."
