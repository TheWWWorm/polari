## Biography

Moger Buckley is a short round-faced commoner from the Southern Way, a "faithful dog" and a "useful man" of Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]], whom he called the "master" behind his eyes. The master taught: “Desire is a valuable thing. “He who wants much is useful.” Buckley expressed his view of people with a proverb "from above or below": anyone can be approached either down, spit on his head, or crawl, on all fours. Sometimes there is one thing, sometimes another - but, according to his observation, no one is more despised than those who just stood on their knees.

## "The Useful People" (January 1775)

### The capital before the coronation

The "Useful People" interlude shows the world through Buckley's eyes. In Faunterra, preparing for the coronation, he lived with his partner, the thug Sixth, playing cards every day in the Pig and Goose Inn. The two of them kidnapped a small station informant of protection left without a supervisor after Backfield's escape, and bought station arrivals and departures logs from him for one hundred and forty ephemera; then Six killed the spy to get the money back. ([[protektsiya|The Protection]])

Under the guise of Saint Sophia the Great, Buckley appeared to Abbot Ferrain of the Church of the Forefathers and, by his evasive words, concluded that Almera herself was apparently negotiating with the Shavans and would be ready to act by spring. Prior [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] he considered a promising ally. At the Shimmery merchant Ira Medea, the cousin of Council of Five member Buckley, holding on cheekily and showing the bill, reached an agreement on the purchase of spark eyes and carried away an eye bracelet and a compact porcelain shot; the merchant explained to him the difference between "lily", "rose", "chrysanthemum" and a deadly "cypress".

### Murder of the Sixth

At Merchant's Downhill, Buckley's got a filly. Buckley loaded the "cypress" into the self-fire, and when the Sixth beat the spy, he let him go - and shot him in the back. The sixth one picked up an eye at the price of two hundred ephes and did not want to give it to Buckley, saying that it belonged to the owner. They played cards on this eye against Buckley's money, and the Sixth won - the king and ace against the two kings - then returned the stone, but announced that he was leaving: he was tired of the swagger and the lie of his partner. Buckley begged him to stay; The sixth agreed only to drink the "world" - and Buckley poisoned him.

### Council in Grace

With the information obtained, Buckley left the capital to the south, in Grace - a port city on the East Sea, where the Duke held advice. There he reported to the Duke, his daughter [[magda-labelin|Magda]], Baron Darryl, and the Marquess of Windley and Graceland: Almera negotiates with the Shavans, and the Council of Five is ready to break the contract with the Crown and sell up to fifty thousand eyes. The Duke decided to buy back the entire stock and sell it cheaply to the bandits, the Shavans and the monks of Almera - "the monks of Almera", Buckley inserted. Magda experienced a self-destruction on knightly armor, and Buckley enthusiastically picked up: “We will build a world without knights,” a world “mixed with money.”

Then he reported that the thief of the Bright Sphere is [[kharmon|Harmon Paula]]: according to the station magazine Buckley traced him to Marengo and the ship, and from there to Laam, where a certain "Khoram" bought a girl and a celestial ship at auction. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]; [[lajem|Laem]]) Buckley sailed to Shimmery with Magda on the flagship Great. At the stern, Magda rudely cut his flattery ("Do you love my ass?") by telling her what she was really interested in; they made a profit deal, and Buckley secretly plotted to appropriate almost all the profits from the saved million.

## "Janmei Hunt": Shimmery (March - April 1775)

### Hunting for Harmon

Sailing from the South Route to Shimmery took six weeks. In March 1775, on the high-speed corvette "The Dog on the Barrel", which had been lent for the cause by Baron Greyhound, a vassal of the Marquis of Greysend, Buckley and his four-man ahead of the squadron and arrived at Laam three days before the arrival of the Labelin fleet. During the voyage, he asked Magda for a corvette, letters of recommendation and the right to choose four people - Seven, Eight, Nine and Dave - each bribed by his weakness; Baron Darryl once beat him and threw him overboard. In Laem, Buckley questioned the drivers and water carriers, found a record in the tea house about the purchase of [[niza|Niza]] shavanka by "Horam Pauline Roberta" and knocked out information about Rico's matchmaker from the merchants. Deputy Halintor warned him that Magda's patronage in Shimmery was weak.

Magda informed him that his plan to buy up Shimmer eyes had failed, and ordered the hiring of "broad-sex" assassins against Erwin; at her sign, Buckley was pushed headlong into the pool water, and then she threatened to drown him or sell him into slavery if he failed in a week. That same night, Rodrigo, a broad-brimmed man, came to him with a knife and offered him services. On his cue, Buckley's men broke into Vanessa-Lilit's house, the wives of Onorico-Meysor's matchmaker, beat (apparently to death) an old servant and waited for Rico to come with a gift for Spring Break. ([[onoriko|Onoriko-Maysor]]) So Buckley went out to Harmon's estate in Melison. There, on his orders, Rodrigo cut Rico's throat in front of his wife and children for fifteen ephesas, and they demanded money from Harmon: Rodrigo was owed one thousand five hundred and fifteen ephesas (one and a half thousand and fifteen for Rico), Buckley ordered to give the rest to himself. Buckley wasn't interested; he thought she was cursed.

### "Grace's Pride"

At the same time, Buckley conceived of seizing two million ephes of gold hidden in water barrels at the Grace's Pride galleon, and checked the mystery by posing as Inspector Magda; Lord Captain Courtney Benefit threw him overboard. The captives - Harmon, Vanessa and her children - he kept in a rented basement. Sheriff's Deputy Halintor tried to arrest him in a port tavern; in the fight, Dave remained to cover the escape. Rodrigo recruited seventy pirates on the old ship "Liana", and at night they attacked the galleon, showering his team with acid from the ballistics, - but knights in patches came out of the stern cabin: Rodrigo gave Buckley to Magde in advance and jumped overboard. Buckles caught; Magda ordered the Ninth to be killed, and two other underlings were later hung on hooks on the Velichava mast. Buckley Magda himself sent in chains to her father on the "Dog on a barrel", noticing that he would skin him.

### Return to the capital
Magda didn't kill Buckley. Her father sent an angry letter: in the Southern Way, a peasant uprising arose, ready to spread to the Lands of the Crown, and the Duke wanted to arm the rebels with a spark so that "with men's hands he could pinch the kairs" - and the Shimmer's eyes still remained in Shimmery. Magda ordered Buckley to be sent on a "dog on a barrel" by the fastest move to [[faunterra|Faunterra]], chained to the mast, so that he would personally answer the Duke, and handed a letter to the captain describing all the events in Laeme. So in the spring of 1775, Buckley again found himself in the capital, at the disposal of Maurice Labelin, who decided to use it for the last time, and then get rid of it.

## "A Doll on the Throne" (Spring 1775)

By order of the Duke of Labelin Buckley had to pass spark eyes to the rebellious peasants, and then go with blackmail to the mistress. The Duke's men were watching him everywhere, apparently going to kill him after the case.

### Podsnezhnikov

As the [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]]’ thirty-thousand-strong army passed Riverbend and advanced across the Crownlands towards the capital, Buckley and the bruiser Seventh came to their camp on behalf of unnamed “powerful masters”, brought their leader [[salem|Salem]] a small chest of spark crossbows, and promised a thousand spark spears and a thousand crossbows at the Holy Field. When he clicked the hook of the tube, [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]'s sword stuck in his chest, and the sword of the Seventh - in Joe's chest. Buckley tried to set Salem up against the Lord Chancellor, but he refused to consider Erwin an enemy and promised only to talk to the lord and Origin; the chest with the self-shooters remained with the Snowdrops.

### Trade with Minerva

Buckley then appeared at [[minerva|Minerva]]'s on the eve of the confrontation on the Holy Field. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) He appealed to General Alexis's staff for an urgent audience, and the on-duty scarlet staff officer, along with Captain Shatterhand, reported him to the Lady, handing over a note signed by Magda Labelin; the secretaries of the Duke of Maurice identified her handwriting, and Mira, intrigued, told him to bring it in. He boasted that he himself gave the peasants a “holy dozen” of sparked self-shooters, and revealed Labelin’s plan: if the peasants with a spark kill a couple of thousand kire, the kire will cut out all the rebels in response. In return, he offered to buy the lives of eighty thousand peasants ten ephes per head: for this, the Labelins will not give the rebels weapons, and Magda will get Erwin's spring Chamber to be displaced for beating the unarmed. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) As proof, he sprinkled Captain Shatterhand in the palm of his hand with a handful of red eyes. Minerva, disgusted with him, wrote a bill for eighty thousand ephesas as a deposit, promising the rest only when accurately executed, and said that she did not trust him personally. Salem later complained to the Empress that Buckley had deceived them and did not give them the promised weapons, and Minerva herself realized that Shimmery had sold thousands of eyes to the Labelins and Buckley bypassing the contract with the Dynasty.

The Duke of Buckley managed to escape from the spies only in the palace; soon after, he was in the hands of the Origins.

## "Janmei Hunt": Death (May 1775)

### Erwin's prison

In Faunterra, Buckley found himself in the hands of [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], who ordered him to learn everything "at any cost". He was tortured by [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]'s kair; Buckley lied a lot and changed his testimony, but for the last three days he kept saying the same thing. By mid-May, in the days of the spring Chamber, the prisoner lost his leg: after the torture of the knee cup, the wound inflamed, and the leg had to be taken away. On May 15—16, Erwin led the Duke of Labelin into prison and showed him a mutilated prisoner with only one eye open, and Buckley said that the Duke of Labelin had ordered him to give the eyes to the Snowdrops, he gave Salem a shot, and then went to blackmail Minerva. Erwin offered the Duke a deal: all about the Light Sphere in exchange for the right to kill Buckley. Erwin also told the duke that he had let Buckley in: according to the prisoner, Labelin's men followed him everywhere to kill him after the case. Labelin said that the Sphere he bought was a fake, assembled from several small items, and that Buckley could confirm this. After listening to the story, Erwin allowed, and Maurice Labelin, to his surprise, did not call the kair, and he took a long knife from the table and stabbed the prisoner in the chest. The fear of death lingered on Buckley's face.

Later, Magda reminded her father: “Moger Buckley wanted to rob me — and died,” and Maurice himself, according to her, said that before Buckley’s death, he “grew up like a jelly,” and it was pleasant.

## In "Fantasies"

In the story "Amicable" from "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]", which the author calls the possible fates of the heroes, [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] told the widow of Onoriko-Maisor Vanessa-Lilit: her husband was killed by Moger Buckley, he also threw her and children into a pit, and Captain [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] "detained Moger Buckley and subjected him to cruel torture, as a result of which the bastards died." This is a version of Jemis: in fact, Buckley was captured by Magda's men, and stabbed by the Duke of Labelin. Vanessa replied that she was glad that "this villain flew to the Star."

## Personality

Passionate with the strong and cruel with the weak, greedy, cunning and cowardly. Buckley perfectly found the weaknesses of people - his assistants he picked up precisely on their weaknesses - and knew how to creep, then to push. He easily betrayed both his partners and owners: he poisoned the Sixth, deceived the Snowdrops, tried to rob Magda. Until the latter, he remained in the grip of animal fear of death: Erwin noticed that nothing remained of the prisoner, “and fear is alive.”

> “Boy or girl? Girl or boy? Just answer and then lie.”
