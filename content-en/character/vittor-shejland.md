## Overview
Vittor Kaylin Agna of the Vivian family is the Earl of Shayland, ruler of a narrow strip of land along the [[torrej|Torrey]] River, master of [[uejmar|Waymar]], and a network of banks spread throughout the north of the empire. His grandfather was a merchant and banker and was born on a ship. His father, [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent]], married the daughter of the old count. Therefore, Vittor is called a graph “in the second generation”, and the nobleman does not take him seriously. Duke Erwin, arguing with his father about his sister's marriage, emphatically called him "Banker Shayland". The merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] imagined that the count owned eight cities, thirteen castles, a navigable river, a thousand square miles of fertile land, and a network of banks “bringing in an unknown darkness of income.”

For most of the heroes, Vittor remained a good-natured, ironic and soft person for a long time. He supported [[adrian|Adrian]]'s rail reforms and was his financial advisor. Only in 1775 it turned out that he was the Puppeteer: the conspirator who held in his hands the Ring of Wilhelm and Ulyanina Dust. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]; [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]])

## Appearance
Since birth, Vittor has unnaturally white skin, “like a feather of a seagull.” In the portrait in The [[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]], which [[minerva|Mira]] looked at in 1774, the count looked like a young man of thirty with curly chestnut hair, a sharp nose and a chin. Myra has only seen her sister-in-law twice. When she was seven years old, she squealed at the first meeting with the count, mistaking him for a revived skeleton. Vittor smiled broadly and handed her palms: “Here, baby, touch — I’m warm, not dead.” His hands were warm and soft. When the sailors on the way to Shayland read aloud the same issue of The Voice of the Crown, [[kharmon|Harmon]] noticed that the portrait does not show the main thing: “the count is white as snow.” His enemies called him "the white-faced." Having become the Chosen One, he wore armor from Sacred Objects, and the Absolute clothed him like a seamless silver cloth.

### Father and family
Count Vincent Shayland told his sons that their youth was not like that of the firstborn. The great-grandfather fled from Halliwell when the count there decided to take the money from the merchants. Grandfather made a huge fortune with his mind, became the richest man in Shayland, bought county debt notes, so that the Great House of Shayland “actually became his”. The old earls were forced to marry Vincent their daughter, but even then did not sit with them at the same table. In order for the vassals to fulfill the oath, the grandfather had to bribe half of them, and intimidate the rest, and keep the Northwood regiment in Waymar for years. Vincent’s motto was “Use what you know, sell what you have.” He punished his sons with stone tongs. The mother, according to the memoirs of Vittor, called the younger, Martin, a “rat bastard”, and then completely abandoned the family. The father allowed his sons to choose bodyguards. Martin took the strongman Sam, and Vittor took Donald, nicknamed Donnell, who could read lips.

In Shayland came the 17th Gift of the Gods. The [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]] took him away from the sunsets, and Emperor Telurian with Prince [[adrian|Adrian]], [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] with his son [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]], [[ajden-almera|Aiden]] and [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]], the Archimandrite of Ellin, [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] with his young wife [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] came to Waymar. ([[ellina|Ellina]]) Vittor flattered the guests: he ordered to hang the Origin banners everywhere and placed a young Richard, who served as a warm to Colonel [[blekberri|Blackberry]]. On the old lighthouse, Richard admitted that his father had forbidden him to stick his nose into the secret of Dar's lodge, and made a promise to Vittor to share the secret if he recognized him. When his father went with the emperor to bed, Vittor received in Waymar the archimandrite, Bishop Gallard and late Northwoods and arranged for the newlyweds Elias and Sybil a feast with dancing. Through Donnell, he learned that Aiden had once advised Telurian to abolish the House of Shayland, and Dar had inclined the emperor in favor of the Shaylands. That same evening, Martin attempted to rape a novice from the retinue of the Archimatrix. Vittor told his brother to be humiliated and promise her anything, just to keep silent, and he arranged for them to be put in one carriage.

At night, Vittor, with his brother, bodyguards and a novice, went down to the bed. The guide was a wild man who was found by the Jaegers. When the Lords left, Vittor exclaimed, "Martin, it worked! Holy Gods, we are saved! I'm a fucking genius!" Here, a burnt savage crawled out of the pit - the future [[paul|Paul]]. ### Prisoner
According to the jailer Lard, after the departure of the guests, an unnamed prisoner appeared in the castle. For about a year he was kept in the tower as a noble, then transferred to prison and tortured for several years in a row, walled in an earthen hole of the lower circle. Count Vincent never conquered him and was afraid of this man. The prisoner "run" after more than ten years of imprisonment, killing the jailers Kilmer and Hay, and in Waymar they spoke of him as a servant of [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]. According to the records of the caretaker [[master-sajrus|Cyrus]] and Lard, a week after the escape, Count Vincent died in his office, locked from the inside with a key. At the time of his death, Vittor was conversing with a priest in a temple built with Vincent's money, and an adjutant with the news rushed there. So around 1770, Vittor became a count.

According to Erwin's account of Cordelia's mother, Vittor offered the prisoner a deal: a secret in exchange for freedom. The prisoner also demanded revenge on the old count. Vittor let him out, hid him in his quarters, and allowed him to appear to his father: Vincent had a weak heart, and one appearance of a prisoner on a hot night pushed him to the Star. The former prisoner became the right hand of the count under the name Paul. Vittor owed his power over the [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects]] to Paul's primal blood. ([[pervokrov|Firstblood]]) From Paul he demanded power over the Objects, invulnerability and immortality.

On May 8, 1774, merchant Harmon arrived in Waymar at the invitation of castellan [[garold-greta|Harold]]. He told his father’s parable that before deciding, you need to sit down and eat, and gave another father’s instruction:

([[lzhe-sfera]]) To Harmon, the count seemed like a clever man who used to control people "not with the cold arrogance of the original, but with a cheerful light in his eyes." The count adored his wife, and this was evident in every glance.

On June 2, 1774, Vittor and Iona were at a summer ball in Faunterra. The Count introduced the wife of "Gloria Northwood", under whose name his vassal Mira was hiding, already as Countess Shayland, and the emperor congratulated the couple on their wedding. At the end of August 1774, the count, dressed "with a needle", followed his wife into the Duke's bedroom, where they brought [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] back from the Beyond. Along with Earl Lilliday and Baron [[stetkhem|Statham]], Vittor witnessed Desmond's son being called by the reigning duke. When Erwin spoke about the Lodge of Dar and the soldiers who served “Lord Adrian”, Vittor was the first to come to his senses and support him: “The Lord is uncompromising and firm. ## In "Only One Star"

After lunch, Erwin asked his brother-in-law why he had not left, and asked him to return to Waymar, report everything to the emperor and thereby save himself and Iona. Vittor refused: Iona, according to him, would never agree to stand aside, and he assured that he “also did not like what Adrian did,” and offered a way to attract one of the Great Houses to Erwin’s side. He only asked for the trust of Origin. His “soul” the count himself told about this plan. So the count joined the Origins, but played a double game. He came to the cell to see the prisoner [[mark-voron|Mark]] and threw off the mask of courtesy: he grabbed the prisoner by the neck and demanded to tell how to beg Adrian's forgiveness for himself and Iona. “When Erwin is pulled up in the Square of the Forefathers, I do not want my wife to be in the next loop.” Mark named a valuable vassal count, imprisoned without his consent in the monastery of Ulyana Sad in the west of Almera. On September 10, Erwin announced that he wanted to see Vittor with Iona in Waymar, and gave his sister forty swords. The couple went home by carriage to the northern port, by sea to the Predesya and further along the river. In early October, the Count's men, led by Inji Price, took her out of [[minerva|Minerva]] Monastery and brought her to Waymar as an honorary prisoner. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]])

### Minerva in Waymar
At Waymar the earl returned around noon with Iona, Martin, the secretary, and a dozen and a half knights. The Northwoods had been waiting for him in the castle for ten days. Mira noted that the count constantly lies to her, but she lies charmingly. In the audience, he explained to the prisoner her position:

The Count said that he loved Iona, his native land and life. Waymar, he said, was burned to the ground three times, and he will do anything to prevent a fourth time. The letter of Myra to the emperor was intercepted, but the count himself ordered the scribe to send it and sent Adrian his letter of the same content, "a point in our favor" in case of the victory of the lord. Vittor then left the castle for three weeks on business; from this trip he brought three new horses, including a toy mare, in which Iona saw a mysterious sign. Martin left with him, but soon returned. While he was gone, Martin kidnapped Mira, and Iona locked Martin up. The Earl also held the old Elias Northwood with a secretary.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
Returning to Waymar, Vittor learned that Iona had thrown Martin into the lower circle of the dungeon and released Minerva without consulting him. He said, “This is my castle, my land, my brother.” Soon came the news of the defeat of the Origins at [[bitva-pri-piksi|Pixie]]. The count found Erwin defeated and decided to appease Adrian: Minerva could not "give" him, so he began to prepare detachments to help the lord. Iona asked if he had ordered Martin to kill, and the count came out and said, “By the Virgin Mary Vivian.” In the morning came the bird about the capture of the capital by Erwin, and letters immediately flew from the castle to Labelin and Faunterra. In front of his wife, Vittor explained the throwing by trying to save her by sacrificing pride. Iona refused to test her husband with Martin's elixir, as Seymour advised. Vittor explained to his wife that his brother had “fooled his mind” and had already summoned the best doctors. In early 1775, Iona left for Faunterra with her brother, and the count remained in Waymar. He referred to important cases and to the wrath of the empress, who sent a dove demanding Martin's extradition to trial, but Erwin felt that "Shayland is afraid of us." Later, when Iona in the capital told her brother about Martin and the quarrel with her husband, Erwin did not support her. Vittor, he explained, is a talented financier and at the same time a lord, a man “unique in economics”. Under Adrian, he looked after the Minister of Taxes, the Treasurer and the tax collectors and pointed out to the lord everything suspicious. Erwin told Iona to keep the union with the Shaylands and love her husband.

In winter Iona lived with her brother in the capital. Whittor sent her a letter on seven sheets. Instead of the main thing, he described the winter Waymar, the sleepy way through the frozen Smokey Dahl, Jane's health, the troubles of the castellan and new horses. Only in the end did he admit: “I miss you unbearably, my soul.” Iona dropped a tear on the paper: her husband seemed to her the best person in the world.

## In "Humans and Gods"
### Siege
Iona's letter and the report of the surviving kair reached Erwin on May 29. Soon the whole empire learned that the Puppeteer was Vittor Shayland. Erwin blocked Waymar and arrested Shayland representatives and bank employees. In the capital, the townspeople smashed the Shayland banks.

Vittor contacted Paul through a milk-white bracelet, and once through him he spoke to Erwin himself. He threatened him with hard labor on oars or a slow execution. The Count treated the wounded with a healing Object, although he was troubled by blood and once he unsuccessfully fused a man's throat. He told the city elders how he had been betrayed by his wife, and ordered that the pillars along the moat be put up and the naked bodies of the twelve kairs be hung on them "as an admonition to the townspeople"; the two diggers then escaped from the garrison. On the third morning, [[perkins|Perkins]], an inconspicuous man with round glasses, came to the castle, looking like a clerk, and then Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]]. The baron from the threshold attacked the count with a sword, Joaquin disarmed him, and it turned out that the attack was a rigged check of the new guard. The count then took Joaquin's consent to kill on his orders, gave him the blue pill, after which the count could kill him by saying only two words, and watered him with his firstblood. He tried to collect [[absolyut|the Absolute]] from the Objects, but it did not work. Richard brought him a Drop of Sun from Paul. ([[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]) [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]] ("Ice"), the sunsets of General Horis and Count [[fleming|Fleming]] sided with the Count.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

The northerners themselves lost only about five hundred people in Waymar, and the city lay in ruins.

Paul survived. The objects of the Absolute on Vittor's body melted, wrapped the corpse, and the count appeared nearby alive and unharmed. So he became immortal, learned to instantly move in space and declared himself the Chosen One.

Before that, he explained to the new knight:

### Hiking to the north
Wittor marched through Northwood to the Origin. With the army, he collected adopted children, one from each city - his "bouquet". The children called him Uncle Wit and played hide and seek with him. He ruled by fear, double taxation, and cunning, pitting Richard and Paul against each other. When the generals argued, he declared that “it would be wise to ask the gods for advice”, disappeared for a few minutes and returned with a decision.

In September 1775, the Chosen One's army marched unopposed through Northwood County: the Knights of Northwood went after [[krejg-nortvud|Craig]] to storm their capital, and the Kairs of Desmond avoided battles. Baron Dorcaster returned to the army. He told his brother and Joaquin to find two living northerners, kill them and throw them into the dead woman's room so that the Almeran knights would take revenge on Desmond's scouts. ## Relationships
### Iona
The marriage began as a deal, but Wittor was reputedly adored by the servants and Harmon. Iona admitted that he hardly loves him. The younger brother always recognized Vittor first and was loyal to him. Vittor protected him from his youth and covered his misdeeds. Martin believed that his brother deliberately hid from him the secret of immortality, because immortality was given by the Absolute, not the elixir. Paul was the source of Vittor's strength, ally and rival. The Count freed him, and Paul gave him the firstblood and the Absolute. Paul promised a test, but admitted that Alanis killed the count. Later, Vittor kept him in chains and pitted with Richard.
