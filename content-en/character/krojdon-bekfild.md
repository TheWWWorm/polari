## Biography
### Captain of the Scarlet Guard
In the summer of 1774, the captain of the scarlet guard, Sir Croydon Backfield, commanded a company in the palace and feuded with [[mark-voron|Mark]], the Crown Raven. He assured [[adrian|Adrian]] that the protectorate was a "useless pack of jackals" and that the entire security of the Crown should be entrusted to the guards. ([[protektsiya|The Protection]]) One day he broke into the kitchen to summon Mark to a fight. Mark Red's servant spat at him as if the Raven had named the dog Lucia, the mother of the Backfield family. Mark cleverly prompted the guard to take under the protection of the printing house "Voices of the Crown". The captain seized the opportunity to stand out, and Mark needed it. At a celebration in honor of the "wave" machine, Backfield cordoned off the garden with his men and triumphantly whispered to the Crow that he himself, without a "praised secret guard", had set up a murder motive in the printing house: the de criminals stole the trial pages of the Voice to undermine confidence in the imperial news. Mark responded by mocking the "ink immunity". In training, the lord fenced with Backfield and listened to his reports. Adrian considered the captain to be "not far away" and his motive was "an attempt to curry favor."

### In "Only One Star"
When in September 1774 Adrian sent Mark to the First Winter, his agent took up Backfield: the coordinators were reassigned, the passwords were changed. Backfield led the defense. He reported to Vladyka that the agents of the Origins were “exterminated at the root”: thirty-six spies were taken, pigeons were confiscated. Adrian explained to him that the rebel must see what the ruler wants. Backfield was collecting charges against [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] and [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria Northwood]]. He also acted as prosecutor at a closed session of the Supreme Court on December 10, 1774, sentencing Minerva and Erwin in absentia. Francis-Ilianus later pointed out that Backfield’s authority was not confirmed by any document signed by the lord. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]])

### Siege of the Palace
In December 1774, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] suddenly seized the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]. Major Backfield, led by a secret guard and a battalion of scarlet guards, besieged him and introduced himself from the side of the bridge:
> “I am called the Red Sickle: I make enemies of the emperor like a peasant makes ears.”
The first attack he led directly over the bridge, hastily and without siege vehicles. Then he stormed the Holy Citadel, and later staged false attacks on the Air Bridge and sorties on the ice of Hanoi. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) One day, five of his brave men with spark weapons crawled under the bridge and almost lowered it. He shouted to Erwin: “In summer he was a captain, now a major, and with you I will become a colonel.”

Backfield had no good fighters, and he threw everything he found into the fight. He mobilized the police, receiving a decree from the Supreme Court, as well as jailers, bandits, convicts and the city shval. His mercenaries robbed the refugees under the guise of "fortification". Backfield executed prisoners taken at the Citadel and threw their bodies at the island, and then the rotting corpses of people and animals, "to lower morale and to create disease." The arrows of his soldiers were smeared in impurity. He exposed the captive northerners naked on the pillars at the bridge, where they froze. Later, [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] accused him of freezing prisoners with ice water and charging parts of the bodies of kairs into stonemasons. Later, the Kairs, beating him, called the names of the fallen at the siege - [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon Origin]], [[brant-stil|Brant Steel]] and Derek Whitehall. He was hated “both by his own and strangers”: Minerva said that the major “robbed his own city”, extracting ammunition, provisions, cars and shells.

[[minerva|Minerva]], arriving in the capital, listened to his praise: of the six hundred northerners, two hundred and twenty survived. She asked him what he boasted about: that in three weeks with a huge majority he did not take the palace, or that he armed beggars, drunkards and bandits? Backfield was tasked with taking the Gauntlet of Power from Mercy Church by force. Erwin, in his world conditions, demanded that “the remains of the bastards of Backfield” return to hard labor, and he himself “died on the spot — he bored me.”

One night, before the fall of the capital, Backfield led Sybil Northwood out of the casemate and took away in a closed crew. The countess decided that she was being taken to the gallows and asked about her daughter. He laughed, "Honey, I don't know anything about Gloria, and there's only one thing about you: your name is Dorothy Sly, you're my cousin." Four days later, Sybil was admitted to a hospital on Faraday Riley Island. ([[ostrov-faradeya-rajli|Faraday-Riley Island]]) Later, while in custody, Backfield boasted to Minerva that he raped the countess three times and she lost the child, but when the empress threatened him, he immediately admitted that he was lying.

### "The Useful People" and "A Doll on the Throne"
After the victory of Origin, Backfield fled from the capital, the top of the defense collapsed. His agents fled with him, and Sybil Minerva never found him. In the winter, Backfield wandered around and thought of running to Shimmery or the Volta. Neither with Minerva, nor with the northerners, nor with Gallard, he failed to agree on his "commodity". Then he joined the captain of the azure guard [[uitmor|Whitmore]], who “lost the property of the Dynasty.” For about two months, they wandered around Almera in search of stolen items. Their squad in blue coats, the locals called "terrible blue gang".

They were based at Whitmore's cousin's estate in Almera. The agents of the parish learned that Major Backfield was staying there in the company of several combat comrades, but did not associate him with the "blue gang": the major, according to him, distracted them with an "forbidden offeree" to his eminence. In late March 1775, Whitmore's men piled up rails with logs and stopped a train on the Alleridan-Orwell branch, in which [[mark-voron|Mark]] and Grandpa were carrying the captured Inge Price, and took the captives to the estate. ([[ded|Grandfather]]; [[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) Backfield laughed at the Raven, who mistook him for the Lord of the Rings: “I always said: you are the worst detective!” He himself was sure that the thief was the Origin: the Earl of Arrowback, they say, was the "Origin's dog", and the Objects were hidden in his castle. Finding evidence, Backfield was going to notify all the Great Houses so that the Duke would come to an end. He mocked Mark's request for coffee and a bedroom and ridiculed Price's demand to release the "runaway convict". Mark noted that Whitmore always had the last word, although he was younger in rank: the squad consisted of the captain's azure guardsmen, not the scarlet major.

### “The ones we forgive.”
In May 1775, at Marengo, in the Palace of Silence, Backfield and Whitmore reported to Minerva about the Abductors of the Objects and the Absolute. Whitmore and Raven vowed to protect the witnesses, and Backfield "only nodded." He named them Inji Price and Baby G, and they were thrown into prison. He then asked for the rank of colonel and reconciliation with Erwin. Minerva recalled that he killed several dear vassals of the Duke, including his beloved cousin. Backfield replied, “War is understandable for a man.”

The main “gift” he saved for Origin. The caretaker of the pigeonhouse, his debtor Birdman, at the behest of the major, responded to letters from the hospital (according to Minerva, the major intercepted them for six months; in Marengo it was about two January letters and one February one), sealing the answers with the coat of arms. Barely took back these letters, in the corridor of the palace he was blocked by Shutterhand. ([[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]]) The captain said that Foremother Lucia "slept with the hoops", and Backfield rushed to chop. Shatterhand deliberately chose a cramped place: the major's blade was stuck in the plaster, the captain pierced it and took the letters. They've revealed Minerva's fate to Sybil. A palace doctor came out of injured Backfield.

### In the "Yanmei Hunt": for the Countess to the island
Minerva still gave him a chance and promised patronage and the position of head of protection if he brought the Countess of Northwood. On May 18, 1775, a major in a shining scarlet uniform appeared in a hospital on Faraday Riley Island as the "cousin" of Dorothy Sly's patient, Major Sly. ([[ostrov-faradeya-rajli|Faraday-Riley Island]]) He burst into Master Mullin's office, shouting, "Get ready, I've found a buyer! I mean, the buyer, he-he," and the memoryless Sybil felt at the sight of him unconscious horror. In private, the major persuaded the master to write out his “cousin”. Since the ship was leaving only the next day, he agreed to leave her in the hospital for another night, finally hitting "either on the shoulder or on the chest." Sybil's neighbor [[karen|Karen]] warned her that the major was taking her to be executed. That same night, [[navi|Navi]] took both women off the island on a boat. In the morning, the schooner of the major went in pursuit and caught up with the Shimmerian "White Panther", who picked up the fugitives. Flags from her said that three dangerous fugitives were on board, and promised a reward immediately upon meeting, but Captain Lu-Aramon left the chase.

### In "Humans and Gods"
Backfield's schooner came about three hours later than the White Panther. The fugitives deliberately bought train tickets to Arden, and they themselves hid in the port hotel "Fresh Breeze" and saw from the window how the major and the doctor of the hospital Finger descended from the schooner. After interviewing the cab drivers, Backfield rushed to the station and set off on a false trail to Arden. Meanwhile, at the turn of May and June, Minerva ordered Beckfield to be found "wherever he is" and called to the capital, but he was never found. Agents of protection now obeyed Mark, and only two or three underlings helped the major. At Arden, he set a trap at the Farewell Temple and then ambushed the fugitives' hotel, but Navi called in a wounded Cyre Reynolds from the X Battalion in advance. Kair beat the Red Sickle, sentencing the names of those killed in the siege. Navi knew it was Backfield who kidnapped [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]] in Arden. Under Dorothy's knife, the major triumphantly answered a question about her. Gloria was executed, and her body was hidden at the temple of Farewell, near the tomb of the lords. Dorothy cried out, “You liar!” but she knew he wasn’t lying and wanted to see for herself. Then at the temple, Farewell, according to Mark, Backfield served as a "brute force" to destroy the gazebo, and Mark arrested him.

The raven brought the major to Minerva in chains. Backfield demanded to recognize the arrest as illegal and reminded of the “contract” with the mistress. Robert Origin listed his war crimes and demanded a trial on behalf of the Great House Origin. Minerva accused him of war crimes, deception of the empress and kidnapping and ordered him to be tried.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree
While the investigation was going on, Backfield was sitting in the Citadel's dungeon and, hoping to mitigate the sentence, told everything about Sybil. When Adrian returned to the capital in August 1775, he was informed of the prisoner, and the lord released the major and returned protection to him. The secret guard scoured Faunterra in search of Nathaniel under the guise of searching for the wife of a joker and fought with the young men of Dodge's Hands for the right to "more love Adrian." Backfield offered to arrest all the untrustworthy, but Dodge challenged him.

Backfield grabbed Sybil and took her to Adrian bound and intoxicated. Pardon Minerva, found at her, he gave for the "table of horses at the races." Karen called him, at all times, "a creep and a thief" imprisoned by Minerva for dishonor. Backfield slapped Sybil on the cheeks and twisted her fingers to make her feel better, and then, on orders, he shut her mouth. Adrian ordered the prisoner to be hanged. Leaving for the First Winter, Vladika made Backfield a colonel and left to defend [[faunterra|Faunterra]] with a spark battalion, five thousand "young men" and one bearer of the Perst. “I serve Peru and the Sword!” shouted Backfield. The stones, which he hastily made in the winter, were almost the only ones in the army of the Crown.

### The Case of Professor Ollie
In March 1776, Colonel Backfield, in a gilded uniform, still led the secret guard of Adrian, who became the mayor of Faunterra. Together with [[ruka-dodzh|Hand Dodge]], he reported to the lord about his moods. Backfield reported that things were bad in the North, and in the eastern Almera Minerva was impressed by many: she flew there on a ship and sent troops to Back to protect against Shavans. The common people of the rest of the lands, according to him, do not know about it and consider Origin a villain, and Minerva - "his litter." Adrian assigned both to supervise Professor [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]] and his laboratory. Adrian's white-masked secretary, [[itan|Ethan]], was also advised to keep an eye on Ollie and his student Alice Cavendish, and Backfield replied, "Thank you. I'll take that into consideration."

When the professor was robbed of a notebook with the diagrams of the recording device, Backfield with Dodge's people conducted an "investigation". He looked through the scientist’s personal papers and found a portrait of Minerva and an invitation to her ball in the bottom drawer. Dodge accused the watchman of serving Minerva, but Backfield agreed with the professor that an illiterate watchman would not have found the right paper. The colonel threw to the doorkeeper Eugene: “I know your birth...” The gray-bearded Eugene, "either a general's coachman or a retired robber", with the "funny" nickname, was by all accounts Inge Price, whom Backfield himself had betrayed to Minerva in Marengo. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) The colonel never recognized him. Later, the professor mentally called Backfield "cretin": the Colonel did not notice the main thing in that box.

Later, the agents turned the lab upside down. Eugene framed Doju's leg and then told the students, "Brothers, window..." Gromil was thrown out the window, and Backfield was surrounded and demanded to surrender the sword: the university obeys only to the mistress. The colonel held out for half a minute, threw the sword on the floor and left, pulling his head into his shoulders. One of the students broke the sword with a kick and threw the handle out the window, and the wreckage of the blade the students hung on the wall as a trophy.

### Birdwatching
By the summer of 1776, Backfield was in charge of the one-armed Shavan Golden Grove, which ran the Bird in the Pera and Sword Palace. ([[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]]) Shavan called him "Ganta Backfield." The colonel flattered him: “You are Adrian’s first blade. You will know nothing of rejection.” The Shavan did not allow to fight: “You are already in battle, horseman.” On his orders, the Bird searched for enemies in the city, and two of Backfield's men recorded everything she saw: secret meetings, weapons, seals, even pigeon mail tapes. According to these reports, the guys smashed caches and seized weapons and letters. When by the summer there were no enemies in Faunterra, the Bird began to monitor the distant lands.

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In November 1776, during the siege of the House, Backfield sat on Adrian's left hand on his war council along with General Joseph Gore, Baron Hugo Darryl, and the legate of the Wilhelm fraternity. Adrian called him "Colonel", and Golden Grive - "Ganty". Backfield admitted his negligence: Minerva persuaded the masters of the guilds to spoil the lines of the railway, and he “did not find an excuse to interfere with it,” and promised to punish the perpetrators. He supported Horus: the fortified knights of the lords are not crushed, the young men are “liquid”, and first you need to hit the scarlet guard. Before the assault, he stood next to the Lord.

## Personality and Appearance
Backfield is blushing, blushing, and loves guard uniforms, swords, and officer jokes. He is vain, self-assured, and painfully proud of his descent from Pramateri Lucia: an insult to Pramateri drives him mad instantly. He is not stupid in small things, but in a serious matter not far away: Mark considered him “the worst detective”, and Adrian is a man who only wants to look good. Backfield is unprincipled and cruel, lies without literally breaking the word, and despises "small bastards", although he uses them. At the same time, he is invariably loyal to Adrian and stubborn: he did not have good fighters, but he knew how to exhaust the northerners.
