## Biography

### Fort Beyond the River

Gwenda was one of two prisoners taken in the autumn of 1774 by the expedition of Count [[fleming|Fleming]] from the mysterious fort to the Beyond (the second was [[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]]). Kyr [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] found her in a hay barn, a thin, dark-haired woman in a sheepskin. She asked who he was, said that the stranger "smells differently," and asked if he had come to kill her; after hearing "not you," she took his hand with both palms and never stepped away from him again. Inside the fort, she insisted that the place was bad and should go; when the count's men entered, the fort disappeared without a trace, and Gwenda only noticed that she had warned.

During the interrogation with [[mark-voron|Mark]], she could not remember anything but her own name - neither her hometown, nor her class, nor her parents; Mark suggested that she wanted to forget the fort so much that she forgot everything else. She spoke of the owners of the fort as “twilight people”, “special”, called the fort a “trap” and claimed that there was no one else behind [[reka|the River]]: the twilight people left, the rest disappeared, “now only a pit”. She also called those who were on the ships “strange” and twilight people — Abbot Hoch, the Count himself and Khair Hedin “with a wart” — and warned Mark that Jemis and the Raven himself were in danger of death. She spoke of herself sometimes in the third person and in short fragments of phrases, as if she had lost words, but felt people unerringly.

When Hedin's kire of Fleming's men raped her, and his fellow kire Tuff was immediately there, they were caught by Jemis, Moi, and Mark, who had run into the howling of Sagittarius' dog. Mark drew attention to its similarity with the northern noblewoman (and even with [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]), and Jemis killed the offender in a duel, defending the honor of the "northern lady"; Tuff, who threw himself at the kire with a knife, was stabbed to death.

### Voice of God

After the betrayal of Fleming Gwend, together with the sailors of the “Seagull” she reached the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. On The Seal, Mark showed her a portrait of Adrian, and she said with unexpected warmth that he was "a man ... like me," agreed that he was restless, but not a man of twilight. On the way, she first heard the chase: on the third day after the departure of the kair, My cry for help was “Distress! Bad people!", and soon on the trail appeared riders in black coats. During the battle at the burning bridge in the gorge, the Gods' Chapel revealed that the trophy bracelet from the fort - the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]] - comes to life only in her hands: Gwenda herself shouted "Give!", Mark shoved her Object, and the bracelet lit up and said "Contact". Mark used this to negotiate with the owner of the Rings, and Gwenda herself entered into his game under the name "Frida", the wife of the shepherd Stanley. All the way to the castle, Mark gave her a bracelet in the evening and through her “prayed to God.” In the First Winter, in the chambers of Lady Sophia, Gwenda, at the request of Sophia and Mark, again made the bracelet glow, bringing it to her lips, but the Duchess took the Object from her hand and herself appealed to his master with a request to heal her husband. ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]])

### At the Court

Following Jemise Gwenda, she found herself in [[faunterra|Faunterra]] and in the spring of 1775 she lived in the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] in the position of his servant; her love for kair was selfless and disembodied - she claimed neither his hand nor his bed. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] was testing her gift on the Hand of the Infirmary: the thimbles taken from the murdered Infirmary were lit up in her fingers when she touched Jemis's scratch, but healing did not occur. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) Erwin then purposely ordered Cyre Robert to draw his sword and kill Jemis if the wound did not heal; Gwenda turned white, whispered “Shameless people! It is impossible!” and tried again, but without success. The subject responded only to a few - to Gwenda, Erwin and Louis; Later, Erwin, going over what he had in common with Louis and Gwenda, put forward a version of contact with [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade; Gwenda herself had [[pervokrov|firstblood]] in her veins. Mark, going through those who were in the hands of the brigade, noticed that Louis and Gwenda “now not very people.”

### The March of 1775

In early June 1775, Gwenda, along with Jemis, marched out of Faunterra with Erwin's army marching on Gallard Almer, and made all the way with him - through Arrowback County and the vicinity of Fliss to the Holliwell River. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) On June 4–5, in Arrowback, when Erwin, who thought Iona was dead, was looking for a way to contact the Puppeteer, his imaginary althessa reminded him of the "strange maiden" Jemis, for the sake of which she was taken on a campaign, and Erwin ordered to call Gwenda with the Object. Soon [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] spoke to him on the bracelet, and Erwin heard the voice of a living sister. At the end of June, the army reached the [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]], the Crown's westernmost outpost at the bridge to Steppe, where Erwin was joined by the corps of Count Lilliday.

### Last Task

In the Glorious Watch, on the day the Snow Count promised his son a miracle bride, Gwenda came to Erwin alone, stuttering and swallowing wine in a volley. She laid out her own plan: to infiltrate the besieged [[uejmar|Waymar]] with a maid (Desmond’s men had only to take her inside), steal from the fighters of the Perth Wilhelm Brigade — they know her and do not take her seriously — and release Iona. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) As a reward, she asked for a title, estate, and letter of credit to become equal to Jemis and achieve his love. When Erwin questioned her resolve, Gwenda's timidity was replaced by an icy hatred: she reminded him of how she got her firstblood, and vowed revenge on the "bad people" who made her "nobody". Erwin promised her Len and the title of Baroness, warned that the probability of death was seven out of ten and that he was not her seigneur, and on the same day gave the ship. He realized that the rush was caused by this news about the bride. Erwin showed her a sealed letter for Iona from himself and Lady Nexia Flame, but immediately burned it on a candle, ordering Iona to give an oral message from him and Nexia. She told Erwin that Jemis knew and allowed everything, but it was not true: she told him that she was going to the archive of the First Winter to look for her family; when he found out the truth, a quarrel arose between him and Erwin. The Voice of God bracelet remained in Erwin's army; later, when the gaunt Onday burned the Northerners' ships in the Steppe, the bracelet was found and sent to Paul, and [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] decided that Gwenda, who wore it, was already dead.

Gwenda herself was not in the burned ships: she broke up with Jemis in the Glorious Watch and sailed north. Jemis then became darker than the cloud, and Father David explained to Erwin that the cairn, without noticing it, yearns for the woman to whom he is accustomed. Erwin held onto a "weak ray of hope" in his campaign: Paul in the Steppe, Vittor does not know Gwenda in the face, and a harmless maid can reach Iona.

### Babysitter of "flowers"

Having reached Northwood, Gwenda was among the governesses who themselves asked to babysit the Shaylands. In September 1775 at [[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]], Lord [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] instructed [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] to choose a babysitter for the children he had taken under the care of [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. Among the formidable Northwood governesses, Gwenda stood out for her futility and fright; she admitted that she was afraid of Finger of Wilhelm, loves children, never punishes them, "can survive" and knows "how to distinguish good from bad," and Joaquin chose her. In October 1775, together with another nanny, Sally, she rode with a dozen children in the roundabout of Vittor, who invaded the Duchy of Origin; at night in the captured castles, the count himself handed her children. Vittor was happy with her: she loved the children “as relatives” and, having learned that Joaquin chose her, praised him for his good choice.

In November, with his entourage Gwenda got to the castle Lillydeev in [[lid|Leed]]. At the mention of this kind, she strangely revived and admitted that she had “crossed” with the Lillidays, but did not explain anything; to the warning about the turtles walled up in the walls, she replied that she had seen many dead.

### Death

In November 1775, Gwenda went down to the basement, where Iona was kept in a cage. When Martin and Joaquin entered, Iona, suddenly awakened from her stupor, broke the bars and beat both of them, and then began to give Gwenda orders. The nurse's hand shone through the cloth of the dress: from the hidden Ring Gwend killed the guards of Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]], on the orders of Iona broke Martin's hand in the elbow and burned the barrels with fire, behind which Joaquin was hiding, repeating: "Milady, I could!"

Then Paul appeared, calling her a “belch” and “squirrel”, “an old friend”. Gwenda threw him that he had taken everything from her and made her "nothing" that she was "no longer a woman", and pointed her hand at him, but she could not shoot him: her hands shook more and more until she dropped them. Paul briefly hit her in the chest and, hugging, kissed her until she stopped fighting in agony. A tiny bubble rolled out of the dying hand; Paul thought it was poison for Iona, and took the Ring from his dead hand.

Vittor announced that Gwenda was "initiated" and therefore sent by [[navi|Nathaniel]], and attributed the failure to Joaquin, who hired her; however, Joaquin remembered the "belch" and realized that Gwenda was once initiated by Paul himself. Gwend's goal was partly fulfilled: in the bubble was Ulyana Dust, with her help, Ion later subdued Martin Shayland and freed herself. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Iona herself told Erwin that if he had come sooner, he would have died like Gwenda. Joaquin later listed Gwenda as one of the dead girls, in whose death he blamed himself: "he hired Gwenda."

## Relationships

### Jemis Lilliday

Jemis rescued her from the fort, avenged the violence and kept her with him as a servant. Gwenda loved him selflessly and for his sake went to her death; Jemis, according to Erwin, saw her as a burden and let her go on a dangerous case, promising nothing.

### Paul

The mocking "my dear, I missed" and Gwenda's words that Paul took everything from her show that she was his long-time victim; according to Joaquin, Paul once "had" her and initiated it. It was in front of him that her hatred was powerless.

## Personality

Gwenda is shy, oblique, and usually almost devoid of will. He speaks in short fragments of phrases, with everyone keeping “quiet mouse”. But behind the timidity there was a hatred for their tormentors, which at crucial moments swept away fear. Her past before the fort and the real name of the family remained unknown. Gwenda's connection to the Lillidays is just a matter of time.
