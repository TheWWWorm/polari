## Biography
Gordon Sue became a company X commander during the siege of the palace at [[faunterra|Faunterra]], following the death of [[brant-stil|Brant Steel]]. He did not have an officer's rank at the time, but he quickly rose in rank because most of the officers died. By the summer of 1775, he was an officer for only six months. Erwin did not regret his appointment. Gordon had tactical ingenuity, held in his company excellent discipline even in peaceful months, played well in strata, thoroughly studied the victorious maneuvers of the Duke in the last war and swore that he could repeat them. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) And, also valuable, he at least sometimes laughed at Erwin's jokes. As the commander of the first, udder company, he had the right to wear the Duke's flag. The captain demanded to be called by his full name, Gordon Sue, and was offended if they forgot the name of his mother. He had a habit of having fun when it came to crap, and Erwin admitted that he himself was guilty of the same.

## In "Humans and Gods"
### Hiking in Almera
In the summer of 1775, Erwin moved against the priory of [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]. Gordon Sue and his company captured Fisher Hut station, and the captain interviewed the captive workers himself. Erwin appreciated that the landing was done perfectly, although he thought Gordon was far away from [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon]]. In the negotiations with Ganta Thunderstorm, Gordon carried a white flag. At the Earl of [[erroubek|Arrowback]]'s castle, he scoffed at the Count's caution and demanded the promised battalion: wait for the infantry from Faunterra to approach for two weeks while the heretic gathers troops, he considered ridiculous. When Colonel Dolph Arrowback accused the Duke of having himself brought General [[veksler|Wexler]]'s Almerians on, Gordon called him into the fight, and Erwin apologized for the "battle flame" not yet cooled in the captain's veins. Released prisoners of the Almerians Gordon offered to "add", believing that they would return to the heretic, and argued with the order in the name of "military honor". Sir Michael's defector Erwin assigned him to a company.

At the military council, when the army was sandwiched between Gallard's army and reinforcements, Gordon Sue offered to go northwest, take [[fliss|Fliss]], capture part of the fleet, and burn the rest of the ships and sail to [[uejmar|Waymar]] for the main enemy, the Puppet Master. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) Erwin accepted this plan, although Mother [[mat-kordeliya|Cordelia]] demanded first of all the arrest of Gallard.

### Fighting in the forest and fire
Depicting that he had abandoned the army, Erwin and a handful of companions went to the monastery of Marek and Simeon, and the battalion was commanded by Gordon Sue. General Wexler attacked the camp at night, but Hyder's Leed Wolves, dressed as the Almerans, killed him and then set fire to the forest behind the enemy's back. ([[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]) Two Almer regiments were dispersed, and the North lost twenty-eight men. To the stunned Dolph Arrowback, Gordon calmly explained that the fire was inflated by Foremother Susan at the request of Agatha, and the Duke went to the monastery to pray and Agatha would hide him from enemies. He swore it was as true as "that my name is Gordon Sue Rose." Then he remembered the prisoners. Among them were Lieutenant Caseworth and the General's standard-bearer, whom Erwin released after the first fight, promising to hang on the next meeting. Gordon ordered them hanged, and Leed ordered each person to hang a sign on his chest: “He fought for heretics.”

The fire did not stop at the stream and threatened the Beaver Forest with dozens of villages. At night, peasants came to the camp on the hill and asked to save them. Most of the officers were behind the immediate march on Fliss, but Gordon Sue decided to put the fire out. A mile from the North with the same fire fought the battalion of the brotherhood of battle Wilhelm, called by the monks from the monastery of Marek and Simeon. Gordon Sue and Mother Cordelia negotiated a truce with the monks' commander until the fire was extinguished, and another four hours after. A day of kairah cut down trees and put out flames, and the Wilhelmins carried water and dug moats. Then the parties cut through the gap with the moat, salted each other and parted.

It cost the captain his reputation. The army was a day late to Fliss, and during this time the surviving knights of Wexler managed to prepare the city for defense. Baron Icewind accused Gordon of violating the Duke's plan, the rest of the officers met him with a frosty silence, Arrowback attributed the decision to himself, and only Cordelia said a few kind words to him. ([[kurt-ajsvind|Kurt Icewind]]) Erwin himself later remarked that "some of the complexity was created by Gordon Sue's kindness", but ordered to thank the Wilhelmines, who had not fallen to meanness.

### Break Defense
On 17 June 1775, the battalion approached Fliss. Gordon Sue pretended that he was preparing an assault, and he himself chose the town-suburb Break for defense, whose rear and flank were covered by a steep cliff above the Smokey Dahlia. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) Ganta Thunderstorm objected that there was nowhere to retreat. Gordon replied that retreat is not part of the plan, and whoever wants to run, let him run now. Arrowback rushed to give a horse fight in the field and demanded command as a senior in rank. Mother Cordelia announced that Agatha approved of the captain's tactics, and Gordon later thanked her. When asked about the Duke, he "without blinking an eye" lied that he would come when necessary.

Half a day and all night the northerners, the people of Arrowback and the townspeople fortified the southern side with barricades. Along the open western edge, Gordon ordered the moat to be dug all the way to the abyss. The moat was only three yards wide, and then the captain bought forty barrels of solvent in the nearby Krasilny, filled the bottom with firewood and prepared a fire barrier. The battalion withstood the onslaught of Gallard's three times superior army, but Fliss's scorers set the cliff on fire, and there was no water in the city. The captain did not know what to do with the horses that had gone mad from the fire. The way out was suggested by Ganta Thunderstorm: the herd was released on the knights of the parish and then they hit with all their might. Erwin came up here with five hundred kairs and decided the outcome of the battle. Later, the northerners shouted fame to Agatha, Lilliday and Gordon Sue throughout the night.

Erwin revealed that Baron Icewind had "swept the spit" to the captain in vain: Gordon obeyed his order exactly, just the baron did not know about it. Before meeting with Steppe Fire, Gordon suggested that Erwin send a courier to Moran, and himself stay "in the ship" and call the leader there: it will be safer. Under Rei-Roy, he, along with Icewind and Leed, was part of the forward detachment, which was led by Erwin himself.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Loss of hand
In July 1775, at the gates of Rei-Roy, the Shavan with the Wilhelm's Fingers was ambushed. The ring-bearer from the wall hit only Erwin. Gordon Sue commanded, "Raise the shields! Cover the Duke!" and threw his company directly into the fire. Less than a minute, the Xs stood a living wall while Erwin went into darkness, and the shots intended for the Duke crippled them. One of the shots cut off Gordon's left arm. He stayed in the saddle and led his company out of the fight, assuring:
> The left hand is nonsense. If it were a skull, it would be worse!”

Two days later, the pruning became inflamed, purple strips of “rotten blood” crawled on the skin. Erwin gave Merritt his last vial of snake-grass juice, although he himself only held on to this potion. The officers, realizing that the captain was likely to die, got lost: Gordon Sue was loved more than all the other commanders. [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] told Erwin with black humor that there was no one to conflict with him in the army: "Icewind is already on the Star, Gordon Sue is about to." [[shram|Scar]] ordered Lieutenant [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]], Gordon's deputy, not to let the captain down. When Erwin handed out Wilhelm's captured Ring to his companies, Fitzgerald refused: Gordon Sue deserves this honor, and while he is ill, he will not dare. Before the battle of Roydana, Erwin told the captain, who was in a fever, that it was an honor for him to serve with him. He, smiling, corrected: “Gordon Sue”, and when asked why it is so important, promised to tell after the victory.

### Recovery
When Erwin sent the wounded to the monastery, and they refused to leave, the one-armed captain suddenly went to the army on his own feet. He chided the wounded: what kind of fighters, if "the cat sneezes - I will fall," and offered to choose an escort lot, four soldiers from each company. He himself asked to stay with the Duke, and Erwin could not find the strength to refuse. On the way, Gordon casually asked if there were snakes left. Under pressure, he admitted: the fever passed a week ago, the doctor canceled the potion, the body began to itch, and the non-existent hand was wildly ill. Then the captain began to depict fever, moaning and rolling his eyes at the doctor and received the drug further. When the grass ran out, he “got up on his feet.”

The one-armed captain stayed with the duke and, together with the army, made a long way north: in August 1775, through Holliwell to the County of Mills, in September, to Faeries and the Barony of Davey on the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]], and from there by sea along the Gran Islands to the Bear Fang and in the [[belomorye|Belomorye]]. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]; [[melnitsy|The Mills]]; [[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) In the County of Mills, when he saw Jenna’s giant mill, he exhaled: “For the sake of the Foremothers, what a giant!” Apparently, it was he who noticed under Father David that Lady [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]] found it difficult to find a groom in the capital, where Lightharts is not in honor. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) This led to an awkward conversation about her ancestry. On the sea voyage, Gordon again shrieked about the snake-grass, and Erwin, who had failed, ordered him to be dragged on a rope for food. Gordon Sue and Hyder Leed approved of Nexia's marriage to [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]], believing that the Duke would only benefit from it. In November 1775, already in the Duchy of Origin, the army stopped in an abandoned inn, and there Erwin announced that with the Leed Wolves he would go to Leed to rescue his sister. Gordon, Scar, and Fitzgerald were detained and committed to the main and two reserve plans. The companies with the train and the Gun moved to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] without the Duke. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) In addition to Jemis, only Gordon Sue and company commanders knew the full plan of the upcoming battle.

### Battle for First Winter
On December 2, 1775, the day of the Battle of the First Winter, one-armed Gordon Sue, along with Scar and Fitzgerald, led an X-cloaks column along the Belomorye Road. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Count [[fleming|Fleming]], as Erwin foresaw, ambushed them in a cramped position. From the bird she was watched by [[adrian|Adrian]] and his companions, who easily recognized the captain. The northerners withstood the shelling from the ledges, until Jemis and the Gun, climbing there, “where even the eagles do not fly”, did not hit the White Seamen from above. Then Gordon's Xs found themselves on the flank of [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]]'s hull and met him with a crossbow volley. Richard's horse fell, he was overturned, throwing the arcana. The Wilhelmines captured the prisoner. Their commander was the same monk with whom the Xes put out a forest fire. Gordon called him to negotiate, and both realized that the fight would destroy both the company and the monks. No one gave the order to shoot, the monks with Richard retreated to the mountains, and Gordon sent scouts for them, but did not pursue. Erwin commended his mercy to a worthy enemy.

In the evening the captain sat in the bedroom of the wounded Erwin with Iona and Nexia. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) He drank the Ordge and in his faces told how the ambush had passed, took the cup from the drunken Iona, stood up for Nexia, who had disobeyed, and, at Iona's request, watched the clock to wake her. He got a chair, and he joked that he was afraid to lose this happiness in a half-burnt castle. Erwin called him a true friend.

### Death
Erwin suddenly realized that Richard could hide among the dead monks, and told the captain to raise the alarm. Richard entered the bedroom. His Perst broke Gordon's blade, and Richard started a "game": everyone had to name the victim, and the survivor he promised to let go. He turned to Gordon Sue first, calling him an idiot who had not checked the corpses. The captain stood up and pointed to Richard himself: he betrayed his family and land, brought the enemy to the First Winter, and such dishonor is washed away only by death.
> "Kill yourself, my lord."

"Wrong answer," said Richard, and Gordon Sue's chest burst from the shot, debris of ribs entering the heart. Lord [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] was killed. So Gordon Sue did not tell me why he held on to his full name.

## Legacy
Erwin remembered Gordon Sue as a brave, loyal and cheerful warrior who saved him at the cost of his hands, spared the Almeran forest and monks and, unlike Lord Desmond, had a good heart. Killing Richard, Erwin told him:
> "Brother, you killed a very good man."
