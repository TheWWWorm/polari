## Overview
Hyder Leed is the captain of the Leed Wolves, a company of scouts and saboteurs from Leed. According to him, the Wolves served the family of the Origins for sixteen generations and never stained themselves with rebellion. Hyder himself proudly recalled how the company distinguished itself “in the night Labelin” (see Night Labelin) and in the pursuit of the Silver Fox and put three hundred trophy spark copies at the Duke’s feet. ([[reznya-v-labeline|Night in Labelin]]; [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]]) [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] called him the best interrogator in his army. Hyder replied that kiru should not be proud of this quality, but the torture case was not dismissed. He knew the secret passage to the Leed Castle and carried a garrot. According to Erwin's observations, Hyder led his family from the ancient rulers of Leed, and Jemis Lilliday called him a countryman: Hyder was born in the quarter where young Jemis spit from the castle wall.

## Biography
### In "The Voice of Winter" and "The Yanmei Hunt"
In the spring of 1775, Hyder, along with the protection agent [[itan|Ethan]], investigated the train crash on the Back: they combed the banks of the river, found and identified the bodies of the dead and compiled a forty-page report for Erwin. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) Erwin explained why he had assigned the case to him:
> “Thanks to Agatha for making you my vassal... you are the best interrogator in my army.”
The conversation took place at Faunterra in March 1775. Hyder admitted that the month bit his elbows, not getting with the Duke to storm the palace, and dreamed of a worthy task. Fishermen who saw the crash, and the people of the count, who first arrived at the place, were interrogated in three steps, but, on the orders of Erwin, they did not torture anyone, they acted “not with pain, but only with fear.” The witnesses wept and prayed for mercy, as it befits the fishermen in front of the kairs, and repeated exactly what was included in the report; Hyder offered to take them again and "squeeze". About Ethan, he reported that he was exactly following orders, but clearly hiding something. Erwin initially ordered Ethan to be put in a stone bag for bread and water, rejecting the captain's request to "use methods". However, on the same day, having received from Minerva a ribbon with the inscription "You are not the lord, my lord. Humbly," the duke gave Hyder the order: "Let everything shake out of Ethan. in any way.” It is the Leed Wolves who are credited with the torture that crippled Adrian's former secretary. [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], in a dispute with Minerva, noted that he knew Hyder well and from his point of view Ethan was "practically whole": it is unlikely that the captain disturbed the duke "with such trifles as his hand and teeth." Hyder was a witness in the case of the body of [[adrian|Adrian]], who was found frozen in the ice, and accompanied the coffin. On May 21, 1775, at the second [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], he was summoned as a witness for the prosecution. He testified that, on Erwin's orders, he had searched the banks of the Beck and found the emperor's body two miles from the crash site: the local peasants, coming out to cut a hole, noticed him frozen in the ice of the bay twenty yards from the shore. The body was identified by Earl Arrowback and Ethan, after which it was placed in a coffin and sealed. When Minerva asked about Ethan, the captain replied that he had left Faunterra for personal reasons, and his testimony was not required. The fact that the court was the "main part" of Erwin's plan was known, except for the Duke himself, only the Althess of Anxiety and Hyder Leed.

He also interrogated [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]]: he lost his leg (the kaira “worked out” the knee cup, the wound was inflamed, and the leg had to be taken away), and the face and mouth were mutilated. On May 15, 1775, Hyder showed the prisoner to Erwin and Maurice Labelin and forced Buckley to repeat the testimony: "Duke. Labelin. I did. For me. Transmit. Eyes. Snowdrops." He also reported that Buckley had tracked down [[kharmon|Harmon]] near Laem, Shimmery. When Labelin mentioned the fake Item, Hyder confirmed that the prisoner had spoken about it, but the captain did not include it in the report, considering it an obvious lie, and then apologized to the Duke. After that, Buckley stabbed Labelin himself with a knife, and the Greeks carried the body in a bag.

### In "Humans and Gods"
In the war with [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], the Leed Wolves were Erwin's eyes and knife. When the saboteurs disconnected the railway, two companies of wolves just passed by train through the Snake. Hyder took all the cavalry and hurried to help the Duke, and sent the infantry to the substation to fix a spark. Towards midnight he arrived at Earl Arrowback's castle; Erwin noted that the captain knows the location of the search for Adrian's body. While the Duke with the Xes went to scout, Hyder took the donjon and placed the sentries. After the battle at the dam, his interrogators interrogated the captured Almerans. At the military council, Hyder offered to release several wounded prisoners in the fortress of Voronovo Pero with a message to Daniel Blackmore, the nephew of the arrested count: he had to go over to the side of the northerners either for the sake of the release of his uncle, or for his death and inheritance. Sir Michael doubted success, but Erwin assigned the case to Hyder. When Erwin, with one company of horsemen of the Leed Wolves, went to the shores of the Smoky Distance, Hyder was in his inner circle with [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] and the bodyguards; at the halt, he praised the bodyguard Quentin his garrote and reasoned that the bright moon was good, "if you want to shoot a sentry." During the night, Erwin ordered him to destroy the enemy scouts, take two alive and find out the number of the detachment, and then raise the company as planned. While the Duke was distracting General [[veksler|Wexler]], his army at the creek attacked two Almer regiments at night. Hyder's company, on the orders of the Duke, conducted reconnaissance in Almeran uniform. Having noticed a large bypass detachment led by Wexler himself, the Wolves came closer and fired a volley of crossbows. "I'm sorry, it's my fault," Hyder later told Colonel Arrowback, "knowing the Wolves would 'prematurely end the battle', they wouldn't. Enraged knights chased them all the way to the camp, but in the meantime a forest fire broke out behind the stream, and the Almeran infantry fled. Erwin later said that he had only drawn “four conclusions”, and the rest was performed by Hyder Leed and [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]]. The captive white knights, whom the Duke had previously promised to hang at a new meeting, Gordon Sue, after consulting with Hyder, hung, and Hyder added: "And each on his chest a sign: 'He fought for heretics'."

Under [[fliss|Fliss]], Hyder proposed sending scouts into the city to open the gate from the inside; The wolves and warriors of Arrowback examined the walls, lost six killed, but the lasa was never found. In the battle on the cliff wolves ambushed the white knights, but they “took with them to the Star almost a hundred northerners.” As a result, the Wolves suffered the heaviest losses: out of two hundred people in the ranks, sixty remained. They were initiated into the Xs and united in one company with the surviving soldiers of Manfried, and Hyder Lied became the commander. He repeatedly offered to break into Fliss and quietly strangle Gallard with a garrot, sending a saboteur under the guise of a released prisoner. Hyder was among the commanders during the assault on cities and on the campaign on [[rejs|Rei-Roy]]. When the army landed from the ships in the Steppe, its scouts intimidated the caught fishermen and shepherds, but they knew nothing. Later, the Wolves discovered traces of a large detachment that had left Rei-Roy for Julian Bridge, and captured two old Shavans who were behind the horde with a kumis cart; under the threat of a knife, they confirmed that [[moran|Moran]] remained at Rei-Roy, and the horde left for the bridge. Hyder, along with [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]] and [[kurt-ajsvind|Icewind]], accompanied Erwin to Rei-Roy.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree
In July 1775, in a night battle at the gates of [[rejs|Rei-Roy]], where the detachment lost ships and Ganta Thunderstorm, Hyder, rushing behind the company of Icewind, came upon the chisel, surrounded by a wall of fire. He took a moment between the flashes and wanted to cut the Shavan of the muscles of his right hand, but the blow came between the ribs, and Hyder brought a dying prisoner to the Duke, blaming himself: "I had to take him alive." At each stop, Erwin demanded a report on the wounded. When he woke up, Hyder asked to be allowed to be questioned by "reliable methods" and urged the Duke not to be present, but Erwin himself talked to the prisoner, broke his will with speech and handed the captain the blueprint of the device. "Precious Agatha... It can work!” — admired Hyder. For two days, the Hyder Wolves treated the prisoner: they disassembled the crossbow, trapped two screws and twisted them into cracks between the bones of the prisoner's skull. By twisting these "barracks", it was possible to subordinate his will, and so the Gun appeared - a living fingernail chained to a cart. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) Hyder proudly demonstrated to Erwin how the Gun carried out any order; later, the Gun's shooter was [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]. In the battle at Roydan, it was Hyder who gave the command "Zalp!" when the shavanks threw bows; when the horde broke into the camp from the rear, the Wolves repulsed the first wave, and then, on Erwin's orders, carried the wounded into the village and took on the role of paramedics. After the battle, he congratulated the duke, twisting his fingers at the temple: "The gun showed itself perfectly." When the commanders hesitated with the choice of the shooter, Hyder was the first to advise to give the Gun to another company: “The place of the Leed Wolves is in the rear of the enemy, and this thing should work at the front.” He singled out two fighters who had already worked with the prisoner. Before the [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]], Hyder offered to send intelligence to the fort itself ("The Foremothers themselves will not notice the scout from the Leed Wolves"), but on the advice of [[shram|Scar]], the scouts were sent to the town next door. The death of the Snow Count in the Glorious Watch Wolves took especially hard: they were born in his domain and knew both the count and many of his kairs. In the steppe [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] painted a portrait of Hyder on his order. In the town of Trident, the captain purchased a large supply of kumis for the detachment, and Erwin reminded him of the order to follow [[otets-david|Father David]] and intercept any letter he sent.

In September 1775, a detachment under the guise of the entourage of the messenger of Hope went through the County of Mills. ([[melnitsy|The Mills]]) At the mother mill, Jenna Hyder asked the watch officer how the mill is run and whether there is a signal connection between the mills with bonfires or mirrors; the officer promised to notify everyone about the "visit of guests" and asked if it was true that the Lillidays were related to the Flames. Then the detachment through Fairis went to the Barony of Davy on the Sunset Coast and plunged into the ships; the engagement of Jemis and Nexia Hyder approved: "Good for the North... Your vassal will be associated with the Flames, and that is the key to Hope.”

In October 1775, Hyder, along with Schram and Lieutenant [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]], was on a ship waiting for Lord Velentyne, [[fleming|Fleming]]'s son-in-law, near the Gran Islands in the [[more-ldov|Sea of Ice]]; when the mercenary captain demanded to leave, Hyder reminded him that the ship was hired by the Great House Origin and "it is not a ship but a cart for the transfer of troops." When the ship Velentyne was captured and the Duke thought about the punishment for the traitor, Hyder silently depicted a gesture of a noose around his neck. In Northwood County, Hyder's men landed on reconnaissance and brought news of the defeat of Desmond and Craig Northwood. Hyder guarded David's father and in the Fang of the Bear caught him trying to convey a message to the order: in addition to the deliberate spy, Murdoch's cairn, two Wolves were assigned to David, whom he did not notice all the way, and in the church of the Forefather Maximian he was intercepted by the captain himself, not allowing him to confess to the priest's agent, and took him to the duke on the seashore. ([[otets-david|Father David]]; [[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) Erwin instructed Hyder to ask David for a code of communication with the order and find out if the Gifts were really falling from the Star; "It's time, my lord," replied the captain, and David hurried to tell the story.

In November, the unit landed in the Duchy of Origin. In the abandoned Inn of the Pass, where Erwin met Fleming, Hyder checked in advance to see if there was an ambush.

When Fleming reported that [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] was sitting in a cage at Leed Castle and was ordered to be killed in the assault, Erwin decided to go there himself and first instructed Hyder to provide an escort for Nexia and Violet Fleming. Then the captain said that he knew the secret passage to Leed Castle: it begins from the weapons quarter, and the lift in it accommodates only three; three scouts could open the gate, and the assault squad recruited from the townspeople - "a whole block of blacksmiths is ready to beat the Shayland bastard." Erwin took only Hyder and [[obri|Aubrey]]'s kire with him, and Jemisa and the Gun sent for the First Winter. After a week of jumping through the snow, in a fever, they stood on the slope of Soratnitsa Mountain at the aqueduct support. The city was just stormed by [[paul|Paul]]'s shawls: Shayland underfed them, and the horde began to plunder Leed, primarily the weapons quarter, from where a secret passage was coming. Aubrey advised to wait, but Hyder, recognizing his advice as "the only reasonable", asked the Duke not to follow him and risk his life to save the city. He proposed a suicide plan: to roll down an icy aqueduct directly to the central square, and then through a narrow pipe to reach the castle tower. Erwin agreed, "To the assault, gentlemen." Hyder was the first to run through the pipe and secure the rope with the cat. Dressed in the uniforms of the murdered Shaylands, the Kairs went to the gallery above the courtyard; Hyder guessed where Iona was being held, and at the right moment shot him with a Shavan crossbow, starting a distracting skirmish while Erwin made his way into the basement to his sister. Hyder and Aubrey then held the defenses in the south tower. All three survived, with no scratch on Hyder himself, and Aubrey was wounded twice. Hundreds were probably killed, but thousands were saved. I am eternally grateful to you," the captain said to the duke, seeing the chavans leave the city from the tower. Those who fled from Leed [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] were not found. Iona and Hyder agreed that the sick Erwin would stay in Leed, and they would go to the army in the First Winter, but the Duke insisted on his own and ordered the captain to saddle his horses, recruit a detachment of citizens and not forget the fireworks. Thus Hyder joined Erwin's small squad of eleven men who had gone to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. In the mountains, he warned about the impending storm and that they would not have time to the X-cloaks, the detachment had to seek shelter. On the day of the battle, he, dressed as a driver on a donkey, participated in the attack on the Shayland army convoy. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Hyder fired fireworks from his position, and then with blacksmiths broke into the road and cut with a sword. Then the detachment entered the valley among the running wagon attendants; when Iona, lying among the corpses, beat the Canids with a whip, Hyder and Aubrey as if casually covered it from both sides. Everyone in the squad survived. Hyder himself picked up seven leads for this squad and vouched for them; when someone tried to take the horse away at night, Aubrey decided that it was a straggler, and Hyder suspected Martin Shayland, who was walking the same way.

After the battle, Hyder Leed and Kair Aubrey were in charge of the Duke's safety. Before the peace talks in the First Winter, they studied the album from cover to cover with poisons and tools that the enemy delegation could use, and instructed the Kairs, and in the negotiations themselves, Hyder sat down with the guests, strictly separating the northerners and strangers. He was among the few warriors left by Erwin in the hall when Adrian showed Bird without Flesh.

## In "Fantasies"
The author calls the stories “Fantasies” possible fates of the heroes; in them Hyder Leed is alive and still serves Erwin.

In the story "Amicable" (August 1776), Erwin placed Hyder at the head of an expedition south of two X companies, [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] and Mireille Nan-Claire with a retinue. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) The Duke recalled his achievements: “In the Steppe, they accomplished a feat, capturing the Gun. In Northwood perfectly tracked down and split the enemy agent. They almost sent me to the Star.” Hyder was to provide security for Mireille, who wanted to get troops in Shimmery and take the throne of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], and look after Jemis, sent to Laam "as punishment". Erwin ordered the visit to be conducted "without corpses." Hyder himself promised to be “cold-blooded, like a frost on the edge of the sword,” and Jemis argued with him all the way which of them was the eldest.

Hyder's younger sister was married to a cyre from a company of Leed Wolves who had died in the Steppe, and Hyder assumed custody of her and her three sons. When the Shavans crushed Leed, he risked himself and even the duke to save them, but the steppemen managed to break into the house. The eldest nephew, a ten-year-old Barney, who ran to the aid of his beaten mother, was kidnapped into slavery, and Hyder considered him dead. The expedition sailed to Laem Harbor, where it was solemnly welcomed by Prince Hector, disappointed by Erwin's absence. At the night slave auction in [[lajem|Laem]] Hyder recognized the boy, unarmed put three Shavan traders and questioned one of them, planting his palm on the dagger, but Barney managed to take the southern man in a white coat. Hyder asked Hector to return the boy and offered to pay twice. Prince Hector Hyder apologized on behalf of the Great House Origin and admitted that he had acted on his own initiative contrary to the Duke's orders. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) Hector did not arrest him: he knew that Hyder had saved Erwin in the Steppe and persuaded him to break into Leed, which protected Iona from death. The next night, Jemis found out the address of Timerett's pimp, whose servant allegedly bought the boy, and in the morning the kairs broke into his house and interrogated him, where they were caught by Sheriff Halintor; after that, the prince deprived the kairs of freedom of movement, and they were under honorable arrest in the palace. It was later revealed that Barney had been ransomed by order of Princess Demeter to test her fiancé Jemis and then return the boy to his uncle. ([[demetra-nellis-lajon|Demetra Nellis-Lyon]])

Then in the luggage of Mireille found a love potion, which she was carrying for Jemis, and soon Hyder and Mireille in front of everyone fell in love with each other; Hector said that the captain "cracked" into her "like a pimple boy." Mireille swore that it was not her who poured the potion into the captain's glass: "the other man took it." Apparently, this was in many ways their common idea: even before the words of Leed “I had one idea” Mireille replied: “I have too!” Hyder admitted that he was married, and Mireille promised to explain everything to his wife and ask the Chapter of Pramatery for a divorce. Calling himself "just a baron", unworthy of such a lady, he fell to his knees in front of her at midnight with a bouquet. Hector demanded that the "mad" drink a turntable potion, and as a reward promised to return Barney and give Mireille a Solar Regiment. The lovers kissed "for the first and last time" and drank, after which Vanessa-Lilit brought to Barney's hall. ([[vanessa-lilit|Vanessa-Lilith]]) They both laughed at each other and said, "It's cool," said the Leed Wolf, and Mireille winked, "We can do it again." In the following years, the Queen and Kier repeatedly remembered this adventure with laughter.

Barney, seeing his uncle, stretched out in soldierly terms and reported: “Captain Leed, let me report! I am healthy and have no loss.” The boy dreamed of joining the Leed Wolves, but the “famous uncle” convinced him that there is a job more worthy than cutting people’s throats. Barney entered the Technical Academy of the First Winter and became a spark engineer.

Jemis, in a conversation with Vanessa-Lilit's widow, claimed that Hyder had been "brutally tortured" by Moger Buckley, "as a result of which the bastards died." In the main cycle of the mutilated Buckley stabbed Maurice Labelin. And at home, according to a joke by Jemis, there was a “mirror aqueduct”, polished to brilliance with “the ass of Hyder Leed and Aubrey’s kire”. In the story "Let's Catch Him", Aubrey recalled how "on the day of the great battle" he was near Iona and Hyder Leed.

In "Ad-ri-an!" (Autumn 1776) Hyder came to Faunterra with Jemis and the sorcerers of Darkwater under the guise of "glassmen". He reported to the Duke: "Count Lilliday is acquainted with the bride, Lady Miray Nan-Claire is seated on the throne, the south is satisfied, there are no losses." Erwin complained that without him, "bad" and "Adrian is about to win the election." Leed immediately suggested, "I'll start with Adrian, the garrote with me," but the duke replied that he "solved the problem differently." At the words of "Queen Mireille" the captain became "honorable to the thrill", and when Erwin praised her martial art, he rose, "as if praised him himself."

## Personality
Hyder is a stern, resourceful and ruthless soldier, an expert in ambushes and secret moves, able to torture a prisoner without hesitation. David’s voice reminded him of “the sound of a blade leaving its sheath.” The ability to shoot a watch or capture the “language” he estimated by the brightness of the moon. He is proud of his company and knows how to laugh at himself. In Fantasies, he and Jemis argue endlessly about who is in charge.

## Relationships
### Erwin Origin
Erwin entrusted Hyder with the dirtiest and most secret assignments: questioning, investigating the Bake crash, guarding, and interrogating Father David. He himself admitted to himself that he “hates the pain of others”, and looked at the work of the interrogator with disgust, but appreciated its results.
### Jemis Lilliday
Hyder prepared the Gun, and Jemis became his shooter. In Fantasia, Hyder officially commanded the expedition and had to supervise Jemis, causing constant humorous bickering between them. Jemis, in turn, himself found Hyder's kidnapped nephew.
### Mireille Nen-Clair
In Fantasia, Hyder and [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille]] played (at least in part) a love fever from a love potion in front of the Shimmeria court and "cured" a turnip in exchange for the return of Barney and the Solar Regiment. Then they repeatedly with laughter and a “note of tender nostalgia” remembered this adventure.
### Barney
Nephew, eldest son of a younger sister. Hyder was his guardian, found him in Laem and convinced him to become not a kire, but a spark engineer.
