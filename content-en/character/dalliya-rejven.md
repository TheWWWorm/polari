## Anxiety in the Main Cycle

### Birth of Anxiety
Starting with "Only One Star", [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] is accompanied by an "althess of Anxiety" - a woman whom only he sees and hears. For the first time she comes on the night of September 10, 1774, the last before the performance of the Northerners from the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] on a hike to Faunterra: she lay down in Erwin's bed, hugged him, "like a gentle althessa", kissed her neck and began to whisper. Then she is still an embodied alarm: her skin is cold, wet and loose, and Erwin remembers the corpses on the Cape River. She kisses his feet with a "slippery dead tongue" and sneakily advises him to flee to the First Winter with a handful of survivors. She also accompanies Erwin on a campaign across the South Road: at the beginning of November 1774, on the Trade Tract, when he looks at the ruins, she whispers in his ear that his war will end in the same - a meaningless ruin. ([[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]])

### Name and appearance
In The Voice of Winter, Erwin “became so close to an imaginary althessa that he gave her a name.” From now on it is not just an alarm, but an alarm with a capital letter. Realizing herself to be special, the althessa "strongly insolent." She walks in a penjoir, sits on the table, casts dark curls from her eyes, and, offended, twinkles and becomes translucent. The alarm becomes a mocking conversationalist and counselor. She teases Erwin and points him to softness ("You're too human, love. The puppeteer will eat you." She examines his blunders in fencing ("You're too kind"), suggests moves, interprets other people's words and letters to him, reciting, jealous and comforting. Erwin sometimes passes on her lines to her interlocutors.

In the winter and spring of 1775, in [[faunterra|Faunterra]], Anxiety is the only one, except [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], who knows how badly Erwin's lessons of family receptions with the Sword of the [[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter]] are going: he complains to her in the evenings, because there is no one else. When Erwin prepares a trap for the people of the Puppeteer among the [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]], Anxiety forces him to calculate the price of the plan: the Puppeteer can avoid it, and then the victims of the peasants will be in vain. "I'm an alarm. I won’t let you forget about such things,” she says. After [[minerva|Minerva]] on the Holy Field reconciled with the Snowdrops and thwarted his plan, Anxiety all two days of the march to the capital argues with him and finally advises: “Marry her, honey!” ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) Minerva, she says, is smart, bold, with a sense of humor, and their son will be born emperor; the oath not to marry by force, she reminds, is easy to get around if Mimi wants it herself.

### The “Yanmei Hunt”
In May 1775, in Faunterra, Anxiety remains at Erwin's side. She applauds him as the only spectator, after the scene played out in front of Captain [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]]. In the royal train, which carries three kings to the capital, she keeps him awake, demanding an answer as to how he will cope with his "problem". After learning that Alanis hid the deal with Znacharka, Anxiety advises to establish surveillance on her, not to drink with her from the same cup and not to kiss her skin, where there may be poison, and she removes the last features of similarity with Alanis from her appearance. ([[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]) She deals with Erwin a certain "factor" that threatens him in the capital, and offers two ways: to withdraw troops from Faunterra, as his father advises, or "to come up with something." She also cuts off his affection for someone else's love story and points to the sound of the words "Light Sphere", forcing him to remember the day when he drank the snake-grass. At the sessions of the Supreme Court of [[menson|Manson]] on May 21–25, she sits on the armrest of his chair, mocks [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], who abandoned the subject of the crash, and notices how pleased Alanis is.

### In "Humans and Gods"
In June 1775, when Erwin, after receiving a letter about [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]'s death, left the capital on a march on Almera, Anxiety returned to him at night on the march. She reproached him that he had "escaped" from her: she seemed to hurry forward in Leixity to choose a ship for them to Waymar. The alarm of the first doubted the death of Iona: the letter was written in an unfamiliar handwriting, the password was combined arms, and the cuir saw only a stab. She ruthlessly pointed out to Erwin that the death of his sister was beneficial to him because it evoked sympathy and made it plausible that he would become the "Knight of Light", and that he believed in Iona's death because the living sister in Vittor's hands was a much worse fate. She suggested that through Gwenda, who was speaking to the Object, she should turn to the Puppet Master and see if Iona was alive. When Erwin called her ridicule disgusting, she burst into tears, and after receiving an apology, immediately subsided. At night on his fight "You are a snake! Goddamn viper!" she just sent him an air kiss.

Later, Anxiety accompanies Erwin in negotiations with [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]]: he advises him to chew cheese "with a clever look" to gain time, and when he finds out that he is married to [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]], he sticks nails in his neck: "You are mine!" Then, when Nexia, under the influence of Ulyanina Dust, tries to kiss Erwin, Anxiety screams that the girl's lips are smeared with poison, and at the last moment pulls him away. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) She is the first to know that the poison is on the thorns of roses. At the end of June, when the northerners on the ships went up the Holliwell River and landed in the Bay of Horseshoe, she whispered: “Today is a special day, my dear! ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) Something is going to happen today.” On the way to Rei-Roy, sitting in the saddle behind Erwin, she asked why the Shavans were carrying herds, and soon the Shavans with the Persians attacked the northerners. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) She also spills common sayings (“Softly stem, so sleep hard”), wondering: “Would you know where it’s from in me?” In the light of "Fantasies" this reservation makes sense: during her lifetime, Dallia was an unknown girl.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree
In July 1775, in a Shavan village in the Steppe, Anxiety accompanies Erwin in the preparation of the Gun-Perst, adapted for firing with the help of a captured Shavan. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) She whispers to him that to destroy the Gun after the war "if you don't want to", "without taunt" approves the request of Jemis to become a shooter and ironically praises Erwin for paying off the inhabitants with a bill, "and then cut the village." During the battle, she first points out to him the noise in the rear, from where the Shavans break into the camp, and after the battle she wonders why he killed the prisoner. In August, when the news of the fall of the [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]] and the death of Count Lilliday comes, she hands Erwin over to the orphaned Jemis, helps him find the answer to the arguments of the cairn and pulls him by the ear so that he does not give [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]] a military secret.

In August and September, on a march west through Holliwell and Mills, Anxiety lashes out at a cold Erwin, lists the reasons why his plan will collapse, and predicts that he will die of a cold. She mocks his joy over the marriage of Jemis and Nexia, assures him that he "can't be jealous", and wonders who is the best couple for the deer - Jemis or [[shram|Scar]]. In November, in the duchy of Origin, she reminds him of all the occasions when Nexia was reaching out to Jemis. Under Leed, where a fever patient Erwin is caught pillaging the city by Shavans, she gives him an orja flask, repeats, "I will survive for my sister" - and, placing her palm on his eyes, shows Iona's vision in a cage in the basement of Leed Castle. ([[lid|Leed]]) After the battle for the First Winter, she climbs up to the wounded Erwin under the blanket, interprets King Shimmery's marriage letter offering Jemis a southern princess as his wife, calls Nexia a "stealth eunuch" and asks if he wants to marry "this devil". ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) In the main novels, the nature of Anxiety is not explained, and it can be taken as a figment of the hero’s imagination.

## In "Fantasies"

The solution is given in "Fantasies", which the author calls possible options for the future.

### Investigation of kair
The true story of Anxiety is told in Fantasy Three: Let's Catch It (July 1776). There was an unprecedented heat for the First Winter, and strange phenomena began in the castle. At night, Iona appeared in [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]'s bedroom: she shot him with a crossbow, raised the garrison on the alarm, but he was not found. Captain [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] saw [[minerva|Minerva]] dead in the Queen's office in a "special cut" penjoir. Lady Sophia was filming, then again wearing mourning. Three kair-ixes, the "first mystic" Aubrey, [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]], and Scar, set out to catch the ghost. The girl Meredith, who fell into the lake during a sortie kair, heard under the water from the ghost of the swollen Lady, which pranks the "other" - Mermaid. It was a girl who fell into a hole and died already in the castle, so her bones do not lie in the lake.

### The story of Odar Spesivyi
With a candle and a book by Megara Estelle, The Secret Horrors of the First Winter, the kairs evoked the spirit of Duke Odar Spesivogo. Once his body went to the Shavans: a bowl was made of the skull, the bones were cut and given to pigs and dogs. Out of fear, his son brought home and buried a corpse in the family tomb. The spirit of Odar, unable to fly to the Star, forever chained to a desecrated chair in the castle hall. Without freezing the kairs, the spirit allowed them to read the continuation of the story, and the air caught fire over the page.

### Girl under the ice
One day they brought a girl to the castle, who fell under the ice. She was not saved, and when the fever stopped her heart, her soul whispered: “I only wanted him to see me...” The spirit of Odar caught her will, "like a small bug", and offered a deal. Somewhere in the Steppe there is a bowl from his skull, and the girl must find it, and then find a man who will crush the steppes and return the skull to the ancestral crypt. Then both will go to the Star. “Little fool” he promised to make his “hand”. Her name was Dahlia Raven.

For twelve years, Dahlia served as a dead lord. His ferocity was imprinted in her subtle irony, stubbornness - flexibility, cold military mind - "the clever mind of a mistress and a spy." She has mastered tactics and strategy, diplomacy and politics.

### Revenge and attachment
Then the new lord was a young man sensitive enough to see and hear her, the same nobleman who once did not honor her gaze. Her first desire was revenge: she followed him to war and whispered nasty things, hoping that he would stumble and die. But he didn't die. He was brave and persistent, but not iron, and flexible, as she herself. Her love affair was long gone, the day he didn't come to her grave. But Dahlia “got along” with him and got used to appreciate his self-irony and “ridiculous heroism”. Ordinary ghosts can't lie, but Dahlia could. She told Odar that "our little wolf" had not yet found the bowl, and had not told Erwin about the skull, because if he had found the bowl, she would have had to go to the Star.

### The Empress's Gift
The Kairs summoned Dahlia by name, threatening to dig out her bones from one of the three pogos near the castle. Then they first saw a ghost: a translucent seductive girl in a dress with a huge neckline. Dahlia tried to intimidate them. She turned into a rotting corpse, then took the form of one-armed Gordon Sue and ridiculed the threats. But when she found that she no longer saw Odar denying her, she gave up.

The Kairs announced that they were giving her to Minerva, whom she would serve until a bowl was found. Dahlia herself called Minerva "the most boring special". Before the empress, she assumed the form of Alanis Almera (Minerva sighed: “Holy gods, I hoped she was alive...”), and then strained: “Dallia Raven at the service of Your Majesty.” Erwin was surprised to be seen by others and explained to Minerva, “The Cairos gave you my althessa.” As he left, he asked Anxiety not to disappear for long. The ghost that frightened the inhabitants of the castle was she: she appeared to [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], Shatterhand, Lady Sophia and Minerva herself.

### "Fantasy Six"

In August 1776, when Erwin began an affair with Minerva, she asked him about Dahlia and worried if the ghost was spying on them. Erwin assured that “there was nothing with Anxiety.” After meeting Erwin after Shavan Neymir said goodbye to the clay vessel where Chara's remains were stored, Dallia, after listening to their dates, again began to ulcerate, and he almost drove her away, saying the real name twice: the repeated name makes the ghost disappear. She apologized, and Erwin admitted he missed her.

In this scene, Dahlia remembers her death. She asked Lord Erwin to see her before she died, and she was told: “The Duke doesn’t care about people like you” and called “a low-born fool.” Erwin asked her to appear as an idealized Alanis-Agata for a fresco in the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant: a scientist who looks twenty-three years old, but has lived seventy, who knows that she will never get out of the desert island, and still knows how to enjoy life. Dalia took a long look at the faces and found what she needed (“Noted after two years of dating?” she shrugged in response to praise), and the “miracle” was then mistaken for the phenomenon of Pramati. For this, she asked for a promise that Erwin would not look for a skull cup for another five years, asked if he would become “her” after death, kissed him and dissolved. Later, when Iona and Mira tried to frighten Erwin with an “afterlife whisper,” he advised them: “Learn from Anxiety.”

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the fall of 1776, in Fantasia Seven, Dallia accompanied Erwin to the election of the lordship in Faunterra. After an argument with Minerva on the train, Erwin called her, but she did not show up. In the capital, when he was walking through the crowded streets to the mansion of the Origins ousted by Adrian’s supporters, she whispered to him: “Walk slowly, admire how beautiful everything is,” and when she saw a stone with the inscription “Nezhenka, die!”, she admitted: “Very nice.” Alone, she ridiculed his belief that Adrian would not be chosen, reminding him that he was also confident about Amessin. At Erwin's meeting with the buzzards Fitzgerald, Scar and Aubrey, who already knew her, Dallia became visible and spoke aloud, although her presence in the real world took away her strength and she gradually turned pale until she disappeared. She was temporarily placed at Scar's disposal for intelligence, since she could appear anywhere, and she promised to help while she had the strength. Aubrey, assigned to Iona, she clearly explained that the bodyguard should protect the mistress from her own recklessness.

## Abilities

- It is visible and audible only to sensitive people; it can be visible to all at will.
- Changes shape: took the form of Vittor Shayland, dead Minerva, rotting corpse, Gordon Sue, Alanis.
- He can lie, which ordinary ghosts do not.
- According to Erwin Mire, he cannot act in the material world.
- Subject to her name: a twice spoken real name makes her disappear.
- It is connected with bones: her skeleton lies on one of the three pogos at the castle. She made fun of the threat to give the bones to the sunsets, but gave up when the Kairs went for the shovels, and Odar denied it.

## Personality

A sarcastic, flirtatious, jealous, prone to black humor (in response to threats, a rotting corpse showed to the kairam), but devoted to Erwin. He says that he does not love him - the love has passed, - but he has long been "related". From Odar, she inherited the mind of a strategist and intriguer, and her flexibility considers them with Erwin a common feature.

## Uncertainties

Fantasies say that Dallia waited for her hour, while “the ruling duke changed.” This does not contradict the main cycle: Erwin became the reigning duke on August 30, 1774, when [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]], struck by a stone disease, transferred power to him, that is, even before the campaign in which Dallia first appeared next to him. "That nobleman" who didn't honor the dying girl's gaze was Erwin: in "Fantasy Six" she remembers asking to call "Lord Erwin", and he replies, "Yes, yes, I did terrible, you will take revenge on me for a thousand years."
