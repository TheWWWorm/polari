## In "Only One Star"
On the night of September 2, 1774, when Evergard burned the Wilhelm's Fingers, Sophie's servant came out of the underground passage after [[khamfri|Humphrey]]'s cheese and stretchers with [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] wounded. In the hunting lodge, she made no sound at the sight of a dozen dead, and Joaquin considered her a brave girl. By order of the lieutenant, she found a healing tool and helped the knight Lance to treat the wound. On the way, Sophie washed Ms.’s wound with drugs four times a day, and Milady went on recuperation. She also came up with a disguise: Alanis put on a cloak with a huge hood and put his stomach under the dress, like Humphrey’s pregnant wife. According to Joaquin, "only a woman from the common people is able to invent such a thing."

At Count [[blekmor|Blackmore]]'s castle, the watchmen at the milady's rooms did not want to let the maid out. This alarmed Joaquin, and at his request, Sophie, who went out to him in a nightshirt, woke up the mistress. When the soldiers of the count went to the assault, Sophie suggested: “We had a barn under the window,” and the fugitives jumped onto his roof. She led the Duchess to the gate. However, the horse was alone, and Joaquin took Alanis away, leaving behind "three good people": Burke, Humphrey and Sophie. Sophie's got some left over for the lady's wound.

## In "Fantasies"
### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the autumn of 1776, a silent girl Sophie arrived in Faunterra by the "Adrian train", along with a one-eyed [[khamfri|Humphrey]], [[dzhonas|Jonas]]'s coachman and Murriel's fortune teller. When asked by Major Handy Dodge if she had her tongue cut off, Humphrey replied, "Life is so much that you want to talk." ([[ruka-dodzh|Hand Dodge]]) He said, “She’s full of shit.” All signs point to the former servant Alanis: the same name, the same companion Humphrey, and her friends served, as they say, "the late lords - Lord Aiden Almera and Lady Alanis."

In the wattage, Sophie was a "soul". Her face expressed “the despair, the bitterness, the pain of a little man,” and people next to her wanted revenge: “These years trampled a poor girl.” The unit was nicknamed the "One-Eyed Gang" or "Sophie's Gang". The disgruntled caught were thrown at her feet, and she waved her hands to be let go. At night, she reached out to Humphrey with a kiss. He refused, and Sophie pressed her hand against her neck: “Then they will kill.” When the gang stopped Almera's carriage, Sophie took the ringleader by the hand, and he spared [[albert-almera|Albert]].

On the day of the opening of the Chamber, Adrian went to the crowd with his newborn son. Sophie silently stretched out her hands and for some reason cried, and the Lord put the baby in her hands: “I trust my people!” She stroked and kissed the baby “with trembling and very terrible tenderness”, and then “the light smiled”.

On the third day of the meeting, during the storming of the Chamber, only people from the mob remained next to Adrian: the “pale girl”, the old woman, the one-eyed and the coachman. Adrian suddenly fell in convulsions. The Golden Griva “understood everything”: “the pale scary girl” sneaked up on Adrian “and killed with a spark.” ([[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]]) He burned it with Wilhelm's Ring, and his last thought was, "That bitch Sophie killed Adrian!" Jonas the coachman shouted to the crowd, "Adrian is dead! Sophie's dead! The finger is dead!” By all accounts, Sophie was the revenge weapon of Humphrey's detachment for the House of Almer. Her mutilated body Humphrey, Jonas and Murriel took with them and disappeared.
