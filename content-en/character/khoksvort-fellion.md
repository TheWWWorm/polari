**Hawksworth Fellion** is a retired minister of the court, the hero of the story "The Death of the Assassin". He served as the "soul of the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]" at the brilliant court of [[adrian|Adrian]], and now lives in the family estate, where he tries to preserve at least the shadow of the former court.

## In "Fantasies"

### Fantasy Eleven: The Death of the Assassin
Fellion names his turquoise knight Sir Rasmus: "Lord Hawksworth Fellion". Mac himself at first can not remember who it is, but at the meeting he recognizes the old man: “everyone who was lucky enough to be in the brilliant court of Adrian” knew him. ([[mark-voron|Mark]]) Fellion speaks with a severe speech defect (instead of "in" he has "f"): "Fy me from the Star returned! I am with you from the dead.”

**Resign.** [[minerva|Minerva]] abolished the entire Ministry of the court. She gave representational and administrative tasks to the secretariat, and supplies and servants to the first lady and her assistants. Fellion calls [[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]] a trap and a verticho, and [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]] a dragon in a skirt. According to his story, the empress said to him: “Go to retirement, you are no longer nude!” The severance payment amounted to five hundred ephesas, and he was offered a position as an ethics teacher at the university. Fellion refused. In the family estate, he recreated the “image of the imperial court”: he hired servants dismissed by Minerva, and established a turquoise guard of eight people. Ceremoniesmaster and manager of it is the Cell.

**Kids.** Fellion has two sons and a daughter. The sons remained in General Davey's regiment and refused the request for money: "We find it foolish of you to make a copy of the yard, and we do not want to encourage your madness with money." ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) After that, Fellion changed his will and wrote off all the property, "without a trace, in favor of his only daughter, Rhiana Fellion." He considers the daughter to be one of his two jewels: "One of them is my daughter Rihanna, and the fluoroe is the Clock of Destiny."

**The hours of destiny.** This is the [[svyashchennye-predmety|sacred object]] of the Fellion family: two azure drops flowing into each other and hanging in the air. They are stored in an underground vault behind a steel door. Only Fellion has the key to the outside door, he knows the combination of internal levers. When the money ran out, he sent a letter to a possible buyer, and instead of him came a man named Inge Price. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) The guest spoke about the rumor that Fellion was going to sell the Watch, and made it clear that only the Empress could give them. Two days later, the guest was found stabbed in a locked vault. According to Fellion himself, only he had the key, and this threatened him with a blaze. Hearing from Mac that he was "end", he fainted, and when he woke up, declared himself seriously ill and, lying in bed in a nightcap and scarf, agreed to run to [[shimmeri|Shimmery]], so as not to get on the stove.

**Solution.** Mac found out that it was not the real Price who was killed, but the thief sent by Fellion's sons, and his guard commander, Sir Rasmus, killed him, and Fellion himself was not guilty of anything. Upon learning of this, the old man immediately recovered and was ready to put Rasmus on trial, but on the advice of Mac agreed to forgive the knight and daughter. Mack also proposed to open a paid museum of court life in the estate. Fellion was still hesitant, but the Cellar convinced him, and it became clear in the face of the old man: he gave up.

## Personality
In the story, Fellion is a soft, impressionable and complaining old man. He praises his daughter as meek and obedient to her father and does not notice her affection for Rasmus. He said, “My spring has passed, and the summer has blossomed. Autumn breathes in your face.
