## Origins
The [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] Kingdom is ruled by the Council of Five - five of the richest houses that own spark eye mines and the secret of their cutting. Each of the Five chooses one of the closest relatives to master the secret and look after hired masters. Horey Quindar was the third child in the house of the Second of the Five, whose original business was tea plantations. Not hoping for an inheritance, he became a master cutter. However, the elder sister married in distant lands, “not on the Southern Way, not on the Sunset Coast”, and his father deprived his elder brother of his inheritance for the riot. So Horey suddenly received a county title.

The tea business of the new count almost did not do, but passionately took up the eyes. He went to the capital, told Emperor Telurian about the use of the eyes not only in weapons, but also in engines, machines and other machinery. As a result, the Crown half increased the purchase of eyes, and Horaeus became the hero of the Shimmerian nobility, and King Francis-Ilian himself greeted him by the hand. This story, according to the narrator, was known in Shimmery by every educated person.

## Abbot of the Abbess
The lands around the Bottomless Gap belong to the Counts of Kindar, including the rock on which the Tower-Zub stands - the citadel of the Maximian Order. ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) Counts have long been patrons of the monastery. The abbot called the new count to visit, but that's why everything was too much. Only in the fourth year of his reign, around the time of the Shutov conspiracy, Horey visited the monastery. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) “Either by talking to the abbot, or by reading sacred texts, or by looking into the abyss,” he decided to change his life. In four years he graduated from the seminary and received the rank. The count generously endowed the monastery, including the Objects, and after the death of the abbot, the brothers unanimously elected him an abbot. He retained his seat on the Council of Five and the eye trade. Shimmerians said that the master cutter became “first count, then rich, and then a saint.”

The Marquis of Miral-Senelius, a vassal of Quindara, called his seigneur, along with the king-prophet, "one of the mighty roots of reason" on which the Shimmerian policy rests. According to him, before each tea party, the count pours one cup into the bottomless Failure as a gift to the gods.

## Order and Prophet
Queendar was in a secret order led by Queen Darkwater's Lady in Darkness. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) At a meeting in Darkwater, Francis-Ilianus explicitly called her "a fellow member of the secret order." ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) According to the prophet, eight years ago the Second of the Five invited him to visit, told him about the order and offered to join it. On the same night, in the monastery, Francis-Ilian had his first prophetic dream: the world bends through a funnel and crumbles into a bottomless Failure. The Prophet stayed in the monastery for eight years to understand whether the order would save the world or become a source of death himself. The Lady in the Dark, hearing this, coldly said that "The second of the Five allowed for willfulness" and it would not happen again.

## Politics in Shimmery
In 1775, the Shimmeria Council of Five split. The Duke of Farway told General [[stetkhem|Statham]], the messenger of Origin, about this when he offered to buy the eyes of the northerners. The first of the Five, Prince Hector, relied on the Fourth. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) The second of the Five "with all his might" stood for Francis-Ilianus, and the Third held for the Second. The fifth came between them. Each pair had an eye stock of about a million ephesas, and Francis-Ilianus offered Hope to buy back the stocks of the Second and Third. [[melison|Melison]]'s Burgomaster Cornelius told Harmon that the Third of the Five, the "king of winemakers", had quarreled with Hector, who had stolen his best althessa. After that, the Third repeatedly visited the prophet in the Abode of the Abyss, urged him to return to the throne and overthrow Hector and so became friends with the Second.

Adrian later accused Quindar of bypassing the Crown's monopoly by selling his eyes to the rebels. The Count replied, "Not to Origina, but to Farway! Hector's trading with Origin!" In May 1775, a regiment of Golden Swords was preparing to escort the cargo of his eyes from Pentago to Hope.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
### Skyship
In the spring of 1775, the Marquis of Miral-Senelius, on behalf of Quindar, tried to buy a celestial ship from [[kharmon|Harmon]] along with the right to a monopoly. The main purpose of the purchase, as recognized by the Marquis, was the descent to the bottom of the abyss. Harmon was supposed to go down there on the ball himself. He flew, but refused to go down, although the servant of the Marquis Booth threatened him with a knife. As he flew over the chasm, Harmon saw the bottom of the chasm through the Light Sphere. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]])

Even earlier, a captured sorcerer, buried in the cemetery, called “Tower-Tooth, Boundless Failure, Second of the Five” the place to look for the Master of the Rings. ([[mik|Mik]])

### Capturing the monastery
The mysterious Hunter - the secretly surviving emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] - intercepted Harmon and decided with his help to capture the Second of the Five. Dressed as Booth, he, along with Harmon, the inventor [[gortenzij|Hortensius]], and the archer [[chara|Chara]], descended on a ball into the courtyard of the Abode-u-Bezdna. Quindar came out to them in the robe of the abbot: a blue robe, a staff with a spiral, a mitre. When he learned that at the bottom of the Failure saw the light, he “almost jumped into the basket.” Assistant Laem sheriff Halintor demanded to give the Light Sphere, and then the Hunter hit the count under his breath and overboard. The ball took off, but crashed into the monastery wall. Quindar, fighting back, broke Harmon's face with his knee and jumped onto the wall. The fugitives fled, and the archers of the count shot them after.

The same night, four captives were brought to the monastery: the Hunter, Harmon, Hortense, and Chara. They were escorted by thirty horsemen in dark shutanas, but these were the disguised Shavans of the Gants of Biray and the warriors of the goat people of Zandur (the real monk was only Vincent). ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]) The hunter threw the ropes, and the monastery was in his hands. He ordered Kindar to pay the Shavans a thousand gold ephes, and Zandur to return the original land of the zunds with a mine of eyes. The Hunter then told the Count to write an order to the regiment of the Golden Swords to leave for forty miles along the northern road, allegedly ahead of schedule accompanying his eyes to Hope. When asked about his secret role, Quindar dissuaded: Adrian spoke with the Objects, and after him the Puppeteer. Then the Hunter cut his ear and said that the Puppeteer does not bother him, but the force that the count represents is another matter. During the night, the count told almost everything that was required of him.

In the morning, Colonel Cleon suspected something was wrong and sent an officer. Quindar, with a rope loop on his leg, went out on the wall and demanded that the regiment perform in Hope, but the mercenaries realized that the count was a hostage. Then Kindar promised Zound chief Zandur a spark mine, and the Zunds attacked the Hunter's shavanks. The count shouted to them from the window: "Davie the horses!", but Harmon crashed into him from the run and stunned him. The hunter locked himself in the tower and hung the count out the window, threatening the mercenaries, and then demanded to show the secret passage: "I will not believe that the secret master of the secret order has no alternate way to escape." Kindar swore there was no way. Then the knights of Baron Darryl and [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]] broke into the monastery, which they recognized in the Emperor's Hunter. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]])

## In "Humans and Gods"
### Advisor to the Emperor
After the capture of the monastery, in late May 1775, Adrian was busy all day and, among other things, interrogated the Second of the Five. Interrogating Quindar and the monks, he learned "something" about the Bottomless Fail, the secret order, the talking Objects, the Star, and "some gardeners." He announced that the Maximian monks were "holy men", freed the count and made him an honorary guest. In early June 1775, Adrian's army marched from the Abode-u-Bezdna across the Lion Mountains to the sea, and Quindar accompanied the emperor no longer as a prisoner, but as an adviser. In addition to him and senior officers, Adrian on the campaign almost did not want to see anyone. Every evening he came to the emperor for tea, and he poured it on Adrian himself, so that the servants would not hear. The Count swore that he had no collusion with the Puppeteer and did not order the Maximians to attack Harmon: the Traveling Monks acted of their own volition. Through the Order, Quindar received news from the north. Once he showed the officers an encrypted message from the capital’s monastery of the order with his translation: the heretics robbed the tomb of Ingrid, the Chamber deposed Adrian, Minerva rules temporarily.

When [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]], having kidnapped Nisa, demanded from Harmon to find out the secret of Adrian, he in despair advised her to grab and interrogate the Second of the Five, because it was from him that the lord learned the secret. ([[niza|Niza]]) Harmon, in revenge for his troubles, dispelled at court conflicting rumors about Adrian's plans. It was said that he wanted to move the capital to the Steppe, abolish knighthood, fly to the Star and raise talking Objects from the bottom of the Gap. According to one of the rumors, the Second of the Five found in the writings of the Forefathers a secret record of Elena the Traveler that Rei-Roy was also founded by the Foremothers, and on the other - his monks learned to control the weather, like Foremother Lyudmila, so that the heavenly ships would always blow a tailwind. Shavans then accompanied the count with such a look as if he were the most important spirit from the horde of Wanderers. When the officers began to demand what was promised, Quindar slammed his palm on the table: “The sun was asleep, what nonsense!” Adrian admitted that he was to blame for the fog, but the purpose of the name refused: the secret is not his, and only the council of masters of the order can remove the seal from his mouth. The second of the Five solemnly affirmed:
> "No secret in history has been so sacred as the purpose of our order."

### "Analysis and Synthesis"
Harmon, hiding at the wall of the tent, overheard the conversation between the count and Adrian about the manuscript of the Forefather Evrian "Analysis and Synthesis". Quindar called it “the child of our order”: the original was destroyed under Wilhelm, and the order preserved and reproduced the text. The Count explained that in the “transporter” described by Evrian it is not speed that is important, but the principle of “deconstruction” of Objects, their “spraying” and “evaporation”. According to him, “our scientists” have developed two versions of what a conveyor is, including that it is a composite object. He assumed that it was him who was collecting the Puppeteer. Through an agent of the order surrounded by Count Fleming, Quindar knew that Fort Kuklavoda in the Beyond had disappeared without a trace. According to the scientists of the order, this meant that the Items can be destroyed by “forced deconstruction” with the help of a deconstructor tool, although the order did not know what it looked like.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Wedding in Grace and the embassy with a casket
In July 1775, Quindar and his monks sailed on Adrian's ship across the East Sea to Grace, where the emperor celebrated a wedding with Magda Labelin; the monks of the Second of the Five discussed which rite was better for her, capital or south. From Grace, Adrian sent Kindar and Baron Darryl as ambassadors to [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. They were accompanied by Harmon and the Shavans guarding the richly decorated casket. Harmon considered him a "gift" to the empress. On the way, the count did not step in when the Baron’s men humiliated and beat Harmon. In the inn "Jack and Jane" Harmon euthanized the ambassadors and ran with a casket to [[minerva|Minerva]]. In the casket were two Light Spheres, genuine and fake, and the [[poyas-kolduna|Sorcerer's Belt]], as well as a letter from Adrian. The gift was not meant for Minerva at all, but for Queen Madeline, that is, Lady in Darkness, and the “gift” was Harmon himself: the Sphere once came to life in his hands. In the letter, the emperor wrote: "From your associate, Count Quindar, I learned about the aims of the secret order." Harmon fled while Quindar, Baron Darryl, and his knights slept at the inn table.

### At the court of Adrian
In August 1775, when Adrian occupied the abandoned Minerva [[faunterra|Faunterra]], Quindar returned to him with Lady in Darkness, Baron Darryl and Sir Peter. Since then, the Second of the Five has constantly accompanied Lady in the Dark, "his fellow order", and led a blind queen under his hand. Adrian greeted him with the words "My friend, glad to see you!" When the queen first met the emperor, Quindar embarrassedly informed the emperor of two "nuances" - with the Sphere and with the visitor, and the queen explained that the visitor had confided in Minerva. In early September, at Faunterra, at the request of Adrian, he presented items taken from prisoner [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]] to the Queen. In October 1775, during Adrian's march across the South Road to the north, he participated in experiments with the Womb on the imperial train: Juhan was told to say a command to the Womb to swallow the rat, and when the experiment failed, the count muttered: "I'm sorry." ([[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]; [[chrevo|The Womb]]) Then he told Adrian that through the channels of the order received the message: the prophet goes to them and “drives what is required.” Together with Francis-Ilian and Adrian, he interpreted prophetic dreams. Once he even objected to the emperor that a dream about a master who became a cripple predicted failure, not success. Adrian told [[karen|Karen]] that he had been thinking about the universe for many years and was ready when the Second of the Five told him the answer. In November 1775, in Main, Quindar, along with Adrian and the prophet, observed how, after a series of experiments, the Bird without the Flesh first came into action. ([[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]])

During the Battle of the First Winter, Quindar, along with the court, watched the battle from Main. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) He was the first to learn about the city of the heavenly ship, asked how the ball and the Glove will affect the fight, noted that Minerva did not cope with the control, and after the defeat of the sunsets, he asked where the Northerners got the Ring. While Bird was floating over the First Winter without the Flesh, he whispered to the blind queen everything he could see. ([[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) The most important of his observations was that the city was “stuffed with people”. When [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] was imprisoned in [[chrevo|the Womb]], the count cried out, "Bravo! It worked!" But Johan Reis, who was supposed to say the command to the Womb, collapsed dead. Queendar whispered in the Queen's ear, and she replied that it was Black Mary's pill. Adrian said that together with Francis Ilianus and Count Quindar, he spent hundreds of hours over the sacred texts, trying to understand whether the Absolute would protect against sparks.

## Role in the Order
Quindar's membership in the order is attested directly. Francis-Ilianus called him Lady-in-the-Dark's brother in order. Quindar himself spoke of “our order” and “our scientists.” Adrian wrote of him as the Queen's "companion", and the narrative calls him her "companion of the order". The Maximian monks, according to Adrian, are “subjects of our new friend the Second of the Five, and therefore of a secret order.” Father David acknowledged that the "Maximian brothers participate" in the order, and the Forefather Maximian was one of the [[sadovniki|Gardeners]]. Adrian called the Second of the Five "Master of the Sorcerer", and having captured the monastery, "the secret master of the secret order." Adrian himself said that he believed in the word “masters of the order”.

## Personality
Quindar is a gusty, gambling and enthusiastic southerner. Harmon compared him to Onoriko's "happiness architect", "only ten times harder". ([[onoriko|Onoriko-Maysor]]) Riza and the staff, according to Harmon's observation, were "all that was holy in him." Under the knife, he quickly surrendered to Adrian, but showed no resentment and became his sincere supporter. He is suspicious: when eavesdropping, he was the first to catch Harmon's snoring behind the tent wall.
