## Biography

### Tournament Fighter

Craig is the heir to [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]] County, one of the Empire's strongest fighters and a multiple tournament winner. Nicknamed the Fangish Knight, he received for a black tournament helmet in the form of a broken bear mouth with silver fangs. In the summer of 1773 at the tournament in Aleridan he became one of the winners of the day of the originals, but in the final bouts he was knocked out of the saddle by Sir Lauren of Hope. ([[turnir-v-aleridane|Tournament at Aleridan]]; [[loren|Sir Loren]]) In the story of this tournament, he is called the second son of the count, although in the rest of the books of the cycle Craig is the eldest son and heir.

Already in 1756, Craig was an adult and married man: together with his father, young stepmother and brothers, he came to the celebrations of the Seventeenth Gift in [[uejmar|Waymar]], laughed at the sight of the abundant supplies prepared by young Vittor, and asked from the threshold whether they had left with Lord Origin and Almer. At the feast, among the few ladies was his young wife; her name and fate are not named.

### In "Only One Star"

In the fall of 1774, the Northwoods, on Adrian's call, rode to the capital and were detained in Waymar by the vassals of [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. Craig tried to force a letter by grabbing the secretary's neckline, but his father told him to sit quietly, "We're in a trap." [[minerva|Mira]] decided that, unlike her father, Craig knew about her stepmother's plans: it was on him that [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] allegedly relied, counting on the death of the old count to share power with her stepson. Later, however, Donald assured Mira: “my brother and I did not know what the stepmother was up to.”

When Vittor showed the Northwoods a letter about the arrest of Sybil and [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]], [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]], without convincing the old count, turned to his son. Craig called Adrian a "power-hungry scumbag" and was indignant: isn't Gloria not good enough for the emperor? Mira tried to warn him that they, like a ram, would break through the wall for Erwin, and then be asked to kill his father; Craig replied that Erwin is a boy, but a northerner, and "a northerner knows how to love the North." The desperate cry of his father that his son would perish with all his army only pushed him:

> "I'm not one to run from danger, Father. The Northwoods are not cowards."

Craig announced that he would bring Northwood's army to [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]'s aid to make it to the capital "before Adrian kills Sybil and Gloria." The old Earl Elias was left in Waymar as an honorary prisoner. Brother Donald, who brought Erwin the news of the performance of the bears, explained that his father “retired from military affairs” and the army is ruled by the Fangish.

Thirteen thousand selected warriors, four thousand of them horsemen, crossed the Smokey Dahl. In Waymar, the sailors recounted a rumor that a Fang Knight with five thousand horsemen and ten thousand infantry was coming to the mutineer. Erwin expected the Northwood route to pass through Doyle, and at the beginning of November 1774 at a council discussed whether to take the city for an ally; Damon offered to leave "this sadness" to Klykastoy, and in the end the Northmen hit Labelin without waiting for the bears.

At the end of November, Craig and his brother joined Erwin in a busy Labelin. Erwin wanted to sit in town and wait for the Silver Fox to come storming Labelin himself, at least until February. Craig laughed at the plan at the council, banged his fist on the table and growled that he was not an vassal to Origin, but an ally and would do what he himself thought was right: "Northwood is not a coward", he would not wait for the capital court to execute Sybil and Gloria, and Erwin, if he wanted, could sit in the Labelin alone. He was supported by Count Lilliday, Colonel Horton and other commanders, and Erwin, realizing that the ally did not obey him, gave up and led the army from the city to meet the Silver Fox. ([[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]]) He later said to himself, "You shouldn't have listened to Craig and left town." On the morning of December 2, at Pixie's, when the whole Imperial army appeared, Craig roared in the staff tent: "Decisive battle! Victory or death!” At the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]] (during the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]) he disobeyed Army Commander [[stetkhem|Statham]] and broke into the rear sparks, but he crept into the reserve spark regiments, was cut off and captured with his brother Donald; to his aid without orders rushed Count Main the Hammer, who died in this battle. Donald had his jaw cut in that battle. After the victory of the rebels in the night Labelin, where the bear regiments were commanded by Count Lilliday, thousands of prisoners of the North were released, among them Donald, and Craig the Silver Fox managed to take with him. ([[reznya-v-labeline|Night in Labelin]])

### A Doll on the Throne.

From captivity, according to Erwin, he released both brothers himself. By the winter, both brothers were in the army in the capital: in January 1775, twelve thousand kire and eight thousand bears stood on the left bank of the Hanai, and after the victory Erwin deployed troops around Faunterra so that the bears, "as a shield", covered him from the threat from Almera. On the dog's court run for Northwood, Craig's younger brother ruled the sled. Erwin approved for the Northwoods the lands of the South Road, along which their army passed, and gave them half of the trophies of the night Labelin.

At the end of February 1775, while seeking clemency for Sybil and Gloria, Erwin explained to Minerva that the county of Northwood was practically without power: old Elias was powerless, and Craig was only interested in the army, and he would soon have to burn his own cities to feed the soldiers; Sybil, unlike Craig, will be completely subject to the Crown. In the theater, the brothers dressed up in the colors of the volcano and laughed themselves, as if they looked like the rude Carrog. ([[liola-i-karrog|Liola and Karrog]]) After the Northerners won, Craig and Donald demanded trophies, possessions in the South Road, and court positions; Erwin complained to his sister that he wrote fairy tales every day to keep "these bonebreakers" away from the control of the empire. The bears were encamped on Canaan. Craig complained to Minerva that Origin had taken the trophies of the South Road, and thousands of bears had died at Pixie; Sybil's pardon, which the brothers attributed to Erwin, he accepted, but growled: "But Sybil herself where? “And Gloria?”

When the runaway Znacharka died in a Northwood camp, Craig demanded her body and a miraculous Object. ([[znakharka|The Healer]]) In a rage, he accused Erwin of "getting everything" - the palace, the capital, the treasury - holding an ally "like a dog" and not sharing fame. He reproached Erwin that he had promised him Sybil, Gloria and fame, but gave him nothing, and threatened to resolve the dispute with a duel; Jemis and [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] were ready to fight the bear, but Erwin understood that any outcome of the fight was unprofitable for him. In payment for the Item Craig demanded a special spark regiment of Northwoods under the leadership of brother Donald, and himself - the title of imperial general and commander of the spark troops of the Crown. Erwin conceded, and then explained to his sister and Minerva that the appointment would rather hurt Craig himself: he would get caught in a conflict with the empress and the generals. At a military council before the suppression of the Snowdrops, Craig hit General Gore in the jaw, and Erwin "by the power of the Crown" appointed him interim commander-in-chief. The new ruler Craig swore without any gratitude - neither for the position, nor for the pardon of Sybil; Donald apologised to Mira that the Northwoods were "as if scoundrels" against her.

During the [[vosstanie-podsnezhnikov|Snowdrop Rebellion]], Craig insisted on a frontal attack by heavy cavalry. On the Holy Field, a detachment under his coats of arms tried to block the path of the Empress's carriage, but she rushed through the formation; then [[minerva|Minerva]], reminding Craig of the letter received the day before, ordered the bears to retreat. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]])

### In "Yanmei Hunt" and "Humans and Gods"

In the House of Representatives, when the Duke of Labelin demanded the return of the lost land, Craig roared "Shame on the ticket!" and demanded to immediately vote and reject his claims - the Northwoods themselves seized a patch along the coast of the Smoky Distance from the Southern Way. A more intelligent brother pulled his sleeve, and the confused Fang refused to vote. At the end of May, when the House was discussing the collapse of the bridge and the finalization of the train passengers, Craig was furiously punching him on the stand, and he was hardly calmed down; and when the joker Manson announced to the House that Adrian was alive, Craig threw out in confusion: “What, really?” — what caused the joke a new bout of laughter. In the spring of 1775, Craig feasted in the capital at the expense of war trophies and enjoyed the title of commander-in-chief, and the county, meanwhile, was in turmoil: the war ate a considerable share of the budget, the peasants and barons were dissatisfied, and the governor Horace barely suppressed the rebellion. Minerva's advisers believed that Craig was only interested in the army, ignoring other issues. The old Count Elias, in conversation with Iona, called his son a bear, whom the gods cursed, giving strength from birth: everything was given to him by force, and the mind did not develop as unnecessary. According to his father, two-thirds of Northwood warriors adored Craig, and to call him the heir to Horace would mean signing the death sentence. Iona, reporting that Erwin and the mistress were very unhappy with Craig, offered to help the old man return to power, but Elias refused: he had enough politics. Meanwhile, the Northwood barons resented Craig's rule, and in the summer of 1775 this became one of the arguments for sending a spark regiment to the North. Sybil, who had lost her memory after learning that her stepson was enjoying influence in the capital, wanted to write to him to help find Gloria, but Navi dissuaded her.

### "The Shadow of the Great Tree"

In the summer of 1775, Craig marched north with Origin from Faunterra. There was not enough ships for the entire Allied army, so Desmond, with Colonel [[blekberri|Blackberry]] and four battalions, moved forward on the water, and Craig, with the bears and General Statham's kires, went around Lake Dymnaya Dahl. On the way, he was going to collect tribute from the cities of the Southern Way under his control and arranged feasts, fist fights and tournaments, but Statham convinced him to refuse entertainment, and the army moved along the northern shore of the Smoky Distance by an accelerated march. Craig with the regiments went to Waymar to help the besieged city of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]]: the bear regiments had to block the bays around Waymar in order to prevent [[paul|Paul]] from landing. Upon learning that Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]] had brought Old Elias' Bear to the Fang by order of Vittor and declared him lawful ruler, Craig, contrary to General Statham's advice, turned the army home. ([[klyk-medvedya|Bear's Fang]]) Due to his departure, Paul's corps landed unimpeded at Waymar, and Desmond had to lift the siege when the city was two days from the fall.

At the walls of his capital, Craig threatened to kill everyone, gave a volley of crossbows and sent rams to the gate, which Dorcaster burned with a feather. Then the Fangish threatened to set fire to the wooden city, but the baron replied that he would sail on a ship and sink the rest of the fleet. For two weeks, Dorcaster fooled Craig with power-sharing negotiations until South Northwood submitted to Wittor and then surrendered the castle and left. After returning the Bear Fang in September, Craig beat his father - the old man had a broken nose and a skewed jaw - and walled it up with Horace in the dungeon, then moved against Vittor again.

In early October 1775, Craig with twelve thousand soldiers (according to Erwin's estimate, ten thousand bears) finally came to the rescue of Desmond at Fox Dol. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) Fighting for the "Chosen One" [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]], nicknamed Ice, understood that the allies were preparing a blow from both sides, and suggested that the first move towards the bears: Craig, enraged by the deception of Dorcaster, rushed "shaking his head, forgetting about caution", and Ice expected to rush at him suddenly. Count Fleming and Dorcaster supported the plan. Desmond took the fight without preparation and coordination with Craig, hoping for a numerical advantage and the opportunity to free Iona from the wagon. At night, the bears secretly led the troops through the thicket and attacked the Chosen Camp with battle axes, but they were swept away by the fire of the Rings and the counterattack of the sunsets. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) The battle ended in defeat: Craig Northwood was killed, thousands of his bears surrendered to Richard, many went over to the side of Shayland, and the county returned under the rule of Elias - essentially a puppet of the Puppeteer. In the Bear Fang, which passed from hand to hand several times over the year, the old earl's supporters — the "grays" brandishing a white streak — after Craig's death beat and robbed his followers, the "drums", and shouted about the deceased: "He was a ram! That's the road!"

Erwin blamed Craig for the defeat of the “cursed fool”: he first distracted by a quarrel with the old count, and then, returning to Desmond, his arrival pushed him to abandon the plan and take the fight in the forest, and finally, “he had enough stupidity to die in battle” - a good half of the Craig swords served for the sake of profit and now went to the enemy.

## Personality and Appearance

Craig is a broad-shouldered black-haired giant with a thundering roar, looking like a real bear; his fists were the size of a mug for ale. He is quick-tempered, pugnacious, and straightforward; in argument he prefers a fist, and in battle he prefers a reckless attack. Erwin called him a "healthy dumb boner", and in conversation with Minerva - "a loud savage, a dream of immature girls"; Dorcaster mockingly praised Craig as "a wise man beaten repeatedly with a mace on his helmet." Craig is proud of the North and the honor of the family and sincerely wanted to save his stepmother and sister. His support in the Bear Fang was soldiers and bandits - well-armed, but small.

## Relationships

- **[[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]]**, the father who thought his son was a fool Craig did not listen to him in 1774, and in 1775 he beat and imprisoned him.
- **[[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]]** is a stepmother; according to Elias, she was smart enough to rule and charisma to cope with Craig. Mira believed he was involved in her plot, but Donald denied it.
- Donald is the younger brother taken prisoner with him at Pixie and standing next to him on the council when Craig hit Gore.
- Horace is a middle brother, a county judge; in 1775, Craig imprisoned him with his father.
- **[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]** is an ally that Craig both despised as a "boy" and respected as a northerner.
