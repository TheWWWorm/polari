## Origin and service

Mark Frida Stanley is a commoner, the son of a shoemaker from a provincial town. Stanley was his father’s name; his mother’s grandmother, whom his mother feared as a fire, Mark, in his own words, did not even know by name: his mother married just to escape from her. Since childhood, he was taught to consider himself a small sucker: to remove the hat in front of the knight, to give way to a carriage with valves and not to raise an eye to the original lady.

At court, he served seventeen years, going from a junior agent through the positions of interrogator, brigadier, special investigator and assistant chief of the secret guard - to the Crown Raven, head of the patronage. ([[protektsiya|The Protection]]) At this summit he held out for six years: three years under Lord Telurian and three years under [[adrian|Adrian]]. During his years of service, he heard the cries of tortured knights, saw the executions of nobles, read reports about the tricks of the first-born ladies - and quietly lost every piet before the nobility: only "people and people" remained for him, some sew boots, others wear a wenzel.

His subordinates at his request called him a "cheef"; noble opponents - contemptuously a "shoemaker". Mark disliked cars and believed in only one genuine evidence - a conclusion. The only one before whom he really was timid was Adrian: Mark did not doubt that the lord knows everything, and his will is the measure of correctness.

### Childhood in the Bishop's Christmas Tree

The story “The Bishop’s Christmas Tree” is dedicated to the twelve-year-old Marek, the son of a drinking shoemaker Stanley, who worked as an apprentice in his father’s workshop for four years and dreamed of becoming a judge, as the hero of the book “Notes of the Judge”. On New Year's Eve, Marek and his friend Josh, the boatman's son (he called Marek "Rick"), climbed into the bishop's garden for a Christmas tree for Josh's girlfriend and overheard the bishop hiring a murderer against the burgomaster. Unwilling to go to the sheriff without evidence, Marek came up with the idea to pass three anonymous notes to the mayor through different scribes. Notes passed the noble girl who rescued the boys from the guard: she was the daughter of a burgomaster. Burgomaster survived and left friends in a hole under the cat on the monument four Hilt; Marek gave half to Josh, even though they had a fight. For one hilt he bought a textbook and literacy lessons. Later, his father froze to death, falling asleep in the snow; the workshop was taken for debts, and Marek with three hidden ephes went to the capital, where he never got to the university and did not become a judge - but, as it is said in the final, he became "someone more important than a shoemaker." The name "Mark" in the story is not named, but coincidences (son of a shoemaker Stanley, departure to the capital, a look "from the bottom up" to the nobility twenty years later) suggest that this is the future Raven, although this is not directly said. In the epilogue “Shadows of the Great Tree” detective Mac recalls that “just thirty years ago” began his first business — also in the winter holiday, on New Year’s Day.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

### The case of Sir Adamar

In the spring of 1774, Mark investigated the death of Sir Adamar, the emperor's cousin. Without a report and without a title ("just - Mark"), he appeared to Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] and, standing close to her, listed the former victims of the conspiracy - the Play Elder, Lady in Darkness and the "poor [[minerva|Minerva]] of Stagforth" - following the reaction. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) Countess Gloria's imaginary daughter (in fact Minerva Stagforth) gave herself a sigh, and Mark noticed that her prank with Ser Adamar was too reminiscent of his own methods. As he was leaving, he brazenly finished the wine from the cup of the Countess.

At first, the Raven suspected that the Countess and her daughter themselves had arranged the murder for the sake of influence at court. At a meeting in the tea parlor, the emperor summoned Mark, and he vowed to find the culprit in two weeks. On a stormy day, Mark invited Mira to his unpretentious black carriage and admitted that he had removed suspicions from her: her stupid prank with bear skin and frivolous behavior at the ball did not get involved with a calculating criminal. He told her that she was more conceited than all three imperial brides put together — only her conceit was to be cleverer in secret — and Mira considered it the best compliment of her life. A game of information exchange ensued between them: Mark made a promise to her to name the conspirator if she found him first. Mira told him about Captain Corvis and Duke Aiden Almer's secret; ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) Mark responded that the men who attacked her and Becca in the woods were found stabbed in the sewer drain. ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]])

### Aiden Almer 's

Mark later explained to Mira at her bedside how he had uncovered the plot. Her hints at Labelin he found intrigue in favor of a friend, but he was hooked by her fleeting belief that the suspicion could be removed from [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]], but not from his children. Taking [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], Mark learned about the February meeting in the tea salon, which he was not informed until June, and reported his findings to Adrian. Mark raised the records of twenty years ago on the Shutov conspiracy: the testimony of two jailers word for word coincided with the story of Aiden. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) One of them drowned a month after the trial, the second received the village and became a petty lord - but, being literate, he wrote down the truth and hid it as insurance. From the letter it followed that Aiden had been hiding the impending coup from the emperor for a month. Only now Mira understood the clue that the Raven gave her in the carriage: for the current murders, Aiden hired rascals from the slums, because the goal was not to kill for sure, but to scare the yard and the Chamber. If the Duke is tried, he will lose his title and power over Almera. Mark said that he does not approve of torture: under them a person will name any names.

Aiden and [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] managed to escape from the capital. Mark admitted that without the help of Mira would not have coped on time, and offered her any reward; she asked only to tell the lord kind words about Becca, and for himself - a party in execution with the emperor. In farewell, he shook her hand as an associate, and revealed that Secretary [[itan|Ethan]] was his agent.

## In "Only One Star"

The old coachman Jonas Holly Martin, who was silent under the beatings, Mark talked about his daughter Lizzie, and he, defending Alanis, threw him a tip on the "red" - the daughter of the Bear. In addition to Aiden's apparent servants and guards, the protection captured sixty-five of his secret agents; Mark ordered the release of all those who can somehow be justified, keeping the list: so he built his own network of people who were kept by the threat of the court. At the weekly Thursday tea party, he reported to Adrian: of the accomplices of the traitor at court, forty-two people are waiting for trial, fifty-eight are released, but taken into account, thirty-nine repented and became protection agents.

At the same time, Mark investigated the disappearance from the imperial archive of the April letterhead and the murder of the printer Jake from Godwin, who dialed the July “[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]”. He built a hypothesis: the attacker etched the text from the original letterhead with seals and signatures, produced a fake letterhead and backed it with a fake Voice page to provoke a war between the lords. Mark asked that all July grants be cancelled and that a general denial be circulated; Adrian agreed only to prepare a rebuttal, and rejected the cancellation of the July grants. The captain of the scarlet guard Croydon Backfield, who called protection before the lord a “useless pack of jackals”, Mark lured a false spree to himself and teased with pleasure. He was disturbed by the strangeness of the Northwood family: the sudden exile of "Gloria" in the monastery and suspiciously empty behind the impeccable manners of the bride of the lord. ### Opal

At the next tea party, Adrian showed him the ultimatum of Erwin Origin, of which the secret guard knew nothing, accused Mark of having overlooked the mutiny and defending Sybil, and sent him to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] - formally an ambassador, in fact with the task of killing the rebellious duke. The network of agents was handed over to Backfield, and Ethan was exiled with Mark. Mark experienced fear and resentment, but not doubt: if the Lord exiled, then it was for what.

Along the way, in the basement at Elfort, Mark gave Ethan his full name and advised him to flee; he refused. They drove into Elena's boarding house by the Crystal Mountains, where Gloria Northwood studied for three years, posing as Lord Ethan, looking for a place for her younger sister. Mark sent Ethan to the imperial dovehouse to warn the lord, and he went on.

### Prisoner of the First Winter

In the First Winter, Mark, helping to pour wine, was able to poison Erwin, but did not take advantage of it. The Duke suspected something was wrong, put the bowl away and under the knife of the sentinel forced Mark to drink from it; there was no poison in the wine. Mark admitted that he was sent to kill Erwin, but chose to first ask him two questions - whether the Bear in bed is good and what happened in the Beyond. Erwin showed him Adrian's letters about [[pozhar-evergarda|Evergard]] and the end of the [[velikie-doma|Great Houses]], listened to the advice to surrender, and then ordered to search the ambassador; finding the poison capsules in the hollow buttons and gate, he was given to interrogators. Mark was tortured "for wear" - starting with needles and drops of alkali and gradually intensifying the pain - pestering about the spark regiments, [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade and Perstakh; on the third day Mark spoke. When he woke up, he found that he had lost surprisingly little: his hands, feet, eyes and ears were intact. The feet were pierced with "nails or something like that", four fingers were broken (three on the left hand and one on the right), the skin was covered with a "grid of wounds" and burns from the broken rod, but only one wound was serious - a strip of bare meat on the arm. This is Mark’s only scar.

In prison, he was visited by [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], who brought painkillers and asked for help. Mark told him that in the monastery of Ulyana Sad in the west of Almera, without the consent of the count, he was kept by a "very valuable vassal", - so Minerva was saved. She visited the prisoner and [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] - in scarlet silk, with a jug of wine and a crystal vase of fruit. She said that Erwin admired his courage and that Mark would be fed from the soldier's kitchen and examined by a doctor, but she could not let him go. When Iona left, Mark threw the vase into the wall with pleasure. Adrian offered Erwin a ransom for the prisoner, but the duke replied that Mark was dead.

### Beyond the River

In the autumn, at [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]]'s request, set forth by the hand of [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Origin]], Mark was taken to the northern flotilla of Count [[fleming|Benedict Fleming]], who was taking care of [[reka|the River]], in a gray petty-bourgeois cloak, to obtain information about the Beyond. [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] nearly killed the "poisoner", but Mark whispered to him a phrase Desmond had said to the kair in private, and he agreed to guard him and his mission. On the ship "The Seal", Mark suffered from sea sickness, and then became friends with the sailors - Larry, Descendant, Salt and Gray John-John - beat them in the bones and became a famous storyteller about the capital and the yard. He learned from the sailors that Fleming himself fell into disfavor with Erwin and was forgiven with the condition to bring speaking Objects from across the River.

At the mouth of the River, Count Fleming and Abbot Hosch questioned Mark about the faith of people beyond the River; pinned to the wall, he improvised that Adrian was a "messenger of the gods", and the pride of the Origins was false, and thereby deeply embarrassed the pious count. Along with Jemis, Moy's kire and their greys, Mark participated in a night raid on a mysterious fort. Inside they found a pile of Rings and Objects near the dried corpse, and in the barn - two prisoners, [[gvenda|Gwenda]] and Luis Maria. ([[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]]) When Fleming's men entered the fort, the one with the cotton disappeared without a trace, leaving a funnel, and more than a hundred soldiers disappeared with it.

Mark interrogated both prisoners. Gwenda did not remember anything except the name, but warned him that Jemis and Mark himself were in danger of death. When Fleming's cues Hedin and Tuff abused Gwenda, Mark pointed out her resemblance to the northern noblewoman, and Jemis got an excuse to call the rapist to a match for the honor of the "northern lady". In the morning, Lieutenant Fleming demanded Mark to the Count; Jemis secretly slipped him an iron spark needle, a gift from his father. Fleming decided to go over to Adrian's side, capture the First Winter and send the heads of the elder Origins to the emperor, and make Mark his envoy. Realizing that without Desmond the war could not be stopped, Mark pricked a cyre with a needle, put a spike to the earl's throat, then hit Fleming with a spark and threw himself into the icy sea through the knocked out window of the White Star. He spent ten minutes in the icy water and reached the Seal, where the kairs and sailors, meanwhile, fought off the Count's men.

At the Seal, Mark assembled Jemis, Moy, Captain [[dzheff-bamber|Jeff Bamber]], Boseman Bull, Descendant, Louis, and Gwenda and outlined his "strangenesses". The villains in the fort always wore red uniforms, as if on purpose; the dove will not reach the Beyond, which means that the order to burn Evergard could not reach there on time without the talking Subject; Adrian would not have given Mark poison instead of Perst if he had owned the Persts. Conclusion: behind the war is a third force that wants Yanmei and Agatha to destroy each other, and Adrian only took Evergard, so as not to look powerless.

## "Speaking to God"

The interlude, told by a sailor named Descendant, describes how the Seal, fleeing Fleming's men, ran aground off the coast of [[oridzhin-zemlya|Origin]], and sailors with kairs reached the First Winter on foot. When Jemis told him to make a bracelet from the fort work, Mark tried everything and estimated it at forty thousand ephes; during the battle at the burning bridge over the gorge, the Baby of the Gods bracelet came to life in Gwenda's hands and said "On the connection". Mark realized that it was the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]] - a means of communication with the master of the fort, and, changing his voice, played out a comedy before an unknown interlocutor: on behalf of the simple-minded shepherd "Stanley" and his wife "Frida", he "prayed to God", begging for health, children and money - in order to ambush the enemy's messengers.

At the mountain outpost, Mark learned that Erwin had taken the capital, and Adrian had died in a train crash on the bridge. He turned pale and said only “Worth” and that now for him it is a matter of honor. In the First Winter, he reported everything to Lady Sophia; she agreed that Adrian was clean before the gods, but she herself entered into negotiations with the owner of the Rings, asking to heal her husband, and thereby canceled Mark's plan. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) Mark was not given a title or money — only forgiven for what he called a duchess fool.

### Train and Whitmore

On the way to the capital, Grandfather reproached Mark with cowardice: The Raven is always looking for a stronger master - Adrian, Erwin, now a gang - although fear serves him as a spur for the mind. The train was stopped by logs on the tracks, and the detachment in blue coats captured the passengers; My granddaughter was shot in the thigh. In the manor, Mark decided that he was talking to Paul himself, but it turned out to be the captain of the azure guard [[uitmor|Whitmore]] and Major Backfield, who themselves hunted the kidnappers. In the morning, after listening to his grandfather's apology, Mark presented his hypothesis to him as an impartial judge: the rebellion was only a grandiose distraction for the sake of theft, and the goal of the Master of the Rings is the Absolute, a collective Object, probably giving immortality. Among the suspects in Erwin's inner circle, he named Jemis, Alanis and Iona; this hypothesis was not confirmed.

## The “Janmei Hunt”

### The Crown Raven Again

In the spring of 1775, Erwin in the masquerade guise of “The Bad King” met with Mark (“Platk”), Grandfather (“Grey”) and Granddaughter (“Baby”) in the tavern “Rust Rail” and played a scene from a gangster novel. Mark reported that the abductee took the "main scumbag" to collect immortality from the Objects, but had not yet succeeded, and the spark was not given to the [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]] by him, but by the "Fat Dolphin" Labelin through [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]]. Erwin gave him 24 hours to check, and then appointed him head of protection; His grandfather was his assistant. Mark was instructed to take control of all the entrances to the city before the opening of the Chamber, as well as two cases - about the resurrected joker [[menson|Manson]] and about the poisoning of Lady in Darkness. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) In mid-May, Mark and Grandfather led the interception of pigeon mail: their people shot down birds over the palace, and among the letters removed were messages to the Earl of Shayland with the question “how to be in connection with the Light Sphere?”. The investigation into the poisoning on the train came to a standstill: the interrogation of the servants did not give anything, no one was allowed to the dying Lady in the Dark, and Erwin demanded to find the poisoner before her death. About the Manson case, Mark reported that the joke was “end”: seven of the nine judges were appointed by Telurian and remembered the Shutov plot, but admitted that he did not see Manson’s motive. Erwin appointed him as Crown's representative at the trial and told him not to interfere with the acquittal if the defendant turned out to be innocent. When Minerva announced the census of Objects, Erwin ordered to intercept and copy all the letters of all the Lords of the House to find among them a message to the Puppeteer; Mark and Grandpa were in charge of the intercept, and only kair Horton was allowed to open the letters. At meetings, Mark, Grandfather and Kair [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]] formed Erwin's nearby headquarters in the hunt for the Puppeteer. According to Erwin, Raven had "half a thousand subordinates".

### Trial of Manson

At the Manson trial, Mark acted as the Crown's prosecutor. ([[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]]) When Francis-Ilianus, proving the insanity of the defendant, asked him to drink a potion of a bubble, which Manson had been drinking for nineteen years, Mark finished it and announced to the audience that it was “crystal clear water”. At first he doubted the guilt of the jester, but watching Manson during the testimony of fishermen, made sure that he killed Adrian, and Erwin allowed him to seek a sentence, so long as the defendant was not prevented from defending himself. He repeated the identification in natural light, forced the fishermen's witnesses to strike the wooden mannequin with a pen, showing that they had seen the blow, and made the identification of the weapon behind the screen where the Empress put the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]]. Under pressure from [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], who had noticed the scars on his hand, Mark admitted that Adrian had ordered him to kill Erwin and that these were signs of torture in the First Winter. Referring to the code of Olgard, he demanded for Manson death as for a vassal who wounded the commander on the eve of the battle. Later, after Erwin's testimony, Mark put forward a version that the fishermen's testimony had been falsified by the men of Earl Arrowback and Baron Bonegan, and Minerva ordered Bonegan's arrest.

## "The Shadow of the Great Tree"

### Navi and Puppeteer

In the summer of 1775, Mark reported to Minerva on the fall of Fliss and the death of Pramateri [[mat-kordeliya|Cordelia]] and suggested that [[navi|Navi]] be declared a god as opposed to Paul: "what difference does it make whether he is a god?" The defense found out who Navi was: Master Mullin of Faraday-Riley Hospital sent a conclusion about the "acute asymmetry of the mind", and the Crow people unsuccessfully overran the judicial archives for 1763–1765. At a meeting with Navi himself, Mark played rough flattery to him, repenting before the “god” for people’s doubts; Navi called himself the “god of navigation.”

Mark advised Minerva not to forgive anyone else - neither the Shavans, nor the Snowdrops, nor General Smile - and admitted that he himself was afraid of Adrian: he sent him north for one mistake, and he would not forgive the enemy for his service. He offered to eliminate Adrian through the secret passages of the rebuilt palace by the hands of Inji Price or Navi; having been refused, he replied: "In vain. It will be you.” At the same audience, he brought a legless kair of [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], which Navi cured.

When [[kharmon|Harmon]] and his companions with the Light Sphere came to the palace, the Crown Raven personally brought them to Minerva; at the sight of the Sphere, Navi grabbed the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]] and fled. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) While interrogating Harmon, Mark threw the Sphere into the bushes for the sake of experience and put Red to see who would pick it up, and then suggested that the merchant himself wanted to kill Minerva, and Navi protected her. Interrogation was interrupted by Robert Origin and Forty-Two, who hired Harmon to build balloons. Navi Mark found the testimony of people at the stations of stagecoaches in the town of Patrick's Chapel in Canaan, not reaching Arden.

### Patrick's Chapel and Resignation

Having received a secret commission from Minerva, the Raven asked for a reward not a letter of credit, but several credentials from different departments for different names - to meet Adrian elsewhere and under a different name. He persuaded her to release Inge Price and [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]], recalling that Adrian would execute the Countess, and Minerva would not forgive himself; having received a bill, he released Sybil.
