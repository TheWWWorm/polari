## The “Yanmei Hunt”

### Moger Buckley
In March 1775, on the eve of [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]'s arrival at Laem, Deputy Halintor alerted her agent, [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] ("Mr. Moe"), in the lobby of the hotel. He wore a light uniform with gold stitching - a roaring lion with a paw on the ball, the coat of arms of [[shimmeri|Shimmery]]. He didn't shake Buckley's hands. Halintor announced that in two days he had beaten and humiliated three people, and offered to name the reasons why he should not be chained to a shameful pillar and put seventeen lashes:
> "We are Laemians, Mr. Moe. Sometimes we are deaf to what is happening outside the city. But everything that happens in the city, hear the walls and tell the streets.

Three golden Hilt did not convince him, and the letter of the House of Labelin, declaring the merchant Horam Pauline ([[kharmon|Harmon]]) a personal enemy of the Duke, he considered humiliating for the Duke himself. The sheriff's deputy warned that Lady Magda's patronage was "a weak defense even in Labelin", and has not yet spoiled the negotiations. He took one piece as the “price of time”, and left two on the table as the “price of connivance in the future”.

In early April 1775, Halintor, with two soldiers in chain mail, appeared in a tavern near the canal, where Buckley was waiting for the mercenary Rodrigo. He knew that Buckley had killed the servants of the white-blooded Vanessa Lilith and kidnapped her and her children, and made a condition: if the prisoners are alive - hard labor, if even one is dead - wheeling. ([[vanessa-lilit|Vanessa-Lilith]]) Buckley pretended to repent, but at that moment Rodrigo entered with the sailors, and the slaughter began. One sheriff was killed and the other seriously injured. Halintor quickly cut open the cheek of one of Buckley's men and whipped the sword of the sailor who wounded the second soldier, but received a stool to the side and a nose smashed with a blow of the head. Buckley escaped by locking the door behind him. Rodrigo later explained that he did not kill the sheriff's deputy "quick as an ostrich" just because there was no payment for it. Vanessa with the children and Harmon, the guard did not find: they were saved by the girl Nisa. ([[niza|Niza]])

### Hunting for the Light Sphere
In May 1775, Halintor, having received a written order from Prince Hector to search for Horam Pauline Robert and seize the Light Sphere from him, first visited Harmon's estate in Melison, and then, at the suggestion of the burgomaster, overtook him at the Abode-u-Bezdna near the Boneless Gap, the monastery of Count Kindar (Second of the Five). ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]; [[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]; [[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]; [[kuindar|Quindar]]) He demanded to leave the ship's basket for a search, but Harmon's companions threw Kindar overboard and took off; [[chara|Chara]]'s arrows forced the sheriff's deputy to bounce.

A few days later, the Hunter ([[adrian|Adrian]] under the guise of a witch hunter) with his Shavan and Zund allies seized the monastery and disarmed Halintor's people, promising to "temporarily assume" his duty as a guardian of the law. During the interrogation, Halintor held firm (“I am not one of those who can easily be intimidated”), made an order and only after threats made a guess: the prince, who broke up with Magda Labelin, is looking for an alliance with Origin, and the Sphere may need a northerner. The hunter left him as a prisoner "until he sees the point."

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, in the story "Amicable" (August 1776), Halintor is already Sheriff Laema. He led a solemn procession that met in the harbor of the northern guests. Prince Hector instructed him to look for [[barni|Barney]], [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]]'s nephew, bought at auction by an althess unknown in a white cloak. The sheriff entered Timerett's pimp's bedroom just as [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] and Hyder Leed were questioning him in their own way, "I see you've done your own thing..." After that, the prince put the kire under house arrest and told the sheriff to take up their case “on balance, after more important matters.”

Princess Demeter knew the sheriff's "new althessa" and hoped that Jemis, looking for the boy, would finish the evening in his office. ([[demetra-nellis-lajon|Demetra Nellis-Lyon]]) In the city they said that Vanessa-Lilit “finally found an alter.” When Jemis and Demeter came to Vanessa, Halintor was sitting at her tea. Jemis initially decided that the pimp and the sheriff had arranged the kidnapping together in order to quarrel the North with the South, and almost lashed out at him, but held out and shook his hand. The sheriff was in perfect control of himself: he recalled that the guard did not have time to save Vanessa in 1775 and that they were saved by Nisa, and assured that everything was being done to find Barney. When he left, Jemis considered him not the main villain, but “the usual selling skin, of which there are a million in the world.” Hector, hearing the suspicion, exclaimed: “Halintor is honest, I am sure of him!” — and Jemis agreed: the boy was bought by the intermediary of Demeter.

## Personality
Halintor is polite, dry and unhurriedly self-confident. He is well aware of everything that is happening in Laem, indifferent to the patronage of the great lords from across the sea and is not afraid of threats, but takes money for “permissiveness”. He is a dangerous swordsman: Buckley's men rated his sword as unexpectedly formidable.
