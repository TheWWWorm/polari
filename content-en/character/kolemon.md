**Colemon** is the eldest of two explorers of Erwin Origin, a hunter from [[spot|Spot]]. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) The grumbler and connoisseur will admit, he led a detachment through the Zamshev Forest and toppled the Soft Fields to [[reka|the River]].

## Personality
Colémon passed for fifty, and with a white pointed beard he resembled Erwin the evil sorcerer Olaf from a children's fairy tale. More than anything else, he liked to grumble: coniferous forest is bad - no squirrels, no hares, oaks are bad - they love buckets, bushes are bad - ticks. He spoke as if “Wisdom itself speaks through his mouth.” With the lord held respectfully - "your lordship", hat down, bow - but looked with reproach, if he did not want to hear how to get from under the bark of the larvae. He called the [[kid|Kid]] "baby" and "small", the soldiers - "native".

Colemon was "superstitious like an old maid." Do not sleep with your feet to the young month; do not knock the moss off the trees; stepped into the stream with one foot - stupa and the second and ask for forgiveness. He convinced the kairs that the earth toads sent male impotence, and the greys then roared toads in each parking lot; a few days later, the whole squad observed his omens. In the middle of the Fields, he ordered everyone to tear a strand from the top with his bare hand and thank the “mother of the nettle”, the bald Theobart was allowed to tear from his beard, and eventually even Erwin obeyed.

And yet Coleman was a man of knowledge. He led a detachment through the Zamshev Forest so that neither veggies nor fangs met on the way, added mushrooms, roots and a dozen species of berries to the diet, almost every evening found a spring and accurately calculated the time of transition.

## In the Explorado
- **Soft Fields (May 1774).** On the edge of the tope Kolemon read the detachment a detailed lesson: how the grass-net is kept on the water, why the perennial network of "cow will hold", and at the gap you need to crawl flat, what is black water, what is the hunter John Badger floated on for three days, how to recognize the shallows of yellow grass and how dangerous the widower and hare snakes are. If he failed, he taught, “and do nothing, Your Grace.” Louis was forbidden to walk barefoot and showed him a widow in the grass.
- **A snake in a tent.** When the kite of the Kid was found in Erwin's tent, Colemon stood up for his partner - "he's small, stupid" - and explained that the baby had tied the bag badly. Erwin later realized that the snake had been planted by a mechanic named [[luis-mariya|Louis]].
- **Bad air.** Colemon confirmed to Kida that the gas above the dry grass is stinky, but not poisonous, and the bypass will take "eight, two days of travel." At the time of Jemis's mutiny, he was scouting with Captain [[teobart|Theobart]]. ([[myatezh-dzhemisa|Jemis's Rebellion]])
- **At the River.** In the forest near the river Kolemon every day darkened, told the legends of the countryside - "Bessovo fire", people-breeds and biting - and urged to turn: "The forest does not give game. The gods are frowning. We better get back.” To cross the River, he said, “is like burying yourself alive in the ground.” At the same time, he showed Erwin a bush of hazelnuts, which he remembered only later. Hungry in the dugout, Erwin realized that in the stories of Colemon about the flora of the Beyond there was no word “flake”: the nuts he found were different, previously unseen, but edible.
- **Farewell (June 6, 1774)** At the crossing Colemon for the last time dissuaded Erwin: "Your Grace, you are going to destruction ... Think about it!” Thanking for the payment, he looked sadly at the coins left in the Lord's purse: they, in his opinion, were destined to perish behind the River. With Erwin's letters, he went back to Spot.

## After
On the way back, Erwin and Jemis "remembered Colemon's warnings well" and bypassed the black water, spending three days on it. In August 1774, Kid, meeting them at Spot, said: "Colémon said: you will not return." So, Coleman himself returned home safely. It is not mentioned in the cycle anymore.
