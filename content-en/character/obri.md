## Overview
Aubrey is a X-Cyre, "merited X" and "palace veteran", adjutant to Duke [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]. ([[kajry|Kairs]]) A fanatical devotee of the lord, he believed in his victory over any army. At the end of the war, Erwin assigned him to his sister, and in Fantasia, Aubrey serves as Lady Iona's personal bodyguard. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) Among kairs, he is considered the "main" and "first mystic", a connoisseur of ghosts.

## In "Humans and Gods"
In June 1775, moving with one company of Leed wolves to Smoky Distance, Erwin, on the advice of a vassal, appointed himself bodyguards: the squire Quentin and the aide-de-camp Aubrey, "merited X-cloaks". Both were delighted, and [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] objected that they already have enough responsibilities. The third bodyguard was the young cyre Arthur the Twin.

When [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]], enchanted by Ulyanina Dust, tried to kill the Duke, Aubrey, along with Jemis and Quentin, broke into the tent and held Erwin scurrying on the floor until he admitted to playing the death scene. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Then Aubrey took away the poisoned roses in his gloves, and sealed the letter of the Puppeteer in another envelope with a warning. Erwin reassured him: Aubrey was appointed bodyguard only in this campaign, the Puppeteer did not know about him, and the letter is safe for him.

At the end of June, at Rei-Roy Castle on Halliwell, Aubrey pulled Erwin off his horse a moment before Finger of Wilhelm's whip struck and shoved his torch away so that the shooter had nothing to aim at. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) Quentin died, and Aubrey, according to Baron Icewind, “laying on fire” next to the Duke and saved only by miracle. ([[kurt-ajsvind|Kurt Icewind]])

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Flight and Mills
In the July battle at Reis, Aubrey passed the Duke's orders to the companies, with him rushed to the Gun, the first-born prisoner, and was wounded in the hand by a random arrow. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) He had to see to it that Lieutenant Fitzgerald left with a shambles company, but he stayed with the Duke. Seeing a handful of survivors from Icewind's company on the shore, Aubrey asked Erwin to "wipe these scum into powder." After the battle, he took the wounded man to the infirmary.

In the summer, Erwin was accompanied everywhere by Aubrey and Fitzgerald. Aubrey reported to him about Jemis and Nexia, until the duke told him to "catch a rabbit" and tell the news to him. Once he replaced Scar for a buy, and the next evening Erwin beat him and Fitzgerald, taking away from both three months' salary. Aubrey and Jemis were the only ones who heard Count Flame mention the dam in private.

### [[lid|Leed]]
In November, in a tavern on the lands of Origin, a cold Erwin fell on Aubrey, when he, stabbing the windows, dropped the door from the hinges. Aubrey offered to dry the raw wood with Wilhelm's Feather and knock the nest off the chimney, and at the same time praised the care of the captive Lady Violetta.

When it became known that [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] was locked up in Leed, Erwin took only Hyder Leed and Aubrey with him. At the walls of the city, which plundered the Shavans, Aubrey insisted that we wait until the horde leaves, and in fury argued with the captain, who offered to sneak into the castle along the icy aqueduct: "You are dragging the duke to the grave!" Still, he went, tied to the rest of the one rope that closes. On the wall of the castle, Lillideev Aubrey was the first to climb the rope, brought to his senses a deafened Erwin with a sip of an orge and changed into a Shayland uniform. In the battle in the yard, he covered himself with a corpse from a bolt and met a soldier with two blades, and later held the southern tower with Leed. He was twice slightly wounded, but did not hide the delight of Iona's victory and promised to find her a new dress instead of the former, "smellish dog."

After his release, Erwin ordered Aubrey to hand over the captured Shaylands to the city court and then "accept the position of my sister's bodyguard." On his way to the First Winter, Aubrey stood on his watch and spooked the night horse thief.

### Battle for First Winter
On December 2, 1775, Aubrey was part of Erwin's small force. During the Battle of the First Winter, he was riding with Iona, and when she lay among the corpses to quietly fire from Perst, he and Hyder Leed "as if inadvertently" covered her from both sides. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) In the evening, Erwin was relieved to learn that “even Aubrey and Leed” had survived. In negotiations with Ambassadors Adrian Erwin and Minerva, they went under the protection of Aubrey and Jemis kire.

## In "Fantasies"
In the summer of 1776, Aubrey was Iona's personal bodyguard in the First Winter. He carries a watch at her door, where a chair with a pillow stands for him, and with him King Francis-Ilianus visited Iona when she locked herself in the mountain after the war. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]])

In Let's Catch Him, it was Aubrey who, while playing a prank with Captain Scar and Lieutenant Fitzgerald, was the first to announce that Ione had the ghost of Vittor, not the Puppeteer himself: he, moving the Absolute, appears with cotton and cold, and there was neither here. They laughed at him: Hyder Leed said that in the valley of the First Winter they were not even noticed with Iona. He assured the guard who saw the dead Minerva that he met a ghost, found the scribe Meredith, who also saw the spirit, and persuaded his friends to catch him. Iona herself warned him about the secret divination in the burned tower, and within an hour Aubrey had already told his friends about it; the three of them were eavesdropping on divination from the attic. The Xes questioned the ghosts of the First Winter, opened the wall with a walled-up warrior, and the bones of the Swollen Lady from the lake were asked to retrieve Iona with the Gauntlet of Power. Aubrey pulled Meredith out of the water, which the drowned woman pulled by the legs. Finally, in an empty refectory X with a skull and the book The Secret Horrors of the First Winter, they cried out to the spirit of Duke Odar Origin and learned that the culprit was Dalia, Erwin's althessa. ([[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]]) She was caught, and Erwin thanked the Kiers that they had found her.

In Lovely, Jemis joked about Leed's "mirror aqueduct", "polished" by Hyder Leed and Aubrey. In "Closer - Further" Aubrey with Scar and Fitzgerald on a strange assignment of the Duke spent the whole night in the city library.
