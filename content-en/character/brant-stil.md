## Origin and appearance
Brant Steel is an Origin house kire from the common people. ([[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]]) He and his brother Seymour were first-generation kairs, born shepherds and the swords of the duke. Brant started warming up at Kair Derrek, the X commander, and said that it was Derrek who made him a warrior. In "Shadow of the Great Tree", [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] recalls that once, walking along a mountain herding path over the Belomorye road, he got caught in the rain and basked in a cave by the fire. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]) The fire was spread by Brant Steel, then still "not a cairn and not a gray, but an ordinary shepherd", who did not even recognize Origin in the face.

Descriptions of Brant's appearance differ. At the tournament, Erwin compares him to his horse Rain after a long race: the same dark-haired, disheveled, uplifted, stepping on his feet. In Faunterra, he is described as a broad-faced light-bred guy, whom the mole on the cheek makes look like a goody. His speech is full of common people’s war: “the dog of his cushy”, “the dog of their gnawing”.

## Biography
### The tournament at Doyle and the fight for Labelin
In the autumn of 1774, in Erwin's campaign to the south ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]), Brant was an assistant and friend of [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon Origin]]. At the heavy cavalry tournament at [[dojl|Doyle]], he placed second with thirty-nine points, behind only Henry Horton (Forty-Two), who scored forty-two points. ([[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]]) Damon, whom Erwin banned from participating, cheered loudly for his friend and almost pulled his hair out when he finally collapsed on the ground.

In the evening, Erwin gathered six winners and ordered them to train for the next “important” tournament for three weeks only. Horton answered for everyone that they would honor, and Brant, hitting himself with his fist in the chest, promised: “We’ll win!”, but then he was outraged: the whole army will laugh if others beat the travelers, and they exercise. Erwin promised that after the competition all six would stand in the vanguard of Damon's cavalry, and he ordered fourteen feet of spears to train instead of thirteen. This second tournament turned into a duel of honor near [[labelin|Labelin]]: six northern knights fought against the traveling, and the infantry of the Southern Way laid down their arms. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] proudly told [[minerva|Minerva]] at breakfast in [[uejmar|Waymar]] that the brother of her guard captain, Seymour's kire, was in that six. Seymour replied that his brother had already received the rank of captain.

### Defence of the Palace in Faunterra
After the capture of [[faunterra|Faunterra]] Brant Steel, along with Henry Horton, "the famous tournament champions and heroes of the battle for Labelin", was among Erwin's officers. In assigning roles before the siege, the Duke instructed him to help himself keep the front wall above the bridge. Brant proposed to destroy the bridge, and Erwin replied that the bridge would be raised when the time came: it was the Yangmei Bridge, although Steel did not see any winches or ropes at him. During the first attack of the Red Sickle, he stood next to the Duke above the gate. When Derrek's kair squad was in danger in the Table Citadel, Erwin sent Steel over the Air Bridge to help him, and this saved the situation. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) In the evening, the Duke handed Steel the command to get some sleep. At night, when the guardsmen and philistines disembarked from the boats, Brant pointed a flashlight at the boats, and when the enemies stormed the gate tower and Erwin fell, stunned by the spark, it was Steel who repulsed him: "My Lord, My Lord! It's me, Steel!" Later, when a dislocated shoulder was adjusted to the exhausted Erwin, Brant held the Duke, pressing him to the floor. He accompanied Erwin and Alanis along the Air Bridge to the Citadel and was the first to make his way into the Citadel's vault of Objects, which proved empty.

Derreck's detachment, sent for the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] to the town hall, was killed, and the guards of Croydon Backfield began to throw the palace of stone-throwing heads and pieces of bodies. Damon, [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] and [[dzhoj-khemmond|Kair Hammond]] demanded land raids to destroy the cars and disperse the petty-bourgeois who helped the guards. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Brant with tears in his eyes asked to entrust the sortie to him: he walked in gray at Derrek and considered himself obliged to take revenge. Erwin chose him. [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], watching the scene, wondered whether the Duke found this reason to be valid or just Steel was the lowest-born of the kairs and he was not sorry.

During the night, a hundred X’s of horsemen passed over the lowered bridge, braved the camp of the guards and attacked the philistines at the stones. The Xs set fire to the cars, trampled and dumped all those who did not have time to run into the icy Hanai. Erwin demanded a signal from the tower to leave, but Brant, being carried away by revenge, led people far along the shore and commanded the departure, only "satisfied". On the way back, the detachment came across a hastily built barrier at the Palace Bridge. Three dozen horses returned without saddles. The crossbow bolt hit Brant in the dimple under the cuff, above the edge of the breastplate. Erwin leaned over the dead and asked for his forgiveness:

> “Forgive me. Outing's a bad idea. Beautiful and bad..."

After his death, the commander of the first company of X-cloaks was [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]], who did not even have an officer's rank at that time.

## Legacy
The Xs remembered Brant as one of their fallen. In "A Doll on the Throne", [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], in late March 1775, guarding Louise's wagon train on the road from Faunterra, encountered Iona's motorcade and her guard captain Seymour and told him that he had seen the death of "Lieutenant Steel": he died, having avenged his mentor, Kair Derreck, on the Guards. When Seymour heard this, he gave his hand to the messenger. When [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] returned from Beyond, the Xes were jealous of him in part because he was not with them on the palace wall and he "didn't mourn Damon Origin and Brant Steel." Kair Reynolds, beating a captured Guard Major nicknamed the Red Serp, sentenced the names of those killed in Faunterra: "Damon Origin. Brant Steel. Derek Whitehall". In Shadow of the Great Tree, Erwin remembers Damon, Seymour Steel, and Brant Steel among those who became "monuments not remembered by any living person."
