## Origins

### House Lightheart
Edgar is the third, youngest son of Jonas Caroline May of the family of Helen, Duke of Lighthart. In the middle of the 18th century, Jonas ruled all [[nadezhda|Hope]] and personally owned two of its largest cities: the Heart of Light citadel and the port of Leongard. He had a quarter of all the gold mined in Hope, and his army consisted of ten thousand swords. In the aggregate of military, monetary and political influence, it is said in the "Humans and Gods", it was only preceded by Vladyka Telurian. According to the Polari Land Guide, fortitude, luck, and pride were hallmarks of the Great Lighthart House. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) The Dukes of Hope of Lighthart remained until 1755.

Jonas' two eldest sons shone at court: one sat in the House, the other served as a guard captain. The third, Edgar, was assigned the role, which the narrator calls the role of the “ugly sheep”. From a young age, feeling a burden, Edgar struggled to please his father. He studied strategy, scripture, politics and finance, learned fencing, horseback riding, negotiating and writing poetry, and did not disdain even secretarial work. The father clapped him on the shoulder and said “Good job”, but without any feeling, and the son became more and more convinced of his worthlessness. Finally, he was married to the daughter of Count Flame, Lighthart's strongest vassal, and Edgar left for Leongard. There his wife bore him two children, and with them he felt himself needed for the first time.

### Jesters' Conspiracy
Jonas plans to bequeath the imperial crown to his grandchildren. He married a daughter to Manson, the brother of Emperor Thelurian, and involved his son-in-law and two eldest sons in a conspiracy. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) Edgar was not dedicated to the idea: he “too firmly believed in knightly ideals” and was not suitable for intrigues. He learned everything from the letter. One morning he was awakened by the crying of his wife and children. In the letter taken from his wife, the father reported the failure of the conspiracy, spoke for the first time about his love and asked the last son to become the keeper of the name and traditions of the clan. The wife lamented that Edgar will also be taken away and she will be left alone. Unable to find strength for a long speech, he said to her only: "You stupid chicken" - and began to console.

The Lord promised freedom to the captive sons and daughters if Jonas surrendered. The Duke came to the capital alone and was deceived: he was executed on the platform with his two sons, and his daughter was sent for execution to a secret place (later it turned out that [[karen|Karen]] survived). Count [[henri-farvej|Farway]] had hope. Through his third secretary, Telurian hinted to the Earl of Flame that he would become duke if he presented "massive proof of devotion", that is, the head of his son-in-law. Flame publicly condemned the former suzerain, but did not follow through.

### Poverty and the New Name
The Lightharts have one Leonhard. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) Farway was taxed with gold, the east-west trade route fell apart, and the ships of the Crown and the Labelins began to enter the ports of [[litlend|Litland]]. Ten father battalions came to serve Edgar, but there was barely enough money for one. "Nearly crying," he dissolved eight and left the two best. Even their content devastated the treasury: fish was served three times a day to the table, guests had to be forgotten.

The father-in-law came to Edgar and said that he was offered to kill his son-in-law for the sake of the ducal title. To make Telurian forget Edgar rather, Flame gave him the upper half of the Flame River flowing into Back. Once the ancestor of the count washed a little gold in this stream, bought a horse and armor for it, and, having served the title, named the genus in honor of the “happy stream”. Having received land, Edgar Lighthart could rightfully be called Flame and pass this name on to his children. Somerset and Nexia were later admitted to Lady Ingrid's boarding house in the capital, and their relationship with Jonas Lighthart was only occasionally whispered.

Since then, Edgar has lived in Leonhard as a misanthrope, scorning people, especially optimists, and frowning on any news, especially joyful news. In addition to the eternal search for money, he knew one thing: he endlessly mused his last battalions, the “shard of the father’s sword”, finding it painful pleasure in the idea that this army, most likely, will never enter the battle.

### Visit of the Prophet
At the end of 1774, after leaving after eight years of seclusion of the Abode-of-the-Abyss, [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] sailed to Leongard, the sea gate of Hope, and visited the count - "the ruler of Leongard", whom the narrative here calls by his family name Count Lighthart. The count, like everyone else, asked the prophet what he had dreamed and what future he was waiting for. The prophet did not dream of the count, but, not wanting to offend the owner, he said what he was sure of: “The worst days of the Lighthart House were left behind.” ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) The Count made a noisy feast in honor of the holy dreamer. From Leonhard the prophet went to the Heart of Light.

## Hartley

Already in the years of the lord [[adrian|Adrian]] Edgar bought a black rat in the port from a sailor-foltic. The sailor praised her: “The most scientific rats! The best advisor!” The Count was attracted not by these speeches, but by the similarity: the gloomy lonely animal reminded Edgar of himself, and the nickname Hartley seemed an ugly echo of the name Lighthart. The rat sits on his shoulder, chews his collar, and when he sets out the plan, he calms down and listens. Then the count touches her neck and asks if anything will come out. The nod means yes, the shaking of the head means no. Sometimes he would sigh, "Oh, if you were with my father... If only he could ask for advice.”

Hartley's first question was about his wife's plan. Nexia became close to the offspring of the Origins, and the wife offered to make friends with the elder through the younger Origin, using kairs to knock the Farway out of the Heart of Light and return the ancestral nest. Hartley said no. Since then, the count often consults with her and, as stated in the text, receives the advice that “in the depths of his soul he expected.” The reason for her wisdom is simple: the rat has a scar from a long-standing fight, nods hurt her, and therefore almost everything she answers “no”.

## In "Humans and Gods"

### Hiking on Aleridan
On the hopes of her parents for her connection with Erwin Origin [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]] on the night of May 21, 1775, she told in Waymar [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]: her father and mother saw in her beauty and upbringing "the means of their revenge" for the damage suffered from the Shutov conspiracy and the rise of the Farvais. According to her, her father directly said that she should “have the right influence” on a person valuable to the family.

When Erwin marched on Gallard Almer, on June 8, 1775, in the County of Arrowback, he received from his intelligence in the Heart of the World a message: Farway had advanced a ten-thousand army to Almera, collecting everything he had, including the Leongard Regiment, former vassals of the House of Lighthart. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]; [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) About a week later, Hope's troops were to approach Aleridan. So Edgar and his regiment were in Almera.

### Negotiations in Erwina camp
On the morning of June 1775 (the chapter of Arrow 7 is marked on June 20–21), Edgar arrived at [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]'s fortified camp with a detachment of four men: he himself in a lilac cloak in the manner of desert warriors, a bannerman with a yellow pennant of negotiations, an aide-de-camp with two sparkly self-shooters and a lady under a veil with a paper roll. He entered the tent alone.

Edgar spoke as Farway's voice and without fail: Hope's troops occupied [[aleridan|Aleridan]] with five regiments, disarming the parish battalion, and if he refused the agreement, he would attack Erwin. Farway demanded to recognize the heiress of Almera [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]], agreeing to marry a minor Albert. In return, Farway promised support for Hope and Almera in the elections, the siege of [[fliss|Fliss]] by Edgar's regiment, his help in the return of the eyes captured by Magda Labelin, and the "very good" dowry of Nexia, one of Hope's spark shops. The last point Edgar set out is particularly sad, recognizing that he is not rich, and his kind is “stained by known events.”

Erwin refused the forced marriage, but asked Edgar himself. He explained that Farway sent him because his regiment was the best in Hope and kept the training of Jonas Lighthart's warriors. Why the count is so despondent and calls the suzerain simply Farway, he did not respond. Then Erwin hinted that if Edgar visits nostalgia for the greatness of the Lighthart House, then he himself “does not mind returning to the glorious old days.” ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) Edgar's eyes flashed for a moment, but Hartley "reasonably" shook her head. The deal concluded: Erwin recognizes Laura, Edgar takes Fliss, and Lilliday's cavalry passes through Aleridan without a fight.

Already leaving, Edgar said "personally" that a detachment of heretics with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]] had captured a train in Hope and was now trying to break through the desert to the west. He also reported that in Aleridan he met a daughter who was carrying a package from [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] to Erwin and asked to return her to his camp, telling her that Erwin did not need her "even with a spark shop in addition". The lady under the veil was [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]], who, under the influence of Ulyanina Dust, tried to poison Erwin. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Nexia had heard nothing about heretics, and Anxiety suspected that either of the two was lying. The news, however, was true: in mid-June, the Faunterra reported that the train from Leongard to the Heart of Light had disappeared a few miles from Leongard, and the rescue team expelled from the city found in the sands the burned corpses of passengers, after which a wave of heretics with the Feathers was sent from Leongard to the Heart of Light. Erwin admitted that Edgar simply felt sorry for his daughter. She was sent to her father with a note: the daughter is not guilty, let her for the time being believe that she killed the Duke, - so the action of Dust will dissipate. Edgar kept her in this delusion for two days.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

### Map and mission of Nexia
In July 1775, Edgar's corps blockaded [[fliss|Fliss]] from land while Farway's fleet locked him out of the lake; Erwin, cut off in the Steppe, wondered if Edgar Flame had finished off the parish. When the horde of [[paul|Paul]] invaded Almera, Farway lifted the siege of Fliss, and Hope's troops had to retreat. Edgar himself gave Nexia a strategic card from Farway's bet and sent it with instructions to Fais. Along the way, she found Erwin and handed him the map. Nexia corresponded with her father in encrypted letters to "Lord Ed. Flame" and, according to her, persuaded him to enter the war.

### Meeting with Karen in Orwell
In September 1775, [[karen|Karen]] sent a wave to her brother at Farway headquarters in Aleridan, signing the children's nickname "Purple" (the word "yours" had to be deleted: there was not enough money for a long message). They met in Orwell for the first time in twenty years. Edgar arrived with his entourage — son [[somerset-flejm|Somerset]], aide-de-camp, day-labourer, and two knights — and at first did not even recognize his sister. The conversation did not stick: at the request to tell about himself, Edgar reported his title and the state of affairs in the entrusted parts. The situation was saved by Somerset with his student stories. Edgar told his son about the Nexia incident in Waymar that his sister had to be sorry because she was ruled by Dust. The map, he added, was given to Nexia himself, sending it “on an important diplomatic mission.”

He then asked Hartley if his sister had the money and if she admitted it, got two “nos” and silently moved Karen envelope with bills: “Don’t you dare thank. I didn't give anything, someone forgot on the table." Edgar admitted that he “wanted to make a bet”, and since he was not expected at the playing table, he asked for Nexia’s help. He considered war no longer an ordinary pastime of lords, but a struggle for ideas and for the future of the world. He saw no future for himself and his sister, “even if the children had appeared.” He asked Karen to come home, but she couldn't leave her husband. Bye-bye, Edgar handed her a piece of paper with a baby watch, and Karen realized that this was the key to the Lighthart family cipher: the principle of coding in twenty-two years has not changed. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) She later told Manson that Edgar had become a gambler and had put, "seems to be his daughter." She thought that her brother, contrary to the saying that people do not change, as if he was going to change with the spirit. Karen regretted that she did not leave with him: how would they have healed in Leonhard, if Manson was not so stubborn. As early as August 1775, when Adrian joined Faunterra, Manson persuaded his wife to go to Leongard and even put her on a train, but she went to another station and returned to her husband.

### On the side of the first winter
General Horton asked Farway for ships to go to help the North. Farway gave them, and together with five battalions Horton sent Edgar's regiment. According to the assessment sounded in the "Shadow of the Great Tree", he wanted to remove from Almera and the Kairs, and "Edgar Lighthart, whose loyalty was not sure." At the end of November 1775, the ships climbed the Twin from Smoky Distance, and the troops landed twenty miles from Main, in the rear of Adrian's regiments. They were discovered by [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. Seeing the coats of arms of Leongard, he decided that the son of the executed Lightharts, too, would willingly throw himself into a fight with Adrian and not too devoted to Farway. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) So Edgar's regiment was on the side of the northerners in the campaign of the Battle of the First Winter. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Upon learning that Karen's five battalions of kire and brother Karen's regiment had stood on the Upper Twin at his back, [[adrian|Adrian]] accused Karen of treason and threw her into the dungeon of Main Castle. After the battle, when Adrian, remaining in Main, sought peace, Horton's corps still stood in his rear; Magda Labelin, forcing her husband to peace, threatened that her Western was already rushing to General Horton. Lord [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]], after the battle, praised Erwin for enlisting the support of the Flames and leading Horton's Adrian Corps into the rear.

## In "Fantasies"

"Fantasies" the author calls possible options for the future. In the summer of 1776, in Fantasia III, Edgar met Karen and Manson at Aleridan Station with the words "Welcome home, Sister", although the Lightharts had previously fought the Almeran Dukes. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) He praised them for their winter activities in the capital and introduced Hartley. His regiment was in constant readiness, he himself lived in the location of the unit. The hotel where he settled his relatives was guarded by his soldiers, and half of the visitors to the tavern welcomed the count, although he had previously been alien to feasts. His regiment is recruited from the natives of Hope. The soldiers themselves call themselves “deserts” and are sure that the Almerans treat them “in a scotish way”.

Edgar explained that Farway, who had captured Almera, had vowed to return Lighthart to the Heart of Light if Nexia would marry Origin and bring Farway the support of the North. Nexia risked herself for Erwin, and Edgar led his regiment to the side of the First Winter. But the daughter quarreled with Minerva and was sent by the messenger to the central lands, and Farway postponed the deal. Edgar admitted to his sister that every month he borrows in the bank for three thousand ephesas for military expenditures and without the Heart of Light is ruined. He promised to assign her rent when everything is settled, and Karen, not daring to complain about the need, promised him half the future fee for the book. Manson noticed that Farway was now afraid of the Lightharts: the Heart of Light stood empty, Farway's troops in Almera, and Edgar had a regiment. Edgar replied: “Hartley always said: there is nothing to dream about.”

When war came to Almera, Edgar sent his daughter "to one side", and Somerset - to Faunterra: "there is safe." Later, his son asked permission to marry Rose, daughter of Earl of Arrowback, and Farway refused. Edgar wrote to him that Nexia was unlikely to achieve the goal, because she "tried to kill Origin", and "Hartley says that Nexia has no chance". He asked his son to wait a month or two. In the summer of 1776, he summoned Somerset to Aleridan and said plainly, "We are ruined," and marriage to Arrowback's heiress "can save us." Farway, meanwhile, threatened Karen: if the family does not fulfill the conditions, Edgar will not pay off his debts and his property will “go under the hammer”.

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the fall of 1776, Edgar, along with Count Erroback, came to Erwin's council at Faunterra. He had a rat named Hartley, a "tailed adviser" with whom he checked on every issue. Edgar warned the Duke that he might not be chosen: the power of the lords was weakened, and in Almer, Reis, Darkwater and the Sunset Coast, it recently changed and did not have time to strengthen. He recalled: “Lady Magda gave birth to Adrian son. The heir to the throne is a guarantee of stability," and Erwin has no child or wife. Edgar suggested that Iona treat the patients in Faunterra and the mob saw that Erwin, not Minerva, gave health. Erwin found it too dangerous. After the death of Adrian, the knights of the Earl of Flame, together with the Kairs and the people of Arrowback, entered the capital and put things in order without a fight. Soon Erwin announced his engagement to [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]].

## Personality

Edgar is gloomy, speaks without feelings, despises optimists and frowns with joyful news; everything he did "was a shade of despondency." He doesn’t even know how to talk to his sister. However, he is honest, committed to knightly ideals, kind to children and sister and knows how to help without humiliating. Erwin reasoned that since Nexia had never lied to him, her father could be trusted in part. Edgar himself considered himself a cautious player who “plays small,” and Karen seemed to be looking for a brother for whom it is not a pity to fight.

## Relationships

### Nexia and Somerset
The children gave Edgar a foothold in life. He defended Nexia from the ridicule of his son and entrusted her with the most risky assignment of his life. Somerset kept it to himself, and in Fantasia he first asked to endure marrying the daughter of the Almer Count Erroback, and then saw in this marriage the salvation of the family. According to Karen, he “put” on his daughter, but later he wrote to his son that she had no chance.

### Karen
He considered his sister dead for 20 years. At the meeting, he silently gave her money, entrusted the key to the family cipher and called home. She calls him "brother," he calls her "Purple."

### Henry Farway
Formally, Edgar is Farway's vassal, but calls him simply "Farway" and serves without love. Farway, in turn, did not trust him.

## Uncertainties

In the letter of confession of Nexia Iona (May 1775), the parents are named differently than in the history of the family in Humans and Gods: "The father is the Earl of Flame, the mother is the née Lady Lighthart." According to the main version, the born Lighthart was Edgar himself, and he received the surname Flame from his father-in-law along with the land on the River Flame.
