## Origins

Karen is the daughter of Duke Jonas Lighthart of the ancient Elena family, "more ancient than Farway and Labelin than even the Brilliant Dynasty itself." In the mid-eighteenth century, her father, Jonas Caroline May of the lineage of Elena, ruled the entire Duchy of [[nadezhda|Hope]]. He received a quarter of all the gold mined in Hope, and gold there was mined as much as in all the other lands combined. He held an army of ten thousand swords, and in total influence he was ahead of only the lord Telurian. In addition to his three sons, the Duke had a daughter, "the most enviable bride of her time", who excelled in beauty, manners and wit. Only her blood was a hindrance: the emperors never married the lady of the family of Elena.

### Marriage with Manson and Conspiracy
In the 2026 edition of [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], Karen is named after [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]] in his book about the Shutov Conspiracy, which he reads: Manson married "Lady Karen - daughter of Duke Jonas" at twenty-eight years old. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) According to the same book, Duke Jonas and his three sons died on the plow. (In the early media, neither Karen nor her father were named.)

According to the story in [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], Duke Jonas dreamed of bequeathing the crown to his grandchildren and chose a workaround. The Emperor's brother [[menson|Manson]] was not constrained by traditions and could marry after his heart. The Duke introduced him to his daughter, and she charmed Manson from the first conversation. At their wedding, guests said that “such a glorious couple has not yet worn the earth.” Jonas then gave his son-in-law a few hints, and Manson caught fire with the thought of a coup. The plot was dedicated to the Duke’s two eldest sons, but not the youngest and not the daughter: “desperately loving Manson, she would be exhausted by anxiety.” (In the book of Ayden Almera, Manson himself was the initiator of the conspiracy; see the Shutov conspiracy.) ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]])

After the failure of the conspiracy, the daughter and two older sons were in custody. Tellurian promised them freedom if the duke came to Faunterra alone, without troops. Jonas came and surrendered, but the lord deceived him. The duke died on the platform with his sons, and before his death he learned that his daughter was "sent for execution - but to another place where her body will never be found." During interrogations, he swore that his daughter knew nothing, but the lord “chosen not to believe”. Karen recalled that Emperor Telurian was silent while the judges read her family's verdict. The Heart of Light and the Duke's title went to [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], and Edgar's younger brother survived only thanks to his father-in-law's surety and took his surname - Flame.

Karen herself, by order of Telurian, was imprisoned in a hospital on the island of Faraday Riley. ([[ostrov-faradeya-rajli|Faraday-Riley Island]]) She did not know whether her husband was alive, and at the court she was considered dead or exiled. Minerva will say, “I was not involved in the plot.”

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

### Faraday - Riley 's
In the spring of 1775, Karen lived in a hospital on Faraday Riley Island under the name Caitlin; her neighbors called her Karen-Katelyn. ([[ostrov-faradeya-rajli|Faraday-Riley Island]]) It was a ghostly, silent woman, sleeping dressed and hardly touching food; she was still guessing “remnants of beauty”, but the new woman was frightened by her resemblance to the future corpse. She shared a room with the chattering Annette and Dorothy Sly of Marengo — the memoryless Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] brought in in December — and invariably called the neighbor a "lady." Karen served as a copyist in the workshop of the master Gustav; Gustav considered her the best in the shop after Navi and the most attentive of the copyists and instructed her to rewrite in ten copies the textbook of navigation, which Navi "absorbed" for a lunch break. ([[navi|Navi]])

When Navi began to torment Dorothy with the demand for "numbers", Karen answered only to her pleas for advice: "Patience, milady." She named her number six. In April, Dorothy asked how long Navi had been in the hospital. Karen replied, “Ten years and two months, and he hasn’t changed a bit.” When Dorothy doubted her memory, Karen broke down for the first time: the neighbor spent less than four months here and already became a “obedient sheep”, and she is eighteen years old and remembers every day who and by whose order she brought and what dress she wore on the first day. Karen refused to tell Dorothy about her past: she will live here “another ten or twenty years” and every day will suffer from memories, and Karen will not take such guilt on herself. Dorothy, in response, considered her a liar and a slob and reduced the conversations to good morning wishes. In May, as Dorothy inquired if it was true that she wanted to run away in the winter, Karen answered “yes” and revealed what she was silent about: Dorothy wanted to run for her taken daughter, screamed in her sleep and called her. Her name was Gloria.

### Escape
On May 18, 1775, her supposed "cousin" arrived for Dorothy, Major Scarlet Guard (Backfield), and Master Mullin agreed to discharge her. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) In the workshop, Karen played a fit of a consumption cough to go out with a neighbor in the yard, and there she said: “Milady, on the mainland you will be killed.” The major, she said, came to deliver Dorothy for execution, because "Yanmei's grandchildren never forgave offenses." Karen suggested that a friend use the last night for an easy death: she can do it “almost painlessly” pillow or show the exit to the steep cliff. She herself once “made the same choice” — chose life — and for many years regretted it.

That same night, when the servants were selling patients to the schooners, Navi led Dorothy out of the corps, and Dorothy came for Karen. She considered escape a “rare absurdity”: the chance of death is two-thirds, but she refused not because of it: “Am I like a rabbit or a rat? I'm not going to die on the run." Then Dorothy shouted, “He still loves you!” and Karen stood up and followed them. To get the women past the drunken sailors, Navi tore Karen's hem, and the fugitives pretended to be a fun company. They pulled up the door of the boatman's booth, took the boat, bypassed the schooner and, replacing each other on the oars, according to Navi's calculations, went to the open sea without a supply of water.

On 19 May, they were picked up by Captain Lu-Aramon's Shimmer tea schooner White Panther. To the captain they were Lady Dorothy of Bald Dole and Lady Karen of the [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]] of the family of Helen; ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) Karen vouched for Navi as "the granddaughter of Elena the Traveler, the patroness of wanderers, merchants and sailors." When the schooner of the major overtook them and flags reported that three dangerous fugitives were aboard the Panther, Karen assured the captain that they were pirates, and Navi bought the rescue with the secret of a new sea route. Left alone with Navi, Karen demanded to explain where and when he learned navigation. Navi called her Lighthart and Dorothy called her Northwood and whispered, "God..."

## In "Humans and Gods"

### Rueilde
"White Panther" came to Ruilde about three hours ahead of the pursuers; Karen clocked in at the port control tower that the major's schooner was three hours and twenty-two minutes behind. The fugitives had money - Navi sold the captain two more navigational secrets - and they began to ride so that they could not be remembered: one portrayed a noble lady, the rest - her servants. Skeptical and sarcastic Karen at first considered Navi simply "blessed" and mocked his divinity. In Ruilde, she placed responsibility on Dorothy in the name of Elena's mother: she dragged her into a "big, hostile, forgotten" world, where Karen has one goal - to find a loved one, and therefore, finding her daughter, Dorothy will not dare to leave her. At first, Karen thought her husband was dead, and she was going to at least find his grave. She spent the first day on the street, wandering along the waterfront, drunk from freedom: she went around a dozen bakeries and coffee shops and did not talk to anyone. A day later she came back in tears: “I found him! He is alive!” — but did not reveal the details to the companions; later she admitted that she had read in the “Voice of the Crown” that her husband was being tried for murder.

Karen announced that she will not leave friends until she fulfills two missions: to help Dorothy find her daughter, and Navi - to get rid of "the pernicious habit of thinking herself a god." She visited the orphanage of the Rwandan Baroness and made sure that Gloria was not there, expressed a cruel guess to Dorothy that the daughter could be put in a house of tolerance, and bought a map and a fresh “Voice” for the search.

### Marengo
At the end of May, the fugitives moved by train to [[marengo|Marengo]]. While Navi was missing in the archive of the imperial court, Dorothy enlightened her friend about fashion: for twenty years, the crinolines and whitewashed eras of the Lady [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]] are a thing of the past. Karen "was completely demoralized", but after a couple of days she gained courage and went to the barber. On the way, she looked into the archive “for an hour” and during this hour found the case of Dorothy. Returning with a short haircut, she first experienced a friend, calling her Lady Sybil, and then posted what she read: Dorothy - Countess Sybil Northwood; On October 14, 1774, she and her daughter were arrested in the palace, and by a decision of the Supreme Court of November 2, Gloria was sentenced to life exile at the discretion of Vladyka Adrian. The discharge caught Navi: he took the same case three days earlier and remained silent. In the evening, Fred, the archivist who followed Karen, appeared in the room; she had traded him for five elens with two words crossed out from the altered page of Gloria's case, "The Temple of Arden." Navi, however, refused to go to Arden: in the "Voice" just came the news of the attack of heretics on the tomb of the Dynasty.

The fugitives took refuge in a seaside hotel near Marengo. Karen, who played the role of a housekeeper, kept the general cash register, tried to reconcile the depressed Dorothy with Navi, tossed numbers to him and even arranged a fake night alarm for their entertainment with an escape on the sheets - without success. That night, while reading The Voice under a dry tree, she found on the fourth page a message about her husband, from which her "heart exploded with a festive fireworks display", and the whole next day hid joy under the haughty cold: the noblewoman of Lighthart's house, "more ancient ... than even the Brilliant Dynasty itself," did not become sensitive. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) Then she took Navi for a night walk and forced him to explain himself: he knew who Dorothy was from the first day in the archive, and he slipped the newspaper to distract her. Navi referred to the "observer effect" - the "Agatha dilemma": if he reveals the calculations, their behavior will change and the entire probability tree will become incorrect; the goal was to win over the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]. Karen believed him. The next morning, the countess asked her name Dorothy and announced that she was going to Arden for her daughter.

### Arden
In mid-June 1775, in Arden, Karen again portrayed a noble lady: she settled in the best hotel "Marion", went around with the "servants" of half a dozen shops and composed for the manager the legend of the strange arrivals. When asked if she knew Adrian, she replied that twenty years ago “his Highness just moved from pony to horse.” She came up with a plan, read in the Memoirs of the Servant of God, which she once copied: a noble lady allegedly wants to attach a pupil of Navi to a seminary, and Navi treats seminarians, and they bypass Arden’s temples as scouts for a coin. In the Iron Horn Inn, Karen asked Navi why he needed heretics, and she heard that being a god was "scourge" and that there was "no one else" to defeat Dark Ido; there they also asked the masons who had seen the crushed tomb at the Farewell Temple. Jealous Dorothy accused Karen of having an affair with Navi, and in response she clapped her hands in front of her nose.

When the hotel [[krojdon-bekfild|Beckfield]] ambushed, Karen two candles in the window filed a conditional sign. Major twisted kair Reynolds, who was called in advance by Navi, and Karen hurried the companions to go "to a more secluded place" - to the Farewell temple, where Backfield admitted that Gloria was executed. While Dorothy was breaking the gazebo at the temple, Karen was sitting in a carriage with Navi, and he explained that he had guessed everything else in Marengo: Gloria is the illegitimate daughter of the parish of [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], and therefore her body was hidden at the tomb of the lords. Karen reproached him that he hadn't told Dorothy before and made her see the body; Navi replied that he needed Minerva to stop Dark Ido. At the same moment, the protection agents surrounded the crew, and [[mark-voron|Mark]] asked, "Are you Lady Karen Lighthart?" After saying goodbye, Navi said to her, “Don’t be afraid. You'll be fine." She was carried in a black carriage, similar to a coffin; Karen understood that Navi had brought them into the hands of the authorities himself, but, “betrayed and abandoned by God,” she believed him for the first time.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

### the Minerva Court
At court, Karen defended Navi: called him a genius and, contrary to her skepticism, believed that he was a god. When [[adrian|Adrian]] moved to the capital, the couple gave Minerva the opposite advice. Manson offered to yield, and Karen warned that Adrian would sooner or later take revenge - "for success and fame. To Yangmei's glove on hand. For sitting on the throne and staying alive." She knew his father well: Telurian trampled all those who were connected with Manson, not excluding the innocent, and Adrian went to his father. Her advice was brief: “Run.” She herself did not leave: Manson put her on a train to Leongard, but she went to another station and returned.

### At the court of Adrian
On the day of Adrian’s return, Karen herself introduced herself to him: “Manson got me two decades ago.” She explained that she was in a hospital on the orders of Telurian without any sentence, and stressed that she could not judge the motives of a person “now deceased”. When Adrian forbade Manson to wear a naval uniform and ordered him to undress donaga, Karen was hard-pressed by her husband's humiliation. Minerva before leaving paid Manson a jester's salary, but the money was not enough: the wave of her brother, signed by the household nickname "Perchinka", left her last Elena.

Near the station, she was found by a sick woman, interrupted by Dorothy. She asked Karen to pour a slow poison on Adrian: “He’s a killer. I buried Gloria alive.” Karen refused — “I’m not a killer!” — and gave her friend all the money. Later, she found in the bag the same pill with the number 7 scratched: Dorothy planted it, taking the bag. Karen hesitated for a long time to open this bag.

In September 1775, she went third-class to Orwell with her brother [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]]. The meeting was awkward, but Edgar quietly left her an envelope with bills. My sister thought he was looking for something to fight for. Upon her return, she was immediately summoned by Adrian. He was asking about Navi, and Karen was being ridiculous. Then the lord ordered to bring a bound, intoxicated Dorothy, who was called Lady Sybil Northwood. Karen objected that Sybil was pardoned by Minerva, but Adrian rejected this. Under threat of torture for her friend, Karen said Navi had dreamed of meeting Minerva and Duke of Origin and was probably in a hurry for the First Winter. Adrian took her on a hike, and Sybil Northwood was later hanged.

In the campaign, Karen suddenly came into fashion at the court. She, "a pedigree but a beggar", "punished but innocent", was discussed in the same way as Gloria or Minerva once was. Some told her the gallows, others saw her as a broken and pitiful victim. Karen played along: she talked about her neighbors in the hospital and moved her lips, looking into the fan, as if reading a novel from him. Meanwhile, the couple quarreled in private over the execution of Sybil. One night, Karen took out a pill, drew a few lines along the key from the nursery counter and sent them out in the morning with a wave. The key to the Lighthart family cipher was given to her by her brother. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) Later, she confessed to her husband that she sent her brother a coded wave: “bumblebees got divorced here.”

### Battle for First Winter
On December 2, 1775, the court in Main watched the Battle of the First Winter through the Bird without the Raft. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]; [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) Karen persuaded her husband to run to her brother, to the bridge over the Upper Twin, but returned and put on the victory of Origin "three deer, one gloria and four agates." On Adrian's charge, she knew that Farway's fleet had landed Horton's keels and her brother's regiment on the Upper Twin, in Adrian's rear. Anticipating the arrest, she gave her husband a pill:
> “You might want to kill someone. I hope you will."

Adrian accused "Lady Karen of Arden, of the maidenhood of Lighthart" of treason and imprisoned her in the prison of Main Castle. She was led away by Shavans, and she looked only at her husband, and he read on his lips: “I love you. I will always love.” The next day, this Black Mary pill was poisoned by [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]], whom Adrian had just ordered to destroy the Hand of the Insignia. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) [[magda-labelin|Magda]] realized that this was a joke, took the murder and demanded that her husband immediately release Manson's wife. Navi at the last negotiations regretted that Karen was not there, and asked Manson to give her farewell words.

## In "Fantasies"

"Fantasies" the author calls possible options for the future. The story “Annoying Little Things” (two summer days in 1776, [[aleridan|Aleridan]]) is led by Karen herself. The reason for the story is a broken nail on the train: the lady does not have the right to complain about a serious, but “an annoying trifle” gives her the right to sympathy.

### Writer
Karen discovered a literary talent. She builds the plot of the novel, and “to warm up” writes short stories and hopes to earn “noble writing work.” Manson is against it. Her novel is about a man who falls into the underworld of the gods, and the gods there are like children. The editor of one of the printing houses met her enthusiastically: "Legendary Lady Karen Lighthart!", "the most beautiful lady of her time." He was ready to pay for the memoirs, but rejected the fiction. On the same day, she bypassed two more printing houses, without success. She did not write memoirs: they would have to talk about pain. Edgar Karen promised half the fee. The couple had almost no money: of the four glories in her purse, three Manson gave for a bottle of hanty.

### The Lighthart Family
Her brother [[edgar-flejm|Edgar]] met her in Aleridan. Henry Farway promised to return Lighthart to the [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]] if Nexia would marry Origin, but after Nexia's quarrel with Minerva, he postponed the deal. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]; [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]) Edgar was on the verge of ruin, and his regiment, recruited from the natives of Hope, stood in constant readiness. On Edgar's shoulder sat a hand rat Hartley, with whom his brother seemed to consult. Karen describes herself as “the Duchess of Hope.” The city went to Farway, she said, "after a failed conspiracy by my father and husband."

On Sunday, [[henri-farvej|Farway]] himself took Karen and Manson to Mass, where he showed the people the bride of Albert Almera - his forty-year-old daughter Evelyn. Along the way, he asked Karen to think of Nexia and her brother Somerset, who wanted to marry the Earl of Arrowback's daughter. Farway threatened that otherwise Edgar's property would "go under the hammer". Karen replied that he served as a "hammer, hammering nails into the coffin of the House of Almer", and she was intimidated by people much more terrible. About the years in the hospital, she noticed that in the “abode of love and mercy” she prayed three times a day and fulfilled the “plan for prayers for the rest of her life.” Together with her husband, she taught Somerset courtship and put on a theatrical mask for this.

### “Passenger”
In Fantasy Nine: The Passenger (1777), Karen is an already published writer. Her novel is out, and she returns from her native [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]] from her first meeting with readers. Edgar's brother by this time "won the city", Nexia is happy in a political marriage, and Somerset, who became a rich heir, "allows himself to be pranked", and his relatives are waiting for Karen to form him. On the Mirage of the Desert train, she solves the mystery of the dead man from compartment number nine, "John Doe". Then in the Heart of Light, along with hired prospectors Pete and Fred, she catches the former steward Edmund, and in the epilogue recognizes the real killer in the poverty at the station. She cannot publish this story and writes it "for training a pen."

### Conversation with Nexia
Karen explained to her niece that dynastic marriage is a "severe matter of calculation." She called Nexia's mother "a stuffed fool", but it was her family's surety that saved Edgar Lighthart, otherwise he would be executed "as brothers", or exiled, "as a sister". Tellurian his father, Meinir, married a despised swamp, and Meinir himself and "beloved son" Manson chose brides after heart. Erwin, according to Karen, is profitable to marry Nexia: he will get the votes of Hope and Almera, and Minerva "hopes to rule by herself." Nexia reproached her: “You married for love!” Then Karen told me something that no one, not even her husband, knows. It was out of love that she did not tell Manson about her suspicions, and he told her about the conspiracy. Tellurian executed her brothers and imprisoned her for twenty years. She was pregnant and lost her baby, and has not been able to conceive since. For twenty years, she thought Manson was dead until she read in The Voice of the Crown that he was being tried for murder. After that, Karen burst into tears, and Nexia consoled “aunt Karen.”

## Remarks to "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

([[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]]) Karen agreed with the condition that she would not soften her expressions. For five months, she sarcastically corrected the "author" in armor, titles, etiquette and laws, questioned [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] and other participants in the events and recounted her disputes with [[menson|Manson]]. Over the most terrible chapters, she drank valerian, and her husband wrote notes to the two chapters instead. In the notes, Karen talks about herself. She was interrogated in prison, and she “wept with joy” on the first day, when she was not taken for questioning. She decided to die of thirst and splashed water, which was brought twice a day, but by the evening of the second day she fell off; then she was carrying a child. Her hope held: the jailers would serve her coffee at noon in a beautiful wooden cup and a jam bun, and Karen dreamed that one day she would buy such a bun on the [[marengo|Marengo]] promenade overlooking the sea. "I survived for the sake of a jam bun," she writes. Twenty-three years later, she met one of her jailers, a beggar on paper, who had been kicked out of the service at Telurian for drunkenness.

In her youth, Karen said, there was a custom: who wanted to talk about love, sent an invitation and a bird feather - white to a pleasant conversation, black to jealousy or separation. On the eve of Sophia's days, [[menson|Manson]] sent her a black pen with the ship's monkey Polundra. Indignant Karen rushed to his ship, and he laughed: “So I knew that you will buy.” It was the most joyful day of her life. She also recalls that she traveled with [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] to the Crownlands, "suffering a lot" while she chose hotels, and knew her "very different."

In the end, Karen admits that the feelings of the characters moved her: she knows what awaits them next, and reads about them with fear and sadness.

## Personality and Appearance

An aging lady of the "chachot species", ugly, but endowed with such dignity that in her presence Minerva felt like an impostor. She is mocking, proud, observant; she thinks and remembers perfectly, has a developed intuition of “granddaughter Elena”. Navi described it as: “He spits poison like a snake, pulls everyone around and eats buns with pounds.” Karen called her number six. She likes mental games like execution, not cards and dice. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) Life "never got rid of" her sense of justice. Her husband loves her without looking back and calls her “diamond of my soul” and “diamond of my heart.” According to her, the couple “don’t really like it when ... they talk about children.”
