## Who He Is

Burke is a mercenary and an outlaw. His face looks more like a ferret than a glorious warrior: a pointed nose, evil eyes. [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] met him a year before the events of the novel on a schooner going from the [[belomorye|Belomorye]] to [[rejs|Reis]]. The ship was stuffed with mercenaries who were going to make money in the [[melnichnaya-vojna|Mill War]]. Burke won cards all the time. The loser of the rumble grabbed his hand and ripped his shirt, hoping to find a drop ace, but a steel "feather" turned out to be in his sleeve. In the blink of an eye, the kaftan of the thunderbolt disintegrated with flaps of cross-cross. When asked in what army they teach such things, Burke only laughed: “What do I look like a servant?”

Burke was followed by a "long train of cities" from which he left in haste: Farway, Whiteshed, Otmel, [[labelin|Labelin]], Grey Cliff, Twinsey, Belomorye. ([[otmel|The Shoal]]) But he loved [[aleridan|Aleridan]]. There, he said, he is “quiet and peaceful” and will someday buy a home.

## In "Only One Star"

**Imaginary protection.** In 1774, Joaquin was out for a country wedding at the miller Hodges. There were three “protective agents” and began to extort money from the elder and the miller, threatening to lock all the guests in the barn. One was Burke, the other was a Fox. Joaquin stood up for the peasants, and the crooks fled. The groom knocked Burke off a pole horse, and Joaquin chased him for a stable. There Burke asked in an angry whisper, "You're totally nuts, Joe?! Did he not know?” In order not to betray a friend, Joaquin punched him in the wall, and Burke shouted loudly. Elder Joaquin's money was taken from him. The next morning they met on the hill. Burke said that the winter was tidy, and in the spring he was “fishing” with the guys, and admitted that the meeting was for the better: there was a real protection around.

**Squire.** Joaquin asked him to be his squire. Burke first asked, “Are you going to feed?” When he found out that they would go to the disgraced Duke Aiden Almer, he was at first indignant. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) Then I decided that I could eat for a month at the duke's expense and leave before the sparklers arrived. In [[evergard|Evergard]] they were not accepted: [[khamfri|Sir Humphrey]] slammed the gate. Burke advised to hire a merchant or an archbishop. Joaquin refused to leave, and the friends spent many days on the hill near the besieged fortress. Burke grumbled that the food and drink were running out, and the search for an underground passage was ridiculed.

**Night of fire.** On the night of September 2, 1774, Evergard was captured and burned. When Joaquin rushed to the castle, Burke held him back: “You won’t make it, fool!” He explained the magic of the attackers simply: “ordinary magic.” At the exit of the underground passage, friends came across fugitives. One patch knocked Burke down, but Sir Humphrey recognized Joaquin and accepted help. Later, he directly addressed the “squire Burke”: the Duchess needs their service. Burke immediately asked about the payment. In the hunting ground, where the fugitives were waiting for archers of the parish, he, along with Humphrey, chopped down the shooters who sat behind the cart.

**The road to Blackmore.** Burke explained to Joaquin why Humphrey was driving [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] a hundred miles past nearby castles: any vassal, having recognized a living milady, could sell her to the parish "thousands for a hundred". In Flathill, he was assigned to sell a notable carriage. The carriage was given to the crooks, and Burke found the merchant inconspicuous tarantas for one and a half Elenas. In the castle of Count [[blekmor|Blackmore]], he, like Joaquin, was accommodated in guest rooms. There Burke had eaten lunch and unsuccessfully looked after the maid.

**Betrayal of Blackmore.** At night, Burke brought "bad news." He saw the sentries let three messengers out of the gate, although the guests were not let out. He pretended to be a servant who wanted to give a message to his wife in Aleridan, and realized that the messengers were going to the parish. When the soldiers of the count went on the assault, Burke, along with Joaquin and Lance, held the ladder. He was the first one to say, “You have to jump,” and after Lance jumped out of the window onto the roof of the barn. In the turmoil of the fire, he unlocked the gate gate gate. Joaquin rode off with Alanis, leaving behind "three good people": Burke, Humphrey, and [[sofi|Sophie]].

**A bonfire in Aleridan.** While wandering, Joaquin went to Aleridan for the execution of heretics accused of setting fire to Evergard. They were taken under Blackmore and tortured for a week. Among those convicted was Cliff Wanda Cliff of Toystone. When the convicts were given the last word, the far left shouted that the person he was tortured about was here and pointed a broken nose at Joaquin. Only then did Joaquin find out in Burke's bloody heretics. The guards of the parish rushed after him, Burke shouted after his full name, and Joaquin barely left. He reasoned that Burke died "only because he saw Alanis Almera after her death."

Later, in "Only One Star", Joaquin told Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] his story and began with the fact that he and Burke spent days at Evergard.
