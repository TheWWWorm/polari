**Kid** is a hunting boy from [[spot|Spot]], a conductor of Erwin Origin's exploit through Soft Fields. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) Stunning and dreary, he became for Erwin a model of a simple man who sees the bright side in everything.

## Origin and craft
How old he was, Kid did not know - "And who knows!" - but in appearance he was about fourteen: short, skinny, with a gun over his lip, which still did not remotely resemble a mustache. Elder Howard called him the best connoisseur of the Soft Fields in all of Spot: four summers and four autumns Kid went to the top for the eggs of the runner, eight times returned alive and with prey. No mention was made of his family. Erwin later called him "a commoner from an ordinary family."

## Personality
Kid was a “dormant ignorant”: illiterate, barely aware of what a book was, not knowing who the emperor and the nobles were. Wars for him happened by themselves, “like thunderstorms or snowfalls.” Of the full name of the lord, he remembered only "Origin" and so called him: "Try venison with raspberries, Origin!" - for which he was twice plowed, although he did not understand why. After a three-hour title lesson, he learned two things: the Origins live in a stone house, "huge as a rock", and if you say "my lord", there will be no porot. He revered the Duke of Desmond almost as a god, and Erwin as the future ruler of the world.

But the Kid was “extremely cheerful.” Every evening he summed up: “It was a good day, eh!” — and Erwin first threatened him with a gag, and then bitterly admitted that the days in the forest were really good. The Greys loved him. He eagerly questioned the mechanic [[luis-mariya|Louis]] about the spark and shone all day, realizing Erwin's explanation that the spark eats the power of the river like a man of porridge.

## In the Explorado
- **Through the Soft Fields (May 1774).** On the first day in the Fields, Kid first recognized the beast (“It’s a fang!”), and in the evening, when the rain poured, he led the squad further: at night, widow snakes hunt in the swamp. When Erwin fell into the quagmire, Kid found himself side by side, shoulder by shoulder in a snout ("My lord, you can stand here!"), and held the losing lord by the hair so that he would not choke.
- **A snake in a tent.** A few days later, a widow was found in Erwin's tent. Kid, white with horror, fell to his knees: "This is my... widow..." - he caught her to target the poison for the arrows. Captain [[teobart|Theobart]] recalled the Northern law: a commoner who endangered the life of a lord is executed. Erwin forgave the boy, announcing that he had pulled him out of the quagmire and they were counting. Later, Erwin realized that the snake had been stolen from Kid and planted in his tent by Louis.
- **Bad air and rebellion.** At dawn, Kid brought nine eggs of the runner, each with three apples. He smelled “bad air” over the dry grass and, proving that it was not poisonous, sat in a stench for ten minutes; when the gas broke out, Kid shouted: “Throw water!” At dusk, he woke up Erwin - "Kairs, they're up to something..." - took him to the conspirators and gave him the sword: thus began the rebellion of Jemis. ([[myatezh-dzhemisa|Jemis's Rebellion]])
- **At the River.** In the woods by the River Kid took Erwin hunting ("You're lying loudly, my lord...") and showed the snake-grass: her juice is poisonous, but wounded animals chew it so that the wound does not rot. "Beasts are sometimes wiser than men."
- **Farewell (June 6, 1774)** The conductors remained on the west bank of the River. Having received the calculation, the Kid, who had never seen twenty agates together, folded them either with a slide or with a column. "May you have many good days!" he said in farewell, and Erwin decided to drink an orja with him when he was back in Spot. Kid and [[kolemon|Colemon]] took Erwin's letters to his father and [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]].

## After
Kid's lesson saved Erwin's life: wounded beyond the River, he poured snake-grass juice into the festering wound. In Agate's prayer, he thanked her "for the Kid that warned of rebellion and told about the snake-grass." In August 1774, at Spot, Kid met the returning Erwin and Jemis: "Origin! That's right! You are back!” When he found out that only two had survived, he almost sat down on the ground: “Do you want to die?” Wolves?... The Magician, right? Erwin didn't say anything and sent him for food. There is no more Kid in the cycle.

Erwin recalled him later: “There is a bright side to everything, as the hunter Kid used to say.” In The Shadow of the Great Tree, while arguing with [[otets-david|Father David]] about honor, Erwin gave the example of “The Kid —that young man from Spot, the ‘good day’”: a commoner who has no reason to hold himself high.
