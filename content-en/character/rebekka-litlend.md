## Origins
Rebecca is the daughter of Lord Douglas Litland, younger brother of Duke Willas, ruler of Litland. ([[litlend|Litland]]) In the texts of the Litlendy cycle, they are called descendants of Pramateri Janmei, but Becca herself, according to the author, is a sofievka, of the Sophia family. ([[pramater-sofya|Sophia the Majestic]]) In the very first edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, it was erroneously called Yanmeyanka ("Rebecca Eleanor Agatha of the Yanmei family"), in subsequent editions it was corrected. Becca spent half of her childhood in Shepherd's Meadows, the largest of the border fortresses that guarded the fords through Holliwell. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) From the tower of this fortress to the south, west and north, only the “green ocean” of grass was visible, and in the east, at the edge of the sky, the neighboring fortress “with a grain of buckwheat”. The girl from an early age saw clashes with [[shavany|Shavans]], raids, blood feuds and funerals. When the adults saw that she did not cry at the sight of the dead, they stopped hiding them from her.

When Becca turned ten, her uncle appointed her father as a representative in the House, and the girl moved to [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) Then, she said, she had plump cheeks and curls, and the men called her "a lovely child." As a child, she believed that there were a dozen horses inside the rail tractor. One day his father persuaded the driver to show her the device of the car. The chaos of pipes and wheels so frightened the girl that since then she decided not to look into the device of things and people: “everything is good as it is seen from the outside.”

She has two older married sisters, C and Molly. The capital whispered that both were barren, and these rumors ruined Becky's chances of marrying the emperor. Becky's mother loves children. She cried when her daughter first won the games because the win made Becca an adult. Now the mother takes turns with her older daughters and every month sends a younger letter with a new means for fertility.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
### Meeting with Gloria
In the summer of 1774, Becca was one of three contenders for [[adrian|Adrian]]'s hand along with [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] and Valerie Gracend. ([[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]]) Secretary [[itan|Ethan]] introduced her to [[minerva|Mira]], posing as [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria Northwood]], as the Empire's best rider. Becca was walking with Ser [[adamar|Adamar]], the master's cousin. Waiting out the storm with Mira, she outlined the Northerner "rules for choosing the Empress of the Brilliant Dynasty." The bride must be between seventeen and twenty-two years old, and she must belong to the family of Yangmei, Sophia or Agatha. Western leaders are "too wild" and defamed by the Horse Wars, Origin is too strong, and Darkwater and Shayland are too young. Finally, for a century and a half, no emperor took a wife from the Great House. The applicant must be "fertile, predictable and safe" - so Becca summed up the requirements. At the same time, Mira noticed the muscles on her bare hand. Becca said that it was the victories at the games that turned Bekka Yuzhanka into Bekka Horse. She remained silent about her own right to the throne, only smiling sadly when Mira guessed.

### Walk in the Jager Forest
Becca invited Mira on a horseback ride in the Jager Forest. She rode a young red stallion and ruled them without spurs and whips, with light movements. The girls eluded the guards to play Sera Adamar: cover the snare with skin and portray the monster. Mira suspected that Becca was leading her into a trap, and at the ravine without a bridge, she refused to jump after her. When Becca left on her own, four riders were cut off. However, the southern woman returned: shot one attacker in the shoulder, raised the horse of the other with an arrow “below her ass”, jumped back across the ravine and took her friend. After that, the girls switched to “you.” The next day, Adamar fell from his horse into a ravine without a bridge and twisted his neck. Mira realized that the ambush was being prepared for him, and they and Becca came across by chance.

### Friend and ally
At the summer ball, Becca represented the friend of the courtiers and made fun of Alanis. In the chocolate parlor, she helped Mira figure out who could be behind the deaths at court, told about the Church and the fact that her uncle-duke in January secretly met with [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]. From her letter, Mira learned the details. Erwin persuaded the Litlens to abandon Becky's claim to the throne in exchange for four fords across Holliwell into lean possession. For six months, the father hid it from his daughter, while she was preparing for games and hoping for the crown.
> “I know now: my price is four crossings over Holliwell.”

When Mira's poisoned body fell down, Becca came to her without an invitation. At first, after a quarrel with her father, she wanted to give up games. She wanted to "jump to the West and marry the first barbarian." A friend persuaded her to perform. Last year, Becca won two types of equestrian competitions, now she had three. After learning from Mira the findings of the investigation, she spoke cold and hard for the first time. She understood that to hand them over to Origin was to condemn the guilty to death, but she compared this to the deaths of Adamar and Lord Stagforth. At the games, the stands chanted "Becca!". The dying Peace asked Mark to convey to the Lord that Becca is “a smart, kind, brave, devoted person.” Before she was taken to the Peace Monastery under the supervision of [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]], Gallarda wrote a farewell note to Becky. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) Sybil hoped that the "Horse" will carry the news of the monasticism of Gloria.

## The “Soul of the West”
### Solved note
The interlude is written as Becky's letter to "Gloria", that is, to the World. Becca admitted that it was her friend who taught her to look for the “second bottom” in things. She first felt it on the podium when Adrian called Minerva Gemma Alessandra his fiancée: Becca suddenly saw the gears of politics that forced him to say exactly that name. After the games, congratulations reached the Litland house. Becca asked her father to let her choose her husband or at least wait a year.

Ethan came to her, looking for the missing Gloria. In August 1774, Becca solved a clue in a note. “Sorrow” pointed to Ulyana Sad, which means that her friend was sent to a monastery by force. His father forbade interfering in Northwood affairs (“We are not bedbugs to poke in other people’s beds!”). According to the books, Becca compiled a list of twenty-four monasteries of Ulyana and asked his father half of his fifty soldiers for a month to go around them. He refused and threatened to burn the letter.

### Moran
The family moved to Lacey's estate to negotiate with Count Damir Reis. There, Becca met the blue-eyed [[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]], the Count's right hand. At dawn, they competed in a race through meadows, gardens, and a river to the apple orchard. Both hit the apple with an arrow, and Moran hit the cutting with fifty steps. Then Moran put an apple on his top, Becca fired, and he caught an arrow on the fly. He said she had a free soul, "like you're from the West." Later, he found her in a meadow behind a grove where she was walking with the mare Luna, and kissed her. Becca stopped the rest with the word “no.” Before dawn, they rode together in the night meadows, and in the morning the father locked his daughter under guard.

### Trap
Becca noticed that her father was hiding a letter with a card from her, and overheard: “This fundamentally changes everything.” The ambassadors proposed to build a protected road through the Shepherds of the Meadows and Reis to the west coast. Father and Lord Coss delayed with the answer by running an "auction". Count Damir and Moran asked Becca to reveal what was in the letter, but she did not know. In the evening, eight pigeons flew from Lacey - a pair to each border fortress by the fords. Becca realized that the family was preparing for war.

At a farewell dinner, Coss demanded half of the Shepherd's Meadows for Litland. Moran shouted, "Rebecca, what was in the letter?" From her face, he realized that an ambush was waiting for them, and Becca shouted the Shavan "Run!". Coss fired a crossbow reinforced under the countertop and fatally wounded the count. Moran killed Becca's loyal knight Gage, threw himself out the window, and ran away. When Coss aimed at him, Becca hit the crossbow bed and then hit the Lord's knee (an ambush in Lacey) with her shoe. ([[zasada-v-lejsi|Ambush at Lacey]]) Gage died that evening, and Count Damir died two days later. Moran is said to have killed five Lithuanians in the fight; the chase did not catch up with him, although Becca, she said, could. The father stopped talking to her, believing that his daughter thwarted his plan. Becca understood Coss's plan: to make obviously insane demands, provoke ambassadors and interrupt them so that Reis would be plunged into turmoil for years. Becca decided that his father "acted like a complete bastard," and concluded:
> "I have the blood of Yangmei... but the soul of the West."

## In "Only One Star"
Becca showed Ethan a note from Mira and deciphered it: "Northern" is in the monastery of Ulyana Sad in the west of Almera. She asked her father for an escort, but he understood what she was doing, and put her daughter under lock and key. Minerva later restored the chain of events: Becca went with a clue to her father, and then to Ethan, and both answered that this was a personal matter of the Northwoods. Rumors of Gloria's imprisonment meanwhile reached Vittor Shayland. In the fall of 1774, after an ambush at Lacey, the Shawnees invaded Litland. In December, at a three-day feast in Bassifor after Adrian's victory, [[menson|Manson]] noticed that of all the Litlands, only Rebecca hides some sadness behind a smile. The jester comforted her with the word "Trrriumph!", and she gently patted him on the shoulder.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
The new Empress Minerva wrote to her friend in vain. She sent three letters, and the fourth took to the dovehouse herself, but there was no answer, although in Litland, according to Mira, it was quiet and [[melorandzh|Melorange]] was considered reliably protected. Iona Origin considered Becca a great candidate for the first ladies of the empress: she had both fertility and a bright temper. Among the Shavans, meanwhile, there were rumors that Rebecca had saved Moran's life in Lacey and that he had vowed to leave Meloran without her; Moran himself confirmed to Neymir that "the daughter turned out to be a man", but answered the question of the betrothal evasively. In negotiations with Minerva, he demanded "a girl, Rebecca Litland", confessing that he loved her. The Empress replied that it would be up to Rebecca to decide. Erwin, while trying to cross over the suspects in the Puppeteers, rejected Moran, in particular because he could not count on "Becca the Horseman to save him."

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
In May 1775, opening the House, Minerva appointed representatives of the Crown "first, herself", and the second - Rebecca Eleanor Agatha, Lady Litland. Erwin reasoned that only a handful of people trusted Mira, and Becca was the only one high enough for the Chamber. The weakened Litlands will accept the appointment as a favor, and it will bring the Empress three votes at once: Becky and two representatives of the House. When Ganta [[ganta-groza|Thunderstorm]] in the House accused the Litlands of killing ambassadors and misappropriating fords, Becca rushed from her seat, but remained silent. She laughed as Francis-Ilian entered the hall with Manson. At Manson's trial, she was the first to applaud Minerva's speech. ([[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]]) When Erwin locked the doors of the House and demanded to vote on the same day, both representatives of Litland raised the flags in favor. At the request of Minerva Becca, evil looked back at the countrymen, and one of them lowered his hand. In voting to depose Adrian, even her father and the rest of the Litlands saved by Adrian did not go against the majority. Only Becca and Peace raised the red flags.

## In "Humans and Gods"
### Change
Something happened to Becca. On the first day, she honestly warned Minerva that her parents had told her to use friendship for politics, and offered to leave if it was embarrassing. Mira hugged her. Becca weaned her friend from drinking along with the lady, to tears of laughter, when Mira pretended to be drunk and Origin believed, supported her in the Chamber and ridiculed enemies, and, when necessary, herself. Upon learning of Waymar's horrors, she genuinely hated Martin Shayland. She advised against trusting Lady-in-the-Dark and questioned the overly alluring plan of ambush in the tomb. But the questions about the war answered evasively: a third of Litland is devastated, and she wants to forget. When asked what had changed, she replied, “Growing up.” About Morana said she doesn't feel anything for him:
> “Until we saw each other, I learned this: to feel nothing.”

### Disputes and Reconciliation
After Minerva's abdication in favor of Adrian Becca, Leila Talmir and Shatterhand became her advisors. Mira began to talk about how to hold the crown, and Becca with the done reason listed the ways, up to the murder of Origin. She then offered to tell [[vittor-shejland|Shayland]] where the Duke would be to be burned by the Rings. It was only then that Mira realized she was hearing sarcasm. Becca recalled that "this horse is no more": the empress herself denied, and Origin goes to war on two people who humiliated her. Minerva put out a friend, and she went to the tavern.

A few days later, Becca came back with an apology. The friends spent the day in the city incognito: they jumped along the embankment, played a lemonade dealer, played hide-and-seek with children, ate sweets. Becca put on cock fights. Then they listened to a vagabond preaching the purification of the Rings by fire. Becca said that this belief “proves that you are no worse than anyone else.” She apologized again for the past advice, and Mira acknowledged that the advice may have been good. Later, Becca criticized Minerva's decision to take [[menson|Manson]] by jester: the master's brother had already been made a solder for humiliation. Then the friends had fun watching the jester take revenge on the yard. To help the war-torn Litland, Mira halved duties on Litland goods and staged a Litland goods fair on Palace Island. Manson said that it was done “for the sake of a girlfriend.” When Mira hesitated, she consulted Layla, Shatterhand, and Becca; Becca was on one side and the other.

### The Siege of Melorange
When news of the heretics arrived, Becca told Minerva in private that she had survived the siege. In March 1775, Steppe Fire had already defeated Prince Hector and surrounded Melorange. The father asked if she still had feelings for Moran. Becca said she cursed him. Father explained with annoyance that Moran offers the world for a hundred ephes to each rider and bride. He asked his daughter to agree. Then, according to the narrator, her love for Moran finally became hatred - "to two men at once."

Becca jumped on the first horse she caught and rushed into the quarter of General William Davey's spark regiment, the only one in the city where the Litland authorities did not recognize. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) Davey said he was ordered to defend the city, not pay tribute to the enemy, and let her stay. Becca lived in an infirmary room and cared for the wounded. An injured field intelligence officer gave her a fake tooth with a porcelain poison capsule. Twenty-year-old Becca inserted the capsule into the gap between her teeth. A week later, the Shavans broke into the city, but they were interrupted, and later repulsed by the second assault. After the siege was lifted, her father, mother and sisters followed her to the barracks, and she could not refuse them all. The officer did not take the tooth back, and Becca kept it in a box as a reminder of "what her family is like."

### Erwin's proposal
On the last day of May, three kairs led Becca to Erwin. Before leaving, she put the tooth in place. Erwin compared Moran to Gett from the Shavan legend and explained that [[paul|Paul]] and the Persians were going to bend the horde to the side of the Puppeteer. ([[legenda-o-gette|The Legend of Gett]]; [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) He suggested that Becca marry Moran to kill Shayland's ambassadors. Erwin compared his idea with the northern song about the "bad four" - a priest, a nun, a robber and a girl. The role of the girl was intended for Becky. Instead of responding, Becca showed the capsule:
> "If you give me to Moran, he'll get a corpse."

At first she laughed at Erwin in the face, and he told the Xams to detain her; after she showed the capsule, she went out, and the Duke did not dare detain her. When the anger passed, Becca realized that the horde’s alliance with the Puppeteer was a terrible thing.

### Departure
Upon learning that a brigade of heretics is breaking through Hope and may be ahead of Erwin with Groza, Becca found no peace for three days. She explained to Meera what a dangerous alliance is: the light cavalry of the Shavans with the Rings will have nothing to stop, and she will "knock out" all the armies. Moran, on the other hand, craves revenge for Melorange. Minerva, under threat of treason charges, banned her from going. At night, the guards found Becca drunk, and the lady canceled the order. She told her to go to Rei-Roy and promised to ransom her from slavery or fulfill her will if Moran killed her. “Thank you, Peace. I was looking forward to your support,” Becca said.

She wrote Erwin a letter in the presence of a guard who confirmed his authenticity:
> “But the new Gette is about to be born in the Steppe. I can, I have to stop it.”

Becca said that he will take a train to Julian bridge and sit on a horse. She asked the Duke to lead the army to the bridge, and if she did not succeed, kill the “monster” at all costs: “You are the Origin, eat you darkness.”

### At the gates of Rei-Roy
On the way to Rei-Roy, the prisoners of the Shavan said that with Moran there was a “strange maiden” who jumped a few days ago. Erwin decided that Moran was keeping Becca to himself. At the gates of the kairah found Becca frozen in a jump from the saddle: her body and horse absorbed "not living and not dead" matter. The skin acquired the color of a dried mummy, the eyes glazed, the hand was brought in, the mouth was opened in an inaudible scream. Moran died in the fall. Cairo, the first to see them, shouted "My Lord!" in fear, and Erwin, coming up to the scream, gave himself a finger on the wall. Erwin, cyre Aubrey and Sagittarius managed to hide from the fire only in the shadow of Rebecca's frozen horse: Perst did not pierce black matter.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
In July 1775, the fate of Becky was not yet known in Faunterra. Erwin sent the last letter from the [[slavnyj-dozor|Glorious Watch]] of Julian Bridge as he was on his way to Rei-Roy, and disappeared. “In the same way,” Minerva noted with alarm, and Becca Litland did not make herself felt. Playing with Navi in the execution, Mira overcame her vices and, among other things, blamed herself: “I’m sad about Rebecca. She sent Rebecca to her death." She told herself that Becca, like Origin, knew what she was taking risks for.

From [[alanis-almera|Alanis]]’s memories, it became known how everything happened. Paul froze Moran with the help of Eternity, the subject that stops time. Not all Shavans saw this, and when Rebecca came to Moran, Paul repeated the "presentation" in front of the horde. With the words “Do you want to connect with your loved one? A noble desire" he threw a handful of black flies into her horse, and the second one into her herself. Alanis first assured her reflection that the souls of Becky and Moran were on the Star. Later, she realized that Eternity does not just kill: the souls of the frozen did not get to the Star and remained imprisoned in the bodies.

The Empire considers Becca dead. Minerva grieved for her. The Litlands considered it an honor to support Adrian, especially since Rebecca, in their opinion, died due to the fault of Mira. [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] later confessed to Erwin that he had been terrified when he saw Moran and Rebecca frozen. Erwin later recalled that Lady Rebecca had a tooth full of poison.

## Personality and Appearance
Becca is short and thin, with dark skin, on which almost no blush is visible, with large dark eyes and moving eyebrows. In the first scene, her eyes are "big and black", and at the ball, where she wore emerald earrings, they are green. Before the games, Mira noticed that her bare hands were emblazoned with muscles. She is lively, witty, sincere, loves sweets, coffee and gossip, talks with horses and considers it quite natural. Becca is "a kind of live coat of arms of the Litlands": she is always in sight, shining and smiling. She considered herself only a rider, far from politics. However, her friendship with Mira taught her to "see the second bottom", and the war and betrayal of her relatives made her an adult and withdrawn. She knew how to hide so that the children could not find her, and, in her own words, she knew how to “ride and charm men.” She paid for her determination with her life.

## Relationships
### Minerva
[[minerva|Minerva]] is a close friend. Becca saved her life in the Yeger Forest, was the first to unravel her exile, and later became her advisor and representative in the House. After Minerva's abdication, she sarcastically ridiculed her plans, but then apologized. Mira first forbade her to go, and then ordered herself to go to Rei-Roy, considering this decision to be “the only correct one.”

### Moran
Moran's Steppe Fire is her love in the summer of 1774. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) He saw in her the "soul of the West", and she saved his life in Lacey. Litland's ruin and his father's offer turned love into hate, but Becca still went to him to prevent the horde's alliance with the heretics. Both were frozen by Eternity at the gates of Rei-Roy.

### Father
Lord Douglas likes to ask for advice and calls his daughter a "vein", but he is weak. Twice he disposed of her fate for the benefit of the house: in January 1774, he agreed to exchange her chances for a crown for for fords, and in the spring of 1775 he wanted to give her to Moran. Becca didn't forgive it.

### Erwin Origin
[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] first thwarted her hopes for the crown, then tried to send her to Moran. She rejected him, but in the end went alone and in the letter still entrusted him with the last hope.
