## Who She Is

Demeter is one of the sixteen daughters of King Francis-Ilianus and sister of Prince Hector. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]; [[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) Girls of her kind, according to the narrator, are famous for the beauty of bodies, but their faces lack grace. Demeter has round cheeks and a sluggish chin, but “body shapes are very convex.”

She studied in a boarding school with [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] and [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]. Alanis recalled her as a "beggar" who "did not burst from importance: ah, I am a princess of the south, a whole daughter of the king!", and the younger girls ran after her with a retinue. Iona said that Demeter lived by the rule “love yourself, then others will love you”, that a third of the course became her fans and that Iona and Alanis had to team up “to put her in her place.”

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

[[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]'s father, the Snow Count, arranged for his son to marry a Shimmerian princess. In December 1775, Jemis showed Erwin a marriage letter with a winged lion from the "First of the Five, glorious over the glorious, keeper of the keys to the Lion's Gate": "the maiden of Demeter from the Great House of Nellis-Lyon" dreamed of relating with "the fearless cyre of Jemis". Jemis said he didn't reject the princess, and wanted to invite Erwin to the wedding.

## In "Fantasies"

"Fantasies" the author calls the possible fates of the heroes. Demeter is the heroine of "Amicable" (August 1776, Laem).

### The Maiden Palace

For sixteen daughters, Francis-Ilian built a separate palace among artificial waterfalls and multi-storey gardens - "a worthy place for a happy childhood" - and allowed the daughters to live there until marriage. The sisters married or left to study, and Demeter remained the last inhabitant of the Maiden Palace. Hector envied her, tried to resettle her and in retaliation paid her only the budgeted infant rent. The palace only became more beautiful: Demeter herself earned money. Its maintenance cost seventy ephes per month.

She was enterprising since childhood. At the age of seven, Demeter staged a “peacock race”: scared the birds with the roar of a snow leopard and took a commission from each bet. When the sisters began to marry, she came up with the idea to send the grooms instead of portraits of the figurine, and three princesses Shimmery successfully married, and a quarter of the income of the first sculptor Laem went to her. While his father lived in the monastery, Demeter published the monthly Diary of a Dreamer, where secular news alternated with Francis-Ilian's "fresh prophecies"; she wrote most of the "dreams of the prophet" herself, and her interpretation was her friend from the philosophical school.

Demeter is a trendsetter of Laem's mod and "trades his fame". Merchants pay her to put their product into fashion: perfumer Mallius, Balteron glovemaker, jeweler Vincent. For the sake of fashion, she went to the theater in different gloves, white and golden. At her feet, snow leopard Yammi, the son of Tammy, a “famous peacock hunter”, sleeps: he sneezes if the aroma suits him, and growls at “prudence”.

### Bridegroom

Demeter had been angry for six months, "from the day of that sweetest conversation with her father": the king promised not to force her to marry, but, "being carried away by the Tree", promised her to Jemis. Jemis chose her, rejecting six or seven northerners, precisely because she grew up in the South. She did not come to meet the groom (“at the sounds of your name, she throws dishes at the wall,” Hector joked). On the eve of the widow Vanessa-Lilit offered to help her in case the meeting with the kire promises “not only joy”. ([[vanessa-lilit|Vanessa-Lilith]])

The meeting took place at Hector's palace when her leopard got into a fight with Jemisa's dog Sagittarius; the prince laughed, "I think you're worth each other." The demeter of the groom’s stake with questions to which he knew the answers in advance, but he remained undisturbed. She spent the night in the same hotel "Black Swan", locking her room on a bolt. The next morning, they were walking around Laem. Demeter volunteered to help in the search for Barney, the nephew of Captain [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]], who was brought to the auction of althess and bought by an unknown person in a white cloak.

Jemis found out that Vanessa-Lilit sent an intermediary, and Demeter was behind her: she “forgot her gloves” to return and give instructions to the widow. In the audience with Hector, he terminated the marriage contract and invited House Nellis-Lyon to respond with blood in the fight. Demeter explained that she wanted to upset the wedding only in spite of her father, and the auction became for her a test of the groom: whether he would help a friend, whether he would first think about Barney or about her bed, he would look with his mind or fists. She found "mind, courage and heart" in him ("I would not go to the altar with a piece of iron"). Jemis replied that the test did not offend him, but he would not forgive cruelty to the child. Then Demeter told me to bring Vanessa, and the widow returned Barney to her uncle.

The last phrase of the story reports that next winter her leopard Yammi and Sagittarius “did manage to make friends” in the city of [[lid|Leed]], the ancestral city of Jemisa, a hint that Demeter still went to the North.

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the autumn of 1776, when asked about the bride by Erwin, Jemis replied: “We got along,” and about her he said: “Girl with pepper.” Hyder Leed reported that "Count Lilliday is acquainted with the bride." In the comic "section" of the Lands of the Crown, Erwin gave Ruild to "Jemis with Demeter, they wanted to live at sea."
