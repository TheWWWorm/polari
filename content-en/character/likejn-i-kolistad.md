Doris Lickane and Shiron Kolistad are captains from the Volta who make money hunting sea monsters. ([[folta|Folta]]) He calls them his “good friends” Mireille Nan-Claire. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], they call Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Origin]] with them to go after the gurken. Almost everything that is known about them is told in two chapters of Mask, where the story is conducted through the eyes of Sofia. Lycaine speaks Polarian "quite clean", Colistad picks up the words with difficulty. Nothing is said about their past and their families in the books.

## In "Humans and Gods"
### Invitation to Maple
In mid-June 1775 in the Bay of Maple schooner "Sea Arrow" flags invited Admiral Volta to visit. On board came Mireille with two companions and presented them as follows: "Captain Doris Lickane and Captain Chiron Colistad are my good friends ... Captains Lickane and Colistad are hunters of sea monsters." Captain [[dzheff-bamber|Jeff Bamber]] asked if they were fishermen. Leakin said, “All fish don’t care. We're hunting a big sea animal! The black bad guy, the keelcap, the mastolo, the bottom of the tree - that's what we need. The Voltaic merchant pays a lot of money!" Kolistad added: “Ancient is more than a ship, and payment is even more ancient!”

Bamber laughed that such monsters do not exist. Then Kolistad, “blanking his teeth”, jumped up: “Pollarites! Never to believe!” Lickane calmed him down with a few words in voltaic. He also explained the purpose of the campaign: seven hundred miles east of the coast of Polaris is a gourmet, the “king of monsters”. When Sophia said that she had never heard of such a beast, Lycaine opened the window of the salon and banged to his knees: “Gods of the sea, forgive these people! They are dark, not to know, not to know - polarities! There is no evil in them.”

The Sea Arrow team dissuaded the Duchess, but Sofia accepted the invitation. In response to kair Glenn, who considered hunting a hoax, she pointed to the ships of hunters: if it is a performance, then too much money is invested in masks. Their sides were ironed, their masts were ringed with steel, their noses were full of rams, and their deck ballasts were loaded with huge harpoons. In the morning, the squadron left Maple: the first was the Guardian, the second was the White Wolf, and the Sea Arrow closed the column.

### Trophies
The Kairs did not let Sofia go on the Foltia ships. Only once did she pay a courtesy visit to the Guardian, surrounded by a dozen swordsmen. The captains invited her to the lower deck to watch trophies from past hunts, but refused to allow all security there: they show trophies only to selected guests. Kair Glenn replied that there was nothing to show there, and Leakin was angry: “now we certainly will not show, you do not deserve!” The dispute was extinguished by Mireille and the judge. ([[ded|Grandfather]]) To make peace had a foltian droma, "the rarest abomination to taste", and trophies Sofia never saw.

### Kolistad on the "Sea Arrow"
At the end of June, as the squadron was emerging from the underwater forest into the "twilight waters", Mireille brought Kolistad to the schooner as an advisor to Bamber. It was the first time he was on board a northern vessel. The northerners decided to treat him respectfully, “but do not allow extra liberties.” Kolistad advised to stay behind "Guardian" and "White Wolf", when hunting do not come close and do not make noise. He also asked me to tell him if the schooner would start acting weird. Bamber and Kolistad sat together on the tank, and the sailors joked that they now had, like the Voltians, two captains.

Kolistad first noticed in the pipe of prey: “It’s time to start fishing!” The Northerners saw only a humpback whale. "Gurken's not here yet," said Shiron Kolistad. - But he's close. Start the hunt!” For several days, the "Guardian" forked from galls to galls, and the sailors decided that the Voltians were just taking the time. The island on the horizon Kolistad did not allow to visit: “Hunting is the first thing, and fun is the second thing.”

When the squadron finally went in pursuit, Kolistad praised the lagging schooner: "The Guardian will be the first to gourmet. He lures him, White Wolf kills him, you watch him.” Bamber then admitted to him that the Sea Arrow was not listening well to the steering wheel. Kolistad himself stood at the helm, and then swore and led Bamber to the duchess: the hunt must be interrupted and the schooner must be pulled to the shore of the island. Sophia mistook the whale for a gurken and saw no danger. Kolistad was amazed: “This is maktuk, food of gurkens! And the gurken is behind him, right over there!" Before their eyes, the whirlpool of the gurken dragged the maqtuk to the bottom. To explain what happened to the schooner, Kolistad could not. He claimed that something bad had happened in the forest, and promised: "Two days - we will be an island. There's the chic-chick and the end of the trouble!" What does “chick-chick” mean, no one understands.

According to the Voltaic maritime law, important decisions are made together, so Kolistad demanded that he be returned to the White Wolf: both captains were needed there. The Cairos wanted to keep him hostage, but the sailors decided that the Voltian on board was in trouble, and he was released. As soon as he met with his partner, the “White Wolf” raised the flags “Course to the island”. Mireille then reassured Sophia: “Shiron Kolistad is very emotional, he expressed himself too much and sowed in vain anxiety.”

### Kilecap
On the way to the island, the "Sea Arrow" opened to flow behind the stream. In the hold, the sailors heard an incomprehensible gnat. On the island, the schooner was pulled to the beach, and on the bottom there was a black network of fused tails of eels - a keelcap that gnawed boards. "The keel is to eat wood," said Captain Kolistad. He lives in a water forest. You touched a tree, a keel-clap. I am a fool.” Bamber countered that the Voltius had saved them. Kolistad explained his mistake: the keelcap does not eat northern ships, and the schooner built in Shimmery is made of foltic boards. The Kairas cut the creature with swords - this was the promised "chick-chick".

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
Captains by name are not named here. Sofia recalls that the Voltaic squadron for five months led the "Sea Arrow" on the disastrous waters. Along the way, they met a mirage, screamers and steel-winged birds, pestering the Metok Islands. One day, the Foltic captain shouted: “Young gourmet — easy prey!” — and led the “Guards” in pursuit. The young gourmet dragged the Guardian with a water funnel, the White Wolf and the Sea Arrow showered the monster with darts and harpoons until it blew its breath. The sailors cut off three tentacles from him, one for the ship. The storm then damaged the White Wolf's steering wheel, and the voyage to the South Mark stretched from two weeks to five. Going ashore at the end of the voyage, Sophia thought that five months of wandering "brought her together with the team and the Voltians."
