## Face and nickname

Juliana Oranta Adelia of the Yanmei family, who went down in history as **Juliana the Great**, is one of the most famous rulers of the Brilliant Dynasty. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) She was described by her portrait as follows: "ruled half a century, put an end to the Horse Wars, established the boundaries of the Empire, since then never violated, and introduced a single code of laws that was able to take into account the mores of all the lands of Polaris." She was called the “Princess of Justice”.

In the portrait, which [[minerva|Minerva]] transferred to the hall "Ladies' Feast" of the Palace of Pen and Sword, a lady of forty is depicted. The slanted eyes and the sly dimples on her cheeks gave her Yangmei, but, unlike Yangmei's Pramateri and Minerva herself, she was tall. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) Her white satin dress was covered not with heraldic, but with “vitally natural” gold patterns: flowers, birds, tiny horses. Diadem made of gold wire resembled ears or candle lights, and the background was a flowering garden. Even by the age of fifty, Juliana remained slender "like a cypress" and was six feet tall.

She got her nickname when she was still young. By the age of thirty, she had carried out a military reform, which brought an impressive victory in Almera, and the nobles began to call her Great. Juliana rebelled against this:
> “Our history is filled with great people, like a duck with apples. Don't you dare call me that. I'm Juliana Broad."

According to her, “great” means huge ambitions, that is, greed, and the ruler is much more valuable than the breadth of views: ruling a large country, you need to see it “from edge to edge, for many years to come.” She obstinately erased the "Great" from documents, books, poems and tablets on portraits. The court minister warned that descendants might attribute the nickname "Wide" to her appearance. The decisive argument was the gift of the Shavan ganta Mirlai: the carpet of the Reiroi master with the image of the immense empress on a tiny throne and the signature "Empress Juliana Wide". After that, the master humbled himself and agreed to be called the Great. The gift was not a mockery: in the West then lush ladies were valued, and the Steppes wanted to flatter the Empress.

## Ascending to the throne

Juliana came to power young, in the XVI century from the [[soshestvie|Descent]], during the Second Horse War. The history of her ascension of the World was found in the book "Love Stories", in the chapter "The Great Juliana, Passionate Juliana". The young emperor chose the maiden Juliana as his bride and, according to custom, announced the engagement in July, at the close of the summer games. The wedding was scheduled for October, the Days of Abundance, but in September, Vladyka fell ill with a pulmonary disease and died suddenly. After weeks of hesitation and negotiation, the Great Houses crowned the "inconsolable bride." So the empress became a woman, only engaged to the sovereign. The same book told about her ardent and secret love for her secretary: he did not dare to answer her feelings and for many years left to wander.

This precedent played a role in the events of 1774. Mira realized that the bride, left without a groom in the interval between the engagement and the wedding, can claim the throne “following the example of Juliana the Great.” However, other heirs would dispute her rights, and that is why the conspirator needed to kill them in order to clear the way for the bride.

## Management Board

The years of Juliana’s reign went down in history as “a long spring”: the empire “flourished with an unfading color.” Among her successes were:
- Liberation of [[nadezhda|Hope]] and the End of the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]
- victory on the Throat;
- rapid development of the marine fleet;
- The hundred-year treaty with [[shimmeri|Shimmery]], still in force in the eighteenth century;
- The imperial code of laws is the crown of her rule.

### Code of Laws

Juliana herself associated her popularity with the code, which carefully took into account the traditions and customs of all lands. Mira, considering the “additional value” that each ruler brings to the state, came to the conclusion that Yuliana she had justice. According to her, Polaris of her era suffered from strife, and the ruler had enough troops to gather the state by force. However, she chose to create laws to which all lands were willingly subject. This code is known as the Julian Law. ([[yulianov-zakon|Juliana's Law]]) It was also referred to in the 18th century: its provisions on execution for rebellion, a section on the proceedings on which Francis-Ilianus relied on the Manson trial. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]; [[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]]) The bishop, whom eight-year-old [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] asked about the burning of heretics, said that Juliana “in her mercy” abolished the church courts and transferred justice to secular judges. Since then, bonfires for sacrilege have burned very rarely.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collector’s edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark considers the main merit of Juliana is not a victory in the Second Horse War, but legislative reform. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) According to her, by the end of the 16th century, the Crown required all lands to live according to the imperial code, dating back to Yangmei and Yumin, and local customs contradicted this. Juliana legalized the special laws of each land: a person obeys the code of the land where he is. The very concept of “legal war”, according to Karen, is a personal innovation of Juliana.

### West and Steppe

After the Horse Wars, Juliana declared the Shepherds of the Meadow on the banks of the Holliwell a common possession of all neighboring lands. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) Four western and three eastern counties were given the right to herd herds there without restrictions. Since its time, it has been considered "as holy a truth" as the fact that the world was created by the gods. Violation of this order in favor of the Litlands in 1774 was one of the charges against [[adrian|Adrian]]. The messenger of Reis bluntly threw him: “You have violated the law of Julian the Great.”

Under Julian, the imperial army besieged Rei-Roy, where the younger Royhar locked himself in. The empress sent angry letters to the general, but the city held out, and the general suggested that the Shavan preserve livestock and land, recognizing her power. So the son of Lazy Vola received the title of Count [[rejs|Reis]]. Erwin interpreted the story differently: the general would easily have found a source of water, but Juliana wanted not to enslave, but to rally the lands of Polaris, and she needed a reasonable gauntlet that could be made her viceroy in the Steppe.

Shavans loved her for engineering miracle - Julian Bridge. He first tied the banks of the sacred Holliwell and opened an easy way from Reiss to Almera. Through the bridge, the steppes sold cattle to the central lands at any time of the year, and a whole rain of silver poured on the Steppe. In order to prevent various Western peoples from fighting at the entrance to the bridge, Juliana ordered the construction of the Fort Glorious Watch. This arched "mile-long giant" connecting West Almera with Holliwell County was the only bridge over Holliwell in 1775, and he was "nearly two hundred years old". It was followed in 1775 by Erwin's troops to the West, and then through it poured into the Almera Horde.

### Capital

Previously, the emperors lived in the gloomy Throne Citadel, where the rulers of the Dark-eyed Dynasty were killed in the Scarlet Trouble, and Bishop Evrian ordered the slaughter of two brothers-dukes. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) After a "difficult victory over the barbarians", Juliana moved the residence to the new Pera and Sword Marble Palace on an island in the middle of Hanoi. ([[khanaj|Khanai]]) Since then, the Citadel has lost its former significance.

## Legacy

- Juliana was named among the founders of the dynasty. The Eternal Hilt "was worn on the belt by Adrian... and Thelurien, and Evrian the Expander of Borders, and Julian the Great, and the Olgard the Founder." Her portrait hangs in the palace gallery among the portraits of Pramateri Janmei, Olgard the Founder and other rulers.
- Her tomb is in the Temple of Farewell in Arden. Passing through the tiers of the tomb, Erwin noticed that after Juliana, the Yanmean symbolism “reached the top”: only sayings, maps and wenzel remained on the frescoes.
- According to Juliana's plan, the summer games in Faunterra are bloodless.
- The boarding house of Elena-u-Lake is often considered founded by Juliana, but, according to his manager, by her visit he had existed for two centuries. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) The mistress admired the local views on women's education and donated thirty thousand Hilt, from which it began to flourish.
- Lady in Darkness is the great-granddaughter of Juliana the Great. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]])
- At the age of twenty, Erwin wrote an essay on the politics of Juliana the Great. Mira read the work “Keys of success of Juliana the Great”. The camera lady assured Mira that Juliana slept on the eve of the coronation "like a baby", and considered a strong sleep a sign of self-confidence.
- Minerva recalled that after she put on the Gauntlet of Power and stopped the war, she was nicknamed in one article “Minerva bearing peace”, and presented herself as the future defender of the people, “the new Juliana the Great”. Of Adrian, she thought he was a great ruler, "perhaps the best since Juliana."
- In the new play by Lady Sophia Origin, the commander of Juliana the Great acts. ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]])
