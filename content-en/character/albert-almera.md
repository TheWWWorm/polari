## Overview
Albert Adelia Abigail is the youngest of three children of Duke Aiden Almer and Duchess Alicia, brother of [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] and Alfred. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) In the summer of 1774, the [[mark-voron|Raven of the Crown]] described him as "a sweet, quiet boy". Albert was very thin and therefore seemed younger than his years, “like those dolls with a huge head and eyes”, and was inferior to the age of [[laura-farvej|Laura]] and [[dzheremi-farvej|Jeremy]] Farway. The older sister he called Ellie and adored her: “With all strict, and with me — good!”

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
Preparing to negotiate with [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] studied Almer's dynastic tree and calculated that Aiden could offer his youngest son, "a sweet seven-year-old boy with no hope of inheritance" as a husband to Farway's granddaughter Laura.

## In "Only One Star"
Albert survived the death of Evergard on the night of September 2, 1774: only a handful of soldiers of the garrison, a couple of dozen servants and a young lord remained alive. ([[pozhar-evergarda|Fall of Evergard]]) Lieutenant Humphrey, who carried the wounded Alanis underground, did not know about the fate of Albert. Alfred’s father and brother were killed. Archbishop [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] declared himself guardian of his nephew and regent of Almera. Some of the barons opposed this and said that you can not arbitrarily take the regency over the great lord without a decision of the council of prime vassals.

## “I want a pony.”
### Under the supervision of my uncle
In the winter of 1774–1775, Albert lived in [[evergard|Evergard]] with the hostages Laura and Jeremy Farway. Gallard introduced him to the guests as the "legitimate heir to the Duchy of Almer". The boy lived in constant fear: the Wilhelmin monks seemed to him “uncle tentacles”, he slept poorly and was afraid of making a mistake in prayer, because his uncle would punish him. He knew that there were thirty-two hearing chambers in the castle, leading to all the main rooms, and that the uncle heard everything. Showed him these vents once by Alanis herself. Albert was the first to notice a strange mute monk in black, who entered his uncle "as if his own".

### Laura's fiancé
Gallard wanted to marry Albert to Laura. According to the priest, the boy was too young to confirm the marriage, but could bring a marital oath. This marriage he demanded and in negotiations for an alliance with Hope, and after negotiations with Lord Finley Farway Laura became the bride of Albert. My uncle told the young people to communicate as much as possible. Albert prayed, walked and dined with Laura, made a list of questions to get to know the bride, and told her his dreams. Jeremy's been beating him up. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) Laura saw him as a "snotty, weak-willed, broken boy", but took care of him like a nanny.

### Sire Adler
Albert's consolation was a thoroughbred crow pony named Sir Adler, whom Alanis sent from the capital with General [[stetkhem|Statham]]'s people, apologizing for not congratulating her brother on the Day of Descent. Next to the pony, the boy revived and became more confident. He asked Laura with hope if Ellie would take him to the capital and if her uncle would kill her. The decree of Empress Minerva, which brought Statham, demanded that Gallard "save the young Albert Almer from being held in captivity under the guise of regency." Under Gallard's agreement with Erwin, the duchy was to depart to Alanis after the death of the prior, and if she was not alive, to Albert.

One day, Jeremy forced Albert to show him and Laura the auditory window at the parish office: he grabbed the boy’s hand and pushed his sister with it, threatening to blame him. Albert led them to the hiding place, but he ran away in fear. Through this window, Farway overheard Gallard talking to the owner of the Rings. Shortly thereafter, Gallard offered Laura a "more mature groom", and she married the prior.

## In "Humans and Gods"
In the summer of 1775, when the ecumenical council raised the question of Gallard's deposition, Erwin and Mother [[mat-kordeliya|Cordelia]] unanimously named the young Lord Albert as the only worthy ruler of Almera. The Crown and the Church were going to insist that the title of duke was given to him, and the regent was to be elected by the lords of the duchy. [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], through [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]], asked Erwin to support Laura's claims, Gallard's "widow", and offered to withdraw the dispute by impersonating Laura as Albert. Erwin replied that the true heiress was Alanis, followed by Albert.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
After Gallard's death, Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], urging Laura to stay with the army, predicted that in Almer she would be offered as the husband of the young Lord Albert. Laura called her inheritance "the whole Duchy of Almera." She was killed in October 1775.

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, which the author calls possible futures, Albert lives and is in [[aleridan|Aleridan]] under the rule of Henry Farway. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) In the summer of 1776, Farway betrothed him to his daughter Evelyn, a widow with four children in her forties, "a real general." Albert barely reached her shoulder and was afraid of her. In the cathedral, she held his hand, and when the fan dropped, the boy raised him only at her whisper. The engagement was scheduled for Monday at the ducal palace, so as not to tease the Almerans, who in the cathedral shouted to the bride “Old woman! Overripe!" [[menson|Manson]] wrote a joke about this couple: Aiden's ghost flies to his son, and "Lady Farway adopted a husband." [[karen|Karen]] Lighthart believed that it is obedience that gives a kind of chance: Alanis and Alfred are dead, and Albert is alive and will outlive his elderly wife, and this gives the Great House of Almer a "little chance of survival". Karen did not know that Alanis, by all indications, is alive and under the name of Mrs. Gale is teaching at an Almer school.

In "Ad-ri-an!" (Autumn 1776) Almera's carriage with towers and suns was stopped in Faunterra by a wattle of one-eyed [[khamfri|Humphrey]]. The Sedocs were called Albert and Evelyn Almera. The boy trembled and babbled: “You... You...", and Humphrey, a former lieutenant of his father's guard, "looked strangely" at him and put his hand on the hilt. Nemaya [[sofi|Sophie]] took the leader by the hand, and he let Albert go: “Go, kid.” Evelyn Humphrey said to stab in a carriage, pour oil and burn. His own men put out the fire and pulled her out alive. Henry Farway later reminded the House that his daughter was “directly attacked.”

## Relationships
- [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] is the beloved elder sister who sent him gifts; she is considered dead, but in Fantasies she is alive incognito.
- [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] is the uncle and guardian Albert feared.
- [[laura-farvej|Laura Farway]] was a bride in 1775 who replaced him with a caring mother; she later married Gallard.
- Evelyn Farway was a bride in 1776 (Fantasy).
