**Ufretin** is the captain of the St. Straus mercenary brigade, six hundred men who served in Shimmery. He constantly chews tobacco with dried flies, holds the fighting ostrich Fury and considers him a saint. It is not clear and calm in the books.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
Ufretin first appears in Iserin in May 1775. The fourth of the Five hired his brigade to "protect Iserin from enemies. From human enemies, not from the spirits of hellfire." He watches with the captain of the castle garrison Bellis as the leopard in the pit tries to kill Fury, and holds a bet with Bellis: "Everyone knows: Fury can't be defeated. He is holy and immortal.” The fly poison from his gum turns the ostrich in his eyes into a huge machine with sparks in the joints.

The brigade, according to the book, is “six hundred guys” obsessed with gambling and various fools; the inhabitants of Iserin complained to the lord about their rampage, but the glory of the mad kept enemies away from the city. When the suburb caught fire, Ufretin and the mercenaries stopped the fire, and he himself saw a monster in the fire. Then the knights of Baron Darryl entered the city (the fire set Magda), and "most of the brigade" fled or failed to gather. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]]) Ufretin managed to shout “To arms!”, but then decided that before him “knights of good”, and did not resist.

[[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]] asked him for six hundred men to serve. Ufretin asked Fury if she was for good; the ostrich clicked his beak without pecking her, and Ufretin said, "Fury says you are for good. So we agree.” Magda then told him to solve the problem with the castle: Bellis refused to surrender him, breaking the oath, and Ufretin stabbed Bellis, according to her view of the dagger, after which he went to "negotiate with the garrison". Magda later told Colonel Horey that the Ostriches' unpreparedness for battle suited her: she checked to see if any of the Burning Sun regiment officers had warned them.

## In "Humans and Gods"
At the [[bezdonnaya-propast|bottomless abyss]], Harmon sees Ufretin among the commanders of mercenary detachments gathered under Adrian, next to the Pope from the regiment of Pasynkov and Colonel Horey: "completely wild eyes, completely covered with red veins", eternal restlessness. At the military council of Adrian, he acquiesces to the lord: “Hurrah! Glory to Yangmei!", and about Origin shouts, "My Fury will poke out his eyes!" Upon learning of Adrian’s dethronement by the House, he says, “Here are the bastards, Your Majesty!” and “Death to the bastards!” He is called upon to raid the Surlion, seize the ships, and keep the city until the troops arrive.

He then goes to Surlion "with a bloody ostrich and a gang of bandits", and is given a company from the Burning Sun regiment to look after him. His letter to Magda with the seal in the form of an ostrich track reports that the detachment crossed the Miran Pass twenty miles and will be in the city the day after tomorrow. Officer Khorey wrote the opposite: the pass is blurred by a storm and impassable. Horaeus believed that his officer was lying, and advised Magda to bring the report of the Straus to Adrian: then the lord will go through the pass to Surlion, where Ufretin and the Pope are waiting for him. What ended this conversation, the scene does not say; later in the camp they learn that the pass on the way to Surlion "was not either collapsed or flooded", and Adrian and Magda decide to go to White Stone Bay, and Adrian still expects that "it will come the regiments of Ufretin and the Pope."
