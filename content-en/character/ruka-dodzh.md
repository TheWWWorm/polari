## Sergeant Southern Way
Dodge's hand served as a combat sergeant in the South Road Infantry, which the Duke of [[moris-labelin|Labelin]] had shielded against the Northerners in the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]. ([[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]) He called himself “a veteran of the Wise River, Labelin and Pixie.” On November 2, 1774, he caught [[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]] in a tavern in [[piksi|Pixie]] Square and took her to the militia, promising to "give the Northers a good kick under their frozen asses."

After the surrender of the militia under [[labelin|Labelin]], Dodge defected to the side of Origin and mustered the former militiamen who became Origin's warriors. ([[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]) His soldiers were not afraid of him: he shouted and scolded loudly, calling them “hornless pigs”, but, according to Joaquin, he was “not evil” and scolded for the sight. At the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]], he disappeared somewhere.

## Podsnezhnikov
In the winter of 1774/75, Dodge was among the peasants of Salem of Summerswith. ([[salem|Salem of Summersweet]]) When the baron's sons attacked the column, it was he who guessed to turn the carts across the road, and the knights got stuck in them. After the fight, he went to Salem: “You have a shortcoming in terms of tactics, commander.” Dodge volunteered to teach people in the ranks and, along with other veterans, organized exercises every morning and evening. Under Lowville, he recognized his Deserter in the Trehpal ("the most noble deserter in the whole war!") and persuaded Joaquin to help him train new recruits: "In battle, you make sense - what's with the goat."

Salem made Dodge one of his three advisors: the sergeant knew the military men, the Tramp citizens, and Joaquin nobles. Dodge praised Tooth's dentist as a good organizer, though uninformed in military affairs, and constantly asked Salem to raise him in rank, "even to the captain." Salem replied, “We are not an army.” Then Dodge and Tooth began to glorify each other. After the battle at the outpost under the Bend, Dodge nicknamed the wounded in the hand of the Tooth "general", and the Tooth - "major". They were subject to two selected hundreds of "young men", the only ones in the army who wore shields and helmets.

When Labelin's agent [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] brought the spark shots, Zub and Dodge immediately locked the box with the weapon in Zub's tent and put a guard of thugs to it. In the Hamptons, the officers honored "the people's General Zub and the people's Major Dodge", and they called themselves "the right and left hands of Chief Salem". Two-thirds of the forty-thousand-strong army now consisted of townspeople under Tooth and Dodge.

Under Hampton, General Joseph Gore demanded to disperse. When he turned the horse around, Zub and Dodge opened fire with self-fire and laid down his guardsmen and a young bannerman. Salem ordered the shooters to surrender, but Dodge announced: “It seems that Chief Salem is a little out of his mind,” and the guys arrested the leader himself (see the Snowdrop Uprising). ([[vosstanie-podsnezhnikov]])

## The Holy Field and Mercy
At the Holy Field, [[minerva|Minerva]] asked who had shot himself. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) From the crowd shouted: "Sergeant!" - and the azure guards grabbed Dodge and two philistines, and then the Tooth. Dodge is trapped in the Table Citadel. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) According to him, during the interrogations, “all the ribs were counted” and coals were thrown on his stomach. Prisoners fed male prisoners every two days.

On June 1, 1775, Minerva ordered Zub and Dodge to be brought to her. Dodge swore that he was a “little man”, and blamed Salem and Joaquin, and from the ranks recorded by interrogators, he renounced. Then Minerva instructed them to persuade five to seven thousand Snowdrops, who became robbers in the Southern Way, to switch to imperial service. For this, they were promised clemency and gloria for each fighter. Dodge was illiterate, and the paper was read aloud to him by Tooth. Bye, Minerva threatened to bury them alive if they were deceived.

## In the service of Adrian
Zub and Dodge gathered the former Snowdrops into a mercenary army, but they did not serve Minerva: Maurice Labelin overbought them and put them under the banner of [[adrian|Adrian]]. Dodge "called himself a major" and became the head of a whole army of robbers. In the summer of 1775, his men entered the Labelin, and some of them habitually shouted not “Adrian!” but “Hand Dodge!”.

In August 1775, when Adrian occupied [[faunterra|Faunterra]] without a fight, the "young men of Major Dodge" dispersed in a festive crowd. Dodge reported to the lord about the cries against him, about the taverns where Minerva was praised, and about the churches where the priests urged to endure. The list was kept by the Tooth, because Dodge himself is “wrong” — he is not literate. He later brought Adrian a list of houses, taverns, and churches. He argued with Major Backfield, who offered to arrest everyone: Dodge had more young men than people in patronage. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) He himself preferred “the loudest” to catch and prick. In one fight on Tkatskaya Street, a house burned down, and it seems that someone died. Adrian found this excess useful and ordered to draw lights on the doors of the evil rebels at night. In early September, when asked about Minerva, Dodge replied that he and Zub “do not like her”: “weak”, because the correct master of them would “hide and there is no wet place left”. Magda Labelin called him a "gadenish", and Adrian agreed: "But a gadenish useful." Maurice Labelin later complained that "Dodge's jerks beat everyone, including cats and rats."

## In "Fantasies"
In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], where the author describes the possible fates of the heroes, in the spring of 1776, Hand Dodge remains with [[adrian|Adrian]], who became the mayor of Faunterra. He is “the leader of the pack of former bandits” and, like Colonel Backfield, the head of the secret guard, is loyal to the “lord” in a doglike way. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Dodge reported that two-thirds of the common people in the Crownlands were for Adrian, and a third for Minerva, and that with his disgruntled people "work as hard as they can." Adrian told him to loosen his grip: "Enough whipped."

Along with Backfield, Dodge was ordered to oversee Professor [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]]'s lab at the university's physics department. When the professor's blueprints for the recording device went missing, Backfield and Dodge, posing as "Major Hand Dodge", broke into the lab and flipped it. The professor threatened to throw the device out the window. Dodge rushed to get the bag, but Eugene's doorman set him up. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) The students beat him up, and Dodge himself, who shouted "Animals are hornless!", shook hands and legs and threw him out the window.

### "Ad-ri-an!"
In the fall of 1776, "Major Ruka Dodge, commander of the special city guard", met "Adrian trains" with visiting supporters of the burgomaster at Faunterra station. He divided them into groups and explained the rules. You can shout “Ad-ri-an” everywhere, supporters of Minerva and the “wolf” place in the slums, you should beat with your fists, and use iron only “as a last resort”. After the victory, Adrian will “generously give a coin.” The fortune teller Murriel, looking at his palm, described his past: men's infantry, desertion, looting, sharpness, pardon, and the one who pardoned, he "desires for weakness". Dodge oversaw the one-eyed [[khamfri|Humphrey]]'s vagabond, told her to scare the lords, "pat" Minerva and the Almerians, and promised: "In a week Adrian will become master." On the day of the opening of the House, he forbade touching the Lords until they "hurt Adrian."

According to the “arrest” plan, several thousand of Dodge’s strongest men formed the third siege ring of the Chamber. Adrian ordered them to keep the lords locked up for two days, to bring to him the unarmed, ready to swear, and to kill the disobedient. Dodge replied, “Death to the goats!” Before the assault, he snapped at the Golden Gryva: "You are a hoofed pig" - and raised the crowd calling "Ad-ri-an!". After the death of Adrian, his guys until the morning robbed the neighborhoods. When the kairs entered the city, some of them were grouped around Dodge, and the rest fled the capital, realizing that the new government would not forgive them.

## Personality
Dodge is a throaty braggart, dreaming of rank, illiterate and pleasing to the strong. His favorite curses are “hornless pigs,” “hornless goats,” and “vegetable goats.” Before Minerva he repented and swore by the Forefathers, before Adrian he clicked his heels and shouted "Glory to Yangmei!". Ganta Biray considered him a jackal and a coward, running from one master to another, and Magda dreamed that he would end his life the same way as Moger Buckley. ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]])
