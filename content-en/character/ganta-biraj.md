## Who He Is
Birai is a ganta, the leader of a small Shavan ghana from [[rejs|Reis]]. Neymir and [[chara|Chara]] considered him cruel, boastful and short-sighted. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) The Companions themselves were among the best horsemen of the horde, "where there is the gante of Birayu." Neymir noticed that Birai is not a special scoundrel: half of the familiar Shavans could escape from the battle "when they smelled fried". Birai respects strength, loves money and prey, and spits out swear words ("hairless tail", "hairless tails"). He justifies his behavior by Stepi’s law: Shavan is a free man and can leave whenever he wants, and “what you cannot protect is not yours.”

## "A Doll on the Throne"
### Litland Raid
In the winter of 1774–1775, after the defeat of the [[moran|Moran]] horde at [[melorandzh|Melorange]], thirteen riders from forty remained from the ghana of Birai. For a forage raid on [[litlend|Litland]], Ganta Court gave him Satellites. ([[ganta-kort|Ganta Kort]]) Five villages have only given empty cellars. The Shavans of Biray burned them and killed the remaining old men, as the chief had ordered. Neymir tried to argue, but Biray cut him off: “I will decide what to do, and you stay quiet!” At the next farm, Birai put the Satellites watchmen in the forest on the hill, and the ambush was waiting in the village itself. Ganta ran away, Neymir whistled to show him the way to the forest, so that he would go to the knights in the rear. Chara and Neymir held a detachment of Lithuanians waiting for Birai to strike, but the Ganta did not return and deserted with the remnants of the Ghan. Court said of him, "Take a donkey's tail."

### The Magician
The companions again met Biray in a tavern in Nadezhda: he had six deserters with him. ([[nadezhda|Hope]]) Ganta opened his hands: “We just ran away. I thought you would follow.” He said that he was deaf in his ear. Chara's arrow cut off his earlobe, and Birai told his men to lay down their weapons. The Shavans served the sorcerer, whom Biray called the "master" and was mortally afraid. ([[mik|Mik]]) The sorcerer hired the Companions and promised to kill all nine if one of them killed the other. At the camp, Biray and Naymir argued about the battle in Litland and about the share of prey, boasted of past exploits: Biray assured that he had raided deep into [[darkvoter|Darkwater]] and removed the lovebird pollen from the swamp witch.

A detachment robbed the banks of the Earl of [[vittor-shejland|Shayland]] in free cities. The people of Birai cut down employees and casual customers, and the sorcerer “drinked” the souls of the dead. Biray did not want such a fate to befall his wounded horseman Haggot. He himself took Haggott to the doctor, and sent the others to confuse the tracks. Chara decided that the wounded man was killed, but found Biraya in the doctor's house: Haggot was sewn, and the gaunt was kept hostage by the doctor's daughter Gladys and was going to take her away as a trophy. Chara asked for the girl, and Biray refused her on purpose. A stranger came in, a future newcomer. He laid down two shavanks, and Chara put a dagger to the throat of the gauntlet. The stranger asked for a squad and bought the girl at the expense of his first share. Birai, “thinking and pretending,” agreed.

### Novichok
The newcomer liked Birai with impudence and a thirst for profit. Ganta saw him as perhaps his future best rider. He asked Neymir what the Newcomer was, and Neymir decided that he was a "rotten lord" who had been banished from the knighthood. In Laxette, the squad was ambushed in a bank, and the gang was profaned all the time. Then the Sorcerer attacked the Newcomer in fury, and he took the Item from him and declared himself a witch hunter. Biray laughed at first, until he realized that Rookie wasn't kidding. Following the Companions, all his riders stood next to the Rookie. “I haven’t made a decision yet!” Birai shouted, but obeyed. In the cemetery near Laxett, the sorcerer gave out from the grave where to look for the Second of the Five. The hunter said, “Ganta Biray, take the unit away.”

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
In May 1775, the Hunter's detachment captured [[kharmon|Harmon]]'s celestial ship. The hunter presented the captives with a “ghant of Birai and his people.” Biray recalled the Law of the Steppe crying to [[niza|Niza]] and, pulling the gag out of Harmon's mouth, asked how much the empress would pay for a "round flyer". Harmon noticed that Birai without jealousy gives way to the Hunter all the decisions and as if glad that he retained the title and got rid of responsibility. At the same time, the hunter looked at the gauntlet, fearing a blow to the back.

When the Hunter captured the Abode-of-the-Abyss and captured the Second of the Five, he introduced his allies: "Ganta Birai with the Shavans of Reis and the wise Zandur with the warriors of the goat people." ([[kuindar|Quindar]]) Biray and Zandur were dragged to Sheriff [[khalintor|Halintor]]'s side, served a dagger to the Huntsman for questioning, and then managed the division of church booty. Harmon, [[gortenzij|Hortensius]], and Chara each had two thousand hilt on the order of the Hunter, and the bill was issued by Birai. He put almost three thousand into Harmon: none of the Shavans wanted to communicate with the “banker’s papers”. During the negotiations from the wall, Biray kept throwing knives ready and pulled the Second by the rope tied to his leg.

## In "Humans and Gods"
Adrian, who had opened as lord, appointed Biray as commander of his new guard and ordered to recruit three dozen reliable fighters. Ganta asked for a crazy salary and got it. The first guardsmen were Haggott, Cosmatius, Gurlah and Girdan. The lord especially ordered to find Chara, but the people of Birai “did not really try.” On May 29, 1775, Birai led Niza to beat Harmon at Abode-u-Bezna. Ganta gladly chose the guards among the three mercenary brigades and glorified Vladyka, although six months ago he smashed Steppe.

At the military councils, Adrian Biray sat next to Lady [[magda-labelin|Magda]], Baron Darryl and hired colonels. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]]) He promised to send messengers to the disgruntled Moran Gants, primarily to Court. During the journey, he led the guard of the Lord. When Harmon launched contradictory rumors about Adrian’s secret plans into the camp, Birai was the first to fall into Magda’s trap: “The capital must move to the Steppe!” Adrian asked him if he was not an idiot and a suicide to make Moran's lair the capital.

Chara, listing Alanis five men who betrayed her, called Biraya the first: "a vile jackal." She regretted not killing him when she met him.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
Birai with Shavans from the guard of Adrian reasoned under Harmon about the marriage of the lord on Magda: a wife "for politics", and for the pleasure of Adrian will lead an althess.

As head of the personal guard, Biray was under Adrian and performed his whims. He reported [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]. On the orders of the Lord, his people plunged into the pond of the first secretary [[dorian-ember|Ember]], who brought orders from Minerva. Adrian himself instructed [[menson|Manson]] to strip naked for wearing a uniform. During the interrogation of the prisoner [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]], Birai beat him and stood with his sword ready while he spoke through the Voice of God. During the experience with [[chrevo|the Womb]], Biray kept away from the crystal coffin as far as possible and threw the rat inside with disgust. Nisa admitted: “People like Birai, I don’t like it.”

On December 2, 1775, Adrian and the court watched the Battle of the First Winter through [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird Without Flesh]]. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Birai bet ten gold on Shayland. No one wanted to bet on the Origins until Manson put a hundred ephesas borrowed from the prophet. “Glory to the Wanderers, there was one fool,” rejoiced the gauntlet. Seeing Charu in battle, he shouted to her, “Stooi, you’ll break!” Shavanov he called "our" and asked to show them. Adrian hoped with his help to return the horde to the bosom of the empire. When Shayland lost, Birai hit the wall with his fist in frustration. He then dragged Manson away from his wife, knocked him down, and found a potion bottle in him. Adrian instructed the Ganta to make sure the jester was taking the medicine in the morning and evening. Later, Birai brought the Womb by order of the Lord, although he did not want to see it, and brought the Voice of God.
