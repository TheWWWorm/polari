Haggott is a Shavan from the detachment of the Ganty of Biray. ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]) In the spring of 1775, he robbed banks with the Sorcerer and was seriously wounded. ([[mik|Mik]]) The sorcerer was going to give his place and share to the newcomer, and Haggott himself was told to kill. He was saved by the Newcomer, that is, the disguised [[adrian|Adrian]]. Since then, Haggott has faithfully served the Hunter, and when he opened, he became one of the first guards of the Lord.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
### Rana
In March 1775, Birai's detachment, along with the [[chara|Chara]] Satellites and Neymir, robbed the banks of the Earl of Shayland in the free cities of Almera. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) In the fourth bank, one guard managed to snatch the knife, and Haggott received a blow to the side: "blood lilac between the fingers." It was easier to kill the wounded, but then the sorcerer “will raise his eyelids and drink his soul.” This fate to his rider Biray did not want. Haggott was tied up and put on a horse to Girdan, and he grumbled, "Because of you, both will perish." The Shavans were angry at Haggott for still being alive, and he pleaded, "Brothers, please... For the sake of the Spirits of the Steppe... don't give up..."

Chara reminded him, "You were one of those who left us to be eaten by the Litlanders." But the pity in her overreacted, and she said that the wounded should be taken to the doctor. Birai and Girdan took Haggott to the nearest village, the rest jumped further to confuse the tracks. The sorcerer assured Chara that Birai had separated to finish off the wounded not in front of her. But in the morning Haggott was alive: the doctor stitched up the wound, and Birai kept his daughter Gladys with him all night as a hostage. When the gang decided to take the girl with them, and Chara asked her to let her go, Haggott interceded: “Ganta, last night she felt sorry for me...” Biri cut it off: “Not her, donkey, but me! You don’t owe her anything!” A stranger came into the house and asked the doctor for help. The sorcerer took him to the detachment and promised: “If you show yourself well, you will get a share of the wounded Haggott.” He replied, “Haggot cannot conceal his grudge when he is dead. You will kill him and take his share.”

### The Night Before Laxette
The novice waited for a check in the raid on the bank in Laxette, and Haggott, still not strong after the wound, understood that after the raid he was finished: "either on the gallows or by the sword of the novice." When the sorcerer announced that the bank would be taken no matter what, only Haggott quietly asked: “Sorcerer, please, do not...” At night, he twice walked past Rookie's bed to trample on the snow. Then he took off to avoid the spurs, crept up to the sleeper, whispered "Tier ton of dust" and struck with a knife. The novice grabbed his hand, kicked the Shavan in the seam on his side and took the knife. Haggott did not pray for mercy: “See, fate will kill me because of this wound. This is how the Spirit of the Steppe decided..." The novice asked what his words meant, and Haggott explained, "It could not have been otherwise." “A lie,” said the newcomer. - It's different." He returned the dagger.

### The Fall of the Magician
The Laxett raid turned into a trap, and the Sorcerer was shot in the thigh. There was no healer in the squad, and Haggott volunteered to do the dressing. The sorcerer took off his pants, and the novice, who was only waiting for him, knocked him down and took away the belt, in which was all the power of the “witch”. The Newcomer assigned Haggott to bind the prisoner, and he first hesitated. When Novichok called the Shavans to "hunt", Haggott was the first after Chara and Ney sided with him. At the cemetery near Laxett, he gave the Hunter a bamboo tube, which was dropped by a sorcerer buried in a well so that he could breathe. The Hunter himself explained why no one stood up for him: “The case of Haggott, thrown by you to tear, showed the extremely low value of the life of a subject in your eyes.”

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
In May 1775, [[kharmon|Harmon]], being in the Hunter's detachment, noticed that two Shavans, Gurlach and Haggott, were "literally ready for anything for the sake of the Hunter", as if he had saved their lives. ([[gurlakh|Gurlakh]]) During the storming of the Abode-u-Bezdna, Birai instructed Haggot to count the monastery coins: "Haggot, you will answer with your head!" When the Gold Swords pinned down the Hunter and Chara, Haggott rushed to their aid, and then saw the heavy cavalry coming from the north window first. With this cavalry came Baron Darryl and Lady [[magda-labelin|Magda Labelin]]. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]]) Presenting himself to Magde, Biray named only Haggot and Charu.

## In "Humans and Gods"
When the Hunter opened as the resurrected Lord Adrian, Biray became the commander of his new guard and the first three guards appointed Haggott, Cosmatogo, and Gurlah. Together with his brother Vincent Haggott accompanied Adrian to the beaten Harmon and laughed with the lord over the merchant. In June 1775, Harmon invented a "great plan" for the Shavans to move the capital to the Steppe. Haggott asked what the Second of the Five had to do with it, and the merchant had to compose further. ([[kuindar|Quindar]])

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
In July 1775, on Adrian's ship, Haggott told a stunned Harmon that the lord was marrying Lady Magda: "Do you think the lord is as evil as a wolf?" To the merchant’s question about the homeland, he answered quite clearly, unlike the others: “We were born on Brock in the possession of the Gant Johan Reis, at the very City of the Flame Bull. Except he came from far away.” About [[niza|Niza]], he said that he did not know exactly, “but so, according to the conversation, like from the Upper Flight, somewhere from the border.” Then the Shavans began throwing knives into the wall, and Haggott was the first to show a squat.

In September, in Faunterra, Haggott brought a box of Objects to Adrian's meeting with Lady-in-the-Dark and prisoner [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]]. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]])

## Trivia
- Haggott's "Tyria Tone of Tyrya" translated as "It couldn't have been otherwise." So Shavan justified the murder, to which he was forced by circumstances. The novice did not agree with this, and mercy was the beginning of his power over the detachment.
- Haggott comes from Brock, from the estates of Johan Reis, that is, he is a countryman of the future prisoner Adrian.
