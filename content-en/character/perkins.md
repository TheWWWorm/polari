**Perkins** is the manager of the central branch of Shayland Bank, the man of Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. Externally, he was a clerk with "brushed elbows", but in the war of 1775 he became the commander of a hired pack and one of the bearers of the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]].

## In "Humans and Gods"
Perkins first appears at [[uejmar|Waymar]] Castle on the third morning after [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]'s rebellion. He comes with a squad of fifteen warriors. The count calls him into the office with Joaquin and [[ajvi|Ivy]] as guards. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) The conversation is in a conditional language ("the mare died", "the cart overturned"), and Perkins constantly asks Joaquin what he understood. He said, “There is no mystery. Governor of the central bank.” The Count orders him to hire fighters, sparing no money, and sends a knight Bert with him. The unit remains in the castle under the command of the knight Reynolds, and Perkins goes to the bank. Ivy confirms to Joaquin that Perkins commands a central detachment, "a whole fortress", and he is an "important bird".

Soon he, along with Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]], restores order in Waymar: the baron's warriors and the "thugs hired by Perkins" comb the streets, disperse meetings, and stop chatter. When [[rikhard-oridzhin|Ice]] appears in the count's office, Perkins stands among the nearest vassals with Perst on his forearm. Ice notes that in the event of which he "shoots Martin", and Perkins offers the count: "you want, I'll shoot? If you don’t kill him with a feather, you won’t kill him with anything!” When Ice asks the Earl for an arrow, he replies, "Martin has no experience, Perkins remains." Ice walks with him and Joaquin around the city, choosing places to ambush. Perkins leads them through the Port Bazaar, and strange couples on the streets turn out to be his agents. When he hears of them, he says, “They are idiots spying on us. I don’t need any crutches at work.” As the northerners approach, his mercenaries, along with the sheriff's constables, train the townspeople to act in case of an alarm.

Intelligence was Perkins' business. After the fall of the city, he guiltyly reports: “they were overcrowded”, knows only what can be seen from the wall. The Count instructs him to support the castellan, Sir Randol, by "the fire of Perst and good counsel".

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
During the siege of Castle Perkins is on duty on a stone bastion at the very bottom of the descent with two dozen soldiers. His men carry the watch seriously, and Perst on his hand is "watered with milk". The Count still doubts the mercenaries: most of them are "bandits and mercenaries, people without honor." When a poisoned well is discovered in the castle, Perkins' men stay outside with the sunsets and are not yet aware of the trouble. Ice offers to send with the sunsets two peppermints, "Perkins and you, Martin."

After the siege, Perkins and the Earl discuss his mercenary pack: during the siege, he recruited retirees, mercenaries, and bandits, and they respected only the one who paid and wore the Ring. He has to go back to running the bank, but people refuse to obey another. He gives the amount needed for the maintenance of the army. The Earl instructs him to conduct an unscheduled double-tax collection throughout the county, freeing only Dorcaster's land. Perkins replies, "Yes, my lord!" and complains only that he doesn't have many people. Joaquin then calls him a "crochowder."

On the night of the Northwood and Kier attack on the Camp of the Chosen, Perkins and his men hold the northern section with Gold. He's the first one to approach Joaquin to report that the prisoner is acting strangely. When the forest goes to the camp, Joaquin tells him to build people. Then Perkins fires the Perst, and he and Joaquin mow the attackers. Perkins' men are cut down, he stops answering the call, and the kair declares, "He's dead." After the fight, it sounds in conversation that Perkins was told to flee at the first threat; Martin admits that Joaquin "scared" him and ordered him to keep the defense. Perkins does not appear in the text.

## Personality and Appearance
Low, in round glasses, with a honed mustache, in a vest and a coat. Joaquin sees him as a "cabinet rat", and with his clever eyes and invisibility he reminds him of Harry's barber. Speaks short. They talk to the count in conditional phrases, and Perkins claps himself on the sleeve in the sign of "handling". In Shadow of the Great Tree, the narrator calls him "faithful to Shayland like a dog."

## Trivia
- Perkins's saying "the mare is dead, the cart is broken, it's time to get over it" Joaquin recalls as an expression of general doom before Ice's arrival; at a meeting after Waymar's fall, Perkins repeats, "it's time to get over it."
