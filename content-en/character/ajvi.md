Sir Ivey is a knight of Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] at [[uejmar|Waymar]] Castle. In "[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]" and "[[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]", he is almost always with Joaquin: the two of them guard the count, and then guard the captive [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) Ivy isn't Joaquin's friend, but he's being nice to him. He is cautious and reluctant to talk about things.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
At a feast in Waymar, Joaquin, wishing to catch the eye of Lady Iona, kicked the new sword of the count's knight and heaped it on him. It ended in a fight. In the book, the knight is not named. In Humans and Gods, Joaquin recalls: "Joaquin fought Ivy — and so remembered Iona."

## In "Humans and Gods"
### The Night of Rebellion
On the night of May 28, 1775, after a battle in the castle, [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] ordered Ivy: "Check all the northerners. ([[perevorot-v-uejmare|Coup in Waymar]]) If anyone is alive, finish it.” The knight returned to clarify the order: “The Countess is still alive. To kill him or not?” Martin pulled a knife from Iona's chest. Ivy warned that she would bleed to death and then grabbed his hand: "My lord, this is the Earl's wife. Let him decide how to be.” Together with Joaquin, he took Iona to Vittor. Then the two of them testified to [[master-sajrus|Master Cyrus]] the identity of the murdered barber Harry Hog. ([[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]]) When the count doubted Joaquin, Ivy vouched for him: "Joaquin had the opportunity to escape, but did not take advantage. I believe him, my lord.”

### Bodyguard
In the following days, the earl took Joaquin and Ivy as guards to negotiate: with the banker [[perkins|Perkins]] and then with Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]]. The Baron's men suddenly threw themselves at Vittor. Ivy got a fist in his eye and rolled on the floor, clutching the enemy. Turns out the attack was rigged to test Joaquin. When asked if this was the case, Ivy replied, “Well, it happens from time to time. More often than I would like.” He admitted that he knew about Wilhelm's Feathers, as well as the knights closest to the Count. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) He himself does not wear Perst: “I will not say about the other knights, this is their business. But personally, I don’t want any Rings...I think...without them, it’s better.” Later, Joaquin lacked a friend to talk to about Perste, "and Ivy is locked up for some reason, just to start talking about the Subject."

When Vittor told the disobedient Iona to "teach obedience," Ivy left it to Joaquin: "Don't be offended, mate, but it's your business. You and Lord Martin are watching her."

### Return of Ice
In June, Ivy was the first to notice and recognize a wet man without a horse and a gun walking to the gate: “I know him... Guys, open the gate!” It was Ice by [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]]. According to the rule of the Count of the alien had to identify three faithful soldiers, so they called two more knights. Ivy greeted the guest joyfully: “How we waited for you! Without you, there is sadness.”

Before the night raid into the city, Ivy helped Joaquin to equip himself, and then led the detachment by a dark park past the gymnasium to the port authority: he "knew the terrain perfectly." At the port authority, Ivy slapped Joaquin on the shoulder, "Good luck, brother," and hid in a square with the cover soldiers.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
In the summer of 1775, Waymar was besieged by [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]]. Ivy with Joaquin and Martin every day took Iona on a cart to the Craft Square and showed the northerners. Martin and the captive were riding in a cart, while Ivy and Joaquin were riding. He noticed that "the sunsets ate the dead." When the skirmish in the square almost ended in shooting, Ivy kept Iona in the grip, and Joaquin threatened to shoot her. From Joaquin, General Horis heard that Ivy had "once" sterilised [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]].

In August, after a commotion over Iona's attempt to escape, Ivy reminded Joaquin of an order to search [[alanis-almera|Alanis]]' room and retrieve the unusual Items. He later collided on the stairs with Martin, whom the count was looking for. When the disfigured Joaquin lay down after the fight, Ivy visited him: “Hold on, brother.” He said that the spy Nathaniel was eliminated, and the “wolf” was put in a cage again.
