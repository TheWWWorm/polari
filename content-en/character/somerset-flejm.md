## Origins

Somerset is the son of Count [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]], the ruler of Leongard, and the daughter of Count Flame. Edgar was born the youngest son of Duke Jonas Lighthart. After the Shutov plot, his father-in-law gave him the upper half of the River Flame so that his son-in-law could bear his name and pass it on to his children, and Vladyka Telurian quickly forgot about him. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) Somerset and his younger sister [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]] studied at the capital boarding house of Lady Ingrid and were accepted in the light and even at court, although their relationship with the Lightharts was sometimes whispered. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) He introduced himself to Ione by his full name: "Somerset Patricia Cleon of the family of Helen, born in the Heart of Light, but came from Faunterra." He considers himself a Lighthart. Earl Arrowback wanted to see him as the son-in-law of "Count Lighthart-Flame".

Somerset attended the University of [[faunterra|Faunterra]] and lived on a student campus. He did not approve of his sister's affair with [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]. According to Nexia, the brother "hates" Erwin and despises him the more he flies up: Erwin could take her to the top and did not take her. Once Somerset with a sword in his hands ran to the capital mansion of the Origins to protect the honor of his sister. Nexia begged him not to challenge Erwin to a duel.

## In "Only One Star"

On November 20, 1774, when the army of the northerners was standing in Labelin, a cold young man wrapped in a blanket was brought to [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]. It was Somerset. He traveled about three hundred miles in bad weather, crossed the front line, and avoided trains, which were especially monitored by [[protektsiya|the protection]]. He brought Nexia's letter. She wrote that the capital was empty, and warned that Adrian was preparing a sudden blow.

Viscount was cold. He stated that the content of the letter is unknown to him, and refused to answer questions about the troops of the Silver Fox: he did not intend to spy for the success of the rebellion. Somerset told Erwin that he was playing with his sister's feelings and had "not a drop of love" for her. He admitted that he wanted to call him in the capital, but he obeyed Nexia: her heart belongs to her. Finally, he warned that the sister loves Erwin and can pay for it: the protector is watching her.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

In May 1775, Somerset and her sister came to [[uejmar|Waymar]] at the personal invitation of [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. Late in the evening, their cab driver got stuck at the castle itself, and Somerset argued with him as [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] passed by. She offered to help. Viscount gallantly bowed, named himself, and dropped his hat upon hearing her name.

At the morning meeting, he thanked Vittor for the opportunity to mediate in an important diplomatic mission and received a spark sword from the count as a gift. While Iona was walking around the city with Nexia, Somerset had long negotiations with Vittor about the position of [[nadezhda|Hope]] and Shayland on the "sacred guard". When Nexia, under the influence of Ulyanina Dust, went naked for breakfast, Somerset tore off his camisole and put it on her shoulders. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Before leaving, he was looking for a ship: Farway was waiting for them in the capital.

## In "Humans and Gods"

From Waymar, the brother and sister sailed to [[fliss|Fliss]], and from there they reached [[aleridan|Aleridan]] by stagecoach to take the train to Faunterra. The trains were not going, and they had already bought detour tickets through Nightrock and Marengo, but learned that Erwin was leading the army to Aleridan. Nexia rushed, then rushed to meet Erwin, then wanted to swim back. Somerset accused her of "female impermanence" and decided that her sister was not herself. He wanted to take her home to Leongard and show her the doctor and how her older brother did not let her go alone. Nexia ran away from him through the window.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

In September 1775, Somerset accompanied his father to a meeting with [[karen|Karen]] at Orwell. The conversation between brother and sister did not stick, and Somerset saved the conversation: at the request of his father, he began to talk about himself. He complained about nerdy mentors and a dean "obsessed with experiences," praised life on campus, and then laid out his "women's morals table" on the Foremothers and the lands where the best types of girls lie diagonally, in the "golden section." Then he took his sister: girls can not draw, it is the law of nature. Edgar convinced his son that Waymar Nexia was ruled by Dust, but Somerset did not believe in Dust and explained everything to his sister's "Erwinian madness". He called Farway's map stolen. He called his aunt "Aunt Karen", and she laughed at his stories to tears.

## In "Fantasies"

Somerset is one of the heroes of "Fantasy the Third: Annoying Little Things" (Aleridan, summer 1776). Karen leads the story, and Somerset tells her and [[menson|Manson]] her story.

### Rose Arrowback
For a long time Somerset was a romantic. He believed in love at first sight, waited for the One, condemned the reveler-colleagues and allowed himself only an innocent kiss. He was baffled by the story of Nexia: the sister rushed into a relationship with a man who did not even think of marrying her, and everyone around him admired it. After everyone in Waymar sympathized with her even when she went naked, Somerset decided that true love was not in high esteem, and went "just to seduce someone." Having received three times in the face, he realized that the fall must also be learned.

When war came to Almera, Edgar sent his daughter to one side, and his son to Faunterra: “it’s safe there.” In a crowded train at Back Station to Somerset, Rose, daughter of Earl of [[erroubek|Arrowback]], is saddled with a purebred agate and one of the three heirs of the proceeds of the spark dam and river. In Faunterra, both volunteered and worked full-time at train stations, construction sites, and infirmaries. At the end of the summer, Rose confessed her love for him. Somerset did not like her, but decided: “to become a scoundrel, you need to go to the end” — and made an offer. The Earl of Arrowback agreed, but Somerset's seigneur [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] responded with a "decisive no."

Somerset felt that Farway was afraid of the Lightharts' rise: if Nexia married Origin and he was the original heiress, they could overturn Farway. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) He wanted to run with Rose to the Volta, but cooled down and wrote to his father. Edgar replied that Nexia was unlikely to achieve the goal, because she "tried to kill Origin", and "Hartley says that Nexia has no chance". He asked his son to wait a month or two and write to Farway again. Farway himself later explained to Karen that Earl Arrowback was the least willing of all the Almeran lords to submit to his authority, and marriage was forbidden "strictly".

### Uh.
While Somerset waited for an answer, he was bored going to open lectures on physics by Professor [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]]. Ollie skipped every second lecture, and instead of him, the classes were led by an assistant who called herself simply "E." It was [[elis-kavendish|Alice Cavendish]]. The first time she turned his outraged cry of "It's a hoax!" into an experience about sound waves, Somerset decided he had met his true love. He placed bouquets under her door, wrote anonymous confessions and guarded her in bakeries, and the gatekeeper of the faculty advised him to cool down: "It's not about you bird." One day, Rose was waiting for him at the entrance to the faculty: her father called her home, and she kept hoping. Seeing E., Somerset threw: “I don’t love you and I never loved you. “Goodbye,” he ran after her.

Then E. was all the time occupied by the professor and visitors - lords, bishops, officers of protection. After the search at the faculty, Somerset confessed his love to her, but this was taken as a joke. Once E. cried at a lecture, and he wrote her a whole page of consolation. She said, “Sir, don’t be under any illusions. I love the other and am devoted to him” — and ran away to Ollie, barely asking if “A.K.” was here.

### Aleridan
In the summer of 1776, Edgar summoned his son to Aleridan. Nexia has "spoiled everything", the family is ruined, and marriage to the heiress of Arrowback "can save us." Edgar put him in a secure hotel with Karen and Manson and told him to sit in the room. Somerset went to Manson for advice on how to get Rose back: after all, he once seduced “the most beautiful and noble girl of Polaris.” The joker took money from him for "deception lessons" and called him a "piece of dung", since he left the bride for a student. The lessons continued tomorrow. Manson showed courtship to his wife, and Karen, in a theatrical mask, evaluated her nephew's attempts: "You were too solicitous... Now it's too hammy..." She was later replaced by a maid. What ended the story with Rose, the story does not say.

## Personality

Skinny, big-eyed, thin-boneed elynovets, mannered and refined: from him, "so did the capital", and the disease even went to him. He's proud, he's hurtful, he's stubborn. Somerset dared: for the sake of his sister, he crossed the front to a man whom he considered a criminal. He makes fun of his sister, but takes care of her. He judges other people's manners strictly, but he himself is easily carried away, and Manson calls him "an idiot" and "a cretin" without fail. Somerset readily admits, "I'm a complete moron."
