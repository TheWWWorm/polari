## Origin of nickname
Henry Horton is the son of Colonel Harvey Horton, one of the officers [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] appointed to command the battalions of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. The Hortons belong to the ancient nobles of the land of Origin: according to Erwin, Lilliday, [[blekberri|Blackberry]], Statham, and Horton, they trace their ancestry "from the depths of the ages". The family has their own castle, Horton.

In the autumn of 1774, in the campaign of [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] to the south (Northern Flash), a heavy cavalry tournament was held under [[dojl|Doyle]]. ([[myatezh-oridzhina]]) It was fought by a hundred horsemen: fifty x [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon Origin]] and fifty kire other units. Captain Henry Horton became the champion: he stood against eleven opponents and scored forty-two points. Second place went to [[brant-stil|Brant Steel]] with 39 points. Even Colonel Horton smiled at the award, whose son took the first prize. The young kair was proud of his nickname “Forty-Two” and then wore it as his name: “Forty-Two. For friends, that’s all.” His favorite saying is, “It’s an affair.”

## Biography
### In "Only One Star"
In the evening after the tournament, Erwin gathered six winners and told them to train for the next decisive competition for three weeks. Henry Horton answered for all ("Almost an honor, my lord"), but when he heard that they could not participate in the battles, he grimly remarked: "This is a disgrace, my lord." Erwin promised that after the second tournament, the champions would stand at the forefront of Damon's cavalry, and ordered to train with spears longer than usual. Erwin later recalled that the victory in the Labelin was brought by six kairs and was led by Henry Horton. Together with Brant Steel, he was considered "the famous champion of the tournament and the hero of the battle for the Labelin". Forty-Two was the first to put on a black X-coat; he was Damon's assistant and favorite.

While defending the palace at [[faunterra|Faunterra]], Erwin commissioned him a rear wall facing the navigable fairway. Forty-two came forward with a smile and promised, "It's a matter of agate, my lord." Erwin warned that it would be "hot as a blacksmith" and told him not to hide his pride in asking for help. In the first days of the siege, when the enemy to distract forces attacked the rear wall, Forty-two sent a message: the attack is weak, "the case for the agate." When they discussed the attack against the stonemasons, Forty-Two also volunteered to lead it, but Erwin chose Brant Steel. By the end of the siege of six hundred men brought by Erwin to the palace, about three hundred remained combat-ready, and of the officers only three remained alive: Damon Origin, Forty-Two and John Sokolik. ([[dzhon-sokolik|John Falcon]]) After Damon's death, Forty-Two, who remained a senior officer, himself appeared to the Duke, and he told him to put on Damon's armor: "Origin should be on the wall." Horton withstood the whole siege, and when Erwin was ready to sob, he stubbornly repeated: “Let’s break through, my lord.”

The Crow of the Crown questioned him about the custom train detained there by the X-cloaks, and went out on this trail to the mystery of the train in which the Items were later taken. He commanded the Kairs, who kept the captive Liam Schellier chained up all night. When [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]], demanding the body of the Insignia and her Subject, offered to resolve the dispute by a duel with Erwin, Forty-Two followed Jemis volunteered to fight the bear instead of the Duke, but Erwin did not allow a fight.

In The Voice of Winter, Forty-Two accompanied Erwin as he returned to the palace after [[minerva|Minerva]]'s antics. At the request of the Duke, he shouted "Glory to Agatha!", and then received an order to collect the "submissive Minerva": Leila Talmir, Captain Shatterhand, banker Conto, minister of ways and mentors. ### The “Yanmei Hunt”
In early May 1775, when Minerva secretly left by train for Marengo, Erwin in anger wanted to send Forty-Two with the Xes to return the "damn doll". [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] countered that two Southern kings were about to arrive and that the Empress could not be arrested in front of them.

Along with [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] and Jemis, Forty-Two was among the few to whom Erwin entrusted the quest for immortality: "reliable as rocks", they despised death enough not to sell their souls for eternal life. Upon hearing the story of the Crow of the Crown, the Forty-Two asked in confusion, "My Lord, but is this not the case?" Erwin distributed sacred texts to his assistants, and Horton got Emilia's favorite treatise: the young kair became interested in girls early, at the age of nine he read Emilia's "Love the Soul", and at thirteen - Miriam's "The Art of Free Love". In this chapter, he is called Harvey Horton, as is his father. Forty-two analyzed the first of the Main Commandments (“Take your place in the world with dignity”) and noticed that for the immortals it loses its meaning.

When Minerva announced the House Census of Items, Erwin instructed Grandfather, Crow, and Forty-Two to intercept all the Lords' letters. Only kair Horton was allowed to open them: “If you get a letter with a broken seal, cut off the hands of the person who broke it.” He, along with Jemis, Raven and Grandfather, was part of a narrow circle that Erwin collected at night for reports. On Erwin's orders, he followed [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] and reported on her studies: the Chamber, the magistrate, the archive of the palace library, a strange trip to Orwell and back. He also reported that David's father disappeared, and then handed Prince Hector Erwin's request: to find in Shimmery merchant Harmon Paulo Roger and the Light Sphere in exchange for military assistance from Origin. ### The Dynasty Tomb
In the spring of 1775, forty-two went down with Erwin to the tomb of the Dynasty in the temple of Farewell (Arden). The detachment was divided: Jemis with a dozen kairs went ahead of the Duke, Forty-Two with the rest closed. In the tomb, the caira moved the lid of the sarcophagus of the Empress Ingrid, in which they found the [[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]. Here the Northerners were attacked by the brigade of [[paul|Paul]] and [[rikhard-oridzhin|Ice]] with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]. Forty-two shouted "To the shelter!" and Jemis dragged Erwin away in a secret passage. Forty-two led the fight because of the sarcophagi. He tossed up his helmet, distracting the enemy, and after the gas explosion led the kires to attack. On the run, he cut one of the soldiers in two, but Ice broke the marble lid with three lashes, behind which the keel was hiding, and the fragment of the plate collapsed on Horton.

Almost the whole squad died: only Erwin and Jemis left the tomb on their feet, three more were carried out alive, and forty-two of them looked the worst. When the soldiers found a living one under the rubble, Ice recognized it (“This is the younger Horton, the Colonel’s son”), called it “half alive” and with hatred left it alive as a “gift” to the Duke: “To the Duke my wishes. The best. Pass it on, Horton." The Voice of the Crown reported that the "greatness of Agatha the Radiant" had spared the Duke and his two best vassals, the Kairs of Jemis Lilliday and Henry Horton, from death.

In September 1775, Erwin, cut off in the Steppe, at the Rust Giants in the County of Melnitz went through the memory of all who had lost in two years; one of the statues — the image, the trunk of which fell from the crumbling legs, — reminded him of the cyre Forty-Two, “one of the most glorious guys” with whom he served.

When he came to his senses, he asked for mercy, and Robert was ready to show it. Nathaniel's help kair did not take for a long time out of disbelief, but in the end he healed him with the Hand of the Znaharka: pelvic fractures and hips and damage to internal organs healed. Two days later, Horton, thin as a skeleton, appeared in a wheelchair to Minerva to thank her for "the doctor who did the miracle." He did not consider himself crippled: he had his hands and head, and he was going to serve Agatha, processing intelligence. Minerva was tormented by shame: no one said that she had ambushed her in the tomb. #### Ministry of Aeronautics and the Battle for the First Winter
In the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], Harmon and Henry Horton together operated a "mixture of an alchemical laboratory and a sewing workshop", both called a ministry. The merchant was referring to the Ministry of Aeronautics, the kair to the intelligence agency. Harmon dreamed of a merchant air fleet and the First Air Guild, Horton - about squadrons of balls for reconnaissance, launching saboteurs and transmitting signals. The difference of views did not prevent the partners from working together. Minerva, stopping at General Horton's battalions on the Back River, informed him that his son had been saved from death. The general dropped a tear of happiness and called himself an eternal debtor.

On the morning of December 2, 1775, the day of the Battle of the First Winter, Forty-Two was already walking on two prostheses of Nathaniel's work and with a cane. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) He was at a camp of aeronauts on the mountain with Harmon, [[salem|Salem]] and Veselchak and defended Navi from Harmon's jealousy. By order of Minerva, they put stuffed animals in the basket instead of people and launched the ball, distracting the enemy: "Case on the agate, Your Majesty." Having drunk “for the Agatha the Radiant and for the First Winter,” Forty-Two sent Harmon to the outpost of the mountain guard at the pass (“Right, Minister, go on foot, shake the fat”), and he himself went to the city through the Rusty Gates: “I’m a cairn. If the city is dying, my place is there.” Salem and Veselchak went with him, declaring themselves citizens of the First Winter. In street fighting, Salem was killed by Perst Joaquin, and Henry Horton survived the battle. During negotiations with the ambassadors, he and Nathaniel tried to pass the Leed Wolves into the tower.

## Personality
A cheerful, reckless and tireless warrior, devoted to Erwin and Agatha, executive and disciplined: without the order of my Lord, he did not allow anything to his people. Neither the injury nor the nearness of death deprived him of his love of life: "A week ago I almost lay in the grave - and now here I am full of strength!" He called Harmon an “honest thief” because he stole from the Puppeteer, and protected him, like Nathaniel, from other people’s distrust. His enthusiasm tired others, but made him a reliable ally.
