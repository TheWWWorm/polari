## Biography
Laura is the daughter of Finley, the second son of [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]], and his wife of the Agatha family. The Duke had twelve grandchildren, and ten of them, like Henry himself, were descended from the Pramatery of Helena; only Laura and her brother Jeremy were descended from the Agatha the Radiant. In December 1773, Laura was twelve years old. Golden-haired, big-eyed, with thin milky skin, she was cheerful, smart and quickly absorbed any science. Her grandfather praised her dancing, singing, and success in poetry and drawing; from the age of nine, she rode.

### Mother and obedience lessons
The mother, a devout agativist, taught her daughter to believe the Foremothers in spite of her father, who laughed at her and called her superstitious and stupid. Her main lesson was repeated throughout her life:
> “Be a good girl and everyone will love you. With love you will get everything you want.”
After one of the quarrels, Finley severely beat his wife, and she fled to her home. Henry said she was dead.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
On December 12, 1773, Laura became a bargaining chip. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], arriving at his grandfather in the city of Farway, hinted that he himself would marry her when she entered her age, and stressed how fortunate it was that the young infant belonged to the Agatha family. In return, Farway promised to vote for the Emperor's reforms. Grandfather assured Erwin that Laura would be taught by the best teachers by execution. Erwin expected to break the bet of Aiden Almer: according to his estimates, he could offer Laura as the husband of his youngest son, seven-year-old Albert. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]])

In the spring of 1774, his grandfather announced to Laura that he had found her a suitor. She saw Erwin only twice and briefly, but his grandfather painted his mind, wit and future home in Faunterra with a view of Hanai: Laura will wear the best dresses of the capital, and Alanis Almera “will die of envy.” Laura was only afraid of the northern cold.

## In "Only One Star"
In the autumn of 1774, after the rebellion of Origin, [[adrian|Adrian]] came into the Heart of Light and wished to see “young shoots of the great branch”. Grandfather and Jeremy trembled with fear, and Laura was not afraid: the emperor stroked her on the back of the head and neck, and she felt in him a strong man who would not offend the “good” girl. Adrian announced that he knew about the collusion with Erwin, and took both grandchildren to the capital hostage. The grandfather, having broken off the engagement, asked Laura for forgiveness; she honestly replied that she understood everything. On the train, Laura asked permission to pour tea to the emperor, asked about the victory at Melorange and asked to show the course of the war on the stratum field. Adrian promised to do it "on the next train", but no hostages were taken in his last line-up from Aleridan to Faunterra. So when the train fell off the bridge on Back, Laura and Jeremy were not in it.

## “I want a pony.”
### Evergard
In December 1774 came the news of the death of Vladyka, and the officer took the children away from [[aleridan|Aleridan]]. On the way, the monks of the parish of Gallard, led by brother Clement, “invited” them to visit, and Laura herself accepted the invitation. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) That's how she got to [[evergard|Evergard]]. In the lower courtyard, a witch was burned in front of her on a pole, and the chains removed from the deceased were immediately hung on a new pole - "as an edification." Terrified that freethinker Jeremy would also be burned, she secretly taught him prayers and repeated with him five times a day "Strengthened by works", thanks to Agatha and "Word to the Seventeen".

### Farway's Double Game
On [[minerva|Minerva]]'s orders, General [[stetkhem|Statham]] and their father Finley came to fetch the children. The father told the children what to say, and Laura, mentally asking for forgiveness from Agatha, lied to the general, as if she had spent only a month in Evergard. Statham announced that after Hope had sent companies to storm the palace, the marriage contract with Erwin ended; Laura, calling herself “Agatha’s granddaughter, like you,” apologized to him for the cowardice of the family, and the general softened. He took the children away from Evergard, and on the train Finley offered the Northerners a deal: the Farway would give a million ephes to buy out Shimmery's supply of spark eyes, and the Northerners would then repay the debt from the Crown's treasury. In farewell, Finley told the general that his wife was "on the Star". But in Marengo, he bought tickets back: the Farway were playing a double game between Origin and Gallard, and his father called his daughter “stupid like her late mother.” Now the prince accepted them as allies. Finley and Gallard arranged for Laura to marry the young Lord Albert, [[alanis-almera|Alanis]]'s brother, for military help in the event of a Northern attack and for the completion of Aiden Almer's planned expansion of the rail path from Aleridan to the Heart of the World. Laura was no longer a hostage and became Albert's fiancée. The boy followed her around, and she was babysitting him. Sister Ellie sent him from the capital crow pony Sir Adler, and Albert allowed Laura to ride it.

### Conversations with the Priory
With her piety and tact, Laura has placed a stern priarkh to her. He would often call her out for talks, ask tricky questions about faith and politics, and always ask her what she wanted. She asked for cocoa, marzipan from the best Aleri workshop, permission to pray with him, albums of Alanis dresses. Gallard confessed to her that the burned witch was the hostess of the salon in Fliss, who inclined the burgomaster to Alanis' side.

### Selection
Once Jeremy made Albert show an auditory window: there were thirty-two such outlets in Evergard. Through him, the brother and sister overheard Gallard arguing with a stranger, the Puppet Master. He received four Objects from the Priory in exchange for "a whole duchy", and now demanded another Object "... la Suna". Gallard, in response, demanded the right to speak with the Objects. Jeremy decided to run to the Heart of Light and tell his grandfather everything, but Laura chose the other side: Gallard, she said, was "not a scoundrel."

Laura came to the parish in tears, complained that her brother offended and ridiculed the mute monk, called her father and Jeremy freethinkers and discovered that her mother was alive and fled from beating her father. When asked if Jeremy could overhear something, she answered with an innocent look that he would not hear anything, since he was mute outside the door. Jeremy could not read a single prayer, and he was sentenced to eight years of obedience in a distant monastery. Laura offered to write to Lord Origin about the freethinking of her relatives. When asked by the priest if she wanted a “more mature groom”, Laura replied that she was eager to learn his name, and as a reward for her “courageous and pious act” she asked:
> “I want a pony!”

## In "The Voice of Winter" and "The Yanmei Hunt"
In the spring of 1775 (March-April) news came from Aleridan: Gallard announced his engagement to Laura, granddaughter of the ruler of Hope. Alanis was furious and even thought to hire murderers, fearing that the parish will give birth to an heir, grandson Farvaev. Erwin accepted the news calmly: the union of Almera and Hope, according to his calculations, ruled out a massacre in the center of the Empire.

## In "Humans and Gods"
When Laura became the wife of the priest, she adored her husband. On June 19, 1775, when the army of the parish under Fliss stormed the town of Obryv, occupied by the Agatov battalion of northerners, she was with her husband in a huge staff carriage - a "monster on the springs", harnessed by six horses - not daring to look at the field and closing with a plate. When Hope's troops occupied [[aleridan|Aleridan]], his grandfather, through Count [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]], suggested that [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] recognize Laura as the heiress of the archpriest, and then pass her off as Albert. Erwin mentally called her “a cunning Laura who managed to turn the priest” and agreed.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### Under Paul
Laura was with her husband when he defected to [[paul|Paul]]. In July 1775, at a meeting with Paul in Almera, she sat on Gallard's side with the Almeran Colonel and the Abbot of the Combat Brotherhood, enthusiastically listening to the "saint" and praying in a whisper. Gallard asked if Farway should be expelled from the Aleridan they had captured, but Paul refused: the Duke of Hope had generously paid him for peace. The priory gave Paul his ships, a battalion of knights, and a battalion of the Combat Brotherhood. At the end of July, a fleet assembled at Fleece Bay drove an army through Smokey Dahl to [[uejmar|Waymar]], where Desmond Origin besieged the county castle. While sailing, Gallard and Laura came to visit Paul's ship. At meetings with him, she kept records of theological conversations, wrote down in a notebook her husband’s questions and answers to the “saint”, and on impertinence Alanis answered with prepared gentleness. Alanis quipped that her uncle’s key was “rusty” and Laura was “picking up the lock with a pin.” When Paul spoke of a ritual after which the Chosen One would be resurrected, Laura fervently assured that Count Vittor was less worthy than her righteous husband, and requested that Gallard be allowed to pass the test first.

### Death of husband
On August 1—2, 1775, in [[uejmar|Waymar]], at the "test of the elect", Laura sat next to Gallard. Alanis stabbed Paul in the back of the head, and Gallard and Vittor were struck by the deadly contents of her powder. Laura screamed and called for help, Gallard in agony plucked out of her hair “red abomination”, and Alanis, spitting on her uncle’s corpse, rubbed on the cheek sobbing widow.

### Widow
Widowed, Laura called herself "Laura Almera, in the maidenhood of Farway" and kept the strictest mourning for one hundred and sixty days: black cloth covered her from the ankles to the top. Vittor entered Waymar with his regiments, paraded, and burned Gallard's body. She took jewelry, outfits, a collection of perfumes and the Duchy of the defeated Alanis. She was followed by two Almeran battalions, a knightly and a monastic, and their commanders recognized her as the rightful Duchess; according to Vittor, she was subordinate to two thousand soldiers, including four hundred knights. Along with Fleming, General Horis, and Bishop Amessin, she sent out pigeons with the news of Vittor's resurrection and that Alanis had killed the priory. ([[oris|General Oris]]) Grandfather wrote that Countess Daynite and Earl Arrowback opposed the Farway authorities, and called her home. Wittor Shayland persuaded Laura to stay with his army, promising to protect her from the new suitors until the end of mourning and send the Almer fleet from Waymar to Fliss, at the disposal of Hope's troops. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) Laura assured him that she herself chose Gallard for love, although she was told another, and with tears hugged the count.

So Laura remained in the army of the "Chosen". In September and early October 1775, she rode with her troops through Northwood County, to Lysium Dol and beyond, usually sleeping between Gold's company and the monks; the ringleaders Martin and Joaquin rode on both hands from her, whilst recounting their exploits. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) In October, the army entered the lands of Origin; Laura admired the "fairyland", and Vittor led the army to the First Winter, replenishing supplies along the way.

She was cared for by [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] and [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]. Whittor warned his brother:
> “Gallard took her hostage, and he didn’t notice how he was at the altar.”

### Iona
Laura scoffed cruelly at the captive Iona, who was sitting in a cage. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) She said that for several years she dreamed of at least approaching the height of Iona and Alanis, showed Iona Alanis bracelet and announced that she had surpassed both. She whispered to Martin that Iona was the witch who had bewitched Vittor, that the rebels were being beheaded, and that “if someone had killed her, it would have been easier for everyone.”

### Death
In October 1775, about a week after the army had entered Origin, the army stopped for a night in the captured castle. Drunk Martin tried to take Laura and strangled her with a pillow. Joaquin found her dead in a torn dress. Vittor reasoned that "the key from Almera is lost" and Farway would have to buy expensively, but Laura's warriors could be saved: if she was killed by Desmond's scouts, the knights would consider revenge a matter of honor. The murder was blamed on the scouts, and later Vittor sought an opportunity to iron out the "roughness" caused by her death, before the Duke of Farway. The Almeran ports and the remnants of the fleet occupied Farway.

## In "Fantasies"
In The Little Things, in the summer of 1776, Henry Farway tearfully told Lady Karen and her husband Manson that Laura had found true love in Gallard's "Holy Great Martyr," and after his death was already on the Star, until, according to him, "enemy scouts" strangled her in bed. This is the same version that was distributed by Vittor; in fact, she was killed by Martin. Count Edgar Flame explained to Karen that Farway made two big bets to keep Almera, and one of them "burned": the granddaughter was first widowed, then killed.

## Personality
Laura is a skilled pretender whose weapon is obedience, tact, and an innocent look. She was able to guess the mood of strong men and tell them what they want to hear. She loved trains and sweets, dreamed of dresses and the “great man”. For the sake of profit, she did not hesitate to betray her brother and father. Joaquin and Martin saw her as a model of purity and fidelity, and Vittor called her "a cunning Agate creature, who is better than any Iona."
