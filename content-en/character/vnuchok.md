**Grandson** is a young man-northerner, an indispensable companion of [[ded|Grandfather]]: his student and bodyguard. Grandfather sends him for food, for a newspaper, to find a hotel. He, like his teacher, does not name anyone. On the instructions of [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], he and his grandfather traveled with [[mark-voron|Mark]] on the trail of stolen items of the Dynasty. ([[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]])

## "A Doll on the Throne"
### House on Milky Avenue (Chapter 5)
In February 1775, Mark was taken to the old house of the Origins on the Milky Avenue of Faunterra and handed over to “the care of a strange grandfather with a blond granddaughter.” When Mark asked if the Grandson would follow him if he ran away, the Grandfather replied, “If you run, then the Grandson will think: whether to run next, or not.” His grandfather taught him to read: “He brings a new “Voice” every morning, we deal with it...” ([[golos-korony|voice of the Crown]]) After instructing Mark to look for Objects, Erwin gave him Grandfather and Granddaughter to help him.

### Investigation (chapters 11–27)
On the trips, the Grandson was keenly interested in the investigation and fell asleep Mark with questions: “So, the mayor is to blame? “What do you think, Raven?” He carefully memorized the parables of Grandfather and tried to interpret them. ([[pritchi-deda|Grandfather's Parables]]) For example, he deduced from Mark’s parable of the curious cat that “curiosity can be both evil and good.” The author notes that he “was a master of stupid questions.” For his mistakes, Grandfather awarded him with backsliders, and for a good question he praised: "Well done, Granddaughter, you learn." When asked by Mark if he was teaching a young man, Grandfather replied, "It's like he's learning. I'm just like, "

His granddaughter helped [[ryzhij|Red]] dig up the bodies of the blacksmith and his assistant. He was the first of the satellites tried to call the strangeness of the case: why the bandits kept the victims alive all night. On the road, Mark, Grandfather and Granddaughter were taken for a suspicious company: “Mark was more like a fool, and Granddaughter was a bone scraper.” Mark represented them as a shepherd, apprentice, and shoemaker. In the castle of [[bek-most|Beck]], the granddaughter had only a club of weapons.

When the “blue gang” caught up with the burned-out inn “Jack Buckler”, it was the Grandson who found the way out: “Follow me”, and first drove the horse into the gate, towards the enemies, and then into the forest thicket. Who would have thought they would listen to him? Two old tricksters - a young fool! " The northerners broke away from the chase and at night they found Mark falling from his horse by the sounds of a jimbuk. All three were then detained by the guard. In Aleridan, the monks began to glorify Ded “your honor”, and he told them: “And now send people to free the Granddaughter.”

### On the Train (Chapter 35)
On the way back, Grandson kept a safe in the compartment of the arrested Inge Price and his girl, Baby. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) When the train was attacked by people in blue coats, the Grandson waved his baton, but the enemy “put a spark in the thigh of the young man”, and he fell.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
On May 4, 1775, Erwin met the returnees to the capital of Grandfather, Mark and Granddaughter in a tavern called Rusty Rail. He arranged the game and gave everyone the nicknames: Grandfather became Gray, Mark - Platk, and "strong boy" Grandson - Kid. When Erwin demanded "my gold", the Kid got confused and got a cuff from Grey. On May 8, Erwin, returning to the capital with the Empress, saw at the station “Grandfather with an unchanged Granddaughter and Crow”.

## In "Humans and Gods"
In June 1775, Grandson accompanied Grandfather and Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Origin]] on the schooner Sea Arrow, Captain [[dzheff-bamber|Jeff Bamber]]. The sailors tried to find out the name of his master from the “little boy with a sword — like a gray, but do not gray”. He replied firmly, “The Lord has made a vow not to name anyone” — and has not even given his own. Then the [[potomok|Descendant]] called his grandfather Skipper, and his - Jung. Jung got into conversations (“And I heard about mastolom...”), first came with the news of the gurken, and on the island cut the way through the thickets with the sword for Sofia, [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille]] and Skipper.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
In early November 1775, the expedition returned to Windley, and Sofia, along with Skipper, Junga, Lady Mireille and the Kairs, came ashore.
