**Sormakh** is a Shavan of great growth, the "strongest" of the riders of the Thunder Gaunt. ([[ganta-groza|Ganta Thunderstorm]]) The Northerners, according to Groza, killed his father and brothers and stole the herd, so he craved revenge on the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]]. In [[marengo|Marengo]], he fought [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] and lost his hand; after that, he is recognized in the text as a one-armed big man.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
On the evening of May 6, 1775, in the garden of the Palace of Silence in [[marengo|Marengo]], Ganta Thunderstorm presented his rider to Erwin: "This is Sormah, the strongest of my riders. The northern wolves killed his father and brothers, and stole his herd." Ganta yielded Erwin to him: "My brothers are healthy and herds are intact, and Sormah wants revenge."

Sormach was huge, "like a bull": "The neck is a ship's mast, the fists are blacksmith's hammers." He cut without respite, struck back with a shield (he hit Erwin in the jaw with the rim of the shield) and shouted: “Die, rat!” Long arms kept Erwin at a distance, and he retreated from the alley to the flower garden. Then he realized, “Sormach is too hot.” Erwin placed his shoulder under the shield, pricked Sormach in the shoulder, and when the wounded man in a rage brought the sword for a slashing blow, met him from the bottom up and cut off his hand. Erwin did not finish off the enemy: “Throw down the shield and deal with the wound. ... I didn't stab your brothers. When the tyrant was alive, you and I fought on the same side. I have not killed a single Shavan yet, and you will not be the first.” Groza said, "Sormach let me down, but I don't have it," but then retreated, and [[minerva|Minerva]] stopped the fight. The wounded were left in the care of the Thunderstorm and the sent doctor.

Minerva explained to Erwin after the fight that the attack was not an attempt: “Shame is twice as heavy on you, drunk with wine and blind with a thirst for revenge. I knew you could do it.” On the eve, according to her, "this very Sormach pounded on the table with this very fist" and demanded to drive the wolves from Faunterra, and other Shavans supported him: they wanted to overthrow the Origins together with spark regiments and plunder the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]].

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
By July 1775, Sormach was in the horde of Paul, the "one-armed big man" next to [[chara|Chara]] (chapter 3 of Star - 1). At the Shavan council, the two of them lead the supporters of the strike on the North: “The younger wolf in the steppe, the older one in Waymar, which means that the First Winter is defenseless.” Sormach shouts, "Death to the wolves!"

On August 1–2, when the horde approached [[uejmar|Waymar]], he, along with Chara, Knuth and Muha, accompanied Paul as an "honorary guard" and said in farewell, "Ganta pus, we are under the castle wall. Let us hear, let us hear.” (Chapter 14 "Star - 4") In November, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]] calls him among those who bet: “Chara, Sormah and twenty other khanids — they have walked half the world, driven by hatred of kairam” (chapter 43 “Sword — 9”).

In Chapter 46, "Sword - 10" Chara, Sormah and Knuth come to Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]] and demand to give them the city of [[lid|Leed]]; Sormach shouts, "We will take our spoils! Whatever the count says, we'll gut the North like a dead sheep!" When the baron showed the Voice of God, Sormach "tried to get a mate", but Knut shut his mouth. Later, [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] and Martin see Sormah coming out of Paul's yurt, and taunts Martin, who was beaten up by Iona: "Babe beat!" ([[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]) Paul orders him and Chara to pick up the riders for the assault on Leed.

On December 2, 1775, in the chapter "To the Day Song", Paul instructs Knut and Sormach to lead the horde "middle way" and clear the castle. Sormach is not mentioned by name.

## Personality and Appearance
Huge, fast for its size, armed with a sword and shield. In battle and in conversation, he is hot-tempered and bloodthirsty: Erwin remarks that he is "almost blind with rage". In the horde, he demands more than others to go to the First Winter and kill the wolves.
