## Biography
William Davey is the son of Baron Ismail Davey, a sundowner whose triangular barony sticks "like a tooth" into the ocean in the upper left corner of the Polaris map. In addition to his son, the baron has a wife and three daughters. William served in the imperial army: first in [[faunterra|Faunterra]], then in [[litlend|Litland]], then in the North. Externally, it is a broad, rough and hoarse-voiced man, a hairy big man with thick eyebrows and moustaches. Erwin believed that he had no more manners than a boar from the Northwood forests. In fact, this “smooth forest beast” is the original knight, the most experienced military commander and one of the best strategists of the Empire. Lord [[lord-klarens|Clarence]] called him "the second general of the Crown".

### Youth (by Lady Karen)
Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark tells about the youth of the general. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) Most of the sunsets, she said, are crafty and love understatement, and William from childhood admired the directness of the Northerners - Northwooders and Kiers. He saw them often: in the port of the barony of Davy stands the Origen fort, one of those set after the Horse Wars. The father, the “true sunset”, chided his son with hints, and when he asked to speak directly, he was offended and stopped talking to him. William went to Faunterra to join the guard. The captain-recruiter did not like sunsets and was going to scold him on horseback, but William offered to play a game of strata and broke it. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) That's how he got into the Red Guard. To guess the moves of the enemy, Karen notes, is not difficult if all his childhood trying to understand the thoughts of his ancestors.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
### Erwin's friend
Davey was a constant opponent of [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] in the execution; behind one of the parties, he recalled that he knew Erwin for three years. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) Of all the emperor's confidants, only Erwin would dare to call him a friend, and Davey was the only one in the world who could joke with Erwin like that. The general called him "you", "the cunning devil" and "the northern outlaw" and said that it was shameful for a northerner to be so cunning, "however like a dog to meow". He assured me that he sees through Erwin: in the head of that girl, palace intrigues, and there is almost no room left for execution.

Six months before one of these parties, [[adrian|Adrian]] entrusted Davy with six spark regiments, half of the imperial spark army, and with each month the general became darker. The day before, he was running a parade instead of the Silver Fox. He admitted to Erwin that, of the four thousand soldiers, at most three hundred had ever seen real combat. He called the battle in the fields “heresy” with mockery: the Crown turns some “feudal turkeys” on others and never mars its copies. In his delirium on an [[ekspedicija|expedition beyond the River]], Erwin saw a party with William in a dream and told Richard's ghost that Iona, Nexia, and William Davey were alive.

### Adrian's Tea Party
In February 1774, Davey was sitting on the emperor's right hand in the palace's tea parlor as Erwin outlined to Adrian the plan of his coalition at [[katrin-katrin|Catherine Catherine]]'s. The general stood up to Erwin and slapped him on the shoulder, and Erwin regretted it: now any words of a friend in his support of the lord will be measured with suspicion. Hearing that Erwin was acting on behalf of the Origins, a distraught Davey muttered, "Are you crazy, Origin?" However, he quickly realized the benefits of the plan: sparklers can suppress any uprising, but the cairns will scare so that there will be no uprising at all. The nobles of the Southern Way, he said, will vote for anything, as soon as the northerner with the sword crosses their border, and the Westerners “scatter” against the reforms in any case: “They still pray to Konya and Bull — what the hell are the rail roads?”. ([[reformy-adriana|Adrian's Reforms]])

The Crow of the Crown only learned of this meeting in June. ([[mark-voron|Mark]]) The mystery persisted because Davey was Erwin's "cuff-mate" and Catherine is loyal to the lord. Clarence rebuked [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] a month before the summer games for asking her to bring her in with Davey: "General Davey drinks with the younger Origin." [[minerva|Mira]] later realized that Catherine, Davey, and Farway were connected to Erwin's plan and that Sybil was looking for them as members of his coalition.

## In "Only One Star"
In the hierarchy of the court, Davey, along with the Silver Fox, stood a step below the emperor and the bride. Lady Sybil and the General were invited to Adrian's tea party with Minerva, but the General reasoned that "the third is not suitable." At the beginning of the rebellion, Erwin believed that Adrian's army was divided into two wings: eight regiments at [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]], seven at Davy. ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]]) After the mutiny, the general fell out of favor because of his friendship with Origin, lost command of the corps and received only a reserve under the beginning.

### Hiking on Melorange
In the fall of 1774, Davey was with Adrian on a campaign against the horde of Moran's Steppe Fire. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) When the emperor sent five regiments north, the general cut Prince Hector off: "Eight minus five was always three. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]) Or in the South, another arithmetic?” In the burned Maple, he advised southerners not to enter a church full of tortured corpses, and told the surviving children not to be caught. Over the night orgies of the Shimmers, he was indignant: “Southern peacocks sleep in saddles!” On the orders of Adrian, he removed the imperial flags and sheltered sparklers among the wagons.

The plan of victory on December 2, 1774 at [[melorandzh|Melorange]] - to put the southern avant-garde and lure the Shavans into an ambush - came up with Davy, although in battle he commanded the reserve. At the awards, he refused praise, gave credit to the emperor and admitted: "Two years drinking with the rebel, but had no idea what he was up to." Adrian replied that he looked after him especially: a "friend of the rebel" could give "rough" advice leading to defeat. The council proved to be perfect, and Vladyka promised the general three regiments from the Southern Way. When the news came "Rebel - took - the palace" and Adrian laughed, Davey also could not stand it and laughed, stabbing himself in the stomach. Going north, Adrian left him with one regiment under Melorange to look after the wounded and for the loyalty of the Litlands.

## "A Doll on the Throne"
In the winter of 1775, Silver Fox reported to Minerva that Davey with a single regiment still stood in Melorange. Generals Smile, Gore, and Davey were among the few who knew the directive to move Items from the capital to Marengo. Minerve was later informed that Melorange had been under siege for forty thousand horsemen for the fourth month; of the combat-ready units in the city were two knight battalions, a couple of thousand riflemen, and Davey's spark regiment.

## In "Humans and Gods"
### The Siege of Melorange
In March 1775, [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]], learning that her father was ready to give her to Moran, rushed to the quarter of the spark regiment - the only one in the city where the authorities did not recognize the Litland. Davey acknowledged that the war would end if Moran was given gold and it, but said, "I was ordered to defend this city, not pay the enemy tribute." Becca lived at the infirmary of the regiment and cared for the wounded. A week later, the Shavans broke into the city, but the militia filled the gap, and a spark regiment interrupted the steppes locked inside. The second assault was also repulsed. Mira, looking for whom Adrian could come for the army, noticed that Davey's regiment in Melorange was "literally asking for a hand" to the former ruler.

### Meeting with Sofia
In early June 1775, at the General's headquarters in Maple, he was visited by Lady Sophia Origin with a judge, [[ded|Grandfather]]. ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]]) Davey was shy in front of his friend's mother and chased adjutants. He complained that after the war in Melorange was "loose", and asked why Erwin had not come to the rescue. He was especially offended that the Voice of the Crown attributed the salvation of the city of Minerva: Moran launched an assault three times, broke into the city once, and accepted the terms of Minerva because he "got on the nose". Sophia ridiculed the desire to read a laudatory article about herself, and the judge told the parable of the horse and rider.

Sofia handed over a package of two papers: Minerva's order to withdraw the defense of Meloranzh and Erwin's letter with a proposal to transfer the regiment to the North, in the Main valley. From Davey, the guests found out that Adrian was alive. The General called the proposal absurd (“the North is not my business. I was supposed to be called to Faunterra") and suspected that Erwin was taking the spark out of the south so Adrian wouldn't come to him for help. Then Sofia played a scene with masks, showing how the capital will have to choose a side. The general, grumpy with his mask, said, “I hate politics.” He honestly warned that he was serving the Crown and, if the bearer of the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]] orders to take the First Winter, would obey the order; Sophia replied that Erwin would not have called him a friend if he was able to break the oath.

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
### The Maina Valley
The general nevertheless took the regiment north and informed Minerva of Main that he had taken up the position "according to the order of the Lord Chancellor, approved by Your Majesty". Minerva did not remember this order; [[lejla-talmir|Leila Talmir]] found it in the records of papers that the lady signed in the evenings drunk. In the late summer of 1775, on her way to the First Winter, Mira met him in the Main Valley. The general, who had been standing there for a month without any sense, expressed everything: the order was "the most idiotic", compiled by the hand of Origin, the regiment is kept in the North, while the horde robs the Crown, and Minerva stole his victory at Melorange. He called her a “shower and hypocrite” and was ready to resign. Mira admitted that she signed the order drunk, and knew almost nothing about the siege. Davey replied that he was loyal not to her, but to the empire, but to the Eternal Hilt and the powers of the Chamber, which means that to serve the empire is to serve it. To the plan to give a fight to the Puppeteer, he said: “We will be defeated.” In the First Winter, Minerva announced, "With me is the Gauntlet of Power, the [[lazurnaya-gvardiya|Azure Guard]], and General Davey's spark regiment." ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]])

### In the first winter
The general, like [[navi|Nathaniel]], [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] and Hyrock, was full of pessimism. ([[artur-khajrok|Arthur Highrock]]) At the feast of Myra for bachelors, he, along with [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], was known as the most enviable groom and immediately started talking about troubles at the front, and then complained: “My father sleeps and sees how to marry me.” Mira admitted to herself that she had to “get along with the rude Davey.” When [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] thanked the Empress for having "personally brought" the spark regiment, Davey tactfully kept quiet about exactly how the regiment ended up in the North. Nathaniel's refusal to give the Items he imprinted with the words "Hitler's Snot". Seeing the festive garlands of foil and wire, Navi asked, "Did General Davey see them?"

### General's father
At the end of September 1775, in the Barony of Davy, [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]], posing as a bride sailing for her own wedding, purchased supplies for Erwin's fleet from Baron Ismail. The old man grumbled about his son, whom he called “this ass”: he served Adrian, then Minerva, and whom he serves now, “he does not write.” The Baron asked if any of the Imperial generals had married, dreamed of "marrying a donkey" to a nice girl like a guest, and hoped to laugh "when the donkey comes back alive".

### Battle for First Winter
In the Battle of the First Winter on December 2, 1775, Davy's sparks were waiting for the enemy in the city itself. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) On the streets of the sunsets, fallen garlands were beaten under the spark, and people in a philistine dress with double-toothed spark spears came out of the doorways. Generals of the Kuklovod then complained that in the city, where intelligence saw only civilians, there was a “whole spark regiment”. In the midst of the battle Minerva gave the general and the azure guard. According to [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]], thanks to Nexia's lead, all strikes coincided in time, and "even General William Davey perfectly chose the hour to attack the sunsets." Erwin said, “Even Davey is here. I called this rude man a long time ago, but, doubted that he would come ... ”

The next morning, the general appeared to Minerva at the town hall: “Your Majesty, this is strange, but we won.” Having received clear orders from her about the prisoners, the fallen and the awards, he was amazed: “Master, have you learned to command?”, immediately confused and imbued with respect for her. He also reported that a white flag was raised over the castle of the Origins.

### “A brilliant calculation”
After the war, the Minister of Education - a young lady of a painful appearance, with a cane and velvet gloves, sarcastic, drinking coffee with hanty - invites the general to check the veracity of the school textbook "Modern History for First and Second Stage Schools", namely the paragraph "Brilliant Calculation of Agatha and Yangmei" about the Battle of the First Winter. The story does not name her, but by all indications it is [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]. Instead of markings, Davey tells long stories. He remembers playing with Erwin, whose portrait hangs over her desk, and how he only retroactively announced that he had "calculated everything". He also talks about Erwin's wedding, where he came with his father. His father called him “My Donkey” and kept trying to marry him until Erwin showed the old man the wedding cost estimate.

The main story is about the days before the battle, in late November 1775. For a week, Davey, who is ill, travels around the city's defenses with Henry Horton's retired keel, [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], sent by [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]]. The original plan was made by Davey, Desmond, [[stetkhem|Statham]], [[blekberri|Blackberry]] and Hyrock. Three battalions of kairs hold an ice maze, a horde bursts into the city through the Rusty Gate, and in the Main Market Square Minerva Gauntlet of Power bombards the trapped enemies. Nathaniel the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]] melts the ice of the lake under the monks, and Davey's hidden regiment completes the survivors. Retired cayres Hasler and Frederick, commanders of the city militia, meanwhile prepared the city for a long siege: barracks, warehouses, roofs, noticeable barricades. Davey politely makes them sort things out.

Then [[minerva|Minerva]] and [[navi|Nathaniel]] arrive. The plan has changed: the bombardment will be at the entrance to the ice maze to annoy Paul and make him chase them, and Hilt is needed against Paul himself, so the ice on the lake will remain. ([[paul|Paul]]) Davey calls it delirium, and three times he is saved from disgrace by Forty-Two. Among other things, he offers to turn the raincoats inside out and ambush Agatha Cathedral, and also - garlands of tinsel under the spark at the height of the rider's head, the invention of Minerva. Dressed as a shepherdess, Nexia Flame sneaks through Fleming's blockade with news from Erwin. His summer plan to encircle the enemy with five Horton battalions and three Snow Counts failed: only about 280 X's under [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]]'s command go to the city, and Erwin himself went to [[lid|Leed]] to free his sister. Davey tells the officers the naked truth and lets go of anyone who wants to leave. He himself remains a "stubborn ass."

## Personality
A straight-line soldier: “I’m not a politician, I can speak directly, or not at all.” He hates politics, he is not strong in sharpness, he is stingy with emotions (“Do I like to sing and laugh?”). He often growls his "Grm", scratches his neck in excitement and is not shy in expressions. He is a pessimist and is always the first to speak of the worst, but he carries out orders, even the most ridiculous ones, and he puts the oath above friendship and personal insults: he serves not a person, but an empire and one who has Eternal Hilt. And when he does, he says, “If I do good, I will do good.” But when there is nothing to brag about, then what is..." Under the rough familiarity hide the friendliness and subtle mind of the strategist.
