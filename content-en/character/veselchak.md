## Who He Is

Veselchak is a peasant from the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]], so called in mockery. He looks at the world in such a way that he clearly sees one thing: everyone will soon die. For this thought, he has a whole set of words: “tombs”, “drains”, “carnations”, “land”, “will come to an end”. Most of all, he likes the word "boots". He is neither a coward by nature nor a whiner. Veselchak cooks well, like many of the peasants of the way, does not hold well in the saddle and prays to Pramatery Lyudmila, patroness of agriculture, and in the mornings - Gloria the Intercessor.

He gave Joaquin his real name only once: "Maximian, son of Isidore and Leopold. Pop in our village is a great inventor. Joaquin replied, “I’ll call him Cheerful,” and he agreed that it was even better. The native village of Veselchak is Zhuravlik, owned by the lord, who visits there "by flying", shouts, stabs people and takes away piglets. Therefore, Veselchak did not expect good from the Lord, and especially from the lady.

## In "Only One Star"

### Militia near Labelin

In the autumn of 1774, during the rebellion of Origin, Veselchak stood in the peasant militia of the Duke of Labelin on the Trade Route in front of Labelin. ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern Uprising]]; [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]]; [[labelin|Labelin]]) He served in the same ten with Joaquin, Salmon, Billy and ten-man Trace. ([[dzhoakin|Joaquin Eve Hannah]]) While the soldiers were digging ditches against the northern cavalry in the rain, Veselchak assured that the kairam of their "grooves" was not a hindrance. He predicted that the Northerners “only teeth to the dead pluck out,” and showed his white incisors: these will be useful to some warm. When the army of [[ervin-oridzhin|Origin]] appeared on the horizon, he said only: “The blades...”

After the capitulation of Labelin, the field infantry fell into the army of the northerners. Veselchak went to the mast under the direction of the cyre and Sergeant [[ruka-dodzh|Hand Dodge]] and said that with such a science, "the shovels will come to everyone." He was not going to run: "You can think you'll run away from the shovel!" He was the first to notice that Joaquin was followed by a cairn: "The land is for you, Deserter."

### Squire

When Lady [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] allowed Joaquin to take a squire, he chose Veselchak: let fate smile on him for once. The cheerleader replied, “What’s not? It won’t get worse.” Joaquin gave him two conditions: not to call him Deserter anymore and never to talk about coffins and blades. A week of fun, honestly. He cleaned the mail, ruled the blades, put a tent, looked after the mare and made goulash. Before going south, Joaquin bought him an inexpensive horse.

In early December, Pixy Veselchak woke up Joaquin with the words “The coffins came to us” and received an earful. ([[piksi|Pixie]]) This time he was right: the Crown's spark shelves came up. Together with Alanis and Joaquin, he left with Erwin's squad, boarded a train at Fairwell station and hit [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. Joaquin offered to escape into the darkness of the fields, but in the end also climbed into the car. Among the select northern nobility, he cowardly “straight, frankly and without the slightest shame”, because the commoner had nowhere to get lost.

### In the palace

At the gate of the palace, Veselchak doubted whether the two Origins could cope with the guard, and Alanis whipped him naked in the face. ([[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]) When the palace fell, he again said, “The boots are on us,” and Erwin asked what that meant. Veselchak explained: the emperor will return with the army and bury them. Erwin acknowledged that the squire had an "enviable tactical flair", and agreed that Adrian would indeed return.

During the siege of the palace, Veselchak lived in a warm lookout at the palace dove. ([[bitva-za-faunterru|Capture of Faunterra]]) He later said that he put out fires and carried out the wounded. On behalf of Alanis Joaquin with a squire sailed Hanai on a boat, breaking through the ice with a bug, and made their way to the station. There Joaquin shouted away the constables, and Veselchak calmly asked them when the train to Aleridan. They delivered Alanis' letter to the Earl at [[erroubek|Arrowback]] Castle.

## "A Doll on the Throne"

### Beck and Snowdrops

While the wounded Joaquin was lying in Beck Castle, Veselchak visited him and led him to the wreckage of the bridge after the train crash. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) He urged Joaquin to go to the Earl of Shayland, not Alanis: "If she were gone, my lady! I almost got my hands on the shoes!” Joaquin suddenly agreed. Having received money from the count, he released Veselchak, but he did not leave: “With you I am a squire, an important person! Without you, it is a terrible thing.”

Under Lowville, both joined [[salem|Salem]]'s [[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]]. In the battle at the outpost on the way to Izlyuchina, Joaquin made Veselchak his vestige. ([[izluchina|Riverbend]]) When the peasants were scattered in the fog, Veselchak suddenly shouted that he saw St. Gloria. People ran to the cry, and Joaquin was able to build them. Upon learning that an unknown rich man gives the Snowdrops sparked self-shooters, Veselchak discouraged Joaquin from driving a racer to Origin. He offered another: to present the Empress with self-shooters as a gift, so that everyone could see that the rebels came in peace.

### Saving Joaquin

Under Hampton, Joaquin was shot with a sparking arrow from a self-shot. Jolly realized whose arrow it was, and rode the horses in advance. Returning to the tent, he found there three murderers: one sat on the wounded, the second strangled him with a blanket. Veselchak hit the first with a dagger in the groin, the second wounded in the side, and the third coped with the lesson of Joaquin: the axe is heavier than the sword, and after the swing there is a second. He took a friend out of the camp. At night, they made their way back through the leaky ditch and, along with Tramp, Salmon and Billy, freed Salem. He refused to flee, and Joaquin and Veselchak left the army.

At the end of March 1775, they joined Louise's convoy on the road from Faunterra. ([[luiza|Louisa]]) When Cyre [[sejmur-stil|Seymour Steel]] of escort [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] brought Louise's guards to their knees, Veselchak was sleeping in a van. Iona took Joaquin prisoner, and Veselchak remained on the roadside.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”

Louise told Joaquin in [[uejmar|Waymar]] that she and Veselchak had split up to help him out in every way. Cheerleader, although he did not hold well in the saddle, went to Faunterra to look for a cyre who remembers Joaquin after the siege of the palace. Louise arranged a meeting for him in Labelin. Citing inquiries at a Waymar port, Joaquin barely remembered "the real name of Veselchak." He wanted to hear again, "what coffins-downs he invented this time."

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

### Story in the inn

In July 1775, the former Snowdrops, who had become [[adrian|Adrian]]'s mercenaries, entered the Labelin. A cheerful man with Salem threw himself across the column, and they gave him teeth. In the inn "Jack and Jane" at the station they met [[kharmon|Harmon]]. Veselchak said that he became a squire at Joaquin and now does not want to “back in the field”. He told how in the capital he was not allowed into the palace, how [[protektsiya|the protection]] agents recorded him in the "assassin" and how he was recognized by [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]]: "Ah, it's you, the coffins of the daughters!" Kair advised to turn to Alanis. She took Veselchak to Erwin, and the duke reported that Joaquin was already free, and gave money for the road. Then Veselchak went to Summerswith to Salem, to whom Joaquin promised to visit. He also told Harmon that Louise "lives - does not bother."

Harmon watered Adrian's ambassadors, and Veselchak and Salem helped drag the sleepers upstairs. Together with Harmon Veselchak opened the box sent as a gift to the Empress. Inside were the Light Sphere with a copy, the [[poyas-kolduna|Sorcerer's Belt]] and Adrian's letter to Queen Madeline. He said, “He who lies in the grave should stay there.”

### Before the Empress

In Faunterra, Veselchak learned that Alanis disappeared, and Iona's entourage died, and returned drunk, saying: "The worms... Earthie... The coffins..." Crown Raven [[mark-voron|Mark]] grabbed the trinity and took it to [[minerva|Minerva]]. While Harmon and Salem were getting lost, the sleeping Veselchak clearly outlined the whole thing: Adrian’s plot, a letter, words about the general with Valerie’s young wife, mysterious “visitors”. He called the three men "of three estates: a merchant, a peasant, and a soldier." Mark later admitted that Salem and Veselchak were "pure as water."

### the Aerial Guild

When Harmon was commissioned to build the balls, Veselchak and Salem became his assistants. On a ship in Canaan, they were carrying Harmona to try all sorts of smelly substances, looking for impregnation for the ball. In Ruilde, Veselchak was the first to notice a torch in a Shimmeria street cook, the same as in the Double Sphere basket. In the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], he supplied the "Ministry" and took bets on when the first disaster would occur. He himself petitioned the empress never to fly on a ball in her life.

### Battle for First Winter

On December 2, 1775, the day of the Battle of the First Winter, Veselchak and Salem were put in a basket of straw stuffed animals, and the ball was launched to distract the enemy. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Forty-two then went to the besieged city. Salem said he'd rather go with him, and Veselchak added, "Me too." According to him, they were accepted in the city as their own, and they have every right “to be stabbed in the coffin together with you.” They dropped Harmon off at the fork and drove off to the Rust Gate.

In the town of Veselchak and Salem defended their workshop. Joaquin broke in with Wilhelm's Perst and hit the first penny-taker without looking. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) The second he took aim and recognized his former squire. He looked up and said, “Why are you here, brother?” What have you done..." There was a dead Salem on the floor. Joaquin ran away.
