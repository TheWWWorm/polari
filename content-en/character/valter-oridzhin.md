**Walter Origin** is the lord of the younger branch of the [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]], the father of [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] and [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon]]. He only appears alive in the books in Robert's memory. Almost the entire cycle the reader knows him as an unnamed skeleton from the dungeon, which the monks nicknamed Silent Jack. Who is Jack, it becomes clear only in the "Shadow of the Great Tree". ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
In June 1774, the kidnapped [[kharmon|Harmon]] was thrown into a stone sack near the Maximian Monastery near [[labelin|Labelin]]. The jailer said, "Take it to Jack, take it to Jack." Touching the camera, the merchant came across a skull with the remains of hair and realized that he shares it with the deceased. From the body there was only a skeleton, curled up in the corner, in pants, the remnants of a shirt and a camsol. Fleeing the cold, Harmon put on a dead man's clothes. On the camsol, he felt the embroidered wenzel and decided that Jack was a nobleman.

In the dark, Harmon wondered how his cellmate died: from frost, hunger, wounds or by his own hand. First, he cursed Jack (“Why made such fierce enemies?”), then imbued with envy and respect for him. He noticed that Jack had died at the dry wall, and not under the trickle of water that the prisoner was given, and decided that the nobleman had killed himself with thirst: “Who gave birth to you, who raised you? Where did you get that kind of willpower?” Harmon came up with a story about Jack saying nothing to the executioners and mocking them. During interrogation, the fat jailer, [[brat-lyudvig|brother Ludwig]], called the deceased Silent Jack and threatened to send the merchant to him for another week.

The sleeve of the camzola was brown from the dead man's blood. When [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] freed Harmon, he remained in this camper, and Louise offered to wash and sew the thing. ([[luiza|Louisa]]) Only now, for the first time, Harmon saw a vein that felt so many times: a bat on flattened wings with an feathered arrow in its claws. "Silent Jack served the Origins. Maybe he was even one of them.” From then on, the merchant had a “strange, inexplicably related feeling” for the Origins. Before going to the monastery, he was again haunted by the image of Jack. Harmon blamed himself for not burying him, and vowed to give him a lavish funeral or send the bones to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], "because Jack's native land is there." After escaping from Labelin, he took with him and camzol.

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
### Memories of Robert
Robert recalled how as a child he could not overcome Agatha's "Minutes" and went to his father for advice. Robert's father spent thirty of his forty years in armor, lost his right eye and left hand in combat, and heard badly after a concussion. Robert could not imagine a higher authority. When asked by his son, the father first growled: “What?!”, and then explained that the Bright Mother teaches to fight and the whole book must be understood from the point of view of war. So he interpreted a few sayings. “Nothing so calms the soul as constant and unshakable” meant: take the castle and place the garrison. Since then, Robert found only tactical advice from Agatha.

### Robert recognizes his father.
In [[uindli|Windley]], Harmon swore to Robert to catch up with the balloon by the deadline: "Let's succeed, I swear by Silent Jack!" Robert was surprised, and the merchant said that Jack also served the Origins. He asked permission to wear his camsol, and Robert allowed: “This is not a two-color cloak, but a simple rag. Carry it yourself.” The treasurer then touched the brown spot on his back and asked for Jack's full name. Harmon did not know him: in the cell he was already sitting with a skeleton. Maximian monks, he said, kidnap people and torture for information about the Objects, and Jack died “a few years ago, maybe all ten.” He said, “I almost died with Jack. That’s why I feel kinship to him,” he repeated in a strange voice: “Nativeness, then...”. Then he asked if Jack had a left hand. Harmon didn't know it was dark in the dungeon. Then Robert began to look at cuffs. The right was badly worn, and the left he brought to his face and felt trembling fingers. He then carefully folded the camsol and pressed it against his chest. Harmon said that Jack, in his opinion, killed himself "to leave the executioners with his nose", and named one executioner - Ludwig's brother from the burned monastery north of Labelin. Robert answered his usual "Aha" and left the camsol to himself, promising the merchant another uniform with a coat of arms.

### Revenge
At the peace talks in First Winter, Robert snapped the Lady in the Dark’s neck and declared it a lawful execution: “In the autumn of sixty-five, my lord father Walter Origin disappeared during the campaign in Windley. We thought he had died in battle and was in a mass grave. But, as it turned out, he was kidnapped by monks subordinate to the Queen of Darkwater, and tortured during the torture. He added that he was ready to provide evidence. So it became clear that Silent Jack was Walter: he was recognized by the calluses and by the mutilation of his left hand.
