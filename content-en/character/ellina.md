## Who She Is

Ellina is the Archimandrite, the High Priest of the Pramatery [[tserkov|Church]]. She came out of the Sofia order and in her youth the very rules of the wedding. During the years of the main action, she is about ninety years old, and her mind is almost extinct. Mother [[mat-kordeliya|Cordelia]] once liked Ellina because she did not play intrigues, like the other sixteen higher mothers. Ellina, “not yet deranged at that time,” brought her closer and gave her the right to speak with her voice.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"

In the summer of 1774, the Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] explained to Mira that the Foremother branch was headed by the Arch-mother of Ellin: by the end of the phrase she forgets where she started, and the holy mothers with her are accustomed to a quiet life and do not think about what is happening outside the walls of the temples. Quite different, according to the Countess, is the case of the Holy Fathers with their resolute [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]].

## "A Doll on the Throne"

**Coronation.** Before the coronation [[minerva|Minerva]] explained that she had to read the prayer in front of the archimedes. ([[koronatsiya-minervy|Minerva's Coronation]]) Ellina, “having met her ninetieth birthday,” lost some of the flexibility of her mind. If she forgets to bless the empress, Minerva should pretend that the blessing has been received. Mother Cordelia rehearsed this ritual with her in advance. Fears came true: after the ceremonial prayer, the decrepit Ellina silently looked with empty eyes and blinked her nose from the cold. Cordelia was hissing, "Your Holiness!" Then Mira herself took the palm of the archimatrix, touched her forehead with it and pronounced a blessing on her behalf.

**Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant.** Lord Chancellor [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]], along with Ellina, announced the laying of the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant in Faunterra. The higher mothers to all his proposals answered: "the decision for the Archimoress Ellina." She herself, meanwhile, was muttering about missing rosaries.

**Confessions of Cordelia.** Minerva told Cordelia that the gods had relieved Ellin "from the burden of reason" and that he would soon have a chance to take her place. Cordelia countered that only the archimper could recognize the Glove on Minerva’s hand as a miracle. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]) Mira replied that the gods had placed Cordelia at the shoulder of the one "that is unable to decide". The church surplus of one hundred and forty thousand Hilt, according to Myra, is waiting for “a new archimatry, more enterprising than Ellina.”

## In "Humans and Gods"

**A visit to Erwin.** On June 1, 1775, after [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]'s death, Cordelia brought Ellin to Erwin in a wheelchair. In the folds of the brocade was seen only a tiny wrinkled face with an empty infant smile. When Erwin kissed the ring, Ellina thought it was a cat: “Moose, you? Cats!" While Cordelia cursed the Puppeteer on her behalf, the old woman was looking for a “go away cat”, waited for soft cheese and rejoiced at the rain at the wedding. Under Cordelia's tender comb, she fell asleep, muttering: "Sophia, hug. Emilia, understand. Miriam..." Erwin realized that Ellina was speaking on behalf of the Chapter, and Cordelia herself on behalf of Ellina. Cordelia showed him a paper with seventeen seals of the higher mothers and the signature of the archimatrix.

**The Ecumenical Council.** On the same day, Elina visited the Lord Chancellor and announced the Ecumenical Council. All the higher clergy of the Crown Lands called to her residence. Gallarda Almera the Archimedes was ordered to appear for confession and repentance. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) Gallard refused, declared himself defamed, and chastised Elina and Cordelia for interfering in the affairs of the Forefathers.

**Without Cordelia.** When Cordelia left for Almera, Mother Alice did not argue with Minerva: Elina “was completely plunged into the twilight”, and the capitol, in fact, remained without a head. ## “The Price of Fear”

In August 1756, Ellina arrived in [[uejmar|Waymar]] for the celebrations of the [[semnadtsatyj-dar|Seventeenth Gift]]. Fifteen mothers, three dozen nuns, and a dozen novice maids were with her. Emperor Thelurian and Prince [[adrian|Adrian]] kissed her hand. At the altar, she first read prayers over two New Gifts, and then, together with the bishop, gave them names: the round ones were called the Light Sphere, the second - the Rose of Happiness. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]])

Wittor Shayland knew then that Ellina's position was solid. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) It “does not tire itself of worldly games” and does not need anything, because it “has nothing to take”. After the departure of the emperor, she did not rush and remained in the castle, and with her Bishop Gallard. Vittor sat the Archimite next to him at a feast in honor of the Earl of Northwood and [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] and asked her to congratulate the young. ([[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]]) Ellina gave the most brilliant wedding speech that the walls of the castle heard, and whispered to Vittor: “I’m back in my best years.” The gloomy Cordelia tried to take her to bed, but the archimper waved away.

That same night, [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] reached out to one of her novices, and Vittor waited in horror for Ellina to find out. Later, the emperor announced that the novice of the archimatrix went for a walk at night and went missing.

Minerva still found the archmatrix in the moment of enlightenment and asked her about the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]. Elina answered with a quote: “Who knows, let him not remain silent.” [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]], who became the Chosen One, demanded that Adrian submit to himself and to the Mother Church: “I am chosen by the gods, and Ellin’s mother is mortal.”

## In "Fantasies"

In early 1776, according to the story “Fantasies”, both churches were left without chapters: Cordelia was killed, and Elina survived out of her mind. Fell into childhood, the archimper favored Origin, stroked him on the head and called him Murchik. On a cold night on February 2, a maid flooded her rooms too much. Ellina couldn’t stand the perfume, hugged the pillow in her sleep, clearly said: “Well, hello, cat...” and died. For four days, the higher mothers prayed for her repose, and then the chapter sank into squabbles for her place.
