Original wiki: https://polari.wwworm.com

The Polari literary cycle, its world and characters, and the Guide to the
Lands of Polari are works by Roman Surzhikov. This package translates wiki
article prose about those works; it is not an official English translation
of the books. References and quotations in the articles originate in the
source wiki and its cited works. English terminology and name spellings are
editorial choices and are not represented as author-approved.

ENGLISH DRAFTS

Prepared with ChatGPT assistance. Most article bodies are automatic drafts;
selected complete articles were translated directly and reviewed by the
assistant. Page titles, summaries, and infobox labels were translated
separately. The manifest and index identify each article's review status.
The collection as a whole has not received full semantic proofreading.

TRANSLATION MODEL

OPUS-MT / Helsinki-NLP, opus-mt-tc-big-zle-en.
Transformer-big model for East Slavic languages to English.
Base model: https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-tc-big-zle-en
