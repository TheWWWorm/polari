Ulyana Sad is the seventeenth Mother, the last in the list of Scripture: “the first is Miriam, the last is Ulyana.” ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) She is the only one of the Foremothers who has left no descendants. Therefore, noble families are usually called sixteen, and in churches there are icons of seventeen Foremothers. Ulyana is called the “sister of death”. It gives the dying and suffering a “cold light” of consolation.

## Death of Ulyana
The most famous story about Ulyana is the story of her death. He was remembered by [[kharmon|Harmon]], dying of starvation in captivity. Ulyana ran into a fan in the woods. The beast crushed the warriors and pierced her with its horn. The wound was so terrible that even the Sacred Objects were powerless. Ulyana asked to save her from suffering, but no one dared to raise his hand to the messenger of the gods. Only the fragile girl Yangmei decided: she drove the style of Ulyana into the heart. Since then, Yangmei has been called the Merciful. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]])

This story was referenced when they argued about the immortality of the Foremothers. [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] said that the Foremothers "certainly were mortal — to take at least Ulyana." [[ded|Grandfather]] asked Mark: if the combination of Objects can gain immortality, “why did Yangmei not do this and did not save Ulyana?”

## Cold light
In the story of "Plaks" the doctrine of Ulyanovsk is set out in detail. ([[plaksa|Crybaby]]) Cold light is "wisdom, quiet bliss, quiet joy, hope and peace," the best that the gods have created. It is the opposite of the hot flame of passion and the cold darkness of despair. Ulyana is the “master of cold light”. The nuns pray to her:
> "Ulyana Sad, sister of death, give me comfort."

Ulyana extinguishes the pain of the body and the suffering of the soul, sadness, hunger, the “confusion of choice” and the “torment of unrequited feelings.” Prayers of the order are named “Death-deliverer”, “Death cleansing”, “Send me a cold light”, “On the way to the Star”. There is a morning prayer "Thanks to the Sad Ulyana". [[otets-david|Father David]] read before the battle "The Way of the Sad Ulyana". There is also a waste hive. It is said that Ulyana cries when a living person is buried.

The people of Ulyana are the image of death and sorrow. “Stop pretending Ulyana Sad!” they say to the dull. Sculptures or icons of Ulyana are placed in the bed of each Gift. Many died in the extraction of the Objects so deep that their souls could get lost in the depths without seeing the Star; "The embodiment of Ulyana Sad" helps them find their way. Joaquin saw in one of these grottoes such a statue of crystal and black glass. With icons, Ulyana is known as a short girl “with a small nose and tearful eyes.”

## Monasteries
The Ulyana nuns live in cave monasteries under a vow of silence and are short-haired. Only the servants of Ulyana, according to [[minerva|Mira]], have such a short haircut. In the monastery, novices dig caves and take out the earth. They are allowed to speak only a few words a day, and before going to bed Mira spent her five words on prayer: “Blessed be, Mother of Ulyana the Sad.”

The sisters dig deep cells “to get closer to the Under Kingdom.” There are many such monasteries: Becca Litland counted twenty-four in the books. ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]) In Ulyana cave monastery in the west of [[blekmor|Blackmore]] County in Almer [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] imprisoned Minerva. There Mira met [[nora-plaksa|Nora]] Plax.

The Order of Ulyana is the poorest in the Chapter. The monasteries of Yangmei are patronized by the Yanmei, the Agathans by the Agathans, and "The Sad Mother left no descendants." The hives live on the modest alms of ordinary parishioners. The abbess of the order was [[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]], who became the voice of the archimper [[ellina|Ellina]].

## Ulyana Court
Ulyana is considered the judge of life and death. In "[[te-kogo-my-proshchaem|Those We Forgive]]," Minerva offered Inji Price a choice: return to the galleys or take an injection of blood, corpse poison and "witch potion" and trust the "judgment of Ulyana Sad." ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) Inge chose the shot and survived.
