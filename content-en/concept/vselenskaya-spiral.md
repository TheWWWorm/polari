The **universal spiral** is the image of the universe in the faith of Polari. According to the teachings of the [[tserkov|Church]], the gods created an ordered world, recapturing it from chaos, and building it turn by turn. Hence, the main sacred sign of Polari is the spiral.

## Teaching
The most complete account is given by Gallard Almer in [[khochu-poni|I Want a Pony]]: ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]])
> For centuries, the gods have been at war with the Lord of Chaos, the Dark Ido. Turn by turn the gods built the Universal Spiral. Where Dark Ido was retreating, a new round of order appeared.”

So, according to him, "and our world has grown, spreading chaos and darkness." Beyond the spiral lies the world of [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]].

### Rots
Polari people imagine a multi-tiered world.
- A world where people live.
- The lower circle, the kingdom of the gods, or the Underworld. Some theologians, according to the Marquis in the "Janmei Hunt", believe that the [[bezdonnaya-propast|bottomless abyss]] leads "directly to the bottom of the universal spiral - into the kingdom of the gods." ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) In the legend told by Neymir in "[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]", the dream goddess comes to a place "where the World Spiral curves into a new turn and the Underworld is replaced by a sublunar world." ([[nejmir|Neimir]])
- **Heavenly, or upper, turn.** According to the scientific books that [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] recounts in [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], the entire celestial turn occupies the ocean. He gives the sky blue, his splashes rain, and the sun floats in it.

Lady [[karen|Karen]], in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, explains that theologians consider the Universal Spiral a miracle of creation, and each turn of it, "be it the underworld or the sublunar, or the celestial sphere, or even the superheaven", is only a modest part of the great whole. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) Therefore, the underground kingdom and the sublunar world are written with a lowercase letter. There she recounts the findings of “theography,” the science of the connection of observable geography with the divine arrangement of the universe. The version that the Lodge of Gifts communicates with the underworld, according to her, is refuted. Some consider the entrance to the world of the gods the Shimmery Bottomless Failure. Others, and more of them, believe that the turn of people in the east is rounded down and itself turns into a turn of the gods, but all the expeditions to the east ended in failure.

There's a void between the turns. From the works of the Forefather Evrian, as explained to Erwin in the "[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]" masters of the secret order (the [[sadovniki|Gardeners]]), it is known that there is no gap between the turns of the air: the sublunar world is covered with a thin layer of "living gas", and the heavenly ship, rising above twenty miles, will collapse. [[otets-david|Father David]], in a conversation with Erwin, reasoned that [[zvezda|the Star]] was not floating in the celestial ocean, but was hanging in a void between turns, about a thousand miles above Polaris. The upper turn lies so far that a bolt launched from there would fly “hundreds of years”, or even millions.

### Other turns
The words “another turn of the spiral” mean another world in Polari. Father David suggested to Erwin to imagine “a turn of the Universal spiral”, where swords are able to destroy cities and evaporate the seas, but there are no wars, because the forces are balanced. Francis-Ilianus told Erwin that [[paul|Paul]], according to the order, "came from other turns of the universal spiral, and maybe from the kingdom of the Dark Ido." ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) Navi himself explained to [[karen|Karen]] and Dorothy that the kingdom of the gods is filled with a “sphere” in which any magic is possible, “but there is no sphere on your spiral.”

### End of the World
In the first prophetic dream of the king-prophet [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] from the “Gate of Trust” the world was plunged into a funnel, and all life fell into a bottomless Failure. ([[vrata-doveriya|The Gates of Trust]]) There was nothing left but a hard, smooth as glass surface of the Universal Spiral, but it went cracking and rain fell into the abyss (see [[sny-frantsiska-iliana|Dreams of Francis-Ilian]]).

## The Sacred Spiral
The sign of the spiral is a symbol of the [[tserkov|Church]] and the most frequent gesture of believers. To “create a spiral,” that is, to depict it with your fingers in the air or on your chest, means to pray, to overshadow yourself with a holy sign or to swear. Peace in “[[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, coin, spark]]” together with everyone creates a spiral after the words “Let us be strengthened by labor and struggle.” Ordinary people swear, “Here’s a spiral!” Prelates make a spiral for the flock, and diligent worshippers make it twice or four times.

The spiral is everywhere:
- copper disc with the Holy Spiral over the portal of the Cathedral in [[smolden|Smolden]], a mosaic spiral on the floor of the Cathedral of the First Winter, the Cathedral of Grace in the form of a sacred spiral;
- The coat of arms of Bishop Fliss with a rod and a spiral, the coat of arms of the Church of the Forefathers next to the coat of arms of Almera on [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s envelopes, silver spirals on the sleeves of the prior himself, the banner of his troops;
- spiral earrings, lamps, tin spirals on sticks and foil spirals for the holiday of Descent;
- round tablets with a carved spiral on the graves near the ashes of "[[dzhek-bakler|Jack Buckler]]";
- Hall of the Chamber, whose ground floor resembles a sacred spiral, and the tomb of Farewell, screwed into the ground by a “descending spiral”; ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]])
- The coat of arms of the Faculty of Physics of the University is "a candle of knowledge against the background of the spiral of the universe."

In amber, the Sun's droplets of fireflies fold into a spiral, a "model of the whole universe." ([[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]])

## The Author's Article
In 2018, the article “Gods of Polari” appeared in the section of the author’s articles “Polaripedia” — the cycle wiki on Fandom. ([Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%B8_%D0%9F%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8)) It is written before the last books of the cycle and expounds popular beliefs without knowing Navi's clues. Here's what she adds.

- **How the spiral grew.** The gods lived in the center of space and created the world around them layer by layer, pushing the former spheres wide. Therefore, the outer coils of the spiral are the oldest, and the sublunar world created by the latter lies closest to the gods, who now live in its center, that is, underground. In the west, the spiral rises upwards, passing into the celestial turn. (Gallard, in "I Want a Pony", on the contrary, says that a new round appeared where Dark Ido was retreating.)
- **Gods.** There are fewer gods than people, but still hundreds or thousands. Each created an entity of the world and has power over it: there is a god of the wind, a goddess of dreams, a god of blacksmithing. They rarely intervene: the things they have created are so wisely arranged that they themselves go the way the gods like. People do not know the names of gods - mortals are not worthy to pronounce them - and call them only by "professions".
- **Death at the Centre.** In the Southern Way, the Lands of the Crown and Almera believe that the bodies of the dead, the gods take for "transfer" and collect from them new bodies, so it is necessary to bury deeper, closer to the underworld, and the decomposition itself is sacred. The soul goes to [[zvezda|the Star]] (for what awaits it there, see [[tserkov|Church]]).
- **Death in the North.** The Northerners agree with the Center about bodies, but not all souls fall on the Star. The soul, attached to the world by memories or unfinished business, remains blind in it. Locked in her grief, she becomes a mad ghost over the years - a guest; having learned to see the world, wanders through it, gains wisdom and sometimes shares it with the living - this is a spirit. Guests in the northern traditions harm people, spirituals help.
- **Death in the South.** Shimmery and Darkwater mix the beliefs of all lands, but most lean toward the Star: otherwise, why did the gods create it? “The sun gives heat and light, the moon creates seasonal cycles... and the star on what?”

## Solve
In the finale of Shadow of the Great Tree, Navi drew a sacred spiral on the carpet with his shoe toe and explained:
> “The truth is that we all live on a spiral, which is also called a galaxy. This is your place; this is your place.”

According to him, Polaris is “not on the lower turn and not on the upper, but exactly in the gap between them”, in the middle of the dark starless space between the arms of the galaxy. On both turns live powerful creatures like him and Paul, whom the people of Polari call gods. The Sun of Polaris is surrounded by a gas-dust nebula, so it is invisible to the world of the gods, and people do not see the rest of the stars. [[zvezda|The star]] above Polaris is a lighthouse set in a spiral gap during an ancient interstellar war (see Star). Upon learning of this, Manson dreamed: “I will fly through the entire universe spiral!”

In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], which the author calls possible futures, Erwin, remembering this mystery, tells his cousin [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]], who was the ghost of [[dejmon-oridzhin|Damon]]: “now I doubt that the Star is the best place on the Spiral.”

## Other views
The Voltians, as mockingly recounted in Humans and Gods, wrote that the world is ball-shaped: "We live not on a spiral, but on the surface of the globe."
