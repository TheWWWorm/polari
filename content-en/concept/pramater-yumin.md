**Yumin** is one of the Holy Mothers, the "Promoter of Justice". ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) Her writings and testaments are the basis of Polari's legal proceedings along with [[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]'s code. The judges refer to the “principles of Pramatery Yumin”, the executioners serve “according to the precepts of Pramatery Yumin”, and the legendary judge of the North, Ded, is called “the sword of Pramatery Yumin”. ([[ded|Grandfather]])

## Gavin's trial
Yumin is associated with the legend of the origin of Ulyanina Dust. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Mothers used silvery pollen for secret correspondence. [[pramater-agata|Agatha]] wrote her a request to borrow the Gauntlet of Power, and Yangmei's student Gavin executed it against his will. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]) At the trial, Gavin could only say one thing: “I complied with the request of Agatha the Radiant...” Yumin, who played the role of a judge, ordered to put the experience. He showed that pollen is harmless to the Forebears, but suppresses the will of the people of the sublunar world. Yumin pardoned Gavin because he "was powerless over himself." The object was called Ulyanina Dust.

The action of Dust is described in Yumin's work "Justice and Action" and in her memoirs. In [[vrata-doveriya|the Gates of Trust]], Francis-Ilianus quoted this work: the Mother of Justice herself claimed that a person who committed a crime under the influence of Dust was innocent. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) In "Janmei Hunt", Dorian Amber read to the House Lords an excerpt from Yumin's memoirs of Dust. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]; [[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]]; [[palata|Chamber of Representatives]])

## Covenants
- Yumin's first axiom: "any doubts should be interpreted in favor of the accused."
- **It's crazy.** A person who is sick with soul and reason is not responsible. The trial of such an accused "is contrary to the laws of Foremother Yumin, and humanity itself."
- **Defendant's rights.** The accused should be informed of the charges, call witnesses, listen to the parties and give him an adviser.

At Manson's trial, Francis-Ilianus accused Judge [[albert-kantor|Cantor]] of violating "Pramatery Yumin's commandments" and the first axiom. ([[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]]) Then he took the joke out of the hall: “By Yumin’s commandments, doubts are interpreted in favor of the accused.” Even before the trial, [[menson|Manson]] was offered to admit himself insane in order to avoid responsibility “according to the precepts of Pramatery Yumin.” Judge Cantor acknowledged that Foremother Yumin's "principles are sacred," and ordered a medical evaluation.

## Honor and Kindness
[[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], tormented by guilt, prayed at the altar of Yumin, “the mistress of justice”, asking to evaluate his actions and impose punishment. From the genus Yumin come the Barons Steel: "Guide to the lands of Polari" mentions Baron Steel "of the genus Yumin", forced to become a vassal of the Order of the Light-bearers. Rhoda Yumin was also Redyar Toystone, who became the “sword of the Forefather Wilhelm” because he liked answers more than questions. In [[yolka-episkopa|The Bishop's Christmas Tree]], the boys argue whether the shoemaker's son can become a judge if Judge Palmer is "Yumin's grandson - the Foremothers of Justice".

## In "Fantasies"
- In the Rutin case, the town tried Kusaka's dog "in full compliance with the law," under Yumin's code: with witnesses, arguments of the parties, and an adviser. Defenders objected that “the Code of Yumin relieves from responsibility those who are not aware of their actions.”
- In Deathly Hallows, the executioner explained that he had become an executioner “for the sake of carrying out just punishments, according to the precepts of Pramatery Yumin.”
