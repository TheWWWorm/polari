**Dark Ido** is the lord of evil in the faith of the [[tserkov|Church]] of the Foremothers and Forefathers, the lord of chaos, with which the gods have been fighting for centuries. He's been credited with all the bad things in the underworld. Most often his name sounds in the brazen proverbs: “Eh, the Dark Ido pulled me into this bend!” In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]] and Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] claims Ido is real and [[paul|Paul]] is his soldier. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

## Faith
The most complete exposition of the doctrine is given by the priest Gallard Almer in the story “[[khochu-poni|I Want a Pony]]”. ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) For centuries, the gods have been at war with the Dark Ido. First, they pushed it and carved out a small space in which they founded an orderly world. The turn-by-turn gods built the [[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]]: "Where Dark Ido retreated, a new round of order appeared there." But chaos still hangs over the world. His “invisible tentacles” sow discord, willfulness, greed and vicious passions among people. When the gods noticed that the “spassion idols” were dividing people, they sent the Twelve Commandments to the world of the [[pramateri|Forebears]].

Hence the protection from Ido: the Covenants of the Mothers, prayer and the sacred spiral. The peasants, seeing the ashes of the inn "[[dzhek-bakler|Jack Buckler]]", "began to pray and create spirals", and the monks "as they could, drove away the Dark Ido with all the devils." According to legend, Ido put a devil on every person to knock a mortal out of the way, and rewards those devils who can use any petty offense. There is also a superstition: “do not remember the Dark Ido - you will call trouble.” The three, according to [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]], pleases Ido, and the four are gods.

There are different paths in the Ido world. Laura believed that in the Beyond "lives a Dark Ido." The Northerners suspected that the items that brought trouble threw people "servants of the Dark Ido." ([[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]]) [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] decided that the fire in the Dara lodge was a battle between the gods and the lord of chaos, who wanted to prevent the appearance of Objects. The Polari Lands Guide tells us that the first settlers of [[litlend|Litland]], who came from the north, declared the jungle a “refuge of Ido” and created a sacred spiral from them.

Among the [[shavany|Shavans]], the lord of evil is not the Dark Ido, but the Spirit of the Worm, the lord of decay and decay. You can only escape from it by getting into the [[orda-strannikov|horde of Wanderers]].

## "Servants of the Dark Ido"
The servants of Ido are called all who are considered the embodiment of evil:
- **Waymar's prisoner.** [[uejmar|Waymar]]’s dungeons believed that the unnamed prisoner made a deal with Dark Ido: “He gave his soul to the Dark One, and he sent inhuman power in return.” The prisoner allegedly broke down the wall with his bare hands, killed two jailers and left through the “witch’s door”. [[minerva|Mira]] found out he was running underground. Wittor's servants whispered that the count had sold Ido's soul so that the prisoner would obey him. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) Mira later realized that the prisoner had become “a servant of the Dark Ido, or rather a servant of the Puppet Master.”
- **Brigade and Puppeteer.** Almeran shepherds spoke of a "band" that cuts people for ritual and serves the Dark Ido. [[mark-voron|Mark]], referring to Erwin, said that the gift in the Beyond was cursed: the key to the Absolute sent the Dark Ido to disrupt the plans of the gods. In 1775, the "[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]" called the heretics with the Rings, who attacked the tomb of Farewell, "servants of the Dark Ido." Erwin in the House called it the Gallard Priory. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) The Church later accused Gallard of conspiring "with the servants of the Dark Ido."
- **Northerners.** For the Origin's enemies, they were wolves. Ice called [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] "the cunningst of the demons of the Dark Ido."

In [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], the masters' machine looked like "a haven for demons - Dark Ido's underlings." In "Confident", a lit-up item called out in the church: "Dark Ido! ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]) The Dark Ido affair!"

## The Author's Article
In the author's article "Gods of Polari" (2018, "Polaripedia" on Fandom; written before the last books of the cycle) describes how Dark Ido is understood in different lands. ([Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%B8_%D0%9F%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8)) The Empire officially recognizes both the Darkness - chaos beyond the spiral - and the god of Darkness, but interprets it in different ways, and this has led to religious conflicts more than once. At the Center, Dark Ido has no power over the world directly and acts at the hands of people, sending them bad thoughts. The Northerners consider this almost heresy: Ido helps a whole host of devils, and he retained power only in deserts, swamps, open seas and Beyond. Shimmerians and Litlanders, on the contrary, believe that Ido does not invade people's lives, but commands from heaven with lights and weather - sends heat and cold, droughts, fires and floods; human vices are the work of people themselves. In the West, the god of Darkness is called the Worm; among the Shavans, his accomplice is the Jackal, the devourer of rot, and the revered gods are the Pervokon and the Primordial Bull, powerful men with the heads of a horse and a bull.

## Navi's Teachings
[[navi|Navi]] called Ido his main enemy. Karen said he fights Dark Ido "about half of his life." According to him, the mortals have long successfully repelled the attacks of chaos, without knowing it:
> “Your faith, your way of life, your world order have built a strong wall in the path of the enemy.”

But a breach opened up in the wall, and Navi felt obliged to stop Ido: "It is unlikely that the gods will send anyone to help me." The only weapon he called intelligence: any other Dark Ido will turn against the owner. Dorothy admitted that the path to the world of Dark Ido exists and getting there is “easier than you think.”

In Shadow of the Great Tree, Navi explained to [[minerva|Minerva]] and [[mark-voron|Mark]] who [[paul|Paul]] was. Ganta pus invented Shavans, and Paul is a "servant of the Dark Ido", and "in the most direct" sense. The servants of chaos do not receive orders: “Orders are part of order, chaos does not use them.” They are ruled by passion, Paul by thirst and contempt. Thirst makes him kill. One day, contempt will force you to open the portal. The key to it is a set of Objects that people call [[absolyut|the Absolute]]. The castle is in a place inaccessible to mortals. Only Paul or Navi himself can open it. The Absolute gives only as a “side effect”.

When asked if Ido would kill all people, Navi replied that "genocide is an instrument of order." How many people will die, it is impossible to guess, but Ido will destroy any order: religion, morality, estates, laws, honor, cities and shops. For this to happen, Ido is enough to show up. Later on the flying schooner, Navi told Minerva that Paul, when he got the Absolute, would escape to the Dark Ido kingdom. Its movement will break a hole between the worlds, and the chaos will flood Polaris. Paul once served with Navi in the same command as a Marine, but "took the side of the Dark Ido."

Mira first discarded these "dark Ido tales", but later became alarmed again. The very idea of the destruction of the world contradicted the Scriptures: only the Foltians believed in such things. After the night, when [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] turned Paul into a fugitive, she did not know who he was - "a servant of the lord of chaos or the Dark Ido itself."

## In "Fantasies"
In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], Erwin believes he has defeated Dark Ido. Meeting churchmen in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], he put on the Voice of Winter because "it is useful to remind churchmen who defeated the Dark Ido." Jemis listed the Duke's merits: he "broke the servants of the Dark Ido and saved the Polari Empire."
