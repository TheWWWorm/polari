The Voice of Winter is the ancestral sword of [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]], which gave the name of the interlude "The Voice of Winter". ([[glas-zimy|Voice of Winter]]) This is not a sacred object, but the creation of human hands, but for the northerners it is almost sacred.

## Appearance
The blade is narrow, no wider than three fingers, and seems fragile, but this appearance is deceptive. The steel is covered with a "sizzle of panicles" that gives it a shade of stormy sky. The blade of perfect sharpening on the edge becomes almost transparent and seems to dissolve in the air. Next to the swords of the Shavans, the Voice of Winter looked "ridiculously thin" like a needle. His handle is black, with a cold rough coat.

## History
The sword was forged by Master Conrad of [[lid|Leed]] for Duke Odar Origin. His service began "in the darkest days of the Empire", in the First Horse War. ([[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]]) The voice of Winter was in Odar’s hands when he swore to protect the empire to the last drop of blood. With this sword, the great gauntlet of Hadinai was killed at the Battle of Doyle, which stopped the Steppe's movement to the north. The same sword inflicted a blow of mercy on the mortally wounded Odar: his son saved his father from suffering and inherited the blade.

The voice of Winter was heard in the Second and Third Horse Wars, chopping the shavanks, gant, and crafty barons of Fairis, who were beating in the back of the imperial troops. He participated in the Golden Campaign against the rebellious Counts in the deserts of Hope. He was prayed to before the battle by Arthur Origin's army: forty-four men surrounded at Fort Lance by an entire battalion. The sword cut down the pirates in the straits of the Granite Islands, drowned along with the flaming schooner, but was raised from the bottom.

When the Earls of [[fleming|Fleming]] treacherously captured the First Winter, Duke Liorei Origin defeated twelve kairs with this sword and then laid down his arms to save the inhabitants of the castle. At his death wish, the sword followed him into the underground dungeon. The voice of Winter lay on the bones of the buried Lyoraeus for four years until the heir raised him and he cut off the head of the traitor Fleming.

The sword got its name two centuries ago because it was “as fast as sound”. Master Conrad has compiled an accurate description of his masterpiece, and therefore it is known that in two hundred and twenty years of multiple edits and sharpening, the blade has lost three and a half ounces of weight. The Voice of Winter is the fastest sword in the world. Only with such a light and durable blade is the Agatha Lightning possible, the last of the five family techniques of the Origins. This is a double cross-cross stroke, "X and another X": the first flash can still be paired, the second is ahead of the enemy's thought.

## Erwin's Sword
### Gift and study
In early 1775, after winning the war, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] gave the sword to his son [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] with the words "you deserve it". Erwin's former sword was nameless, too heavy for a "lord-finch" and forged by a good, but not the best master. Erwin was proud that the sword was possessed by “a dozen great Origins” and he absorbed “the art of twelve generations of blade masters.” A weak swordsman, he hoped that with such a blade he would master the family techniques. His imaginary althes of [[dalliya-rejven|anxiety]] advised to refuse the gift. Cousin [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]], the last connoisseur of the five family techniques, tried the blade with trepidation. He showed Erwin Lightning Agatha by cutting the candle into three equal pieces.

Erwin began to exercise every day and purposely went to the fencing hall in front of the court ladies, strengthening his image as a sword master. In fact, nothing came of it: the blade “did not forgive doubts, did not tolerate humanity.” The alarm explained that Erwin lacked anger. When questioned by [[itan|Ethan]]'s secretary, he put the sword on the table for intimidation, but was unable to hit or threaten seriously; when the althessa put a candle on the table, the sword took off and sliced the air, and the candle fell whole. Later, he crashed into the countertop without hitting a candle or a traffic jam. It was only at the end of the interlude, enraged that Minerva stopped the battle on the Holy Field, that he pushed away the Alarm and with one stroke cut off the stalk of the snowdrop sent to him by Minerva. ([[minerva|Minerva]]; [[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]])

### The War of 1775
In [[yanmejskaya-okhota|the Yanmei Hunt]], in Marengo, Erwin fought the Voice of Winter with Shavan Sormah and cut off his brush. Before the battle, he boasted that this sword had sent to the Star of the Great Chief Hadinai with his sons, three leaders of the Horde of Eagles, a dozen gant and a hundred Shavans. Erwin then challenged Groze's gauntlet by spinning the blade into a "ghost disk". ([[ganta-groza|Ganta Thunderstorm]]) At the trial of [[menson|Manson]], King Francis-Ilianus argued that "even the Voice of Winter can not kill if you do not want to." ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) In the Temple of Farewell, when enemies burst into the tomb, Erwin burst from the sheath of the "heavily heavy" Voice of Winter.

In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], Erwin stabbed General [[veksler|Wexler]]'s knights in the back with them. The voice of Winter “singed with delight”, as if he himself found weaknesses in the armor. After this fight, Erwin himself, looking into the eyes, finished off the mortally wounded prisoner of the Almeran at his request. At the end of June 1775, at Halliwell, at Rei-Roy, Finger of Wilhelm's lash was made by the tip of the blade.

In "[[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]" in the village of Berkha, Erwin executed four captured Shavans with the Voice of Winter. Agatha's lightning "flashed in all its glory" as if by itself. To the blacksmith Bershan, he stopped the point in his mouth without touching the flesh. Together with Lord Velentyne, he intercepted Lady Agnia Fleming's carriages and, rejecting the cloak, showed her the black handle of the Voice of Winter: she was captured. Before descending the icy aqueduct to Leed, Erwin touched the handle of the Voice of Winter and said, "To the assault, gentlemen." At Lillydeev Castle, he stabbed two guards with one punch and placed a blade to [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]'s throat. On the way from Leed, while bypassing the enemy's Fort Skvorok Heaven, he placed a sword to Fleming's kair neck from a met horse watch. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]) In the Battle of the First Winter, he led cavalry with him until his hand was filled with lead and "even the light Voice of Winter" began to feel like a hammer. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])

### Richard's death
The night after the battle, his older brother [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] ("Ice") killed his father and Captain [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]]. After guessing that Richard was hiding in the castle, Erwin immediately demanded his sword hanging on the wall. He then demanded a sword fight, and [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]] served him the Voice of Winter. At this moment, Erwin felt as something “hot and sweet” flowed from the hilt into his fingers: the sword “hung for a fight” with a real master. Agatha’s irresistible lightning failed because Richard’s protective belt stopped the blade within a foot of his neck, only to scratch his brother’s skin. Then Erwin moved the brace on the hilt: “The voice of winter has become sparkly.” The discharge struck Richard, and after [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] had removed the Items from him, Erwin thrust the sword into his brother's jugular vein. When and by whom the sword was provided with a spark mechanism is not explained.

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasia, a possible future, Erwin rarely wears Winter's Voice because he doesn't like the sword that killed Richard. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) Minerva looked at the hilt for a long time, when he still wore it. Erwin girds himself at the ceremonial meetings: at the meeting of the Church Embassy in the First Winter (to remind the churchmen who defeated the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]) and at the meeting of Empress Mira with the churchmen, where he entered with the Steppe ambassadors.
