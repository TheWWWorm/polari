**Roman Surzhikov** is a science fiction writer, the author of the series “Polars”. The information in this article is taken from three streams, where the author answered readers’ questions: April 2021, January and spring 2026 (more about the sources in the article “The Author’s Word”). ([[slovo-avtora|The Author's Word]]) All that is said “according to the author” is his own words. What he said about the world itself and the heroes of Polari is collected in The Author's Word; here it is about himself and his books.

## The Polari cycle
Polari is the author’s first world. The cycle begins with the novel [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], includes six novels and many interludes; after the end of the cycle, the author wrote a collection of short stories "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]". The order of reading is in the section of the book. ([Source](/c/book)) According to the author, the cycle was conceived as his "feudal utopia" - feudalism at its best, which he is capable of (stream January 7, 2026). In the spring of 2026, he said that the Polari was completed about two years earlier.

Paper books "Polars", according to the author, are published at the website of Fantlab. He called it not a large publishing house, but an analytical site, which has a small warehouse and periodicals (spring stream 2026). In the paper “Shadow of the Great Tree” included a story that is nowhere else, and for paper “Fantasies” the author saves another (stream January 7, 2026). ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]) Audiobooks of the cycle are voiced by the reader Kirill Golovin; he also conducted the 2021 stream.

The published author does not rewrite: he likes to believe that the world of Polari exists somewhere in fact, and he considers the published text part of his history. The only exception is Arrow, Coin, Spark: once he found the novel delayed and cut several scenes from it without changing the plot (spring stream 2026).

## Other Books and Worlds
The author aims to create at least two fantasy worlds: most authors, according to him, remain in memory as authors of one world (spring stream 2026).

- **The world of wells.** Second world author. His first novel, Traces on the Water, was about a writer, a soldier and a sailor; by the spring of 2026, it was completed and published in chapters. Readers who came from Polari, the author advised to take it: in his estimation, he is more dynamic and concentrated "Arrow, Coin, Spark". There is a separate wiki about this world, the World of Wells. ([Source](https://mirkolodcev.wwworm.com/roman-surzhikov))
- **"Unlucky Chris".** Social fiction with black humor, which takes place in our world. The author wrote it in parallel with Traces on the Water and in early 2026 hoped to finish both books in the first half of the year.
- **"Ferz is a lonely figure".** The author's only published novel is not from Polari; he himself considers it weaker.

The author will return to Polari, he said, not before creating at least one more world, and “not exactly in the coming years” (stream January 7, 2026). The world of bards may be next, but he did not give details.

## Spiral of Worlds
The sacred symbol of Polari is the [[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]]. The author said that he imagines a metaverse where different worlds lie on different turns of the spiral: Polari is one turn, the world of “Unlucky Chris” is another, the world of wells is the third. It is impossible to physically move from one world to another (spring stream 2026).

## How the author writes
- **No drafts.** The author thinks through the story in his head and writes immediately white. The chapter is read aloud once with a loved one and ruled; the proofreader manuscript goes only before the paper edition (stream January 7, 2026, spring stream 2026).
- **Where the romance begins.** From the image - the world, person or situation. Then the author develops it, finds a conflict and builds a plot; he begins to write when he sees the conflict as a whole, from the beginning to the denouement (spring stream 2026).
- **The “living” heroes.** The main criterion for a successful character is “liveliness”: the hero acts by his character, and not by order of the author (stream January 7, 2026).
- **Consultants.** The first three books “Polars” the author wrote without consultants, studied military history; later consulted with a lawyer and a military consultant. The fencing scenes are entirely fictional (streaming April 18, 2021).
- **Order.** No more than five hours of work a day. In 2026, the author set himself the task of writing 12 pages per week, about 600 per year (spring stream 2026).
- **Music.** He writes to calm music without words - Enigma, Dagda, Dead Can Dance; one album can sound ten times in a row (spring stream 2026).
- **Simple form.** The events of Polari are complex (“plans within plans”), so the author consciously decided to tell them simply, without experimenting with the form (stream January 7, 2026). The exception is the literary game in the collector's edition, where the novel is commented on by Lady Karen (see Author in the world of Polari).
