## Essence

The sacred objects in Polaris have been “silent” for centuries: people owned them, but could not control them. According to tradition, the dust of Ulyanina Dust is harmless to the Forebears, and it deprives ordinary mortals of their will - and this is only one of the indications that the Forebears differed from their descendants. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) “The puppeteer got the firstblood. She is able to awaken the Objects,” [[otets-david|Father David]] explained to Erwin. With the firstblood in the veins, a person can “speak” with Objects — voice, thought, keywords — while others they do not obey.

One firstblood is not enough: you still need to know how to conduct a “conversation”. Led, while teaching [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] and Martin about the treatment of the Rings, warned that some enemies already know that talking to the Objects requires primrose, "but no one knows how to have a conversation." [[navi|Navi]] explained to Minerva that it is necessary to understand the principle of operation of the Subject; the simplest commands for beginners are ordinary words spoken backwards.

## Transfer

The blood is transmitted through the bodily fluids of the carrier. In 1756, [[navi|Nathaniel]] gave Waymar boy Mick a wet paper with his tears, calling it "a seed of magical power"; guard Doug drank his blood. ([[mik|Mik]]) According to Navi himself, at first it was enough to spit or drop blood, even dried. Mick then fell into the hands of the church guard and got the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]]; according to his stories, the then Nathaniel was a kind sympathetic eccentric and gave firstblood only because Mick asked him.

> “Nine people died because of it. Two died, unsuccessfully applying the firstblood, the other seven killed each other in an attempt to take possession of me.

He even sent a girl of fifteen, mistakenly believing that he was her age. Then Navi found, stole and used one Object and made his blood “infertile”; he cannot lose power over the Objects while he is alive. Later, fleeing from the secret order, he played suicide, leaving enough blood in the place of the "death" for the experiments of the order, and he himself got out of the burial well and fled. When Navi showed up at Minerva's, Raven Mark's men secretly extracted his "body fluids" — a cup with traces of saliva, a handkerchief with which the girl soaked his dog-bitten leg — to make sure he did not possess Paul's properties.

Paul “dedicated” his riflemen, kissing survivors on the lips; according to Erwin, Paul also tested his Shavans, pitting them against each other. ([[paul|Paul]]) During the Waymar mutiny, Vittor turned down Joaquin, who asked for the Ring: "You need blood, special blood! You don't have that!" As far back as A Doll on the Throne, the Origins noticed that the Hand of the Insignia only responded to a few — to [[gvenda|Gwenda]], the mechanic [[luis-mariya|Louis]], and Erwin himself, while neither Iona, Lady Sophia, nor Robert Origin were able to revive it. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) Erwin recalled that in the Beyond, he was stabbed with a dagger of a soldier of the brigade, “living like a fan,” and suggested that the “body fluids” of the soldiers of the brigade could get into the bodies of Gwenda and Louis; he suspected a special blood from Minerva, on whose hand the Glove “revitalized”. Minerva herself did not believe until Navi announced to her, “You are initiated!” Minerva, as it turned out, received firstblood in the basement of [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]], “in one way or another.” [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] received it from Vittor: "I tried to kill myself, but Vittor returned. That’s why he gave me the firstblood.” Joaquin Vittor dedicated from the bubble: in early June 1775, the count put before him "a bubble with a thick red liquid, similar to blood" and said: "This is power over Objects ... Drink and wait. On the fourth day, the objects will be subject to you” (“Humans and Gods”, “Sword — 2”). Joaquin decided that it was "someone's blood" and its owner may have died long ago. Whose text she does not say, but to initiate, according to Ice, can only two - Paul and Nathaniel, and Paul, "Waymar prisoner", for years sat in the Shayland prison. So, Vittor had blood in his glasses, by all accounts Paul's. After serving the Puppeteer, Joaquin left Waymar "with Perst in hand and with firstblood in his veins."

Erwin was sure that the firstblood was “transmitted only from Paul”, but Navi’s story refutes this. Minerva’s experience also showed the limits of transmission: Inji Price drank blood from a bubble taken from a dead Znaharka, but the Object did not respond to it, and Minerva wondered whether Znaharka’s death, a small amount of blood, or the conjecture itself was wrong. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) The blood of the carrier, drunk without initiation, can relieve the disease, like the blood of Iona, which she gave to the freezing brother (“You will not be able to talk to the Objects, but the disease will weaken”), and the elixir of Martin Shayland, who partially healed Desmond. [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], the "woman" of the Gnome Gauntlet, tried to get Paul's blood with a leech and then seduce him, but Paul, who was afraid to give her the power of the Rings, fled from her. Then she gave herself to Hanid Alisher and bit his lip, but Perst never obeyed her.

## Significance in the struggle

The firstblood became the main bet in the war for items. The secret Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] had been hunting for Nathaniel for decades, experimenting on his leftover blood, and, according to Father David, wanted to give blood to “all men.” David drew Erwin a society where everyone initiated and has an Object in his craft: the coachman has an Object-horse, jumping through the air, the scientist has an Object-book with all the laws of the universe. Erwin was going to give the firstblood only to those whom he trusted — “mostly nobles” — and for the first time doubted David’s mind. Masters of the order asked Erwin to take Paul alive: "I will not hide, we will be glad to the source of the firstblood." [[adrian|Adrian]] gave the order of prisoners Shavans with firstblood, and in December 1775, he and Magda dreamed of "sharing a vial" of Nathaniel's blood. Navi warned that with the Absolute and the carrier of the firstblood, the order would be able to get to the lighthouse-Star. [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille]], the daughter of Lady in Darkness, told Lady Sophia that shooting from the Rings is only a "curly branch" of horror: using Objects for shooting is "the same as pricking nuts with a gold bar", and primrose gives much greater opportunities. After the Battle of the First Winter, she asked the Origins not to help the order: most of the carriers died or fled, "only you and Lady Iona remained in the valley."

The hosts themselves treated the gift differently. Erwin told the captured gunman, whom he turned into a "Handgun", that the primal blood and power over the Objects exclude him "from the ranks of mankind." Iona, who called herself a "monster" because of the firstblood, said that of the fifty known carriers, only one - Minerva - did not become a beast. Minerva admitted that she herself was going to kill and thought about using the Eternal Hilt, capable of destroying the city.
