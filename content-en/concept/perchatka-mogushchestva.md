## Tradition

According to legend, the Gauntlet of Power is one of the most ancient Objects, the same age as the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]. Yangmei the Merciful wore it, doing her engineering miracles: without touching a finger, she moved stone blocks, in a matter of hours she erected walls and added dams. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) Her most famous act was the ice bridge in the Crystal Mountains: half the flight of an arrow, he crossed the gorge, which could not be seen without dizziness, and Yangmei built it in fifteen minutes with one hand - the one on which the Glove was. So she saved more than a hundred people from the avalanche. The irony noted in the text is that the Merciful Mother was nicknamed not for this, but for the murder of a single girl a month later.

The glove is also mentioned in the history of Ulyanina Dust: once [[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]] asked Yangmei Glove to quickly pave the way, and sent a note written in secret pollen. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Her student, Yangmei Gavin, who read it to fulfill the request, brought wine to the kairam, who guarded the store of Objects: two drank and fell in convulsions, and the third Gavin stabbed with a dagger.

## Storage

The glove did not formally belong to the Dynasty. According to the holy covenants, she could not belong to any of the mortals: the council of prelates-guardians of the Church of Mercy in Faunterra took care of her peace. The object lay in its own "bed" - a bottomless mine, from which it rose only on great holidays and with the permission of the prelates; without the seven sacred keys that were kept from different people, it could not be extracted. Therefore, when in 1774 the Puppeteer stole the property of the Dynasty, the Glove survived - as the Eternal Hilt, it was kept separately from other Objects. Prior [[gallard-almera|Gallard]] interpreted this before [[adrian|Adrian]] as a good sign: the great Mother loves and protects the Lord.

A glove is not a glove. In the casket Minerva found a female brush, cast, as she first thought, of white gold; in fact, the brush was only a stand from a tree petrified over the centuries, and the Object itself was the thinnest film of white metal that preserved the mirror brilliance of liquid, “not frozen for seventeen centuries.” The film sticks to the fingers, repeats every wrinkle and even burr, to the touch cold and almost weightless. Later, Minerva wore an Item in a cylindrical scroll case: a glass rod in silver foil fell on his palm, which, touching the skin, spread mercury and covered his hand. Navi, seeing the Glove for the first time, sighed: in the world of the gods such have not been used for a long time, “because they do not do it.”

## Minerva and Glove

### The Bridge That Wasn't
In December 1774, during the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]], [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]'s army stood under Faunterra, and the Palace Bridge across Hanai was severed. ([[khanaj|Khanai]]) Minerva ordered Major Backfield to take a hundred men "unburdened with piety," to bring the keepers to the Church of Mercy and force them to open the castles, and if they refused, to level the church with a ram; she instructed Lieutenant [[shatterkhend|Shatterhand]] to make sure that the Glove got to her, not to the same place as the other Objects. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]])

> “Yanmei the Merciful has made of ice a bridge across the abyss, than saved from the avalanche of our Forefathers. Look: there is a lot of ice on the river. And I'm Yangmei, and I need a bridge."

The glove was placed on her hand perfectly, without the slightest gap. But neither to raise the ice, nor at least to sweep away the snow Minerva could not and, hiding her hand in the clutch, went to Erwin for negotiations. There she bluffed: showed the Glove as a threat and laid out three arguments. Origin figured out the deception - if she could talk to the Object, she would have broken the ice on Canaan long ago - but Minerva also realized from his believing reaction that he himself did not know who owned the Rings. Negotiations stopped the war. The rumor that Minerva put on the Glove, “which for hundreds of years no one has succeeded in doing,” earned her the nickname “Minerva the Peace Bearer.”

### Recognition of Miracle
In 1775, Minerva demanded from the archimandrite candidate [[mat-kordeliya|mother Cordelia]] certainty: to bless her act with the Glove or to condemn publicly. Cordelia hesitated: the Glove "answered the call, but did not speak", there were no canonical signs of a miracle, and Lord Chancellor Erwin considered the speaking Objects to be the machinations of the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]. Minerva then raised her hand in the Glove and warned that in half an hour she would show it to a crowd of parishioners. Cordelia gave up and hoarsely said, "I bless your Majesty and call the Glove on your hand a miracle."

Erwin later linked the Glove with the primrose: since the Object has “come to life” on Minerva’s hand, there must be special blood in it. ([[pervokrov|Firstblood]]) Minerva herself shrugged off and, by her own admission, sometimes kept the Glove under her pillow.

### Navi's Lessons
In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], [[navi|Navi]] assured Minerva that she had firstblood in her veins (she received it in [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]'s basement), and offered to prove it. He explained that in order to speak with the Object, one must understand the principle of its action, and the Gauntlet of Power controls gravity. First, the object is made weightless, then transferred and returned to him the weight. Minerva looked at the chair - around it there were ripples, like over a hot bridge. With the word "weightlessness" spoken backwards ("mo-se-ven"), she stripped the chair of its weight, and Navi blew it into the air. At the command of the "Lortnock" from her index finger, a beam stretched out, and the chair floated behind him; the second beam from the middle finger allowed to keep the object even; "abolition of control" untied the object, and "mosev" returned to him the weight.

> “Excuse me, Your Majesty, but if I speak with scientific precision, I have succeeded.”

### Defence of Faunterra
In the autumn of 1775, when the horde of the Ganta was marching towards the capital, Minerva made the Glove an instrument of propaganda. At the sight of the sparking parts at the palace, she lifted a ticket into the air and put it, weightless, on the tip of the soldier's spear; The "Voice of the Crown" described a "flashing ticket" and newspaper sales soared. The flow of refugees, according to Crow [[mark-voron|Mark]], has decreased threefold. Minerva went to build fortifications and carried stones, logs and carts with the ground, so that the ramparts grew before her eyes; the soldiers rejoiced at the sight of the Glove on the girl's hand.

Studying was not easy. The glove took away the weight from the axe, turning it into a fluff, and Minerva learned to return the weight at the right moment to split the log; the axe was twisted. To exercise her fingers, she picked up letters, chips, a cup of coffee. ([[stratemy|Stratems]]) Navi considered construction an empty business: fortifications are useless, the main thing is to kill Paul. To intercept Paul over the Smokey Dahlia, he asked Minerva for the Glove, Bird without the Flesh, the ship and the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]], promising with a probability of 86% to survive and save the Glove. ([[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) Later he explained why he would not teach her other Objects: of the thirty-nine Objects in Faunterra, only four work on their own, and of these only the Glove is safe.

### Flight and flying ship
Before [[adrian|Adrian]] entered the capital of Minerva with a hundred swords, Glove and Hilt remained against his regiments. With the help of the Glove, she lifted Inge Price with four rays for a hundred yards to inspect the enemy rear. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) When the camp was attacked by scouts at night, Minerva grabbed everything that fell under her hand, threw up and fell on the pavement, and threw a burning cart into the night sky, revealing shadows on the ropes. On the river under fire, she raised the intoxicated Navi and dipped her head into the water, and then gave him the Object: The glove "flowed" onto his hand. Navi lifted the river schooner into the air and led it faster than the wind; after staying in the sky for about half an hour, he dropped the ship into the lake and slept all day while Minerva watched him with the Glove on her fingers. Magda Labelin, hearing about the flying ship, was amazed: “Whole ship?!” Lady in Darkness noticed that Minerva's successes were limited, and the skill worthy of Yangmei showed only a visitor.

### First Winter
On this schooner Minerva flew to the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], where she announced the transfer of the capital: "With me the Gauntlet of Power, the azure guard and the spark regiment of General Davey." ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) She tried to show people the power of the Subject as often as possible: the boat sailed over the roofs and went down into the fountain in the square, in the Hamptons, the Carrying World showered the philistine with agates from the flying schooner. During the day, Minerva climbed into the mountains and led the "some construction" - an ice labyrinth in front of the castle, which played a role in the future battle. For his plan, Navi asked for a celestial ship, a Light Sphere, and a Glove.

In the Battle of the First Winter on December 2, 1775, Navi took the Glove. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) From the mountaintop, he hurled ice blocks at Shayland's position; the finger-bearers cut them with fire, but the ice-fins still reached the ground, leaving funnels full of steam. [[paul|Paul]], watching the flight of the shells, concluded that the Glove has a range limit and that the shooter, therefore, climbed the mountain. In the underground chapel, Navi, hiding behind the altar, threw benches at Paul. After the deconstructor exploded, he fell into a bottomless pit with the Glove; Minerva hoped that he made himself weightless, but later it turned out that for some reason he did not use the Glove when he fell. When he was picked up, Minerva took the Gauntlet off him and led the boat with the wounded in the air herself - and made sure that Navi was right: it was very difficult to lead the ship.

## Limits and value

The glove is the lightest known Item; according to Navi, even its "hot annihilation" would destroy Faunterra. Mireille Nan-Claire explained that the First Tree reactors were too heavy for the Yangmei Glove, so the Forebears carried them across the mainland in the usual ways. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) The deconstructor does not threaten the Glove, since Paul did not touch it. In the negotiations in the First Winter, Adrian demanded to give him, along with Hilt and the Rings and the Yangmei Glove, and the Allies proposed to use it in the storming of the Rock of Heaven, calling it "ideal for the siege of mountain forts."

## Selection of Minerva

In the last chapter of the cycle, Navi, discovering the truth about the lighthouse-Star, asked Minerva to put on the Glove. He explained that he could not be killed by the sword or by falling from the tower, and the only way was in the Empress's hand: The glove could lift him to a maximum height and throw him on the ground. If he survives, the secret order is likely to gain about eighty percent of his knowledge and, with Adrian's Absolute, reprogram the lighthouse; if he dies, the lighthouse will eventually be forgotten. Adrian begged to give the Glove to him, Jemis and Iona asked to kill, Erwin - to spare. Minerva wanted to remove the Gauntlet and give it to someone, even Iona with her firstblood, but she realized: to give it to anyone is to choose. The cycle ends with Navi’s words: “The choice is yours.”

## In "Fantasies"

In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, the Glove remains with Minerva and serves mainly as a construction site. In 1776, Minerva worked every morning with a Glove on the viaduct of the future railway near the First Winter and conceived a dam on the Twins. Each support of the viaduct consists of granite blocks of a thousand poods. The brigade puts such a block in a day, and the Glove in two minutes, so the builders called the Empress’s working hours “The Change of the Lady”. Minerva tried to teach the Glove to Iona, who also has firstblood, but the lessons failed (later, in another fantasy, Minerva again dreamed of transferring to Iona the construction site). ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) The stones flew up, the axe flew to Iona herself, fragile things fought. Iona barely lifted the harpsichord on the shooting pad of the castle, and then forgot to untangle the rays and threw it into the lake with one stroke.

For Minerva’s birthday, the Aeronautical Guild gave her a flying carriage adapted for flights on the Glove: with glass windows in the floor to see the rays, and weights to balance. That day, the first lady-in-waiting seized the Glove from Minerva in advance so that it would not fly through the air, “like a sparrow.” In the summer of 1776, Bishop Amessin, on behalf of both branches of the Church, demanded that Minerva repent for using the Glove in battle. Erwin countered that Wilhelm's ban only applied to the Persians, and swore that no one had died at Minerva's hands, but Nathaniel had fought. Minerva swore the same thing, and Mother Alicia withdrew the demand.
