Elena the Traveler, or Elena the Seer, is one of the holy Mothers, the fifth in the canon of Scripture after Miriam, Janmei, Sophia and Agatha. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) It patronizes travelers, sailors, cartographers and merchants. They pray before a long journey and in a sea storm. Sailors who survived the storm said, “Glory to Elena the Traveler.”

## Miracles and Objects
According to [[adrian|Adrian]]’s masters who studied the [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects]], “Helena could see from a great distance, through all obstacles.” For this purpose, she served as her personal Subject - [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]], "one of the primordial Objects of the Foremothers." It was later stored in [[shimmeri|Shimmery]]. In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], Adrian, having received the Bird from [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], promised the guests to "repeat the miracle of the Mother Elena."

## Travels
- **Polaris maps.** According to the “Guide to the lands of Polari”, Elena made “very detailed maps”. They were marked with great accuracy silver deposits in the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]]. Therefore, the mountains began to be developed early, in the III century, and next to the mines grew the first northern cities - [[lid|Leed]], Main and Steel.
- **Rei-Roy.** In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], the Second of the Five claimed that, according to ancient writings, [[rejs|Rei-Roy]] was also founded by the Foremothers. Elena-Traveler climbed behind Holliwell and for a long time walked along the Steppe to the west. In the parking lot, she had a beautiful dream, and she realized that this was a sacred place. Elena founded a fort there, which later grew into a city.
- **Boarding.** The boarding house of Elena-u-Lake is built where, according to legend, the Mother visited. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) Her book People and Lands is one of the student books, along with Agatha's Moments and Yangmei's diaries.

## Temples
The churches of Helena the Traveler decorate "pictures of distant lands and raging seas." In Leongard, the sea gate of [[nadezhda|Hope]], stands the temple of Elena-Na-Wave with a majestic colonnade. In Fantasia, the port church in Winslow is painted with ships, seagulls, mermaids and Elena-Travelers. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) In "Confident" the abbess boasted of a sculpture of Foremother Elena in the monastery gazebo - an exact copy of the famous statue. ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]) Harmon had a silver medallion with the image of Elena the Seer, and in captivity he prayed to her.

## The truth about Elena
In Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] said that the Forebears were the crew of a colonial starship, and Elena was its first navigator. When passengers wanted to burn the energy of the surviving bioreactors and send a distress signal, Elena and [[pramater-agata|Agatha]] argued that the signal would not be noticed through hundreds of parsecs. According to them, bioreactors had to be preserved in order to populate the planet.
