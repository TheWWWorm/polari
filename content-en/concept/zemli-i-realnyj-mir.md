[[polari-world|Polari]] lands are fictional, and the author did not name direct prototypes for them. ([[roman-surzhikov|Roman Surzhikov]]) This article separates two kinds of comparisons. The mark "By the Guide" or "according to the author" stands where the "Guide to the lands of Polari" itself compares with our world or the author is on the stream. The mark "It looks like" is in the observations of the author of the article: these are parallels in climate, economy, structure, customs and sounding of names. Each observation is explained, but the words of the author behind it.

## What the author and the guide say
- The Guide compares the land with our world several times. On the climate of Origin: "The first winter is comparable in climate with Moscow, but not with Norilsk." About Northwood: "Compared to the Siberian taiga, Northwood's climate is milder, but the beast is larger and more dangerous." Shayland he calls "this kind of Polarian Albion", and about Folta writes: "From the point of view of the state, Folta is like ancient Athens." He associates Litland with reggae, Reis with Brodsky’s line about cowboys, and attributes to travelers the saying “my house is edged”, which they do not know. The wealth of land there is measured by "GDP".
- According to the author, Polari is conceived as a “feudal utopia”. Feudalism arose there by itself: there are few resources, the environment is hostile, there are few warriors. There was no Christianity in this world. Chastity, says the author, is not European, but Christian: neither the Greeks nor the Romans were his models, and in Polari did not happen what Christianity did in our world (stream January 7, 2026).
- About his other world, from “Traces on the water”, the author said that he takes from the real country only the style, names and clothes, and the structure of the state invents himself (spring stream 2026). There are no such words about the Polari lands, but it is also better to be careful with their similarities.
- In the author’s remarks, written on behalf of the resident Polari, direct comparisons with real countries were not found.

## The Empire as a whole
The Emperor directly rules only the lands of the Crown. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]) Only they pay the Dynasty a permanent tax, and the [[velikie-doma|Great Houses]] serve as troops. The law in their lands comes into force only after the approval of the House, which the Guide calls the "Palat (Parliament of the Empire)". ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) There are even two kingdoms within the empire, [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] and [[darkvoter|Darkwater]]. At the same time in the yard of the XVIII century with a [[iskra|spark]] and rail roads. ([[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]])

It looks like the Holy Roman Empire. There too, the emperor was strongest in hereditary possessions, the electors and princes remained almost independent, the Reichstag claimed imperial tributes, and within the empire there were kings, for example, of Bohemia. [[reformy-adriana|Adrian's reforms]], the universal tax, and the dissolution of the House resemble two real rulers. Joseph II, the emperor-reformer, tried to centralize the patchwork power and came across resistance from the estates. Charles I in England collected taxes without parliament, and it ended in war. The Polari technique, with steam locomotives and electric light, can be compared to 19th century Britain.

## North
### The Origin and the First Winter
[[oridzhin-zemlya|Origin]] is a mountainous land: about 40% of its area is occupied by the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]], and there are few arable land. It lives on wool, steel, iron and silver, and before that military booty. The society is "very conservative feudal", militarized. A commoner can become a warm, and then a [[kajry|kair]]. The imperial laws of caira are only enforced “on a residual basis.” The heir [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] tells his father: “We are poorer, father. We are almost poor.” According to the author, after Almera, Sofia fell into a “severe cold backwater” (stream January 7, 2026). ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]])

According to the Guide: the climate of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] is comparable to that of Moscow.
It looks like (surveillance): the Scottish Highlands. Similarity in the mountains, sheep and wool, poor soil and proud military nobility. Prussia is related to the poor land, the army as the main "fishing" and cult of service. The code of kair, according to which the will of the lord is higher than his life, resembles Bushido.

### Northwood
[[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]] is covered with coniferous forests. He lives in hunting, furs and forest, and in the Bear Fang are the largest shipyards of the North. The Northwoods are called "bears." They have the honor of ballads and epics, feast and bath. The land was settled by fugitives from the growing feudal lords, "free land", and later the Northwoods pirated in the Sea of Ice.

According to the Guide: the climate is milder than in the Siberian taiga.
It looks like (observation): Russian North and Siberia. This is indicated by the taiga, the bathhouse, the Bylins and free settlers, reminiscent of the Cossacks. Piracy and shipping add to the similarities with Viking-era Scandinavia.

### Shayland
[[shejland-zemlya|Shayland]] is a foggy land along the banks of Torrey. It is the "mainstay of urban culture", and [[uejmar|Waymar]] is the financial center of the North. In Shayland there are many pubs and the best in Polari brewers, there is a saying: “Only rich and fools rest at home.” Men and women are more equal here than anywhere else in the empire.

According to the Guide: "such a Polarian Albion", that is, England: moros and "500 shades of gray".
Looks like (observation): England with its pubs and the City. The urban banking culture and women in crafts and finance also resemble the Netherlands. Waymar's name sounds like Waymar, but Shayland has no other German traits.

## Center and East
### Crownlands: Faunterra, Arden, Marengo
According to the Guide, the Crown is the first land in terms of GDP. "Feodals are weak, very much officialdom", here two universities, theaters and conservatories, and in the technique of the Crown is ahead of the outskirts "for a couple of generations". The Palace of the Lords stands on the Palace Island in the middle of the Hanai River in [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. The ancient Arden is considered the ancestral domain of the emperors, but they are only buried there, in the temple of Farewell. [[marengo|Marengo]] serves as their summer residence by the sea.

It looks like (observation): France of the Old Order. This is a centralized court and a strong bureaucracy, and Faunterra is similar to Paris, where the royal palace also stood on the island of Cité in the middle of the Seine. Arden, the tomb city, resembles Saint-Denis with the tombs of French kings. The names sound familiar: Arden resembles the Ardennes, and Marengo is an Italian village known for the battle of Napoleon.

### Southern Way
The [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] is a rain fertile duchy. It has the largest merchant fleet in the Empire, around 1200 ships, and a near monopoly on the route from the south to the North. “Respect is financial success.” Peasants say “Dogs bite, cows graze”, and “Guide” adds: “The saying “my house is narrow” would also be appropriate, if the travelers knew it.” Erwin observes of the travel nobles that they are "too attentive to money, let them talk funny, pulling up the endings of words."

Similar to (observation): Netherlands or Hanseatic lands. Similarity in merchant fleet, rich merchantry and rainy fertile plains. The saying “my house is narrow” and the glory of the best cuisine and the most caring mothers are referred to the Ukrainian village, but this is a joke of the “Guide”, not a prototype.

### Almera
[[almera-zemlya|Almera]] is the "Red Land" of red brick and tile. The climate here is warm, dry and windy. The dukes raised the industry with loans and tax breaks, but the land "fully retained an aristocratic feudal spirit." Three of the five schools of painting come from here. Adrian said of [[aleridan|Aleridan]], "I want the whole Empire to resemble this city."

Similar to (observation): Northern Italy of the Renaissance, where banks, workshops and schools of painting coexisted with the nobility. The name sounds like Spanish Almeria, and the dry windy climate resembles Castile.

### Hope
[[nadezhda|Hope]] is the land of steppes and deserts. She lives in gold mines, survived her “gold fevers”, and luck there is considered a character trait, like courage. The duel "to sweep the black bones" decides who will shoot from the crossbow. Art praises the loneliness and “the futility of man in comparison with the greatness of the universe.” The impregnable Heart of Light once fell after a year of siege by the horde.

It looks like (observation): the Wild West and the California gold rush. Similarity in prospectors, gambling and duels in empty spaces. Dryness and steppes also resemble Australia.

## South
### Litland
[[litlend|Litland]] is a rainforest and jungle in the Holliwell Delta with monsoon rains. He takes out spices, dyes and horses, and buys grain, metal and weapons. The Litlanders are “the most cheerful people of the Empire.” They do not restrain feelings and look freely at marriage.

According to the Travel Guide: "If Reggae had existed in the Polari Empire, it would certainly have originated in Litland."
Similar to (observation): Caribbean islands, Jamaica or Brazil with their music, bright colors and free morals. Spices and dyes for export resemble India or the Spice Islands.

### Shimmery
[[shimmeri|Shimmery]] is a tropical mountain kingdom. It was later settled by colonists who mixed with local tribes. From here, tea, coffee, spices and precious stones are exported, 90% of the deposits of spark "eyes" lie here. The King takes the vassal oath and pays the tax, and the laws are passed by the Council of Five. [[lajem|Laem]] boasts tea houses, poets, and an althess market. In the story “[[nebesnyj-korabl-i-devushka|The Skyship and the Girl]]” is called “the famous planter Obessin-Limar, the father of six”, and among the merchants there is Diaz-Ramiro.

Looks like (observation): Ceylon, which also has mountains, tea, spices and gems. Colonial Latin America resembles planters, a mixture of colonists with local and Spanish sounding names. The merchant republics of Italy are reminiscent of the board of the council of nobles.

## The West
### Darkwater
[[darkvoter|Darkwater]] is a “strange supplier.” These are swamps where people live on “plants”, islands of land among the topi. Witches and wizards are here. The wooden Olbia stands on one hundred and five islets, and it was nicknamed the Wood City. Swamps have fought guerrilla warfare for centuries. The royal title of the land was given by the Treaty of the XIV century.

Looks like (observation): Louisiana bayou, where swamps and voodoo converging, and names like Nan Clair and Mireille sound French. The Polesie resembles a swamp guerrilla war. Olbia bears the name of an ancient Greek colony in the Black Sea region.

### Flight and Chavans
The flight is a dry windy steppe. ([[rejs|Reis (County)]]) The land is 90% livestock, every third rider of the empire rides a horse, and the slave trade flourishes here. Communities are ruled by a gauntlet that "points the way." The main value is freedom, and breaking a person’s legs is almost as hard as killing. [[shavany|Shavans]] revere nature spirits.

According to the Guide: the chapter on the Flight opens with Brodsky's line "...you are welcome from one of the five continents holding on to cowboys", that is, the Flight is directly placed next to the American West.
It looks like (observation): Great steppe with nomadic communities, horse archer and cult of spirits. Cowboys resemble herders-warriors and herds for export, and pampas are Argentine gauchos.

### Hollywell
Holliwell is a frontier of farmers from Almera and nomads from Reis. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) Here they worship water and the Drop-God. The fertile east is protected by "land belts": "valves with palisades and moats, low, but very long."

Looks like (observation): Wild field on the border of Russia and steppe. Earth belts repeat the Serpent shafts and serrated features with which the settled lands were closed from nomads.

### The Mills
[[melnitsy|The mills]] grew on the ruins of the Iron Sun Empire. In the XI century there were 80 thousand free and about 700 thousand slaves, until the uprising of her slaves did not dare. Today, the Mills are ruled by a council of barons and burgomasters, and the heart of the land is the seaside Fairies, Minnis and Offer. Their sailors sailed west in the 14th century, hoping to circumnavigate the world and "come to Faunterra". The Fairis Seafaring Academy is one of the best in the empire, and above the hills are the Three Mother Mills.

Similar to (observation): The Netherlands and Portugal with their burgomasters, mills, seafaring school and westward voyages like Columbus. The Iron Sun Empire with a handful of lords over a mass of slaves resembles Sparta and its helots.

### Sunset Coast
The [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] was founded by slaves who fled north after the fall of the Iron Sun. According to the "Guide", no one takes care of secrecy "as much as the inhabitants of the Sunset Coast." The sunsets are close to the Shavans. ([[zakatniki|Sunsetters]])

Looks like (observation): a community of runaway slaves, such as Jamaican Maroons, or the Cossacks, who also grew up from fugitives. There is too little information about this land to call an analogue more confident.

## Outside the Empire
### Folta
Volta is an island republic of several city-states. ([[folta|Folta]]) Its fleet and trade bring it wealth, and as early as the tenth century it maintained trading posts along every coast. The Senate there is elected by paid vote, the truth is sought in public disputes, and there is no hereditary nobility.

According to the Guide: similar to ancient Athens.
Similar to (observation): Venice and Genoa, maritime republics with factories. The custom of keeping two captains on the ship, a formal and a rehman, reminds the Roman consuls.

### the Beyond
Beyond lies the Crystal Mountains. It is a huge cold continent, and "For centuries the land east of the Crystal Mountains is known as a deserted wilderness." Erwin's expedition went through the two-hundred-mile swamps of the Soft Fields. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]])

It looks like (observation): Alaska or northeastern Siberia, huge cold lands behind mountains and isthmus, where only rare expeditions went.
