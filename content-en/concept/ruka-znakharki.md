## The Healer

In [[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]], the Puppeteer suggested to Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Origin]] that her husband [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] be cured of a [[kamennaya-khvor|stone sickness]] in exchange for a talking item that [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] had mined in the Beyond (the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]]). Sophia agreed, and the Puppeteer sent to the palace his servant - Znaharka. ([[znakharka|The Healer]]) At her gate, dressed as a beggar, were met by guineas [[verner|Werner]], Ragolph and Tenn. On each finger she had a thimble, and between them a red web; pulling her fingers, she tore Tenn's blade from the cross, and cut off the glimmer, hot with touch.

> “Don’t fight, boys, or I’ll tear him off, but I’ll feed you.”

The noblewoman behaved uninhibitedly, called the kairs "boys", and the old duke - "old man". When Jemis told her to stop her, she spread her fingers — the web opened with a scarlet flower — and created a shimmering spot in the air the size of a fist shield, transparent, but with a pink tint; Werner was sure the blade wouldn't cut through. Desmond stopped the skirmish, giving the Lord's word that the Infirmary would receive the Item and see Sophia after healing. Then the thimbles and a web of red steel lettuce, Znaharka put her palm on Desmond's forehead and quietly sang with larynx torn sounds; the glow from the thimbles slipped onto the Duke's face. She announced that she would come twice more and only for the third time would My Lord "rejuvenate".

Erwin and Desmond considered the visit a bait: Znaharka was supposed to lure out and kill the Origins. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], seeing the improvement of her father, doubted and decided to bring the Sign to him. The meeting in the summer theater of the palace broke down: Iona deliberately cut her palm and received healing from the Znaharka for the diamond; [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] demanded to restore her beauty (“If I knew how to return beauty, do you think it would be so?!” replied Znaharka, and then promised that she could, but the price would be high). But then there were footsteps, and Znaharka rushed to run. The Kairs chased her down a dark alley; at the tents she ran upon a tall warrior and fell, and Werner's sword loomed over her. As a result, Znaharka died, one kair was killed, the other lost his leg, and Erwin had a conflict with the people of Northwood. Her corpse, along with the Item, went to [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]], and, according to Erwin, he bought the Item at the cost of the position. Erwin and Iona were convinced of only one thing: The object really heals wounds, which means that the Puppeteer has not only a weapon, but also a means of healing.

## Attempts of the Origins

The item had to be removed from the deceased, cutting off the strips of skin: so tightly the thimbles sat on the fingers. Robert offered to sell the Hand, which was not part of the Origins' estate, but Erwin stated, "We'll learn to talk to the Subject." The first test was [[gvenda|Gwenda]]: the thimbles lit up, but the wound did not heal, even when Erwin, imitating the past case, told Robert to threaten Jemis with the sword. Robert and Jemis did not achieve anything, the Item did not even put it on his hand; Lady Sophia prayed and lit it with incense - without any use. Erwin's thimbles froze and matched in size to his fingers, but nothing else happened. The subject did not speak on Iona. With a thick glow, as on Gwenda, the Object flashed on the fingers of the mentally ill mechanic Luis, who was a prisoner at the brigade, - Iona herself put on his thimbles, - but the healing power did not pour into Luis's head. ([[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]])

Erwin told [[minerva|Minerva]] about this: the words, thoughts, gestures of the Infirmary do not matter, and the Object “comes to life” a little only in the chosen ones - Louis, Gwenda, Erwin himself. From this, he deduced that the “body fluids” of the brigade soldiers could get into the bodies of these people, and suggested a special blood from Minerva. So the Hand of the Znaharki became the first thread to the solution of the [[pervokrov|firstblood]].

## Minerva and Navi

In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], Robert Origin, on behalf of Erwin, returned the bag to Minerva, from which the thimbles fell out, fastened with thin shiny threads. Minerva took the healing object as a slap in the face — a hint of a mutilated secretary [[itan|Ethan]]. Later, she entrusted the Subject to arrester Inge Price, promising freedom if he drank the blood of Znaharka from the bubble and cured a good man. ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) Inge somehow put on thimbles - one squirted from the little finger - but the Subject remained "deaf and dumb".

[[navi|Navi]] knew this Item from the registers as a “scalpel”, that is, the Healer’s Hand, and offered to show how to work with it. He warned that without control of the scalpel and accurate knowledge of anatomy, the Object would only harm; Znaharka’s hand is a “surgeon’s tool,” and an inept person will kill more people than he can cure. Navi's attempt to help Ethan failed, and he was hard at it. Navi considered the scalpel to be one of the four autonomous Objects then in Faunterra.

In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], Navi treated the wounded in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] with the Object: on the kair leg, black from gangrene, he retained a bone and thirty percent of the living muscle, and “grown” the rest by stroking the cleaned flesh with his cuffs. He hated surgery, but he treated each one for a quarter of an hour, fifty people a day.

## Iona the Healer

In the battle for the First Winter by the Hand of the Infirmary, Iona was treated, who had firstblood in her veins. Together with the Subject, she got a note compiled by Dorian Ember's assistant from the lessons of Navi: drawings of wounds, finger positions and keywords on the contrary (for example, "molerep" - "fracture" on the contrary). On this note, having no experience, Iona operated on a half-dead Erwin: the doctor said that with such a wound, the brother would last only until midnight. Having drunk for courage, Iona stated that she had “killed more than a hundred people” and now she had to save two hundred people; she told differently about her patients: she managed to heal eight severe wounds and lost three. The Origins understood the risk: the deconstructor could destroy the Hand, but hoped that the Puppeteer would first blow up the Feathers and Iona would have time to remove the Object.

On December 3, 1775, Vittor dictated to [[adrian|Adrian]] the numbers of Objects vulnerable to deconstruction; the Fingers were followed by the Hand of the Infirmary, the Light Sphere, and the Voice of God. When Magda demanded peace with Origin, Adrian ordered Juhan Reis to destroy the Hand of the Znaharka. ([[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]]) Magda realized that after this, peace with the North would become impossible, because the Object was worn by Iona herself, and rushed to Johan. But the Shavan died at the same moment from the poison of the clock action, and the order was not carried out.

## In "Fantasies"

In the story "Closer - Further", one of the "Fantasies", which the author calls possible options for the future, the Hand of the Znaharka remains with Iona. In the summer of 1776, returning from a penitential journey, Iona received the sick at the Mercy Clinic in the First Winter, founded by Lady Sophia. She treated thirty-two patients a day: The object attached to the body confirmed the diagnosis, and then Iona healed the consumption in minutes or removed the tumor in the evening. The queue with numbers exceeded three hundred, and around the clinic there was a trade in places.

Minerva decided that the Hand's energy supply is large, but limited and takes more to treat than to diagnose. Therefore, she ordered Iona only to make diagnoses so that the doctors led by brother Marius would learn to treat without the Subject. To diagnose, according to her, could and Minerva, which also has primrose. Iona considered herself unnecessary and left, and then secretly, at night, treated those who waited in the “red” queue, which disrupted the experiments of the doctors. To the threat of taking the Item, she replied that the Hand was "An Object of the Great House Origin received in battle as a trophy" and vowed not to touch it again if it was taken away. Erwin reminded her sister of Navi’s words that the energy of the Objects might run out, and Iona admitted that she knew it. Minerva did not take the Hand, the patients ceased to disappear from the wards, but the “ghost” continued to heal the patients at night in the surrounding hotels, and the townspeople decided that the hand of Iona was directed by Agatha.

According to Erwin's plan, a fresco was painted on the first left pylon of the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant, in which Iona touches the wound of the bloody lord with the Hand of the Ink in the background of the burning First Winter and the queue of wounded kairs. Mother Alicia sanctified her.

## A similar item is the puppeteer

In Humans and Gods, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] treated the wounded in the infirmary with his healing Object — a few rings put on his fingers and tied threads, "like a web." The subject was able to do everything that medicine can: spliced tissues, cut better than a scalpel, burned ulcers, glued bones. But Vittor knew little of anatomy and trembled with disgust, and there was more harm than good. Whether this is the same type of Object as the Hand of the Instructor is not specified.
