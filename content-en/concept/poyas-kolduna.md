The **Sorcerer's Belt** is a [[svyashchennye-predmety|sacred object]] in the form of a belt with multicolored plaques. He gave the name and glory to the Sorcerer, the robber of the banks from the “[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]”, and in the “Shadow of the Great Tree” the same belt was defended by [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]]. ([[mik|Mik]]; [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

## Properties
The belt, like any Sacred Object, is obeyed only by the bearer of the firstblood (see the firstblood). ([[pervokrov|Firstblood]]) According to the scenes in "A Doll on the Throne" and "The Shadow of the Great Tree", it is clear that he knew the following.
- **The Invisible Shield.** Bolt, launched into the sorcerer, “broken the invisible shield”, the other flew away from the open palm. Richard had a dozen bolts stuck into his chest, and the blades of kire "lost power." In a duel with Erwin, Winter's Voice, a foot from Richard's neck, "lost speed and swam like a speck in the standing air." ([[glas-zimy-mech|Voice of Winter (Sword)]]) The shield was on by the will of the host: “He’s on, you idiot,” Richard threw to his brother.
- **Sparks.** The wizard removed the sparks from the belt with his fingers. A pack of red sparks, thrown in the face of the guard, knocked him down. The blue threads, which stretched from the fingers to the head of the jailer, charred it with an "invisible flame" without fire, heat and smoke. The blue spark between the thumb and the index finger was shown as a threat. After swiping his hand around the belt and putting his hand to the castle, he let a spark through the iron and broke the door. Touching his belt, he left a green glitter on his fingers.
- **Weakness.** The belt stopped the iron, but bare hands with ropes penetrated the barrier. He did not cover the horse: under Richard, the horse was killed with a crossbow volley. Against the spark blow of the Voice of Winter, which came on the blade, Richard's shield also did not help.

## History
### Magician
In A Doll on the Throne, Waymar orphan Mick, who received his firstblood from Nathaniel, referred to himself as the Wizard. In January 1775, he rescued Neymir and Charu from the Shimmerian captivity, and then, with the gentry of Biray, robbed the banks of the Earl of Shayland in the free cities. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]; [[chara|Chara]]; [[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]) The Shavans believed that he drank the souls of the slain, and the hands of the sorcerer often lay on a "witch belt strewn with colored plaques."

At the end of March 1775, during a raid on a bank in Laxette, the mysterious Novichok deliberately put the squad in danger in order to force the Sorcerer to remove his pants along with his belt. Then, with the blow of a boot, he stunned the Sorcerer and pulled the belt out of his pants.
> “He does not drink souls and does not possess magic! This is all a game... He's just lucky to steal a talking item somewhere. All his magic is just a belt.”

According to Novichok, the value of the Witcher is that he knows who took the Item, “and those people have more.” The newcomer, who was nicknamed the Hunter by the Shavans, turned out to be Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]]. When questioned, the sorcerer first assured: “I stole a belt from a lord ... I don’t know what to call. I met him in a hotel in Farway...” but then I gave out the “Second of the Five” - Count [[kuindar|Quindar]] from the secret order.

### Gift to Queen Madeline
In the “Shadow of the Great Tree”, Adrian sent a casket with Harmon and his ambassadors, officially as a gift to the Empress Minerva. ([[kharmon|Harmon Paula Roger]]) Fearing trickery, the merchant opened it on the road and found there two Light Spheres, genuine and fake, "a belt with many multicolored pockets" and Adrian's letter to Queen Madeline, Lady in Darkness. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) The Emperor wrote:
> “I send gifts as proof of my friendship. Here is your lost Light Sphere and its copy, as well as the Sorcerer’s Belt, seized from the provost who betrayed you.”

Judging by the further, these Objects were with [[minerva|Minerva]]: in the autumn of 1775 [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] brought to the First Winter the treasury and the Sacred Objects, and Navi, hiding from them in the dungeon, severely punished the mistress: "Keep away from him brought the Light Sphere! Get away from the castle! The safety belt, too.”

### Richard's Belt
On December 2, 1775, in the Battle of the First Winter, Richard climbed the northern rampart with Wilhelm's Perst, and the kair bolts did not take him. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Someone shouted that the ice was invulnerable, but Adrian, watching the fight, calmly explained:
> “He just has a protective belt. It was the same with the magician.”

Later, the Xes, dressed in "Belomorye" cairns, knocked Richard off his horse, threw an arcana at him and tied him up. The narrator in this scene calls it the Belt of the Sorcerer. The monks recaptured Richard, and later, in a final duel with [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], stopped the shield with the deadly blow of the Voice of Winter. Erwin moved the brace on the hilt, and the spark discharge on the blade knocked out his brother. Erwin then told [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] to remove the Belt and Perst from Richard: “The object obeyed the firstblood in her veins.”
