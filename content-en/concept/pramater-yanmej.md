Janmei the Merciful is one of the Holy Mothers who came to the underworld at the [[soshestvie|Descent]]. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) In the canonical list of Scripture, she stands second, immediately after Miriam Dark-eyed: "Dark-eyed Miriam, followed by Yanmei Merciful, then the Great Sophia, Agatha the Radiant ...". From it leads the family of the Brilliant Dynasty of the Emperors Polari. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) Her "Diaries" in the empire are read as the main instruction about power, and her name is constantly heard in prayers, oaths and health. For [[adrian|Adrian]] and [[minerva|Minerva]], who were proud of their resemblance to the Mother, she served as a living model of the ruler.

## Nickname "Merciful"
The origin of the nickname was remembered vaguely by the people. [[kharmon|Harmon]], who was dying of starvation in prison, prayed to Yangmei and called her the Merciful, the Deliverer and the Deliverer of Salvation, and then he remembered the legend. St. Ulyana, the seventeenth Pramary, who had no descendants, collided with a fan in the forest. The beast crushed the warriors and pierced Ulyana with a horn. The wound was so terrible that even the Sacred Objects were powerless. Ulyana begged to save her from suffering, but no one dared to raise his hand to the messenger of the gods. One Yangmei, a “fragile girl”, decided, who drove the style into Ulyana’s heart. Harmon understood Pramateri's answer in his own way: the best he could hope for was a quick death.

Later, Adrian, showing the guests the Air Bridge, noted the irony of this story. A month earlier, Yangmei saved more than a hundred people from the avalanche in the Crystal Mountains, but did not become either the Savior or the Creator. She was called merciful only after she killed a single girl. The same episode recalled [[ded|Grandfather]] arguing with the Raven about immortality: Yanmei killed Ulyana to save her from suffering, and "if she could save her from death, she would save her"; therefore, the Object that gives immortality is not in the Scripture. ([[mark-voron|Mark]])

## Ice Bridge
The most famous miracle of Yangmei is the bridge across the abyss in the Crystal Mountains. To escape the avalanche, she erected it out of ice and pressed snow. A bridge half the flight length of an arrow crossed the gorge so deep that it was impossible to look down without dizziness. According to the story heard by the guests of Adrian, the Mother built it in fifteen minutes with one hand, on which the Gauntlet of Power was worn. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]) In the Crystal Mountains, pilgrims are shown a stream over which "Yanmei the Merciful built a bridge with one stroke of her hand." "The Yankees love bridges - the glory of the Pramati family does not give them peace": this story Adrian connected with the Air Bridge, which leads from the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] to the Holy See. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]])

The Yangmei Bridge was an example for Minerva. In December 1774, while defending Faunterra, she took the Glove out of the casket with the words:
> “Yanmei the Merciful has made of ice a bridge across the abyss, than saved from the avalanche of our Forefathers. Look: there is a lot of ice on the river. And I'm Yangmei, and I need a bridge."

The glove sat on her hand perfectly. Later, arguing with the printers, Minerva claimed that Pramatera built the bridge "in five minutes", in this her words diverge from that story. The glove does not formally belong to the Dynasty and is kept in its own box in the Church of Mercy.

## Acts
Kanon remembers Yangmei as the closest adviser to King Wilhelm the Great. The story of the "Agatha dilemma" reports that in 36 AD the defense of Faunterra was commanded by Wilhelm and Janmei, and the defense of Orwell was commanded by the Forefather Maximian and Agatha. ([[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]]) Agatha learned of the nomad maneuver, but did not warn Faunterra, and the capital was taken by surprise (see [[soshestvie|Descent]]).

According to the story of David’s father, in 69 from the Descent, four Forebears, who called themselves Gardeners, offered Wilhelm and Yangmei a different path of development. ([[otets-david|Father David]]) “The King and the Counselor” refused. Kaisers Wilhelm destroyed the books of the Gardeners, and Wilhelm and Janmei issued a law: the distributor of heresy from commoners was burned at the stake, and the original was expelled to the western steppes (see Gardeners). ([[sadovniki|Gardeners]]) Erwin later called the Gardeners those who "hide from Yangmei's anger." Yangmei, on the other hand, banned the scientific work of Forefather Evrian on space and time and on the interaction of Sacred Objects with them. Gardeners restored it in the 18th century; it describes a "deconstructor" device capable of removing the Object from the sublunar world.

Agatha once asked Yangmei for a Gauntlet of Power to quickly pave the way. A note written in secret pollen was read by Yangmei's student Gavin, and the pollen forced him to retrieve the Glove from the vault at any cost. From this case, the nature of the Subject, called Ulyanina Dust, was revealed. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]])

## “Diaries” and Teaching
The Yangmei Diaries is Pramateri's main work. There are several volumes in them: Adrian quoted Mark "the third volume, chapter five", and in Deda's anecdote, the young mother reads to the infant "four volumes of Yangmei diaries". ([[ded|Grandfather]]) There are also "late diaries". In Elena’s boarding house, Janmei’s Diaries are among the student books along with Wilhelm’s Notes on the Nature of Power and Agatha’s Moments. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) Minerva absorbed works on the theory of power, “like good wine,” and selected her assistants, forcing everyone to retell an excerpt from the diaries in their own words. The phrase “right as Yangmei’s diaries” entered the proverb.

Yangmei's teachings boil down to practical advice to the ruler:
- Adrian quoted in The Diaries: "We choose neither friends nor enemies. Enemies are always strong and cunning, friends are complacent and blind.
- Yangmei listed eight enemies of the ruler. The first and most imperceptible she called idleness: the lord can engage in routine and fun and “do nothing”.
- She wrote that a properly organized state apparatus tolerates between one and two-thirds of fools among officials, if they are not entrusted with tasks beyond the "habitual set of tricks."
- She wrote that the ruler, as a businessman, invests his “additional value” in the state, and, following Pramatera, Eleanor called the five steps of a great undertaking. Under this scheme, Minerva performed a training assignment: determined the "additional value" of each emperor of the Dynasty.
- She argued with Wilhelm, who considered fear a reliable tool of power, and argued that the ruler should not forget about the second feeling - love.
- She is also credited with household commandments: “Train your body and fill it with strength,” “Do not promise what you will not give,” “You can not judge a person by one act, either good or bad.”
- Minerva recalled her other words: “Politics is like medicine: a small and rapid pain can be avoided by a long and severe pain,” “Like a red thread, a ruler must see the path to his goal,” “The organ that we do not notice works perfectly ... If the Ministry requires personal intervention of the Lord, it would be nice to replace the Minister.

The first covenant of Yangmei eventually became the prayer and hymn of the Shining Dynasty. It was transmitted by “wave” by the Duke of Farway at the opening of the line to the Heart of Light:
> “Let there be glory in that wisdom which respects gods and men. May the power of wisdom be glorified. May the mercy that is inherent in strength be glorified...

Harmon remembered her other maxim:
> “Men serve gods, but not gods men. Do what you have to do, work hard, hope for your strength.”

Yangmei's teachings, according to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", and gave the name of the Dynasty: "The Yangmei Dynasty" indicates "not so much blood, but the principles of governance of the country, set forth by the Mother".

## Honoring
Yangmei is revered throughout the empire, especially in the [[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]. She is considered the patroness of flowers, and a visit to the spring flower festival in the garden of Lumini is a tribute to Pramateri. Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark is outraged by the following fame: Yanmei is a politician, an engineer and the coolest of the Foremothers, and Agatha the Radiant jokingly called her a "highlight", that is, dried grapes. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) According to Karen, the garden is connected to Yangmei in a different way. The first house on the hill gave a crack, and Yangmei stood up for the mason: loose soil was to blame. She advised not to build on the hill, but to plant a garden so that the roots of the trees would hold the ground together. In his late diaries, Yangmei admits that he has only been in this garden twice in his entire life. The cities ask the Crown for permission to build temples of Janmei the Merciful: for example, Adrian in an open audience allocated funds for the temple in Lowville. The colors of Pramateri are gold on the lazuli, and these are the colors of the Dynasty. The troops of the Crown are called "Yanmei Mercy Forces", one of the regiments was named "Fearless Regiment of Yangmei Mercy". Before the coronation, Minerva had to learn the ceremonial prayer of the Dynasty “Your Hand on My Shoulder” of thirty-two stanzas: it is read in singing, according to the church canon. The oaths “in the name of Yangmei the Merciful” and the cry “Glory to Yangmei the Merciful!” are heard constantly.

People’s attitude towards her is more restrained. The commoners went for help to Gloria the Intercessor, believing that "the arrogant Agatha and the heartless Yangmei" do not care about the common people. There were also freethinkers: the bishop reproached Dom Farway that the Duke's grandson at the table called Janmay "not a messenger of the gods, but just an experienced politician."

## Descendants
The Yangmei family is the second most prominent after the Miriam family. Emperors usually take wives of the Yangmei family. Descendants call themselves “grandchildren” and “grandchildren” of Yangmei: Minerva checked their actions with the “strict code of Yanmei’s granddaughter”. Features of the genus are slanted eyes, dimples on the cheeks, a wide face and a stubborn chin. The Mother herself, judging by the remark about the portrait of Juliana the Great, was low, like Minerva. For more information about the descendants, see Yangmei Dynasty and the Originals. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]; [[pervorodnye|Firstborn]])

## The Truth About Yangmei
In Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] explained that the Forebears were the crew of a colonial starship that crashed eighteen centuries ago. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]) The crew argued: the colonists wanted to burn the reactors for the sake of a distress signal, and the first navigator Elena and astrophysicist Agatha proposed to save them and populate the planet.
> "Wilhelm and Janmay - captain and chief engineer of starship - made the decision".

The rebellions of the colonists were suppressed by force, and bioterraforming began. According to Mireille Nan-Claire, the reactors were too heavy for the Yangmei Glove, and they had to be carried through the mainland in the usual way. Minerva admitted that Yangmei "indicated" this in later diaries.

## The Author's Article
In 2018, an article about Yanmei, signed as an article by Roman Surzhikov, appeared on the Polaripedia — a cycle wiki on Fandom. ([Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%AF%D0%BD%D0%BC%D1%8D%D0%B9_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%BE%D1%81%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B4%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%8F_%28%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D1%8F_%D0%A0%D0%BE%D0%BC%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B0_%D0%A1%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B6%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%29)) It is written before the last books of the cycle and serves Yangmei as it is seen by Polari historians; it does not take Navi's clue into account. Here’s what she adds to the novel.

- **Life.** Yangmei descended into the sublunar world at thirty-eight years old and departed in 76 from the Descent, at one hundred and fourteen - early by the standards of the Forebears. Of the nine fathers, she bore thirteen children, six of them daughters. One daughter died as a child from the blue plague, the other gave birth only to sons, so that only four continued the birth through the female line. ([[sizyj-mor|Blue-Grey Plague]]) Hence the small number of the Yangmei family: Susan had nine fertile daughters, Sophia had eleven.
- **Builder and ruler.** Yangmei spent most of her life in [[faunterra|Faunterra]], which she considered her favorite creation. She participated in the laying of Orwell and Hampton, built fortifications and the first bridges over Hanai, was the first mayor of Faunterra and the first adviser to King Wilhelm. She did not pretend to the crown: she understood that she would not combine the board with engineering, teaching and raising children.
- **Textbooks.** Yangmei has written textbooks on architecture, theoretical mechanics, engineering, and control theory. They, according to the article, are still used by academies and universities; only the treatise “On the Properties of Materials” has been lost - it burned down in the fire of 130.
- **"Diaries".** At the age of eighty, after forty-two years in Polaris, Yangmei counted about two thousand six hundred pages of the diary and was ashamed of many words. In two years, she reworked the entries in Early Diaries — a chronicle of the Descent and the first ten years — and Late Diaries about how to manage people, with commentary and advice. Some of the books were not included. After that, she no longer kept a diary, and the last thirty years of her life are known from the words of other chroniclers. In instructing the princes, the monarchs of the Dynasty say: “Do as Yangmei wrote, and you will definitely not make a big mistake.”
- **Appearance and temper.** A short dark-haired woman with slanted eyes and dimples on her cheeks, "looking like a Korean woman." Smart, pragmatic, powerful and determined, but self-critical and not devoid of humor; she herself recognized the chatteriness and propensity for pathos. She deliberately turned disasters into teachings for the subjects, only telling them: “The lower ruler does it himself. The average ruler tells people what to do. The supreme ruler watches as his subjects do what is necessary.”
- **Mercy.** The article emphasizes what Adrian: Yanmei was not nicknamed the Builder or the Chronicle, but the Merciful - for having killed the mortally wounded Ulyana. ([[pramater-ulyana|Ulyana the Sorrowful]]) Thus, she became the only person in history who killed the Mother. She later wrote: “Having a good heart is a shameless luxury. A rare ruler is rich enough for her."
- **Wilhelm.** With Wilhelm, she had a friendship, but not love: they had no children, and [[pramater-miriam|Miriam]] gave birth to the first heir to the king. Canon explains this by the plan of the Forebears: the descendants of Yangmei were to come to power later, when the state will grow and their ability to govern will become more valuable than charisma.
- **Miriam and the Scarlet Trouble.** The church interprets Miriam and Yangmei's rivalry as a difference of styles, not a struggle for power. But the descendants of Yangmei envied the Miriams, who held the throne for the entire first millennium. By the end of the millennium, the empire had grown so much that organizational talent became more important than charm, and the Yanmei counties of Marengo and Flame flourished. In the twelfth century, during the Crimson Turmoil, the Yanmei Counts of Marengo overthrew the Third Dark-Eyed Dynasty, and the first emperor of the lineage of Yanmei ascended the throne.
- **Agatha.** Yangmei and [[pramater-agata|Agatha]] were friends and did not compete, but differed in views. Yangmei relied on calculation and discipline, Agatha on knowledge and what lies beyond calculation. From this grew two military doctrines: the Yanmei defeats the training and control of the troops (as the troops of Julian the Great fought), the Agatov - awareness and foresight of the commander (the Carriers of Light, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] and Erwin Origina). ([[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]; [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]])

## In "Fantasies"
In [[fantazii|Fantasies]], stories about the possible fate of the heroes in 1776, the name Yangmei accompanies the struggle for the crown.
- [[adrian|Adrian]], checking Professor [[nikolas-olli|Ollie]]'s recording device, said a prayer: "Oh, merciful Yangmei, I feel your hand on my shoulder..." He said that the invention would “save the Yangmei Dynasty” by allowing the ruler to speak to the people without preachers.
- [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] was serving an honorary hard labor: he compiled giant tables of how many stars each day each resident gives to the yard, the army, the training of engineers and doctors. Erwin, after checking the calculations, called them with laughter "proof that the Yangmei dynasty is a useless parasite."
- [[minerva|Minerva]] put granite blocks of viaduct under the First Winter every morning with the Yangmei Glove. Deciding who to put to this work instead of herself, she ironically thought that Yangmei “was just a naval officer,” and she herself is the empress. Bishop [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]], arriving at the construction site, flattered her dryly: “Yanmei the Merciful is proud to look at you.”
- While at peace with Iona, Minerva reasoned about the benefits the Yangmei Dynasty brings to the world: a spark built only by engineers trained at Faunterra and attempts to make medicine a science. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]])
- Yangmeyanka is also named Mireille Nan-Claire: Prince Hector welcomed her as the Queen of Darkwater, noting that "the greatness of the Yangmei traits cannot be hidden under a sea tan." ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]])
