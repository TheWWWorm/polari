**Sacred Objects** are the shrines of the Polari world, the products of the “incomprehensible art” of the gods. It is believed that the hand of a person is not able to create anything like this. Objects do not burn, do not break and are “outside the laws of the underworld”. According to the Scriptures, with their help, the [[pramateri|Foremothers]] healed the terminally ill, called for rain, turned air into water, and water into food, rose into the sky and subjugated nomadic tribes. The power of the Objects in the hands of the Forebears laid the foundation of the Empire.

## Gifts and their extraction

The general structure of the Gifts, their lodges and works on extraction are described in the article [[dary-bogov|Gifts of the Gods]]. The content of the box is called a gift; some products from it are Sacred Objects.

Objects come into the world with the **Gifts of the gods**. In history, at the beginning of the cycle, this happened seventeen times - less than once a century. A huge force breaks the earth's hardness, and a cave appears in the rift - the bed of the Gift, on the grottoes of which the Objects are scattered. The walls of the bed are covered with a hard, diamond-like crust, similar to glass and metal. The Church does not recognize its fragments and other imperfect finds as full-fledged Objects and calls them “small objects”. Extracting the Gift is always difficult and dangerous. The bed is filled with slag, incandescent, filled with poisonous gas or “shimmering” light, harmful to sinners. The most difficult, the Tenth Gift, fell to the bottom of the Smoky Dahl Lake: the then Duke of Almera drained part of the lake for several years. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) The most scarce Gift contained 74 Subjects, the richest 332. By law, one-third of the Gifts belong to the Crown.

The seventeenth Dar fell in July 1756 in [[dom-shejland|Shayland]], fifteen miles west of [[torrej|Torrey]]. The fall caused a forest fire; the count's army, peasants and townspeople held back the fire at the forest clearing until it rained, and only then did people see a column of mareva above the bed over the ashes. Sunsets coming from the west, where the fire was weaker, were the first to occupy the bed and cast off the tired Shaylands. The bed cooled very slowly, the walls were covered with slippery volcanic glass, and people were dying from falls, poisonous fumes, burns, collapses and deadly “Flying Objects”, capable of transmitting a particle of its destructive glow to the rock; on the tenth day of the heat, there was still enough to melt the axe that fell from above. Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent Shayland]] called the Allies, and [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] beat the lodge, and then returned the taken Items at the price of three burned Western cities. In this bed, the body of an unknown “goddess” girl was secretly found in a transparent coffin (see Womb). ([[chrevo|The Womb]]) [[paul|Paul]] came out of it. Later, the bed became a place of pilgrimage: the conductors showed the imprints of Objects in black glass - the first Object found in the form of a clover flower, two rings of the Light Sphere - and the levels deeper than the second remained closed. The eighteenth Dar, unaccounted for, found [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]'s [[ekspedicija|Explorada]] River in 1774. ([[reka|The River]]) The bed was already empty.

## Objects as property
The objects constitute the "property of the original families." Their number is considered a measure of the favor of the gods to the gens. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] described their price as follows: one Item is worth a small baronial flax, two is a ransom for a princess of the Great House, four is a sufficient reason for war, twenty give count weight in the House, fifty is a ducal. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) According to Baron Philip Lowfert, Great Houses are those which possess more than twenty Objects; there are thirteen of them, and only eight families have fifty or more. In the sanctuary of the Dynasty, according to the Earl of Shayland, more than three hundred Objects are stored; it is followed by Almera (one hundred twenty), Shimmeri (ninety-one) and Origina (eighty-seven), with the Duke of Labelin having about sixty. The throne Citadel in Faunterra, abandoned by the rulers two centuries ago, kept in the dungeons the property of the Dynasty - three hundred and twenty-four Objects. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) After capturing the capital in 1774, Erwin, according to Adrian, received “three hundred twenty beautiful but silent Objects.” The emperor sometimes praised the Items for service: so Lady Catherine Catherine received from Adrian a Cup of Joy - the Subject of the Sixth Gift of the Early Miriam dynasty, who at least four times "talked" to the owners; he drank from it, according to legend, learned the meaning of life. Buying a shrine is considered a sin before the Mothers and deception of people, so transactions are conducted secretly. The price of one Item is calculated by tens of thousands of Hilt, as in the history of the sale of the Light Sphere. ([[lzhe-sfera|Sale of the Radiant Sphere]]) “Silent” items are also stored in the cathedrals. According to [[otets-david|Father David]], two Objects in the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] heal dozens of people every year. Monasteries sometimes buy small items.

## "Speaking" Items

According to the definition of Lady [[karen|Karen]], the speaker is called the Object that at least once at the request of a person performed the action: the Wilhelm's Fingers, Wings of Miriam, Susan's Flower. That doesn't mean he'll answer any of the mortals now. The rotation of the Sphere in the hands of Harmon, she said, is not a conversation: Harmon only sees one of its properties.
Scripture teaches that only the forebears could speak with the Objects. The rest of the objects are “silent” or show only certain properties. Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] instructed the masters of the university to study the Subjects, which disturbed the Church. In 1774, it was discovered that the Objects had "spoken". [[paul|Paul]]'s detachment behind the River used the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]], a weapon that, according to Scripture, the Forefather Wilhelm drowned in the sea. Then the Rings burned [[pozhar-evergarda|Evergard]], and the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]] collected [[absolyut|the Absolute]] from many Objects.

As it turned out, for a "conversation" you need [[pervokrov|firstblood]]. The conversation itself is conducted by a strong-willed effort. For beginners, a simplified way serves: commands from ordinary words spoken backwards (for example, a fireball is called by the word "other" - "fire" on the contrary). Items as if alive: Joaquin notes that the Subject "does not like violence."

## Accounting and Census

Large items were entered into the imperial registers; small ones with only one supernatural property (like [[kulon-adriana|Adrian's pendant]] with its "miraculously vivid image") were not taken into account. In 1775, Minerva announced the Census of Objects. Analyzing its results, she and Erwin found that from the previous census thirty-nine items changed owners: twenty-seven went to famous lords for clear reasons (for example, a certain "Third Bird" was presented by Duke Farway to the Duke of Litland), and twelve were listed as "lost" - the owner had the right not to explain the reasons. These twelve came from twelve different Gifts, which led Erwin to the idea of the structure of [[absolyut|the Absolute]].

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collectible edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark explains the accounting in more detail. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) Objects with one divine property are called small: usually it is simply unearthly material without miraculous action. Great ones have two properties and more, and the property of the lords is considered to be on them. The Light Sphere is a big object: unknown to science material and rotation without friction. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) The all-seeing is also large, it has three properties: an unknown crystalline substance, color reflections with colorlessness, and the ability to see the skeleton through the flesh. The register, according to Karen, is specified by the census about once every quarter century. The possession is recorded by the fact: who presented the Subject to the scribe, he is the owner, without any papers. Therefore, if you do not declare the theft before the next census, the subject officially passes to the thief.

Items from the new Gift, according to the custom that leads Karen, are divided into three equal parts: the lord of that land, the lord and the Church, and the rest goes to a good cause by agreement of all three parties.

## Autonomous Items and “Wings”

According to [[navi|Navi]], nine out of ten items are not autonomous: these are the details of large mechanisms, “chastenka” as part of the Absolute, so the Puppeteer, having taken possession of the whole Gift and property of the Dynasty, has gained so limited benefit from them. Of the thirty-nine items that were in the fall of 1775 in Faunterra, only four worked independently: the Gauntlet of Power, the Hand of the Sorcerer ("scalpel"), the Womb and the Eternal Hilt, and of these only the Glove is safe to the inept owner. Each new subject in the hands of one of the parties, he said, twice complicated his calculations of the “tree of probabilities”.

## Energy Items

Navi explained that Objects store a mass-dependent supply of energy: the "hot annihilation" of even the lightest of them, the Gloves, would destroy Faunterra, and the heaviest, the Womb, would evaporate the Smokey Dahl. Objects are protected from such an explosion, and the deconstructor causes only "cold annihilation" - the transformation of a region of space into a vacuum. The Light Sphere shows precisely this "potential" of the Objects. During the storming of Waymar, Vittor's main gun wiped out the slopes of Castle Hill along with thousands of people; [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], not knowing the real name, called it the Sword of the Gods.

## Counterfeit
The secret order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] was able to make copies of Objects from material close to the divine. One such forgery is the false Light Sphere, sold to the Duke of Labelin.

This is what they add.

- **Dimensions.** Small objects are rarely larger than a child's palm, large usually the size of an instrument, convenient for an adult - a foot and a half. The smallest object is Ulyana Dust: a pinch of less than a gram is enough to manifest its property. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) The largest is the belly, the size of an elevator cabin. ([[chrevo|The Womb]]) Outwardly, objects are nothing like, except for one thing: their unusualness is always noticeable to the eye.
- **After the ancestors.** Some time after the departure of the Forebears, there were still people who were able to talk with the Objects, but after several generations they disappeared without passing on the secret.
- **Objects and Faith.** The Church teaches that the Objects are silent because people are still far from spiritual perfection; when humanity grows up to the Forebears, the Objects will speak, and while they are a stake in the future and an incentive for self-development. The teaching itself does not hold on to the Objects: remove them from the Scriptures, and it will remain whole, but miracles helped mortals to believe faster. Objects are revered more than the relics of saints, and even the silent treat and console with the power of faith, like a placebo. Thanks to them, the Church has never known serious rivals, and ancient animal cults almost everywhere gave way to the worship of Objects.
- **Taboo.** The use of force to harm people is prohibited. Eighteen centuries before the events of Arrow, Coin, Spark, the taboo was broken only once: the Forefather Wilhelm killed one person, the leader of the nomads (see Agatha’s dilemma). ([[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]])
- **Where the Gifts Fall.** The only pattern is an arc from Shimmery through Hope, Almera, and the Southern Way to the northeast, to the Origin and Beyond; the time of arrival is unpredictable. There were versions that the Smokey Dahl and the Bottomless Gap were giant lodges of uncured Gifts. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]; [[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) For Smoky Distance, this is unlikely: it was also mapped by Foremother Elena. It looks like a bed, and its age is unknown.
- **How many.** In total, there are more than eight thousand items in Polaris, of which a quarter is large. The Church of the Forefathers owns about 280 large objects, the Church of the Mothers about 250, the Dynasty about 320, each of the thirteen Great Houses from 20 to 120, all other owners together about 400.
- **How they go from hand to hand.** Gifts and rewards for service are considered decent, capture and sale are condemned by the Church. Therefore, the victorious House usually “mercifully” offers peace, and the defeated “from the heart” gives him Objects. The sale is disguised as a reward for fictitious service. The size of the property is not one hundred percent, but a fairly accurate measure of influence.
- **Titles.** In the XII-XIV centuries, the Brilliant Dynasty gave the title of count only to the house that owned more than twenty Objects, and the Duke - more than fifty; other nobles - no higher than the Marquis. The rule was abolished in the fifteenth century, but most present-day counts and dukes still meet its requirements.
- **Catalogues.** Catalogues of Objects are approved by the Imperial Chancellery and updated approximately once every forty years; the next update is scheduled for 1780 - a "blessing number" with the sum of the digits sixteen. Therefore, [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] and Hobart-Synthal rush to build up the property. ([[gobart-sintal|Gobart-Sintal]]) (In the novels, Karen speaks of a census about once every quarter century, and Minerva declared a census as early as 1775.) An educated nobleman should know "in person" from one hundred to two hundred Objects - at least a dozen of the most prominent of the possessions of each Great House.
- **The shimmering objects.** In the dark, they emit a dull bluish glow. These are the only items that are dangerous in themselves for the wearer; the article admits that they emit radiation unknown to Polarian science.

## The True Nature
In Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] explained that the Gifts of the Gods are the crash sites of starships shot down by a false starlight beacon, and the Sacred Objects are the surviving details of these ships. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]) They are made of a very durable material that stores energy. According to his calculations, about one hundred and ten ships fell to the planet, but only eighteen crash sites were found on Polaris.

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, the Church decreed that everyone who fought with the Objects must repent and accept penance. In the summer of 1776, Bishop Amessin interpreted Wilhelm's prohibition broadly, "by any Sacred Subject," and demanded repentance from Minerva for the Glove. Mother Alicia agreed that it was only a matter of personal responsibility, and the demand was dropped. Ganta Court, through Ambassador Neymir, offered Erwin a tribute of a thousand horses, three thousand cows, and two Sacred Objects. ([[nejmir|Neimir]]) Erwin accepted the tribute, but promised in return only three years not to go to war on the flight. In a dispute about the clinic, Erwin reminded Iona of Navi's words: Objects store energy in themselves, it may end, and someday the Hand of the Znaharka will cease to work. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]])
