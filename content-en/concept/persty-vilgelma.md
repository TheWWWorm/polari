## Tradition

According to church legend, the Forefather Wilhelm conquered the Western nomads by the power of the Persians and proclaimed himself the king of Polaris. When the horde broke into Faunterra, Wilhelm burned her leader with his horse with the Rings in anger. Then, in order to protect his descendants, he got rid of the Rings himself - before he died, he drowned them in the ocean - and said: "There is no worse offense for a mortal than taking life through the Subject." Since then, the use of the Objects as a weapon has been considered the greatest sin, and the "Taboo of Wilhelm" - the immutable law of the Church. Erwin, when he first saw the Rings in the Beyond, could not believe his eyes: “Father Wilhelm drowned them in the ocean! Mortals can't own the Rings!"

## Appearance and Action

In the first book, Erwin saw the sparkle commander lift a rounded pinkish “bun” similar to quartz and thrust his hand into it, “like a knife in oil”: the stone rose, ran over the arm and froze the hoop on the forearm. Before the shot, the air in the palm of your hand trembles with gauze. During the shooting, the stone hoop glows, changing color from pink to greenish. The finger throws fireballs and flares, cuts through targets with an invisible "whistle", can break walls, burn gates and shine like a lantern. The finger hits only in a straight line, so General Silver Fox offered to cover himself from the fingernails with earthen ramparts, because of which archers and catapults shoot with a canopy; in the forest, the fingernails lose their advantage. [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]] is able to absorb the flashes of Perst - so in the grotto of Kosuli Navi "drinked" the fire of Paul. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]])

While Perst is silent, he has only one divine property - a special material. Therefore, the people who found the Rings in the boxes of Gifts, unknowingly considered them as small Objects and did not enter them in the main register, and the great lords, who valued only Objects that “glow, spin, fly”, gave them to the church.

## The lesson of ice

Perst’s device is described in “[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]” when Ice ([[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]]) taught [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] and [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] at night. According to him, the Perst is a long-range weapon with one or more types of combat; their Persts had two.
- **Fireballs.** Target fire - up to half a mile, the ball sets fire to the target and from fifty yards burns the patch through; the disadvantage is that the ball flies no faster than an arrow from a long bow.
- **Flesh.** An invisible force moving at the speed of sound; it cannot be evaded, and it does not give out the position of the shooter, but only emits a characteristic whistle. The whip passes freely through the soft and breaks the hard - lats, blades, bones - so that the enemy's body turns into a "bag filled with bone fragments"; a multiple shot at one point can give a crack in the wall.

“Conversation” with Perst is conducted by a strong-willed effort, like throwing a stone: you look at the target and give an order to the hand. It is difficult for beginners to feel the finger as part of the body: a weak signal does not give a shot, and an accidental order leads to a spontaneous shot. Therefore, recruits are taught a simplified way - commands backwards. The word "fire" is not suitable, it is too often said and thought, but "other" causes a ball of white flame the size of a fist. The subject is “reasonable” and understands many words: put it on with the command “ukur an” (“on the arm”), and “lectirp” (“target”) makes the target on which the eye is focused flicker along the contour with a bluish light. The Perst material is warm and pulsates slightly under the fingers, like living flesh. Martin tried to stick his fist in with force, and the Object only hardened; Joaquin realized that Perst "does not like violence," and turning to him mentally, easily plunged his fingers inside - the radiance increased.

## Management

The finger obeys only the person with the [[pervokrov|firstblood]]. “We need blood, special blood! You don't have one!" shouted Vittor Joaquin when he asked for a Feather during the Waymar mutiny. In Paul's horde, the shooters were "dedicated" by exchanging bodily fluids with them (Paul kissed the survivors on the lips), and Perst was "attached" to the owner; according to Erwin, Paul tested his Shavans by pitting them against each other. ([[paul|Paul]]) Such Shavan shooters were called "hanida vir kanna" - "worthy to see". Without the firstblood, Perst is useless, but the Shavans, not realizing this, cut off the hands of the dead persunos and killed each other, taking possession of the "dead hand" with the Object. [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] also could not make Perst speak, taken from Alisher's khanid.

Erwin, after the battle of Rei-Roy, turned the captured Shavan gunner into a "gun" - a live installation on a chair made of wood and iron, where pain was regulated by screws screwed into the skull. ([[orudie|The Weapon]]) Erwin explained to the prisoner that the primal blood and power over the Object exclude him "from the ranks of mankind", and called it "according to function" - the Weapon; The Leed Wolves broke his will for two days. Shooting from this "Tools", [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] destroyed the Persians of the Ondei gant, and in the battle for the First Winter, dragging him to an impregnable rock platform, defeated the White Seamen.

## History in the Series

### Arrow, Coin, Spark (1774)
In the Beyond, at the bed of the Eighteenth Gift, Erwin and Jemis saw men in red uniforms shooting prisoners with fire from "rock hoops"; their commander's name was Paul. Erwin killed two soldiers of the brigade; the second of them was "living as a fang".

### "Only One Star" (1774)
- In early September 1774, a detachment with "talking Objects" burned [[pozhar-evergarda|Evergard]] in an hour, and Duke Aiden Almera died. All believed that the Persians had Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]]; Erwin later referred to the "blasphemous use of the Persians" among the reasons for his rebellion.
- Adrian on the battlefield refused to Prince Hector, demanding the Rings: in a clean field, where the enemy's cavalry is in charge, the savages could "jump to the shooters and take possession of the Rings."
- Erwin, after capturing Faunterra, hoped to find the Rings in the Dynasty vault, but received only "three hundred twenty beautiful but silent Objects." Crown Raven Mark argued that Adrian did not have the Rings, and [[minerva|Minerva]] realized that Erwin himself did not have them.
- Fleming's fleet reached the mouth of the River; Jemis and Mark captured the fort where the pile of Pers was stored. Then the fort with a hundred soldiers disappeared. The bracelet, which captured one of the watch fort and which Jemis took for himself, did not respond to the fighting words: Mark realized that this was not a Perst, but a means of communication with the owner of the fort - the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]].

### A Doll on the Throne (1775)
After the death of Adrian Minerva set a goal to “find the Wilhelm's Fingers”, and Erwin forbade his relatives to mention the “Master of the Rings”: the late Adrian was publicly declared a villain. Raven Mark realized that the kidnappers of the objects of the dynasty and the Master of the Rings is one person: the corpse found by the peasants was burned by the Ring.

- Mother Cordelia Erwin argued that the Rings of the Brigade - and there were about fifty of them, "more than Wilhelm the Great himself had" - were partially taken from the churches of Almera: in two dozen temples small Objects similar to the Rings were kept, but for the last two years they were not carried out on the altar, and the deacons remembered how [[gallard-almera|Gallard]]'s messengers had removed them.

([[perevorot-v-uejmare|Coup in Waymar]]; [[garri-khog|Harry Hogg]]) He killed three kairs on the wall; when Hog was dying on the balcony, Joaquin, hiding behind his body, pointed his hand and whispered "Come on!" - so the kairs of Seymour, Brandon and Gray died.

Only whips", and Led shouted "Zalp!" at every shot. The brigade lost fifteen men and stole a Drop of Sun. ([[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]])

### Humans and Gods (1775)

To Father David Erwin in these days said that, having received absolute power, he would get rid of the speaking Objects: "I swore that I would honor the covenant of Wilhelm", and he would bury the Feathers in a deep dungeon, forbidding entry there. ## The Origin of the Doll's Rings

According to the story of [[navi|Navi]], Paul, with the help of the Light Sphere, found an unknown Gift beyond the River and extracted it by the hands of slaves. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) Paul assured Vittor that weapons are often found in the Dari, "only you do not notice: Wilhelm's Fingers are uncasual in appearance," and offered to get them outside the River and buy them from the Church. The cautious count doubted: the Rings are strong, but not omnipotent, and the "taboo with which Wilhelm sealed them" outweighs their merits - "The Rings are weak and vicious." Those Rings that Paul touched are vulnerable to the deconstructor (see [[golos-boga|Voice of God]]). In the finale, Navi explained that all the Sacred Objects were wrecks of broken starships, so that the Feathers were probably weapons from one of the lost ships; he did not directly mention their purpose.

## In "Fantasies"

In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, after the war, the Church united around the question of the Persians. Lady [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] sent an eight-page penitential letter to the chapter and bowed to the Church; this suggested to the clergy a way to regain authority. High Mother Alicia proclaimed: “The use of the Rings is a terrible sin. Wilhelm's Tabu must be observed," and Bishop Amessin announced that all Persunos must accept penance within half a year, otherwise a reward will be appointed for their heads. In the summer of 1776, the king-prophet [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] addressed the people through a speaking device: who used the Rings, must repent, and unrepentant until the Day of Descent will be found, tried and executed. The appeal was assured by Mother Alicia and Amessin himself, although he personally blessed the archers with the Persians in the battle for the First Winter. Soon, three other bearers of the Rings, like Iona, took up penance.

In the First Winter, Minerva, tired of turning stone blocks with a glove, dreamed of a "pearl" - a servant with pervokrovy who would build instead of her, and demanded that the Shavanskie ambassadors of the Ganta Corta give her one of the khanid ver kanna "not to shoot, but to build dams and bridges." Erwin reminded Bishop Amessin that Wilhelm’s ban only applied to the Rings, not the Gloves, and suggested that he repent himself, because “under your flag, too, the finger-bearers fought.” In Shimmery, it was said that Rei-Roy was stabbed to death by an unrepentant khanid, and two large gauntlets seized on his Perth; Farhan won. Iona, according to a joke by Jemis, “repainted” the Leed castle in black with her own Ring. Some of the Fallen worshippers prayed for Richard Origin as a great chiselling fallen in battle. The father of the schoolgirl Sally, who wore the "red cherry" of the best shooter of the company of Aiden Almer, lost his hand, burned by the Perst Shavans of the Gnoi Ganta; Sally's teacher Mrs. Gale - by all indications the surviving [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], Aiden's daughter - heard this, "silent and lowered her eyes."
