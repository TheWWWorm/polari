Gloria the Intercessor is one of the Holy Mothers. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) In Scripture, she goes sixth: "Dark-eyed Miriam, followed by Yanmei the Merciful, then the Great Sophia, Agatha the Radiant, Elena, Gloria, Susan..." She is called "the kindest of the Foremothers" and "The Foremother with a velvet heart." She hears every prayer, no matter how small, and helps “the poor and the thief.” Gloria is especially loved in the North and among the common people.

## Face
The temples of Gloria are known by their form: they are built in the form of a correct cross. The temples are rich in sculptural groups and “live scenes” on frescoes, as the Intercessor communicated with people a lot. In the cathedral of Gloria, which [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] saw in 1774, the dome is painted with scenes from her life. The frescoes depict people of all classes coming to her bow: "Gloria comforts the orphans, Gloria blesses the fool, Gloria and the shepherds, Gloria at the city fire." Erwin noted the difference with other saints:
> "The arrogant Agatha and the heartless Janmei, and the majestic Sophia do not care about the common people, but the Intercessor will hear anyone."

The deputy is often depicted barefoot - apparently in honor of "her spiritual kinship with the mob." Erwin never understood this image: “the power of weakness.” In Leed, above the portal of the temple, there is a statue of a woman with a piece of bread in her palm, in front of which two stone warriors bow their heads. Gloria's profile is minted on a silver coin, the Gloria (see [[dengi|Money]]). In the markets, a sign with her face above the box means that any item in it is worth a glories.

## Traditions and Covenants
- **Two robbers.** The famous parable of how Gloria spared two robbers. On the fresco, long daggers lie at her bare feet, and the robbers' faces are "crying and gracious". In "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]" this parable is understood in a lesson at school.
- **“We are different, but we are one.”** These words of Gloria about the spiritual kinship of the Foremothers are depicted in the fresco, which Laura Almera recognized.
- **Covenants.** “Honestly work, and do not seek a deceitful way.” The best sages, according to Gloria, are born “not in palaces, but in huts.” In Fantasies, the teacher reminds: “When you help people, you decorate your soul.”
- **Light Sphere.** In the description of the Light Sphere, it is said that the Object "is connected with the deeds of Foremother Gloria" and the Forefathers Jose, Alexander and Lucian. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]])

Gloria left the book "Words of Mercy." [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]], out of respect for her mother and her Pramati, learned her by heart and as a child "threw so bravely to protect the small and weak that the mother cried with tenderness." Laura was able to interpret Gloria the Intercessor’s parables. Many nobles choose to study Gloria, along with Miriam, Elena and Emilia - "the brightest figures among the Foremothers."

## Honoring
According to the Polari Land Guide, the common people of the North "deify two Foremothers: Agatha and Gloria." The northern code of the warrior requires to follow their commandments. This includes protecting the weak and the innocent, “as far as Mother Gloria pleases.” Count Benedict [[fleming|Fleming]] prayed to Gloria and Agatha.

The name of the Intercessor is constantly heard in prayers and vows: “Gloria, Help us!”, “For the sake of Gloria, the Intercessor, do not go”, “St. Gloria, the Intercessor, all the truth.” Her name is Hospitals and Cathedrals. In "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]", two Imperial companies, who spent the night in the cathedral of Gloria the Intercessor, repelled the night attack of the northerners and struck the alarm. Erwin's mother donated to Gloria the Intercessor Hospital.

## Descendants
From the family of Gloria comes Count Benedict [[fleming|Fleming]] and his kairs. Like many descendants of the Intercessor, the count boasts "mighty, high forehead and blue eyes." Her family includes Lillidays. [[mark-voron|Mark]] in the expedition for the River noted that the most fertile in the army were the Earl Fleming and Kair Jemis - "grandchildren of the Intercessor." [[sajmon-lillidej|Simon Lilliday]] surrendered Leed to "the power of the Earl of Lilliday and the name of his mother Gloria." Mark himself doubted her as the patroness of the campaign: “Gloria is a kind woman, where she measures claws with the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]] herself?” If anyone can do that, he thought, it was Agatha.

## Sign
Before going for the River, Count Fleming's grandfather, Xander, prayed at St. Gloria's Cathedral. [[mark-voron|Mark]] promised the count that behind the River "Gloria the Intercessor will show a sign." When the sign came, Fleming decided that Mother Gloria had shown him the truth.
