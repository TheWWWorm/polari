Julian Law, or Julian Codex, is the code of laws of the Polari Empire, named after the mistress [[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]. Juliana ruled for half a century, ended the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]] and established the borders of the empire. According to Minerva, she introduced “the only code of laws that was able to take into account the mores of all the lands of Polaris.” Juliana herself attributed her popularity primarily to this code. In her era, Polaris suffered from strife, and the ruler could force the state to unite. Instead, she created laws “to which all the lands of Polaris willingly submitted.” In the story of her rule, the all-imperial code of laws is called "the crown of everything."

The name "Julian Law" is rare: it is pronounced by Desmond Origin and Duke Willis Litland. They usually say “Julian law”, “Julian code” or “Julian code”.

## Set of vaults
In the appendix to the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark, Lady [[karen|Karen]] explains that Julian law is not one law and not one set, but a “complex of vaults”. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) By the end of the 16th century, the Dynasty required all lands to live according to the imperial code, and the commandment "Let another be" justified local customs, and according to the letter of imperial law, about three-quarters of the inhabitants of the mainland were criminals. Juliana legalized the local order: each land received the right to its own laws, and a person obeys the code of the land where he is. The codex covers eighteen volumes. According to Karen, it enshrines, for example, a special class of [[kajry|kairs]] in [[oridzhin-zemlya|Origin]], [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]'s public forests, polygamy in [[shimmeri|Shimmery]], income tax in [[almera-zemlya|Almera]], and the right to trophies from raids in [[rejs|Reis]] and Holliwell. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]])

A personal innovation of Juliana Karen considers the concept of legitimate war. War is legal if it is openly declared, its conditions are named and leave the enemy the right to exist, it goes only on the lands of the parties and allies, it does not use Sacred Objects, witchcraft and poisons (except [[darkvoter|Darkwater]]), civilians are not exterminated if the enemy himself did not involve them in battle, ambassadors and mercy are inviolable to the prisoners and wounded. The trophies of such a war are legal, and the commander who broke the rules is equated with a criminal. Therefore, according to Karen, the two dukes, saying "Julian law", would have in mind the rules of war, not the Shepherds of the Meadow.

## Provisions
Several articles of the code are mentioned or cited in the novels.

### About the rebellion
In the spring of 1774, before sending his son on an expedition, [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] was angry at Erwin's plan to sell the emperor Origin's support for money, told him to repeat the oath of allegiance and recalled:
> “Rebellion is punishable, according to Julian’s law, by burning, either by death from lye, or by quartering. The nobles are given the right to choose. The call for rebellion is thirty long whips for the commoner and deprivation of title for the original."

The execution is also described in 1775: Adrian threatened prisoner [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]] that he would be laid naked in the square and would pour the clicks on a drop.

### The Fair War
There is no law prohibiting feudal wars. At the spring session of the House of 1775, Snow Count Lilliday, father of [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]], responded to [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] with a quote from the codex: ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]])
> War declared by one Lord to another Lord, with its causes and possible conditions for its termination, shall be recognized as fair war. Neither the Lord nor the soldiers who took part in it can be judged by the laws of peace.

The same “Julian War Code” defines military action as armed skirmishes between warriors of warring lords on the lands of one of them. Murder under other circumstances is not considered part of the war. Therefore, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] forbade [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] to attack the imperial train in neutral Almera. He said his “idiotic family” had followed the code “just since the Julian times.” At Manson's trial, Erwin showed that he found Alanis' plan "dishonest and in violation of the code of war": Almera remained neutral, and fighting on her land was unacceptable. ([[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]]) After the Baek Massacre, representatives of Northwood, Almera, Reis, Holliwell, and Litland in the House condemned Alanis for "violating the laws of fair war set forth by the Julian Code". ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]])

### About the Court
- Under the sixth section, article seventy-second, the court may review its decision after additional investigation, appointed on appeal. In February 1775, the secretary of the Supreme Court reminded Minerva, who had been sentenced to beheading in absentia. Another way is to dissolve the Supreme Court and order a review of its decisions through a vote in the House.
- The charge requires strong evidence; one word from the Lord is not enough. The same secretary complained that this “mass of red tape and procrastination.” To charge the minister with an official crime requires the testimony of at least three independent witnesses, backed up by material evidence.
- [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] at the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]] argued that Minerva and Erwin's absentee sentence violated "the section on litigation, the chapter on the rights of the defendant" and the theses of the code on the role and rights of the prosecutor. Judge [[albert-kantor|Albert Cantor]] objected to him by the same law: under the "chapter of justice, chapter of the supreme court", neither the House nor the sovereign has the right to dissolve the supreme court during the hearing. Minerva denied the defense's request.
- According to the Julian law, the court session should be conducted openly, and anyone can enter. This was referred to by the student seminarians in Confident when they wanted to get to the trial of David Atterton. ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]) The abbess replied that the monastery was a feudal subject, and she was a seigneur in it, and did not let them in.
- Juliana abolished church courts and “left justice in the hands of secular judges.” Since then, the bonfires for the Holy Fathers rarely burn: for theft from the temple they give hard labor, for mocking the Mother - a dozen whips. So the bishop explained the law to eight-year-old [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]. The more terrible Joaquin was in October 1774 to see in Aleridan, as at the will of Gallard Almer on Cathedral Square burned "heretics". ([[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]) The church court is preserved as a trial of the clergy: it has the right to execute and pardon priests of any rank, except for the highest mothers, and a high-ranking cleric is not subject to worldly justice.

### About Road Certificates
In the spring of 1775, in Almera, a monk of the Order of the Forefather Ferdinand, Brother Hemish, and an officer stopped [[mark-voron|Mark]] and [[ded|Grandfather]]. Grandfather recalled that according to the law of Vladyka Telurian, a travel certificate is not required for a traveler. Then the monk referred to the “Julian law of the six hundred and fourth year”, giving the right to detain unknown people who are dangerous to civilians. Grandfather countered that the same law determines the signs of possible danger, and they do not fall under them: on travelers there are no armor, and they have only light weapons.

### About Shepherd's Meadows
In the sixteenth century, after the Horse Wars, Juliana declared the Shepherds’ Meadows along the Hollywell to be the shared possessions of the neighbouring lands. Three western counties and three eastern counties were granted the right to herd herds there without restrictions on one condition: to peacefully coexist with each other (in early media, four western counties and three eastern counties). In 1774, Erwin Origin invited the Duke of Litland to take possession of four fords through Holliwell. The duke was afraid: “This is against Julian the law!” Erwin replied that the law gives into the common ownership of the meadow, "but nothing is said about crossings." When [[adrian|Adrian]] gave the common lands to the Litlands, Ambassador Reiss accused him: “You have violated the law of Julian the Great.” In March 1775 Minerva replied to the Shavan leader [[moran|Moran]], who called Luga his: "General, according to Julian law" - and gave the word of the Empress that the Litlands would no longer try to appropriate them. For Westerners, the common possession of the Lugs since Juliana is "as sacred a truth as that the world was created by the gods" (see [[litlend|Litland]], [[rejs|Reis]]).

## Code in use
The Julian Code is used by judges, lords and representatives in the House. In the leaflets with the agenda of the meeting for representatives left blanks for notes like “Contrary to Juliana code 22.4”. Francis-Ilianus, proving to the court his knowledge of the laws, admitted that he had once “gave Julian’s code” to his own son.

## Other Codes of Law
The Julian Codex is not the only existing codex.
- The code of Olgard the Founder regulates the issues of feudal honor and the behavior of nobles in a military campaign. A knife struck by a seigneur, on it - a heavy vassal treason, for which only the death penalty is imposed. This provision was dealt with at the Manson trial, where it was referred to by prosecutor [[mark-voron|Mark]].
- The Codex of Lord Lexian contains the "Sixteenth Law" of conspiracy against the Crown: "conscious secret actions" aimed at undermining or overthrowing the power of the emperor. Mira checked with him, waiting for charges of conspiracy.
- The Code of Yumin (commandments of Pramatery Yumin) determines the order of the court: the call of witnesses, the debate of the parties, the adviser for the accused. His “first axiom” states that any doubts are interpreted in favor of the accused. By the will of Yumin, the madman is relieved of responsibility. Mother Yumin in ancient times herself played the role of judge.
- The law of Vladyka Telurian “from the sixty-seventh year” states that a travel document is not mandatory for a traveler. He was referred to by his grandfather in a dispute with his brother Hemish.
- The ancient laws of the Forebears were also preserved. The law of Wilhelm and Janmei punished heresy by burning the commoner and expelling the original to the western steppes.

## In "Fantasies"
In The Deathly Hallows (June 1777, Winslow), McFried Crowe's lawyer, Mac, on the move composed the Constable's "twenty-and-twelfth article of Julian's code" and backed it up with a business card, Legal Services. The constable believed and allowed the deceased's belongings to be examined. By all accounts, Mac is [[mark-voron|Mark]], the former Crow of the Crown. At the [[sud-nad-mensonom|trial of Manson]], Mark, in his role as prosecutor, referred to the code of Olgard the Founder.

## Uncertainties
The adjective "Julians" is grammatically derived from the male name, while the bridge and codex are called "Julians". A ruler named Julian is not mentioned in Polari's history. Both references to the Julian Law in early media refer to provisions of the Julian Code (on rebellion and on the Shepherd's Meadows). So it seems to be about the same thing. In the edition of 2026, the Duke of Litland speaks already about the Julian Law, and the form of the Julians remained only in Desmond's words about the punishment for the rebellion.
