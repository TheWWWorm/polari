**Vivian** is one of the Holy Mothers, the patron saint of the holiday. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) She is credited with “legendary ability to dispel discouragement and bring joy.” From Vivian leads the family [[dom-shejland|House Shayland]]: the full name of the Puppeteer is Vittor Kaylin Agna of the family Vivian. She is credited with a covenant that is remembered before the spring holiday:
> “He who does not enjoy life does not deserve long years.”

## Temples
The temples of Vivian are unlike any other. In [[yanmejskaya-okhota|the Yanmei Hunt]], [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] led [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] to the Vivian Temple in [[uejmar|Waymar]]. The altar was replaced by a marble fountain. The mother Vivian held in her hands two amphorae, from which water flowed. Light through the colored glass was scattered over the mosaic floor and white marble walls. The sculptures depicted fun: dancing, hugging lovers, musicians and playing children. “Vivian is the patron saint of the festival,” Iona said. “His temples were built to bring joy.”

The priest of the temple was dressed in white to look like a marble statue: “The servant Vivian should not embarrass the parishioners with his appearance.” He explained why the temples of Vivian are few: the nobles sacrifice their Foremothers to the temples, and the family of Vivian "is not too wealthy." Waymar temple was built recently, with the money of Count [[vinsent-shejland|Vincent Shayland]], and became the largest temple of Vivian in all the northern lands. On the night of Vincent's death, his son Vittor, he said, prayed in this church for his father's health.

## Worship in Shayland
According to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", the Sheilanders are philosophical, but willingly observe the rites. Most of the people there are honored by the Mother Vivian. Her ability to bring joy “finds a great response from the Shaylands.” In the Cathedral of Vivian there were heroes of the story “[[teper-ty-koldun|Now you are a sorcerer]]”. In the "Shadow of the Great Tree", Bishop Vivian came to Vittor's council, and at the "resurrection" of the count, the priest stood before the icon of Vivian. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

## Rod Vivian
The girls of the Vivian family are famous for their cheerful disposition. Lady Miranda, Faraday's bride, "as befits a girl of the Vivian family, was a feast." She “was a life, tirelessly sowed joy.” Count Vincent Shayland assured him that the hint of an alliance with the Origins was sent to him by Foremother Vivian on a sleepless night. Victor swore by "Vivian's Play".

[[kharmon|Harmon]], selling the Light Sphere for the Earl of Vittor, prayed "St. Vivian - the Mother of the Earl of Vittor". Without waiting for help, he decided he was asking for the wrong thing.

## Patroness of Players
Vivian is considered the patroness of the players. In the campaign of 1775, the young [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]] was lucky in the cards, because “the Promotre Vivian gave him good luck.” [[shram|Scar]] comforted the losing duke: “Vivian does not smile — Miriam will kiss!” Erwin realized what it was: “Vivian is the patroness of the players, and I want to hang her grandson.”
