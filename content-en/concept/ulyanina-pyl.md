## Tradition

The story of Dust was told by Erwin Ded in the Yanmei Hunt. ([[ded|Grandfather]]) Under the Forebears, this nameless Object served for secret correspondence: if you write a message with pollen, only the one whose face the writer represented will read it, and the rest will see a blank sheet. One day, Agatha the Radiant, while Janmay was urgently leaving for Orwell, asked her for the Gauntlet of Power to quickly pave the way. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]) Agatha knew engineering and did not distract Yangmei herself, but sent a note made up of secret pollen. The note was read by Yangmei Gavin's student. He could have asked Agatha to wait for Pramatera's return or turn to the Forefather Wilhelm, who had access to the vault, but then the Glove would not have reached Agatha until the morning. Agatha imagined the faces of both Yangmei and Gavin, who was allowed to open Pramateri's mail, so he read the letter. Kayras, who were guarding the vault, saw on the sheet only a comic drawing of a lady on the road and refused. Then Gavin brought them wine: two of the four guards drank and fell in convulsions, the third he stabbed, and the fourth disarmed him. At the trial, Foremother Yumin established that pollen is harmless to the Forebears, but for people of the sublunar world it is terrible: it suppresses the will and makes them blindly perform what they write. Gavin was pardoned, and the Subject was named Ulyanina Dust - in honor of the most tragic of the Foremothers. Francis-Ilianus later quoted Yumin's work Justice and Action, which describes the Dust effect.

## Owners

Before the Shuttow conspiracy, Dust belonged to the Lighthart House. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]; [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) At the trial, it turned out that the conspirators tried to use it against the emperor Telurian, but Dust “did not obey them.” By decree of Telurian, the Object was handed over to the Church of the Forefathers, which was headed a few years later by [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]. At a meeting of the House, Erwin presented Bishop Amessin's intercepted note to Gallard — "what about the apple?" sent on the day Minerva announced the Census of Objects — as proof that Dust was in the hands of the prior. According to Erwin, Gallard passed the Dust and about fifty small objects to the Puppeteer.

Later, in court before his mother Cordelia, Erwin outlined the path of Dust found by his men and protection agents: in 1772 she was taken from the vault of the Wilhelmina Cathedral in Aleridan and transported to the palace of the Priory, and from there, under a reinforced escort, was sent to Fliss and loaded onto a ship. According to Erwin, Gallard received for Dust the attack on Evergard and the death of his brother, the Duke of Aiden. Vittor himself in conversation with Adrian blamed Paul: he allegedly attacked Vladyka Pylya, "against the will" of the count.

## How Dust Works

### Inside: Manson
The chapter about the joke [[menson|Manson]] in "Only One Star" shows Dust from the inside. Dust “decided” for him when to start acting: he heard someone else’s thought in his head, which seemed to be his own. She pulled his eyes to the knives, allowed or did not allow to speak, and when Manson disobeyed, at night she pulled him out of bed, put him on her knees and forced him to bite her own hand for an hour until the blood filled her sleeve; she stopped only so that the wound did not interfere with the goal. Dust demanded to strike on the train, and when the mind of the jester did not find a way, “dived” into the depths of his memory.

### Outside: Iona
In June 1775, in Waymar, [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]] dragged a captive [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] out of a cage and brought a “red leaf” to her face, a blank paper framed in scarlet ink. Iona knew what it was, and she shuddered. Richard threatened to cut off her eyelids, but her eyes opened only when Vittor condescendingly promised once to “forgive” her. Seeing the leaf, Iona shuddered, tried to turn away, and a minute later said in a wooden voice, “Give me a letter.” She then rewrote with her hand a false confession of love with her brother Erwin, put the family marks of authenticity and signed the envelope. Every attempt to resist — to stand up, to break the inkpan, to crumple the sheet — reduced it to a cramp; the pen was stained with blood. After that, Iona was “squeezed dry.”

> "Ulyanina Dust. That's a great thing. If you read the order, you will do whatever you want to do.”

Martin explained this to [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], adding: “Against it, it hurts, you obey it, it’s good.” Then Vittor put a silver apple in front of him and whispered the word with one of his lips - the surface was soft and became viscous like clay; when he left, the Object cooled and solidified, and Richard had to ask the formula: "Zakirp". The lines written in this pollen glowed with silver and then disappeared. With a bill of exchange of silver words, Vittor ordered Joaquin to secretly kill Richard if he rebelled against the Count.

### Limit of action
Erwin noted from Manson's story that Dust's action disappears when the victim believes the order has been carried out. It is painful to resist the order: Manson gnawed his hand, and Nexia, according to Erwin's observation, resisted, but to no avail. It was later revealed that Dust relieved Manson of his habit of distorting words, though she returned at the end of the cycle.

## Application in the cycle

- **Manson.** At the trial of the jester (the Manson trial), Manson admitted that he was twice exposed to Dust - in the summer at the ball and in the winter at the palace of the priory; under her authority, he hit Adrian with a dagger during the crash at Back, but, according to him, did not kill the lord and even helped him out. ([[sud-nad-mensonom]]; [[menson|Manson]]; [[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) Admitting the joke revealed that [[adrian|Adrian]] was alive. In Nan Clair, Lord Emmon exclaimed, "Attack by Ulyanina Dust! In Almera! The day the bridge collapsed!”
- **Nexia.** In Waymar, [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] went into refectory naked one morning and remembered nothing; Iona knew it was about the subject. According to Erwin, the Puppeteer so experienced Dust before use on a new person. Later, under the rule of Pili, Nexia brought to Erwin a letter from Vittor and roses with poisoned thorns: the order was to force the Duke to read the letter, and if he refused, to kill. In the letter itself, also written by Dust, Erwin was ordered to abdicate in favor of Richard or die. The althessa clue helped Erwin realize that there was no poison on the paper, "only Ulyana Dust." Erwin played death to make Dust believe the order was executed. The father of Nexia [[edgar-flejm|Edgar Flame]], he wrote that the daughter is not guilty, and asked at least until the evening not to convince her. Flame didn't tell her daughter that Erwin was alive, and for two days she thought she was a murderer.
- **Iona.** The confession that Dust took from Iona (see above) was used by the Puppeteer against the Origins.
- **Richard.** The night after the Battle of the First Winter, when Richard broke into the castle to his relatives, Desmond Origin explained his betrayal by the fact that Richard was "misled and bewitched by Ulyanina Dust". Richard himself rejected this; in the text it remains the words of his father.
- **Martin.** In [[lid|Leed]] Iona dust crumbs, which gave her Erwin, was able to write only a short order: "Take off the feathers, give me." [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] performed it, and the action of Dust passed, but, broken by defeat, he submitted further.

## Dust and the Absolute

[[navi|Navi]] explained to Minerva that Dust acts on anyone, including the immortal Vittor: it is worth removing [[absolyut|the Absolute]], and the graph will become an ordinary mortal, and forcing him to remove the Object "is not so difficult: Ulyana Dust, for example." According to him, in the Light Sphere Dust was designated by the same unnamed point as the Glove and the Bird. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) Navi believed that Minerva, having solved a possible attack with Dust and scraping matter from paper, would have mastered it as well.

At the end of the cycle, Vittor told Adrian that Dust was stored in his people in the Sky Creatures. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]]) Minerva and the Origins were going to storm the Skrebki, take it there and force Vittor, frozen in the Womb, to abandon the Absolute. Magda Labelin dreamed of the same thing: to take the Dust "from the Shayland shortcomings" and write on the cover of the Womb "Give us the Absolute". And Adrian, who dreamed of the Tree, himself urged Iona: “Apply the Dust of Ulyanovsk and order your husband to give the Absolute!”

## Trivia
- According to Joaquin, Richard, writing an order for the vassals, three times brought out the same thing: the messages to Dust, apparently, were written for each addressee separately.
- Later it became clear that [[otets-david|Father David]]’s advice “not to read the letters,” which Iona considered a warning about poison, referred specifically to Dust: David apparently knew that Dust was at the Puppeteer.
- Educated by experience, Erwin became cautious with letters: after receiving Magda Labelin's message after the Battle of the First Winter, he broke the seal, but "thought about Ulyana Dust" and gave the letter to his sister.
