**Susan** is one of the Holy Mothers, the seventh in the canon of Scripture after Miriam, Janmei, Sophia, Agatha, Helena, and Gloria. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) It is called the Mother of Health. She prays for recovery, and her commandments relate to bodily health: exercise, moderation in food and drink. Susan leads the House of Northwood. ([[dom-nortvud|House Northwood]])

## Flower of Life
In "Janmei Hunt," [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]], who studied Susan's life on behalf of Erwin, recounted her legend. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) The Mother had the Flower of Life, the subject of sixteen petals. Each petal could cure any disease, but only once. Erwin remembered another version, where the petals were thirty-two. Susan saw a thousand patients and each time decided whether to spend a petal or to do with parks and ointments. Having cut the petal, she always said: “Pity.”

One day, a girl was brought to her, sick with a gray sea. To save the city, the child had to be either healed or locked in prison. Susan tore one of the eight remaining petals, and an hour later the girl was healthy. She was the daughter of Susan herself, but the Mother frowned anyway and said, “Sorry.” Robert suggested that Susan regretted not the petal, but the weakness of their means: the gods may have known a way to completely save people from disease and old age. He sought in the Scriptures hints of immortality and [[absolyut|the Absolute]].

In Fantasia, it is said that each legend has a symbol: “Agatha has a feather, Susan has a flower.” ([[fantazii|Fantasies]])

- She healed Joseph the Blind.

## Number seven
Seven is the number of Susan, "the seventh among the Holy Mothers." In the play Liola and Carrog, the doctor brews an infusion on seven petals, and Lady Sophia remarks: "Seven is the number of Susan, the Mother of Health." ([[liola-i-karrog|Liola and Karrog]]) The number helped Dorothy, who lost her memory in the hospital, to remember herself. Navi helped her find the "treasure number" of seven. Dorothy realized what came from the family of Susan, and remembered her mother, Lady Dorina, and brothers. “They didn’t take seven. No one's taking this. I'm the granddaughter of Foremother Susan."

## Rod Susan
Susan is descended from Countess Sybil Dorina Denise Northwood and her children, including [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]]. ([[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]]) Rod Susan is famous for health: “The Countess of Northwood is full of beauty and health ... Rod Susan, whatever you say.” Susan’s descendants “always don’t mind marrying the ladies of the Gloria family.” A joke says, “Suzanne’s grandson will trade his wife for a hunting dog.” Myra, called Gloria Northwood, also became “the kind of Prammother Susan.”

## The Author's Article
In 2018, in the section of the author's articles "Polaripedia" - the cycle wiki on Fandom - an unfinished list of "Saints Foremothers" appeared. - **Character.** Susan is the patroness of health, sports and hunting: a determined, courageous and energetic “Amazon”, a simple-minded choleric, alien to doubt. She was a diagnostician, a trainer and a shooter.
- **Subject.** Here, her Subject is called White Lily: it heals almost any disease, but the number of "injections" is limited - according to various versions, 17, 33 or 138. In the novels, it is the Flower of Life with sixteen (or thirty-two) petals.
