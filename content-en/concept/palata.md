## Organisation
The Chamber is the oldest of the authorities: it arose before the Yangmei Dynasty, before the Supreme Court and even before the Polarian Kingdom became an empire. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]) Already under Wilhelm the Great there was a Council of Forefathers, which adopted laws by vote, and the king himself had only one vote in it. King Marion Despot of the First Dark-Eye Dynasty tried to dissolve the House, which ended with his death and change of dynasty. In 1774, as noted in the Janmei Hunt, "Lord Adrian repeated his mistake."

Representatives are appointed by land managers. If the Lord rules firmly, the representatives follow his will; if the power of his shaky, they can wilfully. The new law is introduced by a vote of four representatives, debated for four days and then put to a vote. During this time, you do not have time to go to the suzerain and barely have time to exchange a pair of birds, so the decision of the representative often makes himself. The law comes into force throughout the empire only if more than half voted in favour; however, in [[faunterra|Faunterra]] and the [[zemli-korony|Crownlands]], the word of the ruler is law and without the House. The Chamber sits constantly, solving minor issues, and the Great Houses keep at least one representative in the capital all year round. It meets twice a year, in April and September, when the laws on troops, fees, court and division of land are adopted. On the eve of the meeting, the lords are given coat of arms with an agenda: they set out the questions, the names of the speakers and their positions, and for the notes left blanks - for example, "Contrary to Julian's codex 22.4".

In quiet times, landlords do not go to the Chamber themselves: the main questions are known for six months, and vassals receive accurate instructions. The Northern vassals of Desmond Origin could not stand the office of Lord Representative, and the Duke changed them every year "so that all vassals would suffer equally."

### Number
About the size of the Chamber in the novels speak differently. In the early editions of [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], [[kolin-felisiya|Colin Felicia]] explained to Mira that the Chamber is thirty-nine people, "three from every area except the Crown Lands"; in the 2026 edition, this scene is cut. In the Yangmei Hunt, it is said that in the early centuries the House consisted of representatives of all the Pramaterin families, two from each. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) As the state split into counties and duchies, the voice of the land, not the family, became more important. By the time of the Third Dynasty, the present order had developed: two representatives from each land, two from the Crown, and a couple from each branch of the Church, “it is the usual thirty-two voices.” Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark also corrects the first novel, which says that the Church does not have a direct voice in the House: according to her, from each branch of the Church, the Forefather and the Foremother, there are two Lord Representatives. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]])

### Building
Erwin liked the building of the Chamber more than the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]], too “Yanmean”, where “subtexts and hints are annoyingly climbing from all the cracks.” Here reigned "rough Miriam charisma": wide granite steps, pedestals with roaring lions, columns thicker than century-old oaks, windows in two human growth, a fireplace the size of a dragon's mouth. Tribunes are like small siege towers, a library is like a temple with shelves instead of frescoes, a refectory is like a forest of columns.

The meeting room is an amphitheater. On the balconies sit spectators allowed to open meetings, and in the shade under the balconies are servants and guards. The chairs of the representatives diverge from the center not by direct, but by curved rays, so that no one looks at the other in the back of the head, and the ground floor resembles a sacred spiral. ([[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]]) There are chairs for two secretaries at each chair. On the back of the chair is fixed the coat of arms of the earth, and so that the coats of arms are visible above the heads, the backs are made exorbitantly high. Hence the expression “sitting on a high chair” to serve as a representative.

## History in the Series

### Erwin's Dream
In his youth, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] dreamed of becoming a Lord Representative from the North. He expected that the father's order, which was able to do on a pigeon tape, is always inaccurate and incomplete, which means that the representative will be able to decide a lot on his own. At the age of twenty-one, he spoke at a public sitting of the House. The dream did not come true: he came to the House already Duke, Lord Chancellor and defender of the House as such.

### Adrian's Reform
Emperor [[adrian]] announced rail roads and a general land tax; a full taxation plan, for the "[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]", was to be announced at the House's autumn meeting in September. (In early media reports, Colin Felicia specified that the reforms were announced at the April meeting: four thousand miles of rails, six new spark shops, and a tax of a tenth of revenue.) The Lords representatives were met with a storm of discontent. He met in September to approve the reform, but only two of the thirteen Great Houses supported him. Coalitions and conspiracies were built around this vote in the summer of 1774. After the revelation of the conspiracy of Aiden Almer and the burning of Evergard, Adrian edictded that all the Lords personally swear him, effectively abolishing the House; Desmond Origin called him “the absolute monarch.” Insurgent soon Origin promised in case of victory to return the House of Representatives ([[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]]).

### Recovery
Under a peace treaty with Origin in December 1774, [[minerva|Minerva]] undertook to reconvene the House. The spring session opened on May 10, 1775. In an alarming time, most of the lands sent the rulers themselves, and the composition turned out to be scandalous:
- From Origin, Duke Erwin and the Snow Count Lilliday.
- from the Southern Way - Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] and Marquis Gracend;
- from Northwood - "two brothers-bears", who did not have legal authority with a living father;
- from Shimmery - rival king and prince;
- from Darkwater, a sick queen with a nephew; with her voice she appointed a Foltician, Admiral Fanno Groc;
- from Mills - Counts Faeries and Minnis;
- from Almera - [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] next to the Earl of Blackmore who betrayed her;
- from Reis - Ganta Thunderstorm, "the robber and the slave trader".

“Every third representative was not perfect,” but this was considered a time line. Erwin opened the session with a speech on Pramatery's wisdom: he asked whether progress justified "violations of eternal values", and recalled the burning of Evergard and the dissolution of the Chamber. Minerva named herself and [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]] as representatives of the Crown. The House approved the Origin Rebellion ("Northern Outbreak") and supported the Census of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects]]. Maurice Labelin demanded the full return of his lands captured by the Origin and Northwoods in the Northern Flash. The Snow Count answered him with a quote from Julian's code of fair war, and Labelin's demands were rejected. ([[yulianov-zakon|Juliana's Law]]) In the House building, Manson was tried, and Bishop Amessin proposed the creation of a "sacred guard". ([[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]]) Later, the Chamber strongly condemned Alanis for the collapse of the bridge and the pursuit of train passengers as a “violation of the laws of fair war”.

After Manson's admission that Adrian was alive, Erwin insisted on an immediate vote: Adrian was stripped of all rights to the throne (Minerva voted against). Judge [[albert-kantor|Cantor]] recalled a single precedent. In the sixteenth century, the sovereign Keylor was deposed for violating the feudal code, and the lords of the Chamber elected a new sovereign who was not a direct heir. Erwin immediately nominated Minerva, but she refused. The election interrupted the news of the trouble with Iona, and later, at the suggestion of Francis-Ilian, he was postponed until victory over the Puppeteer. Minerva remained a temporary ruler.

### After the war
In December 1775, after the Battle of the First Winter, Erwin told Adrian that the Dynasty had ceased to be a reliable power, and the House was "the only body that retained unambiguous legality". ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) He demanded that Adrian appear at the May meeting and answer for his actions, and suggested that in three days to gather in the First Winter a small meeting of the Chamber. It was to have representatives of the Crown, South Road, Shimmery, Darkwater, Origin, Reiss and the Sunset Coast, "which would create a half-consensus" to decide how everyone would "live peacefully until May". [[magda-labelin|Magda]] already hoped that by May he and Adrian will fall in love with the whole Polaris and no one will vote for Minerva.

## In "Fantasies"
In "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]", stories about the possible fate of the heroes, the Chamber in 1776 will elect the Lord.
- **Deadline.** In March 1776, [[adrian|Adrian]] decided to propose to the Lords to postpone the election to autumn: the North is not ready for the next meeting, in Northwood turmoil and famine, in Shayland the powerlessness, the Origins in mourning. Elections were later scheduled for the Days of Abundance; according to Alicia's mother, they were to be held "late in the fall".
- **Candidates.** Adrien, [[minerva|Minerva]] and [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] were considered as contenders. The king-prophet Francis-Ilianus also decided to be elected, because “three other candidates are young”, and at the same time claimed the rank of Priory: “at least one of the two elections I will definitely win.” ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]])
- **Adrian and the people.** Adrian conceived the idea of summoning commoners to Faunterra: to set up free trains, to give food and shelter, and to address them with his voice through a hundred recording devices. His written word he added to the appeal of the Church, which was read in the cathedrals. [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] considered it stupid: the people do not participate in the elections, and the Lords of the House will “bitter” if a hundred thousand hoops begin to teach them who to vote for. In Finborough, the townspeople argued whether it was possible, having come to the capital with the money of Adrian, to vote not for him. Lawmaker McFreed Crowe ([[mark-voron|Mark]] by all accounts) observed that the emperor's election "occurs too rarely" and the law does not describe it.
- **Voices of the Church.** The branches of the Church agreed to vote only for the humble candidate. At first, the Lord expected repentance for the use of the Objects. Bishop [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]] then proposed to Minerva the votes of the Church of the Forefathers, if she would sign the list of candidates for the archdiocese without being crossed out: the Forefathers wanted to elect the archpriest before the lord, because the emperor has the right to delete one candidate from the list (the same with the archimper). Minerva signed it. Erwin, in response to Amessin's attacks, directly asked if he did not claim the rank of priest.
- **Peace with Steppe.** The Lords of the House doubted the legality of elections during the war, and the Church demanded that peace be made with the Shavans. Ambassador Stepi Neymir reminded Erwin: “The stage is four votes: two from Reis, two from Holliwell.”
- **Other calculations.** Erwin refused to move his battalions to Faunterra: the rest of the Lords would consider it pressure on the House, and the election would fail. Darkwater's ambassador, Emmon Darklin, promised to give the votes to the land to Francis-Ilian, if Prince Hector would extradite the sorceress, Mireille Nan-Claire. ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]]; [[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]]) Other lords, meanwhile, kept their calculations: Karen Lighthart told Nexia that marrying Erwin would bring him the voices of Hope and Almera.

### November session of 1776
The election of Vladyka Adrian was delayed until November 1776, hoping that the northerners would not have time to get out of their lands. The first secretary of the House, Baronet Dorian Amber, presided. ([[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]]) He determined the order of the speeches and kept a record. Each lord could bring two companies of personal guards into the city, the rest of the troops were withdrawn from the capital. In the morning, the building was opened by an honorary guard of sixteen knights of different lands. On the table of each representative was a folder, writing accessories and a couple of voting flags: to put the question to a vote, four blue flags were enough. For the first time, speeches in the House were recorded on the recording device of [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]] and [[elis-kavendish|Alice Cavendish]] to lose to their people in the squares.

On the first day, Amber's regulations were approved. Each candidate was given a day to speak and answer questions, in the order of Origin, Arden, Nellis-Lyon, Stagforth. Then followed two days of cross-controversy, and on the eighth day, the vote itself. Sixteen subjects voted: fourteen lands and two branches of the Church. According to Adrian's calculations, seven of them were to vote for him: the Land of the Crown, the South Road, Litland, Northwood, the Sunset Coast, Holliwell and the Forefather Branch. These votes he bought by conspiracy to divide the Duchy of Origin.

**Disclosure and ultimatum.** Erwin spoke the same day. He declared Adrian not a tyrant, but an ordinary criminal: from February to April 1775, he was part of a gang that robbed banks in Almera, and kidnapped people. Adrian admitted both charges. Erwin offered to remove him from the election and arrest him, flags rose more than four. Then Adrian raised his hand and clenched his fist. It was a sign of the Shavan of the Golden Grive, who sat walled up in a hall with the Perth of Wilhelm and the Bird without the Cloth. ([[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]]; [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]; [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) Shavan punched through the wall and took aim at Erwin, and then [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], who also had Perst. Adrian declared the election cancelled. He gave the Lords two days to swear: he who does not swear, "will die on the third day." Only General Joseph Gore and a secretary in a white mask followed him through the breach. Prior [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]] recognized Adrian as emperor, but [[minerva|Minerva]] Glove lifted him into the air. If the ruler is Adrian, she said, then her permission to elect a priory, and with it the rank of Amessina, is also invalid. Amessin remained in the hall.

**Siege.** The house was surrounded by the personal guards of the lords, the monks of the brotherhood of Wilhelm and the young men of the Hand of Dodge. ([[ruka-dodzh|Hand Dodge]]) Lords, including Adrian's supporters, remained in the building. Amber held “the first ever meeting of the Chamber with an overnight stay”, and the gap in the wall was laid with stones with the help of the Glove. On the second day, Erwin gave "three Pramatery's advice". His battalions were already traveling by train from Arrowback. He proposed reducing taxes and dissolving the scarlet guard, and leaving the azure companies as honorary guards. The narrator notes that at this moment, Origin won the election. On the third day, Francis-Ilianus spoke. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) Four cardinals came to the House and admitted that they had rigged the election of the Priory in favor of Amessin. Mother Alicia stripped Amessin of her robe, and Minerva with the Glove placed it on the Prophet's shoulders. Francis-Ilianus withdrew his candidacy for the throne.

**The storm and death of Adrian.** When the Vilhelmians withdrew to the cathedral, Adrian threw the scarlet guard of Gore and Dodge's thugs on the Chamber. The Reds broke through the barricade in the positions of the Lithuanians. But in the midst of the battle, Adrian, who had just allowed the Golden Grove to shoot Iona, fell and died in convulsions. By all indications, he was killed by a spark mute [[sofi|Sophie]] from the detachment of Sir [[khamfri|Humphrey]], who avenged the house of Almer. The Golden Grive burned it with a Perst, Humphrey cut his hand, and the crowd trampled the Shavan. [[dzhonas|Jonas]]'s coachman shouted to the crowd that Adrian had "punished Yangmei". From the cry of "Adrian is dead!" the assault stopped, General [[stetkhem|Statham]]'s X-rays counter-attacked, and Gore retreated and fled the capital. The Lords spent another night in the House congratulating Erwin. Adrian's body was never found.

**Break and compromise.** The next morning, the city was entered by kairs with the knights of Earls Arrowback and Flame. Contrary to Amber's rules, the Lords took an unscheduled two-day break, after which Minerva received the floor. She proposed abolishing taxes and charging the Crown for services: education, spark dams, treatment and protection. The Lords applauded. Before the vote, however, the matter does not come to the story. Francis-Ilianus, modeled on the book of the Forefather Haruko, “The Empire of the Rising,” proposed to share power. Minerva becomes empress, Erwin becomes military ruler, "shogun", or Lord Chancellor. The army of the Crown is reduced to one or two regiments, and the House and Church serve as counterweights. The marriage of the Shogun with the Empress was considered undesirable by the lords. Erwin and Minerva agreed. At the same time, the prophet promised Erwin to marry [[robert-oridzhin|Robert Origin]] and [[roza-erroubek|Rose Arrowback]].
