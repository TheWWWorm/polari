**The author's word** collects the statements of [[roman-surzhikov|Roman Surzhikov]] about the cycle "Polars", made in the back with answers to readers' questions. These are the author’s comments, not the text of the books. Sometimes he complements in them what the novels say in passing, and sometimes he directly refuses to reveal anything. Each statement below conveys the words of the author and is marked with the stream on which it was made. Jokes and places that could not be disassembled in the transcripts are either not included here or are specifically specified.

## Cycle design
According to the author, "Polari" was conceived as his "feudal utopia", that is, feudalism in the best form that he is capable of (stream January 7, 2026). The cycle ends with Navi's final monologue and the choice Navi has given the world. ([[navi|Navi]]) The author said that this question he " said goodbye to the world of Polari" and that the question is of concern to him so far. There is no answer to it in [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], because the author himself does not know it. A question worthy of a book, in his opinion, is just one to which the author has no answer (stream January 7, 2026).

The main mysteries, according to the author, were ready while working on the first book. He knew who the Puppet Master was, how he learned to talk to the Objects, and what his plan was. He also knew the secret of the appearance of Objects, the secret of the Mothers and the secret of Navi (stream April 18, 2021). Which of the heroes will survive, the author considers the issue secondary. On the same stream, he admitted that he did not yet know how the fate of some characters would end. To the question “who is the puppeteer?” he joked: the main puppeteer is the author himself, who makes the reader believe in one thing or another (stream April 18, 2021).

In the course of the work, some plans have changed. According to the author, [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] was originally supposed to survive, but died. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]], on the other hand, was supposed to die, but survived (stream January 7, 2026).

The author says that the events of Polari are complex (“plans within plans”). Therefore, he consciously decided to tell them simply, without experimenting with the form (stream January 7, 2026). He likes to believe that the Polari world exists somewhere in a parallel reality. Therefore, he considers the published part of the history of this world and does not rewrite the plot retroactively (spring stream 2026). Once he found "[[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, coin, spark]]" tightened and removed several scenes from it, but the plot did not change (spring stream 2026).

In 2021, the author said that the continuation of the main plot is not planned. Possible prequel or side stories (stream April 18, 2021). In 2026, he said that he would not return to Polari until at least one more world was created, and “certainly not in the coming years.” Books about the distant past, for example, about the struggle of old houses, are possible only after that (stream January 7, 2026). Back in 2021, he called a book about the [[soshestvie|Descent]] one of the options. According to him, he knows how it went, and among the [[pramateri|Foremothers]] there are those about whom he would like to write (stream April 18, 2021).

## Peace and device
**Order against chaos.** According to the author, the Polari religion is built on one main conflict - between order and chaos. The universe spiral that the gods supposedly built is an ordered fragment of the world, separated from the chaotic universe. ([[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]]) Beyond the spiral reigns primordial chaos, and the gods confront it, bringing order to this small world. [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]] is the lord of chaos (streamed April 18, 2021). There, the author promised that in the fifth book with the Dark Ido will fight Navi and this will clarify some aspects of this character. See also the [[tserkov|church]].

**Mothers.** The author speaks of the Foremothers as real historical figures: they have grandchildren and great-grandchildren. This distinguishes them from the patrons of crafts in his new "well world", which is perhaps only a myth (spring stream 2026). Mothers are more important than fathers because they are more important. The fact is that the noble family is counted according to Pramateri (stream January 7, 2026).

**Without Christianity.** According to the author, there was no Christianity in the world of Polari. Therefore, there are no Christian canons of chastity, monogamy, attitude to carnal love as to sin. A woman there is not considered sinful, her only limitation is physical weakness. Feudalism, according to the author, arose naturally: there are few resources, the environment is hostile, weapons are expensive, and therefore there are few warriors, and the society is agrarian and feudal. The Pramatery religion had already existed by that time, so Christianity had nowhere to go (stream January 7, 2026). Altesses, polygamy, and purchased slaves, according to the author, grew out of his irony over the institution of marriage. ([[alter|Alter and Altessa]]) In feudalism, loyalty is dear to him: a vassal oath is taken forever (stream January 7, 2026).

**Women in power.** The opinion that Polari is ruled by women, the author challenged. Of the thirteen lands, only two were ruled by women: Northwood and [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]. [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] wanted to become a duchess after the death of her father, but did not. Female characters he just has a lot of bright, they are more memorable, hence the impression (stream January 7, 2026). Bright female characters, according to him, loves. His rule: of the three characters, at least one is a woman (streaming January 7, 2026).

**Mysticism and magic.** Magic in the spirit of Hogwarts, with wizards and wands, in Polari, according to the author, no. Mysticism, like ghosts or love potions, is deliberately left on the edge of the knowable: as in our world, it is unknown whether it is real. All miracles are kept on the [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects]], but the author left the visions of the Siblings of the Origins with a mysticism that does not have to be explained (stream January 7, 2026). There are no non-human races in the cycle: according to the author, people like to read about people, and elves and orcs are tired of him (stream January 7, 2026).

**Agatha's dilemma.** ([[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]]) The author clarified that it is closer to the Heisenberg uncertainty principle than to Schrodinger’s cat. Agatha influenced events with knowledge of them: conscious refusal of action is also an action, because we know what it will lead to (spring stream 2026). Full knowledge means being responsible for everything, and it is “truly monstrous.” Therefore, ignorance is a natural part of thinking (stream January 7, 2026).

**Spiral of worlds.** The sacred symbol of Polari is a spiral. The author conceives a metaverse, where his worlds lie on different turns of one spiral: Polari is one turn, the world of “Unlucky Chris” is another. Physically, you can not get from Polari to the “world of wells” (stream January 7, 2026; spring stream 2026). According to the author, “Unlucky Chris” is also partly about the “Navi question”: it shows the society from which Navi and [[paul|Paul]] originate (stream January 7, 2026).

## Subjects and primrose
According to the author, the [[pervokrov|firstblood]] is not transmitted to the child at birth. This is "insurance": otherwise the baby could touch, for example, the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]], which the author calls a "deconstructor". Firstblood can be taken only through initiation after puberty. Most mothers have not consciously initiated their children. Those who still initiated, then considered it a mistake, and their children did not initiate their own: to be initiated, according to the author, “not very cool” (stream January 7, 2026). In the time of the Foremothers, initiation was technically more difficult, and by the time of Navi and Paul, it had become easier. Very few are able to initiate, "less than the fingers on one hand" (stream January 7, 2026).

Without initiation, the primrose, once in the body, acts as a medicine and strengthens health, but does not give power over the Objects. So, according to the author, the blood of Iona had an effect on [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]: she treated him, and he never learned to speak with the Objects. No one who could initiate it, Erwin did not meet (stream 7 January 2026). The universal initiation that [[otets-david|Father David]] dreamed of would be, in the author's words, "horror", and it is good that he failed (stream 7 January 2026).

Objects, according to the author, do not disfigure the owners externally. The ugliness of the Sorcerer and the Infirmary with their Objects is not related ("a cool idea, but no"). ([[mik|Mik]]) But they hurt the soul: exorbitant force harms a person who is trying to swallow too large a piece. This is what happened to Harmon and his Light Sphere and almost all of the Item Owners (streaming April 18, 2021). ([[kharmon|Harmon Paula Roger]]; [[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) In full force Items will begin to be used when they learn to use many (stream April 18, 2021).

[[minerva|Minerva]] got her firstblood in the basement of [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]]: either from the torture instruments on which [[paul|Paul]]'s blood dried, or with a drink from the cup. What exactly worked, she does not know (letter of the author, 2026).

## About Heroes
**[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]].** The fan version that Erwin was written off by Erwin Rommel was rejected by the author. The name refers to both Rommel and Erwin Schrodinger, but the author did not draw a biography of Rommel, they have only a common tendency to non-standard tactics. If you look for a prototype, although he did not think, Erwin’s military and political career is more like the way of Gaius Julius Caesar (spring stream 2026). Most of his own traits, the author, according to him, invested in two characters - Erwin and Minerva, although no hero is not written off from it entirely (stream April 18, 2021). Erwin's sense of humor is the humor of the author himself (streaming April 18, 2021). The author, according to him, composed Erwin’s play in about 30–40 minutes by a stopwatch. So he checked whether the hero could have had time to write it among other events (stream April 18, 2021). A hiking trip through the marshes in the first book, according to the author, was the Duke of Desmond's design. ([[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]]) The expedition scouted the way for the railroad, but most importantly was a test that was supposed to make the son an adult (stream April 18, 2021; see Expedition). ([[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]]; [[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) The burning of prisoners in the Beyond and the shooting of the garrison of [[evergard|Evergard]], the author called evil without reservations. Erwin’s villain, however, does not: he knew why he was doing this (stream April 18, 2021; spring stream 2026).

**[[minerva|Minerva]].** Why some readers do not like Minerva, the author ironically called “a question for science”. She has flaws, but all the characters of Polari have them. In that readers admire Erwin's tricks and condemn the same tricks of Minerva, the author sees "appropriated sexism" (spring stream 2026). The reproach that Minerva is Mary Sue and everything is easy for her, he rejected. At first, she is “nobody”, her power is illusory, she is constantly controlled by others. She was able to turn the imaginary power into a real one only by her own efforts, and the whole third book (Spring Stream 2026) is devoted to this. The author called the bloodless pacification of the Uprising of the Snowdrops a definite good (stream April 18, 2021). ([[vosstanie-podsnezhnikov|Snowdrop Rebellion]])

**[[adrian|Adrian]].** The author is satisfied with the character himself, but not with the way he described it: readers see Adrian as either a kind, or a tyrant, or as a tyrant. Adrian was an authoritarian emperor from beginning to end. In the first book we see him in the best light, through the eyes of the world in love. After “[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only one star]]” he has already been overthrown, and people who do not like him tell about him. Hence the apparent change (stream January 7, 2026). Alanis, like Adrian, was raised for power, but after the fall she managed to find a different meaning, and Adrian saw no other way to the end, except for the struggle for the throne (stream January 7, 2026). Who Adrian loved is up to the reader to decide. All three versions are permissible: Mira, [[chara|Chara]] and the goddess from the pendant. ([[kulon-adriana|Adrian's Pendant]]) The goddess, according to the author, he loved “exactly” (stream January 7, 2026).

**Alert.** According to the author, Althessa Anxiety is real. ([[alter|Alter and Altessa]]) Only the question is open, whether it is a ghost or a phenomenon of the Duke’s subconscious. Is Erwin Agatha also left open (stream 7 January 2026).

**[[gvenda|Gwenda]] and Becca.** ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]) Who is Gwenda, the author, according to him, does not know: "and no one knows, and Gwenda herself could not remember." The character's "mysterious past" sometimes means only that the past is not invented ("sometimes a banana is just a banana"). What happened to Becca Litland later, the author also does not know. Her line, according to him, is not cut off, but logically completed: her love for a certain person led to this outcome (stream January 7, 2026).

**Other heroes.** The real love in the cycle the author called the love of Erwin and Iona, as well as [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] and his dog Sagittarius. He considers [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia]] and Desmond, [[karen|Karen]] and [[menson|Manson]] to be beautiful couples (stream January 7, 2026). [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard]], according to the author, is "not quite stupid", but clearly not Erwin's level. He believed in the conspiracy of his brother and sister easily, because he did not get along with them all his life (stream January 7, 2026). Proverbs Deda the author partly composed in advance and then looked for where to insert them. ([[ded|Grandfather]]) For him, this is a rare case of blanks (stream April 18, 2021). [[mark-voron|Mark]]'s detective line is one of his favorites (stream January 7, 2026).

## What the author does not disclose
- **[[alanis-almera|Alanis]]** is the "Duke of Schrodinger". It is not known if she died or survived and what happened to her. The author admitted that the question “what happened to Alanis” “a little tired” (stream January 7, 2026).
- **[[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]** is also a Schrodinger. But with him, unlike Alanis, according to the author, "plus or minus is understandable, read carefully" (stream January 7, 2026).
- **[[navi|Navi]].** What happened to Navi next, the author fundamentally does not say and intentionally did not show this. There are no hints of his fate in [[fantazii|Fantasies]]: the author did not put this there. How Navi Made His Blood Unable to Initiate, “Still Behind the Brackets” Perhaps this will one day be an interlude, but this promise was not made (stream January 7, 2026).
- **Jemis and the Vision.** Whether Jemis chose Sagittarius himself, the author did not confirm: "he chose someone", and "everything was a little more complicated" (stream January 7, 2026; more in the next section).
- In general, the author usually answers “no” to questions such as “will there be more interlude about such and such.” What is unsaid, he wants to leave unsaid (stream January 7, 2026).

## Unwritten and Future Stories
According to the author, there are three interludes that he conceived but did not write (stream January 7, 2026).
1. **Damn it, [[spot|Spot]].** This is a Gogol story about the remote settlement of Spot, from where two guides went on a campaign with Erwin. There's a hell of a place to piss off one particular resident. The author started it after Arrow, Coins, Sparks, but was distracted by Only One Star. Later to write it was already ridiculous, and a brief interlude he put into one of the stories of Grandfather at the end of the “[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]”. ([[pritchi-deda|Grandfather's Parables]])
2. **"Excess".** This is a story about what happened to the children of the Chosen One, that is, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]. The idea appeared at the very beginning of work on “Fantasy”. The author deliberately does not write it, but saves it as an exclusive for the paper edition of Fantasia in Fantlab, just as in the paper Shadow of the Great Tree there is a story about the battle for the First Winter, which is not found anywhere else. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])
3. **"Immortal Grey".** This is the story of how Kair Jemis met the Sagittarius. According to the author, Jemis was notorious for constantly dying of grays, and grays often have sons of other kairs. He was given a puppy that he didn’t really need. One day, the Mother Gloria the Intercessor appeared to Jamisa and offered to choose a living creature that would not die before him, giving a day for thought. Perhaps the author will write this interlude someday.

Another “fantasy”, according to the author, is already thought out, she has a plot, and it remains only to “get the spirit”. He planned to take it after the second part of “Unlucky Chris” (spring stream 2026). In the transcript, its name sounds like "Excess." It's probably the same story about Vittor's children, but the transcript is illegible here. The story "Let's catch him" from "Fantasies", according to the author, says a lot about the World and Iona (stream January 7, 2026).

## As the author wrote the cycle
- **No drafts.** The author thinks through the story in his head and writes immediately white. The chapter is re-read aloud with Dara and ruled once, so the published chapters then do not change (stream January 7, 2026).
- **“Living” characters.** The main criterion of a successful hero for the author is “liveliness”: the character acts by his character, and not by order of the author. The author adjusts the external events so that the collision leads the plot where it is necessary (stream January 7, 2026). Joaquin's thoughts on flowers for Lady Alanis were born right in the course of the letter, "from inside his head" (stream April 18, 2021).
- **Consultants.** The first three books, the author, according to him, wrote without consultants, and studied military affairs on historical works. He later consulted with a lawyer and a military consultant. The fencing scenes are entirely fictional (streaming April 18, 2021). For the Middle Ages to look believable, it needs to be read about "until you start thinking like a medieval man" (streaming April 18, 2021).
- **Tough scenes.** The scene from the “Yanmei Hunt”, where a mother and children are kept in the basement, the author wrote, already knowing his little daughter, and he was in pain. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) The plot did not allow to bypass this scene (stream April 18, 2021).
- **A book without action.** According to the author, there is little action in A Doll on the Throne: there is a lot of investigation and talk, and this is the price of “mega-intrigue”. He himself lacked action, so “Only one star” he likes more (stream April 18, 2021).
