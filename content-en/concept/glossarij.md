The dictionary collects words that occur in the books of the cycle and may be incomprehensible to the reader. For each term, a brief definition is given by the text of the novels and the Polari Land Guide. If you have an article, it is referenced.

## A
- Abbot, abbess - abbot or abbess of the monastery. In Confidential, the action takes place in a monastery ruled by an abbess. ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]) See the [[tserkov]].
- Agate is a small silver coin and the basic unit of account. On the back she has a pen, a symbol of Agatha the Radiant, hence the nickname "pen". See the [[dengi]].
- The Agateans are descendants of the Light Agate, primarily the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]] and [[dom-almera|Almera]]. ([[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]]) They are known to be arrogant and arrogant. Also formed the words "Yanmeans", "Sofievtsy", "elenovka" and others: according to the kind to which a person belongs.
- Acre is a measure of land area. The Duke of [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]] was the ruler of “twenty million acres of land in the heart of Polaris.”
- The Scarlet Guard is the palace guard at Faunterra. It guards gardens, entrances and stairs, is armed with spark spears and is recruited from the best warriors of the Crown Lands. Above it put only azure guards.
- Alter, althessa is a recognized lover of a noble married lady and, accordingly, a recognized lover of a noble man. Altessa Erwin calls his imaginary interlocutor Anxiety. See Alter and Althessa. ([[alter]])
- The Archbishop is the high rank of the Proto-Fatherly branch of the Church. Archbishop Faunterra simultaneously serves as the head of this branch; this rank was carried by [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]].
- The Archpriest is the high priest, the head of the Pramatery Church. During the years of the cycle, this is the elderly Elina. ([[ellina|Ellina]]) See the [[tserkov]].
- Assignations are hard bearer paper money. Unlike the bill, they are not nominal: the amount is printed, not written by hand. This is a rare means of payment: in 1775, most citizens had no allocations, and [[minerva|Minerva]] wanted to replace money with papers with her name. See the [[dengi]].

## B
- The Baron is a noble title below the Count. Thus, the castellans of the First Winter Arthur Hayrock is "a dedicated kair, Baron Hayrock".
- Baronet is a noble title. It is worn, for example, by the first secretary Dorian Amber. ([[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]])
- Swamps are the nickname of the inhabitants of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]]. A man is teased: “You’re talking like a real swamp. In every word, frogs croak."
- Burgomaster is the head of the city administration. In 1775 [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] served as Lady Burgomaster of Faunterra.

## V
- Your Majesty is an appeal to the Emperor or Empress.
- Your highness is an appeal to the princes of blood and the heirs of the throne. This is how the world was praised when she became the first heiress. ([[minerva|Minerva]])
- Your Grace is an appeal to the Duke or Duchess. The Lord Chancellor was also said to have said, “His Lord Chancellor.”
- Your grace is a respectful address to a nobleman, for example, Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]].
- Your Eminence is an appeal to the Priory.
- Your Holiness is an appeal to the archimandrite.
- Vassal is a nobleman who keeps the land from the suzerain and serves it. The reverse is suzerain.
- A bill is a name bank receipt. Money is put in the bank and received in exchange for a bill, and at the place in another branch of the bank they exchange it back for money. See the [[dengi]].
- The **Great Houses** are the thirteen families of "lords-landlords" who rule the great lands of the empire. See The [[velikie-doma]].
- Spring Dawn is a holiday of hope and bright beginnings in the second half of March. See the [[kalendar]].
- The evening song is an evening church "song" by which the time of day is celebrated. “The time of the evening song” is the usual way to make an appointment. See the [[kalendar]].
- Vladyka, the mistress is the everyday name of the emperor and empress: “Our Lord Adrian”, “Lord Minerva”.
- Grandson (granddaughter) Foremothers - so they say about belonging to the genus: "I am Yanmei's granddaughter", "granddaughter of Foremother Susan". See Mothers. ([[pramateri]])
- Wolves are the Shavans and other opponents call the northern Origins.
- A wave is a spark communication over long distances, over wires and stations. News on the wave goes "faster than a bird." See [[iskra]].
- The Crow of the Crown is the nickname of the head of [[protektsiya|the protection]], the secret guard of the emperor. During the years of the cycle, this is the name of [[mark-voron|Mark]].
- The **eighteenth gift** is the gift that the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]] secretly appropriated. The fort contained dozens of items extracted from this Gift.
- A free city is a city with self-government: for example, [[soltaun|Soltown]] and Fishtown are “free cities”, they are managed by a board of merchant seniors, although they pay a tax to the duke. In the free city of [[finboro|Finborough]] from Fantasia, the mayor has the right to preside over the court.
- The Ecumenical Council is a general meeting of both branches of the Church. In 1775, he summoned the prior of Gallard to the ecclesiastical court and suspended his powers. See the [[tserkov]].

## G
- Gallon is a measure of fluid volume. In the rains, “millions of gallons of water” flowed into the low-lying Labelin.
- Gan is a Shavan unit or community under the rule of the Ganta. Before the battle, the Ganta of Biray “had a gang of forty horsemen.” ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]])
- **Ganta** is the leader of the Shavan community, literally "pointing the way". The gaunts obey the leader of the horde. See Shavani. ([[shavany]])
- A guild is an association of artisans or merchants. The mechanics are also listed by guilds: Luis Maria is the "imperial mechanic of the second guild". ([[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]])
- **Gloria** is a silver coin of eight agates, nicknamed "the scoop" for Pramateri's big eyes on it. See the [[dengi]].
- The **pus-ganta** is in the steppe legend a terrible creature surrounded by thousands of flies; according to the Shavans, a servant of the Spirit of the Worm. This name was given to [[paul|Paul]]: “I command death and decay.”
- The talking subject is the subject with which he managed to “talk”: according to Erwin, he is able to change the world. It is believed that all the Objects speak, but their language is not known; knew it the Forefathers and Foremothers, and during the First Dark-eyed dynasty, the sages managed to talk with one or two Objects, and the last of them died thirteen centuries ago (in the early media - seven centuries). See [[svyashchennye-predmety]].
- The **voice of the Crown** is a printed "news book"; it was produced by a printing shop at the University of Faunterra, the largest in all of Polari. See The Crown Voice. ([[golos-korony]])
- Pigeon mail is a way to quickly forward a note on a tiny paper tape that the pigeon carries.
- Gray is a cyre warrior servant in a gray cloak; it is not the same as a Southern knight squire. The Greys fight on foot, and the kair is the overlord of their Grey. A commoner can become a warm, and after initiation - a kire. See Cairo. ([[kajry]])

## D
- The Gift of the Gods is the coming of the Sacred Objects into the world: the earthly solid bursts, and a cave with the Objects opens in a crack. The last known by 1774 was the Seventeenth Gift; for the hidden Eighteenth see above. See [[dary-bogov]].
- Two-coloured is the nickname of kairs by their red and black coats.
- Day of Descent is the main holiday of the year, December 2. On this day in 1774, the [[bitva-pri-piksi|Battle of Pixie]] took place, and in 1775, the Battle of the First Winter. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) See the [[kalendar]].
- Ten, centurion - commanders of tens and hundreds. The Decimer commands a "dozen" in the company, and Salem divided his army into hundreds and dozens.
- The daytime song is a church "song" in the afternoon: "with the daytime song, therefore, at the fourth hour."
- The Steppe Spirit is the spirit worshiped by the Shavans. They are called, “Spirit of the Steppe.” See hordes of wanderers. ([[orda-strannikov]])
- The spirit of the Worm is the lord of evil, decay and decay among the Shavans. He gradually devours the world; in brazen form, the "Spirit of the Worm." See hordes of wanderers. ([[orda-strannikov]])
- Wanderers are the souls of worthy riders from the Wanderers' Horde. They swear by them and exclaim, “Holy Wandering Spirits!”
- A dozen is the usual measure of account: people, ships, riders are divided and considered dozens.
- Inch is a measure of length. If the Lord nominates Hilt from the scabbard "by an inch", it means an accusation or a hard order.

## Ye
- His (your) veneration is an appeal to a high spiritual person, for example, Archbishop Faunterra.
- Elena is a large silver coin, nicknamed the "flashlight" by the pattern on the back. It is equal to eight gloria, that is, 64 agates: the entrance to the flower festival was worth 64 agates (in early media - two agates). See the [[dengi]].
- **Bishop** - the city's spiritual head; "City Bishops" are members of the Council of the Proto-Patriarchal Branch.

## Zh
- Yellow is the thief’s nickname for gold coins.

## Z
- They are the inhabitants of the coast. ([[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]]) They are not Shavan, but “close to them in blood and culture.” See the sunsets. ([[zakatniki]])
- Steppe's Law is Shavan's Law. According to the Ganta Biray, it allows you to escape from the battle.
- The Twelve Commandments are the twelve commandments that the Forefathers brought. The first of them is “Take your place in the world with dignity”, the twelfth is “Let the other be”. See the [[tserkov]].
- The star is the star of the night sky. It is believed that the souls of the dead are sent to the Star: “his soul has already found itself on the Star.” In Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] explains that the Star is a false beacon. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])
- A star is a copper coin for change. “No stars” means “not a penny.” See the [[dengi]].
- The Landlord is the great lord who rules the whole land of the empire. There are thirteen such lords. See The [[velikie-doma]].
- A snake under your tail! - Shavan's brawl.

## I
- The Chosen is the title that was assumed by [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] after his "resurrection".
- Abundance (Days of Abundance) is an autumn holiday in October. See the [[kalendar]].
- The Xs are the kair elite. They wear a completely black robe with a scarlet oblique cross on their chest, similar to the letter X. See Cairo. ([[kajry]])
- An imperial observer is an official whom the Crown includes as part of an expedition beyond the borders of the empire (Constantine's law). Such an observer on the exploit was [[filipp-loufert|Philip Lowfert]].
- Iskra is electricity. It is produced by spark shops and windmills and transmitted by copper wires. See [[iskra]].
- Spark infantry are regiments of the Crown in scarlet shirts with spark spears. Soldiers of these regiments are called sparkers.
- A spark spear is a spear charged with eyes that strikes the enemy with a spark discharge.
- A spark shop is an enterprise that produces a spark, usually at a dam.

## K
- The Yumin Code (Yumin Testaments) are the laws and legal principles of Foremother Yumin that are invoked in court. They treat doubts in favor of the accused, and the madman gets rid of responsibility. In the “Fantasies” according to the code Yumin in Finboro, even a dog was tried.
- Cairo is a Knight Warrior of the North in the service of House Origin. After initiation, he gets the right to wear a red and black cloak. In the appeal, it is placed before the name: "kair Jemis". See Cairo. ([[kajry]])
- **Stone sickness** is a serious illness that [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] suffered from. See [[kamennaya-khvor]].
- The Chapter is the supreme council of the Church of the Foremothers of seventeen supreme mothers. See the [[tserkov]].
- The Cardinal is a prelate of the Prototheric Branch who stands at the head of the county church.
- Castelian is the governor and commandant of the castle. For example, Baron Heirok was a castellan of the First Winter.
- Quarta is a small coin from Confidential. ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]) It is given when it is not enough for the agate, and a half-tin, half of the agate is named next to it. The denomination in the text is not directly named: apparently, this is a quarter of the agate.
- Cypress is a class of powerful spark eyes. Such an eye is discharged by lightning even when applied to iron or skin. He is armed with only one in ten, the most experienced soldier of spark infantry.
- The fang is a huge beast larger and heavier than a bull, with a plate shell in which crossbow bolts get stuck.
- Claws is the travel nickname of the northern army of Origin.
- The constable is a city guard. In Faunterra, constables wear blue vests and bronze helmets.
- The king-prophet is the nickname of [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]], king of Shimmery, who became famous for prophetic dreams.
- The Crown is the imperial power and state: the "army of the Crown", the Land of the Crown. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]])
- Red Earth is the nickname of the Duchy of Almer: local architects love bricks made of red clay. ([[almera-zemlya|Duchy of Almera]])
- The **puppeteer** is the nickname of the Crown's secret enemy, the owner of the Wilhelm's Fingers and the Eighteenth Gift. Under this mask was hidden [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]].
- Kumys is a steppe drink of Shavans.

## L
- The **Azure Guard** is an elite imperial guard in blue armor. Look at the Guardia Azur. ([[lazurnaya-gvardiya]])
- Glaciers are the southern nickname of the northerners. They’re also called “frozen ass.”
- **Lady in the Dark** is the nickname of the blind Queen of Darkwater, Madeline Nan-Claire. See [[ledi-vo-tme]].
- Lying in the dust - in Shavans means "to die"; “To dust” is to kill.
- The **Leed Wolves** are the northern unit of the Origin, in which Captain [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] serves. His place is "in the enemy's rear."
- A cave is a cave that opens in the ground at the arrival of the Gift. Sacred Objects are scattered on her grottoes. See [[dary-bogov]].
- Lord Chancellor is a title [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] took after taking Faunterra. Opponents called it “fictional.”
- The Lord Captain is the rank of noble-commander; this is the title of, for example, Courtney Benefit.
- The bald tails! - Shavan's brawl. They also scold everything that is insignificant: “The bald tail, not the village.”

## M
- The Master is a University scientist. Adrian instructed the masters to study the Subjects.
- Majordom is the steward of the house and servants of the noble lord.
- A small object is a sacred object with a single miraculous property. Small items are not entered in the registers, unlike “big”.
- A master is a craftsman who has come out of apprenticeships. Also politely appeal to the young craftsman: "young master".
- Mother (holy mother) - an appeal to a nun or abbess: "Long years, holy mother."
- Bears are the nickname of the Northwoods and their warriors. See Northwood House. ([[dom-nortvud]])
- A mechanic is an imperial engineer engaged in including rail construction. The mechanics are divided into guilds.
- The townspeople are the citizens, the class of artisans and merchants: "Almera is the land of the burghers."
- My lady, my lord, are the most frequent appeals to a noble lady and a noble man.
- Mile is the primary measure of distance.
- The minister of the court is the court official in charge of the palace and its economy.
- The Minister of Railways is the minister in charge of rail roads. During the years of the cycle, this post was held by Liam Shelier. ([[liam-shele|Liam Shellier]])

## N
- Nezhenka is a nickname given to the young Erwin Origin by the kairs. Later he accepted it himself.
- The bat is a clawed bat, the coat of arms of the Origins. See [[dom-oridzhin]].

## O
- Fireballs are one of two types of Finger of Wilhelm combat. The second type is “Flat”. See [[persty-vilgelma]].
- Washing - washing the body according to the commandments of the Mothers "at least once a week."
- Orge is a bitter northern swill that spreads cones. The best is considered to be lida. They also clean the blade before healing the wound.
- The **horde of Wanderers** is a posthumous army of worthy horsemen in the Shavan faith. She's going to wander into another world when the Worm Spirit devours this one. See hordes of wanderers. ([[orda-strannikov]])
- A squire is an assistant and associate of a knight. In the North, Gray plays this role, but his position is different: “Grey is your master’s dog.”
- From the Descent is the chronology of Polari: "March 21, 1774 from the Descent of the Foremothers". See the [[kalendar]].
- Father (Holy Father) - Address to the priest.
- Eyes (eyes) are small scarlet crystals that store a spark charge for weapons. They cost more than swords, mails and helmets together. They get their eyes in [[shimmeri|Shimmery]]'s mines. See [[iskra]].

## P
- The deceased is the mother of the dead from the bed of the Seventeenth Gift. Paul said, “I had a Mother with me, but she died on the way. They call it the Fallen.” See the sunsets. ([[zakatniki]])
- Padre is a respectful address to the priest in the early editions of Arrow, Coin, Spark: Padre David (Father David); in the 2026 edition, Father David. ([[otets-david]])
- The House of Representatives is a meeting of the Lords Representatives of the Lands. "Sit on a high chair" means to serve as a Representative in the House: on the back of the Representative's chair is the coat of arms of his land, and the backs are made exorbitantly high so that the coats of arms are visible over the heads of the lords. See House of Representatives. ([[palata]])
- Blood is the blood that awakens the objects. See also primrose. ([[pervokrov]])
- The **firstborn** are the highest knowers, the leading kind from the Foremothers. In contrast, the official nobility comes from civil servants. See Originals. ([[pervorodnye]])
- The first of the Five is one of the titles of King Shimmery. Count [[kuindar|Quindar]] is called the Second of the Five. Each of the Five owns a mine that produces spark eyes.
- The first secretary is a high court official with the monarch. Under Minerva, Dorian Amber held the position. ([[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]])
- The Census of Objects is the accounting of Sacred Objects by imperial accountants. Large items are added to the registers.
- Feather is the nickname of the agate from the drawing on the back.
- Perst, the finger-bearer, is Wilhelm's Perst, a sacred weapon worn on his hand as a bracelet, and a warrior who is armed with it. See [[persty-vilgelma]].
- Executioner (master of executions) - the executor of executions and torture, a member of the guild of executors. A city that does not have its own executioner calls a master from another city, sending a deposit and a copy of the sentence. Craft gives the right to conduct and additional business - to keep a brothel or a gambling house. See [[uolter-dzhejn-dzhon]].
- Pinta is a measure of volume. In the campaign, Paul limited the water for the soldiers to four pints per day.
- The whip is the second kind of shot by Finger of Wilhelm: it beats with air and, unlike balls, goes against the wind.
- The Underworld is the world of the gods. The gods of the Underworld send Gifts, and from there, according to the canon, the Forefathers came.
- The sublunar world is the world of humans, Polaris.
- The **Snowdrops** were an army of peasants and burghers that marched on Faunterra in 1775. See [[podsnezhniki]].
- Sliders are a contemptuous Shavanese nickname for sedentary peasants.
- Half a coin in half agate ("half agate" in the palm of the hero "Confident").
- The midday song is the midday church "song" by which time is celebrated.
- The novice is the youngest inhabitant of the monastery. The novice of the monastery of Ulyana Sad was [[minerva|Mira]].
- Right Miriam - by the will of Miriam Dark-eyed, the first night of the girl belongs only to her. See Mothers. ([[pramateri]])
- The rule of the five coppers is the rule of the wayfarers: the train overcomes the rise no steeper than the slope of the foot ruler, put one end on five copper coins. See the railroad. ([[zheleznaya-doroga]])
- Mothers are holy ancestors who came into the world at the Descent. All the nobles lead from them. See Mothers. ([[pramateri]])
- The Pramaternal branch (Church of the Mothers) is the half of the Church that is ruled by the Archimandrite and the Chapter. See the [[tserkov]].
- The Forefather’s branch (the Church of the Forefathers) is the second half of the Church, which is headed by a priest. Its hundred and twelve parts correspond to the number of the Forefathers. See the [[tserkov]].
- The forefathers are the holy forebears of men. According to the number of descended Forefathers, the Church of the Forefathers is divided into one hundred and twelve parts. Together with their mothers, they are called Forebears. See Mothers. ([[pramateri]])
- Spring is the day of March 8, "eight days before Spring Dawn."
- The prelate is the high spiritual person of the Forefather's branch. Its council consists of one hundred and twelve prelates.
- **Priarch** is the head of the Forefather branch of the Church. During the years of the cycle this is [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]].
- Protection is the secret guard of the Crown. See [[protektsiya]].
- Travel fee is a fee for travel over someone else's bridge, city or lowland property.
- The travelers are residents of the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]].

## R
- The Register of Objects is the record books of the great Sacred Objects. Stored in the Cathedral Citadel. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]])
- The Rays, the Hillmen, are natives of Reis and residents of Holliwell, the ancestral enemies. Holmen despise the reevs for wildness and worship of the horned beast.
- The genus (generic name) is an indication in the full name of the great-grandmother: "Erwin Sophia Jessica of the lineage of Agatha." The number of names is an indicator of fertility.
- A rose is a class of small spark eyes. This eye is placed on the arrow of a lady's self-shooter.

## S
- The **gardeners** are a secret order, according to legend, founded by four Forebears. See [[sadovniki]].
- A shotgun is a small crossbow. There are also spark shots with an arrow charged with the eye.
- Holy Gods! is the most frequent exclamation. They say, "Holy Gods!"
- Sacred numbers are numbers that are considered sacred, such as four, eight, and thirty-two. Sacred and eight, and the Foremothers have the number seventeen.
- The Holy Spiral is a symbol of the Church. ([[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]]) "Create a spiral" - to overshadow yourself with a sacred sign.
- **Sacred Objects** are gifts of gods endowed with miraculous properties: they do not burn, do not break, and “speakers” act. See [[svyashchennye-predmety]].
- Northern law is the unwritten code of the North. The North is united in the face of adversity, honors its traditions, and is ruled by warriors and lords. According to northern law, kair is the sovereign master of gray.
- A seminarian is a student of theological seminary.
- The **Seventeenth Gift** was the Gift that came to [[dom-shejland|Shayland]] in 1756. [[paul|Paul]] came out of his bed.
- The sickle is the weakest feature in strata, the "peasant". In a figurative sense, this is called a small pawn in someone else's game. See Stratemes. ([[stratemy]])
- The syzy mora is a deadly epidemic: “the sick have blue muzzles and lips swell.” See The Blue Sea. ([[sizyj-mor]])
- Cheese is an appeal to the knight: "**Sir** Clive".
- Squire is a young companion and assistant to the knight.
- Glorious - respectful treatment and honorable epithet: in the North so called fearless warriors, in Shimmery - successful merchants.
- Dogs are the mocking nickname of imperial officials.
- The sorceress is the nickname of Gloria.
- Sofiina days are the summer holiday of Pramateri Sofia at the end of June, the time of weddings and engagements. See the [[kalendar]].
- The **descent** is the coming of the Foremothers and the Forefathers to the sublunar world, from which the chronology is based. See The Descent of the Mothers. ([[soshestvie]])
- The capital is a resident of the capital, Faunterra.
- Stratemes is a military board game on the field with chips of two colors. See Stratemes. ([[stratemy]])
- Sir, madam - general polite treatment.
- The sons of the Steppe are what the Shavanovs call Paul ("The sons of the Steppe served me by faith and truth").

## T
- Your Mother! is a rude exclamation of amazement.
- **[[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]]** is the master of evil in the imperial faith. He is credited with all the bad things: "Eh, the Dark Ido pulled me ..." In the Shavans, the Spirit of the Worm takes its place.
- Tirya ton of shooting - farewell words of Shavans over fallen, even enemy ones.
- Troopers are the nickname of Horis soldiers from the Fallen cult who eat the dead. ([[oris|General Oris]]) See the sunsets. ([[zakatniki]])
- Darkness is the most common swearing, especially among the northerners: "Darkness eat!", "Darkness would eat such news."
- A tractor is a spark locomotive that pulls a train. See the railroad. ([[zheleznaya-doroga]])

## U
- An ounce is a small measure of weight. In two hundred and twenty years, the Sword of the Voice of Winter has lost “three and a half ounces.” See [[glas-zimy-mech]].
- The morning song is the morning church "song", it is beaten off by the bell. They set up early meetings.

## F
- The lantern is the nickname of the elena from the drawing on the back.
- Freilina is the Empress's court lady. The first lady is the queen.
- A pound is a measure of weight.
- Foot is a measure of length.

## Kh
- Hanida vir kanna is "worthy to see": Paul's chosen Shavans. See also primrose. ([[pervokrov]])
- Halliwell is a breed of Western war horses. Such horses are highly prized, by the name of Holliwell County. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]])
- Chronometer - the exact clock; they check the time in battle and in the campaign.

## Ch
- The cupbearer is the court servant filling the cup of the Lord. This position in the court is hereditary.
- Black is a dismissive name for the common people.

## Sh
- The jackal is one of the most offensive Shavan insults: "bad cowardly jackal."
- Shavan is originally a "shepherd". Now this is the name of a skillful and fast warrior-horseman of the Great Steppe. See Shavani. ([[shavany]])
- The sheriff is the head of the city guard and constables.

## Shch
- Shield, one-shielded - knight's vestment. The knight passes his "shield" to the eldest son; villages and flax are "one-sided", "three-sided".

## E
- An emissary is a special envoy of the Crown or the Church. So, the emissary of the church court is the “eye of the archimatry”.
- Hilt (coin) is a gold coin of 256 agates. For one hilt, pilgrims could eat for a couple of months. See the [[dengi]].
- Hilt (Eternal) is the sacred dagger on the Lord's belt. It is removed completely from the sheath only during the coronation and upon receipt of the Gift. See [[vechnyj-efes]].

## Yu
- Julian law (Julian code) is the imperial code of laws of Julian the Great. ([[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]) Thus, rebellion on it is punished by burning, death from a click or quartering. See Julian's Law. ([[yulianov-zakon]])

## Ya
- Yangmei are descendants of Yangmei the Merciful; the imperial race also belongs to them. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) See Yangmei Dynasty. ([[dom-yanmej]])
- Yard is a measure of length.
