The box from Beyond is a round metal thing found in the hands of a dead man, with whom a “wild death” occurred. This is the first meeting of the heroes of the cycle with what was happening behind the River, long before it became known about the bed of the Eighteenth Gift, Fort [[paul|Paul]] and the Persians. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]])

## Nahodka
On June 6, 1774, Erwin's expedition crossed [[reka|the River]], and on the east bank, scouts found a corpse under the ash. The dead man sat, leaning his back to the trunk, but it was difficult to call his body: the skeleton was covered with dry gray “dirt”. It was flesh, dried and cracked, “the corpse of a man who was squeezed like a lemon and then dried.” Meat became a gray grainy substance sticking to the bones, "like plaster", and the insides became gray clots under the ribs. The animals and worms did not touch the body: it dried up too quickly. Philipp Lawfert puked, the Greeks whispered, "Wild Death."

The grass under the feet of the dead man also turned to gray dust, and the bark of the ash behind his back grayed, withered and fell away in places. Erwin came up with an idea he couldn’t get away with: an unhappy dry-up alive — crouched under a tree, feeling pain, and... The dead man was wearing leather pants and boots that seemed strangely familiar to Erwin, "such he had already seen, and even more than once," and a crumpled leather jacket was lying behind his back: apparently, she was sleeping off her shoulders when the flesh withered. There were no shirts, bags, or other items nearby.

Between the palms of the corpse that fell on his hips, Erwin noticed the thing and pushed it from under the dead hand with a stick.

> He looked like a round metal box covered with gray scabs.

It seems that this was the last thing the unfortunate held in his hands - perhaps he squeezed the box at the time of death. Erwin would like to wash it and examine it, but did not venture to touch it and did not order anyone to do so.

## Erwin's Guesses
Captain [[teobart|Theobart]] rejected fire (the charred flesh is black, and the burnt one folds into a ball, and this one sits flat) and lye (it dissolves, not dries). Erwin reasoned that the man had died no more than a month ago — otherwise the snow and the April rains would have washed away the gray dust — and that the companions took his belongings, but were afraid to touch the body and did not even remove the good boots. So there was something valuable in the bag, “that was worth the risk.” From the body led two chains of traces: one to the north-west, to the Gulf of the River, where it was lost in the water, the other to the south-east, into the forest. Then the army went out to the stumps, cut down with iron axes, and the dugouts, where twenty or thirty people wintered, and then to the bed of the Gift.

On the way, Erwin shared with mechanic [[luis-mariya|Louis]] the main mystery:

> “What was in the box?”

The dying man sat down under the gum himself and took in his hands not a sword, not a knife, not a cup of wine or medicine, but an iron box. Erwin could not imagine that a person would need something contained in a box at the last minute. The pills are not stored in iron — it rusts; money is carried in purses, not in tins; and it is difficult to imagine a forest savage with a silver box. “If there were a criminal among us, I would certainly have sentenced him to open this box,” Erwin concluded. Louis remained silent and looked depressed.

## What was that?
Directly about the dead under the gum in the cycle is no longer spoken of, but in the “Shadow of the Great Tree” (the chapter “Spark — 6), [[navi|Navi]] explained to [[minerva|Minerva]] where such corpses were taken to the Beyond. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]) Rosa Happiness, the “cleaner” who sterilizes Objects, becomes a deconstructor if the frequency of her radiation is reconfigured. You can do this in Polaris only through the firstblood: enter a drop of dew into the veins of the initiated person and give a series of complex commands.

> “Paul introduced the victims to Rosa Happiness and ordered them to say strange words. They talked and then dried up like mummies.”

Navi would calculate the right frequency in an hour and without casualties, but [[paul|Paul]] picked it up by trial and error, and every mistake cost his life. “It is not known how many mummies remain in the forests of the Beyond...” Shayland sent slaves for experiments; for Paul, who quenched his thirst, these experiments were a “special delicacy.” The last test subject also died, but otherwise: the particles of the firstblood were “annihilated, turning the vessels into sieve”, and Paul realized that the deconstructor had been found. Then he took Rosa of Happiness himself and reconfigured her in his blood.

It all comes down to the fact that the dead man under the gum is one of these victims:
- He withered just like Doug's guard in Now You're a Witcher, who died of Rosa Happiness in 1756: Doug's body became a dry mummy, from which gray dust soared when struck. ([[teper-ty-koldun|Now You Are a Sorcerer]])
- He died in the spring of 1774, two steps away from the sacked Lodge of the Eighteenth Gift and from Fort Paul. In the storeroom of the fort, a detachment of [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] and [[mark-voron|Mark]] found the same dead man - with bones covered with "gray granular substance" - sitting on a pile of Objects, and on the Cape at the River among those killed in the fort were "dried".
- Around him died grass and bark, and his companions did not dare to touch the body, but carried away things: the experience, apparently, put right in the forest.

What was in the box is unknown. Perhaps it was a vessel with a Rosa of Happiness or an Object that the victim kept on order, but the text does not say this.
