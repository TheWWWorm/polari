## Symbol of Power

Eternal Hilt is “the symbol of the centuries-old power of the Dynasty sent by the gods.” The emperor wears it on his belt, but only on exceptional days: at the coronation, the beginning of the war and the trial of the original. Even to push the blade a couple of inches means, according to tradition, to give the said great importance. The coronation ritual includes the transfer of Hilt to the new lord and the exposure of the blade at the minute of laying the Star Crown. The handle of the dagger is depicted on a gold coin - the hilt.

## Under Adrian

### Joke at the ball
At the opening of the Feast of the Abundant Summer of 1774, [[adrian|Adrian]] with a bored look put Hilt up by a third and immediately slipped back - the "Clace-Clace" - when two dukes whispered in his ears, and the pretender to the bride smoothed him with a languid look. The whole room froze, wondering what the naked Hilt meant. Only [[minerva|Mira]] realized that it was a joke on the pleasing crowd, and, having drunk five glasses before, laughed loudly; Adrian answered her with a smile. Later, writing the image of the hidden pretender to the throne, Mira represented him with a crown on his head and the Eternal Hilt on his belt.

### Fight with Shavan
In "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]", the Western ambassadors of [[rejs|Reis]] County came to the palace and shed the blood of the vassal Adrian. The emperor ordered the Eternal Hilt to be presented, belted himself with them and promised to break the West with “the power of honest iron”, without the Rings and Kairs, and then summoned the multi-eyed Shavan to a duel with battle swords. Having received three shallow wounds, Adrian cut the opponent's wrist to the bone, extracted the Eternal Hilt from the scabbard and struck the last blow. Lifting a bloody blade to the eyes of the two surviving Westerners, he let them go with the news of the Steppe Fire; the scarlet drops did not hold on the three-sided blade.

### Habits of the Lord
Manson noticed how Adrian thoughtfully let Hilt out of his sheath on his finger and scatteredly drives him back, and how, giving the order "Five days", carelessly clicks on him with his finger. Under the rule of Ulyanina Dust, the look of the jester was drawn to everything sharp in the cabin of the train - including Hilt on the emperor's belt. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]])

## The fate of Hilt after Beck

After the crash at Bake (December 1774), the disappearance of Hilt, together with the body of the lord, created, according to the courtiers, a “dangerous embarrassment” for the coronation of Minerva: the ritual demanded that the new lord Hilt be handed over and the blade exposed, and a workaround was sought for the ceremony. ([[krushenie-na-beke|Beck Train Disaster]]) Later, Hilt was found on the body taken for Adrian’s corpse, along with a medallion with a “divine woman” (Adrian’s Pendant); according to the agent, they could only be worn by the lord. ([[kulon-adriana|Adrian's Pendant]]) In fact, according to Manson, Adrian himself tethered Hilt and the pendant to the drowned captain Grace. The purified Hilt became Minerva's regalia, and she wore it on her belt: "just a thing, a symbol - but with it she felt" differently.

In "A Doll on the Throne", taking the oath of Moran of the Steppe Fire, Minerva pulled Hilt out of her sheath by herself in anger and waited for the oath, holding her fingers on his handle. ([[moran|Moran Steppe Fire]]) At the trial of Manson, fishermen were checked whether they saw Eternal Hilt on the "general": Minerva put a dagger behind the screen among other knives, and one witness, a fisherman nicknamed Beard, was so afraid to touch the shrine that he laid his hands behind his back, Minerva had to touch the knives herself in turn until he nodded. Fishermen in general identified Hilt Dynasty correctly: according to Minerva, only one witness was wrong in only one knife.

Hilt remains a measure of the legitimacy of power and later. General William Davey said that he would obey the order of the “bearer of the Eternal Hilt.” ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) The originals, urging him not to interfere, reminded him that the Eternal Hilt is on the belt of Minerva. Adrian wrote to Lady in Darkness that "with the Eternal Hilt on the belt" the Tree would grow in a few years, and [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]] concluded that since Adrian did not have Hilt, Minerva was still the empress.

## Navi and Hilt

For the first time seeing Minerva, the young Navi immediately noticed Hilt on her belt and looked at him with regret. He warned that the Eternal Hilt is “the most dangerous thing in inept hands”: “The laws of thermodynamics are no joke.” In The Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] asked to be given Hilt, promising that Paul would then die "with a probability of ninety-two and a half percent." He described the Object as "a three-sided transparent Object, similar to a dagger. Entropy mirror. Demon of the second kind. Symbol of the Brilliant Dynasty", and Minerva sent him to the doctor. Navi later explained why he would not teach her Hilt:

> “It kind of cancels one law of physics — or rather, allows you to ignore it in a certain amount of space-time.”

Scalpel and Worm, according to him, you can kill people alone, and Hilt - boil Hanai or turn the palace into a block of ice; Minerva with Hilt in her hands is like a baby running a racing line-up. Then she realized, “why Hilt is not accepted to be removed from the sheath.”

When the mined Light Sphere was brought to Minerva, Navi grabbed Hilt - it was freezing cold, the flame was burning - he broke the casket, threw the Sphere out the window and fled himself in the same way. Minerva was sure: “Nathaniel is anyone, but not a thief” — and indeed, Hilt was soon found on a bench in the palace park. When the horde was coming to Faunterra, Minerva asked the counselor what the shavana would want to take first, and heard: "The Imperial Treasury, the Crown and Eternal Hilt. Another Glove of Power.” Leaving the capital before the arrival of Adrian, she took the treasury, the crown and Hilt with her.

## The Battle for the First Winter

On December 2, 1775, before a fight in Cosuli's grotto, Minerva removed Hilt from her belt and put it into Navi's hand. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) In the underground chapel, Hilt attracted and “drinked” the fiery flashes of Paul’s [[persty-vilgelma|finger]], pouring out light, and then spewing a flame similar to a fiery sword. A whirlwind swirled around the blade, a frost appeared on the floor: Navi threatened to “freeze everything to absolute zero”, remaining in the calculated “compensation zone”. Before the deconstructor exploded, he put Hilt in the sheaths. When Navi, who had fallen into the abyss, was lifted up, Minerva took Hilt off him and put it on her belt again.

Minerva later admitted that she herself thought of using Hilt, although he could destroy the whole city, and that she was stopped by Navi. In the negotiations in the First Winter, Adrian demanded that Hilt be extradited along with the Glove, the Rings, Nathaniel, and Minerva herself. The deconstructor does not threaten Hilt: Paul did not touch him.

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, Hilt remains with Minerva as a sign of her power until the election of the emperor. In the summer of 1776, Iona, having quarreled with Minerva because of the clinic, reminded her brother that she “only temporarily wears Hilt.” In the story “Let’s catch him” a drowned woman, dragged out of the lake at the First Winter, recounted the complaint of the “Sprayed Lady”: the lake does not know peace, because “this frozen the water of Hilt” during the battle.
