In Polari drink a lot, and the drink is usually seen where a person comes from. The Northerners drink the Orj and the Khanty, the common people and the soldiers - Kosukha, the south and the capital - wine, and Shayland - beer. Ronald Wigham’s father’s rule was: “A good cupboard knows sixteen marks of hanty, thirty-two marks of orge, and sixty-four marks of wine.” Also, the cup should remember the weather of the last twenty harvests “in the eight main wine regions”.

The section “Like” in each paragraph is the observation of a wiki: the author himself does not name real analogues to these drinks in books, remarks and interviews.

## Orge
The main drink of the North. They drink it on every step of the ladder, from kairs to dukes. They welcome guests, warm them, treat them and wash their wounds. Already in the second chapter of [[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]], in the First Winter, [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] is sutured with "a cup of Orja - a bitter potion smashing with cones." To drink with his father for the meeting is a "cursed tradition", and Erwin frowns: "Despicable, vile Orge". Later in the Beyond, wounded, he breads the Orja, washes their wound and knife and already thinks differently: "Blessed coniferous watering!" Orge is transparent: in [[yanmejskaya-okhota|the Yanmei Hunt]], Master Mullin "was drinking something transparent — it seems like a Leed Orge."

The best ordge is made in Leed: "Who does not know the noble Leed swords, and the Leed ordge that warms the soul..." ([[lid|Leed]]) In the Leed inn, [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] and [[martin-shejland|Martin]] were served it in leads - these are "funny wooden cups: square outside, round inside." Ordge's a hard man. Wigam banked for the special case of "twenty-year-old Leed", and [[minerva|Minerva]] served twice as cheap Belomorye instead of Leed. In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], the Castelians offered the Empress in the First Winter three varieties: the "white Ordge of Baron Statham of sixteen years of age", the traditional Leed and local "Joy of Agatha", twenty-four years old. ([[artur-khajrok|Arthur Highrock]]) Minerva chose "The Joy of Agatha": "The taste of the North has poured into her throat - the purest and most unclouded."

According to Wigam's observation, Minerva drinks a cheap orge when he's longing, and wine when he's happy. She herself in "The Shadow of the Great Tree" sends the Orge back: "Orge invigorates, and I need to sleep! Give me some sweet wine!” The Northerners believe in its healing power - in the "Shadow of the Great Tree" the question is: "Why do the Northerners believe that all diseases are treated by the Orge?" There is also a fake Ordge. Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collector's edition tells about the capital nobleman in the province: "The Orge he drank, though he was in a Leed bottle, but the weld was here, behind the village, in the mill, and this is not an Orge, in fact." ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) And Erwin, ruling in the capital, among other holidays, organized the “Festival of the Orja”.

It is similar to (observation): to taste - to gin or Dutch wife-lover. They are also transparent, bitter and smell juniper "bumps", and the ginseng has aged varieties. In terms of its role in the culture, the Orge is closer to Scotch whiskey: it is a drink of the proud mountainous North with the famous “little homeland” (Leed), stamps and years of aging. As a medicine and a means for wounds, it is used as vodka in Russia.

## Hunty
Strong spicy tincture from [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]]. In the first book, Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] pours Mira into a wooden cup of "spicy odorous liquid" with the words: "Hanti is the best medicine for the soul. Have a drink, child.” The world is drinking, and the throat is burning. Old Grimsdale's Hunty is "a smelly thick drink of oak bark color." It is also famous for the hanty of Fox Dol. ([[lisij-dol|Fox Vale]]) In "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]" detective Mac notices that he is "expensive for a simple coachman", and, smelling the flask, recalls Sybil: "Sniff - so you will remember Lady Northwood, the rest of Ulyana her soul..." ([[mark-voron|Mark]]) The Northwood hanty is sold in a bottle with a green bear.

Erwin advises [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] to move away from "bumble" Almeran wine to stronger drinks, "like an orge or a Northwood hanty. They are bitter, but never vomit.” [[kharmon|Harmon]], dreaming of wealth, swears to drink "only the best drink - the Northwood Hunt or the Orge from the First Winter." Minerva, and in Fantasia and [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] drink coffee with hanty.

It looks like (observation): a herbal bitter or balm like Riga, "Egermeister" or "Becherovka", as well as Russian spicy tinctures. There is a spicy smell, dark “oak” color, density, bitterness and fame of “medicine”. Coffee with hanty resembles coffee with balm or Irish coffee.

## Kosukha
Cheap murky tincture of the common people: soldiers, artisans, the poor. In "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]" Minerva in Waymar asked for wine from the watchmen, and she was explained: "Well, the hair... The tincture is so... You’re going to bread on your feet.” She was given a “turbid yellowish liquid.” From one of her stench on her eyes turned tears. " However, Mira later recalled how she drank scab with Shayland's soldiers. Wigam's teacup, that confession was terrifying. In [[yolka-episkopa|the Bishop's Christmas Tree]], the shoemaker's workshop smells of conifers and "burnt from cheap scab." In Fantasies, [[khamfri|Humphrey]] and Sally's father, a former gunman, drink cod, and in Almer, Sally says, "girls her age are not allowed to buy strong drinks." ([[salli|Sally]])

It looks like (observation): moonshine or sivuhah. It is a murky, smelly, cheap and very strong drink, and the soldier's explanation of the name ("scrolls off his feet") is consonant with the Russian "squirrel".

## Wines
**The Southern Way.** From the coast of Grace is a white dry "Dance of the Dolphin" of the 1769 harvest from grapes from leeward slopes blown by a sea breeze. At the tasting in [[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]], the glasses were filled with “a surprisingly clear drink with a light spark of gold,” and the sheriff responded: “Salty! “Sweethearts to him!” On the military map of the Southern Way is a traffic jam that does not allow the North “to go to a good wine: the lands of the Crown”.

**[[shimmeri|Shimmery]].** According to the "Guide", the Shimmers have to hedonism "beautiful climate, abundance of wine and sweets, warm sea, wealth", and the table serves "light cold wines". In the garden of Lumini in [[faunterra|Faunterra]], from some sources, “white wine flowed instead of water.” Royal wine "The Happiness of the Lion" is made on the southern slope of the mountains above the Valley of the Lion's Gate, from "the sunniest grapes in the world"; the sheriff calls it "Golden wine for the most beautiful ladies!". The Shimmery wine-making town of Melison lost its former owner in the "Yanmei Hunt": three consecutive sunny autumns the grapes gained "excess sweetness", and Baron Monat Erlin did not tolerate sweet wines. Archbishop Shimmery [[kuindar|Quindar]] in Fantasies refused to "count as wine the liquid served to the table" and promised to send eight barrels of Shimmery to the town of Winslow.

**[[almera-zemlya|Almera]].** According to the Guide, Almera produces wine and beer. Almer wine, according to Farway, is "very... mmm... hoppy", and Harmon calls it "sour."

**Others.** "Tears of the night" from the distant island of Valcridi - thick and ruby, with notes of almonds from volcanic soil. He was kept in the cellars of Abelheim for five years “and one year in barrels in a ship’s hold.” The wine from the durman berries from the tops of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]] is insidiously drunk and dark: "The wine had the color of a night lake." Wigham brings Minerva an Orcadian kuado of seven years' exposure. In Fantasias, Mac and Lord Fellion drink the "remarkable tart kuado", and [[navi|Navi]], who has been weaned from wine, is "Kissy...". Wigam also mentions mianti and "Scarlet tear", which, in his opinion, has a bitter aftertaste. The cheapest wine at court is an amino from Marengo, but there is also "an amino from Leongard, a five-year aging". For Wigam, Minerva's "fine northern taste" divides wines "even into two varieties: light and strong."

The wine in Polari is also medicine and warming. The doctor tells the sick Mira to drink red wine, in the campaign Erwin drinks "a cup of hot wine with ginger and pepper," and [[obri|Aubrey]] promises him "tea with wine according to the Belomorye recipe."

Looks like (observation): Shimmery with her hedonism, sunny slopes and sweets resembles Italy and Spain, and "light cold wines" are their dining whites. The salty white “Dance of the Dolphin” from the sea coast is similar to coastal whites like albarinho or assirtico from Santorini. "Tears of the night" from the volcanic island, ripened in the ship's hold, resemble Madera, which was once sustained in long sea voyages. Hot wine with ginger and pepper is mulled wine.

## Beer and ale
According to "The Traveler", there are many bars, pubs and taverns in Shayland, and "It is no wonder that it is in Shayland that the best brewers in Polari are found." El drinks everywhere: guineas on the march, Harmon in the inn. The habitation of the Road Pillar in "Only One Star" faded when Count Arrowback paved the way from Fliss to Aleridan and opened his brewery: "Count's beer is tastier than the monastery." Harmon, looking at the interrogation of prisoners, drank cheese pie with a “humble monastery ale.” In poor taverns serve "sour swill, given here for ale". In the "[[poleznye-lyudi|Useful people]]" station hotels invite guests of the coronation: "Passengers punch at the expense of the hotel!"..

Looks like (observation): Shayland, with pubs and a befriending cult behind a barrel of beer, is similar to England and Ireland, and the rivalry between the monastery and county beer is reminiscent of ancient European monastery brewing.

## Freaks of the outskirts
- Drome is a drink of the [[folta|Folta]] sailors. It’s “something wild: a terrible color liquid, to taste — like a mixture of nuclear scab, coconut milk and bull’s blood”, tincture of droma, “a humongous foltic fruit that does not grow anywhere else.” To reconcile, she drank it with the Foltians Lady Sophia in "[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]" - "the rarest abomination to taste." ([[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Jessica Augusta]]) Similar to (observation): island fruit distillates, such as Goan fenies from cashew fruits, or tropical coconut drinks like arak.
- Kumys is drinking [[shavany|Shavans]]. Erwin in the steppe suffered from “this milk, soured so much that it could not deteriorate more.” An analogue here is direct: this is a real kumis, which fermented the nomads' mare's milk.
- Darkwater, according to the “Guide”, is a supplier of spices, potions and “unusual alcoholic beverages”.

Not a drink, but a fool is an echiota, the tincture that [[menson|Manson]]'s jester was fond of.
