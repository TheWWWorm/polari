Iskra is the electricity of the Polari world. Its discovery has changed the life of the empire in recent centuries. Even when the Tenth Gift was extracted, "the spark power was not then known", and the pumps rotated the windmills. And the city of Arden, almost five centuries frozen in development, “reborn again with the discovery of a spark”: it turned out that Hanai near Arden is ideal for a dam, and at the end of the last century it was built. Agatha the Radiant is credited with the prophecy of the discovery of a spark, a printing press and rails.

## History
According to [[minerva|Minerva]], the "spark effects were discovered" a century before the Lord Lexian the First, but for a long time remained "a purely theoretical object". Lexian, who was fond of the sciences, “turned this hobby into profit” and laid the foundation for the development of spark shops. The Polari Lands Guide says that the romance of Boundary Expansion has given way to the "sparkling brilliance of the Science Ages." In the "Janmei Hunt" about the middle of the XVII century, after the Horse Wars, it is said: "Masters studied the habits of a new, almost divine force - sparks. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) The mechanics were building spark machines, the engineers were looking for ways to transfer the spark over a distance." In Fantasias, Agatha the Radiant predicted a spark, and in Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] assured that about magnetic fields and induced currents "More Agatha the Radiant wrote about this." ([[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]])

The novel has penetrated even the most conservative places. Nothing changed in the Citadel for two hundred years, except for the spark lighting held half a century ago. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) According to Lady [[karen|Karen]], the pace of "spark reforms" was the eternal cause of Adrian's disputes with his father: "In order to cover the whole of Polaris with rails and wires, Vladyka Telurian set aside a period of one hundred and fifty years," and Adrian wanted to keep up with his rule. The years of rule of Telurian, Adrian's father, Erwin, in a speech before the House described: "We are accustomed to perceive progress as a good. More sparks, more light, more heat.” According to the Guide, the Land of the Crown is the first richest land of the empire, and the spark technique in them is best developed.

## How to make a spark
Sparks are produced by spark shops ("sparks"). The mechanic Luis Maria explained it this way: the dam raises the water, the water falls through many channels and rotates the wheels, the wheels rotate the shafts of spark machines, and "It's all about magnetic alloy bars" fixed on their disks. ([[luis-mariya|Louis Maria]]) On copper wires, the spark diverges to consumers. Few people know how to build workshops, and the Crown disposes of them as a political tool. Litland, for example, does not have a spark and receives a spark from the Crown Lands. The right to build a shop is the mercy of the Crown. Even before the Shutov plot, [[menson|Manson]] tried to give the House of Lighthart a loan of half a million ephesas to a spark shop in Hope, on behalf of the Crown, but the emperor revealed fraud. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]; [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) In 1774, Erwin hoped that the Duke of [[henri-farvej|Farway]] would support the emperor, "bribed by a spark shop". According to the Guide, there are two spark shops in Hope; one of them Farway promised in the dowry of [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]. The Dukes of Almera Astor and Aiden, having received the right to build spark shops, made them a “catalyst for the development of industry”: banks gave loans for new production, factories paid for spark energy, and Almera came in second place in the empire in wealth. At Telurian, a third spark shop was built on Canaan. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]'s father sent him to an exploit to find a spot for a spark dam on [[reka|the River]]. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) Sparkling cars rotate and windmills on the masts: such stood in the castle of the First Winter, and in the mysterious fort behind the River. Not every river is suitable for a dam. According to the Guide, at the beginning of the 18th century, the mechanics of the Almera studied Halliwell, and the Shavans, angry at the idea of blocking the sacred river, fought for six months with the Duchy. The mechanics came to the conclusion that the workshop on Holliwell is impossible: the river is too powerful. This war was nicknamed the Fool, and the expression "to fight for the dam" entered the proverb.

## Weapons and "eyes"
The main military use of sparks - spark spears, daggers, swords and crossbow bolts, charged with "eyes". These are small red crystals that store charge. The fighter presses the lever under the guard and touches the enemy, and the eye is discharged. Then it is charged again in a special device at the gunner. Sparkling weapons are more expensive than swords and armor together. Raw eyes are mined in the mines of [[shimmeri|Shimmery]]: each of the five families of the Council of Five owns the mines and secret cutting. The narrowed eyes are divided into classes: the smallest "lily" is only deafening and suitable for daggers and ceremonial swords, the "rose" goes to battle swords and swords, the "chrysanthemum" - to long spears of spark infantry, and the most powerful "cypress" (they are armed only every tenth experienced infantryman) is designed to pierce the latin system. The purchase of eyes was a Crown monopoly. She purchased them from Shimmery, and breaking this monopoly in 1775 caused controversy in the House and the War of [[dom-labelin|Labelin]] with Shimmery.

The core of the Crown Army is spark infantry: regiments in red shirts with double-toothed spark spears. The Northerners at the beginning of the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]] saw it as the main threat, and later captured captured captured spears and bought their own eyes. Over the River Erwin's exploits were attacked by a detachment of people in scarlet shirts with spark spears.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collectible edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark corrects the description of the spear. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) According to her, the "double tip" is two side darts with eyes that the spring fires. Iskravik hits from five to ten yards and aims not at the rider, but at the horse: the deafened horse falls, maiming the rider, and does not reach the line. The spear itself then kills the survivors. The coat of mail, Karen adds, has not been worn by spark infantry since the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]].

Former sparklers, she said, say to “feed” the eyes, not “charge”, not to be confused with a crossbow. It is not easy to do this in a foreign city: the spark is not brought to every house, and two people have a charging machine for the whole city. In Naitrok in the [[zolotaya-vojna|Golden War]] there were three of them, and the owners at a fair price fed the eyes of both the hopefuls and the Almerians; after the war one of them was hanged.

## Spark and Politics
The sincere cause has become a powerful force outside the yard. In 1775, the Spark Guild was named "the most influential in the capital"; its mechanical masters make pumps, water boilers and other machinery. According to the Guide, [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] contains 90% of the spark eye deposits, making the kingdom's economy and sovereignty nearly invulnerable.

The emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] was called “the lord of science”. He dreamed of a network of rails and spark shops throughout the empire, and these reforms became the cornerstone of the cycle. Opponents, including [[gallard-almera|Gallard]], saw “talking wires” and cars as a threat to the old order. At the House's April 1774 meeting, Adrian announced a plan: four thousand miles of rails and six new spark shops for the means of universal tax (see [[reformy-adriana|Adrian's Reforms]]). Lady [[karen|Karen]] writes that the work on the voice-on-the-distance, which is now called "wave", began long before the death of Telurian. It also explains where the profits go: the factory gives most of the profits to the supplier of the spark - the lord who owns the dam. Therefore, many say that the main possession of the feudal lords will soon be not land, but spark shops: the counts and dukes will start dams on rivers, barons and marquess - wind stations. Karen herself dreams of a small wind shop.

The right to the dam, Karen says, has long been used by the Crown to pit the lords. Duke Jonas Lighthart repeatedly requested permission to build a large spark shop on the Beck River in Hope, but Telurian refused. But the Duke of Almera got the right to the dam higher along the same river, in Naitrock, near the Lands of the Crown. The offended Lighthart went to war on Nightrock, and the Golden War of 1750 began. "The dam is built where the lord wanted it." Karen also writes that Ayden Almera for the last ten years did not give Hope to build a dam. Now, for the sake of his alliance with Farway, Aiden "allows Hope to get a second sparkle and this pays for Farway's loyalty."

In the spring of 1775, Erwin built his speech on this in the restored Chamber. He acknowledged that the world had improved with the advent of progress, but asked whether progress justified “violation of eternal values.” Erwin called the burning of Evergard the power of the Rings.

## Comparison with the real world
*Watching wiki, not the author's words.* Polari’s spark technology corresponds roughly to the last quarter of the nineteenth century, although the surrounding society remains one of knights and social estates. It develops without steam: electricity does all the work.
- **Workshop at the dams.** Wheels rotating shafts with magnets inside the coils are hydroelectric power plants with dynamo machines (the first hydroelectric power plants appeared in the 1880s, Niagara — in the 1890s). Wind turbines with spark machines are similar to early wind generators, and the fleet "mill" with a pendulum - to the current wave power plants. Navi's explanation in The Shadow of the Great Tree essentially retells Faraday's law of induction and Maxwell's idea that moving charges generate a magnetic field. By the way, in the “Yanmei Hunt” in the middle of the XVII century, when “Mechanics built spark machines”, the Marquis Faraday lives. His last name is the same as that of the physicist, but whether this is a deliberate hint is unknown.
- **"Wave".** It is an electric telegraph by wires on poles along railways. It's not like Morse telegraph with dots and dashes. Rather, it resembles letter-printing and letter-showing apparatuses: the set of letters with a combination of several levers is similar to the Bodo keyboard (1870s), and the drum with letters in the window is similar to the arrow letter-showing telegraphs and the typical wheel of the Yuz apparatus. The lines along the rails and the signal machine that repeats the train code resemble a railway telegraph and late dispatch alarm. Wireless communication, which is studied in the “Shadow of the Great Tree” by the Ministry of Minerva, is answered by Popov and Marconi radio (1890s). The voice “wave” does not transmit, so there is no phone in this sense in Polari: only the Object “[[golos-boga|Voice of God]]” allows speaking in a voice at a distance.
- **Ollie's Sound Recording (in Fantasies).** Cylinders, needles, and raster are Edison's phonograph (1877), and the cylinder is four minutes similar to Edison's Amberol cylinders (1908). But an “amplifier” with negative feedback is already an electronic amplifier from the XX century (the 1920s).
- **Light and life.** Lamps in glass bulbs, chandeliers with a switch, street lighting all night, floodlights, electric samovars, boilers and heating pads - all this is the life of major cities of the 1880s-1900s. Spike "sparkling machine" resembles an electric welding machine, which appeared in the 1880s, and the spark lock of the door is an electromagnetic lock.
- **Transport.** The tractor that takes current from the wire above the path is an electric locomotive, and the city wagons are electric trams (Berlin, 1881). Alanis' dispute with the cab driver's guild repeats the story of the tram replacing the horse. Read more - in the article "[[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]]", the section on comparison with the real world.
- **Guns.** A spear with two rods fired, as Karen describes it, resembles a taser-like electroshock weapon, which also fires two electrodes, but appeared only in the 1970s. The eyes are like batteries. Garlands under the current and "spark networks" resemble electric barriers.
