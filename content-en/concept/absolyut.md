## Scheme and Searches

For the first time the word "Absolute" surfaced in the "A Doll on the Throne": the soldiers of the brigade of the Puppeteer on the skin was tattooed with a scheme of connecting several Items with this inscription. The scheme was seen by Inge Price at a bandit nicknamed Rebro in the inn "[[dzhek-bakler|Jack Buckler]]". ([[inzhi-prajs|Inzhi Price]]) Crown Raven [[mark-voron|Mark]] suggested that the Absolute gives immortality: Paul pulled Erwin's bolt out of the body, and the Rib's skin fused in front of his eyes. He reasoned that "the Object needed another Object - the key" and that the Puppeteer had stolen the Dynasty Objects for the missing parts; Inji only hinted to him that the Absolute is not a weapon.

In "Janmei Hunt", [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] and [[minerva|Minerva]] analyzed the Census of Objects. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]; [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]]) Of the thirty-nine items that changed owners from the previous census, twenty-seven were due to legitimate transactions, and twelve were missing. No system of form and color was found among the missing until Erwin figured out how to group them by Gifts: twelve Items came from twelve different Gifts. Hence he concluded that the Absolute consists of seventeen Objects, one of each Gift. Captain [[uitmor|Whitmore]] and Major Backfield in the interlude "Those We Forgive" also came out on "The Absolute - Weapons Collected from Objects."

Erwin initially believed that the Puppeteer had assembled the Absolute with the fake Light Sphere, which Ice had brought him, and therefore did not become immortal; the fake Sphere, according to his guess, disrupted the assembly for six months. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) Then, seeing in the portrait of the Lady Ingrid pendant in the form of a “wall in a circle”, he realized: “The Light Sphere is a false trace.” According to [[navi|Navi]], Paul saw only three of the seventeen necessary parts in the Shayland storehouse; the rest, according to the register, were kept by the Dynasty and the Church. The last missing piece was a Drop of the Sun, stolen by a brigade from the tomb of Lady Ingrid in the tomb of Farewell (28 May 1775). Ice delivered it to Vittora with a single soldier.

## Testing and “Resurrection”

### Failure in June
In June 1775, Vittor dressed in all parts of the Absolute: he wore a helmet, shirts, bracelets, gloves, a medallion and glasses, and each Item, adjusting to the body, emitted a soft glow. But the device did not work: Martin cut his hand with a paper knife, and blood flowed. Whittor shouted to Ice, "This thing doesn't work!!!" He, laughing, summed up that they had obtained the cursed Gift, bribed the head of the Church, unleashed a civil war, robbed and overthrown the emperor to collect a thing that does not work. Richard suggested that Paul deliberately concealed the formula. Martin threatened his brother that if Paul did not rush, Vittor would take the Absolute apart.

### "The Will of the Gods"
Paul explained to the Allies why the Absolute should not be “simply included”: Erwin trumpeted to the whole world about the theft of Objects, and no one will begin to respect them — even Gallard’s ally will think how to take possession of the Absolute. “Another thing is the will of the gods!” Paul, who became a Gnoi-ganto among the Shavans, offered Vittor to sell immortality for profit and become a saint. Vittor demanded the key, but Paul replied, "First, the test."

### Waymar Chapel
On August 2, a “test” was held in the Waymar Chapel, a sacred ritual with prayers from the Gallard Priory and the monks. [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] poisoned Vittor and Gallard with Nan Claire powder. While dying, Vittor was unable to pronounce the key word Paul called "Transport" - although Martin suggested it backwards ("Tropsnart"). Nevertheless, the objects on the corpse lit up, melted with mercury and wrapped the body; the corpse disappeared and through inhalation appeared nearby - alive and healthy. Vittor then announced that he had ascended to the Star and was sent back by Ulyana; he has since called himself the Chosen One. He appeared and disappeared with cotton, and [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] killed him repeatedly in the battle for the First Winter, and each time he was reborn. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]])

## The Absolute and the Sword of the Gods

Rumors often confused the Absolute with Wittor's other "main weapon". During the storming of Waymar, the count wiped out the slopes of Castle Hill with thousands of stormers at one stroke. Joaquin, not knowing the real name, called this weapon the Sword of the Gods. [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] told Erwin that Vittor "used his main weapon - apparently the one called the Absolute", and when sunset general Horis attributed the Origins' respect to the "Wilhelm's Fingers and the Absolute", Joaquin almost said: "This is not the Absolute, but the Sword of the Gods! ([[oris|General Oris]]) The absolute is called that heap of Objects which the Count wears on himself." He remained silent, deciding that Shayland may have deliberately misled the ally.

## True Purpose (by Navi)

Minerve Navi explained that the Absolute is "not a defense", but "a horse under a saddle", transport. He instantly carries the wearer to any point that he sees with his eyes, so Navi advised to keep the shutters closed. With each movement, the body is destroyed and re-created according to a standard - without wounds, diseases and foreign bodies - hence healing and imaginary immortality; immortality, according to Navi, is only a "side effect".

> “It is necessary to remove the Absolute, and Vittor will become an ordinary mortal. And to make it take off is not so difficult: Ulyanina Dust, for example.

Navi warned that Paul was waging war only to satisfy “hunger”: having been fed, he would write to Vittor Dust “Give me the Absolute”, kill him, put on the Absolute and disappear. According to Navi, the Absolute is the “key” and the “castle” is in a place inaccessible to the mortal. Before the final revelation, he said that Paul, having taken possession of the Absolute, would “break a hole between the worlds” and let the chaos of the [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]] enter Polaris. In the last chapter, he explained the matter differently: with the Absolute and the carrier of the [[pervokrov|firstblood]], you can get to the Beacon Star and change its signal, opening Polaris to the “gods”. Explaining why the Puppeteer has derived so little benefit from the seized Items, Navi observed that nine of the Ten Items are only “gears” of large mechanisms, and the Absolute is one such device: it took years to assemble. Most of the huge “library” of the Sun Drop, according to Paul, is needed precisely for the work of the Absolute.

## Fate

On December 3, 1775, [[adrian|Adrian]] ordered Vittor to go to the Star and fix what he saw, and when he refused in fear, stunned him with a spark discharge that did not make the Absolute work, and imprisoned him, along with the Absolute, in a crystal Womb. This was rehearsed about twenty times, and Francis-Ilianus confirmed from the sacred texts that the Womb of the Absolute does not include. At the end of the cycle, the Absolute is kept by Adrian, and Minerva and the Origins discuss how Dust can get Vittor to give it up. Magda Labelin dreamed of wearing the Absolute with her husband "in turn" and getting rid of any misfortunes. Navi warned that Adrian would give the Absolute to the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]], and he would find the bearer of the firstblood and get to the lighthouse.

Mireille Nan-Claire observed that even powerful objects such as the Absolute and the Deconstructor are incomparable to the "reactors" that grew the First Tree. ([[mirej-nen-kler|Mireille Nen-Clair]])

## In "Fantasies"
In Fantasies, which the author calls possible futures, Vittor, along with the Absolute, is still frozen in the Womb stored in the Throne Citadel of Faunterra. In the story “Let’s catch him” Iona appeared in the First Winter. Kair Aubrey argued that it was a ghost: when the Puppeteer "flying Absolute", he appears with a quiet cotton and blows cool, and this guest appeared silently. Later it was revealed that the ghost takes the form of what the victim fears.
