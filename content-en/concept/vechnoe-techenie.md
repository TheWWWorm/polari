## Description
The Eternal Flow is a Sacred Object from the property of the [[dom-oridzhin|House Origin]], which is stored in the sanctuary of the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant in the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. It is a ring of blue metal about a foot across. In its opening hangs a tiny light, attached to nothing.

If you put the ring on the edge inclined, it does not fall, as if it is held by an invisible support. In fact, the ring is very slow to bend: for a minute on the hair, for a day per inch, and for a week almost lies down. The light, meanwhile, moves from the center down and sideways, in an arc approaches the rim and almost touches it. If you put the ring horizontally, the light gradually floats back and returns to the center by the next holiday.

## In "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
In 1774, at the wedding of [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] and [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] (the [[brak-vittora-i-iony|wedding of Iona and Vittor]]), the Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] ordered that two Premets be put on public display in the cathedral so that the townspeople who did not get to the holiday did not feel deprived. The bishop in the silver robe, together with sixteen priests, carried them out and placed them on small altars. On the south lay the all-seeing, multifaceted crystal, in the facets of which, according to legend, you can see your soul, ill, and even the cause of future death. In the north lay the Eternal Flow. The parishioners stood in line for hundreds of yards, believing that praying over the Subject gave them a year of health and a week of happiness. The cathedral was open around the clock. Those who came before dawn could stand for a few minutes at the blue ring and, with keen vision, notice it falling. When Iona left with her husband in his "river backwater", the bishop carried the Object back to the sanctuary and placed it horizontally. Erwin himself considered this exhibition as part of the efforts of the First Winter to blind wedding guests and hide poverty at home.

This is mentioned in the exploit, at Dar's lodge, when [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] angrily asked the mechanic [[luis-mariya|Louis]] if he had seen at least one Object. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) Louis replied that he had seen three: one at Marengo Cathedral and two at Iona's wedding. Remembering these two subjects, Erwin asked Louis if they were valuable and asked him to think about the answer himself.

Later, in 1774, Baron Darryl referred to the Eternal Flow when the Duke of Labelin was inspecting the fake Light Sphere brought by Harmon. ([[derril|Hugo Derrill]]; [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]]; [[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]; [[kharmon|Harmon Paula Roger]]) According to the description, the inner ring of the Sphere was supposed to hang in the air. The Duke did not believe it, and Darryl replied that in the Cathedral of the First Winter, the light hangs inside the hoop itself. The secretary found the Eternal Flow in the registry. The object was in one of the old volumes, because the property of the Origins is “very ancient.” The register says that the point of glow, like a light, moves freely inside the ring and moves to the edge when tilted. The Duke countered that the light was weightless, and the ring would fall if the axles were removed, and added that all sixty of his own possessions lay quietly in place and none hung.

## Trivia
- The purpose of the object is not disclosed.
- The Origins owned eighty-seven Objects, and this, according to [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], did not give rest to the Duke of Labelin, who had about sixty. Of the objects of the Origin, the Eternal Flow and the All-Seeing are described in detail.
- At the beginning of December 1775, when [[adrian|Adrian]] threatened the First Winter with a deconstruction, it turned out that he was destroying only the Objects that [[paul|Paul]] had touched. The property of the Origins, including the Eternal Flow, is not subject to him.
