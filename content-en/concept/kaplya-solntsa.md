## Description

A drop of the Sun is a pendant that in itself seems simple: a ring of white metal, "possibly platinum", inside which is placed an egg-shaped yellow stone, similar to amber. But in amber shine points the size of a speck, folding into a spiral - "model of the whole universe", composed of tiny fireflies. The chain is not attached to the pendant, but breaks in a half-inch from it: The object hangs in the air. This feature [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] first took for negligence of the artist who painted a portrait of Lady Ingrid.

According to the inventory, the Object was listed in the domain of the Dynasty and came into the world with the Seventeenth Gift. The shape of the pendant - the oval inside the circle - coincided with one of the signs of the scheme of [[absolyut|the Absolute]], tattooed on the bodies of the Kuklovod fighters.

## Lady Ingrid's pendant

Lady [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]], Adrian's mother, fell in love with this object and wore it for the last ten years of her life; in her lifetime portrait of 1765, the pendant is depicted around her neck. According to David's father, Adrian imitated his mother when he began to wear his own Object around his neck ([[kulon-adriana|Adrian's Pendant]]). ([[otets-david|Father David]]) After Ingrid's death, Emperor Telurian, for the sake of his beloved wife, broke the tradition, did not return the Item to the vault, but put it in the tomb. Almost no one knew about this, so when stealing the property of the Dynasty, the Sun Drop survived: the Puppeteer hoped to capture it on the train along with other Objects, but she was not there.

## Erwin's solution

In the “Janmei Hunt”, Erwin and Father David were looking for why [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], who was carrying out the order of the Puppeteer, went to the portrait gallery of the palace. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) Alanis stood for a long time in front of the life and death portraits of Ingrid. Erwin noticed a pendant "hanging in the air", David called the Object, and Erwin realized: A drop of the Sun - from the Seventeenth Gift, as well as the Light Sphere, and it was she who was missing the Absolute. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]) “The Light Sphere is a false trail,” he concluded.

At night, Erwin with three dozen kire rushed by train to Arden, to the temple of Farewell - the tomb of the Forebears and Kings, screwed into the ground by a descending spiral. In Telurian's crypt, the kairs moved the slab of the sarcophagus, it collapsed and split. Inside lay the mummy of Ingrid; when Erwin forged a dagger with an expired shroud, "a star flashed on the mummy's chest" - the pendant was struck by the warm light of amber.

[[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] had already extended his hand to retrieve the Item, but Erwin stopped him. Reason demanded to hide the pendant where the Puppeteer would not find it, but Erwin, humiliated at the trial and tired of being a "reasonable scumbag", decided to act honestly.

> “We’re going to set a trap right here and kill his brigade. But we're not going down to theft. This is the subject of Lady Ingrid, and she will remain with her.”

## Kidnapping

It turned out that Erwin was not alone in the tomb. When the Kairs returned the plate to its place, the barrier closed behind them, and the captain of the azure guard [[uitmor|Whitmore]] came out of the secret passage: “Lord Chancellor, do not light a fire!” The room was filled with hydrogen. By order of Minerva, the guards and warriors of Lady in Darkness - Lady Ingrid's aunt, the only one who could tell Minerva about the Object - ambushed here to burn whoever took the Sun Drop: "The Lord Chancellor did not take it," said Whitmore and ordered to air the hall, despite the doubts of the swamp "jall." ([[minerva|Minerva]]; [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]])

The trap failed. On the night of May 28, 1775, a detachment of "heretics" armed with the [[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]], [[paul|Paul]]'s brigade, along with Ice ([[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]]), broke into Farewell. Jemis knocked the duke down with his fist and dragged him away in a secret passage, from where Erwin saw through the observation gap how his cairns, led by [[sorok-dva|Forty-Two]], were dying. The remains of the gas exploded too late: the explosion only stunned the fighters. Paul and Ice broke the kire; Ice spared the younger Horton — "our gift to the Duke." Ice then reported, "The items are collected. I have a drop of the sun,” and on Paul’s orders he handed him a pendant. The "[[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]" reported thirty-nine dead kairs and fifteen killed heretics, the shattered crypt of Telurian, the desecration of Ingrid's remains and the abduction of the Sun Drop; the crypt of the Forebears remained unharmed. The paper made the case as if Agatha the Radiant had sent Erwin a "crumb of the gift of foresight", and he came to defend the shrine; only the Duke himself, Jemis Lilliday and Henry Horton survived. The next day, at a meeting of the House, Erwin publicly stated that the Drop of the Sun was the missing detail of the Absolute, and blamed the ambush with the flammable gas of the marshes: the Lady-in-the-Dark, according to him, knew about the Object in the sarcophagus and led her own hunt for the Puppeteer. Minerva could not stand his gaze and lowered her eyes.

Later, already on the run, [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], a prisoner of the brigade, saw how a warrior in a half-mask gave Paul a brilliant decoration, and he rubbed it against a silver strip on his forearm - and the decoration lit up with light for a moment. Alanis managed to recognize the Sun Drop. The warrior hid the Item in his breast pocket; Paul told him alone to make his way "to ours" and take the prey, since the direct ways would be blocked. This warrior was Led himself: he later told Vittor that on Canaan Paul had given him a boat and sent him to the count "with a single soldier and a Drop of Sun." Erwin, meanwhile, through the Voice of God assured Vittor that all rivers and roads are blocked and he “will not receive a Drop of the Sun and will not finish the Absolute”, and he himself expected that Paul will reach Waymar no earlier than June 12. Ice arrived there with the Item a few days earlier. According to [[navi|Navi]], the object worn on Paul’s skin became vulnerable to the deconstructor (see [[golos-boga|Voice of God]]).

## As part of the Absolute

The drop of the Sun was the last detail of the Absolute. In June 1775, Victor Shayland dressed in all its parts, and after his “resurrection” in August he appeared in them. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], Vittor unbuttoned a necklace called the Drop of the Sun, as a bore when he was filming the regalia.

On December 3, 1775, Adrian Vittor dictated the numbers of vulnerable Objects without hesitation. When asked if he remembered, the count touched the pendant and explained:

> "A drop of the sun stores information. Paul said, “There’s a capacity, like all the libraries in the world put together.”

Most of this "library" is needed for the work of the Absolute, but a few "pages" remained free, and Wittor used them for notes. Adrian, calling the Object "amazing", ordered the Count to go to the Star and "with my mother's pendant" carefully record everything he would see there. Vittor refused and was imprisoned in [[chrevo|the Womb]] with the Absolute, and therefore with the Sun Drop.
