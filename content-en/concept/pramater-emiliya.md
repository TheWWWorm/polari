**Emilia** is one of the Holy Mothers, the patroness of love, the “mother of pure love.” ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) She is also known as the “creator of the alphabet.” Along with Miriam, Gloria and Elena, she is considered one of the "lightest figures among the Foremothers", and many nobles choose her for in-depth study.

## Emilia's Love
Love in the faith Polari is divided into three types: Miriam, Sophia and Emilia. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], when she married, swore to love her husband. Emilia's love she considered supreme:
> “This is the love of the soul.”

In Emilian love, neither age nor gender is important. The proximity of souls, the relationship of thoughts and views, attention, respect and "unlimited interest in each other" are valued. She does not know the barriers: children grow up, carnal passion goes away, and the Emilian feeling “lives as long as the soul itself is eternity.”

The wedding wish assigns Emilia the role of a peacemaker: “Let Miriam kiss, and Sophia warms, and if there is a quarrel, Emilia will die.” The mother of Ellin, falling asleep, muttered: "Sophia, embrace. ([[ellina|Ellina]]) Emilia, understand. Miriam..." [[otets-david|Father David]] observed that “Emilian women are not allowed to be angry” — Saint Emilia, “the mother of pure love.”

## Books
- **“Love the soul.”** Harvey Horton read this book at the age of nine, barely learning to read fluently. Later, Erwin distributed sacred texts to vassals to search for hints of immortality, and Horton got "his favorite treatise of Emilia."
- **of eternal love.** Cairo [[sorok-dva|Forty-two]] found a place where Emilia writes how to achieve eternal love: spouses need to always teach each other new things, and for this - to learn themselves. He suggested that the advice is designed for the immortals: “Just imagine how an eternal wife can get bored for a couple of centuries!”
- **About the kids.** Valerie Gracend, preparing to become a mother, got acquainted with the books of Foremother Emilia and Forefather Joseph on education. ([[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]])

## The Creator of Alphabet
In the story “[[teper-ty-koldun|Now you are a sorcerer]]” Emilia is called the “creator of the alphabet”. When the printed word helped the sick guy, he was blessed with the name Emilia. Nathan said that the idea about the books to him "sent to the mind of Mother Emilia", and asked to let him and Mick in the cathedral to thank her.

## Images and Worship
- The Heart of Emilia is loved by artists. It was offered as a gift to the ladies along with the “flight of Miriam”.
- The fountain "Tears of Emilia" decorated the vestibule in the interior, which was furnished by Iona. She placed it against the background of rough granite blocks.
- In "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]" among the Objects in the dungeon mention the Tears of Emilia.
- Emilia has her own monastic order. In [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], Octavia's mother of the Order of Emilia depended in some ways on Cordelia's mother. ([[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]]) Dorothy was looking for her daughter in the monastery of Emilia.
