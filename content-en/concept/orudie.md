**The weapon** is what [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] called a prisoner of the Shavan with Finger of Wilhelm, whom he turned into a living weapon during a campaign to the Steppe in the summer of 1775. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) The prisoner was screwed into a strong chair, and the will was broken by screws screwed into the skull. Only he could shoot the Perst, so the northerners controlled his hand with levers and orders. The Gun's shooter was C.I.R. [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]]. All this is described in the “Shadow of the Great Tree”. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

## Captive
In a night battle at the gates of Rei-Roy, where Erwin's army had lost ships and Ganta [[ganta-groza|Thunderstorm]], Captain [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] came upon a chivalry from the hanid vir kanna. Hyder wanted to cut his right arm, but the blow came between his ribs. The dying prisoner was taken after the army. The wound healed frighteningly quickly: the Shavan received [[pervokrov|firstblood]] from [[paul|Paul]].

When the prisoner woke up, Hyder asked to be allowed to be interrogated by "reliable methods". Erwin went to the Shavan himself. He pretended not to understand the Polarian speech. Erwin didn't question him. He said that Perst had excluded Shavan from the ranks of men: his fate was now determined only by the Object on his hand.
> “I’ll call you by function: Gun.”

The prisoner was frightened and spoke in Polarian: “Origin, let me go, I’ll give Perst!” But Erwin got up and gave Hyder a drawing sheet. "Precious Agatha... “That might work!” said the captain. Erwin ordered to eliminate the “remains of the will” in the prisoner: without an order, the Gun should not do anything.

## Organisation
Hyder's Leed Wolves treated the prisoner for two days. They disassembled the crossbow, sharpened the two screws and twisted them into crevices between the bones of the skull. The heads of the screws, the "barrels", stuck over the ears. The screw had to be twisted, and the prisoner was writhing in pain. It was worth the squeeze, and he spread out in an empty happy smile. Hyder proudly showed Erwin that the prisoner obeys any order, even to break his tooth.

The chair according to Erwin's drawing was built by Bershan blacksmith from a Shavan village on the Roydan River. Erwin paid the residents with a bill. The finished gun was “a device made of wood, iron and human flesh.” The prisoner was grafted into a sturdy chair, reinforced with bars. The legs of the chair allowed it to be placed both on the ground and on the back of the horse. The head and left hand are free so the prisoner can eat himself. The right hand with Perst was fixed in the system of levers and hinges: the shooter pointed it in the right direction and could fix it.

## Oath and Shooter
The company commanders examined the gun and were confused. Hyder Leed advised to give it to another company, [[shram|Scar]] to keep away from the shock cavalry. [[fittsdzherald|Fitzgerald]] said he was too young for the honor. Baron Icewind, on behalf of the officers, demanded that Erwin clarify the oath. ([[kurt-ajsvind|Kurt Icewind]]) Erwin vowed to use the Gun only against enemy units with the Wilhelm's Fingers. After the war, he promised to let the Shavan go to the Wanderers' Horde, and to do with the Perst as the Church decides. ([[orda-strannikov|Horde of Wanderers]])

It was impossible to order a kair to be shot with a finger, a volunteer was needed. Suddenly, [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] volunteered. He also took an oath and received the help of two Leed Wolves, who were already working with the prisoner. Jemis practiced in secret, in a stable, and masked the flashes with bonfires. Shooting was easy. The gun itself corrected the shot, Jemis enough to roughly point his hand with a lever and say: “The third left pole. Bey". If the gun was heavy, Jemis would tighten the screws, if he hit eight times in a row, he would give a slack.

## The Battle of Roydan
Erwin understood that the Gun was not the key to victory. The gant Ondei had three chisels on sharp horses, and the northerners had a cripple "chained to a chair." According to Erwin, the Gun added only the word "almost" to the hopeless battle. In battle, Jemis hit the persunos with the first blow from two watchtowers, and then hit the riders on the shore. When the horde broke into the camp from the rear, Erwin, Aubrey and Jemis carried the Gun across the river into a hut on the edge of the village. There, Jemis whispered in his ear, “If you see Ondei’s gauntlet, kill him.” The gun shot and took the gun off its head. Ondey's Shavans decided that one of the khanids had gone over to the wolves, and rushed to the hut. Gun services, bodyguards, and Erwin himself fought back with swords, covering for Jemis. Erwin had forbidden the killing of the captive peasants, although they had seen the secret of the Gun.

## Attitude of Northerners
The X's had a double win. In their minds, they understood that the Gun served a good cause, but the souls resisted the “disgusting instrument.” The Cairos called it "id abomination" and "id instrument". Scar said the battle could have been won without Perst. It's Jemis who's got a grudge. He often spoke with the Gun when he needed to communicate.

On the way, the gun was carried in a kit. Erwin ordered her to be decorated with ribbons, like the boudoir of a noble lady. [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] saw the prisoner when Jemis threw the canopy in front of her. The prisoner, who had not seen women since Rei-Roy, howled, and Jemis had to screw him. Nexia said, “Please don’t call him that. This is a man created by the gods, just like all of us.” Erwin told her that he felt sorry for the Gun and was therefore going to kill him: in his place, he would only want death. He then told Jemis to try screwing: The gun, in his opinion, would be submissive without them.

Altessa Alarm (Dalia Raven) whispered to Erwin that to destroy the Gun after the war "aye as you don't want." ([[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]]) Jemis feared that [[otets-david|Father David]] would report Lady in Darkness's Gun. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) He admitted that the Gun still can not hide, but believed that the Shavans will only carry the story of the “fire-breathing northern wolf”, and David will write everything “just like in the pharmacy”. Erwin refused to let David go.

## Further service
- **October 1775.** On a ship in the Strait of Fang, Erwin ordered the Gun to be carried on deck to signal Lord Velentyne's ship with two fireballs. Then the prisoner was taken to the hold: it was impossible to shoot at the detachments without the Rings.
- **November 1775.** On the pass at the inn "Devil's Hole" The gun was placed on a hill, from where you can see the whole district. Kair Aubrey asked to be removed from the position for half an hour to dry the firewood with a Perst and clear the chimney. Erwin allowed and ordered the gun to warm up. In negotiations with [[fleming|Fleming]], Erwin hid him with two companies.
- **Before the Battle of the First Winter.** Jemis asked to take the Gun to Leed to save [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]. The scar said, "The gun is the key to our victory. You can’t risk them.” Erwin sent Jemis with the Gun to the First Winter, and left for Leed by himself.
- **The Battle for the First Winter.** ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Erwin's backup plan worked on the Belomorye Road. Jemis climbed to the platform, “where even eagles do not fly,” and shot the White Seamen on the ledge. According to [[gordon-syu|Gordon Sue]], he beat them "like cockroaches with a shoe." As Jemis dragged the Gun in, the captain called it "the secret of the century."
- **After the battle.** Erwin proposed to strengthen with the Instrument and Glove of Power the storm of the Creatures of Heaven, where [[vittor-shejland|Vittor]]'s Objects were stored. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]])

Jemis later admitted that he volunteered to shoot, because he considered it "the most shameful service - just for me."

## In "Fantasies"
The prophet Francis-Ilianus told Iona that his last prophetic dream even reported a “wooden structure” to which the Gun was attached. ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) Erwin, sending [[hajder-lid|Hyder Leed]] south, reminded him: "In the Steppe, they accomplished a feat by capturing the Gun."
