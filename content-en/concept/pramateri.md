The Mothers are holy women who, together with the Forefathers (who are called the Forebears), came to the underworld at the [[soshestvie|Descent]]. They gave people faith, laws, and [[svyashchennye-predmety|sacred objects]] and founded the first state from which the Polari Empire grew. All the [[pervorodnye|firstborn]] nobles are descended from the Foremothers. The generic name is indicated in the full name ("Erwin Sophia Jessica of the lineage of Agatha"), and the years are considered "from the Descent of the Foremothers". It is accepted to believe that now Foremothers live in the Underworld of the gods, and good souls go to them to the Star.

## Number and order

It is usually said about the sixteen Foremothers, who gave rise to noble families. The seventeenth is considered to be Ulyana Sad, “having no descendants.” Therefore, there are icons of seventeen Foremothers in the churches, the Church of the Foremothers consists of seventeen orders, and the number seventeen is considered holy: Mother Cordelia advised Erwin to take sixteen companions with her, “there will be seventeen with you, this number of the Foremothers.” There were more fathers: [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] speaks of a hundred and twelve (the same number of parts in the Church of the Forefathers), Paul speaks of a “hundred”. The commoners were advised to read sixteen prayers to the Foremothers and ninety-six to the Forefathers.

Mothers are listed in Scripture in an unaltered order, which is repeated on the altars of the cathedrals: the first is **Miriam the Dark-eyed**, then **Yanmei the Merciful**, Sophia the Great, **Agatha the Radiant**, Elena, **Gloria**, **Susan**... The last one is Uliana. Scripture says that all mothers are holy. However, the nobles consider the nobler the clan, the higher its Mother is on the list. Therefore, families tend to take brides “a higher birth” so that children inherit a more noble name. The exception is the descendants of Agatha: the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]], and then [[dom-almera|Almera]] marry only within his own kind.

The doctrine of equality did not always prevail. According to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", in ancient times, cults arose that glorified one Mother or Forefather to the detriment of others: in Faunterra - the cult of Miriam, in old Almer - the harsh cult of Wilhelm, in the North - the cult of Agatha, from which the heresy of the Light-bearers grew.

## Traditions and secrets

The Bible says that the first parents lived in complete harmony. According to the story of [[otets-david|Father David]], it is a lie: in 69 from the Descent, four Forebears - two Forefathers and two Foremothers ("Gardeners") - saw a different path of development and offered it to Wilhelm and Janmei, but were refused and were forced to hide their teachings. Thus came the secret order of [[sadovniki|gardeners]].

There is also a legend about the origin of Ulyanina Dust: the Foremothers used silver pollen for secret correspondence. When Agatha sent a letter with her help asking to borrow the Gauntlet of Power, Yangmei's student Gavin executed it against his will. The Yumin court found that the pollen is harmless to the Forebears, but suppresses the will of the people of the sublunar world.

In 1756, in the box of the Seventeenth Gift in Shayland, Emperor Thelurian, [[adrian|Adrian]], [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]] and [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]] found the imperishable body of a girl similar to the Mother. They decided to hide the find, vowing to remain silent. [[paul|Paul]] came out of the same bed.

## The Truth About the Forebears

In Shadow of the Great Tree, [[navi|Navi]] recounted that the Foremothers and Forefathers were members of the crew of a colonial starship that crashed eighteen centuries ago. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]) The ship carried two hundred crewmen, six thousand colonists, terraforming reactors and “embryos of the biosphere”; it was knocked off course by a false beacon, the Star. The wreckage of the ship lies at the bottom of the Bottomless Gap. ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) From the surviving crew members came noble families, from the colonists - commoners.

Navi named some of the positions of the Forebears: **Wilhelm** was the captain of the starship, Janmei was the chief engineer, Elena was the first navigator, **Agatha** was an astrophysicist. A conflict arose between the crew and the colonists: the passengers wanted to burn the energy of the three surviving bioreactors in one flash and call for help. Elena and Agatha argued that the distress signal will not be noticed through hundreds of parsecs of emptiness, and the reactors will make the planet habitable. Wilhelm and Janmei made a decision: the revolts of the colonists were suppressed by force of arms, and then began terraforming ("First Tree"). The forebears crossed the hostile continent on foot, carrying reactors on the shoulders of robots, and laid them down in the Boneless Failure, in the [[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]] and under the valley of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]], the entrance to which is now called the grotto of Kosuli. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) Navi called this their greatest feat, "now completely unknown." When it became clear that many generations would live on this planet, the Forebears vowed to hide the truth from their descendants, so that the children would not know that they were victims of a shipwreck. According to Navi, the Foremothers "never cheated on you, but decided to hide something."

Sacred Objects, according to Navi, were the surviving parts of starships made of especially durable material that stored energy in itself; only he, and people in capsules from it, could survive the crash.
