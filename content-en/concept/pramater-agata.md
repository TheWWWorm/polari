**Agatha the Radiant** is one of the Holy Mothers who came to the underworld at the [[soshestvie|Descent]]. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) In the canonical list of Scripture, it stands fourth, after Miriam, Yangmei and Sophia. From it lead the great Houses of [[dom-oridzhin|Origin]] and [[dom-almera|Almera]]. Agatha is revered as a seer and the wisest of the Foremothers, the author of three books full of allegories. In the North, it is revered above all saints. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]] spoke to her all his life as his older sister.

## Face and nickname
Her most revered icon is the fresco "Decision of Agatha the Radiant" in the northern nave of the Cathedral of the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]. On it, the fair-haired beauty stands tall and breaks the goose feather with thin hands. Philipp Lawfert explained the nickname with the color of his hair: Agatha had them “like liquid silver.” The Goose feather is its symbol. It is minted on the back of a silver agate, on the front side of which the Mother herself is depicted. The fresco is also called “Agatha’s Choice”. In the Battle of the First Winter on 2 December 1775, Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], occupying the city, called it a "monument of the cursed Virginian pesce" and ordered [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] to destroy it with "fire and spit". Joaquin, shocked by the beauty of the image, at first refused to shoot at Pramatery, but obeyed and shot the fresco by Finger of Wilhelm. Her death was called the worst loss of the city.

On the first page of the "Illusions" there is a portrait of eighty-year-old Agatha, written off from a lifetime drawing. The youth of the Foremothers lasted half a century: at fifty, Agatha could argue beauty with [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]], and at eighty, the first shadow of old age lay on her face. The features of her kind are a sharp chin, volitional cheekbones and a cold piercing look. Erwin found that in his visions Agatha looked like [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]].

The Polari Lands Guide describes the real Agatha as a woman "tortured by the burden of clairvoyance and decision-making, depressed, avoiding people." As the old adage goes, “Only Agatha can understand Agatha.”

## Traditions
- **Cosuli Grotto.** ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) Tired of hunger, Agatha stopped for a night in the cave and saw a thin white roe. Her warrior companion grabbed the spear, but the Prammother held his hand: "Let her go. She is weak and alone, be merciful." Agatha, out of curiosity, went after the roe. The cave penetrated the rock, and from the western slope of Mount Pramatery saw a flowering valley. The nearby exit of the grotto lies on the slope of the summit of Agatha near the First Winter.
- **Valley of the First Winter.** Eighteen centuries ago, Agatha saw the valley of the future First Winter from the mountainside, when it was white from the November snow. According to the Guide, the First Winter is the holy place where Agatha's feet first touched the lands of Polaris.
- **Goat's Cave.** In the mountains of the First Winter there is a “wolf shrine”, the cave of the Goat, where Agatha, according to legend, milked the goat and gave milk to the first Duke of the Northerners “wolves”.
- **Glove of Power.** Agatha had knowledge of engineering and once asked Yangmei for a Glove of Power to quickly pave the way. The note, written in secret pollen, deprived Yangmei Gavin of his will, and so the action of Ulyanina Dust turned out. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]])

## Agatha's Dilemma
Agatha’s main action is known as the “dilemma” or “decision” of Agatha the Radiant. In 36 AD, from the Descent, the state of the Foremothers consisted of two cities, Faunterra and Orwell, and awaited an invasion by Western nomads. The defense of Faunterra was commanded by Wilhelm the Great and Janmay, and the defense of Orwell was commanded by the Forefather Maximian and Agatha. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) The Shavans were deliberately displayed at Orwell's walls, and Wilhelm sent a strong detachment there. The captive nomads gave out that the horde had bypassed Orwell in a wide arc and was going to an almost defenseless capital. To warn Wilhelm pigeon took Agatha herself.

She understood the price of the letter. The warned Wilhelm will lead the townspeople to the island and make peace with the horde, but the nomads will retain freedom, militancy and dreams of Faunterra's wealth, and several warring states will arise on Polaris. If you remain silent, Wilhelm will have to use the Rings, and the shocked shavana recognize the power of the Forebears. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) Then there will be a single empire that does not know external wars, but the curse will lie on the Rings. Agatha could not consult with her brothers: it would mean divulging information. She broke the pen and hid the news. This gesture is depicted on icons. On the second of May, the Shavans burst into Faunterra, and Wilhelm used the Fingers to burn the horde’s leader together with his horse. The horde bowed to the forebears. Agatha later wrote in Moments:
> “The solution comes not from will, but from knowledge.”

Eighteen centuries later, [[navi|Navi]] explained to Karen the essence of the “Agatha dilemma.” The message itself changes the course of events. In his underground kingdom, this phenomenon, according to him, bears the name of an ancient god, “that you will break the tongue.”

## Books
Agatha left three books. In 1775, Erwin reread them in one night, looking for hints of immortality.
- “Moments” is the first and most famous work. These are philosophical discussions about the connections of events, causes and consequences, the form and essence of things. The text, full of metaphors, everyone understands in his own way: the warrior sees strategy in him, the ruler sees politics, the mother sees advice about education. Erwin believed that the book about everything at once: “everything in the world is connected, and the highest art is to see the connecting threads.” Elena’s boarding house lists “Moments” among the student’s desk books, and Count Benedict Fleming at the trial quoted them: “There is nothing outside the fabric of time.” ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]])
- “Fantasies” are light, witty, “with truly agate irony” discussions about Polari’s future history. Here Agatha predicted the discovery of a [[iskra|spark]], a printing press, rail trains, and a "wave". ([[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]]) She also wrote about what had not yet come true: about ships going without oars against the wind, and about ships flying over the ground. Robert Origin saw in the chapter "On Gravity" the advice on exploration from a balloon, and [[kharmon|Harmon]] was pleased that "Agata herself foresaw" his flying ship.
- "Illusions" is a hard book about self-deception of people: about how they believe sweet lies and hate anyone who makes them think. Agatha debunked even the belief in her own exceptionalism, in unconditional maternal love and in the ability to choose her own path. She was avoided even by the Agathans, and Erwin noticed that the eighty-year-old Foremother wrote her in great disappointment.

The North reads Agatha as a military textbook. Robert's father taught his son to understand Moments "in terms of war", and Robert found "hundreds of tactical advice - and nothing else" in all three books. In the fifteenth century, Lord George Brighthead of House Origin wrote the treatise “The Wisdom of Foremother Agatha Applied to Offensive Strategies”. According to Karen, Mother Agatha taught people to think critically, and Navi with her rewrote “Minds”. He also mentioned that about magnetic fields and induced currents “still Agatha the Radiant wrote.”

## Honoring
The common people of the North deify two Foremothers, Agatha and Gloria. The code of kairs requires to honor their commandments, including Agatha’s covenant of honesty before the suzerain. The Northerners believe that a righteous warrior will enter the Star "in the palaces of the Agatha the Radiant." War cries "For Agatha! For the North!" and "In the Name of Agatha the Radiant" are heard in every battle.

The main shrine is the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant in the First Winter. According to the “Guide”, the majestic Cathedral of Agatha was laid in the VI century by Baron Arthur Leed. This is a community with a hundred spires, guarded by three hundred and thirty-six stone chimeras. Under the cathedral lies the tomb of the Origins, an underground temple half the size of the above. The cathedral houses two silent Subjects, the Many Faces and the Eternal Movement, which heal dozens of people each year. In the autumn of 1774, grateful to Agatha for "two gifts for the day", Erwin promised to build a cathedral in her honor and in the capital. At the beginning of 1775, he, already Lord Chancellor, together with the Archimandrite [[ellina|Ellina]] solemnly announced the laying of the Cathedral of the Agatha the Radiant in Faunterra. At the grotto of Kosuli stands the Agata Convent, and in the cave chapel there is a statue of Agatha with a roe. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]])

Outside the North, Agatha is more reserved. The commoners call it "deprecated." Putevskiy folklore contrasts the kind Sophia with the "evil sterve-Agata", as if envied the family happiness of Sophia. Joke [[menson|Manson]] at the holiday of Descent began the story with the words "Once a slut, a hen-clumsy and a wolf bitch..." - about Miriam, Sophia and Agatha.

### Heresy of the Light-bearers
According to the “Guide”, in the sixth century Baron Arthur Leed, who fanatically adored his mother, who resembled the Foremother, proclaimed Agatha the greatest of the Foremothers. In 572, in the grotto of Cosuli, he met with the abbot of Valencia, and he founded the monastic-knight order of Light Bearers (Lightbearers). ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) The Order taught that Agatha is equal to the gods, that she foresaw the whole history of Polaris and encrypted the centuries-old plan in Moments. The headquarters of the order was the First Winter; there laid the monastery of Light and the Cathedral of Agatha. The order conquered the North, half of the Southern Way and the north of Almera. After the Wars of Despair, he was defeated, and Agatha's leadership was recognized as heresy. Yet for the northerners, Agatha remained the best of the Foremothers, and her doctrine of strategy was the most correct. The adviser to the king, master executioner Reidan Adler, who defeated the Light-bearers, became the founder of the Origin dynasty. ([[stratemy|Stratems]])

## Agatha and Erwin
Erwin Sophia Jessica considered Agatha his great-grandmother "in the eightieth generation" and felt her older sister. His mother’s voice was “heard” with his eyes closed, like a series of pictures. Before the mutiny in 1774, he came to a fresco in the northern nave and asked what he had done to Agatha. In response, he saw the image of a thin girl with a burned head, ready to run, and understood it as a request for help. The northerners later said that Agatha herself had ordered Erwin to oppose Adrian. [[mark-voron|Mark]], on the other hand, believed that Erwin did not speak to his mother with humility, but “as with a mother or sister,” and therefore received a false revelation. In his visions, Erwin met Agatha as a young beauty, thin as a mummy, but only saw her for a long time. She first spoke to him in July 1775, in the steppe after the rout at Rei-Roy: "I spoke to destroy illusions... I am ashamed to look at you.” Erwin retreated, saving the wounded, and Agatha said, "Break them." He replied that her fame was "fanatical fiction", and Pramaturya left with the words: "I will not come again. When you die, don't you dare say my name." That night, she appeared again and repeated the words of Lilliday's kair, which also required a fight. On the eve of the battle for the First Winter, a patient with a pulmonary disease Erwin, Agatha appeared again, apologized that “too long did not come,” and said: “Tomorrow we will meet.” Erwin understood this as a prediction of death. In the midst of the battle, he suddenly decided that it was about meeting with a fresco in the cathedral, and rushed into the attack with the thought: “I will definitely survive the battle, so said the Mother.” He survived, but then learned that the fresco was destroyed, and was afraid that the meeting would happen already on the Star. He later told his sister that Agatha had never let him down, “even when she predicted my death.”

- **Character.** Agatha is the patroness of scientists and the military. She is astute, sharp mind and looks at things nontrivial, but full of internal contradictions and prone to depression. Her talents are mathematics, event prediction and strategy. She is credited with the Object of Agatha's Vision giving clairvoyance, but it is probably fictional.
- **Before the invasion.** The first clashes with the Shavans occurred in 35; for the winter, the nomads retreated to return to the horde in the spring. The ancestors hoped that the horde would stick to the walls, which had not seen before, and go to negotiations. Orwell was fortified by April, and the wall of the Faunterra was not completed; a fortified bridge was erected in reserve on the island, now Palace. After the massacre, Wilhelm turned to dust and the helmet of the leader with horseshoes - the only thing that survived in the fire.
- **The will of the gods.** The church explains why the horde did not look out of the sky [[pramater-miriam|Miriam]]: the gods wanted the burden of the decision to fall on Agatha, and would prevent any other scout.
- **Northern doctrine.** From the choice of Agatha grew the northern military doctrine: it was created by the Light-bearers in the VI-VII centuries and brought to perfection by the Origina. The outcome of the war is decided not by numbers and weapons, but by the knowledge and foresight of the commander. A knowledgeable warlord can be forced to retreat, but he "cannot be defeated except by a more knowledgeable and visionary warlord."
- **The canonical image is true.** Agatha's choice is sung in a dozen ballads, several poems and plays, and he is depicted without count on icons, frescoes and tapestries. Usually this is a blonde beauty of about twenty years old with a small white or silver tunic and sandals: she stands on a stone fortress wall or sits at a camping table and breaks a feather, looking into the distance. The article lists inaccuracies. The pen is broken only in a figurative sense, and the Forebears wrote, most likely, small Objects. It was rainy weather, and Agatha admitted that she was afraid of cold, damp and dirt, so it is more accurate to imagine her in boots, woolen dress and a hooded cloak. Orwell's first wall was wooden. Finally, Agatha just before the invasion turned fifty-nine years old - although she could look twenty years old.

## The Truth About Agatha
According to [[navi|Navi]]'s story in Shadow of the Great Tree, Agatha was an astrophysicist on a starship that crashed near Polaris. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]]) The colonists wanted to burn the reactors for the sake of a distress signal, and Elena (the first navigator) and Agatha (astrophysicist) proved that the signal would not be noticed, and offered to populate the planet. After listening to Mireille Nan-Claire about the First Tree, Iona reminded her brother: “Illusions” “everything is about this.” Erwin admitted that he simply did not understand the rumors.

## In "Fantasies"
The author calls the stories “Fantasies” fantasies about how the fates of heroes can develop. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) In them, in the summer of 1776, the action is connected with Agatha like this.
- **Erwin's visions.** Erwin still saw Agatha and jokingly said that “the day before yesterday” she was in a good mood. In August, he had a dream of “Agatha’s devils” on the Star. It was a crystal palace that served only as the cutting of a giant black ship aimed at one point like a blade; the word "disintegrator" came to mind. Recalling [[navi|Nathaniel]]’s claim that Agatha was a physicist, Erwin suggested that she be written simply as a “scientist lady,” and Agatha appeared before him in all splendor and brought her fist to his nose.
- **Church and visions.** High Mother [[mat-alisiya|Alicia]] explained to Erwin that the Church recognizes only four people in the entire history of Polaris, who spoke with the spirit of Agatha the Radiant. Erwin “repented in pride” and received a mild penance. Alicia decided not to demand public abdication: rumors about his meetings with Agatha "strengthen the faith and peace" of the subjects.
- **New fresco.** The Grotto of Kosuli and the fresco "Agatha's Choice" suffered in the war. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) In the grotto, the impact of the deconstructor broke a hole in the rock, and the face of the fresco had to be painted anew. Erwin was missing all the sketches, and Alicia, who came to sanctify the relics, threatened to leave. Erwin then asked his imaginary althessa, the ghost of Dalia Raven, to take the form of a learned woman, seemingly twenty-three years old, but living seventy years on a desert island, and to appear to the painter. ([[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]]) The Master declared a miracle: the Mother appeared to him at night, took out of the air the volume “Illusions” and read for a long time, allowing herself to be drawn. Then the book disappeared, leaving only a bookmark in the form of a birch branch. Agatha broke it, blessed the artist with a two-fold spiral and “remote to the Star”. Alicia recognized the pencil sketch as the best image of Agatha she had seen, and Erwin explained the birch as follows: Agatha was tired of being "always painted with a pen", and gave a new metaphor. Alicia wanted theologians to explore her symbolism.
- **Honor.** The patients, who were secretly healed by Iona, believed that Agatha was “directing Iona’s hand,” and slept on their feathers because “Agata loves it.” ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) On the pylon of the cathedral, Erwin ordered to write a new fresco with Iona the healer, and Alicia consecrated it as a canonical story. Minerva and Iona, reconciled, recalled the saying “Only Agatha can understand Agatha.”
- **Mrs. Gale.** The teacher of the Almer school, Mrs. Gale, by all indications [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]] of the lineage of Agatha, threatened the student to “break hands” if she again compares herself with Agatha.
