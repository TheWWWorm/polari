Polari people share in two ways. The nobles belong to the Pramatery families, and the genus is seen in the name: "Minerva Gemma Alessandra of the Yangmei family". ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) All the inhabitants, noble and simple, belong to the peoples of their lands: the northerners, the travelers, the Shimmerians, the Litlanders, the [[shavany|Shavans]], the swamps of Darkwater. The word “race” is not in the books. The appearance in the text is connected with the genus, then with the earth. What of this is similar to real nations, below is divided into two parts: according to the author (only what Surzhikov said or wrote himself) and observation (comparison of the wiki by names, climate, customs and appearance).

## Rod and people
The bloodline is the bloodline, not the people. The merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]] explains to [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] that the nobles consider the family nobler, the higher his Mother in Scripture, and therefore marry brides "kind higher": "It's like breeding horses." There are no commoners. [[katrin-katrin|Catherine Catherine]] is said to have said, “Katrine is not a descendant of any of the Foremothers.” The family is inherited by the mother, so the genera do not have their own lands. The Litlands in the swarthy South are descended from Yangmei, as is the Metropolitan Dynasty.

According to the author: on the stream Surzhikov explained that the Pramatery was so “sprayed” because “the genus is marked according to its mother. Well, like the Israelis, nationality is also passed down to the mother.”

## Rod Agatha and the North
Harmon says, "Agatha's family stands apart. They are in no hurry to mix blood with Sophia’s family, not even Yangmei.” Therefore, “the birthmarks of the Agathans are especially noticeable — both in appearance and in the burrow”: “The devils of the Agath family are pride, composure and beauty.” Agatha was nicknamed Svetla, and Baron [[filipp-loufert|Lowfert]] explained that her hair was "like liquid silver". But her descendants have dark hair: [[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]] “hair is a crow’s feather, eyes are dark as resin.” In The Guide, the residents of Origin are described as: “A very proud people with a strong sense of superiority.” A commoner can look good in grays, and then in [[kajry|kairs]]. In "Only One Star," Mark calls North "scary."

- According to the author: about the Origins as a people - nothing. On the stream Surzhikov compared with Caesar only Erwin himself, in military and political career.
- Observance: Mountains, millions of sheep and wool, a military aristocracy with a code of honor and marriages within a kind most resemble the Scottish Highlands with its clans.

## Northwood and the North
The Northwood embassy on tapestries in the palace are "bearded Northwooders". On the roads of Northwood, people are “always stocky and gloomy.” Countess [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil]] and her daughter Gloria are of Susan's family. The Guide writes of the Northwoods: "A harsh, rough northern people who respect physical strength," who appreciate feasts, baths, and bylinas. Residents of the northern village of Spot "were born either red-haired or yellow-haired and curly" and live in stone huts.

- According to the author: "Compared to the Siberian taiga, the climate of Northwood is milder, but the beast is larger and more dangerous."
- Observation: huts, sundresses, baths and bylins are the Russian North and Siberia. Names in English: Elias, Sybil.

## Yangmei and Crownlands
Rod Yangmei will know by appearance. Of Minerva it is said: “A knowledgeable person could notice in her the features of the Yangmei family: a broad face, a stubborn chin, slightly slanted eyes.” [[adrian|Adrian]] has "Yanmean dimples on his cheeks." Mark calls the capital “a refined Faunterra.”

- According to the author: on the stream, where they discussed the actress for the role of Mira, Surzhikov said: "Yanmei was Korean", so that in the veins of Mira "a part of Korean blood flows". Therefore, according to him, the world should play an actress with “a little Asianness in the features.” A pure-blooded Korean woman couldn't pretend to be [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria Northwood]].
- Observation: The names Yangmei and Yumin sound East Asian. The slightly slanted eyes of the genus agree with the words of the author.

## Other types
- **Gloria:** "Like many descendants of Gloria the Intercessor, the count could boast of a mighty, high forehead and blue eyes" (Count [[fleming|Fleming]]).
- Sofya and the Southern Way: Traveling nobles "A little more than nobles should be, are too attentive to money, let them talk funny, pulling up the endings of words." Of these lands, Mark mentions "smoky Hope".
- In addition, the books of appearance of childbirth almost do not describe. In the boarding house of Elena-u-Lake, Mark lists: “here Agatha and Sophia, Lucia and Vivian, Elena and Susan”, and then immediately goes to the lands: “here is the sharp-eyed West and the dark South, the squinty North and the refined Faunterra, the smoky Hope, the miniature Darquater...”

## Shavan West
The Shavans in the books are dark and narrow-eyed. Mira sees them in the “A Doll on the Throne” as follows: “narrow-eyed, dark-skinned, beardless are strangers”, with swords “over the chubby tops”. The tapestries depict "shaved naked westerners". The Shimmers of the Westerners seem to be: "swarm, tan, veiny, flat-breasted, and the eyes flattened like almonds." Catherine was born in Halliwell: "swarm skin, almond-shaped eyes, slender body of a trapper." In "Now You're a Witch" they say, "Everyone knows that Shavans are dark, and you're white, so you're not Shavan." "Guide" calls nomads "the most ancient and ancient inhabitants" of Reis. The leader of the community is called the ganta, which means “pointing the way.” On the death of the Shavans, see the [[orda-strannikov|horde of Wanderers]]. Holliwell, according to the Guide, is "spawned from two different nations": farmers from Almera and nomads from Reis. [[melnitsy|The mills]] also started the Shavan tribes.

- According to the author: “The Guide” opens the chapter on Flight with Brodsky’s line: “... you are welcome from one of the five continents, holding on to cowboys.” "The prevailing style in music is country."
- Observation: the appearance and the word “horde” resemble the Turkic and Mongolian nomads of the Great Steppe, and Chubs are Cossacks. The author himself, judging by the "cowboys" and country, kept in mind the American Wild West with herders.

## Shimmers and white-blooded
Shimmerians on tapestries are "smooth Shimmerians in snow-white togs". The ladies are hiding from the sun. “Only such ladies can be called white-blooded,” Onoriko explains in “The Skyship and the Girl.” Vanessa-Lilit is proud: “the white-blooded lady of the Ludmila family.” ([[vanessa-lilit|Vanessa-Lilith]]) The “Guide” writes that the price of altessa depends on the color of the skin: “the whitest are the most expensive.” The kingdom was settled by the Empire's colonists. The local tribes, whose "king matriarchate" was mixed with the colonists and dissolved in the kingdom. Independence was preserved only by the “zozlorogo zunds”.

- According to the author: about the prototype of Shimmery - nothing.
- Observation: Togas refer to Rome, and compound names (Dias-Ramiro, Onorico-Meysor) sound in Spanish and Italian. The colonists who dissolved the native tribes in themselves are similar to Spanish Latin America. “White blood” in meaning coincides with the Spanish sangre azul: “blue blood” was called pale skin of the nobility.

## Litland
“The Litlanders may be the most cheerful people of the Empire.” In the Littleland jungle and monkeys, people wear bright clothes and “paint hair in lilac, yellow, raspberry.” The Shimmerians consider the Litlanders to be "half-skinned, a cross between southerners and wild Westerners". Becky has dark skin and green eyes. ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]) Prisoner Melana of Litland in Laem is praised as "almost white-blooded".
- Observation: Climate and open temperament are closest to tropical Latin America or the Caribbean. The author did not say that.

## Darkwater, Volta, Beyond
- **Darkwater.** Swamps are "low-heeled and thin; six feet tall and two hundred pounds in weight is a fairy tale hero" ("The Guide"), and Mark has a "miniature Darkwater". Observation: There is no obvious real prototype.
- Volta is an island republic outside the Empire. There is no such thing as “reason is more important than blood.” Foremothers of the Voltians are not honored, “since the Foremothers did not look at them to visit.” According to the author: "Folta is like ancient Athens" ("Guide"), that is, according to the state structure.
- The people do not know. According to Erwin, "In the time immemorial, even before the descent of the Mothers, nomadic tribes roamed the Beyond", but then went to Polaris.

## Forebears and commoners in the "Shadow of the Great Tree"
[[navi|Navi]] says that a colonial starship fell into the world: “two hundred crew, six thousand colonists.” “The surviving crew members became the Foremothers and Forefathers, from them came the noble families. The surviving colonists were considered commoners." This explains the division into original and simple (see [[pervokrov|firstblood]], World of Polari). ([[polari-world|The World of Polari]]) Where different peoples came from - the dark Shavans, the Shimmeri natives, nomads who lived in the Beyond before the Descent - the text does not explain. The author’s words about the Korean Yangmei are consistent with the fact that the crew arrived from the human world, but this is not said in the books.
