**Polari** is the empire in which the cycle takes place. It occupies the mainland of **Polaris**. The narrow isthmus with the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]] connects it with the huge, wild and cold continent of **the Beyond**, where there are no human settlements or maps. The empire has existed for eighteen centuries: years in it are considered from the Descent of the Mothers. ([[soshestvie|Descent of the Foremothers]]) The main action of the novels falls in 1774–1775. This is the time when the millennial feudal system faces a technical revolution, and then the truth about the origin of the world itself.

## Geography
Polaris washes several seas. To the east lies the East Sea, to the north the Ice Sea, to the west the Ocean of the Abyss, and to the southeast the monsoons blow from the Sunny Ocean. On the east bank, Mason Bay crashes into land. Information about the terrain and climate of the land is collected in the "Guide to the lands of Polari".

- The **Crystal Mountains** occupy about forty percent of the land of the Origin in the northeast. They are the only way out. According to the “Travel Guide”, the climate of the “cold north” is milder than it seems: The first winter is comparable in climate to Moscow.
- The Hanai is the largest river of eastern lands. It flows through the [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] and the Land of the Crown, and it has [[faunterra|Faunterra]] on it. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]])
- Smoky Dahl is a huge lake in the center of the mainland. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) From it originates the [[torrej|Torrey]] River, flowing through Shayland to the Sea of Ice, and the lake's evaporation makes Shayland foggy and damp. ([[dom-shejland|House Shayland]])
- Halliwell is a great river of the West. Its delta feeds on the moisture of the Littleland jungle, and on its banks lie the Shepherd's Meadow. ([[litlend|Litland]]) In the sixteenth century, after the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]], the sovereign Juliana declared them shared possessions of the neighbouring lands: three western counties and three eastern ones received the right to graze their herds there. ([[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]])
- In the extreme south, the Lion Mountains of [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] rise. There are two wonders of nature: the [[bezdonnaya-propast|bottomless abyss]], the bottom of which no one has seen, and the Lion Gate - two rocks three hundred feet high above the gorge of the Lions, through which the main road to the kingdom passes.
- To the southwest lie the swamps of [[darkvoter|Darkwater]], and on its shores are the Topi of the Dark Kings, a swampy stretch of sea separating the island of Volta from the mainland. ([[folta|Folta]])

The climate varies from north to south, from the cold and temperate continental in [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]] and Origin through the dry windy steppes of [[nadezhda|Hope]] and [[rejs|Reis]] to the tropics of Litland and Shimmery.

## Empire and Crown
At the head of the empire is the emperor - the "lord", to whom the "Crown" are addressed. Ruled by the Glorious Dynasty of the Descendants of Foremother Yangmei the Merciful. ([[dom-yanmej|The Yanmei Dynasty]]; [[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) Signs of imperial power are the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]], a transparent blade that is extracted from the sheath only at coronation, declaration of war and trial of the original, and the Star Crown. The residence of the Lord is the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] on Palace Island in [[faunterra|Faunterra]], a city on the Hanai River. The crown is guarded by the azure and scarlet guards, and the secret service is carried out by a protector led by the Crown Raven. ([[lazurnaya-gvardiya|Azure Guard]]; [[protektsiya|The Protection]]; [[mark-voron|Mark]]) News and decrees print "The [[golos-korony|Voice of the Crown]]".

### Names of the Dynasty
The dynasty is called the Shining Dynasty, the Ardennes Dynasty and the Yangmei Dynasty. The Guide notes that none of the three names are accurate:
- The ancient city of Arden is considered the ancestral domain of emperors, so the full title of the lord sounds, for example, as follows: "Adrian Ingrid Elizabeth of the Yanmei family, Duke of Arden, Emperor Polari." However, the lords live in Faunterra and [[marengo|Marengo]], and in Arden they are only buried in the tomb of the temple of Farewell. Therefore, the title "Duke of Arden" sounds gloomy and more often refers to the dead.
- The name "Brilliant" was given to the dynasty by its founder Olgard. The Yanmeis of his time seemed restrained and faded next to the luxurious kings of the Myriam family, and Olgard wanted to at least verbally overcome this drawback.
- Not all emperors were Yangmei by blood. If the lord took the wife of the family Sophia or Agatha, his children belonged to the family of the mother, and only the next marriage with the Yankee returned to the dynasty the family of the Merciful. Therefore, the name “Yanmei Dynasty” indicates the principles of Pramateri’s rule rather than blood.

### The Crown and the Land
The emperor directly rules only the lands of the Crown. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]) It is a prosperous land, the first in the empire in wealth, with two universities (in Faunterra and Marengo), an academy of arts, theatres and conservatories. According to the "Guide", the bureaucracy is strong there, and the feudal lords are weak, and the capital lands are ahead of the outskirts in technology for a couple of generations.

In the rest of the lands, the law comes into force only after the approval of the House of Representatives. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) This is a meeting of representatives of counties and duchies: according to later books, thirty-two votes (in early media editions, thirty-nine people, three from each land except the Crown Lands); important laws are considered at spring and autumn sessions. On this balance of the Crown and the nobility is based the policy of the cycle. Traditionally, only the Lands of the Crown pay the Dynasty a permanent tax, and the Great Houses perform vassal service with the army. When Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] decided in 1774 to introduce a universal tax for the sake of rail roads, only two of the thirteen Great Houses remained on his side. Soon he abolished the House by decree, and this was one of the reasons for the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]].

The uniform code of laws for all lands is the Julian law, introduced by Queen [[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]. ([[yulianov-zakon|Juliana's Law]]) Local customs operate next to it: in the North - the Northern Law, and the code of Olgard the Founder regulates the issues of feudal honor.

## Lands and Great Houses
The lands of the empire are owned by the [[velikie-doma|Great Houses]] - thirteen noble families. The Guide describes, in addition to the Crown Lands, thirteen lands.

### North
- The land of Origin with a stronghold The [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] is the domain of the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]] of the genus [[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]]. ([[oridzhin-zemlya|Lands of Origin]]) Their troops make up the [[kajry|kairs]]. The land is mountainous and poor in fertile soil; it lives on wool, steel, weapons, iron and silver, and war booty. The Guide notes that with the growing centralization of the empire, its economy is in decline.
- [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]] is the forest county of the "bears"-[[dom-nortvud|Northwoods]]: hunting, furs, wood, the largest shipyards of the North in the Bear Fang.
- [[dom-shejland|Shayland]] is a narrow land on both banks of [[torrej|Torrey]] with the city of [[uejmar|Waymar]]. In the middle of the 18th century, his count's house actually passed to the Harper bankers. In 1756, the Seventeenth Gift of the Gods came near Waymar.

### Center and East
- [[almera-zemlya|Almera]] with the capital of [[aleridan|Aleridan]] - "Red Land", so called for red brick and tile. According to the "Travel Guide", it is the second in the empire in terms of wealth and the only land where its goods are enough for its own needs.
- The [[nadezhda|hope]] of the Farvais is a land of steppes and deserts, rich in gold and copper. ([[dom-farvej|House Farway]]) Its capital, Heart of Light, is the strongest fortress of the empire. Until 1755, Hope was ruled by the Lightharts. ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]])
- The Southern Trade Route of the Dukes of Labelin with the city of Labelin is the second most populous city in the world. ([[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]]; [[dom-labelin|House Labelin]]; [[labelin|Labelin]]) It controls the only trade route north and the Empire's largest merchant fleet, numbering around 1200 ships.

### South
- [[litlend|Litland]] is a tropical land of the [[dom-litlend|Litlands]], neighbours of the Crown, with the capital [[melorandzh|Melorange]] at the edge of the "rope forests". Litland is rich in spices, dyes, and equestrian plants, but buys grain, weapons, and metal.
- The Kingdom of [[shimmeri|Shimmery]], with its capital Laam and Lion Gate, enjoys conditional independence. The King of the House of Nellis-Lyon takes the vassal oath to the Emperor, and the laws are passed by the Council of Five. In Shimmery lie nine-tenths of all the deposits of spark "eyes".

### The West
- [[darkvoter|Darkwater]] Lady-in-the-Dark is the marshy land of the lords of Nan Clair. According to the 14th century vassal agreement, it bears the title of a kingdom.
- The flight is a step of [[shavany|Shavans]]. ([[rejs|Reis (County)]]) The Counts of Reis own the city of Rei-Roy and the largest herds, but they rule only nominally.
- Holliwell is a plain county on the Holliwell River, whose counts hold trade through the Smokey Dahl. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]])
- Mills are the west coast, which is actually managed by a council of barons and burgomasters. ([[melnitsy|The Mills]])
- [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]] with the capital of Silens. According to the "Guide", no one in Polaris takes care of secrecy as much as its inhabitants.

Great Houses have competed for centuries: the North is at war with the West, and Almera is at war with Hope. The dynasty maintains this balance and holds a trump card against each House.

### Folta
Behind the Tops of the Dark Kings lies the island republic of Volta, which is not part of the empire. ([[folta|Folta]]) For almost a thousand years, it evolved on its own. It is governed by an elected senate, and the right to vote is near the hilt. There is no native nobility there, and the Voltians do not recognize the authority of the Foremothers. In the House of 1775, the voice of the ailing Queen Darkwater was that of a native of the Voltaic Islands, Admiral of her fleet Fanno Groc.

## Religion
The people of Polari honor the Holy Mothers and Forefathers - the Forebears who came to the underworld at the Descent. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) By the canon, they came out of the divine Underworld. The cradle of faith is considered to be the Crystal Mountains and the valley of the First Winter. There are sixteen mothers, and the seventeenth is called Ulyana Sad, who left no descendants. All the nobles lead their families from them, and the order of the names of the Foremothers in the Scripture determines the nobility of the family. The dynasty honors Yangmei the Merciful, the North honors [[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]] and Gloria, the Southern Way honors Sophia, and Shayland honors Vivian. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) The enemy of the gods is [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]], the lord of chaos. The souls of the dead are believed to go to the Star, the brightest point of the night sky.

The [[tserkov|church]] is divided into two branches: the Pramaterina with a chapter of seventeen higher mothers led by the Archmother and the Proto-Fatherly, led by the Priory. During the years of operation, the parish was [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]]. The Church teaches twelve commandments, the first of which is “accept your place in the world with dignity.” The sacred gesture of believers is a spiral.

Not all Polaris peoples share this belief. Shavans of the West believe in the disembodied [[orda-strannikov|horde of Wanderers]], in Mills and Halliwell they also honor water deities, and the Foltians worship the gods of the underworld and water deities.

## Sacred Objects
The world’s greatest saints are [[svyashchennye-predmety|sacred objects]]. These are indestructible products that come to the world with the Gifts of the gods: seventeen (later eighteen) times in history, the earth’s hardness broke apart, and in the cave-“loose” found a scattering of Objects. The number of objects in the family is considered a measure of God's mercy. According to the Earl of Shayland, the Dynasty has more than three hundred, Almera has one hundred twenty, Shimmery has ninety-one, and the Origins have eighty-seven. Only the Fathers knew how to speak with the Objects, according to the Scriptures. In the eighteenth century, the Objects “spoke” again. The fingers of Wilhelm, Absolute, Ulyanina Dust and others became weapons in the hands of [[paul|Paul]] and the [[vittor-shejland|Puppeteer]], and the key to them was the primrose. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]; [[absolyut|The Absolute]]; [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]; [[pervokrov|Firstblood]])

## Techniques
In the XVIII century, Polari is experiencing a revolution associated with a [[iskra|spark]] - electricity. Sparking dams and workshops feed lamps and machines, although only ten dams have been built in a century. The beginning of the spark shops, according to [[minerva|Minerva]], was laid by Bishop Lexian the First, and the spark phenomena themselves were discovered a century earlier. Between the cities there are trains with spark tractors on rail roads, and the “wave” transmits the news for hundreds of miles in minutes. ([[zheleznaya-doroga|Railway]]) Imperial spark infantry is armed with spark spears with "eyes" - crystals from the Shimmerian mines. Next to this are pigeon mail, knightly tournaments and monasteries. Adrian dreamed of connecting the entire empire with rails, “from the First Winter to the Lion’s Gate.” His reforms and the debate about them became the cornerstone of the cycle.

## History in general terms
### Descent and the First Centuries
According to legend, the Foremothers and Forefathers came to the Crystal Mountains, gave people faith and with the help of the Objects created the first state. His first cities were Faunterra and Orwell. Forefather Wilhelm the Great proclaimed himself King of Polari, and Pramaer Janmei became his advisor. The Western nomads were conquered by the power of the Persians, and later the Persians, according to legend, were drowned in the sea as a weapon of the gravest sin. Four of the parents, who called themselves [[sadovniki|Gardeners]], proposed a different path of development. Wilhelm and Janmei rejected it, destroyed their books, and issued a law against this heresy. In the first centuries, people mastered the mainland: In the first year, the forebears passed through the lands of the future Southern Way and mapped them, and from the second century farmers went there. In the III century, according to the maps of the Mother of Elena, silver was found in the North, and the cities of [[lid|Leed]] and Main grew.

Seven centuries ago, before the Troubles, according to one scribe, the entire empire was the size of four current duchies, and the lands of the Crown were split into a dozen warring counties. Almera became the first duchy of the empire in the 5th century, although at that time it was a tiny piece of land.

### The Age of Heresy and the Age of the Light-bearers
In the early Ages, heresy everywhere multiplied cults that elevated one Forebear over others. In 572, the Abbot of Valencia and Baron Arthur Leed founded the Order of Lightbearers (Lightbearers) in the North, proclaiming Agatha the greatest of the Foremothers. By the beginning of the 9th century, the order actually held half of Polari in its hands. King Orsayn of Myriamets only the third campaign, begun in 816, with the help of adviser Sir Reidan Adler, defeated the order in the Wars of Despair. Adler received the First Winter and became the founder of the House Origin. These events are described in the article [[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]].

### Sweet Ages and Scarlet Troubles
After the victory, the Third Dark-Eye Dynasty of the Myriam family entered the heyday of the Sweet Ages. Miriam’s kings moved the court to Arden for two hundred years, turning it into a “small capital” of luxury, where rabble was not allowed. The Gardeners say that the life of knowledge has become a threat to all knowledge.

In the XII century, the country was engulfed by the Scarlet Troubles - a time of feudal strife. The rebels penetrated into the Venerable Citadel of Faunterra by underground passage and killed Emperor Algon III with the whole family, thereby cutting off the family of Miriam. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) According to the northern legends, the soldiers of the North then helped to raise a new emperor to the throne.

### The Brilliant Dynasty
After the Troubles, the Yanmean Olgard Founder took power. As Minerva said, in troubled times, the most valuable resource was determination, and Olgard, “successfully selling it,” became the ruler. His descendants still rule the Empire in the eighteenth century. Among the glorious lords is Augustine the Good, who softened the manners of the nobility; Evrian the Borders Expander, in which the area of the state almost doubled without a single major war; Lexian the First, who gave rise to the spark shops. During the Borders Expansion era, the colonists conquered Shimmery.

### Horse Wars and Juliana the Great
In the 16th century, the horde of Darian the Bad invaded from the West. In the [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]], Aleridan, Blackmore and after a year-long siege, Heart of Light fell. Juliana the Great, who ruled for half a century, put an end to the Horse Wars, established the borders of the empire, which have not been violated since, and introduced a single code of laws. ([[yulianov-zakon|Juliana's Law]]) By her order, the lords left the gloomy Citadel and moved to the new Palace of Pen and Sword.

## The Mystery of Peace
In [[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]], [[navi|Navi]] reveals the truth. The star in the sky is not the abode of the gods, but a signal beacon, which during the war of the “gods” (distant descendants of mankind), the saboteurs moved and reconfigured so that he began to direct ships directly to the planet. Eighteen centuries ago, this lighthouse led to the death of a colonial starship, the wreckage of which lies at the bottom of the Bottomless Gap. ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) The Foremothers and Forefathers are the surviving members of the crew, the commoners are descendants of the colonists. The gifts of the gods are the wrecks of other ships, and the Objects are their details. To survive on a hostile planet, the Forebears raised the First Tree, which changed the nature of Polaris. The secret order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] for centuries prepared the "Second Tree" - the way to the Star. What will turn this knowledge, the cycle leaves open: the last decision is left to Empress [[minerva|Minerva]].
