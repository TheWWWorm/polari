The Holy Church of Polari venerates the Mothers and Forefathers and preserves the Scriptures. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) She serves in cathedrals, monasteries and parishes throughout the empire, holds schools and owns many [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]]. The Church is strong: Emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] feared a direct conflict with it because of his experiments with the Objects, and the candidates for the Archpriest’s cloak and the Archpriest’s diadem are confirmed by the Lord.

## Two Branches

The church is divided into two halves, arranged asymmetrically.

- The Pramatery Church (Pramatery branch) consists of seventeen orders, the number of Foremothers. It is ruled by a chapter of seventeen supreme mothers, led by an archimathera; the way to the chapter is closed for a man. Higher mothers are called “bearers of the diadem.” During the years of action, the archimandrite was the elderly Ellina, a native of Hope. Her voice and right hand was the mother of Cordelia of the Order of Ulyana Sad. ([[mat-kordeliya|Mother Cordelia]]) The chapter also included the mother of Alice, the head of the Agath Order, who dreamed of seeing Erwin on the throne, and the mother of Octavia of the Order of Emilia. ([[mat-alisiya|Mother Alicia]]) The monasteries of the Foremothers are patronized by the descendants of their Foremothers: the Yanmeans are patronized by the Yanmeans, the Agatovs are patronized, and the order of the childless Ulyana has no prominent patrons, why it is the least influential.
- The Church of the Forefathers (Praote branch) is a "crazy labyrinth of dioceses, orders, abbeys, parishes". One hundred and twelve of its parts and one hundred and twelve prelates on the council correspond to the number of the descended Forefathers; among them there are powerful masters of orders, and cardinals of counties, and city bishops, and small provincial abbots, many of whom do not even know each other. It is headed by the archpriest (he is also called the archbishop). The main prayer of this branch is “Let us strengthen ourselves with work and struggle.” In 1756, Ramir-Aslan, a relative of the Shimmerian king, was the archpriest; later, [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]], brother of the Duke of [[ajden-almera|Aiden]].

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collectible edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark explains the difference between the branches in a secular way. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) The Mother Church interprets scripture in its entirety, "as literature in the faculty of arts," and its flocks are nobles, scholars, and officials. The forefather branch, which is ruled by Almer's parish, gives parishioners a "bookworm": everything that is possible simplifies, and misses the rest. Therefore, in the villages, small towns and poor neighborhoods are mostly the Proto-Father's temples, and, according to Karen, they are visited by three-quarters of the inhabitants of the empire.

The primate elects the heads of monastic orders, but the list of candidates is confirmed by the bishop: the emperor has the right to strike out one of them, and he is eliminated from the election; the archimandrite is also elected. The Priory is not subject to the Chapter of the Foremothers. Common issues are decided by the Ecumenical Council of both Churches. In the general vote, the voice of the supreme mother is equal to the seven votes of the prelates, so that all seventeen mothers, and at least a dozen prelates, constitute an absolute majority; the unanimous decision of such a council is binding on the entire clergy of Polaris. According to Karen, the emperor by law can not deprive the parish of the mantle. He can only put pressure on the Church - not to pay temple fees, to cut down the rights of monasteries, not to approve the ranks - and then the Ecumenical Council of both branches, perhaps, will depose the archpriest in order to appease the lord. Or maybe even declare the Lord a heretic.

## The Church Court

The most powerful organ of the Foremother branch after the Chapter is considered to be the ecclesiastical court. He has the right to execute and pardon priests of any rank, except the highest mothers themselves, is a “sword capable of demolishing the head of anyone, even a bishop or an abbot.” The edge of the sword is the emissary of the court, the “eye of the archimatry”; in the Confidential, [[darius-khajtauer|Darius Hightower]] held this position, and this is the highest post available to a man in the Pramateric Church. ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]) The composition of the court is periodically approved anew: in the "Confident" before such approval remained a year. The secret of confidentiality and confession can be abolished only by the highest ecclesiastical court and only in the presence of the confessor.

## Orders, monasteries and temples

The text mentions:
- Wilhelm's fighting brotherhood are warrior monks wearing on top of their armor monastic stoats with a fist on their chests and by training comparable to regular troops; the headquarters is in the lake port of Fliss. It was headed by Bishop [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]]; the prelates of the brotherhood "contest with each other forever", and only his "iron hand" can unite them. In the 1775 war, two battalions of the Brotherhood fought for Gallard. In "Fantasies" the order of Wilhelm owns a printing shop in Almera. The roots of the brotherhood go back to the harsh cult of Forefather Wilhelm, which spread in ancient Almera (then called Marengo and Orwell with the surroundings): iron discipline, strict hierarchy, life for the sake of countless rules.
- **Maximian Order.** His monastery, Abode-of-the-Besdna, stands above the Boneless Failure; the abbot is Count [[kuindar|Quindar]], the Second of the Five. ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) Another monastery of the order burned down under Labelin. Maximian monks are known as connoisseurs of ancient texts. According to Karen, this is one of the three most influential paternal orders: he, along with the Wilhelmines, is allowed to have military units. The Order’s credo is “Observation, Reflection, Action”, it patronizes science, education, and inventors. The headquarters of the order is in Shimmery, at the bottomless Gap, and the monks consider the abyss to be the key to all the mysteries of the universe. Next to them are mentioned Evrianovtsy - the Order of the Forefather Evrian.
- Order of the Forefather Ferdinand: his brothers are famous for their commitment to law and order (in the monastic environment, they have a reputation for "hookers and paper rats").
- **The monasteries of Ulyana Sad.** In the empire there are twenty-four such monasteries; the sisters live in caves and keep a vow of silence. The cave monasteries accept everyone, and there is no legal way to get a novice out of there. In the underground monastery in Almera was sent to [[minerva|Mira]].
- Order of Agatha (headed by his mother Alice; the greatest shrines of the order - the grotto of Kosuli and the fresco "Agatha's Choice" in the First Winter) and the monastery of Sofia - for example, the monastery of Sophia the Wonderworker in "Confident". ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]])

The canon says that the temple reflects the features of its patroness: the Yanmei cathedrals suppress the grandeur, the Agathan churches stretch to the sky with thin spires, the Sophie temples are full of marble babies, the temples of Gloria are full of sculptural groups and living frescoes, the churches of Elena the Traveler are paintings of distant edges and seas, and in the temples of Vivian the holiday replaces. Mention is made of the Cathedral of the Forefather Christen in Nan Claire, the Temple of Elena-Na-Wolves in Leongard, and the Chapel of the Day of Descent in Faunterra.

### Lightbearers

There was also the Order of the Light-bearers, now recognized as heretical. According to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", it was founded in 572 by the abbot of Valencia, the head of the largest Agata monastery, after a meeting with the northern baron Arthur Leed in the Grote of Cosuli. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) The order taught that Agatha is equal to the gods, foresaw the whole story and encrypted the centuries-old plan in Moments, and the duty of the order is to monitor its implementation. Ten of the eleven Agathan monasteries joined it, and the headquarters was the First Winter. The Order hoarded knowledge and wealth, opened schools and academies; in the 8th century it rivaled the Crown itself. In 709, the Duke of Doyle, along with the paramilitary Order of Wilhelm, attacked the Lightbearers for the silver mines, and after the Wars of Desperation, the order was defeated and banned, and Agatha's supremacy was recognized as a criminal heresy. The light-bearers left a deep mark on the spirituality of the northern lands.

## Teaching and Rites

The Church teaches the twelve commandments given by the Forefathers. The first is “Take your place in the world with dignity”, the twelfth is “Let the other be”. Mention is also made of the commandments to honor those who stand above, to be kind to those who stand below, and to work hard. Scripture states that only the Forebears could talk to the Objects, and forbids using the Objects as a weapon. According to legend, Wilhelm the Forefather drowned in the sea. The evil beginning is [[temnyj-ido|Dark Ido]], the lord of chaos. Good souls go to the Star after death. Believers are covered with the sign of the sacred spiral; a copper disc with a spiral adorns the portals of the cathedrals. ([[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]])

According to Karen, the collection “Scenes from the Lives of the Holy Forebears with an Interpretation of Their Moral Meaning”, compiled by Yanmei’s disciples, is called the “Parables” for short; its commemorative hundredth edition was recently published. The Church, according to her, dissolves marriage only in two cases: if the bride was pregnant on the day of the engagement or if she did not give birth to a child in four years.

The Church accepts confession and prescribes penances: for example, Mother Cordelia told Minerva to spend a night without sleep in the prayers of the Sister of Death and drink only pure water for two weeks. By right, Miriam can choose a man for the first night; for this she chooses a confidant - a parent or a respected priest - and the secret of confidentiality is equal to the secret of confession. The church can excommunicate: Gallard "excommunicated" Cordelia before her death, although he himself was deprived of his dignity. Heretics are burned: Gallard, according to Erwin, burned political opponents, accusing them of heresy. The bottomless Church, according to the “Guide”, after many unsuccessful attempts to descend, declared the forbidden Gift a bed, which the gods do not command to touch.

## Wealth

In 1774, the Foremothers' Branch collected 960 thousand hilts in donations. Of these, 540 thousand went to the maintenance of the clergy (except for monasteries that own feuds), 190 thousand to schools under the auspices of the Church, 80 thousand to the renovation of churches, and the surplus of 140 thousand remained in the treasury. This money Cordelia at the request of Minerva placed in banks and gave to the Crown in debt; in the capitulation, this loan was called a bribe. The modest abode of Sophia in the Confident brought the Church more than five hundred ephesas a year.

## The Church in Cycle Events

### Before 1775

- In 1756, at the celebration of the Lodge of the Seventeenth Gift, the delegation of the Forefather's branch was headed by Bishop Gallard Almera, as the prior of Ramir Aslan was gravely ill; the Archpriest Ellina came with fifteen supreme mothers of the Chapter and thirty-two nuns; Cordelia was next to her.
- Gallard was secretly associated with [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]] and was the father of [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria]]. He participated in the substitution of Mira and her poisoning: he offered to send the girl to the monastery of Ulyana. ([[otravlenie-miry|Attempt on Mira's Life]])
- Gallard opposed Adrian's reforms - rails, "talking wires" and experiments with Objects.

### 1775 year

- At Minerva's coronation, the survivor of Ellin's mind forgot to bless the empress. Minerva later forced Cordelia to recognize the Yangmei Glove on her arm as a miracle.
- At the spring session of the House, Bishop Gregor Amessin of the Church of the Forefathers listed the atrocities of the past year and proposed the creation of a "sacred guard" subordinate to the House and Church, not the Crown, based on the Order of Wilhelm. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) Erwin later presented to the House an intercepted letter by Amessin Gallard sent on the day Minerva announced the Census of Objects: "Your Eminence, what about the apple?" (The dust has the shape of an apple.) Amessin called Erwin's speech a "disgusting heresy", and Mother Cordelia, on behalf of the Foremothers, supported it.
- [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] accused Gallard of allying with the Puppeteer, handing him Ulyana Dust and Perstov, setting fire to Evergard, and killing his brother. The captain called the archer to repentance; Gallard refused to appear, declared himself defamed, and chastised Ellin and Cordelia for interfering in the affairs of the Forefathers. The Ecumenical Council removed him from office; one Amessin voted against, immediately leaving the capital. Erwin led a "holy army" against Gallard under the spiritual direction of Cordelia.
- In the summer of 1775, crowds of poor preachers gathered in Faunterra, threatening to punish the gods for "greed, lust and laziness." The captain denounced them as deceitful liars, and Mother Alice asked Minerva to disperse the congregations, but the preachers had permission signed by Amessin, and the mistress refused to use force.
- In the Battle of the Trench, Gallard's army marched under the banner with the spiral and faces of the Forefathers, with the hymn "Let us strengthen ourselves with labor and prayer", and he himself lit the blue star with an Object at the top of the staff.
- Gallard later openly defected to [[paul|Paul]]'s side. At Fliss' gate, Paul killed Cordelia's mother after Gallard "excommunicated" her. Gallard himself died at the hands of [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]; this Minerva reported pigeon tapes, including from Amessin. The Raven of the Crown predicted that the Chapter without Cordelia would "fall into chaos" and the Church of the Forefathers would rally around Gallard.
- At the end of the cycle, it was revealed that a secret order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] had been operating inside the Church for centuries, in which Maximian monks participated; [[otets-david|Father David]] was its master in particular.

### After the War (Fantasy)

The author calls "Fantasies" a collection of fantasies about the possible fates of heroes. According to their account, at the beginning of 1776, both branches were left without chapters: Cordelia was killed by the Gaunt Pus, Gallard died shortly after her, and Ellina died on a cold night on February 2 from suffocation, when the maid flooded the chambers too much: holding a pillow in her sleep, she clearly said "Well, hello, cat..." - and "flew to the Star." Bishop Amessin led the monks of the brotherhood to the First Winter and personally blessed the archers with the Rings, breaking Wilhelm's taboo; he was accused of heresy, but he objected that the Orijns were also fought for by the Ringbearers. The churches broke up into factions: the Vilhelmites stood for the holiness of Gallard and Paul, the Maximians and Euryans, led by the Second of the Five, declared Navi the god, the cult of the Fallen in the northwest deified both “fallen gods”. The chapter counted fourteen "truth variants" for fifteen living higher mothers.

The Shavans renounced the dead Ganty Pus; their largest flock joined the Ganty of Court, who called himself the warrior of the Pramatery of Elena: she seemed to live with the Spirits of the Steppe, and from her all the great Shavans are derived - Paul, Moran and the Court itself. Some sundowners even prayed for Richard Origin as a fallen peppermint. In the capital in the spring of 1776, the highest mothers of Orlando (in purple and gold) and [[mat-alisiya|Alicia]] (in white and silver) stood out.

The situation was saved by [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]: he reconciled the branches, submitted the idea of repentance for the archers and advanced to the archery, remaining an ordinary monk. Bishop [[episkop-amessin|Amessin]] announced that the peppermints should take up penance “in half a year”, and a reward will be appointed for the heads of the unrepentant. Professor [[nikolas-olli|Ollie]]’s talking device was bought with the king’s money because the coffers of both branches were empty. The recorded address of the prophet “on behalf of both branches of the Holy Church”, certified by the highest mother Alicia of the Order of Agatha and Bishop Amessin of the Order of Wilhelm, was lost in the cathedrals: anyone who does not repent until the Day of Descent “will be found, brought to trial and justly executed.” The parishioners fell into the temples, the donations increased several times, and the three men took penance. The Chapter called for the election of the Archimatura in December 1776, after the election of the Lord, and the Protothean branch decided to elect the Prior even before them.

In August 1776, a church embassy arrived in the First Winter - eight carriages and thirty-two horsemen, "only holy numbers": Mother Alicia from the capital and Amessin from the Forefathers. They demanded peace from Erwin with Steppe, and from Minerva - repentance for the fight with the Gauntlet of Power; Alicia rejected this demand, since the mistress did not kill anyone with her hand. The financiers of the chapter believed that at current expenses it was impossible to reduce taxes, and the chapter was afraid of rebellion over Adrian's paper money and told Erwin guarantees of peace in the elections. Amessin obtained from Minerva the signatures under the list of candidates in the parish without deletions, promising her the votes of the Church of the Forefathers. Mother Alicia consecrated a new fresco with Iona Origin in the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant and recognized the miracle of Agatha to the painter - in fact, Erwin sent a ghost to him Dalia Raven in the guise of Pramati. ## The Author's Article
In 2018, the author published an article on the “Polaripedia” — the cycle wiki on Fandom — entitled “The Religion of the Forebears” (with the signature “Norman C”). ([Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%A0%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%B3%D0%B8%D1%8F_%D0%9F%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%B9)) It is written before the last books of the cycle and presents the teaching as understood by the Church. Here's what she adds.

- **Teaching is different from Christianity.** The morality of the Ancestors is close to the Christian, but the corporeality in it is not suppressed: the gods are pleasing to a person who is healthy both spiritually and in body. Differences between people are considered good and a source of development, and everyone has the right to their own view, so long as they do not violate the commandments. The hierarchy is expressed more strongly: people will never be equal, and their place must be understood and accepted. Love is important, but it is not necessary to love every neighbor: “to love everyone means not to love anyone.” Curiosity and thirst for knowledge are encouraged.
- **No sin, no hell.** There is no division between the righteous and the wicked. All souls fall on the Star and form a society there similar to the earthly one, but the classes on the Star determine not the birth, but the beauty of the soul. ([[zvezda|The Star]]) Crime and meanness deform the soul, and its punishment is a general censure and an eternal society of its own kind, without hell and cauldrons.
- **The Twelve Commandments.** Take your place in the world with dignity. “Honor those who stand above you, be kind to those who stand below.” “Work hard to the best of your ability.” “Do not take pleasure in suffering.” “Do not change the time allotted by the gods.” “Don’t take someone else’s.” “Do not profit by deception.” “Develop your body and fill it with strength.” “Give food to the mind and heart.” “Keep your heart open to the senses.” “In love be sincere and faithful to the word.” “Let it be different.”
- **Commandments in two branches.** The forefather's branch simplifies the commandments. Fifth, she reads as "Do not shorten the term measured by the gods" - only the prohibition of murder, while Foremother sees in her and the prohibition to cling to life at the cost of cowardice or humiliation. The seventh reduces to the prohibition of lies, although the Forefathers condemned only malicious deception for profit, and not lies at all. The twelfth says as “Be tolerant of those who are not like you” — about people of a different appearance, not about dissent. In the novels on the doors of the Gloria temple, the commandments are written in the same simplified form: "Do not shorten the period measured by the gods," and [[minerva|Minerva]], who read the Scripture, quotes the full one: "Do not change the period measured by the gods." Branches compete for income dioceses and influence at the court, where by the beginning of the cycle the priest wins, but they do not try to abolish each other.
- **Tolerance and heresy.** To local beliefs - in restless spirits, the [[orda-strannikov|horde of Wanderers]], witch toads - the Church is calm, believing that true faith itself will displace them. From such beliefs, the article "Gods of Polari" refers to the lake fairies of Shayland and the Sunset Coast that live on the leaves of water lilies in Smoky Distance and bring love and good luck, and the Magic Whale, the god of sea waves from the fishermen of Origin and Northwood: in order not to desecrate it, the northern sailors do not meet the need for overboard, and their ships are equipped with ships. ([Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%B8_%D0%9F%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8)) A heretic is not a non-believer, but one who maliciously violates the commandments or distorts the teaching: it is permissible not to believe in the divinity of the Foremothers, to deny their morality - no. In the 13th century, the Brotherhood of Water was brutally persecuted, teaching that people are equal “like drops in the sea.” Later, the persecution subsided: in the 17th and 18th centuries, heresy was given a couple of dozen lashes or a couple of months of work for the benefit of the city, and if heretics are still burned, the reason is certainly political.
