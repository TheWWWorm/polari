The Polari **calendar** counts the years from the [[soshestvie|descent of the Foremothers]]. The chapters of the novels open with a date and place: "March 21, 1774 from the Descent of the Foremothers. Smallden, Duchy of Almer". 1774 from the Descent or 1774. By the beginning of the cycle, according to [[minerva|Mira]], since the time of the Mothers, “seventeen centuries have passed” (in early media publications, eighteen). Centuries are considered to be the same years: for example, the heyday of the Order of the Light-Bearers is attributed to the “eighth century”. The foreigners have their own account: the "sixth century" of the Voltians corresponds to the "twelfth" of the Polarian.

## Months, weeks, time of day
Names of months are familiar to the reader - from January to December. There is also a week with Sunday and Saturday. In Labelin, for example, the fair ran from Friday to Sunday, and the emperor [[adrian|Adrian]] arranged an open visit to the Grand Throne Hall every Saturday until noon and managed to solve thirty or forty requests. Minerva in 1775 once a week, on Saturdays, received from the Minister of Science Isaac Flevin a circular about the latest achievements of science.

The time of day is celebrated with church "songs". They say "at the beginning of the morning song", "to the midday song", "in the hour of the afternoon song" and "to the evening song". The daytime song falls at about four o'clock in the afternoon: when the rumor spread that the portrait would be opened "in the daytime song", people realized - "it was, in the fourth hour." The songs beat the church bells: in the seaside Winslow ("Fantasies") in the hour of the morning song the bell beats eight times. Time is also measured by hours; in 1775 [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] checked with a chronometer.

## Holidays
In the preface to "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]" are called "big Polarian holidays": Spring Dawn, Sofiina days, Abundance, Descent and birthday of the emperor. Below they are described in the order of the year; the date of birth, of course, changed with the Lord. In Fantasia, such a day was [[minerva|Minerva]]'s summer birthday: in 1776 she turned twenty years old, and Lady Sofia arranged a holiday in the First Winter with fireworks, gifts and toasts by lot.

### Spring Dawn
Spring Dawn is a holiday of hope and a “day of bright beginnings” in the second half of March. In 1775, March 8 is called the day of the Spring, "eight days before the Spring Dawn." On this day, people wish each other good luck, share plans and try to start something important: they lay down at home, are hired for service, write the first lines of messages, order dresses and swords. Those close to them are given small gifts with the words: “Let everything come true, what dreams!” Expensive gifts for Spring Dawn are not accepted. The first week after the holiday is called a time of hope.

### Sofiina Days
The summer holiday of St. Sophia, the “mother of all children”, falls at the end of June. The court in “Confident” was appointed on June 23, “on the eve of Sophia days.” ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]) This is the time of weddings, engagements and sprinkling of children: in one monastery, a dozen weddings and twenty-three engagements were scheduled for the holiday. According to Pramateri’s covenant in Sofia, the doors and gates are not locked, “there should be no barriers to the meeting of loving hearts.” The courtyard to these days updates the decoration of the palace. In "[[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]]" Sofiin days are called a holiday of love and motherhood: in 1774, the capital was full of flowers, the crowd celebrated "Sophia - the Mother of Love", and Sofiin day, June 24, began in Faunterra military parade on the square of the Forefathers. In Labelin in honor of Sophia days there was a fair.

### Abundance
Days of Abundance are celebrated in the fall, in October: the palace cupboard received the position "in October, on the eve of Abundance Day". It is customary to pay taxes to this day. Bakers consider it the most long-awaited holiday. Before Abundance, according to watchmen in the Mills, there is always a “quiet week” of windless: then the masters from Minnis stop and lubricate huge wind machines. In Fantasia, the election of the Emperor in the House is scheduled for the Days of Abundance of 1776. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]])

### Day of Descent
December 2 is the main holiday of the year. The city is decorated with garlands and lanterns, gifts are given. A simple milkman from Confident, for example, bought the book “Notes of the Judge” for the holiday of Descent. On this day, it is easier to get an appointment with the ruler. Fighting on holy days is considered a bad thing. And yet, the Day of Descent of 1774 was the victory of Adrian’s army in the [[myatezh-oridzhina|Northern War]], and the Day of Descent of 1775 was the battle for the First Winter. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) Lady Sophia Origin decided to return to the First Winter on the Day of Descent, "like the Foremothers."

### New Year
The New Year is celebrated about four weeks after the Day of Descent: Sofia expected that there would be four weeks left from her return to the new year. For the holiday in Faunterra, the windows are painted with scarves, stars and sweets, and children build ice "winter fortresses". Sophia noticed that Erwin won the last war “under the new year”.

### Other holidays
- **The Abundant Summer Festival.** It was opened by [[adrian|Adrian]] at a summer ball in 1774 with a thank-you prayer.
- Summer games are bloodless contests, conceived by Queen Juliana. They are held in the capital in the summer; in 1774 they were waiting for the choice of the imperial bride. The games open on Sofiyin Day, June 24, a holiday of love; the week of games is followed by a week of feasts and marriage contracts. According to the tradition of the Dynasty, the betrothal of the lords is quietly concluded on Sofiin day, and a week later, at the closing of the summer games, he announces it from the imperial rostrum; the betrothal takes place in October, on the Feast of Plenty, and the coronation of the bride - eight days later. About this world told Becca, and the example of Juliana Peace found in “Love Stories”. (In early media, the engagement was announced on the last day of the games, August 7, and the wedding was played on October 25.)
- The flower festival takes place in spring in the Lumini Garden in Faunterra. Visiting him is a tribute to [[pramater-yanmej|Foremother Yanmei]], the patroness of flowers. In 1774, the entrance cost the elene - 64 agates (in early media - two elenes).
- The Feast of the Seventeenth Gift in 1756 was celebrated in Waymar, Shayland: at the festival in honor of the Seventeenth Gift of the Gods, noble guests gathered.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark clarifies that the summer games take place in July, immediately after the Sophia days, and not in August (in the 2026 edition, the games are also postponed from August to the end of June - early July). ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) According to her, the engagement of the emperor is in July, shortly after the Sophia days, and the wedding is in October, on the Feast of Abundance. Such a gap is needed in part because the bride according to the unwritten “fourth rule” should not be pregnant: before the wedding it will certainly manifest.

## In "Fantasies"
The stories “Fantasies”, the author’s fantasies about the possible fates of the heroes, are opened by the same date formula: “March 1776 from the Descent”, “End of June 1776”, “August 1776”, “June 1777”. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]]) The holidays are also celebrated in them.
- The first week after the Spring Dawn of 1776 is called a time of hope.
- [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] said that on her birthday, March 17, she learned that the Hand of Znaharka does not cure black leprosy, and on Sofiina's days the spirit of [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] appeared to her.
- Lady Sophia took off her mourning for her husband on the one hundred and seventieth day and put it on again a week later.
- The election of the lord in the House is scheduled for the days of Abundance; the townspeople of Finborough were going to release the goods by this deadline and close the workshops for a week to go to Faunterra. The Chapter Pramatery scheduled the elections in the Chapter for December.
