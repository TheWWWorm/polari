The Originals are the highest, hereditary nobility of the Polari Empire. These are the descendants of the Holy Forebears: each noble family belongs to one of the sixteen “noble families”, named after the [[pramateri|Foremothers]]. The genus is indicated in its full name: "Erwin Sophia Jessica of the Agatha family", "Minerva Gemma Alessandra of the Yanmei family", "Gloria Sybil Dorina of the Susan family". The descendants of the genus are called by Pramateri - Yanmei, Agatov, Sofiev, Yelenov - and they willingly call her "grandchildren": "granddaughter Yanmei", "favorite grandson of Agatha the Radiant".

## Meryl of nobility
- **The birth name.** The Foremothers are listed in Scripture in strict order, from Miriam to Ulyana, and the nobles consider the genus to be the nobler the higher its Foremothers are on the list. Therefore, families try to take the brides “from above” so that the children receive a better name: “This is like breeding horses,” explained Harmon Joaquinou. The descendants of Susan are looking for the brides of the Gloria family, the Yelenites are of the Sophia family, and the Sofievs are of the Yangmei family. The Agateans “do not participate in this race.” The external traits and disposition of the genus are considered hereditary: “The Yanmei were born to rule, the Agathans to fight, the Sofii to take care.” The dimples on the cheeks give out Yangmei's blood, volitional cheekbones and a cold look - Agatha's blood. Unlike other firstborns, the Yelenites allow children easy sentimentality and faith in miracles.
- **Property.** [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]] constitute the "property of the original families": the more of them, the higher, it is believed, the favor of the gods. According to Baron Lowfert, Great Houses are those with more than twenty Objects (see Great Houses). ([[velikie-doma|Great Houses]])
- **Antiquity.** The age of the house is also appreciated. Lightharts, for example, were considered "more ancient than Farway and Labelin than even the Brilliant Dynasty itself." ([[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) New noble families enjoy less honor.
- **Land and title.** The hierarchy of landowners includes lords, dukes and counts (heads of the Great Houses - "landlords"), marquises, barons and knights - "sirs". The Northern Originals serve the Origins as [[kajry|Kairs]].

## Birth by mother
The birth is passed on to the mother. The "Guide to the Lands of Polari" explains this by the example of the Dynasty: if the lord took a wife sophievka or agate, his children became Sophiev or agatovtsy, and only the next prince, marrying Yanmeyak, returned to the children of the kind of Merciful Pramati. So, Duke [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] has all the children and grandchildren - the descendants of Elena, and only the granddaughter of [[laura-farvej|Laura]] is descended from Agatha the Radiant. [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] immediately used it, hinting at marriage to her.

## Names
The full name in Polari consists of a personal name and two other names. The commoners apparently have the names of their mother and father ("Mother's name, father's name?" asks Dorothy to an unnamed Navi); so, the merchant's name is Harmon Paula Roger. Harmon, hearing the name "Joaquin Eve Hannah", noticed that Hannah is the name of the grandmother, not the father, and suspected a "chard of nobles" in the young man. The originals have middle and third names - female and repeat the first names of the mother: Minerva Gemma Alessandra - daughter of Gemma Alessandra Hortensia, Erwin and Iona Sofia Jessica - children of Sofia Jessica Augusta. Ends the name of the genus.

## Marriage
A good marriage is a matter of calculation. The original bride must pay the ransom. Count Wittor Shayland, marrying [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], owed the Origins and did not pay in time, and the Origins threatened him with war. ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]]) Among commoners, as one miller wondered, the bride brings a dowry, and among the nobility "the opposite is accepted - the groom pays the bride price." The dowry, however, happens to the nobles: the Duke of Farway promised [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]] in the dowry of the spark shop of Hope. Harmon, having become rich, imagined that forty thousand Hilt would be enough “for a bridegroom’s ransom, and for a palace” for the original wife.

Special rules apply when choosing an empress, which was told by Becca Litland. ([[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]]) The chosen one should be from seventeen to twenty-two years old: the younger ones are considered “stupid and undeveloped”, the older ones are “burdened with soul”. The princess must belong to the family of one of the three Most Holy Mothers: most often it is the Yangmei family, sometimes Sophia, and only occasionally Agatha. The land of the bride is also taken into account: the girls from the West, whose leaders are “too wild” and defamed by the Horse Wars, have no chance, Darkwater and Shayland are “too young”, and Origin is “too strong”. For a century and a half, no emperor married the heiress of the Great House (in early media publications - three centuries). Health is welcome, beauty and mind are “not prohibited, but not mandatory”: the applicant should be “fertile, predictable and safe”.

## Rights and penalties
- Only the firstborn is judged by the exposure of the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]]. If the plaintiff is the Crown and the accused is the original, the case is tried by a supreme court of nine judges; this is how Manson (the Manson trial) was tried. ([[menson|Manson]]; [[sud-nad-mensonom|Trial of Manson]])
- According to Wilhelm and Janmei’s law, the commoner was burned for spreading heresy, and the original was expelled to the western steppes.
- Julian's law punishes rebellion by burning, death by click, or quartering, and the nobles are given a choice. ([[yulianov-zakon|Juliana's Law]]) For inciting the rebellion of the commoner, thirty long whips are sequestered, and the nobleman is deprived of the title.
- The original lady is executed with a sword, not an axe: "As the original lady, I was hoping for a sword," [[minerva|Mira]] quipped as the Crown Raven frightened her with the executioner's axe. ([[mark-voron|Mark]]) She also recalled that for simple manipulation, “firstborns are not sent to the plow.”
- The murder of the original noblewoman, according to Joaquin, is punishable by wheeling.
- According to Lord Clarence, all originals have the right to visit the Sanctuary and get acquainted with its manuscripts.

There are legends about the persistence of knowledge. After the Shutov conspiracy, some conspirators were burned, others were cut, others were buried alive, and the people said: “the firstborn meet death silently.” ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]])

## The official nobility and upstarts
Below the original is the rank of nobility - nobles in the service: families of imperial officials, secretaries, officers. The order is given for service: for example, the father of one of the heroines of the "Placks", the younger judge, "for faithful service ... promised a reward of rank." ([[plaksa|Crybaby]]) The originals look down on the officers. Erwin described the mechanic's lover [[luis-mariya|Louis]] as a maiden "of petty official nobility", the servant's daughter, an "imperial dog". Newly minted noble families are also not respected. The father of Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] was the richest banker, but the originals "despite dealing with him", and Vittor himself as a second-generation Count was not considered equal. In the North, according to the “Travel Guide”, there is a military “social elevator”: a commoner can become a warm, and then a buzzer.

## Primordiality and primrose
The name "firstborn" is consonant with the [[pervokrov|firstblood]] - a special blood that gives power over the Objects. At the end of the cycle, [[navi|Navi]] explained that the originals really come from the crew of the deceased starship, and commoners from his colonists. The inequality goes back to the conflict between them: the colonists wanted to call for help, and the crew decided to populate the planet and suppressed their rebellions by force. At the same time, according to Navi, over eighteen centuries of isolation, both nobles and commoners “were equally human.”
