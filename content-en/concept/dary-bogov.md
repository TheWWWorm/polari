The **gifts of the gods** are rare occurrences of [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]] in the Polari world. The earth’s core is broken, and a cave with products that people consider the creations of the gods is found in the rift. Such a cave is called the bed of the Gift: it serves as a storehouse of treasures, and the Gift itself is its contents. By the beginning of the empire's cycle, seventeen Gifts were known for the entire written history - less than one in a century.

## What the Gifts Bring (Arrow, Coin, Spark)

According to the beliefs of Polari, the Gifts are sent by the gods of the Underworld. The objects in their composition are shrines created by incomprehensible art: the human hand is not able to make anything like this. Scripture tells us that with the help of such products, the [[pramateri|Forebears]] healed the sick, called for rain and sun, received water and food, and ascended to heaven. Their power helped to establish the state.

A gift can contain many items scattered across grottoes, pushed into walls, or hidden in the depths of a bed. Even an unusual cover of walls gives finds: from it break off brilliant fragments, similar to scales. However, the Church does not recognize these imperfect fragments as full-fledged shrines and refers them to small objects. For properties, varieties and use of the products themselves, see [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]].

## Lodge and Extraction (Arrow, Coin, Spark)

Getting treasure out of the box is a separate dangerous job, which is called the extraction of the Gift. The caves are deep, dark and steep; their walls are carved with grottoes and covered with a shiny crust resembling glass and metal. It is as hard as a diamond and does not allow you to drive down hooks. Sometimes such a cover closes the entrance: then you have to lay a mine on the side.

Inside there may be ash and slag, poisonous gases, extreme heat or deadly shimmering light. To get to the Objects, people dig up burnt rock for months. It is impossible to predict all dangers in advance. The Church explains the difficulties by the fact that the gods allow only the worthy and pious to the shrines - after a long work.

According to [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], the extraction takes at least a month. The Shayland Gift required a whole summer, and work on the Smoky Distance stretched for years.

## Possession and Struggle for Gifts (Arrow, Coin, Spark)

Sacred objects are the property of noble families, and their number serves as a measure of God’s favor. Therefore, the new Gift promises wealth and political strengthening. By law, one-third of the objects of any Gift belong to the Crown: the imperial observer [[filipp-loufert|Philip Lowfert]], who accompanies the exploit, counts on a reward for such a valuable gift to the Lord.

The fight for the bed can begin even before extraction. After the appearance of the [[semnadtsatyj-dar|Seventeenth Gift]] in Shayland, Westerners recaptured it from the Count's men. To approach the troops of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond Origin]], twelve Items have already been taken to the Sunset Coast. To return them, the Duke embarked on a campaign that cost the western lands of three burned cities and several thousand lives.

## Known Lodges ("Arrow, Coin, Spark")

- The tenth Dar originated in Almera, at the bottom of the Smoky Dahl Lake. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]]) To extract it, the Duke built a two-mile dam, canals, and reservoirs. Some of the lake was drained only after four years, then for several months pumped water from the cave, which drove twenty windmills.
- The seventeenth Dar came in 1756 on the west bank of [[torrej|Torrey]], in Shayland. ([[semnadtsatyj-dar|Seventeenth Gift]]) This is the last Gift known to the Empire to start a cycle; its emergence led to a War for Objects.
- The eighteenth Dar discovered Erwin's Explorada River in 1774. ([[reka|The River]]; [[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]]) The descent was unusually easy because the bed had already been opened and emptied. The discovery did not bring the expected treasure to the squad.

## "The Shadow of the Great Tree"

In the chapter "Northern Bird", [[navi|Navi]] reveals the origin of the Gifts: these are the crash sites of starships, and the Sacred Objects are the surviving details of the wrecked ships. The people of Polari explained the incomprehensible findings in the language of their religion.

Navi also tells of a colonial starship that disappeared eighteen centuries ago: its remains lie at the bottom of the Bottomless Failure. ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) So the late solution connects the shrines of the empire with the history of the aliens and the true past of the world.
