Stratemes is a military board game popular among the nobility of Polari. It serves as both entertainment, an exercise for the mind and a measure of general talent. Life itself in the cycle is constantly compared with the party in strata. Erwin sees in his associates “tricks on the field with execution”, and Mira, solving intrigues, talks about the “spark of the duke” and the “reciprocal course”.

## Rules
The game is played on a wooden field with schematic images of fields, forests, rivers and hills. The pieces of two colors are lined up in combat order. In the description of the game, black and scarlet chips are named, and in the game of Mira with Adrian, she got scarlet, the emperor - azure. Bone chips: spark chip Mira - "bone round with a gilded zipper." The ranks of chips are indicated by the icons carved on them:
- sickle ("sorrows") - peasants, the weakest chips, suitable only as a living barrier;
- Swords are mercenaries, a good fighting force;
- Coins are merchants who know how to bribe enemy fighters and incline them to rebellion: it is enough to touch the coin of the enemy sword, and he passes to your side;
- horseshoes - knights, fast and sweeping obstacles;
- Sparks are the original nobles who inspire warriors and unite them into battalions. Each player has two sparks, and to knock both means to win.

When Mira explained the rules of [[navi|Navi]], she also reminded others: the spark commands the stratum (as the construction of chips is called), the sword wears armor, the horseshoe pierces the armor of the sword and walks faster than others, the arrow does not receive a retaliatory blow, and the sickle is afraid to leave the regular troops. There are special rules. There is a short game with one spark: the one who loses it immediately loses. For a long game, they put a “double set of chips”. Adrian once assigned Mira a task with one spark for each and on the condition that the red troops pass to the black ones, if those ten moves hold the opponent's castle.

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collector's edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark reproaches the author that the description of the party does not mention "actually executed". ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) According to her, the spark chip, the main on the field, can gather a stratum around itself - a combat connection of seven chips. They walk together from the player's touch and join the fight together. Stratema is the main striking force, the rest of the chips are only auxiliary troops.

During the turn, the player can make no more than three movements with "different chips". However, from the parties it is clear that the moves can be given to one chip: Adrian left the third knight in the forest, “to accelerate the progress” of the other two, and they had six moves instead of eight. Therefore, a large army moves slowly, and Miré in the party with Navi “it took too much touch to control two detachments at once.” The stratum, the construction around the spark, is moved entirely: Navi moved the "left stratum, then the right stratum" with a spade. Missing the move, Mira pounded on the edge of the table. The terrain means a lot: the forest slows down the cavalry, the stream detains the infantry, the hills cover the flanks, and the ruins of the fortress serve as a refuge. A field made by a good craftsman is “always unique,” and the advantageous or dangerous features of an unfamiliar field open up in just a few batches. Classical constructions are known. Adrian placed the army in the "classic order": in the vanguard of the spark and knightly wedge, in the rear of the swords, a coin and a second spark under the protection of the "box" of sickles. [[dzheremi-farvej|Jeremy Farway]], teaching Laura and Albert Almer in Evergard, explained: the Lightbearers' maneuver "can only be repelled by ticks", the symmetrical stratum is good for protection, but not for offense, and the "strateme in the cordon" is called the "Flower of Olgard". ([[albert-almera|Albert Almera]])

Field cells are hexagonal: the thin scars on [[paul|Paul]]'s body fold into a "hexagonal cell network", and Alanis thinks that on his skin, "you can play stratum." The field is great - in Adrian's task, the main army of scarlet stands "in seven cells to the side of the castle", and the second wave of blacks lags "on the good ten cells"; each side has its own home castle. Bilateral chips: Explaining a special rule of the task, Adrian flips the scarlet chip, and "its turnover had the color of coal."

In the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]] there is a room for execution with a precious table. Its six-foot countertop exactly repeats the outlines of Polaris: the islands are kept on silver spokes, the land is divided by gold threads, the cities are marked with rock crystal, the capitals are marked with precious stones, the spark shops are marked with eyes, and the rails and tracts are dotted. The playing field cells respond to a two-day infantry march, so in the mountains and swamps they are smaller, and on such a table you can calculate any war in Polaris "with an accuracy of two days."

## History
According to the "Guide to the lands of Polari", the strata are closely related to the wars of the North. The Bearers of Light (Lightbearers), preparing for revenge in the eighth century, spent years “practising stratems and studying the styles of former commanders in books”. In the ninth century, during the Wars of Despair, their general Heinrich Leed was opposed by the old knight Sir Reidan Adler, an Agathan and “master of stratems” who had once taught the game to the young King Orsein the Miriamite. The king asked him not to crush the Light-bearers, but to “beat” them. Sir Reidan sat at the execution table for a day, and then set the king the conditions. Their competition with Leed "became the most beautiful party included in the textbooks of military affairs." The first condition of Reidan was the title of Duke and the First Winter; after the victory, he became the founder of the Origin dynasty. In Evergard there is a rare icon “Light Carriers Play Stratemes”.

Northern tradition connects the strata with the alliance of the Origins with the Lillidays. When both houses were arguing about the championship, the Earl of Lilliday and the Duke of Origin decided to arrange a fight. Origin insisted on duels in the strata, but the kairs supported the count: the sword fight will reveal "both mind and courage", and the strata - only mind. The fight dragged on, and the wounded count lowered the sword. On the question "Do you agree to the executions?" he replied that the Duke had already proved his mind, the first to understand that it was impossible to cut to death, and recognized himself as a vassal of the First Winter. ([[stvorki-neba|Gates of Heaven]])

## Stratems in the story

### Mira and Adrian
Since childhood, Mira played with her father, Sir Clive, and knew the game perfectly well. Having experienced humiliation at the court, where she was considered a paltry “sorrow”, she replied to herself: “I am alive. I'm smart. I am a spark." Also, "Iskra", are the chapters told from her point of view.

In the summer of 1774, Adrian played in a gazebo with [[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]] in front of the court. The Duke developed a powerful frontal attack in the center. The emperor led the three knights through the forest in a deep bypass, leaving the infantry to their fate, and the courtiers whispered trying to tell him the rescue move. Mira quietly said that the lord would win in six moves: one knight in the forest was a hoax, and the other two were enough to sweep away the sickle guard and knock down both sparks of the duke. Adrian confirmed her calculation, and Aiden admitted defeat, throwing a bad look at the girl.

Then the joker [[menson|Manson]] yelled: “Girl and strata — will be fun!”, and the emperor invited “Lady Gloria” to play. Mira pressed the army to the edge of the field, hiding sparks in the grove and in the ruins of the castle. Adrian solved her trap for the cavalry and threw the knights into the rear, to both her sparks. At the last moment, Mira led the coin to the stream and bribed the azure swordsman who blocked the road. Realizing that the next move would bring her victory, she tried to give in, but Adrian forbade:
> "Lady Gloria, if you're going to give in to me, you know I'm going to think it's an insult."

Her swords came out on the road, the cavalry broke out of the grove, and the emperor admitted defeat. The courtiers quietly withdrew so as not to witness the loss of the lord. Manson broke the awkwardness: he kissed Mira with an ankle and shouted: “Smart girl! I love smart girls! This party made her visible at court and aroused the jealousy of [[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]].

The party with Adrian became a symbol for Mira. In 1775, gathering assistants in the execution room, she took a spark from the field and said that the dream of "not being a sickle, to be a spark" was naive: "The spark is also moved by the hand of the player." During the siege of Faunterra, Adrian, taking it to himself, solved a task similar to the situation in the war. Ollie in Fantasia played on his board "the famous party of Minerva against Adrian".

### Erwin
[[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]] is known as the best player in execution in the entire North. As a young man, a few years before 1774, he beat Mira's father, Sir Clive, "a beautiful player" three times in a row. He told Kair Martin that Martin's uncle, the Bishop of Belomorya, had once defeated the Duke of [[desmond-oridzhin|Desmond]] three times in a row, and he wanted his son to be taught the same. Erwin himself spoke modestly about the game:
> “Stratems are not a simple matter. Just move the chips and try to put them in a pack.”

That is what he built the negotiations on. While leaning Duke [[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]] into the union, Erwin noticed that Adrian "didn't have much chips", and advised to teach Infante Laura the game of stratagems. Farway replied, “Think you’ve got another chip.” Before the 1774 campaign, Kair [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis Lilliday]] told him bluntly, "Stratems are one thing, and battle is quite another." In delusional visions for the River, he played with General William Davey. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]])

### Other parties
- Emperor Telurian, learning about the plot of his brother, outwardly did not give himself away: he joked at dinner with the nobles, and then played a game with his brother in execution. At breakfast the next day, he was “still cheerful and serene.”
- Becoming Empress, Minerva chose the place of the most confidential meetings room for execution in the Palace of Pen and Sword. The military councils of Minerva and Northerners were held at the strata table in 1775.
- In 1775, the courtier [[navi|Navi]], who assured that he had played only once, thirteen years ago, twice beat Minerva. The first time her spark fell into a trap, the second time his defensive stratum went on the attack on the thirtieth move, "in the only suitable moment". For every win, he demanded an answer to the question. According to [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]], Navi is only happy when he beats him in stratum.
- Adrian in Shayland played with his wife [[magda-labelin|Magda]], and she, "unlike Minerva", guessed to lose.
- [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], in a delirium, heard from the imaginary Erwin that the worst thing was "you never learned to play stratums."
- The word “sorrow” has become dirty. Shavanka Chara, telling how Moran betrayed his riders, agreed with the hunter that the leader gave them as a sacrifice, "like sickles."

## The Author's Article
In 2019, an article by Roman Surzhikov about the executions, collected from his posts in the community “World of Polari” on Facebook, appeared on the “Polaripedia” wiki of the cycle on Fandom. ([Source](https://polari.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%A1%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BC%D1%8B)) The rules in it are more detailed than in the novels, and in some ways diverge from them. Here's what she adds.
- **Origin.** The game was invented by [[pramater-agata|Foremother Agatha]] about 50 years after the Descent - as an exercise in foresight. The Lightbearers, fanatical devotees of Agatha, brought it fame in the seventh to ninth centuries; from the ninth century, especially after the Wars of Despair, commanders began to see stratems as a model of real warfare. Until the sixteenth century, the main playing piece was called the “star”; "spark" she was nicknamed when the nobles came into fashion spark weapons.
- **Move and fight.** The move on the article - two "touches" in a short game and four in full (in the novel - "three movements with different chips"). Touching its chip moves it (infantry on the cage, cavalry on two), touching the enemy is an attack from a neighboring cell. You can not touch one of your chips twice per move, and one enemy can be attacked by several. Chips without armor - sickle, bow, coin, spark - are immediately removed from the blow; the sword and horseshoe are turned over by the "wounded", beaten in response, and only the second attack knocks them down. Stratema moves at the speed of its slowest chip, hits all the chips relating to the target, and always responds to the blow. Rebuilding it is expensive, so the one who correctly built the strata from the very beginning wins. The goal is to knock down or drive out the enemy spark in a short game or both in full.
- **Coin.** One coin bribes two sickles or archers or one sword and leaves the field. The horseshoe is bought only two coins at a time, the spark - never.
- **Set.** In a short batch, each has a spark, two horseshoes, four swords, two coins and eight sickles. By convention, two more coins, four bows and special chips are added: the Shavan cavalry (without armor, but at a speed of 3), crossbowmen breaking through armor, engineers building fortifications. For a long batch, the set is doubled. Chips are made of wood, bone or silver; traditional colors are blue and red, but the nobility is ordered by the family. Horseshoe, although it means a knight, so is not called: at the table sit the knights, and it is embarrassing to call them the title of the village. In the novels, however, Adrian leads around the “three knights”.
- **Field.** A simple field is 128 cells without relief, expensive - from 256 to 512 cells with a map of real lands. The road speeds up the chips, the river speeds up the movement along it, but requires extra touch to cross. The forest deprives the cavalry of speed and punching power, archers on the hill and any chips on the wall beat proactively, chips go fast on the water, but do not fight. On the field marked "native land" for placement, and the loss means their loss. The first player puts half of the chips, the second - all, then the first - the rest.
- **Outcomes.** Unconditional victory - all enemy sparks are shot down, their bodies - brings a double bet. At the "merciful" the defeated leads a spark over the edge of the field with the words "Your field, sir", and the winner shakes his hand: "Wise choice". "Dear" victory, with the loss of their spark, do not congratulate - remember the fallen. A draw occurs when the losses are so great that it is unworthy to drive sparks through an empty field.
- **Three and four.** The three play two on one, and a single person has double touches, a strong castle or more knights: for example, [[rejs|Reis]] and Halliwell model against Origin. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]]) The four play permanent alliances or "temporary" alliances where an ally can be betrayed; knowing such rules is disrespectful, but occasionally plays to remember that any alliance is temporary.
- **Label.** A short game lasts one and a half or two hours, a long one lasts three or four, and an invitation to play in the evening is equal to an invitation to stay overnight. The room for execution is quiet, the windows are curtained, the field is brightly lit, best of all with a spark lamp. They eat fish, vegetables and sweets, drink coffee or strong tea, wine - only before and after the party. At first, fifteen minutes of talking about an outsider, looking at the opponent. They play in thimbles, they take no more than a minute to move, but two or three times per game you can think a quarter of an hour. Viewers have the right to predict the outcome out loud, but not to talk about the outsider. The party is a sign of trust: the strata give too much character, and the true enemy will not sit down with you.

## In "Fantasies"
In "[[fantazii|Fantasies]]", stories about the possible fate of the heroes in 1776, the strata remain fun for the nobility.
- At Minerva's birthday party in the First Winter, Prime vassals of Origin argued how it could have been even better to win the Battle of the First Winter, and played it on three stratum fields. General [[stetkhem|Statham]] wanted to show Erwin a new battleground. During the performance, Statham and [[blekberri|Blackberry]] stayed in the castle for the party.
- Erwin joked that after a year of calculations he would be afraid to sit down with [[robert-oridzhin|Robert]] at the strata table. In entrusting the ghost of Dalia to portray Agatha, he advised her to do “something Agathan: well, break the pen, play stratagems.” ([[dalliya-rejven|Dallia Raven]])
- In the execution room of the Palace of Pen and Sword in Faunterra, [[adrian|Adrian]] and [[magda-labelin|Magda]] received the Shavan chief of the Golden Grive. ([[zlataya-griva|Golden Mane]])
