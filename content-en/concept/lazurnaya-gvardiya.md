## Description
The Azure Guard is the elite of the imperial army, the best warriors recruited in different lands of the state. These are noble knights, and ordinary interrogators, as it turned out in the “A Doll on the Throne”, could not even interrogate them properly. Guards in blue plate armor with spark spears stand on the sides of the throne in the [[dvorets-pera-i-mecha|Palace of Pen and Sword]]. At the receptions, four azure guards carry a watch on the fifth stage of the throne elevation, and others, "shining with azure armor", serve as gatekeepers of the throne room.

### Three Guardians of the Lord
According to [[protektsiya|the protection]] agents, "the lord has three guardians: scarlet, azure and invisible." The Scarlet Guard is the "elite infantry of the emperor" with spark spears; it is recruited from the most worthy warriors of the Earth by the Crown. ([[zemli-korony|Crownlands]]) Countess Sybil called the Red Guards "the best soldiers in the empire", except for the azure. (In early media reports, she says the scarlet guard guards palace gardens, entrances, and stairs, and calls her "the best guards.") The azure stands higher, on the second floor of the palace, at the Lord's chambers. The soldiers themselves, ironically notes Mark, believed that the azure guard is different from the scarlet, like a lion from a bull.

The color of the guard is the color of the dynasty. Before the lord Evrian, the imperial troops consisted mainly of knightly cavalry, and the knights of the Crown wore azure cloaks with gold coats of arms: gold on the lazuli - the colors of Pramateri Janmei and the Brilliant Dynasty. The azure along with scarlet and brown remains a dynastic color at Minerva as well.

### Organisation
Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark notes that she has never seen azure guards with spears on the premises: the spear is too uncomfortable there. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) They wear spears only in the guard of honor at celebrations, and on ordinary duty - daggers and spark swords.

The guard is divided into companies. In addition to the usual companies, there was a special azure battalion: Lieutenant Shatterhand served in his third company, Captain Hugo Anne Margaret Grace of the Ludmila family commanded the second. In the barracks outside the palace there was a reserve company. A special directive of the Crown prescribed that at any danger to the Holy See Citadel immediately take out the property of the Dynasty by the forces of a select azure company. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) People from all over the empire came to the guard, and among them there were many northerners. The kairas do not serve in the Crown Guard, but the Greeks who have failed the test, the warriors who have lost their suzerainty, and the northerners who have grown up in a foreign land often find themselves in the scarlet, or even in the azure guard.

## History
### Conspiracy
Similarities with azure were once used by conspirators. In the conspiracy of the Night Falcons, the bribed ceremonymaster let sixty rebellious knights dressed in blue armor enter the palace, “like an azure guard.” In confusion, they reached the emperor's bedroom and forced him to take poison. After that, the rule was established: the bride of the emperor, her brothers and father in the service should be no more than sixty knights.

During the Shutov plot, twenty-five conspirators freely went to the palace: the guard at the bridge missed them for high titles, and the guard at the entrance was bribed. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) Only on the second floor were the azure guards, loyal to the lord. The conspirators laid them with sparks, but in their rooms an ambush was already waiting for them.

## In the cycle
### Under Adrian
Vladyka Adrian fenced with the azure guards. One day, a newcomer, out of ignorance, began to yield to him, and the emperor knocked out his sword three times. In the summer of 1774, Adrian announced that the protection of "Lady Minerva of Stagforth", the second heiress to the throne, was entrusted to the azure guard. In fact, the impostor [[gloriya-nortvud|Gloria Northwood]] lived at the court under this name. In the autumn, when Adrian left with the army to the south, the azure knights, along with other troops, left the capital; Erwin [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia]] wrote about this. At the same time, a detachment of azure under the command of Lieutenant Shatterhand "by special order of His Majesty" rescued the real [[minerva|Minerva]] from Waymar. In December 1774, Captain Hugo Grace came to Aleridan for Peace with the people of the priory, stating that he had been "sent personally by His Majesty". Shatterhand, who locked himself in with the girl, learned from the name of an old colleague in a special battalion. A few days later, on December 22, 1774, Captain Grace was on Adrian's train, and when the train fell off the bridge, it fell into icy water with Sir Sanders and Sir Locke; according to Sanders, the captain had crashed while still in the fall. ([[sanders|Sanders]])

### Loss of Items
In December 1774, [[ervin-oridzhin|Origin]] broke into the Cathedral Citadel. The reserve azure company was raised on alarm, but it was late: the northerners had already occupied the palace. Sir Augustus Mace proposed to immediately go on the assault, but Captain Whitmore strictly besieged him, ordered only to observe the island and went to the burgomaster Escher. ([[baron-esher|Baron Esher]]) The company then took the Dynasty Items out of the Citadel by train towards [[marengo|Marengo]]. ([[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]]) A few hours later, the guards began to fall ill: the poison was in the water, and Captain Whitmore himself was one of the first to fall. He handed over the command to Mace, who, taking care of the soldiers, stopped the train at Orwell. While the healthy were looking for doctors and helping the sick, the bandits who settled there in a volley of crossbows killed almost the entire watch, uncoupled the wagon with the Objects and stole it. Mace was not expelled for this, but since then he has been known as "the most famous loser of the azure guard" and remained at court "out of pity". Whitmore and the surviving azure then at their own risk hunted down the kidnappers along with the scarlet Captain Backfield. ([[krojdon-bekfild|Croydon Beckfield]]) Although Backfield was older, the last word was left to Whitmore the Yankee.

### Minerva Guard
After the war, the guard shrank to one company, and most of the guards in the palace carried Origin's cairns. Minerva appointed Shatterhand as commander of this company, and he proudly said that he “came out as the chief commander of the azure guard.” He immediately asked for funds for the recruitment of the second company, and a little complained about the kire: “the palace was flooded.” Azure became the support of Minerva. They guarded her during interrogations, during negotiations with Moran and at the Holy Field. ([[svyatoe-pole|Standoff at the Holy Field]]) In the personal train of the mistress, the first and last carriages were occupied by detachments of the azure guard. When a jug of water was carried to the mistress, the left of the two sentries at her door, "according to tradition," drank a tiny glass of it.

In the Yanmei Hunt, Origin placed the guard under the supervision of his "special company" - officers with agate ribbons in buttonholes. It was said to be Minerva's revenge for beating him on the Holy Field. The Severians commanded the cordon, the gates and each floor of the palace, and there were enough kire to put red and black on each azure cloak. Once Erwin, playing the villain, expelled Minerva and her maid of honor from the palace, and Shatterhand tried to protect them and threatened: “The Azure Guard does not forgive offenses!” In Marengo, the "shelter of the emperors", there were two more azure companies of Whitmore: one held the station, the other - the palace and the wave. Minerva fled there in the spring of 1775. Later, Whitmore and his company rushed from Marengo to the capital with a march-throw, because the trains did not go. In the same book, Whitmore unexpectedly came out of a secret passage in the tomb of Lady Ingrid in Arden, when Erwin appeared there at night. Together with the marsh, "the sting of the crybola", he kept the Drop of the Sun on the orders of Minerva, who received information about it from Lady in Darkness. The hall was filled with hydrogen, and Whitmore stopped the keels, preventing them from lighting the torches: "Lord Chancellor, if you light a fire, you will burn alive." The order to burn those who would take the Object, he considered not related to Erwin and ordered to open the doors.

The azure ones also figure in the old secret that Count [[elias-nortvud|Elias Northwood]] told Ione. After the War of the Objects of 1756, the azure guards stabbed two bodyguards of young Vittor and Martin Shayland to death in a "match of honor", and the second was Prince Adrian himself. The winners received only a reprimand. Elias suspected that the bodyguards saw something secret, and one of them Shayland somehow saved.

### The Shadow of the Great Tree
"The Azure Guard idolized Minerva". Leaving Faunterra on Adrian's return, she took the azures with her. The courtier explained to Adrian that it was impossible to prevent her from taking the regalia away: the azure "big, strong guys, yes, with spark weapons," and they "went away with it themselves." At the military council, both azure commanders unanimously vowed to defend her "to the last drop of blood." Minerva replied, “I don’t need to be protected. I need a win!”

At the end of August 1775, at Patrick's Chapel, sending Robert Origin with a battalion on ships, Minerva decided to remain "with one azure company". During the night attack, she scolded the attacking commander "Sergeant Shatterhand" in anger and herself built guards around herself, emerging from the darkness. Minerva then moved the capital to the First Winter, where she arrived with the Glove of Power, the Azure Guard and General Davey's spark regiment. ([[uiljam-dejvi|William Davy]]) Later, Whitmore's second azure company returned to her. Minerva immediately felt "twice as powerful", and Whitmore and Shatterhand exchanged "primitive greetings and jealous looks".
