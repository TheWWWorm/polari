## Description

According to [[adrian|Adrian]], the Voice of God is a device “like a wave, but much more perfect”: you can talk to a person at any distance, without wires, with your voice. The connection is established by keywords and the number of the called Object; only the carrier of the [[pervokrov|firstblood]] can control it. There are several such bracelets in Polaris. In the spring of 1775, Erwin reasoned that the Origins had one Voice of God, the Puppeteer had another, and Paul should have a third, if any.

In the "Speaking to God" interlude, the storyteller - a sailor nicknamed [[potomok|Descendant]] - describes the bracelet as a wonderful product similar to an engagement bracelet, but made "out of nothing": the material is smooth as glass, but warm as living skin, and its color is a mixture of amber and milk. ([[govoryashchij-s-bogom|He Who Speaks with God]]) Without gold and stones, he was more graceful than any jewel. When the object came to life, it "shined with moonlight", and from Gwenda's breath the milk material was covered with turbidity.

## Trophies from Beyond (He Who Speaks with God)

In "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]", [[dzhemis-lillidej|Jemis]] got a bracelet in the fort beyond the River: when the detachment caught the sentries by surprise, one of them grabbed the Object, apparently to signal the owner. First, the subject was taken for a feather. Jemis wore it and aimed it into the darkness of the forest, but the bracelet, "with all its milky white appearance" showing indifference, did not respond to any attempt. [[mark-voron|Mark]] also tried on both hands, aloud and mentally, at sunset and at dawn - and eventually offered to sell the Item, because the silent he was worth forty thousand.

Together with the detachment, [[gvenda|Gwenda]] was rescued. During the battle on the burning bridge over the abyss, the bracelet came to life in her hands: she lifted it to her lips, whispered something - and the Subject said in a foreign voice: "On the connection." Mark in two or three seconds guessed that this is a means of communication of the master of the fort, the one who owned the Rings. Depicting a simple-minded peasant, Stanley, "speaking to God", he asked Gwenda to revive the bracelet at every stop and began pious conversations, hoping to lure the enemy into an ambush. "God" never answered, but the bracelet glowed, and Mark knew he was being listened to. One day, the “god” himself summoned him with the words “Contact”.

Then the bracelet was taken by Lady [[sofiya-oridzhin|Sophia Origin]]. Named, she offered a deal to the one who owns the Rings and staged a civil war: let him send a man and heal Desmond from a stone disease, and she will return the bracelet. The answer was brief: bring the bracelet and husband to Faunterra. Thus began the story of Znaharka (see Hand of Znaharka). ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]])

The voice of God was later carried by Gwenda. In the summer of 1775, Onday found this bracelet on one of the burned ships of the Northerners and sent it to Paul. He was pleased with the find and showed Alanis the second same Item on his wrist. Paul was contacted by the Puppeteer. When the Kairs laid siege to Waymar and poisoned the well, Vittor begged Paul to hurry, and he, grinning Alanis, lied that because of the calm will come no earlier than five days, and broke off the challenges.

## Voice and Deconstructor

According to [[navi|Navi]]'s story, the deconstructor was born from the Subject, which in the imperial registers is called the Rose of Happiness: it is a rod covered with drops of liquid tin - a medical "cleaner". The touch of its drop causes other Objects to emit "invisible heat" that burns the infection; so you can sterilize both Objects and entire chambers. If the bearer of the firstblood takes a drop of dew and orders “Pure!”, it will roast from the inside and turn into a mummy. The cleaner can be “translated as a clock” so that it does not clean, but destroys items, but such a setting is not included in the standard list of keys.

Paul could not calculate the desired frequency and picked it up with trials and errors: he introduced the slaves to the Rose of Happiness and ordered them to say strange words, after which they dried up like mummies. Finding the frequency, he took Rosa himself and reconfigured her in his own blood without giving an executive command. Since then, it was enough for him to rub the Object on his skin so that “detonator particles” would dwell on it. There was a silver stripe on Paul's forearm, about which he rubbed the Objects: so Alanis saw him "mark" the Sun Drop. ([[kaplya-solntsa|Drop of the Sun]]) Then, on a command transmitted through the Voice of God, the Object, along with the surrounding space, a radius of several yards passed into a "zero-passive state".

Navi explained to Minerva that the energy of the "hot annihilation" of the Object would be many times greater than the hydrogen bomb: even the lightest of them, the Gauntlet of Power, would destroy Faunterra, and the heaviest, the Womb, would vaporize the Smoky Dahl. Objects are protected from such an explosion, and instead of it there is a "cold annihilation": a certain amount of matter turns into a vacuum - does not burn and does not evaporate, but turns into a zero form, which does not exist for any observer.

You can destroy only those Objects that Paul touched - neither the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]], nor the Glove, nor the property of the Origins to the deconstructor is not subject. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]) That's how Fort Cooking was wiped out. Paul also tried to kill Navi, adjusting to get a mined Light Sphere. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]])

## The Shadow of the Great Tree

Captured in Waymar, Paul bought his life by giving Vittore the deconstructor and the Voice of God with the keys. Adrian, through the captive [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]], the bearer of the firstblood, communicated with Paul by the Voice of God.

### Baron [[dorkaster|Dorcaster]] in [[lid|Leed]]
When Leed surrendered to the Chosen One, [[chara|Chara]], one-armed Sormah and Paul Knuth, the city's commandant, came to the commandant of the city and demanded that the city be given to the Shavans for plunder. The Baron showed them the Perst, and they laughed, “We have two dozen of them!” Then he showed another Object, and Knut turned pale: “The Voice of God...” The Baron threatened that he could make any of their Rings disappear along with the owner, and the Shavans left. In the morning, [[martin-shejland|Martin Shayland]] secretly “borrowed” this Voice from the Baron, showed it to Paul and offered a deal: Leed to the horde, and to him to the captive [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]]. The baron was left with one Perst, and the Shavans plundered the city. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], having subjected Martin to Ulyana Dust, took away the Feather from him, and the Voice of God took Erwin from Martin. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Martin explained that through the Voice it is possible to call not a person, but an Object by number, but did not know the keys themselves, so the Origins, although they had "their own deconstructor", could not control it.

### Death of Paul
On December 2, 1775, Paul demanded that Wittor have the Voice of God with a deconstructor to kill Navi. In Cosuli's grotto, he whispered to the team, but blew up not the fake Sphere on the altar, but the real one hidden next to him in a niche - and died himself. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]])

### Adrian's Keys
The next day, Vittor moved in with Adrian, hoping to get the Voice of God and the Bird. ([[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) At the request of the Lord, he dictated the numbers of all the vulnerable Objects left by the enemies - the Rings of Paul, Chara, Knuth, Flies, and behind them the Hands of the Znaharka, the Light Sphere and the Voice of God - checking with notes in the Drop of the Sun, a total of almost twenty numbers. He then called the "call key" and the "executive key": the numbers could be read not out loud, but only with the eyes. Vittor assured that this would be enough for the foundation and walls of the First Winter to collapse. After that, the count was imprisoned in the Wreath. ([[chrevo|The Womb]])

Adrian called in the voice of God [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]]. He, who had just lost his brother, father and friend in the battle, proposed to convene a small meeting of the Chamber in the First Winter and forgive Adrian as the “assistant” of Kuklovyd. Adrian, on the other hand, demanded surrender and threatened that he would follow the castle through the Bird and, if the white flag was not raised within half an hour, would blow up all the Northerners' Items, "including the whole property of the House Origin", although Vittor himself admitted that these Items Paul did not touch. Then, when Magda demanded peace, Adrian ordered Johan to destroy the Hand of the Znacharka that Iona was wearing. Johan died of the poison at the same moment, and the threat did not materialize: without the carriers of the firstblood, Adrian could no longer use the deconstructor or launch the Bird.
