Disconnections and errors - a summary of the known discrepancies of the Polari cycle. This includes contradictions between books and within one book, typos, errors of early editions and edits made by the author himself. Most of the edits went to the first novel, “[[strela-moneta-iskra|Arrow, Coin, Spark]]” (media): in 2026, the author released his revised edition, and the wiki considers it his canon. Many points are discussed in more detail in the articles to which reference is made. Where we know which version of the wiki holds, that's said.

Joking "mistakes of the author", which Lady [[karen|Karen]] finds in the collector's edition, is part of the artistic design. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) They are collected in the article "[[avtor-v-mire|The Author (in the world of Polari)]]", and only those who then corrected the 2026 edition came here.

## Publications and author's corrections
- **Two texts of the first novel.** Early media publications (including the text in the summary file of the cycle) and the 2026 edition differ markedly. The new edition cuts and cuts scenes, corrects titles, pedigrees, dates and numbers. According to the author, once he found the novel delayed and removed several scenes from it, but the plot did not change (spring stream 2026). Wiki follows the 2026 edition, and early versions are marked "in early editions".
- **Mistake of the first edition of the genus Becky.** In early media outlets, [[rebekka-litlend|Rebecca Litland]] is referred to as "the Yangmei family". The author called this a mistake of the very first edition: Becca is a kind of Sophia (chat "Worlds of RS", October 31, 2026). More details in the sections below.
- **"The Price of Fear".** In the summary text of the series “[[tsena-strakha|The Price of Fear]]” is printed the last chapters of the novel “Humans and Gods”. On FantLab and in stores, it is listed as a separate posthuman, published in 2025.
- **"The Confident" and "The Bishop's Tree".** These stories stand outside the chronological line of the cycle. In some reading order lists (for example, on FantLab) they are put in front of the “Shadow of the Great Tree”, and according to the author in the chat, these are independent stories that can be read at any time. Vicky places them after the "Shadow of the Great Tree" (see Confidential, Bishop's Christmas Tree). ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]; [[yolka-episkopa|The Bishop's Christmas Tree]])
- **Rima or Polly.** In the early media (Chapter 7), [[polli|Polly]] is once mistakenly named Rimma. In the 2026 edition, the name has been corrected.
- **Prints of 2026 edition.** The new media text has its misprints: "from the Descent of the Prameters" in the date of Chapter 7, "four miles" in the description of Shayland (Chapter 12), "Uyemar" instead of Waymar (Chapter 23), "with the str Adamar" and "pen and oven." in Chapter 14.
- **"Fame".** In early media publications, [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]'s surname was once printed as "Fame".

## What changed the 2026 edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark
Below are only notable retcons. Most of the changes of the new edition are a quiet edition that does not contradict anything.
- **Gallard is a prior.** In early editions, [[gallard-almera|Gallard Almera]] is "Archbishop of Faunterra and High Priest of the Proto-Patriarchal Church", equal to the reigning duke, and is addressed by "your lordship". In 2026, he is the primate, the head of the Proto-patristic Church, and “your Eminence” addresses him.
- **Elina instead of Margarita.** The Pramateric branch of the Church in the early editions of the rule of the eighty-year-old "prioress Margarita". In 2026, it is the Archimate of Ellin, as in later books. ([[ellina|Ellina]])
- **Rod Stagforth.** In early editions, Clive's sire genus is not named, and Leila Talmir says he "wasn't original". ([[klajv-stagfort|Clive Stagfort]]) In 2026, he was of Emilia. But Lord [[lord-klarens|Clarence]] from the family of Emilia passed into the family of Lyudmila.
- **Prehistory of the conspiracy.** ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) In early editions, [[menson|Manson]] administered the Crownlands’ ports and a corps of fifteen thousand men, and fell into disfavour over a loan to the [[dom-lajthart|Lighthearts]]. In the 2026 edition, he commands the fleet and a corps of ten thousand marines, and falls into disfavour after marrying Karen Lightheart: Tellurian publicly rebuked his brother, who then set off on a year-long voyage to inspect the western ports.
- **Adamar's killers.** In early editions, Mark gave Mira a “final argument”: Sera [[adamar|Adamar]] was allegedly killed by a disguised sailor from the Southern Way, a vassal of Labelin. In 2026, there is no such evidence: Labelin’s “last argument” is his friendship with General Alexis, and the employer himself, with a northern reprimand, collected unverified bandits in the slums, and Mark sees this as a backup plan, not the machinations of the northern lord.
- **Summer Games in Sofia.** In early editions, the engagement of the emperor was announced on the last day of the summer games, August 7, and the wedding fell on October 25. In 2026, the games open on Sofiyin Day, June 24, and the engagement is announced a week later (see [[kalendar|Calendar]]).
- **Seventeen Foremothers.** In Chapter 5, the "sixteen" Foremothers are replaced by the "seventeen", that is, Ulyana Sad is included in the score. In other chapters (prayers in Chapter 9, bargaining in Chapter 21) the usual “sixteen” (see Pramateri) remained. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]])
- **The Fang Knight is the first son.** In early editions, [[krejg-nortvud|Craig Northwood]] is referred to in chapter 21 as the "second son" of the Earl. In 2026 - the "first", as in the entire cycle.
- **Cut scenes.** The media removed Harmon's visit to Sir [[logan-katrina|Logan Katrina]], scenes with [[kolin-felisiya|Colin Felicia]], and Mira's walk with Ethan at the Amber Gallery. The Duchess of Alicia disappeared from the parade scene.

## Inside "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
### Dates and terms in "Arrow, coin, spark"
- **How many years of conspiracy.** Corvis' letter is dated October 19, 1755, and the conspiracy failed a week later, so that nineteen years had passed by 1774. In the 2026 edition, this is referred to as "twenty years ago" (chapters 14 and 37), "eighteen" (chapter 19, about Farway), and "nineteen" (chapter 34). In the early editions, chapter 14 also contained “eighteen.”
- **Summer Games in August.** In the 2026 edition, the games are moved to Sofiin Day, June 24, but in Chapter 19 about the ball in early June, it is still said that the know will be in the capital until the end of the summer games, "what will be in August."
- **The 17th gift.** The Guide refers to the fall of Dar to August 1756. In Waymar, in the spring of 1774, Harmon remembers that Dar arrived "a decade and a half ago", and at the end of the novel we are talking about "eighteen years" (see [[semnadtsatyj-dar|Seventeenth Gift]]).
- **Erwin's meeting with Aiden.** The chapter with Erwin and Aiden Almera's negotiations is dated September 2, 1773, and in Chapter 13, Erwin recalls having spoken to the Duke "in early December" (see [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin Origin]]).
- **Where was Vittor?** On May 9, 1774, Sybil explained to the emperor that Count Vittor was celebrating his wedding in the First Winter, although Harmon found him at home in Waymar on the same days. Perhaps the countess was only looking for an excuse (see Vittor and Iona's marriage). ([[brak-vittora-i-iony|Wedding of Iona and Vittor]])

### Numbers in "Arrow, Coin, Spark"
- **Labelin's stuff.** Vittor says Labelin has sixty Objects, "or sixty-one" (chapter 15). [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] himself, in chapter 38 of the 2026 edition, boasts seventy, and in early editions sixty (see House of Labelin). ([[dom-labelin|House Labelin]])
- **Section of the Gift.** Karen notes in the Collector's Edition that in the usual three-part section of Dar, the Earl of Shayland would have been assigned forty-seven Items, not thirty-nine: whether he gave eight to someone, "or our author just calculated."

### Names, titles and pedigrees in Arrow, Coin, Spark
- **Rod Becky.** In the 2026 edition, Becca is named "the Sophia family" in both Ethan's presentation and Mira's guess (Chapter 17). However, in the same chapter, arguing with Countess Sybil, Mira still says that Becca "belongs to the Yangmei family". Vicky follows the author: Becca is a kind of Sophia.
- **The Countess of Nexia.** Erwin twice mentally refers to Nexia Flame as "the Countess" (chapters 22 and 35), although she is the daughter of the Count, not the Countess.
- **Benjamin or Benjamin.** Joaquin greets the knight as "Sir Benjamin" and he appears as "Sir [[ben-vomak|Benjamin Womack]]" (chapter 18; see Ben Womack).
- **Aiden's wife.** Duchess Alicia, wife of Ayden Almera, is only named in early editions where she sits next to her husband in a parade. ([[ajden-almera|Aiden Almera]]) In the 2026 edition, Aiden comes with his son and daughter, and his wife's name in the cycle no longer sounds.
- **Remains of deleted scenes.** Scenes with [[kolin-felisiya|Colin Felicia]] from the 2026 edition have been removed, but in chapter 23 he still whispers to Mira at the voice-on-distance show, and her report mentions a "walk with Colin", which is no longer in the book.
- **Julian or Julian.** The code of laws of the Empress Juliana in the edition of 2026 is called "Julianin", but in Desmond's words about the punishment for the rebellion remained the form of "Julians", as if from a male name (see Julian law). ([[yulianov-zakon|Juliana's Law]])
- **Karen's amendments, taken into account in 2026.** Farway's rail road now leads to Aleridan rather than Litland. Tommy is called Erwin's butler, not his grey (see Author (in the world of Polari)). ([[avtor-v-mire|The Author (in the World of Polari)]])

## Between “Arrow, Coin, Spark” and “The Spirit of the West”
- **The Litland family.** In The [[dusha-zapada|Soul of the West]], Becky's father exclaims "we are the Janmei family!", and Becca herself calls herself "Rebecca Eleanor Agatha of the Janmei family, Lady Litland." According to the author, Becca is a kind of Sophia, and the “kind of Yangmei” is a mistake of early publications. Vicky considers Becca a sofievka, and the House of Litland - according to the "Soul of the West" - the home of the Yangmei family. ([[dom-litlend|House Litland]]) How one is combined with the other is not explained in the books.
- **How many Shavans.** Six Shavans came to the negotiations in Lacey, and in the fight Becca sees seven. The father's knights at the meeting were seven, and in battle - a "dozen" (see the ambush in Lacey). ([[zasada-v-lejsi|Ambush at Lacey]])

## In "Only One Star"
- **Logan's cheese castle.** In "[[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]]" (the head of "Sword"), Joaquin drives past the castle of Ser Logan and recalls telling Harmon about it in the spring: "I'll build a lot better for myself." ([[logan-katrina|Logan Katrina]]) This scene is only in the early media, and in the 2026 edition, a visit to Logan is cut.
- **The Silver Fox shelves.** According to Adrian's court, Lis led out of Labelin "incomplete four regiments". In Statham's report and in Erwin's words, "incomplete three" of the twelve (see Labelin massacre). ([[reznya-v-labeline|Night in Labelin]])

## "A Doll on the Throne"
- **Elena's course.** In the early media, the entrance to the flower festival was worth “two deer — sixty-four agates”, from which a course of 32 agates for a deer was deduced. In the 2026 edition it is said "Ilene - sixty-four agates". This is in line with [[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]], where paper fir is sold for seven flories instead of eight: fir ? 8 ? 64 agates. Wiki follows this course (see [[dengi|Money]]).
- **Snowdrops: Numbers.** The chapter "Sword" names forty thousand Snowdrops, Erwin on the council - almost eighty thousand, Buckley - one hundred thousand. Minerva says to write a bill for eighty thousand ephes, and then says that he wrote a deposit “only forty thousand.” General Gore and Erwin speak of four Parliamentarians killed, although Hampton had one of the four lying still "moved" (see [[vosstanie-podsnezhnikov|Snowdrop Rebellion]]).
- **The Smoky Distance River.** Minerva says that only one river flows out of the lake - Holliwell, and the "Guide" and scenes in Waymar consider the Smokey Dahl and the origin of [[torrej|Torrey]] (see Smokey Dahl). ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]])
- **Heart of Light under siege.** Neymir claims that in the First Horse War, the Heart of Light survived a year-long siege, although the city fell after such a siege. Most likely, Neymir is just teasing Chara (see [[serdtse-sveta|Heart of Light]]).

## In the Gate of Trust
- **Prophet's dreams: how many years ago.** In the Gate of Trust, the first dream of Francis-Ilianus was “nine years ago”, and in the monastery the king spent eight years. ([[vrata-doveriya|The Gates of Trust]]; [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) There, both the first dream and the invitation of the Second of the Five are referred to as “eight years ago.” In the Phantasies, the prophet speaks again of the “nine years” (see The Dreams of Francis-Ilianus). ([[sny-frantsiska-iliana|Dreams of Francis-Ilian]])

## The “Yanmei Hunt”
- **Medium or Junior.** In one of the chapters of "Only One Star" Horace Northwood is called the youngest son of the Count. In the Yanmei Hunt and later he is the middle son and the youngest is Donald (see Northwood House). ([[dom-nortvud|House Northwood]])
- **Joaquin and Rantigar.** In the media, Joaquin conveys the words that Count Rantigar said to him personally. In the Yangmei Hunt, he says that he stood under the banners of the count, but personally "unfortunately, he did not meet". ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) Maybe Joaquin is just holding on to a modest legend (?) (see [[graf-rantigar|Count Rantigar]]).
- **How many votes in the House?** In the early media, Colin Felicia speaks of thirty-nine representatives, three from each area. The Yangmei Hunt calls the "common thirty-two voices": two from each land, two from the Crown, and two from each branch of the Church. In the 2026 edition, the scene with Colin is cut, and the discrepancy has disappeared (see Chamber). ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]])

## In "Humans and Gods"
- **Jonas Lighthart's sons.** In the 2026 edition of the media, Aiden's book states that Duke Jonas "and three of his sons died on the plow." Only two elders were executed in [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]], and the third, [[edgar-flejm|Edgar]], survived and became Flame. Early media reports only say that Hope's highest nobility was executed. Wiki follows "Humans and Gods".
- **Nexia's parents.** In the Yanmei Hunt, Nexia writes to Iona, "Father is Count Flame, mother is born Lady Lighthart", although Iona in the same scene calls the born Lighthart Nexia herself. In "Humans and Gods" and later Lighthart by birth named father, Edgar, who received the name of his father-in-law. Wiki follows this version (see [[nekseya-flejm|Nexia Flame]]).
- **Fingernails of the Hand.** In the "A Doll on the Throne" refers to the four thimbles, and in the "Humans and Gods", when Robert gives the subject to Minerva, five fall on the palm (see Hand of the Instructor). ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]])
- **The Arrowback Dam.** In "One Star Only", the height of the dam on the Back River is "one hundred feet", in "Humans and Gods" - "half a hundred yards", that is, one and a half hundred feet (see [[erroubek|Arrowback]]).
- **Melorange instead of Melison.** In "Humans and Gods" (Coin 2), it is said that [[kharmon|Harmon]] "still owned the estate in Melorange." In all other places of the cycle, his estate stands in [[melison|Melison]], and [[melorandzh|Melorange]] is the capital of Litland. Apparently, the author confused two names for "Mel-".

## The Shadow of the Great Tree
- **Who sends dreams.** In the Gates of Trust, Francis-Ilian dreams are sent by the "goddess of dreams", in the Shadows of the Great Tree by the Foremothers, who commanded to prevent the destruction of the world (see the [[sny-frantsiska-iliana|Dreams of Francis-Ilian]]). ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

## In "Fantasies"
- **Stagforth's coat of arms.** Erwin reads "a seagull over the waves" in the handbook, and in [[fantazii|Fantasies]], Catherine speaks of a white seagull "over the wall" (see Stagforth). ([[stagfort|Stagfort]])
- **Rod Arrowbacks.** In "A Doll on the Throne", Earl Arrowback is similar in gender traits to the Yelenite, and his daughter Rose in "Fantasies" is called "Foremother Agatha's pure-blood granddaughter" (see Arrowback). ([[erroubek|Arrowback]])
- **Earl of Arrowback's children.** Mark remembers that the count has a wife and five children, and Rose in “Fantasies” has no brothers and only two sisters.
- **The ninth dream of the prophet.** At lunch, the dream of a crucified man with a burning finger is interpreted as the capture of Paul, and in "Fantasies" it turns out to be a prediction of the "wooden construction" of the Gun (see [[sny-frantsiska-iliana|Dreams of Francis-Ilian]]).
