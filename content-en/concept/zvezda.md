**The star** shines over Polaris along with the Sun and Moon. It explains most of the mysteries of the cycle: the Gifts of the Gods, the Sacred Objects, the origin of the Foremothers, and the purpose of the secret order.

## In the faith of Polaris
According to the canon, the Mothers and Forefathers came out of the Underworld of the gods, and good souls after death go to them to the Star. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) Hence the turns of speech: according to the "Traveler", on the Mills "Are you what, in the steppe gathered?" meant the same thing that in the central lands "gathered on the Star". On the Lion's Gate Shimmery carved the Sun, Moon and Star, and on the pylons to the Star ascend the souls of the heroes. [[alanis-almera|Alanis Almera]]’s saying “There is only one Star” gave the title to the novel “Only One Star”. ([[lish-odna-zvezda|Only One Star]])

According to the "Guide", the luminaries move in the lumen between the turns of the world spiral. ([[vselenskaya-spiral|Universal Spiral]]) The star hangs in the south: sailors noted that from the Voltaic Islands it points strictly to the south, and from the rest of the Polaris is visible in the south-southwest. ([[folta|Folta]])

## The truth about the star
[[adrian|Adrian]] tried to look at the Star first, learning to control the Bird without the Flesh: the light did not reach the surface, but he saw something. ([[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]) In December 1775, he ordered [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], the bearer of [[absolyut|the Absolute]], to move to the Star and report what he had seen, and when he refused in fear, he imprisoned him in [[chrevo|the Womb]].

In the last chapter of the cycle, in the negotiations in the First Winter, Bird was ruled by [[minerva|Minerva]], and [[navi|Navi]] was prompted by the keys. Light flew out of the shadow of the planet, the World was shaded by the sun, and the Star began to grow: first, a flower with fiery petals, and with a “object lens” — a silver metal water mill wheel, with a wide rim, thick tubular spokes and mirror blades burning in the sun. A fragment of the axis was sticking out of the hub, and a lone fire flashed at its end. Size rulers showed thirty-five miles across. “The iron sun of the swordsmen...” Erwin burst out. Adrian replied, “Gentlemen, this is a sacred object. “The greatest and most powerful in the world.”

> “This is a lighthouse. "The False Pirate Lighthouse".

### The Story of Navi
Polaris does not lie on a turn of the galactic spiral, but in a dark starless gap between her sleeves. Its sun is shrouded in a gas-dust nebula: therefore, from the world of the gods it is not visible, and the Polarians do not see other stars. Two thousand years ago, the ancestors of the “gods” fought an interstellar war, and the fleet had to be thrown from sleeve to sleeve. To keep the navigators from straying far away, sixteen beacons emitting neutrino rays were thrown into the void. Fifteen orbited far away, and the sixteenth orbited the lone sun of Polaris, where it could replenish its energy reserves.

The enemy found a way to disrupt flights. The divers' cruiser made its way to the lighthouse, moved it closer to the planet and distorted the signal. Since then, the rays led the ships “directly to the reefs”: coming out of the warp, the ships by inertia crashed into Polaris. The lighthouse was not destroyed by everyone - only those ships in whose calculations he was part of the key four - and the losses were written off as the dangers of warp. After the war, flights through the gap became rare, and once in a quarter of a century, another robot truck disappeared, which no one was looking for. In total, according to Navi, about one hundred and ten ships fell; the Polarians found only eighteen crash sites - the rest fell on the ocean and the southern hemisphere.

> "The gifts of the gods are places of ruin. Sacred Objects are the details of broken starships."

Eighteen centuries ago, the lighthouse led to the death of a colonial starship, the wreckage of which lies at the bottom of the Bottomless Gap. ([[bezdonnaya-propast|Bottomless Abyss]]) The surviving crew members became the Foremothers and Forefathers, and the colonists became commoners. Only [[svyashchennye-predmety|objects]] from MMA, "metameson seal material", and people in capsules from it survived. Read more about this in the articles [[polari-world|The World of Polari]] and [[svyashchennye-predmety|Sacred Objects]].

### The Second Tree
Several members of the crew, the [[sadovniki|Gardeners]], watched the lighthouse and calculated that it would have enough fuel for two thousand hundred years. During this period, you can climb into orbit and reprogram the lighthouse so that it leads ships not to the planet, but next to it: then the next starship will notice the Polaris of the city, and isolation will come to an end. Aircraft did not survive, and the order, founded for this purpose, for centuries directed science to the creation of a flying machine. “In twenty-one centuries you have to get to the Star,” Adrian formulated the essence of the Second Tree. The key to the Star was [[absolyut|the Absolute]], which Navi called the "key" to the "castle" in a place inaccessible to mortals.

Navi explained why this is disastrous: Polaris people and "gods" have become different species, and the world of Homo Art Nouveau will turn Polaris into an amusement park or reserve. He offered Minerva a choice. If he survives, the order is likely to gain about eighty percent of his knowledge and the Absolute, rise to the Star, and change the signal. If he is killed, a couple more ships will break, neutrino navigation will become obsolete, the lighthouse will be forgotten, and he, having exhausted his energy, will remain silent: Polaris will not be found until he himself begins intergalactic flights.

## In "Fantasies"
In "Ad-ri-an!" Minerva in the fall of 1776 argues that while the Star “successfully destroys the ships of Homo Art Nouveau”, and survivors like Paul and Navi can be caught and neutralized. But in three hundred years, the Star will run out of energy, and protection will run out. So, for three hundred years, you need to study the device of the Star, build a dozen combat starships on its model and surround Polaris with a network of guns, and when the civilization of Homo Art Nouveau rots in luxury, carry the light through the galaxy - this will be the Third Tree. Erwin broke the plan: you can get to the Star only with the Absolute, that is, releasing Vittor from the Womb; experiments with the celestial ship Harmon showed that there is no air at the height of the Star; and such a breakthrough requires "ferocious tyranny." Earlier, on her birthday, Amber had suggested to Minerva that "there is more than one Star." ([[dorian-ember|Dorian Ember]])

In 1775, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] sent a "wave" as if he had visited the Star and Ulyana Sad returned him to the world. In Fantasia, the Chosen One — “the white-faced type who flew to the Star, chatted with Ulyana and returned alive” — became one of the five versions that split both branches of the Church.
