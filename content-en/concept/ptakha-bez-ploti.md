## Description

Bird without Flesh is one of the primordial Objects of the Mothers: it was owned by Elena the Traveler herself. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) In 1775, the item was kept in Shimmery, in the palace of King [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] in white stone [[lajem|Laem]]. Outwardly, it is a masterpiece of jewelry art: a golden sculpture of a petrel floating on flattened wings, with the thinnest feathers of white gold, black stars of sapphires along the edge of the wing and a topaz blue eye. The position of the polished crystal is almost invisible, making it seem that the bird is flying. The true miracle is the beak: it consists of light, a beam of rays emanates from the mouth of the petrel and breaks off after a couple of inches.

When the carrier of the [[pervokrov|firstblood]] puts his hand on the head of the Bird and says the keywords, a small blue light flies over the beak. It moves where it is ordered, and the wall or ceiling in front of the audience becomes transparent: a round “window” opens, the diameter of which is set by the manager. The wall remains in place - Magda Labelin, putting her finger in the "hole", felt the stone. Light can fly through obstacles and in the dark, hundreds of miles away. However, the bird has only sight, but not hearing: the court saw what was happening, but did not hear a word.

## The Road to Adrian

[[navi|Navi]] was the first to name the Bird among the items he needed to intercept [[paul|Paul]] over Smokey Dahlia: "With the help of the Bird, I will watch him from a distance, with the help of the Glove, I will quickly approach, and Hilt will strike." Minerva objected that the Bird was kept in Shimmery, and found the price — three priceless shrines — excessive. On the Light Sphere, the Bird, like the Gauntlet of Power and Ulyanina Dust, was displayed only as an unnamed point. ([[svetlaya-sfera|Radiant Sphere]]; [[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]; [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]])

Meanwhile, [[adrian|Adrian]] was making his own plan. It required two Sacred Subjects, [[chrevo|the Womb]] of the University of Faunterra and the Bird of Laem, the Darkwater essence of the luno-eye and the carrier of the firstblood. Adrian at first mistakenly considered [[kharmon|Harmon]] as a carrier, but then he captured two "hanid vir kanna" and handed them over to the Lady in Darkness, who kept them under the influence of the luno-eye. One-armed Adrian was going to give the Perst, and [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]] intended "something more interesting" - to control the Bird and the Womb. Lady in Darkness wrote to her ally Francis-Ilianus, and the king himself brought the Item. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) At the feast, his servants in white robes and turbans brought a palanquin, where instead of a man there was a bird.

> “It is my honor to present to you, gentlemen: Bird without Flesh, the personal Subject of Elena the Traveler. He is able to look at any distance, ignoring obstacles and darkness.

The King presented the Object to the Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] "as a sign of the renewal of a strong friendship" and asked to give the Lord Adrian full access to it. [[karen|Karen]] Lighthart did not sleep that night, thinking of a terrible combination: An object capable of seeing anything, and a Yankee tyrant eager to keep everything under control. [[otets-david|Father David]], urging Erwin to reconcile with the order, warned: The bird is a means of perfect intelligence, with it the army is visible at any distance.

## Battle for First Winter

After a series of experiments, Adrian made the Bird speak; the first successful test was observed by himself, Francis-Ilianus and the Second of the Five. The first thing, according to Adrian, he sent her to examine the Star: the light did not reach the surface, the range was not enough, but flew very close. "We saw that the goal of the Gardeners' Forefathers was still achievable!" he announced to Magda and Manson and promised to show them the Great Tree that evening, but the show postponed: Vittor Shayland knew how to suddenly appear anywhere, and Adrian did not want the Puppeteer to see his experiments. On December 2, 1775, in Main, one hundred and fifty miles from the First Winter, Adrian staged a festive spectacle for the court - watching the battle for the First Winter. ([[pervaya-zima|First Winter]]; [[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) To the sleeping Johan Reis, the marshman sniffed the powder and whispered in the ear of the crew, he put his hand on the Bird's head, and the light rushed over the mountains and glaciers, past the avalanche and the watchtower, to the valley with the castle.

Through the Bird, the court saw an icy labyrinth in front of the castle, trebuchets on the walls, Gordon Sue's keel squad rushing to the city, blocks of ice that Navi threw the Glove at Shayland's position, storming the city and fighting at the shaft. The second of the Five whispered what the blind Lady had seen; the most important thing seemed to her that the city was full of people. When the ringers shot down Minerva's balloon, Johan mistakenly approached Bird to the burning basket, and Manson saw straw scarecrows in it; rising half a mile, Bird noticed a flying boat over the rocks in which Minerva was sitting. Once exhausted, Johan lost power over the Object, and the light woke up again in Main, so he had to fly again. The next morning, when Bird again unfolded the valley of the First Winter in front of the window, Magda was proud to see that Adrian's calculation had been fulfilled: the Puppet Master and Origin had bloodied each other.

## After the battle

On 3 December, Vittor Shayland moved in with Adrian, hoping to receive the [[golos-boga|Voice of God]] and the Bird, but was imprisoned in [[chrevo|the Womb]]. Adrian then threatened Erwin over the Voice of God that through the Bird he would watch the First Winter and, if the white flag was not raised within half an hour, would blow up all the Northerners' Items; surveillance prevented the Origins from carrying out an evacuation. Johan Reiss died of the poison Magda had given him and collapsed on his face at the foot of the Bird. Without the firstblood carriers, Adrian could no longer launch the Object.

Magda Labelin dreamed that a bird without a flesh would flutter over the whole country: “let someone try to start a rebellion.”

## Flight to the Star

In the last chapter of the cycle, Adrian, offended by the violation of his security in the First Winter, demanded an experience with Bird in front of the Allies. He found Navi biased, and Minerva was invited to manage the Object as a neutral person. Adrian introduced her to the list of keys, she held a light on the room and got used to it. On command “straight up, through the roof” the light rose above the clouds into the black sky, where only the Star and the Moon were burning. Then the bird came out of the shadow of the planet, and the audience saw the sun "near the edges of the earth's firmament", and at the request of Navi Minerva turned the light, showing them a round Polaris. This flight predates Navi's account of the lighthouse and the world of the gods. Shocked, Minerva eventually left Bird to hug Nathaniel.
