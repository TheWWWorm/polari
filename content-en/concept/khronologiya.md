The chronology of the cycle reduces to one chronicle the dates that are called the Polari books themselves. Almost every chapter of the novels opens with two lines. The first is the date, for example, "March 21, 1774 from the Descent of the Foremothers", in second place: "Smolden, Duchy of Almera". Chapters about different heroes are mixed up, and sometimes they run ahead or go back a few weeks. The events below are arranged in order, not the way they appear in the books. For each period, it is indicated to which novel or interlude it belongs.

## How to Read Chronology
- The dates in the chapter titles are often approximate: "end of April", "mid-June", "July". Within the month, events are arranged in order of time if possible. Where the book does not name the exact day, it is stipulated.
- “[[kukla-na-trone|A Doll on the Throne]]” hardly names the exact days and even months. Its events are arranged by indirect instructions in the text and in neighboring interludes. Thus, the last chapters of the novel are already in April: according to the “Voice of Winter”, Minerva’s negotiations with Moran and the Holy Field happen after “March has passed, April has begun”, and Minerva returns to the capital during the spring fair.
- The storylines of all the storytellers (Erwin, Minerva, Harmon, Joaquin, Iona, Mark, Manson, Sybil, Alanis, Sofia and others) are compiled here monthly. Lady Sophia’s long voyage is also carried out in a separate section.
- Chapters are called code names of storytellers. For example, “Arrow” is [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], “Iskra” is [[minerva|Minerva]], “Coin” is [[kharmon|Harmon]], “Sword” is [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]. These names are mentioned where they help to find a place in the book.
- For calendars, months and holidays, see [[kalendar|Calendar]].

## The distant past
Here are collected only those dates of the past, which are directly named in the novels or in the “Guide to the lands of Polari”. The account comes from the [[soshestvie|Descent]] (year 0). According to Erwin in the last chapter of the first novel, [[pramater-agata|Agatha the Radiant]] saw the valley of the First Winter "eighteen centuries ago". Harmon, in the first chapter, thinks of the Foremothers as having "died seventeen centuries ago."

### The first centuries
- **1 year.** According to the Guide, the Forebears passed through the lands of the future [[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]] in search of a place for the city, rafted through Canaan and founded [[faunterra|Faunterra]]. ([[khanaj|Khanai]])
- **I century.** Arden, one of the oldest cities of Polari, was founded. Later, a temple was built in it.
- **35–36. "Agatha's Dilemma".** The Mother State then consisted of two cities, Faunterra and Orwell. In the year 35 there were the first skirmishes with Western nomads. In the spring of 36, Agatha learned about the horde's maneuver, but "broke the feather" and did not warn Faunterra. On the night of May 2, the Chavans broke into the city, and Wilhelm the Great turned the Persians against the people for the first time. ([[persty-vilgelma|Wilhelm's Fingers]]) So, according to legend, laid the foundations of a single empire. The story is recounted by [[navi|Navi]] in [[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]].
- **II-IV centuries.** Since the II century, the Southern Way was settled by farmers, in the IV century it was annexed to the Empire. In the North, for the sake of silver, [[lid|Leed]] and Main (third century) and Steel (fourth century) were founded. In the IV-V centuries, the [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] gradually grew from a herding village.
- **V century.** [[almera-zemlya|Almera]], then tiny, was the first to receive the status of duke.

### VI - XII centuries
- **6th century.** In the middle of the century, Baron Arthur Agatha became the Leed lord. In 572, he met with the abbot of Valencia in the Grotto of Cosuli, and from this, according to the Guide, the rise of the Agathan North began. ([[grot-kosuli|Roe Deer Grotto]]) In the 6th-7th centuries, fugitives from the Empire began to inhabit the forests of [[nortvud-zemlya|Northwood]].
- **VIII century. Light-bearers.** In 709 the Duke of Doyle, ruler of the South Road, attacked the North along with the Order of Wilhelm. He died in 730. In 735, the Light-bearers responded and captured the entire north of the Southern Way in four months. In the eighth century, as stated in “I Want a Pony”, the Crown “paled in comparison” with the power of this order. By the beginning of the 9th century, the Lightbearers controlled half of the Polari.
- **9th century.** King Orsayn of Myriametz ascended the throne in 805 and in the spring of 810 opposed the order with twenty-four thousand swords. He later surrendered Faunterra to the Light-bearers and moved the capital to Arden, and by 816 had assembled a new army. In the same century, Olbia was founded in [[darkvoter|Darkwater]].
- **XII century.** The Third Dynasty of Miriam is overthrown. The empire was shaken by the Crimson Troubles. In the West, the Iron Sun Empire grew and collapsed. The insurgent slaves fled north and founded Silens, the capital of the [[zakatnyj-bereg|Sunset Coast]]. The Volticians call this century their sixth.

### XIII - XVII centuries
- **XIII century.** Constantine's law is published: in any expedition outside the Empire include a person directly serving the Crown. Under this law, Baron Lawfert was sent to Erwin's exploits. ([[ekspedicija|Expedition beyond the River]])
- **1319.** Lord Lymon, a pioneer of the Shimmerian coasts, wanted to pass the strait in the Thousand Shards archipelago, but the sailors dissuaded him.
- **XIV century.** Under Lord Lexian II, the boarding house of Helena-u-Lake was founded. ([[pansion-eleny-u-ozera|Elena-by-the-Lake Boarding School]]) The owner of Eloise kept her coronation dress, although it is supposed to burn, and after a year and a half was overthrown. Under the vassalage agreement of this century, the House of Darkwater bears the royal title.
- **15th century.** King Darkwater declared war on the emperor. The [[rejs|Reis]]'s Shavans repulsed the meadow along Holliwell. ([[kholliwell|Hollywell]])
- **The 16th Century: The [[loshadinye-vojny|Horse Wars]].** Darian the Wicked has become "the most dangerous enemy of the empire" in its history. The city of Heart of Light held his army back for a year. After the wars, Lady Juliana declared the Shepherds of the Meadow a common possession, the Duchy of [[nadezhda|Hope]] was formed from the ruined counties, and Northwood's feud with Origin ended. ([[yuliana-velikaya|Juliana the Great]]) In the same century, the House deposed Lord Keylor and elected a new one. Erwin referred to this precedent in 1775.
- **1630, May.** The Faraday Marquis crossed the Thousand Shards archipelago. His on-board magazine in 1775 helped Navi pave the way for the fugitives.
- **XVII century.** At the beginning of the century, seven kings changed in [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] in ten years. By the middle of the century, after the Horse Wars, navigation flourished in the Empire. The largest mill in [[melnitsy|the Mills]], Arina, has been out of action since the early seventeenth century.

### Eighteenth century, before the beginning of the series
- **Early 18th century.** Under Emperor Alexian, an Air Bridge was built between Palace Island and the Holy See. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) The mechanics of the Dukes of Almer tried to build a dam on Halliwell.
- **1732.** The flight ceased to be an Origins' vassal, though the tribute pays off after that.

- **End of December 1748.** The events of [[yolka-episkopa|the Bishop's Christmas Tree]] interlude.
- **About 1750.** Gold War: Lighthart's house attacked Almera, and the lord did not intervene. ([[zolotaya-vojna|Golden War]]; [[dom-lajthart|House Lightheart]]) The “Fear Price” says that it “robbed six years ago.”
- **19 October 1755.** Captain Corvis writes a letter to Sir Clive Stagforth. ([[klajv-stagfort|Clive Stagfort]]) A week later, the plot fails. ([[shutovskoj-zagovor|Jesters' Conspiracy]]) Manson in the same year 1755 deprived of the rank of admiral and nobility, the Lighthart house is destroyed, and the Farway, according to the "Guide", ruled Hope since 1755. ([[henri-farvej|Henry Farway]])

([[mik|Mik]]; [[teper-ty-koldun|Now You Are a Sorcerer]]) The lords find a dead goddess in the bed of Dar and swear to be silent, and [[paul|Paul]] comes out of the lava ("The Price of Fear", the finale of "Humans and Gods").
- **1757–1769.** A year after Dar, a mysterious prisoner was put in Waymar prison. He escaped in 1769.
- **1762.** Lord Tellurian is going to visit his cousin, [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]].
- **1765.** A late portrait of Lady [[imperatritsa-ingrid|Ingrid]] was painted. At the time, [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] served as a junior frailty.
- **About 1771.** [[adrian|Adrian]] comes to power: in the summer of 1774, this is spoken of as an event “three years ago”.
- **1772.** Ulyanina Dust is taken from the vault of the cathedral in [[aleridan|Aleridan]]. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) About the same time, two years before the start of the cycle, [[rikhard-oridzhin|Richard Origin]] disappears into the sea.

## Differences in Dates
- **The 17th gift.** The Guide and The Price of Fear refer to it as August 1756. In Waymar in the spring of 1774, Harmon remembers that Dar arrived "a decade and a half ago", and at the end of the first novel refers to "eighteen years".
- **The Joke Conspiracy.** Corvis's letter is dated October 19, 1755, and the conspiracy failed a week later. But in 1774 it was referred to as "eighteen", "nineteen", "twenty years ago".
