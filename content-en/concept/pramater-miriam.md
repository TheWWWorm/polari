**Miriam the Dark-eyed** is one of the holy Mothers who came to the underworld at the [[soshestvie|Descent]]. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) In Scripture, and on the altar of any cathedral, her name is first: "Dark-eyed Miriam, followed by Yanmei the Merciful, then the Great Sophia, the Agatha the Radiant..." She protects love and pleasure, and according to legend was able to fly. From it were led by the kings of the first dynasties of Polaris, who ruled until the Scarlet Troubles.

## Face and Miracles
The most famous miracle of Miriam is flight. The legends of the [[kristalnye-gory|Crystal Mountains]] show the gorge where Miriam fell, “but not broken, but soared like a bird.” According to the masters of [[adrian|Adrian]], who studied the [[svyashchennye-predmety|Objects]], each Pramary performed miracles with their help: Elena saw through the barriers, Susan called the clouds, and “Miriam was able to climb into the clouds.” Its symbol is wings. They are worn on the robes of the priestess of her order, and the "Wings of Miriam" are referred to as her Subject. Later in “[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]” it was noticed that the Dark Eye “never flew above the clouds”: above it would have died of suffocation.

Because of this flight, Miriam is a favorite image of balloonists. The owner of the balloon in Laeme cried out: “Grow above the ground like Mother Miriam!” In the "Janmei Hunt" aeronauts after the death of Adrian feared a trial for heresy: "Only the Mother Miriam knew how to fly in heaven, and ordinary mortals should know their place!" ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) The story of "Flight of Miriam" is popular with artists.

The sailors call her "Miriam the Beauty." It is said that at the age of ninety, Miriam Dark-eyed “twisted novels.”

## Patron of Love
Miriam is the “pramatere of love.” She is the protector of passion and pleasure, and with her half life conflicted strict Yangmei the Merciful. ([[pramater-yanmej|Yanmei the Merciful]]) Before the wedding, [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] swore to love her husband in three ways: Miriam, Sophia and Emilia.
> “To love like Miriam is to desire, not to let the fire go out, to play with your loved one and enjoy it.”

Miriam's love is the love of a man and a woman: passion, flirtation, play, the joy of the flesh. Her first commandment is “the commandment of sincere love.” Priests at weddings pull "Love you Miriam". Young people are congratulated: “Let Miriam kiss, and Sofia embraces”, and if there is a quarrel, “Emilia will die”.

Miriam wrote the treatise “The Art of Free Love”. Harvey Horton matured to him by the age of thirteen and has since "spent his strength on Miriam's covenants." In the prayer taught by Laura Almera, Miriam is thanked “for the happiness of love and heartfelt joy.”

## Law Miriam
Miriam bequeathed that the first night of the girl belongs only to her: “The first flower will be plucked by the man whom the girl chooses with her heart.” It is the right of Miriam, or the right of the first night. The girl can keep it a secret, but is obliged to choose a confidant - a father, mother or a famous priest. Miriam's right is the plot of the story "Confident": the girl Rose Wingram asked the abbess about him in order to secretly give the prisoner the keys. ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]])

[[minerva|Minerva]] on the eve of the decisive day remembered that she never applied Miriam’s law. [[alanis-almera|Alanis]] in the delirium dreamed that “by right Miriam” singer Richie will belong to her. In Fantasias, Iona reproached Minerva for applying Miriam's law without taking her as a confidante. ([[fantazii|Fantasies]])

## Dark-eyed Dynasties
From Miriam led the first rulers of the state. Items and events are called by their eras:
- **The early Miriam dynasty.** With the Sixth Gift came the Cup of Joy.
- **The first dark-eyed dynasty.** King Marion Despot tried to dissolve the House, and this ended in his death and change of dynasty. ([[palata|Chamber of Representatives]]) There was a comedy in the theater.
- **The second dark-eyed dynasty.** Mysteries are in fashion.
- **The Third Dark-Eye Dynasty.** The time of the "Sweet Ages" and the heyday of tragedy. According to the "Guide to the Lands of Polari", this dynasty, overthrown in the XII century, was famous for much greater brilliance and luxury than the Yankees. Her furniture was appreciated even by Iona, although "the Yanmeans avoid all things Miriam."

"The Guide" tells about the struggle of the Myriamians with the Northern Order of the Light-bearers. King Horey Miriamets did not suppress their heresy. His successor, Orsein the Miriamite, fought Heinrich Leed in the early ninth century and suffered defeats. But in the end, the Miriamns won the Wars of Despair with “inhuman exertion of strength.” Agatha's reign was declared heresy, and the Third Dark-Eyed Dynasty entered the Sweet Ages. In ancient Faunterra, there was a cult of Miriam, who preached “complete freedom of love.”

The dynasty was cut short by the Scarlet Troubles. The rebels infiltrated the Holy Citadel and killed Emperor Algon III with the whole family. ([[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) It is said that the ghosts of the Myriam kings still cry in the Citadel dungeons about everything that the descendants of the Merciful took from them. Dark-eyed kings are buried in the crypt of the Farewell Temple in Arden, next to the relics of the Forebears. The frescoes of the crypt celebrate hunting, feasts and love.

## Descendant
The sailor, nicknamed the [[potomok|Descendant]], assured that not everyone was killed by Miriam. The last Lord had two nephews. They fled to a deserted island called Maternal, and for centuries all the surviving Myriamians gathered there. The Black Descendant considered himself their heir, and [[mark-voron|Mark]] jokingly called him "the offspring of the first dynasty of emperors."
