**Quotations** — a selection of short lines and thoughts from characters in the Polari series, as well as proverbs, commandments, and sayings heard in the world of [[polari-world|Polari]]. The Russian source quotes the books verbatim; omissions within quotations are marked with an ellipsis, “…” Each quotation is followed by its speaker, book, chapter, and circumstances.

## Erwin Origin

> “Nothing beautiful should smell of poverty”
— [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], Arrow, Coin, Spark, chapter 2, “Arrow” (looking down on [[pervaya-zima|First Winter]] from above)

> “This cave, gentlemen, leads to the Underworld. We have found a Gift of the Gods.”
— [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], Arrow, Coin, Spark, chapter 22, “Arrow” (to his companions on the explorada)

> “If there is one thing I have never liked in my life, my lady, it is order.”
— [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], Only One Star, chapter “Arrow” (to [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], standing beside her on the throne)

> “There are no clean victories. Every triumph feeds the vultures.”
— [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], A Doll on the Throne, chapter “Northern Bird – 1” (to his sister [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]], on the price of victory)

> “I hate you, Lord Chancellor. Begin every morning with those words.”
— [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], A Doll on the Throne, chapter “Spark – 10” (to [[minerva|Minerva]], advising her not to conceal her hostility towards him)

> “We are the ones who make traditions, General… We live in an age of change!”
— [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], A Doll on the Throne, chapter “Northern Bird – 6” (to General Gore of the spark troops, who objects to a northerner commanding the army)

> “I am Erwin Sophia Jessica of Agatha's lineage. Enough sitting idle. Let us go to war.”
— [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], The Shadow of the Great Tree, chapter “Arrow — 5” (to a garrison commander, after revealing his name)

> “Characters are forever concealing things; that is what gives a plot its intrigue. Real people lie in just the same way.”
— [[ervin-oridzhin|Erwin]], The Shadow of the Great Tree, chapter “Arrow — 8” (to [[otets-david|Father David]], disbelieving his account)

## Proverbs and Sayings of the World

> “There is only one star”
— a saying about the emperor in the capital, Arrow, Coin, Spark, chapter 17, “Spark” (quoted by [[itan|Ethan]]: “That is what people say here,” in [[faunterra|Faunterra]])

> “My vassal's vassal is not my vassal. It has always been so, my lord.”
— a northern custom, Arrow, Coin, Spark, chapter 8, “Arrow” (Captain [[teobart|Theobart]] says this to Erwin)

> “Origins do not retreat!”
— a saying of the Origins, Arrow, Coin, Spark, chapter 29, “Arrow” (Iona, in a playful exchange with Erwin)

> “You will make decisions without our help. That is a mortal's right, and a mortal's curse.”
— Scripture, Arrow, Coin, Spark, chapter 45, “Coin” (quoted by [[otets-david|Father David]])

> “Accept your place in the world with dignity. You are not the sovereign, my lord. Accept it”
— Voice of Winter (an inscription on the ribbon around a snowdrop brought to Erwin by a servant with the words “A gift from the empress”; the first sentence is the first commandment, see The Gates of Trust)

> “Use what you know, sell what you have.”
— Count Vincent Shayland's favourite saying, Humans and Gods, chapter “The Price of Fear-1” ([[vittor-shejland|Vittor]]'s father)

> “If every way out of a situation is bad, break the situation itself.”
— [[klajv-stagfort|Clive Stagfort]]'s advice, A Doll on the Throne, chapter “Spark – 7” (this is how he taught Mira to play [[stratemy|Stratems]])

> “The hungry do not believe the well-fed.”
— the moral of [[ded|Grandfather]]'s parable of the wolf and the dog, The Yanmei Hunt, chapter “Arrow — 4”

> “Desire is a valuable thing… Someone who wants a great deal is useful”
— a saying of [[moger-bakli|Buckley]]'s employer, Useful People

> “May everything you dream of come true!”
— a wish for Spring Dawn, The Yanmei Hunt, chapter “Witness — 1” (see [[kalendar|Calendar]])

> “The me of today is better than the me of yesterday”
— a saying among the clinic's patients, The Yanmei Hunt, chapter “Witness — 1” (repeated by Dorothy — [[sibil-nortvud|Sybil Northwood]])

> “A decision comes from knowledge, not from will”
— [[pramater-agata|Agatha]] in Moments, Humans and Gods, chapter “Witness-3”

> “It is the sincere, not the all-knowing, who pass through the gates.”
— Lord Emmon Darklin, the [[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]'s castellan, The Gates of Trust (to [[gerion|Gerion]] and the beggar, who made the wrong choice, after the prophet [[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]] passed through the third gate)
