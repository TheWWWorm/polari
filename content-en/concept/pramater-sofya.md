Sophia the Great is one of the Holy Mothers, the third in the canon of Scripture after Miriam and Janmei. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) She is called “the kindest of the Foremothers” and “the mother of all children.” Sofia patronizes the family hearth, care and maternal love, and in the people - weddings and the birth of children. According to Polari's Guide to the Lands, she is the patroness of the South Road. ([[yuzhnyj-put|Southern Way]])

## Patron of Motherhood
The main covenant of Sophia is to give birth to children and put your soul into them. [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] before the wedding divided love into three types, and Sofia love is primarily the love of an adult to a child. It also applies to the husband: “to be with him caring and kind, and condescending when he is weak.” The prayer taught by Laura Almera thanks Sophia “for the health of the parents and their care.”

According to beliefs, Sophia herself brings the souls of children into the world. Valerie Gracend, preparing to become a mother, said that “the Holy Mother Sophia brings to our world the soul of a child.” ([[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]]) She read all the works of the Great Sophia and decided, contrary to custom, to breastfeed the child herself. Dorothy, demanding from [[navi|Navi]] the truth about her daughter, reminded him: “Sophia the Great, the patroness of mothers...”

[[minerva|Mira]] recalled Sophia's words:
> “A woman’s life is too valuable to be entrusted to the care of a man alone.”

According to the Scriptures, Sophia admitted that a noble lady could have more than one man (see [[alter|alter]]).

## Symbol and temples
The symbol of Sofia is a colossus. It is worn on the robes of the priestess of her order. Sofiyevsky temples are “carved with plump marble babies.” Early media outlets mentioned the Basilica of Saint Sophia in [[labelin|Labelin]]. The flagship galleon of the House of Labelin is called the “Great Sophia”.

In the story "Confident" the action takes place in the monastery of Sophia the Wonderworker. ([[konfidentka|The Confidante]]) The charter allows his maids to wear jewelry, and the abbess herself rules weddings. “I serve Sophia for good reason,” she says. The abbess repeats to the girls: “Foremother Sophia has no unloved daughters.” The nuns pray: “Saint Sophia, all-beneficent mother...” Rose Vingrem wanted to go to the monastery to "serve Foremother Sophia", because, according to her, "serve Foremother Sophia means to serve love."

## Sofiina Days
In honor of Sofia celebrate Sofiina days - the time of weddings and engagements. According to the church calendar, it is three days before the feast of Sophia. The monastery of Sophia the Wonderworker was buzzing these days, receiving a thousand guests. The wedding greeting reads: “Let Miriam kiss, and Sophia embraces.” In the southern wedding ceremony, Pramatery Sophia is honored more than in the capital.

## Rod Sofya
Sophia’s family is the third most famous. Emperors most often take wives of the Yangmei family, "sometimes - the family of Sophia the Great." In Fantasies they say that “Sophias are the best wives”, and even “a step above Agatha”. The Yelenites dream of the brides of the Sophia family, and the Sofievos dream of the “great-grandchildren of the Merciful Yangmei.”

From the genus Sophia occur:
- [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] and his daughter [[magda-labelin|Magda]]. Magda called herself “Pramatery’s granddaughter Sophia,” who is “not without feminine wisdom.”
- Marquis Valerie Gracend; ([[valeri-grejsend|Valerie Graysend]])
- The prophet Francis-Ilian, before hermitage, was “the glorious Francis-Ilian of the family of Sophia the Great.” ([[frantsisk-ilian|Francis-Ilian]]) He with a smile lamented to the interlocutor-lord that Pramatery Sophia did not endow him with the sense of humor inherent in the kind of interlocutor;
- Prince Shimmery Hector; ([[gektor|Hector of Shimmery]])
- General [[aleksis-smajl|Alexis Smile]], Baron Smile;
- Scientist [[nikolas-olli|Nicholas Ollie]].

## Sofia and Agatha
Folk travel folklore contrasts the "good mother-Sophia" with the "evil sterve-Agata": as if Agatha was jealous of Sophia's family happiness and began a centuries-old feud. The Guide explains that in reality the hostility of the South and the North is purely material: the Lords of the Travels dug into the silver mines of Main and Leed, and the Northerners dreamed of going to the sea. In [[poleznye-lyudi|Useful People]], [[moger-bakli|Moger Buckley]] assured the abbot that the "mental pain of quarreling with Agatha" was tearing Sophia's heart apart.
