**Lyudmila** is one of the Holy Mothers, the “Promoter of the Farmers”, the patroness of agriculture and weather. ([[pramateri|Foremothers]]) She is especially admired by farmers.

## The Protector of Peasants
"Like many peasants," Veselchak, [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]]'s companion, prayed to Foremother Lyudmila. ([[veselchak|Jolly]]) As he entered the Lands of the Crown and looked at the lights of Faunterra, he whispered, "Help us Lyudmila..." Joaquin, dissatisfied with the easy victory at the [[bitva-za-faunterru|capture of Faunterra]], said, "Don't anger Ludmila, Joe."

In "[[lyudi-i-bogi|Humans and Gods]]", part of the peasants-Sunflowers after the campaign engaged in robbery, instead of "toughly taking up agriculture, as bequeathed to Foremother Lyudmila." ([[podsnezhniki|Snowdrops]]) Their chief Salem of Summerswith condemned the robbers. ([[salem|Salem of Summersweet]])

## Power over the weather
According to legend, Lyudmila knew how to control the weather. In "Humans and Gods" it was said that the Second of the Five and his monks found her secret in the sacred texts and unraveled it. So Lord [[adrian|Adrian]] seems to be able to direct the wind for his heavenly fleet. Adrian's soldiers endlessly argued about it: "Rei-Roy, Lyudmila, the heavenly fleet, the lazy right."

## Rod Lyudmila
According to [[alanis-almera|Alanis]], Lyudmila’s family is “famous for devotion.” Therefore, Adrian trusted the mayor of Faunterra, Baron Escher, who took the objects of the Dynasty from the Throne Citadel. ([[baron-esher|Baron Esher]]; [[prestolnaya-tsitadel|Imperial Citadel]]) From the Ludmila family come:
- Lady Vanessa-Lilit, daughter of Arctin-Lyon. ([[vanessa-lilit|Vanessa-Lilith]]) The guests in her house were given a drink of health "for the Mother Lyudmila";
- Dagober of the battalion of the First Winter, the swordsman of Damon Origin;
- Captain Hugo Anne Margaret Grace;
- Lord [[lord-klarens|Clarence]] Madeleine Fiona, steward of the imperial stables and alter of Countess Sybil Northwood;
- Marquess of Rouen Jeanne Ludmila of Aberville. Ethan described her as "a dull person", "always surrounded by a cloud of sadness".
