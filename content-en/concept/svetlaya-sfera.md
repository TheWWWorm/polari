## Description

The sphere consists of two bluish-transparent rings: the outer about ten inches across, the inner about eight. There is a gap of half an inch between them. An unattached inner ring floats in the air. If you click on it, it spins and turns into a shimmering ball of blue light reflecting everything around. The real object weighs a couple of ounces, warm to the touch, and its ring rotates endlessly, without friction. According to the register, the Sphere is a Great Sacred Object from the seventeenth Gift: it has “at least two supernatural properties.” Its spiritual meaning is interpreted as follows: the rotation of a small ring with the immobility of a large one is a symbol of the cyclical movement of life and the immutability of the will of the gods. In the Shayland register, it is drawn as two transparent rings: an ellipse within a circle.

## History

**Dar.** The Sphere came into the world with the seventeenth Gift of the Gods on the west bank of [[torrej|Torrey]], in the county of Shayland. Because of the Gift of Shayland, they fought with the Westerners, and the [[dom-oridzhin|Origins]] helped them. According to Vittor, the Gift contained 142 Items, of which thirty-nine went to his father. Sphere and Rose of Happiness were exhibited on the altar of Vivian Cathedral in [[uejmar|Waymar]]. In the August 1756 interlude Now You're a Sorcerer, when the Sphere and the Rose of Happiness were already standing on the altar of the cathedral in Waymar, the young Nathaniel, that is, [[navi|Navi]], taught the orphan Mick to awaken the Sphere with the word "idyan." ([[teper-ty-koldun|Now You Are a Sorcerer]]; [[mik|Mik]]) By the word "Adyin" the Sphere unfolded a light map of Polaris with scatterings of dots.

**An imprint in the box.** In the bed of the Seventeenth Gift, in the vitreous mass of the wall of the second level, there remained the imprint of two rings - "one flat, the second a little too much." In the summer of 1775 he was shown to pilgrims by a deacon-conductor; [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]], who first saw the Sphere a year earlier, only then “fully felt” its divinity.

**Nathaniel and the Sphere.** According to Navi, once he himself tried to find Paul with the help of the seeker, but in Polaris the Sphere is “shortsighted”, and he did not take this into account: Paul was out of her sight.

**In Paul's hands.** Thirteen years later, the Sphere came to Waymar prisoner [[paul|Paul]]. He recognized her as a "searcher" and, unlike Nathaniel, was not mistaken: experiments with other Objects showed him how they had weakened in Polaris, and he realized that Nathaniel was alive, but out of sight of the Sphere. With her help, Paul found an unknown Gift beyond the River.

**Sale (1774).** On Paul's advice, Count [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]] gave the Sphere for sale to the inconspicuous merchant [[kharmon|Harmon]]. Paul explained the idea as follows: let the “cluster” walk around the world and offer the Object to everyone - the Sphere will be taken away from him, then from those who took it, and sooner or later it will get to Nathaniel. Sphere was set to give Paul a signal if he was near Navi. Officially, he explained this by the costs of marriage with Iona. ([[iona-oridzhin|Iona Origin]]) The Maximianite monks substituted a counterfeit for the Object, and Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]] bought the fake for 36 thousand hilts. Harmon returned the original with the cunning and cost of the deaths of [[polli|Polly]] and Snape (see The Sale of the Bright Sphere). ([[snajp|Snipe]]; [[lzhe-sfera]]) The raiders, led by a northerner nicknamed Ice, burned down the monastery. Erwin suggested that there were two fakes and that one of them went to the Puppeteer as part of [[absolyut|the Absolute]].

**The secret is revealed.** [[iona-oridzhin|Iona]] learned about the sale from Joaquin and for a long time hid the shame of her husband. Finally, she asked Wittor, "Why did you sell the Light Sphere?", accusing him of secretly selling off part of the property of the house to enemies of her kind, the Labelins. Her source said that the Count had sold the Item “for love” and Iona thought with bitter irony that if she had found out about it a year ago, such a gesture would have made her love her husband. In a dove letter, Iona confided to her brother, "For my sake, for my marital happiness last spring, Vittor sold the Light Sphere to Duke Labelin through Harmon Merchant and Joaquin Yves Hanna." Erwin recalled that he had heard about the purchase of Labelin before and asked about Father David's Sphere at the burned monastery, and then told Minerva that the Sphere belonged not to the Dynasty, but to the Shaylands.

**In Harmon (1774–1775).** Harmon took the Sphere to [[shimmeri|Shimmery]] and kept it in the basement of the manor in Melison, wrapping it in Silent Jack's camsol. Over the [[bezdonnaya-propast|bottomless abyss]], the Sphere came alive in his hands for the first time. The outer ring was pulsated with blue, and the bottom of the abyss became visible: a dark lake and a network of colored lights connected by lines above it. Harmon stunned the sphere of the handy Marquis of Booth, and there was not a scratch on it.

**Adrian's.** The sphere was taken by the leader of the "goat people" Zandur, and [[adrian|Adrian]] took it from him as a war trophy. Adrian promised to give the Item to the Labelins before the end of the year. In the summer of 1775, he sent the original and a copy in the box as a gift to Queen Madeline (Lady in Darkness) along with the [[poyas-kolduna|Sorcerer's Belt]], calling the Sphere "lost by you", and hoping that Harmon, in whose hands the Sphere once "spoke", would be the bearer of the firstblood. ([[ledi-vo-tme|Lady in the Dark]]) In the same letter, Adrian hinted to the Queen that the "visitor" was not one, but three: the woman died, and both men are probably alive. Harmon was sent along with this embassy, but he secretly opened the casket and drove it to [[minerva|Minerva]]. In the palace, Sphere curled up by herself, [[navi|Navi]] yelling "It's a bomb!" and threw it out the window. The object was found in the garden.

**A trap.** Navi explained to Minerva Paul's plan. The Sphere is set to give Paul a signal if he's anywhere near Navi. Then Paul will turn on the deconstructor, and the Sphere annihilates, destroying everything within a radius of about five yards. That's why Navi kept her away from herself. In the First Winter, the Sphere was kept in the crypt of the Cathedral of Agatha the Radiant.

**Death of Paul (December 2, 1775).** In the Battle for the First Winter, Navi turned a trap against the creator. ([[bitva-za-pervuyu-zimu|Battle for First Winter]]) On the altar of the underground chapel under the mountain, he put a fake, and the real seeker was hidden in a niche, where he drove Paul. Paul powered the deconstructor ("[[golos-boga|Voice of God]]"), and the invisible sphere absorbed everything within a five-yard radius around the Subject in the niche, along with Paul himself. Paul fell into a bottomless pit, where Navi fell. The Sphere itself was in the center of this zone and apparently disappeared with it (it is not explicitly stated in the text). Before the explosion, Paul, deciding that the bomb was on the altar, managed to shout to Navi: “Run from the altar! It's a bomb!"

## Properties

Lady [[karen|Karen]] in the collection edition of Arrow, Coin, Spark recalls that the Sphere came with the Seventeenth Gift, and the Forebears did not see it in the eye, so that it is not connected with their deeds. ([[strela-moneta-iskra-kollektsionnoe|Arrow, Coin, Spark (Collector's Edition)]]) The ancestors only predicted some of its properties. Lucian, the inventor of the ball bearing, wrote that one day the secret of a frictionless bearing would be revealed, capable of spinning forever. Father Jose wrote about the Seeker in the form of two concentric spheres. This prediction, as it turned out later, came true literally.

As explained by Navi, the Sphere is a "searcher", a device for finding other Objects. It gives neither the image of the Object, nor the name, nor the function, but shows only its position and "potential" - the remaining reserve of energy: the Glove of Power, the Bird without the Flesh and the Dust of the Ulyana look on it the same points. ([[perchatka-mogushchestva|Glove of Might]]; [[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]; [[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) The probability of meeting Navi with the seeker, according to his calculations, would be less than a percent, if not for the secret order: seeking to study both the Objects and the carriers of the firstblood, he creates “currents” that bring them together, and the probability increased to almost two-thirds. She marks with conditional marks Objects and people with primrose. In the world of the gods, the air is filled with ether, and in Polaris it is not, so here the seeker is “nearsighted” and almost useless. Navi emphasized that the Light Sphere is an Object, and it should not be confused with the "sphere", the magical environment of the world of the gods. It seemed to Harmon that the Sphere had warned him twice about the danger: over the abyss and in the palace. Father David told him that the Sacred Object, like a mirror, strengthens the owner’s temper.
