## Origins

The womb is “the largest of all sacred objects.” It came into peace with the Seventeenth Gift in 1756, in the lands of the [[dom-shejland|Shaylands]], and lay on the fifth level of the lodge - the most dangerous of all. When [[dzhoakin|Joaquin]] accompanied two guests on the bed in the “Yanmean Hunt”, the man asked to show the imprint of the Womb, but the deacon conductor refused to lead them deeper than the second level and only indicated the direction. ([[yanmejskaya-okhota|The Yanmei Hunt]]) By the beginning of the “Shadow of the Great Tree”, the Object was stored at the University of Faunterra, where [[adrian|Adrian]] at one time handed over two dozen items of the Dynasty for study. ([[ten-velikogo-dreva|The Shadow of the Great Tree]])

As explained by [[navi|Navi]] in the cycle finale, the Sacred Objects are crash-surviving artifacts from MMA, a material with exceptional strength. Only they could survive the crash “and also people in MMA capsules.” Adrian immediately clarified: “In the Womb,” and Navi confirmed that one such capsule reached the Polarians and was called the Womb. Of his starship that crashed at the Seventeenth Gift, Navi said, "Lylanna died on impact. The womb preserved her body, but a sharp exit from the anabiosis destroyed her.” From these words it follows that a transparent box, "a form very similar to the coffin", in which in August 1756, the emperor Tellurian, Prince Adrian, Ayden Almera and Desmond Origin found a dead "goddess", and was a Womb; right in the text this identification is made only by the words of Navi.

## Properties

Navi described Minerva’s Womb as an object whose function she would not even understand: it puts a person in a state necessary to survive events that in Polaris can not happen, and with an unsuccessful withdrawal from this state, a person can die. With these words, the young man’s face “for some reason became very sad.” Of the thirty-nine items then in Faunterra, only four, according to Navi, could work independently; except for the Glove on Minerva's hand, they were all dangerous - the "scalpel" (Hand of the Sign), the Womb and the [[vechnyj-efes|Eternal Hilt]]. ([[ruka-znakharki|Healer's Hand]]) According to him, the Objects store energy in proportion to the mass, and the heaviest of them - the Womb - with "hot annihilation" could evaporate the entire Smokey Dal. ([[dymnaya-dal|Smoky Distance]])

When the carrier of the [[pervokrov|firstblood]] puts the palm on the lid, the crystal coffin is filled with a soft yellow glow, and the lid is strained, ready to close. After the keywords, the Womb flickers and closes; the air inside turns white, filled with fog, the moisture turns into ice crystals. According to the explanation of one of the initiates, the Object plunges a person into a certain state, “as if freezes”, and it is impossible to get out of there until the Womb is opened and the prisoner is unfrozen.

## Adrian's Experiences

The Fleshless Womb and Bird were the two subjects upon which Adrian's plan against the Puppeteer was built; for their subjugation, the Darkwater essence of the luno-eye and the bearer of the firstblood, the captive Shavan Juhan Reis, were also needed. ([[ptakha-bez-ploti|Bird without Flesh]]; [[yukhan-rejs|Johan Reis]]) Adrian carried the Womb with him in an armored Object wagon. Shavanka [[niza|Niza]] saw the Lord stroke the Womb and said: “Here is my gift,” and to the question of the Ganty of Biray, to whom the gift was given, he replied: “To him who goes to the lighthouse.” ([[ganta-biraj|Ganta Birai]]) Niza considered the subject creepy and advised Magda to stay away from it: in it, she thought, lives the Spirit of the Worm, digesting anyone who gets caught.

The first experience failed. Johan Reis, at the suggestion of the swamp man, put his hand on the lid, Ganta Birai threw a rat inside, Johan said the keywords twice, but the Womb did not respond, and the rat got out of the coffin. Lady-in-the-Dark promised to prepare a "different approach". Adrian later reported that the experiments were successful.

## Conclusion of the Puppet

On December 3, 1775, [[vittor-shejland|Vittor Shayland]], wearing [[absolyut|the Absolute]], moved to Adrian in Main. Having found out from him the numbers of the Objects and the keys of the deconstructor, Adrian ordered the count to fly to the Star, and when he refused in fear, he stunned him with a spark discharge into the scruff. A cart with a crystal coffin was rolled into the hall for cotton; Lady-in-the-Dark's servants threw Vittor into it, Johan put his palm to the lid and repeated the keywords - and the Womb slammed shut, and the body of the Puppeteer was covered with frost.

The operation was carefully prepared. The first question is whether the Absolute will not protect from the spark - Adrian, Francis-Ilian and Count [[kuindar|Quindar]] unsuccessfully searched for hundreds of hours in the sacred texts, and the answer was found in the essay by Abbot Amessin on the resurrection of the Chosen One. About twenty rehearsals were held, in which the role of the Puppeteer was played by criminals convicted by the Main court: everything had to be done for a brief spark fainting. Francis-Ilianus according to the allegory of the Forefather Evrian concluded that a person after freezing remains alive and completely healthy, so placement in the Womb will not make the Absolute work; there is a risk of death only with unsuccessful defrosting. Adrian thanked Lady-in-the-Dark: without the "dark witchcraft" they would not have conquered either Juhan or the Womb itself.

## Fate

Soon Johan Reis died of poison, and Adrian did not have any carriers of the firstblood, able to unfreeze the prisoner. The Origins and Minerva demanded that Adrian give them the Womb, and called "Absolute and Womb" a fine compensation. Magda and Adrian, on the contrary, dreamed of getting firstblood, getting Ulyana Dust from the Pieces of Heaven, writing on the lid of the Womb "Give us the Absolute" and waking the prisoner. ([[ulyanina-pyl|Ulyana's Dust]]) Navi warned that Adrian would give the Order of [[sadovniki|Gardeners]] the Absolute, “which is stored in the Womb.” By the end of the cycle, Vittor remained frozen in Adrian's Womb.

## In "Fantasies"

In Fantasia, a possible future, in the spring of 1776, by a peace agreement negotiated by Magda Labelin, the “man in the coffin” came under the personal control of her father, the Duke [[moris-labelin|Maurice Labelin]]: Magda did not want to give it to the Origins, and they did not agree to leave it “in the hands of a fanatic from the order.” The fate of the prisoner was to be decided by the church court. Maurice "pulled a crystal coffin out of Adrian's paws" and let the Origins look at the frozen Puppeteer and spit on him. By the summer, the Womb was kept in the Throne Citadel of Faunterra. When Lady Iona in the First Winter saw Vittor in her bedroom, Minerva, Erwin and their men threw in the capital asking if he had escaped from the crystal coffin. The answers reassured: “the white face remains where it is supposed to be,” the sentinels follow the charter, with the Womb “present overseers from both branches of the Church.” Kair Aubrey claimed that Iona saw a ghost: “The womb is a dark matter. Maybe the body is stored, and the soul flew out of it.”
